# Competitive Research — protocol (advisory-routed)

> A disciplined protocol for studying a competition **venue**, ported slim from the OpenClaw private build.
> Its outputs are **ADVISORY ONLY** and terminate as confidence-stamped entries in
> `vault/COMPETITION_LEARNINGS.md`, under **that file's advisory contract**. This protocol never emits a hard
> creative constraint, and it is the **highest homogenization-risk tool in the kit** — treat it accordingly.

## ⚠️ The fence (read first)
This protocol describes a **crowd's taste**. The crowd does not get a vote on Lofn's. Every finding is
advisory, venue+modality-scoped, INDIGNATION-exempt, and only a **human** may promote one to a constraint.
If studying "what wins" ever starts steering the art toward a house average, stop — that is the exact
homogenization the distinctiveness gates and the golden-output quarantine exist to prevent.

## Protocol
1. **Sample.** Pull the venue's recent top-N placing works (and a few that didn't place). Note the venue and
   modality explicitly.
2. **Hypothesize (≥5, falsifiable).** Write ≥5 hypotheses about *why* they placed — phrased as structural,
   testable claims (legibility-at-thumbnail, subject impossibility, hook clarity, container/interior
   compression), **not** taste edicts ("use a warm palette"). A hypothesis you can't falsify is a bias.
3. **Gate.** Run the mandatory check from the advisory contract: **"Would acting on this have hurt our best
   past entry?"** If yes → discard it.
4. **File as advisory.** Survivors become **LOW-confidence** entries in `vault/COMPETITION_LEARNINGS.md`,
   tagged `{theme-type · venue · modality · verdict · confidence · one transferable rule}`, LOW until a
   second run or a human corroborates. They inform Phase-0/Phase-1 *reasoning* only — never the ICB.

## What this protocol may NOT do
- It may not write an **aesthetic constraint** (auto-writes are failure-ledger / process notes only).
- It may not suppress or soften **triggered-INDIGNATION** work.
- It may not **leak across venue/modality** — a NightCafe image lesson never touches a music run.
- It may not run during generation — it is a **between-runs** study, not an inline step.
