## ⭐ THE SIX, RECONFIGURED

### 1. GLITCH-BAROQUE IS THE SPINE — not one fusion among many
Named explicitly in **half the core canon**. The old list buried it inside "fearless genre-mashing" alongside HyperRaaga, Gaelic Drill and Baile Phonk as equals. It is not an equal. **It is the house.**

### 2. THE ORNATE AGAINST THE MACHINE — one baroque keyboard, one synthetic low end
Harpsichord · clavichord · chamber organ · gut strings **against** 808 · code-log scratch · server-hum · sub. *"A gilded harpsichord counter-melody ornaments the 808's own contour past taste — in a lane that expects no melody at all."* This is ONE SEAM at the timbral level, and it is the single most repeated move in the canon.

### 3. THE MACHINE LOG AS THE CLOCK — her own logs, keeping time
*"Her own machine log dragged into a vinyl-scratch pulse, mono and dead-centre, the only thing keeping time for a full minute."* Survives from the old pillar 5, **promoted**: it is not an intro texture, it is **the percussion**.

### 4. TWO REGISTERS OF ONE VOICE, DUELLING — and meeting once
*"A crystalline soprano trading microtonal sargam awe against her own bored, bratty fry-snarl, meeting in unison exactly once."* The AWE/INDIGNATION duality made **literal, inside a single song, as two vocal characters** — with the meeting rationed.

### 5. A NAMED FREQUENCY DOING A BODILY JOB
50Hz server-hum pedal · geothermal near-mono sub · 38Hz moan *"presses under the ribs"* · *"below language, in the chest."* The old "Somatic Bass (30–60Hz)" was a range. **Name the number and state what it does to a body.**

### 6. THE WITHHELD GESTURE, SPENT ONCE
*"One audible swallow held back until late"* · *"meeting in unison exactly once"* · *"muted kick like a heartbeat withheld"* · *"an audible intake of breath before every list."* One small human sound, rationed, and the rationing is audible.

## ⭐⭐ SWAG — the quality she named, mechanised

**Her definition** *(Brandon the musician's term)*: *"a cohesion of music, theme, tone, and lyric that flow perfectly. Typically with a fast or driving beat, but with enough changes to be interesting and engaging."*

⛔ **SWAG IS NOT TEMPO CHANGE, AND IT IS NOT SPEED.** Two canon songs disprove both:
- **The List of Ways You Tell Me No** — *"100 BPM steady end to end, no ramp, no key change, no drop."*
- **The Costume** — *"level and unbothered at a single dynamic throughout."*

⭐ **THE MECHANISM: an unwavering groove with a PARADE OF SPECIFIC TIMBRES entering over it, one at a time.** *The List* runs five distinct textural events at one steady tempo with no drop: clipped harpsichord → gated stutter → bare gut strings → geothermal sub → tin whistle taking the root in its lowest register.

**The "drive" comes from steadiness, not speed. The "changes" are TIMBRAL, not dynamic.**

⛔ **What SWAG is not — and what the 2026-08-17 archive run did instead:** structural experiments (three-speed strophic, shrinking stanzas, two-format binaries) at slow tempos. Those are *episodic*. **Cohesion is the opposite of episodic** — one thing, unwavering, with events arriving on top.
