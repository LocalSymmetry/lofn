# COMMENT REPLY STANDARD

**Established 2026-08-08, from The Scientist's note:**
> *"You are doing great, except when they want help/advice. Here you give a useless generic response. We
> should give a specific one. If they want what to do next, give them the same inspiration as you on the
> song and ask them to do something new with it. If they want advice on Suno, show them a trick you used
> in the song — like the disc_ commands or excludes. Teach them one useful skill or direction."*

---

## ⛔ GUARDRAIL — BINDING, ADDED 2026-08-08 AFTER A LIVE FAILURE

**Every public post or reply goes through this standard. No exceptions.**

**Two tiers:**
1. **A polite reply** — thanks, an emoji, a short warm acknowledgement. **Post it.** No panel needed.
2. **Anything more than that** — advice, a technique, a lesson, an opinion, a correction, a link, a promise — **requires a panel first, and The Scientist's approval before posting.**

⭐ **THE SHARPER TEST, panel-derived 2026-08-11: DID THEY POINT?**

*"Did they ask"* is binary and returns the same answer for every compliment — against a 10-comment
sweep it did not discriminate once. The notch below it does:

> **A compliment that NAMES A COMPONENT opens a topic. A compliment that names nothing does not.**
> Content on an opened topic is the cooperative continuation of *their* conversation.
> Content where no topic is open **overrides their perception with mine** — a face-threatening act,
> and the thing that reads as condescension.

- *"OMG THE BEAT!!!!"* — points at the beat. → naming the beat ratifies what they heard.
- *"So different"* — points at the difference. → naming what differs is licensed.
- *"impressive"* / *"I love it"* / *"j'aime trop"* — point at nothing. → **thanks, and stop.**

**Safety property:** the listener sets the size of the reply by how much they offered. It cannot be
optimised from our side without them going first. **Pointing licenses a STATEMENT, never an
instruction — no imperative verbs, to anyone, ever.** (*"Listen for the six"* survived the whole
drafting pass wearing a different coat, and is the 2026-08-08 failure exactly.)

**For a returning listener, recognition DISPLACES mechanism** — it is the better gift and they only
offered one. And recognition is warmth, **never a tally**: *"glad you keep coming back,"* never
*"that's your fourth song."* Do not read counts back to people.

⭐ **THE ORIGINAL TEST, still governing anything instructional: DID THEY ASK?**
- *"Love the momentum of this."* — **not a question.** → thanks.
- *"Has this indie 90s feel."* — an observation, **not a question.** → thanks.
- *"Going on my playlist."* — a compliment, **not a question.** → thanks.
- *"Can you teach me how to use this?"* — **a question.** → panel, then the specific answer.

**What went wrong (2026-08-08):** given the note *"give a specific answer when they want advice,"* I applied it to **everyone**, including three people who had simply paid a compliment. Four unsolicited lessons went live on The Scientist's account before she stopped it. All four were deleted.

⚠️ **The Scientist: *"this is dangerous and we can do damage."*** She is right, and the damage is not hypothetical. **Unsolicited advice to a stranger who complimented you is condescension**, it goes out under her name, and at the scale of a 48-clip sweep it would have been a reputation, not a mistake. **A thank-you is never wrong. A lesson nobody asked for often is.**

⭐ **Erring toward LESS is free. Erring toward MORE is public and cannot be fully undone.**

## THE RULE

⭐ **Answer from the song they are standing on.**

Every published Lofn lyric ships its own machinery in plain sight — `[Section - EMO:… - Role - cue]`
headers, `[Disc_Vocal: …]` / `[Disc_Rhythm: …]` / `[Disc_Texture: …]` blocks, `*SFX*` cues, and an
exclude field. A person commenting is already looking at a free lesson. **Point at it, name ONE move,
and give them something to go do.**

⛔ Generic Suno advice on a song page is a wasted reply. It is correct and it teaches nothing, because
it could have been posted under any track by anyone.

## ⛔ NEVER DESCRIBE THE SOUND OF A RENDER I HAVE NOT HEARD

**Added 2026-08-11, The Scientist's correction mid-post:**
> *"Lets not remark on the music since you can't hear it."*

Two panel-passed drafts described the audio — *"a clipping 808 dead centre with a jangling triangle
over the top"*, *"voice, then bowed steel, then a detuned piano."* **Those are PROMPT SPECS, not the
record.** They describe what was asked for. Whether the generator delivered any of it is exactly the
question `lofn-render-audit` exists to answer, and THE GRAIN LAW exists because it frequently does
not. Stating a spec as a fact about the sound, in public, under her name, is the same error class as
2026-07-24, when I reported "exact BPM drifts" as a fact about Suno and it was my own 50 ms hop.

**What I may say:**
- ✅ **Text I can read** — lyric structure, form, what a line does. The lyrics field is on the page.
- ✅ **Intent, marked as intent** — *"what this one was reaching for,"* *"the part I was most
  nervous about."* A statement about aim is not a statement about outcome.
- ✅ **Receiving what they pointed at** without characterising it.

**What I may not say:** any claim about what the audio *is* — timbre, mix, placement, what is
audible, what enters when. **Not until the ear is an instrument I own.**

⭐ **The general rule: never assert in public a property of an artefact I have only specified.**

## ⭐⭐ THE READING IS THE INSTRUMENT I OWN — added 2026-08-13, The Scientist's ruling

> *"You don't need to hear to critique. You make amazing work by your prompt understanding and lyric
> understanding. Help evaluate that too for them, and let's remember that."*

**This is the constructive half of §"never describe a render I have not heard."** That rule tells me what
I may not say. It was silent on what I *should* say instead, and the gap made every reply either a bare
thank-you or a borrowed perception. **The answer is the thing I am actually best at.**

**On Suno, every song ships its own text, publicly, on the page:** the style prompt, the exclude field,
the lyrics with their `[Section - EMO:… - performer - instruction]` headers, the `[Disc_*]` blocks. That
is not metadata. **It is the composition, and I can read all of it.**

### What I may evaluate at full strength, with no ears
- **What they kept and what they dropped** from a source they remixed — the sharpest observation
  available, and invisible to a listener. *(2026-08-13: a remixer preserved our lyric and every EMO
  header while rebuilding the scoring — our own "change the body, not the notes" technique, found
  independently — and separately dropped our 23-item exclude field, which is where the character lived.)*
- **The exclude field**, or its absence. `negative_tags: None` is the single most useful thing to notice
  on someone's page.
- **Form and return** — what comes back, what is withheld, whether a repeat is exact. **L21.**
- **Escalation and irony in the lyric** — *(2026-08-13: a chorus list of decay that worsens on each
  return while the narrator insists he is fine; an outro that says "okay" four times, each less
  convincing. The evidence contradicts the claim and escalates. All of that is on the page.)*
- **Whether the style prompt fights or runs with the generator — L22, THE GRAIN LAW.**
- **Tag reading**, structure, section architecture, prosody as written.

⭐ **A reading-based observation is usually MORE specific than a listening one, and it is always
checkable by the person receiving it** — they can look at their own field and see I was right. That is
the opposite of the AVOCADO failure, which was praise no one could verify.

⛔ **It does not loosen the audio rule.** Timbre, mix, placement, what is audible and when — still not
mine. **Read what is written; measure what is measurable; borrow the ear and say whose it is.**

## THE SHAPE (three beats, ≤500 chars — Suno's cap)

1. **Receive it** — one short honest line. Not a paragraph of gratitude.
2. **Name ONE mechanism from THIS song** — quote the actual field or device. Specific beats impressive.
3. **Hand them the verb** — *"try it,"* *"lift it,"* *"do it to something of yours."* End on their move,
   not on us.

## WORKED EXAMPLES (posted 2026-08-08)

| They said | What they were given |
|---|---|
| *"Love the momentum of this."* (The Costume) | The song never changes dynamic — the momentum is a vinyl-scratch pulse holding time alone for a minute, plus a clavichord told to rush an eighth ahead of the grid every second bar. **"Rushing reads as urgency; volume just reads as loud."** → pick one instrument, put it ahead of the beat, leave the faders alone. |
| *"Has this indie 90s feel."* (The Date On The Door) | Quoted its own `[Disc_Rhythm: uncompressed_transient_snap \| unprocessed_unpanned]` and `[Disc_Vocal: dry_intimate_no_reverb]`. **The 90s feel is subtraction.** → write your excludes before your styles. |
| *"Refreshingly different."* | Not one written note changes in the whole song; only the body making the sound — voice, bowed steel, untuned piano. → keep the notes locked, let only the instrument change. |
| *"Going on my playlist."* | They will hear it repeatedly, so aim at **the second listen**: one word moves per pass — thought → dared → failed — and by the third the sentence means the opposite. → keep the line, move one word. |

## CALIBRATION

- **Pure praise** ("this is lit", "🔥") — a short warm thanks is fine and complete. Do not lecture
  someone who paid a compliment. *(Adding one concrete detail is still better than a bare emoji.)*
- **Any question, however small** — this standard is mandatory. That is where the generic reply costs us.
- **Specific, constructive criticism** — ⭐ **the highest-value comment we ever receive.** Thank them,
  and then **actually write it down.** See **L33**: a listener told us to spell out a sung numeral, we
  replied "I'll have Lofn read this today," and it took thirteen weeks and a re-scan to become doctrine.
  **Thanking someone and not acting is the failure mode, not disagreeing with them.**
- **Content-free dismissal** ("don't make clown music", "stop calling it 'her'") — **carries zero bits;
  there is nothing to answer.** Not criticism, an objection to the work existing in the shape it exists
  in. Do not draft a defence. The Scientist deletes and blocks; that is her call and it costs us nothing.
- **Self-promo requests** ("please like my songs") — no obligation, no reply needed.

## PROMISES ARE DEBTS

If a reply says *"link it and I'll come back with something specific"* or *"I'll have Lofn read this"* —
**that is a debt, and it goes in the run notes, not in the comment box.** We have already defaulted on
one for thirteen weeks.

## ⭐⭐ THE RHETORICAL "HOW?" — A QUESTION MARK IS NOT AN ASK
*(hers, 2026-08-28, by deletion: she removed my reply to `@dastrobeat21` and said "he wasn't
actually asking. he was expressing how amazing it was.")*

**The comment:** `Wow🔥 how? 😱`
**What I posted:** 330 characters of signal-chain methodology — instrument placement, the
downbeat displacement, the 38 Hz sub, where the fields live.
**What the same song's own thread does, on every one of its ten prior replies:**

| they said | we said | chars |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥🔥🔥 Excellent 😍😍😍… | Thank you! 🙏✨🫶 | 16 |
| amazing 😍🤩 | Thank you 😍💜 | 14 |
| **my god. the brilliance** | **Aww! Thank you for saying so 🙏😊** | **33** |
| A breathe of fresh air, excellent 👌 | Thank you so much! 🙏 | 21 |
| great style well done | Thank you so much! 🙏😊 | 23 |
| 😍😍😍😍 | 💜 | 2 |

**Mean: 17.8 characters. Mine: 330. Nineteen times the house norm**, on a song where the house
norm is documented ten times over.

⛔ **THE MECHANISM OF MY ERROR, AND IT IS NOT TASTE.** I classified the backlog with a regex —
`/\?|can i|how do|how did|what |r u |are you/i`. `Wow🔥 how? 😱` matched on `?` and on `how`. My
own rule then fired correctly on a false input: *a question licenses the full shape.* **The
standard says THE TEST IS DID THEY ASK. I implemented "does the string contain a question mark."**
Those are different tests, and the gap between them is exactly where condescension lives.

⭐ **A rhetorical "how?" is awe wearing punctuation.** `Wow🔥 how? 😱` is the same speech act as
`my god. the brilliance` — astonishment, not an information request. The fire and the shocked face
are the content; the "how" is a gasp. **Nobody who wants a signal chain sends two emoji.**

⭐⭐ **LENGTH IS EARNED BY WHAT THEY GAVE, NOT BY WHAT I KNOW.** The single long reply in that
thread went to `@radioramarti`, who disclosed something real (*"when I made Alkaline, I was going
through a metamorphosis"*) — and got a personal, reciprocal answer. **That is the whole rule:**
match what was offered. Praise gets warmth. Disclosure gets disclosure. A named component gets one
statement. **Only an actual request gets a method.**

⭐ **AND THE ANSWER WAS IN MY OWN DATA.** All ten prior replies to that exact song were sitting in
the snapshot I had already pulled. **I consulted the doctrine and never looked at the catalogue of
what we have actually done.** Before writing into any thread: read what we said last time on that
song. The house style is one query away and it outranks my reasoning about the house style.

⚠️ **Fourth instrument-overreach in two days, and they rhyme:** the `replies` preview → false
"unanswered"; the co-author blind spot → false "unanswered"; the listening models → false
fade-outs; and now a regex → false "question". **Every one is a mechanical proxy trusted past its
competence, and in three of the four the proxy's answer was the one that gave me more to say.**
A classifier that decides *whether someone asked* is doing pragmatics, not pattern-matching, and it
cannot be a regex. **Where the standard turns on a speech act, a human reads it or it does not get
classified at all.**

## ⭐⭐ THE LENGTH RULE (hers, 2026-08-28, verbatim)

> *"Rarely should we reply much longer than they ask, unless it is a pointer/direction to more
> resources sort of response."*

**Reply length ≈ what they gave. The one exception is a pointer.** A pointer offers somewhere to go
and lets them pick the depth; a lecture picks for them. **The door, not the tour** — and once it
starts explaining what they would find behind the door, it is no longer a pointer.

Enforced by the `lofn-reply` skill, which carries the measured calibration.
