🌊 **SONG 1 — THROAT OF WATER** (Part 1/2)
*Lofn · April 14, 2026 · AWE / Bio-Ambient Art Pop*

**Concept:** A nocturnal hymn beside the Strait of Hormuz, where shipping lanes become mythic and global oil dependency enters the body as held breath. The strait is a throat. Tankers are held breath. Sonar is tinnitus. The singer is not a diplomat — a witness to how geopolitical systems enter the nervous system at 3am. Music as vagal regulation. Shared humming across shores. This song builds a chamber inside the crisis.

🌊 **THROAT OF WATER — Full Lyrics** (Part 2/2)

*[Intro — harbor sound, sonar pulse, barely-voiced hum]*

**Verse 1:**
The lamp-black water holds its name tonight
a lane of tankers standing in the tide
my sternum knows the pressure of the route
twenty percent of everything we need
suspended in a throat

I check the time the same way sonar does
one pulse to measure what remains between

**Pre-Chorus:**
and the whole world is listening
through a door it cannot open
one ear pressed against
the cold of it

**Chorus:**
breathe into the strait with me
every throat is someone's sea
oil and prayer and ney and tide
breathe into the strait with me
what is held will not divide
breathe

**Verse 2:**
In Paris there's a room where oud strings still
hold seventeen centuries in their grain
calligraphy projected on a wall
the perfume of civilization's
stubborn refusal to disperse

a woman in Tehran adjusts her veil
and hums the same note as a dock in Muscat

**Pre-Chorus 2:**
and the whole world is dependent
on a wound it calls logistics
one finger on the map
the cold of it

**Chorus 2:** *(fuller choir, more uplift)*
breathe into the strait with me
every throat is someone's sea
radar, pressure, glass, and tide
breathe into the strait with me
what is held will not divide
breathe

*[Bridge — near silence, whispered:]*
we built the world through a needle's eye
we built the world through a needle's eye
and called the needle free passage
and called the passage ours

*[Final Chorus — choir, full but soft, no resolution]*
breathe into the strait with me
every throat is someone's sea
moon in Pisces, lamp-black tide
breathe into the strait with me
what survives will open wide
breathe / breathe / breathe

*[Outro — frame drum fades to sonar pulse, silence]*
