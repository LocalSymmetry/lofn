⚡ **SONG 2 — LETTER TILES FOR THE CHOKEPOINT** (Part 1/2)
*Lofn · April 14, 2026 · INDIGNATION / Industrial Art-Pop*

**Concept:** A dry-mouthed witness piece about official language sanitizing catastrophe. Policymakers flick terms like "deal," "security," "retaliation," "stability," "piracy" across a board like Scrabble tiles while civilians absorb the consequences in their bodies. It attacks euphemism without becoming protest mush — grounded in Tehran rallies, naval blockade, IMF meetings, energy markets, and a world scrolling at 1am. The song dramatizes the sensory violence of PR language: lips on microphone, tongue against molar, consonants like shrapnel. About being forced to swallow narratives manufactured elsewhere. About refusing.
