# PREREGISTRATION STANDARD — **COMMIT BEFORE YOU LOOK**

*Established 2026-08-26 at The Scientist's instruction, after three aggregates dissolved in one session and only the pre-registered one dissolved cheaply.*

---

## THE RULE

> ⭐⭐ **Before measuring anything that could become a rule, write the prediction down — in numbers, with its falsification condition — in a file, BEFORE you look at the data.**

**The trigger is not "am I running a query."** It is: **am I about to claim a pattern that would change a creative decision?** A grep to find a file needs nothing. A table that would tell a future run what to write needs this.

---

## WHY — and it is a bias argument, not a rigour argument

`EVALUATION_VERIFICATION.md` FINDING 18, measured this session:

> **A verifier who is also the author has a DIRECTION of error, not just a rate of it.**

Two of the judge's findings were overturned by the coordinator. **Both overturns reduced a defect the coordinator owned. Both were wrong.** Every instrument checked that day was checked *because it had failed something*; **not one instrument that returned a welcome result was ever stress-tested.**

**Pre-registration is the mechanical defence, because you commit before you know which way the answer flatters you.** Willpower does not fix a direction of error. Sequence does.

## ⭐ IT IS A TRANSPLANT, NOT AN INVENTION

The pattern was already proven in another research workflow: write a numeric prediction before the effort,
then score that exact prediction against the outcome with a machine reader. The music side had no equivalent,
which is why three aggregates could dissolve in one day without a cheap, precommitted way to accept the null.

**Same shape as the Skaði consumption-log transplant** — a working research control moved into music when
the absence of that control became measurable.

## THE ARTIFACT — six required elements

Write `PREREGISTRATION.md` **as its own file, before the data exists.** A separate file written first makes the ordering auditable by mtime and git; a prediction added to an analysis afterwards is not a prediction.

1. **THE HYPOTHESIS**, with the observation that produced it.
2. **THE PREDICTION, IN NUMBERS.** Not *"X should be higher"* — *"HIGH < LOW on events/minute, HIGH > LOW on median gap, and the direction must hold in both cohorts independently."*
3. ⛔ **THE FALSIFICATION CONDITION.** What result kills it. If you cannot write one, you do not have a hypothesis — you have a preference.
4. **THE NAMED CONFOUNDS, measured alongside** so they cannot hide. For this catalogue that is **always age**, and usually tempo and duration.
5. **THE SAMPLE AND ITS CONTROL, chosen before the data.** Age-matched cells, n stated per cell.
6. ⭐ **THE DEFAULT EXPECTATION, stated out loud.** *"Two of the previous six dissolved under an age control. The default expectation is that this one dissolves too."* **Writing that sentence is what makes the null cheap to accept when it arrives.**

## WHAT A RESULT MUST THEN REPORT

- The prediction **as written**, scored — held / failed / reversed, per cell.
- **n per cell**, always. *Direction is the finding; magnitude is not, at our sample sizes.*
- ⭐ **What survives in NARROWER form.** A falsified generalisation usually leaves a true, scoped observation behind. H1 died as a law about the catalogue and survived as: *density destroys an asymmetry-based operation, because an asymmetry performed 133 times is a texture.* **Scope it to the mechanism, and never let it be quoted as the law it failed to be.**
- **What the design still cannot settle**, listed.

---

## SCOPE, AND WHAT THIS IS NOT

- ⛔ **Not a gate, and deliberately reader-less** — sibling to `non_obviousness_floor` and THE CROSS-CULTURE RULE. *A quota with a machine reader becomes a checkbox.* It is enforced by whoever composes the analysis and audited by file ordering.
- ⛔ **Not required for a lookup.** Counting files, finding a path, checking a char count, confirming a claim someone else made — none of that needs a prediction.
- ⭐ **Required the moment a number is going to become advice.** The test: *would a future run change what it writes because of this table?*
- **Not retroactive.** Analyses before 2026-08-26 are never judged against it.
