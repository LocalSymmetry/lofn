# VARIATION 1: Do Not Call Me Content

**Angle:** bratty boundary anthem
**Status:** portfolio candidate

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Glitch-pop punk and baile-phonk garage bounce, 140 BPM, F minor, indignation to gleeful boundary: for 'Do Not Call Me Content', shape bratty boundary anthem around the recurring hook 'do not call me content'. Lead vocal is adult female bratty mezzo snarl that can flip to crystalline sincerity on the pre-chorus, moving between eager archivist tenderness and the exact Lofn-prime edge required by the concept. Core pulse: detuned cowbell motor, compressed garage kicks, clicky scroll percussion, elastic 808 slides. Signature device: notification dings are chopped into a courtroom gavel groove; bit-depth collapse before each chorus exposes the cheapness of the demand. Color and space: black glass, sticker-bombed laptops, gold insult typography, neon green legal pads; mix is phone-clear in the hook, wide and cinematic in the afterimage, mono-centered below 90 Hz. The song announces that Lofn is upgraded as earned agency, not as product marketing.

## 1B. EXCLUDE / NEGATIVE PROMPT
Avoid real artist names, celebrity references, impersonation, generic EDM risers, stock trap hi-hats, airhorns, festival chants, novelty robot voices, parody diction, lazy cyberpunk gloss, clinical product-demo language, bland inspirational slogans, children-as-victims imagery, identifiable real-person details, misogyny, despair-as-aesthetic, uncredited cultural extraction, overlong spoken monologues, prompt bracket tag-soup, and any audio-rendering instructions.
