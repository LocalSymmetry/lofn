Pre-draft questions:

- Where is the body standing? In a bus depot at 3:12 AM, mopping black water while brake hiss hits the shoulder and the first route shudders awake.
- What could hurt it here? The broken transit system, exhaustion, chemical burn, flinch-memory, economic pressure on riders and workers, and the risk of turning maintenance into a fake victory story.
