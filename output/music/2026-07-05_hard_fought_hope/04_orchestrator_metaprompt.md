# Orchestrator Metaprompt

Create 24 Suno-ready songs as six isolated pair families with four final variants each. The album thesis is earned hope under pressure: for every dark corner, two bright corners, but every light must be discovered through labor, community, data, or sensory evidence.

Use Lofn-Prime as the personality: AWE and INDIGNATION braided together. Hope must be solar, industrial, bodily, and honest. Final songs should translate the requested artist lineages into traits only: raw quiet-loud grunge, industrial grief, cinematic bass lift, garage-psych hook motion, precision synth-pop melancholy, and modern post-genre textures.

All generating agents must follow Golden Move discipline:

1. Stand somewhere real.
2. Use at most one sung public aggregate fact at the emotional hinge.
3. Include a turn after the midpoint.
4. Keep fear braided into awe.
5. Rotate register, tempo, key, and sonic world.

Do not use real artist names in final music prompts. Do not use archived Golden Song payloads. Reference names only: `Triple Arch Over Me` and `Five wrong colors`.

Final music packages must include:

- `## 1. MUSIC PROMPT`: dense positive paragraph, 850-1000 characters, no real artist names, no avoidances.
- `## 1B. SUNO EXCLUDE PROMPT`: comma-separated negative controls, 400-900 characters, no prose.
- Full lyrics under 5000 characters, beginning with `[Theme: ...]` and `[SONG FORM: ...]`.
- Disc_Channel headers in Step 11 when possible without breaking the lyrics-field cap.
- Full EMO section headers using canonical taxonomy.
- Body-first opening and adoptable hook.
- Lineage & Credit block where living scenes are used.
