## 3. TITLE
Bring The Chairs Up
## Hook Note
The imperative hook stays simple enough to sing back while each return adds a new social proof: first chair, missing name, then shared breath. The chair scrape lands just before "up" so the body hears the room arrive before the lyric explains it.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Raspy adult androgynous alto-tenor, A3-E5, dry close center, clipped consonants, audible inhale before chorus entries, tiny laugh-breath on the second hook, neighbor responses staggered left and right as if placed on separate stairwell landings.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cracked-phone buzz cradles isolation, haunts the verse, and remains under the last chord as unresolved fear. Chair scrapes rupture the false phone groove, then become snare ghosts and communal proof. Breath noises in the intro, bridge, and outro mark the singer re-entering the body.
## Lineage & Credit
Garage-psych hook motion, pluggnB haze, raw-room community singing, and object percussion are used as traits, not imitation. Scene lineage is acknowledged through functional roles: blurred keys for dissociation, fuzz guitar for abrasion, chair hits for local rhythm. No real artist references or copied payloads appear.
