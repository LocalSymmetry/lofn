## 3. TITLE
Count Me In The Room
## Hook Note
The hook turns belonging into a countable action. Every chorus keeps the title intact, then mutates the roster from "me" to "you" to "her" to "them," so identity repair becomes a shared pulse rather than a slogan.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Adult androgynous lead, A3-F5, cracked chest register, dry center, small pre-chorus gasp, rough consonants, late neighbor responses from left landing and deep right stairwell with imperfect timing left in the stack.
## Production Dramaturgy
The phone buzz starts as a private metronome of erasure, then cuts at the first chorus so the late chair scrape can answer. By the final chorus the timing flaw becomes the groove, making imperfection the audible proof that real people arrived.
## Lineage & Credit
Fuzzy garage force, soft plugg-key haze, raw-room roll call, and communal stairwell percussion are treated as production traits with visible respect for their scene functions. No real artist reference, named likeness, or copied lyric material appears.
## Major Deviations
- Changed / refused / intensified: Expanded the roll-call counterpoint and made the late chair scrape an accepted groove.
- Reason: The Step 10 variant had strong adoption potential but needed more structural mutation and body-first pressure.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: The song proves hope by counting bodies in a damaged room, keeping fear present.
