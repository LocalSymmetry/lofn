## 3. TITLE
Not Fixed, Still Rolling
## Hook Note
"Not fixed, still rolling" is the anti-wellness chorus: damage remains named, but the body still makes public motion possible.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Adult female contralto, G3-C5, sanded rasp, low-vowel bite, breath clicks before hard consonants, dry close verses, rough gang response in chorus.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: turnstile pulse and depot PA hum. Haunt: brake hiss recognizes the shoulder. Rupture: half-time anger shards in the bridge. Afterglow: full-room worker response leaves dirt audible.
## Lineage & Credit
Industrial alt-rock pressure, precision transit synth-pop mechanics, public-room call-response, and reclaimed kinetic editing. Living-scene energy is credited as rhythmic architecture, not costume.
## Major Deviations
- Changed / intensified: Made the call-response longer and rougher, and moved hope into door-opening labor rather than inspirational language.
- Reason: This hook is strongest when it refuses resolution.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: The song stays useful and bruised at the same time.
