## 3. TITLE
Make The Dead Thing Speak
## Hook Note
This is the hardest rock variant: the hook is forceful and strange, but still legible as a repair-room command.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Adult female contralto, G3-C5, dry low whisper verses, chest-edge chorus belt, gravel on phrase ends, clipped breath before “speak.” Room voices use cracked unison rather than polished stack.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: static and solder tick create the pickup. Haunt: bad-news bleed presses beneath verses. Rupture: fan-only bridge admits smallness. Afterglow: final riff stumbles once so the hope stays hard-fought.
## Lineage & Credit
Trait lineage: raw quiet-loud fuzz pressure, industrial grief as rhythm, bio-ambient green undertow, repair-room foley, and anti-disposal ethics. Golden references are Triple Arch Over Me and Five wrong colors, with payloads quarantined judge-side.
## Major Deviations
Intensified the original fuzz-bass resurrection and kept the title’s oddness rather than sanding it into a cleaner slogan. Expanded sung-line count and made the bridge argue with the song’s own hope.
