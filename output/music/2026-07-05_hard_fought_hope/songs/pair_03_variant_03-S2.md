## 3. TITLE
The Landing Bulb Stays On
## Hook Note
The hook translates the run's two-bright-corners mandate into poor physical evidence: a bad bulb and a split chair. It stays adoptable because the phrase is plain, while the final return adds teeth and communal motion.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Raspy adult alto-tenor, B3-F5, dry close mic, breath clicks, cracked smile tone, sudden chest lift on chorus, neighbor doubles above and below in stairwell depth with unpolished blend.
## Production Dramaturgy
The landing bulb hum cradles the first bright corner, the phone buzz haunts as the dark corner, and chair-seat hits rupture screen trance. The final chorus lets two ordinary objects outsing one dark object while keeping the low buzz as vigilance.
## Lineage & Credit
