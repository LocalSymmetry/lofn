## 3. TITLE
Pass It Down The Table
## Hook Note
The hook makes handoff the adoptable action. The table is not a symbol first; it is the proof engine, moving fear through hands until it becomes food.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Adult female mezzo lead, A3-E5, dry center placement, clean edge with tired grain. Two adult female answer voices are close and practical, panned to table ends and widened only on hook repetitions.
## Production Dramaturgy
Container thuds cradle the grid and move left to right as the social body. The siren overtone haunts behind the window. The bridge removes bass to expose oversized need. The final chorus doubles the relay until the room answers itself.
## Lineage & Credit
Trait lineage: precision alt-pop mechanics, industrial room abrasion, kitchen foley as percussion, communal relay work-song grammar. No real artist names appear in Suno fields.
## Major Deviations
- Intensified: the table-relay logic now governs rhythm, lyric structure, and stereo staging.
- Reason: the Step 10 version had the strongest communal embodiment but needed a larger full-song surface.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: the hook stays simple while the engine carries left-right repair architecture.
