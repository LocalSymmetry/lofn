## 3. TITLE
Hold The Wire Steady
## Hook Note
The chorus is a physical instruction before it is an emotion; its power comes from the hand obeying while still shaking.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Adult female mezzo, A3-E5, close dry center, chest voice verses, clean upper mix chorus, breath grit, hard consonants on “hold” and “steady.” Answer voices are adult neighbors, slightly late, left and right.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: fan tremolo gives the body a borrowed clock. Haunt: narrow radio bleed and table-three news remain under the verse. Rupture: Steady Wire Gate removes drums for one static syllable. Afterglow: rough claps join without polishing the room.
## Lineage & Credit
Trait lineage: green industrial pop, precision synth mechanics, dry repair-room vocal, and mutual steadiness as process. Golden references: Triple Arch Over Me and Five wrong colors are quarantined judge-side; calibration is structural, not payload-based.
