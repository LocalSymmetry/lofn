## 3. TITLE
Two Windows Catch
## Hook Note
The hook is an adoptable count: two rooms answer after dirty rooftop labor, not after certainty or a clean green future arrives.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Female alto-to-mezzo, A3-D5, dry close mic, chest-led verses, mixed chorus, breath grain, scraped consonants, restrained vibrato, grit on the word "catch."
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: inverter click and rubber bass make the body stand somewhere real. Haunt: roof wind and cable rattle keep the grid wound audible. Rupture: bridge fuzz tears open when unequal access is named. Afterglow: late boot squeak stays under the final hook as useful dirt.
## Lineage & Credit
Precision synth-pop, fuzzy alt-rock, rooftop labor foley, and solar-inverter rhythm are used as traits, not artist imitation. Living-scene borrowing is not central; this is an internal Lofn solarpunk/industrial palette grounded in rooftop labor and uneven infrastructure.
## Major Deviations
- Intensified: expanded the chorus and bridge so the hook recurs with mutation and the renewable fact is answered by bodily repair.
- Reason: Step 10 was structurally sound but too short for the full Suno package line target.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: keeps the clean-tech ad risk broken by tar, breath, late squeak, and grid inequality.
