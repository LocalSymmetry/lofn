## 3. TITLE
Share The Light Dirty
## Hook Note
The hook turns shared light into a bodily action: useful, stained, and communal rather than inspirational gloss.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Female mezzo, A3-D5, close mic, chest-register command, clipped breaths, restrained vibrato, sanded rasp on chorus vowels, widened doubles only after the hook lands.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: inverter tick clears the throat of the groove. Haunt: roof wind and cable rattle keep the address uneasy. Rupture: fuzz opens at the renewable-access wound. Afterglow: exposed tool tick keeps labor present after the chorus.
## Lineage & Credit
Solar alt-pop, precision electronic rock, fuzzy guitar pressure, and worksite percussion are processable traits. Living-scene borrowing is not central; the song credits rooftop labor and neighbor repair rather than a technology brand.
## Major Deviations
- Intensified: kept the word dirty in the hook and tied it to billing, tar, nails, and the late room.
- Reason: the phrase could become slogan-like without physical consequence.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: preserves hard-fought hope through useful grime and shared evidence.
