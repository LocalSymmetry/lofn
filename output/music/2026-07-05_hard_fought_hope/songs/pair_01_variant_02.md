## 3. TITLE
Count It By The Cold
## Hook Note
The hook turns counting into evidence. It refuses abstract gratitude and lets the surviving cold chain carry the hope.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Low adult female alto, G3-B4, dry intimate mic, breath-grain consonants, small rasp on sustained vowels, low room-answer doubles on final choruses.
## Production Dramaturgy
The delayed relay makes hope late but audible. Enamel knocks and condensation ticks keep the clinic body present while the final click outlives the band.
