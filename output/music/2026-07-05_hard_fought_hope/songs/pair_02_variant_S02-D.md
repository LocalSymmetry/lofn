## 3. TITLE
Hold One Corner Open
## Hook Note
The hook is a practical command with a damaged body inside it: it asks for one corner, not the whole healed world.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Female alto, A3-C#5, dry close chest voice, cracked mixed chorus, breathy consonants, small rasp on "open," late doubles, contained belt.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: rail squeak and inverter click start the pulse. Haunt: one late room answer stays offset. Rupture: bridge strips to dry voice and damaged guitar when access remains uneven. Afterglow: glass-lung harmony widens while the plain command remains.
## Lineage & Credit
Solarpunk electro-rock, precision synth-pop, fuzzy roof guitar, and foley rhythm are translated into acoustic instructions. Living-scene borrowing is not central; the living source is rooftop labor under unequal infrastructure.
## Major Deviations
- Intensified: kept "not whole" inside the chorus to prevent the command from becoming a wellness phrase.
- Reason: this variant had the highest slogan risk.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: the final lift is earned by broken hands, delayed windows, and the uncorrected squeak.
