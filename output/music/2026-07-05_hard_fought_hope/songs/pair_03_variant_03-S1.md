## 3. TITLE
My Phone Lied Loud
## Hook Note
The hook keeps the phone as antagonist but refuses an anti-phone sermon: it admits the screen carried real wounds, then lets the room answer with a rougher truth. "Bring the chairs up" remains the repair command inside the accusation.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Raspy adult unisex lead, G3-D5, close breath grain, dry scrape on phrase ends, whispered screen doubles near center, neighbor answers high-left and deep-right with raw shout harmony in the final chorus.
## Production Dramaturgy
Phone buzz is cradle and threat, a false private pulse that narrows the verse. Chair squeal cuts it at every chorus pickup, turning an ugly object into harmonic permission. The outro keeps the buzz softer, not erased, so vigilance remains braided into relief.
## Lineage & Credit
PluggnB haze, garage fuzz pressure, cracked-room percussion, and raw communal call-response are credited as living-scene traits. No real artist references, named likeness, or copied lyric material appears.
## Major Deviations
- Changed / refused / intensified: Refused a simple phone-bad reading and kept the feed partly truthful while making the room more complete.
- Reason: The Step 10 version risked sermonizing; the enhanced version preserves moral complexity.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: The song earns hope through contradiction, body evidence, and repair.
