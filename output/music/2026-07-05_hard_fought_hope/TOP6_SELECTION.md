I scored the 24 final standalone Suno packages for:

- Originality

- Viral/adoptable hook potential

## Top 6 Notes

### 1. Bring The Chairs Up

The winner because it has the cleanest listener action: drag the chair, make room, answer the feed with bodies. It is specific without becoming homework and communal without becoming soft.

### 2. Answer The Siren

This is the biggest cinematic build. The siren is not conquered; it is metabolized into table work and rough harmony. Excellent candidate for a dramatic render.

### 3. Not Fixed, Still Rolling

This should be rendered because it carries the run's immune system against fake optimism. The hook says exactly what the whole project means: damage remains, motion continues.

### 4. Keep Tomorrow Open

The broadest emotional phrase in the set, with enough solder smoke and radio crackle to keep it from floating away. Strong final-album candidate even if it needs a gritty vocal render.

### 5. Two Windows Catch

Probably the most accessible "two bright corners" single. The danger is clean-tech gloss, but the tar, late window, and grid wound keep it honest.

### 6. One More Morning Clicked On

This gets the final slot because the clinic relay is the run's clearest "next day will be brighter" proof. It is quiet, tactile, and central to the original promise.
