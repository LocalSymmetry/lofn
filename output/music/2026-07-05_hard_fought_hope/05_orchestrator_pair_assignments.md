# Phase-1 Pair Assignments

Step 05 owns which Step 02 concept fills each slot. These slot assignments are fixed.

## Pair 01 - Accessible AWE / Measurement

- Arm: ACCESSIBLE
- Genre driver: grunge-synth hope anthem with clinic-fridge pulse
- Verse structure: first-person body report, pre-chorus hinge with one public health fact, chorus as adoptable count
- Required unnecessary element: vaccine fridge hum or clinic refrigerator relay
- Bright-corner pressure: child survival progress without erasing the remaining grief
