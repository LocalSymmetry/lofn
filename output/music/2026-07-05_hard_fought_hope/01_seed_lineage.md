# Seed Lineage

## Chosen Lineage

Primary Golden Seed strand: `MUSIC SEED 30: The Farmer Knew`.

Why: the user asked for hope that survives darkness without becoming happy-slop. This seed already carries ordinary duty against world-scale suffering, no easy optimism, and no nihilism. It lets the songs say: "the work is not enough, and still the work matters."

## Structural Alloys

- `THE MEASUREMENT`: one precise data point becomes feeling, not lecture.
- `THE SWITCHBOARD`: AWE interrupted by INDIGNATION, then re-routed into action.
- `Straightening Our Spines`: body-forward defiance, genre as weapon, hook built for a human mouth.

## Golden Move Applied

1. Stand somewhere real: every pair must start with a singer in a physical situation.
2. One wounding fact: at most one public aggregate fact may be sung per song, and it must arrive at the hinge.
3. The turn: each lyric must change its mind after the midpoint.
4. Fear stays braided in: no clean reassurance, no fake sunrise.
5. Rotate the register: vary key, tempo, vocal register, sonic world, and body situation.
