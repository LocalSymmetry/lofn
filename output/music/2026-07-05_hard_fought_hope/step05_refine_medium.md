# Step 05 - Refine Medium Into Six Panel-Pressed Song Seeds

## Selection Verdict

Step 05 selects the twelve Step 02 concepts into the six fixed Phase-1 pair slots from `05_orchestrator_pair_assignments.md`. The winners are C01-C06 because each one directly owns a required slot object, public-pressure family, and verse architecture. C07-C12 are cut into the reserve bench with harvestable organs.

Global emotion braid: Apprehension + Resilience + Solidarity, with Wonder appearing only after evidence. Golden Move discipline: real place, one wounding fact at the hinge, midpoint turn, fear braided into awe, rotated sonic worlds.

## Pair 01 - One More Morning Clicks On

### Singer

Who: a night-shift clinic worker with a low alto voice, too tired to sound heroic.
Physical situation: standing beside a vaccine refrigerator after a power blink, palm on the metal door, waiting for the relay to catch.
Unsaid ache: they know the waiting room will still be full tomorrow, and they are afraid the repair is too small to count.

### Surface

Dominant body image: fingertips flattening against cold refrigerator metal while fluorescent light trembles.
Plain-language ache: I am not claiming the world is fixed; I heard one thing start working again.
Candidate hooks: "one more morning clicked on"; "count it by the cold"; "not saved, still here"; "two lights answered back"; "the day got brighter by hand".
Panel-lean hook: "one more morning clicked on".

### Hook Craft Scan

Stress pattern: ONE more MORN-ing CLICKS on; strong enough for grunge downbeats and easy crowd return.
Vowel/breath shape: open "one/more/on" vowels give breath room after the clipped consonants in "clicked".
Singback potential: high; ordinary words, body-adjacent action, no thesis language.
Title clarity: strong working title without sounding like a wellness slogan.
Emotional adoptability: usable by any listener who needs proof, not comfort.
Risk: "morning" can soften too quickly, so the relay click and cold metal must stay audible.

### Golden Seed Alloy

Source/lineage: The Next Day Has Teeth, alloyed through the Golden Move structure: stand in a real clinic, place one public health fact at the hinge, let a small repair answer doom without denying it.
Primary medium: fridge-hum ambient grunge with relay-click percussion and quiet-loud industrial synth resilience.
Contrast medium: precise public-health hinge inside raw tape abrasion.
Mythic/material escalation: refrigerator door -> cold chain relay -> a kept dose as a tiny moon with teeth -> worker's palm still shaking on the handle.
Exact detail: global under-five mortality has fallen sharply since 1990, while progress is slowing.
Forbidden cliche: angelic nurses, miracle cure, sunrise-as-solution.
Anti-genre contaminant: low clinical relay click used like a kick pattern inside a grunge chorus.
Controlled fracture: the pre-chorus briefly drops to fridge hum and breath count before the fact arrives.
Routing destination: fact to pre-chorus hinge; relay to hook rhythm; mythic cold-chain image to bridge; policy explanation to ghost bank.

### Triple-Arch Ritual Paragraph

A hand on a refrigerator door becomes a hand on a small stubborn planet. The ordinary hum becomes a numbered cold chain, the relay catches after the blink, and the room seems to grow enamel: the next day has teeth because someone guarded its temperature. The human trace is sweat drying under latex, a sleeve cuff dragged through condensation, shoe rubber tacky on clinic tile. Light stays ugly and fluorescent; shadow lives under the waiting-room chairs. Afterimage: a cold rectangle breathing in the dark while the singer finally exhales.

### EMO Dramaturgy Preview

Verse 1: Apprehension + Overwhelm, body report beside the fridge.
Pre-chorus: Dismay + Cognitive Dissonance, one public health fact cuts through doom without cleaning it.
Chorus: Resilience + Hope, the hook counts one functioning morning.
Bridge: Sorrow + Determination, the singer admits the remaining grief and chooses the next task.
Final chorus: Solidarity + Relief, backing voices enter as other workers, not angels.

### Production Dramaturgy

Cradle: low refrigerator hum tuned under the tonic, barely musical.
Haunt: fluorescent buzz sideband follows the vocal in verse gaps.
Rupture: relay click becomes a hard downbeat, then drops out for the hinge fact.
Afterglow: final hook keeps one uncorrected fridge throb under clean harmony.

### Attention Ladder

First 5 seconds: isolated fridge relay, breath, and one raw guitar harmonic.
First hook memory: the clipped phrase "one more morning clicked on" lands on a simple descending contour.
Second-listen reward: relay timing foreshadows the chorus rhythm from the first bar.
Deep-lore reward: cold-chain detail turns the Golden Seed into repair infrastructure, not metaphor.

### Complexity Routing

Hook: only the relay-count phrase.
Verse: body report, cold metal, unslept clinic.
Bridge: mythic cold-chain planet and remaining grief.
Production only: relay click grid and fluorescent buzz.
Sidecar only: source notes for child mortality data.
Ghost bank: any institutional critique too large for the singer's body.

### Forced Differentiation

Medium: clinic-fridge ambient grunge.
Fracture: pre-chorus hum dropout.
Body trace: cold palm and latex sweat.
EMO arc: Apprehension -> Resilience -> Relief.

### Panel-Forced Revision

Before: a clean uplift chorus about better numbers.

### Slot Record
