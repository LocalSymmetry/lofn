## 3. TITLE
Bring The Chairs Up
## Hook Note
The title hook remains a plain command, but this refinement makes every return behave like a stairwell roll call. The listener hears the chair before the chorus explains it, then the final rupture turns names and chair counts into the proof the phone cannot own.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Adult androgynous raspy alto, A3-E5, dry close center, cracked chest mix, clipped consonants, audible breath pickups, one laugh-click before the first downbeat. Adult neighbor voices are staggered left, right, and above as landing-specific replies, never a polished choir.
## Production Dramaturgy
The hard-left cracked phone buzz cradles isolation, cuts into false starts, and fights the bridge like an antagonist. Chair feet move from offbeat interruption to snare ghost to final count engine. PluggnB glass haze stays as a digital shadow while garage guitar, palm hits, rail dust, and concrete slap give the room physical authority.
## Lineage & Credit
Garage-psych supplies rough hook motion and detuned guitar grit; pluggnB supplies blurred key shimmer and dissociative screen-glass shadow; stairwell call-response and object percussion are made from the scene itself. These are trait borrowings, not imitation or ownership claims, and the package uses no real artist names.
## Major Deviations
- Changed / refused / intensified: Rebuilt the standard verse-chorus shape into a roll-call spiral with two chair-scrape false starts, harder phone antagonism, and a final name-count rupture.
- Reason: The top-six set already contains several work-song command forms, so this finalist needed a more feral stairwell architecture while keeping the adoptable title hook.
- Effect on Lofn uniqueness: Hope comes from names, dust, chair paint, and breath on concrete, not from a generalized communal anthem.
