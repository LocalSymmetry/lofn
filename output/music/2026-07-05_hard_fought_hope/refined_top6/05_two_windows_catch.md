## 3. TITLE
Two Windows Catch
## Hook Note
The hook stays a concrete count: two panes catch after a worker's hand, cable, and inverter do their work. The one-beat-late second pane makes the chorus physically distinct from the set and keeps hope small, visible, and earned.
## Vocal Fingerprint
Solo adult female mezzo, A3-D5, dry close mic, chest-led verses, mix-bright chorus doubles left and right, scraped consonants, tar-dust breath grain, restrained vibrato, grit on "catch," with the second-pane double entering one beat late.
## Production Dramaturgy
Cradle: inverter tick, boot scrape, cable rattle, and dry breath claim the first five seconds. Haunt: bill-bruise sub and heat-shimmer pad keep the unequal grid audible. Rupture: bridge fuzz tears across the record-year line. Afterglow: the late square keeps blinking right while tar-glove taps stay close.
## Lineage & Credit
