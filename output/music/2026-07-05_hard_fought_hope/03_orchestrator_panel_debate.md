## Baseline Panel Verdict

The initial brief risks becoming "doomscroll turns into sunrise." The panel rejects sunrise-as-solution, fake comfort, and generic uplift. They keep the user's "two bright corners" idea but require those corners to be embodied: a clinic fridge, an inverter, a bus brake, a neighbor's chair, a repair bench, a hand on a roof rail.

## Group Transform

Transform the run from a theme album about hope into a 24-song field report from places where repair is already happening. Every song must start in a body, name one pressure, find two forms of evidence, and make the chorus adoptable without making it clean.

## Skeptic Transform

If the chorus can fit on a motivational poster, kill it. If the production sounds like trend paint, give the trend a job or remove it. If the fact sounds like a fact sheet, move it to the hinge and make the singer answer it with their body.
