## Headline

The obvious explanation fails. Recent lyrics are not more fragmented or locally disconnected. They have more explicit actions and usually satisfy the system's causal “compulsion” checklist better than the named high bars. That inversion exposes the real failure: the system began treating completion of a semantic mechanism as proof of emotional force.

`The Wedding Takes It Louder` was not simpler. It was organized in the opposite direction. A concrete, serious event and one emotional pressure came first; its many devices served that pressure. The 08-29 run selected six semantic engines first, then required scenes, wants, and actions that made those engines work. The songs parse, but the listener may have to adopt the conceptual problem before finding a pressure she recognizes as hers.

No anti-cleverness, anti-fragment, or general simplification rule is supported. `ASK FOR IT BY NAME` is the decisive cleverness counterexample.
