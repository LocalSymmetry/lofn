6. The old QA was strong on compliance and weak at the Somatic/publish threshold. Fresh QA finds P02/P03 text-SHIP, P04 HOLD, and P01/P05/P06 REPAIR.

8. Cleverness is not a defect and the retired Remark Test remains retired.
