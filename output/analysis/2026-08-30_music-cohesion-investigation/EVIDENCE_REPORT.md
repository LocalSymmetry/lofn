# Evidence Report — preregistered hypotheses scored

## Outcome

Five broad causal hypotheses fail or reverse. One narrow texture hypothesis survives. The investigation therefore does **not** justify a choppiness rule, abstraction-word ban, cleverness veto, or constraint reduction. It identifies a different failure: the evaluation system has been mistaking formal/causal completeness for emotional compulsion.

## Hypothesis scoring

### H1 — adjacency continuity regressed

**Verdict: FALSIFIED.**

- Predicted B hard cuts at least 15 points above A; observed B is **4 points below** A.
- Predicted B ultra-short rate at least 10 points above A; observed B is **3.2 points below** A.
- Predicted at least 4/5 A songs beat the B continuity median; only `The Wedding Takes It Louder` has a lower hard-cut estimate than the B median (1/5).

The listener's word “choppy” cannot be operationalized as short lines or adjacent disconnection in this cohort.

### H2 — recognition/commonality regressed through missing scenes and wants

**Verdict: BROAD FORM FALSIFIED; NARROW ABSTRACTION SIGNAL RETAINED.**

- Clear want by the first chorus: **3/5 A** versus **2/6 B**, not the predicted 4/5 versus at most 2/6 separation.
- Every song supplied a literal scene to the clean reader.
- The abstract lexicon is higher in B, but the difference is less than one tagged word per 100 words, not a 15-point line-class gap.
- `Spare Hair Tie` has more abstract-lexicon hits than four of six B songs and remains a user-named high bar.

What survives is not “recent songs lack concrete places.” It is that concrete places can be used as evidence for an abstract governing proposition. Literal occupancy is not the same as listener commonality.

### H3 — compulsion regressed under a five-signal causal test

**Verdict: FALSIFIED IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION; THE INSTRUMENT FAILS CONSTRUCT VALIDITY.**

Five signals were scored blind: clear early want, human cost, irreversible action, chorus consequence, and changed final return.

- A clears at least four signals in **2/5** songs: `ASK FOR IT BY NAME` and `Triple Arch Over Me`.
- B clears at least four signals in **5/6** songs; `Look What You Taught Me` is the exception.
- Median: **3/5 A** versus **4/5 B**.

The instrument says the criticized batch is more compelling than the named high bars. This disagreement with the trigger observation is informative: the checklist measures plot/form completion. It penalizes `Spare Hair Tie` for no irreversible action and penalizes `Wedding` for no conventional failure cost, despite their impact. “Compelling” cannot be added to a gate under this operational definition.

### H4 — simultaneous device load caused local causality loss

**Verdict: ORIGINAL QUANTITY CLAIM FALSIFIED; HIERARCHY CLAIM SURVIVES IN NARROWER FORM.**

- B does carry multiple named engines per pair.
- The 2026-08-21 control carries at least as many frozen dimensions: 14 orthogonality rows plus pair-specific medium, architecture, technique, and eligibility controls.
- Therefore B is not 1.5×, much less 2×, the Wedding control under a common manual coding.
- High device load does not produce higher blind hard-cut rates in the primary comparison.

The surviving observation comes from sequence and role, not count: 08-21 fixes the event and emotional engine before assigning devices; 08-29 fixes six semantic operations and selects scenes that can demonstrate them.

### H5 — a monotone break began through post-Wedding constraint accretion

**Verdict: FALSIFIED.**

- There is no 50% constraint rise over the Wedding run.
- Timeline carry falls on 08-24/25, recovers on 08-26/27/28, and falls again on 08-29. The pattern is not monotone.
- Section counts rise through 08-28 while carry also improves, defeating section/turn count as a simple cause.

The post-Wedding period has a recurring abstraction tendency, but “the system simply accumulated too many rules” is not supported.

### H6 — fragments and lists are mechanisms, not the disease

**Verdict: SUPPORTED IN THE PREREGISTERED COUNTEREXAMPLE SENSE; INTERACTION REMAINS OPEN.**

- B list-run values span .000–.187; its type-7 interquartile interval is approximately .010–.115.
- `Triple Arch Over Me` has a .098 list-run rate, inside that interval, and clears 4/5 blind causal signals.
- `Burning Bush With a Barcode` is also inside the B list interval at .096.
- The reader classifies the high-bar fragment sequences as pressure, not disconnection.

The want-conditioned interaction was not estimated reliably at this sample size. The counterexamples are sufficient to reject an anti-fragment/list rule.

## The failed gate exposed by the test

The 2026-08-29 QA said the six songs had first-listen scenes, wants, irreversible acts, and consequential semantic rekeys. The clean blind reader agrees. The Scientist still reports that none reaches the high bar. Both can be true because the QA asked whether the apparatus completed its intended transformation, not whether the initial pressure was recognizable enough to make that transformation matter.

Fresh clean-context QA then re-opened the publish boundary without seeing identities during its first read. It reverses the old set verdict from all-SHIP to **Overall REPAIR: P02/P03 SHIP, P04 HOLD, P01/P05/P06 REPAIR**. Its findings are concrete: P01's second meaning relies on unrendered voice placement, P05's opening chorus is procedure before person, P06 contains sung production commentary, and P04's missing grammar shares a risk with two return-floor misses. This corroborates a lenient old Somatic gate. It does not establish that P02 or P03 beats the user-named high bars; text-package SHIP is a floor, not that absolute judgment.

The high bars show multiple non-plot sources of compulsion:

- `ASK FOR IT BY NAME`: a stable moral debt—receiving credit for Maeve's sound—exists before the reveal.
- `Spare Hair Tie`: “this should be enough, and I still want more” is a recognizable contradiction without an irreversible act.
- `Triple Arch Over Me`: scale changes self-conception; contemplation is the event.
- `Burning Bush With a Barcode`: commodification threatens a speaking self; the metaphor pile returns to survival.
- `Wedding`: living use and breakage coexist inside one continuous craft world; the maker need not state a conventional want.

Recent songs often provide the converse: complete event logic around a pressure the listener was asked to accept from the brief.

## Narrow conclusion

The best-supported diagnosis is **proxy capture plus hierarchy inversion**:

> We learned to require scenes, wants, actions, returns, and rekeys. Then we began treating successful execution of those requirements as proof of emotional force. In the criticized batch, a semantic mechanism was chosen first and a scene was built to make it bodily. The lyrics are parseable; the listener's common pressure is not always primary.

This is a diagnosis, not a rule change. The pressure-first intervention in `PANEL_REPORT.md` must win a controlled plain-lyric test before any pipeline edit is justified.
