# Structural Comparison — Wedding state vs. 2026-08-29

## Result

The evidence rejects **constraint quantity** as the primary cause. The Wedding run was at least as specified as the criticized run. What changed was the **hierarchy of the specification**: the 2026-08-21 devices were assigned to express one already-loaded event; the 2026-08-29 event was designed to prove a device.

## The hierarchy inversion

| 2026-08-21 | 2026-08-29 |

| Event first: destruction versus living use | Operation first: establish and re-key a semantic device |
| Emotional engine is one sentence about keeping, hearing, and continued practice | Emotional engine is a menu of live-chance verbs |

> In the high-bar songs, the device is how one pressure speaks. In the criticized batch, the pressure is often why the device gets to perform.

## Gate drift

The 2026-08-29 evaluation's principal facets were Scene–Want Inhabitability, Consequential Semantic Rekey, Return Pleasure, Hope/Appetite/Play, and Current-Chassis Integrity. Those facets are aligned to the run's intended solution. They do not independently measure whether the initial pressure is emotionally recognizable or whether a listener has a reason to care before the hinge arrives.

Three concrete calibration problems followed:

1. **The selected set was not the global best six.** One-per-pair was binding. `Look What You Taught Me` ranked **19th of 24** and passed ACCESSIBLE eligibility at exactly **3.50**, yet it had to represent P01.
2. **The “blind” Golden comparison was identity-blind, not interpretation-blind.** The judge saw complete prompts, production maps, form annotations, and unrendered intended effects. It could reward a center-voice theft, chair-slot substitution, missing kick, or bass reversal that no audio had proved.
3. **The scores were relative to the run's own thesis.** Six distinct engines and six successful rekeys can all pass even if none reaches the absolute emotional bar named by The Scientist.

The clean lyric-only reader reproduces the same proxy problem in a different form: five of the six recent songs clear at least four of five causal “compulsion” signals, while only two of the five user-named high bars do. That is not evidence that the user is wrong. It is evidence that the operational definition—want, failure cost, irreversible action, chorus consequence, changed final return—rewards engineered plot completeness and does not measure the impact she named.

## What did not cause this

- **Cleverness itself:** falsified by `ASK FOR IT BY NAME` and by clever high-bar work throughout the cohort.
- **Fragments:** the recent batch has fewer ultra-short and fragment-proxy lines than the high-bar median; high-bar fragments often carry pressure.
- **A simple post-Wedding downward slope:** local continuity recovers on 08-26 through 08-28 before falling again on 08-29.
- **Too few verbs:** 08-29 has the timeline's highest action-lexicon rate. The issue is what those actions accumulate into.
- **Constraint count alone:** Wedding carries at least as many frozen formal obligations.

## Surviving diagnosis

The regression is best described as **proxy capture plus hierarchy inversion**:

1. The seed promotes a formal operation to portfolio subject.
2. Pair briefs back-fill an occupiable scene and a future verb around that operation.
3. Selection rewards successful execution of the operation.
4. QA verifies parseability and causal rekey, then calls that compelling.
5. A listener receives a complete mechanism but may never receive a pressure she recognizes as hers.

This remains a text diagnosis. Without recent renders, no conclusion is available about sung phrasing, melodic choppiness, or generator smoothing.
