## Lighting plan (three lights, nothing else may shine)
- **Sovereign light:** the one ember-amber lantern, doubled by gold leaf verso behind the glass — every flame in frame owns a second self in reflection. It rules the courtyard half.
- **Loyal rim:** storm light — a low pewter-teal edge from the held sky, rimming Esperanza's shawl and the roofline so her silhouette separates from the dark adobe.
- **Ambient whisper:** the held teal air itself; deep-water luminance from the suspended galaxies inside the standing sea, kept below the lantern's authority.
- Discipline: shadows keep texture (adobe tooth, wool weave); highlights keep texture (leaf-metal burnish); no haze, no volumetric soup — atmosphere is earned by foam beads, grain, and one lifted shawl thread.

## Material & craft-evidence plan
- **Reverse-glass logic:** the image reads as paint applied BEHIND glass — colors flat, edges crisp, a mirror-depth sheen across the whole surface; the gold shows through where the lantern lives, palladium where the sea lives (the two-leaf temperature split is the pair's signature device).
- **Carved-plane adobe:** courtyard walls and door as flat carved color planes, kiln-fired warmth held inside the amber register; no photographic mush.
- **Refractive reward:** at zoom, galaxies of suspended light inside the water column — the venue's twenty-second hold.
- **Holographic sheen:** confined to the tideline seam ONLY; the seam is the sharpest, most resolved zone in frame (F4).
- **Contact point:** foam beading along the doorsill's wood grain — the viewer's hand knows that sill.
