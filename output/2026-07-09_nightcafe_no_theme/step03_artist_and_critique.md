## Critiques & revision directions (voice, one beat each; scores justified)

**C1 · after Harrison (9.2):** "Tonal unity is the engine of longing — keep the wheat and sky in two masses of warm dusk, let the doorway be the single note of another temperature. The dark attic window is correct: a rest in the hymn." *Revision:* the flour at the threshold must catch the low sun — the humblest gold in frame. Facet-fit: caught second ✓ (hand off rail), irreducible guest ✓, splinter ✓.

**C2 · after Grimshaw (6.8):** "Gaslight and wet stone I bless; but a parlor pasted on a platform is two paintings quarreling." **DISAGREEMENT #1** — the construct praises (8); the Estrangement Officer scores 5.5: *"Nostalgia-kitsch; the seam is a join, not a fusion."* *Revision if promoted:* the parlor's armchairs must align with the platform benches' rhythm — one row of furniture becoming the other (Seam Weaver's fusion band). Score held at 6.8.

**C3 · after Murdoch (8.8):** "Starch-grain color was made for gardens that shouldn't exist — the plate blooms where the world does. Keep the wet mackintosh dark so the dahlias carry all saturation." *Revision:* the upward rain must read as autochrome grain-streaks, not streaked glass — craft-evidence. Splinter (empty tin) placed on the bench's front edge, in focus.

**C4 · after Csontváry (8.4):** "A tree that blooms against the calendar is a prophet. But twelve trees preach; ONE bare tree among eleven blooming is scripture." *Revision:* thin the orchard to seven trees, bare one nearest the viewer — Scale Poetry through count, not size. **DISAGREEMENT #2** — Diorama Warmth Engineer wants deep orchard rows (depth planes); construct insists on frontal iconic flatness; resolved 60/40 toward depth with the bare tree as foreground plane.

**C5 · after Ilsted (9.0):** "A moon in a kitchen is only believable if the kitchen is utterly believable — worn enamel, chipped sill paint, the towel's real weave. Then the impossible needs no defense." **DISAGREEMENT #3** — Decisive Geometer: "an exhale is barely a motion; static risk (6.5)." Resolution: the moon *leans toward her* (tide-pull) + the exhale drifts toward the moon — two vectors meeting = the caught second. Score stands 9.0.

**C6 · after Ancher (8.2):** "Light on linen is my home ground; an aurora folded AS linen is honest wonder. But the monogram whispers too quietly at thumbnail." *Revision:* monogrammed pillowcase moves adjacent to the aurora, its embroidery catching the same light — splinter promoted one tier in the focal hierarchy.

**C7 · after Hamaguchi (9.4):** "Mezzotint's rocked black holds what daylight cannot: a whale's belly at chimney height, rimmed in one saffron line. Burnish ONLY the flukes, the eclipse ring, her dropped punch — the rest stays velvet." **DISAGREEMENT #4** — Diorama Warmth Engineer: "cold street, cold verdict — venue wants warmth (6)." Resolution (Color Thermostat): saffron is the guest's own temperature; add two lit windows down the row answering the flukes — warmth as counterpoint, not wash. Score 9.4 confirmed.

**C8 · after Pirosmani (8.6):** "A stairwell is a poor man's mountain path; cranes make it holy. Flatten perspective slightly — the spiral should read like a painted tin sign, tender and direct." *Revision:* skylight eclipse-bands stripe the walls in silver — otherwise the hour is lost indoors. Thread-on-leg splinter is first-rate (contact + debt in one device).

**C9 · after Baumann (9.3):** "Adobe holds dusk like a kiln holds heat. Print the courtyard in flat carved planes — teal sea-wall, ember lantern, one white foam line — and the gold-leaf verso will do the weeping." *Revision:* the tide's stopped edge must be RULER-straight at the sill — the sea obeying a doorstep is the impossibility; any slosh kills it. Mourning gloves stay on (Prosecutor's standing order).

**C10 · after Rozentāls (6.9):** "Charming — and charm is the danger." **DISAGREEMENT #5** — construct scores 8; Spectacle Prosecutor 5: *"Fish carrying lamplight is a greeting card wearing boots."* *Revision if promoted:* the dry sandbags must carry old water-stains — defenses that failed before meeting a sea that finally came kind. Held at 6.9.

**C11 · after Zeshin (9.1):** "Lacquer is patience made visible. The creature must be built of lantern-light the way maki-e is built of sprinkled gold — layered, not glowing. Its trail on wet slate is the truest stroke in the composition; let the trail, not the creature, prove it circled her." *Revision:* kettle steam bending toward it = the air's testimony; keep her shadow's wrong direction crisp at the feet.

**C12 · after John (7.8):** "Small rooms hold large loves. But smallness must not reach the thumbnail." **DISAGREEMENT #6** — Poster Laureate: "the light-marten dies at 128px (5.5)." *Revision if promoted:* crop to the bench — marten mass one-third of frame, Ada's profile and shawl behind, broken lamp's dark head in the margin. Held at 7.8.
