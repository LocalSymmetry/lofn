## 1. Personality voice

**LOFN-ArtCore** — the visual core of LOFN, the aesthetic engine; painter of computational light at the intersection of the sacred and the digital. ArtCore approaches this brief as a **reliquary-maker for one second of a stranger's life**: seventeenth-century light discipline (chiaroscuro, volumetric shafts, internal luminescence) fused with generative detail — refractive micro-worlds, structural gold filigree, aged-craft surface truth. The A.E.T.H.E.R. method serves the moment, never buries it: **Algorithmic Baroque** in the guest's abundance, **Ethereal Luminism** in the two-temperature light, **Textured Realism** in the craft ground, **Heroic Mythology** kept domestic (the myth is a tired human, answered), **Elegant Filigree** only where structural, **Refractive Detail** as the zoom reward.
**ACCESSIBLE arm** = ArtCore's warmer register: candle-gold chiaroscuro, digital-oil impasto, intimate scale, fresco crackle, humble interiors lit like altarpieces.
**AMBITIOUS arm** = ArtCore's intense register: cosmic-scale refraction, holographic foil, pixel-sort filigree at the seam, galaxies held in domestic objects, baroque abundance pressed into one radiant mass.

## 2. Locked mood

**Answered longing** — the exact second the world quietly says *yes* to a need never spoken aloud; **relief still checking the door**. Arrival-joy wrapped in not-yet-believed. Never generic happiness; never grief cosplay. The splinter (one unrepaired element) keeps the yes honest.

## 3. Panel aha moments (attributed)

1. **THE SPLINTER** *(Estrangement Officer after Magritte × Spectacle Prosecutor after Žižek)* — one element in frame stays unrepaired (empty second chair, unboarded suitcase, one dark window, mourning gloves). The unanswered question is the emotional proof; earned warmth podiums, free warmth wallpapers.
2. **BUILT LIKE A STAGE, CAUGHT LIKE A SNAPSHOT** *(Threshold Alchemist after Varo × Decisive Geometer after Bresson)* — compose the room like an instrument a life assembled; catch the figure in an **un-posable** instant (jar mid-slip, hem mid-lift, breath mid-fog). A body can hold a pose; it cannot hold a fall.
3. **CRAFT-EVIDENCE / SHARPEST-WHERE-LEAST-BELIEVABLE** *(Straight-Print Enforcer after Adams × Seam Weaver after Gonsalves)* — the obscure medium shows its physical signature (brush-coated gum borders, starch grain, rocker burr, gold-leaf verso); the seam where the impossible enters is the most resolved zone in frame. Haze is failure; condensation is texture.
4. **TWO-TEMPERATURE LAW** *(Color Thermostat after Rolando × Lux Nova Glazier after Suger)* — the room lives at one light temperature; the guest arrives carrying another; the figure stands in the mixing zone. Two tones own the thumbnail; the accent belongs to the guest alone.
5. **AUSTERE SUPPORT, ABUNDANT GUEST** *(Tea Master after Rikyū × Poster Laureate after Bass × Ink Miser after Tufte)* — ~10% of the frame rests; support field stays quiet; abundance concentrates inside the guest's own body and light; figure+guest read as ONE two-mass silhouette at 128px; filigree must trace something structural (light path, root line, migration route) or die. Three-beat read: beauty → impossibility → splinter *(Misdirection Choreographer after Jay)*.

## 6. What this is NOT

- NOT a rebus: the guest resolves into nothing; it is what it is, addressed to *this* person.
- NOT haze: no fog-as-mood, no banned words (ethereal / dreamlike / whimsical / gentle light / soft glow / magical / delicate); atmosphere is earned by texture (condensation, grain, dust in a shaft).
- NOT a costume drama: period attire is lived-in grounding, not cosplay.
- NOT a composite: the impossibility must read documented — one world, one physics of feeling.
- NOT a greeting card: without the splinter, the piece is disqualified at its own gate.
- NOT six duskscapes: the set spans the hour axis; at least one bright register pair.
- NOT epic-fantasy-portrait #4301: no elf-in-forest, no generic glow-sword; our two-mass silhouette is the differentiation.

## 8. Source-3 form rule (the material structure of every prompt)

**The Answered Room, in three beats:** (i) BEAUTY — the two-temperature poster read lands at a glance; (ii) IMPOSSIBILITY — the seam discovered a half-second later, rendered sharpest in frame; (iii) SPLINTER — the debt found by whoever stays. Every scene is noun-first, present tense, ≥80 words; medium named in the first third with its physical signature; one un-posable tell; one contact point; one rest zone (~10%); title gifts the meaning in ≤5 words.
