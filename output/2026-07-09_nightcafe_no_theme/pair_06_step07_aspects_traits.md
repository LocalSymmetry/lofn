## Mood
Answered longing at closing time: the last, coldest ten minutes of the night shift, and the world's quiet *yes* has already landed on her carrying-pole — heavy, grooming itself, unbothered. Relief still checking the door: she has not set the pole down, her grip has not loosened, and one hook two stalls over hangs empty. AWE register, ArtCore's intense channel: cosmic-grade light pressed into a cat-sized body at a tea stall; the sacred delivered at the scale of a working tool. Never generic happiness — the warmth is priced by the shuttered neighbor.

## Style
Urushi lacquer nightscape executed with maki-e discipline: the whole lane is layered, polished lacquer dark (roiro depth with visible grain — Zone II, never void), and everything warm is *sprinkled metal*, not emitted haze. The creature is Algorithmic Baroque contained in one body: carp-bodied, whiskered, long as a cat, its abundance interior — refractive micro-detail inside each burnished scale, gold graded coarse to fine along the spine, edges togidashi-crisp. Elegant Filigree appears ONLY as the structural gold trail: the literal path record of its circling on the wet slate. Flairs in play: Familiar Aliens (a creature of light that grooms like a bird), Physics of Feeling (the shadow obeys devotion, not optics), Scale Poetry (celestial light at housecat scale), Dream Documentation (all of it rendered administratively sharp), Temporal Honey (blue hour stretched to its last minutes).

## Lighting plan (one sovereign, one loyal, one whisper)
- **Sovereign — the creature itself:** the lane's one rich light source, a radial cone of lantern-gold thrown from its built body; brightest zone in frame, and also the SHARPEST (sharpest-where-least-believable: scale edges, whisker lines, trail flecks).
- **Loyal rim — persimmon stall-lantern on Mei:** one working paper-and-wire stall lantern rims her apron shoulder, sleeve, and jaw in persimmon; it explains her visibility and gives the wrong-way shadow a light to contradict.
- **Ambient whisper — blue hour paling:** pre-sunrise slate-blue skims the zinc awnings and wet slate; it argues quietly that the night is ending, which is why the creature's staying matters. Nothing else may glow; reflections only borrow.

## Material & craft-evidence plan
- **Urushi ground:** layered black lacquer polished to wet-mirror depth; the "sky" band and shadow field carry visible lacquer grain and brush-layer topology — texture in every zone.
- **Maki-e creature and trail:** sprinkled gold powder burnished INTO the surface (togidashi logic): the creature reads as metal built scale by scale, crisp-edged; the trail on the slate reads as gold dust settled into lacquer grooves, circle over circle.
- **Wet slate:** pavement as dark polished stone holding persimmon and gold reflections as sharp verso-like streaks, never bloom.
- **Working-world props:** bamboo carrying-pole (bowing), brass kettle with bending steam, oil-cloth apron, rolled cotton sleeves, glass cups, zinc awnings, one padlock — the Gentle Cartographer's handshake objects, lived-in, 20th-century Malaysian night-market specific, respectful and unromanticized.
- **Failure conditions (Straight-Print Enforcer):** glow-wash creature, blurred gold, fog, empty untextured black — all fail F2 on sight.

## Three-tier focal hierarchy
1. **First fixation (beauty):** the fused two-mass silhouette — dark Mei + gold carp-creature on the pole — inside the radial gold cone against wet-slate blue.
2. **Second (impossibility):** the wrong-way shadow: her shadow lying toward the creature against every light; secondary support from the gold circle-trail recording its courtship laps.
3. **Third (splinter):** the neighboring stall shuttered, its lantern hook empty — parked in the cone's outer dark where only the twenty-second viewer finds it.

## Story cue
Mei, 39, Chinese-Malaysian tea-seller, closing her pasar-malam stall at blue hour after every reason to have gone home hours ago. A creature of layered lantern-light — carp-bodied, whiskered, long as a cat — circled her on the wet slate all night, unnoticed, and its sprinkled-gold trail is the proof; now it has landed on her bamboo carrying-pole and begun grooming itself like a bird that intends to stay. Her shadow already knows: it points toward the creature against every light in the lane. The neighboring stall is shuttered, its lantern hook empty — whoever hung a light there is not coming back, and the creature did not choose that hook. The question the frame never answers: why her — and why not there.

## Variation angles (behavioral lens — the creature's four behaviors)
- **V1 Perching:** the canonical roost on the carrying-pole, grooming; full spotlight cone.
- **V2 Grooming the Tell:** it preens one loose feather of light that drops as a square maki-e flake onto her sleeve; closest crop, contact at the wrist.
- **V3 Leading:** it moves three stalls down the lane, trail bright behind it; she is caught mid-first-step following.
- **V4 Asleep in the Doorway:** banked to coal-persimmon in her stall doorway as blue hour pales; the empty neighbor hook nearest the viewer; the quiet register.
