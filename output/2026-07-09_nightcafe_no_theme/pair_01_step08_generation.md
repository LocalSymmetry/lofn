## V1 · The Doorway — threshold pour of light, figure at gate

```
A gum bichromate print with brush-coated borders, aubergine pigment feathered past the frame edge, holds Kansas Turkey Red wheat in the last minutes of golden hour, where a long-demolished farmhouse kitchen stands open and lit, no path leading to it, wheat pressing unbroken against the doorsill. The doorway pours candle-gold lamplight in raised digital-oil impasto, and through it the rooms recede doorway after doorway, deeper than the clapboard shell could hold, every jamb crisp. Halyna, 63, a Ukrainian-American farm woman in a man's canvas chore coat, silver braid rimmed with low amber, stands at the pasture gate lower left, her hand open just above the rail it has released. Spilled flour dusts the threshold and field grass, the frame's one pearl note. The attic window stays dark. Open wheat rests at left beneath a fresco-crackled dusk sky. Thirty years demolished, and supper is still warm.
```

- ③ Materials: brush-coated borders, Turkey Red wheat, clapboard, canvas chore coat, gate rail, flour, fresco crackle.

## V3 · The Laid Table — table set for two in window light, one chair pulled out

```
A gum bichromate print in aubergine and candle gold, hand-brushed emulsion borders visible, holds a farm kitchen standing lit in evening Turkey Red wheat, window and open door facing the viewer squarely. Inside, in dense digital-oil impasto: an oilcloth table laid for two — round loaf, butter dish, two enamel cups — one chair pulled out, and rooms beyond the table receding door after door past where the outside wall should stop. Halyna, 63, a Ukrainian-American farm woman in a man's canvas coat, silver braid over one shoulder, stands at the gate lower left, her hand just lifted from the rail, knuckles still curved to its shape, face in three-quarter profile. Spilled flour spreads pearl across the doorsill into field grass. Above the bright window, the attic stays dark. Low sun grazes the wheat heads; open field rests at the left edge. No one has eaten here in thirty years.
```

**Word count: 150** (range 80–150 ✓)
- ① Emotional seed: final line "No one has eaten here in thirty years" against the laid table.
- ② Medium as agent: the impasto table setting is the thickest paint in frame — the offer made physical.
- ③ Materials: oilcloth, round loaf, butter dish, enamel cups, emulsion borders, canvas coat.
- ④ Lighting: window-and-door light frontal (sovereign) / low sun on wheat heads (loyal) / aubergine surround (whisper).
- ⑤ Hierarchy: laid table in the bright window → rooms past the outside wall → curved knuckles / flour / dark attic.
- ⑥ Chromatic story: gold held inside the panes; aubergine field; pearl flour crossing the sill toward her.
- ⑦ Incompleteness: the second setting and pulled-out chair; the attic dark above the bright window.

## V4 · The Stair and Attic — the dark attic window carries the frame

```
A gum bichromate print, pigment brushed past its borders in visible strokes, climbs from a wheat-field gate to the gable of an impossible farmhouse at dusk: every lower window burns candle gold in layered digital-oil impasto — doorway, hearth window, stair window — and above them the attic window alone stays aubergine dark beneath a fresco-crackled sky. Through the stair window, lit treads climb toward that one unlit room and stop at its shadow line. Halyna, 63, a silver-braided Ukrainian-American farm woman in a man's canvas chore coat, stands small at the lower-left gate, face lifted three-quarter, her hand open just above the rail it released. Spilled flour dusts threshold and field grass, a pearl seam under all that gold. Wheat grows to the doorsill; no path. Open land rests along the bottom. Every room lit itself for her except one, and she is looking at that one.
```

**Word count: 148** (range 80–150 ✓)
- ① Emotional seed: "Every room lit itself for her except one, and she is looking at that one."
- ② Medium as agent: the print climbs the gable; impasto gold stacks window by window until the print's dark wins the top pane.
- ③ Materials: gable clapboard, stair treads, brushed borders, canvas coat, flour seam.
- ④ Lighting: stacked lit windows (sovereign) / lifted face catching their spill (loyal) / crackled dusk sky (whisper).
- ⑤ Hierarchy: golden window-climb → stair treads stopping at shadow → small figure / flour seam / the dark pane itself.
- ⑥ Chromatic story: gold ascends, aubergine holds the summit; pearl seam grounds the base.
- ⑦ Incompleteness: the attic refuses the light; her look upward is unreturned.
