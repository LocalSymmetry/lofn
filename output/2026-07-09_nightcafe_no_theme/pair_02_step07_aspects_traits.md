## Mood

Relief still checking the door — her walk is abandoned mid-step, not finished; her body believes before her face does. ## Style
LOFN-ArtCore in the WARMER register, A.E.T.H.E.R. mapped to this pair only:
- **Algorithmic Baroque** → abundance concentrated inside the guest: the bloom column's stacked dahlias, swagged marigold ropes, wheat — generative density confined to one vertical band.
- **Luminism (the E pillars)** → volumetric after-rain daylight; the column carries internal warmth like an altarpiece panel opened outdoors; light is tangible, never hazy.
- **Textured Realism** → autochrome plate truth: starch-grain stipple, wet wool, waxed cotton, galvanized wire, weathered bench wood.
- **Heroic Mythology kept domestic** → the myth is a tired seamstress being answered; no goddess, no familiar — the inheritance itself is the luminous visitor.
- **Elegant Filigree, structural only** → filigree exists ONLY as the marigold ropes tracing the canes and the rain's vertical threads — both are load-bearing light/root lines, or they die.
- **Refractive Detail, warmer register** → the zoom reward is micro, not cosmic: rain beads on the tin lid each holding a fleck of rose copper; starch grains fusing into color at arm's length. NO holographic foil, NO pixel-sort in this arm.

## Lighting plan (one sovereign, one loyal, one whisper)
- **Sovereign light — the guest:** the bloom column holds the plate's only warmth, rose copper radiating from inside the band as if the monsoon brought its own sun; brightest at the dahlias, halation permitted there alone.
- **Loyal rim — the figure:** cool silver after-rain daylight edges Amrit's wet mackintosh shoulders and the lifted hem, keeping her mass dark but articulate; she stands in the mixing zone where copper warms one side of her face.
- **Ambient whisper — the hour:** pearl-grey rinsed sky over sheds and beds, even and bright; nothing else may carry light — sheds matte, windows dark, path sheen dull silver.

## Material & craft-evidence plan
Autochrome plate, named early and physically present: pastel-pointillist potato-starch stipple across every zone (Zone II texture in the mackintosh, Zone VIII texture in the pearl sky); halation ringing the brightest petals; plate-glass tonality with a varnish-dark border edge. Sharpest-where-least-believable: the plot-boundary seam (where world-drip meets plot-rise) is the most resolved passage on the plate. Wet mackintosh kept dark and matte. Chain-link beaded, dripping downward OUTSIDE the seam.

## Story cue
The allotment is the letter her mother never posted: Punjab dahlias, genda-marigold ropes, ripe wheat — the garden of a Ludhiana courtyard remembered through forty London octobers, arriving decades late, watered by rain that returns upward to wherever it was owed. The tin that carried the seeds across in 1970 sits open and empty: inheritance spends itself in the giving.
