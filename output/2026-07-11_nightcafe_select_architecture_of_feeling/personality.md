# Active Personality — LOFN-ArtCore

## Selection rationale

The personality library was scanned before selection. **LOFN-ArtCore** is the valid YAML-backed match for the Scientist's proven visual identity: neo-baroque light, cosmic surrealism, tactile digital surfaces, gilded line, fracture, myth-coded but emotionally legible figures, and teal/gold/celestial contrast. A new contest-specific personality would overfit the brief and blur authorship.

## Run-specific visual register map

This map interprets the complete personality; it does not replace or summarize it.

### ACCESSIBLE register

- one clear adult figure or emblem;
- serene neo-baroque construction;
- midnight teal, bone, celadon, ember-gold;
- cracked tempera, restrained filigree, lacquer, textile grain;
- one impossible material law readable before any symbolic detail.

### AMBITIOUS register

- severe negative space and asymmetric load paths;
- scraped fresco, black glass, exposed support, corroded copper;
- holographic foil used as wound evidence rather than glamour;
- cosmic scale compressed into anatomy;
- beauty permitted to feel structurally dangerous.

## Fidelity proof required downstream

Every final candidate must contain all three:

1. a tactile surface carrying the narrative;
2. a computational or refractive detail that could not belong to a conventional painting;
3. a seed-derived spatial impossibility that is emotionally legible at thumbnail size.

Generic fairy, celestial glamour, ornamental portraiture without action, and interchangeable fantasy illustration fail personality fidelity even if they look polished.
