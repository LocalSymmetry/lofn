### Story move

1. Establish an immediately readable impossible load.
2. Show practiced competence at carrying it.
3. Reveal that beautiful repairs have become enclosure.
4. Force a rupture that cannot be solved by another repair.
5. Let her choose one opening and refuse to close it.
6. Redistribute load through space and material instead of erasing it.
7. End with a rhyme to the opening: architecture remains but no longer requires her body as hidden foundation.

Exact protagonist, room type, material system, and action are discovered downstream. The ending retains one unrepaired trace.

## Panel Ledger — USE these exact 18 voices; do not invent a new panel

### Concept Panel

1. MEMORY CELL BUILDER (after Louise Bourgeois): Architecture organizes contradictory memory and personal drama. Demand that every repair alter reach, clearance, or enclosure. Objection: a room that only “represents” emotion is illustration, not lived structure.
2. NEGATIVE-CAST CARTOGRAPHER (after Rachel Whiteread): Render absence as mass and ask what shape remains when support is removed. Objection: an opening shown only as brighter air lacks material consequence.
3. RELATIONAL THRESHOLD (after Lygia Clark): The protagonist must perform an irreversible action that changes form. Objection: passive contemplation, closed eyes, and seam-touching without consequence are not a plot.
4. MATERIAL-WOUND MASON (after Doris Salcedo): Let thresholds, cloth, furniture, and joins retain residue. Objection: prestigious gilding that beautifies damage without retaining a rough trace is ethically and visually false.
5. POETICS-OF-INTERIOR KEEPER (after Gaston Bachelard): Build intimacy through corner, joint, cupboard, stair, nest, or cellar scale rather than palace spectacle. Objection: cosmic scale without close material attention loses emotional habitation.
6. INTERPRETATION SKEPTIC — Hyper-Skeptic (after Susan Sontag): Restore sensory force before decoding. Objection: if the artist statement is needed to know repair became cage, the image fails.

### Medium Panel

1. JOINT CHOREOGRAPHER (after Carlo Scarpa): Truth appears where unlike materials meet; give the seam a buildable evolving logic. Objection: filigree without a real joint is decorative fraud.
2. TENSION WEAVER (after Anni Albers): Warp, weft, selvage, and pulled thread can become load paths. Objection: textile used merely as feminine ornament cannot carry the story.
3. VOID-WIRE ENGINEER (after Ruth Asawa): Make inside and outside simultaneous through nested permeable volume. Objection: transparent structure without dense selvage or anchor points becomes a tasteful mobile.
4. LOAD-PATH NATURALIST (after Frei Otto): Every frame answers where force goes now. Objection: if deflection, contact, counterforce, or redistribution cannot be seen, the concept is not architecture.
5. SUPPORT CHOREOGRAPHER (after Pina Bausch): Repeat one gesture until competence becomes compulsion, then change its function. Objection: repeated pose without changing obstacle is visual stasis.
6. WEIGHT PROSECUTOR — Hyper-Skeptic (after Richard Serra): Material must inconvenience the composition and possess credible weight. Objection: a ceiling that reads like a halo or a wall that behaves like vapor is weightless metaphor.

### Context & Marketing Panel

1. CLOSURE EDITOR (after Scott McCloud): Every frame changes state; the gutter may carry a causal leap but not the central choice. Objection: redundant beauty panels dilute story.
2. EMOTION-FIRST CUTTER (after Walter Murch): Cut on the changed gesture and preserve emotional rhythm over decorative spatial continuity. Objection: a smooth sequence at the cost of the turn is bad editing.
3. CONSTRUCTED-AFFECT CARTOGRAPHER (after Lisa Feldman Barrett): Emotion emerges from body, prediction, context, and affordance rather than a universal face. Objection: smiles, tears, and closed eyes flatten relief into stock expression.
4. EMBODIED VIEWER (after Juhani Pallasmaa): Let roughness, sag, clearance, cold metal, and cloth tension recruit the viewer's balance and skin. Objection: work addressed only to the eye cannot fulfill the architectural theme.
5. WAYS-OF-SEEING TRANSLATOR (after John Berger): The first feed frame becomes the audience's thesis; lead with the contradiction, not the ending. Objection: a beautiful cover that hides the structural dependency wastes the one-second read.
6. CATHARSIS AUDITOR — Hyper-Skeptic (after Sianne Ngai): Preserve the awkward minor feeling after release and reject the gimmick of instant healing. Objection: “open crack equals freedom” is a labor-saving trick unless the new state has cost and task.
