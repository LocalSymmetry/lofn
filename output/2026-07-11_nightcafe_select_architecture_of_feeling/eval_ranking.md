## Method

Competition totals use the required fixed weights:

`Originality × 25% + Technical Execution × 20% + Emotional Impact × 20% + Bold Choice × 15% + Platform Fit × 10% + Viral Potential × 10%`.

Scores are 1–10. The table is sorted by the unrounded weighted total; displayed totals are rounded to two decimals. The five run-specific facets from `eval_scoring_facets.md` are a separate seed/NightCafe fidelity diagnostic and do not alter the competition total.

## Eligibility audit — seven visual properties

1. **BL — Embodied physical location/body evidence (15%)**: contact, posture, clearance, pressure, or aftertask locates the feeling.

4. **SP — Surprising concrete specificity (15%)**: one exact physical witness prevents generic allegory.
5. **CE — Thumbnail/cognitive ease (10%)**: subject and contradiction survive feed scale.
6. **CD — Viewer co-discovery of the structural turn (15%)**: evidence lets the viewer infer the changed load rather than merely receive an explanation.
7. **EG — Entry-gradient/visual threshold (10%)**: an easy first read leads into material and conceptual depth without an abrupt comprehension wall.

### Selected engine — P06: Failure Becomes Mesh

P06 wins the story-engine decision because it already contains eight materially distinguishable causal states: body-fitted compression, repeated over-repair, growing gauge disparity, quiet central failure, strands catching at visible eyelets, load spreading into edge anchors, a settled permeable shell, and ordinary hands performing a modest task while one check survives. The architecture does not merely open; its structural logic changes from one rigid member and one body to a distributed set of relations. The charred brace, black fragment, celadon eyelets, brocade collars, wire gauges, and same adult craftswoman provide a stable continuity bible, while plan-to-ground framing prevents eight near-identical portraits.

- **Best cover: P06V1 — Failure Becomes Mesh.** The broken black interruption, paused adult hands, and four large anchor routes make the contradiction legible before the viewer studies the mesh. It is also the globally #2 single candidate.
- **Best ending: P06V2 — Ordinary Hands.** The loaded shell remains visually active while one hand folds linen and the other still touches ceramic, completing the final-frame rhyme without a cure pose.

### Runner-up — P04: Volume of What Wasn't

P04 is the runner-up, not the winner, despite supplying the top-ranked single prompt. Its exact stair/cast theorem, handprints, oak feet, and floor deflection are exceptionally coherent and differentiated. It loses specifically because reverse chronology hides the decisive set-down from the natural feed order, while repeated black-stair/white-cast arrangements risk making the middle of an eight-frame sequence spatially redundant. P06 offers more visibly distinct material states without surrendering the same causal rigor.
