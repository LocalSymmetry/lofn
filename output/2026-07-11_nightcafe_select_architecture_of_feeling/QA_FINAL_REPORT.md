## Concrete Rejection Arguments

1. **Decorative-web substitution.** Frame 5 asks Flux to coordinate four heavy routes, fine strands, eyelets, brocade, a fragment, hands, anchors, and ceiling deformation. A literal model may equalize the gauges, shrink eyelets into beads, turn collars into jewelry, or produce a cocoon/chandelier. That would erase the changed load path and fail Craft and Intent. The package survives this objection only because the pilot explicitly rejects those readings and permits one bounded simplification.

2. **Thumbnail bandwidth overload.** A 3:4 portrait must simultaneously preserve the adult, separated palms, black fragment, three or four endpoints, and ceiling response. Flux commonly crops corner supports or makes the ceiling lift too subtle at feed scale. If fewer than three endpoints and the ceiling response cannot be read together after the one correction, P06 is rejected regardless of full-size detail.

3. **Structural rhyme can become a verbal claim.** Frame 8 says it returns to the opening bearing line, but a fresh generation can change camera distance, body placement, or axis even with consistent identity. If Frames 1 and 8 do not share a visibly matched axis/body scale while reversing body contact, the ending is explanation rather than form and fails Cohesion.

4. **Identity can fail in non-portrait views.** Frame 2's overhead plan and Frame 6's high oblique view naturally reduce facial evidence. The exact identity sentence cannot guarantee the same woman, hands, forelock, or cuff side. Any two pilot frames losing the same identity triggers P04; any later identity drift requires correction or frame rejection.

5. **Aesthetic continuity can harden into mood-board sameness.** Every prompt repeats the same identity and medium vocabulary, so the renderer may default to eight polished copper-and-plaster portraits while ignoring slackness, engagement, split, catch, transfer, and aftertask. The sequence ships only if each rendered frame visibly changes support state, gesture, and spatial affordance.

The most consequential failure is not malformed detail; it is **false causality**—a beautiful web with no visible receiving support. The next is **false catharsis**—a relaxed or smiling woman in brighter light. The negative-prompt field should carry the provided exclusions; if tokens such as sparks, radiance, halo, smile, or jewelry appear despite negation, reject the candidate rather than explaining them away.
