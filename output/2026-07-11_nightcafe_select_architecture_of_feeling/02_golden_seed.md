# GOLDEN SEED — UNBRACING

## Lineage and why

Primary lineage: **SEED 15 — A Surreal Portrait Reflecting Our Souls**, selected before concept generation because the user's proven work repeatedly turns an adult figure into a vessel, witness, or catalyst for an interior world. It carries the right dual-emotion logic, material contrast, rare-medium pressure, and five-second emotional read.

Supporting structures: SEED 3 contributes decisive-moment series discipline and a recurring worn witness; SEED 14 contributes the visible emotional turn caused by one unexpected encounter. Their payloads and past outputs remain quarantined.

## Seed sentence

> **A body learns that an opening is not the same thing as a collapse.**

## Locked emotional architecture

- Relief after prolonged vigilance.
- Acceptance that safety does not require perfect repair.
- Residual unease: the body keeps checking whether the released load will return.

The exact feeling is **unbracing**—the first full breath after years of carrying a structure no one else could see. It is not serenity, a smiling cure, or uncomplicated liberation.

## Golden pattern retained

1. One invented adult protagonist acts as catalyst, witness, or vessel.
2. Two emotional registers coexist and change each other's meaning.
3. A primary and counter-medium make body and environment visibly different.
4. One surreal law carries the plot; symbols cannot pile up without consequence.
5. First-glance emotional impact opens into second-read psychological depth.

## New structural law

The protagonist and an invented interior exchange physical load. Architecture is causal: every joint, threshold, floor, membrane, and aperture changes what she must hold or where she can move. Each frame alters compression, permeability, support, symmetry, or inside/outside relation. Ornament is forensic evidence.

## Dangerous permission

- Beauty may become oppressive before it becomes useful.
- A crack may be an exit rather than damage.
- The final posture may remain uncertain.
- One frame may become radically sparse after dense ornament.
- The spatial physics may be impossible only when the emotional causality is unmistakable.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Adult protagonist only; no children or identifiable real people.
- No borrowed cultural or grief-specific identity.
- No text inside images.
- No fairy, witch, princess, cute familiar, generic apocalypse, heart, obvious key metaphor, clock, lighthouse, phoenix, castle, or cyberpunk city.
- No living-artist names in render prompts.
- One coherent protagonist, seam, and material law across the final Body of Work.
- The opening establishes an impossible load; the ending reverses its dependency without erasing one rough trace.
- Final packaging is eight frames; exploration remains 6 pairs × 4 variations = 24 candidates.

## Constraint vocabularies

1. Load-bearing anatomy: rib-vault / shoulder ceiling / throat hinge / spine stair / palm foundation / shadow column.
2. Material sequence: tempera→gilded seam→linen / tesserae→grout→water line / lacquer→gesso→wood / copper→verdigris→light-screen / stained glass→missing came→air / brocade→pulled thread→net.
3. Spatial rule: composure thickens walls / repairs lower ceiling / doors appear behind / exhale redraws plan / released symmetry breaks / light enters through refused repair.
4. Worn witness: missing tessera / cuff thread / cup ring / latch without key / black seed / bare backing cloth.
5. Frame grammar: frontal icon / body-room cutaway / overhead load plan / threshold silhouette / material close detail / final wide structural rhyme.

## Personality visual DNA constraint

The valid selected personality is LOFN-ArtCore. ACCESSIBLE pairs use its serene, legible neo-baroque register; AMBITIOUS pairs use its cracked, asymmetric, materially dangerous register. Generic fantasy or generic human-accessible substitutes are invalid.
