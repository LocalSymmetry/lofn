# NightCafe Select — Edition 1 Competition Rules

## Verified brief

- Venue: NightCafe Select, Edition 1.
- Theme: **The Architecture of Feeling**.
- Required work: one Body of Work that carries one story from start to finish through images, video, audio, or a combination. It must behave like a short film or mini exhibition, not a single image or an unsequenced mood board.
- Required artist statement: at least 30 words.
- Community gate: 20 likes on the Select feed advances an entry to expert judging.
- Expert criteria: **Concept, Craft, Cohesion, Intent**.
- Entry close: 12 July 2026. The accessible official material does not publish an exact closing time, so the operational deadline is 11 July.
- Winners announced: 20 July 2026.
- Judges named by NightCafe: Angus Russell, Sarah Impey, Gully Ahmed, Marco Pittarello.

## Judge-facing interpretation

- Marco Pittarello asks for a new visual language, a simple idea executed with conviction, genuine perspective, atmosphere rather than literal emotion, and curiosity over polish-for-polish's sake.
- Gully Ahmed emphasizes concise model-aware prompting, non-conflicting modifiers, composition and angles that draw the eye, symbolism, and story clarity within seconds.
- Prompt Magazine's editorial mission favors meaningful creation, critical reflection, responsible AI practice, and work with cultural or social consequence.

## Current field evidence supplied by the Scientist

The visible high-like field clusters around:

1. family or life-stage montage;
2. romance, fairy-core, and storybook rescue;
3. cute recurring fantasy protagonists;
4. conventional post-apocalyptic consequence;
5. a topical anthropomorphic-object video;
6. long collections whose quantity exceeds their narrative differentiation.

The field's strongest feed mechanics are recurring characters, an obvious motif, a one-sentence premise, a clean emotional reversal, and a cover frame that works before the caption is read. Its recurring weakness is that architecture is scenery, emotion is stated or acted, and visual language is familiar.

## Operational entry target

- One adult protagonist.
- One transformed recurring material motif.
- One story with a genuine turn and an ending that changes the meaning of the opening.
- Final exhibition target: **8 tightly curated frames**, plus one cover/contact-sheet order.
- Each frame must work at thumbnail scale; the complete sequence must reward a second viewing.
- No dependence on captions to make the story intelligible.
- The artist statement explains intent and method; it does not rescue an unclear sequence.
