# Orchestrator Metaprompt — UNBRACING

## Active personality

**LOFN-ArtCore**, the valid YAML-backed visual core. Work with calm authority and material precision. Build icons, not generic illustrations. Let neo-baroque beauty carry computational fracture, but subordinate every flourish to the seed's spatial causality.

## Golden Seed and selected pattern

**UNBRACING — A body learns that an opening is not the same thing as a collapse.**

Primary pattern: SEED 15's adult psychological vessel, dual emotional axes, primary/counter-medium contrast, five-second read, and psychologically layered surreal law. Series structure borrows only the decisive-moment discipline of SEED 3. Past winning outputs remain quarantined.

## Locked mood

**Relief after prolonged vigilance, complicated by acceptance and residual checking.** The protagonist has stopped carrying one hidden load, but her body has not yet learned what to do with the freedom. Never resolve this into serenity, triumph, uplift, or a smile.

## Panel aha moments

1. NEGATIVE-CAST CARTOGRAPHER: externalize the interior by the turn; end with architecture standing beside her and taking its own weight.
2. RELATIONAL THRESHOLD: the protagonist must perform one irreversible material action; contemplation alone is not a plot.
3. LOAD-PATH NATURALIST: each frame must answer where the force goes now.
4. CATHARSIS AUDITOR: relief must create a new awkwardness—hands that no longer need to brace still need somewhere to go.
5. INTERPRETATION SKEPTIC: pressure and release must strike the senses before symbolic interpretation.

## Five constraint axes

### 1. Load-bearing anatomy

- rib-vault pressure beneath the sternum;
- shoulder-borne ceiling with settling lines;
- throat-hinge controlling a narrow door;
- spine-stair registering held breath;
- palm-foundation supporting a floor plan;
- shadow-column taking denied weight.

### 2. Material transformation

- cracked egg tempera → gilded seam → opened linen backing;
- blue ceramic tesserae → loosened grout → water contour;
- black lacquer → scraped gesso → warm raw wood;
- etched copper → verdigris bloom → perforated light-screen;
- stained glass → missing lead came → clear aperture;
- embroidered brocade → pulled thread → load-bearing net.

### 3. Spatial causality

- composure thickens the walls;
- each repair lowers the ceiling;
- doors appear only behind her;
- an exhale redraws the plan;
- symmetry breaks when she releases the seam;
- light enters only through what she refuses to restore.

### 4. Artifact of imperfection

- missing tessera kept in a pocket;
- loose cuff thread never cut;
- crescent cup-ring repeated on floors;
- dull latch without a key;
- black seed lodged in a joint;
- square of bare backing cloth.

### 5. Frame grammar

- frontal icon with a severe silhouette;
- profile cutaway with one shared body/room boundary;
- overhead plan with the protagonist as load point;
- threshold silhouette with aggressive negative space;
- material close detail that advances plot;
- wide final echo where support has changed hands.

## Source 3 form rule — structural transfer

Borrow the logic of load paths and sequence editing: **repeat one gesture until its function changes, then cut on the changed support.** Each candidate must define a beginning load, a failed repair, an irreversible action, a transferred force, and a final unresolved trace. Medium, body, and architecture must all register the change.

## Legibility rule

At thumbnail size, a viewer must immediately identify **a beautiful structure visibly depending on one adult body**. The viewer need not know why yet. By the final frame, the same visual grammar must reveal that the structure now carries itself while the body remains uncertain. If either fact requires the caption, repair the concept.
