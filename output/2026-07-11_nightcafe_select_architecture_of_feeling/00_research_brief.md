# Research Brief — The Architecture of Feeling

## The problem

Create a NightCafe Select Body of Work that makes one feeling physically inhabitable and carries one story from beginning to end. Win both surfaces without confusing them: the feed requires immediate legibility; expert judging requires concept, craft, cohesion, and intent.

## Current creative scene

- The visible Select field favors familiar character-led narratives, warm sentiment, clear antagonists, and repeated protagonists. Many entries are coherent but literal.
- Fantasy romance and cute magic are saturated. Long collections often repeat a mood instead of developing it.
- The sharpest community outlier is a topical anthropomorphic football video: one readable contradiction outperformed more elaborate series.
- NightCafe's current Consistent Character system accepts up to four references and can keep a person or object recognizable across different scenes. Nano Banana Pro is positioned by NightCafe as the strongest identity-locking and edit/refinement route; Nano Banana 2 is fast and strong at complex composition. A practical render strategy is draft broadly, lock the selected protagonist/object, then perform small controlled continuity edits.

## Architecture and emotion research

- Built-environment research supports treating emotion as embodied spatial response rather than a face label. Geometry, openness, curvature, materiality, light, and proportion influence arousal, stress, attention, and affect.
- Louise Bourgeois's architectural work offers a useful conceptual permission: a room can organize tumultuous states of mind; memory can be accumulated like bricks. We borrow the move—space as emotional evidence—not any artwork or iconography.
- The strongest interpretation of the theme therefore makes architecture causal. A wall, seam, threshold, floor, vault, or opening changes what the protagonist can do and what the viewer's body anticipates.

## Chosen emotional territory

**Primary:** relief after prolonged vigilance.

**Secondary:** acceptance that safety does not require perfect repair.

**Residual:** unease—the body keeps checking whether the released load will return.

Working phrase: **unbracing**. This is relief with muscle memory still inside it, not serenity, triumph, or a smiling cure.

## White space

The field shows longing, love, grief, home, family, hope, apocalypse, and cute wonder. It rarely shows relief as an architectural event. It also rarely lets a material rule carry the plot. This is the opening.

## Advisory competition learnings — Phase 0 only

These remain advice, not ICB constraints.

1. **L6, NightCafe image, MEDIUM-HIGH:** one micro-narrative or emotional cue must be decoded in under one second.
2. **Proven-winner formula, HIGH/MEDIUM-HIGH:** dark support, warm internal authority, one emotionally present focal point, museum-grade material specificity, and an unanswered question.
3. **Constraint-system lesson, human-confirmed:** obscure media and limited palettes create productive stakes; each pair must occupy a different corner of the axes.
4. **Generic-fantasy loss pattern, HIGH:** an elf/fairy/romance surface is not differentiation on this venue.
5. **L9 anti-overfit, HIGH:** do not imitate the current leaderboard; ask whether a venue lesson would have damaged our best past entry.

## Source-to-form implications

- Competition advice → feeling first, story complete, personal visual language.
- Neuroarchitecture → change proportion, permeability, curvature, and light across the arc.
- Our winner evidence → clear emblem, impossible interior, transformed ornament.
- Current model capabilities → stable adult protagonist and recurring object are feasible; edits should be small and staged.

## Human-subject and cultural discipline

This is an invented adult protagonist in an invented material world. No identifiable real person, recent tragedy, child, borrowed tribal identity, sacred costume, or culturally locating grief narrative is required.
