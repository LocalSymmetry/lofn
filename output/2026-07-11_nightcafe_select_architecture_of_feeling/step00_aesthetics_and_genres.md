## Panel pressure and self-critique

- The concept panel kept the emotional noun out of the visible scene and insisted on measurable consequences: bending, fastening, transfer, opening, idle hands.
- The medium panel required every proposed aesthetic to survive text-to-image translation as a surface, joint, textile, cast, or light behavior.
- The context panel protected the feed hook: each composition must remain readable as one silhouette plus one impossible architectural proposition at thumbnail scale.
- The Hyper-Skeptics removed generalized serenity, celestial glamour, decorative fairies, and ornamental architecture that does not change the story.
- Cardinality check: aesthetics 50; emotions 50; frames/compositions 50; genres 50.
