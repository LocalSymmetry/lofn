## Pair 01 — ACCESSIBLE — Gilded Compression

**Selected:** C01, The Seam She Polishes.  
**Runner-up:** C02, The Ceiling’s Receipt.  
**Why C01 wins:** C01 is instantly retellable, physically joins body and room, and changes the moral role of gold across the sequence. C02’s modular ceiling is coherent but still needs the title to explain “receipt.”  
**Refined concept:** An invented middle-aged woman repeatedly burnishes a heavy ember-gold seam aligned across her linen shoulder and a low midnight-teal ceiling; accumulated polish reveals years of competent care, one loosened copper fastener exposes the seam as the thing pinning her posture to the room, and she deliberately leaves its final hole open while exposed timber ribs accept the load and her empty hand remains raised from habit.  
**Refined medium:** cracked egg tempera in midnight teal and bone on coarse linen over exposed timber, restrained rubbed gold leaf that shifts from warm care to hard enclosure through adjacency, oxidized copper fasteners, abraded tool paths, compressed linen folds, and one raw ungilded hole.  
**Eight-beat capacity:** burden icon → practiced burnishing → repair accumulation → enclosure reveal → failed fastener → refusal → timber transfer → wide compositional rhyme with hands unassigned.  
**Four pair angles preserved:** The Seam She Polishes; The Ceiling’s Receipt as settling-course pressure; One Fastener Left Open; Hands Without a Job.

## Pair 02 — ACCESSIBLE — Brocade Load Ledger

**Selected:** C03, Selvage Ledger.  
**Runner-up:** C04, Air Through the Pattern.  
**Why C03 wins:** one continuous edge thread creates a stronger causal spine and a clearer object transformation than multiple apertures. C04’s knot recoil is sophisticated, but it risks fashion-editorial pattern beauty.  
**Refined concept:** An invented adult weaver tends a black brocade wall whose celadon warp, ember-coral repair thread, and dull bronze pins converge through her shuttle and dictate the curl of her hands; she notices one undecorated selvage line running continuously through every repair, draws it across the wall, and converts the enclosure into a self-tensioning open net while her fingers retain the shape of the absent tool.  
**Refined medium:** embroidered black brocade and matte celadon linen warp, one physically continuous ember-coral thread, exposed raw selvage, dull bronze pins at verified tension points, restrained gold only in worn stitch holes, and open intervals showing bone plaster behind.  
**Eight-beat capacity:** full textile plane → repeated stitch → tension convergence → body/room boundary → overlong thread → deliberate pull → net transfer → ordinary hands beside breathable support.  
**Four pair angles preserved:** Selvage Ledger; One Thread Longer; The Net Remembers; Air Through the Pattern.

## Pair 03 — ACCESSIBLE — Missing Tessera Plan

**Selected:** C06, The Tile Refuses.  
**Runner-up:** C05, Pocket Weight.  
**Why C06 wins:** a single chipped cobalt tile is the field’s cleanest recurring witness, and its six landings naturally build a sequence. C05 has richer clothing deformation but asks the thumbnail viewer to understand hidden pockets.  
**Refined concept:** An invented conservator repeatedly presses one chipped cobalt tessera into a celadon wall and it falls to a different graphite-marked floor point after every repair, the landings and water-drawn grout contours gradually forming the room’s true circular load plan; she reads the map, sets the tessera beside rather than inside its old place, and leaves a shoulder-height opening while one ember-orange firing mark witnesses the first fall.  
**Refined medium:** spare blue-celadon ceramic tile, oxidized bone grout, one palm-sized cobalt tessera with chipped edges and thumb wear, graphite impact marks, faint water-drawn floor contours, matte bone slip, and one tiny ember-orange fired-clay witness mark.  
**Eight-beat capacity:** apparent defect → first replacement → second fall → cumulative compass → overhead plan reveal → choice beside recess → route opens → circular final return with tile still present.  
**Four pair angles preserved:** Pocket Weight as pressure memory; Grout Line Compass; The Tile Refuses; Blue Opens.

## Pair 04 — AMBITIOUS — Negative Cast

**Selected:** C07, Volume of What Wasn’t.  
**Runner-up:** C08, Absent Wall in Her Arms.  
**Why C07 wins:** the cast precisely fits the missing stair underside, making absence measurable and avoiding a generic portal. C08’s separating ribs are handsome but its eventual passage reads too much like “moving on.”  
**Refined concept:** In reverse chronology, an invented adult woman first appears beside a chalk-white cast already supporting the stepped underside missing from a black-lacquer stair, then the sequence reveals the cast’s arm hollows, her long bracing fit, and the earlier instant she chose to set it onto raw-wood feet; the final return to the original view leaves one copper edge, unfinished handprints, and her decentered body coexisting without a lit doorway.  
**Refined medium:** black urushi-like lacquer on timber, scraped bone gesso, chalk-white casein cast reinforced with coarse linen fiber, raw oak bearing feet, one thin oxidized-copper edge at the contact surface, floor deflection, matte side light, and no radiant opening.  
**Eight-beat capacity:** independent cast → handprints → contact geometry → arm hollow → body as temporary mold → refusal to lift → feet take load → altered first-frame coexistence.  
**Four pair angles preserved:** The Volume of What Wasn’t; Absent Wall in Her Arms; The Cast Stands; Shadow Relieved.

## Pair 05 — AMBITIOUS — Counterweight Shadow

**Selected:** C09, Shadow Takes Attendance.  
**Runner-up:** C10, Load Crosses Noon.  
**Why C09 wins:** the shadow has a unique evolving relationship to both protagonist and ceiling, providing parallel counterpoint across eight frames. C10 is elegant but more passive and visually repetitive.  
**Refined concept:** At the same hour across a sequence, an invented woman’s black-glass shadow thickens from a flat side silhouette into etched planar wedges that transfer their numbered contact marks from her body to a deflecting copper ceiling, until the shadow-column bears more courses than she could and she steps into a narrow bone-white incision of unshadowed floor, glancing back once at the structure that remains upright.  
**Refined medium:** etched copper ceiling plates, verdigris pressure maps, a black-glass relief shadow with crisp planar thickness, perforated dull foil only where contacts migrate, hard bone-white incision marks, coarse linen clothing, and one celadon ground strip.  
**Eight-beat capacity:** side silhouette → dual contact map → miscounted courses → shadow thickening → force paths cross → body separates → unoccupied bearing column → uncertain look-back.  
**Four pair angles preserved:** Shadow Takes Attendance; The Column Miscounts; Load Crosses Noon; No Body Under It.

## Pair 06 — AMBITIOUS — Permeable Wire Room

**Selected:** C12, Failure Becomes Mesh.  
**Runner-up:** C11, Nested Brace.  
**Why C12 wins:** failure visibly becomes distributed capacity, which is the seed’s deepest claim, and the one suspended fragment protects residue. C11’s body-shaped void is striking but can be mistaken for a decorative cage that simply expands.  
**Refined concept:** An invented craftswoman winds increasingly fine dark copper wire around a charred central brace as the view migrates from overhead load plan to ground level, and when the rigid member breaks quietly the strands catch through ceramic eyelets wrapped in brocade selvage, forming a taut open shell whose visible anchors lift the room; one black fragment remains suspended above her slowly lowering hands while the final wide frame gives those hands an ordinary task.  
**Refined medium:** hand-looped dark copper wire in differentiated gauges, a charred timber brace and base, celadon ceramic eyelets wrapped in black-and-celadon brocade selvage, dull holographic foil restricted to true high-stress contacts, ash-casein walls, exposed anchors, and a single broken black length.  
**Eight-beat capacity:** overhead load plan → nested profile → repair density → material hinge → quiet failure → strand catch → tensioned wide shell → ordinary hands beneath continuing load.  
**Four pair angles preserved:** Nested Brace as early compression; Failure Becomes Mesh; Weight in Air; Ordinary Hands.
