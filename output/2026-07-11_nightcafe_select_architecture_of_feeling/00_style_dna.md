# Proven Style DNA — Quarantined Reference Distillation

## Golden-output quarantine

The supplied prior winning images are judge-side evidence only. Their pixels, compositions, and any original prompts are not placed in the ICB or shown to generating pair agents. What moves forward is a structural **Golden Move**, not replicas.

## What our winners repeatedly prove

### The visual identity

- A solitary adult woman, animal, or iconic object becomes a vessel for an interior world.
- The figure and the environment fuse: architecture enters skin; flowers root through glass; cities occupy faces; stairways, water, branches, mosaics, and stars become anatomy.
- Circular authorities recur—moon discs, halos, globes, koi rings, rose vessels—but work best when the circle performs a narrative function rather than merely decorating the figure.
- Fracture is beautiful but consequential: craquelure, mosaic seams, broken glass, gold repair, ink bleed, collage edges, and exposed underlayers.
- The palette is recognizably ours: velvet black or midnight teal under luminous gold, ember orange, coral, celadon, bone, and occasional electric blue.
- The medium appears handmade even when computational: gilded ink, cracked tempera, stained glass, textile collage, engraved line, lacquer, impasto, and paper grain.
- Figures often turn inward—closed eyes, lowered face, profile, cupped hands—while an impossible system reveals what thought alone cannot.

### What actually earned the strongest response

The most-liked supplied works are not the densest portraits. They are the clearest impossible propositions:

- a seated dark figure facing one filigreed golden threshold;
- a living rose and roots held inside a glass sphere;
- one orange koi forming a concentric world in dark water.

This suggests a reliable law: **one unmistakable silhouette + one impossible interior + disciplined support beats maximal detail spread everywhere**.

### The Golden Move

1. Start with a single emblem readable in under one second.
2. Make the emotion a material or spatial law, not an expression label.
3. Give one concrete surface the burden of transformation.
4. Let ornament carry evidence: every seam, petal, tile, or line marks what happened.
5. Keep an unanswered residue after the apparent resolution.

## What must not be repeated

- generic winged women, fairies, witches, princesses, or celestial glamour;
- a beautiful profile with no action beyond contemplation;
- decorative collage whose symbols never change meaning;
- the same moon-halo portrait repeated across a sequence;
- generic digital fantasy gloss, soft-focus sentimentality, or overlit gold;
- a house, whale, moon-at-window, or impossible guest structure that repeats the 9 July **Answered Room** run;
- past winners' exact iconography, wording, or framing.

## Competition translation

Our single-image language becomes sequential by making **one material motif mutate across eight frames**. Character identity stays stable; composition, spatial pressure, and material state change. The final frame visually rhymes with Frame 1 but reverses who or what is carrying the load.
