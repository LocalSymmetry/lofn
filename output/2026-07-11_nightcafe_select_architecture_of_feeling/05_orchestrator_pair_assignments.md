Coordinator Step 05 will select which of its twelve concepts fills each slot and record runner-up rationale and a cut ledger. These are not six relabelings of one concept.

## Pair 01 — The Gilded Compression Slot

- Arm: **ACCESSIBLE**.
- Personality: LOFN-ArtCore, serene neo-baroque register.
- Concept territory: one visibly load-bearing body; beautiful repair gradually becomes compression.
- Medium family: cracked egg tempera, restrained gold leaf, linen support, midnight teal and bone.
- Narrative architecture: chronological eight-beat descent-and-release; one repeated mending gesture changes meaning.
- Composition grammar: frontal icon → shoulder/ceiling contact → hand/seam detail → final wide echo.
- Required technique: color changes moral role through adjacency; gold begins as care, becomes cage, ends as neutral joint.
- Pair rationale: closest to the user's proven portrait language and the safest feed entry, while making ornament culpable.
- Four unique variation angles:
  1. **The Seam She Polishes** — cover contradiction; immaculate repair visibly forces the shoulders upward.
  2. **The Ceiling's Receipt** — settling courses record every prior repair around a calm face.
  3. **One Fastener Left Open** — irreversible refusal to complete the next gilded line.
  4. **Hands Without a Job** — after-state centered on bodily uncertainty, not radiance.

## Pair 02 — The Brocade Load Ledger Slot

- Arm: **ACCESSIBLE**.
- Personality: LOFN-ArtCore, textile-and-filigree register with disciplined support.
- Concept territory: a woven wall or garment-wall records years of vigilance in warp and weft.
- Medium family: embroidered brocade, exposed selvage, dull bronze pins, celadon/ember/black palette.
- Narrative architecture: accretive weave; each frame adds one repeated stitch until a pulled thread converts wall into net.
- Composition grammar: full figure and textile plane → oblique tension detail → body/room shared boundary → permeable final structure.
- Required technique: one pulled thread is causal and physically continuous across all frames.
- Pair rationale: retains ornamental femininity while giving fabric a real load-bearing job.
- Four unique variation angles:
  1. **Selvage Ledger** — the edge lists compressed years through changing stitch density.
  2. **One Thread Longer** — a single overlong repair crosses from clothing into architecture.
  3. **The Net Remembers** — removed enclosure remains as a tensioned support system.
  4. **Air Through the Pattern** — negative space becomes the visible new material.

## Pair 03 — The Missing Tessera Plan Slot

- Arm: **ACCESSIBLE**.
- Personality: LOFN-ArtCore, mosaic/celestial register stripped of glamour.
- Concept territory: a floor or body mosaic redraws its plan around one missing tile kept by the protagonist.
- Medium family: blue ceramic tesserae, oxidized grout, bone slip, water-drawn contours, one ember-orange witness.
- Narrative architecture: circular return; the missing piece appears in every state but changes from defect to permission.
- Composition grammar: overhead plan → palm-scale witness → profile cutaway → final plan with a clear route outward.
- Required technique: halo/circle functions as plan view and load diagram, never decorative aura.
- Pair rationale: translates the user's strong koi/mosaic circles into sequential architecture without copying prior images.
- Four unique variation angles:
  1. **Pocket Weight** — the absent tile is visibly carried while the floor strains to remain complete.
  2. **Grout Line Compass** — cracks orient toward her rather than toward damage.
  3. **The Tile Refuses** — she sets the tessera beside, not inside, its old place.
  4. **Blue Opens** — the mosaic becomes a navigable contour rather than a sealed image.

## Pair 04 — The Negative Cast Slot

- Arm: **AMBITIOUS**.
- Personality: LOFN-ArtCore, scraped-support and black-void register.
- Concept territory: the absent wall becomes a solid volume she carries until it can stand separately.
- Medium family: black urushi lacquer, scraped gesso, chalk-white cast volume, raw wood, one copper edge.
- Narrative architecture: reverse chronology; begin with the impossible cast, reveal the repair history, end at the first refusal from a changed vantage.
- Composition grammar: severe negative space → body/void contact → cast as second figure → final decentered coexistence.
- Required technique: absence has measurable mass; the final opening is not represented by light.
- Pair rationale: most direct architecture-of-feeling proposition and furthest from the current field's fantasy gloss.
- Four unique variation angles:
  1. **The Volume of What Wasn't** — a room-shaped absence bends her posture.
  2. **Absent Wall in Her Arms** — the body embraces a cast too heavy to sentimentalize.
  3. **The Cast Stands** — support transfer shown through raw wood feet and floor deflection.
  4. **Shadow Relieved** — the final shadow no longer reproduces the enclosure.

## Pair 05 — The Counterweight Shadow Slot

- Arm: **AMBITIOUS**.
- Personality: LOFN-ArtCore, copper/glitch register with cosmic scale compressed into anatomy.
- Concept territory: a shadow-column quietly takes the load the protagonist refuses to name.
- Medium family: etched copper, verdigris pressure maps, black glass, perforated foil, hard bone-white incision.
- Narrative architecture: parallel counterpoint; body and shadow perform different support actions until their load paths cross once.
- Composition grammar: side silhouette → dual support diagram → noon-like crossing → unoccupied final column.
- Required technique: shadow behaves as force and geometry, not an emotional mood.
- Pair rationale: gives the user's dark/gold silhouette language a new structural role and a strong cover shape.
- Four unique variation angles:
  1. **Shadow Takes Attendance** — corrosion reveals where the second support has always touched.
  2. **The Column Miscounts** — shadow holds more courses than the body can plausibly carry.
  3. **Load Crosses Noon** — the two support paths intersect in one hard structural turn.
  4. **No Body Under It** — architecture remains upright beside an uncertain unbraced figure.

## Pair 06 — The Permeable Wire Room Slot

- Arm: **AMBITIOUS**.
- Personality: LOFN-ArtCore, refractive-detail register disciplined by wire, tension, and sparse support.
- Concept territory: a nested room starts as a rigid body-bound cage and becomes a tensile shell that carries itself while admitting air.
- Medium family: hand-looped dark wire, brocade selvage, dull holographic foil only at stress points, charred wood base.
- Narrative architecture: spatial choreography; view migrates from overhead load plan into embodied ground level as rigidity becomes tension.
- Composition grammar: plan view → nested profile volume → material hinge close-up → wide structural rhyme.
- Required technique: no weightless mobile; dense selvage and visible anchor points prove tension.
- Pair rationale: highest formal originality and strongest bridge between the user's interior-world signature and actual architectural force.
- Four unique variation angles:
  1. **Nested Brace** — room volumes repeat inside the torso with progressively tighter loops.
  2. **Failure Becomes Mesh** — a broken rigid member redistributes into many small tensions.
  3. **Weight in Air** — the shell holds through visible anchors rather than mass.
  4. **Ordinary Hands** — final wide frame gives the released hands a mundane action while the wire room remains under load.

## Cross-pair distinctiveness check

- P01 is chronological tempera repair and moral color reversal.
- P02 is accretive textile structure and one causal thread.
- P03 is circular mosaic planning and an intentionally absent unit.
- P04 is reverse-chronology negative casting and absence-as-mass.
- P05 is parallel shadow/body counterpoint and one force crossing.
- P06 is plan-to-ground choreography and tensile redistribution.

No two slots share a medium skeleton, sequence grammar, recurring witness, or four-angle naming scheme.

## Rationale for the barbell

P01–P03 supply first-glance figure, textile, and circular-emblem legibility without abandoning LOFN-ArtCore. P04–P06 test absence, counterforce, and permeability at higher interpretive and render risk. Step 05 must fill all six slots; it may not replace ambitious slots with three more portraits.
