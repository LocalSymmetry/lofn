## Territory A — Gilded compression

### C01 — The Seam She Polishes

An invented middle-aged woman in a midnight-teal room has spent years polishing a cracked egg-tempera-and-gold seam that runs from the low ceiling through her shoulder into the wall, until a failed repair reveals that the seam is not holding the room together but fastening her to its weight, and she deliberately leaves its last copper pin open while the ceiling transfers onto exposed timber ribs.

**One-breath retell:** She discovers the beautiful seam she tends is what keeps the ceiling on her shoulder.  
**Agency:** she stops one habitual repair.  
**Series engine:** repair accretion → failure → refusal → transfer → idle hands.

### C02 — The Ceiling’s Receipt

An invented older woman records every crack she repairs as a small gilded rectangle on the ceiling above her, and the rectangles accumulate into a heavy descending ledger that exactly mirrors her hunched posture until one blank rectangle opens into air and the marks redistribute into a self-supporting arch with one unaccounted fracture left visible.

**One-breath retell:** Every repair becomes another unit of ceiling weight until one blank entry releases the structure.  
**Agency:** she refuses to complete the ledger.  
**Series engine:** counted repairs → visual oppression → blank unit → arch.

## Territory B — Brocade load ledger

### C03 — Selvage Ledger

An invented adult weaver stands inside a room whose brocade walls are tethered to her wrists by taut coral and gold warp threads, each mended motif adding another line of pull, until she draws one plain selvage thread through the entire pattern and the wall becomes an open load-bearing net that carries itself while her hands retain the shape of tension.

**One-breath retell:** A weaver pulls one ordinary edge thread and converts her ornate prison into a breathable support net.  
**Agency:** she chooses the structurally humble thread.  
**Series engine:** motif accretion → tether reveal → pulled thread → open weave.

### C04 — Air Through the Pattern

An invented seamstress sits before a dense indigo brocade partition whose floral figures repeat across her dress and skin, and each time she opens a small hole in the fabric the surrounding pattern first tightens around her before learning to distribute force around the aperture, ending with sunlight crossing her ordinary empty palms through a stable field of holes.

**One-breath retell:** Each hole initially tightens the cloth before the pattern learns to hold around air.  
**Agency:** she keeps opening apertures despite the first contraction.  
**Series engine:** puncture → recoil → distributed tension → permeable calm.

## Territory C — Missing tessera plan

### C05 — Pocket Weight

An invented woman in a celadon-and-blue mosaic chamber quietly pockets one loosened tessera after every repair, making her coat heavier as the walls become more perfect, until she empties the blue tiles into a circular floor plan and the room follows their new contour, holding itself around a single permanent gap at shoulder height.

**One-breath retell:** The room grows perfect as her pockets grow heavy; returning the tiles as a new plan frees both.  
**Agency:** she redraws the plan with the withheld pieces.  
**Series engine:** removal → concealed burden → disclosure → replan → missing tile.

### C06 — The Tile Refuses

An invented conservator repeatedly presses one cobalt tessera into a cracked wall where it will not stay, the rejected tile falling into a new position in every frame until its sequence maps a load path across the floor and she understands that the wall needs an opening rather than completion, leaving the tessera in her relaxed palm while water-colored light passes through the gap.

**One-breath retell:** One tile keeps falling until its landings teach her the wall needs a gap.  
**Agency:** she reads failure as instruction.  
**Series engine:** repeated fall → mapped path → interpretation → non-repair.

## Territory D — Negative cast

### C07 — Volume of What Wasn’t

An invented adult woman carries a chalk-white negative cast of the missing volume beneath a black-lacquer stair, fitting her arms into its hollows as though supporting absence itself, and when she sets the cast down it becomes the stair’s independent bearing mass while her former handprints remain as the only unfinished marks on its surface.

**One-breath retell:** She has been carrying the shape of what is missing; when set down, absence becomes support.  
**Agency:** she places the cast outside her body.  
**Series engine:** reverse chronology → fit reveal → set-down → structural autonomy.

### C08 — Absent Wall in Her Arms

An invented woman walks through a scraped-gesso interior holding a black cast of an absent wall that grows lighter as the real walls become more porous, until the object separates into freestanding raw-wood ribs and she passes between them without carrying any piece, though one chalk pressure mark remains across her forearms.

**One-breath retell:** The wall she carries unpacks into architecture she can walk through.  
**Agency:** she permits the solid cast to separate.  
**Series engine:** carried mass → material fissure → rib assembly → passage.

## Territory E — Counterweight shadow

### C09 — Shadow Takes Attendance

An invented woman stands beneath an etched-copper ceiling whose only visible support is her dense black-glass shadow, but at the same hour each day the shadow records one less point of contact with her and one more with the room, eventually bracing the structure independently while she steps into unshadowed celadon light and looks back once.

**One-breath retell:** Her shadow gradually changes employers, moving its points of support from her body to the room.  
**Agency:** she steps beyond the shadow’s outline.  
**Series engine:** parallel counterpoint → contact migration → separation → one check.

### C10 — Load Crosses Noon

An invented adult woman braces a verdigris column while a thin ember-gold sundial shadow cuts across her ribs, and as noon advances the shadow thickens into a structural beam that takes the column’s load, leaving the woman upright with no dramatic gesture while the now-useless bearing plate rests at her feet.

**One-breath retell:** A moving line of shadow becomes the beam that replaces her.  
**Agency:** she removes the bearing plate from her shoulder path.  
**Series engine:** timed crossing → shadow thickening → load transfer → ordinary stance.

## Territory F — Permeable wire room

### C11 — Nested Brace

An invented woman occupies a chamber of looped copper wire nested tightly around the exact geometry of her seated body, every movement threatening to deform the room, until she unlatches one brocade-bound joint and the loops expand into a wide self-tensioning vault whose center no longer requires her silhouette.

**One-breath retell:** A room fitted to her bracing pose expands when she opens one joint.  
**Agency:** she unlatches the joint rather than escaping by force.  
**Series engine:** fitted enclosure → movement threat → joint choice → expansion → center vacancy.

### C12 — Failure Becomes Mesh

An invented craftswoman repairs a charred wooden brace with finer and finer wire until it snaps, but the released strands catch one another across the room and form a distributed open mesh stronger than the single brace, allowing her to lower both hands while one broken black length remains suspended above them.

**One-breath retell:** The brace’s failure distributes its strength across the room.  
**Agency:** she does not replace the broken central member.  
**Series engine:** over-repair → snap → catch → distributed mesh → rough trace.
