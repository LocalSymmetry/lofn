# Evaluation Scoring Facets — NightCafe Select: *The Architecture of Feeling*

## Scope and judging posture

This is a fresh selection-judge pass over the completed prompt portfolio only. It evaluates the 24 Step-10 candidates and the six story engines; it does not generate, revise, repair, or render anything.

The prior-winner quarantine remains binding. `00_style_dna.md` was used only for its abstract structural lessons—clear emblem, causal material transformation, disciplined support, forensic ornament, and unresolved residue. No attachment image, attachment path, prior prompt, prior subject, or prior composition was sought, cited, or copied. No living-artist imitation is used.

NightCafe's four official criteria, as recorded in the run, are treated as follows:

| Official criterion | What is visible in this portfolio |
|---|---|
| Concept | The feeling becomes a spatial law and a changed load path, not an illustrated facial label. |
| Craft | Materials have distinct behavior, joints transmit force, and the renderer receives a coherent focal hierarchy. |
| Cohesion | Character, witness object, palette, and material rule persist while every frame changes state. |
| Intent | The protagonist causes the turn; the opening follows material evidence; one rough or bodily trace resists a cure narrative. |

## Three sequential panel debates

> *Panel voices are model-generated interpretive constructs, each "after" a named source figure's published work. No statement is a quotation of, or endorsement by, the named person.*

Only the frozen 18-seat Concept / Medium / Context ledger is used below. The dialogue is a concise decision record, not private chain-of-thought.

### Debate 1 — Concept Panel: what must count as the idea?

**MEMORY CELL BUILDER (after Louise Bourgeois):** Working from the Cells and house/body forms, accumulated repair counts only when it changes reach, clearance, enclosure, or obligation. The story must retain evidence that this room has trained a life.

**NEGATIVE-CAST CARTOGRAPHER (after Rachel Whiteread):** Working from casts of domestic voids, absence earns authority when it has contour and consequence. The best candidates let a gap, missing unit, shadow, or released interval possess measurable structural weight.

**RELATIONAL THRESHOLD (after Lygia Clark):** Working from hinged and participant-activated form, the protagonist must perform the irreversible verb—omit, pull, place, step, open, or refuse to replace. Contemplation is not the turn.

**MATERIAL-WOUND MASON (after Doris Salcedo):** Working from thresholds and joins that retain residue, the ending must preserve abrasion, handprint, chipped edge, bent pin, broken length, or unfinished backing. Perfect restoration would falsify the intent.

**POETICS-OF-INTERIOR KEEPER (after Gaston Bachelard):** Working from intimate spatial scales, the proposition should remain inhabitable through a shoulder, ledge, stair underside, joint, shuttle, or workbench. Monumentality is earned through close material fact.

**INTERPRETATION SKEPTIC — Hyper-Skeptic (after Susan Sontag):** Wait—“room equals psyche” is not yet a concept; it can be a caption wearing architecture. Pressure, obstruction, mass, and release must strike the senses before interpretation begins.

**Moderator synthesis:** Concept is scored as a **causal architectural turn**. Dissent remains over P04/P05 interpretive latency, so no ambitious candidate receives full credit merely for novelty; it must show the force relation in the still.

### Debate 2 — Medium Panel: when does craft become evidence?

**JOINT CHOREOGRAPHER (after Carlo Scarpa):** Working from crafted junctions, unlike materials need an exact meeting condition: leaf stops at a drilled hole, thread turns a pin, grout returns to a pier, copper compresses against a cast, or wire enters a worn eyelet.

**TENSION WEAVER (after Anni Albers):** Working from warp and weft as structure, thread and cloth score only when density, direction, selvage, and anchor response carry force. Textile used as prestige surface is a craft failure.

**VOID-WIRE ENGINEER (after Ruth Asawa):** Working from nested permeable volume, open intervals can remain load-bearing, but the silhouette needs dense edges and legible anchors. Air is not an excuse for weightlessness.

**LOAD-PATH NATURALIST (after Frei Otto):** Working from physical form-finding, every candidate must answer where force goes now. Deflection, contact, counterforce, and redistribution outrank decorative complexity.

**SUPPORT CHOREOGRAPHER (after Pina Bausch):** Working from repeated action under obstacle, the same hand or shoulder gesture should change function across the arc—skill, compulsion, refusal, afterimage, ordinary task.

**WEIGHT PROSECUTOR — Hyper-Skeptic (after Richard Serra):** Actually, material nouns do not make material weight. If the ceiling, lintel, floor, plate, anchor, or composition is not inconvenienced, the prompt is still weightless metaphor.

**Moderator synthesis:** Craft is scored as **forensic material truth**, not surface richness alone. P06 receives the strongest structural idea but carries a real rendering penalty when wire routes or eyelets threaten cognitive overload; P04 gains craft credit from its severe, buildable contact chain.

### Debate 3 — Context & Marketing Panel: what survives both feed and jury?

**CLOSURE EDITOR (after Scott McCloud):** Working from panel transition and closure, eight frames are justified only when each changes state. The gutter may carry inference, but it cannot hide the central choice.

**EMOTION-FIRST CUTTER (after Walter Murch):** Working from emotion, story, rhythm, and eye trace, cut on the changed support or changed hand. A harder compositional break is preferable to smooth but redundant beauty.

**CONSTRUCTED-AFFECT CARTOGRAPHER (after Lisa Feldman Barrett):** Working from context and interoception, the same neutral face can mean control early and uncertainty late if posture and affordance change. Stock relief expressions reduce emotional credibility.

**EMBODIED VIEWER (after Juhani Pallasmaa):** Working from haptic architectural experience, sag, scrape, low clearance, cold metal, cloth tension, and floor bend should recruit the viewer's balance and skin.

**WAYS-OF-SEEING TRANSLATOR (after John Berger):** Working from reproduction and viewing context, the first feed image becomes the thesis. Lead with one contradiction that can be repeated in a breath, then let craft reward the second look.

**CATHARSIS AUDITOR — Hyper-Skeptic (after Sianne Ngai):** Wait—the most shareable release image may also be the cheapest. Preserve the minor awkwardness: a hand still raised, a shoulder still listening, a gaze returning, or a task only half begun.

**Moderator synthesis:** The portfolio needs a barbell, not one universal winner type. Accessible candidates must clear the eligibility gate and communicate quickly; ambitious candidates may demand a second look but cannot demand the artist statement. The cover must announce the dependency without exhausting the eight-frame discovery.

## Five run-specific scoring facets

These facets are diagnostic weights for seed and NightCafe-criteria fidelity. They do **not** replace the required competition ranking weights in `eval_ranking.md`.

### 1. Causal Architectural Turn — 28%

**Description:** The image makes architecture causal: a joint, gap, thread, tile, cast, shadow, brace, or anchor changes what the protagonist or structure can do. It must be possible to point to the old and new force destination without relying on the statement. Maps to NightCafe **Concept + Intent**.

**10:** One irreversible action and one visibly changed load path are immediately legible; the opening is evidence, not rescue.

**1:** Architecture is scenery or metaphor skin; nothing visibly bears, transfers, obstructs, or opens because of the protagonist's action.

### 2. Forensic Material Truth — 22%

**Description:** Named materials behave differently and retain contact history. Crackle, abrasion, deflection, compression, corrosion, stitch wear, chipped clay, handprints, floor bend, or stressed anchors prove what happened. Maps to **Craft + Intent**.

**10:** Every primary material performs a structural job, the joint is buildable inside the invented law, and the focal hierarchy remains renderable.

**1:** Materials are interchangeable decoration, prestige texture, or generic digital gloss with no force consequence.

### 3. Embodied Unbracing with Residue — 20%

**Description:** The body locates the feeling through shoulder height, ribs, stance, hand assignment, clearance, pressure marks, or a checking gaze. Release never becomes a smile, triumph, or spotless cure. Maps to **Concept + Intent**.

**10:** The body visibly gains a new affordance while muscle memory survives in one precise, non-theatrical trace.

**1:** The feeling is merely named, facially performed, or replaced by brighter light; the body has no credible relationship to the load.

### 4. Eight-Frame Mutation and Structural Rhyme — 18%

**Description:** The candidate occupies a distinct story state and can connect to seven other nonredundant states through one stable character/material bible. The final frame must rhyme with the opening while reversing who or what carries the load. Maps to **Cohesion + Intent**.

**10:** The engine yields eight causal beats, stable witnesses, escalating state changes, and a final visual rhyme that changes the opening's meaning.

**1:** The candidate supports only a single tableau or a sequence of adjacent moods with no cumulative material transformation.

### 5. Cover-to-Second-Read Clarity — 12%

**Description:** At thumbnail size, one adult silhouette or emblem and one impossible dependency read in roughly a second. At full size, material evidence, residue, and the structural turn deepen rather than contradict that first read. Maps across **Concept + Craft + Cohesion**.

**10:** The hook is one-breath adoptable, compositionally severe, and unmistakable from the photographed leaderboard field while rewarding scrutiny.

**1:** The viewer must search for the subject, decode a diagram, read the title, or accept generic fantasy/portrait conventions before the idea appears.

## Scoring protocol locked for ranking

- Run-facet diagnostic: 1–10 on each of the five facets above; weighted by 28/22/20/18/12.
- Competition total: Originality 25%, Technical Execution 20%, Emotional Impact 20%, Bold Choice Quality 15%, Platform Fit 10%, Viral Potential 10%.
- Eligibility: seven visual properties scored 1–5 using 15/20/15/15/10/15/10 weights. ACCESSIBLE candidates require a weighted average of at least 3.5; AMBITIOUS scores are informational.
- Tie discipline: retain the unrounded competition total, then use the five-facet diagnostic and the barbell requirement; do not alter a component score to manufacture pair diversity.
- Engine selection is separate from single-candidate ranking and uses eight-frame expandability, causal architectural change, character/material continuity, final-frame rhyme, and differentiation from the observed field.
