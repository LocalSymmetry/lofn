## THE BENCH — three seats, every prompt

### NORMAN (after Donald Norman) — *the usable state IS the beautiful state; voids honest*
- **V1:** PASS. Each alcove is an unmistakable slot ≥48px, interior reserved dark, inner gold edge seated for the focus ring; the ajar shutter reads as invitation, not control. The wick-lamp is small and off the alcove-frame vocabulary, so it won't be mistaken for a button.
- **V2:** PASS — *conditional and recorded.* The up-glow must **stop at the alcove frames**; the prompt says the windows stay "empty dark contrast-reserved" above the burn. Requirement carried: if a render lets ember wash INTO the void, the void stops being honest — reroll on that failure. As written, honest.
- **V3:** PASS. My original worry — bells that look clickable — is met head-on in the prompt: "plainly ornament, inert, carrying none of the alcove-frame vocabulary and none of the gold focus-edge, so no hand mistakes them for controls." The alcove tops keep the real affordance.
- **V4:** PASS, exemplary. The center is a *declared empty void*, the inner gold hairline is explicitly the focus-ring seat, the plaque is a reserved contrast zone. The beautiful cell and the usable cell are the same cell.
- **NORMAN dissent logged:** "V2 is the one to watch — beauty at the foot must not eat the honesty of the void. I sign it only because the prompt already fences the glow at the frames."

### KARE (after Susan Kare) — *128px, four shapes, named light*
- **V1:** PASS. Four shapes: velvet ground / gold rail-rhythm / niche-voids / foot-ember. Light named (coal-bed below). Rail-rhythm survives thumbnail because the leads are few and confident.
- **V2:** PASS. At 128px: an ember line under velvet with alcove feet — three shapes and a named light. Clean.
- **V3:** PASS — *with a note.* Cool + low-key is the hardest 128px read in the pack (least contrast). Saved by the bell silhouettes against velvet and the gold chains as the high-contrast anchor. KARE: "Keep the bells to three heavy silhouettes — the prompt caps them; do not let a render multiply them into noise."
- **V4:** PASS. One bright-edged niche + plaque = two shapes and a light. The strongest thumbnail in the pack.
- **KARE dissent logged:** "V3 is on my line, not over it. The three-bell cap and the chain-as-anchor are load-bearing — if a reroll is ever needed anywhere, it's the crown's contrast, not its concept."

### CHAYKA (after Kyle Chayka) — *named materials only; could this hang in any other AI arcade? If yes, REROLL now*
- **V1:** PASS. Gilded-INK with visible dry-pen drag, engraved brass with real edge, pressed paper-grain velvet, the off-rhythm wick-lamp and half-open shutter — authored, not generic. Could not hang elsewhere.
- **V2:** PASS — *the closest call in the pack, examined hardest.* Amber-glow shelf is the slop uniform; this prompt disarms it with committed specifics: "banked coals with individual glowing faces, coral-bright at the core and dark-orange at the cooling edges, specific banked coals rather than a wash," gilded-ink seams entering hairline cracks, and the custodian's **pooled-and-cooled molten-gold spill in a brass gutter**. Those anchors are non-negotiable and load-bearing. **Verdict: no reroll — the specificity is IN the prompt.** Flag: if any downstream edit strips the banked-coal detail or the gold-spill, it reverts to slop and must be rerolled.
- **V3:** PASS. Bells "that remember being rung," lived bronze patina, a keyhole with one eye of coal-light — specific and hers. No other arcade's crown.
- **V4:** PASS. The thumb-worn plaque corner and the inner gold hairline "where a lit lattice will later grow" are authored-detail no slop shelf carries.
- **CHAYKA dissent logged:** "I came to reroll V2 and left it standing — but only because the coal is *described as coal*, not as a glow. The moment it becomes honey-wash it is every arcade's wall. Marked, watched, signed."
