## THE FIVE FACETS (the scoring lenses every P1 prompt must pass)

1. **Emission, not illumination — the wall glows because something lives at its foot.** The coal-bed under-glow must read as a *named source* banked at the wall's floor, throwing light **upward** so the velvet warms at the foot and cools as the eye climbs. If the wall looks lit by an off-frame studio lamp, it is a vending machine, not a lit window. Sensory test: you should feel the heat rising, the way you feel a hearth before you see the coals. *(THE LIT WINDOW / Pelton; flair 12 The Wick And The Pour.)*

2. **A wall of thresholds, instant at 128px.** Four shapes and a light: velvet ground · the rhythm of gold rail-and-alcove · the empty niche-voids · the ember line at the foot. At thumbnail the alcove must read as a *holder waiting for a vessel*, never as a finished picture. If the rail-rhythm blurs into a texture field, the composition failed. *(THE PIXEL ROCOCO ENGINEER / Kare, flair 7; THE VESSEL POET / Bachelard — the alcove is the threshold.)*

3. **Voids honest, every cell finished.** Each alcove interior is a **declared empty window** — a contrast-reserved neutral field where the live game thumbnail will sit — and each alcove is nonetheless *finished*: engraved-brass frame, a clean inner gold edge (the future focus ring's seat), a blank brass plaque below (uninscribed — no text on wall pieces). The wall says "everything ready," never "everything done." *(THE SHELF MERCHANT / Yu — finished-ness; flair 11 Nothing Finished, Everything Ready.)*

4. **Named-material velvet — no default-amber, ≥50% quiet.** Velvet carries paper-grain tooth; the gold is **gilded-INK** line, not #FFD700 wash; the leads are **engraved brass** with real edge; the foot-warmth is **coal-ember/coral**, a specific fire, not a generic amber gradient. The backing is dominated by quiet velvet so the little that glows becomes covetable. If a frame of this could hang on any other AI arcade, reroll. *(THE SLOP SENTRY / Chayka; THE VELVET STOREFRONT / Kouthoofd; flairs 8 & 14.)*

5. **Custodial warmth, never solemnity.** The wall is *tended* — a worn-smooth thumbprint on a brass rail, one alcove shutter left ajar, one small mischief per piece — so it reads *welcoming*, not churchy, and yesterday's cells cool to bronze without shame. Reverence that forbids touching is the death of an arcade. *(THE TOY PHILOSOPHER / Caillois; THE DROP RITUALIST / Gage; flairs 1, 4, 13, 15.)*

## GPT IMAGE 2 FAILURE-MODE SCAN (renderer rules §06)

```
□ Storybook Cliché risk .............. MED   → override: engraved-brass HARD geometry + orthographic flatness; coal-bed as a defined ember BAND, not an atmosphere wash; declared paper-grain tooth kills "smooth magic."
□ Warm rim light risk ................ HIGH  → this is the dangerous one. Coal-glow is literally bottom-warm light. Override: light is DIRECTIONAL FROM DIRECTLY BELOW at the wall's foot; brass rails throw their catch-lights on their UNDERSIDES and cast faint shadows RISING upward — the inverse of GPT-I2's default upper-left rim.
□ Centered pastel subject risk ....... LOW   → no central subject (tile/architecture); palette is saturated ember + gilded gold on near-black velvet, never pastel.
□ Reiteration / edit-chain risk ...... LOW   → four self-contained prompts, one generation each, no chained edits.

□ Entropy drift / texture smear risk . MED   → velvet + paper grain + any craquelure can mud the alcove edges. Override: alcove frames are FEW confident engraved-brass leads at high contrast to velvet; reserve craquelure ONLY where gilded ink runs, never across the open velvet field.
□ Overcrowded annotation risk ........ LOW   → no annotations anywhere on the wall.
□ Text illegibility risk ............. N/A   → no text on wall pieces by contract; plaque voids ship BLANK. This removes the risk rather than managing it.
□ Primary read inversion risk ........ LOW   → no diagram/text to invert; the primary read is architecture + up-glow, exactly as intended.
```

**Highest-priority override for all four prompts:** the *warm-rim-light* default. Every prompt names the light as **rising from the wall's foot**, with under-lit brass and upward-cast shadow, so GPT-I2 cannot fall back to its habitual soft upper-left key.

## Panel consultation (Ledger voices by name — each skeptic dissents)

- **THE CABINET KEEPER (Kircher):** "Variety under one order — but this is the *order* layer, the wall itself. Your danger is the opposite of the vessels': a grid of identical niches reads as a warehouse. Break the rhythm — an odd bay, a taller alcove, one shutter ajar."
- **THE STAINED-LIGHT ARCHITECT (Tiffany):** "Few, thick, confident brass leads for the alcove frames. The wall is not where filigree lives — save that for the masthead. Lead-lines carry the eye; filigree would clog the 128px read."
- **THE GILDED INKWRIGHT (Klimt):** "The gold is *structure* here — rail, frame, divider are one continuous gilded-ink line. Let the line do the drawing of the whole interface; the velvet is the ground it breathes on."
- **THE SHELF CYNIC — Norman (skeptic, DISSENT):** "Stop admiring the glow. Every alcove is a 48px-plus target and it must *look like a slot for a thing*, not a painting. If a player can't tell an empty alcove from a decorated panel, your beautiful wall is friction. Keep the void obviously a void — reserve its contrast, seat the inner gold edge where the focus ring will land, and don't you dare gild the middle of it."
- **THE COMPRESSION AUDITOR — Tufte (skeptic, DISSENT):** "A velvet-black ground is nearly free bytes; your alcove ornament is not. Prove each tile earns its kilobytes: declare the 2–4 tones, keep ≥50% quiet velvet, and remember a *tileable* piece is ONE tile — do not hand me a bespoke ten-foot mural where a repeat unit would do."
- **THE SLOP SENTRY — Chayka (skeptic, DISSENT):** "Amber-glow shelf is the AI slop uniform — I've seen ten thousand. Disprove it with the *specific*: gilded-ink gold that shows the pen's dry-drag, coal-ember that is coral-at-the-core not honey-wash, engraved brass with an actual bevel and a thumb-worn wear-mark. If it could be any arcade's wall, it is nobody's wall. Reroll on sight."
