## THE SHARED INVARIANT (the V1 base DNA — all four pieces inherit it unchanged)
A velvet near-black reliquary wall carrying **paper-grain tooth**, held ≥50% quiet. **One continuous gilded-ink gold line system** draws every rail, alcove frame, and divider (the gold is structure, not garnish — *THE GILDED INKWRIGHT / Klimt*). Alcove frames are **few, confident engraved-brass leads**, never filigree (*THE STAINED-LIGHT ARCHITECT / Tiffany*). The light family is the **coal-bed under-glow**: warmth is born at the wall's foot and rises (*THE LIT WINDOW / Pelton*, flair 12). Every alcove interior is a **declared empty thumbnail void** with reserved contrast and a clean inner gold edge (the seat for P4's focus ring); every plaque is a **blank brass void** — no text on wall pieces. Accessible warm palette only: **velvet-black · gilded gold · ember-coral · bone**. Orthographic for the tiles.

## V3 — THE WALL-TOP with HANGING BELL-AND-CHAIN ACCENTS  ·  *axes: vertical position + furniture*
**Changed axes (2):** (a) **composition position** — mid-wall → the wall's **top/crown**; (b) **furniture** — bare rail → **hanging bell-and-chain accents** (the custodial furniture; the wall's one mischief). Light naturally cools with distance from the foot — a *consequence* of (a), not a third independent axis.
**Frame / optics:** wide orthographic elevation of the wall's crown, alcove *tops* along the bottom of frame, the crown molding and hanging brass across the top. **1536×1024.**
**Tiling:** left↔right **seamless**; **top edge = terminal** crown (not tiled); bottom edge aligns to V1's top rail so V3 caps any V1 row.
**2–4 tone puzzle:** velvet near-black · gilded gold · bone. **3 tones, cool end** — ember appears only as a faint far-below memory rising into the frame's bottom; the crown is the pack's quiet.
**128px read:** *the tended crown* — a dark top hung with a few bell silhouettes and gold chains, calm.
**Declared voids:** alcove-top windows (upper thumbnail portions) along the bottom; the bells and chains are **decorative accents, NOT voids**.
**Flairs (woven):** 15 One Mischief Per Vessel (lead — one bell tilted as if just rung, its chain caught mid-swing: a static implication of sound) · 2 The Second Ember (a keyhole worked into the crown molding with a single eye of warm light behind it) · 5 Breath, Not Blink (ships static; the swing is implied, never animated) · 13 Cooled Bronze Remembers (the crown's brass wears a lived, faintly-patinaed warmth).
**Tiffany's rule honored:** the bells are few and heavy-leaded — three at most across the width — so the crown reads as furniture, not a chandelier of clutter.
