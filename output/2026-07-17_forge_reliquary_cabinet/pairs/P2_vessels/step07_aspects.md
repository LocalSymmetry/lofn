## THE HELD INVARIANT BASE (do NOT mutate across the four)

Everything here is constant so the four read as one family (after Kircher — variety under one order):

- **Medium:** verre églomisé — the world painted and gilded on the *inside-back* of the glass so the vessel **emits, not reflects** (after Glomy × Pelton) — plus gilded-ink kintsugi seams in their **dormant hairline** resting state, plus burnished **engraved brass** furniture (after Klimt — one gold line does frame, seam, and structure).
- **Light family:** ONE warm named source only — a single **wick** and/or a **seam-leak** (gold enters from the crack, Flair 3) — casting one **hard directional shadow to the lower-right**. Warm arm only. No teal, no electric-blue, no ambient fill.
- **Palette puzzle (3–4 tones):** velvet-black ground · gilded-gold · ember/amber · bone speculars. Nothing else.
- **Container law:** vessel fills ~75% of the plate; the interior is a **declared empty matte-charcoal void** with **inner-rim wick-light** so the vacancy feels inhabited; the remainder is quiet velvet-black with paper-grain tooth.
- **Finished-ness (after Yu):** lid / latch or cradle + a small **BLANK** brass plaque (reserved text-void). Exactly **one thumbprint**, **one mischief**, **one second-ember** per vessel (Flairs 4 · 15 · 2).
- **Surface law:** paper-grain / dry tempera tooth under everything; hard engraved lead-lines, no soft-focus (after Wyeth × Tiffany — reserve the crack-map to the seams; keep lead-lines few and confident).

**What is allowed to vary per variation: the VESSEL SILHOUETTE (primary axis, always) + exactly ONE of {optical framing · light placement · mischief gesture}.** That is the 1–2 axis budget. Forbidden: changing medium + light-family + palette + mood together.
