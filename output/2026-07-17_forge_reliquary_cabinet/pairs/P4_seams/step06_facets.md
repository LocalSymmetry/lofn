## THE FIVE FACETS (prompt-judging rubric — sensory + emotional)

1. **The crack is the light, never lit by one.** Every seam glows from *within* the fissure — a linear self-emission pouring outward from the molten interior the crack reveals — never a rim, never an ambient wash, never an external key. If the gold reads as *lit* rather than *emitting*, the piece is a painted vending-machine and fails (Pelton's law, ported to a seam).

2. **Five states, five geometries — provable in greyscale.** Desaturate the piece to pure luminance and the five states must still be five *different shapes*: thickness AND pattern change, never brightness alone. Adjacent states differ by a delta a stranger names in under a second (broken→beaded, thin→meshed, meshed→one-fat-bead, bead→calm-matte). This is NORMAN's ladder; it is the never-color-alone floor as *aesthetics*.

3. **The lit-lattice IS the focus ring.** State 3 is the thickest, most cross-linked geometry on the ladder — the one state that reads as a *cage of light* at a glance — because it does double duty as the keyboard-focus indicator (≥3px-equivalent stroke at a 48px target). Beauty and affordance are the same object here or the pair has failed its only job.

4. **Molten remembers it is a frozen moment.** The pressed state implies motion — one fat bead mid-pour — but ships as a *static truth* (Breath-Not-Blink): a photograph of a bead caught, meniscus tight, hottest core leaking blue-white, gravity legible in the sag. Motion is *described*, never required. Reduce-motion users get the whole story from the still.

5. **Cooled bronze keeps its warmth; no shame lives here.** The visited state is medium-gauge, matte, continuous — a seam that has cooled with dignity, faint ember-memory still deep in one junction (Gage's no-FOMO law made material). Yesterday's game is not grey and ashamed; it is bronze and remembered.

## THE GEOMETRY LADDER (the canonical spec every prompt obeys)

**One crack-map, rendered five ways.** All variations draw the SAME network so the states read as one seam *evolving*, not five different cracks. Canonical crack-map (fixed across all four prompts):

> a primary diagonal fissure running lower-left to upper-right; two branch-forks off its upper third (one Y-fork, one T-junction); a short spur off the lower third; and one small isolated tempera island-shard near center where three hairlines nearly meet but do not touch.

| # | State (UI role) | THICKNESS | PATTERN (greyscale signature) | Gloss / tone | 128px read |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dormant** (rest) | thinnest | **BROKEN** hairline — visibly dashed, wide gaps | dim, low-spec bronze-gold | a faint interrupted thread |
| 2 | **Waking shimmer** (hover) | thin (hairline) | continuous line + **3–4 large, sparse round beads** | brightening gold, beads catch a spark | a thread wearing a few bright dots |
| 3 | **Lit lattice** (focus/selected) | **thicker** (2–3×) | **cross-linked MESH** — secondary struts bridge the forks into a net/cage | brightest structural gold | a bright cage / ring |
| 4 | **Molten pour** (pressed/active) | **widest** | single dominant channel carrying **ONE FAT traveling bead** (frozen) | molten; hottest core leaks blue-white | a bright bar with one blob |
| 5 | **Cooled bronze** (visited) | medium | **calm CONTINUOUS** line — no beads, no mesh | matte umber-bronze, ember-memory in one junction | a calm solid line |

**Greyscale adjacency proof (NORMAN's test, pre-checked):**
- 1→2 — broken → continuous-with-beads (PATTERN delta; the fix below sharpens it for 128px)
- 2→3 — thin line → thick mesh (THICKNESS + PATTERN delta; unmistakable)
- 3→4 — many thin crossings → one wide channel + one solid blob (PATTERN inversion + widest THICKNESS)
- 4→5 — wide + blob → medium continuous matte, blob gone, gloss→matte (THICKNESS down + PATTERN + specular delta)

**Ambitious-arm register (this pair rides the intense arm):** the gold is the cooler *brass-lemon* end (greener than the warm P2 vessels), the night-ground carries the faintest **midnight-teal** undertone, and the **electric-blue leak appears only at the molten core** — physically the hottest gold going blue-white, aesthetically Flair #9 (*teal is the night, gold is the life; electric blue only as a leak*).
