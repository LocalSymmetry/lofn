## Set logic (the four as one system, not four ideas)
V1 **hero** (molten, full filigree) → V2 **stamp** (the seam alone, favicon) → V3 **furniture** (the accent glyphs) → V4 **reduced** (hairline, mobile). The spine (string, media, crucible source, seam-line system, palette) is invariant; only **seam-state** and **filigree-density** slide across the set — from V1's molten-maximal to V4's dormant-minimal, with V2 isolating the seam-as-mark and V3 isolating the seam-as-icon-touch. One name, expressed at four scales of attention. Every piece states its declared void; every piece is a PIECE, never a finished view (V1/V2 excepted as finished brand art, which the brief's TEXT EXCEPTION licenses).
