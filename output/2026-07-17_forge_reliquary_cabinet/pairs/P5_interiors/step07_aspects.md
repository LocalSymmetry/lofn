## V3 — PAPER-BIRD THERMALS · "paper birds ride thermals"

## V4 — THE PROOF SHEET · the trio at TRUE 128px  *(KARE's deliverable — the legibility audit)*
- **Moving axes:** optical presentation (orthographic contact-sheet, no perspective) + scale (literal ~128px cells). Worlds + palette HELD from V1–V3.
- **Layout:** a flat matte midnight-teal proof-board, mostly quiet; three small portrait cells evenly spaced in a row, each ≈128px tall, each ringed by a hairline gilded registration border with small gold printer's corner-ticks.
- **Cell contents (each = four shapes + one light, reduced to the bone):** LEFT = descending water-spiral + blue seam-leak upper-right; CENTER = ivory carousel wheel + two marionettes + gold footlight + blue seam-leak upper-left; RIGHT = gold thermal + five-bird rising V + blue seam-leak upper-right.
- **The whole point — the squint test:** each world must be nameable from silhouette alone at this size (descending-spiral / turning-wheel / rising-flock). This sheet is where KARE signs or kills the pair.
- **Palette discipline pushed hardest:** teal + gold forward, electric-blue as the single third accent, bone-cream only where birds/bone require it.
- **Presentation:** landscape **1536×1024**, orthographic and even; generous quiet teal margins separate the cells. NO text inside cells — silhouettes are the identification; gilded corner-ticks only.
