## THE FIVE FACETS (the scoring lenses every P5 prompt must pass)

1. **Emission, not illumination — the world is painted on the inside back of the glass and GLOWS toward you.** Verre églomisé is the whole engine: gold-leaf and gilded ink behind the pane, so light passes *through* the image to reach the eye. If an interior reads as a lit diorama photographed from outside — reflections, cast studio key, dimensional depth-of-field — it has failed the medium. Sensory test: the pane should feel like a lit window at night seen from the cold street, not a shadowbox on a shelf. *(THE REVERSE-GLASS MASTER / Glomy; THE LIT WINDOW / Pelton; flair 12 The Wick And The Pour.)*

2. **Four shapes and a light, instant at 128px — this pair lives or dies here.** Each world must resolve, at thumbnail, to exactly four legible silhouettes and one named source. KARE's budget is law: "intricate bone carnival is mush unless the carnival is ONE wheel and TWO figures." If a squint at 128px yields a texture field instead of *descending-spiral / turning-wheel / rising-flock*, the piece is rerolled, not rescued. The V4 proof sheet exists to prove exactly this. *(THE PIXEL ROCOCO ENGINEER / Kare, flair 7.)*

3. **Intimate immensity — the inside is felt larger than the outside.** The miniature is a *concentrated* thing: the stair descends *forever* to a vanishing gold point, the thermal *rises* out of frame, the carnival is a whole festival in a phial. The impossible must read as a vast interior seen through a small window — approach-warmth, lean-in wonder, the certainty of welcome before you reach the door. Emotion is the locked mood: *the pull of a lit window at night*, never dread. *(THE VESSEL POET / Bachelard; the locked mood.)*

4. **Teal is the night, gold is the life, blue is the only leak — named materials, 2–4 tones.** Midnight-teal ground holds ≥⅓ of every frame quiet; gilded gold is the living line; **exactly one** electric-blue seam-leak per world is the cold voltage that stops the scroll. Every gold is a named material — gold-leaf-behind-glass or gilded-ink — never #FFD700 fantasy-wash. If a frame of this could hang on any other AI arcade, reroll. *(THE SLOP SENTRY / Chayka; palette law flair 9; flairs 8 & 14.)*

5. **Warmth without a horror register — wonder legible to someone's mother in one glance.** The AMBITIOUS arm goes *deeper* into impossibility, never into gloom. The bone carnival is the acid test: carved rounded puppet-bone the color of old ivory and candle-honey, laughing not grimacing, a child's toy theatre — celebration, welcome, one mischief per world. No goth gloom, no Halloween grim, no menace. If a stranger recoils instead of leaning in, the register has failed. *(THE STREAM WEAVER / Chen — no lore gate, warmth outsells mystery at first contact; THE TOY PHILOSOPHER / Caillois — one mischief keeps it a toy; flairs 1, 4, 13, 15.)*

## GPT IMAGE 2 FAILURE-MODE SCAN (renderer rules §06)

```
□ Storybook Cliché risk .............. HIGH  → interiors are exactly where GPT-I2 reaches for "magical worlds." Override per prompt: flat reverse-glass emission, hard gilded lead-line contours, off-center DIAGONAL composition, deep saturated teal — stated positively, never as a negation of banned words.
□ Warm rim light risk ................ MED   → gold under-glow / footlight is bottom-warm by design, but the PRIMARY light is a hard-edged electric-blue seam-ribbon (a cold key), which is itself the anti-rim override. Name the blue seam-leak as the dominant source; keep gold as a rising line-glow, not an ambient halo.
□ Centered pastel subject risk ....... MED-HIGH → interiors default to a centered hero + pastel air. Override: each comp falls on a descending/rising/rotating DIAGONAL weighted off-center; teal is deep, not pale; the palette is saturated, never pastel.
□ Reiteration / edit-chain risk ...... LOW   → four self-contained prompts, one generation each, no chained edits.
□ Reference contamination risk ....... MED   → "bone carnival" can pull Day-of-the-Dead / Halloween real motifs; "paper birds" can pull the generic origami-crane stock cliché. Mitigate: NO skulls-as-décor, NO calavera marigold iconography, NO text; specify carved TOY puppet-bone + folded songbird forms, authored not stock. No real logos, no inscriptions.
□ Entropy drift / texture smear risk . HIGH  → the pair's core danger. Impossible worlds beg to over-detail into mush; craquelure + gilding + a busy interior can mud the four-shape read. Override: reserve egg-tempera craquelure ONLY where a gold seam runs; hold ≥⅓ quiet teal; cap the silhouette count at KARE's budget.
□ Overcrowded annotation risk ........ LOW   → no annotations; no specimen markers.
□ Text illegibility risk ............. N/A   → interiors carry NO text by contract. This removes the risk rather than managing it. (Text is banned where the piece doesn't call for it; these don't.)
□ Primary read inversion risk ........ LOW   → no diagram/text to invert. For V4 the intended primary read IS the silhouette audit — by design, not inversion.
```

**Two highest-priority overrides for all four prompts:** (1) the *entropy/mush* default — every prompt hard-caps to four shapes + one light and reserves craquelure to the seams; this is KARE's make-or-break. (2) the *horror-register* default on V2 — heavy positive warmth language (ivory, candle-honey, laughing, toy theatre, a child's puppet-carnival, welcome) so GPT-I2 cannot slide bone toward grim.
