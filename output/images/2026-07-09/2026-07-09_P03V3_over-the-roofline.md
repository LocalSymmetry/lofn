# Milk for the Moon

## Generation Prompt

A cyanotype-over-platinum duotone of a kitchen sash window at the lamplight-daylight crossover, a frame within a frame: glazing bars in matte smoked-umber platinum, verdigris pre-day sky beyond the weathered pane. Low and enormous behind the terrace chimney pots the full moon stands pearl-exact, maria etching-crisp, the sharpest zone in frame, leaning faintly toward this one window; the kitchen lamp answers as a warm umber disc reflected in the glass, two round lights negotiating across one pane. Rosa, thirty-four, Filipina night-shift nurse in scrubs and wool coat, lanyard on, stands at the sill in three-quarter profile between the two temperatures, hands quiet on the counter; her breath shows, exhaled at last, drifting toward the moon's side of the glass. On the sill an empty bowl of pooled pearl light, two mugs, one dusty. The sky between chimneys rests empty. The night shift ends; something waited up.
