# PHASE 0 — CORE SEED

## The commission

A material bible and asset prompt pack for **ONE MORE DOOR**: a first-person game about walking a
corridor and choosing between doors, trading what you carry for what might be behind the next one.

Founder-set commitment, 2026-07-28: **LITHOPHANE PORCELAIN — the image only exists when lit.**

## Why this material is not decoration

A lithophane is a subtractive-light medium. Thickness *is* the image: where the porcelain is thick
the light is stopped and reads dark; where it is carved thin the light floods through and reads
bright. Unlit, it is a blank pale slab that tells you nothing. Backlit, a picture blooms out of
apparently featureless clay.

That property **is the game's information system.** An unlit door shows nothing. Light is the
finite thing the player spends to learn what is behind a door *before* committing to it. The
material argues for the mechanic rather than dressing it.

- **L1 · HIGH — container / object-world:** keep the outer silhouette clear; pack the **interior**

- **L2 · HIGH — realism loses to impossibility:** in object-world fields a *real* place

  not a documentary of a real lithophane workshop.** Invent the impossible interior.
- **"Emanating Light as Formula" (top-1% observation):** warm light radiating *from the subject
  itself* against a dark ground is close to a requirement. A lithophane does not receive light —
  it **emits** it. This material is that formula made literal.
- **"Recursive Wonder" motif (proven crowd-pleaser):** a world contained inside an object. A door
  panel with a world glowing inside its own clay is exactly that.

## Golden Seed anchor — and what is preserved

**SEED 11 — The Homebrew Reliquaries** (primary). Preserved structurally:
- **The Artifact of Imperfection** — every part carries a tangible, lovingly worn, non-digital
  flaw. In this material that vocabulary is specific: kiln warp, firing speckle, mould seam,
  chipped arris, glaze pooling in a carved trough, a thumbprint left in the greenware.
- **Light as Character** — light is not a lamp, it is a *participant*. Here it is promoted
  further: light is the only thing that makes the image exist at all.
- **Legendary at thumbnail** — the part must read as obviously significant at phone size.
- **Wrongness through normalcy** — the corridor is mundane; what the clay does is not.

**SEED 4 — Moonlake Églomisé Garden** (secondary). Églomisé is reverse-painting on glass, where
the *light passing through* is the primary material. Lithophane is its ceramic cousin. Preserved:
the discipline of a technique where the medium's physics, not the palette, produces the beauty.

## THE EMOTIONAL ENGINE — locked mood

Not "mysterious." Not "atmospheric." The mood is:

> **PATIENT DREAD, LIT FROM BEHIND.**

The tenderness of a made object — someone carved this by hand, slowly, and fired it — colliding
with the fact that you must spend your only light to find out what it says, and that reading it
uses it up. Awe at craft; dread at cost. Both at once, in one slab of clay.

*Lofn's duality, exactly:* AWE at a material that must be made slowly and cannot be rushed;
INDIGNATION at the sixty games this studio has made that nobody outside the building has ever
seen. This is the first artefact meant to be *looked at* by a stranger.

## THE FIVE FRESH CONSTRAINT AXES

Each pair draws one option per axis. None recycled from prior Forge or NightCafe runs.

**AXIS 1 — CLAY BODY** (what the porcelain is)
`bone-china (warm, high translucency)` · `parian (sugary, matte, sculptural)` ·
`grogged stoneware (coarse, barely translucent — light fights it)` ·
`slip-cast eggshell (near-paper thin, terrifyingly fragile)` ·
`celadon-glazed (a green-blue skin over white body; light turns colour on the way out)`

**AXIS 2 — THE CARVING HAND** (how the relief got there)
`wheel-thrown then hand-gouged` · `pressed from a worn plaster mould, seams visible` ·
`carved wet with a loop tool, tool-chatter left in` · `sgraffito through a dark slip` ·
`3D-printed and then hand-finished badly on purpose`

**AXIS 3 — THE LIGHT SOURCE** (what is behind the panel)
`a single candle, guttering` · `a cold electric filament, too blue for the clay` ·
`daylight through a distant window, indirect` · `another lithophane, lit in turn` ·
`something alive and dim that is not identified`

**AXIS 4 — WHAT THE IMAGE IS OF** (what blooms when lit)
`the room on the other side of the door` · `the person who carved it` ·
`what you are carrying, seen from behind` · `a place that no longer exists` ·
`the corridor you are standing in, one moment later`

**AXIS 5 — THE FAILURE OF THE OBJECT** (its Artifact of Imperfection, promoted to a rule)
`a firing crack that splits the image` · `warped so it no longer sits flush in its frame` ·
`one corner too thick — a permanent blind spot` · `repaired with visible staples` ·
`glaze pooled in a trough and drowned a detail forever`

## THE THUMBNAIL TEST (baked in, per SEED 15's discipline)

A viewer at phone size, in five seconds, must be able to say: *"that is a slab of clay with a
picture inside it, and the picture is made of light."* If the part needs a caption, it has failed.

## NEUTRAL DISPATCH BRIEF (no personality injected — this is what the panel debates)

> Design the complete material identity for a first-person game whose every surface is backlit
> porcelain that is invisible until lit. Deliverable is separable engine PARTS — door panels in
> unlit and lit states, corridor walls, floor and threshold, the light source, frame trim, and
> small carried objects — never composed scenes, never HUD, never typography. The material must
> read at mobile-web resolution, hold silhouette when unlit, and carry its own imperfection
> vocabulary. Light must be *carved*, never faded: alpha-gradient glow is forbidden by the
> consuming style contract. Determine what makes this material legible as a playable space rather
> than a gallery object.
