## 4 · RAW-PASS NOTES *(honest, before refinement)*

**What is already right.** All four hinges are register handoffs with the chant cut mid-syllable and a dry
voice completing it, at the top of the final drop — a **substitution at a junction the arrangement already
wants**, in the **lyric**, not the production spec. All four sing **"five hundred"** exactly once, spelled in
words, at the hinge, **responded to** by a private act rather than recited. All four choruses are
**byte-identical ×3** and no justification note has been written about any of them. Every flat-list item is
**verb-first**, which is what keeps the pair legal under its outright `[Object. State.]` ban. Nowhere does the
singer reply to the chat.

**Known weaknesses to fix at step 09, named now so step 09 has a job:**

1. **The rebound word drifts.** Across the sixteen couplets the internal rhyme sits anywhere from word 2 to
   word 5. Guide 2 directed *beat 3 of a shrinking bar*; several Scroll-1 couplets put it too late and several
   Scroll-3 couplets put it too early to read as beat 3. **Audit all sixty-four couplets at step 09.**
2. **V1 couplet 8 ("the flight gets in tonight") has lost its attribution frame** while its neighbours keep
   theirs — the decay is supposed to be gradual across Scroll 2, not a hole in the middle of Scroll 1.
3. **V2's Break section is a new section that the other three do not have**, which risks V2 reading as a
   different song rather than a different angle. It earns itself — the arm dropping *is* V2 — but it must be
   one line and a cue, never a section with weight.
4. **V4 couplet 16 carries two voices in one unit**, breaking the one-voice-per-couplet rule. Deliberate, and
   it needs to be defended in writing or reverted.
5. **The approach openers are all first-person declaratives of the same shape** (*"I am holding / I am putting
   / I am watching / I am going to"*). Four songs, one grammatical gesture. Two of them must change.
6. ⚠️ **V4's opener is future-tense in feel** (*"I am going to make this last"*) and **D5 is present tense
   only.** It is a present-tense intention rather than a narrator who knows the outcome — but it is close
   enough to the line that it should be rewritten rather than argued.
7. **Turn check, provisional:** V2's turn (the arm drops, the picture improves) and V4's turn (the phone
   becomes the thing with the fixed duration) are strong and land past the midpoint. **V1's and V3's turns are
   weak** — V1's *"she stops trying to read them"* is not yet in the text at all, and V3's *"she is watching a
   stream of someone watching"* is implied rather than staged. **Both must be built at step 09.**
