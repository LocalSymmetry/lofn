## ⭐ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT ANSWERS (GOLDEN MOVE #4 — required in writing, both)

### 1. WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?

Folded forward over a waist-high steel barrier at the front of an all-standing indoor floor, both forearms flat
on the top rail, hips against the padding, feet on a strip of gaffer tape somebody laid down at load-in. She
queued from four in the afternoon for this exact metre of rail. The support act has not started. The house PA
is running a pre-set playlist at conversation-killing volume; the room is a wall of talking underneath it. A
security guard's forearm comes over the rail every few minutes with a paper cup of water and goes away again.
Her wristband is damp. There is a shoulder in her back that belongs to nobody in particular, and it does not
move when she breathes out.

### 2. WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

⭐ **Ordinary things, and nothing worse.** A rail-shaped ache across both forearms. A bruise on the hip bone
tomorrow. A phone that goes down somewhere under two hundred shoes and comes back with a cracked corner. A
voice gone for two days. Somebody's elbow finding her ear on a jump. Dehydration and a warm cup of water.
**No crush. No emergency. No casualty. Everybody in this room goes home tonight and so does she.**

⭐ **And the honest answer, which is the song:** the most dangerous object in the room is the thing she brought
in past the bag check — **she is quietly disappointed that the night is safe.** The terror-adjacency is not
the crowd. It is her own wanting.

## ⭐ BUILD BY LEVEL — a state machine, not a ramp (QA N2; three pairs build by level, this is one)

The arrangement **never accretes**. It occupies one of five states and **jumps** at section boundaries. A
crossfade anywhere in this pair is a defect.

| state | what is running |
|---|---|
| **S0 · FLOOR** | house-PA bleed only: distant skank-drum wash, room talk, no 808 |
| **S1 · LEVEL** | full clipping 808 + triplet hats + chant. This is the default; the song lives here |
| **S2 · TWO-BIT** | Flair #15 BIT-DEPTH COLLAPSE — hi-fi → 2-bit at the loss of certainty. Full energy, quantised to gravel |
| **S3 · GILDED** | 808 out, the ornament alone, exposed, absurdly ornate |
| **S4 · MAX** | S1 + gang layer + Hammond + the one triplet fill |

⛔ **No state is ever a hole.** D10: the hinge is a **substitution at a junction the arrangement already
wants**, never a specified mid-song void — measured smoothed 2 of 2.

## THE FIXED ARCHITECTURE (Phase 1, non-negotiable, identical across all four variations)

- **Verses: 6 / 6 / 6 bars**, dense **internal** rhyme, two written lines per bar.
- **Chorus: TWO LINES ONLY, repeated four times, byte-identical.** Shortest chorus in the run.
  ⛔ **Write it and say nothing about it.** No waive request, no justification note, no "this is the mechanism"
  paragraph. `gates.yaml → chorus_repetition_requires_no_justification: true`.
- **A1 CONSONANT HAMMER:** the return arrives **early** — a plosive/fricative cluster at **line-HEADS**
  (alliterative anaphora), sustained through every verse. **This is the pair's rhyme-debt payment.**
  End-rhyme is displaced into internal position; the head-hammer plus the 2-line chorus ×4 pay the debt in full.
- **A2 HINGE — MIX INVERSION:** the background becomes **lead for exactly one line**, then loses again.
  ⭐ **AUDITOR'S ALTERATION, BINDING:** this is **written into the lyric as a voice swap**, not asked of a
  fader. A wide line is cut off mid-phrase; a dry close line takes the front; the wide layer wins back
  immediately. Legible on paper → survives the render (THE GRAIN LAW, L22).
- **A3 DURATION — THE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT:** one section is **audibly, deliberately too short**. The
  section is **named per variation below** and **is not fixed, not repaired, not commented on.**
- **Flairs:** #15 BIT-DEPTH COLLAPSE · #3 THE SWAP AT THE JUNCTION · #10 CHANT A ROOM CAN HOLD.

## THE CROSSING — how the two-layer stack resolves, and where the numeral goes

**ABOVE** = the house PA, wide and indifferent, playing a pre-set playlist nobody in the room chose, made of
songs shaped to reach a hook by fifteen and a payout at thirty. **BELOW** = one body on a rail, talking to
nobody, hands busy.

**The crossing, once per song, audible, in the lyric:** a fragment of the overhead song lands — **and it is a
HOOK, not a statement** — and it contains the pair's allocated numeral, **"thirty seconds," spelled out in
words**. It is cut off mid-phrase. A dry, close, single line from the rail takes the front and covers it. Then
the PA wins again and the drop lands.

⛔ **THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST IN THIS PAIR.** The answering line is a **body fact**, never an analysis.
It states a duration the body has already served. **The listener does the arithmetic; the lyric never does it
for them.** If any draft contains a line that says what the number means, that draft has written the essay
this run bans and is rewritten on the spot. **Sung once. Responded to. Never explained.**

## PER-VARIATION DIRECTION

*Four angles, four head-hammers, four too-short sections, four crossings, four chants. Nothing but the fixed
architecture is shared.*

### V1 · **SHE NAMES THE WANT**
- **Direct:** the want is stated **in the opening block**, flat, once, in plain words — before the first
  chorus. The remaining ~80% of the song is her **declining to retract it**. No re-litigating, no softening,
  no "but". FIRESTARTER's condition: if it arrives as a reveal, she knew all along and the song is a liar.
- **Head-hammer:** **P / B** (`push · press · palm · paid · pit · barrier · both · bruise · back`).
- **Too-short section:** ⭐ **the BRIDGE — four bars where the form has taught eight.** The chorus arrives
  early over the top of it. Nothing repairs it and nothing mentions it.
- **Crossing:** at the **top of the final drop**. The PA fragment carries the numeral; the rail-mic line
  answers with the hour she has been standing there.
- **EMO arc:** Suspense → Eagerness → **Moral Outrage** → Defiance → Resolve *(taxonomy values, embodied)*.
- **Chant:** four syllables, plain words, **zero venue vocabulary**, first-person, shoutable by a stranger who
  has never been at a barrier (RIOT INSTIGATOR's condition).
- **Do not write:** an explanation of the want; a justification; a second character; any plural pronoun.

### V2 · **THE PERSON BESIDE HER**
- **Direct:** the stranger at her left elbow is having a **genuinely, uncomplicatedly good time** and is
  rendered with **real affection and physical specificity** — this is the NEON CHEERLEADER's condition and the
  RELENTLESS OPTIMIST's, and it is the reason the want lands. ⛔ **The stranger is never a foil, never naive,
  never the lesson.** She is simply better at tonight. The want is spoken *past* her, not to her.
- **Head-hammer:** **S / SH / ST** sibilant cluster (`shoulder · spare · shout · still · sweat · stranger ·
  strap · shove`).
- **Too-short section:** ⭐ **the post-chorus tail after chorus 2 — two bars where it has been eight.** The
  next verse starts before the room has finished the chant.
- **Crossing:** the bar before chorus 3. ⭐ **The stranger's voice — background all song — becomes the lead
  for exactly one line**, over the top of the PA fragment carrying the numeral. Then she loses again and the
  PA returns. The crossing and the affection are the **same event**.
- **EMO arc:** Affection → Eagerness → **Contempt** *(at the ceiling, never at her)* → Admiration → Resolve.
- **Chant:** built from the stranger's body, not her mood. Four syllables or fewer.
- **Do not write:** the stranger noticing the want; a reply; envy; irony; any plural pronoun. ⛔ **No proper
  name for her** — the affection is carried by physical detail.

### V3 · **THE PUSH**
- **Direct:** **pure body mechanics.** The verses are load, angle, friction, breath, weight transfer, footing,
  the rail's temperature, forearms, the taped mark, the shoulder that returns. ⭐ **The want appears ONLY in
  the chorus** — the verses never touch it, so the two chorus lines land as the only interior in the song.
- **Head-hammer:** **B / BR** (`boot · brace · breathe · barrier · bone · back · bar · bend`).
- **Too-short section:** ⭐ **the OUTRO — six bars short of resolution.** The song stops while the physics is
  still running.
- **Crossing:** the top of drop 2. The **rail itself** — the boots, the breath, the steel, background all song
  — takes the front for one line over the PA fragment carrying the numeral.
- **EMO arc:** Vigilance → Impatience → **Moral Outrage** → Zealousness → Defiance.
- **Chant:** must be sayable **through a clenched jaw**; hard consonants, no open vowels needing air.
- **Do not write:** interiority in the verses; simile; any plural pronoun; any injury above a bruise.

### V4 · **NOTHING HAPPENS**
- **Direct:** the enormous thing **arrives exactly on schedule and is merely beautiful.** No catastrophe, no
  epiphany, no lesson, no upgrade. She goes home. ⭐ **D9 is load-bearing here: nothing in the song is allowed
  to make the ordinary night retroactively precious.** The keeping is the act; the night stays ordinary. And
  ⛔ **D5: she does not get the moral** — the last line is present-tense and knows nothing.
- **Head-hammer:** **K / hard C** (`cold · coat · count · carry · clear · corner · card · come`).
- **Too-short section:** ⭐ **the FINAL DROP — half the length of every other drop in the song.** The biggest
  moment is the shortest one and nothing repairs it. *(The form performing its own subject: this is the
  variation where the too-short section and the angle are the same idea.)*
- **Crossing:** at the false-arrival junction — the house lights coming up. The room's own flat hum, wide and
  indifferent, carries the PA fragment with the numeral; her dry unimpressed close line takes the front for
  exactly one line.
- **EMO arc:** Eagerness → Amazement → **Disappointment** → Forgiveness → Numbness *(held, not resolved)*.
- **Chant:** the same two lines the room has shouted three times already, arriving where nothing has happened.
  Unchanged. **That is the point and it gets no note.**
- **Do not write:** a redemption; a "but at least"; a future tense; a lesson; any plural pronoun.
