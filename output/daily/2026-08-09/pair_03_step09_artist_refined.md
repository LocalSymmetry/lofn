*A song that asserts its final emotion from line one is a corpse. The turn must CONTRADICT or COMPLICATE the

**Step file:** `skills/music/steps/09_Generate_Music_Artist_Refined.md` under its 2026-07 OVERRIDE.

**Self-critique.** Two things I do not like and am not hiding.

**One.** The repair that fixed V1's end-rhyme also made the verses *more* repetitive across all four songs at
once, and repetition is the pair's whole surface. There is a real risk that V1–V4 now share a texture even
though they share no line: four songs whose verses all go *A / A / B / B*. It is defensible — it is the
assigned architecture and it is what the character is doing — but it is also the shortest path to the metrics,
and I took it in all four. **The guard is that the four choruses share no word of structure** (five-line echo,
five-line echo, five-line echo, five-line echo — but four entirely different hooks, four different chants,
four different swaps) **and the four turns are four different kinds of turn.** If the clean-context judge with
all twenty-four songs in view says these four read as one song, the repair is to break the couplet form in two
of them, and V2 and V4 are the ones that can take it.

**Two.** V3's hinge — the other person completing her sentence *wrongly* and being pleased — is the best line
in the pair and it is one degree away from making the other character a villain, which **D2's sibling logic
forbids just as firmly as it forbids blaming the phone**. It survives because they are *right* that they have
heard the story, they are being generous, and the song never suggests otherwise. If a single word of contempt
enters that exchange at step 11, it breaks.
