**21 · THE FRONT OF THE CROWD** — *THE FIRESTARTER (after Powers):* she says the want **flatly, early, once**,
and then spends the song not taking it back. *THE NEON CHEERLEADER (after Tillie):* the person next to her is

**22 · ENGINE OFF** — *THE MACHINE-GUN DRUMMER (after Barker):* the count is a **human counting**, not a
click. *THE RIFF ARCHITECT (after Puget):* when the music returns, the bass line is the same shape in a
different key — the ear knows before the listener does.

**23 · THE ARM AT FULL EXTENSION** — *THE HALF-PIPE WITNESS (after Hawk):* the couplets never answer each
other; sixteen strangers, sixteen unrelated sentences. *THE DIGITAL REBELLION SOCIOLOGIST (after boyd):* the

condescension this run banned.
