## 6. WHAT THE PANEL CHANGED DURING DRAFTING *(the supplied 18 voices, not a new panel)*

  with a human voice slightly behind the grid, written into the lyric, in all four.
- **THE RIFF ARCHITECT (after Puget)** — the four tonality swaps were originally one swap reused four times.
  **Split into four distinct swaps** (minor third up / metre re-division / semitone down / dropped beat) so the
  variations differ where it is audible rather than only where it is lexical.
- **THE GRIT SHAPER (after Perry)** — the audible inhale is written into V2's spec instead of left to chance.
- **THE ANGST CARTOGRAPHER (after Rodrigo)** — *"the pen is chained to the board, and I'm not"* replaced a
  generalised line about leaving work. The humiliating specific beats the summary every time.

  V1 verse 3 read *"the second comes at the same time for everybody"* → replaced with two named bodies
  (*"it lands on me and on the girl coming on at once"*). V3 verse 3 read *"whoever's under it"* → replaced
  with objects (*"me and the lamp and the thing going out"*). **Measured surviving cohort hits across all four
  songs: 0.**
- **THE DYNAMIC RANGE AUDITOR (after Katz) ⚠️** — his objection to the false outro is **honoured, not
