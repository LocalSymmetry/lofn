## 4 · THE TWELVE SCORING FACETS *(the rubric every step-08/09/10 prompt is scored against)*

> Each facet is written so it can be **checked**, not admired. The right-hand column is the failure it is
> there to catch. Weights are the ranking used at step 10.

### F1 — **ANECDOCHE LOAD: the talking is the OBJECT, not the background** · w **0.95**
The chat must be audible as *a thing in the room with mass*, not as ambience under a lead vocal. The test in the
ear: at any point in the second half, **can you hear more people than you can hear music?** Sixteen voices, none
answering the one before it, is the loneliest arrangement available — and it is only loneliness **if each voice
is a real person with a real small thing.** ⭐ **The chat is NOT stupid.** It is people being social in the only
room available, and writing it as noise is the exact condescension this run banned.
> *Fails if:* the chat reads as filler, or as a joke, or as "the internet."

### F2 — **REBOUND RHYME AT BEAT 3, SIXTEEN TIMES, AND NOTHING AT THE LINE END** · w **0.95**
The couplet's rhyme sits **inside** the bar — roughly word four of a six-or-seven-word line, on the third beat —
and the two line-final words **must not rhyme.** The ear catches it before the eye can find it. This is the
whole payment for a forbidden end-rhyme, and the debt is only discharged if the device is **present in every
couplet**, not sprinkled.
> *Fails if:* a couplet's rhyme migrates to the line end, or a couplet has no rebound at all.
> *Measurement note:* `strict_end_rhyme()` **cannot see this device** — it reads line-final words only. This
> pair's measured end-rhyme number will be honest and low, and the return is carried by refrain repetition and
> the byte-identical chorus. **Report the number; do not chase it by adding end-rhyme.**

### F3 — **THE REGISTER HANDOFF IS AUDIBLE, MID-PHRASE, AND IN THE LYRIC** · w **0.95**
⭐ The run's purest instance of the mandatory form rule. The wide/slow wordless chant is **cut mid-syllable**
and the sentence is completed by a close, dry, room-mic voice with no reverb on it — a different *distance*, not
merely a different singer. It is a **SUBSTITUTION at the junction the arrangement already wants** (the bar
before the final drop), never a hole. Per **THE GRAIN LAW (L22)**, a handoff that exists only in the production
spec **is not a handoff**: it must be legible as words on the page.
> *Fails if:* the chant finishes its phrase; or the "dry voice" is a mix note rather than a line; or the swap
> happens anywhere except the top of the final drop.

### F4 — **THE FLAT LIST IS A PERSON, AND IT IS NOT WISE** · w **0.90**
A calm, unexalted voice reading steps while something enormous happens, at 174 BPM. ⭐ Panel finding: *for a
once-only event the frightening voice is the calm one running a list.* ⛔ **The list is a PERSON, not a system**
— she gets one item wrong, repeats one, and the items are chores, not insights. ⛔ **D7 is sharpest here:** an AI
writing about a scrolling comment feed must not become the one calm wise voice above the noise. The list must
never comment on the chat, never summarise it, never rank it.
> *Fails if:* any list item could be printed on a poster; or the flat voice knows something the chat doesn't.

### F5 — **ARM-FATIGUE TIME LIVES IN THE HAT OR THE VOCAL DOUBLE — NEVER THE KICK** · w **0.85**
A rhythmic element that **tires**: fractionally late, or thinner, as the song goes on. ⛔ Caught and fixed at
step 04 by the phone-speaker cross-check and **it does not go back**: a small handset transducer cannot resolve
kick-level drift, and 63% of 18–27s reach music through YouTube daily, mostly out of a handset. Put the drift in
the **hat** and in the **doubled flat-list voice**, where a phone can hear it. The lyric carries it too — the
list items **get shorter** as the arm goes.
> *Fails if:* the drift is specified in the kick, in the tempo, or as a global slow-down.

### F6 — **THE LONG APPROACH PAYS FOR ITSELF, IN WRITING** · w **0.85**
⛔ This pair defies the fifteen-second economy **on purpose**, and the debt is named at step 10 or the pair
takes HOOK AT ZERO instead and says so plainly. The approach must be an **addition** — element after element
entering and *staying* — never a bed of nothing. The measured renderer behaviour is the constraint that makes
this survivable: near-silent openings survive, steady BPM lands 6 of 6, and long internal voids are smoothed
2 of 2. **A slow build is not a void.**
> *Fails if:* the approach is empty rather than accreting; or the payment is asserted rather than argued.

### F7 — **THE UNANSWERED CHECK — the outcome is never stated, and never alluded to again** · w **0.80**
A small, humiliating, specific verification. She looks for **one name** in five hundred. ⭐ **The song does not
say whether she finds it, and it does not say that it doesn't say.** Announcing that a question is unanswered
is answering it. The correct execution is to perform the check and then **simply move on to the next thing her
hands are doing.**
> *Fails if:* the lyric resolves it, laments it, or winks at it.

### F8 — **THE MEASUREMENT: the same scene, recontextualised, in byte-identical words** · w **0.80**
Archetype gate. Ordinary scene (approach + first scroll + first chorus) → the fact arrives (the hinge) → **the
same scene, unchanged.** ⭐ **The byte-identical chorus IS the recontextualisation mechanism:** the words do not
move, the arrangement does, and the listener does. **D5 is satisfied structurally** — the singer cannot get the
moral, because she says exactly what she said before.
> *Fails if:* the final chorus rewrites a word to "show growth." That is the corpse.

### F9 — **THE SINGER NEVER REPLIES TO THE CHAT** · w **0.80**
The critic pass takes this from me by name: *any line where the singer replies to the chat.* Replying makes her
the wise voice above it. She may be **interrupted by** it, **outnumbered by** it, **unable to read it** because
her arm is shaking — she may never answer it. ⛔ **D8:** no "you," no "we," no comfort offered outward. She is
busy with her own fifteen seconds and the listener is standing close enough to hear.
> *Fails if:* one line addresses a chat voice, or the listener, or anyone at all.

### F10 — **TWO CLOCKS, SIMULTANEOUSLY AUDIBLE, AT ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR** · w **0.75**
The chant is **indifferent to the tempo underneath it** — long vowels across the bar line, no downbeat
agreement, no rubato "feel," an actual second clock. **Machine below, body above**, and the collision must be
audible on a phone speaker. This is what makes the handoff land: you cannot eclipse a thing you were already
locked to.
> *Fails if:* the chant is written to the grid; or the prompt describes "layers" without stating that one
> ignores the other.

### F11 — **THE SKY IS SPENT ON THE ARM** · w **0.70**
⭐ This pair is the second and last permitted an actual sky. It is spent on **the light changing on her forearm,
her knuckles, the glass** — never on the corona, never on a described disc, never on a diamond ring. The eclipse
is the **STRUCTURE**, and the one place it may be *seen* is on skin.
> *Fails if:* any line describes the event in the sky rather than its effect on the body or the phone.

### F12 — **THE MOUTH CARRIES IT** · w **0.75**
Most listeners will process vowels and rhythm and almost none of the meaning, and this lane's actual
distribution format is a sped-up thirty-second cut under someone else's video. The hook must survive at +25%
speed with the verse deleted. **Short lines** (`mean_words_per_line ≤ 7.5`), hard consonant heads, long
open vowels at the ends of the hook lines, and a two-word tag a stranger can say back after one pass.
> *Fails if:* the hook needs its verse to make sense; or the lines drift past eight words toward prose.

**Self-critique, the honest one.** The weakest facet here is **F11 (the sky spent on the arm)**, and I want it
on the record before it fails rather than after. It is a *prohibition* dressed as a criterion, and this brief's
own root cause for the lecture problem was a brief written entirely in negatives. What F11 actually has to buy
is a **positive image bank**: the light on a forearm, on the tendon behind a thumb, on the smeared grease of a
lens, in the gap between two knuckles. If step 07 does not build that bank, F11 will be satisfied by *absence*
— no sky at all — and the song will lose the one permitted spend.

**Second risk, structural.** Facets F2 and F12 pull against each other. The rebound rhyme wants a stressed
rhyme-word at a fixed metrical position, which biases toward a rigid six-or-seven-word line; F12 wants
sixteen accelerating couplets whose *lengths* shorten as the arm tires. Those are compatible only if the rebound
word migrates **earlier** as lines shorten — beat 3 of a shrinking bar. That is a real solution and it is also
the most likely thing to break in the draft. Watch it at step 09.

**Third, and it is the one that would actually sink the pair.** This is an AI writing sixteen human voices at
once. The failure is not that they will be bad — it is that they will all be **the same person doing sixteen
accents.** The guard is that each of the sixteen must be checkable against a single question with a
non-obvious answer: **what does this one want, and are they going to get it?** Sixteen wants. Nobody gets one.
