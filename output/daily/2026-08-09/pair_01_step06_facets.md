## 3. PANEL PRESSURE — the three Hyper-Skeptics, on P01's rubric

> **THE HARDCORE ELDER (after Rollins) — DISSENT, CARRIED FORWARD UNRESOLVED.**
> *"I said at step 03 that eighteen is a warm thing where a cold thing should be, and I have not withdrawn it.
> Here is my problem with your rubric: facet b says the tape stays bad, and then facet d builds you a kitchen
> with somebody else in it. That is a warm room. You have banned the sentence 'this matters' and then built a
> set where the audience supplies it for free. If the ninth watch is going to be honest, the second body in
> that kitchen cannot be company. It has to be somebody who is not watching."*
> **RULING — sustained, and it changes the artifact.** The second body in the kitchen **does not look at the
> screen, does not speak, and is not comforting.** They are running a tap. The proximity is the point and the
> warmth is not offered. Written into P01-d as a hard condition.

> **THE DYNAMIC RANGE AUDITOR (after Katz) — CONDITIONAL YES.**
> *"Facet d is the only interesting engineering claim in this file and it is also the one the renderer will
> fight. 'The loop never grows' is a subtraction stated as a plan, and subtraction is what I measured being
> smoothed — two of two. If the loop is genuinely identical for three minutes the generator will get bored and
> add something for you. So do not ask for sameness. Ask for a **replacement per room**: same loop, different
> ROOM SOUND — headphone-close, then a small speaker in air, then a hard kitchen. Those are timbral swaps, not
> level moves, and swaps survive."*
> **RULING — sustained.** P01-d is now specified as **three acoustics, one loop** — a substitution per room,
> never a density change. This also protects D10 at the level of the whole arrangement, not just the hinge.

> **THE COHORT ABOLITIONIST (after Cohen) — YES, ON THE SAME CONDITION AS EVERY PAIR.**
> *"A catalogue is the one grammar in this run that physically cannot hold a cohort — there is nowhere in a
> list to put a 'we'. Fine. But watch facet k. 'Four angles on one night' is the sentence that becomes 'a
> shared experience' if anybody gets sentimental at step 09. The gate is applied **line by line at step 10**,
> counted, and reported as a number. Not asserted."*
> **RULING — accepted.** D6 is run line by line at step 10 and the kill count is reported as an integer.
