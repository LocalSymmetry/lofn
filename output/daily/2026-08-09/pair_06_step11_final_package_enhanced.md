## §1 · THE ENHANCEMENT PASS — one distinctive structural device per variation

The contract requires *at least one distinctive literary or structural device applied to each pair's lyrics.*
Four were applied, one per variation, each chosen because it deepens the assigned emotion rather than
decorating it. **None of them touches a chorus** — the three byte-identical choruses stand exactly as written.

### V1 · ⭐ **ANADIPLOSIS AT ONE SEAM**
Sixteen strangers, none answering the previous one — that is the architecture, and it is airtight, and airtight
is slightly inhuman. So **once**, in the middle of Scroll 2, the last word of one couplet becomes the first word
of the next: someone asks for a charger and *asks a lot and asks **again***, and the next voice opens
***"again — a stranger says the light on the wall."***
For one bar it sounds exactly like a reply. **It is not one.** One person is looking for a cable and the other
is describing a wall going white. ⭐ **The accidental appearance of dialogue in a room where nobody is talking
to anybody is a sharper statement of anecdoche than sixteen clean misses**, because it shows you the shape of
the thing that is missing. Used **once**, never explained, and it is the only seam of its kind in sixty-four
couplets.

### V2 · ⭐ **THE SYLLABLE STAIRCASE — a flat list that collapses in exact arithmetic**
V2 is the arm, and the arm is a quantity being spent. The flat list now collapses on a countable grid:
**five items of five syllables → three items of three syllables → one item of one syllable.**
*Lock the elbow out. Drop the shoulder down. Turn the wrist a bit. Breathe out and then hold. Shift onto the
left.* → *Lock it out. Shift the weight. Breathe out now.* → *Lock.*
⭐ **The form is the fatigue.** It is audible without being announced, it is verb-first at every step so the
`[Object. State.]` ban stays clean, and **it is a structure the listener feels as running out of breath**
rather than a line that says she is tired. `5×5 → 3×3 → 1×1` is the shape of an arm going down.

### V3 · ⭐ **THE UNMARKED CENTRE — her own line moved onto the seam of the sixteen**
V3's whole angle is that she is not the protagonist of the room; she is one of the five hundred. Her own chat
line was sitting at couplet 5, early and legible. **It is now couplet 8 — the last unit of the first half, the
exact centre seam of the sixteen — inside the section where attributions have begun to dissolve.** Strangers
on both sides of her are talking about a prayer and a work shift. **Nothing marks it.** No tag, no register
change, no arrangement lift, and the fifteen lines around it are treated identically.
⭐ **She is structurally at the centre of the room and completely invisible in it, and the song never points at
this.** That is the pair's emotion executed as geometry.

### V4 · ⭐ **THE HOST-LESS CODA — the room gets the last word**
Her screen dies eight lines before the end. Previously she still sang the final line, which quietly restored her
to the centre of a song whose entire argument is that the room does not need her. **The outro now hands the last
two sung lines to the chat: *"still here / still here"* — byte-identical, from two different people, addressed
to nobody, after the host has gone.**
⛔ **This is not a community forming**, and the difference matters: they are not speaking to each other, they are
independently marking presence into a void, which is the same minimum act as the stranger in V1 who types the
name of a country and a bird emoji. ⭐ **The loneliest object in the room, given the last word.**

### Applied to all four · **the code-scratch signature reaches the lyric field**
LOFN-PRIME's *AI Code-Scratch* pillar — her own logs time-stretched into a vinyl-scratch texture — was living
only in the production prompts. It now appears as an SFX cue inside each lyric field, worded differently in
each: `*code-scratch*` · `*log scratch, one pass*` · `*scratched log texture*` · `*code-scratch, then hum*`.
**It is a texture and never a voice.** No machine speaks, explains, comforts or knows anything in any of these
four songs. ⛔ **D7 holds: Lofn is part of the flood, not the cure for it.**

**What was deliberately NOT enhanced, and why.** The three byte-identical choruses in each song are untouched
and no note is filed about them — exact chorus repetition is correct craft and requires no defence. The four
titles are unchanged: all four name a thing, and the measured catalogue direction (a title naming a thing takes

## §6 · THE DESCRIBE-RENDER CHECK, RE-RUN ON THE ENHANCED TEXT

**What this literally sounds like out of Suno.** Roughly forty seconds of a slow, wide, wordless female choir
over a warm sustained organ, joined by a scratchy digital grain and one dry muttered voice, then a rigid hat,
then sub, then a hard rolling 174 BPM break. A bright pitched-up female lead delivers short, fast couplets whose
rhymes land in the middle of the line rather than the end, punctuated three times by a large repetitive chorus
with a shouted two-word tag. Near the end one wordless choir line stops abruptly and is finished by a close,
unprocessed, almost-spoken voice; then the biggest chorus lands and the song runs out through a thinning hat.

**Adversarially — the one way this renders generic.** ⭐ **The chant layer locks to the grid.** Suno pulls every
vocal element onto the beat, and a layer instructed to refuse the tempo sits in the same family as the
programmed tempo transformations measured flattened 2 of 2. If it quantises, the two clocks become one, the
ABOVE/BELOW stack disappears, and this renders as *competent hyperpop drum and bass with an organ pad.*

**The repair, already in the shipped prompts and unchanged by this pass.** The chant's independence is stated
twice in every prompt in different words; it is written in long-vowel invented syllables a renderer will stretch
rather than snap; and ⭐ **the hinge is built so it survives even if the chant quantises** — the crossing is a
wide-choir → bone-dry-close-mic swap, and that lands whether or not the chant was ever off-grid. **The
load-bearing effect does not depend on the risky one.**

**Second predicted deviation, declared and not repaired.** The forty-second approach will probably render at
twenty-five to thirty. Near-silent openings survive but the generator compresses. The payment argued at step 10
§0 survives a shortened approach, and per THE GRAIN LAW a generator deviation is raw material, not a failure.
