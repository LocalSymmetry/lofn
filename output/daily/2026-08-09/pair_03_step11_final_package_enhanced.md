⭐ **Each song receives a fifth structural device it did not have at step 10, and in every case the device is
another way of making the fixed lag *visible in the words*, so the intent cannot be smoothed away at render.**

| | the step-11 device | what it does |

| **V1** | ⭐ **COLLAPSING REFRAIN** — in the tag her chant sheds a word each return (*No — you go* → *No — you* → *No*) while the late voice keeps all three words | The lag stops being a *timing* fact and becomes a **length** fact. Two voices singing the same chant at different lengths is a difference a phone speaker cannot hide. |
