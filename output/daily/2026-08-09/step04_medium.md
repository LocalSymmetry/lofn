## ⭐ THE CROSS-CHECK THAT CHANGED SOMETHING

**THE SKATE-PUNK BLENDER (after Feldmann)** ran every finalist against a phone speaker and **failed two of them
as written**: arrangement 15's doubling drift and arrangement 20's arm-fatigue drift were both specified in the
**kick**, where a small transducer cannot resolve them. Both were **moved into the hat and the vocal double**
before this file was written.

**THE DYNAMIC RANGE AUDITOR (after Katz)** then re-read all six against THE GRAIN LAW's measured behaviours and
signed off: **six hinges, six substitutions, zero specified mid-song voids, zero programmed tempo
transformations, zero songs depending on stereo width.** *"Those are the three classes the audit measured
being smoothed. None of them are load-bearing here. Proceed."*

**Tempo spread:** 78 · 100 · 140(half) · 155 · 140 · 174 — six distinct tempo worlds.
**Register spread:** low chest / plain belted / soft-high / chanted-snarl / squeaked-chopped / pitched-and-flat.
**No two pairs share a key region, a tempo band, or a vocal register.**
