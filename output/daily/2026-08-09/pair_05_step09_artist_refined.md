## 1. ⭐ THE ONE THAT MATTERED — A D5 BREACH THE SCANNER DID NOT SEE

Step 08's self-check reported *"future-tense markers: none"* for all four songs. **It was wrong, and the reason
is instructive.** The scan pattern was `\bwill\b` / `\bgonna\b` / `\bgoing to\b`. V3, verse 1, line 7 read:

> *"It'll get there late and it'll get there fine."*

`it'll` is `it will`. **A word-boundary scan for `will` cannot see a contraction**, so a genuine breach of the
run's hardest ban passed a clean self-check twice.

**Both were fixed, in that order:**
1. **The scanner** — widened to catch `'ll`, `about to`, `any minute`, `later`, `someday`, `one day`,
   `tomorrow`. *(`DISPATCH_PACKET` §8: when a scanner hits a line, fix the scanner, not the line. The corollary
   this pair met is the mirror of it — **when a scanner MISSES a line, the line is still wrong.** A scanner
   that returns "none" is evidence about the scanner before it is evidence about the song.)*
2. **The line** — rewritten to present tense: *"It gets there late and it gets there fine."* Same scansion,
   same meaning, no narrator who knows how anything turns out. The re-scan over all four songs, with the wider
   pattern, returns **zero**.

⚠️ **Note for QA:** the widened pattern is in `_work/pair_05/selfcheck.py` and its result is quoted in step 10.
Every other pair in this run is running a `\bwill\b`-class scan unless it widened one too.
