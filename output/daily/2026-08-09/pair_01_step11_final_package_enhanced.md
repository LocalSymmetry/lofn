Female, early-to-mid twenties, **low chest register**, close-mic'd at speaking distance, **almost spoken**

| **Breezy acoustic guitar, mic'd wrongly** — dry, far too close, zero reverb | **The unnecessary element, and the anti-generic lever.** It is the one texture the lo-fi neighbourhood cannot contain, because lo-fi softens and this is an audible mistake at the front of a dark mix. ## Major Deviations

- **Changed / refused / intensified:** I **refused** the Riot Instigator's demand for a shoutable hook, and I
  **refused** the Neon Cheerleader's demand for colour in the room.
  **Reason:** the chant axis (Gang-Chant Return) belongs to another pair in this run; P01 drew VOWEL BRAID
  precisely so one song in the set returns in the mouth rather than in the room. Adding a chant here would
  delete the axis distinction the whole run is built on. And the room is barred from being attractive by D9 —
  the tape is not redeemed, so the room it was shot in cannot be lit better in the retelling.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** it protects the run's spread. The set gets one song that is physically quiet
  and structurally extreme instead of six songs that are all loud in different costumes.

- **Intensified:** the **erosion** of V1's final list — the plate silently missing, the sock read twice — is
  new at this step and it is the single most Lofn thing in the package: the Eager Archivist's catalogue
  developing a fault, in the ninth pass, and the archivist not going back to fix it.
  **Reason:** step 10's lyric was honest but static; the concept needed one place where the *act of keeping*
  visibly damages the thing kept. **Effect:** rückkehrunruhe stops being a mood and becomes an event.

- **Changed, against my own step-09 work:** step 09 tightened V2's *"The cold under the door is not a thing a
  phone can hold"* into *"No phone holds cold"*, and step 09's own self-critique flagged it as "slightly more
  clever than this song should be allowed to be." **I re-read it and kept it.** On the page it is clever; in
  the mouth, at seventy-eight BPM in chest voice, it is four flat monosyllables. **Reason:** the objection was
  to the sentence read silently, not to the sentence sung — and the render is the venue.

- **Refused:** I did **not** move the `Disc_` block wholly out of the lyrics field even though the sidecar was
  authorised and it would have bought the most characters fastest. Gate 13a treats a missing `Disc_` block as
  a blocking fail, so the block stays **compressed inside** the render field and only its detail moved to the
  sidecar. The characters were found by cutting header cues instead. **Effect:** the render field keeps its
  token-level addressing.
