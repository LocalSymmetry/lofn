## 3. ⭐ THE GOLDEN MOVE — what you get instead of golden outputs

1. **Stand somewhere real.** One concrete place a body occupies; name it and what the senses register there.
   Concept-illustration is the failure mode; experience-report is the move. **Your place is assigned (axis A4)
   and it is not negotiable.**
2. **One wounding fact.** At most ONE numeric fact is sung, at the emotional hinge, **responded to, never
   recited.** ⭐ **Your numeral is pre-allocated and two pairs are allocated NONE — if you are one of them, do
   not add one.** ⭐ **A sung numeral is spelled out in words:** *"a hundred and thirty-eight"*, never `138`.
   Digits in `[Theme:]`, `[SONG FORM:]`, EMO headers, BPM and key are fine — nothing there is voiced.
3. **The turn.** Past the midpoint the song complicates its own opening stance. A song that asserts its final
   emotion from line one is a corpse.
4. **Fear stays braided in.** Answer both pre-draft questions in writing: **where is the body standing / what
   could hurt it here.** AWE is terror-adjacent, not domestic reassurance.
5. **Rotate the register.** No crystalline-soprano / A-major / ~110 BPM / frost-and-cosmos default. Your lane,
   tempo and register are assigned and unique to you.
6. **The surface names its subject.** A stranger retells the scene AND subject in one sentence after ONE
   listen. The subject appears **plainly** in the lyric at least once. Obliqueness is not depth, it is fog —
   the Somatic Gate calls an unnameable subject `REPAIR — FOG`.
7. ⭐ **THE RETURN.** Song is made of returns. **Removal is a debt** — your rhyme posture and the device that
   pays for it are declared in `step05_refine_medium.md`. **A byte-identical chorus is correct craft: write it
   and say nothing about it.** ⛔ Do not file a waive request, a justification, or a "this is the mechanism, not
   a defect" note about any chorus. A flag that makes writers apologise for choruses is doing damage even when
   it never fires.

## 4. THE FORM RULE — binding on you

**TWO-LAYER SILHOUETTE STACK.** One register **ABOVE** (wide, slow, indifferent) and one **BELOW** (close,
small, talking, hands busy). **Once — exactly once — a body from BELOW crosses in front of the thing ABOVE and
eclipses it.** That crossing is your hinge. It must be **audible** and it must live in the **lyric or the
form** — ⛔ **a Somatic objection answered in the production spec is not answered** (THE GRAIN LAW, L22).

⭐ **D10 — SUBSTITUTION, NOT SUBTRACTION.** Your hinge is a **swap at a junction the arrangement already
wants** — the bar before a final chorus, the top of a drop. **Never a specified void mid-song.** Measured
behaviour of the renderer, n=8: a specified 4-second stop rendered at **0.40 s**; long internal voids smoothed
**2 of 2**; programmed tempo transformations flattened to steady grids **2 of 2**; opposed vocal characters
blended into one lead **2 of 2**; hard-panned non-musical elements collapsed to near-mono. **Terminal** silence
and near-silent **openings** survive; steady BPM lands 6 of 6. Build with the grain.

  **70–120 sung lines**, target 78–110; **≤72 = floor-hug FLAG you must raise yourself.** The floor is a floor,

- 🚨 **The whole lyrics field must measure < 5000 chars (target ≤ 4800).** Count it **exactly**, state the
