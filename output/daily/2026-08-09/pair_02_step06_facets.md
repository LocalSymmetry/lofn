## 1. THE ARRIVAL, INVERTED — what the shape has to do

Every beat of the arrival song is present and **honoured, not parodied**: the leaving, the road, the gate, the field, the count, the three choruses, the gang. **The thing does not show up.** The inversion is *structural*; the delivery carries **zero irony**. The prayer hook is addressed TO the sky and the sky is **a closed lid** — an obstruction, an antagonist made of water. ⛔ **Not tourism.** She travelled a long way and arrived somewhere **ordinary**: a farm gate, a paid field, a burger van, mud. No place is named that a passport would recognise.

⭐ **The live engine (Flair #5, IT MIGHT BE CLOUDY):** the weather variable is **held open past the point of comfort and never resolved.** No song in this set states that it cleared, and no song states that it did not. The cloud is present tense in the last bar of all four.
