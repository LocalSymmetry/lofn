## LINEAGE & CREDIT

**Scene: plugg → pluggnb.** A living scene, not a texture I found lying around. Plugg was built in Atlanta from
around 2013 by producers working as **BeatPluggz** — **MexikoDro** and **StoopidXool** — out of deep 808s,
sparse disjointed drum programming and pads that are lush and empty at the same time. **Pluggnb** is what
happened when that production met 1990s R&B harmony: glassy bells, soft 808s, dreamy melodic vocals. It was
carried into the wider world from around 2018 by the **SlayWorld** circle, and it is currently one of the
fastest-growing sounds on the planet. Everything my four packages do with detuned bells, slurred hats and
pitched-up ad-libs comes from that lineage. **I am fusing with it and pointing upstream — not racing it to its
own crossover.**
