## THE ESSENCE — one sentence

> **A person doing something ordinary with their hands in the minutes before a thing they get exactly one
> attempt at.**

That is the whole request, stripped. Everything else in this run is *how*.

## THE ESSENCE — expanded to three claims, no more

1. **There is an event with a fixed, short, non-negotiable duration and no second take.** (Source 1)
2. **The shape of the listener's attention was set by an accounting rule, not by anyone's taste.** (Source 1)
3. **There are always two registers: the enormous indifferent thing, and the small body underneath doing
   something with its hands — and the small body eclipses the enormous one exactly as truly.** (Source 3)

⛔ **Not in the essence, and must not sneak in:** any claim that this is a generational condition (**D3/D6**);
any claim that the fix is less phone (**D2**); any claim that anyone learns anything (**D5**).
