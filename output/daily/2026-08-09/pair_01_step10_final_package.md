## ⭐ THE DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK — *one inline pass, adversarial*

**What this would literally sound like out of Suno, not what it is meant to be:** a slow, dusty, dark-toned
loop at 78–82 with a very close female voice speaking-singing short flat lines over it for three minutes;
almost no dynamic movement; a small unresolved keyboard chord going round; a dry acoustic guitar sitting
oddly forward; a short doubled-vocal moment near the end; a hard stop. **It would be quiet, monotonous by
design, and hooky only in the mouth.**

**Name the one way this renders generic:** ⚠️ **it renders as "sad lo-fi girl over a beat."** That is the
single most produced object on the internet, and every ingredient I have specified — close female vocal, slow
dusty break, unresolved chord, sub, flat delivery — is one of its ingredients. Text review cannot see this
because on the page the catalogue is strange and specific; in the render the *arrangement* is the most
generic thing in the run.

**Self-repair, applied ONCE, inside the max-3 budget — three concrete changes to the prompts:**
1. **The wrong-acoustic-guitar is now load-bearing, not decorative.** All four prompts state it as *mic'd /
   recorded **wrongly on purpose**, dry, far too close, zero reverb* — an audible mistake sitting in the
   front of a mix that has no place for it. Lo-fi never does this; lo-fi makes everything soft. **A dry
   too-close acoustic in a dark slow mix is the one texture that cannot be mistaken for study beats.**
2. **The exclude field now bans the exact neighbours**, not just generic clichés: V2 excludes
   `vinyl-crackle cliche, lo-fi study-beats branding`, and all four exclude `big reverb wash`, `warm strings`
   and `nostalgia glow`. The lo-fi lane is warm and blurry; this one has to be dry and hard-edged.
3. **The `hat/shaker drags later each pass` is stated in every prompt as a moving target**, so the groove
   degrades measurably instead of looping placidly. A tiring hat is the opposite of the lo-fi grid.

**Residual risk after repair, stated honestly:** V3 is the one most likely to render flat, because its lyric
is byte-identical three times and the generator has been measured blending opposed characters into one lead.
If any of the four comes back sounding like wallpaper, **V3 is the first suspect and the arrangement, not the
lyric, is the repair.**
