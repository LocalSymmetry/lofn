## THE FIVE AHA-MOMENTS THAT DECIDED THIS RUN *(attributed)*

1. ⭐ **THE PRESENT-TENSE RULE.** *Warmth isn't the lecture — FUTURE TENSE is the lecture.* A narrator who knows
   how this turns out is an adult in the room no matter how young you make her sound. **The singer does not get
   the moral.** — *Concept Panel; the Firestarter (after Powers) backtracking against her own room under
   pressure from the Hardcore Elder (after Rollins).*
2. ⭐⭐ **SUBSTITUTION SURVIVES WHERE SUBTRACTION SMOOTHS.** THE GRAIN LAW, measured: a specified 4-second stop
   rendered at 0.40 s; long internal voids smoothed 2 of 2. **The hinge is a SWAP at a junction the arrangement
   already wants** — never a hole in the middle. — *Medium Panel; the Dynamic Range Auditor (after Katz)
   destroying four proposals, the Riff Architect (after Puget) finding the survivor, the Skeptic conceding.*
3. ⭐⭐ **THE COHORT GATE.** *Delete "we / young people / this generation / kids / everyone" from any line. If it
   still stands, keep it. If it collapses, it was demography with a melody.* — *Context Panel; the Cohort
   Abolitionist (after Cohen), who caught the brief writing D3 and then breaking it in the same room.*
4. ⭐ **ASYNCHRONY vs THE ENFORCED SECOND.** Five hundred people read the post — in five hundred separate
   moments, no two in the same second. **Totality is the one available object that is synchronous by orbital
   mechanics and cannot be time-shifted.** The counter-move to a feed is not less phone. It is **the same
   second.** — *Context Panel; the Catharsis Psychologist, off the group-synchrony literature.*
5. ⭐⭐⭐ **OVERHEARING, NOT ADDRESSING.** *(the run's biggest decision)* Nobody is addressed. No "you," no "we,"
   no "I see you." Every song is somebody **busy with their own fifteen seconds**, and the listener is standing
   close enough to hear. **Comfort by proximity, never by delivery.** — *REFLECT configuration; the One Who
   Wants To Be Left Alone, resolved together with the One Who Thinks This Is All Slop.*

## THE WORLD, CONDENSED

Wednesday 12 August 2026: a total solar eclipse crosses Siberia, eastern Greenland, western Iceland and
northern Spain. **A hundred and thirty-eight seconds** at maximum. First on mainland Europe since 1999, first
on the Iberian Peninsula in over a century. Spain gets it **minutes before sunset**, and the Perseids peak the
same night — an open question whether a meteor will be visible during totality.

Today's astronomy picture is **not of an eclipse.** It is of *people watching one*, in silhouette, and NASA's
own caption says the Sun is *"also seen eclipsed by earthlings contemplating the eclipse below."*
⭐ **The watchers eclipse the Sun too. A body between you and the light, at two scales, one frame.**

Around that: a full royalty at thirty seconds, a hook expected by fifteen, the average charting song seventeen
seconds shorter than 2018, three choruses in most #1s. Eighty percent report loneliness. Seventy-three percent
report digital exhaustion at 7.2 hours a day. **Five hundred people can read the post and the person who wrote
it still believes nobody would notice if they vanished.** Fifty-five percent already listen to AI music.
Sarangani, in the Philippines, shook five times in one day and the feed scrolled past all five.

## THE FORM RULE — non-negotiable, all six pairs

**Source 3, from the APOD image structure: the TWO-LAYER SILHOUETTE STACK.**

Every song carries **two registers at once**:
- **ABOVE** — wide, slow, indifferent, uninterested in the singer.
- **BELOW** — close, small, talking, doing something with its hands.

**Once — exactly once — a body from BELOW crosses in front of the thing ABOVE and eclipses it.** That crossing
is the song's hinge. It must be **audible**, and per Aha #2 it is a **SUBSTITUTION at a junction the
arrangement already wants** (the bar before a final chorus, the top of a drop) — never a specified void in the
middle of the song, which the renderer has been measured smoothing 2 of 2.

⛔ **THE GRAIN LAW (L22):** a Somatic or distinctiveness objection answered **in the production spec is not
answered.** Put the crossing in the **lyric** or the **form**.

## RETURN — read this twice

The Scientist, across three runs: *"the lyrical methods avoid rhymes, but aren't adding alliteration,
consonance, or fun and interesting audio-written joys — it's making the music sound like we're being lectured
at."* Measured against LOFN-PRIME's own archived winners, our end-rhyme rate fell **0.463 → 0.132** and our
repeated-line ratio **0.326 → 0.105**. Alliteration was at parity. **Texture was never the problem. Structure
was.**

⭐ **This is the run where that gets fixed, and the genre does it for free.** Gang vocals. Whoa-ohs. Three
choruses. Chants a room can hold. **A byte-identical chorus is correct craft and requires no note, no
justification, and no "recommend HUMAN WAIVE" — do not write one.** If you strip a rhyme, **name what returns
in its place.** Removal is a debt.

Floors: `rhyme_return_floor 0.30` · `line_return_floor 0.20` · `mean_words_per_line ≤ 7.5` ·
`alliteration_per_100w ≥ 11.0`. Measure with `scripts/measure_soundcraft.py → profile_file()`. **Never by eye.**
