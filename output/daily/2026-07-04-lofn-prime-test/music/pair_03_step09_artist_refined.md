## 4. Complete Step Output

### Pair-level objections resolved first (apply to all four)

**Grief-Ethicist / Concept-Skeptic — "You are wearing a real heatwave and a real quake as a costume. Name one thing that stops this being a real victim."**
Resolution: the ABSTRACTION is structural, not cosmetic. (1) Every song is voiced by a *witness*, never the dead — so no death needs to be specific. (2) No place-name, no date, no identifiable event is ever sung; V4 in particular strips magnitude/location entirely (facet 12). (3) The one numeric fact that touches real reportage (the heatwave excess-dead count, V1) is sung ONCE and immediately *refused* — the lyric contradicts the statistic with a single warmth. The news sets the emotional weather; it never supplies a body. Verdict: passes, and the refusal-of-the-number is now the hinge of the whole pair.

**Medium-Skeptic — "Six Lofn-Prime pairs. Prove this one isn't the archive's HyperRaaga→bit-swell→Baile-Phonk reflex with a whistle sticker on it."**
Resolution: Pair 03 uses NO Baile Phonk, NO HyperRaaga, NO 90→140 collapse, NO quantum bit-depth swell as a chorus-trigger. The spine is piobaireachd (slow ceòl-mór lament) + post-punk negative space, through-composed, *ending quiet*. The drill lineage is present only as a sub-808 ghost in the silences. The two signatures per song are named and described to production detail (the tin-whistle's taorluath grace-flicks, the ledger-rustle percussion, the tap-drip ostinato, the skin-close single snap). Verdict: passes; this is the anti-template pair by construction.

**Duality-Warden / Context-Skeptic (inverted) — "A lament's failure mode is the opposite of an INDIGNATION piece's: not too hard, but too soft — a bereavement card. Where's the clean fear / the edge?"**
Resolution: restraint is not softness. The edge is the *withheld* belt (facet 7), the scored silence, and the single rupture (one inhale, one drawer, one caught breath, one dry snap). The tenderness has teeth because it refuses catharsis: no key-change lift, no choir, no loud release. The clean fear is the ordinary-interrupted detail — a knife left in the bread, a tap no one turned off, a second cup going flat — a body stood in a real place with one clean dread let in (metaprompt). Verdict: passes; guard against any line that tips into consolation-as-slogan (flagged per-variant below).

### VARIANT 1 — "The Kettle Still Warm"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Concept-Skeptic):** "The sung number — 'two thousand and twenty-five' — is you reciting the news. That's a weather-report-in-meter fail, and it dates the song to a headline."
**Resolution:** the number is not recited, it is *staged and then refused*. It arrives ("They counted two thousand and twenty-five"), is broken by a single inhale (the only rupture), and the very next lines reject the frame: "I won't hold a number. I'll hold the warmth. / You were not a figure in a heatwave sum. / You were a kettle still warm." Per the one-fact rule this is exactly the intended use — one number, at the hinge, *answered*, not listed. No other statistic appears. Refinement made: removed a second near-numeric ("all day") ambiguity and ensured the number is the sole digit-bearing content; the reply now converts statistic→person in three lines.

**Secondary objection (Context-Skeptic, softness):** "'That is a kind of love. Let it be enough' risks being a fridge magnet."
**Resolution:** kept, but *earned* — it lands only after the witness has done unglamorous physical acts (rinsing the cup, straightening the cloth) and explicitly disclaimed status ("I am not family... I am the one who happened to be near"). The comfort is self-addressed and hard-won, not a slogan aimed at the listener. Sharpened by adding the "borrowed pan" detail so the witness's ordinariness is concrete before the blessing.

**Identity sharpening:** V1 is the *warm* one — the human-kindness angle. Its distinctness is the blessing-arc (testimony → self-blessing → carrying the warmth out the door). The refrain "still warm" mutates: kettle → person → what the witness carries home.

### VARIANT 2 — "Coroner's Plainsong"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Medium-Skeptic):** "'Plainsong over a drone' is a mood, not a signature. And a coroner singing is a gimmick that could go mawkish fast."
**Resolution:** the signatures are now production-specific: *Certificate Plainsong* = one close-mic unmetered vocal line, each phrase ending on an unresolved interval, over a drone-thin tin-whistle Highland figure; *Ledger-Rustle Percussion* = the ONLY kit is pen-scratch, paper, a sliding drawer, ASMR-close and arrhythmic, with the silences scored. The gimmick risk is answered by the FORM doing the emotional work: the litany of official verbs (I measure / I record / I file / I close) that *sheds a verb per line* until only breath remains — the bureaucracy audibly failing to contain the grief. Not mawkish because it never emotes; it processes, and the processing cracks.

**Secondary objection (Concept-Skeptic):** "This is the most literal 'a number that should be a name' — does it tip into thesis-statement?"
**Resolution:** it states the thesis as *action*, not slogan: "I will write your name. I will not write it small. / Not one of many. One. Just one. Just you." The clerk refuses their own instrument. The abstraction holds — no real record, no real name, the certificate is a device. Sharpened by adding the shed-verb "Litany D" ("I do not measure. I remember. / I do not file. I keep.") so the refusal is enacted grammatically before it's stated.

**Identity sharpening:** V2 is the *procedural* one — grief seen from the desk that turns bodies into lines. Its distinct skeleton is the verb-litany that loses verbs. The refrain "I write" mutates into "I write your name" into "I won't write it down as one of many."

### VARIANT 3 — "The Nine-Forty to Nowhere"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Context-Skeptic):** "This is just V1 with a bus stop instead of a kitchen — same witness, same cooling-drink object, same blessing. Two variants collapsing to one song with nouns swapped."
**Resolution:** measured and refuted. Within-pair lyric SequenceMatcher V1↔V3 = 0.042 (far under the 0.42 ceiling). The skeletons differ in kind: V1 is *retrospective* (the witness arrives after; testimony→blessing) while V3 is *real-time and non-resolving* (the witness does not yet know; the dread accumulates bus by bus as the arrangement strips layer by layer). V3's engine is the timetable-countdown refrain ("the nine-forty / the next one / the last one / no one"), which is a coming-to-know, not a blessing. The object differs too: V1's kettle is *found* cold; V3's second seat and flat cup are *held open* against knowledge. Refinement: pushed the not-knowing later so the hinge is the moment comprehension arrives, and made each passing bus remove a specific instrument.

**Secondary objection (Medium-Skeptic):** "'nine-forty' — is that a sung numeric fact, breaking the one-fact rule?"
**Resolution:** it's a spelled bus-time *device* (a fictional timetable), not a research statistic, and it is deliberately NOT sung as a number at the hinge (the hinge carries zero digits). To remove all doubt it is rendered in words ("nine-forty") so no digit appears in any sung line; the measured digit-bearing sung-line count is 0. The recurring time is the refrain's identity, functioning like "still warm" in V1 — a mutating motif, not a fact.

**Identity sharpening:** V3 is the *waiting* one — grief as an unresolved present tense at a shelter no one is watching. Distinct skeleton: real-time layer-subtraction keyed to buses. Rubato tin-whistle that drifts off the beat is the sonic signature of a thing that won't arrive.

### VARIANT 4 — "One, and Then Not One"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Concept-Skeptic, hardest):** "You are drawing charge from the real shallow quake. A place-name, a magnitude, or a body count would 'authenticate' it — and the temptation is right there in the brief. If any of that leaks, you've located a real disaster's real dead. This is the worst failure available to the pair."
**Resolution:** V4 is the maximally-abstracted variant by design (facet 12). NO location, NO magnitude, NO date, NO death toll is sung — verified: 0 digit-bearing sung lines, no proper nouns, the EXCLUDE PROMPT explicitly bans "location names." The shallow-quake energy lives ONLY in the sound-frame: one dry, close, un-reverbed snap at the very top that never repeats (the *Skin-Close Snap* — "a snare too close to the skin"), and then negative space. The lyric's only "number" is the breaking count itself ("one, and then not one"), which is the arithmetic-of-grief device, not a statistic. The physics informs the mix, never the facts. Verdict: passes the ethics gate cleanly; the abstraction is what makes it honourable.

**Secondary objection (Context-Skeptic):** "A survivor counting could read as cold / clinical — where's the tenderness that makes it a lament, not a report?"
**Resolution:** the coldness is *named as a defence* and then tenderly explained: "I keep the count because the feeling's too big — / if I let it be a person it's too much." The prayer blesses exactly this: "Bless the ones who count because they can't cry." The counting *is* the grief, deferred. And the refrain humanises as it decays — losing its number ("one, and then not one" → "and then not" → "and then just not") until arithmetic itself fails, which is the emotional climax rendered as subtraction. Refinement: added the table-setting verse ("I set the table anyway. Old hands know how.") so the numbness has hands and a body.

**Identity sharpening:** V4 is the *numb-tender* one and the quietest — grief that survives as arithmetic because feeling is unbearable. Distinct skeleton: breaking-count refrain over a tap-drip ostinato with almost nothing to strip because it starts stripped. It is the set's floor of volume; the whistle is the only thing that ever rises, and it recedes.
