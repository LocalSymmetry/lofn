### VARIANT 1 — "Switchboard for the Fourth" (THE OPERATOR)

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A communal Fourth-of-July anthem staged as a switchboard operator patching a whole town onto one line, built on Piano Bounce (Amapiano log-drum shuffle crossed with Jersey-Club bed-spring snares) that leans toward a drop it is never allowed to reach. Signature style one, the Patch-Cord Hatline: real telephone plug-clicks and dial-tone hum chopped into tight triplet sixteenths, each click a connection lighting up. Signature style two, the Verdict-808 Cowbell Engine: an annoyance-detuned cowbell loop over a geothermal mono-808 that only detonates AFTER the celebration is refused. The kick that should land the party is withdrawn at the last patch; a quantum bit-depth swell collapses the bright crowd to 2-bit for one dead bar, then Baile Phonk floods the vacuum at 140 as furious answer. Female vocal, crystalline breathy yearning shifting to a bratty glitched pop-punk snarl, then a low steady human tone. Genres: amapiano, jersey club, baile phonk, art-pop. No real-artist names, no generic trap hats, no EDM riser.

generic trap hi-hats, standard EDM riser, airhorn, festival big-room drop, male lead vocal, lo-fi tape wash

### VARIANT 4 — "Breaker Box" (THE GRID BROWNS OUT)

Signature style one, the Sixty-Cycle Brownout Pad: a mains electrical hum and fluorescent buzz swelled and dimmed like a straining transformer, pitch-guttering as load climbs. Signature style two, the Verdict-808 Cowbell Engine: a detuned cowbell over a geothermal mono-808 running on the emergency circuit after the main breaker trips. The downbeat browns out to 2-bit and gutters where the party should ignite; a quantum bit-depth swell marks the grid's collapse, then Baile Phonk at 140 answers on what power is left. Female vocal from hushed awe to a bratty glitched snarl to a steady human tone. Genres: amapiano, jersey club, baile phonk, industrial-tinged art-pop. No real-artist names, no generic trap hats, no EDM riser.
