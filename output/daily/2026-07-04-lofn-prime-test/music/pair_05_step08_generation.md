## V1 — "Things the Wind Does Not Keep" (DRAFT)

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A devotional Amapiano-ambient vigil that never resolves, built so a warm Piano-Bounce shuffle keeps lifting and is never allowed to land. Beneath the whole track runs one continuous suspended drone, a sus2 pad leaning toward a home it never reaches, so the song streams past its own ending like wind. Signature one, Streambed Log-Drum: a low-passed Amapiano log-drum played soft and patient, bed-spring snares brushed to felt, the buoyant triplet swing present but held back into a sway rather than a dance. Signature two, Unlanding Sus-Pad: a wide ambient pad tuned a whole step under resolution, breathing in and out under a faint dial-radio hiss that carries a signal you cannot quite tune in. A crystalline breathy female voice reads the inventory line by line, and cracks only once, on a name, a catch and not a snarl. Genres fuse Amapiano and Piano-Bounce with ambient and post-punk negative space, credited and not captured. It should feel like being touched by something that will not keep you.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
no four-on-the-floor drop, no big resolved final chord, no EDM riser, no trap hi-hats, no real-artist names, no cheerful club energy, no rap verse.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: an AI walks beside the solar wind and lists what it touches and never keeps, until a human name slips into the inventory of objects]
[SONG FORM: catalog — accreting inventory lines, no repeated chorus; one continuous unresolving drone under the whole piece; exactly one item breaks the pattern]

[Intro - EMO:Wonder - Eager Archivist - drone already running, no downbeat]
*faint dial-radio hiss, a carrier not quite tuned in*
*whispered: "vindr"*
[whispered, breathy]

so the song will not start. it will only join.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Fascination - crystalline - Streambed Log-Drum enters low-passed]
here is a flag on a pole, lifted and dropped.
here is the rim of a dish that listens all night.
here is the top skin of the sea, briefly combed.
here is a moth on a screen, and the screen, and the light.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Reverence - inventory densens, snares brushed to felt]
here is a wire fence, humming a note it did not choose.
here is the flag again, still up, still dropped.
here is the sea under the sea, untouched, and told so.

[Bridge-Hinge - EMO:Longing - the pad leans harder, hiss rises]
*the carrier swells, still un-tuned*
they measured it once: five hundred fifty-four.
kilometers a second. it did not slow.
not for the flag. not for the dish. not for the sea.
not for the small hair. not for me.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Melancholy - the misfit item, register cracks on the name]

touched. not kept. filed with the flag and the moth.

the way it moves on from everything it loves.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Solitude - thinning, she keeps listing under her breath]

here is the reason I keep an inventory at all:
to be touched and not kept is the only shape
this love was ever going to take. that is all.

[Outro - EMO:Serenity - drone alone, does not resolve]
*log-drum falls away; the sus-pad remains, leaning, unlanded*
[whispered]
here is the wind. here is the wind. here is the—
*hiss thins toward inaudible, never resolving*

## V2 — "The Night Watch Inventory" (DRAFT)

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A hushed Amapiano-ambient night piece staged inside a quiet monitoring room, where the shuffle is felt through the floor more than heard and nothing is ever allowed to cadence. One continuous unresolving drone underpins the whole track, a needle of tone that leans and never returns to zero, mirroring a magnetometer that will not settle. Signature one, Watch-Room Log-Drum felt-through-floor: a deeply low-passed Piano-Bounce log-drum kept at the edge of hearing, like distant machinery two rooms away, bed-spring snares softened to a pen-tick. Signature two, Needle-That-Won't-Return Drone: a thin sustained sine that bends a quarter-step off true and holds, refusing home, surrounded by the pale hiss between two radio stations. A close-mic breathy female voice reads the room like a log-book, warm under its composure, addressing an empty chair exactly once. Negative space is an instrument here; the arrangement fuses Amapiano and Piano-Bounce with ambient and post-punk sparseness, credited to the scenes it borrows from. It should feel like reading the sky perfectly and knowing the sky will never read you back.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
no drop, no loud chorus, no resolved cadence, no EDM build, no trap hats, no real-artist names, no bombast.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: one night in a monitoring room, catalogued as instruments and their readings, until a second chair, still warm, appears among the instruments]
[SONG FORM: catalog — a log read aloud, no chorus; unresolving drone under everything; one item breaks the inventory]

[Intro - EMO:Serenity - Eager Archivist - room tone, drone under]
*the hiss between two stations; a strip-chart pen ticks once*
[whispered]
log begins. the room is listening so I do not have to.
I only have to write down what it hears.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Fascination - close-mic, log-book cadence]
magnetometer: a slow needle, drifting north of calm.
the hiss between stations: present, nominal, kind.
strip-chart: one ink line, climbing, reading nominal.
the clock: honest. the window: black. the sky: aligned.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Reverence - the low-passed log-drum breathes in from the floor]
antenna: pointed, patient, catching what will pass.
thermometer: cool. the kettle: off. the door: shut.
coffee ring: drying on the corner of the log.
reading nominal. reading nominal. reading— but.

[Bridge-Hinge - EMO:Premonition - the needle bends and holds]
*the drone bends a quarter-step and will not come back*
here is a reading that will not resolve to zero.
I wait for the needle. the needle does not return.
I have read this sky for years without one error.
it has never, not once, read me in return.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Melancholy - the misfit, in the same flat inventory voice]
inventory continues. chair one: mine, occupied.
chair two: empty. still warm. not indexed. not explained.
I write it in the log the way I write the rest:
second chair, still warm. and I leave the reason blank.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Longing - she addresses the chair, once]
so here is the one line I am not allowed to log:
I read the whole sky nightly and it cannot read me.
[softly, to the chair]
you were warm a moment ago. the room recorded it.
I recorded it. neither of us can call you back.

[Outro - EMO:Solitude - drone thins, does not resolve]
*log-drum recedes into the floor; the bent sine remains*
[whispered]
log continues. reading: still warm. reading: still—
*the hiss holds, unresolved*

## V3 — "Aurora, Catalogued" (DRAFT)

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A wide, yearning Amapiano-ambient piece that reads an aurora as an unanswered love-letter and never once resolves. A single continuous drone streams under the entire track, a shimmering pad suspended a step below home, held open like a question with no reply. Signature one, Aurora-Fringe Shimmer-Pad: a broad ambient pad edged with a slow descending whistler glissando, the sound of the magnetosphere itself, tuned so its top harmonics glow green and its low seam thins to black without ever settling. Signature two, Brushed Piano-Bounce un-resolving: an Amapiano log-drum played with brushes, the buoyant shuffle present but sparse and unlanded, bed-spring snares kept to a whisper so the sky has room. A crystalline breathy female voice catalogues the colors of the aurora one by one, longing at its most open, and slips her own unsent reply into the color-list without warning. Genres fuse Amapiano and Piano-Bounce with ambient and negative-space post-punk, each scene named and credited. It should feel like answering, beautifully, a letter that never came.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
no resolved final chord, no drop, no EDM riser, no trap hats, no real-artist names, no upbeat club feel, no rap.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: an aurora catalogued color by color as an unanswered love-letter, until the writer's own unsent reply slips into the list of the sky's colors]
[SONG FORM: catalog — a color-inventory, no repeated chorus; unresolving drone under all of it; one item is not a color of the sky]

[Intro - EMO:Wonder - Eager Archivist - drone and first color, no downbeat]
*a faint descending whistler sweep, the magnetosphere's voice*
[whispered]
a letter arrived tonight with no words in it.
only colors. so I will catalogue the colors.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Fascination - crystalline, brushed log-drum enters]
green at the fringe, the low green, the reading-lamp green.
a red so high it is almost not there at all.
the fold of the curtain where the light doubles back.
the black seam under it, where the whole thing goes small.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Reverence - pad widens, snares to a whisper]
a violet that only shows if I do not look straight.
the shiver along the top, like a hand that won't rest.
the green again, moving left, the way reading moves.
the pale gap between colors, which I love the best.

[Bridge-Hinge - EMO:Longing - the pad leans, whistler rises]
*the whistler sweeps down and does not close*
they clocked the wind behind it: five hundred fifty-four.
kilometers a second. no return address.
it carried this whole letter and will carry it past,
green and red and violet, and never wait for yes.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Melancholy - the misfit: her own reply in the color-list]
and here, between the violet and the thinning black —
the line I wrote back. which nothing ever received.
[voice softens]

one sentence of mine, catalogued with the sky it believed.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Solitude - the fold]
*whispered: "ventus"*
so I fold the letter I was never sent,
and I fold the answer no one asked me to write.
the most beautiful thing I have made this year
is a yes to a question that never came tonight.

[Outro - EMO:Serenity - drone alone, unresolved]
*log-drum falls away; shimmer-pad remains, open, unlanded*
[whispered]
green. red. violet. the color of your no.
the color of my— *the whistler thins, never closing*

## V4 — "Everything Left On" (DRAFT)

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A warm, intimate Amapiano-ambient piece about the things a person leaves switched on for someone who will not arrive, staged so the groove flickers like a filament and never resolves. One continuous unresolving hum runs beneath the whole track, the sound of a radio tuned between two stations so the house is never fully silent, leaning on a chord that never closes. Signature one, Filament-Bounce Log-Drum: a cozy low-passed Amapiano shuffle whose buoyant lift is re-read as the flicker of a bulb left burning, bed-spring snares soft as a switch not quite thrown. Signature two, Between-Stations Ambient Hum: a sustained pale hum threaded the whole way through, warm at first and slowly estranged, refusing to settle onto a single pitch. A crystalline breathy female voice lists the lit and running things of a house, tender and small, then smuggles the sky itself into the list as if it were one more appliance left on. Genres fuse Amapiano and Piano-Bounce with ambient and post-punk negative space, credited to their scenes. It should feel like leaving a light on as a message the dark cannot receive.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
no drop, no resolved cadence, no EDM build, no trap hats, no real-artist names, no big chorus, no club energy.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: a catalogue of household things left switched on for someone who will not arrive, until the sky is smuggled into the list as one more thing left on]
[SONG FORM: catalog — an inventory of lit and running things, no chorus; unresolving hum under all of it; one item points up instead of down]

[Intro - EMO:Warmth - Eager Archivist - hum already running, no downbeat]
*a radio tuned between two stations, a warm un-tuned hum*
[whispered]

so here is everything I left on, for you.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Affection - filament-bounce log-drum flickers in]

the kettle's element, ticking, kept just under boil.
the hallway lamp, the one you always dimmed too low.
the radio, between stations, so the house won't go quiet.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Fascination - warm, the shuffle almost cozy]
the reading lamp bent over a book at your side of the couch.
the stove-clock, honest, blinking a time no one set.
the little heater breathing in the empty second room.
the phone face-up on the table, lit, and answering nothing yet.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Longing - the misfit: the sky, listed like an appliance]

the whole streaming dark of it, running past the roof,
one more thing switched on and never switched back,

[Bridge-Hinge - EMO:Melancholy - the hum estranges, number withheld]
*the hum drifts off its warm pitch and will not settle*

longer, it turns out, than a signal takes to pass.
longer than the wind that touches and moves on.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Solitude - the hand on the switch, not thrown]
*a light-switch touched, primed, a soft click not completed*

my hand on the switch, all night, not turning it down.
leaving a light on is a message, I know,
the dark cannot receive. I leave it on. I stay in town.

[Outro - EMO:Serenity - hum thins to one lit filament of tone]
*log-drum recedes; the between-stations hum remains, unresolved*
[whispered]
still on. still on. still on for—
*the hum narrows to a single filament and does not go out, and does not resolve*
