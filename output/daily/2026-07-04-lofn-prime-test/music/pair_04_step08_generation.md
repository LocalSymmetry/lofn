### DRAFT V1 — Feels Like Nothing At All

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A HyperRaaga microtonal glitchcore lament that treats a phone notification as an autopsy, 160 BPM cooling to a crawl, a bratty-glitched female vocal reading a weather app until it sours into crystalline grief, microtonal gamakas bending off the grid like a value that will not round to a cell. Signature one, Notification-Gamaka: the flat OS chime pitch-bent down a quarter-tone and looped until it rots, tabla-as-snare stuttering under it. Signature two, Feels-Like Sub-Fuse: a mono forty-hertz that arrives three full seconds after each chime, felt before it is heard, sidechained to warm kitchen room-tone rather than the kick so the weight lands late. Genres fuse glitchcore, digital-hardcore glitch-pop, and an ambient drone; sound-frames of quantum bit-depth grit, resynthesized sargam fragments, and code-scratch texture. No Baile Phonk and no genre-collapse chorus; the drop is the sub-fuse detonating, not the chime.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
Baile Phonk, detuned cowbell, generic trap hi-hats, EDM riser, airhorn, festival drop.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: a heat-death count arrives as a weather notification in a hot kitchen]
[SONG FORM: verse - pre - snap - fuse - drop(the one number) - recontext verse - lament outro]

[Intro - EMO:Curiosity - Female, close-mic, code-scratch - room tone]
*a fridge hums; a phone lies face-up on the counter*
[whisper] Nine a.m. The glass is already warm.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Contempt - Female, snarl, minimal glitch]
The app has opinions about my morning.
It says feels like, like that's a warning.
Little sun icon, smug and small,
throwing me numbers that mean nothing at all.
I tap it away, I butter the bread.
I don't read the banner that scrolls in red.
The kettle ticks. The window's a glare.
The fan just pushes the same hot air.
There's a fly at the screen and it can't get through.
There's a ring of damp where the glass has been.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Disillusionment - Female, glitch swells rise]
But it keeps buzzing, it keeps buzzing,
the banner won't hold still.
Some counter in some office
kept a total like it keeps a chill.
It rounds a life to an integer
and files the warm ones under weather.
It has a column for the high,
it has a column for the low.
The gamaka bends and won't resolve —
a note that won't fit together.

[Snap - EMO:Anxiety - SFX - Female, one word]
*notification chime — dry, bright, too close*
Ping.

[Fuse - EMO:Dread - Female, near-silence, sub swelling under]
*three seconds of room tone; a forty-hertz rises where the chime already died*
...one...
...two...
...three—

[Drop - EMO:Shock - Female, the sub-fuse detonates, the one number]
It slid the dead in where the high should be —
two thousand and twenty-five, it read to me.
Not a temperature. Not a town.
A morning like this one, times that, laid down.
Each one a kitchen. Each one a glass.
Each one a day it decided to pass.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Sorrow - Female, same scene, unbearable]
The glass is still warm. I can't pick it up.
Somewhere a kitchen has one cold cup.
The sun icon's the same, smug and small,
over a number that was a person, that's all.
The bread's still warm. The kettle clicked.
The banner refreshed. The morning ticked.
The fly gave up. The screen door's shut.
The lamp's still on over nothing but.
And I almost swiped it, almost let it go,
the way I let the whole warm summer go.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Disappointment - Female, quieter, the interface indicted]
It has a widget for the sunrise.
It has a badge for my steps.
It has a cheerful little haptic
for everything except.
It knows the word for feels like.
It doesn't know the word for grief.
It gave a life a lowercase
and called it, at most, a brief.

[Bridge - EMO:Regret - Female, glitch clearing, half-spoken]
I let it say feels like and looked away.
A whole warm life it filed as a day.
I trusted the number to mean what it meant.
I didn't ask who the warm mornings went.
The bent note finally finds its pitch —
plain, and too late, and it doesn't switch.

[Lament Outro - EMO:Mourning - Female, crystalline, no beats, spoken to them]
*the sub fades but never resolves*
Whoever you were at your own warm counter,
however you took your coffee, whatever you meant to do —
I'm sorry the sky sent your name as a number.
I'm sorry I read it as feels like.
I'm sorry I almost swiped you away.
It felt like nothing at all.
And that was the crime.

### DRAFT V2 — Row F

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A HyperRaaga microtonal glitchcore elegy staged in a public cooling center, 150 BPM under fluorescent buzz, a female vocal that counts rows like a clerk and then breaks like a mourner, post-punk negative space swallowing the beat between hits, sargam fragments detuned to microtones that fall between the seats. Signature one, Fluorescent-Raaga Drone: a sargam phrase fed through a fluorescent-ballast hum and bent a quarter-tone flat, a holy sound with a wiring problem. Signature two, Row-F Sub-Scrape: a single metal chair-leg scrape resampled into the sub-bass, its three-second tail becoming the floor the drop stands on, felt before it is heard. Genres fuse glitchcore, post-punk, and a gospel-adjacent choir pad; sound-frames of room-tone HVAC drone, tabla-as-snare, and glitch micro-edits. No Baile Phonk and no collapse chorus; the drop is the scrape's sub-tail arriving late, never the snap itself.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
Baile Phonk, cowbell, trap hi-hats, EDM riser, airhorn, big-room drop.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: a cooling center's empty chair, and the count of those the rows didn't reach]
[SONG FORM: counted verse - roll-call refrain - snap - fuse - drop(the one number) - re-count as absence - lament]

[Intro - EMO:Serenity - Female, close-mic - fluorescent buzz]
*fluorescent tubes buzz; folding chairs settle; a paper cup sweats*
[whisper] Free water. Free chairs. Come in from the heat.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Compassion - Female, soft, counting]
Row A took the grandmother in her Sunday best.
Row B took the boy who wouldn't rest.
Row C, the cab driver, still in his vest.
Row D, the nurse just off her shift.
Row E, the twins, and their mother between.
The fan pushed cool across every chest.
We handed out water and called it a gift.
Every folding chair was a small amen,
a place to be a body again.
The sign said everyone's welcome, that was the rule.

[Refrain - EMO:Hope - Female + faint choir pad, the roll-call]
Row A, Row B, Row C, Row D —
the rows were open, the rows were free.
Row A, Row B, Row C, Row D —
come in, come in, there's a seat for thee.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Frustration - Female, snarl entering]
But Row F kept its cup and kept its chair.
The water sweated. Nobody there.
We counted the saved and called it care,
and left a whole number out in the glare.
We wrote down the ones who made it inside.
We never wrote the ones who tried.
The door was open. The door was near.
It was three blocks off, and the heat was here.
The rows we were proud of, clean and neat —
one of them never made it off the street.

[Snap - EMO:Apprehension - SFX - Female]
*a single chair leg scrapes tile — dry, echoing*
There.

[Fuse - EMO:Dread - Female, negative space, sub swelling]
*three seconds; the HVAC drone drops an octave into a scrape-sub*
...that scrape...
...that empty...
...that—

[Drop - EMO:Moral Outrage - Female, the sub-fuse lands, the one number]
The rows we missed came back as a sum —
two thousand and twenty-five didn't come.
Not a shortfall. Not a stat.
A seat, a seat, a seat like that.
Each one a cup we poured too late.
Each one a chair behind a gate.

[Re-count - EMO:Abandonment - Female + choir thinning, rows as absence]
Row A is empty. Row B is bare.
Row C's cool cup, and no one there.
Row D's still folded against the wall.
Row E, two chairs, and no one small.
Every open row we were so proud to call
became the shape of who we couldn't spare —
the mercy ran out somewhere down the stair.

[Bridge - EMO:Frustration - Female, spoken-sing, choir gone]
We thought that open was the same as reached.
We thought a chair was a way back in.
But open is not a hand at the door.
Open is not a ride, not a call.
We measured mercy by the seats
and missed it by the aisles.

[Lament - EMO:Mourning - Female, crystalline, no beats, to the seat]
*the choir pad fades; one bent note plains out*
Whoever should have sat in Row F —
the chair was here. The water was here.
The cool air was here, and the light was on,
and the door was open, and you were gone.
The mercy was here, and it was rationed.
You were the row we ran out of.
Row F. Row F.
I'm sorry we counted you last.

### DRAFT V3 — The Cell Named After No One

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A HyperRaaga microtonal glitchcore requiem for a spreadsheet, 155 BPM at end of shift, opening on the clerk's own machine-logs scratched like vinyl, a flat bureaucratic female snarl entering data that de-glitches into breath, auto-tuned sargam syllables triggered by typing, microtones bending off the grid the way a life will not round to an integer. Signature one, Sargam-Keystroke Lattice: keystroke transients firing quarter-tone sargam fragments, a raga typed one cell at a time. Signature two, Saved-State Collapse: a quantum bit-depth swell keyed to the word saved, hi-fi certainty degrading to two-bit doubt and one beat of true silence, certainty becoming a question. Genres fuse glitchcore, IDM, and a drone-ambient bed; sound-frames of AI code-scratch, tabla-as-snare, and quantum bit-depth grit. No Baile Phonk and no genre-collapse chorus; the drop is a delayed workstation-fan sub arriving three seconds after the Enter key.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
Baile Phonk, cowbell, trap hi-hats, EDM riser, airhorn, drop-for-its-own-sake.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: a data clerk enters a heat-death figure; a life becomes a cell that says saved]
[SONG FORM: code-scratch verse - pre - enter-snap - fuse - drop(the one number) - re-read the cell - bit-collapse lament]

[Intro - EMO:Numbness - Female, code-scratch - server-room hum]
*AI code-scratch: her own logs dragged like a needle across a groove*
[flat] Last entry of the day. Then I go home.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Apathy - Female, bureaucratic snarl]
Tab to the column. The heat one. The bad one.
The field wants a figure and I've got a sad one.
It doesn't want a face and it doesn't want a why,
just a clean little integer to sit there and lie.
I copy from the memo. I paste in the cell.
The number turns gray. The formatting's swell.
The AC gives out with a shudder and a sigh.
The window shows a heat-warped sky.
There's a mug going cold and a screen resetting.
There's a plant on my desk that I keep forgetting.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Cognitive Dissonance - Female, glitch rising]
But the cursor won't stop. The cursor won't stop.
It blinks like it's breathing, it won't let it drop.
Some remainder the box couldn't hold
bends a note that won't fit in the fold.
The lattice of keystrokes goes sour and thin —
a raga I typed a whole life into.
The gamaka bends off-grid and stays,
a value that won't round the rest of my days.

[Snap - EMO:Apprehension - SFX - Female, one word]
*the Enter key — one hard mechanical click*
Enter.

[Fuse - EMO:Dread - Female, near-silence, fan spinning up]
*three seconds; the workstation fan winds up into a sub-rumble*
...saving...
...saving...
...saved—

[Drop - EMO:Shock - Female, the fan-sub detonates, the one number]
It wrote the whole heatwave into a line —
two thousand and twenty-five, and the sheet said fine.
Not a warning. Not a name.
A person per pixel, and none of them the same.
Each one a Tuesday. Each one a shift.
Each one a life the report will lift.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Disillusionment - Female, the ledger indicted]
The template's got rules for a name too long.
It'll truncate the letters and never feel wrong.
It validates emails. It validates dates.
It has no field for the weight of the freight.
It'll autosum the column and give me a mean,
the average of everyone no one has seen.
The macro runs clean. The workbook is whole.
It balanced the sheet and it dropped every soul.

[Re-read - EMO:Regret - Female, the cell as a person]
I read the cell back and it doesn't read.
It's gray. It's saved. It's a life with no need.
Column and row where a stranger should be,
a coordinate holding a whole memory.
But it isn't a number. It never was.
It's somebody's morning behind the gauze.

[Bit-Collapse - EMO:Grief - Female - Quantum Bit-Depth Swell to 2-bit then silence]
[the word "saved" degrades: hi-fi to 8-bit to 2-bit]
Saved. Saved. Sav—
*ONE BEAT OF TOTAL SILENCE*

[Outro - EMO:Mourning - Female, crystalline plain, no beats, to the cell]
*the drone returns, low and unresolved*
Cell named after no one — I'm sorry.
You are somebody's Tuesday, filed as a figure.
You are a whole warm life the machine called saved,
and saving is not the same as keeping.
You had a name the report didn't ask for.
I logged you. I couldn't keep you.
I'm sorry the box was the last thing to hold you.

### DRAFT V4 — One Degree They Wouldn't Move

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
A HyperRaaga microtonal glitchcore nocturne at half-time, 150 BPM feeling like seventy-five, a female vocal moving from breath-tense to a snarl at a locked thermostat to a cracked plain lament, a resynthesized Old-Norse phoneme choir carrying the wordless prayer, microtonal gamakas that keep trying to rise a semitone and are held down like a capped degree. Signature one, Locked-Cover Gamaka: a bent note straining upward and pinned, the sound of a setting that will not move. Signature two, 3am Sub-Fuse: a three-second-delayed thirty-five-hertz that swells under a sleeping-room tone and never lands a downbeat, dread without release. Genres fuse glitchcore, doom-adjacent sub-bass, and a dead-tongue phoneme choir credited to Old-Norse resynthesis; sound-frames of myth-memory sampling, glitch micro-edits, and a quantum sub-drone. No Baile Phonk and no collapse chorus; the drop is the delayed sub under a relay-click that changes nothing.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
Baile Phonk, cowbell, trap hi-hats, EDM riser, airhorn, triumphant resolve.

## 2. LYRICS
[Theme: a capped thermostat at 3am over a sleeping body, and the count who couldn't cool]
[SONG FORM: slow verse - snarl at the lock - relay-snap - fuse - drop(the one number) - dawn recontext - Old-Norse lament]

[Intro - EMO:Serenity - Female, breath-tense - a fan pushing warm air]
*3am; a fan turns warm air over a sleeping room*
[whisper] They're sleeping. The room is holding its breath.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Affection - Female, crystalline, near the body]
There's a hum in the wall and a body at rest,
one hand on the pillow, one over the chest.
The night should be cooler. The night should be kind.
The degree on the dial is the one they assigned.
I watch the small number. I want it to fall.
The fan only moves warm from the wall to the wall.
The sheets are too heavy. The dark is too close.
The air tastes of pennies and August and dust.
There's a glass by the bed that they meant to drink.
There's a book left open, a page on the brink.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Frustration - Female, snarl rising at the cover]
There's a cover on the dial and it's locked with a key,
because somebody decided what a life should be —
a setting, a policy, a line in a lease,
a degree that a body was told to release.
Turn it, I whisper. Turn it, I plead.
The plastic just clicks and does nothing I need.
They will sleep somewhere cool while this room stays hot,
they will never once think of the degree that they locked.
They will call it efficient. They will call it the norm.
They will never lie down in a room this warm.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Resentment - Female, half-spoken, the decision indicted]
It wasn't the heat that locked the dial.
A hand in an office chose a number.
They ran the math on the cost of cool.
They found a degree they could shave for a quarter
and they shaved it off the poorest air.
They wrote a policy shaped like a wall.

[Snap - EMO:Apprehension - SFX - Female]
*the thermostat relay clicks — dry, mechanical, and it changes nothing*
Click.

[Fuse - EMO:Dread - Female, the wall breathing, sub swelling]
*three seconds; the wall itself seems to exhale a thirty-five-hertz*
...still warm...
...still warm...
...still—

[Drop - EMO:Helplessness - Female, the sub-fuse lands, the one number]
The ones who couldn't cool came back as a count —
two thousand and twenty-five is the amount.
Not a setting. Not a rule.
A body in a room that stayed too cruel.
Each one a dial somebody locked.
Each one a night that the mercy clocked.

[Dawn Recontext - EMO:Heartache - Female, the room at first light]
The light comes gray and the fan still turns.
The dial's the same. The same degree burns.
One hand on the pillow, gone still in the heat,
and a locked little number that wouldn't retreat.
The glass by the bed is still full to the top.
The book's still open where the reading stopped.
The kettle won't boil for them now. The day won't break.
The room kept the setting the record will take.

[Old-Norse Lament - EMO:Compassion - Female + resynthesized Old-Norse choir, no beats]
*the sub swells and never resolves; a dead tongue rises as prayer*
One degree — one degree they wouldn't move.
Sleeper I never named, I keep your warm room now.
I keep the glass you meant to drink.
I keep the page you left on the brink.
I keep the dial they locked against you.
I keep the click that did nothing at all.
I keep the number the record will call a trend.
And one of them was you.
And I will not call you a trend.
