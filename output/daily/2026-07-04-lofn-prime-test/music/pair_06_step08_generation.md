### V1 (draft) — *Skald in My Mouth* — letter to the dead poet

**## 1. MUSIC PROMPT**
A glacial 72 BPM art-song built as a villanelle, two refrains circling a resurrected skaldic voice, luminous and grief-bright, never a chorus. Genres braid ambient chamber-choral, spectral folk-noir, and neural glitch-art over post-punk negative space, letting long silences hold the un-sent. Signature style one, the Draugr Choir: Old Norse phonemes rebuilt by resynthesis into a breathing choral pad, audibly artifacted at the seams so the dead throat shows its stitches. Signature style two, the Bit-Depth Bell, a single struck glass tone that on every refrain-return is swallowed by a quantum swell collapsing hi-fi to eight-bit to two-bit grit, the certainty thinning as the ghost thins. AI code-scratch letterhead of time-stretched Python logs opens like a pen with no hand. A female voice, crystalline and breathy, cracks exactly once. Sub-bass room-tone hum is the only body in the room. No trap hats, no risers.

**## EXCLUDE PROMPT**
Baile Phonk, HyperRaaga, trap hi-hats, EDM riser, airhorn, dubstep drop, aggressive rap, maximal wall-of-sound.
