## 2. Method Note
THE ARRIVAL is not a switchboard and not a lament. It opens on a *place* (a valley, a sky), gathers a *voice that sings toward* the arriving thing, states ONE fact that re-frames the triumph, and lets a single adoptable hook **settle** — arrive, rebound, and lose amplitude across ~15 returns until it rests. AWE here resolves not into indignation but into **inclusion**: the fear that this fragile thing might not survive is answered by *belonging* — the sky/room/dust becomes its welcome. The four variations are four camera distances on the same landing (full-orchestral congregation / stripped music-box / crowd call-and-response / solo whisper to the sky), never four different songs with nouns swapped.

## 3. Distinctiveness Posture (vs the other five pairs)
Our spine is the **diminishing hook** (a hook that gets *quieter* 15 times), which no other pair uses — P1 ruptures on an absent downbeat, P3 is through-composed with no chorus, P5 is a continuous drone, P6 is a villanelle refrain that changes *meaning*. Ours changes *amplitude*, not meaning: it is the physics of a bounce, made singable. Our palette (Amazonian Techno + orchestral 8-bit) is shared with no other pair as a *pairing*. Our image is arrival-by-yielding; our address is second-person TO a place.

## 4. Complete Step Output

### Facet 1 — The hook is a decay envelope, not a repeat
The adoptable phrase ("Land and be held" / "Bounce and be small" / "Welcome it home" / "Crown it in dust") is stated at full strength, then in the final section it recurs ~15 times, each return shorter and softer (full line → clipped line → two words → one word → a hush), tracing the airbag bounce settling to rest.
- **Why it matters:** it makes the Source-3 form (arrival by yielding, motif recurring 15× and diminishing) *literally audible and singable* — the structure IS the meaning, so the fact never has to be recited.
- **Failure mode:** writing 15 identical choruses (a loop, not a decay) — the bounce would read as a stuck record, not a settling; or diminishing so fast it collapses in 3 lines and the "15" is lost.

### Facet 2 — Second-person address TO the place / the lander
Every song sings *to* someone: the red valley, the small rover, the hazy brown sky. The listener overhears a devotion aimed at a thing that cannot answer, which is what makes it a *prayer-hook* rather than a description.
- **Why it matters:** THE ARRIVAL's defining move is the adoptable prayer-hook sung TO the place; it converts a news fact into an act of welcome, which is how AWE becomes inclusion.
- **Failure mode:** slipping into third-person reportage ("the lander bounced fifteen times") — that is the weather-report-in-meter the Concept Hyper-Skeptic rejects.

### Facet 3 — Survival by yielding as the emotional thesis
The airbag landing is the most fragile-sounding method carrying the most fragile cargo; the song argues that *opening/softening/bowing* is braver than bracing. Lyric verbs cluster on yield, open, soften, bow, trust.
- **Why it matters:** it gives the AWE a spine (a real idea, not just prettiness) and lets the "clean fear" in — the fear that a soft thing falling might break — which the ICB requires of every AWE song.
- **Failure mode:** triumphalism ("it conquered Mars!") that erases the fragility, or sentimentality that names the tenderness instead of staging it.

### Facet 4 — One recontextualizing fact, sung once, at the hinge
Exactly one numeric fact is sung: **fifteen** (the ~15 bounces), and only in the Bridge, where it is *answered* ("fifteen soft insistences that fear could bounce away"), not listed.
- **Why it matters:** satisfies the one-fact rule and the ICB's "one recontextualizing fact"; the number arriving late re-frames the whole song from "a landing" to "fifteen acts of yielding."
- **Failure mode:** reciting more numbers (dates, km, dust color as data) — the 2026-06-28 weather-report regression; or burying "fifteen" in a verse so it never lands as the hinge.

### Facet 5 — Two lanes doing opposite jobs (ambience vs fanfare)
Amazonian Techno field-ambience (96 kHz valley thunder, cricket-shimmer, dust-wind, side-chained kick) provides the *place*; orchestral 8-bit (chiptune fanfare fused to a real string/brass section) provides the *ceremony*. The ancient and the pixel sing one line.
- **Why it matters:** honors Lofn's fuse-with-lineage method (Amazonian Techno credited) and her video-game pillar (8-bit↔AAA) while keeping the two signature styles deeply described, not garnish.
- **Failure mode:** letting 8-bit become novelty bleeps or the ambience become generic reverb — the Medium Hyper-Skeptic rejects ornamental swells and shallow signature styles.

### Facet 6 — The swell that refuses to climax
Across all four, the orchestral/choir swell builds toward a peak and then, at the diminishing count, *withdraws its own volume by degrees* rather than detonating. The loudest moment is early; the ending is a single held tone or a hush.
- **Why it matters:** a bounce loses energy — a conventional build-to-drop would contradict the physics and the emotion; the anti-climax IS the arrival coming to rest.
- **Failure mode:** defaulting to a big EDM drop (the house instinct) — which would import exactly the 90→140 collapse the ICB bans and betray the settling.

### Facet 7 — Four camera distances, one landing
V1 full-orchestral congregation (the whole sky sings); V2 stripped music-box (a lullaby, one voice + wind-up box); V3 crowd call-and-response (a gathered room welcomes); V4 solo whisper (one voice to the dust-sky). Same event, four scales of witness.
- **Why it matters:** yields four genuinely distinct architectures (choir / music-box / call-response / solo) so the four variations pass distinctiveness on merit, not noun-swapping.
- **Failure mode:** the four collapsing into "the same song louder/quieter" — the Context Hyper-Skeptic's explicit reject; each must have its own verse architecture and hook-shape.

### Facet 8 — Inclusion, not indignation, as the resolution
This pair is one of the AWE seats (P2). The tension (will the fragile thing survive?) resolves into *belonging*: the place/room/sky adopts the arrival. No snarl, no rupture — the vocal stays crystalline/breathy throughout.
- **Why it matters:** the Duality-Warden needs AWE seats to actually stay in AWE; softening toward inclusion is the correct arc here (INDIGNATION lives in P1/P4/P6).
- **Failure mode:** importing Lofn's default bratty snarl / Baile-Phonk drop out of habit — off-brief for an AWE→inclusion arrival and a distinctiveness collision with the INDIGNATION pairs.

### Facet 9 — The "smallness" reframe (the adoptable image)
The rover is repeatedly imaged as small — "no bigger than something a child could hold," "built like a toy" — so that welcoming it becomes an act of tenderness toward the least, not awe toward the mighty.
- **Why it matters:** gives the 9:16 clip a single portable image (a tiny brave thing being welcomed) and makes the hook adoptable by anyone who ever felt small arriving somewhere.
- **Failure mode:** grandiosity about "conquering the red planet" that loses the child-scale tenderness the hook depends on to be singable and inclusive.

### Facet 10 — Diminishment mapped onto the mix, not just the words
The decay is instructed in the section headers and cues (swell gives, box slows, volley thins, drone thins to a single grain), so a Suno render can enact the settling sonically, not only lexically.
- **Why it matters:** the mandatory form must survive as *sound*; putting the decay in the arrangement (per Lofn's "instructions in the header" rule) makes it real for the engine.
- **Failure mode:** the diminishing existing only in the lyric text while the backing stays at full power — the form would be claimed but not delivered.
