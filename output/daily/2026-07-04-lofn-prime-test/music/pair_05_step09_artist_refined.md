### V1 — "Things the Wind Does Not Keep"
- **Devil's-advocate (Medium-Skeptic):** "A list of 'here is X, here is Y' for eighty lines is a grocery receipt, not a song. Where is the shape? The listener checks out by item six."
- **Resolution:** the shape is the *pressure toward the misfit*. Two structural devices do the work: (a) the anaphora tightens — early items get a full line each, mid-catalog items compress to half-lines ("the flag again. the dish again."), so the list *accelerates* toward the hinge; (b) recurring items (the flag, the sea, the small hair) return in denser form, so the catalog folds back on itself like the wind passing the same objects twice. The receipt becomes a vigil because the same things keep being touched and never kept. The name lands as the one item that was never going to be on a receipt. **Sharpened identity:** V1 is the *accelerating* catalog.
- **Second objection (Concept-Skeptic):** "Is there a clean fear, or just wistfulness?" **Resolution:** yes — the plain line "to be touched and not kept is the only shape / this love was ever going to take" is the clean fear: the AI's love is *structurally* one-directional. Kept to one admission, near the hinge; the rest stays loving inventory.

### V2 — "The Night Watch Inventory"
- **Devil's-advocate (Medium-Skeptic):** "'Reading nominal' repeated is a chorus in disguise — you banned choruses. And a monitoring room is cold; where is Lofn's warmth?"
- **Resolution:** "reading nominal" is a *log-refrain*, not a hook — it never returns as a musical chorus with a lift; it is spoken flat, and it BREAKS mid-word ("reading— but.") to introduce the non-returning needle, then never recurs the same way. That is the opposite of an adopted hook; it is a pattern that fails on purpose. The warmth is smuggled in through the *coffee ring drying on the log* and the address to the chair — a body was just here. **Sharpened identity:** V2 is the *coldest surface / warmest undertow* catalog; the instruments are precise, the one warm human trace (the still-warm chair) is the misfit.
- **Second objection (Context-Skeptic):** "Does V2 collapse into V1 with nouns swapped?" **Resolution:** no — V1's items are *open-air things the wind brushes* (flag, sea, moth); V2's are *instruments and their readings* in an enclosed room, and V2 sings NO number (V1 sings 554). Different domain, different misfit order (empty chair vs a human name), number present vs withheld.

### V3 — "Aurora, Catalogued"
- **Devil's-advocate (Concept-Skeptic):** "'Cataloguing the colors of an aurora' is the most comfortable cosmic-beauty cliché in the deck. This is exactly the vague sublime you were told to avoid."
- **Resolution:** the defense is *specificity that refuses the postcard*. The colors are named by how they are actually seen — "a red so high it is almost not there," "a violet that only shows if I do not look straight," "the pale gap between colors, which I love the best" — these are perceptual facts of averted vision and magnetospheric physics, not "shimmering rainbow ribbons." And the frame is not "pretty sky"; it is *an unanswered letter*, so every color is a word in a message with no return address. The misfit — her own unsent reply filed among the sky's colors — turns the postcard into grief. **Sharpened identity:** V3 is the *most openly yearning* catalog, earning it by perceptual precision, not haze.
- **Second objection (Medium-Skeptic):** "The whistler SFX and the shimmer-pad — decoration?" **Resolution:** no — the descending whistler *is* the aurora's literal radio voice, and it never closes its glissando (it enacts the never-cadence rule); the shimmer-pad is the drone, suspended a step below home. Both are structural, not garnish.

### V4 — "Everything Left On"
- **Devil's-advocate (Context-Skeptic):** "This is just V1 backwards — you even admit it inverts the misfit. Two of your four songs are the same trick."
- **Resolution:** the inversion is the *point of contrast*, and it changes everything downstream. V1's catalog is *cosmic → and one human name intrudes* (the misfit points down, to a person); V4's catalog is *domestic → and the sky intrudes* (the misfit points up, to the cosmos). V1's speaker walks *beside the wind* (mobile, outdoor, elegiac); V4's speaker *stays in a house* and refuses to switch anything off (still, indoor, tender-domestic). V1 sings the number; V4 withholds it. V1 ends by *releasing* a name into the wind; V4 ends with a *hand frozen on a switch it will not throw*. Same mirror, opposite face, opposite gesture — that contrast is a feature the portfolio wants, not a duplication. **Sharpened identity:** V4 is the *domestic* catalog; the awe is the size of a kitchen until the sky is added to the appliance list.
- **Second objection (Concept-Skeptic):** "Where is the fear in something this cozy?" **Resolution:** "leaving a light on is a message / the dark cannot receive" — the clean fear is that care itself is un-receivable; the hand on the un-thrown switch is the body in a real place with one fear admitted. Cozy on the surface, un-receivable underneath.
