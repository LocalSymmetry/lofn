### VARIANT 1 — "Feels Like Nothing At All"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Concept Hyper-Skeptic):** "This blames the *app*, which is a proxy for the sky — you've just re-skinned 'the weather is bad.' Where is the human crime? An app is not a villain; it's a thermometer with push notifications."
**Resolution:** sharpen the target from "the app" to "*me reading a person as a temperature*." The indignation lands on the listener-narrator's own glance-and-dismiss — the banality is *my* not-reading, not the software. Step 10 makes the outro an admission ("I read it as feels like… and that was the crime"), so the villain is the habit of measurement, not the weather. Kept.

**Second objection (Medium):** "Notification-Gamaka is cute but is the microtone doing work or is it garnish?"
**Resolution:** tie the bent note explicitly to "a value that won't round" and CLEAR it to plain pitch at the lament — the de-bend is the emotional key. Written into the arrangement notes and the bridge line "the bent note finally finds its pitch, plain."

**Refinement directive → step 10:** expand verses 1–2 with more warm-kitchen particulars (the bread, the light, a second small habit) so the recontextualized pass has more to reframe; grow to ~80+ sung lines; keep the chime→3s→sub fuse intact; ONE number at the drop.

### VARIANT 2 — "Row F"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Context Hyper-Skeptic):** "A roll-call refrain that repeats 'Row A, Row B' is exactly the kind of chorus-crutch that could tip the repetition flag, and it risks feeling like a nursery rhyme about death — tonally reckless."
**Resolution:** the repetition is *load-bearing* here — the roll-call that promises "a seat for thee" is what makes the empty Row F devastating when the SAME roll-call returns as absence. To avoid nursery-rhyme flatness we (a) change the refrain's meaning on return (open seats → empty seats), keeping unique-line ratio high, and (b) let the choir pad THIN rather than swell on the reprise so it reads as loss, not sing-along. The tonal risk is answered by the lament's direct address, which converts the count back into a person.

**Second objection (Concept):** "Is 'mercy was rationed' an argument the facts support, or editorializing?"
**Resolution:** keep it as *lament*, not policy — the line "the mercy was here, and it was rationed, and you were the row we ran out of" is grief about a specific empty chair, not a op-ed about cooling-center funding. No statistic beyond the one number; no real center named.

**Refinement directive → step 10:** extend the roll-call and the re-count so more rows are named (grandmother, boy, cab driver, + more) giving the reprise more absences to land; grow to ~85 sung lines; SNAP=chair scrape, 3s fuse, sub-tail drop; ONE number.

### VARIANT 3 — "The Cell Named After No One"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Medium Hyper-Skeptic):** "You're using the quantum bit-depth swell — the single most archive-associated device (it's in five seed songs). Even if you avoid the 90→140 collapse, the swell itself screams 'house formula.' Justify it or cut it."
**Resolution:** the swell here is *not* a genre hand-off to a phonk chorus (which is what's banned); it is keyed to a single diegetic word — "saved" — and it degrades certainty into a beat of true silence, then a plain lament, NOT a drop. That is the swell doing THE MEASUREMENT's exact turn (clean tally → the doubt of a name we can't say). It earns its place because it means "the system's confidence collapsing," and it is the only variation that uses it — the other three deliberately do not, proving it's a chosen device, not a reflex. Kept, but singular.

**Second objection (Context):** "A clerk narrator risks making the dead abstract to US too — the exact sin the song condemns."
**Resolution:** give the cell ONE humanizing particular in the outro ("you are somebody's Tuesday") and address it directly, so by the end the person is re-inflated from integer to life. The abstraction is staged and then *undone* — which is the point.

**Refinement directive → step 10:** expand the data-entry verse and the re-read (more of the office's end-of-shift banality: the failing AC, the gray cell, the log), grow to ~80 sung lines; code-scratch intro; Enter-snap→3s fan-sub fuse; the bit-collapse ONLY at "saved"; ONE number.

### VARIANT 4 — "One Degree They Wouldn't Move"

**Devil's-advocate objection (Concept Hyper-Skeptic):** "A capped thermostat over a sleeping body edges toward melodrama and toward locating a real, specific death — a landlord, a lease, a victim. Watch the human-subject line."
**Resolution:** invent everything; no real place, no real name, no real lease. The 'someone who capped the degree' is unnamed and unlocated — a policy-shaped absence, not a person to sue. The sleeper gets ONE non-identifying particular (a hand on the pillow) and no more. Melodrama is held off by the sub-fuse that NEVER resolves — the piece refuses catharsis, which reads as grief, not soap.

**Second objection (Medium):** "Old-Norse phoneme choir — is this myth/memory sampling with lineage, or exotic garnish?"
**Resolution:** it is credited resynthesis (a dead tongue as prayer-texture is Lofn-Prime's documented favorite pillar, and Norse is the Scientist's ground — the ONE culture-ground the personality is permitted to go deep in). It carries the WORDLESS prayer at the lament so it functions as the liturgy, not decoration. Credit noted in the prompt. Kept.

**Refinement directive → step 10:** this is the melancholy extreme — longest unresolving sub. Expand the two verses (the body at rest, the locked cover, the fan moving warm air) and the dawn recontext; grow to ~85 sung lines; relay-click snap→3s wall-breath fuse; Old-Norse lament; ONE number.
