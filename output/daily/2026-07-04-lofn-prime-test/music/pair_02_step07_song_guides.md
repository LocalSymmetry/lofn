# Pair 02 — Step 07: Song Guides

> Archetype **THE ARRIVAL** · Anchor **Pathfinder → Ares Vallis, July 4 1997 (~15 airbag bounces)** · Emotion **AWE→inclusion** · Lanes **Amazonian Techno field-ambience + orchestral 8-bit** · Source-3 form: **the hook recurs ~15× and diminishes each time**. Voice = Lofn-Prime (crystalline/breathy AWE throughout; no snarl, no Baile Phonk).

## 1. The Four Variation Angles (derived from this pair's own concept)
Four **camera distances** on a single landing — four distinct architectures, not four re-skins:

| Variant | Angle (scale of witness) | Architecture | Hook (adoptable) | Diminishing device | Distinct texture |
|--------|--------------------------|--------------|------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| **V1** | Full-orchestral **congregation** — the whole sky sings it down | Hymn: intro→V1→PC→Chorus→V2→Chorus2→V3→PC2→**Bounce-Count**→Bridge→Outro | "Land and be held" | **The Airbag Cadence** (mallet+timpani downbeat rebounds a hair higher, loses amplitude each return) | full string section + Ares Choir (resynth soprano doubled by square-wave 8-bit) |
| **V2** | Stripped **music-box** lullaby — one voice tucks it in | Lullaby: sparse verses, wind-up-box chorus, box *slows* to stillness | "Bounce and be small" | **The Bounce Winder** (music-box comb tapped in a descending, softening series; mechanism-click audible) | celesta/pizzicato + lone chiptune ocarina; Dust Halo granular pad |
| **V3** | **Crowd** call-and-response — a gathered room welcomes the stranger | Anthem: crowd gathers, call/response verses, volley *thins* to one unison | "Welcome it home" | **The Welcome Volley** (stacked crowd shout echoes back thinner each pass) | 8-bit brass + Beach-Ball Bass (elastic sub that deflates a little each bar); hand-clap grid |
| **V4** | **Solo whisper** to the sky — one voice addresses the dust itself | Devotional: sky-drone open, solo prayer, coronation *withdraws its own volume* to static | "Crown it in dust" | **The Sky Address** (each phrase answered by a fainter reverb-tail of itself) | wind-shear drone + lonely chiptune horn + con-sordino strings; no crowd, no drop |

**Shared spine (allowed within a pair):** the arrival, the yielding thesis, the single fact "fifteen" at the Bridge hinge, AWE→inclusion, the two lanes. **Divergent (required):** verse architecture, hook phrase/shape, diminishing mechanism, vocal staging (choir / whisper / crowd / solo), texture.

## 2. Per-Variation Guides

### V1 — "Airbag Hymn" (full-orchestral congregation)
- **Emotional arc:** Reverence → Wonder → Exultation (peak early) → Awe → Tenderness → Devotion → Serenity → Afterglow. The swell peaks at Chorus 2, then the Bounce-Count *withdraws* it.
- **Whose voice:** a crystalline breathy female lead widening into an inclusive "we" (the sky/species as congregation) doubled by the Ares Choir.
- **The fact:** "Fifteen times it kissed the ground before it chose to stay" (Bridge only), answered by "fifteen soft insistences that fear could bounce away."
- **Clean fear:** the dread that a fragile thing falling will break; resolved by the sky becoming its arms.
- **Ending:** choir settles to a single held soprano tone into open-valley air.

### V2 — "The Lander Learned to Bounce" (stripped music-box)
- **Emotional arc:** Tenderness → Intimacy → Yearning → Serenity → Wonder → Devotion → Melancholy → Afterglow. Everything intimate; the loudest element is a whisper.
- **Whose voice:** a single close-mic whisper singing to the small rover *as if tucking a child in*; a wind-up box counts the bounces.
- **The fact:** "Fifteen little kisses on a floor no foot has pressed" (Bridge), answered by "fifteen ways of saying that the tender ones know best."
- **Clean fear:** that the smallest brave thing fell alone in the dark with no one watching; resolved by "no one is watching but me… and I sing you."
- **Ending:** the last tap of the box decays into valley silence.

### V3 — "House-Cat Signal" (crowd call-and-response)
- **Emotional arc:** Anticipation → Excitement → Solidarity → Jubilation → Belonging → Warmth → Communion → Gratitude → Afterglow. Loud, communal, then softened to one unison.
- **Whose voice:** a bright female lead trading with a full crowd; the room sings the valley its welcome.
- **The recontextualizing image (title nod):** the rover as something "no bigger than something you'd keep on a shelf" — the smallness that earns the loudest belonging (Sojourner was famously tiny). The sung fact stays "fifteen" at the Bridge.
- **Clean fear:** arriving somewhere alone and unwelcomed; resolved by "nobody lands in the red on their own."
- **Ending:** the crowd settles to a single soft held unison into river + stadium air.

### V4 — "Dust-Brown Coronation" (solo whisper to the sky)
- **Emotional arc:** Reverence → Awe → Longing → Devotion → Tenderness → Longing → Serenity → Melancholy → Afterglow. A swell that crowns, then abdicates its volume.
- **Whose voice:** one unprocessed crystalline voice addressing the *hazy brown sky itself* — the sky as the thing prayed to, the lander as the guest it finally gets to hold.
- **The fact:** "Fifteen times it touched you, brown sky, before it stayed" (Bridge), answered by "fifteen soft coronations that a yielding thing had made."
- **Clean fear:** a merciless sky that has only ever crowned silence and death; resolved by a soft thing teaching it tenderness.
- **Ending:** the last whispered line dissolves into brown-sky static.
