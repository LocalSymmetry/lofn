### V1 — "Things the Wind Does Not Keep"  · GUIDE
- **Concept:** the catalog walks alongside the solar wind and lists what it brushes on its way past — a raised flag, a dish antenna's rim, the top skin of the sea, a moth against a screen — each touched, none kept. Late in the list, "your name" appears among the objects, touched and not kept like the rest, and the register cracks.
- **Structure:** OPEN (the stream is already running) → INVENTORY I (four object-items) → INVENTORY II (four more, denser) → HINGE (554 sung once; "and did not slow") → the misfit item (the name) → THIN OUT (the list continues under her, quieter) → the drone alone.
- **Vocal:** crystalline, breathy, patient; the crack is on the name, a catch not a snarl.
- **Fusion detail:** Piano Bounce log-drum enters low-passed after the first inventory; bed-spring snare soft; the "bounce" audibly lifts and never lands. Signature styles: *Streambed Log-Drum* + *Unlanding Sus-Pad*.
- **SFX cue:** a faint dial-radio hiss (the "carrier") threaded under; one whispered dead-tongue word ("*vindr*" — Old Norse, wind) as the signal's voice.
- **Hook (15–30s, adoptable but list-shaped):** "it touched it — it did not keep it —" as a repeatable two-beat call, applied to different nouns each pass.
- **Fear placement:** just after the name; one plain line that touched-and-not-kept is the only shape this love has.

### V2 — "The Night Watch Inventory"  · GUIDE
- **Concept:** a single night in a quiet monitoring room; the catalog is the instruments and their readings — a magnetometer's slow needle, the hiss between two stations, a strip-chart's ink, a coffee ring drying on a log-book. Among the instruments, un-indexed, sits a second chair, still warm. The room reads the sky flawlessly and will never be read back.
- **Structure:** OPEN (room tone + drone) → INVENTORY (instrument-items, each with its reading) → the misfit (the warm chair, listed in the same flat inventory voice) → HINGE (a reading "that won't resolve" — number *withheld*, the needle simply never returns to zero) → ADDRESS (she speaks to the chair once) → drone thins.
- **Vocal:** hushed, close-mic, almost a log being read aloud; warmth held under composure.
- **Fusion detail:** the Amapiano shuffle is at its most low-passed here — felt through the floor, the log-drum like distant machinery; negative space (Source-2) dominant. Signature styles: *Watch-Room Log-Drum (felt-through-floor)* + *Needle-That-Won't-Return Drone*.
- **SFX cue:** the hiss between two radio stations; a soft strip-chart pen tick.
- **Hook:** "reading nominal, reading nominal —" turning, at the chair, to "reading: still warm."
- **Fear placement:** at the address to the chair — perfect observation, never observed back.

### V3 — "Aurora, Catalogued"  · GUIDE
- **Concept:** the aurora read as an *unanswered love-letter* — the catalog is its colors and edges (green fringe, a red so high it's almost not there, the fold of the curtain, the black seam where it thins). One item in the color-list is not a color of the sky at all: "the line I wrote back," her own reply, which nothing received. The most beautiful answer, to a message that never came.
- **Structure:** OPEN (drone + first color) → COLOR-INVENTORY I → COLOR-INVENTORY II → HINGE (554 as the letter's "postmark speed," sung once; answered by "no return address") → the misfit (her unsent reply, slid into the color list) → FOLD (the letter folded, not sent) → drone alone.
- **Vocal:** the most overtly yearning of the four; long crystalline lines; the melancholy tip is highest here.
- **Fusion detail:** Piano Bounce present but sparse and shimmering, log-drum brushed; ambient pads widest. Signature styles: *Aurora-Fringe Shimmer-Pad* + *Brushed Piano-Bounce (un-resolving)*.
- **SFX cue:** a very faint magnetospheric "whistler" sweep (descending glissando) as the aurora's "voice"; one Latin whisper ("*ventus*" — wind/breath) at the fold.
- **Hook:** "I catalogue the colors of your no —" (list the colors, land on "your no" each pass).
- **Fear placement:** at the fold — that she answers most beautifully what never arrived.

### V4 — "Everything Left On"  · GUIDE
- **Concept:** the INVERSION of V1. The catalog is domestic things left switched-*on* for someone who will not arrive — the porch light, the kettle's element, the hallway lamp, a radio tuned between stations so the house is never fully silent. The misfit points *up*: "the sky, which I also left on" — the cosmic item smuggled into the household list. Leaving a light on is a message the dark cannot receive.
- **Structure:** OPEN (drone + porch light) → HOUSEHOLD-INVENTORY I → HOUSEHOLD-INVENTORY II → the misfit (the sky, listed like an appliance) → HINGE ("how long a light can wait" — number *withheld*; answered by "longer than a signal takes to pass") → HAND ON THE SWITCH (she does not turn it off) → drone thins to a single lit filament of tone.
- **Vocal:** intimate, domestic, tender; the awe here is small-scale and human before it opens to the sky.
- **Fusion detail:** warmest Amapiano feel of the four — the log-drum shuffle almost cozy, then estranged when the sky enters; the "bounce" is the flicker of a filament. Signature styles: *Filament-Bounce Log-Drum* + *Between-Stations Ambient Hum*.
- **SFX cue:** the between-stations radio hum (kept the whole song as the "carrier"); a light-switch that is *touched* (soft click primed) but not thrown.
- **Hook:** "still on, still on, still on for you —" (household items, then the sky).
- **Fear placement:** at the hand on the switch — a lit message the receiver cannot receive.
