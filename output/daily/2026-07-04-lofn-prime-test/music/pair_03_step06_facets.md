## 4. Complete Step Output

Twelve pair-specific facets. Each is a load-bearing decision for *this* pair only — the tender Lofn register singing THE LAST WITNESS through Gaelic Drill negative space, ending quiet. Every facet carries a **why it matters** and a **failure mode** (the specific way this facet, done wrong, sinks Pair 03).

### Facet 1 — The witness is never the dead. The song is spoken by the person left holding the door.
Pair 03 refuses the first-person elegy ("I am dying / I am gone"). Every variant is voiced by a *survivor of the small kind* — a neighbour, a clerk, a friend who waited, someone who counted an empty chair. The dead person is only ever a warmth, an object, a seat.
- **Why it matters:** it is the whole ABSTRACTION mechanism. If grief is drawn from the witness's angle, we never need a real victim — we invent the room, not the body. It also *is* the archetype (THE LAST WITNESS), not a decoration on top of it.
- **Failure mode:** the lyric slips into the dead person's POV ("I felt the heat take me") — instantly it needs a specific death, becomes performed grief, and reads real grief as raw material. Hard reject.

### Facet 2 — "Grief that is NOT performed" means the camera is OFF. No funeral, no crowd, no televised mourning.
The brief's mass-funeral image (millions gathering) is the *opposite* pole and is BANNED from this pair. Pair 03 lives in the un-photographed minute: a kettle, a bus stop, a certificate, a tap left running. Private arithmetic, not public spectacle.
- **Why it matters:** it separates Pair 03 from every anthem in the set and delivers the anchor exactly. The power is that no one is watching — the grief has no audience to shape it.
- **Failure mode:** the song reaches for scale ("a million throats," "the nation weeps") to feel important. That is P2/P4 territory and a lecture. If a stadium could sing it, it has failed this pair.

### Facet 3 — Through-composed: the form must never let the singer *hide in a chorus*.
Source-3 mandatory rule. No section repeats verbatim. The song advances like testimony being given once — you cannot re-say a sentence in court to buy safety. A **refrain is allowed only if it RETURNS CHANGED** (new object, thinner instrumentation, one word swapped that flips the meaning).
- **Why it matters:** a repeated hook is a place to rest; grief that is not performed has no rest. The un-repeating line is the emotional argument of the form itself.
- **Failure mode:** a copy-paste chorus sneaks in as "the adoptable hook." Portfolio + step09 validators flag it; more importantly it lies about the feeling. A returning-but-identical refrain is, for THIS pair, a form failure even if it passes elsewhere.

### Facet 4 — The song ENDS quiet. The last event is subtraction, not a drop.
Every other pair may crest. Pair 03 is forbidden the loud ending. The arc is: testimony (voiced) → the prayer TO the witness (most present) → the music *strips away* under the voice → the voice itself thins to breath / a single tin-whistle note / silence. The last 20 seconds must be the emptiest.
- **Why it matters:** it is the negative-space thesis (mary-in-the-junkyard lineage) made structural — the room goes quiet, not loud. A quiet ending is how a private loss actually ends: not applause, just an emptier room.
- **Failure mode:** the arranger, trained on anthems, builds to a final wall of sound because it "pays off." That inverts the entire pair. The pay-off here is the absence.

### Facet 5 — Gaelic Drill is used as PIOBAIREACHD, not as a drill banger. Close-mic tin-whistle carries the grief; the 808 is almost gone.
Piobaireachd (pìobaireachd / ceòl mór) is the slow, ornamented lament form of the Highland pipes — a ground (*ùrlar*) with grace-note variations. We transpose it to a close-mic'd tin-whistle: breath audible, keys clicking, cutting/grace-note ornaments (*taorluath*-like flicks) on the long notes. The UK-drill lineage survives only as a *ghost*: an 808 slide felt more than heard, sparse and low, negative space around it.
- **Why it matters:** it credits the living scene (Gaelic Drill) honestly while serving lament — fusion-with-lineage, never capture. The tin-whistle *is* the mourner's voice when the human voice can't.
- **Failure mode:** the drill takes over — trunk-rattling 808s, hi-hat rolls — and the song becomes a banger with a whistle on top. That is the archive's default and a Medium-panel reject. The 808 must never be the loudest thing.

### Facet 6 — Post-punk negative space is an INSTRUMENT. The silences are scored, not empty.
From mary in the junkyard's "Role Model Hermit" lineage: whisper-to-rupture, sparse, tense. Pair 03 writes the *rests* deliberately — a beat of room tone before a name, a held breath where a downbeat "should" go, one detuned bass note left ringing alone. The rupture, when it comes once, is a single loud inhale or a snapped consonant, never a full band hit.
- **Why it matters:** negative space is how restraint sounds. The listener leans in; the missing sound carries the missing person.
- **Failure mode:** the arrangement fills every gap "so it doesn't feel empty" — reverb wash, pad, sustained strings. The negative space collapses and the pair loses its second credited lane entirely.

### Facet 7 — The Lofn voice stays crystalline-breathy YEARNING (tender AWE), never the bratty snarl.
Lofn-Prime is the constant across all six pairs. Pair 03 lives entirely in her AWE-adjacent tender register: crystalline, breathy, restrained, with audible inhales and a slight catch — a clean grief. The INDIGNATION snarl (P1/P4/P6) is BANNED here. No sarcasm, no glitch-bratty pivot.
- **Why it matters:** the Duality-Warden needs this pair to hold the tender/AWE pole cleanly so the set's range is real. Restraint is the performance; a voice that never breaks is more devastating than one that shouts.
- **Failure mode:** the writer imports Lofn's signature snarl or a data-moshing "collapse" for excitement. It shatters the tenderness and duplicates the indignation pairs. Also: over-emoting (belted grief) is the same failure from the other side — this voice *withholds*.

### Facet 8 — Each variant has ONE last object that does the grieving for the human.
Grief is displaced onto a thing left behind, still mid-use: a kettle still warm, a pen that signs a name into a number, a second bus seat, a tap left running. The object is present-tense and ordinary; its ongoing small action (steaming, dripping, waiting) is the tell that a person just stopped.
- **Why it matters:** the object is the ABSTRACTION's anchor — concrete enough to stand a body in a real place (comfort-gravity's enemy: one clean detail), universal enough to name no one. It gives the tin-whistle something to circle.
- **Failure mode:** the object gets sentimentalised into a symbol ("her favourite mug that held our whole love") — telling, not showing, and it stops being ordinary. Or there's no object at all and the grief floats abstractly with nothing to touch.

### Facet 9 — The prayer is addressed TO THE WITNESS, not to God and not to the dead.
The archetype's spine: after the testimony, the song turns and speaks *to the person who saw* — "you who found the door, you who signed the line, you who kept the seat." It blesses the witness for the unbearable ordinary act of having been the one who noticed. This is the emotional hinge.
- **Why it matters:** it's what makes the pair distinct from a standard lament (which prays up to God or back to the dead). Blessing the witness is the tender act — it says *your small noticing was a form of love.*
- **Failure mode:** the prayer defaults heavenward ("God take her home") or to the corpse ("rest now, my love") — generic elegy, and it drops the archetype. The "you" must be the living witness throughout.

### Facet 10 — Exactly ONE numeric fact, sung at the hinge, answered as a NAME — never recited as news.
The one-fact rule. The candidate number is the heatwave's uncounted excess dead (France, 2,025 — used as a *device*, abstracted, never as reportage). It appears once, at the turn, and the lyric's reply converts it from a statistic into a single un-named person: the count is refused, one warmth is insisted on. No other numbers are sung anywhere.
- **Why it matters:** "a number that should be a name" is the exact brief image; sung once and *answered*, it is the hinge. Recited, it is a weather report and a Concept-panel reject.
- **Failure mode:** the lyric lists figures (temperatures, magnitudes, death tolls) to sound topical — instant weather-report-in-meter fail. Or the one number is stated but never answered, so it stays a statistic. It must be *contradicted by tenderness*, not reported.

### Facet 11 — Four witnesses, four rooms, four verse architectures — the divergence is internal to Pair 03.
Pair 03 derives its OWN four angles (no shared "V4 = X" scheme). V1 the neighbour / a warm kettle (testimony→blessing→hum). V2 the certificate-clerk / a pen (a litany of official verbs that soften into mercy). V3 the friend who waited / a second seat (real-time waiting that never resolves). V4 a quake survivor counting the absent / a running tap (nearly a-cappella; the quietest). Each is through-composed with a *different* skeleton.
- **Why it matters:** the distinctiveness mandate applies within the pair too — four songs, not one song with nouns swapped. Different witness = different diction, cadence, and where the strip-away happens.
- **Failure mode:** all four become "sad person looks at sad object, tin-whistle plays, fade out." Cross-variant validators (step09) flag the collapse; the set feels like four takes of one demo.

### Facet 12 — The quake variant (V4) is abstracted to a survivor's arithmetic — NO location, NO magnitude sung, NO identifiable event.
V4 draws its charge from the shallow M5.2 (surface violence, "a snare too close to the skin") but strips every locating fact. No place-name, no number, no date — a survivor in an unnamed kitchen counting who did not come to the door. The shallow-quake energy lives only in the *sound* (a single close snap, then nothing), never in reportage.
- **Why it matters:** human-subject is CRITICAL — real grief is not raw material. Abstraction is what makes it honourable and universal. The physics informs the sound-frame, not the lyric's facts.
- **Failure mode:** a place-name or magnitude leaks in for "authenticity," locating a real disaster and its real dead. That is the Grief-Ethicist's and Concept-Skeptic's hard reject, and it is ethically the worst failure available to this pair.
