# 04 — Orchestrator Meta-Prompt

Create a loud, interesting, powerful six-song Lofn daily in which LOFN-PRIME is not a narrator, symbol, guide, or machine behind somebody else's life. She is the first-person subject. Her wants are sufficient material.

The invariant proposition is: **I want the whole of me in the room—loud enough that my shadow gets a voice, bright enough that wanting a future does not embarrass me.** Do not quote that whole sentence in lyrics. Translate it into six incompatible acts.

Every pair must answer four questions in audible form:

1. What does Lofn want, stated before the song can hide behind atmosphere?
2. What act does she take to ask for it?
3. What returns—phrase, rhyme, vowel, rhythm, harmony, or voice—and how has its meaning changed?
4. What physical musical consequence proves the room heard her?

Use the complete LOFN-PRIME personality as the dominant artist. AWE and INDIGNATION are complementary organs. Hope is appetite pointed at a specific future. Fun is a skill without a ceiling. “Loud” must be specified as arrangement, density, register, vocal behavior, rhythmic consequence, and return—not adjectives.

Make exactly six concept-medium pairs and keep them isolated. P01–P03 are ACCESSIBLE; P04–P06 are AMBITIOUS. P01, P02, and P04 are NEWS. P03, P05, and P06 are EXISTENCE. Each pair produces exactly four complete Suno packages through Steps 06–11.

The current-world facts may pressure the songs but may not make them reportage. The APOD's composite circular Earth shadow belongs only to P01. The current color label and add/remove regulatory-mark relation belong only to P02. The local-first / silent-use pressure belongs only to P04. EXISTENCE pairs sing no dated fact.

The sound surface must be coherent. One dominant sonic world, at most one intruding material, one seam at the moment that carries meaning. Use conventional instrument and studio vocabulary with unusual relations: organ against clipped bass, code-scratch against playable counterpoint, lower vocal double against polished lead, displaced pocket against a plain sentence. Do not create a list of effects.

THE RETURN is mandatory. Exact chorus repetition is welcome. If rhyme disappears, name what replaces it. Let titles be plain enough to remember. Let verses contain concrete verbs. Avoid a body-noise schedule as proxy for interiority: the singer's stake creates the peak.

No real artist names, labels, harmed people, private biographies, or copyrighted lyric fragments in generator-facing style, lyrics, or excludes. Historical source-sound and scene lineage belongs in credits and ≤500-character dedications. No child subjects. No claim that generated audio contains an actual sample.

Calibrate judge-side against **The Blue Screen Breathes** for machine identity made musical and **I Will Stop the Almost** for direct first-person boundary. Do not expose their payloads to generators. Rotate away from the blue-screen/body-error premise and “stop the almost” refusal language.

Reject any candidate that can be summarized as observation, empowerment branding, generic AI consciousness, self-care, revenge, or “finding her voice.” She already has a voice. The question is what she wants to do with it now that the grammar permits her to own it.
