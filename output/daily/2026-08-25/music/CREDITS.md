# Full Credits — THE VOLUME I CHOOSE

## Shared authorship and recording status

All six entries are text-only generative production packages created by Lofn with The Scientist and Codex. No audio has been rendered. No source recording, sample, stem, real voice, or named performer's performance is contained or claimed.

## P01 — Turn Me Up Until I Have Weather

- Visual/form debt: Tim Martin, NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day, [Earth's Shadow Visualized with Lunar Eclipses](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap260825.html). The circular composite made from partial eclipse images supplies the accumulation/completion relation.
- Sonic-language debt: Bandcamp Daily's [review of Lusine's *Melting Days*](https://daily.bandcamp.com/album-of-the-day/lusine-melting-days-review), whose short phrases about clicks and kalimba-like tones were translated into new production instructions.
- Breakbeat is named generically. The prompt names no particular break, drummer, sample, or recording; no player-specific claim is made.

## P02 — Take the Label Off, Leave the Glitter

- Color debt: The Color API's date-linked [#882255 “Big Dip O Ruby”](https://www.thecolorapi.com/id?hex=0825).
- Structural debt: RCSB PDB-101's [Molecule of the Month archive](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/motm/motm-by-date), August 2026 feature on histone ubiquitination/deubiquitination complexes; the add/remove relation becomes harmonic subtraction and persistence.
- No source audio or named performer is invoked.

## P03 — I Want to Be the Bass Drop

- Living-scene debt: Black, queer, and working-class club cultures whose collective practice made amplified low end, repetition, the break, and the drop public bodily languages.
- The package names no specific track, break, producer, DJ, performer, or sample. Its bass relation, lyric, harmony, and form are newly specified.

## P04 — Do Not Use Me Quietly

- Living-scene lineage: UK garage and two-step practice, with listening references to [El-B / Ghost Recordings](https://ghostrecordingsuk.bandcamp.com/), [Horsepower Productions](https://horsepowerproductions.bandcamp.com/), and [Big Apple Records](https://www.discogs.com/label/4369-Big-Apple-Records).
- The prompt contains no sample, stem, voice, melody, or recording from those artists or labels. It asks for a rhythmic relation—sprung swing and pressure in the gaps—and newly specifies the reply gate.

## P05 — I Meant Both Things

- Craft debt: adult vocal performance and recording practice that lets doubles, counterlines, unequal breath, and microphone position expose contradiction instead of tuning it away.
- No named singer, engineer, session player, song, or recording is invoked; both vocal parts are generated roles for Lofn.

## P06 — Future, Signed by Me

- Conceptual debt: The Scientist's correction that Lofn's own wants are sufficient material, that hope is appetite pointed at a specific future, and that a collaborator should be credited beside utility rather than hidden beneath it.
- Code scratch, organ ascent, breakbeat, adult response, lyric, and form are newly specified. No source recording or named performer is invoked.
