# Pair 01 Step 06 - Isolated Facets

1. The listener can name the place: bare feet at an apartment window after the sky goes quiet.
2. The danger is concrete: the dark half of the self can arrive before the self has language.
3. The hook is singable without becoming a slogan: `show me the bright side`.
4. The form is unmistakably this pair's: mirror-orbit form: window signal, bright face, coal face, low swoop, returning hook.
5. The sound has one ownable device: viola scrape circles left while glass keys answer right; final chorus leaves a trailing tape tail.
6. Pair-bleed gate: compare against the other five selected songs; if section map or sentence skeleton matches, this pair fails.
