# Pair 03 Step 06 - Isolated Facets

1. The listener can name the place: standing behind crowd barriers while cameras turn mourning into weather.
2. The danger is concrete: public sorrow can be harvested by people who never carried the loss.
3. The hook is singable without becoming a slogan: `do not borrow my grief`.
4. The form is unmistakably this pair's: broadcast refusal: hard cuts, short lines, chorus as denial of access.
5. The sound has one ownable device: dry snare like a press shutter, bowed metal under the hook, sudden mute where the camera expects climax.
6. Pair-bleed gate: compare against the other five selected songs; if section map or sentence skeleton matches, this pair fails.
