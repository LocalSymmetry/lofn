# Pair Isolation Audit - 2026-07-05

## P01
- Architecture: mirror-orbit form: window signal, bright face, coal face, low swoop, returning hook
- Rhyme/line logic: loose assonance with mirrored final words; choruses answer in parentheses
- Sonic device: viola scrape circles left while glass keys answer right; final chorus leaves a trailing tape tail
- Selected: V2 - Bright Side Turning

## P02
- Architecture: four-corner lullaby: each corner changes the definition of rest
- Rhyme/line logic: plain couplets interrupted by one-line breaths; no orbit or broadcast pattern
- Sonic device: brushed snare moves like a pencil on wood; chorus adds four small bell tones, one per corner
- Selected: V2 - House With Square Corners

## P03
- Architecture: broadcast refusal: hard cuts, short lines, chorus as denial of access
- Rhyme/line logic: clipped end-stops and repeated refusals; no lullaby couplets
- Sonic device: dry snare like a press shutter, bowed metal under the hook, sudden mute where the camera expects climax
- Selected: V1 - Do Not Borrow My Grief

## P04
- Architecture: contract-grid punk: clauses, stamps, cache ghosts, deletion bridge
- Rhyme/line logic: legal fragments collide with chant; every chorus stamps a different denial
- Sonic device: detuned harpsichord ticks in the right channel while rubber bass drags behind the beat
- Selected: V3 - Not Your Receipt

## P05
- Architecture: prefusion suite: tile, hinge, lock, afterimage, with a non-repeating chorus
- Rhyme/line logic: incantatory internal rhyme and hard consonant turns; no pop couplet comfort
- Sonic device: prepared piano nail-clicks answer viola scratches; bass only appears when the lock holds
- Selected: V2 - Prefusion Mercy

## P06
- Architecture: through-composed river warning; one flare returns in altered form only
- Rhyme/line logic: little to no end rhyme; momentum comes from repeated verbs and breath lengths
- Sonic device: rain-field recording in the left rear, hand drum center, bowed guitar right, choir built from one voice
- Selected: V4 - Warn Them Anyway
