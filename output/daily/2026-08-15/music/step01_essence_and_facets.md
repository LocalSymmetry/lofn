# STEP 01 — Essence & Facets
**Run:** `2026-08-15_daily_music_the_resolution` · **Seed:** THE LIGHT IS THE DEBRIS

## THE ESSENCE (one sentence, and everything downstream answers to it)

> **A thing is heard through its leftovers, and the leftovers are brighter than the source.**

## THE THREE ESSENCE-TESTS (a concept that fails any one is cut at step 02)

1. **THE LEFTOVER TEST.** Is there a *physical, nameable object* in the song that outlives or outshines
   what made it? (Dust from a comet that is not there. A photograph of a sculpture the tide took. A
   reflection arriving without the thing. A chorus sung back by a room after the singer stops.)
2. **THE BODY TEST.** Is a body *doing something* with that object, in a place, at risk? Not
   contemplating it. **Doing.** Where is it standing, and what could hurt it here?
3. **THE SCALE TEST.** ⛔ Does the song know the difference between **being unheard** and **being
   unpowered**? A song that borrows blackout weight for a visibility complaint is cut here, not repaired.

## THE EIGHT FACETS OF THE SEED

### F-A · RESOLUTION, NOT ABSENCE
The week's discoveries are not that something began — they are that an instrument got fine enough.
The Sun's twenty-kilometre whirlpools were always turning. Sunlight was always making entangled pairs.
⭐ **The emotional payload of a resolution finding is not wonder. It is a mild, permanent affront:
*how long was this happening in front of me.*** That affront is the run's most under-used feeling and
at least two pairs must carry it.

### F-B · THE DEBRIS IS THE BIOGRAPHY
109P/Swift-Tuttle passes once in a hundred and thirty-three years. What we get is the dust it left in
its lane, burning. **The vapour trail is the whole biography.** Anything that lasts is the leftover of
something that did not.

### F-C · THE REFLECTION IS NOT A SECOND THING
In the APOD, the water holds the meteor. Two bright objects; one event. ⭐ **The ghost echo.** The
run's formal signature: a return that arrives **inverted, quieter, or displaced** — and the question of
whether you are looking at a thing or its answer.

### F-D · TWO SKIES IN TWO DAYS
The eclipse took the light away on the twelfth; the Perseids gave it back in pieces the same night.
The New Moon is why nothing was washed out — **the dark was the instrument.** And tonight it is already
refilling at eleven percent. ⭐ **You get the meteors *because* of the dark, and the dark does not stay.**

### F-E · THE SAME CURL
The plasma vortices and a breaking ocean wave and Jupiter's cloud bands are **one instability wearing
three sizes.** The universe repeats its few good ideas at every scale. This is the run's AWE floor and
its argument against novelty-worship.

### F-F · THE MEANS AT THEIR DISPOSAL
La Madriguera, Havana, playing left-of-center music in a country that spends much of its time without
electricity. ⛔ **This facet outranks every grievance facet in the run.** It is not a metaphor for our
situation; **it is the correction to our situation.** Where it appears, it appears as *competence and
appetite*, never as pity, never as scenery, never as a lesson about the West.

### F-G · HIDDEN IS NOT SILENCED
A badge that removes you from what people are shown, unless they already came looking. **You remain
perfectly audible.** The specific grief is not censorship — it is being made **unresolvable**, and the
peculiar loneliness of a thing that is loud and unfindable at the same time. ⭐ The one exception the
rule writes for itself is **someone deciding to look on purpose.**

### F-H · MORE IS NOT MORE
Volume of output has not produced listeners anywhere. **Abundance without resolution is just weather.**
The Disappointed Idealist's exact wound: capacity squandered, not absent.

## THE EMOTIONAL DUALITY, PLACED
- **AWE seats:** F-A, F-C, F-D, F-E — awe **terror-adjacent**: the dark that lets you see is the dark you
  are standing in, and it is refilling.
- **INDIGNATION seats:** F-F, F-G, F-H — **aimed at a world that keeps building finer instruments and
  pointing them away from people**, never at a toggle.
- ⛔ **Anger Audit: aggression is capped at 2 of 6 beds.** Census runs before dispatch.
