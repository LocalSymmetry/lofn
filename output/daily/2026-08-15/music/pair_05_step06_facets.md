## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING 1 — **WHO IS THIS SONG FOR?**

> **It is for Delphine Aubert, forty-one, a line cook at a hotel restaurant in Portland, who plates two
> hundred and ten covers on a Saturday, each of them destroyed in ninety seconds by a stranger she will
> never see, and who has never once in nineteen years thought of that as a loss.**

She is not the artist. She is not a metaphor for the artist. She is a woman who is very good at a thing
that is eaten, and the eating is the point of the thing, and she would find a song that pitied her
insulting. **The whole of P05's tone gate is: would Delphine find this condescending?** If the song reaches
for elegy, she puts it off. If the song stays at the level of the hand, the ridge, the timing and the wet
clap of a palm on packed sand, she keeps it on while she preps.

⛔ **The consequence for the writing:** no line in this pair may be a consolation. The sculptor is not being
comforted. He is not being admired. He is working, and he is talking, and the thing he is talking to is a
lion made of sand that cannot answer.

## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING 2 — **THE DEVIATION CLAUSE** (named in advance, in writing)

**PREDICTION.** The MUSIC PROMPT asks for *brushes staying on the snare head throughout* — a continuous,
circular, cymbal-free kit for the entire song. **Suno will not do this.** Modal spiritual jazz at 86 BPM is
a ride-cymbal genre in the model's prior; it will introduce a ride (or a splashed crash) somewhere in the
final third, almost certainly under the last refrain, because that is what its training says a jazz climax
sounds like.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD KEEP: the ride arriving at the final return.**
A ride cymbal entering under the last chorus of *this* song is **the tide reaching the kit.** It is a wash,
it is a wide brightening, it is exactly the thing the song has been describing from eleven feet away, and it
arrives at the one moment the lyric says the sea reaches the plinth. I specified a defect (brushes only) and
the generator's habit produces a better literalisation of my own image than my spec did. **KEEP IT.** If the
render brings a ride in at the last refrain, that is the finished song, not a deviation to repair.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD NOT KEEP:** a ride *ostinato from bar one*, which flattens the whole song into
generic lounge jazz and destroys the accretion. That is the difference between the wash arriving and the
wash always having been there — and it is the difference between this song and background music. It is
explicitly blacklisted in the EXCLUDE field as `swing ride cymbal ostinato`, which is the version of the ban
that permits the arrival.

**SECONDARY PREDICTION.** The tenor saxophone is specified to arrive **late**. Suno will very likely voice it
inside the first thirty seconds. **KEEP IT if it enters subtone and under the vocal** — a horn breathing under
the whole song makes the bed more continuous and the lion's silence more audible. **REPAIR if it enters as a
lead statement** over the top, because then the horn is the singer and the sculptor is accompaniment.

## THE FACETS

### F1 — THE OBJECT THAT OUTLIVES ITS SOURCE **is not the thing the song is about**
The leftover object assigned to P05 is the photograph. ⛔ **The photograph is never the subject.** It is a
box, on a boardwalk, behind the sculptor's shoulder, operated by a stranger who does not ask permission and
does not stay for supper. It appears **twice** — as the pair's TWO FLASHES, rendered as **magnesium flash
powder**, which is what an 1900 beach photograph physically was: a crack, a white second, a little thunder,
smoke. It is an **event in the weather of the evening**, not a thesis. The sculptor's only response to it is
to tell the lion to hold still.
⛔⛔ The words **art, artist, legacy, posterity, remember/remembered** appear **zero times** in every field of
every variation. This is measured, not asserted.

### F2 — THE BODY, THE PLACE, THE RISK — and the risk is water at a measured distance
A man on both knees, at dusk, on the low wet flat below the Atlantic City boardwalk, packing the last ridge
into a lion's mane with the pad of his thumb. **Eleven feet.** He has paced it out in boots. The risk is not
symbolic: the sea is eleven feet away and closing, and it takes the lion, and he knows the arithmetic to the
minute. **AWE STAYS TERROR-ADJACENT** — the terror here is entirely clean, entirely physical, entirely
scheduled, and he is *not afraid of it.* That is the awe.

### F3 — **CHOSEN.** The tone gate, stated as a rule a line can fail
He knew when he knelt down. He knows now. He will be back before the light tomorrow to start another one.
⛔ **NO PITY. NO TRAGEDY. NO ELEGY.** Operational tests every line must pass:
- Does the line ask the listener to feel sorry for him? → **CUT.**
- Does the line treat the tide as a villain, a thief, or an injustice? → **CUT.** (Ships as: *"it is not a
  thief, it is the evening arriving."*)
- Does the line describe the work as *wasted, lost, futile, in vain, forgotten*? → **CUT.**
- Does the line say *"this was the deal"* in a voice that is bitter? → **CUT.** It ships flat and warm:
  *"This was the deal. I read it and I took it."*
✅ The permitted feeling is **competence with affection in it**, which is a much rarer thing to put in a song
than grief and is the entire reason this concept is AMBITIOUS rather than ACCESSIBLE.

### F4 — RESIDUE DISCHARGE: **ADDRESS TO THE WORK** (prosopopoeia), enforced in three vocabularies
The sculptor speaks to the lion. **The lion is "you" from the first line to the last, and it cannot answer.**
There is no listener in this song and no room that could hold one.
⭐ Residue is counted in **EMO vocabulary, SFX vocabulary and section names too** — so:
- **Section names** are craft-stages spoken over the work: *Wetting Down · Ridges · Packing the Chest ·
  Before the Light · The Sheet · A Hand's Width · Boots On.* No `Chorus 2`, no `Bridge`, no address to a room.
- **EMO headers** name emotions **held by the man toward the work** — *absorbed tenderness · steady craft ·
  unbothered amusement · tender competence · quiet certainty* — never audience-facing emotions
  (*uplifting, anthemic, triumphant* are banned from every EMO header in this pair).
- **SFX** is the work's own sound, not the world's: the **wet clap of a flat palm on packed sand**. ⛔ No
  surf sample, no gull sample, no crowd — those are all in EXCLUDE. The sea is present only as a *musical*
  event, which is the whole seam.

### F5 — THE ONE SEAM: funk vocabulary welded to a chamber-jazz bed, **placed at the tide**
One dominant material: **modal spiritual jazz drift** — Rhodes, upright, brushes, tenor, 86 BPM, sacred
changes, nothing hurrying. One intruder, and only one: **the physical vocabulary of funk**, carried
case-exact from Source 2 — *"superhumanly funky bass"* describing an upright played chamber-quiet and felt in
the chest rather than heard, and *"downright salacious slow jam"* describing a tenor's breathy dusk phrasing
over modal changes that have no business being salacious.
⭐ **THE SUBJECT ACTS ACROSS THE SEAM.** The join is placed at the moment the sea reaches the plinth, and it
is a **formal** seam the listener is allowed to notice: the long free-verse line collapses for four lines
into short physical phrases (*"Palm flat. Press. Lift."*) — the groove grows hips exactly where the tide
arrives — and then the long line returns. **The seam is in the FORM and the LYRIC, not only in the
production spec** (GRAIN LAW compliance: a Somatic objection answered only in the spec is not answered).

### F6 — THE COMPOSITE, WITH P05's LITERAL ELEMENT AND ITS **NAMED DEFECT**
| element | P05 realisation | scale |
|---|---|---|
| **CONTINUOUS FAINT BED** | ⭐ **LITERAL — whole song.** Brushes circling the snare head from second zero to the end; Rhodes and upright never both absent. | whole song |
| **TWO FLASHES** | figurative → two magnesium flash-powder cracks, first at the end of section 4, second late; each a brief horn crest that settles. **Neither at the very start.** | brief |
| **ONE REFLECTION (ghost echo)** | figurative → the lion's face in the fingernail-thin sheet a spent wave leaves: **inverted, darker, further off, and it arrives before the sea does.** Returned quieter in the outro as a goodnight. | section |
| **ONE VISIBLE JOIN** | the funk/chamber seam above, at the tide | single-bar |

⛔ **THE NAMED DEFECT, QUOTED, AND IT IS NON-OPTIONAL:** *"a woody upright bass, felt in the chest more than
heard,"* plus *"a room return a half-second longer than the space around her would give,"* with *"brushes
staying on the snare head throughout."* **Without the defect the bed becomes the song** — that is Panel C's
finding and it is the single largest render risk this pair carries. A perfectly recorded, correctly reverbed
spiritual-jazz bed is elevator music with a lion in it.

### F7 — FORM: long-lined free verse, slow accretion, **and the rhyme arrives like a wave**
Terminal rhyme on **every fourth line only**. The scheme is a chain, not a stanza: **each fourth line rhymes
with the previous fourth line**, and the chain is **pulled into the `-ing` family every time it approaches a
refrain**, because the refrain's fourth line is always the word **morning**. The rhyme therefore lands at a
fixed interval **whether or not the sentence is ready for it** — which is the tide, in prosody.
⚠️ **FLAG, DECLARED IN ADVANCE:** a 1-in-4 rhyme density will measure **below** `rhyme_return_floor: 0.30`
on `scripts/measure_soundcraft.py → strict_end_rhyme()`. That is the assigned form, not a defect. **THE DEBT
IS DISCHARGED BY THE REFRAIN**, which returns byte-identical four times, plus the anchor line *"Hold your
face for me, lion. I have got your face."* returning inside and outside it. Removal is a debt; this is what
returns in its place, named in writing per L21.
⚠️ **Second declared FLAG:** `mean_words_per_line_ceiling: 7.5`. Long-lined free verse is the assignment;
this pair will sit at or just over it. Measured, reported, not repaired.

### F8 — THE ONE-FACT RULE, AND WHERE THE HINGE IS
Exactly one numeric fact is sung: **eleven feet**, spelled in words, at the emotional hinge, and it is
**responded to, not recited** — *"Eleven feet, and I paced it out in boots… so I will spend it here, on my
knees in the sand."* Every other measurement in the drafts (nine ridges, seven this morning, sixty minutes,
three faults) was **cut on sight** during drafting; only distance survives.
⚠️ **Exception, flagged not hidden:** **V2 puts the fact in its refrain**, so it returns five times in that
variation. It is still ONE fact, and it is answered in the same breath every time (*"…and I am in no
hurry"*). Raised as **FLAG F-3** at step 10 for the human read.

### F9 — WHAT THIS PAIR MAY NOT TOUCH (collision guards, run-wide + P05-local)
`water` (P02 owns it — P05 uses **tide** and **sea**) · `sky` (P03) · `current` (P06) · `dark` in any title ·
any equipment inventory (P01/P06 territory) · any audience, crowd or room (P01's second person is the room;
P05 has **no room at all**) · any blackout, power, generator or electricity imagery. **The unpowered/unheard
distinction does not apply to this song and must not be smuggled in:** nobody is being silenced here. A man
is finishing a mane before the sea arrives.
