All four angles are derived from **this pair's concept only** — a woman having a poor night in a beautiful empty corridor at the end of a shift. No angle is imported from another pair, and the four differ in **section map, temporal frame, dominant lexical field, and where the anger sits**.

## ANGLE V1 — **THE SHIFT, IN REAL TIME** · working title *"You Can Hear Me Fine"*
**Temporal frame:** the last twenty-odd minutes, forward, once.
**Lexical field:** signal path — cable, mic, level, loop, pedal, case.
**Where the anger sits:** in the *equipment working perfectly.* Every functioning thing is a small insult.
**Section map:** Intro · V1 · Pre · Chorus 1 (FACT) · V2 · Self-Address · Chorus 2 (ACCUSATION) · V3 (the hinge, one person) · Chorus 3 (KEEP GOING) · Outro.
**Distinguishing move:** the most literal of the four. Nothing is metaphor. It is a person doing a job badly with good gear.

### Draft fragments (V1)
> Cable in. / Cable in. / Amp light on. / Nothing broken. / Nothing broken here.
> Tile to the roof. / Tile to the floor. / Every note / comes back to me / bigger than I sent.
> The room is good. / The room is very good. / I am not. / I am not.
> Missed the change. / Missed it again. / Hand went early. / Hand went early. / I kept it in.
> Coil it tight. / Coil it tight. / Tile's still perfect. / Back here tomorrow. / **Nothing about this is fine.**

## ANGLE V2 — **THE ROOM IS HAVING A BETTER NIGHT THAN I AM** · working title *"The Tiles Do The Rest"*
**Temporal frame:** suspended — the whole shift held as one long tail.
**Lexical field:** the architecture — tile, grout, glaze, ceiling, bend, grate, glass, the tail, the bloom.
**Where the anger sits:** in the *beauty of the room.* The corridor is described lovingly and every loving line is an accusation.
**Section map:** Intro · V1 · Chorus 1 (FACT) · V2 · Self-Address · Chorus 2 (ACCUSATION) · Break (the room alone) · Chorus 3 (KEEP GOING) · Outro.
**Distinguishing move:** this is the variation where SOURCE 2 lands hardest — the reverb as *downright salacious slow jam*, *steamy*, gorgeous and wasted. The prettiness is the payload and the injury.

### Draft fragments (V2)
> Glaze on the tile. / Glaze on the tile. / Warm as a mouth. / Nobody in it.
> This room could hold / a real one. / Holds me instead. / Holds me instead.
> Sings back better / than it got. / Every time. / Every time.
> It doesn't care / who's in it. / That's the part. / That's the whole part.

## ANGLE V3 — **THE ONE WHO CAME ON PURPOSE** · working title *"She Stood By The Map"*
**Temporal frame:** narrow — Tuuli arrives, stays, leaves; the rest is peripheral.
**Lexical field:** the body of the other person — coat, boots, the wall map, the glass, the distance down the corridor.
**Where the anger sits:** in the *refusal to weigh her.* Enough / not enough, adjacent, unranked, and it will not be settled.
**Section map:** Intro · V1 · Pre · Chorus 1 (FACT) · V2 (she arrives) · Self-Address · Chorus 2 (ACCUSATION) · V3 (she leaves) · Chorus 3 (KEEP GOING) · Outro.
**Distinguishing move:** the hinge numeric fact — **"one person came"** — sits at the exact centre and the two lines after it are the two incompatible valuations. The most intimate variation and the coldest ending.
⛔ Guard: Tuuli is never thanked, never idealised, never made into a lesson. She stands there. She goes.

### Draft fragments (V3)
> Coat still zipped. / Stood by the map. / Read it twice. / Didn't read it.
> **One person came.** / That is enough. / That is not enough. / Both are true. / I won't choose.
> Didn't clap. / Good. / Don't clap. / I'd have heard that too.
> Coat went round the bend. / Sound went with her. / Sound went with her.

## ANGLE V4 — **TOMORROW, THE SAME** · working title *"I Coil The Cable Wrong"*
**Temporal frame:** iterative — this night is every night; the shift is a habit, not an event.
**Lexical field:** repetition and habit — again, same, back, still, the wrong coil, the same eleven minutes late.
**Where the anger sits:** in *continuation.* The risk stated plainly: this is simply the job now. Ends angriest of the four.
**Section map:** Intro · V1 · Chorus 1 (FACT) · V2 · Self-Address · Chorus 2 (ACCUSATION) · V3 · Chorus 3 (KEEP GOING) · Outro · Coda (hard).
**Distinguishing move:** the only variation in which the *future* appears, and it appears as a threat rather than a hope. **The last section is the loudest and the fastest.**

### Draft fragments (V4)
> Same tile. / Same hour. / Same wrong coil. / Same wrong coil.
> Hands know the way / without asking. / That's not skill. / That's a rut.
> I'll be back. / I'll be back. / Not as a promise.
> Set it up again. / Play it worse. / Play it worse. / **Still won't be enough of nothing.**
