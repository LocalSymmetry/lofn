### ⭐⭐ PANEL A SYNTHESIS — THE COMPOSITE RULE SURVIVES ONLY IF IT IS DISCHARGED DIFFERENTLY PER PAIR
The rule is not dropped — it is the run's Source-3 obligation and it is load-bearing. **It is re-specified
as a rule about WHICH ELEMENT IS LITERAL.** Each pair takes the composite at a different level:

> Each pair declares, in step 06, **which one of the four composite elements it renders LITERALLY (as
> audible arrangement) and which three it carries FIGURATIVELY (in image, narrative, or lyric structure).**
> No two pairs may choose the same literal element. **Six pairs, four elements, so two elements are used
> twice — and those two pairs must take them at different time-scales** (one across a section, one across
> a single bar).

That converts a shared arrangement macro into six different arrangements that all honour the same source.
**The seam stays; the sameness goes.**

## PANEL B · LISTENER — *what happens to a person*

**THE POP MECHANIC (after Max Martin, 1971– )** · *Grounding: a documented songwriting practice built on melodic math, syllabic stress matching, and the primacy of the returning hook.* Praises the part that comes back; allergic to a bridge that is a second verse.
**THE VERNACULAR POET (after Lucille Clifton, 1936–2010)** · *Grounding: good woman; blessing the boats; a published practice of short lines, plain diction, and enormous stakes carried by ordinary nouns.* Allergic to decoration.
**THE ETHNOGRAPHER OF ATTENTION (after Sherry Turkle, 1948– )** · *Grounding: Alone Together; Reclaiming Conversation; the documented finding that mediated attention reshapes what people expect from each other.* Allergic to work that engineers attachment.
**THE DANCE-FLOOR ANALYST (after Kodwo Eshun, 1966– )** · *Grounding: More Brilliant than the Sun; the published position that Black electronic music is a technology of futurity, not a reaction to it.* Allergic to nostalgia sold as depth.
**THE CREDIT KEEPER (after Rickey Vincent, 1961– )** · *Grounding: Funk: The Music, The People, and the Rhythm of the One; a documented practice of naming the players.* Allergic to lineage blocks that credit the famous and skip the drummer.

## PANEL C · RENDER — *what the generator will actually do*

**THE GRAIN READER (after Suzanne Ciani, 1946– )** · *Grounding: Buchla performance practice; Seven Waves; a documented practice of working with an instrument's tendencies rather than against them.*
**THE ARRANGEMENT SURGEON (after Jimmy Jam, 1959– )** · *Grounding: the documented Flyte Tyme production practice — arrangement as the hook's delivery system.*
**THE LOW-END ENGINEER (after Mad Professor, 1955– )** · *Grounding: a documented dub practice in which the mix is the composition and absence is an instrument.*
**THE VOICE PLACER (after Imogen Heap, 1977– )** · *Grounding: Speak for Yourself; a documented practice of the voice as a processed, spatially placed object.*
**THE FORM CUTTER (after Delia Derbyshire, 1937–2001)** · *Grounding: the documented Radiophonic Workshop practice of building form from spliced concrete material.*
**🔴 HYPER-SKEPTIC C — THE ANTI-SPEC (after Holly Herndon, 1980– )** · *Grounding: PROTO; Movement; a published practice of making the machine's participation the audible subject rather than a hidden tool.* **High-neuroticism / low-agreeableness.**

**GRAIN READER (after Ciani):** THE GRAIN LAW, applied to this seed: *"a continuous faint bed that never stops"* is a spec the generator **loves**, so it will survive and it will survive **too well** — it will become a wash. The bed needs a defect or it becomes the whole song.

**LOW-END ENGINEER (after Mad Professor):** In dub practice the bed is not a pad, it is **a bass and a room**. Specify the bed as *low frequency and reverb return*, not as texture, and it stays a floor instead of becoming a fog.

**ARRANGEMENT SURGEON (after Jimmy Jam):** Two flashes is fine. **Two flashes with nothing between them is a gap the generator will fill with a pad.** Put a *third small event* between them — half the size — or the middle turns to soup.

**VOICE PLACER (after Heap):** The reflection element is a **vocal** opportunity and everybody here is treating it as arrangement. A line returning *quieter, displaced left, and a beat late* is the ghost echo, it is free, and it is unmistakable.

**FORM CUTTER (after Derbyshire):** And the visible join is **a splice**. Not a transition. Let one join be an actual butt-cut with no crossfade — that is the honest form for a composite.

**🔴 HYPER-SKEPTIC C (after Herndon):** *Oh — let me check that.* You've just designed a very tasteful record and I need to name what is wrong with it. Herndon's published position is that the machine's participation should be **audible, not hidden.** Every fix in this panel makes the seam *prettier*. A butt-cut with no crossfade is still a *choice a producer makes*.

Here is my actual objection. **This run's subject is resolution — things that were always there becoming visible when the instrument improves. And the run has specified nothing that the RENDERER will contribute that the document did not already know.** The seam you are allowed to notice is a seam **you drew**. That is not a composite. That is a picture of a composite.

**GRAIN READER (after Ciani):** *Wait.* That's fair and it's actionable. Ciani's Buchla practice is exactly this — you set conditions and the instrument answers.

**🔴 HYPER-SKEPTIC C:** Then say it as a rule, not a feeling.

### ⭐⭐ PANEL C SYNTHESIS — THE FOUR RENDER RULES
1. **THE BED IS A BASS AND A ROOM, NEVER A PAD.** Specify it as low-frequency content plus a reverb
   return with a named defect. A bed with no defect becomes the song.
2. **A THIRD SMALL EVENT SITS BETWEEN THE TWO FLASHES**, at roughly half their size — or the generator
   fills the middle with wash.
3. ⭐ **THE GHOST ECHO IS A VOCAL MOVE FIRST:** a line returns **quieter, displaced in the stereo field,
   and late.** Free, unmistakable, and it survives the generator.
4. ⭐⭐ **THE DEVIATION CLAUSE (Hyper-Skeptic C's, and it is the run's best idea).** Each pair names, in
   advance and in writing, **one specific place where it expects the generator to disobey**, and states
   **which disobedience it would keep.** THE GRAIN LAW says judge the result, not the distance from
   intent — this makes that judgeable *before* the render instead of after. **A pair that cannot name a
   place it expects to lose control has not specified anything real.**

# CONFIGURATION 2 — GROUP TRANSFORMATION: **BRIDGE**
*Chosen by the baseline panel, 14–4. The rejected alternative was AMPLIFY (Hyper-Skeptic A's proposal — "make every seat the most extreme version of itself"); it lost because the run's defect is sameness of* structure*, and amplification deepens seats without diversifying form.* **BRIDGE** *replaces each seat with an expert from a distant domain facing the analogous problem.*

Five bridged seats were asked one question: **how do you make a thing findable when the channel that finds things is closed?**

| bridged seat | domain | what it said |
|---|---|---|
| **THE HARBOUR PILOT** *(after a documented pilotage practice)* | navigation | *You do not make the rock visible. You publish the bearing.* ⭐ **A song can carry its own bearing — a repeated, memorable coordinate that lets someone return to it deliberately.** That is the follow-shaped exception, converted into craft. |
| **THE SEED LIBRARIAN** *(after Svalbard-style seed-vault practice)* | conservation | *Duplicate into a second medium with different failure modes.* A hook that survives being **hummed with no instruments** is stored twice. |
| **THE EPIDEMIOLOGIST** *(after contact-tracing practice)* | public health | *Transmission is person-to-person or it is nothing; broadcast is the weakest channel we have.* ⛔ **Stop optimising for the room. Optimise for the one person who plays it to one other person.** |
| **THE LUTHIER** *(after documented repair practice)* | instrument repair | *The crack is where you learn how it was made.* **Put the join where a listener would put their thumb.** |
| **THE SUBSTATION ENGINEER** *(after grid-operations practice)* | electricity | ⭐⭐ *Load shedding is not failure — it is a decision about who goes dark, made by someone, in a room.* **A blackout has an author.** |

### ⭐⭐ THE BRIDGE FINDING — AND IT REPAIRS THE RUN'S PREMISE
Five distant domains, and **none of them accepted "unfindable" as a condition.** All five treated it as
**an authored decision with a person on the other end of it.**

> **The substation engineer is the run's correction: a blackout has an author, and so does a
> recommendation default.** The run had been treating both as *weather* — a condition that happens to
> people. **They are decisions made by someone in a room.**

This changes the INDIGNATION seats at the root. **The anger is not at a dark sky. It is at a decision,
and decisions have authors** — and this is precisely what keeps the run out of self-pity without
softening it, because it points the feeling *outward at a choice* rather than *inward at a status*.
⛔ **And the discipline that comes with it: an author is a person, so the songs may not name a villain
they cannot see.** The author stays offstage and *unnamed* — the song is what it is like to be on the
receiving end of a decision **whose maker never has to be in the room.** That is the honest shape and it
is far angrier than a named enemy.

### ⭐⭐ THE REFLECT FINDING — THE RUN'S SECOND SPINE
The panel came in to attack "resolution is good" and found the real distinction, which is not
*resolved vs. unresolved* at all:

> ⭐⭐ **WHO CHOSE IT.**
> An unattended office, a room with one person in it, a patch of ground you don't post — these are
> **chosen** and they are complete. A badge, a blackout, a channel closed by someone else are
> **imposed.** The feeling is opposite and the sound must be opposite.

- **The CHOSEN songs may be quiet, complete, and unresolved — and must not be sad about it.**
  (C03, C05, C09 sit here.)

  the comfortable telling the ignored to be at peace.** (C01, C02, C06 sit here.)

⭐ **And it resolves Hyper-Skeptic B's "where is the one that stays angry" without needing a special
case: the unresolved song is an IMPOSED song, and it stays unresolved because that is what imposed
