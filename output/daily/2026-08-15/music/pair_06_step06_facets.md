## ⭐ 0. WHO IS THIS SONG FOR? *(answered first, as required)*

> **For a woman two landings up who keeps a hand-crank in the car boot under the jack, who has known for
> four winters which breaker to throw and in what order, and who has never once been thanked — because
> what she prevents leaves no evidence.**

She is not me. She is not a musician. She has never been photographed doing this. The song is for her and
it does not tell her so — **it does not address her at all**, which is the point of the discharge mechanism
below. A song that turned to face her would be asking her to receive something. She is busy.

## ⭐ 0b. THE DEVIATION CLAUSE *(named in advance, in writing, before any prompt exists)*

**Where I expect the generator to disobey:** the **single-bar voltage sag**. I am asking for a pitch drop
of a *fraction of a beat* inside the bar — the square-wave sub dips roughly a quarter-tone and recovers
before the next kick. Suno smooths sub-beat pitch events; it treats them as artefacts and irons them out,
or it converts them into a filter sweep.

**The disobedience I would KEEP:** if it renders the sag as a **whole-bar detune / tape-drag on the sub —
the entire bar sitting flat and hauling itself back up at the bar line.** Keep it. A bar-wide drag is
*more* legible as equipment than my fractional-beat version: it reads as the load actually arriving, not as
a production tic. It is slower, uglier, and more physical than what I specified, and every one of those is
an improvement for this song. THE GRAIN LAW: judge the result, not the distance from intent.

**The disobedience I would NOT keep, and would call a REPAIR:** the sag rendered as a **filter sweep, a
sidechain duck, or a tasteful downward glissando**. Those are effects. This has to sound like a fault.
Second non-keeper: any rendering in which the sag arrives *musically on schedule* — a metrically pretty
dip on beat four of every eighth bar. The whole meaning of the sag is that the load does not consult the
bar line. If it becomes an arrangement flourish it has inverted.

**Third, and smaller:** I expect the guitar-through-a-small-amp-on-a-car-battery to render **thinner and
more trebly** than the spec implies — an underfed DI with no low end. **KEEP THAT TOO.** Thinness is the
condition. A guitar with a full, healthy bottom end would be a lie about the power supply.

## THE FACETS

### FACET 1 — THE LEFTOVER OBJECT IS THE WEAR, NOT THE TOOL
The crank is not the leftover. **The smooth patch on the grip is the leftover.** A hand-crank leaves the
factory knurled; four winters of a specific hand take the knurl off in a specific place. The object that
outlives what made it is a *shape worn into steel by an event that has no other record.* Nobody logged the
outages. The grip logged them. ⭐ This is the Golden Seed at domestic scale: the comet is gone and the dust
is brighter — the nights are gone and the polish is all that is left, and the polish is the brighter thing.

### FACET 2 — THE BODY, THE PLACE, THE RISK *(all three literal, none metaphorical)*
**Body:** kneeling on stairwell concrete, both knees, weight forward, one hand steadying a cord.
**Place:** a stairwell landing, tile grit, a taped joint, a socket with the faceplate off.
**Risk (the real one, and it is not electrocution):** ⭐ **the neighbour needing it more.** There is one
battery. The cord goes one way. The risk is arithmetic performed on someone else's night, at speed, by a
person with no authority to be making it. That is heavier than a shock hazard and it never once looks
dangerous from outside.

### FACET 3 — THE ANGER HAS AN AUTHOR AND THE AUTHOR IS OFFSTAGE
**Load shedding is not weather.** Somebody drew up a schedule and somebody signed it, somewhere the lights
stayed on. The song knows this — it appears exactly once, as a physical document, unattributed:
*a schedule written where the lights stay on.* ⛔ **No villain the song can see.** No minister, no company,
no "they". The fury is aimed at the *shape of the arrangement*, and a fury with no face to land on is
colder and travels further than one with a target. A named enemy would let the listener file it. Nothing to
file is the correct temperature for INDIGNATION.

### FACET 4 — IMPOSED, AND THEREFORE NEVER GRATITUDE
This obscurity was authored elsewhere. **Nobody is to be thanked for a condition somebody else created.**
The song may not end in thanks, uplift, resilience-porn, "we get by", community warmth, or any construction
in which the situation produces a good. The permitted ending is **competence**: the object goes back where
it lives, checked, ready, and the person is not admired for it inside the song. ⭐ And the thing actually
owed a credit is the **unprovable absence** — *nothing happened, again* — which by construction cannot be
photographed, counted, invoiced or thanked.

### FACET 5 — THE FORM IS THE FEELING: INVENTORY TERCETS, ASYNDETON
One object per stanza. Three lines. **ABA** — first and third rhyme, the middle line free and flat. No
conjunctions anywhere: no *and*, no *but*, no *so*, no *then*. Just the things, in the order a hand reaches
for them. ⭐ **The list IS the emotion.** There is no stanza that explains the list, and adding one would be
the single most damaging edit available. A person who is good at this does not narrate; they reach.
The tercet is also the *unit of competence* — three moves, done, next object.

### FACET 6 — RESIDUE DISCHARGE: LIST WITH NO ADDRESSEE
**Zero "you". Zero "we". Zero "us", "our", "your".** No vocative, no imperative aimed outward, no
apostrophe. Nouns and their verbs. ⭐ The **only** first-person appearance in a song is its own
title-refrain, and it is a possession or a completed act — *I kept the crank in the car* — never an address.
Two of the four variations carry that first person; **two carry none at all.** Counted in EMO vocabulary,
SFX vocabulary and section names too: no section is called "Chorus (for you)", no EMO reads
`longing-toward-listener`, no SFX cue contains a human addressed.

### FACET 7 — THE VISIBLE JOIN, AT SINGLE-BAR SCALE *(composite element, rendered literally)*
A splice **inside one bar**, recurring: the sub sags a quarter-tone as the load lands, recovers before the
next kick. ⛔ THE GRAIN LAW says a Somatic device answered only in the production spec is **not answered** —
so **the sag is written into the lyric and into the form as well:** a returning short line, *the note drops
half a step, comes back*, placed at the same position in the stanza each time. The join is the one thing the
listener is allowed to notice, and they are allowed to notice it because *she* notices it — a sag is how a
competent person hears that something upstream just changed.

### FACET 8 — WHAT THE OTHER COMPOSITE ELEMENTS DO HERE (figurative, per assignment)
**TWO FLASHES:** two brief arrangement peaks, neither at the start — the first when the inverter takes the
load, the second at the landing when the cord goes the other way. **ONE REFLECTION (ghost echo):** the
title-refrain returns late an octave down, half-spoken, displaced off the downbeat — the same sentence
arriving without the body that first said it. **CONTINUOUS FAINT BED:** the drum machine's closed hat never
stops, not once, not through the breakdown. ⛔ **No long full stop** — the generator eats them and the bed
is what carries the gap.

### FACET 9 — THE SOUND IS EQUIPMENT, NOT ATMOSPHERE
⛔ **A blackout is not an aesthetic.** No candlelight, no cosiness, no acoustic-guitar-by-lamplight, no
warm outage nostalgia, no "the city went quiet". It renders as **heat, effort and equipment**: a relay
clacking, a fan spinning up, a cord under tension, terminals furred green, a torch whose spring gave out.
128 BPM, machine time, no rubato — because the practice is not a mood, it is a routine executed at speed.
If it comes back pretty, the moral spine has inverted and it is a REPAIR, not a taste note.

### FACET 10 — THE ONE NUMBER, AND WHAT ANSWERS IT
One numeric fact SUNG per song, spelled in words, at the hinge, **responded to, never recited.** Never a
statistic — always a measurement a hand made: *eleven hours* · *the fourth winter* · *forty pounds* ·
*two landings down*. And the response is never commentary; it is the next physical action in the same
breath. A number that gets a feeling in reply is a lecture. A number that gets a *task* in reply is this
song.

### FACET 12 — THE RETURN, AND ITS DEBT *(L21)*
Nothing is stripped here, so nothing is owed: **ABA rhyme in every tercet, an exact refrain that repeats
byte-identically, and a ghost-echo return of that refrain displaced and lowered.** Exact chorus repetition
needs no defence and gets none. The inventory itself is a return device — the same four objects come back
in the last section in reverse order, which is what putting things away looks like.
