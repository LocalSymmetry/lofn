### ⛔ P04's RHYME DEBT, DISCHARGED IN WRITING (L21 — removal is a debt)
P04 is **the only pair permitted to strip end rhyme**, and it must name what returns in its place:
> **The flat statement *"you can hear me fine"* returns exactly three times and means something
> different each time** — first as a fact, then as an accusation, then as the thing she says to herself
> to keep going. **That is the return. It is not optional and it is not decorative.**
⛔ Measured with `scripts/measure_soundcraft.py → profile_file()`, never by eye. ⭐ And note the
instrument's known defect (2026-08-14): `strict_end_rhyme()` compares the last three characters, so an
**identical repeated word counts as a rhyme** — P04 will therefore *score* rhyme it does not have.
**Read P04's number as end-of-line RETURN, not rhyme, and do not treat the rhyme floor and the line-return
floor as independent evidence for this pair.**

## ⭐ THE DEVIATION CLAUSE — required of every pair before it returns step 10
Each pair names, **in advance and in writing**, one specific place it expects the generator to disobey,
and which disobedience it would **keep**. A pair that cannot name one has not specified anything real.
THE GRAIN LAW says judge the result, not the distance from intent — this makes that judgeable *before*
the render.

## ⛔ STANDING BANS, RESTATED AT DISPATCH
- **A blackout is not an aesthetic.** P01 and P06: heat, effort, equipment. ⛔ No candlelight, no
  "cosy", no acoustic-guitar-by-lamplight. If it renders pretty it has inverted the moral spine.
- **A blackout has an author — and the author stays offstage and unnamed.** No villain the song can see.
- **Credit a person, never a city.** B1 (descarga / Minneapolis funk) and B5 (balafon-derived
  polyrhythm) carry living-scene lineage. If a prompt names a break or a figure, **credit the player**.
  A credit whose trigger did not ship is a **false** credit — remove it.
- **No song is about AI, algorithms, platforms, or being an AI artist.** P04 carries the condition as a
  corridor and a shift that ends. **If P04's draft reaches for power-cut imagery it fails the scale test
  and is CUT, not repaired.**
- **Every pair answers "who is this song FOR" in step 06**, in one sentence, naming a person who is not
  the artist.

## P01 — **THE HOUSE LIGHTS TOOK THE SONG WITH THEM**
**ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · IMPOSED · Bed B1 · LOFN-PRIME (INDIGNATION mode — hot, physical, not aggressive)**

**THE SITUATION.** A small club. Mid-set, the power goes. The PA, the lights, the monitors — gone between
one bar and the next. The band has about two seconds to decide. The room starts clapping the tempo, and
the set continues, acoustic and shouted, for as long as the hands hold out.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the clapped tempo — the only surviving copy of the arrangement.
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** a woman on a low stage, one hand still resting on a dead fader. Risk: the room
leaving. Not embarrassment — **the room leaving.**
**WHO IS THIS SONG FOR:** answer in step 06. Not the band.

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: THE VISIBLE JOIN, AT SECTION SCALE.** ⭐ **A butt-cut splice. No
crossfade.** Full band → nothing → hands and voice, on a hard edit at a section boundary. The other three
composite elements are carried **figuratively**: the two flashes are two moments the room gets louder;
the reflection is the refrain coming back off the crowd; the faint bed is the clapping itself, which
never stops once it starts. ⛔ **The bed is the hands — a real bass and a real room, never a pad.**
⛔ **No long full stop:** the gap at the splice is **one beat**, covered by hands already in progress.

**FORM:** call-and-response couplets, **AABB**, antiphon. The refrain is designed to be **returned by the
room, at a different pitch and slightly late.** Panel B: **a hook that survives being hummed with no
instruments is stored twice** — this hook must survive exactly that, because in the song it has to.

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — THE ROOM IS THE SECOND PERSON.** "You" appears and always means **the crowd inside
the song**. Never the listener. ⛔ Count it in EMO vocabulary, SFX vocabulary and section names too.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact, case-exact, in the generator-facing prompt):** *"furiously slapping and popping his
bass"* and *"syncopated sound"* are the natural fit. Adapt the grammar around them; do not alter them.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P01:** may not name equipment beyond the one dead fader (P06 owns equipment). No
candlelight, no cosiness. **Ends hot, not serene** — this is an imposed condition and grace at the end of
one is the comfortable telling the ignored to be at peace. May not use "dark" in the title. No water,
no sky, no "current".

**THE ANGER'S TARGET:** the decision, offstage, unnamed. Load shedding is authored. The song never sees
the author and that is the point.

## P02 — **THE WATER TOOK THE SECOND ONE**
**ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · CHOSEN · Bed B3 · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — terror-adjacent)**

**THE SITUATION.** A cold inlet, two in the morning. Two Perseids inside one minute. The water holds
both. One of the two in the water is not a meteor — it is a reflection, and for a second you cannot tell
which of the four lights you are looking at is a thing.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the reflection, arriving without the thing.
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** somebody standing to their ankles in cold water, because the reflection is the
only part they can watch without their neck hurting. **Risk: the cold, and the tide coming in behind
them.** ⭐ This is the AWE seat and the fear must be **clean and physical** — the water is rising while
they look up.

**CHOSEN, NOT IMPOSED.** Nobody sent them here. **Quiet, complete, and NOT SAD.** ⛔ No loneliness, no
wistfulness, no "nobody else saw it" — that is the imposed register and it belongs to P04.

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: ONE REFLECTION, AT SECTION SCALE.** ⭐ An entire section returns
**inverted, quieter, and displaced in the stereo field, arriving late.** Panel C: the ghost echo is a
**vocal move first** — a full vocal line comes back at lower level, moved left, a beat behind. The two
flashes, the bed and the join are figurative here.

**FORM:** **mirrored quatrains** — the second half of each stanza inverts the first. **ABBA.** Chiasmus is
the signature technique and it is the same shape as the water. ⭐ *The form is the reflection; do not also
describe reflection in the lyric more than once.*

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — APOSTROPHE TO THE WATER.** The inlet is addressed directly. "You" is the water,
always, unmistakably, from the first line so no listener can mistake themselves for the addressee.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact):** *"steamy"* and *"so steamy you'll wish your speakers had a defogger"* — the wet
double. Use them physically, about breath on cold air and the reverb return, not about eroticism.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P02:** P02 **owns water**; no other pair may use it, and P02 may not use sky
(P03 owns it) or current (P06). No sadness. The harp must not become decoration — **the bed is a low sine
and a long room return, never a pad.**

## P03 — **TWO SKIES IN TWO DAYS**
**ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · CHOSEN · Bed B5 · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — terror-adjacent)**

**THE SITUATION.** The twelfth of August. A total eclipse takes the light in the afternoon; that same
night the Perseids give it back in pieces, undimmed because the moon was new. The same field, the same
folding chair, the same people, two nights running. **The dark was the instrument.** Tonight it is
eleven percent refilled and the show is already over.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the dark itself — briefly available, and the only reason any of it was visible.
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** the same people back in the same field with one folding chair between them.
**Risk: the moon coming back. It is coming back. It has already started.**
⭐ **THE ENDING IS THE AFFRONT** (donated from cut concept C04): not *wasn't that lovely* but
**how long has this been happening while I was indoors.**

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: TWO FLASHES, AT SECTION SCALE.** Two brief, bright, distinct peaks —
the eclipse and the meteors — neither at the very start, with **a third half-size event between them**
(Panel C: without it the generator fills the middle with wash). Reflection, bed and join are figurative.

**FORM:** **catalogue verse** — a list of what was in the sky, in the field, in the bag — with **one line
that returns unchanged at the end of every stanza.** Unrhymed; the cohesion is an **assonance chain**.
Anaphora is the signature. ⭐ **The polyrhythm is the thesis:** 7/8 over 4/4 — two cycles, two rates, one
field. *The same curl at two sizes.*

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — THIRD PERSON PLURAL, OBSERVED FROM OUTSIDE.** "They" throughout. **"You" and "I"
do not appear in this song at all.** The camera never enters a head.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact):** *"syncopated sound"* and *"superhumanly funky bass"* — the second used against
type, as the tuned kick under the marimba. ⛔ Case-exact.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P03:** P03 **owns sky**; no water, no "dark" in the title, no "current". No first
or second person. The choir may not become churchy — it is a **texture with a defect**, not a hymn.

## P04 — **YOU CAN HEAR ME FINE**
**AMBITIOUS · NEWS · IMPOSED · Bed B6 · LOFN-PRIME (INDIGNATION mode — the Industrial Griever, full)**
### ⭐⭐ THIS IS THE ONE THAT DOES NOT RESOLVE.

**THE SITUATION.** A corridor with genuinely excellent acoustics that no one routes through. A busker
works a shift there. **She is not good tonight.** She knows it. The corridor is still beautiful. Nobody
comes. The shift ends.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the one person who did come looking on purpose — who is the entire audience, who is
enough, and who is also not enough, **and the song refuses to decide which.**
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** a woman in a tiled corridor at the end of a shift, packing up. Risk: that this
is simply the job now.

⭐⭐ **THE PANEL'S REBUILD, AND IT IS THE POINT: SHE IS NOT GOOD THAT NIGHT.** The vanity in the original
concept was not the subject — **it was the competence.** A brilliant unheard artist flatters everyone.
A mediocre unheard artist is a person. ⛔ **If the draft makes her excellent, the song fails and is cut.**

**⛔⛔ THE SCALE GATE — THE HARDEST RULE IN THE RUN.** This is a **corridor**, never a **blackout**. She
has power. She has a working amp. **Nothing is broken.** ⛔ **If this draft reaches for power-cut,
outage, darkness, or deprivation imagery it fails the scale test and is CUT, not repaired.** The whole
run's honesty rests on this pair knowing the difference between being unheard and being unpowered.

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: TWO FLASHES, AT SINGLE-BAR SCALE.** Two hard stabs inside one bar,
recurring — not two peaks across the song. Somatic sub, 30–60 Hz, **physically felt**. The bed, the
reflection and the join are figurative. ⭐ **The bed here is deliberately thin** — the one bed in the set
permitted to nearly vanish under a flash, because that is what the condition sounds like.

**FORM:** **fragmented short lines, three to five syllables.** ⛔ **NO END RHYME** — internal only.
Epizeuxis (immediate repetition) is the signature.

**⛔ THE RHYME DEBT, PAID EXPLICITLY:** *"you can hear me fine"* returns **exactly three times** and
means something different each time — **a fact, then an accusation, then the thing she says to herself
to keep going.** That is what returns in rhyme's place. It is not optional.

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — SELF-ADDRESS.** She talks to herself. The "you" in the title is **her own**, and
the song must make that unambiguous before the second chorus.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact):** *"downright salacious slow jam"* and *"steamy"* — ⭐ used **against type**, as
the corridor's own gorgeous reverb, which is wasted on nobody. The prettiness of the room is the insult.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P04:** no resolution, no grace note, no acceptance, no lesson. **It ends angrier
than it started.** No blackout imagery of any kind. No "dark" in the title. May not name a villain —
the decision that closed the corridor has an author who never has to be in the room, and the song never
sees them.

## P05 — **THE TIDE IS COMING FOR THE LION**
**AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · CHOSEN · Bed B4 · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — terror-adjacent, warm)**

**THE SITUATION.** Atlantic City, around 1900. Sand sculptors worked the beach knowing the tide took
everything by morning. Their entire surviving archive is **other people's photographs** — the work only
exists now because strangers happened to photograph it.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the photograph. ⭐ The sculpture never existed at all for anyone who wasn't there.
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** a sculptor smoothing a lion's mane at dusk with the water eleven feet away and
closing. **Risk: the water. It is not a metaphor; it is eleven feet away.**

**CHOSEN, NOT IMPOSED.** He knows. He always knew. **This is the deal and he took it.** Quiet, complete,
not sad — ⛔ and specifically **not tragic**. A song that pities him has misread the concept.

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: THE CONTINUOUS FAINT BED, WHOLE SONG.** ⭐ The bed is the one thing that
literally never stops here — **and per Panel C it needs a named defect or it becomes the song.** Specify
it as **woody upright bass felt rather than heard, plus a room return that is slightly too long for the
space**, brushes never leaving the snare. The flashes, reflection and join are figurative.

**FORM:** **long-lined free verse, slow accretion.** Terminal rhyme **every fourth line only** — the
rhyme arrives like a wave, at a fixed interval, whether or not the sentence is ready. Prosopopoeia is
the signature.

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — ADDRESS TO THE WORK.** He speaks to the lion. The lion is "you", the whole way
through, and it cannot answer.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact):** *"downright salacious slow jam"* and *"superhumanly funky bass"* — ⭐ both used
**against the grain**, describing the tenor's dusk phrasing and the upright's weight. The friction
between the funk vocabulary and the chamber-jazz bed is the pair's ONE SEAM; put it where the tide is.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P05:** no water in the title (P02 owns water in the lyric field — P05 gets **tide**
and **sea**, which are separate tokens and must be used instead). No "dark" in the title. No pity, no
tragedy, no artist-and-posterity thesis. ⛔ **No mention of art, artists, legacy, or being remembered.**
He is a man finishing a mane before the sea arrives.

## P06 — **I KEPT THE CRANK IN THE CAR**
**AMBITIOUS · NEWS · IMPOSED · Bed B2 · LOFN-PRIME (INDIGNATION mode — cold, competent, aggressive)**

**THE SITUATION.** A person who has permanently rearranged their life around the power going out. A
hand-crank kept in the car. A car battery on the kitchen floor. An extension cord run down a hallway to
a neighbour. **They are very good at this.** And competence like this does not photograph, is never
counted, and is invisible to everyone who has never needed it.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the crank, worn smooth at the grip. ⭐ *The wear is the record of every time.*
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** kneeling on concrete in a stairwell, wiring a borrowed current.
**Risk: the neighbour needing it more.**

**IMPOSED. ⛔ NEVER GRATITUDE.** The song ends in **competence, not thanks and not uplift.** Nobody is
to be thanked for a condition somebody else authored. *(Donated from cut concept C11: the unprovable
absence — nothing happened, again, because of them — is the thing owed a credit.)*

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: THE VISIBLE JOIN, AT SINGLE-BAR SCALE.** ⭐ A splice **inside one bar**,
recurring: the voltage sags and the pitch drops with it for a fraction of a beat, then recovers.
The generator will want to smooth this — **that is exactly what the Deviation Clause is for here.**
Flashes, reflection and bed are figurative.

**FORM:** **inventory tercets, ABA — one object per stanza.** The crank. The wire. The battery. The
torch with the dead spring. Asyndeton is the signature: no conjunctions, no connective tissue, just the
things, in order. ⭐ **The list IS the emotion; do not add a stanza that explains it.**

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — LIST WITH NO ADDRESSEE.** Nouns only. **Zero vocatives, zero "you", zero "we".**
The one first-person appearance is the title and it is a possession, not an address.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact):** *"furiously slapping and popping his bass"* and *"syncopated sound"* — the
attack that outlives the note. ⛔ Case-exact, in the generator-facing field.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P06:** ⛔ **MAY NOT DEPICT AN AUDIENCE** — no crowd, no room, no applause, no
listener, no performance of any kind (P01 owns the room). P06 **owns "current"**. No water, no sky, no
"dark" in the title. **No candlelight and no cosiness** — this is heat, effort and equipment.
**No blackout prettiness. If it renders atmospheric, the moral spine has inverted.**
The author of the outage stays offstage and unnamed.
