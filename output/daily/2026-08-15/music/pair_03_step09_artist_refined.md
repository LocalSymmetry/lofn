## 1. THE FOUR REFUSALS *(what was cut, and why the cut is the refinement)*

### R1 — ⛔ THE THESIS WAS BEING SAID OUT LOUD
Draft V2 contained the line *"two cycles, two rates, one field."* That is the **argument**, and ICB ban 4 says **name the thing, never the argument.** It also violates the catalogue's whole reason for existing: a list cannot lecture, but a list containing a thesis-sentence can.
**Repair:** the polyrhythm is now stated only as a **frequency** — *"Once in a lifetime. Once every year."* — which is the same finding rendered as a fact about the sky rather than a claim about the song. **The idea survives; the sentence about the idea does not.**

### R2 — ⛔ THE ENDING KEPT TRYING TO BE LOVELY
Three of the four drafts closed on something adjacent to gratitude — a warm folding-up, a last look. That is precisely the failure the brief was built to prevent: **the ending is an AFFRONT.**
**Repair:** every closing block now runs *"That is not a lovely feeling. / That is a small, permanent offence."* and then hands straight back to the returning line, which lands **cold**, not warm. The affront is aimed at **nobody** — no villain, no institution — which is what keeps it inside the governing distinction. ⛔ Checked explicitly: **no draft mistakes a default-off switch for a power cut.** There is no switch in this song. There is a moon.

### R3 — ⛔ THE NUMBERS WERE BREEDING
First drafts carried between four and six numerals apiece — *thirty summers · two in the afternoon · six grids · three stars · two fields away · six hours.* The ONE-FACT RULE allows **one sung numeric fact**, at the hinge, **answered rather than recited.**
**Repair:** every research-flavoured numeral was cut. What survives is **"eleven percent"**, once, at the thinnest point of each song, immediately answered — *"It is coming back. It has already started."* The remaining numeral-words (*one chair, two people, two came over at once*) are **object counts**, not data, and they are flagged rather than hidden in the SELF-CHECK.

### R4 — ⛔ THE WATER KEPT LEAKING IN
The Golden Seed's own physics is a **breaking wave** (the Kelvin–Helmholtz curl that bands Jupiter and now the Sun), and P02 owns water. Early drafts reached for *tide*, *ripple*, *washed out*, *damp grass*.
**Repair:** the curl now ships as **wind crossing a dry field in travelling bands** — the same instability, none of P02's vocabulary — and V3 promotes it to a title. *"Next year this same night is washed out"* became *"…is lost to it."* **Measured: zero water-lexicon hits across all four lyrics.**

## 2. THE ONE ADDITION THAT MADE V3 A SONG

Draft V3 (camera at chair-leg height) had **no body in it** and therefore failed clause 2 of the GOLDEN MOVE. It was a field, beautifully observed, doing nothing to anyone.
**Repair:** the aluminium chair, the four dents and the swap were pushed forward into every block, and the flash-one image was rebuilt around **shadow bands** — the real ripples of light that race across the ground in the seconds before totality. That is not decoration: it is **the song's own title phenomenon happening to the humans**, at a third size, and it puts the bodies inside the physics rather than beside it. V3 stopped being a landscape and became the only variation where **the field is the witness and the people are the intruders.**

## 4. ⚠️ THE FLAG THIS PAIR CARRIES INTO STEP 10 — AND WHY IT IS NOT A REPAIR

**`strict_end_rhyme` = 0.101 / 0.152 / 0.181 / 0.203 against a floor of 0.30.** All four songs sit under it. That is not an accident and it is not laziness.

**L21 says removal is a debt, and the debt is discharged in writing here:**
> P03 is assigned **unrhymed catalogue verse with an assonance chain**. What returns in place of rhyme is **(a) one line that comes back UNCHANGED at the end of every stanza** — a different line in each of the four songs, so it is a spine and not a tic; **(b) a four-line Return block that repeats byte-identically three times**; and **(c) anaphora**, which is this pair's signature technique and returns at the **head** of the line where rhyme would have returned at the tail.

**And the return is measured, not asserted:** `line_return` comes out at **0.215–0.253 against a floor of 0.20 — all four clear it.** The song returns. It returns at the beginning of the line and at the end of the stanza instead of at the end of the line. ⭐ **Exact repetition needs no defence; the stripped rhyme does, and that is what this paragraph is.**

## 5. VOCAL AND ARRANGEMENT REFINEMENTS CARRIED INTO STEP 10
- **The choir is now absent from the half-size event in all four.** Its absence is how the middle event is *measured* as half — a listener does not need to be told the peak is smaller if the widest element simply does not come.
- **The tuned kick is described by its JOB, never by its weight.** The Source-2 phrase *"superhumanly funky bass"* names the role; the instrument doing it is a pitched kick. The phrase is therefore **load-bearing against type**, which is what the brief asked for, rather than an ornament pasted in to satisfy a counter.
- **The negation sweep was run on the style field, not the lyric.** Suno reads *"no sub"* as a request for sub. Every refusal moved to EXCLUDE; the MUSIC PROMPTs measure **zero negation tokens**. Negation stayed in the lyric on purpose — *"No cloud. Nothing crossed."* is the most frightening line in the song and nobody is generating audio from it.
- **The `*SFX*` cue is a manufactured object in three of four** (a chair leg, a gate ticking as it cools, a flask lid) — never a weather sound. Weather is the bed; the seam is metal.
