## THE FOUR ANGLES — what actually differs

The pair DNA is fixed (catalogue verse · anaphora · unrhymed / assonance chain · a line returning unchanged at every stanza end · third-person plural only · two section-scale flashes with a half-size event between · 7/8 over 4/4). **What varies between the four is: WHAT IS BEING CATALOGUED, WHERE THE AFFRONT ENTERS FROM, and WHICH RETURNING LINE CARRIES THE SPINE.** Four different returning lines means four different songs, not four mixes.

| | **V1 — THE INVENTORY** | **V2 — THE TWO RATES** | **V3 — THE GRASS** | **V4 — THE REFILLING** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **title** | *One Folding Chair* | *Two Skies in Two Days* | *The Bands in the Grass* | *Eleven Percent of the Moon* |
| **the catalogue is of** | **objects** — bag, field, sky, in that order | **frequencies** — how often each thing happens | **what the field was doing** while unwatched, camera at chair-leg height | **a closing window** — what is left of the dark, counted down |
| **the affront enters via** | an **object**: the torch with red tape, bought that morning for a thing that turned out to be annual | a **rate**: once in a lifetime beside once every year | **duration**: the grass has been doing this without an audience the whole time | **a clock**: it has already started, and next year is gone |
| **returning line (unchanged, every stanza)** | *And none of it was arranged for them.* | *Once in a lifetime. Once every year.* | *And the grass went on doing it either way.* | *And the moon was already on its way back.* |
| **temperature** | warmest / most accessible — a bag on a kitchen table | most **thesis-forward**; the polyrhythm is stated in words | most **texture-forward**; lowest camera; least human | most **terror-adjacent**; the risk is in every stanza |
| **the hinge number sits at** | section 9, after both flashes | section 9, answered by the returning line itself | section 9, spoken flat under the choir's defect | **the title**, then section 9 as the answer |
| **the half-size event is** | a kettle in a kitchen, six hours of nothing | the drive between, counted in cattle grids | wind crossing the field with nobody in it | the moon rising a hand's width and setting again |
| **what it risks** | cosiness → guarded by the cold through the shoe soles and the affront | lecturing → guarded by the list form; the thesis is *named as a frequency*, never argued | coldness / no body → guarded by the chair, the dents, the swap | monotony of dread → guarded by the flatness of the report |

⛔ **Deliberately NOT four angles:** *four production treatments of one lyric*, *four moods*, *four vocal registers*. Those are mixes. Each of these four re-writes the inventory from scratch.

## FIRST DRAFTS *(lyric spine only — refined at step 09, packaged at step 10)*

### V1 — *One Folding Chair* · the inventory
Opens on the bag, not the sky. Three anaphoric blocks (`In the bag:` / `In the field:` / `In the sky:`) with the same four-beat list shape. Flash one is the afternoon going out with **no cloud and nothing crossing** — the wrongness is that there is no visible cause. Half-size event: they fold the chair, drive the lane, someone puts the kettle on, **nobody talks**, and then they pack the same bag again. Flash two: two at once, green at the head, **dust, and the thing that shed it is not even there.** Hinge: *In the sky: eleven percent of the moon* → *It is coming back. It has already started.* Affront: **it does that every August**, over the roof, above the shut curtains, for free, and nobody had ever said.
**First-draft weakness found:** the affront section drafted with six numerals in it (*thirty summers*, *forty years*). Cut to zero — one research number per song, and it is *eleven percent*.

### V2 — *Two Skies in Two Days* · the two rates
Opens on the two clocks as a **frequency list**: things that happen once in a place and never again; things that happen every year and nobody attends. The returning line **is** the thesis — *Once in a lifetime. Once every year.* — so the last stanza's affront lands as the list finally applying to *them* rather than to the sky. Half-size event counted in **cattle grids on the lane**, which is the second rate rendered as a physical object hit at intervals. Flash two is **the year-clock**, and it is the brighter one, deliberately: the annual thing beats the once-in-a-lifetime thing, which is the entire finding.
**First-draft weakness found:** wanted to say *"two cycles, two rates, one field"* out loud. That is the argument, not the thing. Replaced with the cattle grids and the list.

### V3 — *The Bands in the Grass* · the grass
Camera at chair-leg height for the whole song. Catalogues **what the field was doing** — wind crossing in travelling bands, the lamp on the post coming on by itself, moths at the lamp, the gate ticking as it cools. The Kelvin-Helmholtz curl ships here as **wind bands in dry grass** (⛔ the water image is refused; P02 owns water) and it is the song's one figurative reach: *the same curl at two sizes.* Humans enter only through the chair, the dents and the swap. Flash one and flash two are both **witnessed by the field**, not by the people. Affront: the grass **went on doing it either way**, which is the most impersonal possible statement of *how long has this been happening.*
**First-draft weakness found:** without the chair the draft had no body and failed the Golden Move's clause 2. The chair, the four dents and the swap were pushed forward into every block.

### V4 — *Eleven Percent of the Moon* · the refilling
The most terror-adjacent. The moon is in every stanza, growing. Catalogues **what is left**: how much dark, how many minutes of it, how much of the year's one clean night has already been spent. Flash one is **memory** (the afternoon, recalled from inside the second night) and flash two is **present tense** — so the song's two peaks sit on two different tenses, which is the composite's two-moments-stacked rendered as grammar. Hinge: the title returns as the answer to itself. Affront lands as **the routine scale of the thing** — every August, and the window closes a little earlier each year of a life.
**First-draft weakness found:** drifted toward doom; the CHOSEN clause was invisible. Repaired by keeping *nobody sent them, nobody was owed* as a stated fact in the body of the song — they are **not victims of the moon**, they came anyway.

## SOURCE-2 PLACEMENT PLAN *(generator-facing MUSIC PROMPT only, case-exact)*
Both required phrases ship in **all four** MUSIC PROMPTs:
- **`superhumanly funky bass`** — always **against type**, describing the **role** the tuned kick occupies (pitched, dry, no weight), never a slap bass. The phrase names a *job*, and the kick does that job with the wrong instrument.
- **`syncopated sound`** — always attached to the **marimba's disagreement with the kick**, i.e. to the kinetic defect, so the phrase is load-bearing rather than decorative.

## DEDICATION TRIGGER PLAN
The interlocking marimba figure is **balafon-derived** and it ships in all four MUSIC PROMPTs as an explicit request. **Therefore the credit is owed and is real, not decorative** — a person, never a city or a region. No second player credit is claimed, because no second named figure ships. Written fresh per song; ⛔ **never one block pasted across four.**
