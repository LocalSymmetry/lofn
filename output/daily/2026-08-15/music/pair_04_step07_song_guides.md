## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING — WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?

**In a tiled service corridor, on the hard floor, feet apart, at twenty-two minutes past ten at night.**
White square tile from the floor to about shoulder height, painted block above that, a hard flat ceiling, a long straight run with a bend at the far end. A wall map behind glass, a fire cabinet, a drain grate she has learned to stand off because it eats the low end. Her practice amp is on the floor angled up at her knees. A small keyboard on a folding stand, a loop pedal, a jack cable she has coiled wrong for eleven years. The case is open at her feet with the coins from an earlier shift still in it. **Her hands are cold and the room is warm.** She has about twenty minutes left of a shift she set herself.

## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING — WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

**Not the room, and not the equipment — everything works.** That is exactly the danger.

1. **That this is simply the job now.** The real risk is not collapse, it is *continuation*: she will pack up, she will come back tomorrow, the corridor will still be beautiful, and the shape of the night will be identical. Nothing dramatic ever happens to her. **She just keeps being at work in a room with perfect acoustics and no traffic.**
2. **That she was bad tonight and heard it.** The room is so good it returns her mistakes at full quality. **A great room is a merciless monitor.** The corridor gives her every dropped change back, bigger than she played it. She cannot pretend she did not hear.
3. **That one person came and she cannot make that mean anything.** If Tuuli were enough, the night would resolve. If Tuuli were nothing, the night would at least be clean. She is both, and holding both is the injury.
4. **Physically:** cold hands making the misses worse, the amp cabinet heating her shins, the coins going into a pocket, the case corner catching her ankle on the way out. Small, dull, real.
5. **That there is nobody to be angry AT.** The decision that stopped routing people through here was made by someone who has never stood on this tile. The anger has no target, so **it stays in the body and gets louder.**

## GUIDE A — VOICE

- **Female vocal, adult, unpolished.** Low-mid, close-mic'd, more spoken than sung, drifting slightly flat on sustained notes **on purpose**. She is a competent-to-poor performer having a poor night, and the vocal must render that: no runs, no belting, no vibrato control, no virtuosity anywhere.
- **Placement:** dry and close, left of centre. Her **own doubled whisper** answers from far right, a half-beat late, at the depth of the corridor — that double is the room, not a harmony part. It is the only "second voice" in the song and it is still her. Self-address, staged.
- **Register drops as the song goes.** By the outro she is nearly speaking, lower, harder, faster. **Anger, not defeat** — the tempo of the delivery increases while the pitch falls.
- ⛔ **No pretty vocal moment anywhere.** If a listener could describe the vocal as "beautiful", the song has failed F1.

## GUIDE C — SELF-ADDRESS, MADE UNAMBIGUOUS BEFORE CHORUS 2

A short bridge sits between verse 2 and chorus 2 in every variation. It asks the question directly and answers it — *who is the you here, nobody is here, the you is me* — so that when the accusation lands in chorus 2, there is no reading in which she is scolding a listener, a passerby, or the absent audience. **She is scolding herself.** Everything before that bridge is retroactively re-heard as self-address the moment it lands.

## GUIDE D — FORM AND THE TWO FLASHES

- **Lines are three to five syllables.** No exceptions worth making.
- ⛔ **No end rhyme.** Internal rhyme and consonance only — the return happens *inside* the line, never at its edge.
- **Epizeuxis is the signature and it is also the composite element.** Every immediately doubled line is the **two hard stabs inside one bar, said aloud.** The listener hears the same gesture at word scale and at transient scale. This is how the somatic spec gets into the LYRIC and the FORM, not only the production notes (GRAIN LAW).
- **Section map is short and asymmetric**: the sections get *shorter* toward the end while the density goes *up*. Nothing widens.
- **The kinetic defect:** the second stab of each pair lands fractionally early. The groove trips forward every bar and never settles into a pocket. A limp, not a swing.

## GUIDE E — THE SEAM (one dominant material, one intruder)

**Dominant:** purely synthetic — 30–60 Hz sub felt in the sternum, two dry stabs per bar, clipped granular debris, a corroded saw pad thin enough to nearly vanish.
**Intruder (one, and only one):** the corridor's natural reverb tail, gorgeous, long, blooming on every consonant.
**Seam location:** the end of every vocal phrase, where a flat unimpressive line is handed back by a magnificent room.
**She acts across it:** she keeps playing into the return, and in the final section she plays *harder* into it, deliberately, out of spite. **That is the only "development" the song contains and it is not growth.**

## GUIDE F — ARRANGEMENT ARC (all four variations share this shape, not its content)

1. **First five seconds:** two dry stabs land inside the first bar with nothing else under them, then the sub arrives late and low. **No intro build.** The song is already at work.
2. **Verses:** sub + stabs + thin pad. Vocal close and flat. The room tail is the only thing that sounds expensive.
3. **Chorus 1:** stab density doubles; the pad ducks almost to nothing under each pair and crawls back.
4. **Bridge (self-address):** everything drops to sub and voice. **No silence** — the bed carries the gap, thin and continuous.
5. **Chorus 2:** the accusation. Glitch debris enters, skittering across the field. First real distortion on the vocal.
6. **Chorus 3 / outro:** highest density, lowest vocal register, fastest delivery, hard stop on a statement. **It ends louder and angrier than it began.**

## GUIDE H — WHAT "GOOD" LOOKS LIKE FOR THIS SONG

A listener finishes it and cannot tell you whether the woman was talented. They can tell you **the room was gorgeous, she was having a bad night, one person came, and she is going back tomorrow** — and they are annoyed about it in their chest, at 40 Hz, with nobody to blame. **That last part is the whole design.**
