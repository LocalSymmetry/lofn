# STEP 02 — Twelve Concepts
**Run:** `2026-08-15_daily_music_the_resolution` · Essence: *A thing is heard through its leftovers, and the leftovers are brighter than the source.*

Each concept declares: **the leftover object** (test 1), **the body / place / risk** (test 2), and the
**scale check** (test 3). Titles obey the ban — a concrete physical noun or a first-person act, no
abstract-argument nouns, no persona prefix.

---

### C01 · **THE HOUSE LIGHTS TOOK THE SONG WITH THEM** — *(cut candidate; see verdict)*
Blackout in a small club; the PA dies mid-bar and the room keeps the count with hands.
**Leftover:** the clapped tempo, which is the only surviving copy of the arrangement.
**Body/place/risk:** a woman on a low stage, one hand still on a dead fader, deciding in about two
seconds whether to be embarrassed or to keep going. Risk: the room leaving.
**Facet:** F-F. **Arm:** ACCESSIBLE. **Axis B:** NEWS.
**Verdict:** ⭐ **PASSES ALL THREE.** The strongest concept in the pool.

### C02 · **I KEPT THE CRANK IN THE CAR**
A person who has permanently rearranged their life around the power going out — a hand-crank, a car
battery, a hallway extension cord run to a neighbour — and is *good at it*, and finds that competence
does not photograph.
**Leftover:** the crank, worn smooth at the grip.
**Body/place/risk:** kneeling on concrete in a stairwell, wiring a borrowed current. Risk: the neighbour
needing it more.
**Facet:** F-F. **Arm:** AMBITIOUS. **Axis B:** NEWS. **Verdict:** PASSES.

### C03 · **THE WATER TOOK THE SECOND ONE**
Two meteors; the water holds both, and one of the two in the water is not a meteor at all.
**Leftover:** the reflection, arriving without the thing.
**Body/place/risk:** somebody standing to their ankles in a cold inlet at 2 a.m. because the reflection
is the only part they can look at without their neck hurting. Risk: cold, and the tide.
**Facet:** F-C. **Arm:** ACCESSIBLE. **Axis B:** EXISTENCE. **Verdict:** PASSES.

### C04 · **HOW LONG WAS THAT HAPPENING**
The affront of a resolution finding: an instrument gets fine enough and you learn a thing has been true
your whole life, in front of you, at twenty kilometres wide.
**Leftover:** the old photographs, which were never wrong — just too coarse.
**Body/place/risk:** a technician at 4 a.m. with a new lens and every previous year of their own work
sitting in a drawer. Risk: their own record.
**Facet:** F-A. **Arm:** ACCESSIBLE. **Axis B:** EXISTENCE. **Verdict:** ⭐ PASSES. *The under-used feeling.*

### C05 · **THE TIDE IS COMING FOR THE LION**
Atlantic City, c. 1900 — sand sculptors who worked knowing the tide took it, whose entire surviving
archive is other people's photographs.
**Leftover:** the photograph; the sculpture never existed to anyone who wasn't there.
**Body/place/risk:** a sculptor smoothing a mane at dusk with the water eleven feet away. Risk: the water.
**Facet:** F-B. **Arm:** AMBITIOUS. **Axis B:** EXISTENCE. **Verdict:** PASSES.

### C06 · **YOU CAN HEAR ME FINE**
The precise loneliness of being loud and unfindable — not silenced, **unresolved**; and the one exception
written into the rule is somebody deciding to look on purpose.
**Leftover:** the one person who came looking, who is the entire audience and is enough and is also not
enough, and the song refuses to resolve which.
**Body/place/risk:** a busker in a corridor with excellent acoustics that nobody routes through. Risk:
the shift ending.
**Facet:** F-G. **Arm:** AMBITIOUS. **Axis B:** NEWS.
**Verdict:** ⭐ PASSES — **but scale-flagged.** Must be a *corridor*, never a *blackout*. If the draft
reaches for power-cut imagery it fails test 3 and is cut, not repaired.

### C07 · **THE SAME CURL AT EVERY SIZE**
The plasma vortex, the breaking wave and Jupiter's bands are one instability in three sizes; the universe
repeats its few good ideas and is not embarrassed about it.
**Leftover:** the shape itself, which outlives every medium that carries it.
**Body/place/risk:** a swimmer under a breaking wave, held down the length of one bar, watching the curl
from inside. Risk: the obvious one.
**Facet:** F-E. **Arm:** AMBITIOUS. **Axis B:** EXISTENCE. **Verdict:** PASSES.

### C08 · **IT WAS DOING IT WITHOUT US**
Sunlight was making entangled pairs before anyone built a laser to do it — the machine was a detour, and
the finding is an apology dressed as a discovery.
**Leftover:** the sunlight, which owes us nothing and was never waiting.
**Body/place/risk:** somebody carrying a cone of glass out into a field at noon to catch something free.
Risk: looking foolish; the sun.
**Facet:** F-A + F-H. **Arm:** AMBITIOUS. **Axis B:** NEWS. **Verdict:** ⭐ PASSES.

### C09 · **TWO SKIES IN TWO DAYS**
The eclipse takes the light on the twelfth; the meteors give it back in pieces the same night. The dark
was the instrument — and tonight it is already eleven percent refilled.
**Leftover:** the dark itself, briefly, and the fact that it does not stay.
**Body/place/risk:** the same field, two nights running, same people, one folding chair. Risk: the moon
coming back and it being over.
**Facet:** F-D. **Arm:** ACCESSIBLE. **Axis B:** EXISTENCE. **Verdict:** PASSES.

### C10 · **THE ROOM SINGS IT BACK WRONG AND BETTER** — *(cut candidate)*
A hook returns from the crowd a half-step flat and two beats late and is better for it.
**Leftover:** the crowd's version, which is now the real one.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT — TWIN OF C01.** Same engine (a room carrying the music when the source fails),
same conclusion. L20/L14: same fact + same device + same conclusion is invisible to pair isolation.
**Harvest:** *the return arrives flat and late and is better* → donate to C01 as its ghost-echo mechanic.

### C11 · **NOBODY CLAPPED FOR THE MAINTENANCE**
Reverence for the people who keep a thing running — the ones whose whole job is that nothing happens.
**Leftover:** the absence of an incident, which is unprovable and is the work.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT — fails test 1.** The leftover is a negative; there is no nameable physical object,
and the concept resolves into a thesis. **Harvest:** *the unprovable absence as a credit* → donate to
C02's dedication logic and to the credit-the-drummer instinct.

### C12 · **I AM NOT GOING TO EXPLAIN THE COMET**
A guide at a viewing site who has stopped explaining and just points.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT — fails test 2 and brushes ban 2.** The body is doing nothing but *withholding*,
which is a posture, not an action, and the concept is really about the artist's relationship to
audiences. **Harvest:** *pointing instead of explaining* → donate as a lyric discipline to C09.
