## ⛔ NO GENERIC V1–V4 TEMPLATE. THE FOUR ANGLES ARE DERIVED FROM ONE QUESTION.

The concept is a body standing in cold water at two in the morning with a copy of a burnt-out comet grain at its feet. **The only free variable is the relationship between the body and the water** — the water is the addressee, so the four variations are **four different things the second person is doing to her.** Everything else (bed, form, apostrophe, seam) is fixed by the brief, which is why the angles have to come from here and nowhere else.

| | the water is… | the verb the body owns | what rises across the song | vocal staging | where the bold device lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | a **keeper** — it had the light first and it kept the second one | **stand / reckon** | the tide, named in body parts | dead centre, mouth-close | the last chorus returns **alone through the room send**, quieter, left, a beat behind |
| **V2** | an **arriving body** — slow, punctual, irreversible | **wait / decide in advance** | ⭐ a **low sine gliding up one semitone and staying there** — the only irreversible element in the pair | starts right of centre, migrates to centre as the water rises | the sine's glide, and a chorus that comes back inverted through a long plate |
| **V3** | a **surface that breaks and reassembles** | **touch / put a hand flat** | the cold, up the wrist and the forearm | centre and dry, with a copy of the voice low-left one beat behind | the middle **drops to the room return alone** while the sine keeps walking |
| **V4** | a **witness to something warm** | **breathe / laugh** | her own steam, which outlasts the light | centre, very close, exhale mixed as loud as the words | **breath is the percussion**; the reverb only lights when she breathes |

**Cross-check for angle bleed:** the four take different *tenses of attention* (a reckoning already made · a decision held in advance · a single gesture repeated · an involuntary reflex). No two share a chorus grammar, a section map, or a rising element. V1 is the only one carrying a sung number. V2 is the only one whose bed changes pitch. V3 is the only one with a hole in the middle. V4 is the only one where the percussion is a body function.

## FIRST DRAFTS

### V1 — **ANKLE DEEP AT TWO IN THE MORNING**

Draft intro (apostrophe declared in word one, chiasmus, ABBA):
> Water, you are wide and cold,
> and I am here and I am late,
> late and here, I stand and wait
> in the wide, in the cold.

Draft verse — the object arrives before the body does:
> You had the light before I did,
> you laid it face down in the deep,
> face down in the deep you keep
> the hot half of the thing you hid.

Draft verse — CHOSEN, stated as logistics, never as lack:
> Nobody sent me down to you.
> The gravel quits, the water starts,
> the water starts and takes the parts
> of me that standing air can't do.

Draft — **THE SEAM, said once, plainly**:
> My shin can tell me where you end,
> your edge can tell me where I start,
> where I start, where you end — the part
> between them is the part I spend.

Draft chorus — the one sung number, answered not recited:
> Twice in one minute — you took the second,
> you rolled it under and you kept it whole,
> kept it whole and rolled it under; the cold
> came up my ankle, and I stood, and reckoned.

**GHOST RETURN (the literal composite element):** the intro quatrain and the chorus quatrain, **line order reversed**, sung at lower level, moved left, entering a beat late. Typed backwards into the lyrics field so it cannot be lost in production.

*Note to self:* "the hot half of the thing you hid" is the whole seed in six words — the light is the leftover and the water is holding the wrong half of it. Keep.

### V2 — **THE TIDE CAME UP MY SHINS**

Draft chorus — the water as a hillside that is always arriving:
> You come at me the way a hillside can,
> too slow to see and never not arriving,
> never not arriving, never striving,
> and now you have my shins. That was the plan.

**"That was the plan"** is the CHOSEN clause for this variation, and it is the reason the song is not afraid: she picked the height in advance. The terror is real and it is scheduled.

Draft verse — the reading, taken off her own leg:
> You started at the stone and took the stone,
> you took the stone and started up the shoe,
> up the shoe and past it — that will do
> for one more, and then the walk back home.

Draft — the reason she is looking down (F2, ergonomic, unromantic):
> My neck is not a telescope, so no.
> I take the easy half and take it here.

*Note to self:* V2 must not narrate dread. The measurement narrates it. Every stanza names a higher part of the leg and the singer stays level.

### V3 — **I PUT MY HAND FLAT ON THE WATER**

Draft chorus — the most literal chiasmus in the pair:
> I put my hand flat on you and you broke,
> I took my hand back up and you were whole,
> you were whole, I took my hand back up — the cold
> went to the wrist, and neither of us spoke.

Draft verse — the four-light moment, the **one and only** reflection description, and it never says the word:
> Four came down and one was not
> a thing at all — I could not tell,
> could not tell, and just as well:
> I did not need to know the lot.

*Note to self:* the arrangement hole in the middle is where the hand goes down. The bed keeps walking underneath — **no full stop**, because a specified silence renders as a gasp and then a smear.

---

### V4 — **MY BREATH KEPT GETTING IN THE WAY**

Draft chorus — the warm intruder crossing the cold dominant, i.e. the seam sung:
> My breath kept getting in the way of it,
> the warm of me across the cold of you,
> the cold of you across the warm — and through
> the middle of my own white, there it lit.

Draft verse — the second leftover, and the seed delivered by a body:
> The steam of me went up and stayed
> a second longer than the light,
> longer than the light, and right
> in front of it, and unafraid.

*Note to self:* this is where **"steamy"** stops being a borrowed word and becomes a fact about the air. The line *"a second longer than the light"* is the seed — the leftover outlasts the source — spoken by a person instead of by a comet. It is the strongest single line in the pair and V4 is built around it.
