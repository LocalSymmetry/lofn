## ⭐ THE TWO ANSWERS THAT OPEN THIS FILE

**WHO IS THIS SONG FOR?**
For **Marit Halvorsen**, a night-shift hydrographic technician who has read tide tables for eleven years and has never once gone down to water for a reason that was not work — this is the song that gives her back the half hour she keeps almost taking.

**THE DEVIATION CLAUSE — where I expect the generator to disobey, and which disobedience I would KEEP.**
I expect the **ghost return** to disobey. I have asked for a full section that comes back *quieter, moved left, and one beat late*; Suno's most likely answer is a **simultaneous doubled or harmonised vocal with no time displacement**, because the model builds picture and pulse in one pass and a deliberate late entry reads to it as an error to correct.
- **KEEP:** if the displacement lands in **register instead of in time** — the return arriving an octave down, or as a low harmony under the lead, still quieter and still left. *Inverted* was always the primary word; a register inversion is more musical than a delay line and it is still the water holding the thing upside down.
- **DO NOT KEEP:** the return arriving **in unison, on the beat, at equal level**. That is not a reflection, that is a chorus double, and it collapses the one composite element this pair was assigned.
- **ALSO DO NOT KEEP:** a **swept harp glissando**. The brief bans the harp as decoration; a glissando is the generator reaching for the instrument's cliché and it must be excluded in the EXCLUDE field, not merely hoped against.

## THE FACETS

### F1 — THE LEFTOVER OBJECT IS THE REFLECTION, AND IT IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE FOUR THAT CAN BE TOUCHED
Two grains of comet dust burn out. Their light reaches the eye and reaches the water. The water's copy is not the meteor and arrives anyway — a leftover with no source left in the frame. It is also the only light in the scene at ankle height, i.e. **the only one inside arm's reach of a standing body.** That is why the song is set in the water and not on the gravel.

### F2 — THE BODY IS IN THE WATER FOR AN ERGONOMIC REASON, NOT A SPIRITUAL ONE
She is standing in it **because her neck hurts.** Looking down is easier than looking up. This is the single most important protection against the pretty-contemplation default: the reason is boring, physical, and true, and it makes the awe an accident of posture rather than a pose.

### F3 — THE RISK IS A NUMBER GOING UP BEHIND HER
Cold water, two in the morning, and **the tide rising while she is looking the other way.** The fear is clean: the waterline is a measurement — ankle, shin, calf — and it does not stop because she is busy. ⭐ The terror is not that the water is hostile; it is that it is **punctual**. She leaves when it reaches the calf, and leaving is an action, not a defeat.

### F4 — CHOSEN, AND THE PROOF IS THAT NOBODY IS MENTIONED
Nobody sent her. Nobody is coming. ⛔ And the song never says so mournfully — the *absence of other people is never a topic*, because a topic would make it a lack. It is stated once, flatly, as logistics: *"Nobody sent me down to you."* Everything else in the lyric is what she is doing. **No loneliness, no wistfulness, no "nobody else saw it."**

### F5 — THE SEAM IS THE WATERLINE ON THE SKIN
ONE dominant material: **cold salt water.** ONE intruder: **a warm body with a pulse in it.** The seam is the **line where the two meet on her shin** — and the subject acts across it: she stands, she puts a hand flat down, she breathes across it, she walks out through it. The join is placed exactly where the meaning is, and the lyric says so once: *"the part / between them is the part I spend."*

### F6 — THE FORM IS THE REFLECTION, SO THE LYRIC IS NOT ALLOWED TO BE
**Mirrored quatrains, ABBA, chiasmus:** the second half of every stanza inverts the first, and the rhyme closes back on itself. The whole poem is a surface with a copy under it. ⭐ Therefore the lyric **describes reflection at most once**, and the one time it does, it never uses the word — it is the four-light confusion: *"Four came down and one was not / a thing at all — I could not tell."* Every other instance of mirroring is structural.

### F7 — THE GHOST ECHO IS A VOCAL MOVE BEFORE IT IS A PLUG-IN
An entire section returns with **its lines in reverse order**, sung at lower level, displaced left, arriving late. It is written into the lyrics field as a section, not left to production. **A Somatic objection answered only in the PRODUCTION SPEC is not answered** — so the ghost return is literally typed out, backwards, in the lyric.

### F8 — THE ADDRESSEE IS THE WATER, DECLARED IN THE FIRST WORD
Residue discharge: **apostrophe to the water.** The first sung word of every variation is **"Water,"** or an unmistakable second-person address to the inlet, so that no listener can slip into the "you" slot. "You" is wide, cold, rising, and it does not answer. This is also policed in the **EMO vocabulary, the SFX vocabulary and the section names** — no section is named for a listener's feeling and no EMO field addresses anybody.

### F9 — HER BREATH IS THE SECOND LEFTOVER, AND IT OUTLASTS THE FIRST
At two in the morning her exhale stands in the air as a visible cloud. **It lasts longer than the meteor did.** That is the seed's line delivered by a body instead of by a comet: the leftover is brighter and slower than the source. It is also where **"steamy"** earns its place honestly — steam on cold air and the wetness of a long room return, physical, never erotic.

### F10 — THE BED IS A FLOOR AND A ROOM, NEVER A PAD
B3 is **one low sine holding the floor + a long room return + bowed strings with the hair-grain audible + a harp that is struck, damped, stopped or prepared.** ⛔ The pad is the failure mode of this pair and it is attacked three ways: the harp is a struck wire, the strings are bowed where the noise is, and the "atmosphere" is not a synth at all — it is the **convolution tail of the harp itself**, which means the ambience cannot exist unless something is played.

### F11 — THE KINETIC DEFECT IS A HEEL IN SHALLOW WATER
Sixty-eight beats per minute, and the pulse is a person shifting weight in water — so it **limps**: one stroke in every three, four or five arrives a sixteenth off. A body standing in cold water for twenty minutes does not keep time. The groove's defect is the body's, and it is what keeps the bed from becoming wallpaper.

### F12 — THE ONE SUNG NUMERIC FACT
**"Twice in one minute."** It sits at the emotional hinge of V1's chorus and is **responded to, not recited** — she answers it by staying put and taking a reading. Spelled in words. Every other variation carries its number differently or not at all; no variation carries two.
