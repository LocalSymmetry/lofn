## P05 — **THE TIDE IS COMING FOR THE LION**
**AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · CHOSEN · Bed B4 · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — terror-adjacent, warm)**

**THE SITUATION.** Atlantic City, around 1900. Sand sculptors worked the beach knowing the tide took
everything by morning. Their entire surviving archive is **other people's photographs** — the work only
exists now because strangers happened to photograph it.

**LEFTOVER OBJECT:** the photograph. ⭐ The sculpture never existed at all for anyone who wasn't there.
**BODY / PLACE / RISK:** a sculptor smoothing a lion's mane at dusk with the water eleven feet away and
closing. **Risk: the water. It is not a metaphor; it is eleven feet away.**

**CHOSEN, NOT IMPOSED.** He knows. He always knew. **This is the deal and he took it.** Quiet, complete,
not sad — ⛔ and specifically **not tragic**. A song that pities him has misread the concept.

**COMPOSITE — LITERAL ELEMENT: THE CONTINUOUS FAINT BED, WHOLE SONG.** ⭐ The bed is the one thing that
literally never stops here — **and per Panel C it needs a named defect or it becomes the song.** Specify
it as **woody upright bass felt rather than heard, plus a room return that is slightly too long for the
space**, brushes never leaving the snare. The flashes, reflection and join are figurative.

**FORM:** **long-lined free verse, slow accretion.** Terminal rhyme **every fourth line only** — the
rhyme arrives like a wave, at a fixed interval, whether or not the sentence is ready. Prosopopoeia is
the signature.

**RESIDUE DISCHARGE — ADDRESS TO THE WORK.** He speaks to the lion. The lion is "you", the whole way
through, and it cannot answer.

**SOURCE 2 (≥2 exact):** *"downright salacious slow jam"* and *"superhumanly funky bass"* — ⭐ both used
**against the grain**, describing the tenor's dusk phrasing and the upright's weight. The friction
between the funk vocabulary and the chamber-jazz bed is the pair's ONE SEAM; put it where the tide is.

**⛔ BANS SPECIFIC TO P05:** no water in the title (P02 owns water in the lyric field — P05 gets **tide**
and **sea**, which are separate tokens and must be used instead). No "dark" in the title. No pity, no
tragedy, no artist-and-posterity thesis. ⛔ **No mention of art, artists, legacy, or being remembered.**
He is a man finishing a mane before the sea arrives.
