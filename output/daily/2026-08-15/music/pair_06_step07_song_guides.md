# P06 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES
**Run:** `2026-08-15_daily_music_the_resolution` · **ICB sha:** `7d4dd66f2a6e` · **Pair:** P06
**Bed B2** — battery post-punk / generator techno hybrid · **128 BPM** · guitar through a small amp running
off a car battery · drum machine · **square-wave sub that detunes slightly as the voltage sags**

---

## ⭐ WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?

**Kneeling — not standing — on the concrete of a stairwell landing, second flight, both knees down, grit
under the kneecaps, weight forward onto the heel of one hand.** The other hand is holding an orange
extension cord under tension so the taped joint does not take the strain. A car battery is on the tile
beside her on a folded towel, because bare terminals on a wet-mopped floor is the one mistake she has never
made. The stairwell light is off, obviously; the torch is in her teeth because its clip spring died two
winters ago and nobody makes that spring any more. It is not cold. **It is hot** — a stairwell with no air
handling, in a building with no lift, in the fourth hour.

## ⭐ WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

Not the current. **The current is the part she is good at.** What could hurt her:

1. ⭐ **The arithmetic.** One battery, two doors. The machine two landings down does not stop, and she is
   the one deciding, at speed, with no authority to be deciding it. If she is wrong nobody will ever be able
   to prove it and neither will she.
2. **The concrete.** Four winters of kneeling on it. Knees are the injury this practice actually produces
   and no one counts it.
3. **The heat.** Fourth hour, no air moving, a fan she cannot afford to run off the inverter.
4. **The invisibility.** The successful night looks exactly like a night in which nothing was needed. ⭐
   **Competence at this leaves no evidence of itself** — that is not sentiment, it is the structural fact
   the song is built out of, and it is why the ending may not be thanks.
5. ⛔ **And what will NOT hurt her, and must never be staged as if it might: the aesthetics of the outage.**
   No candlelit hush, no hands warming, no city gone quiet and beautiful. The blackout is not dangerous
   because it is dark; it is dangerous because it is a schedule.

## GUIDE 1 — THE SPINE *(binding on all four variations)*

**One sentence:** A person who rearranged her whole life around the power going out, working an inventory
of objects at speed, in a stairwell, for someone else's night, ending in competence and never in thanks.

**The one thing this song must do that no other song in the run does:** show *skill* under an imposed
condition, **without any of the skill being witnessed**. Everything else in the record has a listener
somewhere in it. This one is airtight: nothing here is heard by anybody.

**The line that must not appear, in any variation:** anything that converts the practice into a virtue.
No "keeps going", no "still standing", no "makes do", no "gets by", no "somehow". ⭐ Those are the register
of admiration, and admiration is thanks with the receipt torn off.

## GUIDE 3 — FORM AND THE JOIN

**Macro form: ABA.** *A* = the inventory, moving outward from the car. *B* = the landing, the cord, the
arithmetic, the schedule named once. *A'* = the inventory again, **in reverse order**, which is what putting
things away looks like. The last object handled is the first one named.

**The JOIN, at single-bar scale — written into THREE surfaces so the GRAIN LAW is satisfied:**
1. **Production:** square-wave sub drops ~a quarter-tone for a fraction of a beat, recovers before the kick.
2. **Lyric:** a returning short line — *the note drops half a step, comes back* — same slot each time.
3. **Form:** it lands *off* the phrase, never at a section boundary. The load does not consult the bar line.

**Two flashes:** (1) the inverter fan taking the load, mid-A. (2) the landing, in B — the loudest four bars
in the song, then straight out, no ceremony. Neither is at the start.
**One reflection (ghost echo):** the refrain returns late, **an octave down, half-spoken, off the downbeat.**
**Continuous faint bed:** the closed hat. It never stops. Not in the breakdown, not under either flash.
⛔ **No long full stop.** A specified four-second silence renders at 0.4 s; the hat carries the gap instead.

## GUIDE 4 — VOICE AND DELIVERY

**Female lead, close, dry, centre, almost no reverb** — a voice in a concrete stairwell should be *dead*,
not cathedral. Delivery is **flat, unhurried, exact** — the tone of someone reading back a part number.
⭐ **Cold, not hot.** Not shouted, not pleading, no vibrato, no melisma, no rise at the end of a line.
The fury is entirely in the **refusal to inflect** while the sub does the shouting underneath.

**The one place the voice changes:** the hinge number. Not louder — **slower**, one beat behind the grid,
then straight back on it for the action that answers it. That is the whole dynamic vocabulary.

**Double the refrain in unison at the octave, never in thirds.** Thirds are consolation.

## GUIDE 6 — SOURCE 2 PLACEMENT *(generator-facing, case-exact, ≥2 per package)*

**Chosen:** **"furiously slapping and popping his bass"** and **"syncopated sound"** — *the attack that
outlives the note.* The slap is a percussive transient that arrives before the pitch does and is still
audible after the note dies: **the leftover louder than the source**, which is the Golden Seed as a bass
technique. The syncopation is the kinetic defect — the groove lands early and stays early.

⛔ These go in the **MUSIC PROMPT** field, case-exact, grammar adapted around them, never altered, never
paraphrased, never in EXCLUDE. **A dedication may credit the writer of the phrases as the source that
supplied the vocabulary — but no living artist is named in a style field, and no player is credited for a
figure the prompt did not actually ask for.**
