## ⛔ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS, ANSWERED CONCRETELY

### Q1 — WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?
**On a stage twenty centimetres high in a hundred-capacity room off a side street, three feet from the
front row's shoes, boards flexing under her heels, right palm flat on a fader that no longer does
anything.** The room is warmer than the street by about eight degrees and getting warmer. Her shirt is
already through at the back from the first forty minutes. She is close enough to the front row to hear
individual people breathe, which she has never been before, because until eleven seconds ago there was
a PA between them.

### Q2 — WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?
**The room leaving.** Not embarrassment — *the room leaving.* Concretely: clapping is muscular work, and
untrained arms hold a steady tempo for roughly four minutes before the count sags into a slow handclap,
which is the sound of a room deciding to go home. She cannot ask them to stay; asking is how you lose
them. She can only be **worth the arms.** Every choice she makes for the rest of the night — which song,
which key, how fast, when to shut up — is priced in other people's forearms. That is the stake, and it
is why the song is hot: **the danger is not the cut, it is the four minutes after it.**

*(Secondary, real and unsentimental: an unamplified voice at full effort over a hundred clapping people
is a shouting load. She will be hoarse tomorrow and possibly for the week. She does it anyway. The song
does not congratulate her for it.)*

## THE SONG GUIDE

### CORE STATEMENT
**One beat of nothing, and then a hundred people turn out to be the equipment.** The house lights took
the song with them; the room handed it back, worse and louder, and that worse louder copy is the one
that finished the night.

### THE SEAM (one, visible, at section scale)
- **Dominant material:** a live funk/descarga band in a small hot room — congas, bongó, timbales, güiro,
  electric bass slapped and popped dry and close, one baritone saxophone, human voices.
- **The single intruder:** **an empty beat.** Not a sound — the absence of one, exactly one beat long,
  at a section boundary, with hands already in progress underneath it.
- **Where the join is placed:** at the top of the second verse, i.e. at the moment the song would
  normally get *bigger*. The generator wants to build there; the cut takes the build away.
- **The subject acts ACROSS it:** she starts a phrase amplified and finishes it unamplified. The
  sentence survives the seam; the sound of the sentence does not.

### COMPOSITE RULE — FOUR ELEMENTS, ONE LITERAL
| element | rendering |
|---|---|
| **VISIBLE JOIN** | ⭐ **LITERAL, section scale.** Butt-cut, no crossfade, one-beat gap covered by hands. |
| **TWO FLASHES** | figurative — two moments the room gets **louder**: (1) the clap doubles when the room finds the offbeat; (2) the response lands **on top of** the call after the break. Neither at the start. |
| **ONE REFLECTION** | figurative — the refrain returned **off the crowd**: late, pitched down, ragged. The GHOST ECHO made of people. |
| **CONTINUOUS FAINT BED** | figurative — **the clapping**, which never stops once it starts, buried under the loud sections and never absent. ⛔ Never a pad. |

### FORM — CALL-AND-RESPONSE COUPLETS, AABB, ANTIPHON
The verse architecture is **paired lines with a stage-world call and a room-world answer**, chant
grammar, short lines, consonant-carried rhythm. AABB throughout. The refrain returns four times:
1. her mouth, band behind her (**source**)
2. her mouth, no band (**across the seam**)
3. the room's mouth, flat and late (**the reflection**)
4. both at once, colliding (**flash two**)

### THE ANTIPHON'S PLOT (F4)
first response **ragged and too quiet** → she goes back and gets it → second response **on top of her** →
third response **starts before she does.** The song stops being hers and that is the win, not the loss.

### THE ONE SUNG NUMERIC FACT
**"About four minutes" — how long an arm holds a tempo.** It arrives at the emotional hinge as an
*instruction to the band*, not information to the listener: *"about four minutes in a pair of arms —
so play the fast one first."* Responded to, never recited. Spelled in words. **It is the only number
sung in the song** (counts like "the one" and "the two" are funk positions, not facts, but they are
swept anyway in step 10).

### VOICE
Female lead, throat-forward, unpolished, close and centre, **half a step ahead of the beat all night**
because she is leading a room that is behind her. Not pretty. Not belting-as-technique. The sound of
someone making themselves audible across a hundred bodies with no help, which has a specific grain: the
consonants get violent, the vowels get short, the ends of lines get bitten off.

### ARRANGEMENT ARC (all four variations share the arc; the angles differ)
1. **cold open, dry** — bass and one clap, no count-in, five seconds
2. **full band, mid-song already** — we arrive after the start, because the night did
3. **refrain (source)**
4. ⭐ **THE SEAM** — one beat of nothing, hands already going
5. **hands and voice only** — the longest exposed section; refrain across the seam
6. **flash one** — clap doubles on the offbeat
7. **the reflection** — refrain returned by the room, late and low
8. **descarga break** — hand percussion and bass rebuild, no keys, the horn re-enters last
9. **flash two** — response on top of call
10. **last refrain, hottest**
11. **cut off mid-phrase.** Not a fade. Not a ritardando.

### ⛔ HARD DON'TS FOR THIS GUIDE
no candlelight · no cosiness · no acoustic-guitar-by-lamplight · no equipment beyond the one dead fader ·
no *dark* in a title · no water · no sky · no *current* · no audience-flattery · no visible villain ·
no serene ending · no fade-out · no pad in the gap · no long full stop · no listener address · no
gratitude for the cut · nothing about being unheard — **she is heard perfectly and she is unpowered.**

### THE FOUR VARIATION SEATS (derived, detailed in step 08)
- **V1 THE TWO SECONDS** — real time, the decision, the seam dead centre.
- **V2 THE DEAD FADER** — the hand that has nowhere to be; slowest, heaviest, most physical.
- **V3 THE RETURN** — the room's side; antiphon maximal, the reflection is the whole second half.
- **V4 THE ARITHMETIC** — endurance; the four minutes; hottest and fastest-feeling, cut off first.
