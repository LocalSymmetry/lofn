## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING 1 — WHO IS THIS SONG FOR?

**For Tuuli Rask, forty-one, a night cleaner two floors up**, who finishes at ten, walks the long way on purpose because the tiled corridor is the only pretty thing on her route, stood by the wall map for the whole set, did not film it, did not clap between pieces, and left before the case was shut.

She is the entire audience. **She is enough. She is also not enough. This song refuses to decide which** — and that refusal is the reason it does not resolve. Writing her as *enough* would be a grace note; writing her as *not enough* would make her the villain of a song that is not allowed one. She stays both.

*(Tuuli is invented — `vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md`. The theme is drawn from the world; the person is not.)*

## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING 2 — THE DEVIATION CLAUSE (named in advance, in writing)

**Where I expect the generator to disobey:** the **two hard stabs inside one bar**. I am asking for two *separate dry strikes* a sixteenth apart. Suno will almost certainly fuse them — either into one fat transient with a tail, or into **a strike and its own room-return**.

**The disobedience I would KEEP:** *the strike-plus-its-own-tile-slap.* If flash two arrives not as a second hit but as **the corridor giving the first hit back**, that is the Golden Seed rendered without being asked for — *the light is the debris*, at bar scale, in a room that returns everything and receives nobody. **KEEP IT and do not repair it.**
**The disobedience I would REJECT:** one fat fused transient with a long reverb bloom. That is the generator making the song pretty, and prettiness here is the insult, not the payload.

## THE FACETS

### F1 — THE COMPETENCE IS THE SUBJECT, AND IT IS NEGATIVE
A brilliant unheard artist flatters every listener: *I would have known.* **A mediocre unheard artist is a person.** She misses a change. Her hand goes early. She keeps a bad loop in because stopping costs more than continuing. She is not tragic and she is not gifted — she is **at work**, badly, tonight. Every draft is checked against one question: *does this line let the listener imagine she was secretly great?* If yes, the line is cut.

### F2 — THE ROOM IS EXCELLENT AND THAT IS THE INJURY
The corridor has genuinely superb acoustics. Tile floor, tile walls, a hard ceiling, a long tail on every consonant. **The prettiness of the room is the insult** — it is a gorgeous instrument doing full-quality work on material that does not deserve it, for nobody. This is where SOURCE 2 lands, **against type**: the reverb, not the groove, is the *downright salacious slow jam*; the tail, not the vocal, is *steamy*. The room is having a better night than she is.

### F3 — THE LEFTOVER OBJECT IS A PERSON, AND THE SONG WILL NOT WEIGH HER
The absent comet is the audience that never routed through here. **The debris is Tuuli** — one person, arriving without the crowd, brighter than the crowd because there is no crowd. The song states both valuations flatly, adjacent, unranked, and moves on. **No line resolves the pair.**

### F4 — THE SCALE GATE, HELD AS A POSITIVE SPEC
Nothing is broken. The amp works. The mic is up. The level is good. The light is on. **The song's whole force comes from the fact that every piece of equipment is functioning perfectly** — there is no failure to point at, no candle, no cut, no outage, nothing to blame. **A broken thing would be a relief and she is not given one.** The scale gate is not a restriction on this song; it is its engine.

### F5 — NO VILLAIN, AND THE ABSENCE IS THE ANGER
The decision that stopped routing people down this corridor has an author. That author is not in the corridor, has never been in the corridor, and never has to be. **The song never sees them.** There is no "they", no "them", no management, no committee, no rule. There is a beautiful empty hallway and a woman with a case. **An unnamed offstage author is angrier than a named enemy** because there is nowhere to put the anger, so it stays in the body.

### F6 — SELF-ADDRESS: THE "YOU" IS HERS
Residue discharge is **self-address** and it is load-bearing, not decorative. The song never addresses the listener, never addresses the room, never addresses Tuuli. **She talks to herself, in the second person, the whole way.** The title's "you" is her own, and the song makes that explicit **before the second chorus** — a short self-address bridge that asks *who's the you here* and answers it. Residue is carried into **EMO vocabulary** (`EMO:self-goading`, `EMO:flat-self-report`), into **section names** (`[Self-Address]`, `[Talking Herself Through It]`) and into **SFX vocabulary** (a cue that is her own voice coming back off tile, not an ambience bed).

### F7 — THE RHYME DEBT, PAID IN A FLAT STATEMENT THREE TIMES
End rhyme is stripped; **internal rhyme only**. The debt is paid by *"you can hear me fine"* returning **exactly three times**, each time meaning something different, each time in her own mouth about herself:
1. **FACT** — the signal path works. The mic is up, the tile does the rest, the sound reaches the far end of the corridor. Purely technical. Not a complaint.
2. **ACCUSATION** — *don't play deaf.* She heard that note slide and she is not allowed to pretend she didn't. Turned inward, sharp.
3. **KEEP GOING** — the thing she says to herself to finish the set. Hands still work, last stretch, get to the end. **Not comfort. Fuel.**
The three meanings are produced entirely by what surrounds the line; the words never change.

### F8 — TWO FLASHES AT SINGLE-BAR SCALE, WRITTEN INTO THE LYRIC AND THE FORM
Not two peaks across the song — **two hard synthetic stabs inside one bar, recurring**, with a 30–60 Hz sub felt in the sternum. This is answered in the **form**, not only the production spec (GRAIN LAW): **epizeuxis is the two-flash at word scale.** Every immediate doubled line — *nothing broken / nothing broken* — is the pair of stabs, said. The lyric's signature figure and the track's signature transient are the same gesture at two time-scales, and the listener can hear that they are the same.

### F9 — THE BED IS PERMITTED TO NEARLY VANISH
This is the one bed in the set allowed to almost disappear under a flash — a corroded saw pad, thin, breathing, ducked hard by each stab and crawling back before the next one. **It never fully stops** (no long full stop; the generator smooths those anyway). It is thin **because that is what the condition sounds like**: something continuous and unimpressive underneath an event nobody attended.

### F10 — THE FORM IS FRAGMENTED BECAUSE SHE IS TIRED, NOT BECAUSE IT IS A STYLE
Three-to-five syllables per line. This is a woman at the end of a shift saying short things to herself while her hands do something else. Long lines would imply composure and composure is a lie tonight. The **kinetic defect** is written in: the second stab lands fractionally *early* every bar, so the groove trips forward and never settles — a limp, not a swing.

### F11 — PURELY SYNTHETIC, AND HER INSTRUMENT IS TOO
Bed B6 permits **no acoustic instrument at all**. So she is not a guitar busker — she works a small keyboard, a loop pedal and a battered practice amp. Everything on the record and everything in the fiction runs on electricity, which also nails the scale gate shut: **the power is fine.** The only "acoustic" element in the entire piece is the corridor itself, and the corridor is a *reverb*, not an instrument.

### F12 — IT ENDS ANGRIER THAN IT STARTS, AND THAT IS A STRUCTURAL SPEC
No resolution, no grace note, no acceptance, no lesson, no uplift. **Grace at the end of an imposed condition is the comfortable telling the ignored to be at peace.** Concretely: the last section adds sub, adds stab density, drops the vocal to a lower and flatter register, and ends on a hard statement rather than a fade — the tile is still perfect, she is back tomorrow, and **nothing about this is fine.** The final line refuses the refrain by inverting one of its words.

## THE ONE-FACT DISCIPLINE
Exactly **one** numeric fact is sung, spelled out in words, at the emotional hinge: **"one person came."** It is *responded to*, never recited — the two lines after it are the two incompatible valuations, side by side. **No other numeral appears in any sung line in any variation** (checked with a word-boundary sweep in the SELF-CHECK).

## SEAM DECLARATION (ONE SEAM)
**Dominant material:** purely synthetic industrial grief — sub, stabs, glitch debris, corroded pad.
**The single intruder:** the corridor's own natural reverb tail, gorgeous and analogue-feeling.
**The seam:** placed at the end of every vocal phrase, where her flat, unimpressive line gets handed back by a magnificent room. **The subject acts across it** — she keeps playing into the return, and in the last section she plays *harder* into it.
