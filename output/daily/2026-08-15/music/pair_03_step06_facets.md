## ⭐ 0. WHO IS THIS SONG **FOR**? *(a person who is not the artist)*

**Marta Oduya, a night-shift ward clerk in Bolton, fifty-one, who on the twelfth of August drove nine miles to a field she has passed twice a week for twenty years, watched the light leave the afternoon, went home, made tea, said almost nothing, drove back at midnight with the same folding chair, and spent the whole way home mildly furious that nobody had ever mentioned it does that every August.**

She is the audience. Not a stargazer. Not grieving. Not lonely. She went **because she wanted to**, nobody sent her, and the song's job is to hand her back the exact temperature of the drive home — which is **not wonder and not sadness.** It is **offence at the size of what was routinely available and never said.**

## ⭐⛔ 1. THE DEVIATION CLAUSE *(named in advance, in writing, before any render exists)*

**WHERE I EXPECT THE GENERATOR TO DISOBEY: the 7/8 marimba over the 4/4 tuned kick. It will straighten it.**

Suno will most likely resolve the cross-rhythm into a single 4/4 with a *syncopated* marimba figure sitting inside the bar — one grid, one rate, decorated.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD KEEP:** if it renders the seven as a **limping four** — a bar that arrives a beat early and drags the downbeat with it, so the marimba and the kick audibly **disagree about where the cycle ends** — I keep it, without repair. The audible fact I need is **TWO RATES IN ONE FIELD**, not the notation. A limp is a legitimate render of "the same curl at two sizes."

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD REJECT (repair, not keep):** a clean, quantised 4/4 in which the marimba lands on the grid and the kick lands on the grid and **nothing disagrees**. That is one rate. That kills the thesis, and the thesis is the form, not the arrangement.

**SECOND, SMALLER DEVIATION — the tuned kick.** I expect weight to creep in. **KEEP** it if the kick still reads as a **note** (a pitch a listener could hum). **REJECT** it if it becomes an untuned low thump — that is a drone with a transient, THE GRAIN LAW's exact failure, and B5 explicitly carries no sub.

**THIRD — the choir.** I expect it to drift toward warmth and unison-church. This one is **never a keep.** The choir is a **texture with a defect** (slightly sour, secular, tuned a shade flat, breath audible). If it renders devotional the song has become a hymn about the sky, and the affront is unrecoverable.

## 2. FACETS

### F1 — THE LEFTOVER OBJECT IS **THE DARK ITSELF**
Not the meteors. The **dark** — briefly, accidentally available, and the only reason any of it was visible. The eclipse's dark was the moon's cast-off shadow; the night's dark was a new moon's absence. Both are **leftovers of something that is not there**. The seed's line lands exactly: *a thing is heard through its leftovers.* The meteors are the second-order proof — dust outshining the comet that shed it.
**Ban compliance:** the word *dark* is free in the lyric (it is the subject) and **forbidden in every title.**

### F2 — THE BODY, THE PLACE, THE RISK — AND THE RISK IS A CLOCK
**Body:** two people, one field, **one aluminium folding chair between them.** They take turns. One stands, one sits, they swap. **Doing, not contemplating:** unfolding, dragging, seating the legs into the same four dents, lying back on flattened grass, writing a date on a card.
**Risk (this is what makes it AWE and not a scented candle):** **the moon is coming back. It has already started. It is eleven percent refilled and the window is closing in front of them, tonight, measurably.** The terror is not a predator; it is a **rate**. The dark is on loan and the loan is being called in while they sit in it.

### F3 — ⭐ ONE SEAM · N = 1
- **DOMINANT MATERIAL (the world):** the field at night — cold grass, moving air, the smell of cut grass going black.
- **THE ONE INTRUDER (the wound):** **the aluminium folding chair.** Manufactured, thin, cold, squeaks. It is the only object in the song that was made in a factory.
- **THE SEAM:** where the chair legs press into the turf — four dents that were still there on the second night.
- **THE SUBJECT ACTS ACROSS IT:** they **carry the same chair through both skies.** One object crosses the join between the two nights, and it is the only thing that does.
⛔ No second intruder. No collision engine. The variety belongs across the run, never inside this frame.

### F4 — THE COMPOSITE, RENDERED LITERALLY AT SECTION SCALE
| event | where | scale | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **opening** | bars 1–8 | low, one mallet | **not** a flash — the ICB forbids a peak at the very start |
| **FLASH ONE** | section 3 | full bright peak | the afternoon: the light leaves, birds stop, cold comes up through shoe soles |
| **HALF-SIZE EVENT** | section 4 | **half** peak, marimba only, no choir | the six hours between skies — a kettle, a lane, a kitchen |

⭐ **Panel C's finding is honoured:** without the third half-size event the generator fills the middle with wash. The kettle section exists to be **structurally load-bearing**, not decorative.

### F5 — THE FORM IS A CATALOGUE, AND THE CATALOGUE IS THE ARGUMENT
Three inventories — **in the bag / in the field / in the sky** — with **anaphora** as the signature and **one line returning UNCHANGED at the end of every stanza.** Unrhymed; cohesion carried by an **assonance chain** (the flat /æ/ of *bag, flask, black, grass, packed, plank, lamp* against the /aʊ/ of *out, down, ground, hour* at the flashes).
**Why a catalogue and not a narrative:** a list has **no argument in it.** It cannot lecture. It lets the affront arrive in the last stanza as a *finding* rather than as a claim the song has been building toward. The list is also the honest grammar of two people packing a bag twice in one day.

### F6 — RESIDUE DISCHARGE: **THIRD PERSON PLURAL, OBSERVED FROM OUTSIDE**
"They" throughout. **"You" and "I" appear zero times** — measured in code, and measured in the **EMO vocabulary, the SFX cue and the section names too**, not only the sung lines. No gendered singulars either (*he stood / she sat* is replaced by *one stood, one sat, then they swapped*) — the camera never enters a head, and never picks a favourite.
**The cost, accepted:** the song forfeits intimacy of address. **What buys it back:** proximity of *object*. If nobody may be addressed, the microphone gets close to the things — the squeak, the elastic band, the four dents.

### F7 — ⭐ THE POLYRHYTHM IS THE THESIS, NOT THE PRODUCTION
7/8 over 4/4. **Two cycles, two rates, one field.** The eclipse runs on one clock (once in a lifetime, in one place); the Perseids run on another (every August, everywhere). They coincided once, by accident, over the same grass. **The same curl at two sizes** — the wind coming over the grass in travelling bands is the same instability that curls plasma twenty kilometres wide on the Sun.
⛔ **THE GRAIN LAW compliance:** this is *not* answered in the production spec alone. It is in the **form** (two flashes at section scale, on two different structural clocks) and in the **lyric** (*"Once in a lifetime. Once every year."* is V2's returning line). The spec is the third statement of it, never the first.
⛔ **The water image is refused** — P02 owns water. The Kelvin-Helmholtz curl ships as **wind bands in dry grass**, which is the same physics and none of P02's vocabulary.

### F8 — ⭐ THE ENDING IS AN AFFRONT (donated engine, C04)
Not *wasn't that lovely.* **How long has this been happening while they were indoors.**
The affront is **mild and permanent** — the exact register of finding out that something enormous is routine, free, annual, and was never mentioned. It is aimed at **nobody**: there is no villain, no institution, no author onstage. That is what keeps it inside the ICB's governing distinction — **this is not a grievance about being unheard and it is emphatically not a power cut.** It is the correctly-scaled offence of a world that keeps things fine-grained and points them away from people.
**CHOSEN, not imposed:** *Nobody sent them. Nobody was owed.* Quiet, complete, **not sad** — and then offended.

### F9 — THE HINGE CARRIES EXACTLY ONE NUMBER, AND IT IS ANSWERED
**"In the sky: eleven percent of the moon."** — immediately **responded to, never recited**: *"It is coming back. It has already started."* Spelled in words. Every other numeral in the lyric is an **object count** (one chair, two people, two at once), not a research datum. Flagged, not hidden, in the SELF-CHECK.

### F10 — THE CHOIR IS A TEXTURE WITH A DEFECT
Wide, high, tuned a shade flat, breath left in, no vibrato, secular vowels. It never sings a word. It arrives **only at the two flashes** and is **absent from the half-size event** — its absence is how the middle event is measured as half. ⛔ It may not become churchy; the EXCLUDE field carries the whole devotional bloc.

### F11 — THE FIRST FIVE SECONDS
One rosewood marimba, one mallet, **close enough to hear the mallet head**, playing seven against nothing. No pad, no swell, no riser. The kick does not arrive until bar five, and when it does it arrives **as a pitch**. A listener should be able to tell within five seconds that this is a room-sized record about a field, not a cinematic record about space.

### F12 — WHAT DISQUALIFIES A DRAFT OF THIS PAIR
- a "you" or an "I" anywhere, including a section name
- water, a current, or *dark* in a title
- a devotional choir
- an ending that resolves into gratitude, wonder, or peace
- an untuned sub
- a numeric fact recited rather than answered
- an argument named instead of a thing named

### F13 — CROSS-PAIR ISOLATION SELF-AUDIT
No line factory, section map, rhyme scheme or hook grammar is shared with any other pair — I have read only the ICB and my own step-05 brief. **This pair's structural fingerprint:** anaphoric catalogue · unrhymed with an assonance chain · a line returning unchanged at every stanza end · third-person-plural only · two section-scale flashes with a half-size event between · 7/8 over 4/4. **P03 owns sky.** No water, no current, no *dark* in any title.
