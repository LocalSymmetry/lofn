## THE FOUR ANGLES — derived from P06's concept alone

All four are the same practice seen from four distances. **None of them is a different song about a
different situation** — the pair is one person's inventory, and the variation is *which object the hand is
holding, and how far from the car it has got.*

### V1 — **I KEPT THE CRANK IN THE CAR** · *the canonical inventory*
**Distance from the car: zero.** Boot open, jack up, the whole kit named in the order a hand reaches for
it. This is the reference rendering of the bed: drum machine and battery guitar in balance, the sub sag
every eighth bar. **First person: refrain only.** **The one number: *eleven hours*** — pencilled in a
glovebox log, answered by the crank coming out, not by a feeling.
*Ends:* the crank goes back under the jack.

### V2 — **THE GRIP WORE SMOOTH** · *the wear as the record*
**Distance: the object in close-up.** Every stanza is an object *seen through what use did to it* — the
knurl gone, the tape hard, the terminals furred, the spring dead, the notch filed in the fuse-box door so
it opens by feel. ⭐ This is the Golden Seed at its purest in this pair: **the wear outlives the nights that
made it, and the wear is the brighter thing.** Sonically the sparsest of the four — guitar-forward post-punk,
fewer notes, air between them, the machine holding a plain four. **First person: ZERO.**
**The one number: *the fourth winter***, answered by the hand finding the notch without looking.
*Ends:* the grip fits the hand that wore it.

### V3 — **BATTERY ON THE KITCHEN FLOOR** · *mass, weight, the haul*
**Distance: indoors, the interior.** The domestic scene as a load-bearing problem — tile, towel, terminals,
inverter, the socket with the faceplate off. Heaviest of the four: techno-forward, sub dominant, guitar
reduced to one muted stab on the offbeat. **First person: ZERO.**
**The one number: *forty pounds***, answered by both arms and a braced foot.
*Ends:* towel folded, terminals clean, floor clear.

### V4 — **I RAN THE CORD DOWN THE HALL** · *the landing, the neighbour, the arithmetic*
**Distance: two landings.** The stanza inventory gets shorter and faster because the decision is being made
while she moves. The risk facet is fully present: one battery, two doors, no authority. Most aggressive —
double-time hat over the same 128, hardest edit at the second flash. **First person: refrain only.**
**The one number: *two landings down***, answered by the cord going that way.
*Ends:* ⭐ **nothing happened. again.** — the unprovable absence, credited to nobody, thanked by nobody.

⛔ **Cross-check against P01 (the other blackout song):** P01 is one event in real time, call-and-response,
a room, and its conclusion is *because of other people*. P06 is a practice years wide, inventory grammar,
no room at all, and its conclusion is *because of preparation*. No shared object, no shared refrain
grammar, no shared section map. **P06 does not depict an audience in any variation.**

## DRAFT MATERIAL — the tercet engine

**Rule set on every draft line:** ABA (lines 1 & 3 rhyme, line 2 flat) · **asyndeton** — no *and / but / or
/ nor / so / yet* anywhere · zero *you / we / us / our / your* · no *room, crowd, audience, applause,
listener, stage* · no *dark, candle, cosy, warm glow* · no water, no sky · **one numeric fact per song.**

### Draft — the crank (V1 A1)

> steel handle, colder than the wheel arch
> the knurl gone smooth in one exact track

*(Kept. The third line is the whole seed in seven words: the knurl is gone, the nights are gone, the
smoothness is the only surviving record and it is the brightest thing on the object.)*
