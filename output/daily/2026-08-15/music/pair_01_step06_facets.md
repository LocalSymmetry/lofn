## ⛔ THE TWO OPENING ANSWERS (required before any facet)

### 1. WHO IS THIS SONG FOR?
**For Delia Quintana** — invented, sixty-one, a retired bus dispatcher who has stood at the same spot by
the left-hand pillar of that hundred-capacity room every other Thursday for eleven years — **because she
is the one who started clapping, and no song has ever been addressed to the person who starts the
clapping.**

*(She is a fiction, per `vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md`: the condition is drawn from the world, the
person is invented. The song never names her. She is the ear the writing is aimed at.)*

### 2. ⭐ THE DEVIATION CLAUSE — where I expect the generator to disobey, and what I would KEEP
**EXPECTED DISOBEDIENCE:** the butt-cut. I have asked for a hard edit at a section boundary with a
one-beat gap covered by hands already in progress. **Suno will almost certainly refuse the cliff and
render a filtered duck instead** — a fast low-pass swallow of the band with a tail, claps rising under
it, everything technically continuous.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD KEEP:** if the band does not vanish but **collapses to the centre** — stereo
width slamming shut from wide to mono at the boundary while the claps stay wide — **I keep it and I
would ask for it next time.** It is the same event (something was taken out of the room) executed with
the generator's grain instead of against it, and per THE GRAIN LAW the result is judged, not the distance
from intent. Precedent: stereo *narrowing* into a climax was banked as a technique on 2026-08-15.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD NOT KEEP, AND WOULD CUT THE TAKE FOR:** a **crossfade with a reverb tail**, or
any keyboard arriving to "hold" the gap. A pad in the hole is the generator being polite about a power
cut, and it inverts the moral spine — it makes an imposed condition sound like an aesthetic. Second
non-keeper: a **fade-out ending**. This song has to be cut off, not laid down.

## FACET EXPANSION — twelve facets

### F1 · THE ARITHMETIC OF HANDS (the physics that makes this a thriller)
Clapping is **work**. Untrained arms hold a tempo for roughly four minutes before the count sags and
the room self-corrects into a slow handclap, which is a different and worse song. So the set order
inverts in real time: **you play the fast one first, while the arms are fresh.** This is the ticking
clock and it is entirely physical — no metaphor required, no villain visible. It is also where the
run's ONE sung numeric fact lives, and it is **responded to, never recited**: the number arrives as an
instruction to the band, not as information to the listener.

### F2 · THE DEAD FADER IS THE ONLY EQUIPMENT IN THE SONG
P06 owns equipment; P01 gets exactly one object and it is a **fader under a palm that no longer moves
anything.** It is the perfect P01 prop because it is a *control*, and the song's subject is a decision
made somewhere else. She does not take her hand off it. That is not sentiment — it is that the hand has
nowhere else to be, and a hand with nowhere to be is the most honest picture of an imposed condition I
can put on a stage. **No PA, no monitors, no breaker, no cable, no generator, no amp, no mic.**

### F3 · THE ROOM IS THE SECOND PERSON — AND IT IS A SPEAKING PART, NOT A DEVICE
"You" appears often and always means **the hundred people three feet from her shoes.** She gives them
instructions, she argues with them, she thanks one of them wrong. The listener is never addressed,
never flattered, never invited to feel included. ⛔ **Counted in EMO vocabulary, SFX vocabulary and
section names too:** section names are stage-world (*Hands Only*, *Room Answer*, *Break*, *Last Count*),
EMO values are drawn from the run taxonomy and none of them is a stance toward a listener.

### F4 · THE ANTIPHON IS AN ARGUMENT, NOT A SINGALONG
Call-and-response here is not celebration-by-default. The first response is **ragged and too quiet**,
and she has to go back and get it. The second is on top of her. The third overtakes her — the room
starts the refrain before she does, which is the moment the song stops being hers. **That progression is
the plot.** An antiphon where the room is right the first time is a beer advert.

### F5 · THE REFRAIN MUST SURVIVE BEING HUMMED WITH NO INSTRUMENTS
Panel B's rule, and this song has to prove it literally. Constraints therefore: **six words or fewer per
line, one syllable dominant, no melisma, no interval wider than a fifth, and the rhythm carried by the
consonants** so it works shouted. Test applied: can a sixty-one-year-old woman at the left-hand pillar
return it correctly on her second hearing, at her own pitch, half a beat behind? If not, it is rewritten.

### F6 · THE FLASHES ARE TWO MOMENTS THE ROOM GETS LOUDER — NEITHER AT THE START
**Flash one:** the room finds the offbeat and the clap doubles in density — the sound of a hundred people
realising they are the drummer. **Flash two:** after the descarga break, the response comes back **on
top of the call instead of after it**, which is louder by collision, not by volume. Neither flash is a
production event; both are behaviours in the room, so the generator cannot smooth them away by turning
something down.

### F7 · THE REFLECTION IS THE REFRAIN COMING BACK OFF THE CROWD — INVERTED, LATE, WRONG-PITCHED
The GHOST ECHO, made of people. It arrives **displaced** (late), **inverted** (her line ends up, theirs
ends down), and **quieter than the source in energy but louder in mass.** It is the seed in one line
made audible: *the leftover is brighter than the source* — the room's version is worse in every
measurable way and it is the version that finishes the song.

### F8 · HEAT, NOT ATMOSPHERE — THE BLACKOUT IS NOT AN AESTHETIC
The register is **sweat, salt, breath, forearms, throat, the smell of a hundred people, the boards under
her heels.** ⛔ Nothing about how beautiful it is when the lights go. No candles, no cosiness, no
acoustic-guitar-by-lamplight, no "we found something purer without the electricity." If it renders
pretty it has inverted the moral spine and it is a REPAIR. The word *dark* does not appear in a title.

### F9 · THE AUTHOR IS OFFSTAGE AND STAYS THERE
**Load shedding is authored.** Somebody chose the schedule, and the song never sees them, never names
them, never gets to shout at them. The anger has no face to land on, so it lands on the **work** — which
is why the song is hot rather than aggressive. This is also the pair's whole relationship to the run's
governing distinction: **this is a power cut, literally, not a metaphor for a toggle.** Nothing in this
lyric is about being unheard. She is heard perfectly. She is unpowered.

### F10 · WHAT ENDING HOT ACTUALLY MEANS
No resolve, no thank-you, no "and in the end we didn't need it anyway." **Grace at the end of an imposed
condition is the comfortable telling the ignored to be at peace.** The last line lands mid-phrase, the
count still running, arms still up — the song is cut off, not concluded. The generator's default is a
tidy ritardando or a fade; both are excluded in the EXCLUDE field and both would be a failed take.

### F11 · THE BED IS PEOPLE, AND IT NEVER STOPS ONCE IT STARTS
Continuous faint bed = the clapping. ⛔ **Never a pad.** Once the hands start they run to the last bar,
including under the loudest band sections, where they are buried but present. This is what makes the
one-beat gap survivable — the gap is not silence, it is **the band gone and the hands already going**,
which is the whole thesis in one beat.

### F12 · WHY THIS IS A CELEBRATION AND WHERE IT STOPS BEING ONE
The indignation and the joy are the same energy in this song: **the room is delighted, and the delight
is a rebuke.** A hundred people demonstrating in real time that the necessary equipment was them all
along is a party and a verdict at once. ⛔ Where it stops: the song must never say the cut was *good
for them*. That is the sentence a comfortable person writes. The set was ruined. They finished it anyway.
Both are true and the song holds both without reconciling them.
