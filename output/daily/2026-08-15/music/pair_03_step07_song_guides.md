## ⭐ THE TWO QUESTIONS, ANSWERED FIRST

### **WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?**
**In a mown hay field nine miles outside a town, on the second night, at roughly one in the morning.** Ground still holding the day's heat at the ankles and giving it up fast. Cut grass going black underfoot and smelling of it. A five-bar gate at the top corner. A lamp on a post at the far end that comes on by itself. **Two people, one aluminium folding chair between them, seated into the same four dents it made yesterday afternoon.** A canvas bag at the chair's foot: a flask, a torch with red tape stuck over the lens, a pencil, a plank of card, a blanket packed flat. One of them is in the chair. The other is flat on their back on the grass with the blanket under their shoulders, which is the better seat and both of them know it.

### **WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?**
Five answers, ordered from the small true ones to the one that is the song.
1. **The cold coming up through the shoe soles**, which it did in the afternoon, in August, in daylight, which is the wrong way round and is the first thing that frightened them.
2. **The chair.** Thin aluminium, one leg sinking, a squeak that carries further at night than it has any right to. It is the only manufactured thing in the frame and it is the thing most likely to fail.
3. **The lane home** in the dark with the eyes wrecked by the torch, which is why the torch has red tape on it.
4. ⭐ **THE MOON, WHICH IS COMING BACK.** Eleven percent tonight. It has already started. The dark they are sitting in is **on loan**, the loan is being called in **while they sit in it**, and next year this same night is washed out. **The risk is a rate, not a predator** — and a rate you can watch is worse, because there is nothing to do about it and it is visibly in progress.
5. ⭐⭐ **The finding itself.** That it does this **every August**, over that field, over their roof, over the road, for free, and nobody said. That does not hurt like grief. **It hurts like an insult that cannot be returned to a sender**, and it is permanent, and it will be there next August too.

**Why this is AWE and not a scented candle:** the awe and the fear are the *same fact*. The dark is what made it visible; the dark is expiring; they are watching the expiry in real time and choosing to stay.

## VOCAL GUIDE
**Female lead, adult, plain, unornamented, centre and slightly behind the marimba, speaking-close** — the microphone is on the grass, not in a booth. Chest register throughout, almost no vibrato, no runs, **no ad-libs**. She is **reporting**, and she is not in the field: she is the third-person camera at the treeline. The one permitted rise is at the hinge word *eleven*, and the one permitted drop is on *nobody had ever said.*
⛔ She never addresses the listener. She never addresses the two people. **There is no vocative in this song.**

## HARMONIC / TONAL GUIDE
Modal, no functional cadence, no resolution to a tonic at the end — the song stops on the returning line, not on a chord. Marimba in a raised-fourth mode so the bright peaks read as *strange-bright* rather than *major-happy*. Choir a shade flat against the marimba on purpose; the beating between them is the defect and it is the only "reverb" the choir gets.

## RHYTHMIC GUIDE — ⭐ THE THESIS
**Marimba: 7/8. Tuned kick: 4/4. 96 BPM felt in the four.** The cycles realign every 56 eighth-notes — roughly every seven seconds of a listener's attention — and **the realignment is never marked.** Nothing lands on it. That is the whole idea: two rates that coincided once, by accident, over one field.
**Kinetic defect:** the marimba's final stroke of each seven-cycle arrives a beat early against the kick and drags the next downbeat with it. Audible limp. Wanted.

## LEXICAL GUIDE — the assonance chain
**Spine A (flat /æ/), the catalogue register:** bag · flask · black · grass · packed · plank · lamp · band · back · had · that.
**Spine B (/aʊ/), the flash register:** out · down · ground · hour · around · loud.
**Spine C (/ɪ/), the hinge:** it · in · did · still · sitting · lifting.
Unrhymed at the line end by design; **the return is carried by the unchanged final line and by anaphora**, which is this pair's discharged debt for stripping rhyme.
