## ⭐⭐ THE RULE THIS RUN DERIVED — **THE CREDIT FOLLOWS THE FIELD**
Four pairs ruled on **Larry Graham** independently, in isolation, and all four agree under one rule:

> **A technique RAISED in the STYLE field owes its player. The same technique BANNED in EXCLUDE owes
> nobody.** The credit follows **which field the token is in**, not whether the words appear in the file.

P01 and P06 credit him (slap-and-pop raised in style); P02 and P04 decline (slap bass refused in EXCLUDE);
P03 credits **Kélétigui Diabaté** for the balafon figure its marimba copies; P05 declines, no figure raised.
⛔ **P06's first draft of all four dedications said *"no named figure is asked for, so no player is
credited"* — and that was FALSE**, while reading exactly like diligence. **An over-credit is visible and
embarrassing; an under-credit reads as a completed check.** This is `DEDICATION_STANDARD`'s own thesis
landing on the standard itself.

⚠️ **AND IT DOES NOT RESOLVE ITS HARDEST CASE — held for The Scientist.** P02 credited five composers for
techniques that are **not breaks**; P05 declined all credit while its style field raises *"brushes already
circling the snare head"* as the first sound and the literal composite element of the whole pair. Both
reasoned, both in writing, **both cannot be right.** Only a human promotes a lesson to a rule.
