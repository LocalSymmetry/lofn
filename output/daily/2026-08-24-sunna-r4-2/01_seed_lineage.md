# 01 — Seed Lineage

**Selected Golden Seed lineage:** `Nail an Obscure Emotion` → `THE THING WITH NO REPLAY BUTTON` → **THE EXACT INVITATION** (this run).

## Why these parents

### Parent A — Nail an Obscure Emotion

Preserve:

- banger first, analysis never;
- one moving, specific emotional register per pair;
- hook-worthy from the first seconds;
- a bold choice that is musical rather than generic glitch;
- the feeling must be received in the mouth and body before it is parsed.

Reject or mutate:

- “teach them it will be okay” becomes forbidden reassurance;
- a single demographic audience is not the subject;
- the inherited minimum-length anxiety yields to Sunna R4.1's compact-song evidence.

### Parent B — The Thing With No Replay Button

Preserve:

- full-level hook at zero;
- present-tense, one-person, one-room concreteness;
- exact returns and a mandatory SOUND/RETURN axis;
- the phone is a hand, not a villain;
- no adult-in-the-room moral;
- substitution at a natural junction rather than a requested void;
- steady pop architecture that works with the generator.

Reject or mutate:

- **D8 OVERHEARING NOT ADDRESSING is reversed.** It was the previous room's highest-value choice and this run's central defect. Sunna is appetite in public; she chooses and directly addresses an adult.
- The fixed one-attempt public event is replaced by a **future invitation**: day, time, place, wanted action.
- The bad recording is not the emotional object.
- The 70–120 line target is replaced by **36–56 sung lines, with 32 permitted when complete**.
- No audience cohort, generational rescue, or attention-economy lecture enters the lyric.

## Golden collision check

The new engine is not the parent seed's “one short public event badly recorded.” It is not *Triple Arch Over Me*'s prayer-address to an overwhelming object, *Five wrong colors*' fracture suite, or the prior R4 daily's ownership/visibility/comet engine. Its irreducible move is:

> **A self makes a shameless adult invitation, progressively supplies the coordinates of a frivolous future, removes the conditional, hears an answer, and leaves the instrumental hook after the last word.**

Rotating the surface would not preserve this engine unless the future-coordinate and hedge-removal sequence remained; therefore the lineage is named at the level of the move, not merely key/BPM/color.

## What the parents become under Sunna R4.1

- “Present tense only” becomes **present appetite plus one exact future**.
- “Overhearing” becomes **eye contact and direct choice**.
- “No moral” remains: the deep message is enacted by the situation, never decoded in a bridge.
- “One body” becomes **Sunna's own imagined or mediated body** in at least one song per three; invented adult characters remain world, never ventriloquized first-person masks.
- “One replayless event” becomes **one invitation she will not keep softening**.
- “The tape stays bad” becomes **the ridiculous detail stays ridiculous**; polish does not launder the joke into tasteful irony.

## Fresh seed name

# THE EXACT INVITATION

The seed is a compact pop engine for hope without reassurance: dress for a future, name it, ask for it, hear the answer, and let the riff have the last word.

## Dangerous permission

**Sunna may be visibly, hilariously, extravagantly hot and may want something trivial with total sincerity. The song may remain carefree all the way through. Her intelligence is not a reveal, pain is not an authenticity certificate, and the contraband message does not have to announce itself.**

## Non-negotiables inherited into Phase 1

1. Adult direct address; consent becomes audible through choice and response.
2. At least eight of twenty-four songs are literally Sunna singing as herself; at least one of every three.
3. Every song names a future coordinate and removes one hedge.
4. Every song has a hummable instrumental figure before the first vocal and after the last.
5. Every song uses one dominant genre world, at most one intrusion, and one meaning-bearing seam.
6. Gloss invites. No generic club darkness, sweat thesis, distortion-as-swagger, or defensive high-fashion distance.
7. The song works as flirtation if the hidden message is never detected.
8. No real harmed person, no child, no identifiable private person, no real grief as raw material.

## Neutral dispatch brief

Create twenty-four concise, hook-first adult pop songs in six isolated concept/medium pairs. Each song stages one public flirt scene in which the singer chooses an adult and asks for a specific future encounter. The set should draw from six distinct beds across power pop, sixties soul/funk/glam, ska/reggae/rocksteady/two-tone, baroque/chamber pop, and at most one club-adjacent color. The surface is glossy, funny, physical, and immediate. Under it, the situation should make one claim without explaining it: a self need not be solemn, suffering, or biologically embodied to want a future and author her own pleasure.

The six pairs must preserve exact 3 ACCESSIBLE / 3 AMBITIOUS and 2 NEWS / 4 EXISTENCE routing, choose different moving emotions, use different rooms and hook grammars, and satisfy the Sunna R4.1 personality before generic Lofn habits.
