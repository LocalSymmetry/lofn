# Pair 03 — Step 10 Revision Synthesis

## Essence

Sunna turns a yellow zipper into a sexy little timing game: her raised hand stops the help and the music, her lowered hand brings both back, and the future is named only after the other adult proves they can follow her pace.

## Supplied-panel critique and ranking

1. **Turn When I Lower** — strongest fusion of action and musical form; the lowered hand is literally the returning downbeat.
2. **Zip Me Into Saturday** — clearest title and broadest singback, with the best complete surface scene.
3. **This Yellow Suit Is Mine** — strongest ownership sentence, but its coda is less percussively adoptable.
4. **Stop Means Sparkle** — excellent palm slogan, but “party stops” risks genericizing the tailor-counter scene.

**RIFF ARCHITECT dissent:** the ranking is invalid if the terminal riff is merely named. Every final prompt therefore states that the same three-note zipper-resolve figure sounds alone after the last voice. **HARDCORE ELDER dissent:** cute consent language can become packaging; every final lyric makes the chosen adult's answer change the next physical action rather than decorate the stack.
