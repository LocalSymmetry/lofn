Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, lacquer-bright and honeyed chest-led mezzo A3-E5, using clipped pickups and held final vowels. Strutting electric bass stays mono-center beneath dry stomp-claps, tight kick, crisp tambourine and clean guitar doubles. Her close lead sits center; title stacks widen while the adult answer stays narrow and dry. Rising questions lose the downbeat. The clavinet/guitar cell takes one post-vocal bar with a pen click. Electric band uses acoustic claps, tambourine, paper and pen; guitar remains clean electric throughout.
