# Blind Song Package

## Title
Your Name Wears Pink

## Music Prompt
124 BPM glam-funk power pop, high-gloss and teasing with folded-paper snap; the first five seconds pair a three-note clavinet cell center-left with a descending clean-guitar fourth right, then leave the fourth beat blank under a paper fold. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, honey-bright and lightly raspy chest-led mezzo G3-E5, using low pickup notes and sharp upward command turns. Electric bass struts mono-center beneath dry kick, stomp-claps, tambourine and lacquered guitar doubles. Her close lead owns center; each question folds the groove smaller while the adult reply moves from dry right toward center. At the unfolded-page hinge, a black pen writes the second name; claps vanish and clipped two-tone guitar carries the pulse until the title drops into a statement. Full groove restores the missing beat, then the hook cell and paper snap close hard. Electric band, paper and pen only; no acoustic guitar.
