# Blind Song Package

## Title
Shake It Into Mine

## Music Prompt
Shameless high-gloss bass-led rocksteady at 102 BPM, intimate and shaped as an expanding hand-to-hand verb round. A zigzag five-note walking electric-bass pickup stays within a fourth and ends on a held second; two bone-dry center-left guitar strokes answer before the vocal, and the identical figure returns after the final line. Forward mono bass stays exact under late rim-clicks, shallow organ bubbles, and one low brass reply. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, glossy throaty full-chest mezzo and honeyed bite; long-I glides and breath-held caesuras make her phrasing distinct. A precise adult contralto owns “Salt too?” across a rim gap. Sunna answers one beat late and lowers her palm; shaker contact releases the sole hand-played dembow figure. Each later round gains a reciprocal verb before tight thirds open once. Lacquered transients frame dry shaker, appetite, and bass.
