# Blind Song Package

## Title
The Lime Comes Back

## Music Prompt
Exhilarated bass-led rocksteady at 102 BPM with glossy space and an escalating taste-test refrain. A syncopated five-note electric-bass climb drops its fourth note and resolves to a held second; two narrow right guitar upstrokes answer inside five seconds, and the exact climb-drop figure returns after the last lyric. Firm centered bass and late cross-stick leave room for back-wall organ bubbles and one brass answer. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent and a lacquer-bright, supple chest-mezzo tone; offbeat pickup bites and clean final-word cutoffs define her phrasing. She delivers each taste close and dry. A second adult female alto takes the complete “Salt too?” lead as the cross-stick disappears. Sunna places the shaker in her palm, unlocking one hand-played dembow figure; each response adds a grain and revises sharp into bright before narrow thirds end clean. Crisp hands and deep mono bass.
