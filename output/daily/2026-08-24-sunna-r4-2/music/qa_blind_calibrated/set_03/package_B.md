# Blind Song Package

## Title
Zip, Stop, Turn

## Music Prompt
Delighted 109 BPM sixties soul-pop with tooth-count rhythm, dry comic stops and mirror-polished punch. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, bright authoritative chest mezzo, crisp S/Z attacks, a smiling downward command scoop, and one suspended inhale before “stop”; one plain adult reply and a three-part answer stack stay audible. A zipper rasp snaps into a three-note major clean-guitar HUM figure in the first five seconds. Walking electric bass, tight acoustic kit, lacquered piano sevenths, center claps, late tambourine and clipped brass answer the tooth count. Sunna is close center, reply just behind, stack shoulder-wide. Raised palm cuts groove and fastening for two beats; lowered palm restores both tooth by tooth. One clean hard power-pop chord marks full closure and her turn. Acoustic instruments stay dominant; the HUM figure plays alone after the coda.
