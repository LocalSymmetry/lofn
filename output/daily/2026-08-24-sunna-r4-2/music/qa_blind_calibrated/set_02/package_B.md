# Blind Song Package

## Title
Two Blue, You Choose

## Music Prompt
105 BPM high-shine dembow-pop, cheeky challenge energy with competitive spring. Sunna is a late-twenties-to-early-thirties adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, lacquer-bright chest-led mezzo A3-D5, delighted steel and amused warmth; precise consonant taps and a smiling catch precede commands. Signature figure: blue-ball knock triggers a rounded three-note synth-bass pickup lightly doubled by electric bass, answered by two short dry rocksteady-organ notes around the voice. Dry center kick and snare anchor elastic mono bass; guiro and rim flick left, muted electric guitar ticks right, and glossy claps drag twelve milliseconds. Close rounds alternate scores with one firm adult reply on beat one. The windmill steals one beat from a sustained long-A call while organ holds the gap; one roll per player restores the bar. Girl-group thirds open around the sole final title, followed by the unchanged bass-organ receipt.
