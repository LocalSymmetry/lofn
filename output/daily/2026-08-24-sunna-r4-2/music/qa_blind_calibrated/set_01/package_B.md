In the first five seconds, dry electric guitar whistles up-up-down-up within a fifth beside one rim tap and fork clink. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, bright chest-led mezzo and polished ruby edge, using syncopated number phrasing and half-line echo catches. Walking pick bass stays centered; candy-hard slightly-late drums, handclaps, tambourine, compact thirds and clipped electrics flash left-right. Couplets withhold the hour while purse, finger and stool actions accumulate. At the threshold, one downstroke drops out and a two-tone upstroke answers her pointing gesture; the adult's two-forks reply restores the bar. The exact-minute rondo returns, then the four-note figure and fork clink stop clean. No acoustic guitar or second seam.
