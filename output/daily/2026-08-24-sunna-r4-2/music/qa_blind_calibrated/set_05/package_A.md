# Blind Song Package

## Title
Pick the Laughing One

## Music Prompt
Baroque sunshine pop at 121 BPM, candy-crisp and assertive; the first five seconds carry a dry five-note acoustic harpsichord climb, downward leap, and booth-flash snap. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, lacquer-clear chest mezzo and mischievous warmth, using one-beat rests after questions and a quick triplet curl on the title. Tight live kit and centered melodic electric bass hold the groove; glassy tambourine, left harpsichord, right pizzicato acoustic strings, and shallow-wide three-part replies stay crisp. One handclap lands half a beat late at selection. The adult's two-beat answer to Frame Two flips the later order to Frame Four then Frame Three; a single dry ska-horn phrase replaces the ornate answer only there. Final voices split around center for one title return, and the same harpsichord cell lands after the last word with a clipped horn tail. Acoustic guitar stays absent.
