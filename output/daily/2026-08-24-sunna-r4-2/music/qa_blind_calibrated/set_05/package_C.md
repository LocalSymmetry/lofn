# Blind Song Package

## Title
Pretty Busy Tomorrow

## Music Prompt
Baroque sunshine pop at 121 BPM, buoyant high-shine energy; the first five seconds expose a dry five-note harpsichord cell rising stepwise before a playful downward jump, followed by one photo-slot snap. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, bright chest-led mezzo and polished playful tone, using crisp consonants and a smiling command catch. Percussive acoustic harpsichord sits left, dry pizzicato acoustic strings right, melodic electric bass and tight live kit centered, candy-hard tambourine and shallow-wide three-part answers. Her lead stays close and center. A missing tambourine strike marks the paper tear; the adult's three-beat choice reorders the later couplets, and one short dry ska-horn answer replaces the ornate counterfigure at that seam. Final refrain thickens in interlocking thirds, then the same harpsichord cell returns alone with one horn tail. The palette stays harpsichord-, string-, bass-, and drum-led.

## Exclude Prompt
Generic orchestral grandeur, museum-chamber stiffness, harpsichord continuo without a pop groove, lounge jazz, cabaret acting, comedy voices, childish vocals, breathy ingénue delivery, male lead, vague mixed accent, soft-focus pads, cinematic strings, acoustic guitar, piano ballad, trap hats, EDM risers, supersaw drops, distorted guitars, club kick, reggae bed, repeated horn section, brass padding, long solo, tempo drift, half-time breakdown, spoken exposition, solemn bridge, beauty-versus-brains moral, technology lecture, body-part inventory, clock-time lyric, fade-out, and any real-artist imitation.
