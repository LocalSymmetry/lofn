# Pair 06 — Revision Synthesis

## Describe-Render Self-Check

These four renders should arrive as compact 124 BPM glam-funk flirt records whose three-note clavinet cell and descending clean-guitar fourth can be hummed before any title lands. Each begins in teasing question grammar, then lets a visible pink page, a moving black pen, and a second written name change the sung contour into a declaration; the main render risk is flattening that hinge into generic chorus lift, so every package removes percussion and gives the clipped two-tone guitar sole custody of the pulse at the signing instant. The two revised packages retain the strongest punctuation and checkbox mechanisms, while the two synthesized packages turn the fill-in and fold ideas into fresh declarations with sharper double meanings.
