## Concept 01A — Friday Looks Good on Me — primary

At a chrome diner after an escalator door is held, Sunna reserves the stool beside her with a red purse. She names Friday at eight seventeen but leaves the purse in place until the chosen adult sets down a second fork and asks for the seat. She moves the purse; the unchanged title becomes a confirmed shared table. Power-pop linear reservation.

## Concept 01B — Make It Two Forks — reserve

The same diner world is organized by fork count rather than title vanity. Each return adds or withholds one utensil; the chosen adult's `make it two forks` triggers the only threshold upstroke and purse movement. Strong answer mechanism, weaker first-person title.

## Concept 02A — Make It Nine — primary

At a blue-windmill miniature-golf hole, Sunna wagers: beat her score and choose the next round. The blade removes one beat from her long-A hook. The chosen adult writes nine-oh-five, circles the next round, and places a second blue ball beside hers; two rolls restore the full bar. The title is a competitive command, not a request for acceptance. Dembow-pop scorecard rounds.

## Concept 02B — Nine-Oh-Five, Beat Mine — reserve

The counter-time becomes the hook and the chosen adult leads the second round. It maximizes equality and reply ownership but loses the primary title's short singback. The same blade debt and two-roll repayment remain mandatory.

## Concept 03A — Turn When I Lower — primary

At a tailor counter, Sunna directs a chosen adult through the yellow zipper: raised palm stops zipper and groove; the adult confirms the stop; lowered palm restarts both. A hard clean chord arrives only at closure and her turn. Saturday at seven forty appears in the coda, after help has proven it follows her pace. Soul-pop stop/start antiphony.

## Concept 03B — Zip Me Into Saturday — reserve

The broad title carries the future more immediately, while the same palm mechanics govern action. It is the more adoptable singback and the less fused action/form title; retain as reserve if the primary hook proves too procedural in audio.

## Concept 04A — One Lime, Red Chair — primary

On a marina dock before next low tide, Sunna opens one red chair and cuts one lime. The chosen adult asks `Salt too?`, takes melodic ownership, receives the shaker, and starts the only hand-percussion seam. Lime and salt enlarge to a tide line once, then return to sting, shaker, chair, and hands. Rocksteady cumulative call-response.

## Concept 04B — Yes—Right There — reserve

The answer phrase becomes the foreground hook while lime and chair remain the accumulating world. It makes reciprocity unmistakable but is less visually singular than the primary object title.

## Concept 05A — Pretty Busy Tomorrow — primary

Beside a photo booth, Sunna tears a rejected four-shot strip into frames. The chosen adult taps the third; that frame crosses hands and determines the order of the later couplets. One horn answers the transfer. The original five-line refrain returns exactly after the selection, now meaning an authored future rather than a booth instruction. Baroque sunshine-pop cut-up rondeau.

## Concept 05B — Bring the Reject — reserve

The chosen adult's blunt invitation becomes the hook and yields a quicker public-facing chorus. It retains the tear and frame-routing engine but exposes the machine classification more directly, risking defensive satire.

## Concept 06A — Hot Girl Future Tense — primary

At a copy counter, Sunna slides a black pen across one pink page and sings the title as a rising question. The chosen adult crosses the curl, signs beside her, and writes Thursday at ten. Claps disappear at pen touch; a clipped guitar holds pulse; the melody drops into declaration only after the second name exists. Glam-funk question-to-declaration form.

## Concept 06B — Pink Means Thursday — reserve

The pink page and future day become a cleaner title. The punctuation engine survives, but the concept is less shamelessly funny and less specifically Sunna than the primary.

## Prune and reserve logic

The six primaries win on the combined hook/action/form test. Reserves retain one useful organ each: two-forks answer, counter-time hook, broad zipper title, direct salt answer, blunt reject invitation, and pink-day compression. None may replace a primary without inheriting that slot's action, form, arm, axis, genre bed, and single seam.
