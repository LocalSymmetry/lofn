# Pair 01 — Step 06 Music Facets

These eight scoring facets preserve the original five-facet substance while making each success criterion and characteristic failure explicit. Weight totals equal 100%.

### Facet 1 — Escalator-to-Diner Action (14%)

Why it matters: The song must begin inside the immutable action: Sunna steps from the department-store escalator into the chrome diner, places her red patent purse on a stool, and points to the open place beside her. This physical sequence makes the invitation legible before any explanation can flatten it.

Failure mode: A generic diner date, Friday-night montage, fashion inventory, or narrated backstory in which the escalator, purse, pointing gesture, and open stool do not causally connect.

### Facet 2 — Exact Future Coordinate and Confirmation (14%)

Why it matters: Friday at eight seventeen turns appetite into an executable future, while confirmation must remain withheld until the chosen adult answers “make it two forks.” Anticipatory vanity should grow into a mutually chosen plan without correcting or humbling Sunna's pleasure.

Failure mode: A vague someday invitation, premature certainty, an unearned reassurance chorus, or any version where Sunna confirms the reservation on the adult's behalf.

### Facet 3 — Four-Note Hum-Back Return (14%)

Why it matters: The clean-guitar whistle contour—up-up-down-up within a fifth—must be instantly singable before the first vocal and return alone after the final word. It gives the piece a recognizable nonverbal arrival and receipt.

Failure mode: A decorative lick, overlong solo, changing contour, buried opening, or ending that abandons the four-note return for a fade or unrelated flourish.

### Facet 4 — Title Return and One-Seam Integrity (10%)

Why it matters: “Friday Looks Good on Me” must return exactly, and the one two-tone upstroke substitution must occur only when Sunna crosses the threshold and points beside her. The seam carries the decision rather than introducing a second stylistic world.

Failure mode: Paraphrased title returns, multiple rhythmic seams, a full ska or reggae takeover, or a technique display detached from the threshold action.

### Facet 5 — Sunna's Audible Vocal Identity (12%)

Why it matters: The lead must be unmistakably Sunna herself: a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, bright chest-led mezzo, crisp smiling consonants, command catches, and confident forward placement.

Failure mode: A generic pop singer, breathy sad-girl delivery, coy laughter, apologetic upspeak, childlike affect, male lead, or a narrator singing about Sunna from outside her wants.

### Facet 6 — Direct Want and Adult Reciprocity (8%)

Why it matters: Sunna's direct want and right to stop must remain clear, while the chosen adult's two-forks answer supplies audible reciprocity. The hook should depend on two adults choosing the same table, not on possession or compliance.

Failure mode: Look-don't-touch posturing, coercive ownership, a passive or voiceless partner, abstract consent language, or an adult reply that does not change the musical and narrative state.

### Facet 7 — Lacquered Power-Pop Strut (16%)

Why it matters: At 116 BPM, melodic pick bass, candy-hard slightly-late drums, dry handclaps, tambourine, clipped electric guitar, and compact thirds must create high-fidelity shine that pulls the listener toward the empty stool. The mix should leave a confident rhythmic hole and keep acoustic/electric roles explicit.

Failure mode: Club darkness, four-on-the-floor drift, distorted swagger, fog pads, acoustic-guitar wash, decorative heritage color, crowded reverb, or production gloss that erases the invitation's physical timing.

### Facet 8 — Retellable Objects, One Mythic Flash, No Contraband (12%)

Why it matters: Purse, stool, fork, pie case, and pointing finger must produce one first-listen flirt scene. The pie case may widen once into Friday already portioned, then return to handled objects and action; the deeper claim lives only in Sunna reserving a future. The full immutable ICB, all 18 supplied panel voices, the plural Special Flairs palette, and the Hyper-Skeptic's veto against thesis vocabulary remain active.

Failure mode: Ontology, identity thesis, body-noise inventory, moral explanation, seriousness reveal, multiple symbolic worlds, or a piece whose materials become the subject instead of the reservation action.
