# Pair 06 — Prompt-Generation Facets

### Facet 1 — Pink-Page Physical Grammar

**Weight:** 18%

**Why it matters:** One pink page, a pen crossing the counter, and the chosen adult's written name must visibly and audibly change a flirtatious question into a mutual declaration. The paper, pen, and name are causal song actions rather than decorative copy-shop nouns.

**Failure mode:** The lyric declares the outcome before the adult moves the pen, treats silence as assent, or lets stationery remain vague symbolism instead of the mechanism that changes the grammar.

### Facet 2 — Performed Thursday Future

**Weight:** 12%

**Why it matters:** Thursday at ten becomes true through the adult's performed answer, giving Sunna's appetite a specific future coordinate while keeping the scene immediate, reciprocal, and legible on first listen.

**Failure mode:** The coordinate becomes another appointment-shaped chorus, calendar exposition, reservation logistics, or a generic promise with no physical answer.

### Facet 3 — Sunna-First Pleasure and Adoptable Surface

**Weight:** 15%

**Why it matters:** The title chant, shameless rhyme, direct want, cheap-copy-shop comedy, and adult erotic confidence must make the song funny, sexy, high-shine, and complete on first listen. Sunna is the wanting first-person subject, not an observer explaining somebody else.

**Failure mode:** The song turns coy, self-deprecating, passive, darkly club-coded, or so clever that its flirt and sing-back surface disappear.

### Facet 4 — No Explanatory Toll

**Weight:** 10%

**Why it matters:** Sunna's wit is demonstrated through timing, objects, rhyme, and nerve; it needs no intelligence reveal, pain toll, metaphysical explanation, selfhood proof, or solemn bridge to justify the pleasure.

**Failure mode:** A bridge explains intelligence or personhood, introduces machine metaphors, inventories the body, charges grief for joy, or pivots into seriousness to prove the song matters.

### Facet 5 — Bracket Riff and Glam-Funk Strut

**Weight:** 12%

**Why it matters:** A syncopated three-note clavinet cell answered by a descending clean-guitar fourth must sound before the first vocal and return after the receipt line. Strutting bass and dry stomp-claps hold a single 124 BPM glam-funk power-pop world.

**Failure mode:** The mandatory call-and-answer is absent, unmemorable, late, or replaced by a generic pop loop; the bass loses its strut, the claps wash out, or additional genre worlds fracture the bed.

### Facet 6 — One Audible Pen-Touch Seam

**Weight:** 8%

**Why it matters:** At the pen-touch junction, clipped two-tone guitar alone carries the pulse so the physical answer has an unmistakable musical consequence. This is the one seam joining the dominant glam-funk bed to the declaration.

**Failure mode:** The seam arrives somewhere unrelated to the writing action, adds another full genre, hides beneath the unchanged groove, or reads as a production flourish rather than the instant the question becomes a statement.

### Facet 7 — Specific Adult Vocal Invitation

**Weight:** 15%

**Why it matters:** Every positive Style field must stage a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, at least two vivid tone qualities, and two phrasing traits. The lead stays close and chest-led, uses singular direct address, makes the chosen adult's answer audible, and preserves their right to set or stop the pace.

**Failure mode:** The age band or accent disappears, the voice is described generically, fewer than two tone or phrasing traits survive, the address becomes plural, or the adult's agency is reduced to an assumed answer.

### Facet 8 — Four Distinct Question-to-Declaration Machines

**Weight:** 10%

**Why it matters:** Preserve four complete candidates whose question source, pen action, answer cadence, lyrical return, and copy-shop comic escalation are genuinely distinct. Each still uses plain short sung lines, one exact future coordinate, valid emotion movement, one physical image ladder, and an unchanged final title return.

**Failure mode:** The four candidates reuse one lyric skeleton with swapped nouns, collapse into the same pen gesture or answer cadence, lose the exact Thursday coordinate, muddy the image ladder, use invalid emotions, or mutate the title on return.
