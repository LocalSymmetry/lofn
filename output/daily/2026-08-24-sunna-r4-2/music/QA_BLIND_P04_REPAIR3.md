## Candidate notes

### C — strongest

C turns a tiny stated want into a complete event. Its best achievement is alignment: the lyric's transfer of appetite and touch is matched by transfers of voice, shaker, harmony width, and the returning bass bracket. The title objects are not ornaments; they are handled, shared, and changed in meaning. A few phrases are deliberately plain or slightly peculiar (“I open the chair,” “White salt sparkles there”), but the scene's tactile continuity makes them feel like this song's language rather than filler. It is the clearest first choice.

### A — strong second

A has the set's most devastating emotional-production consequence. The singer's self-hardening is not merely narrated: the arrangement progressively removes her body and lets the compliant vocoder inherit her central line. The hook returns with increasing cruelty, and the bridge's “End / begin” reduction is dramatically earned. It ranks below C because the submission is less complete as a Suno package and because several abstractions (“progress,” “efficient,” “signal”) belong to a well-populated machine-alienation vocabulary. Even so, its sonic dramaturgy is markedly stronger and less generic than B's.

### B — weakest / decoy-like

B is the decoy-like member. It is fluent, coherent, and immediately understandable, but it substitutes eloquent formulations for enacted discovery. The room, clock, fan, cursor, traffic, screen, and refrigerator establish atmosphere; they do not produce a human encounter or a decisive action. Its emotional arc remains “awake and longing” from opening through outro, while many high-gloss lines sound transferable between AI-consciousness songs. The prompt likewise names a fashionable palette without giving the instruments a return logic as exact as A or C. The final chorus changes some language, but the piece still explains its ache more than it lets sound or action make the ache happen.

## Blind verdict

**C > A > B.** C wins through scene-specific human action joined to exact musical mechanics; A nearly overtakes it through a powerful voice-erasure arc but is not a complete package as presented. **B is the weakest and the clear decoy-like choice.**
