# Blind Song Package

## Title
Shake It Into Mine

## Music Prompt
Shameless high-gloss bass-led rocksteady at 102 BPM, shaped as a salt-shaker wager beside one lime and one red chair. A zigzag five-note walking electric-bass pickup stays within a fourth, ends on a held second, and returns unchanged after the last line; two bone-dry center-left guitar strokes answer it. Forward mono bass sits under late rim-clicks, shallow organ bubbles, and one low brass reply. Sunna is a late-20s-to-early-30s adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, glossy throaty full-chest mezzo and honeyed bite; long-I glides and breath-held caesuras mark her phrasing. A precise adult contralto owns “Salt too?” across a rim gap. The first title hook lands with salt in Sunna's palm; “Only if you do mine” crosses the shaker between hands, and the second shake releases the sole hand-played dembow. Green marina water and one white wake enlarge the color image once before both salted lime halves bring it home. Lacquered transients frame dry shaker, appetite, and bass.
