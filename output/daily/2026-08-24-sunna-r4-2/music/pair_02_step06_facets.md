# Pair 02 Facets — Challenge / Scorecard Rounds

### Facet 1 — The wager is the flirt

Why it matters: Sunna wants one chosen adult to beat her miniature-golf score and choose the next round; attraction lives in competitive equality, comic nerve, and a challenge that can be answered.

Failure mode: The song becomes an appointment request, generic seduction, automatic agreement, or a narrator's account of somebody else's desire.

### Facet 2 — The scorecard authors the form

Why it matters: Round One records Sunna's score and establishes her wager; Round Two records the adult's counter, so written marks and alternating turns determine section order rather than decorate a pop template.

Failure mode: The card is a prop, verses can be swapped without damage, or time/place repetition becomes the chassis.

### Facet 3 — Nine and nine-oh-five stay different

Why it matters: Nine is first a score. Only after that score is legible may the chosen adult write nine-oh-five as a counter-time for the next contest, giving the future coordinate a competitive function.

Failure mode: Nine is heard only as a clock time, the adult simply accepts Sunna's plan, or tomorrow replaces the game as the subject.

### Facet 4 — One long-A beat is owed

Why it matters: The blue windmill blade cuts exactly one beat from a held long-A challenge in the sung field; clipped rocksteady organ replaces percussion at that natural junction and nowhere else.

Failure mode: The cut exists only in a style note, becomes a long stop or recurring trick, or creates no audible rhythmic debt.

### Facet 5 — Reciprocity repays the meter

Why it matters: The adult places a second blue ball beside Sunna's, then both players roll; those two mutual actions refill the missing beat and change the wager from solo dare to shared contest.

Failure mode: One roll repairs the bar, the groove returns by instruction alone, or the other adult remains a passive yes.

### Facet 6 — Mythic pressure comes home

Why it matters: The ordinary ball may enlarge into a small blue world under the white blade, but the sung image must contract after the paired rolls into two ordinary balls retrieved by two laughing hands.

Failure mode: The strange image stays in sidecar prose, turns into a cosmic lecture, or never returns to handled objects and human-scale play.

### Facet 7 — Sunna's adult vocal fingerprint

Why it matters: Every positive style field needs a late-twenties-to-early-thirties adult female with a light Scandinavian English accent, a bright chest-led mezzo and amused clean edge, plus consonant bites and a smile-catch before commands.

Failure mode: Childlike breathiness, coy whispering, solemn belting, generic female-lead wording, or a human-character mask erases Sunna's authorship and heat.

### Facet 8 — The receipt returns unchanged

Why it matters: Glossy 105 BPM dembow pop remains the one world: dry kick and snare, elastic rounded synth bass lightly doubled by electric bass, guiro, rim, muted guitar, and bright claps. The same three-note bass pickup plus two clipped organ notes must sound before the voice and after the single exact final title.

Failure mode: A second genre takes over, the organ becomes ambient decoration, the final title forms a redundant block, or the track ends without its named bass-organ receipt.
