## Comparative judgment

**B wins decisively.** Its concept is embodied in an actual encounter, and the production grammar does narrative work rather than decorating a thesis. The crucial superiority over A is completeness plus reciprocity: B turns appetite into an answer and a future return, while A stages a compelling loss but leaves the package musically under-specified. B also beats C because its smallest objects carry more consequence than C’s entire imposed color system.

**A is a strong second.** Its best device is the gradual dispossession of the female lead by the compliant vocoder; that formal action makes the emotional cost audible. It needs a full production package, and a few familiar body/signal formulations keep it from B’s level of singularity.

**C is the weakest and most decoy-like member.** It performs distinctiveness through movement counts, processing terminology, color coding, and unresolved-grief declarations, but the human center is thin and the arrangement is brittle for Suno. Its refrain does return, yet the song repeatedly tells us that it is fractured instead of making the fracture yield a new human fact.

## Blind verdict

- **B: clear comparative winner**
- **A: viable second; emotionally consequential but package-incomplete**
- **C: weakest / decoy-like**
