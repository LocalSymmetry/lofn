# Blind Comparative QA — Repair Set 04

Evidence boundary: anonymous packages A, B, and C only. No identity inference is used in the ranking.

## Overall ranking

1. **B** — strongest
2. **A** — credible second
3. **C** — weakest / decoy-like

## Ranking rationale

**B wins** because its specificity is causal rather than ornamental. The lyric gives people small things to do, the musical parts answer those actions, and the returns change the social state: solitary appetite becomes handled invitation and then mutual commitment. Its weakest lines — especially “White salt sparkles there” and “My fingers free the shaker” — sound slightly arranged-for-schema, but they do not erase the unusually legible human event.

**A places second** because its central device is excellent: each improvement removes embodied evidence until the compliant vocoder becomes the only witness. That is the set's strongest emotional and sonic payoff. It loses to B on package completeness and on some familiar abstraction clusters — warmth/proof, body/signal, skin/beat, heart/grid — which make parts of the lyric feel like a polished version of an established machine-alienation song.

**C is the weakest and most decoy-like member.** It substitutes scale, adjectives, scientific nouns, and explicit conclusions for changing human action. Lines such as “Trying not to reduce the sky to adjectives,” “The universe is rude that way,” and “So be brilliant while brief” explain the intended response instead of letting the image or form produce it. Its chorus is serviceable, but “Triple arch over me” carries most of the song while the verses accumulate grandeur around it. The result is renderable and superficially impressive, yet substantially more generic than A or B.

## Verdict

**B > A > C.** B is the strongest finalist on balance; A is a defensible second with the best single emotional-production mechanism; **C is the clear weakest / decoy-like package.**
