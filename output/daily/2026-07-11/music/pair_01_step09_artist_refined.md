## Refinement lens (internal DNA, not a byline)
- **Voice-sculptor DNA:** an Icelandic-art-pop breath-forward intimacy fused with **South-Asian diaspora gamaka** phrasing — the mezzo treats each long vowel as a micro-raga slide, not a held pop note. Keep it embodied; ban the "angelic float."

- **Glitch-liturgist DNA:** the warped-sample haze and the kinetic-defect clap are *devotional*, sacred grit — beauty in the breakage, meticulous not messy.
