## Substantive distinctiveness facets (≥8, this pair's own bones — for step06 distinctiveness gate)
1. **Reveal-engine:** a NUMBER becomes a BODY-FEELING (367 km → chest pressure / trembling water). Owned by P03; distinct from P01 obscuration-parts, P02 unreleasable-item, P04 held-breath-break, P05 already-past-line, P06 maker-untouchable.
2. **Aperture mechanism:** scene RECONTEXTUALIZATION — same kitchen re-lit, objects returned changed. Not widening (P01), not groove-drop (P02), not synthesis (P04), not false-clearing (P05), not code-scratch (P06).
3. **Signature device:** Pressure Swell — chest-pressing sub-bass, no visible hit. Distinct from Aperture-widening, Constant-Hand, Guillotine→Verdict, genre-jump, Code-Scratch.
4. **Verse architecture:** plain declarative quatrains (cognitive ease) + ONE long recontextualizing line at the fact. Contrast P01 long-breath couplets, P02 anaphora list-stanzas.
5. **Register:** B minor → D, mezzo-with-chest, 88→112 BPM. Unique in the run.
6. **Rupture logic:** felt pressure, no downbeat — the opposite of P04's guillotine micro-silence and P05's genre-jump pivots.
7. **Fear braid:** the ground itself is not solid; grief with no visible source — dread located in the earth's depth, not the sublime far (P01) or contagion (P05).
8. **Lineage:** Baile funk (funk carioca, Brazilian) surfacing as the recontextualization engine — named and pointed upstream. P04 also touches Baile-Phonk but as detonation; here it is a warm bounce SURFACING under grief.
9. **Title-as-body-feeling:** "Three Hundred Miles Down" is the *felt* depth (the km fact rounds into the gut) — the title IS the reveal-engine in miniature.
