Five laser-targeted scoring facets for the CATALOG-form, Constant-Hand pair. These are the rubric every V1–V4 prompt must satisfy.

## Facet notes (why these five)
- Facet 1 guards the FORM (catalog / anaphora / the one broken line) — the Sapphic Cataloguer (after Carson) and Legibility-Editor (after Rubin) both demand concrete, countable objects.
- Facet 2 is the non-negotiable Aperture + Constant Hand mechanic (Flairs #1, #5, #9); the Medium Skeptic (after Albini) checks it is a genuine bed-drop, not a reverb swell or a quiet passage.
- Facet 3 keeps the emotion taxonomy-exact and braids the clean fear (Griever-Ethicist after Weil).
- Facet 4 enforces Register Rotation (#10) so the same-personality run doesn't converge on the house voice.
- Facet 5 enforces anti-FOG (#11) + the one-fact rule (#13); the Concept Skeptic (after Lebowitz) kills recited numbers and unnamed vagueness.
