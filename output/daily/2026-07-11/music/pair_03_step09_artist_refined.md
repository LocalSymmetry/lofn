## What the refinement pass changes (flair, not scene)
- **Hard-'d' consonance chain** threaded through every fact-line: *down / deep / dread / didn't / does* — binds the number to the body-feeling sonically.
- **Anaphora on the re-lit objects** at each aperture (the glass, the tap, the hum, the light) so the recontextualization is *audible* as return-with-difference.
- **Polyptoton on move** (move / moved / moving / movement) — the reveal-engine (number→body-feeling) carried in a single mutating verb.
- **The plain lines get plainer** (cognitive ease per the verse architecture); ornament is quarantined to the ONE long fact-line, which is allowed to run long and breathless (enjambment across the number).
- **Pressure Swell kept DRY** in every music prompt — "presses the chest with no drum," no riser, no reverb-frosting (Albini-skeptic adopted).
- **Aperture = parting not fade** restated in every prompt: stereo widens, Baile surfaces, key lifts — never "quiets," never "drops out to silence" (except a single charged half-beat before the harder return, used at most once).
- **Register locked:** mezzo-with-chest, Bm→D, 88→112 — no soprano, no A-major, no frost-cosmos house lexicon.
- **Lineage line** standardized: credit Baile funk (funk carioca) and point upstream to its São Paulo / Rio scene producers.

## Per-variation flair emphasis
