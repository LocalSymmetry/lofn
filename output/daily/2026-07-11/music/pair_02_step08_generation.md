# PAIR 02 — Step 08 Generation (4 Raw Drafts) · "What The Hand Won't Let Go"

Raw generation pass: four drafts from the four variation angles, each a CATALOG built on the anaphora "I set down…" with the Constant-Hand aperture. These are the working skeletons; step 09 refines the prompts to producer-grade density and step 10 finalizes. All four: intimate alto, F Dorian, 92 BPM; folk-pop → UK 2-step → gospel-lift; ≥3 genres, ≥3 music frames, ≥1 SFX, one-fact "eleven percent" at hinge, named subject once.

---

## Draft V1 — "This I Set Down" (easy releases)
- **Draft music prompt (raw):** Tender future-nostalgic folk-pop, F Dorian, 92 BPM. Opens on close-mic nylon guitar + cardboard-box scrape (hard L). Intimate female alto, breath-soft. Easy-release list-verses over brushed guitar + off-grid shaker; tin-whistle sheen (R). Aperture = low end drops out, bare held voice, returns as clipped 2-step; final third gospel-lift (organ, gang-chant, claps). Blacklist: EDM risers, trap hats, soprano sheen, key-lift.
- **Draft lyric map:** Intro (moon/box scene) → V1 (keys/coat/map/stone/grudge/summer) → Refrain "This I set down" (set it down/lay it low/let it go) → V2 (letters/number/winter/door/sorry/salt/mirror) → V2b (birthday/porch/plate/vow/ring) → **Aperture: "one voice held up with nothing around / Holding on"** → V3 (2-step: anger/maps/ghost/comb/clock) → Refrain → **Bridge/hinge: "Eleven percent... almost all dark, and still I hold on"** → **Climax broken line: "I can't set down your name"** → Resolution (gospel: carry the one, list goes on) → Outro.
- **Anchor lines:** "I carry your name like a coal through the night." / "The sky sets it down. Why can't I too?"
- **Named subject:** *your name.*

## Draft V2 — "The Bed Keeps Changing" (form foregrounded)
- **Draft music prompt (raw):** Shapeshifting folk-pop, F Dorian, 92 BPM. Opens on lone folk guitar + box-flap tap + ring-knock (L). Alto holds one long line that never moves. Engine = bed changes clothes: folk → (drop) → 2-step → (drop) → gospel. Stereo collapses to mono at each drop, widens on each new genre. Blacklist: same family + "each genre return must be audibly different, not a filter tweak."
- **Draft lyric map:** Intro ("the bed can change; I don't") → V1 (calendar/costume/stage/corner) → **Aperture 1: "I don't move"** → V2 (2-step: grudge/doorways/trains) → Refrain (bed keeps changing / voice stays still) → V3 → Refrain → V3b (county/house/apron/oath) → **Aperture 2: "Still I don't move"** → **Bridge/hinge: eleven percent** → V4 (gospel: failure/fear/church/shame) → **Climax: "I can't set down your voice on the machine"** → Resolution ("Play me in any key... this one I don't set down") → Outro.
- **Anchor lines:** "Same voice on the top; brand-new world beneath." / "Play me in any key, dress me in any sound."
- **Named subject:** *your voice on the machine.*

## Draft V3 — "The One That Won't Go" (climax foregrounded)
- **Draft music prompt (raw):** Aching folk-pop, F Dorian, 92 BPM. Opens on folk guitar + single ring-tap on concrete (L). Alto low and grief-warm, thumb-worn ache. Quick easy list; 2-step enters as the voice fixes on the unreleasable; aperture drops floor to nothing on the words she can't finish; gospel returns warmer but the ache stays unhealed. Blacklist: "do not resolve grief into a tidy uplift; keep the ache unhealed; no key-lift for catharsis."
- **Draft lyric map:** Intro (ring/frost/box near full) → V1 (fast easy list; "the easy's not there") → Refrain → V1b (letters/ledger/photograph/promise) → V2 (2-step: "my thumb finds the band and it will not come free") → **Aperture: "one voice and one ring / I can't"** → V3 (grief: "to open my hand is to let your face blur / If release is a death, I won't be its friend") → **Bridge/hinge: eleven percent; "I am not the moon"** → **Climax broken line, expanded: "Not the ring. Not the ring. Not the ring."** → Resolution (gospel: "the letting is holy, and so is the none") → V4 (set down the schedule/rule/lie; "the one that won't go") → Outro.
- **Anchor lines:** "They never held gold that was welded to you." / "If release is a death, then I won't be its friend."
- **Named subject:** *the ring I still turn.*

## Draft V4 — "Setting It Down Anyway" (resolution)
- **Draft music prompt (raw):** Warm folk-pop rising to gospel, F Dorian, 92 BPM. Opens on folk guitar + door-key set on wood (L). Alto warm, steadying, hopeful lean. Releases over brushed guitar/shaker; 2-step lifts the middle; aperture bares the voice; floor returns and GROWS into a full gospel congregation answering line-by-line. Warmest, most lifted. Blacklist: "keep the gospel earthy, not stadium-huge; the lift comes from arrangement, not a key change."
- **Draft lyric map:** Intro ("last of three dark nights... I have made my truce") → V1 (blame/ending/anger/story/mornings) → V1b (keys/coat/plate/county/church) → Refrain → V2 (2-step: guilt/shame/map) → Refrain → V2b (birthdays/seat/grief-as-rabbit) → **Aperture: "Setting it down"** → **Bridge/hinge: eleven percent; "the dark makes the new"** → **Climax broken line: "I can't set down the sound of your key in the door"** → Resolution (gospel: "Setting it down is not throwing away — it's choosing what stays. And you stay. And I stay.") → V3 (choir: "both of them, both of them, gold") → Outro.
- **Anchor lines:** "I don't have to lose you to lighten the load." / "The letting is love and the keeping is love."
- **Named subject:** *the sound of your key in the door.*

### Draft-stage notes (carried into step 09)
- Every "lull" is realized as a FORM change (bed drop → new genre), never a fade — verified against ICB §2 north star at draft stage.
- The Constant Hand must READ as one sustained line, not a repeated phrase — step 09 tightens the music prompts to say "one sung line stays unbroken while the bed changes genre beneath it."
- Kinetic defect (off-grid shaker + diegetic object sound) seeded per song: box-scrape (V1), box-flap/ring-knock (V2), ring-tap (V3), key-click (V4).
- Line counts at draft: all four pushed into the 70+ band via catalog list-density (the form's native strength).
