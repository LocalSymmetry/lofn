# PAIR 03 — Song Guides (step 07)
*"Three Hundred Miles Down" · 6 compact guides (direct, don't draft)*

**Where the body stands:** at a kitchen sink, late, an ordinary night, alone. Hands in warm water. A glass on the counter. The floor holds one hum. The senses register: the meniscus, the fridge's single note, the cold of the tap, a spoon's small weight, the window's quiet street.
**What could hurt it (run-wide risks):** (a) the trembling glass becoming a horror-movie scare-cue instead of a *measuring instrument*; (b) the pressure-swell rendering as a generic reverb-frosted EDM sub-drop; (c) the aperture reading as a fade/quiet-down (forbidden — must be a re-lit re-frame that keeps moving); (d) reciting research numbers (only ONE fact, responded-to); (e) house-soprano/A-major default (must be mezzo-chest, Bm→D); (f) NEWS identifiability (none — no place/person/date).

## Guide 1 — Somatic-Measurement Authenticity
Storytelling: *in medias res* domestic report + object-as-instrument (the glass is a seismograph the body can read). Mood arc: numb ordinariness → a pressure that has no source → revelation felt as weight. Instrumentation: warm Rhodes/felt-piano, close-mic breath, then a dry sub-bass swell that presses the chest with NO kick. Production: intimate mono verses, breaths audible; the number lands on a held sub, not a hit. Structure: verse quatrains hold the mundane; the fact is ONE long line. Hook technique: the title as felt-depth. Poetic devices: synaesthesia (depth felt as weight), enjambment across the fact line. Voice: first person, mezzo with chest. **Guard:** keep the swell dry; the water measures, it does not jump-scare.

## Guide 2 — Recontextualizing Aperture (form re-frames, never quiets)
Storytelling: the return-with-difference (same objects, changed) + volta. Mood: overwhelm parting into a clear, wider room — not calmer, *changed*. Instrumentation: at the aperture, stereo widens, warped-sample bed thins to reveal a surfacing Baile bounce (detuned cowbell, geothermal 808) — a different engine, still driving. Production: hard stereo-widening as the "clearing"; no volume drop. Structure: post-fact, re-light the glass/tap/fridge/spoon/window one by one, each now meaning something else. Hook: chorus re-enters harder after the widen. Devices: anaphora on the re-lit objects; antimetabole ("the room in the water / the water in the room"). **Guard:** the aperture is a parting, NOT a fade — if it goes quiet, it fails.

## Guide 3 — Pressure-Swell Craft (signature #6)
Storytelling: withheld climax (the rupture is internal). Mood: dread with no object; the chest knows before the mind. Instrumentation: mono 30–60 Hz sine-and-growl swell rising under a held vocal; NO drum at the moment of the fact; then the Baile engine surfaces after. Production: dry, chest-first, sidechained lightly to breath; no riser, no crash. Structure: the swell peaks exactly on "three hundred and sixty-seven kilometres down," then the response line rides its decay. Hook: the swell IS the hook's engine. Devices: caesura-as-wound before the number; consonance on hard 'd' (down/deep/dread). **Guard:** never let it become a cinematic drop; it is a body event, not a spectacle.

## Guide 4 — Register-Rotation & Emotional Arc
Storytelling: mind-changing-in-real-time (the turn past midpoint). Mood: Melancholy → Overwhelm → Revelation, braided with Existential Angst (the ground not solid). Harmony: B minor verses lifting to D major exactly at the reveal — a lift that is also a fear (the far is indifferent). Tempo: 88 creeping to 112, the body speeding up. Vocals: mezzo with chest, small breaks, no whistle-soprano. Structure: the lift arrives with the fact and holds through the prayer. Devices: polyptoton (move/moved/moving), end-vowels open for sustain. **Guard:** do not resolve the fear cheaply; the D is bittersweet, not triumphant.

## Guide 5 — Lineage-Legible Fusion & Cognitive-Ease Verse
Storytelling: plain-speech testimony (declarative quatrains) that earns one ornate line. Mood: ease → rupture → prayer. Fusion (≥3, named): warm art-pop foundation → surfacing Baile funk (funk carioca) bounce → cosmic-pop lift. Lineage & Credit: name Baile funk in the lyrics field and point upstream to São Paulo/Rio scene producers — fusion with credit, never capture. Instrumentation: art-pop keys + Baile cowbell/808 + wide cosmic pads. Structure: quatrains → long fact-line → Baile-surfaced chorus → cosmic outro. Devices: alliteration in the plain lines; internal rhyme kept sparse for ease. **Guard:** Baile surfaces as WARM bounce under grief, not a phonk detonation (that's P04) — keep the lanes distinct.

## Guide 6 — Wildcard: The Meniscus As Cosmos (cross-disciplinary)
Borrow from still-life painting (a single lit object holds the whole world) and seismology (a needle reading a distance it cannot see). The ring of light where the glass meets the counter becomes a small horizon; the trembling water a cosmos in miniature. Let one section zoom entirely into the meniscus (V3's territory) — the smallest surface carrying the largest depth. Production: micro-close-mic water sounds pitched into the pad; a single glass-tap as a bell. Devices: metonymy (the glass for the ground), zeugma (one verb binding water and grief). **Guard:** stay concrete — the meniscus is real glass on a real counter, not a symbol lecture; anti-FOG, name the deep quake plainly once.
