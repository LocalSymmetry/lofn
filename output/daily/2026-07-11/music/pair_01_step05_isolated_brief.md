## 0. The one-sentence spine
A body on a tar-and-gravel rooftop, at the dark of the moon, when the low haze tears open on ONE clear sight of a star-cloud impossibly far — and is lifted **and** terrified by the distance. Messier-24 made personal: not "space is big," but "*this* gap, over *my* roof, tonight, is a keyhole onto ten thousand light-years, and it does not know my name."

## 1. Where the body stands / what can hurt it here (S1 grounding — answered concretely)
- **Stands on:** a flat city rooftop — tar paper still warm from the day, loose gravel/grit underfoot, a low parapet wall, antennae and water-tanks in silhouette, a near-new-moon (waning crescent, ~11%, three nights to dark) giving almost no light. Charged air (Kp ~4.3) — a faint restlessness, hair lifting.
- **What can hurt it:** not a fall off the roof (too domestic) — the *vertigo of scale*. The far is indifferent; it could swallow the self. The sublime is terror-adjacent. The clean fear left UNRESOLVED: the distance is real and it isn't mine; the gap owes me no mercy; it will close.
- Resonance, not reportage: the day's collapsing news (a truce breaking, a border crossed) is only the *hum of the city beneath* — never named. The rooftop is the whole world.

## 2. Section architecture (mine alone — THE ARRIVAL)
Environmental open (NO beat 6–10s, room tone + tanpura drone) → **sensory-inventory verses** (long-breath couplets, present tense) → **prayer-TO-the-clearing chorus** (hook addresses the window itself) → **APERTURE** (texture WIDENS, 4/4 → 6/8 float, reverb/register open to wide clear space) → **the one fact at the hinge** ("ten thousand light-years," responded-to) → fear-braided verse → return → **the dust RE-CLOSES** (a form-parting shut, never a fade to silence — room tone + pulse persists under it).

Order invariant across all 4 variations; the *emphasis* rotates (V1 arrival / V2 measurement / V3 vertigo / V4 loss).

## 3. Aperture mechanism (MINE — arrangement-widening, Flair #1)
The lull IS the aperture and it MOVES. Dense luminous HyperRaaga stack (tanpura + sargam gamaka + glass-harmonica sheen + warped-sample haze) literally **WIDENS**: stereo image opens hard L↔R, reverb tail unfolds from a tight close-mic room to a cathedral-wide clear space, vocal register lifts, and the **meter releases 4/4 into a 6/8 float** — a *lift*, not a drop. Then the dust re-closes: image narrows, reverb collapses back to the room, meter re-locks to 4/4. NOT the shared "window/dust" LINE — realized purely as arrangement geometry (L12 guard).

## 4. Reveal-engine (MINE): obscuration parts → distance revealed → smallness felt.
Three-beat internal logic every song rides: (a) the grey haze *unstitches* (a seam, a slack), (b) the deep field is *seen* (a crowd of suns, a country I will never consume), (c) the self is *dwarfed* (a mote on a rooftop, barely alive). Smallness is the payload, not the setup.

## 5. Hook grammar — the PRAYER-TO (Flair #8)
The chorus is a prayer addressed **TO the clearing / the window itself** — second person, imperative, tender: "Hold the gap open… window in the dust, stay torn, stay broken." It is adoptable (singable in one read), names the subject plainly ONCE per song ("window in the dust" / "the clear far sight" — anti-FOG, Flair #11), and mutates its verb across the four variations while keeping the "Hold the gap open" spine recognizable:
- **V1 the first sight:** *Hold the gap open* (stay, let me look).
- **V2 the fact lands:** *Hold the gap open — count with me* (measurement tilt).
- **V3 the fear of the far:** *Hold me to the roof* (don't let me fall up into you).
- **V4 the clearing closes:** *Close now, window* (I have seen; the prayer inverts to release).

## 6. Rhyme / line logic (MINE — long-breath couplets)
- Verses: **7–9 lines**, couplet-led (AABB / AABBCC) but broken by **internal rhyme** and **enjambment** so they never read as stacked quatrains (anti-monotony mandate). Present-tense, first-person sensory. One long recontextualizing line allowed near the fact.
- Poetic devices carried through all four: **internal rhyme** (iron/cooling; keyhole/room), **anaphora** ("Hold the gap open…"), **polyptoton** (far / farther / farthest; small / smaller), **synaesthesia** (grey on grey, the taste of iron air), **epizeuxis** ("so far, so far, so far" at the aperture), **caesura** (em-dash breath-stops in the aperture).
- Chorus: 6 lines, vowel-final for sustain, the hook line repeated as bookend.

## 7. Register (rotated OFF house fingerprint — Flair #10)
**Warm mezzo** (NOT crystalline soprano), breath-close, South-Asian-diaspora inflection, unhurried; gamaka micro-slides on long vowels; a small catch at the top of each phrase. **D Mixolydian** (the natural-7 / C-natural gives luminous-but-grounded warmth, not major-key sweetness). **104 BPM.** Palette = warm haze + deep field, NOT frost-and-cosmos crystalline. This is the anti-clone guard: house winner = soprano/A-major/110/frost; mine = mezzo/D-Mixo/104/warm-dust.

## 8. Production arc (S2 texture weaponized as FORM, not noise)
Bandcamp F10 unsettle ("raw, mechanical, uneasy… never quite lets the listener settle") enters as a **restless off-grid groove** (kinetic defect, Flair #7): a hand-clap/finger-cymbal that lands a hair late, an asymmetric 4/4 that leans. The unsettle is *productive* — it makes the aperture's 6/8 release feel like true relief. Sub-bass **pressure swell** under the fact (chest, no hit). **Stereo widening at the aperture** is the signature move. No silent release section — the dust-close keeps the room-tone pulse alive.
