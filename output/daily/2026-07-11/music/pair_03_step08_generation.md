## V1 draft — "Three Hundred Miles Down" (before-the-fact, mundane dominant)
**Music (raw):** Warm nocturnal art-pop at 88 BPM in B minor, opening on a felt-piano and one dripping tap; a mezzo-with-chest female voice, close and intimate; Rhodes, warped-sample bed; at the fact a dry sub-bass Pressure Swell presses the chest with no drum; then a surfacing Baile-funk bounce (detuned cowbell, geothermal 808) re-lights the scene as the key lifts to D and the tempo climbs to 112; stereo widens at the aperture; cosmic-pop pads on the outro.
**Hook seed:** "Three hundred miles down / something moved and moved me too / no crack in the wall, no sound in the room / just the water telling the truth."
**Draft V1 (mundane held long):**
- Verse: The tap runs warm, the glass runs clear. / I rinse the same glass every night. / The fridge holds one long note in here. / The street outside is small and bright.
- Turn/fact line: And then they said it plain — *three hundred and sixty-seven kilometres down* / too deep to crack the street, deep enough to move the water in my glass.
- Recontextualized: Now the tap, the glass, the hum, the light — / the same, and none of them the same.

## V2 draft — "Three Sixty-Seven Down" (the fact / measurement foregrounded)
**Music (raw):** Art-pop that treats a number like a body; 92→112 BPM, Bm→D; the measurement-language (down, deep, kilometres) sung as feeling; Pressure Swell peaks on the number; Baile cowbell engine surfaces as the count becomes a pulse; wide cosmic close.
**Hook seed:** "Three sixty-seven down / a number I can feel / it isn't on the wall / it's the weight behind my ribs, and it's real."
**Draft V2 (the number is the spine):**
- Verse: They measure it in kilometres. / I measure it in breath. / A depth I'll never stand upon / moved the floor beneath my chest.
- Fact line: *Three hundred and sixty-seven kilometres down* — too far to see, too deep to name, / and still it found the water, still it found the small of my back.

---
## V3 draft — "The Water In My Glass" (somatic close-up)
**Music (raw):** Everything zoomed to the meniscus; micro-close-mic water pitched into the pad, a single glass-tap as a bell; 88→108 BPM, Bm→D; Pressure Swell under a held vowel; Baile bounce surfaces small and warm; the ring of light on the counter as a horizon.
**Hook seed:** "The water in my glass / knew before I did / three hundred miles beneath the floor / and the little wave it hid."
**Draft V3 (zoom to the meniscus):**
- Verse: A ring of light where the glass meets wood. / A skin of water, still as glass. / Then a shiver crosses it — / a small tide, and it will not pass.
- Fact line: They'll say *three hundred and sixty-seven kilometres down* on the morning news — / but the water in my glass already told me, in a language made of moving.

---
## V4 draft — "Let It Hold" (measurement-as-prayer outro dominant)
**Music (raw):** The measurement turns to petition; adoptable prayer-hook; 90→112 BPM, Bm→D lifting into a gospel-tinged cosmic-pop; Pressure Swell as the knees-give moment; Baile bounce surfaces as the congregation-body; wide, warm, unresolved.
**Hook seed (prayer-TO the ground):** "Let it hold, let it hold / three hundred miles down, let it hold / I can't see you, I can't fight you / floor of the world, let it hold."
**Draft V4 (prayer):**
- Verse: I don't know how to pray to stone. / I've never learned the words for deep. / But my hands are wet, my chest is pressed, / and the glass is trembling in its sleep.
- Fact line: *Three hundred and sixty-seven kilometres down* is farther than any faith of mine — / so I say to the dark of the ground: whatever you are, whatever holds you, hold.
