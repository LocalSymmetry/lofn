## What changed from step 08 → step 09 (applies to all four)
1. **Opening 5 seconds made literal + kinetic.** Each prompt now names the exact first sound and a defect: box-scrape / box-flap + ring-knock / ring-tap / key-click, panned, against the dead-center guitar. Banned openers avoided (no "Begin in…/Use…/Build…/Chronology:").
2. **Spatial staging explicit.** Guitar dead-center; diegetic object hard-left; tin-whistle sheen far-right; vocal center-close; stereo collapses to mono-center at each aperture and widens as the new genre enters. (Answers the Medium panel: an aperture the ear can locate.)
3. **The Constant Hand stated as a mechanic, not a metaphor.** "One sung line stays unbroken while the bed changes genre beneath it" — so Suno reads the sustain, not a repeat.
4. **Aperture disambiguated from a fade.** Every prompt and every exclude says the low end LIFTS (a parting) and the voice is left BARE — "do not fade the volume down to make the lull." This defends ICB §2 + the S3 aperture rule and the L12 guard (no shared window/dust phrasing; the mechanic is purely arrangement).
5. **Register-rotation lock.** F Dorian / 92 / intimate alto / no crystalline-soprano — restated in every exclude ("no bright pop-soprano, no whistle-register belt; stay in F Dorian; no triumphant key-lift").
6. **Char budget.** All four music prompts measured to 911–955 (target 870–960); excludes 593–608 (band 400–900).

## Per-song refinement emphasis
- **V1 (easy releases):** softened the mix language to "warm and woody"; made the gospel-lift the arrangement climax so the "lull" is provably a form-bloom, not a quiet. Kept the coal-image resolution as the emotional payload.
- **V2 (form foregrounded):** foregrounded the two bed-drops and the three-genre sequence in the prompt itself ("the song's engine is the bed changing clothes under that line"); exclude adds "each genre return must be audibly a different genre, not a filter tweak" to force real schisms.
- **V3 (climax):** pushed the vocal descriptor to "low and grief-warm… a thumb-worn ache"; exclude forbids tidy uplift and catharsis key-lift so the fear-braid stays unresolved (Golden Move #4).
- **V4 (resolution):** prompt now says the floor "grows into a full gospel lift… a congregation answering the lead line by line"; exclude keeps it "earthy and human, not stadium-huge" so the warmth reads as intimate resolve, not worship-pop.
