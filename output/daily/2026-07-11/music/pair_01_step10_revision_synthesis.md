## DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (one pass)

**Prediction — what these prompts would actually PRODUCE:**
- **First 5s:** room tone / wind on gravel (V1), a phone clicking dark (V2), charged-air hiss (V3), a slow exhale on tar (V4), each over a lone low tanpura drone with **no beat** — a body on a roof, not a synth intro. ✓ matches the "environmental open (no beat 6–10s)" seed.
- **Vocal placement:** a warm mezzo, dead-center, breath-close, gamaka-sliding — NOT a soprano floating over pads. Doubled a third below in choruses; gang-chant only widens at the close. ✓ register rotated.
- **Groove:** an off-grid four that leans (finger-cymbal a hair late) — the productive unsettle (F10), restless but musical, never club-generic. ✓ kinetic defect present.
- **Where the aperture lands:** at ~60% of runtime the stereo tears open L↔R, reverb unfolds room→cathedral, meter drops to a floating 6/8, and (V2/V3/V4) the sub-bass presses the chest under the sung fact; then the image narrows back with the pulse still moving. ✓ aperture = arrangement-widening, a parting not a fade.

**Diff against seed + Golden Move:** stands somewhere real (the rooftop) ✓; ≤1 fact at the hinge, responded-to ✓; a real turn past midpoint (awe → the vertigo of an indifferent far) ✓; fear braided, unresolved ✓; register rotated ✓; subject named plainly once ✓.

**Adversarial — "the one way this renders generic":** Suno often hears "warm female vocal + tanpura + wide reverb + slow tempo" and produces **placid new-age worship wallpaper** — pretty, static, no motion, the aperture indistinguishable from the verses because the whole track is already washed in reverb.

**Self-repair (applied once):** to force the aperture to READ as a change rather than blend in, the prompts (a) keep the verses **tight and close-mic'd / room-sized** so the cathedral-open is a genuine contrast, (b) bind the widening to an audible **meter release (four → floating six)** and a **hard stereo tear**, not just reverb, and (c) keep the **off-grid kinetic-defect groove driving** through the verses so the song never sits still — the lull is the *6/8 float*, a different engine, not a quiet. The exclude prompts explicitly ban "a quiet dropout as the only lull," "fade-to-silence release," and "icy synth-pad lead / new-age wash." Drift repaired.
