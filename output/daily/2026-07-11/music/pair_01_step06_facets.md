# PAIR 01 — "The Window" · SCORING FACETS (step 06)
*Five weighted facets specific to this pair. Each judges whether a draft prompt earns the seed. JSON at foot.*

## Facet 1 — Aperture-as-arrangement, not aperture-as-line (weight 0.28) — HIGHEST
Does the widening happen in the *sound geometry* — stereo image tearing open L↔R, reverb unfolding from close room to cathedral-wide, register lifting, **meter releasing 4/4 → 6/8** — and then re-closing as a form-parting (not a fade)? A draft that "describes" a window in the lyric but keeps a static mix FAILS. The lull must be a different engine still moving (North Star). Sensory test: on a phone speaker you should HEAR the room get bigger, then smaller.

## Facet 2 — The far felt as smallness + braided fear (weight 0.24)
Does the reveal-engine land its payload — *smallness felt*, not just distance described — and does the clean fear stay braided and UNRESOLVED (the far is indifferent, could swallow the self)? Reverence without vertigo is a postcard. Test: is there a line past the midpoint where awe curdles toward dread without the song rescuing it cheaply?

## Facet 3 — Prayer-TO hook: adoptable, second-person, subject named once (weight 0.20)
Is the chorus a tender imperative addressed TO the clearing/window (not a thesis about space), singable after one read, mutating its verb per variation while keeping a recognizable spine — and is the subject named plainly exactly ONCE (anti-FOG)? Hum-back test must pass. Test: could a listener adopt "Hold the gap open" as their own prayer on the walk home?

## Facet 4 — Warm-mezzo / D-Mixolydian register, rotated off house frost (weight 0.16)
Does the voice read as warm mezzo (breath-close, gamaka slides, small phrase-catch) over D Mixolydian at 104 BPM with a warm-dust palette — audibly NOT the crystalline-soprano / A-major / 110 / frost-and-cosmos house fingerprint? Test: swap-check — if you could describe it as "female vocal over frosty pads," it drifted.

## Facet 5 — HyperRaaga lineage integrity + the one wounding fact (weight 0.12)
Are ≥3 genres and ≥3 frames drawn in with HyperRaaga *credited* upstream (not extracted), and is exactly ONE numeric fact ("ten thousand light-years") sung at the hinge and RESPONDED to (never recited beside other numbers)? Test: is the fact answered with a human image ("before the word for far existed"), and is the gamaka/sargam a real structural presence, not a garnish?
