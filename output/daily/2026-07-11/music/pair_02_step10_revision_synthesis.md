## DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (one pass)

**Predicted literal render — first 5 seconds (all V):** A single close-miked nylon-string guitar, dead-center, warm and woody, a slow F-Dorian figure at 92 BPM; over it a dry diegetic object sound panned left/hard-left (box-scrape V1 / box-flap+ring-knock V2 / ring-tap V3 / key-click V4); a breath, then the intimate alto enters center-close. No beat yet — an environmental, hand-and-object open. This matches the seed's "dense/tense verse" entry and stands somewhere real (the cold back step).

**Predicted literal render — the bed-drop to the Constant Hand:** Mid-song, the sub-bass, shaker and pulse cut out inside a bar (not a decrescendo), the stereo collapses to mono-center, and one sustained alto line hangs bare ("Holding on" / "I don't move" / "I can't" / "Setting it down"). Then the floor rebuilds in a *different* genre — clipped UK 2-step (shuffled hats, rounded sub-bass), and later a gospel lift (drawbar organ, gang-chant, widening claps) — while that one vocal line does not move. This is a parting, not a fade; the lull is a different engine still moving.

**Diff vs seed + Golden Move:**
- Seed (THE SWITCHBOARD / Break Rigid Thinking / APERTURE): ✓ audible genre jumps (folk→2-step→gospel), one BOLD move per song (the bed-drop-and-genre-swap), aperture that parts then re-closes in a new genre. ✓ dynamics-through-form, never through volume.
- Golden Move: ✓ stands somewhere real (back step, 4 a.m., box, crescent low in the east). ✓ one wounding fact ("eleven percent") at the hinge, responded-to, never recited. ✓ the turn (easy releases → the one that won't go). ✓ fear braided and, in V3, left unresolved. ✓ register rotated (F Dorian / 92 / alto — not the house soprano/A-major/frost fingerprint). ✓ named subject once (anti-FOG).
- L12/L14: ✓ aperture realized as bed-drop-to-Constant-Hand — no shared window/dust line; reveal-engine (inventory-reaches-unreleasable-item) distinct from all five other pairs.

**The one way this renders generic:** Suno hears "acoustic folk → soft 2-step → gospel outro, gentle female vocal, sad-but-hopeful breakup song" and delivers a tasteful coffee-house ballad — the bed-drop softened into a normal quiet-verse-then-lift, the Constant Hand not actually sustained, the emotion arriving pre-resolved and warm from bar one (a corpse per Golden Move #3).

**Self-repair (applied once):**
1. Every music prompt AND every exclude now explicitly forbid the fade-substitute: "expose the voice bare; do not fade the volume down to make the lull," and "each genre return must be audibly a different genre, not a filter tweak." This forces the parting to read as a form-event.
2. The Constant Hand is specified as "one sung line stays unbroken while the bed changes genre beneath it" (a sustain, not a repeat) so Suno holds the note across the schism.
3. The turn is protected by keeping the opening stance plain and un-resolved: the easy-release lists use deliberately flat rhyme and the terminal emotion (Liberation/Hope, or in V3 unhealed Grief) only arrives after the hinge — no song asserts its ending from line one. V3's exclude explicitly bans a tidy uplift so its ache stays open.
4. Register-rotation restated in every exclude ("no bright pop-soprano; stay in F Dorian; no triumphant key-lift") to block the house-clone soft-focus.
