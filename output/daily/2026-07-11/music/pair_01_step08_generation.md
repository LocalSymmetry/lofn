## V1 — "the first sight" (pure arrival) → title: **Hold The Gap Open**
- **Angle:** the awe of the seam itself; the reveal as gift; fear present but light.
- **Hook (prayer-TO):** *Hold the gap open, hold the gap open — / window in the dust, stay torn, stay broken.*
- **Structure:** No-beat env intro → V1 sensory inventory → pre-chorus (seam) → chorus → V2 (crowd of suns) → **APERTURE** (4/4→6/8 widen) → fact hinge → fear-verse (turn) → chorus → bridge → dust-close outro → lineage credit.
- **Signature couplet (aperture):** *the beat lets go of its four hard corners, / we are in six now, we are in the drift.*
- **Fact:** ten thousand light-years → *this light left before the word for far existed.*
- **Turn:** *The far does not know me, does not bend to my name — / and still I am lifted, and still I am flame.*

## V2 — "the fact lands" (measurement-tilt) → title: **Ten Thousand Years Of Far**
- **Angle:** the number is the pivot; measurement reframes everything; the fact does the wounding.
- **Hook (prayer-TO, verb-mutated to counting):** *Hold the gap open, count it out with me — / window in the dust, how old is what I see?*
- **Structure:** env intro → V1 (I came up to escape the news, the phones) → pre-chorus (a clean far sight opens) → chorus → **APERTURE** early-ish → the fact detonates at the widening's peak → V2 recontextualized (everything I called old is young) → fear-verse → chorus → bridge (the answer) → dust-close.
- **Measurement-tilt couplet:** *older than the alphabet, older than grief, / older than the first hand that begged for relief.*
- **Fact + response (the hinge):** *Ten thousand light-years — I say it out loud — / this light left before the word for far was allowed.*
- **Turn:** the number stops being trivia and becomes vertigo: *and I am the youngest thing on this whole cold roof.*

## V3 — "the fear of the far" (vertigo foregrounded) → title: **Hold Me To The Roof**
- **Angle:** the sublime as terror; the far could swallow you; the prayer becomes a grip on the parapet. Darkest of the four.
- **Hook (prayer-TO, inverted to self-preservation):** *Hold me to the roof, hold me to the roof — / window in the dust, don't let me fall up into you.*
- **Structure:** env intro (charged air, hair lifting) → V1 (the gap opens too wide) → pre-chorus (I lean and the leaning doesn't stop) → chorus → **APERTURE** (widening reads as *falling upward*, disorienting) → fact hinge (the far is indifferent) → fear-verse (the swallow) → chorus → bridge (I grip the low wall) → dust-close = relief the gap shut.
- **Vertigo couplet (aperture as fall):** *the floor tips up and the sky becomes a throat, / six beats wide and nothing left to hold the note.*
- **Fact + response:** ten thousand light-years → *and it will not notice if I let go — / that is the mercy and the horror of the show.*
- **Turn (reverse of V1):** here the widening is a threat, and the dust-close is the rescue: *close, please close — I have seen enough to keep.*
- **Fear braid = foregrounded, still unresolved** (relief that it closed, not that the far is safe).

## V4 — "the clearing closes" (loss of the view) → title: **Close Now, Window**
- **Angle:** elegy — the gap is already narrowing; the whole song is watching it go; carrying the shape of it down the stairs.
- **Hook (prayer-TO, inverted to release/blessing):** *Close now, window, close now, gentle — / window in the dust, I will keep what you lent me.*
- **Structure:** env intro (the seam is already thinning) → V1 (I got here late, it's going) → pre-chorus → chorus (a blessing on the closing) → **APERTURE** (a last full widening — one more look, the biggest) → fact hinge (memory of the far) → fear-verse (what if I never see it again) → chorus → bridge (I carry it down) → dust-close = full shut, room-tone only, no fade to silence.
- **Elegy couplet:** *the seam is a wound that is learning to heal, / and I press my eye to the last of the real.*
- **Fact + response:** ten thousand light-years → *it waited that long to be seen for a breath, / then folded itself back behind the grey's death.*
- **Turn:** grief becomes gratitude without erasing the loss: *I don't get to keep it. I get to have had it. / That has to be enough. I make it enough.*

- **Fact identical & singular** (ten thousand light-years) but the *response* differs each time (word-for-far / youngest-thing / it-won't-notice / waited-that-long).
- **Aperture identical mechanism, different meaning:** V1 gift · V2 revelation-peak · V3 falling-upward threat · V4 last-look elegy.
- **Subject named once each:** "window in the dust" (all four, the anti-FOG plain-name), placed differently.
