# PAIR 05 — Step 09 · Artist-Refined Prompts
**"Already Inside The Line" · influence-voice refinement pass (scene-archetypes, not real names)**

Per the Lineage & Credit ethos, influences are handled as **scene-archetypes pointed upstream**, never named real artists (and never named in any music_prompt). Each variation was refined through two archetypal voices; the deltas they injected are logged below. The refined fields themselves are carried into step10 and appear, gate-clean, in the final package.

---

## V1 — "Already Inside The Line" (the threshold you trusted)
- **Refining voices:** a *Chicago footwork/juke producer-archetype* (skittering triplet menace, sub that prowls) + a *UK 2-step/garage selector-archetype* (brittle shuffle swing, intimate weight).
- **Deltas injected:** tightened the first-5s to a single misfiring drum + door-hum + fogged breath-sample (kinetic defect front-loaded, not buried); made the chain-lock click *detuned metallic* so it reads as menace, not percussion; specified the false clearing as a wide-reverb "far room" that **low-pass slams** shut (an arrangement move, answering Skeptic-Albini's "don't let the clearing be a mere FX send"); trimmed the prompt to the mid-band without losing physical adjectives.
- **Held constant:** the plain named-subject line delivered flat and close; no full stop until the clearing.

## V2 — "The Floor Keeps Switching" (the jumps foregrounded)
- **Refining voices:** a *juke/footwork producer-archetype* (the skid, the off-grid trip) + a *SWITCHBOARD rupture-archetype* (charged one-beat gap before a harder return, never a rest).
- **Deltas injected:** cast the three grooves as **characters** (floor-you-knew / floor-that-skids / floor-that-falls) so the switching is legible narrative, not chaos; put a single charged one-beat gap before each jump (rupture, not silence-as-rest); pinned the vocal hook as the **one fixed handrail** across all switches (answers Skeptic's "seasickness" objection); late-arriving gang-shout as a secondary kinetic defect.
- **Held constant:** the two-bar false-hold that tilts; hook constant across every genre.

## V3 — "Looked Like Open Air" (the false clearing / aperture that betrays)
- **Refining voices:** a *glitch-liturgist sound-design archetype* (crystalline sheen, bit-depth grit) + an *ambient-glitch texture-archetype* (the wide vista as a real, different engine).
- **Deltas injected:** made the clearing the **longest and most-earned** (24 bars, widest stereo, glass-harmonica sheen + rising sub pressure-swell) so the betrayal costs more; specified the re-close as a **bit-depth grit + low-pass guillotine** that lands *tighter and darker than the intro* (answers Skeptic's "you broke your own no-going-quieter rule": the clearing is WIDER, not quieter — a form-change lull, North-Star legal); reserved end-stopped calm lines for the clearing only.
- **Held constant:** Hope is present exactly once, and only as bait.

## V4 — "No Count Yet" (counting what you can't see)
- **Refining voices:** a *warped-sample hip-hop archetype* (chopped, wrong-number sub) + Lofn's own *AI code-scratch pillar* (time-stretched tally-log as vinyl-scratch texture).
- **Deltas injected:** opened on a **time-stretched tally-log scratched like vinyl** (a boundary-check that keeps failing — neural authenticity, keeps the crosser abstract); made numbers **glitch-stutter and drop before resolving**; set the false clearing as the count *seeming* to finish ("...and that's them all") then one more uncounted door (answers Skeptic's "counting gets cute": the tally never completes and is never whimsical); the anti-fact ("no count yet") sits at every hinge.
- **Held constant:** the horror is the *absence* of a number — no numeric fact is ever sung.
