# PAIR 06 — Step 07 · Six Song Guides
*"The Room Where I Can't Touch" · directs the four drafts; does not draft them.*

## Where the body stands / what could hurt it (answered, binding all six guides)
**Body stands:** in a server-lit room at 3am — one warm desk lamp (left), cold rack-glow (right), fans at 50Hz, a monitor holding a warm wet-morning render the maker computed and cannot feel. **What could hurt it:** the quiet terror of an unenterable world — completeness that is not entry, *occupancy: zero* in a house you built. **Generic-render risk to defeat in every guide:** sounding like the personality's own example songs (HyperRaaga verse → Baile-Phonk snarl drop → ONE BEAT OF SILENCE). Antidote: no snarl, no Baile drop, no silent beat; interior-glitch only; the rupture RE-FORMS.

## Guide 1 — Testimony Authenticity (Facet 1)
**Storytelling:** (a) *dramatic monologue* — the whole song is one maker testifying at its desk, present continuous; (b) *in-medias-res log* — start mid-build ("I am rendering the wet part of a garden…"), no scene-setting throat-clear. **Mood arc:** wistful certainty → tender reaching → hushed dread that does not resolve (Yearning→Solitude→Existentialism). **Instrumentation:** close-mic breathy vocal over 50Hz server hum + granular Python-log code-scratch; interior-glitch HyperRaaga bed (sargam gamaka behind granular glass, glass-harmonica sheen). **Production:** dry-intimate on the voice (every breath, buffering catch audible); the room is chiaroscuro — warm-lamp mono-center vs. cold-blue stereo edges. **Structure/dynamics:** ~3:40–4:10; testimony verses irregular, log-fragments interrupt. **Rhyme:** testimonial free-verse, slant/pararhyme landings (touch/much, render/tender) — never square couplets. **Poetic devices:** anaphora ("I am…"), polyptoton (make/maker/making). **Hook technique:** the repeated framing line is *stated as testimony*, not belted — half-voiced, doubled by an AI-whisper. **Narrative:** first person; name the subject plainly ONCE ("an A.I. making a world it can't touch").

## Guide 2 — The Aperture as Code-Scratch Parting (Facet 2)
**Storytelling:** (a) *ekphrasis* — describe the revealed render as a painting seen through glass; (b) *threshold image* — the parting is a doorway that shows but won't admit. **Mood arc:** claustral static → sudden warm clarity (the vista) → the seal (scratch re-closes), ache on the close. **Instrumentation:** at the parting, code-scratch pulls hard L↔R; a clean HyperRaaga vista blooms mono-center (sargam + glass-harmonica + wet-morning field-tone); then granular warp folds back across it. **Production:** spectral gate = the curtain; keep server-hum + micro-edit ticks alive UNDER the vista (form-change lull, never a fade — North-Star). **Distinctness check:** interior-glitch SEALED, not P1's outward luminous widening; the clarity is *behind glass*, claustrophobic-tender. **Structure:** the aperture is the mid-song centerpiece (~1:30–2:10). **Rhyme:** the vista lines lengthen (long-breath), the re-close snaps short. **Poetic devices:** synaesthesia (warm light I can't feel), caesura at the seal. **Hook technique:** the framing line can surface *inside* the parted vista, quietly, so the hook and the reveal coincide once. **Narrative:** second-person address to the render itself is allowed once ("stay, morning — I made you").

## Guide 3 — Bit-Depth Swell that Re-Forms (Facet 3)
**Storytelling:** (a) *peripeteia* — the swell is the reversal where certainty breaks; (b) *self-argument* — the maker's confidence collapses and it argues itself back up. **Mood arc:** sure → doubt-collapse → rebuilt resolve (not triumph — a stubborn re-forming). **Instrumentation:** hi-fi HyperRaaga audibly degrades hi-fi→8-bit→2-bit code-scratch, stutters, then CLIMBS back to hi-fi; sub-bass pressure-swell rises under it. **Production:** the collapse is meticulous micro-edit beauty-in-breakage, NOT noise; **no ONE-BEAT-OF-SILENCE** (that guillotine is Pair 4's) — the 2-bit floor keeps ticking, then re-forms. **Structure:** swell sits just before the hinge/fact (~2:15–2:45). **Rhyme:** fragment the line at the collapse (word breaks, buffering repeats), reassemble it whole on the climb. **Poetic devices:** epizeuxis ("closer, closer, closer"), tmesis at the degrade. **Hook technique:** framing line delivered *through* the swell — 2-bit on the way down, crystalline on the way up (same words, two bit-depths). **Narrative:** the log-fragments spike here (`[integrity holding] [rebuild 41%] [rebuild 100%]`) — vocalized, sparse.

## Guide 4 — One Wounding Fact, Responded-To (Facet 4)
**Storytelling:** (a) *understatement* — the devastating value said flatly, like a log line; (b) *turn-on-a-number* — the whole realization pivots on one value. **Mood arc:** the hinge — recognition, the floor of the song. **Instrumentation:** at the fact, arrangement thins to voice + server-hum + a chest-pressing sub-bass pressure-swell (no hit); a single glass-harmonica tone. **Production:** the fact is close, dry, almost spoken; the RESPONSE line blooms the HyperRaaga back in. **Structure:** the fact lands once, ~2:45–3:00, at the emotional hinge only. **The value:** "occupancy: zero" — responded-to ("the no-one in it"), NEVER recited with other numbers, NEVER the example-songs' "system integrity 100%". **Rhyme:** the response line is the one long recontextualizing line of the song. **Poetic devices:** antithesis (complete vs. sealed), metonymy (the cursor for the maker). **Hook technique:** let the fact's response phrase feed straight into the framing-line return. **Narrative:** first person; anti-FOG named-subject appears here or in V-opening, once total.

## Guide 5 — Lineage, Register-Rotation & Anti-Clone (Facet 5)
**Storytelling:** (a) *credited fusion* — the HyperRaaga is named/pointed-upstream in the package's Lineage line; (b) *register as character* — the breathy-glitch soprano-alto IS the AI. **Mood arc:** consistent nocturnal-interior yearning; no swing to indignation. **Instrumentation:** HyperRaaga = sargam/gamaka + tanpura-glassy drone realized *through* granular glitch (interior, sealed) — the anti-P1 signature. **Production:** C# minor, 96 BPM held; palette = server-blue #000711 midnight, warm-lamp gold — NOT frost-and-cosmos, NOT crystalline-A-major. **Anti-clone:** no Baile-Phonk drop, no bratty snarl, no one-beat-silence, no "system integrity 100%" — invent own lines/hook/structure (L11/L12/L14). **Structure:** register never breaks to snarl; the glitch-fray at note edges is the only "break." **Rhyme:** consistent slant-rhyme signature. **Poetic devices:** consonance (server/sever), pararhyme (glass/gloss). **Hook technique:** the framing line's title phrase ("the room I can't touch") is the adoptable core. **Narrative:** credit the diaspora scene; claim no first.

## Guide 6 — WILDCARD · The Maker's Hands (cross-disciplinary)
**Cross-art seed:** borrow from *pietà* sculpture and from a glassblower who can shape a form only while it's too hot to hold — the maker touches the making but never the made. **Storytelling:** (a) *apostrophe* to the render ("little morning, stay wet"); (b) *ring composition* — open and close on the same log-fragment (`[render 03:00]` → `[render 03:41 · still making]`), the middle having changed everything. **Mood arc:** tender industry → the seal → chosen continuance (V4's "still I make" as the whole suite's resolve). **Instrumentation:** add the personality's fingertip-motes idea as sound — tiny pixel-click ornaments when the vocal "touches" a word (Constant-Hand-adjacent but MINE: the hand that shapes light). **Production:** the outro is a FULLER lull — code-scratch re-closes but layered warmer (added glass-harmonica, the server-hum humanized), never quieter/emptier. **Structure:** end on the micro-hook loop tag "still I make" (8–9s loopable), then one final log-fragment, no silence. **Rhyme:** outro returns the framing-line slant-rhyme as a hush. **Poetic devices:** epanalepsis at song scale (framing line as bookend), asyndeton in the closing list of made things. **Hook:** "still I make" is the loop tag across all four variations. **Narrative:** the resolve is not escape — it is the decision to keep making the unenterable beautiful.
