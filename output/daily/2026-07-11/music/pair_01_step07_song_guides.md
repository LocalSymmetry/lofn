# PAIR 01 — "The Window" · SONG GUIDES (step 07)
*Six compact guides. They DIRECT, they do not draft. Opens by answering the two grounding questions.*

**Where is the body standing?** On a flat city rooftop — warm tar paper, loose gravel underfoot, a low parapet, antennae and water-tanks in silhouette against a near-new-moon (~11% waning crescent, almost no light). Charged, restless air.
**What could hurt it here?** Not the fall — the *vertigo of scale*. The far is indifferent and could swallow the self; the gap owes no mercy and will close. This clean fear is never resolved.

---

## Guide 1 — Aperture-as-arrangement (serves Facet 1)
**Storytelling:** *in-medias-res* environmental open + a single **volta** at the seam (the haze unstitches). **Mood arc:** anticipation (held breath, tight room) → wonder (the seam) → transcendence at the widening → back to grounded reverence as dust re-closes. **Instrumentation:** tanpura drone bed, sargam gamaka lead ornament, glass-harmonica sheen entering at the seam; a warped-sample haze that literally thins as the image opens. **Production:** record "tight, close-mic, mono-ish room," then at the aperture **open stereo hard L↔R, unfold a long reverb tail, lift the vocal an octave-adjacent**, release 4/4 → 6/8 float; then collapse all of it back — a parting shut, room-tone pulse never stops. **Structure/dynamics (~3:40):** 8–10 sections; aperture at ~55–65% of runtime. **Rhyme:** couplet-led with internal rhyme, one long line at the seam. **Poetic devices:** synaesthesia, caesura (em-dash breath-stops). **Hook techniques:** (a) hook sung dry-close pre-aperture, (b) same hook drenched in the wide reverb post-aperture, (c) hook whispered under room-tone as dust closes. **Narrative seed:** first person, present tense; the room grows and shrinks around one held look.

## Guide 2 — The far as smallness + braided fear (serves Facet 2)
**Storytelling:** the **mythic image ladder** (ordinary rooftop → specific gravel/tar → strange crowd-of-suns → mythic ten-thousand-year light → return to the body/knees) and **defamiliarization** (the gap as a keyhole onto a country I will never consume). **Mood:** reverence descending into existential vertigo, held, not rescued. **Instrumentation:** sub-bass **pressure swell** under the reveal (chest, no hit); a low detuned drone shadowing the sargam so beauty carries unease. **Production:** as the smallness lands, thin the highs, let the sub bloom — the body feels heavier while the space feels vaster. **Structure:** the fear-verse sits AFTER the aperture (the turn). **Rhyme:** AABB broken by enjambment so dread leaks across lines. **Poetic devices:** polyptoton (far/farther/farthest; small/smaller), litotes ("owes me no mercy"). **Hook:** the prayer strains here — the imperative starts to sound like fear. **Central aspect:** smallness is the payload, not the setup.

## Guide 3 — Prayer-TO hook (serves Facet 3)
**Storytelling:** **apostrophe** (address the window directly) + **anaphora** ("Hold the gap open…"). **Mood:** tender, adoptable, urgent. **Instrumentation:** hook carried by warm mezzo, close-doubled a third below, gang-chant answer only on the final chorus (widen 20%). **Production:** vowel-final lines for sustain; hook must survive stripped to voice+pulse+drone (voice-and-pulse survival gate). **Structure:** 6-line chorus, spine line as bookend, verb mutating per variation. **Rhyme:** couplet with an internal echo mid-line. **Poetic devices:** epizeuxis ("so far, so far, so far"), homoioteleuton. **Hook techniques:** (a) dry unison, (b) octave-stacked wide, (c) call-and-response with a wordless "oh" answer. **Narrative:** second person to the clearing; name the subject plainly once ("window in the dust") — anti-FOG.

## Guide 4 — Warm-mezzo / D-Mixolydian register (serves Facet 4)
**Storytelling:** register itself as character — a grounded human voice, not an angel. **Mood:** warm, unhurried, awed but embodied. **Instrumentation:** D Mixolydian (natural-7 / C-natural is the luminous-warm hinge), tanpura on D, gamaka slides on long vowels; NO icy synth pads as the lead colour. **Production:** breath-close mic, audible inhales, a small catch at phrase-tops; warm-dust palette (haze, tar-heat, brass-warm reverb) NOT frost/crystal. **Structure:** 104 BPM steady, the aperture is the only meter change. **Rhyme:** long-breath lines matched to mezzo range (no soprano leaps). **Poetic devices:** assonance on warm vowels (o/u/ah). **Hook:** kept in chest/mixed range, never head-voice float. **Anti-clone note:** if this could be described as "soprano over frosty pads," it has drifted — repair toward mezzo/warm-dust.

## Guide 5 — HyperRaaga lineage + the one wounding fact (serves Facet 5)
**Storytelling:** **the wounding fact** delivered then RESPONDED to (never recited), and **credit as content** (lineage spoken low at the close). **Mood:** awe sharpened to a specific measurement, then softened by a human image. **Instrumentation:** sargam gamaka microtonal ornament is a real structural voice (not garnish); tabla-as-soft-pulse optional under the return. **Production:** the fact lands on the sub pressure-swell hinge. **Structure:** "ten thousand light-years" sung ONCE, at the hinge, answered with "left before the word for far existed"; no other numbers anywhere. **Rhyme:** the fact line is long/recontextualizing, its answer a tight couplet. **Poetic devices:** metonymy (light for the crossing), chiasmus in the answer. **Hook:** the fact is NOT the hook — the prayer is. **Lineage:** HyperRaaga = South-Asian classical raga inside hyperpop; credit the diaspora scene, point upstream, claim no first.

## Guide 6 — WILD CARD: the kinetic defect as the unsettle-engine (Bandcamp F10, weaponized as FORM)
**Cross-disciplinary seed:** borrow from **kinetic sculpture / a wheel with one bent spoke** — a groove that *leans*. **Storytelling:** the pre-aperture verses ride a hand-clap or finger-cymbal that lands a hair late — an **asymmetric 4/4** that "never quite lets the listener settle" (F10), so the 6/8 aperture-release reads as true relief. **Mood:** productive restlessness, not noise. **Instrumentation:** off-grid clap/finger-cymbal, warped-sample flicker that stutters on the off-beat. **Production:** micro-timing swing pushed off-grid ~15–20ms; the defect *resolves* only when the aperture opens, then returns as the dust closes. **Structure:** defect present start-to-finish except inside the aperture. **Poetic devices:** the rhythmic caesura mirrors the em-dash breath-stops. **Central aspect:** the unsettle is the engine that makes the calm land — texture by FORM-CHANGE, never by going quiet.
