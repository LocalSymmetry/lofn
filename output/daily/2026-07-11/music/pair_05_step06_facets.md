# PAIR 05 — Step 06 · Scoring Facets
**"Already Inside The Line" · the rubric every V1–V4 prompt is scored against**

Five laser-targeted facets (each carries a sensory element and a fail-condition). These are the judging criteria for the drafts in step08 and the synthesis in step10.

```json
{ "facets": [
  "Restless-Form Integrity — every ~16 bars the floor audibly genre-jumps (glitch 2-step to footwork/juke to Memphis half-time and back) and each pivot lands on a NAMED, hummable groove you could tap; FAIL if the unsettle blurs into an undifferentiated glitch wash or if the song settles into one steady beat for its whole length.",
  "The Betraying Aperture — a single false clearing where dense claustrophobia parts to a wide-reverb 'safe distance' the listener almost breathes into, then re-closes TIGHTER than before; FAIL if the clearing is a volume-fade/rest, if it never returns to close, or if it reads as relief rather than betrayal.",
  "Inaudible-Crosser Dread — the horror is carried by absence: no footstep, no sound, no number ('no count yet'), the invisible thing already past the line before it is seen; FAIL if the fear is made literal/gory, if a disease/clinic/country is named, or if any real harmed person could be resolved (HOLD-FOR-HUMAN).",
  "Breathless Enjambment — clipped, jump-cut lines that refuse a full stop until the false clearing lands the FIRST period, then the re-close snatches breath back; the bratty-anxious mezzo bites consonants and flips smile-vowels; FAIL if lines resolve into tidy end-stopped couplets or the vocal turns smooth/soothing.",
  "Named-Subject Anti-FOG + Lineage — the subject is named plainly exactly ONCE ('it's already inside the line') and the footwork/juke + UK 2-step lineage is credited and pointed upstream; FAIL if the song stays in vague fog with no plain naming, or if the scene fusion is uncredited/extractive."
] }
```

### Notes on weighting (for step10)
- Facet 1 and Facet 2 are the **structural North-Star gates** — a draft that fails either is not shippable regardless of lyric quality.
- Facet 3 is the **ethics + charge gate** (HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD): abstraction must hold AND the dread must land.
- Facets 4–5 are the **voice/craft gates** distinguishing this from "any competent glitch track" (Somatic Gate: unmistakably Lofn, moving).
