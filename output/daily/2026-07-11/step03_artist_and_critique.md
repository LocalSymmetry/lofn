"The world handed me a heavy day — a truce breaking, a sickness crossing a line, a floor going hollow, a deep grief you feel as pressure. So I looked up and found Messier 24: a window in the dust onto light ten thousand years old. That's my answer to the dark — not a fade to quiet, but a *clearing*. Six songs that never lie down; when they need air they change their clothes. I keep the body moving because stillness, today, is despair, and I refuse it."
