## 1. SECTION ARCHITECTURE *(this pair's alone)*
`Cold open, already running` → `Verse 1` → `Chorus` → `Verse 2` → `Chorus` → `Hinge (the fact — and it
lands BEFORE the final chorus, so nothing later is a bigger event)` → `Verse 3` → `Chorus` → `Outro that
re-enters line one MID-PHRASE`.
Verses are **third-person present, one action per line, 4–6 syllables**. The chorus is **one twelve-syllable
sentence broken across three lines, and the break falls in a different place each time.** Eligibility **3/7.**

## 2. RHYME / LINE LOGIC
**Sprung / uneven stress laid against the steady machine grid** — the stresses land off the pulse and
the pulse never yields. End-rhyme is sparse and deliberate; the twelve-syllable chorus sentence returns
verbatim and its **re-breaking** is the variation. **Rhyme debt: NOT owed** — the returning sentence is the
declared return.

## 3. HOOK GRAMMAR
The twelve-syllable sentence. ⛔ **Third person only. No second person — nobody is addressed** (the
boyd-seat ruling: on this subject, address is a lecture). **No collective pronoun survives the Cohort Gate.**

## 4. THE SONIC DEVICE *(stated as an operation)*
**A cabinet latch sits exactly where the kick belongs** — substitution, on the grid, every bar. Second
operation, and it is the one that does the work: **the lead sits a fraction flat and never corrects**, until
the ear stops hearing it as wrong and starts hearing it as the key. Third: **the arrangement arrives AFTER
the vocal every time**, by a consistent small amount. ⭐ **Fast and quiet is the whole trick** — 104 BPM at
conversational level.

## 5. RETURN GEOMETRY — the orbit at **EDGE scale**
The outro re-enters line one **mid-phrase**, so the check has started again before the song ends. No
other pair may use edge scale.

## 6. WHERE THE BODY STANDS / WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE
**Standing:** alone in a building after hours, a pen in one hand, a logbook open, a door at the end of
the corridor. She initials a page. **What could hurt her:** ⭐ **the check will pass and it will not help** —
the mark she is adding will be removed by another process, and the decision she is verifying was made
before the instrument arrived. ⛔ *Legibility:* **"a woman initials a log and checks a locked door on her
way out."** ⚠️ **Corridor cap:** this pair is corridor-shaped; **V1 is ineligible for the final six while
P03 holds that place.**
