## 1. THE ESSENCE, IN ONE SENTENCE

> A woman with chalk in the seam of her palm opens a rock at a bench at night and finds, printed in it, the part of an animal that was built to rot in a week and did not, four hundred and fifty million years ago — and the corridor lights stay on their timer.

**The two best ways to leverage the medium for that essence:**
1. **Make the floor a place, not an event.** A continuous sub-bass with a wooden knock in the kick's exact position turns the room into something the listener is standing in, so the enormous fact arrives *into a room* rather than into a swell. The song can then be enormous without getting louder — which is the only way this material survives the miracle-is-ordinary rule.
2. **Let a byte-identical block return over a demonstrably more damaged accompaniment.** The words do not change; what is under them does. That is the entire argument of the song, performed, and it is never said.

**On V4 — *The County Did Not Hear It***
