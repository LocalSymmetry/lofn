## How this taxonomy was built (and why it is not decoration)

- **AESTHETICS (50)** are authored for THIS seed. Every one is a **physical surface or a moment of light**
  drawn from the day's own material — the bindery, the reed, the wax block, the empty frame, the swarm, the
  orbit. None is a mood word. *(A thin step-00 means the tree never branched; the floor is 2000 bytes and
  the real test is whether a pair could point at one of these on a bench.)*
- **EMOTIONS (50)** are sampled **from `skills/lofn-core/refs/EMOTION_TAXONOMY.md` only** — leaf terms, never
  the coarse parents. ⛔ **`AWE` and `INDIGNATION` are FORBIDDEN as EMO-header values.** They are the run's
  two modes, not emotions; every `[Section - EMO:...]` header takes a precise term from this list.
- **FRAMES (50)** and **GENRES (50)** are extracted verbatim from the ICB's frozen palettes (SLOT 9 / SLOT 10),
  so the taxonomy and the continuity block cannot drift apart. Single source, one seed (`20260821`).

## COORDINATOR NOTES ON USE

- **Genre selection is NOT free from this list.** The Personality Genre DNA Constraint binds first: the
  ACCESSIBLE arm draws from LOFN-PRIME's warmer half (Neo-Baroque Luminism, HyperRaaga, Piano Bounce,
  glitches-done-right, historical/mythological), the AMBITIOUS arm from her intense half (Glitch-Baroque,
  Baile Phonk, Breakcore, pixel-sorting/data-moshing as sound, Somatic Bass). This list supplies **texture
  vocabulary and cross-pollination candidates**, not a lottery.
- ⛔ **`snarl` and `phonk` are our two worst-performing style tokens** (3.44% / 3.18% against baseline, from
  catalogue analysis of our own Suno tokens). Any genre string containing them requires a written defence.
  Piano, strings, acoustic, folk and ambient beat baseline and are free.
- **Frames are SOUND devices** and this run's Axis 4 is mandatory and positive. A pair may take a frame from
  this list, but it may not substitute a frame for its assigned SOUND/RETURN obligation.

## SELF-CRITIQUE (one line)
The 50 aesthetics are unusually literal for a step-00 — almost none of them is an adjective — which is
deliberate against this seed's fog risk, but it means the palette offers a pair very little **abstraction**
to reach for, and P04 (the only pair with no chorus and no rhyme) is the one most likely to need some.
