## REFINED 1 · **V1**

**THE PROCESS COMPOSER's objection, verbatim in temperament:** *four of the five things you wrote down are
things that do not happen. "No reverb. No synths. Restrained." Subtraction is the cheapest answer and on
this house's own render evidence it is the first thing the generator smooths away. Tell me what the
arrangement DOES, in verbs, and put every negation in the field that was built for negations.*

**Changes made (flair only, no new scene):**
1. Banned opener `Begin with…` struck; the prompt now leads on genre + tempo + key, which is where the
   embedding weight sits.
2. Both style-field negations moved wholesale into `1B`. The acoustic axis is now stated **positively** —
   *real upright piano, pedal creak, room hum kept, tracked live in one small tiled room* (handoff
   conflict 2).
3. The kinetic defect written as an operation: **the stride lands a hair late every other bar.**
4. The refusal written as an operation too: **the band keeps its footing while the voice is proved right.**
   A refusal that is only implied is a refusal the generator will not perform.
5. Vocalist spec built out to contract depth — age, register, accent, two tone descriptors, two quirks
   (breath audible between clauses; plosive-forward diction) plus spatial staging.

**Refined music prompt (step-09 state, 1000 chars — ⚠️ over the target band, see verdict):**
> Night-shift workroom gospel at ninety-two BPM in C minor, lifting to E-flat major at the hinge. The first
> five seconds are a ceiling ballast ticking, a stack of hardbacks knocked square on a steel bed, then a
> flat guillotine thump landing exactly where a kick drum would sit. Lead is a woman in her late thirties,
> plain northern English alto, dry and plosive-forward, straight-toned, breath audible between clauses,
> singing a hand's width from a ribbon mic dead centre at conversational volume. Real upright piano a step
> to her left, woody and close, its left hand bouncing a two-beat gospel stride that arrives a hair late
> every other bar. Wire brushes on a coated head behind her right shoulder. Pedal creak, page rustle and
> the room's own hum stay in the mix, tracked live in one small tiled room. Verses run flat and even; the
> refrain lifts only in the piano. At the hinge a single gasba reed enters high and bent and never returns,
> and the band keeps its footing while the voice is proved right.

**⚠️ CRITIC VERDICT:** every content gate passes; **length fails the target band at 1000 chars, sitting on
the hard cap.** Handed to step 10 for a clause-level trim — *trim a clause, never truncate a phrase.*
Resolved there at **952**.

**Refined title:** `The Clean Cut` → **`I Know The Glue`.** The working title named the concept; the refined
title is a line the singer actually says, it is inside the hook, and it is four words a stranger can repeat.

## REFINED 2 · **V2**

**THE RAIN-GAP PRODUCER's objection:** *you have the right instinct and the wrong execution. Yes, take the
kick out. But the gap is not a silence — the gap is a substitution, and something audible has to be
standing exactly where the drum was. And get the bin out of the hard left; a non-musical element parked in
one speaker collapses to mono in the render and takes the whole idea with it.*

**Changes made (flair only, no new scene):**
1. Banned opener `Use…` struck.
2. **Hard-panned bin removed**; the bin is now *inside the groove* — a **bin-lid clank doubling the thump
   on alternate bars** — which is substitution, and it survives a mono fold.
3. **The long silence before the last chorus deleted.** Ruling 4 and L22: an interrupting silence renders
   as four-tenths of a second of nothing. What replaced it is the short syllable in the refrain's third
   line, which the brushes now fill — a hole with something in it.
4. Reed cut from "throughout" back to **one entry at the hinge**. N = 1; the seam is a seam, not wallpaper.
5. "Sad and tired" replaced with a real vocalist spec identical in shape to V1's, staged closer.
6. Kinetic defect stated: **brushes swing while the piano stays straight, so the backbeat sits between the
   two and never settles.**

**Refined music prompt (step-09 state, 968 chars):** as printed in step 10, before the trim of
`landing inside it` → `landing inside`, `sitting exactly in` → `exactly in`, `staged a hand's width` →
`a hand's width`, `kept low` → `low`, and `are kept in the mix` → `stay in`. **Resolved at 933.**

**Lyric refinement:** the Verse-1 line `Part of a word is on the shred` was rewritten to
`A letter shows along the shred`, because the original duplicated the refrain's own object and blunted it
on arrival. The refrain keeps `The strip on top still says a word` — the object should land once, in the
hook, not twice.

**Refined title:** `The Offcut Bin` → **`I Always Look`.** Same reason as V1: the concept name went out and
the sung line came in.

## REFINED 3 · **V3**

**THE HALF-PIPE WITNESS's objection:** *show the falls. The version of this that travels is not the one
where the cutter is a villain and it is not the one where she is a victim either. She is good at it — she
has a technique, she is slower than the new hand, she notices which spines cut clean — and the song does
not rescue her from that. Also: your raw draft builds to a big emotional final chorus. That is the one
place this pair is forbidden to land.*

**Changes made (flair only, no new scene):**
1. Banned opener `Chronology:` struck, and the section list removed from the style field entirely — the
   form lives in the lyric headers, not in the prompt.
2. **Acoustic guitar, soft drums and strings deleted.** They are a generic substitute for an arrangement
   this pair already owns; a generic substitute is a repair, not a choice. Guillotine Gospel restored:
   upright piano, brushes into stick-butt on the rim, thump in the kick's grid position.
3. **`builds to a big emotional final chorus` struck** — it drives the thesis into the FORBIDDEN SECTION.
   Replaced with the true dynamic: *nothing marks the moment the vocal turns out to be right.*
4. **Reed solo demoted to a single entry.** A solo is a feature; this is a seam.
5. **Second vocalist cut.** The kid is reported, never voiced, so nobody in the song is spoken to and the
   overhearing rule holds without effort.
6. Kinetic defect inverted for this variation and stated: **the left-hand bounce flattens bar by bar into
   something machine-steady as the room speeds up under her.**

**Refined music prompt (step-09 state, 971 chars):** as printed in step 10, before the trim of
`a flat guillotine thump` → `a guillotine thump`, `the only voice on the record` → `the only voice here`,
`something even and machine-steady` → `something machine-steady`, and `A four-line aside` → `One aside`.
**Resolved at 940.**

**Refined title:** `The New Person` → **`The Kid Won't Call`.** The refined title carries the whole
anticlimax and it is the refrain's first line.

## REFINED 4 · **V4**

**THE LUTHIER's objection:** *I cut cane for a living. I destroy a plant to make an instrument, and I am
not troubled by it, because **I choose the piece — I hold it, I flex it, I reject most of it.** Your draft
has decided in advance that this is a tragedy and it says so in the style field. Take the opinion out and
give her the gesture back. Whether anybody was holding it is the only difference there is, and it is shown
by hands, not stated by a narrator.*

**Changes made (flair only, no new scene):**
1. Banned opener `For an adult human singer…` struck.
2. **The thesis deleted from the style field.** `about loss and memory`, `the archive is dying`, `nobody
   cares` — all gone. The last of those also invented a villain, which ruling 1 forbids: nobody chose.
3. **Lush pads, big reverb and emotional vibrato removed** — they contradict the small tiled room and the
   straight-tone spec, and they are the exact grammar of the eulogy this run is not writing.
4. What replaced the opinion is a gesture: **a crate lid prised up, dust in the throat, a cough.** She is
   holding it. That is the difference, and it is shown.
5. Kinetic defect stated: **the thump lands a sixteenth early every fourth bar and is never corrected.**
6. The room's single move — workroom to kitchen and silently back — written as a **reverb change with
   nothing announced**, so it cannot inflate into an emotional bridge.

**Refined music prompt (step-09 state, 936 chars):** in the target band on the first refined pass and
carried into step 10 unchanged.

**Lyric refinement:** `I never opened it. I wait` → `I haven't opened it. I wait`, so the Turn is free to
have her open it two lines later and still leave the outro's `I have not read a word of it` true. Opening
and reading are different acts, and the song needs both to stay unresolved.

**Refined title:** `The One She Kept` → **`It Doesn't Weigh A Lot`.** The working title also carried a
numeral into the pair's one place a numeral is not wanted; the refined title is the refrain's short third
line and it is the whole unresolved question in five words.
