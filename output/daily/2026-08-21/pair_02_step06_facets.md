## 0. WHAT THIS PAIR IS SCORING FOR

**Concept (C-20):** In a town under an earthquake swarm, a woman has learned to size a shock by which shelf
rattles first. She is not frightened any more, which frightens her daughter.

**Medium (arr. 17 — Shelf-Rattle Dorian):** 76 BPM half-time, D Dorian, **static — no cadence until the
final bar**. Deep funky bassline entering late. **ONE** squeezebox/accordion figure as the single
non-modern element. Dry close vocal. Floor tom.

**The seam (SKOP-01 · N = 1):** the world is a modern, dry, deep-bass room; the single intrusion is the
accordion figure carried in from a wedding down the road; **the seam is the doorway** — the point where the
outside music reaches a kitchen that is counting. The body doing something across it: **she moves plates.**

**The pair's allocated sung fact:** ***ten kilometres down*** — once per song, at the emotional hinge,
**responded to, never recited**, spelled in words. ⛔ No other number is sung in any of the four songs.

**Special Flairs (motif, never phrasing):** **#10 A Chant A Room Can Hold** · **#13 The Survivable Wound** ·
**#8 The Substituted Downbeat**.

**⛔ FORBIDDEN SECTION:** the **BRIDGE**. The thesis may not land there, and it may not land as a portable
line anywhere (Ruling 6). It is reachable only by having gone through the song.
