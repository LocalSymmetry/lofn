## 2. THE ANCHOR, IN FULL

> ### Archetype 2 — THE LAST WITNESS
> **Territory:** The person who was there and is almost gone. Not grief — **URGENCY**.
> **Template:** single unaccompanied voice (speaking) → the witness's specific sensory memory → chorus: prayer addressed TO the witness ("Tell me again") → bridge: voice returns, fragmented, music strips away → final chorus: listener alone, witness gone, prayer continues.
> **Key:** minor → relative major. **Tempo:** 80–95 BPM. **Vocal:** intimate, close-mic.

### PRESERVED (the winning pattern's actual engine)
1. **Urgency, not grief.** Nothing in this run mourns prettily.
2. **The testimony and the music's disappearance share one clock.** Structural, not decorative.
3. **Close-mic intimacy.** Corroborated independently by our own catalogue measurement (CLOSE +0.21pp, n=74).
4. **Minor → relative major**, available to every pair; two must actually use it.
5. **A body with specific sensory memory**, not a position.

### ⛔ ROTATED (and why each rotation is forced)
| Component | Rotation | Forcing reason |
|---|---|---|
| Prayer hook addressed **TO** the witness | ⛔ **Addressed FROM the witness, or TO something established as unable to receive it** | Shares the benchmark's addressee shape (L18); and the Context room's **OVERHEARING NOT ADDRESSING** ruling makes any address a lecture on this subject |
| Thesis in the final chorus | ⛔ **Thesis lands at the TURN; the final chorus complicates it** | Golden Move rule 3 + the **PORTABLE-THESIS VETO** (panel Config 2) |
| Music **strips away** at the bridge | ⛔ **Music SUBSTITUTES at the bridge** | Ruling 4 — subtraction is what the renderer smooths (L22, render-measured); absence is unfalsifiable, replacement survives |
| The witness is a person, almost gone | ⭐ **The witness is already destroyed. The one who can still hear it is the thing made out of it.** | This is the seed's single inversion, and it is what makes it *today's* |
| 80–95 BPM across the set | **68 / 76 / 84 / 92 / 96 / 104**, one per pair | uniformity of tempo is uniformity of body; **none at 110** (benchmark) |
