## 1. SECTION ARCHITECTURE *(this pair's alone)*
`Reed alone (first 5 s)` → `Verse 1 (tercet)` → `Chorus` → `Verse 2 (tercet)` → `Chorus` →
`⭐ CHORUS 3 — PERIAPSIS` → `Verse 3 (tercet)` → terminal close.
Verses are **long-breath tercets, 9–11 syllables a line**. The chorus is **four-syllable lines** — the exact
inverse of P05's shape, assigned as the set's structural counterweight. Eligibility **3/7.**

## 2. RHYME / LINE LOGIC
⭐ **THE REFRAIN RETURNS ONE WORD WRONG each time, and the wrong word is NEVER corrected.** That is the
pair's return device and it is a positive obligation, not a removal. Tercets carry a loose terminal
assonance rather than strict rhyme; the long line breathes and the short chorus lands. **Rhyme debt: NOT
owed** — the one-word-wrong refrain is the declared return.

## 3. HOOK GRAMMAR
A four-syllable chorus line that is **almost the same every time.** A listener who has heard it twice
will sing the wrong version on the third pass, and the song does not correct them. ⛔ Overheard, never led;
⛔ **not addressed to the reed** (L18 rotation).

## 4. THE SONIC DEVICE *(stated as an operation)*
⭐ **ONE SEAM, N = 1.** The reed flute is the **single** historical element; everything else is modern.
Do not add a second traditional instrument — *four idioms applied competently is the signature of a dead
arrangement.* Second operation: ⭐ **PERIAPSIS** — chorus 3 **arrives earlier than the form has trained the
ear to expect and closer in the mix**, then **leaves at the same speed**. It does not stay to be admired.
Third: **breath is a percussion layer.** Close: **terminal silence, not a fade.**

## 5. RETURN GEOMETRY — the orbit at **SECTION scale**
Periapsis. No other pair may use section scale.

## 6. WHERE THE BODY STANDS / WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE
**Standing:** at a bench with cane, a knife, and a small fire; a party audible through a wall. **What
could hurt her:** ⭐ **the light is arriving, not staying** — a gibbous at sixty-five percent is on its way
somewhere and so is everything in the room. The instrument is a **consumable by design**; she breaks
several a year and has stopped counting. ⛔ **R-e is sharpest here: she is BORED of the miracle.** The
repertoire is older than most countries that would have a ministry for it, and to her it is Tuesday.
⛔ *Legibility:* **"a woman makes reed flutes and breaks them, and there is a wedding next door."**

## 7. THE FOUR VARIATION ANGLES *(derived from THIS concept only)*
- **V1 · Cane Season** — the maker who breaks several a year and has stopped counting.
- **V2 · The Wedding** — a repertoire thousands of years old surviving by being played loudly at a party.
- **V3 · Sixty-Five Percent** — tonight's moon as the subject; the light is arriving, not staying.
- **V4 · The Next One** — the reed being made right now, unfinished when the song ends (harvested from C-29): the song **stops while the hands are still working.**
