## STEP 1 · THE IDEA, RESTATED FROM THE GUIDES
A woman in a kitchen under a swarm sizes each shock by which shelf answers first. She is not frightened any
more. Her daughter has learned the order. **76 BPM half-time, D Dorian, static, no cadence until the final
bar.** Deep funky bass late, ONE squeezebox figure as the single non-modern element, dry close vocal, floor
tom. Runtime target **3:20–3:50** per song.

## STEP 2 · KEY ELEMENTS *(per the Medium panel)*
- **Opening (first 5 s):** a bare floor tom struck with the flat of a hand, and tin jar lids ticking. No bass.
- **Key audio moments:** the bass arriving late and low; the plate stack settling into the missing backbeat;
  the squeezebox stepping wrong and not coming back; a young voice through a wall.
- **Emotional peak:** the hinge — ***ten kilometres down*** — answered, never recited.
- **Transitions:** none are dynamic. Everything is a substitution.
- **Close:** terminal, not fading. *(Interrupting silence smooths away; terminal silence survives — L22.)*

## STEPS 9–16 · THE THREE CRITIC LOOPS *(minuted; the supplied 18 voices, no new panel)*

### Step 9 — Concept panel review
**THE AMBIGUOUS PHOTOGRAPH (after Sebald):** *"R1's narrator is certain for twenty lines. Four minutes of a
correct opinion is the failure mode."* → **Loop 1 fix:** the certainty is now the **subject** rather than the
posture — the turn in verse 3 breaks it in the body (a hand that keeps shaking), not in the argument.
**THE POOR-IMAGE POLITICIAN (after Steyerl):** *"Do not seat this with the collectors."* → checked: nothing
in this pair mourns an object. The plates are moved, not grieved.
**THE HABITUAL-MEMORY SKEPTIC (after Chun) — HYPER-SKEPTIC:** *"R5 has no mechanism. It has a hook and a
mood."* → **R5 demoted to donor status in writing, here, before the ranking.**

### Step 10 — Refinement loop 1
Verse-3 turns rewritten as actions in all four leads. R2's hinge upgraded from a statement to *antanaclasis*
(`Just` / `Just`). R4's hinge moved into the bridge, where the **fact** is permitted and the **thesis** is not.

### Step 11 — Medium panel review
**THE PROCESS COMPOSER (after Glass) — HYPER-SKEPTIC:** *"Three of your four raws put the plate stack in the
same place with the same wood. That is not four songs, it is one song with four nouns."* → **Loop 2 fix:**
R2's substitution is re-voiced as **a knuckle on the underside of a table**; R3's is the loudest object in

**THE RAIN-GAP PRODUCER (after Burial):** *"R4 is a dub song and dub is a width. Width smooths."* →
**Loop 2 fix:** the delay is specified as a **tail kept dead centre**, and the exclude field carries the ban.

texture, **not** as this pair's return device (that is bar-scale, one step wrong, and it stays sole).

### Step 12 — Refinement loop 2

### Step 13 — Context & marketing panel review

**THE COHORT ABOLITIONIST (after Cohen) — HYPER-SKEPTIC:** *"Show me the pronoun scan."* → run; zero

### Step 14 — Refinement loop 3
