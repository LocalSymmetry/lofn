## THE FIVE TECHNIQUES PER SURVIVING GENRE
*(Template requirement: for each final genre, list ≥5 distinct composition/stylistic techniques, drawn from the Seed Music Frames palette in ICB SLOT 10.)*

### G1 — **Industrial gospel, quiet** *(arr. 15, 16)*
1. Left-hand piano bounce carrying gospel motion under a lyric with no consolation in it.
2. **Substituted downbeat** — a non-musical impact in the kick's exact position, on the grid.
3. Room tone kept in the vocal chain; no gate, no de-noise.
4. Anaphora on a work-verb as the verse's rhythmic engine.
5. **Withheld acknowledgement** — no fill, cymbal or lift after the lyric's most confirmed moment.

### G2 — **Rolling half-time bass music** *(arr. 17, 18)*
1. Deep funky bassline entering late and never on the first repeat.
2. **One-step-wrong figure** at bar scale, uncorrected.
3. A living-idiom melodic element (accordion, funaná-derived) as the single non-modern layer.
4. Backbeat removed and **replaced** by a domestic impact.
5. Internal rhyme chained across the caesura, never at the line end.

### G3 — **Slinky almost-R&B with Neo-Baroque harmony** *(arr. 19, 20)*
1. Rhodes-to-upright **substitution rupture** at the midpoint.
2. Stanza economy as arrangement: eight lines, then four, then two.
3. Vowel ladder climbing across the stanza, resetting at each shortening.
4. **Byte-identical refrain**, three times, undefended.
5. Chorus harmony thickened Neo-Baroque while the verse stays two-part.

### G4 — **Glitch-Baroque over cosmic synths** *(arr. 21, 22)*
1. Data-moshed string figure treated as **material**, not artefact.
2. Sub-bass as the room's floor rather than a rhythmic event.
3. **Held vowel at three widening intervals.**
4. Monosyllabic verse breaking into a polysyllabic returning strophe.
5. The same event re-staged at three widening mic distances.

### G5 — **Frenetic drum-machine pulse, dry and quiet** *(arr. 23, 24)*
1. Machine grid at 104 BPM mixed at conversational level — **fast and quiet is the whole trick**.
2. **Latch substitution** in the kick position.
3. Sprung/uneven stress against the steady pulse.
4. Lead a fraction flat, uncorrected, until it reads as the key.
5. Outro re-entering the first line **mid-phrase**.

### G6 — ⭐ **Gasba seam over a modern world** *(arr. 25, 26, 27)*
1. Reed flute as the **single** historical intrusion; everything else modern (**N = 1**).
2. Lead vocal Auto-Tuned to an alien timbre — *the effect is the character, not a correction*.
3. **Periapsis** — one chorus arrives earlier and closer, then leaves at the same speed.
4. Refrain returning **one word wrong**, never corrected.
5. Breath used as a percussion layer; **terminal** silence at the end, never an interrupting one.

## ⛔ MEDIUM-PANEL RULINGS THAT BIND EVERY PAIR

1. ⭐⭐ **SUBSTITUTION, NOT SUBTRACTION.** Every removal in this document is paired with something audible in
   its place. *Rationale is render-measured, not aesthetic:* a specified four-second full stop rendered as
   four-tenths of a second; hard-panned non-musical elements collapsed to near-mono; **interrupting silence
   is filled 2/2**. Absence is unfalsifiable in a mono-ish render. Replacement survives.
2. **TERMINAL silence survives (1/1); INTERRUPTING silence does not (0/2).** A song may end in silence. No
   song may depend on a hole in its middle.
3. **NO SONG DEPENDS ON STEREO WIDTH.** Spatial language belongs in the prompt as *staging*, never as a
   load-bearing mechanism. *(And the one time the renderer inverted our width spec — narrowing into the
   climax instead of widening — the inversion was truer than the intent. Deviations are raw material.)*
4. **STEADY TEMPO ARRIVES (6/6 within 5 BPM); TEMPO DRAMATURGY DOES NOT (0/2).** Every pair states one
   tempo and keeps it. No programmed accelerando, no half-time-to-double-time transformation as a plan.
5. **OPPOSED VOCAL CHARACTERS BLEND (0/2).** No pair may build its structure on two distinct singers
   staying distinct. One lead; doubling and treatment are fine.
6. **THE MELODY IS NOT NEGOTIABLE.** A degradation process with no tune under it is an atmosphere. Fourteen
   arrangements died at this step for exactly that.
7. ⭐ **THE DEVIATION CLAUSE.** Every pair names **in advance**, in its step-10 package, one place it expects
   the generator to disobey and **which disobedience it would keep.** This turns THE GRAIN LAW from a
   post-hoc excuse into a pre-registered prediction.

arrangement whose operation is *survival under treatment* rather than *treatment itself*, which is the

being forced into a pair whose concept does not want it.
