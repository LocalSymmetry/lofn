## 3. THE GOLDEN MOVE (what generators get INSTEAD of golden songs)

1. **Stand somewhere real.** ONE concrete place the body occupies. Name where it stands and what the senses
   register there. Concept-illustration is the failure mode; experience-report is the move.
2. **One wounding fact.** At most ONE numeric fact is sung, at the emotional hinge, **responded to**, never
   recited — and **spelled out in words**, never digits. *(This run pre-allocates the fact per pair; see §5.)*
3. **The turn.** Past the midpoint the song contradicts or complicates its opening stance. A song that
   asserts its final emotion from line one is a corpse.
4. **Fear stays braided in.** Every AWE song carries a clean fear it does not resolve cheaply, and answers
   both pre-draft questions concretely: **where is the body standing** / **what could hurt it here**.
5. **Rotate the register.** No crystalline-soprano / A-major / ~110-BPM / frost-and-cosmos default. This
   run's tempo ladder is 68 / 76 / 84 / 88 / 92 / 104 and **none of them is 110**.
6. **The surface names its subject.** A stranger retells the scene AND subject in one sentence after ONE
   listen; the subject appears plainly in the lyric at least once. Fog is not depth. `REPAIR — FOG`.
7. **THE RETURN.** Song is made of returns. **Removal is a debt** — a pair that strips rhyme names what
   returns in its place, in its own isolation brief, before a line exists. **A byte-identical chorus is
   correct and requires no defence. Say nothing about it.**

## 4. THE SUNO OUTPUT CONTRACT (hard gate — non-waivable)

**Order every creative prompt: seed → permission → songmaking → QA contract LAST.** Never lead a pair
subagent with the character checklist; it will write to the form instead of the seed.

### `## 1. MUSIC PROMPT`
- ONE standalone, copy-paste Suno style prompt per song. **Dense PROSE PARAGRAPH, 850–1000 chars — write
  into the mid-band, target 870–960.** ≥985 chars raises a **boundary-hugging FLAG**.
- **Must end as a complete sentence.** A mid-phrase truncation to fit the cap is a **hard fail** — trim a
  clause instead.
- **NOT** categorized `key:value` brackets. The dense-paragraph mandate is the newer authority and wins
  over any stale bracket convention still in a reference file.
- ⛔ **NO REAL ARTIST, GROUP, COMPANY OR PERSON NAME IN ANY FIELD THAT REACHES THE GENERATOR** — not the
  style prompt, not the exclude field, **not the lyrics**. Name the **scene and the technique** (gasba,
  funaná, raï, canntaireachd, Memphis half-time — public idioms). Name the **people** in the dedication.
  *Verify by scanning the three pasted fields against this run's own credited roster, never by a
  proper-noun heuristic — a heuristic over-matches bolded lyric phrases and under-matches the names that
  matter.*
- **Mandatory order:** genre/micro-genre + tempo/energy/opening → vocalist spec with spatial staging →
  instrumentation/palette with physical adjectives → arrangement arc → bold sonic device.
- **Must include:** a vivid **opening moment** (first 5 s), **spatial language**, a **kinetic defect**
  (asymmetric groove), and an explicit **positive** acoustic declaration (conflict 2).
- **Banned openers:** "Begin in/with…", "Use…", "Build the track from…", "Chronology:", "For an adult
  human singer…".

### `## 1B. SUNO EXCLUDE PROMPT`
Separate negative field, **400–900 chars (hard max 1000)**. Concrete blacklist terms and failure classes —
this is where every literal "no X" lives.

### `## 2. LYRICS`
- Opens `[Theme: <scene-pressure / emotional OS>]` then `[SONG FORM: <named form & sequence>]`.
- **Full EMO headers** — `[Section - EMO:<emotion(s)> - <Role> - <cues>]`. The emotion comes from
  `skills/lofn-core/refs/EMOTION_TAXONOMY.md`, **never bare AWE/INDIGNATION**.
- ≥1 standalone `*SFX*` cue. **70–120 sung lines** (target 78–110; ≤72 raises a floor-hug FLAG).
- 🚨 **THE ENTIRE LYRICS FIELD MUST MEASURE < 5000 CHARACTERS (target ≤ 4800).** This is a Suno render
  limit, and the field holds *everything you paste into the lyrics box*: `[Theme]` + `[SONG FORM]` + the
  Disc_Channel block + every header + every SFX cue + every sung line. **Measure it exactly; never
  estimate. State the measured count.** If over: cut/merge sung lines, then tighten headers, then move the
  Disc_Channel block and production metadata to a `## Production Sidecar` *outside* the field. The line
  target yields to this cap — a renderable 64-line song beats an unrenderable 110-line one.

### `## 3. TITLE`

`## 1. MUSIC PROMPT` / `## 1B. SUNO EXCLUDE PROMPT` / `## 2. LYRICS` / `## 3. TITLE`.)*

## 7. THE REPAIR LOOP, THE QUARANTINE, AND THE BREAKER

- **Max 3 attempts** per failing gate, repaired in place.
- **Attempt 2.5 — cognitive grace:** a rescuable near-miss (a 5002-char lyrics field, a 1004-char prompt) is
  normalized once before the breaker fires. Forgive the rescuable, not the broken.
- **No-progress halt:** compare the **specific failed gate's measured value** across attempts, never raw
  bytes. If it does not move, stop early.
- **QUARANTINE terminal (3rd failure):** the pair is marked `quarantined`, its artifact is **NOT consumed
  downstream**, and the coordinator emits **"N of 6 pairs broke open at step X (gate: …)"** to The Scientist
  **before QA**. Never a silent 5-shipped-as-6.
- **REDIRECT:** once the no-progress predicate fires, the repair brief **must** carry a sideways proposal —
  promote a step-05 cut-ledger reserve, re-derive that pair's variation angles, or re-run the skeptic
  transformation for that pair's slice. **The brief proposes; the coordinator decides; The Scientist sees
  it.** A sideways route spawns a NEW pair chain — **the frozen ICB is never edited mid-run.**
- **Run-level circuit breaker:** if **one named gate** fails across many pairs, **STOP and name the gate**
  rather than burning 24 × 3 retries on a broken constraint.
