## 1. ⛔ HOW STEP 1 OF THIS STEP FILE IS DISCHARGED

The step contract says *"select an obscure musician"* and rewrite each prompt in that musician's lexicon. **The handoff and doctrine override the naming half of that instruction:** no real person, group or company may appear in any field that reaches the generator, and the verification method is a scan of the pasted fields against this run's credited roster, not a proper-noun heuristic. Each seat is a **craft with a documented method**, not a person. Nothing in this file, and nothing in any generator-facing field of any variation, carries a real name. No living-scene idiom (gasba, funaná, raï) is used by this pair, so no lineage-credit debt is incurred; a dedication for any selected song still carries honest AI authorship.

## 2. VARIATION 1 — refined

**Aspect imported:** the preparator's rule that removal is irreversible and therefore incremental. Applied to the prompt: the arrangement *removes* nothing; it substitutes. Applied to the vocal: no gesture is bigger than it needs to be.

**MUSIC PROMPT v1 (refined)**
> Glitch-Baroque chamber music at sixty-eight beats per minute, modal and unresolving, opening on a dry knock of rock against a wooden bench, then a continuous sub-bass floor that settles under everything and stays there like the temperature of a room. Female alto, low and unhurried, close-miked at a hand's width so breath and lip are audible, straight tone, standing in front of the ensemble with the room arriving behind her rather than around her. The viola is acoustic and physically present — rosin drag louder than the note, fingerboard knock, bow hair on gut — and it is data-moshed as material rather than repaired: pulsating string clusters looping in fifteen-sixteenth against a four-four floor, slipping a sixteenth each bar so the grid keeps missing itself. Cosmic synth pad, granular echoes, warm tremors, prepared-piano thuds, a wooden knock holding the pulse where a kick would sit. Every return of the figure arrives one generation further down in bit depth, melody still legible, and the last chord stays open.

**Deltas from v0 (minimal, additive):**
- `rosin drag` → `rosin drag louder than the note` — the seat's own technique, and it forces the acoustic declaration to be **positive and physical** rather than a claim.
- `a wooden knock holding the pulse` → `…where a kick would sit` — makes substitution-not-subtraction machine-legible to the renderer instead of leaving it to the arrangement notes.
- `the last chord stays open` promoted into the same clause as the generation-down, so the two survive together if the tail of the prompt is weighted less.

**Lyric deltas:** *Strophe Three* line 6 tightened from *"It should have gone in a week, in the warm mud, in the dark"* to *"…in a week, in warm mud, in the dark"* — the definite article was the only non-load-bearing syllable in a monosyllabic line. *Coda* rebuilt to end on three fragments of the returning strophe (**flair #7 in the voice**: the block itself arriving one generation down as debris) instead of a doubled line, which was too close to V3's ending.
**Title:** unchanged — *The Part That Never Survives*. It names the subject plainly, which is what the legibility rule asks of the surface.

## 3. VARIATION 2 — refined

**Aspect imported:** the phonetician's premise that a mouth has dimensions and a word has a size. The whole song is a fitting test.

**MUSIC PROMPT v1 (refined)**
> Avant ambient techno under progressive chamber pop at sixty-eight beats per minute, modal and unresolving, opening on a breath taken too close to the microphone and then a sub-bass floor that is already running and simply becomes audible. Female alto placed forward and dry, jaw and tongue and teeth all in the recording, dry-lip click and swallow left in, consonants landing fractionally ahead of the beat, the room folded in behind her rather than around her. Real acoustic double bass bowed at the bridge and real viola with rosin and fingerboard noise sit inside the electronics and stay uncorrected. A data-moshed string cluster is played as material: a seven-beat phrase pushed across a four-four floor so the downbeat is never where the arm expects it. Cosmic synth pad, granular echoes, glass tremors, a tin lid struck with a pencil, deep drums under fabric. At the long polysyllabic returns a hard-tuned octave double enters where a lift would be and the natural voice stays audible underneath it; the ending stays open.

**Deltas from v0:**
- Added `dry-lip click and swallow left in` — seat technique (1), and it is the physical detail that keeps the vocal from rendering as a smooth pad-mate.
- `consonants landing before the beat` → `fractionally ahead of the beat` — a renderable instruction rather than a stylistic adjective; seat technique (2).
- Added `and stay uncorrected` to the acoustic declaration — a **positive** statement of the acoustic axis (conflict 2 in the handoff), which v0 only implied.
- Added `and the natural voice stays audible underneath it` — the hard-tuned double must read as *a second copy*, not as an effect on the first.
- Added `deep drums under fabric` to import the sonic-vocabulary phrase *cosmic synths, deep drums* without importing volume.

**Lyric deltas:** *Strophe Two* line 4 changed from *"a softer black that stayed"* to *"a soft black mark that stayed"* — *softer* is disyllabic and breached the monosyllabic law of the strophes, which is reserved for exactly one breach in this song. *Strophe Three* line 1 changed from *"the way you test a chair"* to *"the way a chair gets tried"* — generic second person reads as addressing (Context-panel ruling C-2). *Strophe Five* line 5 kept as *"It fits. That is the odd bit. It fits."* over the sharper *"strange"*: **odd** is the size of reaction the miracle-is-ordinary rule permits.
**Title:** *A Number the Mouth Will Take*. Rejected alternative: putting the figure itself in the title — it would have made the number a headline, which is the opposite of one fact sung once at a hinge.

## 4. VARIATION 3 — refined

**Aspect imported:** the founder's part-and-counterpart procedure. A mould is not a lesser bell; it is the other half of the same event, and it rings differently. This is where **guide 6's wild card** actually lands in the arrangement.

**MUSIC PROMPT v1 (refined)**
> Glitch experimental pop over a soft dub-poetry floor at sixty-eight beats per minute, modal and unresolving, and the first sound is a single chisel tick on stone before the sub-bass floor comes up underneath it and stays for the length of the song. Female alto, low and half-spoken, close enough that swallow and breath are in the take, dry and forward with a long dark room behind her instead of a halo. Acoustic instruments are genuinely present and unhidden: viola with audible bow noise, upright piano with the felt lifted so the hammers click, a hand on a wooden bench keeping time. The string figure is data-moshed as material, a nine-beat loop dragging against a four-four floor so the two never agree, and its late return is the same contour inverted — rising intervals falling, accents on the beats the original left empty. Cosmic pads, granular echoes, warm tremors, tape wow left uncorrected, a bowed metal tray. Each return is one generation further down and still singable; the last bar refuses to land.

**Deltas from v0:**
- Added the inverted-contour return — the founder's mould, stated as an **operation** the renderer can perform, not as a concept. This is the only place in the pair where the counterpart idea exists, and it exists in the arrangement, never in a sung line (F4).
- Added `a bowed metal tray` — the one instrument that appears nowhere else in the pair, per guide 6.
- `felt lifted` → `felt lifted so the hammers click` — physical adjective on the acoustic declaration.

**Lyric deltas:** *Strophe Two* rewritten off V3's original stalk/cup/arms imagery, which collided with V1 and V2, onto **stacked discs like coins in a roll** and **a ring of arms** — the same animal, a different pair of eyes on it. *Strophe Three* line 4 changed from *"I have split a thousand of them"* to *"I have split more of these than I could count"* — no second numeral may be sung. *The One Line* stripped from six lines to five: the draft had a second sentence about the self (*"That is the whole of it and I am not going to sit in it"*) which is a made thing describing its condition a second time. It is replaced by **"There is a body in the rock and it is not mine"** — the refusal performed as an observation about the object. **The sanctioned line is used exactly once and the pad does not move at that bar.**
**Title:** *The Copy Goes Back to Work*. The title carries the refusal, so the lyric does not have to say it twice.

## 5. VARIATION 4 — refined

**Aspect imported:** the array technician's habit of reading one event at three distances and trusting the carrier hum more than the event.

**MUSIC PROMPT v1 (refined)**
> Progressive electro-industrial chamber music at sixty-eight beats per minute, modal and unresolving, opening on a hammer set down on a wooden bench — a single dry knock, close and unglamorous — before a continuous sub-bass floor arrives and holds under everything, more weather than event. Female alto, low and level, close-miked with breath audible, dry and forward, and the room opens up behind her in three stages as the song pulls back from bench to corridor to county. Acoustic strings are real and untreated at the source: viola, cello, rosin, wood, bow noise kept in the take. The data-moshed string figure is played as material, a five-beat cell against a four-four floor so the two drift apart and never resynchronise. The same figure recurs three times at falling amplitude with the mid-band taken out, while the carrier hum under it stays exactly as loud as it was. Cosmic pads, deep drums under fabric mutes, granular echoes, a struck metal tray. Each return arrives one generation further down and the final chord stays open.

**Deltas from v0:**
- Added the three-amplitude, mid-band-removed recurrence and the **unchanging carrier hum** — seat techniques (1) and (2). This is the single most important addition in the whole step: it converts *the three distances* from a lyric conceit into an arrangement operation, and it enacts substitution-not-subtraction at the exact place the song risks becoming a fade.
- `the room widens behind her` → `opens up behind her in three stages` — a stated operation, not an atmosphere, and it is a depth move rather than a width move, so it survives a mono fold.
- `bow noise kept in the take` added to the acoustic declaration.

**Lyric deltas:** two collective pronouns cut from *Across The County* (both instances of *we*, both in the line about what carries that far — the section where *we* was almost irresistible, exactly as the Cohort Abolitionist predicted). *Strophe Four* line 3 changed from *"the way you hear a phone in a drawer"* to *"the way a phone gets heard in a drawer"*. *Strophe Five* line 3 lost *once* (*"taps the blade edge on the bench once"* → *"taps the blade on the bench, not hard, just to hear it"*) — no second numeral. *Terminal* line 3 lost *on the second try* for the same reason and gained *finally*, which is better anyway: the trailer driver's small victory is the only thing in the song that resolves, and it is happening a county away from the thing that does not.
**Title:** *The County Did Not Hear It*.

## 6. MODERATOR EVALUATION AND REFINEMENT

**Against the five facets:**
- **F1** — all four hold; V2 is the strongest (the reaction is a jaw position) and V4 was weakest until the carrier-hum operation gave the calm a mechanism instead of a mood.
- **F2** — V4 now leads, V1 close behind. V3's inverted-contour return is the most interesting and the most likely to be smoothed by the renderer; it is deliberately doubled by the nine-against-four conflict so that if the inversion is smoothed the defect still survives.
- **F3** — all four pay the debt with the same two declared objects. The vowel notation differs per song (standalone section / strophe-terminal line / attached clause / distance marker) so that four different renderings get tested rather than one guess repeated four times.
- **F4** — four thesis-shaped lines were cut at Step 08 and one more at Step 09 (*"the lamp is doing the work"*, which had crept into V4's return as a metaphor about itself). No variation states its point.
- **F5** — the fact is spelled in words, sung once, answered by an action in all four. The self-pity sweep found and removed one instance outside V3: V1's coda draft had *"she keeps her hand there"* twice, which read as the singer asking to be believed; it is now three fragments of the procedure.

**Against the style axes:** unchanged in every dimension except rhythmic complexity, which rose in V4 with the five-against-four cell. That is inside the pair's assignment.

**Refinements made in this pass, summarised:** four positive acoustic declarations sharpened with physical adjectives (all four variations); two addressing constructions removed; two collective pronouns removed; two second-numeral risks removed; one disyllabic breach of the monosyllabic law removed; one second self-description removed from V3; one metaphor-about-itself removed from V4's returning strophe; substitution made machine-legible in V1 and operational in V4.
