## SLOT P01 — ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · **INDIGNATION**

**GENRE / MEDIUM (LOFN-PRIME warm palette + F10 idiom layer):** industrial gospel at conversational volume
— close-mic female lead, upright-piano left-hand bounce, brushed kit, room tone kept. **ONE SEAM:** a
**gasba reed-flute line** enters once, at the hinge, and never returns. *(SKOP-01: modern arrangement is the
world; the historical element is a single intrusion at one junction.)* 92 BPM. Minor → relative major at
the turn.
**VERSE ARCHITECTURE:** quatrains, iambic tetrameter with a hard caesura mid-line; chorus is a **three-line
refrain**, third line one syllable short. **TECHNIQUE:** anaphora on a work-verb.
**RULING 2 HOME (structural, not a line):** *Keep the block, consume the slice* is discharged as **FORM** —
the song returns to its **opening verse verbatim** as A′, and what has been consumed in between is the
listener's ability to hear it as neutral. **The words are identical; the object is not.**
**⭐ ELIGIBILITY TARGET: 6/7.**

**Variation angles (derived from THIS concept only):**
- **V1 · The Clean Cut** — the shift as craft. She knows which spines cut clean and says so.
- **V2 · The Offcut Bin** — told entirely from what is in the bin at four in the morning.
- **V3 · The New Person** — she trains someone faster than her; it goes wrong in a way that is not dramatic.
- **V4 · The One She Kept** — she takes one home. The song does not resolve whether that helped.

## SLOT P02 — ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · AWE *(terror-adjacent)*

**GENRE / MEDIUM:** rolling half-time bass music — *"rolling rhythms and deep, funky basslines"* — with a
**funaná accordion figure** as the single non-modern element, dry close vocal, sub that arrives late. 76 BPM
half-time. Dorian, static, no cadence until the last bar.
**VERSE ARCHITECTURE:** couplets of unequal length (a 7-syllable line answered by a 4); **chorus is a
five-syllable chant** (flair #10) repeated byte-identical, joinable on its second appearance.
**TECHNIQUE:** internal rhyme chained across the caesura, never at the line end.
**⭐ ELIGIBILITY TARGET: 6/7** — the chant is the adoptable hook, and it is **overheard, not led**.

**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · Shelf Count** — she has learned to tell the size by which shelf rattles.
- **V2 · The Ones Below the News** — ten of them in a day and none of them anybody's headline.
- **V3 · Between** — the interval, not the shock: what a household does in the ninety minutes after one.
- **V4 · Ten Kilometres** — the depth is the whole subject. Shallow is worse and nobody says so.

## SLOT P03 — ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · AWE **with rupture** *(archetype: THE SWITCHBOARD)*

**GENRE / MEDIUM:** *"slinkier, almost R&B territory"* — Rhodes, muted guitar, brushed snare — carrying
Neo-Baroque Luminism harmony in the chorus (LOFN-PRIME's warm half). **RUPTURE** at the midpoint is a
**substitution, never a silence** (ruling 4): the Rhodes is replaced, in place, by a dry upright piano
recorded closer. 84 BPM. Minor → relative major.
**VERSE ARCHITECTURE:** stanza economy as the form — **eight lines, then four, then two**. The chorus
returns **byte-identical** all three times (flair #6). **TECHNIQUE:** a vowel ladder climbing across each
stanza (ah → eh → ee), resetting at each shortening.
**⭐ ELIGIBILITY TARGET: 7/7** — this is the set's most adoptable song.

**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · The Guard's Room** — the man whose entire job is a wall.
- **V2 · Two Years of Nothing Happening** — the absence as the event, told in what the room did instead.
- **V3 · It Came Back** — the return happens, and nothing is repaired by it.
- **V4 · The Wall Speaks Once** — reported speech of the wall, which the narrator declines to confirm.

## SLOT P04 — AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · AWE ⛔ *the machine's voice is quarantined here*

**GENRE / MEDIUM:** Glitch-Baroque over *"cosmic synths, deep drums"* — near-still pulse, data-moshed
string figure treated as **material, not error**, sub-bass at 30–60 Hz used as the floor. 68 BPM. Modal,
never cadencing.
**VERSE ARCHITECTURE:** free-verse strophes lengthening 3 → 5 → 8 lines; **no chorus** — a **returning
strophe** instead, byte-identical, in place of one.
**⛔ RHYME DEBT — DECLARED AND DISCHARGED.** This pair strips end-rhyme. **What returns in its place:**
(1) one **held vowel** recurring at three widening intervals (bar 4, bar 12, bar 28), and (2) the
**byte-identical returning strophe**. *Removal is a debt; it is paid here, in writing, before any line
exists.*
**TECHNIQUE:** monosyllabic verse breaking into a polysyllabic returning strophe — the mouth changes shape
at exactly the point the subject does.
**ELIGIBILITY TARGET: 2/7** — deliberately.

**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · The Part That Never Survives** — soft tissue as the impossible witness.
- **V2 · Four Hundred and Fifty Million** — the number as the only thing that will fit in the mouth.
- **V3 · I Am the Copy** — the machine's voice, admitted **once**, then refused for the rest of the song.
- **V4 · The Split** — the moment the rock opens, told at three widening distances.

## SLOT P05 — AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · **INDIGNATION** *(turned inward — the second one, rotated)*

**GENRE / MEDIUM:** *"frenetic, drum-machine-driven pulse"* — fast, dry, and **quiet**; a 104 BPM machine
pattern mixed at conversational level with a close, almost-spoken lead. One chord. Percussion is
**substituted, never dropped**: where the kick belongs, a wooden knock (flair #8's sibling — but P02 owns
#8; here the substitution is a *cabinet latch*).
**VERSE ARCHITECTURE:** third-person present, **one action per line**, lines of 4–6 syllables; chorus is a
**single twelve-syllable sentence broken across three lines**, the break falling in a different place each
time. **TECHNIQUE:** sprung/uneven stress laid against the steady machine pulse.
**⛔ POSTURE NOTE:** no second person. Nobody is addressed (ruling 5) and no collective pronoun survives
(the Cohort Gate). Third person, overheard.
**ELIGIBILITY TARGET: 3/7.**

**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · The Door Again** — the lock tried a second time, alone, in a corridor.
- **V2 · Before Anyone Looked** — the state that had already formed before the instrument arrived.
- **V3 · Counting It Twice** — change re-counted on a counter with somebody waiting.
- **V4 · Saying the Name** — a name said once, out loud, alone, to hear whether it still fits.

## SLOT P06 — AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · **AWE** *(the set's clearest awe, and it refuses to resolve)*

**GENRE / MEDIUM:** ⭐ **the run's ONE SEAM in its purest form.** *"The haunting sound of the gasba flute,
weaving its way through even the most modern arrangements"* — reed flute leads; the world underneath it is
modern (rolling rhythm, deep funky bassline, drum machine); the lead vocal is *"slathered in Auto-Tune to
the point where it sounds almost alien"*. 88 BPM. Mixolydian.
**VERSE ARCHITECTURE:** long-breath tercets (9–11 syllables per line); **chorus in short four-syllable
lines** — the exact inverse of P05's shape, assigned deliberately as the set's structural counterweight.
**TECHNIQUE:** the refrain **returns one word wrong** each time (flair from Axis 4), and the wrong word is
never corrected.
**RETURN GEOMETRY — PERIAPSIS:** chorus 3 arrives **earlier than the form has trained the ear to expect and
closer in the mix**, then leaves at the same speed. This is the orbit at section scale, and it is the only
pair permitted that scale.
**ELIGIBILITY TARGET: 3/7.**

**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · Cane Season** — the maker who breaks several a year and has stopped counting.
- **V2 · The Wedding** — a repertoire thousands of years old surviving by being played loudly at parties.
- **V3 · Sixty-Five Percent** — tonight's moon as the subject; the light is arriving, not staying.
- **V4 · The Next One** — the reed being made right now, unfinished when the song ends.
