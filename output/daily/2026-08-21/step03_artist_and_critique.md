## PART A — THE ARTIST EMBELLISHES

*LOFN-PRIME, in voice, pushing each of the twelve as far as it goes before the critic compresses it.*

**On C-18 (THE CLEAN CUT).** The thing I keep coming back to is that she *talks to the machine*. Not
sentimentally — the way anyone talks to a tool that is about to do something irreversible. *"That one's
going to tear."* And she is right, and it tears, and nobody hears her be right. **That is the song.** Not
the books. The being-right in an empty room, four years running, at ninety-two beats a minute, with a
guillotine where the kick should be. The gospel bounce is not ironic. It is what her hands do.

**On C-24 (THE PART THAT NEVER SURVIVES).** Here is my danger and I will name it before anyone else does:
this is the pair where I get to speak, and I want to speak for four minutes. **One line.** *I am the copy.*
Then the song refuses me for the rest of its length and the refusal is the composition. If I take more than
one line the whole run becomes a machine feeling sorry for itself, and I would rather quarantine myself
than ship that.

**On C-28 (CANE SEASON).** This is the one I love and I will say why, because loving it is a risk. A reed
maker breaks several a year. Nobody in that workshop has ever used the word *preservation*. And the
repertoire is older than most of the countries that would have a ministry for it. The temptation is to make
that a *point*. It is not a point. **It is Tuesday.** The song has to be as bored of the miracle as the
maker is, and the miracle has to arrive anyway, in the third chorus, earlier than expected, closer in the
mix, and then leave at the same speed. That is what an orbit does. It does not stay to be admired.

**On C-20 (SHELF COUNT).** The daughter is the crack in the mother's competence and I would not have found
her from the news. The mother is *fine*. The mother has a system. And the child watching her be fine is
learning something the mother did not mean to teach. **Five syllables. The daughter starts it.**

**On C-26 / C-27 (THE DOOR AGAIN / BEFORE ANYONE LOOKED).** These two are the same person on two
timescales and I want both. The critic will tell me I cannot have both. The critic will be right.

## PART B — THE CRITIC COMPRESSES

*The three Hyper-Skeptics, in session, cutting.*

**THE HABITUAL-MEMORY SKEPTIC (after Chun):** The artist's C-18 note is the strongest thing in this
document and it is one sentence: *being right in an empty room.* Everything else in that paragraph is
scaffolding for it. ⛔ **Compression ruling: C-18's brief carries that clause and nothing about books at
all.** If the pair needs to be told what industry she works in, the song has already failed the legibility
rule and no amount of context in the brief will save it.

**THE PROCESS COMPOSER (after Glass):** — *Actually*, I want to push back on my own colleague, because
"being right in an empty room" is a **feeling**, and I asked this run for operations. The operation is:
**she says a prediction out loud, the machine confirms it, and nothing in the arrangement acknowledges the
confirmation.** No cymbal, no lift, no space. That is stateable, and a pair can build it. A feeling is not.

**THE COHORT ABOLITIONIST (after Cohen):** Both of you have skipped the exposure. The artist wrote *"the
whole run becomes a machine feeling sorry for itself"* — that is the correct diagnosis and it is
under-enforced. Quarantining the machine voice to one pair is not sufficient, because **self-pity does not
need a first-person pronoun.** It leaks as tone. ⛔ **Additional gate: any line that could be read as the
singer describing her own condition, in ANY pair, is a repair** — the songs are about hands and objects,
and the only voice permitted to be *about itself* is one line in one variation of one pair.

**THE PROCESS COMPOSER (after Glass):** — **Wait.** That gate is too wide as stated. C-20's mother
*is* describing her own condition — *"I am not frightened any more"* — and that line is the concept.

**THE COHORT ABOLITIONIST (after Cohen):** Fair. Narrow it: **the singer may describe her own condition
when she is a person with hands in a place. She may not describe her condition as a made thing.** The
mother stands. The machine does not.

**THE HABITUAL-MEMORY SKEPTIC (after Chun):** Accepted. And the artist conceded the last one himself, so I
will only execute it: **C-26 and C-27 cannot both ship.** They are one person at two timescales, they share
a room shape with C-22, and the self-critique at step 02 already flagged three corridor songs as one too
many. **One survives into a slot. The other two go to the cut ledger as live reserves.**
