## 1. SECTION ARCHITECTURE *(this pair's alone)*
`Intro (bass absent)` → `Verse 1` → `Chant` → `Verse 2` → `Chant` → `Bridge (the daughter)` → `Verse 3` → `Chant ×2`.
Verses are **couplets of unequal length**: a 7-syllable line answered by a 4-syllable line, every time,
never varied. The chant is **five syllables, byte-identical on every appearance.**

## 2. RHYME / LINE LOGIC
⭐ **INTERNAL rhyme chained across the caesura, never at the line end.** The 7-syllable line rhymes
*inside itself* and hands the sound to the 4-syllable answer. End-rhyme is incidental and unplanned.
**Rhyme debt: NOT owed** — internal rhyme is a positive device, not a removal, and the byte-identical chant
is the return.

## 3. HOOK GRAMMAR
**Five syllables. The daughter starts it, not the mother.** It must be joinable by a stranger on its
second occurrence. ⛔ It is **overheard** — a child singing in another room, not a call to the listener.
No collective pronoun anywhere in it.

## 4. THE SONIC DEVICE *(stated as an operation)*
**A bar-level figure returns ONE STEP WRONG every cycle and is never corrected.** State it as the
operation: the accordion's four-note figure moves one scale degree on each repeat and no other element
adjusts to it. Second operation: **where the backbeat was, a plate stack settles** — substitution, never a
hole. The deep bass **enters late and never on the first repeat**.

## 5. RETURN GEOMETRY — the orbit at **BAR scale**
The one-step-wrong figure at bar level. No other pair may use bar scale.

## 6. WHERE THE BODY STANDS / WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE
**Standing:** in a kitchen in a town under a swarm, one hand flat on a shelf edge, listening. **What
could hurt her:** ⭐ **the floor is a lid.** Ten kilometres is *nearer* than ninety-nine and nearness is the
threat — the shallow ones are worse and nobody reports them. The second hazard is the one the mother cannot
see: her daughter is learning her composure. ⛔ *Legibility:* **"a mother counts earthquakes by which shelf
rattles."**

## 7. THE FOUR VARIATION ANGLES *(derived from THIS concept only)*
- **V1 · Shelf Count** — she has learned to size a shock by which shelf goes first.
- **V2 · The Ones Below the News** — ten in a day and not one of them anybody's headline.
- **V3 · Between** — the ninety minutes after, and whether the plates go back.
- **V4 · Ten Kilometres** — the depth is the whole subject; shallow is worse and nobody says so.

## 8. ⛔ THIS PAIR'S BANS

charge, invent the people (`HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD`). ⛔ No news-reader register. ⛔ No second number sung.
⛔ No "we". ⛔ No line about a light that is leaving. ⛔ `snarl` / `phonk` may not appear.
