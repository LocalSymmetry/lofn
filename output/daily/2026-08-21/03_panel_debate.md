---

**POOR-IMAGE POLITICIAN (after Steyerl):** Working from the poor-image argument — I want to open by refusing the run's premise. A book that is scanned is not destroyed. It is *released*. The object dies and the file circulates. Six songs mourning a pulp bale is six songs on the wrong side of history.

**ANALOGUE HOLDOUT (after Dean):** Dean's published position is the opposite and I will hold it. The claim "the file is the book" is a claim that the *carrier* is neutral. It is not. A scan has no gutter shadow, no foxing, no previous reader's pencil, no paper weight in the hand. You have not released it; you have made a *description* of it and thrown away the thing described.

**HABITUAL-MEMORY SKEPTIC (after Chun):** Both of you are doing mood. **State the mechanism.** Chun's published argument is that digital memory is not storage, it is *regeneration* — the file is not sitting somewhere being permanent, it is being rewritten constantly, and it survives only as long as somebody keeps paying to rewrite it. So the honest sentence is not "the book was destroyed and the file lives." It is: **"the book was destroyed and replaced with something that must be continuously re-made by an institution that has no obligation to continue."** If a song wants a fear, that is the real one, and neither of you named it.

**GLITCH CODIFIER (after Menkman):** — Wait. That reframes my whole contribution. I came here to offer compression artifacts as texture. But if the mechanism is *regeneration under maintenance*, then the interesting artifact is not the JPEG block. It is **the identical copy that is nonetheless a different object because it was re-made this morning.** That has a musical form: a refrain that returns byte-identical and is *not* the same refrain. Sonic difference: zero. Semantic difference: total.

**DISINTEGRATION COMPOSER (after Basinski):** Then be careful with me. The Disintegration Loops work because the tape *actually* shed. Simulated decay is a costume. If a song here wants disintegration, the loss has to be structural — something the song genuinely does not get back — not a filter on the master.

**AMBIGUOUS PHOTOGRAPH (after Sebald):** And the narrator must not be certain. Sebald's method is a photograph with no caption beside prose that will not confirm it. The failure mode of this run is a narrator who has already decided that the cutting was wrong and spends four minutes being right about it.

**POOR-IMAGE POLITICIAN (after Steyerl):** *Actually* — I concede the maintenance point, and it sharpens my objection rather than killing it. My objection is now: **do not seat the songs with the collectors.** The people harmed by the pulping of a rare edition are, overwhelmingly, institutions and dealers. The people served by circulation are everyone without a library card. If the run forgets that, it is writing property law with a chorus.

**HABITUAL-MEMORY SKEPTIC (after Chun):** That I will sign. **Ruling I want on the record: no song in this set may be about the value of a rare object.** The subject is the *procedure*, and the procedure's victim is not the book.

# ═══ CONFIGURATION 3 — SKEPTIC TRANSFORM: **BRIDGE** ═══

*Chosen by **THE HABITUAL-MEMORY SKEPTIC** (after Chun), who stated the selection criterion:*
> *"Every room so far has argued about whether destroying a thing to read it is bad. That is the wrong
> question and it is wrong because our domain is provincial. **There are whole disciplines where destroying
> the object to read it is not a scandal — it is the method**, with two centuries of ethics attached.
> Seat those people. They have already had this argument and they wrote it down."*

**BRIDGE** = for each domain, find a completely different domain facing the analogous problem, and seat
someone who has solved it there.

**Analogous problem:** *the object must be consumed, sectioned, cored, or killed in order to be read.*

**G1. THE SECTIONING PATHOLOGIST** — *role-anchored: diagnostic histopathology.* GROUNDING: the specimen is fixed, embedded, and **sliced into micrometre ribbons** to be readable at all; the block is retained, the slice is consumed. TEMPERAMENT: allergic to squeamishness about method; insists on the retention protocol.
**G2. THE DENDROCHRONOLOGIST** — *role-anchored: tree-ring dating.* GROUNDING: an increment borer drills a **core out of a living tree** to read its years; the tree survives the wound; the core does not go back. TEMPERAMENT: praises a wound that is survivable and asks whether this one is.
**G3. THE SEED-VAULT KEEPER** — *role-anchored: crop-diversity genebanking.* GROUNDING: a stored seed is only alive while it is **periodically grown out and re-collected** — viability is maintained by planting, not by cold. TEMPERAMENT: allergic to storage described as safety.
