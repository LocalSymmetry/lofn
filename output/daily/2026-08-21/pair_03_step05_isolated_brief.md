## 1. SECTION ARCHITECTURE *(this pair's alone)*
⭐ **STANZA ECONOMY IS THE FORM: eight lines, then four, then two.**
`Verse 1 (8 lines)` → `Chorus` → `Verse 2 (4 lines)` → `Chorus` → `RUPTURE` → `Verse 3 (2 lines)` → `Chorus` → `Synthesis tag`.
The **SWITCHBOARD** discharge: Section A is AWE, the RUPTURE is a **substitution**, Section B is the same
room in INDIGNATION, and the tag is a synthesis that **includes the damage** — beauty that neither mode
reaches alone. ⭐ **This pair targets 7/7 eligibility: it is the most adoptable song in the set.**

## 2. RHYME / LINE LOGIC
**A vowel ladder climbing across each stanza** — ah → eh → ee in the stressed positions — **resetting
at each shortening**, so the 8-line stanza climbs furthest and the 2-line stanza barely starts. End-rhyme
is loose and secondary; the ladder is the spine. **Rhyme debt: NOT owed** — the ladder plus the
byte-identical chorus are the declared returns.

## 3. HOOK GRAMMAR
A chorus **addressed to nobody**, sung *about* a wall, that a listener can adopt without knowing what
it refers to. ⛔ **It is NOT addressed to the absent object** (L18 rotation) — the thing is established as
unable to receive it before the chorus first lands. **Byte-identical all three times. Say nothing about it.**

## 4. THE SONIC DEVICE *(stated as an operation)*
⭐ **THE RUPTURE IS A SUBSTITUTION, NOT A SILENCE.** At the midpoint the Rhodes is **replaced, in its
exact place, by a dry upright piano recorded closer** — same figure, same bar, different instrument, no
gap, no filter sweep, no drop. Second operation: the **plate reverb describes a room larger than the
arrangement justifies**, so the space is always slightly wrong for the size of the sound.

## 5. RETURN GEOMETRY — the orbit at **REFRAIN scale**
The byte-identical chorus. No other pair may use refrain scale.

## 6. WHERE THE BODY STANDS / WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE
**Standing:** on a folding chair in a gallery room, facing a wall with a lighter rectangle on it, rope
stanchions slack. **What could hurt him:** ⭐ **it survived and nobody is coming for it** — the thing comes
back after two years and **nothing is repaired by that.** He still comes; the room is still a room; the
two years happened. ⛔ *Legibility:* **"a guard keeps working the room after the painting is stolen — and
after it comes back."**

## 7. THE FOUR VARIATION ANGLES *(derived from THIS concept only)*
- **V1 · The Guard's Room** — the man whose entire job is a wall.
- **V2 · Two Years of Nothing Happening** — the absence as the event, told in what the room did instead.
- **V3 · It Came Back** — the return happens and nothing is repaired by it.
- **V4 · The Wall Speaks Once** — reported speech of the wall, which the narrator declines to confirm.

## 8. ⛔ THIS PAIR'S BANS
⛔ Do not name the painting, the museum, the city or the thief. The **charge** of the moment, invented
people. ⛔ No art-history register, no "masterpiece", no valuation — **this is not a song about a rare
object's worth** (Panel-A ruling). ⛔ No thesis in the last verse. ⛔ No "we". ⛔ No line about a light that
is leaving. ⛔ `snarl` / `phonk` may not appear.
