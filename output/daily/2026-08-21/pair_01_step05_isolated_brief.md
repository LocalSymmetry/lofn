## 1. SECTION ARCHITECTURE *(this pair's alone)*
**A → B → A′, and A′ is A word for word.**
`Verse 1 (A)` → `Chorus` → `Verse 2` → `Chorus` → `Turn (the hinge — the fact lands here)` → `Verse 3 (A′ = Verse 1, byte-identical)` → `Chorus` → short terminal tag.
⭐ **RULING 2's STRUCTURAL HOME — this is the whole pair.** *Keep the block, consume the slice* is discharged
as FORM: the song **returns to its opening verse verbatim**, and what has been consumed in between is the
listener's ability to hear it as neutral. **The words are identical. The object is not.** Nothing in the
lyric may point at this. If a line says "the same words mean something else now," the device is dead.

## 2. RHYME / LINE LOGIC
Quatrains, **iambic tetrameter with a hard caesura mid-line**. Strict ABAB end-rhyme in the verses —
this pair does **not** strip rhyme, so no rhyme debt is owed. Chorus is a **three-line refrain whose third
line is one syllable short**, and that shortness is the only irregularity permitted in the whole song.

## 3. HOOK GRAMMAR
A **prediction said out loud to nobody.** The hook is a work-sentence, not a prayer, and it is
⛔ **addressed FROM the witness, never TO the object** (the L18 rotation). Overheard, never led — nobody in
this song is being spoken to. **Delete every collective pronoun.**

## 4. THE SONIC DEVICE *(stated as an operation)*
**A paper-guillotine thump REPLACES the kick in exactly its grid position** — substitution, never a
gap. Second operation, and it is the one that carries the meaning: **the arrangement never acknowledges the
vocal's confirmed prediction.** She says the spine will tear; it tears; there is **no fill, no cymbal, no
lift, no space** at that moment. The absence of acknowledgement is scored, not felt.
**Positive acoustic declaration** in the style field (real upright, brushed kit, room tone kept); every
literal "no X" lives in the EXCLUDE field.

## 5. RETURN GEOMETRY — the orbit at **WHOLE-SONG scale**
Verse 3 is Verse 1, byte-identical. No other pair may use whole-song scale.

## 6. WHERE THE BODY STANDS / WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE
**Standing:** at a guillotine on a night shift, hands on the clamp, fluorescent overhead, a pallet at
her hip. **What could hurt her:** the blade, obviously — but the real hazard is that **correct procedure IS
the danger**: she is four years good at this and she has stopped reading the covers, and she remembers
stopping. ⛔ *Legibility:* a stranger must be able to say **"a woman cuts the spines off books on a night
shift"** after one listen. Say plainly what happens. Do **not** say what it means.

## 7. THE FOUR VARIATION ANGLES *(derived from THIS concept only)*
- **V1 · The Clean Cut** — the shift as craft; she names which spines cut clean and which will tear.
- **V2 · The Offcut Bin** — the same shift, but every image comes from the bin at four in the morning.
- **V3 · The New Person** — she trains someone faster than her; it goes wrong in a way that is not dramatic.
- **V4 · The One She Kept** — she takes one home. The song does not resolve whether that helped.
