## STEP 1 · THE ESSENCE, IN ONE SENTENCE
**A woman with one hand flat on a shelf edge has learned to read the ground by which jar answers first, and
the reading has gone so quiet in her that her daughter has learned it too, by heart, in the next room.**

**Two ways the medium carries it:** (1) **stasis with a defect** — one Dorian chord that never cadences,
under a four-note squeezebox figure that moves a scale degree wrong every cycle and is never corrected, so
the listener's ear does the flinching the narrator will not do; (2) **substitution at the backbeat** — a
dry wooden plate stack lands a sixteenth late exactly where the two and the four should be, so the groove
limps forward instead of opening a hole that the renderer would smooth shut.

figure is smoothed into a charming passing tone and nothing in the mix is wrong. **Repaired once:** the
