## ⭐ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS — ANSWERED CONCRETELY, BEFORE ANY GUIDE

### **1. WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?**

On the fourth-floor corridor of an office-and-laboratory block at twenty past eleven at night, forty
minutes after the last other person left. She is standing, not sitting. Her weight is on her left foot
because the right one is half-turned toward the stairwell she is about to take. In her left hand is a
ring-bound logbook with a soft cover that has curled from being carried under an arm; in her right hand is
a hotel-blue ballpoint that is running out on the downstroke. The book is open on a page ruled into four
columns: **ROOM · TIME · CONDITION · INITIALS.** She is filling the fourth column. Her thumb is flat on the
facing page to keep it from turning. The corridor lights are the kind that stay on all night on the safety
circuit, so nothing dims. Twelve metres away, at the end, is a grey fire door with a metal push plate at
hip height and a cabinet to the left of it whose latch does not sit properly in its keep. **The room she is
in is small enough that her own page-turn comes back off the far wall.**

Across her four songs the body relocates but the posture does not: **the bench room with the printer** (V2),
**the front counter with somebody waiting on the other side of the glass** (V3), **the stairwell landing
with a card reader and a badge on a strap** (V4). Same building, same hour, same hands.

### **2. WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?**

Not the building. Nothing in this room is dangerous. **What could hurt her is that the check will pass and
it will not help.** Three edges, in the order she meets them:

1. **The mark she is adding is scheduled to be removed.** Another process — another pen, another shift, a
   maintained equilibrium of writing-on and taking-off — will lift her initials before morning. She knows
   this. She is not being deceived; she is being *maintained*.
2. **The thing she is verifying was settled before the instrument arrived to measure it.** Her signature
   confirms a state that had already formed. She is not the check. She is the paperwork the check needs.
3. **Nobody asked.** Ruling 1: the offence is **abdication**, not loss. There is no villain in this
   corridor. The round exists because it was inherited, and the person who would have had to decide to stop
   it never opened the book. ⛔ *Held fear (Axis 5, this pair's instance of `correct procedure IS the
   danger`):* **correct procedure is not protection; it is the shape the abdication took.**

⚠️ This is an **AMBITIOUS-arm, INDIGNATION-turned-inward** song at eligibility **3/7**, so the fear is not
resolved and the comfort never arrives. But R-c binds: she may describe her condition **as a person with
hands in a place**, never **as a made thing**. And R-e binds: the astonishment is the listener's job.

## GUIDE 1 — **THE LEVEL HOLDS, AND THE LATCH IS THE CEILING** *(facet 1)*

**Central aspect:** fast and quiet is the whole trick. 104 BPM at conversational volume.
**Storytelling technique A — *litotes of scale*:** the largest event in the song is a piece of hardware
falling into its keep. Let magnitude live in what is *at stake*, never in what is loud.
**Storytelling technique B — *the shift report*:** narrate as a person filing, not as a person feeling.
**Mood & arc:** begins in **Vigilance** (alert, unremarkable, competent), settles into **Composure** worn
as a working skill, cracks briefly into **Frustration** aimed at nobody, arrives at **Disillusionment** at
the hinge, and closes in **Persistence** — which is neither recovery nor defeat, only the next round.
**Instrumentation & texture:** dry drum machine at speaking level; a metal cabinet latch on every downbeat
where a kick would be; ballpoint on paper as the hi-hat layer; a knocked wooden desk edge; one low round
sub that never rises. **One chord. No lift, no build, no drop.**
**Production & effects:** close mic at a hand's width, small live room, the room's air deliberately
audible, master left open. **Level is set by the latch** — everything else lives underneath it. Positive
acoustic declaration is mandatory in the style field; every literal "no" moves to the exclude field.
**Structure & dynamics:** 3:20–3:50. Cold open already running (no intro), three verses, three choruses, a
hinge before the final chorus, an outro that re-enters line one mid-phrase. **What changes across the song
is what occupies the downbeat, never the level.**
**Rhyme scheme:** verses are near-unrhymed and stress-driven (the declared return is the chorus sentence,
so the rhyme debt is **NOT owed**); chorus is one twelve-syllable sentence, verbatim each time, re-broken
across three lines in a different place every occurrence; hinge is free-verse with lengthening lines.
**Poetic devices:** **asyndeton** (actions listed with no connective, one per line) and **consonance** on
the hard stops `k / t / p` so the words themselves click like the latch.
**Hook technique ①:** the chorus sentence delivered dead level, close, no vibrato, the last syllable
released early so the machine finishes the bar alone.
**Hook technique ②:** re-break the sentence so the *same words* land on different beats — the hook becomes
recognisable by rhythm rather than by melody.
**Hook technique ③:** double the chorus consonants with the latch so the mouth and the hardware agree
exactly once per line, then drift.
**Narrative seed:** the round, done correctly, twice.
**Perspective:** third person, present tense, singular. **No second person. No collective pronoun.**
**Emotional tone signal:** the sound of somebody being good at a job that does not need them.

## GUIDE 2 — **THE SUBSTITUTED DOWNBEAT** *(facet 2)*

**Central aspect:** ruling 4 — **substitution, not subtraction.** Absence smooths in the render; a wooden
object survives.
**Storytelling technique A — *metonymy of trade*:** name the tool, not the job. Plate, nib, keep, tray,
strap, tape.
**Storytelling technique B — *the room around the event* (Panel B-5):** describe the surroundings of the
verification and never the thing being verified.
**Mood & arc:** **Composure → Impatience → Self-reproach → Skepticism → Vigilance.** No catharsis is
available and none is faked.
**Instrumentation & texture:** the latch is the anchor; when a layer would drop out, an object takes its
place at the same rate — pen-scratch where the hats were, a page-turn where a snare would be, a struck desk
where a tom would be. ⛔ **Never a hole. Never stereo width as the effect. Never a long interrupting
silence** (terminal silence is permitted and is used once, at the very end).
**Production & effects:** all hand-objects recorded live in the same room as the voice; no object may
sound sampled. The sub is mono and constant. Reverb comes only from the actual room.
**Structure & dynamics:** insert one **Latch Break** with no lead vocal, where the machine hats stand aside
and pen-on-paper takes their exact rate — this is the substitution rule made audible as a section.
**Rhyme scheme:** verse lines **4–6 syllables, one action per line**, deliberately un-end-rhymed;
occasional internal consonance only. Chorus as guide 1. Hinge lines lengthen to 9–14 syllables, then snap
back — the fact arrives as a change of line-length, not of volume.
**Poetic devices:** **epanalepsis** (a line beginning and ending on the same word, so the sentence closes
on itself like a keep) and **polyptoton** (write / written / writes).
**Hook technique ①:** land the chorus's first stressed syllable exactly on the latch, then let the rest
slip early.
**Hook technique ②:** make one chorus occurrence arrive with the pen-layer instead of the hat-layer, same
level, so the return is recognised by *material* rather than by dynamics.
**Hook technique ③:** end the last chorus with the latch alone completing the bar.
**Key sequence:** the bar in which the hat layer is replaced by paper without any change in level — this is
the pair's single most falsifiable production claim.
**Special effects:** micro-edited page-turn transients used as ghost notes (LOFN-PRIME pillar 2, *glitches
done right* — meticulous micro-edits, beauty in the breakage).

## GUIDE 3 — **THE FLAT THAT BECOMES THE KEY** *(facet 3 · Special Flair #11)*

**Central aspect:** **Slightly Under, And Staying** — pitch a fraction flat, uncorrected, until it becomes
the tuning. Motif only; it may never be described in the lyric as a technique.
**Storytelling technique A — *the uncorrected detail*:** whenever the narrator notices an error, she leaves
it. The refusal to fix is the characterisation.
**Storytelling technique B — *free indirect procedure*:** her interior state is legible only through the
order in which she does things.
**Mood & arc:** **Solitude → Self-doubt → Contemplation → Existential Angst (hinge) → Dignity → Reflection.**
Dignity is the correct word and it is not comfort: it is a person declining to perform a correction for an
empty room.
**Instrumentation & texture:** one chord, no lift, so the flatness has nothing to resolve against and
becomes the harmonic fact of the song. A drone or pad may not "swell"; if it moves, it moves in timbre.
**Production & effects:** ⛔ **no pitch correction on the lead, ever** — this lives in the exclude field,
because the style field must state it positively (*"a lead that sits a fraction under and stays there
until the flatness reads as the key"*).
**Structure & dynamics:** the flatness must be established **within the first five seconds** and never
resolved. A song that only goes flat at the end has written an effect, not a condition.
**Rhyme scheme:** free stress-verse in the verses; the chorus sentence returns verbatim. **Rhyme debt: NOT
owed** — the returning sentence is the declared return, and its re-breaking is the variation.
**Poetic devices:** **homoioteleuton** (like-sounding line endings without true rhyme) and **caesura**
placed mid-line so the singer's breath, not the bar, decides where a line halts.
**Hook technique ①:** the chorus sung with the final vowel released a beat early, uncorrected.
**Hook technique ②:** a spoken-adjacent delivery in which one syllable per line is pitched and the rest is
speech — the pitched syllable is always the flat one.
**Hook technique ③:** in one section, replace the sung chorus with the same words half-spoken over
pen-on-paper at the same level.
**Vocal technique:** female lead, mid-thirties, English, unhurried, almost spoken, close-miked at a hand's
width; **audible page-turns and breath are part of the vocal, not bleed.**
**Narrative seed:** a name, a time, a condition, an initial — said out loud to hear whether it still fits.

## GUIDE 4 — **SPRUNG STRESS AGAINST A GRID THAT NEVER YIELDS** *(facet 4 · Axis 4)*

**Central aspect:** the mandated positive sound/return device. Stresses land off the pulse; the pulse never
adjusts. This is a **rate**, not a gesture.
**Storytelling technique A — *the verb list*:** one action per line, 4–6 syllables, present tense, third
person. ⛔ This is the opposite of the banned catalogue — a list of *destroyed things* has no verb; a list
of *actions* is nothing but verbs.
**Storytelling technique B — *the inherited round*:** she never explains why the round exists. Custody
register: **the one who inherited it and never asked.**
**Mood & arc:** **Impatience → Frustration → Embarrassment → Disillusionment → Persistence.** The
indignation is real and it is pointed inward; it never raises its voice and it never finds a culprit.
**Instrumentation & texture:** the machine grid is metronomic and unswung; the *human* layers swing.
Swung Brazilian-funk sixteenths lean against the straight grid so accents keep landing early — the rhythmic
debt is described by **mechanics** (swing ratio, accent placement, detuned cowbell, low round sub), never
by a genre label. ⛔ `snarl` and `phonk` may not appear in any style field.
**Production & effects:** every arrangement entry lands a consistent hair **behind** the vocal — a fixed
small lateness that reads as a room, not as an error.
**Structure & dynamics:** the disagreement must be present in bar one and still present in the last bar.
No section resolves it.
**Rhyme scheme:** verses of 12–14 short lines; the chorus is the only metrical certainty in the song, and
even it moves its break. Stanza lengths vary deliberately between songs — length is not complexity.
**Poetic devices:** **anaphora** used sparingly on the verb position (*She writes… She reads… She lets…*)
and **antanaclasis** on a working word that means two things in one shift (*fast* = quick / *fast* =
secured; *keep* = retain / *keep* = the socket a latch sits in).
**Hook technique ①:** the chorus break-point moves every occurrence, so the ear cannot pre-place it.
**Hook technique ②:** put the strongest consonant of the sentence on the weakest beat.
**Hook technique ③:** let the machine finish alone after the last chorus line, for exactly one bar.
**Rhythm & melody:** melodic range under a fifth; the tune is carried by stress, not by interval.

## GUIDE 5 — **THE FACT IS ANSWERED BY A HAND** *(facet 5 · the hinge · Special Flair #5)*

**Central aspect:** ***thirty femtoseconds*** — **the ONE sung fact**, spelled in words, arriving **once**,
at the emotional hinge, **responded to and never explained.** ⛔ No science-explainer register anywhere.
⛔ No other number is sung in any of this pair's four songs — no digits, no cardinals, no ordinals.
**Placement is load-bearing:** ⛔ the hinge lands **BEFORE the final chorus**, so nothing later in the song
is allowed to be a bigger event. The last chorus is not a climax; it is the round continuing.
**Where the number physically is:** printed on an object already in the scene — a card taped to a
doorframe, a warm sheet from a bench printer, a page carried down from upstairs, a poster lifting in a
stairwell draught. **Flair #5, Findable Not Found:** it is present in the index and absent from every hand.
Somebody printed it. Nobody read it until now, and reading it changes nothing.
**How it is answered:** by a motion the body was going to make anyway — a thumb laid flat on the word, a
sheet smoothed, the handle tried again, her own name said after it. **The response is physical and the
round does not change.** That non-change *is* the event.
**Mood & arc at the hinge:** **Disillusionment + Self-reproach**, held at the same volume as everything
else. ⛔ The line-length lengthens here and the level does not.
**Storytelling technique A — *reported speech of the object*:** the printed thing is quoted; no narrator
confirms it.
**Storytelling technique B — *the unasked question* (G-3)*:* nothing in the song asks what the number is
for, because nobody ever did.
**⛔ FORBIDDEN SECTION — THE CHORUS.** The thesis may not land there. It must be reachable only by having
gone through the song, and it may not appear as a nodding-sized line anywhere (the Portable-Thesis Veto).
**Poetic devices:** **antimetabole** across the write/erase pair (what is added / what comes off) and
**epizeuxis** used exactly once, on a repeated action, to mark the hinge without raising the volume.
**Hook technique ①:** deliver the fact on the same pitch as the surrounding speech, so the ear hears a word
and not a headline.
**Hook technique ②:** let the latch land inside the phrase containing the number.
**Hook technique ③:** follow the fact immediately with the smallest possible action, unaccompanied by
comment.
**Narrative seed:** the check that was already true before the check existed.
**Legibility floor:** a stranger must retell **scene AND subject** after one listen — *"a woman initials a
log and checks a locked door on her way out."* The subject appears **plainly** at least once.

## GUIDE 6 — **WILDCARD · THE CALIBRATION CERTIFICATE** *(cross-disciplinary import)*

**The import:** metrology has a document this song is shaped like. A calibration certificate says an
instrument was correct **on a stated date, against a stated reference**, and begins expiring the moment it
is issued — a claim about a past moment, maintained by a schedule nobody in the room set. **That is the
form of her signature and of the song.**
**Storytelling A — *the document as stanza*:** ROOM · TIME · CONDITION · INITIALS is a four-beat figure;
one verse in one song runs those fields in order without naming them. **Storytelling B — *the expiry
clause*:** the turn is not an event, it is **a date passing inside a sentence.**
**Mood & arc:** **Vigilance → Cognitive Dissonance → Disapproval → Dignity** — a competent person reading a
schedule, ⛔ disapproval and never outrage.
**Instrumentation:** a tick that is *not* a clock — a card reader waking, a detent, a chiptune-thin pulse —
off the grid so it argues with the latch. **N = 1 holds:** one world (the machine room), one intruder (the
hand-objects), one seam (the latch on the downbeat, where the hand and the meter are the same event).
**Production & structure:** certificate flatness as a mix decision — one distance, one level, one plane,
depth from object identity; the outro re-enters line one **mid-phrase** (EDGE scale) and **stops inside a
line**, the pair's one permitted silence.
**Rhyme & devices:** a stanza shortening by a syllable a line to a single stressed word, at most once per
song and never in the chorus; **zeugma** and **asyndeton** across a whole stanza.
**Hook ①:** the condition column's value as the hook's grammatical object. **Hook ②:** twelve syllables
scanning as a form field. **Hook ③:** end mid-word on the machine.
**Deliberate risk (Non-obviousness floor):** a metrology document as song form cannot be locally justified —
kept because it alone turns **a date passing** into a musical event without volume.
