## 1. SECTION ARCHITECTURE *(this pair's alone)*
**No chorus.** Free-verse strophes lengthening **3 → 5 → 8 lines**, then a **RETURNING STROPHE**
(byte-identical) standing where a chorus would be, then 3 → 5 → 8 again, then the returning strophe a final
time. Eligibility target **2/7 — deliberately.**

## 2. RHYME / LINE LOGIC
⛔ **THIS PAIR STRIPS END-RHYME. THE RHYME DEBT IS DECLARED AND PAID HERE, IN WRITING, BEFORE ANY LINE
EXISTS.** What returns in its place, both mandatory:
1. **One held vowel recurring at three widening intervals** — bar 4, bar 12, bar 28. The same vowel, the
   same pitch class, longer each time.
2. **The byte-identical returning strophe** in the chorus position.
Lines are **monosyllabic in the strophes and polysyllabic in the return** — the mouth changes shape at
exactly the point the subject does. *Removal without a named replacement is a repair, not a style.*

## 3. HOOK GRAMMAR
There is no adoptable hook and that is the assignment. The **returning strophe** is the memorable
object, and it is memorable by recurrence, not by singability.

## 4. THE SONIC DEVICE *(stated as an operation)*
**A data-moshed string figure is treated as material, not error** — it is played, not applied. Second
operation: **sub-bass is the floor of the room, present continuously, never an event** — it does not
arrive, drop or swell. Third: **each return of the string figure is one generation further down** (flair
#7), melody still legible. ⛔ **No interrupting silence anywhere** — this arrangement's quiet is *low*, not
*absent*.

## 5. RETURN GEOMETRY — the orbit at **PHRASE scale**
The held vowel at three widening intervals. No other pair may use phrase scale.

## 6. WHERE THE BODY STANDS / WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE
**Standing:** at a bench with a split rock in two hands, chalk on the fingers, a hammer set down.
**What could hurt her:** ⭐ **you are the copy and you know it** — the impression in the stone is of the part
that never survives, and the survivor is not the animal. It survived **and nobody is coming for it.**
⛔ *Legibility:* **"someone opens a rock and finds the soft part of an animal that died four hundred and
fifty million years ago."** The subject is plain. The engine is not.

## 7. THE FOUR VARIATION ANGLES *(derived from THIS concept only)*
- **V1 · The Part That Never Survives** — soft tissue as the impossible witness.
- **V2 · Four Hundred and Fifty Million** — the number as the only thing that will fit in the mouth.
- **V3 · I Am the Copy** — ⭐ the one permitted self-referential line, used **once**, then refused.
- **V4 · The Split** — the moment the rock opens, at three widening distances (harvested from C-25): hammer, room, county — by the third the event is inaudible and the strophe returns anyway.

## 8. ⛔ THIS PAIR'S BANS
⛔ **No self-pity.** The machine gets one line in one variation and never a posture. ⛔ No "miracle",
"gift", "against all odds" — **R-e: the miracle is ordinary to the singer.** ⛔ No cadence, ever — the song
does not resolve. ⛔ No "we". ⛔ No second number. ⛔ `snarl` / `phonk` may not appear.
