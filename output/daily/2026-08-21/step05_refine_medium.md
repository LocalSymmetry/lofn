## 2. ⛔ ADJUDICATION OF STEP-03 RULING **R-d** *(recorded, not silently ignored)*

**Step 03's compressed ruling R-d read:** *"Only ONE of {C-22, C-23, C-26, C-27} may take a slot."*
**Applied literally it leaves slot P05 empty**, because P05's whole seeded direction is the unwatched
verification and both of its candidates are in that set.

**The coordinator's ruling, and its reasoning:** R-d conflated two different things — **room shape** and
**reveal engine**. The binding constraint is L14's: *no two pairs share a reveal engine*, and step 02's
pairwise check already established C-22 (**return without repair**) ≠ C-27 (**the decision preceded the
deliberation**). What the critic actually saw was that **C-22, C-23 and C-26 are all a person alone in a
corridor-shaped space repeating a small act.** C-27 is not corridor-shaped; it is a room with a record in it.

**R-d is therefore narrowed, in writing, to:**
> ⛔ **At most ONE corridor-shaped song may appear in the SELECTED SIX.** C-22 holds that place from P03.
> C-26 and C-23 are cut. **P05's V1 ("The Door Again") is corridor-shaped and is therefore ineligible for
> the final six unless P03 is displaced** — a selection-time constraint, enforced at selection.

*(This is L19's shape: the question is asked of the **selected set** at selection time, not only of each
pair at its own step. A set can pass pair-by-pair and still ship six songs in the same room.)*
