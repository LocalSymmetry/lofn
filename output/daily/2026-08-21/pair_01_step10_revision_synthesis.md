# ═══ DEVIATION CLAUSE ═══

**Where I expect the generator to disobey:** the **byte-identical third verse.** Suno's strong habit on a
repeated block is to vary the melody or the delivery the second time through — a lift, an octave, an ad-lib,
a doubled line — precisely because a literal repeat reads as an error to it.

**Which disobedience I would keep:** if it returns the third verse with the *same* words and a **plainer,
more tired delivery** than the first — flatter, later, less breath — keep it. That is the device working
better than specified: the words are identical, the object is not, and the generator will have found the
difference in the body instead of the harmony. **Which I would reject:** any melodic embellishment, added
harmony, or dynamic lift on the return; that converts a retained block into a reprise, and a reprise is the
opposite song.
