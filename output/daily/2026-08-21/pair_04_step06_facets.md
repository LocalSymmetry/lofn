## 2. THE FIVE FACETS

### F1 · ORDINARY HANDS, TERRIBLE OBJECT
Does a body stand in one nameable place doing small correct work — chalk in the seam of the palm, a lamp lowered until the light runs flat, a thumb laid on a print — while the thing under the hands is the impossible one? The facet fails the moment the singer supplies the astonishment: the register must stay at the temperature of a shift, and the terror must arrive through **what is factual** (the corridor lighting is on a timer; nobody is arriving), never through what is exclaimed.

### F2 · THE FLOOR IS PRESENT, THE FIGURE IS AN EVENT
Does the sub-bass read as the **room's floor** — continuous, unarriving, never a drop — while the data-moshed string figure reads as **played material** rather than an applied artefact? The facet fails if the quiet is *absent* instead of *low*, if any moment depends on stereo width, or if the arrangement can be described as "atmospheric" without loss. Each return of the figure must be audibly **one generation further down** with the melody still legible: degradation you can point at, not a filter across the master.

### F3 · THE DEBT IS PAID IN AUDIBLE OBJECTS
End-rhyme is gone. Are both replacements **events a stranger could point to on a second listen** — the same held vowel returning at bar four, bar twelve, bar twenty-eight, longer each time and on the same pitch class; and a byte-identical returning strophe standing where a chorus would be? The facet fails if either is decoration. It also fails if the mouth does not change shape: monosyllabic in the strophes, polysyllabic in the return, so the jaw moves at exactly the point the subject does.

### F4 · REACHABLE ONLY BY HAVING GONE THROUGH IT
Can the song's point be lifted out as one sentence and nodded at? If yes, the song has already spent its listener. The thesis may not sit in the opening verse, may not sit in any line at all, and must be arrived at only by having crossed the whole arc: split → look → number → response → the strophe returning unchanged over a changed meaning. A stranger must still be able to retell the **scene and subject** — *someone opens a rock and finds the soft part of an animal that died four hundred and fifty million years ago* — after one listen. Legible surface, illegible engine; fog is not depth.

### F5 · THE NUMBER IS ANSWERED AND THE VOICE DOES NOT PITY ITSELF
Is *four hundred and fifty million years* spelled in words, sung once, at the emotional hinge, and **responded to** — a jaw left half open, a word not written in the notes, a flat *right, then* — rather than recited or explained? And is every register free of a made thing describing its own condition, with the single sanctioned exception (V3, one line, refused for the rest of the song's length)? The facet fails on any second sung numeral, any collective pronoun, any address to the listener, and on self-pity arriving as **tone** even where no pronoun does.

## 3. SELF-EVALUATION AND REFINEMENT

- **First pass** produced a sixth facet, "cosmic scale versus domestic scale". Cut: it duplicated F1's terror clause and invited exactly the vastness-adjectives the arrangement is supposed to make unnecessary. Scale is carried by the sub-bass floor and the number, not by a rubric line.
- **F3 was strengthened** from "the returns are present" to "the returns are events a stranger could point to". Presence is unfalsifiable at 68 BPM; pointability is not.
- **F2 was strengthened** with the negative test *"can this be described as atmospheric without loss?"* — the only phrasing that catches the render risk before a render exists.
- **F5 absorbed** the cohort gate and the overhearing rule rather than getting its own facet, because all three are the same failure: a voice that has turned toward an audience.
- Checked against the style axes for this pair — tempo low, energy low-but-dense, harmonic complexity modal/unresolving, rhythmic complexity high (asymmetric loop against a square floor), timbre richness high, organic/synthetic braided at one seam, vocal prominence forward, narrative emphasis high. No facet rewards a lift, a resolution, or a hook, because this pair has none of those by assignment.
