## 0. ⛔ THE NAMING RULING, APPLIED TO THIS STEP

The step-09 contract asks for an *"obscure musician"* lens per candidate. **This run forbids a real person,
company, artist or group name in any field that reaches the generator** (`06_music_handoff.md` §4; ICB SLOT 1
§7 and metaprompt §9), and the standing doctrine is that **a name in the prompt is an instruction to
imitate, while a name in the dedication is a debt paid.** Naming a real musician here would put that name
one copy-paste away from a style field, which is exactly the accident the ruling exists to prevent.

**Discharge:** the six lenses below are **role-anchored seats** — a working practice, described by its
method, with no person attached. They do the job the step file wants (a distinct lexicon, two signature
techniques, an emotional focus) and carry zero name risk. This is the same construction the run's own panel
uses for its role seats (the Luthier, the Sectioning Pathologist, the Living-Tradition Bearer).

**Idioms remain nameable** — `dub poetry`, `Brazilian funk / tamborzão swing`, `garage soft rock`,
`avant ambient techno`, `funk futurepop`, `glitch experimental pop` are public scenes and techniques, not
people. ⛔ `snarl` and `phonk` appear in no style field, and the rhythmic debt to Brazilian-funk-phonk
hybrids is carried by **mechanics** — swing ratio, early accent placement, detuned detent, low round sub —
never by the label.

## 3. ⛔ THE MODERATOR'S REPAIR THAT MATTERED — MEASURED, NOT NOTICED

**Defect found:** all **six** raw music prompts carried **literal negations inside the style field** —
*"never correcting"*, *"a grid that will not follow"*, *"the level does not"*, *"refuses to swing"*,
*"without ever getting louder"*, *"a mono sub that never moves"*. Every one of them was a *persistence*
claim written as a negation.

**Why it is a defect and not pedantry:** conflict 2 in `06_music_handoff.md` is resolved in writing —
**the acoustic axis is stated positively in the style field and every literal "no X" moves to the exclude
field**, because Suno reads a style prompt as a bag of positive tokens. A style field containing
*"never corrected"* is a style field containing the token **corrected**, and this pair's entire thesis is a
lead that stays flat. *(This is the collision that shipped five defects on 2026-08-15.)*

**Repair, applied to all six:**

| before | after |
|---|---|
| never correcting | **holding there** |
| a grid that will not follow | **a straight grid that holds its placement** |
| the level does not (change) | **the level stays put** |
| a grid that refuses to swing | **a perfectly straight grid** |
| without ever getting louder | **at a steady low level** |
| a mono sub that never moves | **a mono sub that holds one level** |
| depth from object identity **rather than reverb** | **depth comes from which object is sounding** |
| One chord, **no lift** | **One chord, one level** |

**Measured outcome: 6 of 6 refined prompts contain zero negations; 6 of 6 sit inside the 850–1000 band.**
Every displaced negative now lives in `## 1B. SUNO EXCLUDE PROMPT`, where the pair needs it most — loudness,
builds, drops, lifts, risers, sweeps and pitch correction are all exclude-field business for this song.

## 4. LYRIC-SIDE REFINEMENTS *(deltas only — the bodies are finalised at step 10)*

1. **Numeral sweep, all four bodies.** ⛔ *No numeral other than the one sung fact* — cardinals **and**
   ordinals. Removed and replaced: *once → again* · *twice → through again* · *both → the same* ·
   *the second page → the other page* · *at last → and holds* · *the last door → the low door* · *half numb
   → lost its feel* · *the joined one → the joined name* · *he stack gets one page tall → the stack gets
   taller here*. **Remaining numeral in every song: `thirty femtoseconds`, once, at the hinge.**
2. **Counting without numbers (R4/V3).** The `[Count]` section counts by **motion and object** — left to
   right, back to left, the lip doing the work, the place kept by touch — because a counting song that
   sings a number breaks the one-fact rule on its own subject. The constraint improved the section.
3. **Hinge lines lengthen; level does not.** In all four, the hinge is the only place the 4–6-syllable verse
   rule is suspended. **The fact arrives as a change of line-length, never as a change of volume.**
4. **Chorus grammars separated** so no two songs share hook grammar: concessive (R1) · tautological
   procedure (R2) · undo-and-repeat (R4) · refusal-of-correction (R3).
5. **R5's chorus candidate cut.** *"What she puts on the page comes off before the light"* is a
   **conclusion**, and this pair's FORBIDDEN SECTION is the chorus. R5 keeps its two-pens arrangement and
   its write/erase antimetabole and loses its hook. Donor, not song.
6. **R6's body problem stands.** A certificate is not a person and the pair requires a body in the first
   thirty seconds. R6 donates the four-column stanza figure and the shortening-stanza device; it does not
   ship.
7. **Self-Pity Gate sweep (R-c).** Every line that risked describing her condition **as a made thing** was
   cut. What survives — *"the sheet does not need her"* — is a description of a **person with hands in a
   place** reading a piece of paper, which is what the gate protects.
8. **Cohort Gate + second person: zero instances** across all four bodies at draft, and re-verified
   mechanically at step 10.
