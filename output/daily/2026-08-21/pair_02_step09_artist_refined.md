## STEP 3 · THE REFINED PROMPTS *(flair injected; no new scene content)*

### REFINED 1 · *Which Shelf Goes* — seats I1 + I2 + I5
> Calm, terror-adjacent Dorian funk in half-time at seventy-six BPM, one chord held and never resolved. The
> first five seconds are a floor tom struck with the flat of a hand and a ring of tin jar lids ticking on a
> shelf in a small tiled kitchen, no bass anywhere in the room. Female lead, late thirties, plain low alto,
> dry and unornamented, clipped consonants, breath audible, landing a fraction early on the short lines,
> tracked close and mono-centred with the only ambience the tiles give back. Warm Rhodes on a single held
> Dorian chord, brushed wooden kit, deep funky electric bass entering late under the second refrain and
> rolling from there. One four-note squeezebox figure carried in from a wedding down the road moves a scale
> degree wrong on every repeat and nothing follows it. Where the backbeat should be, a stack of plates
> settles a sixteenth late.

*Flair note (I1):* the squeezebox is a **working** instrument, not an heirloom — it plays through the whole
song the way a band plays through a power cut, and the wrongness is never a lament.

### REFINED 2 · *Up Before Across* — seats I5 + I2
> Still and terror-adjacent Dorian funk at seventy-six BPM in half-time, static, no cadence until the last
> bar. It opens with a floor tom alone in a cold tiled room, eight bars before anything else exists, and a
> sub-bass swell that rises **before** the drum instead of with it. Female lead, late thirties, low plain
> alto, staged lower and closer than usual, cheek-to-tile proximity, audible breath, several line ends
> falling into near-speech. Held electric-piano cluster suspending one Dorian chord, deep funky bass
> arriving latest of all beneath the bridge, the four-note squeezebox figure walking off the note on each
> repeat and simply continuing to walk. A knuckle on the underside of a wooden table lands exactly where
> the backbeat is not, dry and close, so the groove limps forward on its own weight.

*Flair note (I5):* the table knuckle is the same operation as the plate stack in a different piece of wood —
the pair's substitution device, deliberately re-voiced so its two strongest songs do not share a sound.

### REFINED 3 · *The Kettle Is the Clock* — seats I4 + I2 + I5
> Suspended and patient Dorian funk, half-time, seventy-six BPM, held on one chord that never cadences. The
> opening five seconds are a kettle's metal ticking as it cools and a brushed floor tom in a small tiled
> kitchen, close and dry. Female lead, late thirties, plain alto, a shade warmer here, one long held vowel
> per verse, tracked near the capsule and mono-centred. Felt-muted upright piano re-voicing a single Dorian
> chord with all its colour in the attack, deep round bass entering under the second refrain and staying
> below the tile rather than rising. The squeezebox figure takes a wordless section of its own, a step
> further wrong than before. Where the backbeat should be, a flat stack of plates settles on tile, the
> loudest wooden object in the mix.

*Flair note (I4):* stasis is not emptiness — the chord is re-struck differently each bar, so nothing changes
harmonically and everything changes physically.

### REFINED 4 · *Marks on the Doorframe* — seats I3 + I1 + I6
> Quiet and unhurried Dorian dub-funk at seventy-six BPM in half-time. It opens with a young voice alone
> through a wall, unaccompanied, before a single instrument exists, then a dog next door starting before
> the floor moves. Female lead, late thirties, plain low alto, dry and close, clipped consonants, pitched a
> fraction flat and staying there. Floor tom with a folded cloth over the head, round deep bass arriving
> late, short delay repeats stacked dead centre as a tail rather than a space, sub folded to mono so the
> low end is a pressure. The squeezebox figure is heard through the wall rather than in the room and steps
> wrong each time. A shoulder knocking a swollen door open lands where the backbeat is not.

*Flair note (I3 + I6):* dub here is a **column**, not a panorama — the echo goes deeper into the middle,
never wider; and the door is the second-loudest object, because it is the thing that records the year.

### REFINED 5 · *Through the Wall* — seat I1 **(DONOR)**
> Bare and patient Dorian funk at seventy-six BPM. The five-syllable chant is the whole architecture: it
> opens the song unaccompanied, returns over a settling plate stack with no bass at all, and closes with a
> second voice joining a fraction under and never correcting. Female lead, late thirties, dry plain alto,
> close and mono-centred. Floor tom, one squeezebox figure a scale degree wrong on every repeat, deep bass
> arriving late.

*Critic ruling:* the chant architecture is excellent and the verses have no body doing anything, which is
**L38's** exact failure in its music form. **Donor.**

### REFINED 6 · *Out of True* — seat I6 **(DONOR)**
> Dry and close Dorian funk at seventy-six BPM. A shoulder knocking a swollen door open is a percussion
> element and a pencil dragged on soft wood is a texture, both mic'd at hand distance in a small tiled
> hallway. Female lead, late thirties, plain alto, very close on the wood sounds. Floor tom, one held
> Dorian chord, deep bass late, one squeezebox figure a step wrong each repeat.

*Critic ruling:* the best object in the pair and the worst song — the reading has no reader in it.
**Donor.**
