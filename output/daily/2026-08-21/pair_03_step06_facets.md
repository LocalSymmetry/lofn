## 1. THE POSITION — what C-22 *is*, stated as a place before it is stated as an idea

A folding chair in a gallery room. Parquet, a radiator that ticks when the heat comes up, two rope
stanchions that have gone slack at the clip. On the wall in front of the chair there is a **pale flat patch**
— paint that never took the room's light because something covered it for a long time. A man sits in the
chair for a shift. His entire job is that wall.

The thing that made the wall a room was taken. He still comes. **Two years** later it comes back, is
re-hung a few centimetres off the old hooks, gets new glass, and **nothing is repaired by that.** The two
years happened. The room is still a room. He still sets the chair down where the chair goes.

⭐ **The subject is the PROCEDURE, not the artefact** (Panel A1, and it is a hard ruling on this run). This
pair does not sing about what was worth. It sings about **a shift that continued** — which is the only part
of this event a stranger has ever personally been inside.

**Legibility target, one sentence, one listen:**
> *"A guard keeps working the room after the painting is stolen — and after it comes back."*

⛔ **THE BANS, restated so they cannot be lost downstream.** No name for the painting, the museum, the city,
or the thief. No art-history register, no "masterpiece", no price, no valuation, no rarity. No `we / us /
our / everyone`. No line about a light that is leaving. No thesis in the last verse. `snarl` and `phonk`
appear nowhere. Every sung numeral spelled in words, and **no number is sung anywhere in these four songs
except `two years`, once per song.**

## 2. ESSENCE — one sentence, sensory, load-bearing

> **A man is paid to stand in front of a rectangle of wall; the rectangle is empty for two years and then
> full again, and the standing does not change — which is the tenderness and the offence arriving in the
> same body, on the same clock.**

**The AWE side:** he is *good at it.* He knows which clip on the stanchion has gone soft, he knows the hour
the radiator starts, he knows how the parquet sounds under a stranger's shoe versus his own. Competence
performed on nothing is one of the most moving things a human body does, and he does not find it moving.
*(R-e: astonishment is the listener's job.)*

**The INDIGNATION side, and it is quiet:** when it comes back, **nobody asks him anything.** The room is
declared fine. New glass, new hooks, a fresh coat over the pale patch — a decision made about a room by
people who never sat in it. **The offence is abdication, not loss** (ruling 1). There is no villain in this
song and there is no shouting in it.

## 4. ⭐ THE SEAM — **N = 1**, and where it is placed

- **THE WORLD (dominant material):** slinky, almost-R&B **neo-soul chamber pop** — felted Rhodes, palm-muted
  electric guitar, brushed snare on a coated head, upright bass buzzing lightly on the frets. *(SOURCE 2,
  imported as sound behaviour: "slinkier, almost R&B territory.")*
- **THE WOUND (one intruding material):** **neo-baroque continuo** — suspensions, 4–3 resolutions, a passing
  third under the bass — which **exists only inside the chorus** and is silent everywhere else.
- **THE SEAM (one, placed at the junction that carries the meaning):** the **substitution bar** at the
  midpoint, where the Rhodes hands its exact figure to a **dry upright piano recorded closer** — same bar,
  same notes, no gap, no filter sweep, no drop. The intruder does not enter *beside* the world; it takes the
  world's chair.

⛔ **NO GASBA IN THIS PAIR.** The day's reed idiom belongs elsewhere in the set. Adding it here would be a
second intruder and would break N = 1. *Field note for the director: this is a deliberate abstention, not an
oversight.*

⛔ **SUBSTITUTION, NOT SUBTRACTION** (ruling 4, render-measured L22). Nothing in this pair depends on a long
interrupting silence, on stereo width, or on a hollow mix. Where something leaves, **something audible is
exactly where it was.** Terminal silence is permitted and unused.

## 5. ⭐ THE SOUND / RETURN DEVICE — **THE VOWEL LADDER**, specified so it can be checked

**The rule:** across each verse stanza the stressed vowels climb **ah → eh → ee**, and the ladder **resets at
each shortening**. Because the verses shorten 8 → 4 → 2, the ladder climbs furthest in Verse 1, gets
two-thirds of the way in Verse 2, and **barely starts in Verse 3.** The form and the sound axis are the same
object; the song runs out of ladder exactly when it runs out of stanza.

| Stanza | Lines | Ladder reach | Worked stressed targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Verse 1** | 8 | full `ah → eh → ee` | `latch · patch · hands · brass` → `bench · kept · held · edge` → `keys · sleeve · evening · street` |
| **Verse 2** | 4 | `ah → eh` (+ one `ee` at the very end) | `back · clasp` → `shelf · held` → `keys` |
| **Verse 3** | 2 | `ah` → `eh` only, **never reaches `ee`** | `hands` → `left` |

**Rhyme debt: NOT OWED.** End-rhyme is loose and secondary by design, and the two returns are declared:
the **vowel ladder** and the **byte-identical chorus**. *(The `RETURN FLOORS` in `gates.yaml` are still met
structurally — three identical chorus blocks and a recurring short refrain put the exact-repeated-line ratio
well above `line_return_floor: 0.20`.)*

⭐ **Exact chorus repetition needs no defence.** Nothing further is said about it in any artifact of this
chain, and no variation mutates a chorus to buy a similarity margin.

## 9. WHAT WILL FAIL THIS PAIR — carried forward to steps 07–10

1. ⛔ **The chorus drifting into an address to the missing thing.** It is the single most natural sentence
   this concept produces and it is banned (L18 rotation). **Countermeasure:** a tautology of refusal inside
   the world — *a wall is not a listener* — placed **before** the first chorus, in the room's own vocabulary.
2. ⛔ **Preciousness.** One adjective about worth and this becomes a song about a valuable object, which
   Panel A ruled out in writing. **Countermeasure:** every noun in the lyric is a piece of hardware, a
   surface, a piece of clothing or an hour.
3. ⛔ **The thesis climbing into Verse 3.** Verse 3 is two lines, it is the last verse, and it is the
   forbidden section — which is exactly why the summarising instinct will aim there. **Countermeasure:**
   Verse 3 is furniture and hands only; the synthesis is deferred to the tag, and the tag names damage
   rather than meaning.
4. ⛔ **A second number.** "Eight hours", "four bolts", "half past" all feel like texture and all break the
   one-fact rule. **Countermeasure:** the drafts are scanned for every number word before they are written
   out, and the scan is run again at step 10.
5. ⛔ **The rupture rendering as a drop.** A generator asked for a midpoint change will reach for a filter
   sweep, a bar of silence or a half-time. **Countermeasure:** the substitution is stated as an operation in
   the style field (*hands its exact figure to*) and every one of the smooth alternatives is named in the
   exclude field, where negation actually works.
6. ⛔ **The mean line drifting toward prose.** The ceiling is 7.5 words per sung line and this concept wants
   long explanatory sentences. **Countermeasure:** monosyllabic hardware nouns, `caesura` inside lines, and
   the ladder — which rewards short stressed words.
