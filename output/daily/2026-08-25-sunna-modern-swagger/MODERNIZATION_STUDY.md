# Alexis → Sunna Modernization Study

## Question

Why did Alexis carry reggae, ska-adjacent offbeats, and 1990s rhythmic language without feeling trapped in their recording eras, while the last Sunna daily still sounded old?

## Evidence inspected

- Complete library contracts: `skills/orchestration/personalities/alexis-dreams.yaml` and `sunna.yaml`.
- Alexis packages and production reasoning in `output/2026-05-30_forced-bimbofication-v2-alexis/`, `output/2026-05-30_embers-reignite-v2-alexis/`, `output/2026-05-30_solar-healer-alexis/`, and `output/2026-05-31_alexis-daily-v1/`.
- Sunna R4.2 selected packages in `output/daily/2026-08-24-sunna-r4-2/music/suno_render/`.

No Alexis lyric or prompt payload is copied into this run. The transferable object is a method.

## Diagnosis

Alexis separates **genre identity** from **era surface**. She keeps a bodily invariant—offbeat skank, one-drop space, swung break, rapid melodic cadence—then makes the surrounding record unmistakably current with dry mono sub, gated transient design, glitch edits, electronic lift/drop geometry, diamond-close vocals, and synth-scale contrast.

Sunna R4.2 often did the reverse. It kept complete historical ensembles and arrangement manners—live soul kit, walking electric bass, harpsichord with pizzicato strings, rocksteady organ, power-pop guitars—then applied polish. High shine modernized the finish, not the record's time grammar. Worse, the exclude fields repeatedly removed the very mechanisms that could have broken the museum glass: EDM drops, electronic drums, synthetic sub, pitch processing, and club-derived transitions.

## Transfer law

> **KEEP THE BODY; REPLACE THE CENTURY.** One ancestral rhythmic or melodic invariant may survive. The drum engine, low-end physics, vocal posture, edit language, and section geometry belong to now.

### Five replacements

| Layer | Keep from lineage | Replace with current surface |
|---|---|---|
| Rhythm | one offbeat, one-drop hole, swing ratio, stomp cell | programmed transient architecture, asymmetric fills, sub/kick interlock |
| Harmony | one chord color or riff contour | wavetable/FM/resampled timbre; no period-correct ensemble |
| Bass | melodic or negative-space role | mono sub plus audible upper harmonic, sidechain, short decay |
| Voice | call/response, cadence, singback | close adult lead, hard doubles, formant shadow or pitch-chop answer, modern comping |
| Form | return and crowd hook | hook-at-zero, short runway, chorus/drop by ~0:35, post-hook mutation, abrupt receipt |

## Hard pre-dispatch test

Every pair must answer all five.

1. **Ancestral invariant:** What single thing survives?
2. **Century replacement:** Which four era-signaling layers are replaced?
3. **Now-marker:** What post-2020 production behavior is audible without reading the prompt?
4. **Swagger gap:** Where does confidence come from a withheld note or beat?
5. **Tribute-act test:** Could this render plausibly be sold as a revival record? If yes, rewrite.

## Sunna-specific boundary

This does not turn Sunna into Alexis. Alexis's gloss can armor and deflect; Sunna's gloss invites and donates nerve. Alexis's rapid density is optional; Sunna still favors short sung lines, crowd ownership, plain appetite, hope, adult reciprocal flirtation, and a bassline or synth-riff the room can return. Sunna's instinct wins any conflict with the selected panel.
