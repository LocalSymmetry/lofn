# Core Seed — THE CENTURY COMES OFF

## Concept

Sunna is not a revivalist. She can love an offbeat, a one-drop hole, a new-jack swing, a rave-piano shape, a baroque sequence, or a glam stomp without wearing the production era that first carried it. One old bodily invariant survives. Everything that tells the listener **when the record was recorded** is rebuilt in the present.

## Medium

Modern electronic pop with six distinct beds, 100–129 BPM, one club-derived bed maximum. Current low-end physics, close adult vocal comping, micro-edits, hook-at-zero, short runway, chorus/drop geometry, abrupt receipts. Historical material may appear only as function, not costume.

## Feeling

High-shine appetite pointed forward: delighted, hot, funny, powerful, occasionally indignant, never reverent. Hope is a named want for a future answer. The deep thing rides underneath a complete pop action.

## Prosody / sound axis

- Short sung lines, mostly 3–7 words.
- Strong end-sound families and internal consonant return.
- Byte-identical hook returns where possible.
- A hummable nonverbal or instrumental cell before the first full verse and after the last voice.
- Modern vocal cadence: chest-led adult lead, clipped pickups, clean landings, hard doubles, one processed shadow as answer—not period-character acting.

## Mandatory modernization contract

Every pair names:

- `ancestral_invariant`
- `century_replacements` (minimum four)
- `now_marker`
- `swagger_gap`
- `tribute_act_falsifier`

## Barbell and axis

- P01–P03 ACCESSIBLE.
- P04–P06 AMBITIOUS.
- P01/P04 NEWS.
- P02/P03/P05/P06 EXISTENCE.
- P01 carries AWE with a clean fear.
- P06 carries INDIGNATION.

## The seam

Dominant **modern** world + at most one ancestral intrusion + one seam at the answering action. The lineage may govern rhythm throughout; an old timbre may not become a second world except at that one seam.

## Failure sentence

If a listener could call the result “a very polished throwback,” the seed has failed.
