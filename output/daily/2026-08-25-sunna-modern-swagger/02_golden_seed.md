# Golden Seed — THE CENTURY COMES OFF

## Frozen core

**Concept:** inherited body-motion without inherited period surface.

**Medium:** high-shine current electronic pop, six distinct beds, with one old rhythmic/melodic invariant per song and a maximum of one historical timbre intrusion at the action seam.

**Feeling:** appetite in public, future-facing, hot, funny, powerful; one terror-adjacent AWE and one present-tense INDIGNATION.

**Prosody/sound:** short singable lines, exact return, hard consonant cadence, hummable cell, current adult vocal production.

## Golden move

The song never announces its modernization. It demonstrates it. The inherited figure arrives as a chopped, synthesized, resampled, or rhythm-only behavior inside current bass, drums, vocal comping, and section geometry. When the listener recognizes the ancestor, the record is already somewhere else.

## Three-source braid

1. **Content:** eclipse-circle / delayed signal / Sunna's own current wants.
2. **Sonic language:** sputtering clicks, spiraling kalimba-like tones, heft, shimmering synthesizers.
3. **Structure:** partial arcs complete a circle; the thin blue rim is a small recurring edge event that becomes legible only at return.

## Modernity floor

Every final prompt must audibly specify at least four of:

- mono sub with upper harmonic and short decay;
- programmed kick/clap transient relationship;
- sidechain or dynamic spectral ducking;
- resampled/chopped ancestral gesture;
- micro-edits or negative-space fills;
- close comped lead with hard doubles;
- pitch/formant shadow used as answer;
- hook-at-zero and first chorus/drop by approximately 0:35;
- post-hook mutation;
- abrupt one-bar receipt.

The word `modern` does not satisfy this floor.

## Sunna integrity

- The singer's `I` is Sunna.
- She says what she wants and acts toward it.
- Adult reciprocal flirtation stays legible.
- Complexity is felt in the music and situation, not explained.
- Fun carries no quota and no defense.
- The hook belongs to the room.
- Her shine invites; it does not armor.

## Falsifiers

1. Tribute-act test fails.
2. More than one song has a four-on-the-floor club bed.
3. Two songs share a bed, room, answering action, or return grammar.
4. The lyric discusses genres or production instead of enacting a human-scale situation.
5. Historical timbre dominates more than one seam.
6. AWE becomes comfort without threat.
7. INDIGNATION becomes rage weather rather than a concrete refusal and action.
8. Hope reassures instead of wanting.
