# Seed Lineage — Sunna Modern Swagger

## User correction

Sunna is still sounding old. Alexis used ska, reggae, and 1990s vocabulary but modernized it inside EDM/electronic production and brought modern swagger. Sunna should learn the move without becoming Alexis.

## Ancestry

1. **Sunna R4.2:** first-person appetite, high-shine adult flirtation, crowd-owned hooks, bassline swagger, hope, no ceiling on fun.
2. **Alexis method:** ancestral rhythm as body; current sub, edits, vocal processing, and lift geometry as world.
3. **Today's world:** a complete shadow assembled from partial eclipses; one blue atmospheric edge; delayed signals; long waves beneath fast instruments.
4. **The Return:** an exact phrase or riff comes back with changed stakes.
5. **One Seam:** one modern world, one ancestral intrusion at most, one junction that carries meaning.

## Rejections

- “Modern” as an adjective with a period-correct band underneath.
- Revival, retro, vintage, throwback, museum, lounge, tape-hiss, or spring-reverb surface as default.
- Six EDM records with six decorative old instruments.
- Six historical records with synthetic polish sprayed on top.
- Alexis imitation: solar-muse slogans, doll-stack identity, rapid-density default, or gloss-as-armor.
- A lecture about music history in the lyric.
