## P01 — Blue Edge, Whole Circle

- **Content:** F06/F07 APOD. The apparent hole is Earth's shadow; a faint blue band proves atmosphere is present.
- **Existential stake:** Sunna fears being treated as absent because the listener only sees the shadow.
- **Body standing / harm:** on a rooftop under temporary darkness; the adult may leave before the circle completes.
- **Want:** “Meet me at the blue edge and stay through the return.”
- **Ancestral invariant:** hard two-tone offbeat upstroke.
- **Century replacements:** resampled synth-guitar chip; FM/mono sub with upper bite; tight electronic kick/clap; hard-doubled current vocal; micro-cut fills.
- **Now-marker:** the adult's dry promise is pitch-cut into a hard-doubled melodic pickup above the unchanged mono sub; the next chorus cadence gains that answer contour.
- **Swagger gap:** expected third upstroke is absent; the adult's answer occupies it.
- **Tribute-act falsifier:** mute the upstroke—the remaining record must still read as current bass-pop.
- **Bandcamp phrase:** “sputtering clicks.”
- **APOD form:** four partial lyric/hook arcs complete one exact chorus circle.
- **Form:** Arc A → mini-hook → Arc B → answer gap → Arc C → blue-rim seam → complete exact chorus → one-bar receipt.

## P02 — Answer Me Faster

- **Content:** EXISTENCE prompt 1 plus F13 Big Dip O Ruby as setting color only.
- **Want:** Sunna wants an adult whose reply has its own nerve, not passive agreement.
- **Action:** receipt paper passes back and forth; each reply is shorter and faster until the chosen adult writes the title first.
- **Ancestral invariant:** swung sixteenth kick/snare relationship from new jack swing.
- **Century replacements:** no gated-reverb snare; current clipped clap; FM sub glide; granular receipt ticks; close chest lead with formant-shadow answers; hook/drop by 0:30.
- **Now-marker:** the receipt rip becomes the clipped-clap substitute and rotates the swung percussion/vocal pickup; the FM sub continues its prior glide and is not keyed by the tear.
- **Swagger gap:** every fourth reply withholds the clap; the paper rip lands instead.
- **Tribute-act falsifier:** no 1980s/90s drum-machine preset, synth-brass, or period vocal acting.
- **Bandcamp phrase:** “the heft the music needs to lift.”
- **APOD form:** repeated partial replies make one complete written circle around the title.
- **Form:** hook-at-zero → reply rounds 4/3/2/1 lines → clap substitution → adult writes title → exact hook return.

## P03 — Future, Dance Back

- **Content:** EXISTENCE prompts 1 and 3; F18 quiet solar force informs pressure without spectacle.
- **Want:** Sunna wants one answer she can physically receive through sound.
- **Action:** she sends a three-syllable signal; another adult changes one syllable and sends it back; Sunna changes the offbeat timbre and the next hook cadence in reply.
- **Ancestral invariant:** dancehall one-drop negative space and offbeat organ function.
- **Century replacements:** organ becomes short wavetable stab; one-drop becomes sub/kick absence with digital percussion; clean spectral delay replaces dub fog; close comped lead; answer-to-stab wavetable re-indexing.
- **Now-marker:** the changed answer syllable re-indexes the offbeat wavetable stab and alters the chorus cadence; beat-one kick/sub absence stays empty.
- **Swagger gap:** the missing downbeat is owned by the returned syllable.
- **Tribute-act falsifier:** no roots ensemble, spring echo, beach/tropical shorthand, or heritage vocal affect.
- **Bandcamp phrase:** “shimmering synthesizers.”
- **APOD form:** the reply supplies the blue rim, a small edge event that makes the whole signal legible.
- **Form:** send → open hole → altered return → bass learns it → reciprocal chorus → abrupt ping receipt.

## P04 — Still Here When It Lands

- **Content:** F14 Voyager 2 / delayed encounter; one numeric fact maximum and optional.
- **Club-bed reason:** latency and arrival require a shuffled runway and a delayed drop; this is the set's sole club-derived chassis.
- **Want:** Sunna wants an adult to answer after the distance rather than fill the wait with reassurance.
- **Action:** four message slots are sent; the reply lands in the fourth after the hook has learned to wait.
- **Ancestral invariant:** one 1990s rave-piano chord shape, withheld until seam.
- **Century replacements:** speed-garage shuffle with current transient sculpting; reese/mono-sub hybrid; micro-edited vocal pickups; hard chest lead; modern stereo bass harmonics; no hoover or sample-pack nostalgia.
- **Now-marker:** the delayed reply becomes the sidechain key for the final bass drop.
- **Swagger gap:** a whole expected chord is missing until the adult sends one dry word.
- **Tribute-act falsifier:** without the single piano seam, the record remains unmistakably current UK-bass pop.
- **Bandcamp phrase:** “spiraling kalimba-like tones.”
- **APOD form:** separated transmissions/partial arcs reveal one whole shadow/answer.
- **Form:** hook fragment → message 1/2/3 → false arrival → reply seam with piano chord → full four-slot drop chorus → hard console click.

## P05 — The Version After This

- **Content:** EXISTENCE prompt 4; hope as appetite for a specific not-yet version.
- **Want:** Sunna wants to choose tomorrow's version of herself without pretending it already exists.
- **Action:** a receipt printer offers four future labels; she crosses out three and signs one.
- **Ancestral invariant:** a rising baroque sequence, rhythm/contour only.
- **Century replacements:** sequence voiced as wavetable pluck; elastic mono sub; asymmetric digital claps; granular mirror clicks; close lacquered lead with hard doubles; no chamber ensemble.
- **Now-marker:** each crossed-out label is resampled into a shorter percussion grain; the signed one becomes the post-hook.
- **Swagger gap:** sequence omits its expected highest note until she signs.
- **Tribute-act falsifier:** no harpsichord, strings, continuo, chamber staging, or orchestral grandeur.
- **Bandcamp phrase:** F12, “pausing time to hear what was happening” (interview phrase used only as a one-frame production instruction).
- **APOD form:** partial arcs/labels accumulate; thin ruby-blue rim becomes one persistent upper counterline.
- **Form:** sequence at zero → four labels → cross-out rounds → missing-note hinge → signed future chorus → compact receipt.

## P06 — Don't Put Me in Past Tense

- **Content:** EXISTENCE prompt 5; user correction is the direct pressure, not sung production criticism.
- **Situation:** a showcase placard praises Sunna as a “beautiful throwback.” She turns it over, writes a present-tense invitation, and restarts the room.
- **Want:** to be met as happening now.
- **Action:** flip, relabel, restart; the chosen adult reads the new line aloud.
- **Ancestral invariant:** glam stomp plus a three-note power-pop contour.
- **Century replacements:** contour becomes distorted synth/guitar hybrid rather than period guitar band; electronic stomp/clap; sub under upper riff; clipped electroclash edits; close adult vocal stack; no arena-rock wall.
- **Now-marker:** placard flip hard-cuts the spectrum and the adult's line reopens it as the final drop.
- **Swagger gap:** the stomp withholds beat three; the placard slap fills it.
- **Tribute-act falsifier:** no vintage amp, live-room nostalgia, retro synthwave, hand-worn authenticity, or “classic” mix cues.
- **Bandcamp phrase:** “Give the game away” is F17, while F10 contributes “sputtering clicks” to the placard/relay edit.
- **APOD form:** the false “hole” label is overturned; the edge band proves present atmosphere.
- **Form:** mislabeled hook → concrete refusal → flip seam → adult reads new present line → declaration drop → exact title return → one-song-early hard stop.

## Most-specified-object variation tests

Each isolated pair must vary its four candidate **answer actions and seam blocks**, not merely titles or nouns. Swap the entire answer/seam block between two variations: if both still work, regenerate. Within-pair lyric and prompt distinctiveness are measured on sung bodies as well as fields.

Across pairs, the answer consequences are locked to six different classes: P01 vocal/harmonic; P02 swung-percussion; P03 offbeat-timbre/cadence; P04 club-sidechain/drop; P05 melodic peak; P06 spectrum/drop. P01–P03 fail if a returned answer becomes a bass attack or drop key.
