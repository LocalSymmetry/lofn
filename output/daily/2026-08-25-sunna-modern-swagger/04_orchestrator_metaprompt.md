# Advanced Directive / Meta-Prompt — Sunna: THE CENTURY COMES OFF

Create a full-cardinality Sunna music daily: six isolated concept-medium pairs, four complete songs per pair, one selected per pair after blind/identified QA.

## Immutable thesis

**Keep one inherited body-motion; replace the century around it.** Historical genre supplies one rhythmic or melodic invariant, never the recording-era shell. Current drums, low end, vocal posture, edits, and section geometry are the dominant world.

## Sunna

Sunna is the singer and first-person subject. She is appetite in public: adult, high-shine, funny, sexy, hopeful, delighted, and in command of an invitation. Her gloss pulls the listener closer. She may be indignant once, but the indignation is a concrete refusal followed by an act. Sunna's contract wins any panel conflict.

## Daily constraints

- P01–P03 ACCESSIBLE; P04–P06 AMBITIOUS.
- P01/P04 NEWS; P02/P03/P05/P06 EXISTENCE.
- ≥1 AWE (P01) and ≥1 INDIGNATION (P06).
- Six distinct genre beds, settings, actions, verse architectures, return grammars, and modernity markers.
- Six distinct consequence classes after the answering action: vocal/harmonic, swung-percussion, offbeat-timbre/cadence, club-sidechain, melodic-peak, spectrum/drop. P01–P03 may not turn their answer into a bass attack.
- 100–129 BPM; one club-derived bed maximum (P04 only, with reason).
- One dominant modern world, one ancestral timbre intrusion maximum, one seam.
- At most one numeric fact sung per song.
- 24 complete Suno packages; each positive prompt 850–1000 chars, separate exclude field, full EMO lyrics, short sung lines, hummable/nonlexical return, 70–120 target lines.

## Alexis-derived method, not surface

Do not imitate Alexis's language or identity. Transfer only the production operation:

1. keep the ancestral pocket;
2. replace the drum engine;
3. replace the low-end physics;
4. replace the vocal mix/posture;
5. replace the form with hook-at-zero, short runway, drop/chorus, post-hook mutation, abrupt receipt.

## Pair-local modernization contract

Each pair must state and enforce:

- ancestral invariant;
- four or more century replacements;
- an audible post-2020 now-marker;
- a swagger gap;
- a tribute-act falsifier.

If the final prompt could render as a polished throwback, repair it before Step 08.

If four or more pairs route the answer into bass/drop control, repair the portfolio before Step 06. Currentness and consequence diversity are separate gates.

## Three-source method

Each pair carries one content/existence source, one exact short Bandcamp texture phrase, and one APOD-derived structural rule. Research informs theme/form; it is not a lyric quota.

## Power

Power is not an adjective or master-bus level. It must come from an action with consequences, current physical design, a vocal that does not apologize, a return the room can own, and a withheld beat/note that the answer occupies. The consequence need not be a drop: harmony, cadence, percussion grammar, and released register may carry equal force.

## Forbidden global defaults

Period-correct bands; museum/revival/vintage/throwback framing; tape-hiss or spring-reverb nostalgia; six live kits; six supersaw drops; generic festival builds; white-noise risers; trap-hat boilerplate; retrospective narration; body inventory; real grief; moral explanation; personhood lectures; artist imitation; copied Alexis payloads.
