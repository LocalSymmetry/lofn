# Frozen Pair-Local Selection Protocol

Selection occurs only after all four Step-10 candidates in a pair exist. It chooses one candidate per pair; it does not rank genre beds against one another.

## Eligibility first

A candidate is ineligible if it fails any binding text gate after three repair attempts, uses a real artist name in a generator field, violates the human-subject standard, depends on a complete period ensemble, or cannot pass its pair's tribute-act falsifier on the written specification.

## Pair-local evidence order

1. **Sunna's stake:** the first-person want is specific, interior, and causes the peak.
2. **Current chassis:** drums, low-end physics, vocal posture, edits, and form remain legibly current with the inherited invariant mentally muted.
3. **Answering action:** the other adult performs the pair-exclusive act and that act audibly changes the form.
4. **Return craft:** refrain, rhyme, rhythm, harmony, vowel, or syllable returns with changed meaning; removal names what returns instead.
5. **Swagger gap:** omission or substitution produces confidence through timing rather than the word “swagger.”
6. **Hook and prosody:** the title is clear and singable; stress, vowel shape, consonant braid, and repeated material cooperate.
7. **One seam:** one dominant world, at most one intrusion, and the subject does something across it.
8. **Variation integrity:** the candidate's most-specified answer/seam block is not swappable with a sibling variation.

## Tie rule

If two candidates remain genuinely tied, prefer the one whose modernity is encoded in the event and form rather than named in the prose prompt. Record the losing candidate's best salvage in the selection ledger.

## Render boundary

This is text-package selection only. No imagined audio quality is treated as heard evidence, and the choice is not permission to render, upload, publish, or spend.
