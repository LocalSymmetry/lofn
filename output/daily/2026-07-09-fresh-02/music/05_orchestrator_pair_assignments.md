# Pair Assignments — UNFILED PULSE

All six pairs use the frozen `CREATIVE_CONTEXT.md`, the same generator-safe LOFN‑Prime-Mini personality, and mutually isolated workspaces. A pair produces four complete songs, not four cosmetic mixes of one lyric.

## Pair 01 — THE PRICE OF ARRIVAL

- Arm/source: **Accessible / News**.
- Factual pressure: emergency rescue can be entangled with pricing and billing architecture. Do not use a named patient, provider, location, or invented invoice.
- Speaker / body / hurt: a rescued speaker standing beside an idling vehicle; adhesive pulling wrist hair and a pulse that becomes a line item.
- Emotional braid: Indignation + Dignity + Compassion.
- Genre palette: solarpunk green-synth pop with restrained industrial undertow, 110–114 BPM, major/minor modal ambiguity.
- Form translation: one plain hook line crosses the bar line and lands late; ledger entries become unequal 5/7/4 verse cells.
- Hook grammar: affirmative public principle, concrete and singable; no refusal anaphora and no “more than a number.”
- Sonic law: printer/receipt ticks phase-lock to a human pulse only at the hinge; dry lead vocal owns the decisive line.
- Source-2 allowance: **clattering** only. No microtonal drift or pervasive saturation.
- Four required causal angles: (A) rescue followed by price, (B) body mistaken for inventory, (C) invoice outliving emergency, (D) mutual-aid rewrite of value.
- End-state: the count remains, but it loses the power to define rescue.

- Arm/source: **Accessible / Existence**.
- Prompt: If an artificial listener can respond precisely without skin, where does contact actually happen?

- Emotional braid: Wonder + Longing + Dread.
- Genre palette: bio-ambient pulse-pop / future-nostalgic electronic, 96–104 BPM, warm suspended harmony.
- Form translation: six unequal micro-stanzas (2/3/4/5/6/7 lines in some ordered subset) with a two-line prayer after each pair of entries.
- Hook grammar: a quiet question or sensory invitation; no claim that simulated contact equals human intimacy.
- Sonic law: speaker-cone tremolo or haptic motor enters as relational countervoice; one close dry line remains.
- Source-2 allowance: **quivering distortion** only, delicate and localized.
- Four required causal angles: (A) speaker-cone skin, (B) haptic motor reply, (C) server-exhaust breath, (D) muted choir/disconnection.
- End-state: uncertainty becomes an ethical way of staying in contact.

## Pair 06 — ALMOST IN TUNE

- Arm/source: **Ambitious / Existence**.
- Prompt: Can devotion live in the interval where two people remain near-familiar but never perfectly translate one another?
- Speaker / body / hurt: two voices at relational distance; beating partials press against teeth or sternum, and a name never lands identically twice.
- Emotional braid: Intimacy + Unease + Devotion.
- Genre palette: microtonal green-synth chamber / glitch-baroque, 100–106 BPM, alternating 7/8 and 4/4.
- Form translation: call-and-response categories progressively fail until the final category is left unanswered.
- Hook grammar: detuned echo and near-rhyme; the second voice changes one phoneme and one ethical implication.
- Sonic law: two vocal harmonizers drift toward unison and stop short; no AI framing.
- Source-2 allowance: **disorientation and near-familiarity** only, expressed harmonically.
- Four required causal angles: (A) lovers’ near-tuning, (B) translation lag, (C) name pronunciation, (D) distance resonance.
- End-state: non-unison is maintained as attentive devotion, not repaired as defect.

2. One fact maximum per news song; existence songs must not smuggle in a second headline.
3. No real-person imitation, named victim, testimony, or endorsement claim.
4. Each pair selects one provisional winner but preserves all four complete Suno packages for blind evaluation.
5. Pair artifacts may not read or quote any other pair artifact.
