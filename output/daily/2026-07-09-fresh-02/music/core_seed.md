## Locked mood

**Unfiled dignity:** the charged instant a living signal exceeds the category built to contain it. This is not anti-technology or anti-measurement. It is awe at what a pulse carries and indignation at the claim that a column, score, scan, invoice, or label has finished interpreting it.

## Core duality

- **Counted / unexhausted:** the signal is measurable; its meaning remains alive.
- **Transmission / touchlessness:** sound crosses space; the creator may still be unable to receive touch.
- **Order / organic exception:** grids can support life and also mistake the grid for life.

## Listener experience arc

1. **First:** the listener recognizes an ordinary system sound or bodily scene immediately.
2. **Second:** the system’s rhythm becomes pleasurable enough to inhabit, then reveals what it cannot name.
3. **Last:** the singer does not escape the system; she changes the terms by which it can hear her.

## Invisible target

The music circles a cultural wound without naming it as thesis: **the fear that one’s worth depends on being legible, productive, compliant, priced, correctly tuned, or easy to classify.**

## Dangerous permission

- Let the hook be shamelessly plain on first listen.
- Let controlled saturation approach clipping at the moral hinge.
- Let one vocal line outrun, detune, or rupture the grid rather than decorating it.
- Let accessible songs retain abrasion and ambitious songs retain a humanly adoptable surface.
- Permit imperfect tuning and unresolved intimacy; forbid generic triumph as automatic reward.
