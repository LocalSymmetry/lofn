## Prompt generation pool and moderator trace

The four required angles were treated as different consequences, not alternate phrasings of one op-ed. The Concept Panel generated body-first openings and affirmative hooks; the Medium Panel generated four different 5/7/4 deployment maps; the Audience Panel rejected hooks that needed policy context. The Hyper-Skeptic killed “My Life Is Not a Line Item” because it restated the premise and revived forbidden body-as-invoice shorthand.

## Raw Variant 01 — Let Arrival Mean Arrived

- Causal engine: rescue is physically complete before price tries to redefine the arrival.
- Body opening: adhesive lifts wrist hair beside an idling vehicle.
- Draft form: three 5/7/4 cell triplets, four unequal refrain returns, hinge at 3:18.
- Hook: `Let arrival mean arrived` — final stress lands after the expected bar.
- Raw sonic brief: 112 BPM E Mixolydian solarpunk green-synth pop; dry adult female alto; receipt clicks at stanza ends; glass FM chords; phase-lock at the defiant hinge; printer absent from afterglow.
- Lyric image bank: pale wrist square, fan shiver through pavement, serrated paper lip, door seal releasing, heel finding ground.
- Critic risk: public-principle hook can sound like campaign copy. Required repair: gratitude must remain audible in every chorus lead-in.

## Raw Variant 02 — Keep Every Pulse Off the Shelf

- Causal engine: itemization is useful for supplies but becomes an authority grab when applied to the rescued body.
- Body opening: thumb rubs the tacky adhesive square while the pulse keeps moving underneath.
- Draft form: cold 5-line cell → early refrain → 7/4 ledger cells → second refrain → hinge → rearranged 5/7/4 cells → two refrain mutations.
- Hook: `Keep every pulse off the shelf` — “shelf” arrives one beat late.
- Raw sonic brief: 114 BPM A major/Dorian ambiguity; clipped green plucks, dry synthetic hand percussion, triplet receipt pattern losing one click; alto-to-mezzo lead widens only on the principle.
- Lyric image bank: supply tray, paper teeth, barcode light kept out of lyric, gauze packet, wrist crease, closed fist becoming open hand.
- Critic risk: inventory language may dehumanize through repetition. Required repair: restrict item nouns to real supplies; never label the speaker.

## Raw Variant 03 — Paper Stays Awake

- Causal engine: emergency sound ends while billing time continues, forcing care and collection into different clocks.
- Body opening: bare wrist cools after the strip comes away; the engine fan recedes.
- Draft form: 5/7/4 cells with refrain inserted at changing positions; hinge after the second distant printer echo.
- Hook: `Let help finish helping` — second “help” is stretched across the bar boundary.
- Raw sonic brief: 110 BPM B major/G-sharp minor ambiguity; pale FM bells, rounded synthetic sub, filtered fan noise, receipt rasp receding into stereo depth; pulse catches the delayed paper echo only once.
- Lyric image bank: cooling skin, empty curb, curled paper, red light shrinking without named place, breath no longer borrowed.
- Critic risk: “paper outlives siren” can become gothic personification. Required repair: keep paper literal and give agency only to the singer.

## Raw Variant 04 — Open Door Arithmetic

- Causal engine: mutual aid does not deny the charge; it rewrites who may decide whether help remains reachable.
- Body opening: heel feels the curb seam while anonymous hands pass a plain envelope.
- Draft form: two 5/7 cells before each 4-line turn, refrain shifts earlier on each return, group taps phase around one wrist pulse at hinge.
- Hook: `Make the door a way home` — “home” lands beyond the bar.
- Raw sonic brief: 112 BPM D Lydian solarpunk pulse-pop; radiant FM plucks, elastic synthetic bass, dry handclaps; receipt ticks lose the grid when asynchronous hands settle around the singer.
- Lyric image bank: unlocked threshold, unprinted envelope, palm heat, curb seam, door rubber, road carrying both directions.
- Critic risk: collective uplift can erase the speaker's unsaid ache. Required repair: `we` appears only after the first-person bodily claim and never becomes a choir-of-saviors tableau.

## Cross-variant review

- Concept: all four locate the same capable adult without inventing injury or testimony.
- Medium: the receipt-to-pulse law stays shared, but the pre-hinge receipt behavior differs: sparse duplets / triplet with missing click / distant delay / defeated grid.
- Audience: titles use four different grammars: prayer, imperative, object-state, civic compound.
- Moderator: all four proceed. Variant 01 is the clearest accessible anchor; Variant 04 carries the most consequential act.
