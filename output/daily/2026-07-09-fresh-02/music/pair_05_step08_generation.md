## Raw 01 — SAY SAME WITH YOUR RIBS

- Angle: depth burden.
- Hook seed: “Say same with your ribs,” with neutral SAME first and wounded SAY later.
- Form: five-beat spiral — calibration → ledger cell → depth test → second cell → free-time hinge → mutated test → residual coda.
- Fact plan: the only factual lyric in the pair, “M five point one,” once at the hinge; the following line answers it bodily.
- Sound thesis: identical kick attacks, first tail ends at sternum, second falls through the floor; microtonal contrabass grains beat against the teeth.
- Turn: the singer first blames the label, then admits the label is accurate and limits what it may conclude.
- Raw title alternatives rejected: “Depth Is Not a Footnote” is a thesis; “Another Floor” lacks the phonetic test.

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Adult female contralto-to-mezzo A3–E5 uses chest-led phrases, clean edge, crisp dentals, and a narrow head-tone flare. Signature Depth Twins at 0:04 share one dry membrane-and-metal attack; the first sub tail stops at sternum height while its twin descends through a mono 36 Hz floor, making the hook tactile before language. Dry center vocal leaves bar lines in free-time arcs while the grid stays fixed; restrained doubles widen only on depth tests. Calibration cells tighten into a clipped hook, the 2:04 hinge suspends voice across three measures, and the final test strips to palm-close lead, equal kick heads, unequal tails, then one residual low decay under the last stressed word.

## Raw 02 — UNDER THE THRESHOLD

- Angle: threshold label.
- Hook seed: “Put your ear under the threshold,” compressed to “Under the threshold” on returns.
- Form: additive threshold staircase — lintel → gate I → under-refrain → gate II → subthreshold free-time break → reclassified refrain → high-line coda.
- Fact plan: no number or scientific event wording.
- Sound thesis: five-beat dry membrane grid under a pitch-threshold tone that splits by cents; equal kick heads reveal unequal sustain only after the first refrain.
- Turn: the singer stops trying to abolish the threshold and makes it disclose what it leaves below.
- Raw title alternatives rejected: “Below the Line” is generic; “Subthreshold” is too technical for the hook.

## Raw 03 — THE SCORE HAS WEIGHT

- Angle: same score, different body.
- Hook seed: “The score has weight / when the weight scores me,” using antanaclasis rather than an aphorism.
- Form: equivalence palinode — statement → counterweight → score refrain → body map → equal-sign fracture → reversal → weight-mutation coda.
- Fact plan: no event reference, locations, roles, or number.
- Sound thesis: bright label-tone stays constant while two equal kick attacks create different rib pressure; a dry free-time alto overtakes the display tone without silencing it.
- Turn: the body does not disprove the score; the body changes the score’s jurisdiction.
- Raw title alternatives rejected: “Equal Sign Fracture” overexplains form; “Weight Scores Me” sounds punitive.

## Raw 04 — MY UNDERGROUND DOUBLE

- Angle: underground double.

- Form: interleaved surface/undertone suite — surface I → undertone I → overlap → tunnel bridge in free time → surface II → double coda.

- Turn: the speaker stops treating the undertone as an impostor and hears it as the burden hidden by her clean surface signal.
- Raw title alternatives rejected: “Second Floor Below” repeats floor imagery; “The Longer Vowel” hides the bodily stakes.
