# Step 02 — Supplied Panel Pressure

The supplied 18-seat ledger was used; no new panel was invented.

## Concept panel

- Dissent: the first pass produced six variations on “a person exceeds a number.” The Hyper-Skeptic rejected the thesis as generic.
- Alteration: every surviving concept now contains a situated body, a specific pressure, and a consequential limit on the system’s authority.
- Killed concept: **The Dashboard Cannot Love You** — thesis title, no costly turn, and AI cliché.

## Medium panel

- Dissent: a global saturated glitch palette would make the day feel like one long plug-in demo.
- Alteration: each pair receives one incompatible sonic law and only one dominant Source-2 pressure term.
- Killed concept: **Clipping at the Edge of Humanity** — saturation was mood paint rather than narrative event.

## Context and audience panel

- Dissent: rescue billing and speech scanning could convert vulnerable lives into prestige texture.
- Alteration: concepts target public values, consent architecture, labels, and routes; no identifiable private person, invented testimony, or victim ventriloquism.
- Killed concept: **Her Last Ambulance Bill** — fabricated subject and manipulative specificity.

## Counter-moves that survived

1. A dry decisive vocal line in every concept.
2. One fact maximum per news-derived song.
3. Accessible hooks state a usable principle or permission; ambitious hooks may repeat changing sound/category instead.
4. The final act limits measurement’s authority rather than disproving measurement.
