## Guide 1 — The Checked Box

- Central engine: a tiny interface action is enlarged into blanket permission.
- Body report: fingertip heat is secondary; the primary scene remains tongue against the ridge behind the teeth, throat tightening before speech.
- Storytelling: use metonymy (the box for the contract) and progressive anaphora whose hard initial changes object: click / clause / category / consent.
- Arc: suspicion in the mouth → oppression under overhead labels → indignation at “granted” → defiance that redraws the boundary.
- Structure: through-composed cells of 5/3/7/4/6 lines; no chorus return. A four-line hook family mutates twice.
- Rhyme: consonance and pararhyme; no neat end-rhyme couplets.
- Hook options: “Checked is not chosen”; “Click cannot carry yes”; “A mark is not my mouth.”
- Sonic thesis: clipped synthetic relay ticks become a false handshake; an overhead consonant swarm labels each click.
- Hinge: one register exchange at ~2:02; overhead swarm falls into 38 Hz sub while the dry lead rises to centered mix register.
- Saturation: one short brink-clipping flare only on the system's false-certainty phrase.
- End: the interface remains functional, but its claim to consent is revoked.
- Runtime: 3:35–3:55, 78–92 sung lines.

## Guide 4 — The Log Cannot Hold Intention

## Guide 5 — Dry Vocal Proof

- Purpose: score every candidate's decisive line without relying on glitch spectacle.
- Body rule: the decisive boundary must survive as an unprocessed adult female line with audible teeth and breath.
- Technique: place one nearly unaccompanied chamber-vocal span immediately after the register inversion, never before it.
- Melody: compact minor-third cell that expands to a tritone only when the singer names the boundary.
- Rhythm: remove kick for one phrase but do not use total silence or blackout-cut grammar.
- Spatial rule: lead center; one short room reflection behind it; all classifier fragments already below.
- Hook test: speakable, then singable, then memorable after one silent minute.
- Failure: if removing the consonant swarm destroys the lyric's meaning, repair the lyric.
- Failure: if the dry line sounds like policy prose, replace abstraction with mouth physics.
- Failure: if the line claims victory, retain suspicion in the final vowel.
- Applied to: all four variations.
- Runtime effect: 8–12 seconds inside the through-composed hinge aftermath.

## Guide 6 — Wildcard: The Ceiling Learns the Wrong Mouth

- Cross-disciplinary image: an institutional façade becomes a second jaw above the singer, but it never speaks the private sentence.
- Material translation: clipped headers behave like false teeth; the organic voice keeps saliva, breath, and timing the model cannot own.
- Storytelling: prosopopoeia limited to architecture, not a victim or private person.
- Arc: the ceiling's jaw appears competent, then its competence overreaches, then it loses vertical authority.
- Structure: no section repeats; every passage changes line count, register, and terminal consonant.
- Production: synthetic clipped-metal jaw snaps, granular sibilants, dry alto, 36–41 Hz sub-floor.
- Kinetic defect: kick omits the beat beneath the most certain institutional word.
- Hook technique: changing hard-consonant anaphora over three different melodic contours rather than a static chorus.
- SFX allowance: exactly one short mouth-related cue per final lyric field.
- Human-subject law: no name, date, place, case role, message quotation, or identifying tuple.
- Ending: one private syllable remains unowned without being heard.
- Use: inject strangeness into the four angle-specific guides without importing a fifth song.
