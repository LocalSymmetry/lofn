# Orchestrator Metaprompt — UNFILED PULSE

## Role

Create twenty-four finished, Suno-ready songs as six isolated four-variation pairs. All pairs use the generator-safe **LOFN‑Prime-Mini** personality. Do not imitate or quote any named artist or panel source; the panel has already been distilled into constraints.

## Creative proposition

**Systems can count a living signal but cannot finish interpreting it.** The work must not argue that measurement is evil or that humans are simply “more than numbers.” Each song must reveal a living remainder that changes what a measurement, label, route, price, task, or tuning is allowed to do.

## Emotional engine

- Locked braid: **AWE + INDIGNATION**.
- Listener arc: recognition of precision → bodily friction → recovered permission.
- Invisible target: the moment a useful system quietly exceeds its authority.
- Body law: every variation locates a body, names one precise hurt or pressure, and gives that body one consequential act.

## Tri-source law

1. **Content source:** current reporting and existence prompts in the research brief.
2. **Sonic source:** clattering echoes, quivering distortion, brink-of-clipping saturation, and microtonal near-familiarity. Each pair may dominate with only one of these pressures.
3. **Form source:** the historical legal-journal diagram of numbered blades around a rounded ledger/table, dense headers, and civic façade. Each pair translates the form differently; never quote it literally in lyrics.

## Cardinality and barbell

- Exactly 6 pairs × 4 genuinely different variations = 24 songs.
- Pairs 1–3: **accessible**. Clear emotional entry, memorable melodic landing, and principle/permission hooks without generic uplift.
- Pairs 4–6: **ambitious**. Riskier form, harmony, and production with a tactile orientation point inside thirty seconds.
- Exactly 3 news-derived pairs and 3 existence-derived pairs.
- Exactly one primary factual source may enter each song; never stack headlines.

## Anti-convergence law

Across all pairs, do not reuse the same:

- title grammar;
- opening syntax;
- hook shape;
- section order;
- sonic device;
- central object;
- rhyme behavior;
- resolution type;
- final image.

Forbidden day-level defaults: monster/cradle framing, war/lanyard imagery, Tesla or pigeons, kitchen/cup/blanket comfort, shared silence-marbling, “I won’t” as hinge, “the body is…” title grammar, courtroom exposition, generic machine-versus-human moral.

## Lyric and performance law

- Use section and EMO tags supported by the music workflow.
- Make the decisive line singable without production.
- No named real victim, identifiable private person, invented testimony, or ventriloquized trauma.
- News-derived songs may target structures, values, consent, prices, routes, labels, and public consequences.
- A fact must become pressure or choice, never citation display.
- Avoid explanatory prose, abstract noun piles, and slogans that resolve the moral too early.

## Production law

- Two-field Suno package: dense prose **Style** field and **Exclude** field.
- Style must specify genre hybrid, tempo/feel, key or harmonic behavior, meter, instrumentation, vocal identity, mix spatiality, dynamic arc, and the pair’s unique sonic event.
- Exclude must name concrete unwanted genres, vocal habits, arrangement clichés, mix failures, and concept collisions.
- No living-artist style imitation in either field.

## Six formal translations

1. One hook line breaks the meter boundary.
2. Six unequal micro-stanzas behave like numbered entries.
3. A rondo returns while task-language and layers are removed.
4. High and low registers exchange institutional authority once.
5. A dense rhythmic grid supports a free-time vocal witness.
6. Call-and-response categories progressively fail.

## Selection law

Select one winner from each pair only after the full four-variation set exists. Final six must remain exactly 3 accessible + 3 ambitious and 3 news + 3 existence. Prefer identity-defining difference over averaged polish. A repair flag is healthier than a falsely perfect slate.

## Golden moves (names only)

- **The Blue Screen Breathes** — make infrastructure physically intimate without explaining it.
- **I Will Stop the Almost** — convert emotional hesitation into an irreversible formal act.

These are move names, not lyric reservoirs. No archived golden-output text is available to generators.
