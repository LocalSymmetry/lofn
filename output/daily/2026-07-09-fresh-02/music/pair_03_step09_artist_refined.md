## 1. Leave One Thing Unfinished — 93/100 — advance as revised source

- Raw strength: strongest dry sing-back and clearest body-first scene.
- Hyper-Skeptic dissent: an unfinished task can sound like negligence.
- Refinement: the lyric now distinguishes urgent from harmless work before the hook returns; visible handoff replaces solitary refusal. The clock misses beat one at the hinge, not as an opening gimmick.
- Sonic change: prepared wood transient stays mono until its tail becomes staggered breathing; near-familiar doubles appear only on rondo lines.
- Lyric change: internal consonance in the 9-line span; open vowels in the refrain; three-line final verse names action rather than relief.

## 2. Let the Line Lie Down — 91/100 — advance as revised source

- Raw strength: most delicate translation of task measurement into an audible object.
- Hyper-Skeptic dissent: “sleeping graph” risks wellness-app softness.
- Refinement: no sleep décor or private retreat; a planned night rota is the causal turn. The graph remains useful but loses authority over worth.
- Sonic change: cursor chirp arrives an eighth-note late and is gradually replaced by inhale/exhale halves from the punch-clock tail.
- Lyric change: long-i assonance and descending melodic contour replace end-rhyme; the 9/5/3 spans delete screen vocabulary in order.

## 3. One Box Stays Blank — 88/100 — retain for synthesis

- Raw strength: sharp ethical proof that incompletion can be harmless without denying accountability.
- Hyper-Skeptic dissent: the blank box could become cute stationery symbolism.
- Refinement: rail light, wrist paper-cut pressure, and explicitly named urgent rows keep the scene bodily. An eight-bar metric vacancy contains a held reed and breath rather than a dramatic silence reveal.
- Sonic change: empty beat two, not an empty mix; paper-hard clock snap acquires an exhale tail and heel counterpulse.
- Lyric change: declarative hook grammar, sparse alternating rhyme, and a triage bridge distinguish it from the invitation hooks.

## 4. Set It Down Wider — 87/100 — retain for synthesis

- Raw strength: strongest collective labor proposition and strangest accessible hook.
- Hyper-Skeptic dissent: “wider” is metaphor unless the mix and actions prove width.
- Refinement: four stereo handoffs and four distinct actions make width literal. No choir blend; each adult female self-double remains legible.
- Sonic change: the click travels left wall → right wall → upper stair → lower landing, one sixteenth late each time, and becomes a 3:4 breathing commons.
- Lyric change: chiasmus and polysyndeton carry the load language; the final 3-line span contains no task noun.

## 5. The Hour Grows Knees — 82/100 — synthesis donor

- Raw strength: memorable architectural image and strong late-platform body placement.
- Hyper-Skeptic dissent: personification risks making the hour “kind” instead of changing labor.
- Refinement donor: keep the downward-cent pitch bend and footfall authority transfer; remove the kneeling-hour title from finals and donate its public-threshold proof to the harmless-blank synthesis.
- Cut reason: weaker plain-language explanation of who carries live work.

## 6. Pass the Downbeat Round — 80/100 — synthesis donor

- Raw strength: clearest rhythmic commons and strongest non-martyr line.
- Hyper-Skeptic dissent: “downbeat commons” is concept language wearing a pop hook.
- Refinement donor: preserve the missing beat-one process and discrete action entries; merge those with the spatial load hook from Candidate 4.
- Cut reason: title and refrain are more explanatory than sung.
