### Style

Adult female contralto A3–D5 and lyric mezzo B3–F5 carry dry chest-to-mix tones, precise neutral-English diction, breath-grain edges, and distinct consonant attacks. Signature Near/Dear Gate at 2:16: paired vocal harmonizers drift inward, stop one beating partial short of unison, and the clipped digital-harpsichord grid loses a note. Contralto stays close center-left; mezzo answers close center-right; refrains widen while each lead remains dry.
