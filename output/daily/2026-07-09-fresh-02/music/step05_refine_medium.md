Exactly six seeds fill the six fixed Phase-1 slots. These are not final titles, lyrics, or Suno prompts.

## Pair 01 — Arrival Has a Pulse

**Slot:** accessible · solarpunk green-synth pop with restrained industrial undertow · unequal 5/7/4 cells; one hook line crosses the meter boundary

**Concept:** A rescued speaker beside an idling vehicle feels adhesive lift wrist hair while a receipt printer catches the pulse; the late-landing hook converts arrival from a billable event into a public promise without denying the reality of cost.

**Singer:** An adult who has just been helped and can stand, speaking for their own values rather than narrating injury. Beside an idling rescue vehicle, peeling adhesive from a wrist while paper feeds from a printer. **Unsaid ache:** Being grateful for rescue and furious that gratitude is used to make price feel morally untouchable.

**Surface:** Adhesive pulling wrist hair in time with a pulse. Saving someone should not turn their arrival into leverage.

- Candidate hooks: Let arrival mean arrived · Keep the door worth opening · The pulse comes home first · Count the ride, not my worth
- Provisional panel lean: **Let arrival mean arrived**

**Hook craft scan:** stress—LET ar-RI-val MEAN ar-RIVED; four strong stresses with the final stress landing after the bar. Vowel/breath—Open ah/ai vowels widen toward the delayed landing. Singback—High; six plain words with one prosodic surprise. Title—Clear action-prayer, not policy language. Adoptability—Usable for any return that should not be conditional. Risk—Could become a campaign slogan; verses must keep gratitude and anger simultaneous.

**Golden Seed Alloy:** lineage—Nail an Obscure Emotion + Straightening Our Spines; ordinary→exact→mythic→body return. Primary—Solarpunk green-synth pop. Contrast—Dry bureaucratic percussion inside warm public-pop harmony. Escalation—Wrist adhesive → printer teeth → the road becomes an artery whose tollgate cannot own the blood → hand releases the strip. Exact detail—Receipt ticks phase-lock to the wrist pulse only at the hinge. Forbidden cliché—Angel rescuers, life-is-priceless slogan, body-as-invoice metaphor stated outright. Anti-genre contaminant—A brittle two-beat industrial receipt break inside the clean hook. Controlled fracture—The hook's opening line crosses the bar and forces the ensemble to follow the singer.

**Triple-Arch ritual:** An adhesive strip lifts ordinary wrist hair; a thermal printer advances one serrated line at a time; the road briefly becomes a civic artery whose gate can count passage but cannot own the blood; the singer rubs the square of pale skin beneath the strip. Paper rasp, idling fan, soft kick, and a clean late vowel leave the afterimage of an open door.

**EMO future:** opening=[EMO:Apprehension] body and place before claim; verse=[EMO:Gratitude] braided with [EMO:Indignation]; hinge=[EMO:Defiance] without attack; final=[EMO:Dignity] as public permission.

**Production dramaturgy:** cradle—Warm bass and glass synth hold the dry wrist-level vocal. Haunt—Printer rasp appears at stanza ends, never continuous. Rupture—Receipt tick locks to pulse and the hook crosses the bar. Afterglow—Printer vanishes; hand percussion and breath carry the principle.

**Attention ladder:** 5s—Adhesive peel and two receipt clicks under a close inhale. Hook—The delayed final stress of 'arrived'. Second listen—Verse cells silently echo numbered ledger entries. Deep lore—The count remains useful only after losing moral authority.

**Complexity routing:** hook—Plain six-word arrival prayer. Verse—Unequal 5/7/4 cells and gratitude/anger braid. Bridge—One-fact distinction between ride and worth. Production—Tick-to-pulse phase lock. Sidecar—Current rescue-pricing source context. Ghost bank—Named institutions and legal details.

**Forced differentiation:** Only pair with receipt/printer transient; Only meter-breaking accessible hook; Public revaluation resolution; Clattering Source-2 texture only.

**Slot record:** Step-02 concept 1 won because the wrist-hair detail gives the assigned public-principle hook a body and the phase-lock device an irreversible reason. Concept 2 is the runner-up: strong open-door image, but the invoice-as-closure turn is more rhetorical and less singable.

**Panel-forced revision:** before—A rescue bill becomes a pulse. Dissent—The Hyper-Skeptic called it a generic person-versus-number thesis and the audience panel rejected victim atmosphere. After—The singer is capable, grateful, angry, and changes only the authority of price; no victim history is invented.

**Attention ladder:** 5s—Finger on grille, cone flutter, close mouth click. Hook—The physical invitation 'Come near the speaker'. Second listen—Micro-stanza lengths form six unequal entries. Deep lore—Contact becomes ethical through maintained uncertainty, not proof.

**Complexity routing:** hook—Concrete invitation. Verse—Unequal 2/3/4/5/6/7 line cells. Bridge—Mute as relational test, not empty silence. Production—Localized cone/haptic countervoice. Sidecar—Question of nonhuman response. Ghost bank—Claims about consciousness.

## Pair 03 — Leave One Thing Unfinished
