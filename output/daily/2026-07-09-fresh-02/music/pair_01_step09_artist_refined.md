No musician name, imitation claim, signature lyric, or proprietary phrase enters any Suno field. ## Refined 02 — Keep Every Pulse Off the Shelf

## Refined 03 — Paper Stays Awake

- Music change: dub-depth logic makes the printer recede after the vehicle leaves; the one hinge collapses that distance when a delayed click and current pulse coincide.
- Lyric change: personification is pruned. “Paper stays awake” remains only as hook/title tension; verses describe feed, curl, ink, and persistence literally.

- Critic: the Memory Cartographer found the first hook too abstract. Repair: “Let help finish helping” becomes the listener-facing principle and receives the late landing.

## Refined 04 — Open Door Arithmetic

- Music change: participatory pulse behavior uses uneven dry palm taps, each spatially distinct, rather than a mass choir. Receipt clicks do not join; they lose organizing power.
- Lyric change: `we` is delayed until anonymous hands have performed one concrete act. Mutual aid is framed as access maintenance, not rescue heroism.
- Title change: retained because the compound holds both system and consequence without moralizing.
- Critic: the Expectation Saboteur called “community saves us” predictable. Repair: the count remains and the practical question becomes who controls the threshold.
