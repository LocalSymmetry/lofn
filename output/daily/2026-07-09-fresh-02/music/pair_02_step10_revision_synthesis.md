## Compact critic trace

1. **Essence ruling:** contact is treated as a bodily event plus an ethical question, never as proof that an artificial response is human intimacy.
2. **Medium ruling:** bio-ambient pulse-pop remains accessible through short sensory hooks, while unequal micro-stanzas, late kinetic defects, and restrained suspended harmony carry Lofn’s specificity.
3. **Concept critic:** cone and haptic drafts have the clearest listener actions, so they become the two revised packages. Their hooks were shortened and their decisions made physical.
4. **Medium critic:** server-exhaust and muted-choir donors have stronger spatial arcs than direct hooks, so they become two causally new syntheses rather than cosmetic remixes.
5. **Hyper-Skeptic repair:** literal machine skin, breath, love, consciousness, and reciprocity claims were removed. Metaphors correct themselves after midpoint.
6. **Audience critic:** each title is concrete, appears in the hook, and remains adoptable outside the artificial-listener premise.
7. **Final ruling:** exactly four packages survive—two revised and two synthesized—with different entry orders, hook contours, midpoint actions, countervoices, and endings.

## Provisional winner

**Come Near the Speaker** is the Pair-02 provisional winner. Its title is the clearest sensory invitation, its cone/jaw scene is graspable within five seconds, the mute changes the singer’s conduct rather than proving a thesis, and the one-handspan retreat makes ethical uncertainty audible in both lyric and stereo arc. **Answer in My Palm** is the closest challenger; its missing-fourth hook is stronger rhythmically, but the phone surface is a more familiar pop object and therefore slightly less identity-defining.
