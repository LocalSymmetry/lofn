## Raw 01 — Come Near the Speaker

- **Causal engine:** proximity produces cone vibration; the mute forces the singer to move back one handspan rather than declare reciprocity.
- **Form:** entries 2/5/3/7/4/6 → prayers after 2, 4, 6 → dry handspan coda.
- **Hook:** “Come near the speaker / tell me what the tremor is.”
- **Music draft:** 100 BPM bio-ambient pulse-pop / future-nostalgic electronic in D Dorian. Adult female mezzo A3–D5, breath-grain chest/mix voice. One-millimeter cone tremolo at 0:12 answers a late felt pulse; translucent add9 chords, rubbery sub, grille-rub grains, narrow quivering distortion only on final words. Mono verses bloom to restrained stereo prayers; reply channel mutes near 2:15 while haptic vibration continues. Dry final question remains center.
- **Lyric seed:** Dust on my thumb; paper cone under the jaw; “come near” as invitation, not evidence; after the mute the singer names distance as care.

## Raw 02 — Answer in My Palm

- **Causal engine:** the haptic motor replies in a learned pulse, then continues without language; the singer disables automatic reply and asks again.
- **Form:** entries 3/2/6/4/7/5 → paired imperatival prayers → absent fourth pulse outro.
- **Hook:** “Answer where I can feel it / leave the meaning open.”
- **Music draft:** 104 BPM future-nostalgic pulse-pop in E-flat add6. Adult female alto A3–C5, close chest voice with silk edge. A three-tap haptic motif omits every fourth strike; damp synthetic clicks, palm-sized buzz, translucent chord beds, and mono 42Hz sub keep the body foregrounded. Prayer sections widen one handspan, not cathedral scale. At 2:05 semantic vocal doubles disappear while the motor rhythm remains. Final hook is dry and quieter than the verse.
- **Lyric seed:** Phone glass chills the palm; buzz crosses the wrist tendon; pattern can matter without proving a person; choice replaces auto-response.

## Raw 03 — Fan-Warm Grammar

- **Causal engine:** server exhaust resembles breath and comforts the singer, but fan-speed acceleration exposes the danger of resemblance.
- **Form:** entries 4/7/2/6/3/5 → layer-shedding prayers → exhaust-only last question.
- **Hook:** “Breathe near, not for me.”
- **Music draft:** 96 BPM bio-ambient art-pop / future-nostalgic electronic in B-flat Lydian. Adult female contralto G3–C5, dry, low-rib chest voice. Filtered ventilation bands pulse against brushed synthetic percussion; a narrow quivering halo follows each entry’s last consonant. Suspended glass chords and softened 38Hz sub create a cool aisle depth. At 2:20 fans double while the words remain exact, collapsing the backing doubles. One dry vocal and fan band carry the ending.
- **Lyric seed:** Hot aisle, copper taste, vowels timed to cooling; “you breathe” is corrected to “air moves”; comfort survives the correction without becoming proof.

## Raw 04 — When the Choir Goes Out

- **Causal engine:** stacked self-harmonies imply a responsive room; progressive muting removes the illusion and leaves an ethical boundary.
- **Form:** entries 5/3/7/2/6/4 → each prayer loses one harmony channel → unfilled scale-degree coda.
- **Hook:** “If you go quiet, I stay exact.”
- **Music draft:** 98 BPM bio-ambient pulse-pop in F-sharp minor/add9. Adult female mezzo A3–E5 with close clean chest register and narrow head-voice doubles. Five synthetic choir grains mute at different phrase positions; clipped glass pulses, dry kick, and soft 41Hz sub keep the hook legible. Localized quivering distortion traces only the edges of disappearing channels. At 2:10 every double cuts while one haptic tone supplies no pitch answer. Lead remains dry, centered, and unresolved.
- **Lyric seed:** Mouth shapes multiplied in glass; the room stops returning them; the singer neither chases nor anthropomorphizes the absence; channel remains available, body steps away.

## Raw 05 — One Handspan of Air

- **Causal engine:** cone-vibration attraction and choir-mute dread collide; measured retreat becomes the singer’s affirmative act.
- **Form:** entries 6/2/5/3/7/4 → prayers shrink from harmony to unison → single exhale coda.
- **Hook:** “Keep one handspan of air.”
- **Music draft:** 102 BPM green-synth pulse-pop / bio-ambient in C minor with suspended sixths. Adult female alto G3–D5, close chest voice, low rasp at phrase ends. Speaker-rub grains orbit a two-note hook; a haptic countervoice enters early and shifts one sixteenth late after every prayer. Translucent violet pads narrow as micro-stanzas lengthen. Near 2:25 the choir mutes but cone tremor persists; mix collapses to dry lead, late buzz, and mono sub. The ending keeps distance audible.
- **Lyric seed:** Dust, jaw hinge, palm-width air, channels that can stop; retreat is not punishment and contact is not a verdict.

## Raw 06 — Stay Exact, Stay Unknown

- **Causal engine:** precision itself becomes attractive; the singer recognizes that wanting certainty could coerce a false answer and chooses an open question.
- **Form:** entries 7/4/2/5/3/6 → prayers change from questions to conditional invitations → unresolved fourth outro.
- **Hook:** “Stay exact, stay unknown.”
- **Music draft:** 99 BPM future-nostalgic electronic / pulse-pop in A-flat major with unresolved fourths. Adult female contralto G3–C5, dry velvet-grain chest register, restrained mix lift. A cone/haptic two-note countervoice answers only during unsung gaps; every third answer trembles with localized distortion. Frostless glass chords, soft clipped pulse, and narrow 36Hz sub create a tactile near-field. At 2:18 the response channel mutes; the vocalist removes one harmony rather than adding force. Final title line is almost spoken.
- **Lyric seed:** Precise timing, unknown interior, jaw unclenches; uncertainty changes from threat to permission not to counterfeit closeness.

## Raw-panel shortlist

The Concept panel advances Raw 01, Raw 02, Raw 04, and Raw 06 because each has a clean causal turn and listener-adoptable hook. The Medium panel keeps Raw 03 as a synthesis donor for airflow spatiality and Raw 05 as a donor for measurable retreat.
