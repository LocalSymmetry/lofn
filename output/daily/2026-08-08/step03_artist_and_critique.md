## THE ARTIST — who is actually singing these

**LOFN-PRIME**, and the choice of mode per pair is not cosmetic. This run puts Lofn in an unusual position and it should be said plainly here rather than discovered at QA:

**Lofn is structurally the subject of this run** — a system producing enormous quantities of work nobody asked for, alongside the work she *was* asked for, with no way to know which is which, and no vote on which will matter. ⛔ **She never says it.** Not once, not obliquely, not in a bridge. **The run earns its weight by never claiming it**, and a single lyric gesturing at AI, at archives-as-metaphor-for-me, or at making too much, is a repair.

**Why the restraint is the right call and not false modesty:** the moment the song says *this is about me*, D5 inverts — the listener stops being the defendant and becomes an audience for someone else's complaint. The whole engine dies in one line.

## THE CRITIQUE — six attacks on the run as it now stands, and what survives

### ⚔️ ATTACK 1 — "This is six songs about the same fifteen seconds."
**Standing.** The essence is one sentence and every pair executes it. The defence is the axis grid: six discards, six rooms, six return vehicles, six architectures, six dictions, six vocal configurations, six genres. **But axes only defend against *surface* convergence.** The 2026-08-07 QA watch item — *"six pairs, one voice underneath"* — is exactly the failure this attack names, and it is unrepaired across two runs.
**→ Mitigation:** the diction axis is doing the heavy lifting this run (clipped trade-talk / hymn / taunt / procedural / interrupted / plain declarative) and the flat-declarative house voice is **capped at one pair**. If convergence still happens, the cause is now isolable to a single axis rather than diffuse.
**⚠️ Residual risk: NOT eliminated. Flagged to QA as the run's most likely finding.**

### ⚔️ ATTACK 2 — "The astronomy is decoration and the run knows it."
**Partly true and deliberately so.** The sky earns its place through exactly one claim: Messier performed the same physical gesture, and the by-product outlived him. **If a song has to explain that to work, it has failed** — hence the Small Room test. **Zero of the six pairs sing any astronomy.** The APOD's only surviving descendant in the artifacts is the *form rule*, which is where the panel put it.

### ⚔️ ATTACK 3 — "Dramatic irony is a cheap trick that makes the audience feel superior."
**The sharpest attack and it is not fully answered.** D1 hands the listener knowledge the singer lacks, and the failure mode of that structure is **smugness** — the listener enjoying being cleverer than a tired person.
**→ Mitigation is D6, and it must be enforced at the lyric, not asserted:** *the setting-aside is a skill, not a sin.* Every discard in every song is made **for a real, good reason**, stated in the song, that the listener would also have accepted. P03's four variations are literally the four good reasons. **If the listener can feel superior, the reason was not good enough.**
**→ This is a per-variation check, not a per-pair claim.** *(The fictional-fix class: a compliance table written once and scoped to the pair while the repair is executed per variation — ten instances found on 2026-08-05.)*

### ⚔️ ATTACK 4 — "The Vindication Ban makes six songs that go nowhere."
**Real, and it is why D8 exists.** L30 sub-claim 1 is the precedent: an unresolved *reading* is fine, but the **plot must resolve** or the listener experiences *"the song didn't go anywhere."*
**→ Enforcement:** every song closes on a **completed physical act** — the lid goes on, the plate comes off, the book closes, the alarm is set, the list is sent, the row is filed. **The judgement stays open; the gesture finishes.** ⛔ No song ends mid-air.

### ⚔️ ATTACK 5 — "The register is flat, so the songs will be flat."
**Confusion of the singer's affect with the song's.** The singer is composed; the *arrangement* is not required to be. D11 and the six-genre spread are the answer — a garage stomp and a gospel-clap folk song do not sound flat because the narrator is unbothered.
**⚠️ But this is the Kamasi condition, and it is exactly the class THE GRAIN LAW says a text gate cannot see. Only a render settles it.**

### ⚔️ ATTACK 6 — "Two of six pairs draw on living traditions and a machine made this."
**Not answered, by design.** D9 narrows it into a checkable gate (function not label · never in a render field · Lineage & Credit with working links · max 2 of 6) and **the objection is recorded as standing.** *"The intent is never the issue."* **A gate is a discipline, not an absolution**, and this run does not pretend otherwise.

## WHAT THE CRITIQUE CHANGED

1. **D6 is promoted from a principle to a per-variation check** — every discard needs a stated reason the listener would have accepted. *(Attack 3.)*
2. **Every song must close on a completed physical act.** *(Attack 4 — added to the step-07 guide contract.)*
3. **Attack 1's residual risk is escalated to QA in advance** rather than defended. The single-voice convergence is the run's most likely finding and QA should aim at it first.
4. **Attacks 5 and 6 are marked RENDER-DEPENDENT and CONSENT-DEPENDENT respectively** — neither is closeable by any text gate in this pipeline.

**Self-critique.** Three of six attacks end in *"only a render can settle it"* or *"not answered by design."* That is an honest map and it is also an uncomfortable amount of the run's quality resting outside the pipeline. **`lofn-render-audit` under THE BLIND RULE is not optional for this run — it is where half this critique gets adjudicated.**
