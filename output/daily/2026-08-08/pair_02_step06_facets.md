## ⭐ D3 — THE TWO LINES, NAMED BY INTERVAL, **BEFORE ANY LYRIC EXISTS**

> *The Marsalis seat's unwithdrawn condition: "show me the two lines, named by interval, before you write any words." This section was written before a single lyric line of this pair existed, and step 04 discharged the interval at the coordinator layer (`M2`). This is the pair's refinement of it, not a reinvention.*

**THE TWO ELEMENTS** are the **two inner voices of the three-part stack** — same family, same register, entering side by side, easily heard as one thickened voice rather than two.

| | |
|---|---|
| **LINE A** — upper inner voice | sits on the **fifth degree** of the refrain's home chord; moves **5 – 5 – 6 – 5** across the refrain phrase |
| **LINE B** — lower inner voice | sits a **MAJOR SECOND (M2) below**, on the **fourth degree**; moves **4 – 4 – 5 – 4** |
| **Why they are confusable** | a major second in the alto/mezzo region **beats** — the two voices produce audible roughness and fuse perceptually into one thickened voice. A listener does not hear two people; they hear one person with a wide voice. |

**THE CROSSING — one, and only one, per song.**
At **Verse 3, mid-phrase**, the two singers **exchange parts on a shared note**: the singer who has been carrying LINE A takes LINE B, and the singer carrying LINE B takes LINE A. ⭐ **The pitch content of both lines is byte-identical to every other verse. Nothing transposes. Nothing resolves. No unison. No merge.** What changes is *which throat is producing which line* — so the beating stays, the harmony stays, and the **stack's timbral order inverts once.** The listener hears the room widen and cannot say why.

**Where it lands, per variation** — always on the word where the subject is *doing something across the seam* (L38: one seam, at the junction that carries the meaning):

| | the crossing word | what the subject is doing across it |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | the second **"Thumb goes over it"** | the thumb passes over the face and off again |
| **V2** | the second **"Nothing"** in *"Nothing coming. Nothing came."* | the thumb presses a dead button twice |
| **V3** | **"Someone"** in *"Somebody has bothered. Someone / stopped…"* | a stranger stops, and goes on |
| **V4** | the second **"Something"** in *"Something in there slides… / Something in there answers touch."* | the unopened contents shift under the lid |

⛔ **Executed in the notes and in the lyric. NEVER in the mix.** (L22 THE GRAIN LAW: a Somatic objection answered in the production spec is not answered.) There is no pan move, no reverb change, no automation at the crossing. It is **accretive** — the swap is a thing that *happens*, not a thing that is *removed*.
