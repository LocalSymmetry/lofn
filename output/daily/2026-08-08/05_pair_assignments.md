# THE SIX PAIRS

## ⭐ P01 — THE END OF THE REEL
**ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — documentary, warm, unhurried)**

**Concept.** A tape operator sits in a van that is not going anywhere, outside a session, at the end of a fourteen-hour day, logging takes. The last take on the reel was recorded for one reason: to mark where the reel ended. Nobody played anything. It is thirty seconds of the room — the air conditioning, someone's chair, a cough. She writes **n/g** beside it and closes the book. **It is the only take on the reel with the room in it.**

**The crossing (D3 — name the two lines by interval before writing a word).** Two figures in the same register: **the hum she does under her breath while writing**, and **a hum already on the tape** from the amplifier. Same pitch class, a **minor third apart at the crossing**, entering side by side and easily confused. At one moment — second A-section of the AABA — hers passes through the tape's and **neither bends**. No unison, no resolution.

**The wince (D11).** She writes *n/g*. The listener has just spent thirty seconds inside the take.

**Variation angles** *(derived from THIS pair's concept — the four things she could be doing while she writes):*
- **V1 · THE LOG BOOK** — she is writing. The song is the handwriting.
- **V2 · THE DOOR OPEN** — someone else comes to the van; she keeps writing through the conversation.
- **V3 · WHAT THE ROOM DID** — the take itself, from the tape's point of view, with her voice arriving only at the end.
- **V4 · DRIVING HOME** — after; the hum is still in her, and she cannot place it.

**Eligibility target ≥5/7.** Body in the first thirty seconds ✅ · adoptable wordless hook ✅ · vast TAM (everyone has finished a shift) ✅ · one specificity moment ✅ · cognitive ease (AABA, warm) ✅ · vocal co-discovery ⚠️ *bounded by D1 — she may discover the hum is stuck in her head; she may never discover what it means* · sonic threshold ✅.

## ⭐ P02 — THE TUB
**ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — communal, hymn-shaped)**

**Concept (S1 anchor: Public Domain Review — *Photographs of Atlantic City Sand Sculpture, ca. 1880–1920*).** Somebody is clearing a parent's house. On the kitchen table after the good light has gone: a plastic tub of things that are **not anything** — a laminated card, a key to a car that is gone, and a photograph of a sand sculpture on a beach, taken by a stranger, of a thing that was always going to be washed away that night. **The photograph is the only thing left of the sculpture and it is in the tub of things that are not anything.** They put the lid back on and put it on the shelf. Autumn, they'll do it properly. They won't.

**The crossing.** Two voices in the three-part harmony sit a **major second apart** — close enough to beat against each other, easily heard as one thickened voice. At the third verse they **swap parts mid-phrase** and cross once. Neither line changes; the listener hears the room widen and cannot say why.

**The wince.** The refrain is a hymn refrain about keeping. **The lid goes back on. Nothing is kept.** *(D8: the song does not end with the object being kept — it ends with the tub back on the shelf and the singer leaving the room.)*

**Variation angles** *(the four things in the tub):*
- **V1 · THE LAMINATED CARD** — a bus pass or a rota, with a face on it.
- **V2 · THE KEY** — to a car that was sold in 2009.
- **V3 · THE PHOTOGRAPH OF THE SAND** — the stranger's beach picture. *(The pair's strongest card.)*
- **V4 · THE LID** — the tub itself; four verses in which nothing is taken out.

**Eligibility ≥5/7.** ✅ across; hook is a genuine communal singable refrain.
⛔ **D9 applies.** Function, not label. Lineage & Credit with links.

## ⭐ P03 — THE ROTA
**ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · LOFN-PRIME (INDIGNATION mode — bratty, chanting, blown out)**

**Concept.** The person who organises the thing — the rota, the group chat, the shift swap, the after-work drink — is in a back office at closing, doing it for the ninth time this year, and leaves one name off. **For a good reason.** They're quiet. They wouldn't want to. Last time they said no. Every reason is true. The song is a taunt, second person, delivered by someone who is *good at this*, and the person left off **never finds out** *(D4)*.

**The crossing.** The **shouted pre-chorus** and the **sung chant** occupy the same register — a fourth apart, close enough to be mistaken for one voice getting louder. At the second chant they cross: the shout goes under the sung line and comes out the other side. Neither is altered. **The song does not get bigger at the crossing** — that is the whole trick.

**The wince.** The taunt's target is ambiguous for two verses and resolves in the last chant to **the singer**, who does not notice. *(D1 — the singer never arrives at the insight. The chant's three-word answer means one thing when the room says it and another when the listener hears it, and only the listener hears the second meaning.)*

**Variation angles** *(the four good reasons):*
- **V1 · THEY WOULDN'T WANT TO** — the kindest reason, and the most used.
- **V2 · LAST TIME THEY SAID NO** — the evidence-based reason.
- **V3 · THERE WASN'T ROOM** — the logistical reason; a table of six.
- **V4 · I DIDN'T THINK** — the honest one, and the shortest song.

**Eligibility ≥5/7.** Body ✅ · adoptable chant ✅ · vast TAM ✅ · cognitive ease ✅ · sonic threshold ✅.
⭐ **This pair carries the run's INDIGNATION requirement in the accessible arm**, which is where it is hardest and where it counts.

## ⭐ P04 — BELOW THE THRESHOLD
**AMBITIOUS · NEWS · LOFN-PRIME (INDIGNATION mode — cold, procedural, no heat)**

**Concept (S1 anchor: USGS, today — fifteen quakes at magnitude four and a half or above, one flagged significant).** A duty analyst in a corridor between two doors, filing the day's events. Fifteen rows. **One clears the bar and gets a notification, a map, a name.** The other fourteen get a row. Somewhere under one of the fourteen a person felt the floor move and told somebody and was believed by nobody, because there is no row for that. Her own measurement came out **wrong** — below the threshold by a rounding — and she files it correctly, because filing it correctly is her job and she is good at it *(D6)*.

**The crossing.** Two tones in the same register: the **alert tone that fires** and the **alert tone that does not**. Identical timbre, a **semitone apart**. At the single 4/4 refrain inside the 7/8, the un-fired tone passes through the fired one. Neither changes pitch. *(Executed in the notes, never in the mix — D3, L22.)*

**The wince.** She rounds correctly and the song agrees with her.

⭐ **This is the pair carrying the ONE SUNG NUMBER.** At the hinge, one figure, **spelled out in words**, responded to and not recited. Nothing else numeric is sung anywhere in this pair.

**Variation angles** *(four positions on the same corridor):*
- **V1 · THE ROUNDING** — the arithmetic itself, sung as a procedure.
- **V2 · THE ONE THAT CLEARED** — the significant one, and how ordinary it was.
- **V3 · THE CALL THAT CAME IN** — someone rang; there is no field for what they said.
- **V4 · END OF SHIFT** — the corridor, the two doors, and neither of them opening.

**Eligibility 0–3/7 by design.** Odd meter, procedural diction, no adoptable hook. This is the ambitious arm doing its job.

## ⭐ P05 — TWO MINUTES EIGHTEEN
**AMBITIOUS · NEWS · LOFN-PRIME (AWE mode — tender, spacious, unhurried)**

**Concept (S1 anchor: the total solar eclipse of 12 August 2026 — four days out; maximum totality two minutes and eighteen seconds).** Two people in a shared room the night before travelling to see it. One has been quietly building toward this for **thirty years** — has the filters, knows the second-contact timing, has never told anyone how much it matters. The other came because they were asked. On the booking, in the other's handwriting, **her name is written second.** She sees it. She thinks nothing of it. **A whole life, unseen, summarised in the order of two names, and only the listener reads it that way** *(D1, A3 = a whole life unseen)*.

**The crossing.** ⭐ **The form rule made literal.** Two mezzos, same register, deliberately confusable. Two independent strophes with different words and different melodies begin separately; **from the second minute they run simultaneously.** They cross exactly once — one line passes through the other on the same note — and **neither adjusts.** No harmony arrives. No unison. They finish in different places.

**The wince.** She is happy. She is genuinely, uncomplicatedly happy about tomorrow. *(D4 — nobody finds out, ever. The song does not hint that the other will notice.)*

**Variation angles** *(the four hours of that night):*
- **V1 · THE PACKING** — filters, a folding chair, sandwiches; the inventory is *acted*, never listed.
- **V2 · THE FORECAST** — cloud, and what each of them says about it.
- **V3 · THE HANDWRITING** — the booking page. The pair's centre of gravity.
- **V4 · THE ALARM SET FOR FOUR** — lights out; two people not sleeping, in the same register.

**Eligibility 0–3/7 by design.** Two simultaneous strophes defeat cognitive ease on purpose.
⭐ **The Kamasi condition lands here:** if the two strophes get comped into a duet, the pair's entire argument is deleted. **Flag for the render audit.**

## ⭐ P06 — NOTHING FURTHER
**AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · LOFN-PRIME (INDIGNATION mode — industrial grief, somatic)**

**Concept.** A field at the hour the light goes flat. A mast is being taken down. The contractor writes the last line in the station log — **"nothing further"** — and screws the plate off the box. *(S1 texture: today's top builder thread was a list of names being put up for sale.)*

**And then the burden.** ⭐ **The spoken burden between stanzas is the same words, in a different mouth, much later** — somebody doing the identical gesture, who has never heard of the first and never will. **The gesture repeats. Nobody is vindicated.** *(D13 made literal — the same act, two hundred years apart. D4 held: the later voice is not a witness and not a rescuer; they are just another person finishing up.)*

**The crossing.** Two low sustained elements in the same register: **the mast's own resonance in the wind**, and **a struck metal plate** an octave-and-a-fourth below the vocal. They enter together and are hard to tell apart. At the third stanza the struck note passes through the wind tone and **neither bends**. The stanzas shrink 8 → 6 → 4 → 2 lines around it.

**The wince.** *"Nothing further"* is accurate. Everything about it is accurate.

**Variation angles** *(the four things being unbolted):*
- **V1 · THE PLATE** — the call sign coming off.
- **V2 · THE LOG** — the book itself, and the last line's handwriting.
- **V3 · THE FIELD AFTERWARD** — the concrete pad, and what grows on it.
- **V4 · THE SECOND MOUTH** — the burden takes the whole song; the sung stanzas shrink to nothing.

**Eligibility 0–3/7 by design.**
⛔ **This pair spends the run's one flat-declarative allowance.** No other pair may use the house voice.

## RUNNER-UP RATIONALE (why each winner took its slot)

- **P01 over THE FERRET OF COMETS** — same interval (*the same afternoon*), same hand (*paid to sort*), but a living body and a present tense. The reel take **is** M30: recorded to mark the end of something, containing the only true record of the room.
- **P02 over THE SAND SCULPTURE** — identical S1 anchor; P02 puts a hand on the photograph. The sculpture concept had no verb.
- **P03 over THE OTHER LIST** — the institutional version had no room and no body. P03 gives the accusation a kitchen-sink scale and makes the accused the singer.
- **P04 over THE FOURTEEN** — the analyst is the hand; the victim is not the subject; **the setting-aside is a skill, not a sin** (D6). The victim version would have scolded.
- **P05 over TWENTY-EIGHT THOUSAND LIGHT-YEARS** — the distance is the *form*, not the *lyric*. Two mezzos in one room execute it without a word of astronomy.
- **P06 over THE BOOK OF NOT-COMETS** — the catalogue narrating itself is the blocked archetype. P06 keeps the two-hundred-year interval and puts a person with a screwdriver in front of it.
