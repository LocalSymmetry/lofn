## §1 — THE PANEL LOOP, RUN (steps 1–20, condensed to what actually changed)

**Steps 1–2 · Key elements.** Opening: a hum before a word, so the hook is established as material rather than as decoration. Key audio moment: the crossing at the second A of the second pass. Emotional peak: **there isn't one, and that is the design** — the peak is in the listener. Closing: a completed physical act inside the last A-section, then the hum alone.

**Step 3 · Hooks (2 per panelist, then refined).** The room converged fast and then argued about one thing.
- ⚠️ **THE MAXIMALIST (after Kamasi):** *"A wordless hum is not a hook, it is an absence with a name. You have written four songs whose chorus is a shrug."*
- **THE SMALL ROOM (after Boilen):** *"It is the most adoptable thing in the run. A stranger can do it in a car after one listen. A chorus you can only do if you remember the words is a worse hook than one you cannot help doing."*
- **THE CINEMA CARRIER (after Jarmusch):** *"One cue, repeated. The audience does the work."*
- **Moderator ruling:** the hum stands, **but it is not asked to carry the return by itself.** Every A-section closes on a fixed three-line spoken-shaped tag so the words return too. ⭐ **The Maximalist's objection is what produced the tag**, and the tag is the best thing in the pair.

**Steps 4–6 · Spectrum, structure, elements.** Literal 55 / inventive 35 / transformative 10 — deliberately low transformation, because the pair's whole argument depends on nothing seeming unusual. Structure locked to the assigned AABA 32-bar, stated three times, with the crossing at the second A of the second pass. Tempo 78, no key change, no modulation, no build by level.

**Steps 7–8 · Drafts.** Two music prompts and two lyric drafts per angle. The discarded lyric drafts all failed the same way and it is worth recording: **they explained.** Every rejected draft contained a line telling the listener what the moment meant. *(Jarmusch seat: "the moment a song tells me what it means about attention, I stop attending.")*

**Step 9 · Concept-panel critique.**
- **THE POLITICAL ENGINE (after Fela):** *"Who benefits? A woman in a van is not an institution."* → **Answered, not dodged:** nobody in the song is the villain and the song does not look for one; the defendant is the listener (D5) and the tag hands them the verdict seven times.
- ⚠️ **THE TRADITION CUSTODIAN (after Marsalis):** *"Show me the tune."* → §1 of step 06, in notes, before a word existed. **He accepts that and states his suspicion is not withdrawn.**
- **THE RAW HORN (after Mekurya):** *"The body is tired. Do not let her sound wise."* → every line is under seven words on average and half-spoken.

**Step 10 · Refinement 1.** Removed every take-number from every sung line. ⭐ **This pair spends no sung number at all** — the run's one numeric fact belongs to P04 — and "take one, take two" is a sung numeral. Replaced throughout with position language: *the tail*, *the last*, *the top of the reel*.

**Step 11 · Medium-panel critique.**
- **THE HARD SECTION (after Brenneck):** *"A sustained low hum is the first thing any machine will smooth away."* → ⭐ **The single most important production decision in this pair:** the amplifier hum is specified in the render field as a **held combo-organ note — tuned, quiet, unmoving.** Same sound, same job, written the way the generator will honour. *(step04 bans untuned drones by name.)*
- **THE GLOBAL TRIO (after Speer):** *"Reproduce the feel, not the surface."* → no instrument in the prompt is named for a tradition, and the mode is described by its intervals.

**Step 12 · Refinement 2.** Made the crossing **accretive** — a vibraphone octave is *added* at the crossing and nothing is removed. Subtractive specs get smoothed; accretive ones survive.

**Step 13 · Context & marketing critique.**
- **THE CATALOGUE NUMBER (role-anchored):** *"A number you can point at beats a name you have to interpret."* → all four titles name a physical object or a person in a place. None names an argument.
- **THE BLOG ARCHIVIST (after Shimkovitz):** *"What travels is a thing somebody found, not a thesis somebody wrote."* → the tag is the thing.

**Step 14 · Refinement 3.** Titles cut to five or six words, each appearing verbatim inside its own fixed tag.

**Step 15 · All panels.**
- ⚠️ **THE APPROPRIATION QUESTION (after Simon):** *"You have used a scene's grammar and paid it with a paragraph."* → the credit block is in `step06 §8`, it has **nine fetched-and-verified links**, three candidate links were rejected as broken, and the tradition's name is nowhere in any render field. ⛔ **He does not withdraw, and the artifact says so rather than closing the file.**
- ⚠️ **THE LATE-CAPITALISM AUDITOR (after Jameson):** *"Show me you are not consoling anyone."* → no line in any of the four hints that anyone learns anything. Checked line by line.

**Step 16 · Refinement 4.** Pre-write scan of all sung lines caught **two live defects**, both fixed before the artifact was written rather than after:

2. **`"Neither one's moving."` (V3)** — carries the number-word *one*, in a pair that spends no number. → **`"Neither's moving."`**

**Steps 17–19 · Titles.** *The Log Book On My Knee* · *Somebody At The Van Door* · *Room Tone On The Tail* · *The Hum In The Cab*. Each names a **thing**. ⛔ No persona-brand prefix. Rejected for naming an argument rather than a thing: *What Doesn't Count*, *The Only Take With The Room In It*, *Nobody Finds Out*.

**Step 20 · Final sweep.** Rhyme-scheme letters and syllable bars stripped. ## §4 — SELF-CRITIQUE

1. **The best thing in these four is the tag, and the tag was the Maximalist's idea by accident.** He attacked the wordless hook as an absence; the answer was to stop asking the hook to carry the return and give the words their own. The result is a refrain that says *it's not a take* seven times over a room the listener has been standing in. **That is the wince, and it is repeatable, and it is free.**
2. **V3 is the most beautiful and the most structurally exposed.** Six of its seven A-sections have no singer in them, and D1 is a rule about withholding *from a singer*. If she is not present, there is nobody to withhold from and the dramatic irony has no host. **The step-10 critique should attack this and I expect it to survive with a repair rather than intact.**
3. **V4 leaves the assigned room.** A4 ROOM is *a vehicle that is not moving*, and V4 spends its last third moving. Declared here, not discovered later: the room is the **inside of the van** either way, which is what the axis names; and the movement is the point, because the crossing's other half gets left behind and she can never check it again. **If a re-stat rules the axis broken, this is where to look.**
4. **The honest weakness of the whole pair is that it is quiet, level and unhurried in a run that already fears greyness.** Nothing here builds. The Maximalist withdrew his objection *conditionally* on the gap being audible, and a text gate cannot tell him whether it is. **Only a render audit closes it, and until then his objection is live and I am not claiming otherwise.**
