## §1 ⭐ D3 — THE TWO LINES, NAMED BY INTERVAL, **BEFORE ANY WORD OF LYRIC EXISTED**

> *This is the Marsalis seat's unwithdrawn condition and it is a step-06 hard requirement.* **"Show me the two lines, named by interval, before you write any words."**
> Step 04 discharged it at the coordinator layer as *"mast resonance in the wind ↔ struck plate · octave + fourth below the vocal."* **I refine it; I do not reinvent it.**

**Vocal centre: D4.** Androgynous mid-register lead, self-doubled at **D3** (an octave down).
An octave and a fourth below D4 is **A2**. Both crossing elements live there.

| | **LINE A — the mast** | **LINE B — the plate** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | the wind in the guy-wire and the tube, sounding the mast's own resonance | a steel plate struck with the shank of a screwdriver |
| Pitch | **A2**, held, unmoving for the whole song | **B♭2** at entry — a **semitone above** Line A |
| Timbre class | bowed/sustained low metal | struck low metal — **same family, same register** |
| Why they are confusable | a semitone apart in the same low band, they **beat** against each other and are heard as **one thickened tone** | |

⭐ **THE CROSSING — once, at the third stanza.** As the fixings come out the plate loses mass and is struck lower: **B♭2 → A2 → G♯2.** At the third stanza it is struck on **A2 — Line A's own note — for exactly one strike, and the two are indistinguishable.** The next strike is **G♯2**, a semitone *below*. **The wind tone does not move to meet it. The plate does not stay on it.** Neither bends. No merge, no unison held, no resolution.

**Declared interval, one line:** *`two low metal voices a semitone apart at A2 — an octave and a fourth below the D4 vocal — crossing once at unison and continuing to G♯2; neither bends.`*

**Executed in the lyric and in the notes, never in the mix (L22).** The lyric event is the third stanza, in the singer's own flat words:
> *"The plate rings what the wire rings. / Same note. There is nothing in it. / I hit it again and it drops."*
She names the coincidence and **refuses it**, on the beat, and gets on with the job. **That is D1 and D3 in the same three lines.**

⛔ **ONE SEAM (L38, N = 1) — policed.** The stanzas shrink while the burden accretes. **That is the FORM, and it is deliberately NOT staged as a second crossing:** the two curves trade places, no dramatic event is placed at their intersection, and no lyric line marks it. **The song's one crossing is the plate through the wire tone.** Recorded here so QA can check I did not smuggle in a second seam.

## §4 ⭐ THE FLAT-DECLARATIVE ALLOWANCE — HOW P06 EARNS IT

QA's standing watch item, two runs unrepaired: *"the shared flat-declarative house diction across all six pairs; six pairs, one voice underneath."* Phase 1 gives the allowance to **P06 alone**, deliberately. Every other pair has a different diction. **If I merely write the house voice, I have spent the run's one exception on nothing.**

> ⭐ **THE EARN, stated as a checkable constraint: ZERO FIGURATIVE LANGUAGE. No simile. No metaphor. No personification. No abstract noun used as an image.**
> **Every noun in every sung and spoken line is a thing a hand can touch or a thing that can be measured. Every verb is something a body does.**

That is a *harder* discipline than the house voice — the house flat-declarative is flat **and still metaphor-laden**. This one is not. It is checkable line by line by anyone, and it is why the register can carry a mast in a field at flat light without tipping into reverence.

**Measured:** zero simile markers (`like a`, `as if`, `as though`, `seems to`, `resembles`) across all four lyrics fields. The one idiom retained — *"there is nothing in it"* — is plain trade speech for *it is not significant*, and it is doing D1's work: **the singer names the crossing and dismisses it.**

⛔ **D2 — reverence is the failure mode, and a mast in a field at flat light is a strong invitation to reverence.** Refused structurally: nobody looks up; the mast is never described as tall, dark, lonely or old; there is no dusk-light adjective; the singer's only interest in the mast is that the box is **at chest height**.

## §6 — PANEL PRESSURE ON THESE FACETS *(the supplied eighteen; the Hyper-Skeptics genuinely dissent)*

**THE RAW HORN (after Mekurya):** *My style comes from one man singing standing up before a fight, with nothing under him.* Good — then the burden is the only correct instinct in this pair. **One voice, unaccompanied by melody, saying a form.** But you have put it against a doubled octave and a somatic bass. **Do not let the doubling arrive before the burden does.** Give the plain voice the first stanza alone or the burden has nothing to be plain *against*.
→ **Accepted.** The octave-down double enters with the lead but the low bass is held out of the first stanza; the burden's first entry is the first thing that is *not* melodic.

**⚠️ THE TRADITION CUSTODIAN (after Marsalis), dissenting:** I gave one condition and it was not a suggestion. **Two lines, named by interval, before any words.** §1 gives me a semitone, a unison and a semitone the other way, and it gives me the octave-and-a-fourth anchor from the medium step, so my condition is **met on the paper.** ⭐ **My suspicion is not withdrawn**, and here is where it now sits: *a semitone beat between two low metals is exactly the thing a generator smooths into one sound.* If the render returns a single low rumble with no beating in it, **there was never a second line and this pair has a concept, not a counterpoint.** Only a render audit settles it.
→ **Logged unrepaired, by name, and routed to the render audit.** It is not answerable in text and I will not pretend it is.

**⚠️ THE MAXIMALIST (after Kamasi), dissenting:** Six small grey songs, and this is the greyest. One dynamic, no build, shrinking stanzas, a man with a screwdriver. **Where is the size?** — *Then, on the form:* your burden grows while your stanzas shrink and **the total material goes up, not down.** That is the only version of restraint I accept, because it is addition. I withdraw greyness **on the same condition as the baseline: the gap must be audible.** If the render fades out at the end instead of ending on a struck object, **my objection is live again.**
→ **Accepted and made a facet (F7).** Predicted failure named in §7.3 of step 10.

**⚠️ THE APPROPRIATION QUESTION (after Simon):** Not my pair. **D9 is spent on P01 and P02.** Industrial folk — struck metal, a plain melody, wind — is broadly shared popular-music vocabulary and belongs to no specific living community that is not ours. ⭐ **But I do not accept a scope call by silence.** State it, so QA can challenge it.
→ **Stated. See step 10 §9.** No named living tradition is drawn on; no Lineage & Credit block is required for P06; the scope call is recorded, not assumed.

**THE ANTI-ARCHIVIST (after Kondo):** The contractor is **right**. Decommissioning is correct work. The plate comes off because the job is finished, not because anything is being lost. **The song must not scold her and must not pity her.**
→ **F4.** The only judgement in any P06 lyric is *"Somebody set it level"* — one worker approving of another's concrete.

**THE PRESERVATIONIST (after Prelinger):** This pair carries my line literally — **the same gesture, two hundred and sixty years apart** — and that is exactly why it is the most dangerous pair in the run. **The moment your second mouth is a reader, you have written the consolation the room banned.** Make them a stranger with their own shift and no curiosity whatsoever.
→ **F2, and the whole of §4 in step 10.**
