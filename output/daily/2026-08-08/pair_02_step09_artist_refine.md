## THE REFINEMENT PASS — five seats, and what each actually changed

**1 · THE HARD SECTION** *(after Brenneck)* — **the largest change, and it is D11.**
> *"'Recorded in a kitchen' is a noun. It will render as a reverb preset. Tell it what the room DOES."*
**Added to all four:** the singers **bleed into each other's microphones**; entries come **by ear rather than on a count**; the players are **close enough to hear each other breathe**. This is step 04's concrete definition of one-room, and the raw prompts had none of it. ⭐ **Size comes from the space, not the arrangement.**

**2 · THE MODERN ETHIOPIAN VOICE** *(after Gigi)* — **the D9 discipline, at the sentence level.**
> *"The function is: three close voices carrying one tune with nobody in front. Say THAT. Never the label."*
**Changed:** every genre claim is stated as **behaviour** — *"all three carrying the tune together"*, *"singing as one thickened voice"*, *"nobody stepping forward at any point"* — so the render field describes what the voices *do* and never what tradition they came from. The only category words retained are **folk** and **hymn**, both of which describe the form actually written.

**3 · THE BIG-BAND TRANSLATOR** *(after Gershon)* — **the chart.**
> *"Everybody knowing which bar the change lands in is what made it function."*
**Sharpened:** the crossing is stated as a **part-swap at the third verse, mid-phrase, with neither line altering** — an instruction a section can execute, not an atmosphere. ⛔ Removed any temptation to describe it as a widening or a lift.

**4 · THE SMALL ROOM** *(after Boilen)* — **the front-load.**
> *"If it needs a press release, it fails."*
**Changed:** the vocal configuration now sits in the **first sentence** of every prompt, because it is the one thing that must survive: *no lead voice at any point.* Suno weights the front, and a solo lead appearing anywhere deletes the pair.

**5 · THE CATALOGUE NUMBER** *(role-anchored)* — **findability.**
> *"A catalogue makes a thing locatable by someone who does not know its name."*
**Kept:** BPM and key stated plainly in every prompt. A number you can point at beats a mood you have to interpret. *(Digits in a style prompt are not sung; the one-fact rule does not reach them.)*
