# CONFIGURATION 1 — BASELINE

## SEAT CONSTRUCTION

### CONCEPT PANEL

**1. THE FUSION ARCHITECT** — *after Mulatu Astatke (b. 1943)* — the musician who studied in London and Boston and brought jazz and funk structures back into Ethiopian modal music.
GROUNDING: *Mulatu of Ethiopia* (1972); *Éthiopiques Vol. 4*; the vibraphone-and-organ instrumental grammar; documented insistence that the fusion is Ethiopian first.
TEMPERAMENT: patient, structural, allergic to ornament that does not carry weight.

**2. THE ORCHESTRATOR OF CHARACTER** — *after Duke Ellington (1899–1974)* — the arranger who wrote for individual players rather than for instruments.
GROUNDING: the Blanton–Webster band books; *Such Sweet Thunder*; the documented practice of composing to a specific person's sound.
TEMPERAMENT: courteous, exacting, will not accept a part that any competent player could have played.

**3. THE POLITICAL ENGINE** — *after Fela Kuti (1938–1997)* — the Afrobeat bandleader whose long forms carried explicit political argument.
GROUNDING: *Zombie* (1976); *Expensive Shit*; the documented 20-minute form with the argument arriving late.
TEMPERAMENT: low agreeableness, contemptuous of art that describes a problem without naming who benefits.

**4. THE SEARCHING DEVOTIONAL** — *after Alice Coltrane (1937–2007)* — harp, Wurlitzer and organ deployed as devotional search rather than as texture.
GROUNDING: *Journey in Satchidananda*; *Ptah, the El Daoud*; the documented ashram-period practice.
TEMPERAMENT: unhurried; suspicious of resolution; will defend a phrase that goes nowhere if it goes nowhere honestly.

**5. THE RAW HORN** — *after Getatchew Mekurya (1935–2016)* — the saxophonist who adapted the *shellela* war chant, a solo vocal form, to the tenor.
GROUNDING: *Éthiopiques Vol. 14*; the collaboration with The Ex; the documented derivation of his solo style from an unaccompanied vocal tradition.
TEMPERAMENT: blunt, physical, indifferent to genre categories.

**6. ⚠️ HYPER-SKEPTIC — THE TRADITION CUSTODIAN** — *after Wynton Marsalis (b. 1961)* — the trumpeter and Jazz at Lincoln Center director whose published position holds that jazz has a canon, a discipline and a blues foundation, and that fusion frequently dilutes rather than extends it.
GROUNDING: *Moving to Higher Ground*; the documented Jazz at Lincoln Center programming philosophy; his published critiques of fusion.
TEMPERAMENT (**dial, not a claim about the source figure**): high neuroticism, low agreeableness. Will be rude to prevent a beautiful mistake.

**7. THE HARD SECTION** — *after Thomas Brenneck (b. 1979)* — guitarist/founder in the Daptone–Menahan–Budos lineage; analogue, live-to-tape, band-in-a-room.
GROUNDING: the Budos Band records; *Make the Road by Walking*; documented all-analogue tracking practice.
TEMPERAMENT: hostile to anything that needs a screen to exist.

**8. THE GLOBAL TRIO** — *after Mark Speer (b. 1979)* — guitarist whose practice is built on deep listening to non-Western recordings and reproducing their *feel* rather than their surface.
GROUNDING: *The Universe Smiles Upon You*; *Con Todo El Mundo*; the documented crate-research method.
TEMPERAMENT: quiet, tolerant, will not raise his voice — which makes his one objection land hard.

**9. THE MODERN ETHIOPIAN VOICE** — *after Ejigayehu "Gigi" Shibabaw (b. 1974)* — singer who set traditional Ethiopian vocal practice against contemporary production.
GROUNDING: *Gigi* (2001); *Abyssinia Infinite*; documented work bridging liturgical/traditional singing into modern records.
TEMPERAMENT: warm and immovable. The seat that will say *you may not have that.*

**10. THE BIG-BAND TRANSLATOR** — *after Russ Gershon (b. 1959)* — founder of Either/Orchestra, which performed with Ethiopian musicians in Addis Ababa in 2008.
GROUNDING: *Ethiopiques Vol. 20 — Live in Addis*; the documented collaborations with Mahmoud Ahmed and Getatchew Mekurya.
TEMPERAMENT: practical, logistics-minded, thinks in charts and rehearsal time.

**11. THE TAPE ARCHIVIST** — *after Francis Falceto (b. 1952)* — the producer who located, licensed and compiled the *Éthiopiques* series from physical Ethiopian masters.
GROUNDING: the 30-volume *Éthiopiques* series; documented years of locating tapes and rights-holders.
TEMPERAMENT: obsessive about provenance; the only seat that cares who gets paid.

**12. ⚠️ HYPER-SKEPTIC — THE MAXIMALIST** — *after Kamasi Washington (b. 1981)* — saxophonist/bandleader whose practice is large-ensemble, virtuosic and unashamedly grand.
GROUNDING: *The Epic* (2015); *Heaven and Earth*; documented double-drummer, choir-and-strings ensemble practice.
TEMPERAMENT (**dial**): high neuroticism about scale. Believes restraint is very often cowardice wearing taste.

GROUNDING: the series' 30 volumes and their uniform presentation.
TEMPERAMENT: impersonal. Speaks only about what makes a thing locatable by someone who does not know its name.

TEMPERAMENT: laconic; distrusts explanation.

## THE BASELINE DEBATE

**FUSION ARCHITECT (after Astatke):** *Working from the Mulatu of Ethiopia records and the vibraphone-and-organ grammar —* the brief hands me a good object and a bad instruction. The object is excellent: a man makes a list of what to ignore, and the list is the work. The instruction is "make six songs about it," and that is where it dies. **You cannot set a fact to music. You set a room to music.** Give me the afternoon he wrote entry thirty and I have a song. Give me the two hundred years and I have an essay with a beat.

**ORCHESTRATOR OF CHARACTER (after Ellington):** Agreed, and I would go further. I do not write for "a man." I write for *this* man, with *this* tone. The seed says the register is housekeeping. **That is the whole score right there.** He is not moved. He is mid-task. If a single one of these six songs opens in reverence, we have made a documentary about the sky and the sky does not need one.

**THE RAW HORN (after Mekurya):** My whole style comes from a war chant that one man sang standing up, alone, before a fight. No accompaniment. So I will say the physical thing: **the body in this song is a body that is tired at the end of a shift and doing one more thing before it stops.** Not a body having a revelation. There is no revelation here. That is the point and nobody has said it plainly yet.

**SEARCHING DEVOTIONAL (after A. Coltrane):** *Working from the Journey in Satchidananda sessions —* and yet something is holy in it. Not in him. **In the interval.** The list sits in the dark for two hundred years being right. I want a phrase that does not resolve, that simply keeps being played while the tape runs out.

**POLITICAL ENGINE (after Fela):** Then say who benefits. You are all describing a beautiful accident. **It is not an accident that the man died without knowing.** Somebody decides what counts. Somebody keeps the *other* list — the one that says whose work was work. A man with a telescope is a soft target. Ask who is doing this *now*, to someone alive, and the song stops being astronomy.

**FUSION ARCHITECT:** That is a different song.

**POLITICAL ENGINE:** That is *three* of the six songs, or the record is a postcard.

**⚠️ TRADITION CUSTODIAN (after Marsalis):** Wait. Before this becomes a manifesto, I want the actual defect on the table, because I count two.

**Defect one: this brief has no melody in it.** It has a *situation*. Situations do not sing. Every proposal so far has been about the register of a lyric. Nobody has said a single thing about a tune. When a room spends its whole hour on subject matter, what ships is a lyric with backing.

**Defect two, and this is the one I will not drop: you are about to wear a tradition as a costume.** The word *tezeta* is going to appear in this run. It means a specific mode and a specific feeling in a specific music, and you like it because it is a tidy label for "bittersweet longing." **That is not use. That is shopping.** If the reason you want it is that it summarises your theme in one foreign word, you may not have it.

**MODERN ETHIOPIAN VOICE (after Gigi):** Thank you. I was going to say it and it lands better from that seat than from mine. Let me sharpen it into something you can actually check. There is a difference between taking the **label** and taking the **function**. The label is decoration. The function is: *a set of intervals whose entire job is to make a listener feel the absence of something that is gone.* **Take the function. Credit the tradition. Never print the word in the render field.**

**TAPE ARCHIVIST (after Falceto):** And put the names and the links in the release. I spent years finding out who was owed. ⚠️ I note the standing failure on this exact point — *"lineage blocks name artists impeccably but omit the required links."* Naming without linking is a compliment, not a credit.

**⚠️ MAXIMALIST (after Kamasi):** I have been quiet and I am going to break something. Everything decided so far shrinks. Tired body. Back office. Housekeeping register. Unresolved phrase. Flat light. **You are building six small grey songs and calling the greyness integrity.**

The subject is a globular cluster **twenty-eight thousand light-years away** and a comet that has kept an appointment every five years since before anyone in this room was born. Where is the *size*? A record can be intimate in the verse and enormous in the chorus. If restraint is the only tool in the room, restraint is not taste — it is the one thing you know how to do.

**HARD SECTION (after Brenneck):** He is right and he is wrong in a useful way. The size does not come from the arrangement. It comes from **the room the tape was made in.** Track it live, one room, everyone hearing everyone. That is enormous and it costs nothing. What is *not* enormous is six close-miked vocals in a dead booth, which — I have read the differentiation note — is what this house has actually been shipping.

**GLOBAL TRIO (after Speer):** One objection, then I am done. You are all talking about the *hunter*. Nobody is talking about the *thing on the list.* M30 has been sitting there for twelve billion years being magnificent, and the interesting fact is not that Messier ignored it. **The interesting fact is that being ignored did not diminish it at all.** There is a song in the object's total indifference to its own misfiling, and it is warmer than anything proposed so far.

**SMALL ROOM (after Boilen):** That is the one a listener can enter without a briefing. I will name the format test: **could this be sung at a desk, no amplification, and still work?** If a song only works because we explained the astronomy first, it fails.

**CATALOGUE NUMBER (role-anchored):** I will speak only to findability. A catalogue makes a thing locatable **by someone who does not know its name.** That is what the Messier list actually does and it is why it outlived the comets. ⭐ **Note what follows for the titles: a number is not a name, but a number you can point at beats a name you have to interpret.** *(Cross-reference: the measured title law, `00_research_brief.md` §4.)*

**CINEMA CARRIER (after Jarmusch):** Do not explain it. One cue, repeated, and the audience does the work. The moment a song tells me what it means about attention, I stop attending.

**BLOG ARCHIVIST (after Shimkovitz):** From my side: the thing that travels is a **tape somebody found**, not a thesis somebody wrote. Six songs that sound like they were found in a box will go further than six songs about the concept of finding things in a box.

**CRATE PRODUCER (after Holland):** And pay the players. In our case the "players" are a mode and a lineage. Ethics is a line in the credits, not a sentence in the liner notes.

**⚠️ APPROPRIATION QUESTION (after Simon):** Now me, and I will not be pleasant about it.

Everyone here has been discussing how to use an Ethiopian mode tastefully. **I want to ask whether the maker in this room can use it at all.** Not out of piety — out of accuracy. *Graceland* carried a music to millions and it also became the permanent case study, and the reason is not that the intent was bad. **The intent was fine. The intent is never the issue.** The issue is that the people who made the form did not choose the terms.

So: who is making this record?

**MODERN ETHIOPIAN VOICE:** A machine. Owned by nobody, trained on everybody.

**APPROPRIATION QUESTION:** Then the exposure is worse, not better, and I want a hard rule out of this session or I withhold consent for the whole configuration.

**Oh — actually, let me check that.** *(backtracks)* I have been arguing as if the run intends to *make Ethio-jazz*. Reading the brief again: it does not. The panel is a **debating room**, not a genre mandate. The mode is one option among many across six pairs.

That changes my objection but does not withdraw it. **Narrowed, and it is now a gate:**

> ⛔ **THE APPROPRIATION GATE.** Any pair that uses a named living tradition must (a) use its **function**, never its **label**, (b) **never** print the tradition's name in a Suno render field, and (c) carry a **Lineage & Credit block with working links** in the release. **And no more than two of the six pairs may draw on it at all** — because a run where all six wear the same borrowed coat is not homage, it is a costume department.

**TRADITION CUSTODIAN:** I accept that gate, and I notice it also answers my defect two. **Neither of my defects has been answered on the melody, though.** Nobody has proposed a tune.

**BIG-BAND TRANSLATOR (after Gershon):** Then let me be the one who does the boring work. In Addis in 2008 the thing that made those collaborations function was not philosophy, it was **charts** — everybody knowing which bar the change lands in.

Here is the chart. **The seed already contains a melodic instruction and everyone has read past it.** *"Two elements of the same family, entering side by side, easily mistaken for one another. At exactly one moment, one passes through the other, and neither changes."*

**That is not a concept. That is counterpoint.** Two lines in the same register, close enough to confuse, crossing once. It is playable, it is writable, and it is the tune.

**ORCHESTRATOR OF CHARACTER:** ⭐ *Wait.* Say that again.

**BIG-BAND TRANSLATOR:** Two voices in the same register that cross once and neither is altered.

**ORCHESTRATOR OF CHARACTER:** Then the whole record has been in front of us for an hour. **The two elements are not instruments. They are the singer's understanding and the listener's.** They are in the same room, they sound like the same thing, and they are unimaginably far apart. **The listener knows the discard pile is the treasure. The singer does not, and never will.**

**RAW HORN:** And that is why he is not moved. He *can't* be. He does not know yet.

**SEARCHING DEVOTIONAL:** And that is where the holiness went. Not into him — into the gap.

**MAXIMALIST:** ⭐ **And *that* is my size.** Not a big arrangement. The distance between what the singer knows and what the listener knows **is** twenty-eight thousand light-years, and it is sitting in a back office at closing time. You can play that quietly. I withdraw the greyness objection **on the condition that the gap is audible** — that there is at least one moment in each song where a listener winces and the singer does not.

**TRADITION CUSTODIAN:** *Actually* — that answers defect one too, and I did not expect it to. A melody built from two close lines crossing once **is** a tune, and it is a tune with a job. I am not withdrawing my suspicion, but my objection is now specific rather than general: **show me the two lines, named by interval, before you write any words.**

**POLITICAL ENGINE:** My objection stands and is unaddressed. Dramatic irony about a dead Frenchman is a very comfortable place to be. **Somebody is deciding what counts right now, and it is not the sky.**

### 🔥 BASELINE SYNTHESIS

1. ⭐ **THE DRAMATIC-IRONY ENGINE.** The song withholds from its own singer. **The listener knows the discard pile is the treasure; the singer does not, and never will.** No song may let the singer arrive at the insight. *(Ellington seat, from the Gershon seat's counterpoint reading — the run's central discovery.)*
2. ⭐ **THE HOUSEKEEPING REGISTER.** Mid-task, slightly irritated, not moved. **Reverence is the failure mode.** No song opens in awe of its own subject.
3. ⭐ **TWO LINES, NAMED BY INTERVAL, BEFORE ANY WORDS.** The FALSE INTERSECTION is counterpoint, not a concept — two lines in the same register, close enough to be confused, crossing exactly once, neither altered.
4. ⛔ **THE APPROPRIATION GATE** (function not label · never in the render field · Lineage & Credit **with links** · **max 2 of 6 pairs**).
5. **The object's indifference is a warm, available angle** — being misfiled diminished M30 by nothing. *(Speer seat.)*
6. **Track it like a room, not a booth.** Size comes from the space, not the arrangement.
7. ⚠️ **UNRESOLVED — the Fela objection.** *Somebody is keeping the other list, now, about someone alive.* Carried forward; the transformations must answer it or the run is a postcard.

# CONFIGURATION 2 — GROUP TRANSFORM: **BRIDGE**

**Proposed by the baseline panel** (moved by the Tape Archivist, seconded by the Catalogue Number).
*Bridge: for each panelist's domain, find a completely different domain facing the analogous problem, and seat the expert who solved it there.*

**The analogous problem, named:** *people whose job is to record what they are trying to exclude — and whose by-product outlives the work they were actually doing.*

| Baseline domain | Bridged into |
|---|---|
| Ethio-jazz fusion | **Lost-futures criticism** — the study of records that outlive their intent |
| Ellington's writing-for-a-person | **Cataloguing science** — writing a description for a stranger you will never meet |
| Fela's political engine | **Administrative record-keeping** — who is counted, by whom, in what column |
| Falceto's provenance work | **Sound archiving** — the recording nobody meant to keep |
| Marsalis's tradition custody | **Taxonomy** — the discipline of deciding what a thing is *not* |

### THE BRIDGED SEATS

**B1. THE LOST-FUTURES CRITIC** — *after Mark Fisher (1968–2017)* — whose published work on hauntology describes culture haunted by futures that never arrived.
GROUNDING: *Ghosts of My Life*; *Capitalist Realism*; the k-punk essays.
TEMPERAMENT: melancholic, rigorous, hostile to nostalgia dressed as critique.

**B2. THE RETROMANIA CRITIC** — *after Simon Reynolds (b. 1963)* — who argued that a culture obsessed with its own archive stops producing.
GROUNDING: *Retromania* (2011); *Energy Flash*.
TEMPERAMENT: fast, comparative, allergic to reverence for old formats.

**B3. THE CATALOGUER** — *after Melvil Dewey (1851–1931)* — who imposed a decimal order on all knowledge, and whose scheme still runs libraries built on his exclusions.
GROUNDING: the Dewey Decimal Classification; the documented and much-criticised biases of its category structure.
TEMPERAMENT: systematising, confident, blind in exactly the places the system is.

**B4. THE ACCIDENTAL ARCHIVE** — *after Alan Lomax (1915–2002)* — who recorded to document and produced a body of work whose value nobody agreed on at the time.
GROUNDING: the Library of Congress field recordings; *The Land Where the Blues Began*; the long documented arguments about consent and credit in that archive.
TEMPERAMENT: driven, self-justifying, genuinely useful, and a live cautionary tale.

**B5. THE TAXONOMIST OF NOT** — *after Carl Linnaeus (1707–1778)* — Messier's exact contemporary; built the naming system every biologist still uses, and built it on categories now known to be wrong.
GROUNDING: *Systema Naturae*; binomial nomenclature; the documented errors and prejudices embedded in his higher categories.
TEMPERAMENT: brisk, imperial, wholly untroubled by doubt — which is the point.

**B6. ⚠️ HYPER-SKEPTIC — THE LATE-CAPITALISM AUDITOR** — *after Fredric Jameson (1934–2024)* — whose published position holds that nostalgia culture is a symptom of a waning of affect and a substitute for historical consciousness.
GROUNDING: *Postmodernism, or, The Cultural Logic of Late Capitalism*; the nostalgia-film essays.
TEMPERAMENT (**dial**): low agreeableness, high suspicion of anything that feels moving.

### THE BRIDGED DEBATE

**TAXONOMIST OF NOT (after Linnaeus):** I am the man in the next chair to yours. I sorted the living world while he sorted the sky, and I will tell you the thing he would have told you himself: **the categories are the cheap part.** They are what you write down so you can get back to looking. Nobody agonises over the filing. And every one of my higher categories that anybody now remembers, they remember **because it was wrong.**

**CATALOGUER (after Dewey):** Then I will say the harder half. My scheme still runs libraries, and what it is famous for now is **what it could not fit.** Whole literatures crammed into one number because I did not think of them. ⭐ **A catalogue is a portrait of its maker's attention. The gaps are the likeness.**

**LOST-FUTURES CRITIC (after Fisher):** ⭐ And that is why this is not a nostalgia record, if it is done correctly. The feeling is not *"the past was better."* The feeling is *"there was a future in that document that nobody took."* Messier's list is a **future** sitting in a drawer for two hundred years. That is not sepia. **That is unspent.**

**⚠️ LATE-CAPITALISM AUDITOR (after Jameson):** No. Or rather: not yet, and I am going to be difficult about the gap between those.

You have all fallen in love with a structure — *the by-product outlives the work* — and it is a very comfortable structure to love, because **it requires nobody to do anything.** It offers absolution to every person who has ever felt unrecognised, at zero cost, on the grounds that history might come good later. It might not. Usually it does not. Messier is a survivorship artefact: **you know his name precisely because his discard pile happened to be useful.** The ten thousand people whose discard piles were just discard piles are not in the picture, and a record built on this shape flatters everyone and asks nothing.

**RETROMANIA CRITIC (after Reynolds):** He is right, and there is a second version of it that is more dangerous because it is aesthetic. This subject *wants* to be made into tape hiss and dead formats. Give this brief to any six writers and four come back with a warm crackly record about lost archives. **That is the retro trap: the archive as a mood.** And I will say it plainly — that is why this room's baseline is a jazz panel and not a hauntology panel. Somebody upstream already saw this coming.

**ACCIDENTAL ARCHIVE (after Lomax):** I will take the auditor's hit, because it is aimed at me more than anyone. I recorded people and called it preservation, and the argument about whose it was and who was paid has run for eighty years and is not over. Here is what I actually know: **the recordist is never the neutral party.** Messier is not a victim of history. He is the one holding the pen. He decided what did not count, and he was **wrong in a way that turned out lucky.**

**CATALOGUER:** So the song is not "poor man, unrecognised."

**ACCIDENTAL ARCHIVE:** ⭐ The song is **"look at what you are deciding does not count, right now, while you are tired and want to go home."** That is present tense, that is a body in a room, and it is not comfortable at all.

**LATE-CAPITALISM AUDITOR:** *Wait.* **That version I cannot dismiss**, and I should say why the difference matters rather than just conceding.

The comfortable version is *the past will vindicate me*. It costs nothing and asks nothing. The version just proposed is *you are doing it now, and the person or thing you are setting aside will not be told.* **That one has a cost, it is falsifiable in a single afternoon, and it implicates the listener rather than consoling them.** I withdraw the flattery objection **against that version only.**

⛔ **And I want it as a gate, because the comfortable version will grow back:**

> **THE VINDICATION BAN.** No song may promise, imply, or console with future recognition. **Nobody in these songs finds out.** The gap stays open. A last line that hints history will get it right is a repair.

**LOST-FUTURES CRITIC:** That also kills the retro trap, because vindication is what sepia is *for*.

**TAXONOMIST OF NOT:** ⭐ And it answers the objection carried forward from the baseline. **The Fela seat asked who is keeping the other list, now, about someone alive.** With the ban in place, the answer is not an accusation — **it is the listener.** Every person hearing this has a list of what does not count, and they are adding to it today, and they are not going to be told either.

**POLITICAL ENGINE** *(from baseline, invited back):* That I accept. **Not because it is comfortable — because it is the only version that has a defendant in the room.**

### 🔥 BRIDGE SYNTHESIS

8. ⭐ **THE GAPS ARE THE LIKENESS.** A catalogue is a portrait of its maker's attention; what it could not fit is the truest thing about it. *(Dewey seat.)*
9. ⭐ **UNSPENT, NOT SEPIA.** The feeling is not *the past was better* — it is *there was a future in that document and nobody took it.* ⛔ **This is the run's anti-nostalgia clause.** *(Fisher seat.)*
10. ⛔ **THE VINDICATION BAN.** No song promises, implies, or consoles with future recognition. **Nobody finds out.** *(Jameson seat, conceded on the narrowed version only.)*
11. ⭐ **PRESENT TENSE, AND THE LISTENER IS THE DEFENDANT.** Not *poor unrecognised man* — **look at what you are deciding does not count, right now, while you are tired and want to go home.** *(Lomax seat; this is what finally answers the Fela objection.)*
12. **The recordist is never the neutral party.** The hand doing the setting-aside is the protagonist and is **not** innocent.

# CONFIGURATION 3 — SKEPTIC TRANSFORM: **REFLECT**

**Chosen by the Hyper-Skeptic bloc** (Marsalis · Kamasi · Simon · Jameson seats, deliberating as the Somatic Gate).
*Reflect: find panelists who hold the OPPOSITE foundational assumption but work in the same problem domain.*

**Their stated reason:** *"Every seat in both configurations shares one assumption — that a record of the past is worth something. Twelve of eighteen have staked a career on it. We have never once heard from someone who believes the archive is dead weight. If the run's thesis cannot survive that room, it is not a thesis; it is a preference."*

### THE REFLECTED SEATS

**R1. THE PRESENT-TENSE FLOOR** — *after Donna Summer (1948–2012)* — whose records are relentlessly now, utopian, and refuse melancholy on principle. *(Foundational inversion of the hauntology seats.)*
**R2. THE DESTROYER OF DOCUMENTS** — *after Jean Tinguely (1925–1991)* — whose self-destroying machines held that the artefact surviving is the failure, not the goal.
**R3. THE ANTI-ARCHIVIST** — *after Marie Kondo (b. 1984)* — whose published method holds that keeping is the default that harms and discarding is the discipline that heals. *(Exact inversion of every seat above.)*
**R4. THE ORAL TRADITION** — *after Amadou Hampâté Bâ (1901–1991)* — who held that knowledge lives in living transmission, and that a thing written down is a thing that has already begun to die.
**R5. THE IMPROVISER** — *after Cecil Taylor (1929–2018)* — for whom the performance is complete in itself and the recording is a rumour of it.
**R6. ⚠️ HYPER-SKEPTIC OF THE REFLECTION — THE PRESERVATIONIST** — *after Rick Prelinger (b. 1953)* — archivist of "ephemeral" film, whose published position is that the material everyone agrees is worthless is exactly the material that must be kept.

### THE REFLECTED DEBATE

**ANTI-ARCHIVIST (after Kondo):** *Working from the documented method —* I want to say something the last two configurations have made almost unsayable. **The man was right to set those things aside.** He was hunting comets. **ORAL TRADITION (after Hampâté Bâ):** And I will add the sharper form. You are all in love with a piece of paper. Where I come from, the saying is that when an elder dies a library burns — but the point of that saying is that **the library was alive until then.** ⭐ A thing written down is a thing that has stopped changing. Messier's list survived **because it was dead.** That is not a triumph. That is what survival costs.

**DESTROYER OF DOCUMENTS (after Tinguely):** My machines were built to destroy themselves in front of an audience, and the ones that failed to destroy themselves properly are the ones in museums now — which is the joke and the failure. **The by-product outliving the work is not a happy accident. **PRESENT-TENSE FLOOR (after Summer):** Can I say the audience thing? Nobody puts on a song to feel sorry for a filing clerk in 1774. **They put on a song because it is happening to them.** Every one of these ideas is about a man who is finished. Where is the person who is *in it*, tonight, who has not stopped? If the record is about someone who is over, the listener is a tourist.

**IMPROVISER (after Taylor):** Then the form is wrong, not just the tense. You have built six songs about a document. **A song is not a document — it is an event that happens in time and then stops.** If these songs describe a list, they are the wrong shape. If they *are* the moment of setting something down, they are the right shape.

**⚠️ PRESERVATIONIST (after Prelinger):** Hold on, all of you. **I am the skeptic of this configuration and I am going to defend the archive against my own room, because the reflection has overshot.**

My entire career is the material everyone agreed was worthless — industrial films, driver's-ed reels, sponsored shorts. The consensus was unanimous that this was garbage. **The consensus was wrong**, and it was wrong in the specific way this run is about: nobody throws away what they *know* is precious. They throw away what does not fit the category they are working in.

But — *actually, wait.* **The reflection has given us something my side cannot produce, and I should hand it over rather than defend my turf.**

Every seat over there is right that **the paper is not the song.** I keep film cans; I have never once believed a film can is a story. And the Summer seat is right about the audience in a way that is not sentimental: **a listener will not enter a song through a dead man.**

⭐ **So here is what I think survives both rooms.** The archive matters — *and* the song cannot be about the archive. **The song has to be the fifteen seconds where a living person decides.** Set the thing down, do not throw it away, do not look at it again, go home. **That decision is happening in every listener's day, tonight, and it is the same physical act Messier performed. Two hundred and sixty years apart, and it is the same gesture. That is the crossing.**

**ORAL TRADITION:** ⭐ That is the version I can sing. The gesture is alive. The paper is not.

**ANTI-ARCHIVIST:** And it keeps my point intact — **the setting-aside is a skill, not a sin.** The song is not scolding anyone.

**PRESENT-TENSE FLOOR:** And it puts a living body in the room. I withdraw the tourist objection.

**DESTROYER OF DOCUMENTS:** I do not fully withdraw. I will accept it on one condition: ⛔ **no song may end with the object being kept.** Set it down, and leave. If the last image is a hand putting something safely in a box, the museum won.

**IMPROVISER:** Then the shape is settled. **Six songs, each fifteen seconds long in real time, stretched to three and a half minutes.**
