## §1 — THE ESSENCE, AND THE TWO WAYS THE MEDIUM CARRIES IT

**Essence, one sentence:** *A tired woman in a van with the doors shut writes two letters beside the only thing on the reel that has the room in it, and she is right to, and she will never know.*

**Two ways the medium carries it, and no third:**
1. ⭐ **Counterpoint, not production.** Two low wordless sounds enter side by side and are indistinguishable for one whole pass of the form; at one bar hers passes under the other and settles a minor third below a note **she does not have in her scale**. The unison is not forbidden — it is *unavailable*. **A rule the generator cannot break by accident is worth more than a rule it is told not to break.**
2. ⭐ **A byte-identical three-line tag, seven times.** It says *it's not a take* over a room the listener has been standing in. The words do not change; the listener does. That is the entire dramatic engine, and it costs nothing to render.

## §3 — THE CRITIQUE, IN THEIR VOICES

### V1 · THE LOG BOOK
**SMALL ROOM:** *"This is the one I could put in front of a stranger. Nine seconds in there is a door, an engine, a book and a knee — I know where I am and I know what she is holding. The tag is the best writing in the run: I can do it in a car after one listen and I do not need to remember any words to do the hook."*
**⚠️ TRADITION CUSTODIAN:** *"Two complaints and one of them matters. The small one: the second and third lines of the first section sit side by side and could swap places, which is the exact list-shape this run banned. **The one that matters:** at the crossing she sings 'neither of them bends.' That is a musician's sentence. She has just told me the principle of the piece, which means she understands it, which means the whole withholding engine has been handed away in one line for the sake of a nice image. Fix that or the pair is decorative."*

### V2 · THE DOOR OPEN
**SMALL ROOM:** *"The strongest single moment anyone has written today: he reads it upside down, says right, and gets down, and does not say anything about it. Two people fail to notice the same thing and neither is a fool. And the crossing is done by a man talking — no fader, no trick."*
**⚠️ TRADITION CUSTODIAN:** *"Then look at the line four bars earlier. 'I start again and don't know it.' A singer cannot report her own unawareness. The moment she says it, she knows, and your dramatic irony is a costume. I will accept this song when that line stops being a confession and starts being a fact."*

### V3 · WHAT THE ROOM DID
**SMALL ROOM:** *"The most beautiful of the four, and the hardest to enter. I do not know where I am standing until the last minute."*
**⚠️ TRADITION CUSTODIAN:** *"That is not a taste problem, it is a structural one, and I am surprised nobody has said it. Six of the seven sections have no singer in them. **Your binding decision is that the song withholds from its singer. If there is no singer present, there is nobody to withhold from** — you have written a beautiful description of a room and called it dramatic irony. The device is worth keeping. It needs a host."*

### V4 · DRIVING HOME
**SMALL ROOM:** *"The last section is the truest thing here. She tries the hum against the radio, it does not match, she gives up and drives. Nobody in the history of the world has ever found out where a tune came from and this song knows it."*
**⚠️ TRADITION CUSTODIAN:** *"And your crossing happens on a public road. The pair's room is a vehicle that is not moving — that is the assignment — and you spend the single most important bar of the song in traffic. Worse: the second sound just *stops*, with no cause. Sounds do not stop. **Somebody stops them.** Give me the hand that does it and you have both your room and your ending."*

## §6 — ⭐ THE DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK *(one inline pass, per variation, adversarial)*

*Predict what the prompt would literally PRODUCE. Then name the one way it renders generic. Then one repair, reusing the existing loop.*

### V1
**Predicted render:** a warm mid-tempo jazz-soul instrumental bed — vibraphone, organ, brushed kit, upright bass, an acoustic guitar on the back of the beat — under a low woman half-speaking short phrases, with a hummed refrain twice. Around four and a half minutes, level, no chorus in the pop sense. It will sound pleasant and slightly like library music, which is correct.
**⚠️ The one way this renders generic:** ⭐ **the held organ note becomes a chord pad.** A generator hears "combo organ holding one low note" and gives you a warm sustained *chord*, because that is what organs do in this genre. The instant it is a chord, there is no second line, there is no crossing, and this is a nice jazz ballad about a woman doing paperwork.
**Repair applied (one pass):** the prompt now reads *"holding one low note **at one steady level, same pitch throughout**"* — level and pitch both pinned, positively phrased, no negation for the generator to invert. Room description trimmed to pay for it.
