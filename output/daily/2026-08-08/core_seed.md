## 2. THE SEED — one paragraph

Charles Messier hunted comets. The faint smudges that fooled him — the ones that **did not move** — he wrote down **so he could stop being fooled.** That list is the Messier Catalogue, and every backyard telescope on Earth is still pointed by it. His thirteen comets are forgotten. He died in 1817 believing he was a comet man; the king had even named him *the Ferret of Comets*. **The discard pile outlived the hunt and he never found out.** Today's picture is the joke landing 250 years late: comet 10P/Tempel 2 — back at perihelion six days ago, on time, the way it has been every five years and four months since 1873 — crosses M30. The dust trail **appears to pierce the cluster.** The comet is three and a half light-minutes out. The cluster is twenty-eight thousand light-years out. *The touch is entirely real to the eye and entirely false in space.*

## 3. THE EMOTIONAL ENGINE — the wrongness is in the normalcy (element 3)

**Nobody was moved at the time.** The catalogue was **housekeeping.** It was a man doing admin so his real work would go faster. There is no scene of revelation, no night he wept at M30 — he wrote it down to get it out of the way, went back to the eyepiece, and waited for something to move.

The dread and the tenderness both come from **what did not change.** He kept working. He kept the list current. He co-discovered seven more comets. The list sat there being the most important thing he would ever do, in silence, for fifty-seven years, and then for another two hundred.

> ⭐ **THE ACHE, in one line: you do not get to know which of your work was the work. The scoring happens after you, and it is done by people using your notes for something you were not doing.**

**AWE face:** somebody, right now, is doing the thing that will outlast them, and it is annoying them.
**INDIGNATION face:** the systems that decide what counts are keeping *their* list, and it is not the same list, and you will never see it.

## 4. THE MANDATORY FORM RULE — ⭐ THE FALSE INTERSECTION
*(Source 3, from the APOD image's literal structure. Binding on all 24 songs.)*

Every song carries **two elements of the same family** — same timbre class, same register, entering side by side, easily mistaken for one another. At **exactly one moment**, one **passes through** the other — a line, a figure, a hit, a word — **and neither changes.**

- **No merge. No duet resolution. No synthesis section.** They touch and stay apart.
- Executed **in the lyric and in the notes** — never in the mix. *(L22 THE GRAIN LAW: a Somatic objection answered in the production spec is not answered.)*
- **Accretive** — add a second element, add a crossing. *(2026-08-07 finding: accretive specs survive the generator; subtractive ones get smoothed.)*
- **One crossing per song. Not two.** *(L38 — N = 1, ONE SEAM.)*
