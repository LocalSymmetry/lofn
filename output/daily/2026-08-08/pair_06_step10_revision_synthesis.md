## §1 — THE ESSENCE, IN ONE SENTENCE

> **A tired competent person in a flat-lit field writes the correct last line in a book, takes a stamped plate off a box, sets it down and drives away — and somewhere else, much later, a stranger finishing an unrelated shift says the same words for the same reason and will never know it.**

**The two best ways the medium carries it:** (1) **the burden is spoken and the sung line never says its words**, so the pair's central phrase exists only in a mouth that is not the protagonist's; (2) **two low metals a semitone apart in one band**, one held and one descending, crossing once at unison — the false intersection executed as pitch rather than as an idea.

# §4 ⛔⛔ D4 — THE VINDICATION BAN. HOW THE TWO MOUTHS ARE KEPT UNCONNECTED.

> **Finding F-C** (`step05_refine_medium.md` §2): *"P06's second mouth is one line from breaching D4. If the later voice reads as a witness — someone who found the log and understood — the pair has delivered the vindication the run banned."*
>
> **D4, verbatim:** *no song may promise, imply, or console with future recognition.* **Nobody finds out.** It was won against a specific objection — that *"the by-product outlives the work"* is a comfortable shape that flatters everyone and asks nothing.

## §4.1 — The five structural guards, applied to all four songs

**1. ⭐ THE WORDS ARE BOILERPLATE, AND THE LYRIC SAYS SO.**
The burden's own spine is *"It is not a thing you mean. / It is a thing you put."* The phrase repeats **because it is what the form takes when a job ends**, not because anyone read anything. The second mouth also says *"Same as the last time"* — establishing that they have closed jobs this way before, many times, with no reference to anyone. **The repetition is explained inside the song by trade convention, so the listener never has to invent a connection in order to explain it.**

**2. ⭐ THE TWO VOICES SHARE THE GESTURE AND NEVER SHARE A PLACE.**
⚠️ **The honest version, because my first draft of this guard overstated it and my own scan caught it.** The two mouths *do* share object **classes** — a plate, a box, a book, a pen — and **they must**, because the entire pair is the claim that *the physical act is identical*. Handing the second mouth a completely different set of objects would delete the thesis. **What is never shared is a place.**

- **The sung side carries, and no burden ever names:** mast, guy-wire, ladder, pad, ballast, grass, concrete, field, track, van, ledge, page, biro.
- **The burden carries, and no sung stanza ever names:** tin, tray, lid, lamp, stair, street, shelf, step, rags, stub, coat, pocket, palm, strip light, sheet.
- **Neither voice ever refers to the other, quotes the other, or names a location the other could be standing in.**

⚠️ **One genuine collapse was found by scan and repaired.** V3's burden originally read *"Pad stays. The pad is not coming off"* — and **the pad is the sung side's own site landmark**, so the two mouths read as standing on the same slab, which is the F-C breach arriving through the back door. **Rewritten to the second mouth's own objects:** *"Studs left proud. Grind them off. / Cap on the stub. The cap is not coming off. / Rags in the bag. Bag on the shelf."* **Post-fix, V3's burden and sung side share no site noun whatsoever.** Recorded because a defect found and repaired is worth more to the run than a claim asserted.

**3. ⭐ THE SECOND MOUTH IS NEVER LOOKING.**
No line in any burden contains *find, found, read, remember, before, someone else, whoever, once, still, at last, after all, understand, understood, knew, know, again, back then, used to.* **Verified by scan across all four burdens: zero occurrences.** They are not searching, not curious, not addressing anybody, and not aware that there is anybody to address.

**4. ⭐ THE ACOUSTIC PLACES THEM SOMEWHERE ELSE, IN THE LYRICS FIELD ITSELF.**
The SFX cue attached to the burden's first entry is `*a strip light ticking, water moving in a pipe below a concrete stair*` — indoors, hard surfaces, machinery — against the sung side's `*wind across open ground, a steel wire sounding one low note*`. **The listener hears the identical sentence arrive in a different room.** That is the whole device, and it is carried in the lyric and the SFX cue, **never in the mix** (L22).

**5. ⭐ NOTHING IS CONCLUDED.**
No song ends with anyone learning anything. Three end with a plate going down on concrete and a person walking to a vehicle; one ends with a stranger's plate coming off a door. ⛔ **No line in this pair says, implies, or leaves room for the idea that the first person will be understood, remembered, thanked, read, credited or found.** The book stays where it is. **Nobody comes for it.**
