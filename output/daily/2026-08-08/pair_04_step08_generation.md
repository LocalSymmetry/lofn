> Cold and procedural. Odd-meter chamber prog tracked live in one take, everyone standing in the same hallway, no overdubs. Bass clarinet carries the seven and is the only tired thing in the room. Viola bowed with almost no pressure, felted upright piano struck mid-keyboard, fretless bass fingered not plucked, kit cut down to rim and closed hat with one brushed snare. Two small struck bells run the whole way through: one on B natural on every downbeat, the identical bell on B flat every eight eighth-notes, never resetting, walking late through the bar. It opens on the walking bell alone. A four-bar 4/4 refrain returns four times unchanged; at the third return both bells land together a semitone apart and neither moves. Everything is close-miked a hand's width out, one level throughout, no reverb tail. It grows by instruments arriving and never by volume, and ends thinner than it began.
