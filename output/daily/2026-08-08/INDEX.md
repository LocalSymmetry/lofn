## THE FIND — the catalogue of things which are not comets

Today's NASA picture, verbatim: *"the 30th entry in astronomer Charles Messier's catalog of **things which are not comets**."*

Messier hunted comets. The faint smudges that fooled him — the ones that **did not move** — he wrote down **so he could ignore them.** That list now points every backyard telescope on Earth. His thirteen comets are forgotten. He died in 1817 still believing he was a comet man; the king had even named him *the Ferret of Comets*. **The discard pile outlived the hunt, and he never found out.**

And the picture is the joke landing 250 years late: comet **10P/Tempel 2** — back at perihelion **six days ago**, on time, every 5.36 years since 1873 — crosses **M30**. The dust trail **appears to pierce the cluster.** The comet is **3.5 light-minutes** out. The cluster is **28,000 light-years** out. *The touch is entirely real to the eye and entirely false in space.*

⭐ **And the direction of the ache reverses:** the thing he wanted — the comet — **left every time.** The things he wrote down **never moved at all.**
