## 0 — ⭐ THE PAIR'S BINDING DECLARATIONS, IN ONE PLACE

**D3 — THE TWO LINES, NAMED BY INTERVAL** *(named at step 06, before any lyric existed)*
> **LINE A, the tone that FIRES: a struck bell on B♮4, on the downbeat of every 7/8 bar — once every seven eighth-notes.**
> **LINE B, the tone that DOES NOT FIRE: the identical bell on B♭4, a SEMITONE below, once every eight eighth-notes from bar one, never resetting.**
> Same sample, same envelope, same level. **Neither ever changes pitch, rate or level anywhere in any of the four songs.** Because seven and eight do not agree, B♭ walks one eighth later through the bar every bar. The bar-count before the **third** statement of the 4/4 refrain is set so its phase is **zero there and only there** — the un-fired tone passes through the fired one, a semitone apart, and comes out still walking. In D Dorian **B♮ is the note that makes the mode and B♭ is the note that would make it ordinary**, so the one moment the un-flagged event is in time is the one moment the song sounds unremarkable. ⛔ **Executed in the notes and in the lyric, never in the mix.**

**⭐ THE SUNG NUMBER — the run's entire allowance, spent here.**
**V1 only. The exact words as sung: `Four point five is the bar. I know`.** Spelled in words, never digits; placed **mid-line**, never at the rhyme position; at the hinge, strophe 4 of six.
**RESPONDED TO, not recited — and the response is a SPEED, not a feeling:**
> `I put the figure in. Not slow. / I have never put one in slow.`
**V2, V3 and V4 sing no numeral of any kind.** Determiners spelled in words (*one of them*, *the rest*) are not numeric facts and are not digits.

**THE WINCE (D11), per song:** V1 — *"I have never put one in slow."* · V2 — *"The rest of them go as I wrote."* · V3 — **she reads it back, she gets it right, and the caller thanks her.** · V4 — *"Nobody signs it but me."*
**AND, in all four: the fourth refrain is byte-identical and nothing happens at it.**

# 13 — SELF-CRITIQUE

**What this pair actually is, said plainly:** a procedural song in 7/8 in which every line-end lands on one vowel, one number is sung, and a bell that never fires walks through a bell that does — once — because seven and eight do not agree. **Eligibility is 0–3/7 and that is the assignment, not a shortfall.** There is no adoptable hook, cognitive ease is deliberately defeated, and the vocal co-discovery property is unavailable by D1.

That is the first form-rule execution in this chain that is robust to its own most likely render failure.

**The weakest thing here, and I would rather say it than have QA find it:** ⚠️ **the vowel constraint is doing more work than the images are.** Ninety line-ends on /oʊ/ is a real device, but it exerts constant pressure toward the same twenty words — *row, code, note, no, told, hold, close, goes, slow, below* — and in a few places (V1 S5, V2 S6) the line exists because the vowel needed feeding rather than because the hand needed to do something. **A reader who is unmoved by the constraint will read those lines as thin, and they will be right.** The mitigation applied was to require a physical verb in every strophe; the mitigation is not complete.
