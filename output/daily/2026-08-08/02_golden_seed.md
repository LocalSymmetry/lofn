### Why this panel and not the obvious one

The thematically obvious pick was **Hauntological Soundscapers** (#14) — Mark Fisher, Derrida, Boards of Canada, Ghost Box, "Nostalgia for Lost Futures," "The Persistence of Memory." On a run about archives it is the bullseye.

⛔ **Rejected as the baseline, deliberately.** Its fifteen Special Flairs are **media artifacts** — vinyl crackle, tape hiss, cathode hum, sepia reverb. That is *the medium promoted from SUBSTANCE to SUBJECT*, which is the exact disease today's own analysis diagnosed (`ANALYSIS_2026-08-08`, L37/L38). A hauntology baseline would have produced a beautiful, dead, monochrome run **about media**, and it would have done it while agreeing with itself.

**Ethio-Jazz Futurists wins on three counts:**
1. Its flairs are **sonic materials**, not media artifacts — vibraphone, Farfisa, modal tenor sax, brushed snare, fuzz guitar. Those are the precise antidote to the measured Glitch-Baroque / dry-close-mic monoculture (`00_research_brief.md` §4), and they rhyme with Source 2's imported Bandcamp vocabulary (Hammond organs, spacey jazz, textured folk, claps and shakers).
2. ⭐ **Tezeta** — a named Ethiopian *mode* meaning bittersweet longing for something gone. It is the emotional key of Messier's catalogue **and it lives in the notes**, not in a reverb setting. That is L22 THE GRAIN LAW satisfied at the panel-selection layer.
3. Its three skeptics attack this run exactly where it is weakest: **dilution** (Marsalis), **understatement vs grandeur** (Kamasi Washington), and ⭐ **the ethics of an outsider adopting a tradition** (Paul Simon / *Graceland*) — a live, unavoidable objection to a machine using Ethiopian modes, which must be answered honestly and in writing, not dodged.

**Hauntology is not discarded — it is earned.** It enters at the **GROUP TRANSFORM (BRIDGE)** in `03_panel_debate.md`, as a bridged domain rather than a baseline aesthetic. That is what transformations are for.
