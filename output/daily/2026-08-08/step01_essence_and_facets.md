## THE ESSENCE — one sentence

> **A competent, tired person decides that one thing does not count, and goes home; the listener knows what it was, and the person never will.**

Everything downstream is a rotation of that sentence through the five axes. **The astronomy is the rhyme, not the subject** — it is in the run only because Messier performed this exact gesture and the by-product outlived him, which is what licenses the scale.
