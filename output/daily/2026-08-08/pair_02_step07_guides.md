## GUIDE 1 — THE HAND ON ONE OBJECT · *(P02-1, P02-3)*
*Seats driving: THE RAW HORN (after Mekurya) · THE IMPROVISER (after Cecil Taylor) · ⚠️ dissent from THE CATALOGUER (after Dewey)*

- **Storytelling technique 1 — SINGLE-OBJECT SERIALISATION.** Each song takes **one** thing out and spends four verses on it. The tub's other contents are never enumerated; they are *underneath*, and they only ever announce themselves as a sound or a shift.
- **Storytelling technique 2 — CAUSAL STAIRCASE.** Verse *n* is impossible unless verse *n−1* happened. You cannot find the bubble before you lift the card; you cannot press the button before the key is out; you cannot see the crease before you have peeled it up.
- ⚠️ **The Dewey seat objects:** *"A catalogue is a portrait of its maker's attention — you are throwing away the portrait."* **Answered, not dodged:** the exclusions stay, but they stay as the tub's *contents*, never as the song's *lines*. The gaps are still the likeness; the song just refuses to read them out.
- **Mood arc:** begin mid-task and mildly inconvenienced → become briefly absorbed by a physical detail → return to the job. **No ascent. No descent.** The arc is a small detour and a return.
- **Instrumentation:** the hand's actions are **articulated by the shakers**, not by the drums — there is no kit. A jangling triangle marks the offbeat continuously so the song has a pulse without a build.
- **Production:** one room, one continuous implied take, bleed named. Entries by ear.
- **Structure:** refrain first, then 4 × (12-line verse → 6-line refrain), refrain doubled at the close. **No bridge.**
- **Rhyme:** quatrains with lines 2 & 4 landing on identical three-character endings (`-ide`, `-old`, `-ing`, `-ace`), so return is *measurable*, not merely felt.
- **Poetic devices:** ⭐ **ANAPHORA** (*"Somebody has… Somebody has…"*, *"Nothing in this tub…"*) and ⭐ **EPIZEUXIS** (*"Keep it. Keep it. Keep it flat."*) — both are hymn-native and both carry return without rhyme.
- **Hook technique A:** the refrain's third line is the device line and should sit slightly *higher* in the stack than the rest — the one place all three voices open out.
- **Hook technique B:** the closing epizeuxis is a genuine congregational hook — three repetitions, one closing adjective. It is designed to be singable by a room of people who have heard it twice.
- **Hook technique C:** the SFX cue is the hook's *shadow* — the physical sound of the object (a lid clicking, a key rattling) placed once, at the top, before any voice.
- **Central aspect:** the tub is something that **happens to** the singer. It is never a shape the song adopts.
- **Emotional tone signal:** competent. Faintly irritated. Not moved.
- **Lyric structure:** 84 sung lines. Mean line under six words. Short declarative sentences; full stops mid-line.

## GUIDE 2 — THE ASYMMETRY OF KNOWING · *(P02-2, P02-9)*
*Seats: THE ORCHESTRATOR OF CHARACTER (after Ellington) · THE ANTI-ARCHIVIST (after Kondo) · ⚠️ THE LATE-CAPITALISM AUDITOR (after Jameson)*

- **Storytelling technique 1 — THE FLAT TRUE SENTENCE.** Once per song the singer says something that is *practically accurate and totally blind*: **"Card is fine. The card will last." / "Key is fine. The car was sold." / "That's the photo. That's the whole of it. Some sand."** No interpretation follows. The listener supplies it.
- **Storytelling technique 2 — THE UNANSWERED ADDRESS.** The singer twice asks a small logistical question of an empty room. Nobody answers, and the singer does not notice they were asking anybody.
- ⚠️ **The Jameson seat's live condition:** the comfortable version — *the past will vindicate me* — **grows back if unwatched.** ⛔ **D4 is absolute here:** no line hints anyone will ever be told, and the last word of every song is a **departure**, never a discovery.
- **The Kondo condition, checked FOUR TIMES, not once (ATTACK 3):** the reason the lid goes back on must be one the listener would also accept.
  **V1** — you cannot bin a photograph of a person and you cannot file a bus pass; there is no category.
  **V2** — nobody throws away a key until they are certain, and certainty is not available tonight.
  **V3** — a stranger bothered, so it is not rubbish; and it fits nothing in the house, so it is not filing.
  **V4** — deciding needs a whole day, and this is not one. ⭐ **If the listener can feel superior, the reason was not good enough.**
- **Mood arc:** the singer's affect is flat end-to-end; the **listener's** moves from mild interest to dismay. Two arcs, one room, unimaginably far apart.
- **Instrumentation:** the flat true sentence is sung **in full three-part harmony with no dynamic change whatsoever.** Nothing marks it. That is what makes it land.
- **Production:** ⛔ absolutely no production emphasis at the wince. No solo, no drop-out, no reverb tail, no lift. **A Somatic objection answered in the mix is not answered.**
- **Structure:** the wince sits at the end of verse two in V1/V2, verse three in V3, verse four in V4 — deliberately un-templated so the four songs do not share a hinge position.
- **Poetic devices:** ⭐ **ANTANACLASIS** (*fine* meaning *undamaged* and *fine* meaning *acceptable*) and ⭐ **ASYNDETON** (*"Out. The hall. The stairs. The night."*).
- **Hook A/B/C:** the flat sentence is deliberately **not** the hook — it is a verse line, unmarked, so a first listen may miss it entirely and a second cannot.
- **Vocal technique:** no vibrato swell anywhere. Three voices, level, unhurried, breathing together.

## GUIDE 3 — HYMN WORDS, PLASTIC OBJECTS · *(P02-4, P02-7)* — **the pair's ONE DEVICE**
*Seats: THE MODERN ETHIOPIAN VOICE (after Gigi) · THE SMALL ROOM (after Boilen) · ⚠️ THE TRADITION CUSTODIAN (after Marsalis)*

- **The device in one line:** genuine liturgical grammar — *keep, hold, abide, bide, shall not want, past all perishing, shall not fade* — applied without irony to **laminate, a rubber button, a gummy photograph, and a lid that has stopped sealing.**
- ⚠️ **The Gigi seat's rule, which is the D9 gate in practice:** take the **function**, never the **label**. The function borrowed here is *three close voices carrying one tune with nobody in front, and a refrain a room can join on second hearing.* ⛔ **The tradition's name never enters a render field.** Credit goes in the release block, with **working links**.
- ⚠️ **The Marsalis seat's dilution charge, answered:** the hymn is not a costume laid over the song — the **form is genuinely a hymn** (numbered verses, invariant refrain, no bridge, no modulation), and the diction is genuinely liturgical. The collision is between the *words* and the *objects*, which is one seam, not a style pile-up.
- **Mood arc:** the refrain is **assurance**. It promises permanence. It is sung the same way after the room is empty as before anyone touched anything.
- ⭐ **THE WINCE:** the refrain is about **keeping**. The lid goes back on. **Nothing is kept.**
- **Instrumentation:** a **Hammond-type drawbar organ holds ONE chord** under the entire song and never swells — the congregational bed. Jangling triangle on the offbeat. Claps and shakers enter at verse two by **addition**, never by gain.
- **Structure & dynamics:** four verses, no build, refrain identical each time. ⛔ **No final-chorus enlargement.** The only new event in the whole arrangement is the part-swap and (in V4) the piano.
- **Return vehicle:** ⭐ **byte-identical refrain, six appearances, character-for-character.** **This needs no defence and none is filed** (`gates.yaml → chorus_repetition_requires_no_justification: true`). No pre-emptive mutation was made.
- **Rhyme:** the refrain carries the pair's densest return — its own end-rhymes recur inside a four-line window, so the return floor does not depend on the verses.
- **Poetic devices:** ⭐ **EPIZEUXIS** and ⭐ **HOMOIOTELEUTON** (the `-ing` chain: *wearing / missing / perishing / believing*).
- **Hook A:** state the refrain **first**, before verse one, unaccompanied but for the organ — the hook arrives before the story.
- **Hook B:** the device line is the third line of every refrain and is the one a listener will quote.
- **Hook C:** end the song on the refrain, **after** the singer has left the room, so the assurance is heard with nobody in it.
- **Small Room test:** every refrain is singable at a kitchen table, unamplified, by three people. If it needed the arrangement, it would have failed.

## GUIDE 4 — THE CROSSING THAT CHANGES NOTHING · *(P02-5)* — **Special Flair #15**
*Seats: THE BIG-BAND TRANSLATOR (after Gershon) · THE HARD SECTION (after Brenneck) · ⚠️ THE MAXIMALIST (after Kamasi)*

- **The chart, not the concept.** Two inner voices, **major second apart**, both in the alto/mezzo region, beating audibly and fusing into one thickened voice. At verse three they **swap parts mid-phrase on a shared note.** Pitch content unchanged. No merge, no resolution, no unison.
- **Where:** the exact word is fixed per variation in step 06 — always where the subject is **doing something across the seam**.
- ⚠️ **The Kamasi condition, live:** *"I withdraw the greyness objection on the condition that the gap is audible."* This pair's answer is that the crossing is **accretive** (a thing arrives: the voices exchange) rather than subtractive (nothing is removed, nothing stops). Accretive specs survive the generator; subtractive ones get smoothed.
- ⚠️ **The Brenneck seat's condition:** the size comes from the **room**, not the arrangement. Two women and one man standing close enough to hear each other breathe, in a kitchen, is enormous and costs nothing.
- **Production:** ⛔ **nothing in the mix marks the crossing.** No widening, no automation, no pan, no reverb change. If it is not in the notes it did not happen.
- **Structure:** one crossing per song. **Not two.** (L38 — N = 1.)
- **Instrumentation at the crossing:** unchanged. The organ holds. The triangle keeps time. The claps do not stop.
- **Emotional tone signal:** the listener should feel the room widen and be **unable to name the cause**. That is the whole effect and it must not be explained anywhere in the lyric.
- **Key sequence:** refrain phrase = **5–5–6–5** over **4–4–5–4**; the swap happens on the second beat of the third bar of the verse's middle quatrain.
- **Special effects:** none. This flair's entire content is that **nothing is added and nothing changes.**

## GUIDE 5 — THE KITCHEN, AND GETTING OUT OF IT · *(P02-6, P02-10)*
*Seats: THE HARD SECTION (after Brenneck) · THE PRESENT-TENSE FLOOR (after Summer) · ⚠️ THE DESTROYER OF DOCUMENTS (after Tinguely)*

- **The room, specified so it renders:** hard surfaces, a table, **one lamp**, a **fridge running**, a chair that gets moved, a door, a hall. Furniture height and arm's length. Named in the first thirty seconds.
- **Special Flair #8 — Smoke Without A Fire:** a room that has been used all day. Not atmospheric; *used*.
- **Special Flair #12 — Modern Antiquity:** liturgical grammar and a plastic storage box, simultaneously.
- ⚠️ **The Tinguely seat's price (D8), which he did not withdraw:** ⛔ **no song ends with the object being kept.** This pair's ending is **the lid on, the tub on a high shelf, the lamp off, and the body out of the room.** The tub going *up* is a **deferral**, not a treasure — the shelf is where things go to not be done.
- **The Summer seat's floor:** a living body, present tense, tonight, who has not stopped. Every verb in every verse is present.
- **Mood arc:** the last quatrain of every song is pure logistics — chair, lamp, hall — and is the calmest writing in the piece.
- **Instrumentation:** the arrangement does **not** thin out at the end. The claps keep going after the singer has left. The room's own hymn continues without anybody in it.
- **Structure & dynamics:** single dynamic by design; growth by **addition** only (claps at V2, piano at V3 in V4). ⛔ No risers, no drops, no section-boundary reverb changes, no long full stops, no untuned drones, no hard-panned non-musical elements.
- **Poetic devices:** ⭐ **ASYNDETON** in the exit lines and ⭐ **POLYPTOTON** (*press / pressed / presses*).
- **Narrative & POV:** first person, present, unnamed, ungendered. **Collective delivery** — three voices, one person's thoughts. That gap between *one mind* and *three throats* is itself the pair's quiet argument and is never stated.
- **Run-time:** 3:30–4:10 at 63–72 BPM.

## GUIDE 6 — ⭐ WILDCARD · THE OBJECT'S INDIFFERENCE · *(P02-8)*
*Seats: THE GLOBAL TRIO (after Speer) · THE LOST-FUTURES CRITIC (after Fisher) · ⚠️ THE RETROMANIA CRITIC (after Reynolds) · ⚠️ THE APPROPRIATION QUESTION (after Simon), standing*

- **The cross-disciplinary import — CONSERVATION REPORTING.** A museum condition report describes an object with total physical precision and **zero sentiment**: *gloss lost across 50 % of recto; adhesive residue verso; horizontal crease through centre; recent fingerprint over older fingerprint.* ⭐ **Write the photograph as a condition report and the retro trap becomes impossible**, because a condition report has no *back then* in it — only *now, and this is what it is like to touch.*
- ⚠️ **The Reynolds seat's warning is the reason this guide exists:** *"Give this brief to any six writers and four come back with a warm crackly record about lost archives."* ⛔ **F-D repair, binding:** the photograph is **a physical object handled in the present** — sticky, creased, the wrong size, thumb-marked. **No sepia. No "back then." No "they don't make them like that."** It is never a window.
- **The Fisher seat's correction:** the feeling is not *the past was better*. It is **unspent** — there was a future in that object and nobody took it. Unspent is a property of **now**, which is why it renders as texture and sepia does not.
- ⭐ **The Speer seat's warm angle, which is this pair's secret:** being misfiled **diminished the thing by nothing.** The sand was magnificent and the tide took it and the photograph does not care that it is in a box of things that are not anything. ⭐ **The photograph's entire content is that a stranger bothered.**
- ⛔ **D1 holds absolutely in V3:** the singer **never works out what the photograph is.** They say *"some sand."* They are right. They are also holding the only thing left of it. **They will not be told.**
- ⚠️ **The Simon seat did not withdraw and is not pretended away.** D9 is a discipline, not an absolution: function not label, no tradition name in any render field, and a **Lineage & Credit block with working links** in the release. Both were verified live before this pair's step 10 was written.
- **Instrumentation for the wildcard:** in V4 only, **a gentle piano refrain** doubles the sung refrain from verse three and plays alone at the close — the one moment in the pair where an instrument, not a voice, has the last word.
- **Hook:** the condition-report line *"Someone's fingerprint below it, / older than the one I've set."* — ⭐ this is the pair's **specificity that resists decoration**: it would ruin the photograph, and it is the truest thing in the song.
- **Emotional tone signal:** interested. Not moved. Slightly annoyed about the stickiness.

**Self-critique.** Guide 6 is the strongest and it is also where this pair is most likely to fail, because the *condition-report* discipline is a writing instruction and the generator does not read guides — it reads a style prompt and a lyric. If V3 renders as a warm nostalgic ballad about an old photo, **this paragraph is where the pair predicted it**, and the cause will be that a hymn about keeping, sung warmly in three parts, is *itself* nostalgic-sounding regardless of what the words say. That risk cannot be closed from inside a text pipeline; it is flagged to `lofn-render-audit` under THE BLIND RULE, where the audio goes out **alone**, before the prompt.
