## THE LOCKED MOOD — precise, not "sad"

> ## COMPETENT, MID-TASK, SLIGHTLY IRRITATED — AND THE LISTENER FLINCHES.

From `EMOTION_TAXONOMY`, the singer's register is **Composure + Ennui** with a thread of **Apathy (Detachment)** — the flat, skilled, faintly bored state of someone doing the last thing before they can leave. The emotion the *listener* has is **Dismay + Recognition**, and ⛔ **it is never available to the singer.**

**This is the whole engine. Write the mood of the person, not the mood of the song.**

Failure modes, named:
- ⛔ **Reverence.** No song opens in awe of its own subject. A hushed opening about the sky is this run's death.
- ⛔ **Melancholy as a default setting.** *Sorrow · Nostalgia · Sentimentality* are not the target and are how this brief goes wrong. The singer is not sad. The singer is **finishing up.**
- ⛔ **Pity.** The setting-aside is a **skill**, not a sin. Nobody in these songs is a fool.

## THE FIVE AHA-MOMENTS (attributed — from `03_panel_debate.md`)

1. ⭐⭐ **"The same gesture, two hundred and sixty years apart."** *(THE PRESERVATIONIST, after Rick Prelinger — handed across the reflection, against his own side.)* The song is not about a catalogue and not about a dead astronomer. **It is the fifteen seconds in which a living person decides something does not count, tonight, in a room.** That physical act is identical to the one that made the Messier catalogue. **That is why the sky is allowed in the song at all, and it is the only reason.**

2. ⭐ **"The listener knows first."** *(THE ORCHESTRATOR OF CHARACTER, after Duke Ellington, off the Gershon seat's reading of the form rule.)* The two elements that cross are **the singer's understanding and the listener's**. Same room, same register, unimaginably far apart. **The singer never arrives at the insight.** This is what makes the register flat and the song hurt.

3. ⭐ **"The gaps are the likeness."** *(THE CATALOGUER, after Melvil Dewey.)* A catalogue is a portrait of its maker's attention, and **what it could not fit is the truest thing about it.** Every one of these songs is a portrait drawn entirely in exclusions.

4. ⭐ **"Unspent, not sepia."** *(THE LOST-FUTURES CRITIC, after Mark Fisher; seconded hard by the Reynolds seat.)* The feeling is **not** *the past was better*. It is *there was a future in that document and nobody took it.* ⛔ **This is the anti-nostalgia clause.** The retro trap — archive-as-mood, tape hiss, dead formats — was named in the room and is banned.

5. ⭐ **"You are doing it right now, while you are tired and want to go home."** *(THE ACCIDENTAL ARCHIVE, after Alan Lomax; it is what made the Jameson seat withdraw the flattery objection, and it is the only version that answers the Fela seat.)* **The listener is the defendant, not the mourner.**

## CONDENSED WORLD CONTEXT
*(Full brief: `00_research_brief.md`. This is what a pair needs in one paragraph.)*

Today's NASA picture is a comet next to a globular cluster, and the caption calls the cluster *"the 30th entry in Charles Messier's catalog of things which are not comets."* Messier hunted comets; the smudges that fooled him he wrote down **so he could ignore them**; that list now points every backyard telescope on Earth and his thirteen comets are forgotten. He died in 1817 not knowing. In the picture the comet's dust trail **appears to pierce the cluster** — the comet is three and a half light-minutes away, the cluster twenty-eight thousand light-years. **The touch is entirely real to the eye and entirely false in space.** Elsewhere today: fifteen earthquakes over magnitude four and a half, of which **one** was flagged significant; a total eclipse in four days lasting **two minutes and eighteen seconds** at maximum, which people have organised a year around; and a photographic collection of Atlantic City sand sculptures from 1880–1920 — pictures of things that were always going to be washed away, which are now the only thing left of them.

- **NOT a list song.** No enumerations, no inventories, no "and then, and then." *(D7 · `core_seed.md` §5)*

## THE LEGIBILITY RULE

> **A stranger must be able to point at the thing being set aside, and at the hand setting it aside, inside the first thirty seconds — without knowing anything about astronomy.**

And the title law, measured today: **a title names a THING, never an argument.** *The Date On The Door*, not *The Body Is The Loophole*. **No persona-brand prefixes.** Tokens that travel: `close`, `room`, `body`. Tokens that die: `system`, `pure`.

The Small Room test, from the panel: ⭐ **could this be sung at a desk, unamplified, and still work?** If a song only functions because the astronomy was explained first, it fails.
