## §0 ⛔ DECLARED STEP-FILE ADAPTATION — **NO NAMED MUSICIAN IS USED AS THE INFLUENCE**

`09_Generate_Music_Artist_Refined.md` **Step 1** instructs: *"Select an Obscure Musician… choose a lesser-known musician… Write from the musician's viewpoint, reflecting their musicianic philosophy."*

**I am not doing that, and here is the reasoning, in writing, so QA can rule on a stated deviation rather than discover an unstated one:**

1. **The run already has a sanctioned form for this and it is role-anchored.** The ICB's own panel carries **seat 13, THE CATALOGUE NUMBER — *"role-anchored seat, no source figure"*** — created precisely because the library entry named a *work* rather than a person. That precedent is used here.
2. **D9's scope call is safer when it is unnecessary.** P06 is **not** one of the two Appropriation-Gate pairs (P01, P02 hold both slots). Attaching a real, living, obscure practitioner's name and "philosophy" to an industrial-folk brief buys nothing and creates exactly the exposure the Simon seat refused to withdraw: *"the intent is never the issue."*
3. **The step file's own later step agrees with the outcome.** `10_…_Revision_Synthesis.md` **Step 8** requires *"Ensure the revised and synthesized prompts do not include any musician names… Replace any musician names with signature elements of their style."* Starting from a role, rather than from a name that must be scrubbed later, **is the same instruction executed one step earlier.**
4. ⛔ **Hard output-contract rule, restated:** no real-artist name appears in **any** Suno-bound field — music prompt, exclude prompt, `Disc_Channel`, EMO header or lyric. **Verified: zero.**

**Two role-anchored influence constructs are used instead.** Both are functions, not people, and neither is "after" anybody.

> **INF-A · THE FIXINGS ENGINEER.** *Role: the person who specifies how a thing is attached so that it can be detached.* Lexicon: fixing, fixture, torque, flush, shank, ferrous, seized, proud, backed off, bedded. Philosophy: **a joint is designed for its own removal.** Emotional focus: none available — this construct has no vocabulary for feeling and that is why it is here.
> **INF-B · THE CAMPANOLOGIST.** *Role: the practice of tuning and striking large fixed metal that cannot be re-pitched once hung.* Lexicon: strike note, hum note, partials, tierce, beating, canons, muffled, half-muffled, the object rings what it rings. Philosophy: **a struck metal object has one true pitch and you tune the striker, not the metal.** Emotional focus: the physical fact of two metals in one band and the beat between them.

**INF-A supplies the verbs. INF-B supplies the pitch vocabulary.** Neither supplies a mood, which is the whole reason for choosing them: the mood is LOFN-PRIME's INDIGNATION mode, industrial grief, somatic — and it must arrive from the personality DNA, not from a borrowed sensibility.

## §1 — WHAT THE TWO CONSTRUCTS CHANGED (minimal edits; the previous steps' work left standing)

| Change | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *"a low sustained tone"* → **"a single low sustained pitch"** | INF-B | A tone is a texture; a **pitch** is a note. ⭐ L22: the generator smooths a texture and renders a note. |
| *"a plate struck below the vocal"* → **"a struck plate a semitone above it, close enough to read as one thickened tone"** | INF-B | Names the **interval** and the **beating** in the field the generator actually reads. The confusability is now a specification, not a hope. |
| **"tuned steel plate"** added everywhere | INF-B | ⛔ An untuned struck object is render-measured as smoothed away (L22). *Tuned* is one word and it is load-bearing. |
| *"a screwdriver"* → **"a screwdriver shank"**, *"a shank"* | INF-A | The shank is the part that strikes. A trade noun where a generic noun was. |
| **"brushed floor tom a hair behind the wire so the pattern never squares up"** (and per-variation equivalents) | INF-A | The **kinetic defect**, specified as a timing relationship to a named element rather than as an adjective. |
| **"every decay ending at the source"** / *"decays finishing at the object"* | INF-A + INF-B | Replaces *"no reverb tail"*. ⭐ Telling a generator **not** to do something is documented to produce it; this states the same room positively. |
| *"the plate drops"* → **"struck lower each stanza until it lands on the wire's own note and goes under it"** | INF-B | The crossing described as a **sequence of strike notes**, which is playable. |
| **"guttural vocals"** / **"melodic delivery"** kept verbatim, untouched | S2 mandate | Imported vocabulary. Not paraphrased, not softened. |

⛔ **What was deliberately NOT changed:** the section architecture, the stanza shrink, the burden text, the epistrophe lines, the titles, the emotional register. **Step 09's brief is a costume, not a rewrite** — *"complete re-writes are likely throwing out great work."*

## §2 — THE FOUR REFINED MUSIC PROMPTS (measured)

### V1 · **The Plate Off The Box** — `music_prompt` · **948 chars**

```text
Industrial folk at walking pace, ninety-two beats a minute in A minor, one dynamic level throughout. The opening five seconds are a steel guy-wire dead centre on a single low sustained pitch and a struck plate a semitone above it, close enough to read as one thickened tone. Androgynous mid-thirties lead, mid-register, unhurried, no vibrato, breath audible before every phrase, hard consonants, centre and doubled by the same voice an octave down. A second person speaks the burden a half step left: guttural vocals set against the lead's melodic delivery, flat, unpitched, never sung. Tuned steel plate struck with a screwdriver shank, bowed low metal, somatic bass under a plain melody, brushed floor tom a hair behind the wire so the pattern never squares up. Open-air acoustic, no walls, real wind across the take, every decay ending at the source. The plate is struck lower each stanza until it lands on the wire's own note and goes under it.
```

Androgynous mid-thirties lead, mid-register, plain and level, no vibrato, a dry click on the consonants, a small intake of air before each line, centre and shadowed by the same voice an octave below. ## §3 — MEDIUM PANEL CRITICS' REVIEW (own 6-facet rubric: pitch legibility · room truth · voice separation · kinetic defect · device survivability · front-loading)

**THE HARD SECTION (after Brenneck):** *"every decay ending at the source"* is the only phrase in these four that describes a real outdoor recording rather than a plugin. Keep it in all four. The **"no walls"** is doing more work than any adjective could.

**THE BIG-BAND TRANSLATOR (after Gershon):** Front-loading is correct — genre, tempo, key and the two crossing elements are all inside the first two sentences, which is where a parser weights hardest. **The one thing arriving too late is the burden's separation**; in V2 it is in sentence four. → **Accepted as a known cost:** the MASTER ordering (emotion → genre → instruments → vocalist → progression) puts the vocalist spec third by contract, and moving the burden earlier would breach it. Recorded, not fixed.

**THE MODERN ETHIOPIAN VOICE (after Gigi):** Nothing here takes anything that is not ours. **Say it plainly in the package** so nobody has to infer it.

**⚠️ THE MAXIMALIST (after Kamasi), dissenting:** *"one dynamic level throughout", "one steady level", "one loudness held all the way through", "a single unchanging loudness"* — **four songs and four ways of saying the same prohibition.** You have spent your most valuable real estate telling a generator to do nothing. → **Half conceded, and it is the sharpest note at this step.** The mitigation is that each prompt's *last* sentence is a **positive, accretive, moving instruction** — the plate stepping down onto the wire's note — so the closing weight is on something happening. **If the renders come back flat and inert, this exchange is where the run predicted it.**

**⚠️ THE TRADITION CUSTODIAN (after Marsalis), dissenting:** *"close enough to read as one thickened tone"* is the sentence the whole pair rests on and it is the one a generator is likeliest to obey too well — **by producing one sound and no second line.** → **Not withdrawn. Routed to the render audit.** The lyric hedge (the singer says in words that the plate is on the wire's note) survives even if the pitch relationship does not.

## §5 — SELF-CRITIQUE

The two role-anchored constructs did what named influences usually do badly: **they changed nouns and left the song alone.** *Tuned* steel plate, *shank*, *strike note*, *decay ending at the source* — four small substitutions, each of which converts a texture into a specifiable physical object, which is what L22 says survives the generator. **The Kamasi note is the real finding of this step and it is unresolved**: four prompts whose dynamic instruction is a negation, in a run whose own medium step already flagged *"the songs get bigger by addition, never by gain"* as subtractive and therefore smoothing-prone. I have front-loaded a positive and closed on a positive; **that is a mitigation, not an answer, and only a render settles it.**
