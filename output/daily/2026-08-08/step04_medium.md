## THE MEDIUM DECISION — one room, six bands

**Every pair is tracked as a band in a room, not as parts in a booth.** This is D11 and it is the single production decision that carries the most weight, because it is the one that answers the Maximalist's size objection **without** enlarging any arrangement.

**What "one room" means as a Suno-bound instruction** *(concrete, because a vague version renders as nothing)*:
- **bleed is allowed and named** — the kit is audible in the vocal mic, the vocal is audible in the room mics;
- **entries are by instrument, not by grid** — someone comes in slightly early or late because they heard a cue, not because a bar started;
- **one continuous take is implied** — no per-section re-amping, no section-boundary reverb changes;
- **the room has a size and the prompt says what it is** — a van, a kitchen, a corridor, a field.

⛔ **Exactly one pair (P04) is dry and close-miked**, and it earns it: a procedural song about a threshold needs a room with no opinion. That is the whole allowance. *(Measured cause: 7 of the last 12 clips were dry-and-close.)*

## ⛔ WHAT THE MEDIUM MAY NOT DO

- **No Glitch-Baroque. No HyperRaaga.** Measured bans.
- **No tape hiss, no vinyl crackle, no cassette warble, no "found recording" framing.** D10 — the retro trap, named in the room and banned. **Analogue warmth is a property of the *room*; it is never a *texture applied on top*.** ⭐ *(This is L37 at the production layer: the depth is made OUT OF the material, never laid ON TOP of it.)*
- **No section-boundary reverb changes, no risers, no drops, no build-into-final-chorus by level.** The songs get bigger by **addition**, never by gain.
- **No long full stops, no untuned drones, no hard-panned non-musical elements** — L22 THE GRAIN LAW, render-measured: these get smoothed and the intent is lost.
- **No amplitude vocabulary** in any style prompt: `relentless` `explosive` `battle` `brutal` `raw` `aggressive`. ⭐ **NAME THE PERSON, NOT THE VOLUME.**

**Self-critique.** The one-room posture is **accretive** (add bleed, add a real space) which is what L22 says survives the generator — good. But **"the songs get bigger by addition, never by gain" is subtractive** (it forbids a build), and subtractive specs are exactly what the 2026-08-07 finding says get smoothed. **The mitigation is that each pair's crossing is an accretive event** — a second element arriving — so the moment the arrangement most needs is built the way the generator will honour. **If the renders come back with conventional builds into final choruses anyway, this paragraph is where the run predicted it.**
