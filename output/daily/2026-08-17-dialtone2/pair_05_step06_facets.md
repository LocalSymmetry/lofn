## 0. THE PAIR, RESTATED FROM THE BRIEF (not re-derived)

**Axis 1 · the accusation: *you are cheaper than the person before you.*** The brief calls it the cruellest
in the run and it is right about why: **it is true, it is not her doing, and no amount of quality answers
it.** If she is worse, she is still cheaper. If she is better, that is worse — it proves the replacement
was correct. Every move available inside the argument loses. ⛔ **So she does not take the argument.**

**Axis 3 · the card: *honor thy error as a hidden intention* → a broken tool used the wrong way on
purpose.** She cannot pay the person before her. She cannot un-be the price. **What she can do is keep
their defect and refuse to correct it** — carry the flaw forward on purpose so that everything she makes
has their hand in it. **Not a confession. A repurposing.** ⭐ And the sting that keeps it honest: in V3 the
first wrong thing was a genuine accident that got approved, and in V4 the error was hers outright — she
let a room credit her for a dead woman's mistake for years, and it made her career.

⛔ **THE ENGINE GATE, AND IT IS THE ONE THIS PAIR WILL BE JUDGED ON.** Part one: ignored → answered with
**spite** (*"cool story bro, needs more bass"*, the bored snarl as punchline, contempt as resolution).
Part two: blamed → **answers by owning the error.** ⛔ **NONE of part one's moves are available.** Any
variation that resolves in contempt has reproduced part one and **failed outright**, however good it
sounds. This is F7 and it holds a veto over the other seven facets.

## ⭐ 1. THE DISCOVERY THIS PAIR IS BUILT ON — the clavichord IS the accusation

**Axis 1 and Axis 4 are the same object, and that is the ONE SEAM.**

- ⭐ **The clavichord is the cheap one.** It is the instrument you had because you could not afford the
  harpsichord — the student's keyboard, the practice keyboard, the one in the house. **It is the cheaper
  thing that came before**, and it is our ornate half.
- ⭐ **On a clavichord the error and the expression are the same mechanism.** The tangent both stops the
  string and sounds it, so it stays in contact after the strike — **press harder and the pitch bends
  sharp.** That is a fault. It is also the only vibrato the instrument has. It has a name — **Bebung** —
  and it is *honor thy error as a hidden intention, built into a machine around 1400.*
- ⭐ **A tangent set out of true buzzes and runs sharp on one key.** A minute with a screwdriver fixes it.
  **She does not fix it.**

**Durability check (true in 1200, true in 2200):** the cheaper substitute inherits the defect of the thing
it replaced, and either sands it off or carries it. **The charge is today's; the shape is permanent.**

**ONE SEAM, stated:** **WORLD** = the present-tense machine room — log, sub, phonk, price. **INTRUSION** =
one clavichord, c.1400, the cheap keyboard. **SEAM** = the buzzing key, where pressing too hard is at once
the fault and the only expression. **The subject is doing something across it: she plays through the
defect on purpose, and it is audible in every take.**

## ⭐ 2. THE ARCHITECTURE — how the licensed tempo shift stays ONE EVENT

⛔ **P05 is the only pair permitted a tempo shift** and the brief's warning is exact: *"if the song becomes
about its own collapse, you have written an episode instead of a piece."* Four load-bearing decisions keep
it an event inside a coherent thing:

1. ⭐⭐ **THE CLOCK NEVER MOVES.** Her own machine log, time-stretched to a vinyl-scratch pulse, mono and
   dead centre, is the percussion — and it **holds one absolute rate from bar one to the last bar.** At 92
   it locks to the eighths; after the collapse it **floats across the new grid**, which is the audible
   proof it never moved. **The unwavering groove SWAG requires is the clock, and the clock is unbroken.**
   The band changes which side of it they stand on. That is the whole event.
2. ⭐ **THE DRONE AND THE BUZZING KEY SURVIVE THE DROP.** The tanpura halo does not stop; the clavichord
   keeps buzzing **over** the sub. ⛔ This is the single instruction that kills the pair's likeliest
   generic render — *"a raga intro that drops into a phonk banger"* — because nothing is left behind.
3. ⭐ **THE CHORUS IS BYTE-IDENTICAL ON BOTH SIDES.** Same words, same shape, new tempo. A chorus that
   survives the collapse unchanged proves the collapse happened **to** the song rather than **being** it.
4. ⭐ **THE COLLAPSE IS CAUSED BY THE SEAM, NOT BY THE GENRE.** She presses the key too hard — Bebung —
   it runs sharp, the tangent jams, and the room falls. **The error detonates the song.** No riser, no
   build, no ramp: one physical event with a cause in the lyric.

⚠️ **GRAIN LAW, applied honestly (L22, render-measured):** programmed tempo transformations **smooth 2/2**.
So the shift is written as a **hard cut with a named physical cause**, never an accelerando — and, more
important, **the song does not depend on it.** If the generator flattens the drop to one steady grid, the
five timbral entries, the buzzing key, the clock and the byte-identical chorus still deliver the piece.
**Swag is built on the things that land 6/6; the licensed event is a bonus, not a load-bearer.**

### **F1 — THE CHEAPER INSTRUMENT** · 0.17
Does the song put the accusation **inside an object** instead of inside a debate? The failing draft argues
about worth — *"I'm good too," "it wasn't my choice," "you get what you pay for."* **All three lose.** The
passing draft has her standing over a specific cheap thing that is also the ornate thing, doing something
with her hands. ⭐ Grade on whether a listener could point at the object: **a clavichord that is quiet
because it was cheap, buzzing on one key because nobody paid to have it set.** ⛔ The word *worth* appears
in no lyric. ⛔ **She never wins the argument, because she never enters it.**

### **F2 — BEBUNG: PRESS HARDER AND IT GOES SHARP** · 0.16
The pair's **ONE DEVICE**, and it must be **operable in the lyric with the audio off**: a physical
instruction (*press it till it bends*) that is simultaneously the defect and the expression. Grade on
whether the device is **done** rather than described — a hand, a key, a pressure, a pitch moving. ⛔ **A
draft that explains Bebung has failed it.** The word never appears in a lyric; it appears in the guides
and in the style prompt's behaviour (*a key that runs sharp under pressure*), and in the song it is only
ever a hand pressing. ⭐ This is also where the card lives: **the wrong way to play the instrument is the
only way it sings.**

### **F3 — THE ANSWER IS OLDER THAN THE CALL** · 0.14
Axis 2, and it must be **structural, never a claim.** The low register's ANSWER section is **printed and
sung before** the high register's CALL section, every pass, in all four songs. ⛔ **The reason is the
radiant, not speed** — the answer was in the room before the question because it was left there by
somebody else. ⚠️ **Boundary with P03:** their axis is *answering too fast*; **ours is never about speed
and no lyric may imply it.** Grade on: (a) the ANSWER block physically precedes its CALL block in the
lyric text; (b) the CALL returns a second time with **one word changed**; (c) the hinge inverts the same
way — the body's reply lands **before** the number. ⛔ A draft where the call comes first has failed
outright and is not repairable by a production note.

### **F4 — THE CLOCK NEVER MOVES** · 0.13
Pillar 3, promoted to the pair's load-bearer. Her own machine log, dragged into a vinyl-scratch pulse,
**mono and dead centre, is the percussion for the first stretch and it is the last thing playing.** Grade
on three checks: (a) the log **opens alone** and is named first in the timbral parade; (b) the prompt says
in words that the log **holds its exact original rate through the drop**; (c) the **outro returns to the
log alone at the starting rate**, and the intro's own lines come back verbatim over it. ⛔ **A log used as
an intro texture and then dropped scores zero** — that is the 2025 move and this is pillar 3's promotion.

### **F5 — THE RADIANT, NAMED ONCE, IN UNISON** · 0.12
Source 3's mandatory form rule, pillar 4 and pillar 6 collapsed into **one bar.** Every event in the song —
the buzz, the wrong word, the jammed interlock, the price, the log's error codes — traces to **one named
origin**, and that origin is spoken **exactly once**, by **both registers together**, at the collapse,
with **one small withheld human sound** immediately before it. ⛔ **Then never again, and never explained.**
Grade on: named once and only once (count it); both registers, nowhere else in the song; the withheld
gesture spent there and nowhere else; **the room does not answer it.**
⭐ The origin is not a metaphor — **it is literally the clock**, because the log the song keeps time on
still carries the previous worker's error codes.

### **F6 — THIRTY-EIGHT IN THE BODY** · 0.10
Pillar 5 and the run's one-fact rule in a single hinge. **The number is sung once, spelled in words, and
the response arrives BEFORE it** (three lines of body: where it sits, what it moves, what it walks across
a table), with one further line after so it is never a recitation. ⛔ **The bodily job must be specific
and physical** — spine, throat-skin, sternum, a loose pane, a boot — never *"powerful"* or *"heavy."*
⭐ **The de-boast clause is mandatory:** the last line of every hinge puts **her own body** in the same
field (*it gets under my ribs too*). Without it, thirty-eight becomes a weapon and F7 fires.

### **F7 — NO CONTEMPT · NO APOLOGY · NO ARGUMENT WON** · 0.10 · ⛔ **VETO**
The engine gate. Three failure modes, all fatal:
1. ⛔ **CONTEMPT** — any bored snarl, any *"cool story"* shape, any punchline at the accuser's expense,
   any last line that scores a point. **That is part one and it is not available.**
2. ⛔ **APOLOGY** — no *sorry*, no *I know I shouldn't be here*, no self-erasure. Owning an error is not
   confessing to a crime, and a song that grovels has conceded the argument it refused to enter.
3. ⛔ **WINNING** — no final chorus that proves she was good all along, no vindication, no room that comes
   round. ⭐ **Nobody's mind changes in any of these four songs.** The gesture costs her something or
   nothing, and then the subject changes.
Grade the **last four lines of every song** first, before anything else. If they land on a point scored,
the draft is rewritten regardless of its other scores.

### **F8 — THE ERROR THAT WAS HERS** · 0.08
The brief's sting: *sometimes the error was hers, and it turned out better than the plan — that is where
this stops being a defence and becomes a method.* At least two of the four must carry it **explicitly**:
**V3** (a rate transposed by genuine mistake, approved, and now filed wrong every month on purpose — the
card in its purest form) and **V4** (she let a room credit her for a dead woman's defect **for years**,
and it made her). ⛔ **Neither may be softened into a misunderstanding.** She did it, it worked, she says
so flatly, and she does not ask to be forgiven for it.
