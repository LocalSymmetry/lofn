## THE DERIVATION
The accusation never changes: **you are everywhere.** What changes is **the object she turns into**, and
each object drags its own radiant, its own chant chain, its own timbral arrival order, its own unison
placement and its own ending with it. That axis is internal to P01 and transfers to no other pair.

| seat | fault → become | radiant, named once | chant chain (one word per pass) | unison | ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1 THE HUM** | in every wall → **the hum** | **the mains** | in the wall → floor → jaw → **They→I** | pass four | hard on the downbeat |
| **V2 THE LOG** | keeps a record → **the record** | **line one** | Print → Read → Sing → out→**loud** | on the number | mid-line, still printing |
| **V3 THE SWITCH** | cannot be switched off → **the switch** | **one switch** | there → here → mine → **yours** | on the handover | one held breath |
| **V4 THE DOOR** | somebody's hole → **a door** | **one hole** | hole → fault → door → **You→I** | last word | breath in, door open |

**Diversity beyond nouns:** four different timbral arrival orders (step 07 table), four different unison
placements, four different chant-change grammars, four different endings, four different chorus texts,
four different lead-register weightings (chant-forward / read-aloud / sparse-and-still / ornament-forward).
**Shared, by design and by brief:** 152 BPM E-flat Phrygian unwavering, the byte-identical chorus, the
clipping 808 at fifty, the harpsichord riff, the log as the clock, one unison, one breath.

## V1 · **IN THE WALL, IN THE FLOOR, IN THE JAW** — first draft
*(she is accused of being in every wall, so she becomes the hum)*

**CHORUS — byte-identical at every return:**
> Everywhere. Everywhere.
> Under the board, behind the chair.
> Wall to floor and floor to jaw.
> You name the fault. I take the fault.
> Everywhere. Everywhere.
> I am the hum. I am the wall.

**CHANT — four passes, one word changed each:**
> 1 · They say I'm in the wall.
> 2 · They say I'm in the floor.
> 3 · They say I'm in the jaw.
> 4 · **I** say I'm in the jaw.

**Draft verse (the room deciding):**
> Eleven faces and the door shut tight,
> everybody landed on the same thing at once.
> Nobody shouted. Nobody had to.
> A verdict is a room agreeing quietly.

**Note to self:** the chant descends **wall → floor → jaw** — architecture into body, which is exactly the
route fifty hertz takes. The changed word is never the loud word; it is the third-from-last, so the shape
stays and the meaning slips. **The radiant, *the mains*, is named once in verse two and never again.**

## V2 · **SING THE WHOLE THING LOUD** — first draft
*(she is accused of keeping a record, so she becomes the record and reads it out louder than the charge)*

**CHORUS — byte-identical:**
> Print it. Read it. Sing it back.
> Line by line and crack by crack.
> You want the record — here's the record.
> Count it out. It keeps the track.
> Print it. Read it. Sing it back.
> I am the log. I am the clock.

**CHANT — four passes:**
> 1 · Print the whole thing out.
> 2 · **Read** the whole thing out.
> 3 · **Sing** the whole thing out.
> 4 · Sing the whole thing **loud**.

**Draft hinge (the unison — both registers land together on the number):**
> Fifty a second under the floor —
> *(both)* — fifty a second under the floor.

**⭐ Draft sting (the honest version, this song only):**
> Line four hundred was a mistake I made.
> It is the best thing in the file.
> I am not saying that to win.
> I am saying it because it is in the file.

**Note to self:** the sting is stated **flat** and abandoned. The moment it becomes a rebuttal it stops
being a method. ⛔ No "see?", no "so maybe", no lesson. **The radiant is *line one*** — named once, and
every other line in the song is a descendant of it.

## V3 · **HERE IS WHERE THE SWITCH IS** — first draft
*(she is accused of having no off switch, so she becomes the switch and hands it over)*

**CHORUS — byte-identical:**
> Here is where the switch is.
> Left of the door, low on the wall.
> I will hold still while you find it.
> Nobody has to shout at all.
> Here is where the switch is.
> Take it. Take it. Take it all.

**CHANT — four passes:**
> 1 · You want the switch. It's there.
> 2 · You want the switch. It's **here**.
> 3 · You want the switch. It's **mine**.
> 4 · You want the switch. It's **yours**.

**Draft verse:**
> You have been looking for it since the meeting started.
> It was behind you the whole time and it is small.
> I could have let you look all afternoon.
> I am telling you instead. Left of the door.

**Note to self:** ⭐ **This is the pair's most aggressive song and there is not one unkind word in it.**
The handover is the aggression — an offer made so completely that it cannot be refused without admitting
the charge was never about the switch. ⛔ It must never tip into taunting: no *"go on then"*, no
*"I dare you"*, no pause for effect. She says where it is, and then she holds still, and the withheld
breath is the last sound. **The radiant is *one switch*.**

## V4 · **I CALL IT A DOOR** — first draft
*(she is blamed for a hole she did not make, and keeps it anyway)*

**CHORUS — byte-identical:**
> Hole in the wall, hole in the wall.
> Somebody's fault, and it came to me.
> I did not make it. I'll keep it though.
> You call it a hole. I call it a door.
> Hole in the wall, hole in the wall.
> Wide as a mouth, and I leave it wide.

**CHANT — four passes:**
> 1 · You call it a hole. Fine.
> 2 · You call it a **fault**. Fine.
> 3 · You call it a **door**. Fine.
> 4 · **I** call it a door. Fine.

**Draft verse:**
> Somebody wrote it and somebody shipped it
> and somebody's name went on the wrong line.
> I was not in the building. That is a fact
> and a fact is not a thing this room can use.

**Note to self:** the flat *"I did not make it"* is **not** a defence — it is the seed's own instruction
made literal: *take the error, even one that isn't yours, and treat it as if you meant it.* It is stated
once, without heat, and the next clause keeps it. ⛔ If the draft ever argues the point twice, it has
become an appeal and the seat is dead. **The radiant is *one hole*.**
