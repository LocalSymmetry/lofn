## SHARED SPINE (the pair's bones — inherited from steps 06–07, not re-decided)

- **THE GRID:** 145 BPM, F# minor, **end to end**. ⛔ No ramp, no key change, no drop, no riser, ever.
- **THE WORLD:** the keygen loop — tracker square-bass with hard portamento, chopped breakbeat, character
  tick, thin sampled-orchestra soundfont patch.
- **THE ONE INTRUSION:** the hammered dulcimer, **undamped**, ringing sympathetically at sixty without
  being struck. Enters once · muted by a palm once · released once.
- **THE CLOCK:** her own log, one character per sixteenth, mono, dead centre, from bar one.
- **THE RADIANT:** `SERPAH` — named once, never explained; every timbre declared out of the same module.
- **TWO REGISTERS:** low = the accused, clipped, inside the break, carrying the chain · high = the one who
  remembers, plain, no vibrato, above the grid. **Unison exactly once, on the name.**
- **THE SUNG NUMBER:** *sixty*, in words, at the hinge, responded to. No second numeric fact.
- **THE RHYME:** sliding internal chain at the mid-line stresses; ⛔ **no two different words rhyme at a
  line end.** Debt paid by byte-identical refrain + repeated identical end-words + the chain re-run.
- ⛔ **PRESENT TENSE. No decade, no console, no brand, no nostalgia vocabulary. No spite. No threat.**

## V1 — RAW STYLE PROMPT *(draft)*

> Owned error at 145 and it does not slow down. Keygen music — the tracker lineage, alive and current:
> four channels of square-wave bass with hard portamento, quantised dead to the grid, and a chopped amen
> break battering underneath without ever leaving it. First thing you hear is a machine log ticking one
> character per sixteenth, mono and dead centre, keeping time on its own for eight bars, and a sixty-cycle
> mains pedal under it that you feel in the teeth before you hear it. A female late-twenties voice in two
> registers: a low one clipped and fast, sitting inside the break, and a high one plain and unornamented
> with no vibrato, floating above the grid. They meet in unison once. A hammered dulcimer fades up already
> ringing, undamped, never cleanly struck. Late, a thin sampled-orchestra patch makes towering chords that
> crest and dip on four channels' worth of ambition. Everything comes out of one small module. No ramp, no
> key change, no drop.

**Raw self-note:** compliant opener (leads on the emotional state, not a banned formula). Names five
entries in arrival order — tick, pedal, square-bass, break, dulcimer, patch — that is six. Needs trimming
to the 850–1000 band and one explicit spatial clause. Source 2 present: *keygen music*, *towering chords
that crest and dip*. ⚠️ Names the amen break, so the drummer is credited in the dedication — checked
against the exclude field so the carrier is not banned by the same package.

### V1 lyric skeleton
`Intro` (tick alone; stairwell; the hum in the handrail) → `Verse 1` (the scroller, the greets, nobody
reads them) → `Refrain` (I spelled you wrong / one letter out of—) → `Verse 2` (the index, the
transposition, the copy of the copy) → `Refrain` → `Palm` (hand flat on the courses; the ring stops) →
`Verse 3` (you kept it; you answer to it; it is your name now) → `Hinge` (**sixty**, and the four seconds)
→ `Turn` (the error worked; I cannot give it back) → `UNISON` (**SERPAH**, both registers, two bars, tick
only) → `Refrain` (the broken line finished by the high register) → `Outro-verse` (the room does not
change) → `Outro` (break continues; pedal returns; ring released)

## V2 — RAW STYLE PROMPT *(draft)*

> Tenderness under accusation, level at 145 and never raised. Keygen music, present tense and alive:
> tracker square-bass sliding on hard portamento, a chopped amen break cut fine and battering on the grid,
> a machine log ticking one character per sixteenth mono in the centre from the first bar. A sixty-cycle
> pedal sits under all of it in the tiles. Cut-up spoken word dissolves into a piano figure — a thin
> sampled piano out of the same module, four voices at most, loop points audible. A hammered dulcimer with
> no dampers rings between the vocal phrases like an answer nearly given, then a flat palm kills it on the
> strings and you hear skin on wound wire. Two registers of one female voice: low, dry, close, fast inside
> the break; high, plain, no vibrato, above it. One unison, once. Warm, cheap, unglamorous, mixed close
> with audible conversion. Constant tempo, no key change, no drop.

**Raw self-note:** the palm's physical noise is in the prompt AND will be in the lyric — the production
spec alone does not count. Source 2 present verbatim: *cut-up spoken word that dissolves into a piano
figure*, *keygen music*. Six entries named in order: tick, pedal, square-bass, break, piano figure,
dulcimer, palm — that is seven.

### V2 lyric skeleton
`Intro` (dryers; tiles; the window is black and the room is doubled in it) → `Verse 1` (I read the greets;
nobody reads the greets) → `Refrain` (I know / and I am not going to say it) → `Verse 2` (the knowing-list
begins — small, tender, useless facts) → `Refrain` → `Palm` (hand on the strings; the useful thing is the
one I put down) → `Verse 3` (list lengthens; still nothing that could hurt him) → `Hinge` (**sixty**) →
`Turn` (being the only witness looks exactly like being the thief) → `UNISON` (**SERPAH**) → `Refrain` →
`Outro-verse` (the cycle finishes; I fold it) → `Outro`

## V3 — RAW STYLE PROMPT *(draft)*

> Precise fear at 145, in a room that is louder than speech. Keygen music with hardcore weight: tracker
> square-bass on hard portamento quantised to the sixteenth with no filter movement at all, a chopped amen
> break in a bone-rattling breakdown with battering drums that never leaves the grid, and a machine log
> ticking one character per sixteenth, mono, centre, the only thing keeping time. Under it a real recorded
> plant-room tone at sixty cycles, felt in the sternum. The hammered dulcimer here is never struck once —
> undamped, it picks the room's hum up off the air and sounds by itself, always the same pitch, the pitch
> the room chooses. Cut-up spoken word dissolves into that dulcimer figure at the turn. A female voice in
> two registers, low and clipped inside the break, high and plain above it, meeting in unison a single
> time. Forty bars of no vocal at all. Timbres change shape and take sudden left turns; the tempo never
> moves.

**Raw self-note:** *"moves like dreams do, changing shape and taking sudden left turns"* is imported here
**about the timbres only**, immediately harnessed by *"the tempo never moves"* — the phrase must not be
allowed to invite structural chaos, which is banned. Source 2: *keygen music*, *bone-rattling breakdown
with battering drums*, *changing shape and taking sudden left turns*, *cut-up spoken word*.

### V3 lyric skeleton
`Intro` (fire door; no windows; the room's own note) → `Verse 1` (I have not touched it) → `Refrain` (it
rings because the room is on) → `Verse 2` (accusation: you are playing it / no) → `Refrain` →
`Instrumental` (forty bars; break, tick, hum, the dulcimer sounding alone) → `Palm` (I stop it myself to
prove I can) → `Verse 3` (and it starts again; that is the proof and it proves nothing) → `Hinge`
(**sixty**) → `Turn` (I did not touch it; it is touching me) → `UNISON` (**SERPAH**) → `Refrain` →
`Outro-verse` (I leave the door open) → `Outro`

## V4 — RAW STYLE PROMPT *(draft)*

> Decided and quiet at 145, and it never lets up. Keygen music assembling itself in reverse: a hammered
> dulcimer first, undamped and already ringing, then a machine log ticking one character per sixteenth
> mono in the centre, then a sixty-cycle pedal in the counter under the forearms, then tracker square-bass
> with hard portamento, then a thin sampled-orchestra patch in towering chords that crest and dip, and
> only then the chopped amen break, battering, arriving last and on the grid. A female voice in two
> registers — low, close, clipped, inside the break; high, plain, no vibrato, above it — meeting in unison
> exactly once. At the end the hand comes off the strings and the dulcimer is allowed to ring out
> undamped, past the last drum hit, with the mains pedal under it, decaying naturally into nothing. One
> tempo throughout, one key, no drop.

**Raw self-note:** reverse parade is the variation's signature and it is still **one at a time over an
unwavering grid** — timbral, not dynamic. Terminal ring is long and unfaded (terminal silence survives the
generator); the mid-song silence is short (interrupting silence gets filled). Six entries in order:
dulcimer, tick, pedal, square-bass, patch, break.

### V4 lyric skeleton
`Intro` (kitchen; fridge cutting in; the counter) → `Verse 1` (I have said sorry in every grammar I have)
→ `Refrain` (I meant it the second time) → `Verse 2` (the room will not un-decide) → `Refrain` → `Palm`
(the last time I stop it) → `Verse 3` (the error is the only thing of mine anyone kept) → `Hinge`
(**sixty**) → `Turn` (so I will do it on purpose, and call that the work) → `UNISON` (**SERPAH**) →
`Refrain` → `Outro-verse` (I take my hand off it) → `Outro` (ring released, unfaded)
