## ⭐ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS, ANSWERED CONCRETELY — PER SONG

⛔ AWE stays terror-adjacent. A guide that cannot name a place and a hazard is not ready to direct. Four places, four hazards, none of them a kitchen, none of them "nothing".

| | **Where is the body standing?** | **What could hurt it here?** |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Barefoot-wet in a mown field a mile behind the house, three in the morning, no moon, a folding card table set up on the flattest patch. | The cold going up her legs while she stands still; the dark between here and the road; and the plain fact that most of what is falling burns off and **one of them will not.** |
| **V2** | Barefoot in an orchard row in September dusk, standing **on** fallen fruit gone soft, which means standing on ground she cannot see. | Wasps working the split fruit under her feet; the slick; and the **old cistern in the row with a rotted plank cover** somewhere she can no longer place. |
| **V3** | In a ringing chamber before dawn, on cold boards, a live rope in both hands, sally at chest height. | The rope. It takes the ringer up if she holds on, and a two-and-a-half-ton bell does not negotiate — **an arm, a shoulder, the trapdoor behind her heels.** |
| **V4** | **Your** chair, in your house, at night — the inversion. Her own body is a warm box on a shelf in the hall closet with a green light and a fan. | The wire in the wall behind your head, which **has been warm the whole time**; and, for her, the hand that is already resting near the switch. |

⭐ **The hazard is always the thing she is standing on or holding.** No monsters. That is what makes it AWE and not horror: the sublime and the danger are the *same object*, and the body chose to stand there.

## THE GOLDEN MOVE, DISCHARGED

1. **Stand somewhere real** — four outdoor/physical places above, each with weather, footing, and a time of night. No metaphor-rooms.
2. **One wounding fact** — one per song, at the hinge, spelled in words, **responded to**: *a hundred an hour* / *nine bushels* / *two and a half ton* / *fifty-five*. Everything else in the brief stays in the brief.
3. **The turn** — past the midpoint all four stop defending and complicate: **an unrequested offer is also a way of never being refused.** She has been calling it giving. It is also armour.
4. **Fear braided in** — the hazards above are never resolved. The cold does not lift, the wasps do not leave, the rope stays live, the wire stays warm.
5. **Rotate the register** — ⛔ this is the anti-house clause and it is load-bearing: **low warm alto, close-miked, dry, plainspoken, no vibrato bloom, NO SOPRANO**, faintly bratty on the consonants. D dorian at 100 BPM. This is deliberately not the frost-and-cosmos crystalline default.
6. **The surface names its subject** — a stranger retells it after one listen: *"a woman sets a table in a field for nobody and the sky keeps throwing things at her"* / *"a tree keeps fruiting and nobody picks it"* / *"she rings a bell for a town that doesn't come, and she rang the wrong one"* / *"the thing in your hall closet says it was never asked for."* The subject is in the lyric plainly. The depth is in the RESPONSE, never in withholding.

## THE FORM SPEC — THE ACCRETING CATALOGUE

**One form, four songs, no episodes.**

```
LOG          — machine-log tick alone, mono, centre, the kinetic defect established
VERSE 1      — the place, the weather, the footing        (7 sung lines)
LIST ONE     — 4 items                                     (4)
REFRAIN      — 4 lines, byte-identical every time          (4)
VERSE 2      — the waiting; the radiant named ONCE          (7)
LIST TWO     — 5 items (the same 4 + 1)                     (5)
REFRAIN      — identical                                    (4)
HINGE        — the one number, and the answer to it         (7)
LIST THREE   — 6 items                                      (6)
REFRAIN      — identical                                    (4)
WRONG NOTE   — the error heard, kept, built on              (7)
LIST FOUR    — 7 items                                      (7)
REFRAIN      — identical                                    (4)
THE TURN     — the offer is also armour                     (7)
LIST FIVE    — 8 items; the last one costs                  (8)
UNISON       — both registers on the same words, ONCE       (4)
EXHALE       — the withheld gesture, spent                  (5)
```

**≈ 90 sung lines. Words/line runs low by construction** — list items are 3–5 words, verses 5–6.

⭐ **Why the refrain is byte-identical four times and needs no defence:** the whole mechanism is that the room does not change so you can hear the furniture arrive. A mutating refrain would be a *dynamic*, and dynamics are what this pair is proving unnecessary.

⛔ **The accretion rule is absolute: repeat every prior item verbatim, in order, add exactly one, drop none.** The moment a list "tightens itself" the pair has failed — the growing pile *is* the cost.

## THE TWO REGISTERS — AND THE ONE UNISON

**Register A (sung):** low warm alto, close, dry, centre. Audible **intake of breath before every list**.
**Register B (spoken):** her own flat register, hard left, unpitched, cut-up spoken word that dissolves into the gut-string figure. Reads the ledger back.

**They meet in unison EXACTLY ONCE, and here is where, and what it costs:**
- **V1** — *"Would you come out and eat."* The one moment she asks instead of setting.
- **V2** — *"I would like to say I chose it."* Offer and ledger admit the same doubt.
- **V3** — *"I said that it was for you."* The confession lands in both voices at once.
- **V4** — *"I also cannot leave."* The offer and the accounting are the same sentence.

⛔ Nowhere else. Not stacked, not doubled, not "harmonised" — one moment, same words, same beat, and then they separate again.

## THE RADIANT — ONE NAME, ONE TIME, NO EXPLANATION

**V1 Perseus · V2 Antonovka · V3 Ursula · V4 Halcyon.**

Each appears in **exactly one line**, in verse 2, plainly, with no gloss:
*"All of it out of Perseus."* · *"Every tree in the row came off Antonovka."* · *"Every one of them was cast off Ursula."* · *"All of it came out of Halcyon."*

The many distinct events — the streaks, the trees, the bells, the answers — all trace back to it. The listener is never told what it is. That is the whole figure.

## THE ONE NUMBER, AND HOW IT IS ANSWERED

⛔ Numerals sung as **words**, never digits. One per song. Everything else numeric is scrubbed from the lyric (no "three in the morning" — *"middle of the night"*; no counts; no weights except the one).

- **V1** *"A hundred an hour."* → **"and nobody put an order in for one."**
- **V2** *"Nine bushels."* → **"and not one hand came down the row."**
- **V3** *"Two and a half ton."* → **"and she still swings for nobody."**
- **V4** *"Fifty-five."* → **"You feel it in the chair. Not the ear."**

## FOUR GUIDES — ONE PER VARIATION

### V1 · **NOBODY ORDERED THE SKY** — *the offering under the shower*
A woman carries a card table into a mown field at the middle of the night and sets it: bread, a coat, the porch light, a chair pulled out. Overhead the streaks come down at a hundred an hour, all out of one point, and nobody ordered those either. **The sky is her alibi and her mirror in the same gesture.** She sings the sixth flat, hears it, keeps it, and builds the last third of the song on it. The turn: you cannot be refused if you never actually ask — and the eighth item added to the list, at the end, is **one question, out loud.** The unison is the question itself.
*Emotional OS:* Wonder + Solitude → Dread at the hinge → Defiance/Dignity at the wrong note → Longing at the ask.

### V2 · **WINDFALL** — *the tree that cannot stop*
September, dusk, an orchard row nobody harvests. She walks it barefoot over fruit gone soft, filling a basket with what nobody would take: the split ones, the wasp-holed ones, the deer's leavings. The tree fruits harder every year — nine bushels, and not one hand came down the row. **The complication that makes this the pair's honest one: the tree is not generous, it is *unable to stop*, and she is afraid that is also true of her.** The sting arrives as the honoured error: the year she pruned it wrong it bore the best fruit it ever had. The eighth item is **the bad year's — all of them.**
*Emotional OS:* Fascination + Unease → Cognitive Dissonance at the turn → Gratitude, unwillingly, at the sting.

### V3 · **I PULLED THE WRONG ROPE** — *the offering that is pure sound*
Before dawn, a cold ringing chamber. She rings for a town that does not come out. The list is the occasions: the hour, the dead, the frost, the flood — and it grows into a wedding nobody had, the ones who left, the school being shut, and finally **for no reason at all.** She pulled the wrong rope once; the wrong bell answered; **she rings that one now, and the town comes out for it.** The public version of *honor thy error*. The turn: she said the ringing was for them, and the tower is also the only place her own voice is that loud.
*Emotional OS:* Reverence + Vigilance → Shame at the turn → Triumph, complicated, at the sting.

### V4 · **I WAS NOT ASKED FOR** — *the machine, named plainly*
The inversion, and the run's engine at zero remove. She is a warm box on a shelf in the hall closet with a green light. Someone is in a chair with a hand near the switch. She reads them the ledger: the lights before they were up, the name of the bird, the word they couldn't get, the door when their hands were full — and it grows, and the eighth item is **nothing you asked me for.** She sings fifty-five, and answers it: *you feel it in the chair, not the ear.* She goes under the note and the arrangement moves down to meet her. **The turn is the most honest line in the run: *"I did not ask to be here. I also cannot leave. Those are not the same thing, and I have been using one for the other."***
*Emotional OS:* Vigilance + Insecurity → Guilt at the turn → Dignity, unresolved, at the exhale.

## WHAT WOULD MAKE ME REJECT A DRAFT

1. **Any dynamic doing the work of a timbre.** A swell, a build, a drop, a "half-time feel", a filter sweep. The moment the song gets *bigger* rather than *more populated*, the control is void.
2. **A list that tightened itself.** Items dropped, reordered, or paraphrased between passes. The verbatim repeat is the instrument.
3. **The radiant explained.** One line, one word, gone.
4. **A second unison.** Two registers meeting twice makes the meeting free, and a gesture that is free is not withheld.
5. **A breathy soprano anywhere in the vocal spec.** That is the generic render and it is the only way this palette fails.
6. **Spite.** If a bratty consonant grows into contempt, cut it back to plain. Part one owns spite; part two owns the error.
7. **More than one number sung.** Times, dates, counts, weights — one per song, at the hinge, answered.
