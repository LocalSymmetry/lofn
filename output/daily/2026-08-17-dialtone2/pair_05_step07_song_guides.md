# ⭐ THE TWO QUESTIONS, ANSWERED BEFORE ANY GUIDE IS WRITTEN

## **WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?**

**It is standing, in four rooms, and in three of them it is standing exactly where somebody else used to
stand.** That is not a theme. It is a floor position, and it is the pair.

- **V1 — a back-room repair bench, winter, mid-afternoon.** Coat on indoors because the heat is off in the
  back. One strip of low sun across the bench, dust in it. A clavichord case open, lid up, the wrestplank
  showing. Cold hands: the **first press of the day is always wrong** because the fingers are stiff, and
  on this instrument a wrong press is a wrong pitch. Brass tangents in a row like teeth. A screwdriver on
  the bench, within reach, the whole song.
- **V2 — the desk that was hers, a floor emptying at the hour she used to leave.** ⭐ **The chair is still
  set for a different body** — seat height, lumbar block, armrests — and it is the most physical fact in
  the song. Screen glow, the fan wall through the wall behind, a paper cup. The building's lights go off
  in banks, from the far end.
- **V3 — night shift at a machine, standing, boots on a steel plate.** Heat off the housing. Oil smell.
  A guard that is supposed to stop the machine when it opens, and a **bent screwdriver jammed in the
  interlock so it reads closed.** Her left hand goes in past the guard because that is what the shortcut
  is for. Somebody else bent that screwdriver.
- **V4 — a warm room full of people, and she is in the doorway with a glass she is not drinking.** Bodies
  raise the room tone. The venue's floor is loaded from below and she can feel it through her shoes before
  she can hear it. She is being congratulated. **The body is standing still and would like to leave.**

⛔ **NOT a stage, not a studio, not "the night."** Four rooms you could sweep. Each has a door, a light
source you could name, and one object she is going to do something with.

## **WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?**

⭐ **This pair is the run's NEWS-anchored ambitious slot, and its stakes must be physical, not rhetorical.**
Four hazards, one per room, all real, none metaphorical, and **none of them resolves**:

1. **V1 — a clavichord string is a wire under tension and the tangent is a brass blade.** Cold fingers,
   thin wire, sharp brass. A snapped string whips. And the string that is *most* likely to go is the one
   on the key she keeps pressing too hard. ⭐ **The hazard and the honouring are the same key.**
2. **V2 — the chair.** Six hours in a chair set for a taller body puts the load in the low back and the
   right shoulder, and it does not come out at the end of the shift. Plus the thing she has to hear: **a
   recording of the other woman's voice in the training file**, still in the system, played on purpose.
3. **V3 — the finger.** This is the sharpest hazard in the pair. The interlock is held open by a bent
   screwdriver so the spindle keeps turning with the guard up; her hand goes in past it to clear a jam
   because it is faster; **the machine does not know she is there.** ⛔ It is never resolved, never
   removed, and she never says the word *safe.*
4. **V4 — the moment before speaking**, and the floor. Thirty-eight through the boards into the shoes, a
   warm room, a compliment landing, and a sentence she has swallowed for years. **The physical risk is
   small and the social cost is total**, which is the correct proportion for this one.

⛔ **No metaphor stands in for any of these.** *A weight in my chest* is decoration and gets the draft
rewritten. **A tangent, a chair height, a spindle and a doorway are facts.**

# THE SIX GUIDES

## GUIDE 1 — **THE CHEAPER INSTRUMENT** *(facet F1, weight 0.17)*

**Storytelling techniques.** (a) **The object that carries the argument** — the accusation is never
debated, it is placed in the room as a thing with a lid and a serial plate, and she works on it.
(b) **The declined action** — a tool is within reach for the whole song and is not used; the drama is a
minute of work that does not happen.
**Mood & emotional arc.** Open in **Vigilance** — dry, procedural, somebody arriving at a bench. Move to
**Recognition** when the defect is found and understood. Land on **Resolve**, at the same temperature as
the opening. ⛔ **No lift.** The last chorus is not brighter than the first.
**Instrumentation & texture.** A time-stretched machine log dragged to a vinyl-scratch pulse, mono and
dead centre; a tanpura drone halo in F; a clavichord so quiet the room has to shut up for it, one key
buzzing; close-mic breath; tabla-as-snare. **The clavichord is mic'd close because it cannot be mic'd any
other way** — that quietness is the cheapness, audible.
**Production & effects.** Small hard room, no treatment, **hum kept in the master.** Sub mono. Breaths not
de-essed. The clavichord's key noise — the thunk of the key bed, the tangent's contact — is **left in and
mixed up**, because the mechanism is the subject.
**Structure & dynamics.** 92 BPM, F minor, fifteen sections. ANSWER always before CALL. One collapse at
~62%, caused by a hand. 140 after, same clock. ~3:20–3:40.
**Rhyme.** Verses: a tight rhyme chain on hard single syllables (*key / see / free*, *bends / hands /
ends*). Chorus: **byte-identical repetition**, three times, needing no defence.
**Poetic devices.** **Anaphora** (*I did not… I could…*) and **antimetabole** at the seam (*the wrong note
is the only note that came with a hand on it*).
**Hook techniques.** (1) A physical instruction as the hook — *press it till it bends* — that a listener
can perform on any object in reach. (2) The clock alone in the first seconds, so the room learns the pulse
before any word. (3) The chorus returning **unchanged after the tempo collapse.**
**Narrative seed.** *She is given the dead woman's instrument, finds the one key set wrong, and puts the
screwdriver down.*

## GUIDE 2 — **BEBUNG: PRESS HARDER AND IT GOES SHARP** *(facet F2, weight 0.16)*

**Storytelling techniques.** (a) **The device performed, never explained** — the song contains an
instruction and a consequence and no exposition. (b) **The physical hinge** — the song's largest structural
event has the smallest gesture as its cause: one finger, more pressure.
**Mood & emotional arc.** Verses hold at conversational level; the pressure builds **in the hand, not in
the mix.** ⛔ **No riser, no build, no swell.** The event is a pitch moving and a key jamming.
**Instrumentation & texture.** The clavichord's bend must be **audible as pitch**, so the top line doubles
it with a gamaka-style microtonal slide and then leaves it exposed. The tanpura holds one note through it
so the bend has something true to be sharp against. ⭐ **The drone is the reference the error is measured
by** — without it the bend is just a sound.
**Production & effects.** Pitch drift is *not* corrected anywhere. ⛔ **No transparent pitch correction on
any voice or key.** The bend is left ±40 cents. One take.
**Structure & dynamics.** The Press section is **five lines and under ten seconds**; it is followed
immediately by the collapse. ⛔ Nothing may sit between the cause and the consequence.
**Rhyme.** The press section drops end rhyme entirely — the return is carried by the repeated imperative.
**The debt is named and paid:** what returns in place of rhyme is the chorus hook, whole, in the next bar.
**Poetic devices.** **Epizeuxis** (*I press. I press harder.*) and **polyptoton** on the bend (*bends /
bent / bending* — used once each, never stacked).
**Hook techniques.** (1) The hook is an imperative in the second person, so the listener is the one
pressing. (2) The pitch moving under a held drone is the most legible "wrong" in music and needs no gloss.
**Narrative seed.** *The only way this instrument sings is the way that breaks it.*

## GUIDE 3 — **THE ANSWER IS OLDER THAN THE CALL** *(facet F3, weight 0.14)*

**Storytelling techniques.** (a) **Inverted antiphony** — the response section is printed and sung
**before** the section it responds to, every pass. (b) **The second ask, one word changed**, so the room's
insistence is audible without a new argument.
**Mood & emotional arc.** ANSWER blocks sit in **Dignity**: chest register, flat, close, unhurried, no
edge. CALL blocks sit in **Skepticism** and then **Unease**: higher, thinner, ornamented, genuinely asking.
⛔ **The CALL is never a villain.** The room is not stupid and is not sneering; it wants the note cleaned
up, which is a reasonable thing to want.
**Instrumentation & texture.** The low register sits on the sub's shoulder, dead centre; the high register
is placed slightly wide and further back, with the drone under it. **The physical staging is the
argument** — the answer is nearer to you than the question.
**Production & effects.** The low register is **almost spoken**, conversation volume, breath audible, no
reverb. The high register carries the only wet signal in the mix.
**Structure & dynamics.** ANSWER (5 lines) → CALL (4 lines) → CHORUS. Twice, identically ordered. At the
hinge the same inversion happens **inside** one section: three lines of body, then the number.
**Rhyme.** ANSWER blocks close on the chorus hook, so the answer rhymes forward into a thing that has not
been sung yet. CALL blocks rhyme **backward** into the answer that preceded them.
**Poetic devices.** **Hypophora inverted** and **anadiplosis** across the ANSWER/CALL join.
**Hook techniques.** (1) A hook that has already been sung twice before the first chorus arrives, so the
chorus lands as a **return** rather than an introduction. ⭐ *(L21: song is made of returns.)*
**Narrative seed.** *The answer was in the room before the question, because somebody left it there.*
⚠️ **P03 boundary, binding:** ⛔ **no lyric may imply she is quick.** The priority is **inheritance**.

## GUIDE 4 — **THE CLOCK NEVER MOVES** *(facet F4, weight 0.13)*

**Storytelling techniques.** (a) **The unbroken witness** — one element present in every bar, unchanged,
so every other event is measured against it. (b) **The verbatim return** — the intro's lines come back
word for word at the end over the same pulse.
**Mood & emotional arc.** Flat by design; the clock has no feelings and that is why it can be trusted.
**Instrumentation & texture.** Her own machine log, time-stretched into a **vinyl-scratch pulse, mono and
dead centre**, alone for the opening seconds. ⭐ **Percussion, not intro texture.** At 92 it locks to the
eighths; after the collapse it **holds the same absolute rate and floats across the new grid**, and that
floating is the proof. It is the last thing playing.
**Production & effects.** Mono, dry, centre, no ducking — it is **never side-chained to the sub**, because
a clock that flinches when the bass arrives is not a clock.
**Structure & dynamics.** Named **first** in the timbral parade in every prompt. Present in the outro
alone. ⛔ A log that stops after the intro **scores zero on this facet**, and no arrangement rescues it.
**Rhyme.** N/A — the log is not sung. Its **content** is the radiant's error codes, which is why the
clock is the origin rather than a reference to it.
**Poetic devices.** **Ostinato as a rhetorical figure**: the thing that does not change is what makes the
change legible.
**Hook techniques.** (1) A pulse learned in the first seconds and rewarded in the last. (2) The tempo
event heard as **the band moving, not the clock moving.**
**Narrative seed.** *The thing keeping time is made out of somebody else's mistakes.*

## GUIDE 5 — **THE RADIANT, NAMED ONCE, IN UNISON** *(facet F5, weight 0.12)*

**Storytelling techniques.** (a) **The single origin** — Source 3's form rule taken literally: the buzz,
the wrong word, the jammed interlock, the rate on the invoice, the error codes in the clock and the
compliment in the doorway are all streaks off one point. (b) **The unrepeated word.**
**Mood & emotional arc.** **Revelation of the flat kind.** ⛔ Not a climax, not a reveal, not a swell. Both
registers arrive on one word at the same instant and the room does not answer.
**Instrumentation & texture.** The collapse bar is **the clock and two voices and nothing else** — the
drone drops out for exactly that bar and comes straight back under the sub. ⭐ The emptiness is the
loudest thing in the song and it costs nothing to make.
**Production & effects.** ⛔ **No reverb tail on the name**, no double, no harmony, no delay throw. Dry,
centre, one take, both registers. **The withheld human sound** — a swallow, an over-long inhale, a hum,
or the singing voice dropping into a speaking voice — lands **immediately before it and nowhere else in
the song.**
**Structure & dynamics.** ⛔ **Once.** Count it in the lyric. Never in a title, never in a chorus, never in
a second verse. ⛔ **Never explained** — no line says who she was, what happened, or why it matters.
**Rhyme.** None. It is the only unrhymed, unreturned word in the song, which is what makes it an origin.
**Poetic devices.** **Apostrophe**, spent once.
**Hook techniques.** (1) A single word held out of the whole song is the cheapest and most durable
withholding there is. (2) The line **after** it does the work: *nobody in here says it back.*
**Narrative seed.** *She says it once, out loud, and nothing happens.*

## GUIDE 6 — **THIRTY-EIGHT, AND WHO ELSE IT REACHES** *(facet F6 with the F7 veto and F8 sting)*

**Storytelling techniques.** (a) **The body reporting before the mind** — three physical facts, then the
number that explains them. (b) **The de-boast** — the last line of the hinge puts her own ribs in the same
field as everybody else's.
**Mood & emotional arc.** **Recognition**, then straight back to the chorus. ⛔ Not triumph. **Thirty-eight
is not a weapon and the song must not enjoy it.**
**Instrumentation & texture.** A trunk-rattling near-mono sub at 38 Hz under tamborzão kick and detuned
cowbell — **and the tanpura halo and the buzzing clavichord still on top of it.** ⭐ That overlay is the
piece; without it this is a phonk drop with a raga intro, which the brief names as the failure.
**Production & effects.** Sub mono and centre, felt before it is heard. ⛔ No sidechain pumping, no
riser, no white-noise sweep, **no silence before it** — interrupting silence gets filled by the generator
and the pause is not worth the risk. Terminal silence at the very end **is** kept.
**Structure & dynamics.** Six lines: three of body (spine · throat-skin · a cup walking on a table), the
number alone on its own line, one line of what it costs, and **the de-boast.**
**Rhyme.** The three body lines rhyme forward onto the line after the number, so the number itself sits in
a rhyme hole — which is how a listener knows it is the fact.
**Poetic devices.** **Asyndeton** through the body lines; **understatement** on the number.
**Hook techniques.** (1) A number a listener can look up. (2) A claim they can test with their own chest.
**Narrative seed.** *A body answers before a room decides — and hers is in the room too.*
**F8's clause, carried here:** in **V3** the wrong rate on the first invoice was a **genuine mistake that
got approved**, and she now files it wrong every month **on purpose**; in **V4** she **let a room credit
her for years** and it made her. ⛔ Neither is softened into a misunderstanding, and neither asks for
forgiveness.

## ⛔ WHAT ALL SIX GUIDES ARE FORBIDDEN

- ⛔ **Contempt in any register.** No snarl-as-punchline, no scored point, no *"needs more bass"* shape.
- ⛔ **Apology.** The words *sorry*, *my fault*, *I shouldn't* appear in no lyric.
- ⛔ **Vindication.** No final chorus that proves anything, no room that comes round, no key change.
- ⛔ **The word *worth*, the word *value*, the word *replace*.** The accusation lives in an object.
- ⛔ **Real artist or group names** anywhere in a prompt, an exclude field or a lyric. **Dedication only.**
- ⛔ **House lexicon** (`vault/gates.yaml → house_lexicon`): no *triple arch*, *more sub and more sky*,
  *small astonished laugh*, *frost-air pad*, *starfield percussion*, *clear silver tone*, *dew-bright*,
  *glass harmonica sheen*, *crystalline* anything, *small enough to understand*, *make my little fear*.
- ⛔ **Sargam syllables in the sung lyric.** The HyperRaaga lane is carried as **vocal contour and
  ornament instruction in the style prompt**, credited in the dedication, and pointed upstream —
  **fusion with lineage, never capture**, and nothing devotional or ceremonial is written.
- ⛔ **A second numeric fact.** Thirty-eight is the only number sung in each song, spelled in words, once.
