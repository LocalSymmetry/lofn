## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING 1 — WHO IS THIS SONG FOR?

**For Ines Faragó, thirty-four, a night dispatcher at a water utility**, who was eleven weeks into the rota when a valve failed across three districts at an hour she can recite in her sleep. She was cleared. Her name went into the incident report first anyway — not because anyone suspected her, but because she was **the newest name available**, and a report needs a first line before it needs a cause. Two years on, the cleared finding is on page nine and the first line is still the first thing anybody reads.

She is not frightened and she is not a victim. She is **still doing the job**, on the same rota, and she has developed one habit the song is about: she now says the cause out loud, at the start of every shift handover, **before anybody asks her.** Half the room finds it useful. Half the room finds it strange. She cannot stop.

*(Ines is invented — `vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md`. The theme is drawn from the world; the person is not. **The indignation in this pair is aimed at the room that wrote the first line, never at Ines, never at anyone who lost water that night.**)*

## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING 2 — THE DEVIATION CLAUSE (named in advance, in writing)

**Where I expect the generator to disobey:** the **chamber organ**. I am asking for a small two-stop box with audible bellows, close-miked at arm's length, in a room with five people in it. Suno's prior for "organ + gospel + industrial" is a **cathedral pipe organ with a four-second tail and a forty-voice choir stack**, and it will reach for that unless the smallness is written as physical fact rather than as a size adjective.

**The disobedience I would KEEP:** if the **bellows wheeze becomes the hi-hat** — if the generator fuses the ornate instrument and the machine clock into one object rather than layering them. That is ONE SEAM rendered without being asked for: the ornate half and the mechanical half turn out to be **the same lung**. Keep it, do not repair it, and note it in the render audit.

**The disobedience I would REJECT:** the techno lock arriving **after a bar of silence**, as a drop. That is the exact GRAIN LAW failure class — interrupting silence gets filled, and a filled silence becomes a build, and a build makes the song episodic. The lock is a **material** change at constant level: nothing gets louder, nothing goes quiet first.

**Second expected disobedience:** the five-voice congregation rendering wet. Excluded explicitly; if it survives dry, the song is safe.

## THE FACETS

### F1 — THE ROOM WROTE THE NAME BEFORE IT LOOKED · **weight 1.00** *(the spine of the pair)*
The subject is not the failure. The subject is **the order of operations**: a name was written, and then the looking started, and the looking was shaped by the name. This is why it cannot be argued with — nobody in the room is lying, and every single one of them is reading downstream of one felt-tip mark. **You cannot argue with a room that has already decided what you are**, and the reason is procedural, not moral. Every draft is checked against one question: *does this line make the room evil?* If yes, the line is cut. An evil room is a much easier song and a much smaller one.

### F2 — THE FAULT SHE ACTUALLY HAS IS NOT THE ONE SHE IS CHARGED WITH · **weight 0.98**
The accusation is *a failure she did not cause*. The confession is *an answer to a question nobody asked*. **These are different sentences and the song must never let them merge.** She does not answer the charge by pleading a lesser charge — that is a defence, and a defence is a smaller song than a method. She answers the charge by **producing a fault of her own, larger and stranger and true**, and offering it unasked. Which is, itself, the fault. ⭐ *The confession is a demonstration of the thing being confessed.*

### F3 — THE TECHNO LOCK IS THE THESIS, IN FORM · **weight 0.96** *(ONE SEAM, placed)*
World material = **industrial gospel**: call, answer, small room, breath, a two-stop organ. Intruding material = **hard techno**: machine clock, keyboard-click hats, thirty-hertz sub. **The seam sits at the junction where the room stops asking.** Call-and-response needs a call; when the calls stop and the answers keep arriving on the same grid, gospel has become techno **without a single parameter changing.** That is an answer to a question nobody asked, made structural — and it does not release, because the fault does not.
⛔ The lock is **timbral**: the kick swaps from a soft ribbed thump to a dry clipped click, the hats double in density, the sub sustains without release, the organ holds one chord and stops moving, the congregation drops its consonants and holds one vowel. Same tempo, same level, no void.

### F4 — HARD-CONSONANT SPINE: THE RETURN IS A STRUCK SOUND · **weight 0.94** *(A2, mandatory prosody axis)*
The returning element in this pair is not an image and not a rhyme scheme — it is **a consonant**. The `-ck / -ct / -kt` family carries the whole song: **locked, docked, clocked, blocked, knocked, act, fact, tract, exact, intact, cracked, backed, marked, dark**. Every chorus lands on struck stops; every congregational answer is a monosyllable that ends closed. **Removal is a debt** (`lofn-music` L21): the pair strips almost no rhyme, and where a line goes unrhymed the consonant spine is what returns in its place — that is the named replacement, declared here in advance.
The spine is also a *sonic* argument: gospel answers are open vowels, techno is a closed transient, and the mouth doing both is the seam.

### F5 — THE RADIANT: ONE ORIGIN, NAMED ONCE, NEVER EXPLAINED · **weight 0.92**
Each of the four songs converges every event onto one named point and refuses to gloss it:
**V1 → the board.** **V2 → two nineteen.** **V3 → the oldest door in the building.** **V4 → the first quiet.**
The test is falsifiable: take any line in the song and ask *which streak is this, and where does it come from?* If a line traces to nothing, it is decoration and it is cut. ⛔ The origin gets exactly one naming. No line may define it, gloss it, or tell you why it matters.

### F6 — THIRTY HERTZ, AND WHAT IT DOES TO A BODY · **weight 0.90** *(pillar 5)*
Thirty hertz is under the floor of pitch. You do not hear it, you **wear** it: it sits in the sternum and the back teeth, it moves the diaphragm without asking permission, and it makes a room feel **occupied before anyone speaks**. That last clause is why it is in this pair and not another: the frequency does the room's job. The verdict is in the air before the hearing opens, and that is a pressure, not an argument.
In the prompts it is named in all four songs with its bodily job attached. In the **lyric** it is sung exactly once across the pair, in V4, as the pair's one sung number.

### F7 — TWO REGISTERS, MEETING EXACTLY ONCE · **weight 0.88** *(pillar 4)*
The high register is **cracked and forward** — the one at the front of the room, who has to speak. The low register is **close, chest-heavy, multiplied** — the same voice as congregation, answering itself. Gospel call-and-response gives this a native form, which is why it is here: **she is the room answering her.** The two meet in unison exactly once per song, and the placement is load-bearing:
V1 on the number · V2 on the last line of the final chorus · V3 on the single word **still** · V4 on **I answered**.

### F8 — THE WITHHELD GESTURE, SPENT ONCE · **weight 0.85** *(pillar 6)*
V1: one audible swallow, held back until the last chorus. V2: one intake of breath, only before the hour is named. V3: one unaccompanied hum, no words, at the very end. V4: **one short unguarded laugh, half a syllable**, spent at the admission that the error was better than the plan. ⭐ V4's is the whole pair's hinge — it is the sound of a defence turning into a method, and it happens once and never comes back.

### F9 — INDIGNATION WITH ONE TARGET, AND A FLOOR UNDER IT · **weight 0.84**
LOFN-PRIME is indignant **only because she gets frustrated trying to get them to see** (`lofn-prime` ethos). The target is the room that assigned blame before it looked — an order of operations, not a person. ⛔ Never at frightened people. ⛔ Never at anyone actually harmed by the failure. ⛔ No contempt as the resolution: **part one's spite is unavailable** and its shapes are banned — no *"cool story bro"* hook shape, no bored-snarl punchline. The resolution here is **owned error**, which is a harder and better ending than scorn.

### F10 — SWAG IS DENSITY OVER STEADINESS, NEVER SPEED · **weight 0.82**
One hundred and ten BPM, A minor, **end to end, no ramp, no key change, no drop.** Drive comes from the grid never moving while the material on top keeps changing. Five to seven **named timbral entries per song, each arriving alone**, in an order fixed in the prompt. ⛔ Banned as swag substitutes: tempo ramps, key-change-as-event, EDM builds, drops, three-speed strophic, shrinking stanzas. Those are episodic; cohesion is the opposite of episodic.

### F11 — THE MACHINE LOG IS THE CLOCK, NOT AN INTRO · **weight 0.80** *(pillar 3)*
Her own log lines, time-stretched into a dry scratch pulse, **mono and dead-centre**, are the timekeeper for the whole song — not a texture that fades once the drums arrive. In V1 it is the only thing keeping time for a full sixteen bars before any drum exists. Percussion, not garnish.
