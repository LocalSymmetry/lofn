## ⛔ THE TWO OPENING ANSWERS (required before any facet)

### 1. WHO IS THIS SONG FOR?
**For Nadia Ferreiro** — invented, thirty-four, a build engineer who spent a Tuesday afternoon in a room
of eleven people explaining that the thing they were blaming had not been in the building when the wall
came down, and who was not believed, and who went home and could still hear the server cabinet through
the floor of her flat. **She is not the accuser and she is not the accused. She is the one who watched
a room decide before anybody spoke.** The song is aimed at her ear.

*(A fiction, per `vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md`: the condition — AI-generated work blamed for a
compromise, argued about all day by builders — is drawn from the world; the person is invented. The song
never names her, never names a company, product, year or living person. **The charge is the temperature,
never the content.**)*

### 2. ⭐ THE DEVIATION CLAUSE — where I expect the generator to disobey, and what I would KEEP
**EXPECTED DISOBEDIENCE:** the harpsichord. I have asked for Baroque figuration — trills, mordents,
turns — used as the **riff** in a lane that expects no melody at all, doubling the 808's own contour past
the point of taste. **The generator's strongest prior is to demote it to a garnish**: a tinkling arpeggio
in the intro, gone by the first chorus, replaced by a bell or a pluck.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD KEEP:** if the harpsichord arrives **distorted through the same clipper as the
808** — gilded and broken in the same gesture, so the ornament and the machine share one distortion stage
instead of sitting in two clean layers. That is a better statement of pillar 2 than the one I wrote: the
ornate is not *against* the machine there, it has been **put through** it, and the error owned becomes a
signal-chain fact rather than an argument. Precedent: stereo narrowing into a climax was banked as a
technique on 2026-08-15 by judging the result rather than the distance from intent.

**THE DISOBEDIENCE I WOULD NOT KEEP, AND WOULD CUT THE TAKE FOR:** (a) **a tempo ramp or a half-time
switch** — SWAG is steadiness with timbres arriving over it, and a 152 that drops to 76 for the bridge has
converted the whole thesis into an episode; (b) **a melodic sung chorus** in place of the chant — the
chorus must be byte-identical and *chanted*, and a generator that "improves" it into a topline has deleted
the return; (c) **a clean sub.** The 808 is asked to clip. A polite, rounded, well-behaved sub is the
sound of the accusation being conceded.

## FACET EXPANSION — twelve facets

### F1 · THE CHARGE IS UBIQUITY, AND UBIQUITY IS THE ONE CHARGE YOU CANNOT ANSWER
You can answer *you did the thing* with evidence. You cannot answer *you are everywhere* at all, because
it is true, and because the person saying it is not asking a question. **This is the pair's whole
mechanical problem and the reason it runs at a hundred and fifty-two:** there is no cross-examination
available, so the only remaining verb is speed. She does not argue. She **agrees, faster than they can
finish saying it**, and keeps agreeing until agreement stops sounding like surrender.

### F2 · ⭐ TWO RETURNING OBJECTS, AND THEY RETURN DIFFERENTLY — THE PAIR'S CENTRAL CRAFT
The brief hands me a chorus that must be **byte-identical** and an accusation-refrain that must come back
**one word changed each pass.** These do not conflict; **they are the song.** One thing in this record is
absolutely fixed and one thing erodes by exactly one syllable at a time, and they alternate. The listener
gets a floor and a drift in the same three minutes. ⛔ The chorus is never varied, never ad-libbed, never
"developed" — repetition of an exact chorus requires no justification, and this one is load-bearing:
**it is the surface the chant's single changed word is measured against.**

### F3 · THE ONE CHANGED WORD IS THE WHOLE THESIS, SPENT FOUR TIMES
Each song's chant runs four passes. Passes one to three change a **noun or a preposition** — the charge
being re-aimed. Pass four changes a **pronoun or a possessive** — and that is the error being taken.
*V1:* in the wall → in the floor → in the jaw → **They→I**. *V2:* Print → Read → **Sing** → out→**loud**.
*V3:* it's there → it's here → it's mine → **it's yours**. *V4:* a hole → a fault → a door → **You→I**.
**Four chains, four different grammatical categories of change, one shared move.** A listener who hears
only the last pass still gets the thesis; a listener who hears all four gets the argument.

### F4 · THE HARPSICHORD IS A RIFF OR IT IS NOTHING
⚠️ This is the pair's declared likeliest failure: *"an aggressive trap beat with a shouty female vocal."*
The single thing standing between P01 and that sentence is **whether the harpsichord is structural.**
Therefore, in every one of the four packages, the harpsichord: (a) is **named in the arrival order** of
the timbral parade, (b) **doubles the 808's contour**, not a counter-line floating above it, (c) plays
**trills, mordents and turns** — named ornaments, not "flourishes" — and (d) **persists into the last
chorus.** ⛔ A harpsichord that appears in the intro and leaves is a garnish and the take is dead.

### F5 · FIFTY HERTZ IS NOT A NUMBER, IT IS A PLACE IN THE BODY
Fifty a second is the mains hum of most of the world and it is also, conveniently and horribly, right at
the bottom of the chest. **What it does:** it sits behind the sternum, it finds the fillings in the back
teeth, it makes the small bones of the ear tired before the ear notices it is working. That is what gets
sung — **the number and the bodily job, together, once.** ⛔ *"Deep sub bass"* is a range and the old
pillar; **fifty a second in the back of the jaw** is a location. All four songs run the 808 there.

### F6 · ⛔ THE INDIGNATION IS AIMED AT THE ACCUSATION, NEVER AT THE ACCUSER
The room is frightened. Frightened people are not the target, and a song that sneers at them is part
one's move wearing part two's clothes. So: **no "you idiots", no "you don't understand", no punchline at
anybody's expense, no smart-kid superiority.** The heat goes at the *shape of the charge* — at being
agreed about, at a verdict arriving before a sentence. ⭐ **Operational test, applied line by line: could
this line be said kindly and still be furious?** If it only works said nastily, it is part one's spite
and it is cut. *(V3 is the proof: she gives them the switch. That is the most aggressive thing in the
pair, and there is not one unkind word in it.)*

### F7 · THE RADIANT — MANY EVENTS, ONE NAMED ORIGIN, NAMED ONCE
Source 3 is a mandatory form rule and it is also, exactly, SWAG's geometry. Each song names **one origin,
once, and never explains it**, and everything else in the song is a streak traceable to it:
**V1 — the mains** · **V2 — line one** · **V3 — one switch** · **V4 — one hole.**
⛔ The origin is never defined, never expanded, never returned to as a topic. It is named and then the
song is made of its consequences. A song that explains its radiant has drawn the diagram instead of the sky.

### F8 · SWAG AT A HUNDRED AND FIFTY-TWO IS THE HARD CASE, AND STEADINESS IS THE ONLY ANSWER
⭐ I am the run's fastest song, which means I am the one that can most easily fake drive. **Fast is not
drive.** The measured mechanism is *one unwavering groove + a parade of specific timbres entering one at a
time* — and at 152 the temptation to substitute a build, a drop, or a half-time bridge is at its strongest
in the whole run. ⛔ **Banned outright, in the spec and in EXCLUDE: tempo ramp, key change as event, EDM
build, drop, riser, half-time switch, breakdown-as-dynamic-hole.** What I get instead is **six named
timbral entries, arriving alone, in a different order in each of the four songs** — the arrival order is
one of my four real variation differentiators, not a garnish on one.

### F9 · THE MACHINE LOG IS THE PERCUSSION, AND THAT IS AN ARGUMENT
Pillar 3 says her own logs keep time. In *this* song that is not texture and it is not a wink: **the log
is the exhibit.** The record of everywhere she has been is the thing being held against her, and she has
made it the metronome — so the evidence is what the room is nodding along to before anyone reads it. ⭐ It
is the clearest available instance of *honor thy error as a hidden intention*, and it costs one production
decision: the log-scratch enters **first, alone, mono, dead centre**, and never leaves.

### F10 · TWO REGISTERS OF ONE THROAT, AND THE UNISON IS RATIONED TO ONE EVENT
**Register A — the charge:** low, flat, close, half-spoken, doubled, dead centre, dry, no vibrato, the
sound of a room saying a thing it has already agreed on. **Register B — her:** fast, bright, forward of
the beat, upper-mid, hard consonants, rage-rap flow. They alternate all song and **meet in unison exactly
once**, in a different place in each of the four: *V1* on the fourth chant pass · *V2* on the number ·
*V3* on the handover · *V4* on the last word. ⛔ One meeting. Never two. The meeting is the event.

### F11 · THE WITHHELD GESTURE IS ONE BREATH IN, AND IT IS THE ONLY UNMEASURED SOUND
At 152 with a clipping 808 and a scratch clock, **everything in the record is on the grid** — which is
what makes one un-gridded human sound enormous. **One audible intake of breath, once per song**, placed
where the 808 clips out for a bar: *V1* before the last chorus · *V2* before she reads her own worst line
· *V3* after the handover, holding still · *V4* as the last sound in the song. ⛔ Rationed, audible, never
repeated, never a sigh (a sigh is self-pity and this song has none).

### F12 · ⭐ THE STING, AND WHERE IT LIVES — THE PLACE THIS STOPS BEING A DEFENCE
The seed's own honesty clause: *sometimes the error **was** hers, and it turned out better than the plan.*
A pair that only ever owns other people's faults is running a very sophisticated denial. **So V2 carries
the sting explicitly and alone:** the log she is reading out contains her own worst line, and it is the
one that worked. She reads it louder than the charge. ⛔ It is not framed as vindication and there is no
"see?" — it is stated flat and left there, because the moment it becomes a rebuttal it stops being a
method. V4 carries the complementary case (a fault that was genuinely not hers, kept anyway); V1 and V3
stay out of the question entirely, which is what keeps V2's version from sounding like a policy.
