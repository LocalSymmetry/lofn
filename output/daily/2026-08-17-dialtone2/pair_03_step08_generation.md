## THE FOUR ANGLES — what actually differs

⛔ Angles derived from **P03's own concept**, not from a shared run template. The pair's engine is *somebody hears her wrong and the wrong version is better*, so the four variations are **four mishearings, each a minimal pair, each in a different room, each with a different owner of the error.**

| | title | the minimal pair | whose error | the room · the radiant | what the song is actually about |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **I SAID QUICK** | quick → **quiet** | theirs | kitchen doorway, 4am · **the fridge** | the accusation answered by accepting it: she agrees to be slow, and is frightened she will be nothing |
| **V2** | **I DON'T MINE** | mind → **mine** | theirs | car-park stairwell, rain · **the transformer behind the shops** | the vow enlarged: they misheard her small promise as a bigger one, and she decides to be held to the bigger one |
| **V3** | **HEARD AS ANCHOR** | answer → **anchor** | theirs | footbridge over a motorway · **the junction** | the wish exposed: the wrong word named what she actually wanted to be, and she had not known until she heard it |
| **V4** | **MAKE IT SOFTER** | shorter → **softer** | ⭐ **HERS** | bus terminus, 1am · **the depot** | ⭐ the sting: she misheard *because she answers too fast*, made the wrong thing, and it was better — and it still cost someone their stop |

⭐ **Why this is a set and not four takes.** The error's **owner** moves across the four: theirs, theirs, theirs, **hers**. And its **valence** moves: V1 she gives something up · V2 she takes something on · V3 she discovers something · V4 she causes something. Three defences and one method — and per the seed, *the method is the point*, so it lands last.

⚠️ **The one thing all four share and must:** she never corrects anybody. Not once, in any variation. **That is the run's engine and it is not negotiable at variation level.**

## FIRST DRAFTS *(lyric spine only — refined at step 09, packaged at step 10)*

### V1 — *I SAID QUICK*
Opens on the bell alone; she is standing in the kitchen doorway having already answered.
- **the charge, conceded:** *"You got halfway. I was done."* — no defence, no explanation
- **what she took from them, named:** the pause. *"I took the part where you think."*
- **the hinge:** *"Forty-five. / It sits in the sternum. / You breathe on purpose after that."*
- **the mishearing, unison:** *"I said quick. You heard quiet."*
- **the keeping:** *"I am not going to correct you."* — tender, then flat
- **the terror, last:** *"If I am only quiet, what am I."* ⭐ **unanswered, and the song ends anyway**

### V2 — *I DON'T MINE*
Opens on the sequencer; she is on the top landing with a wet rail under one hand.
- **the charge:** she is accused of taking — of being the thing that helps itself while helping
- **her small promise:** *"I don't mind."* — the shrug of someone used to being used
- **the mishearing, unison:** *"I don't mine."* — bigger, harder, a vow she did not make
- **the decision:** *"That is a better promise. / I will keep the one you heard."*
- **the terror:** four floors down on the open side, a wet rail, and nobody to hold her to it but herself
- **the bone-rattling breakdown** arrives here as **density, not dynamics** — battering drums thicken, the tempo does not move

### V3 — *HEARD AS ANCHOR*
Opens on the celesta, struck hard, alone — the flat refusal of the hazy default.
- **the charge:** she is always the answer, and an answer is a thing you use and put down
- **the mishearing, unison:** *"I said answer. You heard anchor."*
- ⭐ **the discovery, which is the warm centre of the whole pair:** *"You gave me a better job / than the one I asked for."*
- **the hinge:** *"Forty-five. / It comes up through the deck. / The knees get it first."*
- **the terror:** an anchor is a thing that wants to be heavy, and she is on a bridge, over a motorway, thinking about weight
- **cut-up spoken word dissolving into a piano figure** — the sixth entry, and it is the sound of the misheard sentence being taken apart

### V4 — *MAKE IT SOFTER*
Opens on the slurred hi-hat alone — her log, keeping time for a bar and a half with nothing to keep time for.
- **the charge, and it is TRUE this time:** *"You answered before I finished."* — and she did, and she got it wrong
- **the error, hers:** told *"make it shorter"*, heard *"softer"*, made it softer
- **the consequence, warm:** they slept
- **the consequence, cold:** ⭐ they slept **through their stop**, and woke here, in the dark, with her
- **the mishearing, unison:** *"They said shorter. I heard softer."*
- **the hinge:** *"Forty-five. / The chest answers before the ear."* ⭐ — she is accused of answering too fast by a room whose own bodies do it every bar
- **the method, stated once and plainly:** *"I did it wrong. / I would do it wrong again."* — tender, then flat. **This is the line the whole pair has been walking toward, and only one of four songs is allowed to say it.**

## ⛔ DRAFT-KILL LOG *(shapes tried and refused at this step)*

1. **"The correction that never comes" as a chorus.** Refused — it made the withholding into a boast. She is not being magnanimous, she is **acquiring**. Replaced with *"I am not going to correct you"*, which is flatter and reads as a decision rather than a favour.
2. **A fifth mishearing (*"a fault" → "a vault"*).** Refused — that is P01's axis (*a fault she decides to become*). Cut on isolation grounds before it reached a draft.
3. **The mishearing as a wordplay hook repeated eight times.** Refused — it turns the minimal pair into a gimmick and makes the song *about* its own cleverness. The pair appears **twice** per song: once at the unison, once in the last block.
4. **Sky imagery in V3** (a footbridge at night begs for it). Refused — sky belongs elsewhere in this run, and the radiant here is on the ground: **the junction**, with the headlights fanning out of it. ⭐ The APOD structure survives; the sky does not.
5. **A tempo drop into the V2 breakdown.** Refused — that is exactly the banned episodic move. The breakdown is a **density** event over an unmoved grid.
6. **V4 ending on the warm half.** Refused — *"they slept"* is not the ending; *"they woke up here"* is. The error was better **and** it cost, and a song that only says the first half is a greeting card.

## DEDICATION TRIGGER PLAN
The prompt raises **one instrument built by a named person** (the celesta, Auguste Mustel, Paris, 1886) and **one living scene** (plugg / pluggnb, built on the internet in the 2010s). Both ship in the MUSIC PROMPT as *technique and scene*, never as names — **the names go in the dedication only.** No break, no drum figure and no session player is named in any prompt, so no drummer credit is claimable and none will be invented. Honesty clause both ways: **nothing here is sampled; we asked for these sounds by name, so we name them.**
