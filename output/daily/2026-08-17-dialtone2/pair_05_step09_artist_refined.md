## 1. THE SEVEN ROLES — one binding note each, and what it actually changed

### **PRODUCER** — *"Your cheap instrument must be audibly cheap, or Axis 1 is only in the lyric."*
A clavichord is the quietest keyboard there is; that quietness **is** the cheapness, and it is the one property a generator will helpfully remove. **Changed:** every prompt now says *close-miked clavichord too quiet for the room* (V1) / *close-miked clavichord* with the key noise and tangent contact kept up in the mix, and every exclude field bans **harpsichord** by name — the loud, expensive sibling is the single most likely substitution and it belongs to P01.

### **COMPOSER** — *"A drone is not decoration here; it is the reference the error is measured against."*
A pitch that runs sharp is only legible as *wrong* if something true is sounding under it. **Changed:** the tanpura halo is written as an unbroken entry in all four and is explicitly instructed to **play on over the sub** after the collapse. Without it the bend is just a sound; with it, it is an error, and the error is the subject.

### **SONGWRITER** — *"Two of your choruses were arguments. Choruses lose arguments."*
**Changed:** V1's chorus was rebuilt from *That is the only way it sings* to **Sings sharp, and that is the singing** — a statement of what the instrument does rather than a defence of it — and the second couplet became the trade verb: **Not going to true it. Not tonight.** V4's chorus lost *And I let a whole room say my name* (a sentence about credit) for **My name in the room, never hers** (a fact about a room), and gained **Lean like she leaned**, which puts the previous woman's hand back inside the hook.

### **ARRANGER** — *"Nothing may sit between the cause and the consequence."*
**Confirmed and enforced:** PRESS (5 lines, under ten seconds) → the withheld gesture → **the name in unison** → the hard cut. No riser, no sweep, no bar of silence — ⛔ **interrupting silence gets filled by the generator**, so the collapse is written to arrive on top of a running clock. Terminal silence at the very end **is** kept, because terminal silence survives.

### **VOCAL COACH** — *"The staging is the argument."*
The low register sits on the sub's shoulder, dead centre, almost spoken, breath audible, no reverb. The high register is placed slightly wide and further back, ornamented, and carries the only wet signal in the mix. **The answer is physically nearer to you than the question.** ⛔ **The CALL is never a villain** — the room is not stupid and is not sneering; it wants the note cleaned up, which is a reasonable thing to want. V4 goes furthest: **there the call is a compliment**, and it is still the accusation.

### **MIX ENGINEER** — *"A clock that flinches when the bass arrives is not a clock."*
**Changed:** every prompt now states that the log holds its **exact original rate** on both sides of the cut, and every exclude field bans **sidechain pumping**. The log is never ducked to the sub. ⛔ **No transparent pitch correction on any voice or key** — the bend is left where it lands, and *transparent pitch correction* is named in all four excludes because it is the one plug-in that would erase the entire premise.

### **CRITIC** — *"Your press section is the only unrhymed block in the pair and you are about to be measured."*
⭐ **Held, and declared rather than repaired.** Axis 2's device — *the answer arrives before the call* — is a **sequence** property, not a **terminal** property; it lives in the printed order of the blocks, not in the line endings. The press block drops end rhyme on purpose, because a rhyme there would make the pressure sound composed rather than done. ⛔ **The debt is named and paid in the next bar: what returns in place of rhyme is the chorus hook, whole.** The floors themselves are met with room to spare and were not bought by deforming the device (§3).
