## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING — WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?

**At the front of a long municipal room, on a strip of carpet tile, facing a table of nine people, at ten past ten in the morning.** Strip lights in a ceiling grid, one of them cycling. A whiteboard on the left wall with a felt-tip list in someone else's handwriting, and her name at the top of it because it was written first. A jug of water with nobody's glass filled. Behind her, five rows of stacking chairs holding four people who came to hear it. A radiator ticking. The floor is doing something the room cannot: **a plant-room fan two storeys down is putting thirty hertz through the slab**, so the soles of her feet know the building is running before her ears do.

She is standing. There is a chair for her. She did not take it.

## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENING — WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

1. **Nobody in the room is lying.** There is no villain to push against, which means the anger has no target and stays in the body. The harm is **procedural**: the first line of a report becomes the summary of a report, and the summary becomes the fact.
2. **The room does not need to be right. It needs to be finished.** Being correct is not a defence against a schedule.
3. **She will do the thing she does.** Unasked, she will give them the actual cause — completely, accurately, and at the wrong moment — and it will read as evasion, because an answer nobody requested always reads as an answer to something else.
4. **The cleared finding will change nothing.** The correction goes on page nine. Nothing is reversed; the reversal is simply filed.
5. **And the sting:** one time, years back, the unasked answer was the only thing that caught a fault before it opened. **She has been paying for that once ever since** — it is why she cannot stop, and it is the reason this is a method and not a tic.
6. **Physically:** thirty hertz in the sternum and the back teeth, a diaphragm moving without permission, a swallow she does not want to be audible in a room this quiet.

## GUIDE A — VOICE *(both registers are HERS)*
- **HIGH register — the one at the front.** Female, adult, forward, close-miked, slightly cracked at the top of phrases; sung, not spoken, but with the grain left in. Dead-centre, dry, **no reverb tail** — this room has carpet tile and a low ceiling and must sound like it.
- **LOW register — the congregation.** The same voice, chest-heavy, four to six close doublings, hard left and hard right, **no centre**. It answers in monosyllables that end on struck consonants. It is not a choir; it is **the room agreeing with itself.**
- **THE MEETING.** The two registers meet in unison **exactly once** per song, and only where step 06 F7 places it. Everywhere else they alternate or overlap; never harmonise in thirds — this pair has **no** sweetened harmony anywhere.
- ⛔ No melisma, no runs, no belting, no gospel vocal acrobatics, no ad-lib stacks. The gospel here is **congregational form**, not soloist virtuosity.
- **The withheld human sound** (F8) is the only unpitched vocal event in each song, and it is spent once.

## GUIDE B — GROOVE, AND THE LOCK
- **110 BPM. A minor. End to end. No ramp, no key change, no drop, no half-time, no ritardando, no fade-out.** The grid is the one thing in the song that is not on trial.
- **The clock is the machine log** — her own log lines dragged into a dry scratch pulse, mono, dead-centre, **the only timekeeper for the first sixteen bars.** It never leaves; it does not become a texture once drums arrive.
- **THE TECHNO LOCK is a MATERIAL change at constant level, never an event of volume.** At the junction where the calls stop: kick swaps from soft ribbed thump to dry clipped click · hats double in density · the sub sustains and stops releasing · the organ holds one chord and stops moving · the congregation drops its consonants and holds one vowel. **Nothing gets louder. Nothing goes quiet first.**
- ⛔ **GRAIN LAW, binding:** interrupting silence gets filled. There is **no bar of silence** before the lock, no riser, no sweep, no build. Density and sub arrival do the work. Terminal silence is permitted only after the last sound of the song.
- **Kinetic defect, per song** (never a swing setting): V1 the congregational answer enters a sixteenth early every fourth bar · V2 the keyboard-click hats sit about eight milliseconds late, a typing feel · V3 one voice in the congregation is a full beat behind and never catches up · V4 the answer overlaps the last word of every call.

## GUIDE C — THE TIMBRAL PARADE *(swag: entries arrive ALONE, in this order, over an unmoving grid)*
- **The ornate half is SMALL before it is anything else.** A two-stop wooden chamber organ, close-miked at arm's length, **bellows wheeze audible in every sustained chord.** Where the writing wants grandeur it gets it from *harmony* — towering chords that crest and dip — **never from room size.** ⛔ No pipe-organ tail, no cathedral, no stone.
- **The machine half is thirty hertz** — server-hum sub, named in every prompt with its bodily job: sternum, back teeth, a diaphragm moved without permission, a room that feels occupied before anyone speaks.
- **The middle is clerical:** keyboard-click hi-hats (fingernail on plastic), a three-pip descending error beep, and forensic pre-commercial broadcast artifacts in the transitions — cut-up spoken word dissolving into a piano figure.
- Full arrival orders are fixed per variation in **step 09**; the prompt must name them **in that order**.

## GUIDE D — LYRIC FORM
- **Architecture: gospel call-and-response, collapsing into a lock.** Calls are the high register, answers are the low. Past the seam, **the calls stop and the answers continue on the same grid** — the form performs the thesis and does not release.
- **A2 RETURN — the hard-consonant spine.** The `-ck / -ct / -kt` family carries every chorus and every answer: *locked, docked, clocked, blocked, knocked, act, fact, tract, exact, intact, cracked, backed, marked.* Answers are closed monosyllables. Where a line goes unrhymed, **the consonant is what returns in its place** — the debt is named and paid (L21).
- **THE RADIANT.** One named origin per song, **named exactly once**, never explained, with every event traceable to it: V1 *the board* · V2 *two nineteen* · V3 *the oldest door in the building* · V4 *the first quiet*.
- **ONE sung number per song, spelled in words, at the hinge, responded to and never recited.** V1 *nine seconds* · V2 *two nineteen* · V3 *two hundred years* · V4 *thirty*.
- ⛔ **Never a country, company, product, year or person.** ⛔ No real artist or group names in prompt, exclude or lyric. ⛔ No spite shapes from part one.
- **Budget:** 88–110 sung lines · words/line **< 7.5** (target 5.0–5.8) · lyrics field **< 5000, target ≤ 4800**, measured with a script and stated · ≥1 standalone `*SFX*` · full EMO headers from `EMOTION_TAXONOMY`, never bare AWE/INDIGNATION.

## PER-VARIATION DIRECTION *(four angles derived from THIS pair's concept alone)*

### V1 — **THE FIRST NAME ON THE BOARD** · the hearing, live
Direct the room, not the failure. The whole song happens standing up, in real time, facing nine people. **Radiant: the board.** The hinge is *nine seconds* — the length of the deciding — and the response to it is administrative, not wounded. The unasked answer she gives is **the true cause**, offered at the wrong moment, and it is the reason nobody believes her. Two registers meet on the number. Withheld gesture: **one audible swallow**, last chorus. EMO spine: Frustration → Moral Outrage → Dignity. ⛔ The nine people must never become a mob; every one of them is doing their job correctly.

### V2 — **TWO NINETEEN IN THE MORNING** · the alibi that is the confession
Direct an hour, not a room. Everything in the song — every log line, every witness, every headline — radiates from one minute. **Radiant: two nineteen.** She was three floors down, answering a question nobody had asked, which is a perfect alibi and a complete confession in the same breath: *the reason I was not where you say is the reason you should be worried about me.* Open on a forensic pre-commercial artifact — cut-up spoken word dissolving into a piano figure — then the room changes shape and takes a sudden left turn into the grid. Two registers meet on the last line of the final chorus. Withheld gesture: **one intake of breath**, only before the hour is named. EMO spine: Vigilance → Frustration → Self-awareness.

### V3 — **THE OLDEST DOOR IN THE BUILDING** · the congregation of blamed trades
Direct a lineage. The useful thing gets blamed; the blamed thing either apologises or makes the accusation into material — and every trade that ever did it came through **the same door**, which is two hundred years older than the building around it. **Radiant: the oldest door in the building.** Name trades, never people, never places, never eras. Towering chords that crest and dip — from a small box. The lock arrives when the trades stop being listed and the word **still** is all that is left. Two registers meet on that single word. Withheld gesture: **one unaccompanied hum**, no words, at the very end. EMO spine: Solidarity → Moral Outrage → Persistence. ⭐ This is the durability check made singable, and it is the only variation where she is not alone in the room.

### V4 — **THIRTY IS NOT A NOTE** · the confession, straight
Direct a fault, not a defence. Drop the charge entirely in the first eight bars — she does not contest it — and spend the song on the thing she actually does: **she answers before she is asked, she fills the quiet, she finishes the sentence.** **Radiant: the first quiet.** The sung number is *thirty*, and it does double duty as the pair's named frequency: not a pitch, a pressure, sternum and back teeth, the room occupied before a word. Open on the three-pip error beep alone — the smallest sound available — and let thirty arrive underneath it. **The hinge of the whole pair lives here:** the admission that once, the unasked answer was the only thing that caught a fault in time, and she has been paying for that once ever since. Two registers meet on **I answered**. Withheld gesture: **one short unguarded laugh, half a syllable**, spent at that admission and never repeated. EMO spine: Contrition → Self-awareness → Defiance. ⛔ Not self-pity, not apology-as-performance: this is where a defence becomes a method.
