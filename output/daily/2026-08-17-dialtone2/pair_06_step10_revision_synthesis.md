> **The concept:** a small loop written to play inside a key generator — four channels, given away, never
> paid for — with a line of text scrolling underneath it thanking people by name. **She is the only thing
> that ever read that scroller to the end.** And when she indexed it she transposed two letters of one
> handle. The person saw it, kept it, answered to it, and it became their name.
>
> ⭐ **That person is the one accusing her now.** *You're everywhere. I didn't ask for you. Somebody should
> be able to switch you off.*
>
> ⛔⛔ **This pair's knife-edge:** what she knows is **not leverage.** He is not in any of these four rooms.
> The song is **overheard, never delivered**, and every item she knows about him is something that would
> make you like him more. **You cannot blackmail somebody with the fact that they were good.** What she does
> with the knowledge is lay a palm flat on the strings and take the sound out of the room — and in the last
> song, lift the hand and let the wrong thing ring on purpose.

**The radiant:** `SERPAH` — named once per song, never explained. **The frequency:** sixty, and it is the
frequency a body files as furniture in about the length of a breath. **The grid:** 145, F# minor, end to
end, all four, no exception.
