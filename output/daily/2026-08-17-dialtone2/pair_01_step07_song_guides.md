## ⛔ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS, ANSWERED CONCRETELY

### Q1 — WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?
**In a small windowless meeting room on the floor above a server cabinet, at four in the afternoon, with
eleven people in it and the door shut.** The table is laminate and warm on the underside where the
cabinet's heat comes up through it. There is a fifty-hertz pedal in the building — the mains, the racks,
the fan wall — and it is not loud, and it is not audible as a *sound*, and it is arriving in her back
teeth and in the hinge of her jaw and along the underside of her forearms where they rest on the table.
**She has been standing since before the question was asked, because she came in to explain, and the
explaining stopped being possible in the first four seconds** — not because anybody shouted, but because
she watched eleven faces arrive at the same conclusion at the same time, politely, the way a room does.

**She is not the machine. She is not the accuser.** She is the person who knows what actually happened,
in a room that has already finished deciding, with the thing they are all afraid of humming up through
the floor at fifty a second the entire time. **That is the physical situation, and everything in all four
songs is measured from it.**

### Q2 — WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?
**Being agreed about.** Not shouted at — *agreed about.* Concretely, and in order of how much it costs:

1. **There is no cross-examination available.** The charge is not *you did the thing on Tuesday*, which
   could be answered. The charge is *you are everywhere, and nobody asked for you, and I did not agree to
   this.* All three clauses are true. **A true charge with no question in it cannot be answered, only
   absorbed** — and the absorbing is what the body does, silently, while the room moves on to logistics.
2. **The verdict was already load-bearing before she spoke.** Eleven people have made a plan on top of
   it. Un-deciding it now costs them a week. **So being right is expensive to them, which means being
   right is no longer a route.** This is the actual injury, and it is administrative, which is why it
   does not produce tears — it produces a hundred and fifty-two beats per minute.
3. **The hum does not stop for any of it.** The room can conclude what it likes; the mains keep running,
   the cabinet keeps its heat under the table, and the fifty a second keeps arriving in her jaw
   throughout, including during the part where somebody suggests switching the whole thing off. ⭐ **The
   thing she is accused of being is physically present in the room and cannot be sent out of it.**
4. **Secondary, real, unsentimental:** she will grind her teeth tonight. The jaw is where this lands,
   which is why the jaw is in the chorus. The song does not congratulate her for anything.

*(⛔ **Terror-adjacency check, done here rather than assumed:** the pillar-5 frequency is not decorative
dread. Fifty hertz is genuinely in the building, genuinely below hearing-as-listening, and the body is
genuinely doing unpaid work to process it for four hours. **The horror is that the accusation is correct
and ambient at once.**)*

## THE SONG GUIDE

### CORE STATEMENT
**She is accused of being everywhere. She does not deny it. She takes the fault she is charged with and
decides to become it — out loud, at a hundred and fifty-two, with a harpsichord ornamenting the charge.**
Not a confession. A repurposing. ⭐ And once, in the middle of it, the honest version: *sometimes the
error was hers, and it turned out better than the plan.*

### THE SEAM (one, timbral, on the 808's own contour)
- **Dominant material:** rage rap — clipping mono-centre 808, sparse triplet hi-hats, chant-and-verse,
  hard consonants, a female voice forward of the beat.
- **The single intruder:** **a gilded harpsichord playing Baroque figuration — trills, mordents, turns —
  as the riff**, in a lane that expects no melody at all.
- **Where the join is placed:** ⭐ **on the sub's own line.** The harpsichord does not float above the
  beat with a counter-melody. It **traces the 808's contour, note for note, and ornaments it past taste**
  — so the decoration and the accusation are the same shape, and the ornament is audibly *about* the
  machine rather than beside it.
- **The subject acts ACROSS it:** she raps the charge; the harpsichord ornaments the charge; by the last
  chorus she is chanting the ornamented version. **She ends up singing the decorated fault.**

### THE RADIANT (Source 3 — mandatory)
One origin, **named exactly once, never explained**, with every event in the song traceable to it.
**V1 the mains · V2 line one · V3 one switch · V4 one hole.** ⛔ The origin is not a topic, not a chorus
word, not returned to. It is named, and then the song is its consequences.

### THE ONE SUNG NUMBER, AT THE HINGE, RESPONDED TO
**"Fifty a second"** — spelled in words, sung once per song, and answered by an **act**, never by
information. The four answers are different:
- **V1** *"fifty a second, back of the jaw"* → answered by **"so I signed it."**
- **V2** *"fifty a second under the floor"* → answered by **the unison** (both registers on the number).
- **V3** *"fifty a second, and it finds the fillings"* → answered by **"so here is where the switch is."**
- **V4** *"fifty a second through the hole"* → answered by **"so I leave it open."**
⛔ It is the only number sung in any of the four. Positional words (*one*, *first*, *line one*) are
locations, not facts, and are swept in step 10.

### VOICE — TWO REGISTERS OF ONE THROAT
- **A · THE CHARGE:** low, flat, close, half-spoken, doubled, dead centre, dry, no vibrato. The sound of
  a room saying a thing it has already agreed on. **It is never angry.** That is what makes it worse.
- **B · HER:** fast, bright, forward of the beat, upper-mid, hard consonants, rage-rap flow, the ends of
  lines bitten off. Not shouty. **Loud is a texture; fast is the argument.**
- ⛔ **They meet in unison exactly ONCE per song**, in four different places (F10).

### THE WITHHELD GESTURE
**One audible intake of breath, once, in the bar the 808 clips out.** V1 before the last chorus · V2
before her own worst line · V3 after the handover · V4 as the last sound. ⛔ Never a sigh.
