## ⛔ UNIVERSAL — binding on every pair
1. **≥4 of the six 2026 Sound-Pillars per song**, each thesis-bearing.
2. ⭐ **SWAG: one unwavering groove + ≥5 NAMED TIMBRAL ENTRIES arriving one at a time.** Drive from steadiness, not speed. **Changes are timbral, never dynamic.** ⛔ No tempo ramps, no key-change-as-event, no EDM build, no three-speed/shrinking-stanza structural experiments — those are episodic and episodic is the opposite of cohesion.
3. ⛔ **THE RADIANT (Source 3):** many distinct events, **every one traceable to a single named origin.** Name the origin once; never explain it.
4. **Source 2 vocabulary, imported into prompts:** *cut-up spoken word dissolving into a piano figure* · *bone-rattling breakdown* · *changing shape and taking sudden left turns* · *towering chords that crest and dip* · *keygen music*.
5. ⛔ **The engine is BEING BLAMED, ANSWERED WITH THE ERROR OWNED.** Part one's spite is unavailable — no *"cool story bro"* hook shape, no bored-snarl punchline, no contempt as resolution.
6. ⛔ **Two registers of one voice, duelling — meeting in unison EXACTLY ONCE.**
7. **One sung number, spelled in words, at the hinge, responded to.** 88–110 sung lines · words/line < 7.5 · lyrics field < 5000 (target ≤4800) · music prompt 850–1000 (target 870–960).
8. ⛔ **No real artist or group names in the style prompt, exclude field, or lyrics — dedication only.** *A name in the prompt is an instruction to imitate; a name in the dedication is a debt paid.*
9. **Dedication ≤500 chars, measured.** ⛔ If the prompt names a break or a figure, credit the player.
