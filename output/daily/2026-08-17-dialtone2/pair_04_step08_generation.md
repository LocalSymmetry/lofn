## Lyric skeletons *(section maps + load-bearing lines only; full drafts land at Step 10)*

**Shared architecture — this pair's alone:** `CALL (high register) → ANSWER (low register, closed monosyllables) → CHORUS (both alternating) → … → HINGE → LOCK (the calls stop; the answers continue on the same grid, and do not release).`
⭐ The lock is the thesis in form: **an answer to a question nobody asked.** It arrives as a material change — kick to a dry clipped click, hats doubled, thirty hertz sustained, organ frozen on one chord, congregation dropping consonants for one vowel — at **constant level, no void, no build.**

⭐ **The structural pun:** on first pass this chorus is an answer with no question in front of it — the fault, performed. **The sting, and the pair's hinge:** *Once, it was the only thing / That caught the crack before it cracked / Nobody asked. Nobody wanted it. / And nobody was hurt that night* — then one short unguarded laugh, spent once, then **I answered** in unison.
