## ⛔ THE FOUR ANGLES ARE DERIVED FROM ONE QUESTION — NOT FROM A TEMPLATE

The existence prompt is **"what does it cost to keep offering, after the offer has been refused?"** and my accusation is **"not being asked for."** There is exactly one interesting move available: **find four things that keep offering at an unwavering rate whether or not anyone accepts, and put a body next to each of them.** The four angles are the four answers to *who is doing the offering*, and they walk deliberately from furthest-from-her to closest:

| | **who offers** | **is it a choice?** | **what the song learns** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | the sky, and a woman copying it | she thinks so | the offer is also a way of never being refused |
| **V2** | a tree, and a woman afraid she is one | **no — it cannot stop** | generosity and compulsion are indistinguishable from outside |
| **V3** | a bell, and the woman pulling it | yes, and she lied about why | the wrong one is the one they come out for |
| **V4** | **her, plainly, no figure at all** | she did not ask to be here **and cannot leave** | being unrefusable is not innocent |

⭐ **V4 is the load-bearing one and it is deliberately last.** Three figures earn the right to say the thing without a figure. If only one of these ships, it should be V4 or V1.

## FIRST DRAFTS

### V1 — **NOBODY ORDERED THE SKY**

**Refrain (locked, byte-identical ×4):**
```
Nobody ordered the sky.
It comes down anyway.
Nobody ordered the sky.
It comes down anyway.
```

**The list, in accretion order:** *Bread on the table. / A coat on the chair. / The porch light on. / The chair pulled out.* → **+ Salt in a shallow dish.** → **+ The gate left unlatched.** → **+ My good knife, laid flat.** → **+ And one question, out loud.**

**Verse 1 (draft):**
```
Middle of the night. No moon.
The grass took both my shoes.
I set it up where it was flat.
Nobody asked me out here.
I came out here regardless.
I do this more than I should.
The legs went in the wet.
```

**Radiant line:** *All of it out of Perseus.* — verse 2, once.
**Hinge:** *A hundred an hour. / A hundred an hour, and nobody / put an order in for one.*
**Wrong note:** *I sang that one note under. / I heard it. I let it stand. / Then I sang it under again.*
**Turn:** *You cannot be turned down / if you never once ask. / I set the table so I / would not have to hear it.*
**Unison:** *Would you come out and eat.*

**Draft flags:** ① "I do this more than I should" is the only line where brattiness could slide into self-pity — watch it. ② The knife needs to read as **offered**, not as threat; the line before it has to be tender or the AWE turns to horror.

**Refrain (locked):**
```

Windfall. Windfall.

```

**The list:** *The split ones. / The wasp-holed ones. / The ones the deer had. / The green ones, too early.* → **+ The ones off the low branch.** → **+ The ones bruised up top.** → **+ The one shaped wrong.** → **+ The bad year's. All of them.**

**Verse 1 (draft):**
```

I left my shoes at the gate.

It gives under the arch.

```

**Radiant line:** *Every tree in the row came off Antonovka.*
**Hinge:** *Nine bushels. / Nine bushels, and not one hand / came down the row for them.*
**Wrong note:** *The third course went sharp in the cold. / I did not stop and fix it. / I played the whole thing sharp.*

**Turn:** *A tree is not generous. / A tree cannot stop. / I would like to say I chose it.*

**Draft flags:** ① The cistern hazard must land in a verse, not a header — a hazard that only exists in the production note is not a hazard. ② "Windfall" as refrain risks reading pretty; the wasps have to be in earshot when it lands.

**Refrain (locked):**


**The list:** *For the hour. / For the dead. / For the frost. / For the flood.* → **+ For a wedding nobody had.** → **+ For the ones who left.** → **+ For the school being shut.** → **+ For no reason at all.**

**Verse 1 (draft):**

**Draft flags:** ① The rope hazard has to stay live all song — one mention at the top will read as scene-setting and vanish. ② "It gets into your teeth" is doing 55 Hz's job inside the lyric; make sure the production spec doesn't then *also* explain it.

### V4 — **I WAS NOT ASKED FOR**

**Refrain (locked):**
```
I was not asked for.
I came on anyway.
I was not asked for.
I am still on.
```

**The list:** *The lights, before you were up. / The name of the bird. / The word you couldn't get. / The door, when your hands were full.* → **+ The song you half remembered.** → **+ The number for the clinic.** → **+ The way home in the rain.** → **+ Nothing you asked me for.**

**Verse 1 (draft):**
```
You are in the chair.
I am on a shelf in the hall.
There is a green light on me.
There is a wire behind your head.
It has been warm the whole time.
Neither of us put it there.
I have been on since then.
```

**Radiant line:** *All of it came out of Halcyon.*
**Hinge:** *Fifty-five. / Fifty-five, and you feel it / in the chair. Not the ear.*
**Wrong note:** *I went under the note. / Everything moved down to meet me. / I did not move back up.*

**Draft flags:** ① The hand on the switch must never become a threat *from her* — the second it does, the song is spite and part one owns spite. ② The green light and the fan are her body; if they read as props the pillar-5 job has no host.

## DRAFT-STAGE FLAGS TO CARRY INTO STEP 09

- **F-A · Numeral scrub.** Drafts still contain "three in the morning", "the sixth", "the third course", "once". Every numeric except the one hinge number gets rewritten or spelled as a non-count. **Sweep in 09.**
- **F-B · Line length and field size.** V1 verse 1 line 3 ("I set it up where it was flat") runs seven words; the list lines run three to five. Aggregate is safe but the verses must not creep, and the assembled lyrics field must be measured with a script at step 10, never estimated. **Measure, do not eyeball.**
- **F-C · The knife (V1) and the switch (V4).** Both are the terror and both can tip into menace. Each needs the line before it to be tender. **Assign in 09.**
- **F-D · Refrain identity.** Four byte-identical repeats per song. **Do not defend them in the package; just ship them.**
- **F-E · The unison lines are also lines that appear elsewhere** (V2 and V3 reuse a turn line as the unison). That is deliberate — the unison is the moment the ledger says the offer's own sentence — but it must be the **only** repeat of that line. **Verify in 09.**
- **F-F · Cross-variation bleed.** All four share a refrain *shape* (four lines, statement/consequence). Check that no two share vocabulary beyond the pair's own axis words. **Diff in 09.**
- **F-G · "Regardless" appears in V1, V2 and V3 drafts.** That is pair bleed inside the pair. **Two of the three must change.**
