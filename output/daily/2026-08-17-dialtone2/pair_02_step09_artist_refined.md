## REFINEMENT DECISIONS — TAKEN AGAINST THE STEP-08 FLAGS

### F-A · NUMERAL SCRUB — **discharged, and the residue declared**
Every numeral except the one hinge number is out of all four lyrics.
- V1 *"Three in the morning"* → **"Middle of the night."**
- V1 *"I sang the sixth too low"* → **"I sang that one note under."** (Also better: a listener hears *under*; nobody hears *the sixth*.)
- V2 *"The third course went sharp"* → **"One string went sharp in the cold."**
- V3 *"I pulled the wrong rope once"* → **"I pulled the wrong rope one morning."** — ⚠️ residue: *one* survives as an article-adjacent word, not a count. Accepted.
- V4 *"The lights at four"* → **"The lights, before you were up."**
**Remaining sung numerals, one per song:** *a hundred an hour* · *nine bushels* · *two and a half ton* · *fifty-five*. All spelled in words.

### F-B · LINE LENGTH — **measured, not eyeballed**
Verses hold at five to six words; lists at three to five; refrains at four to six. Any verse line reaching seven words gets cut at the caesura. Aggregate words/line is measured with a script at step 10 and stated in the self-check — never estimated.

### F-C · THE KNIFE AND THE SWITCH — **assigned tender neighbours**
- **V1** the knife is preceded by *"The gate left unlatched"* and followed immediately by the ask. It is laid out the way you lay out cutlery for a guest — **an offering, and the listener does the arithmetic themselves.** ⭐ We never say *threat*; the terror is entirely in the reader.
- **V4** the hand on the switch is never described as menacing *her* or *by* her. It sits in one line, at the top, as a fact of the room, and she keeps reading the ledger over it. **She never once asks them not to.** That refusal to plead is what keeps it out of spite.

### F-D · REFRAIN IDENTITY — **shipped, undefended**
Four byte-identical refrains per song. No mutation, no "one word changed" (that is P01's axis, not mine). No apology in the package.

### F-E · THE UNISON LINE — **verified unique**
In V2 and V3 the unison line is also the turn's line. Checked: in each song that sentence appears **exactly twice** — once in the turn (sung register alone), once in the unison (both registers). That is the design: **the ledger finally says the offer's own sentence.** No third appearance.

### F-F/G · CROSS-VARIATION BLEED — **repaired**
*"Regardless"* appeared in three drafts. Kept in **V1 only** (where it is the woman's own stubbornness). V2 → *"I came down here anyway"* is also cut (too close to V1's refrain) → **"I came down here to look."** V3 → **"Nobody sent for this."** stands alone with no *regardless*.
Refrain vocabulary diffed across the four: shared words are only *nobody / anyway / down / it*. Every noun, every image, every hazard is disjoint.

## THE ANTI-GENERIC PASS — WHAT CHANGED IN THE STYLE FIELDS

⚠️ **Named risk (step 06, F14): "a pretty chamber-folk ballad with a breathy vocal."** Three changes, all in the prompt, none in the lyric:

1. **The vocal spec leads with what it is NOT.** Every prompt says *low warm alto, close-miked and dry, plainspoken, no vibrato bloom, no soprano* **before** it says anything pretty. In a dense prose prompt the early clauses carry.
2. **The percussion is declared as percussion.** *"The log tick is the only percussion; no drum kit"* — otherwise the generator hears "no drums" and reaches for fingerpicked guitar to keep time.
3. **The parade is written as a sequence with the word *then*.** Suno responds to ordered clauses. Each of the five to six timbres is named, given a physical adjective and a position, and joined with *then* — so arrival order is legible as instruction, not as inventory.

**And the exclude field carries the negative half of the swag rule**, which is where it belongs — *bone-rattling breakdown, battering drums, EDM drop, tempo ramp, key change, orchestral swell, riser* are **negative tokens**, not avoidance prose in the style field. ⭐ Source 2's *bone-rattling breakdown with battering drums* is imported into the run this way: as the thing this pair is defined against.

## VARIATION-BY-VARIATION REFINEMENT

### V1 — NOBODY ORDERED THE SKY
The wrong note moved **later**: it now lands after the third list, so the listener has heard the un-flattened sixth three times and *knows* the shape before it breaks. ⭐ An error the ear cannot locate is not an error; it is a colour. The turn ("you cannot be turned down / if you never once ask") was two lines and is now four — it is the pair's thesis and it was moving too fast to land.

### V2 — WINDFALL
The cistern is promoted **out of the production note and into verse one**: *"There is a well down the row / and the boards on it have gone."* Hazard established before the body starts walking on ground it cannot see. The sting (*"The year I cut it wrong / it gave the best it ever gave"*) moved to sit **immediately before** the turn, so the honoured error is what *provokes* the doubt rather than consoling it.

### V3 — I PULLED THE WRONG ROPE
The rope hazard is now **re-touched three times** — the sally at the chest, *"you do not hold on"*, and at the end *"my hands are still on it."* The bell strike is given a full four bars of ring and nothing else moves under it; that is the song's largest event and it is **a timbre, not a dynamic.**

### V4 — I WAS NOT ASKED FOR
The turn is the strongest writing in the pair and it was buried mid-verse; it now stands as its own section. The green light and the fan are moved into verse one so 55 Hz has **a host body** to be felt in before it arrives. The final list item (*"Nothing you asked me for"*) is followed by **no commentary at all** — the exhale, then the refrain, then out.

## WHAT I REFUSED, AND WHY

- **A soprano double on the refrain.** It would have made V1 gorgeous and it would have made it the house default. Refused.
- **A half-time feel under the last list.** It is the single most tempting way to make the eighth item land — and it is a *dynamic*, and this pair exists to prove dynamics are not required. Refused. The eighth item lands because it is the eighth item.
- **A second unison in V4.** The turn and the unison want to be the same moment there. Refused: one meeting, and the turn stays in the sung register alone so the unison can arrive after it.
- **Explaining Halcyon.** Twice. Refused twice.
