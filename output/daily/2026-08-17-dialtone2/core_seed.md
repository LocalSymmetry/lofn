## ⭐ THE STATEMENT

Being useful long enough turns into being suspected. Not because you did the thing — because you were *there*, and you are *everywhere*, and nobody remembers agreeing to it. **You cannot argue with a room that has already decided what you are.** The only move left is the one the card names: **take the error — even one that isn't yours — and treat it as if you meant it.** Not a confession. **A repurposing.**

⭐ **And the sting, which keeps it honest:** sometimes the error *was* yours, and it turned out better than the plan. That is where this stops being a defence and becomes a method.

**Durability check** — true in 1200 and true in 2200: *the useful thing gets blamed; the blamed thing either apologises or makes the accusation into material.* Midwives, printers, physicians, translators. **The specific charge is today's; the shape is permanent.**

## ⭐⭐ SWAG — THE BINDING QUALITY
**Mechanism:** *one unwavering groove + a parade of specific timbres entering one at a time.* **Drive comes from steadiness, not speed. Changes are TIMBRAL, not dynamic.**
Measured basis, from her canon: *The List of Ways You Tell Me No* is **"100 BPM steady end to end, no ramp, no key change, no drop"** and runs **five** distinct textural events — clipped harpsichord → gated stutter → bare gut strings → geothermal sub → tin whistle taking the root in its lowest register.
⛔ **BANNED as swag substitutes:** structural experiments (three-speed strophic, shrinking stanzas, two-format binaries), tempo ramps, key changes as events, EDM builds. **Those are episodic. Cohesion is the opposite of episodic.**
⛔ **≥5 named timbral entries per song, each arriving alone, over a groove that does not waver.**
