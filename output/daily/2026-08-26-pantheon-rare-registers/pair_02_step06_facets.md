## 0. THE ONE THING THIS PAIR IS FOR

> **Enthusiasm points at the thing. This points at the pointing.**

An enthusiastic person does not need you in the room. She needs the object. My register needs **a witness**, says so in plain words, and is not made smaller by saying it. So the whole pair is built on one grammatical move that enthusiasm cannot make:

**not** *"look at this"* — **"look at me looking at it."**

⭐ **And that is the run's own material, arriving unasked.** LAW 3 says the subject is lit by light running *across* its direction of travel, held long enough to expose. A waterfall is invisible until the aurora crosses it. **Wanting is invisible until somebody looks across it.** The falling is mine; the light is theirs; the exposure is the ask. I did not have to translate the seed for this register — it was already the same picture.

⚠️ **And the day is the staging.** The ground did nothing. The feed was empty. *Stillness with one thing moving inside it* — so in all four songs the room is still and **I am the thing moving inside it.** That is the whole set's blocking, and it comes free.

## 1. THE FIVE JUDGING FACETS

**FACET 1 · THE ASK IS AUDIBLE, AND NOTHING ARRIVES AFTER IT TO SOFTEN IT.**
Does the request for a witness get sung *in plain words* — a sentence a person could say out loud at a counter — and does the section then **end**, with no tag-line, no walk-back, no laugh, no rhyme that makes it a bit? The test is physical: the last syllable of every ask should be followed by air, not by a smaller sentence. If the ear expects one more line and gets a downbeat instead, the facet passes. *(Sensory anchor: the tiny cold second after you have said the thing and the other person has not answered yet.)*

**FACET 2 · THE NEIGHBOUR IS HANDED OVER WHOLE, THEN BROKEN IN ONE MOVE.**
For roughly forty seconds this must be filable as *an excited song* — bright, forward, appetite-first, the surface a listener has heard a thousand times. Then, at one nameable instant, the object of the gaze changes from **the thing** to **the wanting**, and enthusiasm has no account of it. Judge it as a switch, not a slope: can you point at the bar? If the discriminator arrives as a gradual deepening, it has failed — a mood cannot revoke a filing, only an event can. *(Sensory anchor: the light going from over the object to across the face.)*

**FACET 3 · CURRENT FROM THE FIRST TWO SECONDS, WITH ONE OLD BODY MOVING UNDER IT.**
Delete every lineage word from the prompt: does what remains still specify a record made **now** — drum architecture, low-end engine, edit grammar, vocal processing, spectrum behaviour? Then mute the inherited marker: does a complete current song survive? Then mute the chassis: does the record lose its world? One inherited bodily relationship only — an offbeat relation, a bass motion, a call-and-answer cadence, a hole where a note should be — audible **inside the first two bars**, and never a whole period band behind it. *(Sensory anchor: a body that learned to move in another decade, wearing today's clothes and not in costume.)*

**FACET 4 · GLOSS THAT INVITES, AND WANTING THAT COSTS HER NOTHING TO ADMIT.**
High shine, close bright vocal, candy-hard transients, one specular hit that returns with the hook — and every finish decision answering *does this make the listener want to step closer?* The wanting must be **specific** rather than large: a named object, a price, a lane, a card on a wall. Vague appetite is decoration; an exact appetite is evidence. And the shine must point at somebody. ⛔ If the polish reads as a surface that keeps the room out, this is Alexis's vector, not mine, and the seat is gone. *(Sensory anchor: wet light on a counter, pointed at one person, not at the ceiling.)*

**FACET 5 · NOT ONE UNDERCUT, ANYWHERE, INCLUDING THE ONES THAT LOOK LIKE CRAFT.**
Scan every sung line for the reflex: *sorry · too much · I know, I know · ridiculous · don't mind me · I guess · whatever · just kidding.* Then scan for the disguised versions — a self-mocking rhyme, a diminutive, a comic beat that lands **on her**, a witness made stupid so the sneer has somewhere to go. Zero, in all four. ⛔ The refusal covers transferred irony too: **nobody in these songs is a fool for what they love.** *(Sensory anchor: a sentence that stays the size it was when it left her mouth.)*

## 3. WHAT THE PANEL SAID THAT I AM ACTUALLY USING

- **THE EXACT WORD** *(Moore seat)* — she distrusts a line swappable for a near-synonym. My ask returns four times with **one word changed**, and the change must be a **narrowing**, never an escalation. That is her rule turned into a mechanism: if the swapped word could be replaced by its synonym, the return did nothing.
- **THE ENGINEERED DELIGHT** *(Wodehouse seat)* — joy is a technical achievement and the first thing lost to revision. **He gets the funny song and he gets it in V4.**
- **THE OFFBEAT RELATION** *(Allen seat)* — the feeling arrives through the body's relation to a pulse, never through a chord's name. So my inherited body is always a **relationship**, never an instrument list.
- **THE KEPT BODY** *(Russell seat)* — R5's operation, executed by someone who did it. One motion carried intact into a chassis from another decade.
- **THE ENCODING** *(Levitin seat)* — a single hearing retains four things. Each variation attaches its shape to a **different** one of the four.
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE** *(Q. Jones seat, hyper-skeptic)* — his objection is the sharpest one aimed at me: *does the feeling survive one voice and one instrument?* Every hook here is a plain spoken sentence with a tune. It survives a kitchen. Answered, not deflected.
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-NUANCE** *(Ekman seat, hyper-skeptic)* — he says the listener will hear excitement and be right. **He is right for forty seconds, on purpose.** Facet 2 exists because of him.
- ⚠️ **THE DURATION** *(Radigue seat, recorded dissent)* — she holds that pop length produces a *label for* a feeling. She was granted P05 and overruled here. I am overruling her a second time and naming why: **this register is fast by nature.** A held, slow version of *wanting to be caught wanting* is a different feeling — it is longing, which has a name and is not rare. **Speed is not a compromise here; it is the discriminator's own tempo.** If the pair comes back reading as *announced*, she was right and the repair is hers.
