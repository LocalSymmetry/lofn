## 3. TITLE

The Back Wall Goes First

### ⚠️ RAW GAPS
- **V1-a — the hook has no answering half.** A3 is *a two-note figure that answers itself*; the vocal hook
  only asks. **The room has nothing to sing back into**, so the discriminator is near the hook, not in it.
- **V1-b — the Call-home resolves tidily**, four clean statements and four clean tags. A song whose refused
  element is the arrival should not end neatly.
- **V1-c — the two beats of silence sit in the drop-slot**, which is the safest place in music to put
  nothing: everyone knows what belongs there, so it reads as a gap in the file rather than a decision.

## 3. TITLE

Any Second Now, Not Now

### ⚠️ RAW GAPS
- **V4-a — the Read completes its sentence** (*"before it happens."*). For the variation whose whole subject
  is a suspension, finishing the opening sentence is the single worst choice available.
- **V4-b — no answering half in the hook**; the antithesis is spread across two lines instead of held inside
  one, so a listener can chant the claim without chanting the refusal.

  than a decision.
- **V4-d — every section has drums somewhere.** The song is never shown naked.

## PANEL PASS — CRITIQUE AT THE RAW STAGE
*(the supplied 18-voice ledger; the Hyper-Skeptics genuinely dissent)*

**THE HOOK MATH** *(after Max Martin)* — *"All four hooks are questions with no reply. You have written four
calls and no responses, in a song form literally called CALL-BEFORE-THE-MOVE. A room cannot own a hook it
has no part in. **Put the answer inside the line.**"* ⭐ **Adopted — the single largest raw defect, and it
repeats in all four.**

**THE MORPHOLOGY OF FEELING** *(after Langer)* — *"Your A3 axis is 'a two-note figure that answers itself.'
The music has it; the words do not. The form of the feeling is a claim and its own withholding, so the
lyric hook should be **two notes** too."* ⭐ Adopted, and it turns the defect above into a design.

⚠️ **THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE** *(Hyper-Skeptic, after Quincy Jones)* — *"V4 will not survive one voice and one
instrument; right now it survives on silence, and silence is not material. And in V3 the arrangement is
arguing with the lyric — you have written a song about two people and mixed it in thirds."* ⭐ Both adopted.

**THE DELIVERY** *(after Simone)* — *"Nothing here is a vocal decision yet. Every distinguishing choice is a
chart choice. Give me four things a singer would refuse."* ⭐ Adopted; step 09 carries them.

**THE NEGATIVE SPACE** *(after Davis)* — *"V4's Read finishes its sentence. Take the full stop away."*
⭐ Adopted.

**THE ENGINEERED DELIGHT** *(after Wodehouse)* — *"There is a dog asleep at a barrier and you have not paid
it off. You have a joke and you are letting it lie down."* ⭐ Adopted; V2 gets the payoff and the laugh.

**THE OFFBEAT RELATION** *(after Tony Allen)* — *"V2's stab is on the offbeat and the vocal is not. The
body's relation to the pulse has to include the voice."* ⭐ Adopted.

⚠️ **THE ANTI-NUANCE** *(Hyper-Skeptic, after Ekman)* — *"V1 reads as excitement from line one to line
thirty. That is your plan and I accept it — but your break comes at line forty-three, and nobody is still
listening for a revision that late."* ⚠️ **Partly adopted, partly overruled.** The R1 moment stays where it
is *(it has to be earned)*, but **the hook itself becomes the discriminator in all four**, so the
contradiction is present from the first chorus. **The disagreement is recorded.**

⚠️ **THE ANTI-INTENTION** *(Hyper-Skeptic, after Klosterman)* — *"You have not written down what you think a
listener will feel, so you cannot be wrong later."* ⭐ Adopted — R6 predictions are filed in the step-11
package, before any render.

**THE EXACT WORD** *(after Moore)* — cuts V4's *"and none of them have made it"* to *"and none of them
have"*, and flags *"It's the smallest thing that proves a room"* as the best line in the pair. Kept.

**THE SCENE DEBT** *(after Chang)* — *"You have taken an offbeat relation, an answer from another register,
a melodic bass and a habit of silence. Four scenes. Name them where names belong."* ⭐ Adopted — the
dedication, never the fields.
