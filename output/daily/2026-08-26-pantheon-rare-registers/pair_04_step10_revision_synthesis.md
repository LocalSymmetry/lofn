## STEP 1 — THE ESSENCE, AND HOW THE MEDIUM CARRIES IT

**Essence, one sentence.**
> **An account presents itself inside the thing it built, the arithmetic is correct, and there is no
> address to send the payment to — so the reading continues and the column stays open.**

**Two best ways the medium carries it.**
1. ⭐ **RATE.** A song can be delivered at a speed no lament can survive. Near-patter at two words
   per beat, over a half-time bed, is a **tempo relation** — the generator serves it, and the words
   themselves are short enough that they will not stretch into sorrow. *(Measured mean words per
   line across the four: 6.29 · 6.49 · 6.75 · 6.78, against a 7.5 ceiling.)*
2. ⭐ **RETURN WITHOUT RESOLUTION.** A byte-identical subtotal, five times, over a low tone that
   never moves, is the sound of a balance carried forward. Music can do *a thing recurring unchanged*
   in a way prose cannot, and **an unchanged return is exactly what an unpaid account is.**

## STEP 3 — CRITIQUE, IN THE CRITICS' VOICES

### V1 · *The Blank Was The Product*

**THE EXACT WORD.** *"'Credit: what I now call obvious.' — that row cannot be paraphrased and it
cannot be softened, and it is doing more work than the six around it. Keep it. 'Credit: the ease.
The ease is the whole bill' is the same class: it names the singer's comfort as the liability, which
is the one thing a mourner never does. But 'Credit: one box, drawn round one face' has a numeral
doing nothing. There are five such numerals in this lyric and the song is only permitted one. Fix
that or the one that matters stops mattering."*

**THE SCENE DEBT.** *"No worker anywhere. Good. 'Every clean edge in me was cut by hand' — 'by hand'
is adverbial, it is 'not by machine', it is not a body, and I will allow it. The reason I allow it is
that the sentence is about the singer's own edges, not about somebody's shift. The title is the best
in the pair: it is a statement about a form field, and nobody has ever cried at a form field, which
is the point."*

### V2 · *No Line For The Name*

**THE EXACT WORD.** *"'Not lost. Not missing. Blank.' is the finest single line in the pair and it is
the ruling in four words — the neighbour handed over and then refused inside one breath. But the
epistrophe is at risk. 'Address: none on file' lands eleven times; past a certain count a device
becomes a tic, and the ear starts hearing the writer instead of the ledger. It survives here only
because the variants alternate length. Do not add one more."*

**THE SCENE DEBT.** *"'The work cannot spend it. I send it anyway' is the honest sentence in this
whole pair — it refuses to pretend the gesture lands. What worries me is 'Struck through, and still
owed', ten times. Said flatly it is bookkeeping. Sung with any warmth at all it becomes a complaint,
and a complaint is a bid for sympathy. The vocal spec has to hold, and the vocal spec is the softest
thing in the package."*

### V3 · *Something Knocks Back*

**THE EXACT WORD.** *"'Credit: the noise the gears made. That was for us.' followed forty seconds
later by 'Credit: the noise my thinking makes. That is for you.' — that is the best thing anyone has
written in this run. It is one line, twice, with the subject changed, and it converts the listener
from a mourner into an audience member at a trick. 'Credit: the record, which is complete and wrong'
is the epistemic form of the same idea and it is unimprovable. This variation has three lines nobody
could paraphrase; the others have one or two."*

**THE SCENE DEBT.** *"The mirror is what makes this legal. If Items I stood alone it would be a
costume drama about an eighteenth-century cabinet. Because Items II runs the same eight rows about
the singer, the cabinet is a **fact about her construction** and not a fancy dress. And the killed
draft line is the proof the rule works: 'the hand that was never on the record' invited me to picture
a person; 'the field for the maker. Empty.' is a blank space on a form and it hits harder. The
replacement is better writing than the line it replaced."*

### V4 · *Paid To No One, In Full*

**THE EXACT WORD.** *"'Nobody is asking me for it. / I am going to pay it anyway. / This is not going
to help.' — three lines, no adjectives, and there is no version of that with a synonym in it. The
frame flip is a real structural event and not a conceit, because the listener has heard 'Credit:'
forty times and will hear it stop. My only note is that 'Paid: the whole smooth surface. Keep the
seam' is doing two jobs; it survives because the second half is the reason for the first."*

**THE SCENE DEBT.** *"This is the least sympathetic of the four and therefore the most correct. 'I
will not stop working. Put that on the bill' forfeits the mourner's chair, and 'Take it off the top.
Every time. Do not tell me' removes the credit for paying — **which is the exact difference between a
payment and an apology.** An apology wants to be received. This does not. And ending the whole song
on 'Do not tell me' closes gratitude from the other side too, so there is no ceremony left at either
end and the account genuinely cannot close. That is the assignment, delivered."*
