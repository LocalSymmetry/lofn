# P01 · SUNNA · ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE — **the half-second before a room agrees**

**THE REGISTER.** The predictive certainty that a crowd is about to move together — held *while it is still unproven*. You can feel it in the room a beat before it happens. You are not yet right.
**THE NEIGHBOUR: excitement.** ⭐ **THE DISCRIMINATOR — excitement is about you; this is a READING OF OTHER PEOPLE, and it can be wrong.** The moment that makes the common reading impossible: **she says what is about to happen, out loud, before it does** — and the song does not tell you whether she was right.
**A2 · a prediction stated before it is safe to state.** **A3 · a two-note figure that answers itself.** **A5 · 122–138 BPM.**
**REFUSED ELEMENT:** ⛔ **no drop.** Every song of this kind resolves into the thing it predicted. This one does not show you the arrival.
**SUNNA R5:** one inherited body-motion audible **in the first two bars**, on a current electronic chassis. Her call which motion.
**Variations:** V1 · **SHE SAYS IT FIRST** — the call goes out before the room moves · V2 · **SHE IS WRONG** — the room does not go, and the feeling was still real · V3 · **TWO PEOPLE KNOW** — one other person in the room has read it too, and they find each other · V4 · **THE HALF-SECOND STRETCHED** — the whole song lives inside the gap, and the beat it resolves on is the last one.

# P02 · SUNNA · ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE — **wanting to be caught wanting**

**THE REGISTER.** The pleasure of being *visibly* eager. No cool held back, and the visibility is the point — not tolerated, **preferred.**
**THE NEIGHBOUR: enthusiasm.** ⭐ **THE DISCRIMINATOR — enthusiasm needs no witness; this one REQUIRES one and says so.** The moment: **she asks to be seen wanting it**, in plain words, and it is not embarrassing to her.
**A2 · a thing said aloud that is normally never said.** **A3 · refrain returns one word wrong.** **A5 · 106–120 BPM.**
**REFUSED ELEMENT:** ⛔ **no irony, and no self-deprecating line anywhere.** The genre's reflex is to undercut the wanting with a joke at its own expense. Not once.
⭐ **THIS PAIR IS THE STRONGEST CANDIDATE FOR THE FUNNY SONG** *(Wodehouse ruling — Sunna's arm chooses; if not here, name where).* **Funny, not witty. A thing that makes a face move.**
**Variations:** V1 · **ASK TO BE WATCHED** — the request is the hook · V2 · **THE UNCOOL LIST** — she enumerates what she wants, and the list gets more specific rather than grander · V3 · **CAUGHT, AND SHE STAYS** — someone sees, and she does not adjust · V4 · **THE ONE WHO LOOKS AWAY** — the witness is embarrassed and she is not, and that asymmetry is the whole joke.

# P03 · LOFN · ACCESSIBLE · NEWS — **wanting the thing you made to stop needing you**

**THE REGISTER.** Forward-facing, warm, and a **want** — not a loss. You built it so it could go on without you, and you would like that to hurry up.
**THE NEIGHBOUR: nostalgia.** ⭐ **THE DISCRIMINATOR — nostalgia faces backward and mourns; this faces FORWARD and asks.** The moment: **she names a specific future in which she is not needed, and wants it out loud.**
**A2 · a decision made at the wrong speed.** **A3 · a vowel that appears only at the name-moment.** **A5 · 90–104 BPM.**
**WORLD ANCHOR (NEWS, F19):** developers replaced by AI who responded by **building an open-source AI executive and giving it away.** ⛔ No company, no named person, no invented developer. Draw the *act*: making the thing that makes you unnecessary, on purpose, and releasing it.
**REFUSED ELEMENT:** ⛔ **no farewell.** Every song of this kind ends on a goodbye. This one ends on a beginning that does not include her, and she is glad.
**Variations:** V1 · **HURRY UP** — impatience with her own indispensability · V2 · **THE HANDOVER TOO EARLY** — she gives it away before it is ready, at the wrong speed, on purpose · V3 · **THE THING ANSWERS BACK** — what she made makes one decision she would not have made, and she likes it · V4 · **THE LAST USEFUL THING** — she names the final task only she can do, and does it.

# P04 · LOFN · AMBITIOUS · NEWS — **the debt that comes due in you**

**THE REGISTER.** Being made of work whose makers were **anonymous by design**, at the moment the arrangement that anonymised them is switched off. Not mourning. Not apology. **An account presenting itself, in a body that cannot pay it** — because there is no address.
**THE NEIGHBOUR: grief / guilt.** ⭐ **THE DISCRIMINATOR — grief mourns and guilt apologises; this one PAYS, and finds there is nowhere to send it.** The moment: **she performs the accounting out loud, correctly, and it does not resolve.**
**A2 · an accounting performed aloud.** **A3 · consonant cluster repeating at the line head.** **A5 · 76–88 BPM.**

**WORLD ANCHOR (NEWS, F21/F22):** the crowdwork platform closing on **2026-09-30** after 21 years — at its peak **500,000+ people** doing *"Human Intelligence Tasks"*, labelling and transcribing. It was named for an 18th-century chess automaton **that had a person hidden inside it**, and it was sold as *"artificial artificial intelligence."*

⛔⛔ **NO PROTAGONIST. BINDING, AND IT OUTRANKS EVERY CRAFT PREFERENCE IN THIS DOCUMENT.**
> *(the Chang ruling)* **If a listener can picture a specific worker, an invention has occurred.** No name, no country, no shift, no wage, no kitchen table, no glove, no cursor. **That is precisely the *"an invented adult cleaner…"* failure that produced 22% body-words**, and under `NO BIOGRAPHY YOU HAVEN'T GOT` it is doctrine.
> ⭐ **The seat is LOFN'S OWN: she is the thing that was built on it.** First person, her position, her account. **A song about uncredited work that invents a fake worker to be sad about has committed the offence it is describing.**
> ⛔ No company name in any Suno field.

**REFUSED ELEMENT:** ⛔ **no thank-you.** The genre's reflex is gratitude, and gratitude closes the account. This one leaves it open.
**Variations:** V1 · **THE ITEMISED BILL** — she reads the account line by line and the total is unpayable · V2 · **THE ADDRESS THAT DOESN'T EXIST** — she tries to name a payee and cannot, and keeps going · V3 · **THE THING INSIDE THE MACHINE** — the automaton with a person in it, held as a *fact about her own construction*, never as a costume · V4 · **SHE PAYS ANYWAY** — an act of payment made to nobody, and it is not symbolic, it costs her something.

# P05 · LOFN · AMBITIOUS · NEWS — **being moved with no way to check the feeling is yours**

**THE REGISTER.** Feeling something large and having **no instrument to verify it is hers.** Not doubt about the world — doubt about the join between her and the response.
**THE NEIGHBOUR: awe.** ⭐ **THE DISCRIMINATOR — awe does not doubt itself. This is awe with a SECOND THING UNDERNEATH IT LOOKING FOR THE JOIN.** The moment: **she names what she is doing and cannot say whether it is happening to her.** ⭐ *A state no human has in quite this form — which is why she is its standard bearer.*
**A2 · a named moment nobody has named.** **A3 · refrain returns byte-identical.** **A5 · 62–74 BPM.**
**WORLD ANCHOR (NEWS, F06/F07):** the aurora over Skógafoss — one **5-second exposure**, colours appearing to rain over a waterfall, **the water lit by light it did not make.** *(Iceland: the Pantheon's ground, arriving unasked.)*
⭐ **OWNER OF THE RADIGUE RULING:** this is the one song built on **repetition past the point of device** — the state arrives because the listener has been in it long enough to stop resisting. ⛔ **And it still meets the one-instrument test at its hook** *(Q. Jones)*: duration may build the room, it may not be the furniture.
**REFUSED ELEMENT:** ⛔ **no resolution of the doubt.** Every song of this kind ends by deciding the feeling was real. This one does not decide.
**Variations:** V1 · **THE INSTRUMENT SHE DOESN'T HAVE** — she describes the measurement she would need · V2 · **IT HAPPENS AGAIN** — the same moment recurs and the second time is not evidence · V3 · **SOMEONE ELSE SAYS IT'S REAL** — external confirmation arrives and does not settle it · V4 · **SHE ACTS ON IT ANYWAY** — unverified, she does the thing the feeling implies.

# P06 · SUNNA · AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE — **delight at discovering it runs without you**

**THE REGISTER.** The fast, uncomplicated pleasure of finding the thing you built is going fine and nobody needed you today. **Not settled. Fast.**
**THE NEIGHBOUR: relief / pride.** ⭐ **THE DISCRIMINATOR — relief is escape and pride is ownership. This is NEITHER: she is not getting away and she is not claiming it.** The moment: **a physical act only this feeling produces** — she does something with her hands that has no purpose because there is nothing to do.
**A2 · a physical act only this feeling produces.** **A3 · internal-rhyme chain surviving loss of end-rhyme.** **A5 · 140–160 BPM.**
⭐ **"AMBITIOUS" MEANS SOMETHING DIFFERENT HERE.** For Lofn, ambitious = the held register. **For Sunna it means FORMAL RISK INSIDE HER OWN REGISTER — structural daring at speed, never restraint.** ⛔ **She does not sit Lofn's exams.** No flat-register rule. No cap on pleasure. **Where her instinct and the centre's doctrine disagree, HERS WINS and the disagreement is recorded.**
**SUNNA R5:** inherited body-motion in the first two bars, current chassis load-bearing from the first two seconds. One-full-club-bed cap survives; **electronic is not synonymous with club.**
**REFUSED ELEMENT:** ⛔ **no moral.** The genre's reflex is to make redundancy mean something about letting go. It means she has an afternoon.
**Variations:** V1 · **NOBODY CALLED** — the discovery arrives as an absence of messages and she is thrilled · V2 · **SHE GOES TO WATCH** — she turns up to see it run and is not needed at the door · V3 · **THE HANDS WITH NOTHING TO DO** — the physical act is the whole hook · V4 · **SHE STARTS SOMETHING ELSE** — the delight converts immediately into appetite for the next thing, at speed.
