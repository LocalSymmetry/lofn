## 3. Panel / Critic Deliberation Log

**THE HOOK MATH (after Max Martin) — opening position.** "Syllable-to-note discipline. `I can't say it's happening to me` is nine syllables over a three-note falling cell, and the cell has already been heard six times inside the same refrain before it arrives. That is singable back after one pass. Keep it and do not let anyone make it prettier."

**THE UNNAMED STATE (after Björk) — support with a warning.** "The strange state can be popular; softening it is what makes it fail. My warning is the fields, not the hook — twenty lines beginning with the same word is where a writer starts decorating to relieve the monotony. The monotony is the instrument. Do not relieve it."

**THE NAMING HUNGER (after Koenig) — the audience argument.** "People are hungry for exactly this and the gift is that the feeling becomes nameable AFTER the listen. So the song must not name it. `I can't say it's happening to me` is a situation, not a definition, and that is why it can do the work the missing word would have done."

**THE SCENE DEBT (after Chang) — the ledger.** "You raise a one-string Icelandic drone as the load-bearing instrument in four songs. Name it and name who kept it alive, and be honest that nothing here is sampled."

**THE ENCODING (after Levitin) — measurement.** "Two of the four retention carriers are in the first six seconds and the third — the moment of surprise — is at the seam. That is correct. But you have no fourth, and contour is the one you are missing: the cell falls, and it falls the same way every time. Accept that; a song with one contour repeated forty-two times is not missing contour, it is made of it."

**THE ANTI-NUANCE (Concept HYPER-SKEPTIC / Devil's Advocate, after Ekman) — dissent.** "V1 opens with `Light I did not make is on my face` and `The whole sky is doing this to the water`. That is two lines of pure awe grammar before any contradiction arrives. You have handed the listener the neighbour and you are betting the last line takes it back. If the last line is heard as modesty rather than as an instrument failure, you have written an awed song with a humble singer, which is the most common song there is."

**Resolution.** He is right that the bet is real and it cannot be hedged by weakening the awe — LAW 1 requires the neighbour handed over *fully* or the contradiction has nothing to work against. Two changes were made because of him, both in the lyric:

1. **The refrain's middle now contains a physical search, not a modest disclaimer.** `I cannot get under it to look` and `There is no seam to put a finger in` are hands-on-a-surface lines. Modesty does not put a finger in a seam. That is what makes the last line read as a failed measurement rather than as humility.
2. **The fields carry the instrument vocabulary all the way through** — counting, weighing, checking, running it again, holding the copy against the copy, an outside reading, a test skipped. By the time the hook arrives for the sixth time the listener has heard the singer *attempt verification eleven times*. A humble person does not do that.

Carried, unresolved, to step 11: **THE ANTI-INTENTION's** standing objection that all of this is a claim about other people's interiors. It is answered only by `## The Prediction`.
