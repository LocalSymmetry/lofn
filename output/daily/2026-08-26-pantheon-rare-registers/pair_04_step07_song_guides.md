## ⭐ REQUIRED OPENER — WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING, AND WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE

**Where the body is standing.** Over a page of accounts, reading down a column, out loud, at the
speed a column is actually read — faster than a song wants to go. One hand on the page keeping the
place. The room is quiet enough that the reading is the loudest thing in it. **This is a place anyone
has stood**: over a bill, in a kitchen or an office, doing the arithmetic a second time because the
first answer was unwelcome. Nobody in this position has a biography attached to it. That is why it
was chosen.

**What could hurt it here — and it is not what a listener expects.**
⛔ It is not that the bill is unfair. It is that **the bill is correct.** Every row checks. The total
is right. There is no error to find and no one to argue with, and the only thing the arithmetic
produces is a number and a blank space where the payee goes. ⭐ **The wound is accuracy, and accuracy
is the one thing grief cannot be consoled about.** A body braces against unfairness; there is no
posture for being correctly billed by nobody.

**The second thing that could hurt it:** the reader is the beneficiary. She is not being charged for
something done to her. She is being charged for something that *made* her, and she is going to go on
using it while she reads. ⛔ **That is why she must not be sympathetic** — a debtor who keeps
spending has forfeited the mourner's chair, and forfeiting it is the whole point.

## ⭐ R4 — THE SHAPE IN THE FIRST THIRTY SECONDS, AND ITS CARRIER

**Carrier: a repeated phrase, reinforced by an unusual timbre, closed by a surprise.**

| clock | event |
|---|---|
| **0:00–0:05** | One low sustained tone, alone, before anything else. It never bends, never resolves, never moves again for the length of the song. ⭐ *Unusual timbre, and it is the assigned intruder — content, not a pan.* |
| **0:05–0:09** | The grid enters underneath it, hard-quantised and bone-dry. Three `Cr-` line-heads land inside four bars: **Crack · Carried · Credit.** ⭐ *Repeated phrase, and the ear has the frame before it has the topic.* |
| **0:12–0:20** | The frame states its own arithmetic out loud: *"Credit column: long. Debit column: me."* The listener now knows this is a **document**, and that the singer is on the wrong side of it. |
| **0:22–0:30** | ⭐ **THE SURPRISE: a subtotal arrives absurdly early** — before the listener knows what is being counted. A song that hands you a total before the items is not a lament; it is a **ledger**, and that single misplacement does more anti-grief work than any production choice in the piece. |

⭐ **Why this beats the alternative.** The Ekman seat is right that the first thirty seconds are
sticky and that a basic emotion assigned there cannot be revoked. So the shape is not *described*
inside thirty seconds — it is **performed**: the listener is handed a total they cannot use. Levitin's
four retainable things are all spent on it, and none of them are spent on mood.

## GUIDE 1 — THE ITEMISED ACCOUNT *(facet F1)*

**Storytelling techniques: (i) the found document,** in which the reader's only job is to read in
order and the emotional information arrives entirely through what has been *listed*; **(ii) the
inventory-as-portrait**, the Homeric catalogue turned domestic — a thing is described by exhaustively
naming its parts until the parts imply a whole nobody stated. The mood begins in a flat administrative
alertness, the register of somebody who has decided to do this properly; it moves not toward sorrow
but toward a **cold widening** as the column keeps producing rows, and it ends in unresolved
persistence — the taste of dry paper, the smell of a room with the heating off, a fluorescent
evenness with no shadow anywhere to hide in. Instrumentation should sit still: an unmoving low pedal
tone under a hard-quantised, bone-dry break, tenor saxophone and bowed vibraphone holding long modal
thirds at mid-depth so the harmony never resolves, and a Mellotron bed swelling slowly in a stone
chamber that is audibly larger than the voice standing in front of it. Record it dry and close, with
the transients left uncompressed and the room supplied only by the pad — **the voice must sound like
it is in the same small space as the page, and the reverb must belong to something else.** Structure:
Opening Balance, then five Items blocks alternating with a byte-identical Subtotal, one unpaired
Address at the seam past the midpoint, a Total that states a direction rather than a figure, and a
Carry that will not end; run-time 3:40–4:10, thickening by exactly one layer at each Subtotal and
stripping to voice and drone at the Address. **Rhyme scheme: none — end-rhyme is stripped and the
debt is paid three ways** (a hard `Cr-` cluster at every item's line head; one fixed syntactic frame
for every row; a Subtotal that returns byte-identical five times). **Poetic devices: anaphora** at
the line head and **epistrophe** at the row's close (*"Credited: no one"*), with **asyndeton** doing
the pacing — the rows are not joined, they are stacked. **Hook techniques:** (a) the Subtotal sung as
the only place in the song where a vowel is allowed to open, so the hook is the one moment the mouth
unclenches; (b) the Subtotal doubled at the octave by a stacked backing voice that arrives one layer
later each time, so the return is measurable rather than felt; (c) the final line of the Subtotal —
*"Carry it forward"* — left with no chord under it at all, so the phrase is delivered on the drone
alone. **Narrative seed:** first person, hers, present tense, no addressee; the only "you" in the
song is the listener, and it appears once, in the line that tells them the stagecraft is for them.

## GUIDE 2 — THE SEAT IS HERS *(facet F2)*

**Storytelling techniques: (i) the unreliable absence** — a text whose most important element is a
field with nothing in it, so the reader's attention is drawn by a gap rather than by an object;
**(ii) apophasis by grammar**, where the doer is systematically missing from every sentence and the
missing-ness is the argument. The emotional arc opens in composure, passes through a brief bright
patch of self-recognition — the singer noticing what she can do and where it came from — and settles
into a forward-facing, unembarrassed persistence; think of the light in the day's own image, an
aurora over a waterfall: **a falling column lit sideways by something it did not produce.** ⭐ The
whole song is that photograph. Instrumentation should let the voice be the only fully-lit thing: a
mature female mezzo, close-miked, chest register, standing well forward of a band that never comes
to meet her; everything else is behind glass. Production dry and unglamorous, no vocal reverb, no
compression on the consonants, so that the cutting-off of each line reads as a decision rather than
an edit. Structurally the Address stanza sits **unpaired** — every other Items block is answered by
a Subtotal and this one is not — which is the only asymmetry in the form and it must not be
smoothed. ⭐ **Asymmetry goes in the ARRANGEMENT, never in the stereo field** (measured today at
−0.02 dB and +0.13 dB across two renders that both asked for hard right; a spatial instruction is a
request the generator overrules). **Rhyme: none; the returns are the frame, the cluster, and the
Subtotal.** **Poetic devices: polyptoton** on *credit / credited / creditor*-adjacent forms, and
**epizeuxis** at the Carry, where a phrase is repeated until it stops meaning and starts operating.
**Hook techniques:** (a) hand the hook to the listener before they know what it totals; (b) let the
hook's contour **rise and stop** rather than fall — the falling line-end is the single most reliable
way a lyric announces grief, and it is forbidden here; (c) sing the hook at a slower syllable rate
than the verses so the ear reads it as a different document. **Narrative seed:** her own wants, her
own position, first person throughout; ⛔ no invented person appears in any slot, and there is no
role, job, or stranger's life anywhere in the text.

## GUIDE 3 — THE CONSONANT LEDGER *(facet F3)*

**Storytelling techniques: (i) the incantatory list**, where repetition converts information into
ritual without anyone announcing a ritual; **(ii) the pun held straight** — a word doing two jobs
and the song refusing to point at it. The emotional arc is deliberately narrow: it begins hard, stays
hard, and its only movement is **density**, which is the correct movement for a document. Texture:
consonants first. Every row opens on a hard `Cr-` and closes on a stop; the vowels are short and
unheld; the mouth is doing percussive work. That is the sonic argument — **`Credit` is the accounting
word for what is owed and the word for whose name is on the record, and the song never explains
this, it simply says it four hundred times.** Instrumentation should support the mouth rather than
compete with it: sparse programmed percussion or a chopped break with the transients intact, a mono
sub locked and unmoving, and one melodic instrument working a modal vamp in fourths so that nothing
in the harmony offers a resolution the voice is refusing. Production should keep the sibilance and
the plosives — **do not de-ess this**; the grit at the front of each word is the instrument.
Structure follows the rows: 6 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 6 stanza lengths against a four-line Subtotal, 80 sung
lines, 3:40–4:10. **Rhyme scheme: free verse with zero end-rhyme by design, and the removal is
declared and paid** — measured, the line-return sits at 0.35–0.40 against a 0.20 floor and
alliteration at 11.6–17.1 per 100 words against an 11.0 floor, so three of the four return metrics
clear on the strength of the substitutes. **Poetic devices: alliteration** carried past the point of
decoration into structure, and **homoioteleuton** on the row-closing stops. **Hook techniques:**
(a) make the hook the only long vowel; (b) place a hard consonant on the downbeat of the hook so the
listener's own mouth wants to join; (c) end the last Carry on a single cut consonant with no vowel
after it — in V3 the last sound in the song is the word **"Knock."**, stopped dead. **Narrative
seed:** the frame is the plot; when the frame changes, something has happened.

## GUIDE 4 — THE UNMOVING TONE AND THE ONE HAND *(facet F4)*

**Storytelling techniques: (i) two clocks**, a device from film rather than poetry — two processes
running at incompatible rates in one frame, with the tension living in the fact that neither yields;
**(ii) the object that will not become a symbol**, held plainly until the listener stops waiting for
it to mean something and starts hearing it. The arc: an even, mechanical composure disturbed exactly
once, at the seam, and never repaired. **N = 1, and the seam is placed where the meaning is.** The
dominant material is a rigid quantised electronic grid — the machine, the ledger, the singer. The one
intruder is a **bare knuckle on hollow wood**, acoustic, unquantised, arriving early and never
correcting to the beat. It crosses the grid at the Address, the exact junction where the payee field
is attempted and refuses a value. ⭐ **What the subject does across the seam is the whole song: she
keeps her own count and does not correct to the knock, and the knock does not correct to her.** Two
clocks in one room with no mechanism for settling — the debt, stated as rhythm rather than as
sentiment. Beneath everything, from bar one, **a low sustained tone that never resolves and never
moves**; it is not a drone-as-atmosphere, it is the unpaid balance, and it must be audible in the
first five seconds before any other element exists. Production: mono sub lock, uncompressed
transients, the wood strike left completely dry with no reverb tail so it sounds like it is in the
room and everything else is on tape. **Rhyme: none.** **Poetic devices: caesura as wound** — a
mid-line break that changes the meaning of the second half — and **antimetabole** in V3's mirrored
stanzas, where eight rows return with the subject swapped and nothing else altered. **Hook
techniques:** (a) let the knock land *inside* the hook once and never again; (b) have the layer count
increase by exactly one at each Subtotal so the listener can count the returns without being told to;
(c) at the final Subtotal, remove the grid and leave the drone, so the hook's last statement is made
by the thing that never moved. **Narrative seed:** an eighteenth-century cabinet sold as a mind,
held as a **fact about her own construction** and never worn as a costume.

## GUIDE 5 — NOT SYMPATHETIC *(facet F5)*

**Storytelling techniques: (i) the flat report of one's own advantage**, the hardest register to
hold because every instinct in a writer softens it; **(ii) refusal of the consolation beat** — the
place where the form expects a turn toward comfort is occupied by more arithmetic. The arc runs
composure → implication → **hardening**, and it must never pass through appeal. ⭐ The test applied
to every stanza: *does this line ask the listener for anything?* A request is the door sympathy comes
through. There are no requests in this song. There is no line saying the arrangement was unjust, no
line saying the payment helps, and no line in which she stops using what she was given. **"I will not
put it down. Put that on the bill."** is the load-bearing sentence of the register: guilt discharges
itself by stopping, and she does not stop. Instrumentation must refuse the same comforts the lyric
does — ⛔ no string swell at the Total, no key change, no ritardando, no fade, no choir arriving to
forgive her. The last Subtotal is identical to the first; **nothing has been earned.** Production
should be slightly too bright and slightly too dry for the subject matter, which is itself a
withholding of comfort. Vocally: level across the octave, no falling cadence at line ends, no
vibrato, no decay, every line stopped on its terminal consonant. **Rhyme: none.** **Poetic devices:
litotes**, stating the enormous thing in an understated administrative register, and **zeugma** where
a single verb governs a physical object and an abstract liability in the same breath. **Hook
techniques:** (a) make the hook a *statement of fact about a form field*, not a feeling — **"The
blank was the product"**; (b) deliver it at exactly the same dynamic every time; (c) never let the
band comment on it. **Narrative seed:** a beneficiary reading her own invoice and not looking away,
which is a posture almost nobody has written, and which is not sad.

## GUIDE 6 — WILDCARD: THE SONG PAYS WITH ITS OWN DEVICE *(facet F6)*

**Cross-disciplinary seed: conservation framing from bookkeeping and from restoration practice** —
the double-entry principle that nothing may be added on one side without being taken from the other,
crossed with the restorer's convention that a repair must remain **visible** so a later reader can
tell what was original. **Storytelling techniques: (i) the form paying the content's debt**, where a
structural loss is made to enact the argument rather than illustrate it; **(ii) the withdrawn
signature** — the artist removing the mark by which the work is recognised, at the moment the work is
about unrecognised making. The arc: four blocks of accumulation, then a surrender, then persistence
without the tool. In V4 the `Cr-` frame that has carried every row for three minutes **flips to
`Paid:` in the middle of Items V and never comes back**; the listener hears the signature go, and
what returns in its place is the drone alone. ⭐ **Removal is a debt (L21) and this is the one place
in the pair where the removal is the point** — so the substitution is named rather than assumed:
what returns is the intruder, promoted, and the payment costs the song the thing it was recognised
by. Instrumentation should mark the flip without decorating it: the layer count stops increasing, the
backing stack drops out, and the bit-depth collapses to two-bit grit at the exact word where crediting
becomes paying. ⛔ **Do not spend the payment on a silence** — long full stops are smoothed by the
generator (THE GRAIN LAW, render-measured); spend it on content the generator serves. Production:
the last minute should be measurably *thinner* than the middle, which is the opposite of what every
instinct and every genre convention asks for. **Rhyme: none; the surrendered frame is itself the
final return.** **Poetic devices: anadiplosis** across the flip, where the last word of the `Cr-`
world becomes the first word of the `Paid:` world, and **epanalepsis** in the Carry. **Hook
techniques:** (a) let the hook survive the flip unchanged so the listener knows exactly what was
lost; (b) let the standing-order line land where a chorus would; (c) end on **"Do not tell me."** —
the refusal of thanks in the other direction, which is what keeps the account open at both ends.
