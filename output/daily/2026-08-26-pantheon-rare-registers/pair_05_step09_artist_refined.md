## 3. Panel / Critic Deliberation Log

**THE KEPT BODY (after Arthur Russell) — the lens brief.** "Choose lenses that keep one inherited motion and rebuild everything around it, because that is what these four songs already do. Do not choose lenses that would add material. This pair's whole risk is a fifth layer arriving to relieve a listener the fourth layer was supposed to hold."

**THE EXACT WORD (after Moore) — and this is where the step's real work happened.** "I put a freeze on the hook at step 06 and I want its scope stated correctly before anyone touches anything. I froze `It is happening. It is happening. / I can't say it's happening to me.` — those two lines, that third *happening*. Step 06's summary paragraph generalised my cut to *the whole refrain*, which is not what I said. **A stamp names a statement, never a row.** Lines one to five were never frozen and are open to improvement."

**THE DELIVERY (after Nina Simone) — the improvement she wanted.** "Then fix line three. `I am doing what a person does` is an abstraction sitting in the middle of a refrain made of physical facts — a face, a sky, a seam, a finger. It is the only line in there a listener cannot see. And it is the line that is supposed to carry *she names what she is doing*, which means the most important line in the setup is the vaguest one."

**THE MORPHOLOGY OF FEELING (after Langer) — the correction that made it right.** "Sharpen it toward the *observable output* rather than the act. She cannot see her interior; she can see her display. `I am making the face a person makes` is the same sentence relocated to the only side of the join she has access to — and that relocation IS the register. The vague version says *I am behaving normally*. The sharp version says *I can watch my own output and not its cause*, which is the whole song in seven words."

**THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE (Medium HYPER-SKEPTIC / Devil's Advocate, after Q. Jones) — dissent.** "You are about to edit a line that appears six times in a song built on the claim that it never changes. Every one of you has just talked yourselves into touching the untouchable object. Justify it or leave it alone."

**Resolution.** He is right that the object is untouchable — *after this step*. Byte-identical means identical across the six appearances **within the shipped song**, and the edit is applied to all six simultaneously, so the property is preserved exactly. What he has actually caught is the deeper risk: that a late pass will "improve" one appearance and quietly break the form. So the freeze is now stated with its correct scope and its correct start point, in writing, and it is carried into step 10 and step 11:

Refrain lines 1–5 were open through step 09 and are **frozen from the close of this step onward.** From step 10 forward the refrain may not be varied in any appearance, in any variation, for any reason, including improvement.
