## ⭐ STEP 2 — WHAT EACH LENS CHANGED (minimal edits; additions over removals)

### V1 · THE SOUND ENGINEER WITH ONE GOOD EAR
> *"You've built the biggest moment in the record out of a stack. A stack is one person eight times. Put a room on it, ten under what you think, and stop straightening the doubles — the smear is the arrangement, not a fault in it."*

**Changed:** the third-chorus maximal wall is now **crowd-height through a short mirror plate** rather than a studio stack, and the doubles are explicitly **misaligned twenty-five milliseconds and left uncorrected.** ⛔ `no live room` became `no live drum room`, because the old wording would have excluded the very thing she added — the EXCLUDE AUDIT catching itself in the act.
**Kept:** everything else, including the sidechain device and the invariant sub.
**Lyric:** one word — *Second in the line* → ***Still** second in the line.* The queue has not moved in three minutes and now the lyric knows it.

### V2 · THE PERCUSSIONIST WHO WON'T USE A CLICK
> *"The list narrows and your hats double, fine. But they double on the beat they're told to. Nobody excited is on time. Let them arrive a hair early every single time and don't pull them back."*

**Changed:** the kit is now **dragged eight to fourteen milliseconds behind the bass and never quantised back**, and the doubling hat **arrives a hair early every time.** The percussion-grid consequence is now a *human* mutation rather than a programmed one — which makes the one bar of engineered silence land harder, because it is the only perfectly placed event in the song.
**Kept:** the note-identical bass walk, the flat one-note delivery, the wrong-side-of-the-bar re-entry.

### V3 · THE 5AM REGULAR
> *"You've got somebody across a pool looking at her. Then you mixed the answer voice right up against her ear. That's not the room, that's her own head. Put it back where the person actually is. I'll tell you if it moves me from over here."*

**Changed:** the answering voice is **set back at room distance** instead of stacked close. ⭐ **This is the single best edit in the pair** — the witness now *sounds like* a witness, at the far end of the tiles, and the lead's step-nearer-not-louder decision suddenly has something to be near *to.* The A/B test she runs — *does it move a person who never asks for anything* — is also the Q. Jones one-instrument test wearing different clothes, and the hook survives it.
**Lyric:** the Restart's closing two lines were reflective, which is the archive's register, not mine. Replaced with **appetite pointed at the future** — the thing that was measured absent from this whole specification and is now the seat's forward-facing half.

### V4 · THE HORN PLAYER WHO IS ALWAYS LATE
> *"Eight bars in. Four notes. Gone. Don't write me a part for the whole song, I won't be there for it."*

**Changed:** a **four-note clipped figure enters eight bars in, plays once, and never returns.** ⭐ It is not decoration: the record's whole subject is one person arriving, being present for a very short time, and leaving — and now the arrangement does that too, eight bars in, without a single word commenting on it. **The band does not know what the lyric is doing, and this is the one place it accidentally agrees.**
**Kept:** negative space, the missing third note arriving alone in the last bar, the over-sung answer stack.
**Lyric:** *the one doing it* → *whoever was doing it* — no biography, better mouth-feel.
