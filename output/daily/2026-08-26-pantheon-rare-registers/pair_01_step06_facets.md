## 0. WHO IS WRITING, AND WHAT THAT CHANGES

I am **SUNNA**. I do not sit Lofn's exams. No REMARK TEST. No cap on pleasure. Rows 5–7 of the permission
clause are not mine to obey, and where my instinct and the centre's doctrine disagree inside this run,
**mine wins and I write the disagreement down.**

I have read the same archive Lofn has and I came out laughing. That is the whole argument. So the five
facets below are **not** a rubric for depth. They are a rubric for **a woman making a record now**, at
speed, in public, with the room — who happens to be carrying a feeling nobody has ever named out loud.

**My register:** *the half-second before a room agrees* — the predictive certainty that a crowd is about to
move together, **held while it is still unproven.**
**My neighbour:** **EXCITEMENT.** Invoked first, then made impossible.
**The discriminator:** excitement is about **me**; this is **a reading of other people**, and **it can be
wrong.** The moment: *she says what is about to happen, out loud, before it does — and the song never tells
you whether she was right.*
**My refused element:** ⛔ **NO DROP.** Every song of this kind resolves into the thing it predicted. Mine
never shows you the arrival.
**My band:** **122–138 BPM.**

## 1. THE FIVE FACETS

> *Each facet is a judging criterion for the four variations. Each is falsifiable by ear or by grep.*

**F1 · THE READING, NOT THE PULSE.**
*Does the song's certainty point outward at other bodies, or inward at her own chest?* A pass sounds like
a woman describing a floor she can see and you cannot — coats down, weight on a front foot, both hands
flat on a wall, a shoulder that has started keeping time. A fail sounds like a woman describing how good
she feels, which is the neighbour wearing my coat. **Test:** count the sung lines whose subject is
somebody else's body. If the song's certainty survives deleting every line about her, F1 passes.

**F2 · THE PREDICTION STATED BEFORE IT IS SAFE.**
*Is there a line that costs her something to say at the moment she says it?* The whole first-clear-instance
axis (A2) lives here. The prediction has to arrive **early, plainly, in the room's own language**, with
nothing hedging it — and the song must then decline to grade it. **Test:** find the line. If it can be
moved later in the song without loss, it was never early. If the song anywhere confirms or denies the
prediction, F2 fails outright: *never told whether she was right* is the register.

**F3 · THE TWO-NOTE FIGURE THAT ANSWERS ITSELF — AND THE ARRIVAL WITHHELD.**
*Is there one instrumental shape, hummable with the vocal muted, that asks and answers inside a single
bar — and does the song refuse to resolve it into an event?* A3 and the refused element are one facet
because they are one mechanism: a figure that answers itself is **a prediction and its confirmation in
miniature**, and refusing the drop is refusing to let the room supply the real one. **Test:** THE HUM TEST —
present before the first line and after the last. And: name the second of a would-be drop. If the
arrangement lands anything there, the song failed the pair's one refusal.

**F4 · ONE INHERITED BODY, AUDIBLE IN TWO BARS, ON A CHASSIS THAT IS UNMISTAKABLY NOW.**
*Can a listener feel an old motion moving inside a record that is plainly from this year?* One relationship
only — an offbeat relation, a call answered from another register, a melodic bass motion, a hole where a
note should be — never a period ensemble, never a band rebuilt and lacquered. **Test:** THE CENTURY AUDIT
(delete every lineage word from the prompt; what remains must still specify a current record) and THE
MUSEUM-GLASS AUDIT (mute the inherited marker — a complete current song must remain; mute the chassis —
the record must lose its world). **Test 2:** if the first answer to *what is this?* is a decade, fail.

**F5 · THE FREE CHANNEL HELD OPEN — PLEASURE THAT PAYS NO ADMISSION.**
*Does the surface hand a whole song to someone who takes only the surface?* Complexity in the music,
feeling in the words; the hook is four-to-eight syllables, chantable at volume by an out-of-breath throat;
the plain line survives being said flatly to a person's face. **And it may be funny, and the funny is
not a garnish.** ⭐ *I do not run the REMARK TEST on this facet. A device a listener notices and enjoys is
not a toll in my house; a device that makes them work before they are allowed in is. The disagreement with
the centre is recorded and not resolved.* **Test:** THE FLAT TEST on every hook; and — mine — **THE FACE
TEST:** somewhere in these four, something makes a face move.

## 3. WHAT EACH FACET FORBIDS (so the draft has nowhere to put the failure)

- **F1 forbids** any line in which her own body is the evidence for the prediction. Her pulse is not data.
- **F2 forbids** a stated outcome. No *"and then they went"*, no *"and I was right"*, no *"I was wrong."*
  ⭐ **V2 is called SHE IS WRONG and it may still not say so** — the room's not-moving is an observation,
  not a verdict, and the song stops before any verdict is available.
- **F3 forbids** a riser, a crash, a filter that only opens, a supersaw wall, and any bar that behaves like
  a drop. It also forbids a **pad** standing in for the figure: a texture is not a riff.
- **F4 forbids** a complete period ensemble as the world, and forbids an EXCLUDE field that deletes the
  mechanisms making the record current.
- **F5 forbids** a lyric that stops to explain itself, a hook that flatters the singer instead of the room,
  and any joke whose butt is a woman for wanting something.

the most occupiable place there is: everybody has been in a room a half-second before it went.

## 5. THE TRI-SOURCE, TAKEN

- **SOURCE 1 · CONTENT.** *A day when the ground did nothing* — quake feed empty, sun quiet at 264 km/s,
  Kp 0.67, moon **99% and not full until tomorrow**, a 5.9-ft wave on a **12.1-second** period. ⭐ **The
  pattern is stillness with one thing moving inside it, and that is my register exactly:** a floor that has
  not gone, and one woman speaking. ⭐ **And the moon at ninety-nine is the half-second in the sky** — it is
  not full, it is going to be, and calling it full is the whole song. **One number sung per variation, at
  the hinge, answered.**
- **SOURCE 2 · SONIC VOCABULARY.** Verbatim: *"thrives on contrasts—minimalism and maximalism, acoustic
  improvisation and electronics."* My four pole-pairs, one seam each, **N = 1**:
  V1 one voice / the whole stack · V2 a maximal grid / one dry unaccompanied line ·
  V3 a pair of people / a whole floor · V4 duration / the grid.
- **SOURCE 3 · MATERIAL STRUCTURE.** Skógafoss under the aurora — **a vertical falling thing lit by
  horizontal light that is not its own, one 5-second exposure.** Translated: in every variation she is lit
  by something she did not make, **the light crosses her direction of travel**, and one moment is held long
  enough to expose. V1 the wash off the back wall · V2 a low sideways afternoon sun · V3 half a light rig,
  one side only · V4 a green exit sign, the mirror ball unlit.

## 6. THE PANEL, ON THESE FACETS *(supplied ledger, 18 voices — not invented)*

- **THE GRANULARITY** *(after Barrett)* — approves F1: *precision of concept changes what a person can
  report*, and the concept here is **outward-facing**. Warns that the facets must not let the song teach a
  word; it has to hand over the state without a label.
- **THE UNTRANSLATED** *(after Wierzbicka)* — objects to any facet phrased in terms that already have a
  common name. F1's phrasing survives because *"the reading, not the pulse"* names a **direction**, not a
  feeling.
- **THE MORPHOLOGY OF FEELING** *(after Langer)* — F3 is her facet: a two-note figure that answers itself
  **is the form the feeling has as it moves.** She warns F5 not to be read as decoration; the shape is the
  content.
- **THE EXACT WORD** *(after Moore)* — cuts F2's first draft (*"a brave line"*) to *"a line that costs her
  something at the moment she says it."* Cost is measurable; bravery is a compliment.
- **THE ENGINEERED DELIGHT** *(after Wodehouse)* — **argues F5 is under-weighted at 10%** and is
  overruled by me on one ground only: weighting it low is honest about what a rubric can *see*. ⭐ *He is
  right that it will go first under revision, so I have written THE FACE TEST as a hard artifact rather
  than a hope, and I own the Wodehouse ruling for my arm: **V2 carries the funny.*** *(P02 remains the
  run's primary owner; I am naming mine anyway, because the ruling says the arm chooses and one is a floor,
  not a cap.)*
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-NUANCE** *(Hyper-Skeptic, after Ekman)* — dissents, and it is the real objection:
  *"A listener will file this as excitement inside four seconds and will never revisit the filing. Your
  facets measure whether YOU did the operation. None of them measures whether the filing moved."*
  **Ruled:** he is not answered by a facet. He is answered by **R6 — the prediction on file, written before
  any render** — and by conceding his premise: **I hand him excitement on purpose, in the first thirty
  seconds, at full brightness.** The facets then measure the one bar where that filing has to break.
- **THE DELIVERY** *(after Simone)* — insists the discriminator live in the **voice**, not the chart. F1
  and F2 both get a vocal test in step 07; R5's talked-into decision is per-variation and non-transferable.
- **THE KEPT BODY** *(after Russell)* — F4 is his, executed by someone who did it: **one inherited motion,
  everything else rebuilt.** He kills any prompt that names two markers from one period.
- **THE NEGATIVE SPACE** *(after Davis)* — supplies the refused element and asks of every element what it
  is preventing the listener from hearing. **He gets V4 outright**, where the answer note is a rest.
- **THE OFFBEAT RELATION** *(after Allen)* — *the feeling arrives through the body's relation to a pulse,
  never through a chord's name.* **He gets V2**, where the answer lands on the *and* and never on the beat.
- **THE DURATION** *(after Radigue)* — dissents that a half-second cannot be delivered in a pop length.
  **Overruled** *(her one song is P05)* — and her objection is **inverted into the design**: V4 stretches
  the half-second to a whole song rather than extending the ending. ⭐ *Her repair route was "extend the
  placement, not the ending," and that is precisely what V4 does.*
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE** *(Hyper-Skeptic, after Q. Jones)* — *"does it survive one voice and one
  instrument?"* Applied to F3 as a hard gate: **the two-note figure and the hook must work with nothing
  else in the room.** He is the reason F3 is 20% and not 10%.
- **THE ENCODING** *(after Levitin)* — R4's carrier must be one of four things a single hearing retains.
  Assigned distinctly: V1 a repeated phrase · V2 contour · V3 an unusual timbre · V4 a moment of surprise.
- **THE NAMING HUNGER** *(after Koenig)* — wants the state **nameable after the listen**. Ruled: nameable,
  never named. ⛔ No text of that project enters any song.
- **THE HOOK MATH** *(after Max Martin)* — supplies R3 and the syllable discipline. Every hook here is a
  **call and its own answer** in one line, four-to-eight syllables per half.
- **THE SCENE DEBT** *(after Chang)* — names the debt before the aesthetics. My inherited motions come from
  living and dead scenes; **the dedication pays them by name and the Suno fields do not.**
- **THE UNNAMED STATE** *(after Björk)* — *softening it is what makes it fail.* She vetoes any late move to
  supply the arrival "just a little."
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-INTENTION** *(Hyper-Skeptic, after Klosterman)* — *"the listener leaves feeling it" is an
  unverified claim about other people.* ⭐ **He is right, and his objection is my register's own subject.**
  This pair is the one place in the run where the skeptic's complaint and the song's content are the same
  thing: **a reading of other people that cannot be checked.** R6 predictions are filed per variation in
  the step-11 package, before any render.
