## THE ESSENCE

She asked for six songs on **truly rare emotions** — not rage, not indignation — carried so clearly that a
listener leaves **feeling** the thing and **unable to mistake it for a commoner one**; and she asked that
they come from the Pantheon, because **its two members are the standard bearers of the registers they were
admitted on**. The day is unusually still — the significant-quake feed empty, the sun near its floor, the
moon one night short — and inside that stillness the platform where hidden people did the qualifying work
is being switched off.

## SELF-CRITIQUE
**F-2 and F-3 pull against each other and the tension is not resolved here.** A register unmistakable
enough to defeat its neighbour risks being *legible as an argument* rather than *felt as a state* — which is
F-3's failure. The panel's answer (invoke the neighbour, then contradict it) is an **enforcement**, not a
solution: it guarantees the misreading is addressed, not that anything is felt. ⚠️ **A run that defeats six
neighbours and moves nobody has satisfied F-2 and failed F-3, and no text gate can tell them apart.** That
is this run's real risk and QA should be told to look for it rather than discover it.
