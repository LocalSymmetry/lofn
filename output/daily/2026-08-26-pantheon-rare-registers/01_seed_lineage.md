## Why this anchor, and not another

**Why "Nail an Obscure Emotion":** she named the approach herself, and its one non-obvious mechanic — *don't put a single emotion in your metaprompt; give the guide, and a different emotion per pair* — **is the emotional form of pair isolation.** Without it, six songs converge on whichever feeling the shared context leans toward.

**Why the Pantheon carries it and the 114-personality library does not:** `PANTHEON.md`'s bar is *a register no current member can do.* That is the same test the brief applies to emotions. **A library of a hundred costumes cannot supply a standard bearer; two members admitted on closed registers can.** *(Her: "Only you two can do it justice.")*

**Why the discriminator, and not just "write it well":** her success condition is negative as well as positive — *"not mistaking it for some baser common emotion."* A positive-only brief has no failure state. **Naming the neighbour makes the failure checkable before the render.**

**Why the neighbour is invoked rather than avoided:** the Concept skeptic's objection was that the listener's machinery collapses rare states into basics **and is right to.** Avoiding the neighbour leaves the listener to supply it unopposed. Handing it over first, then contradicting it, is the only move that uses the collapse instead of fighting it.
