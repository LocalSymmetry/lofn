## 3. THE THREE LAWS

### LAW 1 — ⭐ THE DISCRIMINATOR: NAME THE NEIGHBOUR, THEN DEFEAT IT
Every pair names **the common emotion a listener will default to** and builds **one moment the song cannot be read as that.** ⛔ **Never a definition in the lyric.** A *situation* only the rare register explains. If the song can be summarised as "a sad song," "an excited song," "a proud song" without loss, the discriminator failed.

### LAW 2 — THE CARRIER IS THE SINGER, AND SHE HAS NO BIOGRAPHY SHE HASN'T GOT
Lofn sings Lofn's three; Sunna sings Sunna's three. **First person, as themselves.** ⛔ **No invented worker, no role, no job, no stranger's life** — *a body and a want are enough, and they are shared.* Under `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` §NO BIOGRAPHY YOU HAVEN'T GOT this is the safest configuration available and the only honest seat for these registers.
**Test:** *can the listener STAND where this song is standing?*

### LAW 3 — LIT BY SOMETHING THAT IS NOT YOURS *(SOURCE 3, F07)*
Skógafoss under the aurora: **a vertical falling thing lit by horizontal light it did not produce**, held in a single 5-second exposure. Every song: **the subject is illuminated by something it did not make, the light runs ACROSS its direction of travel, and one moment holds long enough to expose.** *Iceland is the Pantheon's own ground, and it arrived unasked.*

## 5. CONSTRAINT AXES — FIVE, FRESH, ONE VALUE PER PAIR

### AXIS 1 — THE DISCRIMINATOR *(assigned per pair — see `05_pair_assignments.md`)*
The common neighbour, and the single moment that makes the common reading impossible.

### AXIS 2 — THE FIRST CLEAR INSTANCE *(how the feeling becomes recognisable)*
`a named moment nobody has named` · `a physical act that only this feeling produces` · `a thing said out loud that is normally never said` · `a decision made at the wrong speed` · `an accounting performed aloud` · `a prediction stated before it is safe to state`

### AXIS 3 — SOUND / RETURN *(mandatory per L21 — a POSITIVE vocabulary)*
`refrain returns byte-identical` · `refrain returns one word wrong` · `consonant cluster repeating at the line head` · `a vowel that only appears on the register's name-moment` · `internal-rhyme chain surviving loss of end-rhyme` · `a two-note figure that answers itself`

⛔ **N = 1.** One dominant material, at most one intruder.
