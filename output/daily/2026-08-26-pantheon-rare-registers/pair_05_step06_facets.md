## 3. Panel / Critic Deliberation Log

*The three Devil's Advocate / Hyper-Skeptic seats supplied by the ICB are used as supplied — THE ANTI-NUANCE (Concept), THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE (Medium), THE ANTI-INTENTION (Context & Marketing). No panel was invented. Each Devil's Advocate genuinely dissents below and each dissent changed the artifact.*

**THE ANTI-NUANCE (Concept HYPER-SKEPTIC / Devil's Advocate, after Ekman) — opening objection.**
"You have been handed the one register in this run that is a *hair* off a basic emotion. A listener hears a slow song, borrowed light, a large sky, a woman singing about being under it — they will file AWE inside eight seconds and they will not revoke it. Whatever you do at 3:10 is decoration on a label already applied."

**THE MORPHOLOGY OF FEELING (after Langer) — counter.**
"He is right about the label and wrong about the remedy. The song does not have to *avoid* the shape of awe; it has to present a form awe does not have. Awe's form is a single vector — outward, upward, one direction. This state's form is a vector with a **failed return path**: something goes out and nothing comes back to confirm it left from here. That is a shape, it is articulable in sound, and it is not awe's shape. Put the failed return in the *form* — a refrain that comes back unchanged while the world around it empties — and the label revokes itself."

**THE EXACT WORD (after Moore) — cut.**
"Then the refrain must not contain a single word that could be swapped for a near-synonym without loss. `It is happening. It is happening. / I can't say it's happening to me.` — the third *happening* is the whole song. Do not vary it, do not elegantly re-word it, do not let a later pass improve it into two different verbs."

**THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE (Medium HYPER-SKEPTIC, after Q. Jones) — the condition on the grant.**
"You have been given the run's one repetition licence and I want the receipt. Duration may build the room; it may not be the furniture. Sing me that refrain with one instrument and no processing. If it does not stand up, the six returns are a stall and you should say so instead of defending them."

**THE DURATION (after Radigue) — the grant's owner.**
"He is asking the right question of the wrong object. The refrain is not a hook floating on duration; it is a **cell** — one falling three-note figure said seven times with different words. A cell survives one voice and one instrument by construction, and the length is what lets a listener stop resisting it. But I concede his test: write the cell, not a tune, or he is right."

**THE ENCODING (after Levitin) — the thirty seconds.**
"A single hearing retains four things. You get the refrain first, which is a repeated phrase, and I want one more: an **unusual timbre** inside the first two seconds. Not at 0:30. At 0:01."

**THE ANTI-INTENTION (Marketing HYPER-SKEPTIC, after Klosterman) — the standing objection.**
"'The listener leaves holding the rare one' is an unverified claim about other people's interiors. File the prediction before the render or the whole exercise is a mood board with a rubric stapled to it."

**THE SCENE DEBT (after Chang) — the credit.**
"If you raise a one-string Icelandic drone as the load-bearing instrument, you name it and you name who kept it alive. The instrument is not scenery."

**Resolution — what changed because of the objections:**

1. **Ekman's objection is not answerable by production, so it moved into the lyric.** The discriminator is the last line of the refrain, sung six times: `I can't say it's happening to me.` Facet 1 tests it, and tests it *as the sung hook*, not as an intention.
2. **Q. Jones's condition became Facet 2, written as a strip test with a stated pass condition** — and the honest answer is recorded rather than assumed. The refrain was rebuilt as a repeated three-note falling cell specifically so the test can pass; before that rebuild it was recitative and it would have failed.
3. **Levitin's demand moved the doubled source to bar one.** The first sound in every variation is a single acoustic gesture that arrives twice, ninety milliseconds apart, and never locks — the register audible as timbre before a word is sung.
4. **Radigue's cell/tune distinction is now a named production fact**, not a hope.
5. **Klosterman's objection is Facet 5's second half and `## The Prediction` in the step-11 package.**
6. **Chang's objection is the dedication**, measured at 492 characters, naming the langspil and the revival that rebuilt it, with the honesty clause both ways (nothing is sampled; the instrument was asked for by name).
7. **Moore's cut is binding on every later step:** the refrain is frozen from Step 08 onward and may be improved only by being left alone.
