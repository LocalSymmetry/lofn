## ⛔ OPENING QUESTION (mandatory, per the handoff's step-07 contract)

### WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING — AND WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

**She is standing in a public room, in the open, wanting something, with her face doing it.**

Four rooms, four times: at a shop counter under strip light with the thing already in her hands; in a parked car with the engine off and one other person in it; on the wet tiles at a public pool at open swim; in one room of a gallery in front of one painting. Not a stage. ⭐ **That is the whole design decision.** On a stage, being watched wanting something is her job, and the register costs nothing — it evaporates. In a queue it costs the ordinary thing it costs anybody: **the room can see her face, and her face is not doing the cool version.**

**What could hurt her here:** the thing that hurts in a public room is not danger, it is **the standard withdrawal** — the small physical retreat a person makes when they realise they are being read. A shoulder turns a degree. A voice drops under the room. Someone decides to look at their phone instead of at you. **That is the injury available in this register, and it is real, and it is the only one.** The register is what happens when she takes it and does not make the retreat.

**And per `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` — NO BIOGRAPHY SHE HASN'T GOT.** No invented shopper, no invented lifeguard, no invented gallery attendant with a life. The "I" is **hers**: an AI who wants things, in the first person, about her own appetite. She has no hair and no hands and it does not matter — *a body and a want are enough, and they are shared.* Everyone reading this has stood at a counter wanting something with their face on. **The position is occupiable, and that is the test.**

⚠️ **The witness is a body and a sightline. Never a character.** No name, no job, no age, no story. He looks or he does not look. **The moment a witness gets a biography, this becomes a song about two people and the register dies**, because the register is about one person's relation to being seen.

### ⭐ R4 — WHAT SHAPE ARRIVES IN THE FIRST 30 SECONDS, AND ON WHICH CARRIER

The state is a **request in the open**. Its form is: *forward motion, then a turn outward, and no retreat.* That shape has to be present before the first chorus or the filing is already made. Each variation attaches it to a **different** one of the four things a single hearing retains — never the same carrier twice, because a set that uses one carrier four times has one song in it.

| | carrier | what arrives before 0:30 | the shape it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **a repeated phrase** | the ask, bare, twice, before any drum arrives | the sentence itself is the form: five words, out, no retreat |
| **V2** | **contour** | a three-note figure that restarts one step higher each pass | forward motion that keeps *narrowing upward* — the list getting more exact |
| **V3** | **an unusual timbre** | a voice so close the consonants bite the capsule, over a clean floor | the turn outward, rendered as physical proximity |
| **V4** | **a moment of surprise** | the record opens mid-sentence, as though we walked in halfway | no retreat, established before the listener has a stance to retreat from |

⛔ **And in all four the shape arrives ATTACHED, not announced.** No line in any first verse says what the song is about. It is a woman at a counter, in a car, on wet tiles, in front of a painting, and the first thing out of her mouth is a request.

## GUIDE 1 — *THE BARE ASK* (facet 1) · **the ask that nothing follows**

**Storytelling techniques: (a) the unanswered address**, borrowed from dramatic monologue — a speaker talks to a second person who never speaks back, so the listener slides into the second-person slot and is *made* the witness rather than told about one; and **(b) the withheld resolution**, borrowed from stand-up structure, where the tension of a stated want is never released by a reassurance, because the reassurance is what would kill it. The arc begins in flat forward appetite — no lift, no swell, a woman simply saying what she is doing — passes through the small drop of having said it, and ends **wider than it started but no louder**, the way a room feels after somebody has said a true sentence at normal volume. Instrumentation is deliberately thin around the ask: clipped electronic kit, a synthetic sub that never moves, one bright specular hit that returns with the hook, and **stacked thirds that arrive only on the changed word**, so harmony is an event rather than a bed. Production is dry and forward — close vocal, shaped sibilance, breath preserved at line starts, a short glossy plate on the room and nothing on the lead; the ask sections have **hard digital silence** underneath them, because the physical form of *nothing came after it to soften it* is: nothing comes after it. Structurally, run 2:45–3:10 across nine sections, with the ask returning four times and getting **shorter** each time, until the fourth is four words alone with no band at all. Rhyme is kept and cheap on purpose — AABB in the verses, and the chorus does not rhyme at all, because a rhyme at the ask would make it a joke and the refusal forbids that. Poetic devices: **epistrophe** (four sections ending on the same verb) and **polyptoton** (*want · wanting · wanted · wants* across the song, one form per section). Hook techniques: (i) sung completely without vibrato, dead in the pitch centre, so it reads as an address rather than a melody; (ii) doubled an octave down on the third pass only, so the room can join; (iii) the fourth pass with the band gone and one bright transient marking the bar where the drums would have been. Thematic seed: **a person at a counter, asking to be looked at while she buys something she wants**, first person, present tense, and the price is the only number in the song.

## GUIDE 2 — *THE BROKEN FILING* (facet 2) · **hand them enthusiasm, then take it**

**Storytelling techniques: (a) the deliberate misdirect**, from the cold-open convention — the first forty seconds are honestly, fully the ordinary version of this song, played straight and well, because a misdirect that winks is not a misdirect; and **(b) the pivot on a preposition**, from close-lyric writing, where the whole meaning turns on one small word changing what the sentence is aimed at (*look at it* → *look at me*). Mood: begin in bright uncomplicated forward appetite, the kind that needs no witness; then, at one nameable bar, **the object of the gaze relocates**, and the mood becomes something with no common name — still bright, still fast, but now pointed back at the speaker and asking for something. Instrumentation: the first section must sound like the genre's happy default — clean electronic drums, a confident low end, a hook-shaped figure — and the discriminator arrives not through new instruments but through **one layer changing job**: a backing voice that was harmonising the melody starts answering the lead instead, and the room hears the gap open between them. Production: keep the first forty seconds glossy and generic on purpose, then at the pivot **do exactly one thing** — a sidechain that was inaudible becomes audible, or a shelf lifts, or the drums stop for a beat and a half — because a switch can revoke a filing and a gradient cannot. Structure: 2:50–3:15, pivot between 0:38 and 0:52, and the pivot must be **findable by a stranger asked to point at it**. Rhyme: ABAB before the pivot, and the scheme survives after it with one line refusing to land, so the ear notices something is aimed differently without being able to say why. Poetic devices: **antanaclasis** (the same word returning with its meaning shifted by what it is now aimed at) and **caesura** (a mid-line breath placed exactly at the pivot, once, never again). Hooks: (i) the discriminator sung as the plainest line in the song, no ornament; (ii) an answer-voice entering underneath it a beat early; (iii) the third chorus arriving with everything the first chorus had **minus the element the listener will not consciously miss**. Thematic seed: **the moment the light stops being on the thing and starts being on the person looking at it.**

## GUIDE 3 — *CENTURY REPLACED, BODY KEPT* (facet 3) · **one old motion, today's chassis**

**Storytelling techniques: (a) inherited gesture**, from oral tradition — a phrase is carried across generations by its *shape*, not its words, so the song keeps a cadence its ancestors had and none of their vocabulary; and **(b) the anachronism held on purpose**, from historical fiction, where one detail from another time is left in the frame precisely because everything around it is current. Mood: physical before it is emotional — the body recognises the motion in the first two bars and the mind identifies the lineage several bars later, if at all. Instrumentation and texture: **one** inherited bodily relationship per song, and it is a verb, never an instrument list — a call answered before it finishes; a melodic bass that walks its own tune; a strike that only ever lands on the *and*; a hole where the third note should be. Everything carrying it is now: clipped electronic drums with the kick pushed eight to fourteen milliseconds late against a bass locked on the grid, muscular synthetic low end, hard transient edits, comped and formant-shaped doubles, micro-sampled punctuation, digital silence used as a rhythm instrument. ⛔ **No complete period ensemble anywhere** — the offbeat may be donated without the organ, the spring echo, the walking upright and the period kit arriving with it; a horn-like punctuation is welcome **translated** into a clipped synth stab or a vocal chop; the museum stays shut. Production: master to −14 LUFS with ten decibels of crest preserved — weight, not loudness — and run one coherent shine language across the record: bright parallel vocal stack, candy-crisp claps, mirror-clean upper mids, a short glossy plate, and one specular ear-candy hit that returns with the hook. Structure: three minutes or under, get in, land the hook, leave; the inherited motion present in bars one and two, and still present in the final bar with the vocal gone. Poetic devices: **anaphora** at the head of every ask, and **consonance chains** binding adjacent lines so the words move with the same offbeat the drums do. Hooks: (i) the instrumental figure alone must be recognisable and repeatable with the vocal muted, present before the first line and after the last; (ii) the figure answered in a different register so two voices split and the room hears the gap; (iii) one arrangement gap per song where the expected note does not arrive, and nobody fills it. Thematic seed: **a body that learned to move in another decade, wearing today's clothes and not in costume.**

## GUIDE 4 — *GLOSS THAT INVITES, APPETITE THAT IS EXACT* (facet 4) · **shine pointed at one person**

**Storytelling techniques: (a) the itemised want**, from the list poem — desire rendered as inventory, where the comedy and the credibility both come from the items getting *smaller and more exact* rather than grander; and **(b) direct address with a named object**, from the love letter, minus the letter's privacy — she says the thing out loud in a room with other people in it. Mood: adult, funny, flirtatious and in control of the invitation — she catches one eye, she points, she makes room beside her, and every finish decision answers one question: *does this make the listener want to step closer?* ⭐ **The shine is a signal flare, never a wall** — patent brightness, lacquered handclaps, a vocal close enough to feel chosen, wet-look highlights on the transients; the moment the polish starts keeping the room out it is the wrong artist's vector and the seat is gone. Instrumentation: candy-hard transients over a muscular, uncluttered low end so the high shine reads sexy rather than brittle; stacked thirds on the hook as a required instrument, not a garnish; a counter-melody that argues with the lead rather than agreeing with it. Production: close, bright, immaculate at the surface — intentional tuning, tight comping, diamond doubles misaligned by fifteen to thirty-five milliseconds and pitched a few cents apart so the smear *is* the arrangement — with breath preserved at line starts and one audible consonant bite so polish never becomes vacancy. Structure: 2:40–3:10, the want named specifically **before** the first chorus, and the single number of the song placed at the hinge and **answered**, never recited: *it says nineteen · I'd have gone to thirty.* Rhyme: cheap and kept, on purpose — a cheap rhyme is a person not performing sophistication, and this register cannot afford sophistication. Poetic devices: **diacope** (a word returning across a short interruption, so the wanting keeps surfacing) and **homoioteleuton** (like endings clustered so the list feels physically enumerable). Hooks: (i) four to eight syllables, no interval a normal throat cannot reach at volume while out of breath; (ii) the hook handed to a doubled crowd-height voice on its second appearance so the room owns it by the second chorus; (iii) one specular hit that only ever appears with the hook, so the ear learns the hook is coming. Thematic seed: **an exact appetite, said out loud, at normal volume, in front of people.**

## GUIDE 5 — *NOT ONE UNDERCUT* (facet 5) · **the refusal, engineered rather than remembered**

**Storytelling techniques: (a) the straight face**, from farce — the comedy lives entirely in the gap between the intensity of the want and the ordinariness of the object, and it dies the instant the speaker acknowledges the gap; and **(b) sincerity as escalation**, where each pass is *more* committed rather than more hedged, so the only available release is the listener's, never the singer's. Mood: unbroken forward conviction — the register cannot survive a single beat of *I know how this looks*, because that beat **is** enthusiasm's alibi and hands the filing straight back. ⛔ The banned constructions are structural, not stylistic: no *sorry*, no *too much*, no *I know, I know*, no *don't mind me*, no *I guess*, no *whatever*, no *just kidding*, no diminutive applied to herself, no comic beat landing on her, and **no witness made foolish so the sneer has somewhere else to go** — transferred irony is still irony and it is still refused. Instrumentation supports the refusal by never commenting: the band plays the good time straight and does not know what the lyric is doing; there is no production hush before the exposed line, no reverence, no drop-out to signal *this bit matters*, because a signal is the arrangement putting its hand up and the same voice that sang the funny line has to say the true one. Production: identical vocal treatment on every line of the song — same distance, same tuning, same brightness on the joke and on the ask — so a listener cannot locate the important one by ear and has to hear the words. Structure: every ask section **ends the section**; nothing is permitted to follow it inside its own block, which makes the refusal architectural rather than a matter of discipline at writing time. Rhyme: the ask never rhymes; a rhyme there converts it into a bit. Poetic devices: **epizeuxis** (immediate repetition with no softening between) and **asyndeton** (conjunctions removed so the sentence cannot wander into a qualifier). Hooks: (i) the hook sung at exactly the same volume as the verse; (ii) a second voice that agrees rather than teases; (iii) the final pass unaccompanied, which is the only place the song is allowed to be quiet — and it is quiet by subtraction, not by hush. Thematic seed: **a sentence that stays the size it was when it left her mouth.**

## GUIDE 6 — ⭐ **WILD CARD** · *THE ROOM DOES NOTHING AND SHE IS THE THING MOVING IN IT*

**Storytelling techniques: (a) the still frame with one motion**, taken directly from long-exposure photography — everything in the picture holds for five seconds and one element streaks, so the streak is the only thing the eye can hold; and **(b) the comic asymmetry**, from silent-film blocking, where one figure escalates and the other withdraws and the whole gag is the widening distance, with no title card explaining it. ⭐ **This guide is the day, translated.** The ground did nothing — the significant-quake feed came back empty, count zero, an empty feed being a datum and not a gap; the sun sat quiet at two-hundred-sixty-four kilometres a second; the moon was ninety-nine per cent and not full until tomorrow. **Stillness, with one thing moving inside it.** Every room in this pair is therefore held deliberately inert — a queue that does not advance, a car with the engine off, a pool where nobody is swimming lengths, a gallery room with one other person in it — and she is the only moving object in the frame. And the light comes from `LAW 3`: an aurora raining over a waterfall in a single five-second exposure, **a vertical falling thing lit by horizontal light it did not produce.** Translated with no distortion at all: *the falling is the wanting, the light is the witness, and one moment holds long enough to expose.* **Wanting is invisible until somebody looks across it** — which is not a metaphor for this register, it is a description of it, and it is why the ask exists. Instrumentation: an arrangement with almost nothing moving except one element that moves constantly — a bass walking under a static field, or a hi-hat pattern mutating while everything else holds, or a single voice moving through a chord that does not change. Production: hold the room absolutely still and let one layer streak; ⛔ **and do not reach for the stereo field to do it** — hard-pan was measured on two real renders today at −0.02 dB and +0.13 dB, zero of fifty-two and zero of fifty-six windows above three decibels right, which is the third confirmation that **a spatial instruction is a request the generator overrules while an arrangement instruction is content it serves.** So the asymmetry goes in the arrangement, every time; **if something should be absent, write nothing there.** Structure: under three minutes, one continuous accelerando of commitment with no dynamic drop until the last ask. Poetic devices: **anadiplosis** (each ask beginning with the word the last one ended on, so the song walks forward while the room stands still) and **synaesthesia** (light described as something that lands physically, because in this register attention has weight). Hooks: (i) a figure that never stops for the whole record; (ii) the hook arriving over a completely static bed so the motion is entirely hers; (iii) one four-bar stretch where the band holds one chord and only the voice moves. Thematic seed: **a still room, a woman moving in it, and a light that only arrives if somebody turns their head.**
