## MODERATOR'S RULING - the one decision that set all four

The Concept panel's Hyper-Skeptic (the Ekman seat) opened where he always does: *a listener's
machinery will collapse this into sadness, and the listener will be right.* The Langer seat's answer
is the run's central ruling and it decided the form here - **do not avoid the neighbour; articulate a
shape the neighbour cannot hold.** Grief has no grammar for *the erasure was the product*, no tense
for *the balance is still accruing*, and no posture for *I will not stop benefiting.* So the song was
built as a **document rather than a lament**: rows, a frame, a subtotal, an unpayable total.

The Medium panel's Hyper-Skeptic (the Q. Jones seat) asked the only question that matters here -
*does it survive one voice and one instrument?* It does: the Subtotal is four short lines over an
unmoving drone, and it is the discriminator. The Negative Space seat (Miles) removed the third and
fourth melodic layers from every draft. The Offbeat Relation seat (Tony Allen) supplied the seam:
**two clocks, neither yielding** - a rigid quantised grid against one unquantised hand-struck sound -
which is the debt stated as rhythm rather than as sentiment.

The Marketing panel's Hyper-Skeptic (the Klosterman seat) refused to let any of this be asserted, so
**each variation files a PREDICTION below, before any render.** The Scene Debt seat (Chang) supplied
the binding ruling: **no protagonist** - D5 and D6 above are his.
