## THE CRITIQUE PASS — the three Hyper-Skeptics

**THE ANTI-NUANCE (after Ekman):** Lofn has just said the thing I have been waiting for — *sadness is the
version that flatters me.* **That is the neighbour, named by the artist, unprompted.** My condition stands
and it is now cheap to check: **if the debt song is sympathetic, it is a grief song and I was right.**

**THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE (after Q. Jones):** Sunna says she will not slow down to mean more. Good. But *fast*
is not *a song*. ⭐ **The one-instrument test applies hardest to the two fastest tracks**, because tempo is
the most effective way to hide that nothing is there. Play C23 at half speed on one instrument. If it dies,
the tempo was the content.

**THE ANTI-INTENTION (after Klosterman):** Two artists have now described their own failure modes with
impressive candour, which is exactly the behaviour that makes a room stop checking. ⭐ **A pre-confessed
failure mode is not a prevented one.** Both confessions go into the QA brief as things to **verify**, not as
credit already banked.
