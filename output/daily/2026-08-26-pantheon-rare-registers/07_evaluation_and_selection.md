## ⚠️ DISSENT — where the BRIEF constrained the work badly

### DISSENT 1 — The brief over-determined the song, and its own arithmetic was unsatisfiable

Each isolated brief handed down, complete and before a line existed: the register (§1), the neighbour and the exact discriminating moment (§2), A2/A3/A5 (§3), **the section order with exact stanza lengths** (§4), the rhyme logic (§5), the hook grammar (§6), the sonic device (§7), the refused element (§8), and four variation angles (§9). That is not a brief. It is a specification with four verse slots left open.

And the run's own doctrine says what happens next. **R3.2, quoted by P02 from the ICB:** *what is specified but never varied gets templated, and the tighter the specification, the tighter the template.* The brief manufactured exactly that condition and then relied on individual agents to notice.

**One noticed.** P02 spent the permission clause to vary the most-specified object — the ask's grammar and its narrowing slot change per variation — and shipped **0%** shared sung lines. **P03 accepted §4–§8 whole** and shipped **35%** shared sung lines: 25 of 72 lines identical across all four songs, including the entire chorus. Same brief structure, opposite outcomes, and the difference is that one pair disobeyed.

Worse: **the numbers do not close.** P05's §4 fixes a refrain returning **six** times and fields thinning **8/6/4/2**. That is 7 × 6 + 20 = **62 sung lines**, against a stated floor of 70. The brief specified a shape that violates the brief's own number, arithmetically, with no way out but the permission clause. P06 hit the same wall from the other side and had to reinterpret its 6/5/4/3 stanzas as *pairs* (12/10/8/6) to reach a legal length. **Two of six pairs had to break the brief's own arithmetic before they could write a song.**

`CONFLICT_RESOLUTION_01` closes on the right sentence — *"a constraint set that has to be adjudicated is a constraint set that was not finished"* — and then files the permission clause being spent as evidence the clause works. It is evidence the clause works **and** evidence the constraint set was not finished. Both. The check that is owed is arithmetic and cheap: for each pair, multiply §4 out and compare it to the line floor *before* dispatch.

### DISSENT 2 — P03's world anchor and LAW 2 contradict each other, four sections apart, in the same document
