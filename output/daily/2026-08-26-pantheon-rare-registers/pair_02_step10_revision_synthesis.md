## STEP 1 — THE ESSENCE, IN ONE SENTENCE

> **She is standing in a still room, wanting something exactly, with her face doing it — and instead of taking that off her face she asks the room to keep looking.**

**The two best ways the medium carries it:** (1) **a request sung as the hook**, because a sung line is the only thing a listener repeats, and a listener who repeats *look at me wanting it* has performed the register rather than been told about it; (2) **a room held completely still under one moving layer**, because visible eagerness is only visible against stillness — put it in a busy arrangement and it is just more busy.

---

## STEP 2 — THE TWO CRITICS, AND WHY THESE TWO

⛔ Drawn from the supplied 18-voice Panel Ledger. No panel invented.

**⚠️ THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE** *(Medium Panel hyper-skeptic, after Quincy Jones)* — decades of arranging practice and one repeated position: **the song has to be there first**, arrangement cannot rescue material. He is the correct first critic here because this pair's entire discriminator is a *decision about where a gaze points*, and a decision like that is exactly the sort of thing that can live in a production note and nowhere else. His test — *does it survive one voice and one instrument* — is the only instrument that catches that failure before a render does.

**THE HOOK MATH** *(Context & Marketing, after Max Martin)* — melodic math, syllable-to-note discipline, structural symmetry, unromantic about everything else. He is the second critic because **R3 is his ruling**: the discriminator lives in the hook or the hook carries the neighbour. He does not care what the song means; he cares whether a person can sing it back after one pass, out of breath, at volume. If he cannot, the register never leaves the room.

*The Wodehouse seat sits in on V4 alone, because the funny ruling is his and he owns the verdict on whether it landed.*

## STEP 3 — THE CRITIQUES

### V1 · LOOK AT ME WANTING IT

**THE HOOK MATH:** *"Best hook of the four and it isn't close. Four syllables, three, then the answer — a normal throat gets that on one hearing, and the answer line is a gift because a room can take it without learning anything. The mutation is correct: Chorus 1 lands on `I will not make my face do the other version`, Chorus 2 and 3 land on `that is the part I asked for`. The refrain returns and one thing is wrong in it, which is what you were asked for. No note."*

**⚠️ THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE:** *"Hook survives a piano. Song does not, yet — and the problem is verse one. You've written four lines of what is NOT happening. `Not moving, not moving, not moving.` That is a stage description, not a report from a body. You told me she's been holding the thing the whole time and then you didn't put a single sensation in her hands. Give me one physical fact from inside the body and I'll believe the rest of it. Right now the chorus is carrying a verse that isn't there."*

⭐ **Adjudication: he is right, and it is the one real defect in the pair.** GOLDEN MOVE rule 1 — *stand somewhere real, name what the senses register.* The verse names the room and forgets the hands.

### V2 · I WANT IT IN WRITING

**THE HOOK MATH:** *"`I want it in writing` is singable and the receipt idea is genuinely the discriminator — nobody excited asks for a document. But you have built the payload at item seven and then buried the best line in the record, `you did not do that on purpose`, inside an enjambment across two lines with a gearstick in the middle. Straighten it. And there are seven ordinals in a song that is allowed one number."*

**⚠️ THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE:** *"The one-note delivery for six items is a real idea and it is also a real risk — that is an instruction a generator can simply decline, and if it does, you have six lines of list with no shape. Build the shape into the words so it survives the machine ignoring you: make the items physically shorter as they narrow. Then the flattening is in the text and no one can smooth it out."*

### V3 · YOU CAUGHT ME, STAY THERE

**THE HOOK MATH:** *"`You caught me. Stay there.` — five syllables, two full stops, and the second sentence is the one that kills the neighbour. An excited person does not instruct the witness to remain. And `the whole point was the being seen / not the water` is the plainest statement of a rare state I have been handed today. It is in the chorus, which is the only place it counts."*

**⚠️ THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE:** *"Verse two is the best writing in the pair — `it is not mine, it comes across` — and you got there without a metaphor, which is why it works. My note is on the restart. You closed it on a resolution: `I am going to want things in front of people from now on.` That is a person announcing a policy. A policy is not an appetite. Tell me the next thing she actually wants."*

⭐ **Adjudication: sustained.** And it is the personality's own correction — **hope is appetite pointed at a specific future thing, not optimism about oneself.** A vow is the archive's register; a want is hers.

### V4 · SOMEBODY WATCH THIS ⭐

**THE HOOK MATH:** *"Three words and a two-word answer. That is the whole hook and it is the strongest structural object in the pair, because the answer is sung by the same person, which the listener works out for themselves somewhere around the second chorus. Nothing to fix."*

**⚠️ THE ANTI-ATMOSPHERE:** *"One voice, one instrument, it plays. The mid-sentence open is the right kind of cheap. My note is the turn — you take the band away, you get quiet, and you put a dead painter's intention in there. That is the shape of a moral whether you meant one or not, and this record cannot afford one for four bars."*

**THE ENGINEERED DELIGHT** *(Wodehouse, on the funny ruling):* *"`He is doing it slowly, so it counts as looking around` is the sentence. That is a face moving. The escalation is correct — she keeps making it easier for him, which is the only way this kind of comedy compounds — and the resolution is perfect because it solves nothing and satisfies her completely. **The claim is upheld: this is the funny one.** Do not let the turn go grave. The moment a joke explains why it was worth making, it stops being one, and lightness is the first thing revision takes."*

## STEP 5 — TOP TWO, REVISED

### V4 — the turn, de-moralised
```
One of us in it.                          →   One of us in it, still pointing.
…that somebody wanted to see happen       →   …that somebody wanted to see happen
and left in.                              →   and left in, because that was the good bit.
```
⭐ *That was the good bit* is Sunna's own **WHICH BAR** format — naming the exact place something is good, with no irony and no lesson attached. It converts four bars of near-eulogy into the most characteristic thing she does. And *still pointing* means her arm never came down, which is funnier and refuses the deflation the empty room was offering.

### V3 — the restart, from policy to appetite
```
I am going to want things                 →   I want to do this again
in front of people from now on.           →   somewhere with more people in it.
```
⭐ Hope, correctly built: **a specific thing that has not happened yet, wanted out loud, unembarrassed to be caught wanting it.** A vow describes a person; a want moves her forward. It is also funnier, and it makes the last chorus a plan rather than a summary.

## STEP 6 — BOTTOM TWO, REBUILT

### V1 — verse one gets a body
```
The queue is not moving.                  →   It has gone warm where I'm holding it.
The doors are not moving.                 →   The queue has not moved.
The card reader is asleep.                →   The doors have not moved.
Nothing in here is moving.                →   The card reader is asleep.
Not the air. Not the man.                 →   Somebody's basket is against my heel.
Nothing in here is moving.                →   Nothing in here is moving.
I am the only thing that is.              →   I am the only thing that is.
```
⭐ **`It has gone warm where I'm holding it`** is the whole register in one sensation: she has been holding it long enough to heat it, and she has not put it down once. That is the wanting, made physical, with no adjective anywhere near it. **`Somebody's basket is against my heel`** supplies the queue's pressure from behind — the room now presses on her and she still does not turn around, which makes verse two's refusal cost something.

### V2 — the best line straightened, the ordinals cut back
```
Four: the song that came on                →   Four: the song at the wrong turn.
at the wrong turn.                         →   Five: the part of it
Five: the part of it                       →   where the bass climbs.
where the bass climbs.                     →   Six: your hand went to the gearstick
Six: your hand                             →   on the climb.
went to the gearstick on the climb         →   You did not do that on purpose.
and you did not do it on purpose.          →   I saw you not do it on purpose.
That is six things.                        →   That is six things.
That is a small list                       →   It is a small list.
and I am not shortening it.                →   I am not making it smaller.
```
⭐⭐ **`I saw you not do it on purpose`** is the register handed to the other person and then taken back — for one line *she* is the witness, which is the only place in four songs where that happens, and it makes the seventh item land as an exchange rather than a demand. The gearstick line now sits on its own beat instead of inside an enjambment. **The one sung fact remains `six`, answered by `there is a seventh and it is the small one`;** the ordinals are the list's *form*, not facts about the world, and that reading is declared rather than assumed.
