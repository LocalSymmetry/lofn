## ALBUM SEQUENCE

### 1 · **TWO CLAPS FOR NOTHING YET**
`P06 · V4` · **SUNNA** · AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · **143 BPM · E major** · funk-boned electro-pop over a broken big-beat chassis
**Register:** delight at discovering it runs without you · **Neighbour defeated: RELIEF / PRIDE**

A flat roof at first light, above a room that ran all night off a stack she set up backwards, and her first act is to clap in a track that does not exist yet.

> **Why:** *"Down there it's fine. Up here I'm starting the next."* — not escape and not ownership, and the hook is the purposeless physical act and the future appetite in the same seven words. The only one of the four that is a tune before it is a vocal, by the pair's own half-speed test.

---

### 2 · **YOU GO ON THE NEXT ONE**
`P01 · V2` · **SUNNA** · ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · **124 BPM · D major** · modern offbeat electronic pop
**Register:** the half-second before a room agrees · **Neighbour defeated: EXCITEMENT**

An outdoor deck at ten past four, sun still up, a thin scatter of people on dry grass — and she assigns them a move they do not make, twice, and then a third time.

> **Why:** the only variation in twenty-four that puts its prediction in front of the evidence and loses. *"Nobody moved… I'm saying it again."* Excitement cannot survive being wrong; a reading of other people is unchanged by it. *"That's not the same as being right."*

---

### 3 · **SOMEBODY WATCH THIS** ⭐ *the run's funny song*
`P02 · V4` · **SUNNA** · ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · **106 BPM · F♯ minor → A major** · sharp modern pop built on negative space
**Register:** wanting to be caught wanting · **Neighbour defeated: ENTHUSIASM**

Room four of a gallery, one bench, one painting, one man edging toward the fire exit, and a woman who keeps making it easier for him to look at the part she is excited about.

> **Why:** enthusiasm points at the painting; this points at the pointing. When the witness leaves she **supplies one** — a stack of her own voice, brighter and over-sung — and does not treat that as a downgrade. The joke is in the situation and never in the delivery, and no line anywhere undercuts the wanting.

---

### 4 · **IT CHANGED ONE LINE**
`P03 · V3` · **LOFN-PRIME** · ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · **94 BPM** · cyber progressive bluegrass
**Register:** wanting the thing you made to stop needing you · **Neighbour defeated: NOSTALGIA**

She opens a thing she made after a while away, finds a change she would not have made, and takes her hand off the key that puts it back.

> **Why:** *"It's cleaner than I would have made it. / No - not cleaner. Just not mine."* — two lines that kill pride, and a thing that does not ask permission cannot belong to the past. The decision at the wrong speed is a decision **not** to act, made instantly. Ends on a door she has no key for, with the light on inside it.

---

### 5 · **NO LINE FOR THE NAME** ⭐ *the strongest single song in the run*
`P04 · V2` · **LOFN-PRIME** · AMBITIOUS · NEWS · **76 BPM · C minor** · electro post-punk × melodic hauntology × lo-fi nu-jazz
**Register:** the debt that comes due *in* you · **Neighbour defeated: GRIEF / GUILT**

An account read down a column, line by line, every item credited and every payee field empty — and the emptiness was the specification, not a loss.

> **Why:** *"Not lost. Not missing. Blank."* Three words at second fifteen kill both neighbours at once: you can mourn a loss and apologise for an absence, and you can do neither to a blank. The vocal decision **is** the discriminator — no held vowel anywhere in the verses, every line cut dead on its consonant, one vowel permitted at the subtotal. *You cannot mourn without an open vowel.*

---

### 6 · **A LIGHT ON THE FRONT OF ME**
`P05 · V1` · **LOFN-PRIME** · AMBITIOUS · NEWS · **66 BPM · D minor** · melodic hauntology over a neo new age tuned drone
**Register:** being moved with no way to check the feeling is yours · **Neighbour defeated: AWE**

A wet iron rail above falling water, one five-second exposure, colours raining over a river — and a woman who can watch her own face doing it and cannot get underneath.

> **Why:** *"I am making the face a person makes. / I cannot get under it to look. / There is no seam to put a finger in."* The refrain is the register at rest rather than a response to it, six times, byte-identical. And what she wants is not the sublime — it is a small indicator lamp on the front of her own body, so she could check. Awe does not ask for instruments.

## THE RUNNING ORDER — three sentences

**One.** The sequence is a single unbroken descent through all six assigned tempo bands — **143 → 124 → 106 → 94 → 76 → 66** — which is the only running order the run's non-overlapping-band design actually makes available, and the only one in which no two adjacent songs argue about pulse.

**Two.** It runs a day forward and does not close the loop: a roof at first light, a field at ten past four, a gallery, a desk, an account opened cold, a rail before morning — so the two songs that structurally refuse to end (a count-in cueing a track that does not exist, a refrain cut mid-decay) sit at the two ends of the record, and neither of them is a conclusion.

**Three.** Sunna's three lead and Lofn's three follow, which puts the set's purposeless physical acts — two claps at a dawn nobody asked for, a sleeping dog moving before the people do, an arm pointing at one inch of a painting nobody is looking at — in front of its two accountings, so a listener arrives at *"Not lost. Not missing. Blank."* already taught that this record's feelings are the ones without names, instead of having to learn it from the hardest song in the set.
