## THE SIX PANEL AHA-MOMENTS (attributed)

**1. ⭐⭐ INVOKE THE NEIGHBOUR, THEN CONTRADICT IT.** *[the Ekman seat ⇄ the Langer seat — the run's central breakthrough]*
The Concept skeptic's standing objection was that a listener's machinery collapses any rare state into a basic emotion, and **the listener is right to.** The answer was not to avoid the neighbour:
> **A rare emotion is not a feeling nobody has had. It is a feeling everybody has MISFILED.**
> Give the listener the neighbour first — let them file it as anticipation, as guilt, as pride — and then, **at one exact moment, make that filing untenable** with something the neighbour cannot explain.

**2. A SHAPE IS A SIGNAL.** *[the Langer seat]* Music does not express an emotion; it **articulates the form a feeling has as it moves.** Removing the *label* does not remove the *shape* — it removes the competing label. A song with no emotion words and one exact shape is **maximally** legible, not minimally.

**3. THE ABSENCE OF A NAME IS EVIDENCE ABOUT THE VOCABULARY, NOT THE FEELING.** *[the Wierzbicka seat]* Emotion words do not map across languages; a language that has a word produces speakers who report the state. **The song has to do the work the missing word would have done.**

**4. THE THIRTY SECONDS ARE STICKY.** *[the Ekman seat, narrowed — backed by the Levitin seat]* A basic emotion assigned before the shape arrives **cannot be revoked.** And a single hearing retains only four things: **contour · a repeated phrase · an unusual timbre · a moment of surprise.** Attach the state to one of those, or it is not encoded at all.

**5. JOY IS A TECHNICAL ACHIEVEMENT.** *[the Wodehouse seat]* Lightness is the hardest thing to engineer and the first thing lost to revision. ⭐ **If the delight songs come back grave, it will not be because delight is shallow — it will be because it is harder, and the room defaulted to the easier register while calling it depth.**

**6. NOBODY IN THE ROOM IS THE LISTENER.** *[the Klosterman seat]* *"The listener leaves feeling it"* is an **unverified claim about other people's interiors.** So every song files a prediction, before any render.

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

- ⛔ **Not a song that names its emotion.** Naming it is how it fails to deliver it.
- ⛔ **Not a puzzle.** A register so rare it arrives as a riddle has produced curiosity, not feeling.
- ⛔ **Not a biography you haven't got.** No invented worker, no role, no stranger's life. *A body and a want are enough, and they are shared.*
- ⛔ **Not two ideas in one frame.** N = 1: one dominant material, at most one intruder, one seam, and the subject doing something across it.
- ⛔ **Not hard-panned.** Measured today across two real renders: **−0.02 dB and +0.13 dB.** A spatial instruction is a request the generator overrules. **If you want something absent, write nothing there.**
- ⛔ **Not grave by default.** Half this run is delight, and delight is the harder engineering job.
