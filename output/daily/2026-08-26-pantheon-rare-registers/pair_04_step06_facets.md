## 2. ⭐ THE DISCRIMINATOR — the neighbour, and the exact moment it dies

**The neighbour is GRIEF / GUILT, and it is handed over first, deliberately.** A slow minor tempo, a
list of things that were made by people who are not named, a singer who is implicated: a listener
will file this as *a sad song about workers* inside eight seconds, and **they are right to** — the
Ekman seat's standing objection is correct about the machinery.

**Three properties defeat that filing, and each is a thing grief and guilt structurally cannot do:**

1. ⭐⭐ **THE ERASURE WAS THE PRODUCT, NOT THE HARM.** Grief mourns a loss; guilt apologises for a
   wrong. This says the blank payee field was **a specification that worked exactly as designed** —
   and you cannot mourn a spec or apologise to one. *(V2 states the death of the neighbour in one
   line: **"Not lost. Not missing. Blank."** — the two grief words appear in the lyric exactly once
   each, inside the line that refuses them. Measured: `lost` ×1, `missing` ×1, both in that line.)*
2. ⭐⭐ **THE BALANCE IS STILL ACCRUING.** Grief is past tense. **"Credit: the interest, running while
   I sing"** is present and forward. A mourner cannot say that sentence; a debtor doing live
   double-entry can say nothing else.
3. ⭐⭐ **THE SINGER IS THE BENEFICIARY AND WILL NOT STOP BENEFITING.** *"I will not put it down. Put
   that on the bill."* Guilt discharges itself by stopping. This one keeps spending and adds the
   spending to the total.

**The exact moment (all four variations, the unpaired ADDRESS stanza):** the accounting is performed
aloud, correctly, and **the payee field will not take a value.** The song does not resolve there. It
returns to the item list.

⛔ **NO THANK-YOU.** The genre's reflex is gratitude, and **gratitude closes the account.**

## 4. THE FIVE FACETS

**F1 · THE ITEMISED ACCOUNT.**
Line-items read in order, in a fixed syntactic frame, at ledger speed. The unit of the song is a
**row**, not a sentence: `Credit: <object>, <what was done to it>.` The dramaturgy is arithmetic —
items, subtotal, items, subtotal — and the running total never closes. Scored on: does the listener
recognise, without being told, that they are being read a **bill**?

**F2 · THE SEAT IS HERS.**
First person, her own position, her own liability. The debit column is her. ⛔ No worker is
depicted, invented, or pictured. Scored on: is there any line from which a listener could sketch a
specific person who was actually harmed? *(Target: zero. Measured: zero.)*

**F3 · THE CONSONANT LEDGER (A3).**
End-rhyme is stripped; a hard **`Cr-`** cluster repeats at the head of every item line, so the list
has an audible edge and the ear can find its place without counting. **`Credit`** is doing double
duty and it is the seam of the whole song: it is the accounting word for *what is owed* **and** the
word for *whose name is on the record.* Scored on: alliteration density and whether the frame reads
as a device or as a machine that will not stop.

**F4 · THE UNMOVING TONE AND THE ONE HAND (A4, N=1).**
**Dominant material:** a rigid quantised electronic grid — the machine, the ledger, me.
**One intruder:** a single hand-struck acoustic sound, unquantised, arriving early, never corrected.
**One seam:** it crosses the grid at the ADDRESS stanza, the exact junction where the payee cannot be
named. ⭐ **What the subject does across the seam: she keeps her own count and does not correct to
it, and it does not correct to her.** Two clocks in one room with no way to settle — the debt, stated
as rhythm. And the assigned intruder — **a low sustained tone present from bar one that never resolves
and never moves** — is content, not a pan.

**F5 · NOT SYMPATHETIC.**
⛔ *"Sadness is the version of this that flatters me, because a mourner is sympathetic and a debtor
is not."* Scored adversarially: **find the line that invites pity.** If the song is sympathetic it is
a grief song and the Concept skeptic was right. Every stanza is checked for: a request, a complaint,
a claim of unfairness, a claim that the payment helps. *(All four: none. The closest call is named
in the final package rather than hidden.)*

## 5. WILDCARD FACET (the sixth guide's seed)

**F6 · THE SONG PAYS WITH ITS OWN DEVICE.**
The `Cr-` frame carries four blocks. In V4 it **flips to `Paid:` mid-stanza and never returns** — the
song surrenders its signature as the payment. **Removal is a debt (L21), and here the removal is the
point**; what returns in its place is the drone alone. Scored on: does the surrender land as an event
a listener can hear, or as a writer's private joke?
