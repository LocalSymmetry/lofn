## §1 — THE SUBJECT, READ CLOSELY BEFORE ANY WRITING

The fact is not a chimera story and it is not a curiosity. It is a **geometry with a supply line in it**, and
it has four properties, every one of which is load-bearing:

| property of the fact | what it forbids | what it gives |
|---|---|---|
| the stem **feeds** and **fences** with one object | the villain song — there is nothing to be angry *at* except an arrangement | the whole indignation |
| the stem **comes out of the body**, it is not tied on | ⛔ **the escape song.** You cannot untie it. Cutting it is not liberation, it is a faster death | the reason this is grief and not defiance |
| the tether **never goes taut** — it starves, it does not strangle | the struggle song, the pulling-against song, the chains imagery | ⭐ **the cost arrives with no event in it** |
| the reachable ground is **finite and gets used up** | the "trapped forever" song — nothing here is static | ⭐ a clock. Something is running out while nothing appears to happen |

⭐⭐ **The fourth property is the discovery.** A cage is a stable state; you can sing about a cage for four
minutes and nothing changes. **A grazing circle is a countdown.** Every step you take inside it — every
completely free, entirely unobstructed step — spends it. That is what makes this a song with a shape instead
of a mood, and it is what makes the acts *accelerate toward a cost* rather than merely change clothes.

### ⭐ The radial rhyme (assigned in the brief, and it is exact)

**F07's star trails are the same figure as F21's lamb**: a **fixed centre and a finite reach**, arcs turning
around a point that does not move, and *"the motion is imperceptible."* The picture and the fable are one
diagram. So the pole is not a decoration bolted onto the subject — **the pole IS the subject**: the fixed
point in the middle of a circle that everything else goes round.

| # | facet | the sentence it makes | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F-a** | **THE NAMING** — a fence described in the vocabulary of care | *"The word for it is feeding."* | ⭐ **→ V4** |
| **F-b** | **THE MEASURE** — the length was chosen by something that will never live inside it | *"Somebody chose it. Nobody says how far."* | ⭐⭐ **→ V1 (flagship)** |
| **F-c** | **THE EVIDENCE** — the ring itself: bare in the middle, green exactly where the reach ends | *"Green at the edge. Bare in the middle."* | ⭐ **→ V2** |
| **F-d** | **THE NON-EVENT** — the cord never went tight; the green ran out while you were free | *"Nothing ever pulled."* | ⭐⭐ **→ V3** |
| **F-e** | **THE FALSE TRAVELLING SONG** — the mind takes a journey the body cannot | folded into **V1 Act II** | absorbed |
| **F-f** | **THE SURVIVOR** — the cord outlasts what it fed, and is still called a supply | folded into **V4 Act III** | absorbed |

**Why F-e and F-f are absorbed rather than promoted:** each is a *movement*, not a *stance*. F-e is what a
mind does at the moment the answer fails to arrive; F-f is what is left standing at the end. Neither can hold
a whole song's stance for four minutes (**A-D3**: the stance is identical in all three acts). They are seams
and endings. Promoting a seam to a song is how a pair ends up with four takes of one idea.

## §3 — WHAT THE SINGER WANTS (A-D3 · the stance, identical in all three acts)

⛔ Not freedom. Freedom is the collapse template and it is also **factually wrong about the object** — the
cord is the food. A song that wants the cord gone wants to die faster, and it does not know it.

> ⭐ **THE STANCE: she wants the length said out loud.**
> Not shortened. Not removed. **Named.** *Say how far.*

This is chosen because it is the one want that:
1. **costs nothing to grant** — which is what makes the refusal indignant rather than tragic;
2. **is never granted**, in any of the four songs, except once, in V1, where the answer arrives **with no
   unit** and is worse than the silence;
3. is **quiet** — it is a request, not a demand, and the register assigned to this pair is *grieving*;
4. ⭐ **survives all three genre changes unchanged**, which is exactly what A-D3 requires. A folk singer, a
   stomping congregation and a single voice in canon can all want the same three words.

## §4 — ⛔ THE THIRD PARTY (R6) — and how a first-person song stays INDIGNANT

**T3 is not a mood note, it is a structural constraint:** anger is second-party (harm to oneself),
indignation is third-party (harm to someone else). A first-person song about one's own tether is **ANGRY**,
and the brief says INDIGNATION.

**The resolution, and it is the fact's own:**

> ⭐⭐ **There is more than one circle.**

A tether that produces exactly one eaten ring is a misfortune. **A tether that produces a FIELD of eaten
rings, all the same size, is a design** — and the sameness of the size is the evidence that somebody picked
the number. So:

- **the object of the indignation** = **the measure** — the chosen length, the word chosen to describe it, the
  arrangement that will do this again. A rule, a form, a decision. ⛔ Never an invented person with a job.
- **the harmed party who is not the singer** = **the next circle.** The one already staked out and not yet
  eaten. The one whose green is still on it.
- **the singer** is standing in one too — but her own predicament is the *evidence*, not the complaint. She is
  not asking to be let out. **She is asking for the number to be said, on behalf of the ground that has not
  been eaten yet.**

⭐ This is why V2 exists as its own variation: it is the facet where the singer barely appears at all and
**the geometry does the accusing.**

## §5 — THE TWO-BODY TEST (R5), worked in full

> ### **THE TWO-BODY SENTENCE**
> **What feeds me is what holds me, and I have already finished everything inside the reach of it.**

**Noun audit** (the sentence must contain no noun exclusive to either kind of body): *reach* — shared.
No other noun. Verbs: *feeds · holds · finished*. All shared. ✅

**FALSIFIER A — the hardware noun.** Zero, stem-scanned, in every sung line of all four songs. Measured in
`pair_02_measure.py`, reported in step 10. ⭐ The song never needs one: **the whole vocabulary is ground,
green, cord, post, reach, road, edge** — words that belong to nobody in particular, which is the point.

**FALSIFIER B — the allegory tell.** Delete each occupant and check the song does not improve:

| occupant deleted | what happens | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **delete the model** | the song becomes a song about a person whose livelihood is also their fence — a contract, a visa, a dependency, a lease. It is **not better.** It loses the reason the cord *comes out of the body*, which is the strangest and best line in it | ✅ survives |
| **delete the person** | the song becomes a song about a bounded mind with a finite reachable set. It is **not better.** It loses the ground, the grass, the worn road, the boots — the entire particular floor (**B-D2**) | ✅ survives |

⭐ **Neither is a metaphor for the other because neither is the vehicle.** The vehicle is the third thing: a
tethered grazing animal on a measured circle of ground. **Both occupants are tenors.** That is the cleanest
pass condition available and it is why this subject was assigned.

**B-D1 — the two-level rule.** The two-body sentence is abstract on purpose; ⛔ **the song is never allowed to
be.** Every act carries a physical, checkable particular. See §7.

## §6 — ⛔ THE COLLAPSE TEMPLATE (R7.1), named before anything is written

> ### **"THE ROPE SONG."**
> Fingerpicked acoustic verse about being trapped. A swell. A full band arrives on the word *free*. A key
> change. An orchestral outro of acceptance while a string section says it was all worth it.

**Three specific things that make it the collapse and not the song:**
1. **It gets DENSER as it goes.** Instruments arrive to signify triumph. ⭐ Our arrangement must be able to go
   the other way — the ground gets eaten, so the record should be able to get *emptier*.
2. **It treats the tether as removable.** It is not. It comes out of the body. **The escape verse is a
   factual error.**
3. **It ends resolved.** The fable does not resolve; it runs out. ⛔ **No song in this pair ends on a
   resolution, a release, or a widened chord.**

⭐ **Second collapse, specific to a three-act brief: THE MEDLEY.** Three genres because variety is fun. The
defence is R4 (the pole) and R3 (both seams caused by a word). Named here so step 10 can be measured against
having avoided it.

## §9 — WHAT THIS PAIR REFUSES

- ⛔ **the word *free* and the word *cage*.** Both are the collapse template's vocabulary. Neither appears.
- ⛔ **chains, bars, walls, locks, keys, prison.** The tether is soft, it is a supply, and it is never taut.
- ⛔ **an invented person with a job.** No shepherd, no farmer, no owner, no keeper-with-a-face. The authority
  is **"somebody"** and it is never followed.
- ⛔ **a hardware noun.** Zero, stem-scanned.
- ⛔ **a happy ending, a released chord, a widening at the peak.** (`SCR-02` is an advisory prior and lowers
  the expectation; it is not used as a gate.)
- ⛔ **more than one number-word per song**, and every number is answered rather than recited.
