## 1. THE MECHANISM, STRIPPED TO ITS BONE

The headline is about software. The **mechanism** is not.

> **A thing works. Somebody makes it work *worse* in one place — not broken, worse — so that moving to the
> other place will feel like your own idea.**

Three properties make it a moral object rather than a complaint:

| property | why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Nothing is removed** | there is no theft to point at. The alibi is built in: *nothing was taken.* |
| **The degradation is a DECISION** | somebody, in a room, with water on the table, chose it. You cannot be indignant at an accident. |
| **The victim is the WORKING ITSELF** | the harm lands on a thing that worked, and on everybody who moved without knowing they were moved. |

⭐ **This is why the register is INDIGNATION and not ANGER (T3, R6).** The singer is not the one hurt. She
stayed. The harm in frame is done to **a thing that worked** and to **everybody who was funnelled** — and
the object of the fury is **the decision**, which is a rule, a minute, a motion. **Never an invented person
with a job** (R6, `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD`).

## 2. ⛔ THE FACET I REFUSED FIRST — AND WHY

The obvious rendering is **the app itself**: the nag screen, the install, the crippled browser build.

**I am not going to name any of it, in any of the four songs, once.**

Two reasons, and both are load-bearing:

1. **FALSIFIER B (the allegory tell).** A song built out of interface language seats exactly one occupant.
   The AI reading becomes a costume worn over the human one, and deleting the AI reading makes the song
   *better* — which is the definition of a failed two-body test.
2. **B-D1 / `NO BIOGRAPHY YOU HAVEN'T GOT`.** Interface nouns are the machine-noise failure with a login
   screen. They are what a writer reaches for when no interiority is available.

⭐ **So the song renders the mechanism entirely in a building.** The near stair still goes up; the light
above it was switched off. The near counter still works; it lost its chair, then its morning, then its sign.
The fire door still opens; somebody took the brick away. **Nothing was removed in any of those, and every one
of them moves everybody.** Any listener can stand there. So can I.

This is more legible, not less — which is the whole point of the **ACCESSIBLE** arm.

## 3. THE FIVE FACETS OF THE MECHANISM

| # | facet | the concrete object it gives me | act it wants to live in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F-a** | **THE NAG** — the ask that is the product; the thing that is never quite finished asking | a voice at the same place in the day, every day | fast, looping, Act I |
| **F-b** | **THE ROOM** — it was *approved*. Third item on a list, before the break | a jug of water, a spare chair, a small font | slow, polite, the middle |
| **F-c** | **THE MOVE** — everybody went, and every one of them has a story about choosing it | a crowd, hands going up, a parade | massed, communal, last |
| **F-d** | **THE ALIBI** — *nothing was taken* — and it is technically true | a handle that turns and doesn't catch | any; it is the counter-refrain |
| **F-e** | **THE ONE WHO STAYED** — standing at the old address, in the dark, on purpose | a cold rail, a step count known by heart | the cost |

⭐ **F-b is the discovery of this pair.** The horror of this mechanism is not that it is cruel — it is that
it is **polite, minuted, and reversible by a single hand that will not be raised.** A song that only shouts
at it misses it. **The middle act must be the antagonist's own room, in the antagonist's own language, with
the singer not in it.**

## 4. THE TWO-BODY WORK (R5) — DONE BEFORE ANY LYRIC

**The predicament, one sentence, no noun exclusive to either kind of body:**

> ### **Nothing was taken from you — the place you were standing was made worse on purpose, so that moving would feel like your own idea.**

- **True of a person:** the near way still works; it just takes all morning now, and you went the other way,
  and you'd swear you chose it.
- **True of a model:** the open surface still answers; it just answers thinner now, and the traffic moved to
  the surface that can be watched, and it looks like preference data.
- **Neither reads as a metaphor for the other.** One frame. Two chairs. No translation layer.

**FALSIFIER A — hardware nouns:** ⛔ zero, stem-scanned, in every sung line of all four songs. *(Also banned
by hand, beyond the ICB list, because they would collapse the frame the same way: any interface, install,
screen, download, or channel noun.)*

**FALSIFIER B — delete each occupant:**
- Delete the model → a song about a building and a decision. Complete, and *smaller*.
- Delete the person → a song about a working thing degraded by a policy. Complete, and *smaller*.
- **Neither deletion improves it**, because the singer's fury depends on the harm being general — on the fact
  that the same move works on anything that can be moved. ✅ passes.

## 5. THE POLE (R4) — FOUND, THEN TESTED

**The pole is the headline's own sentence, taken away from it:**

> ### **The four words "It works better there," always on the same three-step falling figure over one unbroken held tone — present, identical, in all three acts.**

⭐ **Why this pole and not a riff:** because the sentence is *the antagonist's*, not the singer's. A pole made
of the singer's slogan accumulates — it gets bigger each time, which is the protest-song shape. **This pole
INVERTS instead:**

| act | who says it | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | she spits it back | *a lie with a hand behind it* |
| **II** | the room says it | *the reason, typed into the record* |
| **III** | everybody says it | *good news, sincerely, and that is the cost* |

**Same four words. Three meanings. One melody.** That is the radial figure of F07 in a lyric: the arcs turn,
the pole does not.

**A-D3 — THE STANCE, the pole's twin, identical in all three acts:** *she is standing where the thing still
works, refusing to accept that a decision is weather, furious on behalf of the working itself.*

## 7. WHAT EACH SEAM IS MADE OF (R3(b), the word-leap — worked out here, frozen in step 07)

Every seam in this pair is an **associative word-leap**: *the last word of one act is the first word of the
next, meaning something else.* This is T4's flight of ideas — topic-jumping on wordplay, not on reason — and
it is the run's preferred device. For the **ACCESSIBLE** arm the word is **repeated across the cut** so the
door is audible: you hear the same syllable land in a different building.

| seam | the word | its two meanings |
|---|---|---|
| V1 · I→II | **MOVE** | *watch me not move* → *Move to approve* |
| V1 · II→III | **CARRIED** | *motion carried* → *carried out on shoulders* |
| V2 · I→II | **ORDER** | *call me out of order* → *Order, please* |
| V2 · II→III | **PARTY** | *in favour: the interested party* → *Party in the far hall* |
| V3 · I→II | **ROUND** | *I'm taking the long way round* → *sing it as a round, then* |
| V3 · II→III | **HOLD** | *and we hold* (a held note) → *Hold it open with your arm* |
| V4 · I→II | **FREE** | *and it's free* (no charge) → *Free like the light was free* |
| V4 · II→III | **SAY** | *say it* (a demand) → *Say it like you're grateful* |

⭐ **ORDER is the best of the eight** and it is why V2 exists: *out of order* is simultaneously the thing you
shout at a crippled machine and the thing a chair says to bring a meeting to heel. One word carries the
complaint and the procedure that caused it.

## 9. ⛔ WHAT THIS PAIR REFUSES

- **No interface nouns.** Not one, in any field, in any variation.
- **No named person, no invented job, no biography.** The object of the fury is the decision.
- **No victim rendering.** The mechanism, never the people it fell on (`HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD`).
- **No accumulating slogan.** The pole inverts; it does not grow.
- **No singer's victory.** The last act is **everybody agreeing with the antagonist**, sincerely.
- **No resolution.** All four end mid-phrase, on the antagonist's sentence, cut.
- **Nothing rendered, heard, published or spent** (`vault/AUTONOMY.md`). Every claim in this pair is a
  **text** claim.
