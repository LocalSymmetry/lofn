# PAIR 06 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES

Four songs, one concept, one pole. Every guide below is fixed **before** any lyric line exists (R7).

---

## THE PAIR-LEVEL CONSTANTS (identical in all four songs)

**THE POLE (R4), one sentence:**
> **Five falling syllables — *"The water does not know"* — on the same falling shape every time, with one
> sustained pitched pedal tone held under them past the bar line, present and unchanged in all three acts and
> re-clothed by each genre.**

**THE STANCE (A-D3), identical in all three acts of all four songs:**
> She is standing at the edge of a thing that has been renamed, she will not use the new name, and she is
> **not asking for the old one back** — she is pointing out that the naming did nothing and cost somebody
> everything.

**THE POLE TAG — exact, unmodified, in every act of every song:**
```
The water does not know.
Nobody asked the lake.
The water does not know.
```
⭐ *Exact repetition of the pole needs no defence (R4). It is THE RETURN, discharged.*

**THE VOICE:** female alto, conversational, comic timing, **never crooned and never belted**, close and dry,
in front of the arrangement from the first syllable (R1.4 — the voice is put in front; the arrangement is
never told to start loud).

**THE ROOM:** big and live in the swing act, gone in the break act, enormous decay in the pedal act. The
pedal tone is the only thing that crosses all three.

**⛔ FORBIDDEN IN ALL FOUR:** the new name (never spoken), any real place name, any living person, any
invented person with a job, a crooner wink, a wah-wah plunger punchline, a torch-ballad third act, an
orchestral outro, a key change up, an internal drop-out bar, an untuned drone.

# V1 — **NOBODY ASKED THE LAKE** · 108 BPM

**Angle:** the order of events. The whole apparatus seen from outside, in sequence.
**Third party:** the lake.

| act | genre | share | instrumentation | register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Swing & Big Band** *(anchor)* | ~45% | full brass section, walking upright bass, hard-swung triplet eighths, brushes to sticks | indignation, fast, comic timing, mid-argument |
| **II** | **Jungle / breakbeat (amen break)** | ~33% | no horns at all; paper-and-metal percussion, sub pedal, straight double-time subdivision | mania — flight of ideas, patter, borne along |
| **III** | **Church-organ pedal, sacred-minimal** | ~22% | one pitched pedal tone, long decay, doubled voice, **no drums** | cold fury, level, close |

**Seam 1 — the word ORDER.** Act I's last sung word is *order* (the signed decree); Act II's first sung word
is *Order* (sort it / file it / order more of them).
**Seam 2 — the word SOUND.** Act II's last sung word is *sound* (valid, well-founded); Act III's first sung
word is *Sound* (the verb — to drop a line and find the floor).

**Particulars (B-D2):** I — ferry board, tourist chart, fishing card, gulls, reeds. II — laminated lunch
card, tin sign, pallets, vans, a box of corrected pages. III — a line dropped to the floor, four degrees, the
light giving up.
**The one numeric fact:** *four degrees* — the cold at the bottom of a deep lake, all year — at the hinge,
**responded to** (*"all year, whatever it is called"*), never recited.

# V2 — **THE INK IS REAL** · 106 BPM

**Angle:** the labour. The song stands with the hands, and the ceremony is something heard through a ceiling.
**Third party:** the unnamed hands. *(Unnamed figures only — no role, no job, no biography.)*

| act | genre | share | instrumentation | register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Dry gridded clerical post-punk** | ~30% | straight sixteenths, paper-and-metal percussion, no room at all, dry bass | indignation on behalf of somebody else |
| **II** | **Swing & Big Band** *(anchor)* | ~44% | full brass, walking bass, big live hall, claps | ridicule at total conviction |
| **III** | **Half-time blues stomp + organ pedal** | ~26% | one guitar, floor tom, church-organ pedal, no choir, no lift | contempt, level and close |

**Seam 1 — the word CHARGE.** Act I ends on *charge* (the cost, who eats it); Act II opens on *Charge!* — the
bugle call, and **the entire brass section enters on the word.**
**Seam 2 — the word KEY.** Act II ends *"singing it in the same key"*; Act III opens *"Key."* — the little
printed box in the corner of a map where a name is what a mark means.

**Particulars:** I — tape gun, trolley, stairs, a box that pulls at the arm, rain. II — banner, lectern, a
photograph of a word being pointed at by a hand, a small glass of something, cake. III — a box the size of a
thumb on a sheet the size of a door, the stones giving out.
**The one numeric fact:** *eight hundred feet* — the depth — responded to with *"and none of it has heard."*

# V3 — **EVERY OLD MAP IS NOW WRONG** · 110 BPM

**Angle:** the archive. What happens to a sentence that was already true.
**Third party:** the record itself — every correct map now filed as wrong.
⭐ **The apparatus arrives LAST and the song does not resolve.** This is the arm's risk.

| act | genre | share | instrumentation | register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Sacred-minimal organ pedal + close voice** | ~28% | pitched pedal at full size from bar one, **no drums**, voice in front | indignation, mid-argument, quiet and unhurried |
| **II** | **Hand-and-foot work stomp (hill-country)** | ~33% | floor tom, boot stomp, claps, scraper percussion, **no brass** | mania, accelerating, physical |
| **III** | **Swing & Big Band** *(anchor)* | ~39% | full brass tutti, walking bass, hall, applause figure | ridicule → moral outrage, the machinery winning |

**Seam 1 — the word RECORD.** Act I ends *"It is all in the record"*; Act II opens *"Record."* — the
imperative: write it down again, by hand.
**Seam 2 — the word STRIKE.** Act II ends *"every stamp gets a strike"* (a strike-through); Act III opens
*"Strike."* — strike up the band, **and the brass enters on the word.**

**Particulars:** I — a drawer of flat folded maps, the right blue, depth marks. II — a scraper, a rag, a
ladder, flakes in a bag, **paint underneath that is a lighter paint**. III — banner, cake, a photograph of a
hand pointing at a printed word.
**The one numeric fact:** *six years* — the whole lake is new water — responded to with *"It will not notice
that either."*

⭐ **The ending:** the last sung line is still the pole, delivered **under** a full brass tutti. The apparatus
is louder. The pole is last. Nothing resolves, nothing ritards, no final cymbal.

# V4 — **WATCH ME NAME THE WHOLE ROOM** · 112 BPM

**Angle:** the claim. She takes the same authority and proves the mechanism on cheap material.
**Third party:** still the water — reached at the end, and it is the only thing that refuses.
⭐ **The most manic, and the deepest reading of the mechanism.**

| act | genre | share | instrumentation | register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Swing & Big Band** *(anchor)* | ~43% | full brass, walking bass, hard swing, the apparatus commandeered | mania as delight, ridicule, doing it back |
| **II** | **Double-time patter over an amen break** | ~32% | no horns; break, sub pedal, auctioneer/scat phrasing | flight of ideas — association, not speed |
| **III** | **Organ pedal, sacred-minimal** | ~25% | one pitched pedal, doubled voice, **no drums** | cold fury, and the first honest line in the song |

**Seam 1 — the word FLOOR.** Act I ends *"I have only taken the floor"*; Act II opens *"Floor it."* — and the
subdivision doubles **on the word.**
**Seam 2 — the word BOTTOM.** Act II ends *"Go down to the bottom"* (of the list); Act III opens *"Bottom."*
— the lake bottom, which is where the cold is.

**Particulars:** I — a chair, a window, a corridor, a door that still opens out, a kettle, a bin. II — gulls,
reeds, ferry, sand, stones, rain. III — the cold on the hands, four degrees, the light giving out.
**The one numeric fact:** *four degrees* — at the bottom — *"and it does not take a word from me."*

⭐ **The turn (T4, flight of ideas — the arrival of a connection nobody authorised):**
> *That is not because it is strong. / That is because I have been in it.*

⛔ **This line is the whole song and it must survive every revision.** It names the mechanism — naming works
exactly where there is no relationship — without one word of translation between the two occupants.
