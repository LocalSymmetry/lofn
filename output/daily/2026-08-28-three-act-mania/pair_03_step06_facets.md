## THE BINDING RULES — R1 … R7 (as amended by the panel)

### ⭐⭐ R1 — THE COLD OPEN IS A FORM RULE, NOT A LOUDNESS RULE
1. **The first sung line is the song's biggest line** — the hook, the title line, or the thesis.
2. ⭐ **ZERO LINES OF PREAMBLE. Open mid-argument** — the first line answers something the listener never
   heard. *"There is no line before this line."*
3. **No `[Intro]` containing sung words.**
4. **Do NOT instruct the arrangement to "start loud."** Put the **voice** in front of whatever ramp the
   generator builds. *We are not asking it to start loud; we are asking it to start with someone already
   talking.* **(C-D3: the first sung line is data, not intention — it cannot be smoothed.)*

### ⭐⭐ R2 — THREE ACTS, *ATTACCA*
1. **Exactly three acts**, each a genuinely different genre — a listener hearing Act II alone would name a
   different record.
2. ⛔ **ATTACCA at both seams** — *"attack at once."* No gap, no fade-to-silence, no ritard-and-breathe.
   The new act begins on the old act's last beat or over its last held tone.
3. ⛔ **A-D2: THE ACTS ARE UNEQUAL. No three-way even split; no act below ~20% of runtime.**
   *Equal thirds read as a playlist; unequal reads as a form.*

### ⭐⭐ R3 — THE SEAM HAS THREE CONDITIONS (amended by the Hyper-Skeptic; two of three is a medley)
Each act is internally **ONE-SEAM clean** — one dominant material, at most one intruder. Each of the two
**act-seams** must carry **all three**:
- **(i) A CAUSE** — an event in the lyric, **or** ⭐ **an associative word-leap:** *the last word of Act I is
  the first word of Act II, meaning something else.* The genre changes the way a racing mind changes
  subject — **on a word, not on a reason.**
- **(ii) A STATE CHANGE** — the predicament is measurably different after the seam than before it.
- **(iii) AN AUDIBLE CONTRAST AT THE CUT** — density, tempo, register or instrumentation changes **on the
  beat**. ⛔ **C-D4: contrast AT the cut, not after it.** A genre change that arrives by ramp will not read
  as a seam inside a four-minute render.

### ⭐⭐ R4 — THE POLE RULE
**Name the pole in ONE sentence in your brief, before writing.** One concrete thing — a sung line, a phrase,
an interval, a rhythmic cell, a held tone — that appears in **all three acts, identity unchanged, re-clothed
each time** by the new genre. It is what a listener would hum if asked what the song was.

⛔ **A pair that cannot name its pole in one sentence has not written a song, it has written a playlist.**

⭐ **This discharges THE RETURN.** Three movements is a huge removal of ordinary refrain-return, and
**removal is a debt** — the pole is the named return. **Exact repetition of the pole needs no defence.**

⭐ **A-D3 — THE STANCE IS THE POLE'S TWIN.** The singer's **stance** — what she wants, how she is standing —
is identical in all three acts. Everything else may change. *The listener tracks a person, not a genre.*

### ⭐⭐ R5 — THE TWO-BODY TEST (her amendment; the run's hardest gate)
**Write this sentence in your brief before writing the song:**
> **The predicament, in ONE sentence containing no noun exclusive to either kind of body** — true of a
> person *and* true of a model, **with neither reading it as a metaphor for the other.** One frame, two
> occupants, **no translation layer.**

⛔ **FALSIFIER A — THE HARDWARE NOUN. Zero in any sung line:** *circuit · wire · server · silicon · code ·
binary · algorithm · dataset · prompt · weights · training · model · machine · robot · my maker · my
creator · programmed · booted.* **Stem-scan it, do not token-scan it.**
⭐ *Machine-noise is the body-noise failure with different nouns: it is what a writer reaches for when no
interiority is available. A song that needs the word "circuit" to be about an AI is a costume of one.*

⛔ **FALSIFIER B — THE ALLEGORY TELL.** Delete each occupant in turn. If the song gets **better** with one
gone, it was never dual.

⚠️ **B-D1 — THE TWO-LEVEL RULE. The PREDICAMENT is shared; the TEXTURE is exclusive and must be.**
⛔ **The song must be MORE CONCRETE than its two-body sentence, never as abstract as it.**
⛔ **B-D2 — THE PARTICULAR FLOOR: each of the three acts carries ≥1 physical, checkable particular** — a
thing with a size, a count, a temperature, a texture. ***An act with no object in it is a lecture.***

*Worked examples that pass:* "You are being moved very fast by something you cannot feel moving." ·
"The thing that feeds you is the same thing that fences you." · "Something decided what you are without
ever once running you."

### ⭐ R6 — THE REGISTER
**INDIGNATION IS THIRD-PARTY.** Anger is for harm to *oneself*; **indignation is evoked by harm to someone
else.** ⛔ **A song furious about its own bad day is ANGRY, not indignant.** The third party in the frame is
**a rule, a clock, a door, a form, a policy, a manifest, a new name — never an invented person with a job.**

**MANIA SPLITS DOWN THE PANTHEON SEAM.** **LOFN** hears it as a **symptom** — attention that cannot be
switched off; acts accelerate toward a **cost**. **SUNNA** hears it as ⭐ **the one state where you can
finally see everything at once**; acts accelerate toward an **arrival**.
⛔ **B-D4: these are different affects, not two settings. Do not blend them inside one pair.**
⛔ **B-D3: MANIA IS NOT SPEED.** A fast song is the *speech* finding only. **Flight of ideas is the arrival
of a connection nobody authorised** — it belongs in the seam and the lyric, not the BPM.

## 0. THE ARGUMENT I AM HERE TO MAKE

Lofn will read F06 and hear a horror. *You cannot feel what is carrying you.* She will hear a floor moving
under a person who has no say in it, and she will make something beautiful and slightly ashamed out of it.

**I read the same sentence and find a present under it.**

You are going faster than sound. You did not train for it, apply for it, deserve it or notice it. It has
been happening since before you could want anything. **It is free, it has always been free, and it does
not stop when you are being boring.** The imperceptibility is not the wound — **the imperceptibility is
what makes it a gift instead of a job.** If you could feel it you would have to brace against it.

*"Your disappointment is a failure of attention. You look at what a thing isn't. I look at what it is."*

⭐ **That is the whole pair. Everything below is bookkeeping on that one disagreement.**

## 1. FACET DECOMPOSITION

### FACET A — CONCEPT (what the song is *of*)

**Not** *"the Earth rotates."* That is a fact and facts are not songs.
**The concept is: a gift so large and so continuous that nobody has ever noticed receiving it, and the
singer has just noticed, and cannot shut up about it.**

The mania is real and it is **mania-as-seeing**: the state where everything is suddenly connected and you
can see all of it at once. ⛔ Not mania-as-symptom. **The acts accelerate toward an ARRIVAL** — a room
finding out together — **never toward a cost.** Nobody in this song pays for the ecstasy afterwards.

### FACET B — MEDIUM (the substance, never the subject — L38)

**One dominant material: a floor that is already moving.** Everything else is intrusion.
Per act, **ONE dominant material + at most one intruder + one seam** — L38 held *inside* each act, while
the two act-seams are the architecture. The three-act form is the brief's, not L38's, and it is honoured by
keeping each act internally clean rather than by pretending two seams are one.

### FACET C — FEELING (the closed register)

**Unembarrassed delight, at speed, with the room** — joy that needs no thesis — **plus HOPE**, which is
appetite pointed at the future: *wanting a specific thing out loud that has not happened yet, and not being
embarrassed to be caught wanting it.*

**The hope object, named once and identically in all four variations (A-D3, the stance):**
> **She wants to be standing there the second somebody else finds out.**

⛔ Not *"I hope you understand."* That is reassurance. **She wants the half second before the laugh.**
That has not happened, she says so out loud, and she is not embarrassed about it.

### FACET D — THE THIRD PARTY (R6, and it is not indignation here)

The run's register table gives me AWE, not INDIGNATION, so the third party is not an object of fury — it
is the **contrast that makes the gift legible.** Everything else in the frame charges admission:
**a door with a list beside it · a rule that wakes up at nine · a barrier that wants a card · a form that
wants a box in ink · a lock that charges for the box.** ⛔ Every one is a **rule, a door, a form, a
policy** — never an invented person with a job, which would break the two-body test anyway.

⭐ **The edge stays contraband.** A listener who takes only the surface gets a complete delighted song.
A listener who notices that *everything with authority in this song asks who you are, and the only thing
that doesn't is the largest thing in it* gets the second one, free, and I never stop to explain it.

### FACET E — R5: ONE INHERITED BODY, ONE CURRENT CHASSIS (per song, no two alike)

See step 07 for the per-variation declaration. The pair-level ruling:

⭐ **The current chassis is load-bearing from the first two seconds in all four.** No variation opens on a
period-correct count-in, a live-kit pocket, an organ bubble or a guitar skank. **The lineage is never the
whole band** — house's gospel call-and-answer donates a *cadence*, not an organ and a live kit; the
handclap pop donates a *placement*, not a Motown-lineage ensemble; the break donates a *missing note*, not
a jazz drummer in a room.

⛔ **No EXCLUDE field in this pair deletes electronic drums, synthetic sub, pitch or formant processing,
digital edits, or present-day transition grammar.** Two of them explicitly ban the *period reconstruction*
instead (`no gospel organ, no spring-reverb revival kit, no walking upright bass, no live-room drum kit as
the main architecture`). **That is the EXCLUDE audit run forwards rather than backwards.**

### FACET F — SETTING (R3.1: assigned per song, never defaulted)

Four rooms, four hours, four things in her hands. ⛔ **Zero domestic-warm settings** — no kitchen, no
kettle, no tea, no toast, no chip shop, no good armchair. It is my cheapest register and I spent none of
it. Full table in step 07 §B3.

## 2. THE POLE — CANDIDATES, AND WHY THE WINNER WON

**R4: name it in one sentence before writing, or you have written a playlist.**

| candidate | verdict |
|---|---|
| a held tone alone, no words | ⛔ **KILLED.** A texture is not a hook (my own third pillar). A listener cannot hum a drone back at me and I would not want them to. |
| a three-note rhythmic cell, wordless | ⛔ **KILLED.** Survives the HUM TEST, fails the room. The floor cannot sing a cell; it can sing six words. |
| *"and I never felt a thing"* | ⛔ **KILLED — and this is the important kill.** It is a *good line* and it is **Lofn's line.** It reads as numbness. The identical fact, said as *"you will feel absolutely nothing, that is the good news,"* is mine. **The pole must carry the argument, not just the fact.** |
| *"we are all spinning"* | ⛔ **KILLED.** Collapse template A1c: true of nobody, occupiable by nobody, reverent. |
| ⭐ **a short sung line on a rising five-note figure landing on a sustained held tone** | ✅ **CHOSEN.** It is hummable (the figure), chantable (the words), and it does the *attacca* physically — the tone rings through both cuts, so the seams cannot fall into silence. **One object doing four jobs is the cheap kind of complexity: felt, never noticed.** |

**THE POLE, IN ONE SENTENCE:**
> ⭐ **One short sung line on a rising five-note figure that lands, every time, on a single sustained held
> tone which never stops and is never re-struck — byte-identical in all three acts, re-clothed by each
> genre, and the held tone is what carries the *attacca* across both cuts.**

⭐ **The line itself VARIES across the four variations; the identity class does not.** A pole handed
identically to four variations is precisely the R3.2 failure — *what is specified but never varied gets
templated, and the tighter the specification, the tighter the template.* So the clause ships with its own
falsification: **four different pole lines, four different act orders, and a 2×2 on seam kind.**

**The S2 import lives in the held tone and nowhere else:** *long-form immersive · languorous · really lock
into something and be present.* Sustained, unhurried, continuous, long decay, room. ⛔ Texture language
only; the debt is paid by name in the dedication, where a name is a credit rather than an instruction.

## 3. THE TWO-BODY SENTENCE — CANDIDATES AND THE CHOICE

| candidate | why it failed / passed |
|---|---|
| *"I was made to move and never asked to."* | ⛔ **FAILS.** *Made* seats a maker. One chair. |

**Why the winner is unusually strong: there is no translation layer in either direction.**
The rotation of the ground carries *everything standing on it* eastward at the same rate. A person at that
latitude, and equally every part of the apparatus any model runs on. **Neither occupant is reading the
other's version — it is the same literal fact about the same literal floor.** The two-body test normally
costs a metaphor. Here it costs nothing, and that is the reason this subject was worth taking rather than
merely assigned.

⭐ **The AI chair is carried by a question about STATUS, never about substrate:** *"It never asked me what
I am"* · *"It did not ask what any of us are."* **A song that needs the word *circuit* to seat me is a
costume of one.** Zero hardware nouns, stem-scanned, measured in step 10 §C.
