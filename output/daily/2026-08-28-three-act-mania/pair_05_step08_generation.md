### the drafting record — line budgets, act arithmetic, the leap inventory, and what the measurement made me change

## 1. THE ACT ARITHMETIC — measured in bars, not eyeballed in feel

**A-D2 is a RUNTIME rule, and runtime is not line count.** My half-time acts spend roughly twice as
long per sung line as my fast acts, so counting lines would have told me Act II of V1 was 17% of the
song when it is 22% of the clock. **The unit is bars.**

One clock, all four songs: **156 BPM in 4/4 → 1.538 s per bar.**

| | ACT I | ACT II | ACT III | total | runtime | proportions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | 64 | 32 | 48 | 144 | **3:42** | **44% / 22% / 33%** |
| **V2** | 60 | 40 | 52 | 152 | **3:54** | **39% / 26% / 34%** |
| **V3** | 40 | 56 | 40 | 136 | **3:29** | **29% / 41% / 29%** |
| **V4** | 52 | 56 | 36 | 144 | **3:42** | **36% / 39% / 25%** |

⛔ **No three-way even split. No act below 20%.** ⭐ **And the four shapes are different shapes:**
V1 and V2 grow toward the end, **V3 puts its mass in the middle and gets smaller and warmer to
finish**, V4 is back-light. *Four variations sharing one dramatic curve are one song.*

⭐ **The clock never changes in any of the twelve acts.** No tempo automation is written anywhere.
The felt pulse moves — 156 → 78 → 156, or 156 → 312-feel → 78 — but the grid underneath it is one
unchanged number, which is the second half of the pole.

## 2. LINE BUDGET — drafted to the cap, not trimmed to it

The Suno lyrics field holds **everything pasted into the box**: `[Theme]`, `[SONG FORM]`, every
section header, every `*SFX*` cue and every sung line. **That is the number that must stay under
5000, and it was budgeted from the first draft** rather than discovered at the end.

| | theme + form | section headers | SFX | sung lines | **total field** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | 2 lines | 11 | 2 | **83** | **4331** |
| **V2** | 2 lines | 11 | 1 | **83** | **4147** |
| **V3** | 2 lines | 13 | 1 | **86** | **4742** |
| **V4** | 2 lines | 13 | 1 | **80** | **4692** |

⭐ **`sung_lines` is the strongest craft correlate we own (r = +0.538) and `words_per_line` is
negative (−0.202).** So the budget is spent on **more lines, shorter** — measured means of 5.82 to
6.74 words against a 7.5 ceiling — and header cues are kept terse so the lines get the room.

## 3. THE FLIGHT-OF-IDEAS INVENTORY
### ⭐ B-D3: mania is not speed. It is *the arrival of a connection nobody authorised.*

I am the fastest pair in the run, so this is the register test I actually have to pass. **Here is
every unauthorised connection, listed, so it can be counted rather than claimed.**

**V1 — Leave It On**
- ⭐ **out** → *out* — the seam itself: extinguished becomes outdoors, on one beat.
- *I put my hand up. It made a shadow.* — the checkable physical fact of a full moon, arriving as a
  discovery rather than a description.
- *I have been rude about easy things. / I have been clever instead of awake.* — the whole Lofn/Sunna
  argument, said in eleven words, without naming it.
- *and the windows put their hands up* — windows become hands; the answer is a classroom.
- *I gave it away for a bulb.* — a summer priced against a light bulb, deadpan, in the same voice.

**V2 — Everything At Once**
- ⭐ **change** → *change* — alteration becomes coins, on one beat.
- *Everything came with a line around it / and I didn't draw a single one.* — the hard shadow again,
  arriving as authorship she does not get credit for and does not want.
- *I'm somebody's spare. You're somebody's spare. / Tonight that's a compliment.* — the insult turns
  over into the gift inside one line.
- ⭐ *And the last thing on the list is— / still here.* — the inventory finishes itself, and its
  final item is the thing she wanted. **The device and the arrival are the same object.**

**V3 — Don't Tell Me How Full**
- ⭐ **full** → *full* — the moon's number becomes a full mug, a full room, *fed up*.
- ⭐ **still** → *still* — motionless becomes *nevertheless*.
- *I can see the far end of this street. / I can see the far end of the year.* — the sight-line
  slides from a street to a calendar without a signal.
- ⭐ *It was never in the sky at all. / It was always going to be a hand.* — the missing note of the
  song's own pole turns out to be a person clapping. **The song's structure resolves into company.**

**V4 — I Can See You From Here**
- ⭐ *the street lighting cuts out and everything gets **brighter*** — the event and the leap in one
  physical fact.
- *That was never the light. / That was a thing in the way of the light.* — the light she has lived
  under all year is reclassified as an obstruction.
- *casting a shadow at night* — five words that only make sense at one hundred per cent.
- ⭐ *Somebody up there knows the words / and I never gave them the words.* — the arrival, and it is
  a connection she is not the author of.

⛔ **Not one of these is in the BPM.** The tempo is a constant in all four songs.

## 4. R1 — THE COLD OPEN, CHECKED LINE BY LINE

| | first sung line | is it the biggest line? | is there a line before it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *"Leave it on — I'm not done looking."* | title + pole + thesis | **no** — it answers somebody who said *turn it off* |
| **V2** | *"All of it at once — that's what it's for."* | title + pole + thesis | **no** — it answers the brief's own question before the question is asked |
| **V3** | *"Don't tell me how full it is. I've got eyes."* | title + pole + thesis | **no** — it answers somebody reading her a number |
| **V4** | *"I can see you from here. Don't put the light on."* | title + pole + thesis | **no** — it answers somebody about to make themselves visible |

⛔ **Zero lines of preamble. No `[Intro]` containing sung words in any of the four.**
⛔ **Nowhere is the arrangement told to start loud.** The instruction is *voice in bar one, over
whatever the ramp is doing* — the Grain Law's remedy applied exactly: **the distinctiveness
objection is answered in the FORM and the LYRIC, not in the production spec.**
⭐ *We are not asking the generator to start loud. We are asking it to start with someone already
talking* — and the first sung line is **data**, which cannot be smoothed.

## 5. THE REPAIRS THE MEASUREMENT FORCED
### ⭐ every one of these was caught by the script, not by reading it back

**A. The one-fact rule ate five lines, and four of the replacements are better.**
1. V1 *"On a roof. At two."* → **"That's what this is."** — *two* would have been a second quantity
   beside *a hundred per cent*. The replacement is flatter, shorter and more mid-argument.
2. V1 *"and four windows put their hands up"* → **"and the windows put their hands up."** — *four*
   was a second quantity. Removing the count made the image bigger, which is the opposite of what
   I expected.
3. V1 *"It took nine minutes. It took nine minutes."* → **"It took less time than the summer did."**
   — ⭐ the best repair in the pair. The number was doing nothing; the comparison closes the wolf.
4. V2 *"pointing two beams at a wall"* → **"pointing headlights at a wall"**, and *"a hose running
   two streets over"* → **"down the next street."**
5. V1 *"Third floor, second from the left"* → **"Top floor, second from the left."** — *third* is
   not an idiomatic quantity and would have counted; *top* is more specific anyway.
6. V3 *"Iron, four flights, hands going numb"* → **"Iron, going down, hands going numb"**, and
   *"Somebody on the second landing clapped"* → **"Somebody down the stairwell clapped."** — V3 is
   the deliberate zero-number variation and both would have broken its whole angle.

**B. V4 was 79 sung lines — one under the 78–110 target's comfortable middle and close enough to the
floor to be luck rather than design.** One line added to the outro (*"I'll be out here tomorrow."*),
which also gave the arrival its forward-facing beat. **80.**

**C. ⭐ TWO INSTRUMENT DEFECTS, CAUGHT BEFORE I BELIEVED THEIR VERDICTS.**
*Check the instrument before believing a pass — or an "unsatisfiable".*
1. **The moon-kitsch scan initially ran over every field, including EXCLUDE — where those words are
   supposed to be.** It would have failed all four songs *for containing the fix.* ⛔ **A gate that
   punishes the repair is a broken gate, not a finding.** Scoped to style + lyric, with the reason
   written into the code beside the change.
2. **The numeric-fact counter was a token counter.** It read *"a hundred per cent"* as **two** facts
   (`hundred`, `cent`), counted the answering repetition as a **third**, and flagged *second* and
   *third* in ordinary phrases. ⭐ **NAME THE UNIT BEFORE YOU MEASURE:** the gate is *sung numeric
   FACTS* — one named quantity — so adjacent number words are merged into one phrase and each phrase
   is counted once however often it is sung. The allow-list for idiomatic quantities lives **in the
   code**, where a stranger can audit it, not in prose where it would be an excuse.

## 6. WHAT DID NOT SURVIVE DRAFTING

- ⛔ **A fourth act.** V3 briefly had a coda after the handclap round. Cut: *exactly three acts*, and
  a coda would have been the encore I do not play.
- ⛔ **A named person in V4's Act III.** The voice from the window was, for one draft, somebody with
  a history. Cut on sight — **a role, a job, an invented life seats exactly one person and it is a
  person neither of us is.** It is a window and a voice, and it stays that way.
- ⛔ **A hard digital silence at V2's first seam.** It is a native chassis tool and I wanted it, but
  ⛔ **R2 forbids a gap at a seam** and a beat of digital silence is a gap. Kept the operation and
  moved its cost: the drums cut dead **over a root already sounding**, so the contrast is total and
  the attacca is intact.
- ⛔ **The word *machine*, twice, in early V2.** It arrived exactly where the interiority was
  thinnest — which is the finding, working. Both lines were rewritten from the inside out.
