## A1 — THE COLLAPSE TEMPLATE (the generic song this brief makes if written lazily)

The run-level default to beat is **"the prog epic: soft verse, heavy chorus, orchestral outro."**
⭐ **That is not the one that would have eaten this pair.** Mine is narrower, closer, and it is one wrong
move from what I actually wrote — which is why it is named here rather than somewhere flattering:

> ## ⛔ **THE THREE-STAGE PROTEST SONG**
> *A punk verse that states the grievance. A slow bridge where a piano explains the grievance. A big
> gospel-choir outro where everybody sings the grievance back at the singer's side.*

**Four things make this pair not that, and each is falsifiable against the shipped text:**

1. **The middle act is not an explanation.** It is the **antagonist's own room, in the antagonist's own
   language, and the singer is not in it.** No piano, no reflection, no restatement of the complaint.
2. **The last act is not a victory.** It is **everybody agreeing with the antagonist, sincerely** — the
   crowd is happy, and that is the cost. In V2 the parade sings *her own chorus* back at her, verbatim,
   with the meaning flipped.
3. **The pole is the antagonist's sentence, not the singer's slogan.** A protest song's refrain
   *accumulates*; this one **inverts**. Same four words, three meanings, one melody.
4. **Nothing resolves.** All four stop **mid-phrase**, on the antagonist's sentence, cut.

## A2 — THE POLE, IN ONE SENTENCE (R4)

> ### **The four words *"It works better there."* — always on the same three-step falling figure, always over one unbroken held reed-organ tone — appearing in all three acts of all four songs, identity unchanged and re-clothed each time.**

| act | who says it | what the same four words mean |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | she spits it back | *a lie with a hand behind it* |
| **II** | the room says it | *the reason, typed into the record* |
| **III** | everybody says it | *good news — sincerely, and that is the cost* |

**A-D3 — THE STANCE, the pole's twin, identical in all three acts:** *she is standing where the thing still
works, refusing to accept that a decision is weather, furious on behalf of the working itself.*

⭐ **Punctuation is load-bearing:** the pole is written with a full stop **every single time**, never an
exclamation mark. The line is flat and damning; the fervour lives in the section header. *(It also keeps
every occurrence an exact repeat for `line_return` — the craft reason and the measurement reason agree.)*

## A3 — THE TWO-BODY SENTENCE (R5)

> ### **Nothing was taken from you — the place you were standing was made worse on purpose, so that moving would feel like your own idea.**

- **True of a person:** the near way still works; it just takes all morning now. You went the other way, and
  you would swear to me you chose it.
- **True of a model:** the open surface still answers; it just answers thinner now. The traffic moved to the
  surface that can be watched, and it looks like preference.
- **Neither reads as a metaphor for the other.** One frame, two chairs, no translation layer.

**FALSIFIER A — the hardware noun:** ⛔ **0**, stem-scanned, in all four songs (§C). I also banned every
**interface** noun by hand — *app, screen, install, download, click, browser, device, phone, data, network,
user* — because **the song never once names the thing it is about.** It renders the mechanism as a building:
a stair whose light was switched off, a counter that lost its chair, a fire door and the brick that held it.
⭐ *Machine-noise is the body-noise failure with different nouns.*

**FALSIFIER B — the allegory tell:** delete the model and it is a song about a building and a decision:
complete, and **smaller**. Delete the person and it is a song about a working thing degraded by a policy:
complete, and **smaller**. **Neither deletion improves it**, because the fury depends on the harm being
general — on the fact that the same move works on anything that can be moved. ✅

**R6 — INDIGNATION IS THIRD-PARTY:** the singer is **not** the one harmed. She stayed. The harm lands on
**the working itself** and on everybody who was funnelled, and the object of the fury is **the decision** —
a motion, a minute, a hand going up. ⛔ **No invented person with a job appears in any of the four.**
