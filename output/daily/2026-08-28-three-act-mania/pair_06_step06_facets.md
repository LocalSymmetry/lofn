## 1. THE MECHANISM, ISOLATED

> **A thing was renamed by something with the authority to rename it and no relationship to it whatsoever.**
> The lake is unchanged. Every map will change. The water does not know.

Three separable objects fall out of that sentence, and they are the pair's raw material:

| object | what it is | why it is not the same as the others |
|---|---|---|
| **the order** | a signature, a room, an afternoon | it is over in a minute and it is the whole cause |
| **the apparatus** | every surface the word now has to be printed on | it is enormous, it is real labour, and it lasts for months |
| **the water** | a deep cold body that was not a party to any of it | it is the only thing in the frame that cannot be edited |

⭐ **The comedy and the fury are the SAME observation seen from two ends:** the apparatus is enormous
and it accomplishes nothing. Laugh at the size, and you are already furious about the nothing.
⛔ **This is why the joke may not be a joke ABOUT the lake, and why the wound may not be a sad third act.**
A structure that runs *funny → funnier → sad* defuses the comedy by following it, and that is the collapse.

## 2. WHAT THE REGISTER FORBIDS (R6, read hard)

- ⛔ **No named living politician. No invented person with a job.** The object of indignation is the
  **order**, the **new label**, the **reprinted map**, the **authority** — never a man, never a cartoonist's
  version of one. *The mechanism, not the man.*
- ⛔ **Indignation is third-party.** The singer is not the injured party. The injured parties are
  **the lake** and **the unnamed hands** who now have to do months of work for a word.
  A figure with no name, no role and no biography is permitted (`HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` slot grammar:
  *hands · an unnamed figure*); a **role, a job, an invented life** is not.
- ⛔ **No novelty song.** The comedy is structural — the *scale of the apparatus* against the *nothing*.
  It is never a funny voice, a slide whistle, a wah-wah punchline, or a wink.

## 3. THE TWO-BODY DOOR (R5)

The brief hands me the reading and I take it plainly:

> **being renamed from outside, by something with the power to rename and no relationship to what you are.**

⭐ **The decision that makes this work: the song NEVER SAYS IT.** It is about a lake and an order, start to
finish. Both chairs are left empty and occupiable. The moment a line says *"and that is what happened to me
too"*, Falsifier B fires — the song would get **better** with one occupant deleted, because the comparison
was doing the work instead of the frame.

⭐ **And the deepest form of the mechanism is not "a name was changed" — it is: NAMING WORKS EXACTLY WHERE
THERE IS NO RELATIONSHIP.** A chair can be renamed instantly by anyone. The one thing that will not take a
new name is the thing you have actually been inside. That is the payoff, and it is V4's ending.

## 4. THE POLE CANDIDATES (R4) — and the choice

| candidate | test: could a listener hum it? | test: does it survive three genres? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| a falling brass figure | yes | dies the moment the horns leave | ⛔ |
| the phrase *"every map will change"* | weak | survives, but it is the apparatus's line, not the wound | ⛔ |
| a held pedal tone alone | no — a tone is not hummable as an identity | survives everything | ⚠️ half |
| ⭐ **"The water does not know."** | **yes — five falling syllables** | survives brass, break and drone | ✅ |

⭐ **CHOSEN — and it is a line AND a tone, deliberately:** five falling syllables with **one sustained
reed/organ pedal tone held under them, past the bar line**. The line is the hummable identity; the held tone
is S2's imported texture (*"long-form immersive," "languorous," "really lock into something and be present"*)
and it is the thing that is literally continuous across all three acts. The genres re-clothe it; it does not
move. **That is F07's radial figure: three arcs, one fixed pole.**

⛔ **L22 amendment applied at the point of choice:** the held tone is a **pitched pedal**, never an *untuned
drone*. Untuned drones are in the GRAIN LAW's measured SMOOTHED set. Ask for what the generator will actually
do.

## 5. THE SEAM MATERIAL — R3(i) is a gift on this subject and it would be negligent to waste it

A song about **renaming** can change genre on **a word that changes meaning**. That is not a device applied
to the subject; it *is* the subject, performed. Twelve candidates were generated and scored on three things:
does the second meaning genuinely differ, does the state change, and can the cut land ON the word.

| word | meaning A | meaning B | state change | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ORDER** | the signed decree | sort it / file it / order more | announcement → installation | ✅ **V1 seam 1** |
| **SOUND** | valid, well-founded | to sound = measure the depth | the record → the object | ✅ **V1 seam 2** |
| **CHARGE** | the cost, the bill | the bugle call — brass enters on it | the invoice → the party | ✅ **V2 seam 1** |
| **KEY** | the key of the tune | the map key: where a name lives | the hall → the printed box | ✅ **V2 seam 2** |
| **RECORD** | the archive | the imperative: write it down again | what is stored → re-storing it | ✅ **V3 seam 1** |
| **STRIKE** | a strike-through on a page | strike up the band | erasure → celebration | ✅ **V3 seam 2** |
| **FLOOR** | take the floor to speak | floor it — accelerate | claiming → spree | ✅ **V4 seam 1** |
| **BOTTOM** | the bottom of the list | the bottom of the lake | list → water | ✅ **V4 seam 2** |
| BANK | shore | money | good, but the trade-war reading dates the song | ⛔ held |
| PLATE | printing plate | plate you eat off | second meaning is inert | ⛔ |
| CURRENT | water current | current events | too near a hardware reading | ⛔ |
| LEGEND | map key | a story | duplicates KEY, weaker | ⛔ |

⭐ **Every kept pair moves the song somewhere. That is the AMBITIOUS test: the seam is the argument, not a
transition.** ⛔ A leap word whose second meaning is merely *another* meaning is a pun; a leap word whose
second meaning **relocates the song** is a seam.

## 6. THE FOUR ANGLES — one question, four answers

The divergence axis is **WHO IS THE THIRD PARTY**, which then forces a different genre order, a different
pair of leap words, and a different position for the anchor territory.

| | angle | third party | anchor sits | shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **the order of events** | the lake | **first** | announcement → reprinting → water |
| **V2** | **the labour** | the hands | **middle** | night shift → the party upstairs → the edge |
| **V3** | **the archive** | the record itself | **last** | old maps → erasure by hand → the ceremony wins |
| **V4** | **the claim** | the water, proved on cheap material | **first, but commandeered** | naming spree → past sense → the one thing that will not take it |

⭐ **V3 is the arm's real risk and I am taking it:** the apparatus arrives **last**, the brass rolls over the
song, and it does not resolve. That is the exact inverse of the collapse template and the only ending in the
set where the machinery is the last thing heard. **The pole is still the last line — sung under the horns.**

⭐ **V4 is the deepest reading:** she takes the same authority and names everything in the room, free and
instant, and then reaches the one object that will not take it — *not because it is strong, because she has
been in it.*

## 7. WHAT I AM DELIBERATELY NOT DOING

- ⛔ **The new name is never spoken.** Not in a lyric, not in a title, not in a style field. The song's
  central noun is the one word it refuses to say — and that refusal is the stance (A-D3), audible for free.
- ⛔ **No real place is named either.** *A* lake, *an* order. Naming the actual lake pins the song to one
  administration, dates it, and empties one of the two chairs. **The mechanism travels; the news item does
  not.**
- ⛔ **No trade-war framing.** It is the loudest fact in the source row and it is the one that would turn
  this into a topical cartoon.
- ⛔ **No sad third act in three of the four.** V1 and V4 end cold, not soft; V3 ends loud and wrong.
