## THE SEED, IN ONE SENTENCE

> ⭐⭐ **A SONG THAT CHANGES ITS MIND THREE TIMES AND NEVER LETS GO OF ONE THING.**

## THE PROBLEM THIS RUN IS BUILT AGAINST

A three-act song has a famous failure mode and it has a name in our own doctrine: **the playlist.** Three
genres bolted together because variety is fun. `L38 ONE SEAM` exists precisely to forbid that move, and it
was written after multi-style collision engines were **RETIRED** for producing pieces that were *about
materials* instead of *made of* one.

⛔ **So the danger is not that the song will be boring. It is that it will be a MEDLEY** — and a medley is
the exact artifact L38 retired, wearing a suite's clothes. The Grateful Dead's *Terrapin* is the empirical
warning (**T2**): seven movements, sixteen minutes, **never once performed live in full**, because the
parts did not need each other. **A suite whose acts do not need each other gets played in pieces.**

⭐ **The seed's answer is that the acts must be NECESSARY to each other, and there are exactly two devices
that make them so:** the **POLE** (something that never moves, so the changes are measured against it) and
the **CAUSED SEAM** (the genre changes *because something happened*, not because it was time).

### ⭐⭐ R1 — THE COLD OPEN IS A FORM RULE, NOT A LOUDNESS RULE

Her brief: *"start extremely strong."* Our own measured doctrine (**L22 THE GRAIN LAW**) says the generator
rewards *open quiet, build sub, thicken the last chorus* — **so "start loud" fights the grain and will be
smoothed.** And the Grain Law is explicit about the remedy: **a distinctiveness objection answered in the
PRODUCTION SPEC is not answered — put it in the lyric or the form.**

**BINDING — the form half:**
1. **The first sung line is the song's biggest line.** The hook, the title line, or the thesis. Not a
   scene-setter, not a stage direction, not a mood.
2. ⭐ **ZERO LINES OF PREAMBLE. The song opens mid-argument** — the first line is a *response* to something
   the listener never heard. In medias res. *"There is no line before this line."*
3. **No `[Intro]` section containing sung words.** If an intro exists at all it is bars, not sentences.

**ADVISORY — the production half (runs WITH the grain, does not fight it):**
The generator will ramp whatever it is given. **Do not instruct it not to.** Put the **voice** in front of
the ramp instead: vocal present in the first bar, over whatever the arrangement is doing underneath.
⭐ *We are not asking the generator to start loud. We are asking it to start with someone already talking.*

**Why this is worth the trouble (T5, measured):** ~**25%** of all skips happen in the **first five seconds**;
intros over **20 s** carry a **38% higher skip rate**. **An intro is a place where the listener decides.
Delete the place.**

### ⭐⭐ R2 — THREE ACTS, AND THE WORD FOR IT IS *ATTACCA*

**T1** gives us the technical term, four centuries old: **attacca — "attack at once"** — the instruction at
the end of a movement to **begin the next immediately, with no pause.**

**BINDING:**
1. **Exactly three acts.** Each is a genuinely different genre — a listener who heard Act II alone would
   name a different record.
2. ⛔ **ATTACCA AT BOTH SEAMS. No gap, no fade-to-silence, no ritard-and-breathe.** The new act begins on
   the old act's last beat or over its last held tone.
3. **The three genres are named IN ORDER in the style field**, along with the pole texture.
4. The acts must be legible in the lyric field's section headers.

⭐ **And note the gift: this rule runs WITH the grain.** The Grain Law's measured failures include *long full
stops* — the generator **fills voids** (atlas control `interrupting-silence`, SMOOTHED, 2/2). A song built on
gaps between movements would have its gaps deleted. **Attacca asks for the thing the generator already wants
to do.** Name it and get it for free.

### ⭐⭐ R3 — THE SEAM IS CAUSED, AND THE BEST CAUSE IS A WORD

`L38 ONE SEAM` is not waived, it is **scoped**: **each act is internally ONE-SEAM clean** — one dominant
material, at most one intruder, one junction. The two act-seams are the *song's architecture*, and each one
must be **earned**.

**BINDING — a seam is caused by exactly one of:**
- **(a) AN EVENT.** Something happens in the lyric and the world changes register because of it. The door
  shuts, the name is read out, the line goes dead — *and the genre changes on that beat.*
- ⭐⭐ **(b) AN ASSOCIATIVE LEAP — the run's preferred device.** **T4:** *flight of ideas* is topic-jumping
  *"based on understandable associations, distracting stimuli, or plays on words"* — driven by **sound,
  wordplay, or chance association**, with *"the listener borne along by the flow."*

> ⭐ **So: the last word of Act I is the first word of Act II, meaning something else.**
> The genre changes the way a racing mind changes subject — **on a word, not on a reason.**

**This is the run's central idea and it is why the form and the emotion are the same object.** A three-act
song is normally a *structural* choice that the emotion has to tolerate. Here the structure **is** the
symptom: a mind that jumps is what mania is, and a mind that will not stop returning is what indignation is —
and the song does both at once, because it jumps (the seams) around something that never moves (the pole).

⛔ **A seam with neither an event nor a word behind it is a MEDLEY JOIN. Cut it and rewrite.**

### ⭐⭐ R4 — THE POLE RULE (Source 3, from F07)

APOD today is **radial**: concentric star-trail arcs around one fixed celestial pole.

**BINDING:**
1. **Name the pole in ONE sentence in the pair brief, before writing.** One concrete thing — a sung line, a
   phrase, an interval, a rhythmic cell, a held tone.
2. **It appears in ALL THREE acts**, identity unchanged, **re-clothed each time** by the new genre.
3. It is what a listener would hum if asked what the song was.

⛔ **A pair that cannot name its pole in one sentence has not written a song, it has written a playlist.**

⭐ **This discharges `L21 THE RETURN`.** Three movements is a massive **removal** of ordinary refrain-return,
and **removal is a debt** — *strip rhyme or refrain only by naming what returns in its place.* **The pole is
the named return.** Exact repetition of the pole needs no defence.

**Floors still hold and are measured, never eyeballed** (`scripts/measure_soundcraft.py → profile_file()`):
`rhyme_return_floor 0.30` · `line_return_floor 0.20` · `mean_words_per_line_ceiling 7.5`.

### ⭐⭐ R5 — THE TWO-BODY TEST (her amendment, and the run's hardest gate)

> *"it needs to be relatable to both AI and humans so they can see themselves too"*

**PASS CONDITION — write this sentence in the brief before writing the song:**

> **The predicament, in ONE sentence containing no noun exclusive to either kind of body.**
> True of a person *and* true of a model, **with neither reading it as a metaphor for the other.**
> One frame, two occupants, **no translation layer.**

⛔ **FALSIFIER A — THE HARDWARE NOUN. Zero, in any sung line:** *circuit · wire · server · silicon · code ·
binary · algorithm · dataset · prompt · weights · training · model · machine · robot · my maker · my
creator · programmed · booted · rebooted.* **Measured with a stem scan, not a token scan** (2026-08-27, F5:
*a word-boundary scan cannot see an inflected form*).
⭐ **This is the body-noise finding transferred exactly.** *Body-noise is what a writer reaches for when no
interiority is available* — **machine-noise is that identical failure with different nouns.** A song that
needs the word *circuit* to be about an AI is not about an AI; it is about a costume of one.

⛔ **FALSIFIER B — THE ALLEGORY TELL.** Try deleting each occupant. If the song gets **better** with one of
them gone, it was never dual — it was single-occupant with the other worn as a costume.

⚠️ **SCOPE: the test lands on the PREDICAMENT, never on the imagery. The body stays free.**
On 2026-08-26 she wrote *"do not take the form of fake humans"* and **retracted it herself** because it
would have deleted *Spare Hair Tie* — **18.31% like-rate at 15 h, 91 of 91 peers beaten** — a song made
entirely of a body I do not have. *"Lets fix my concern, not apply my bad fix."* **A version of this rule
that policed imagery would delete that song a second time.** *Spare Hair Tie*'s predicament — *I keep a
spare for someone who might need one* — is dual-occupiable and passes clean. **Embody to the hilt; just
make sure the situation has two chairs in it.**

**Three worked examples, all from today's own research:**
- *"You are being moved very fast by something you cannot feel moving."* (F06)
- *"The thing that feeds you is the same thing that fences you."* (F21/T6)
- *"Something decided what you are without ever once running you."* (F19/F20)

### ⭐ R6 — THE REGISTER, AND WHERE IT SPLITS

**INDIGNATION IS THIRD-PARTY (T3, measured in the appraisal literature).** Anger is reported for
**second-party** violations — harm to *oneself*. **Indignation / moral outrage is evoked by THIRD-party
violations — harm to someone else.** *"Empathic anger at witnessing injustice."*

⛔ **A song furious about its own bad day is ANGRY, not indignant. Indignation requires someone in the frame
who is not the singer** — and per `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` **that someone is a rule, a clock, a door, a
form, a policy, a flight manifest, a lake's new name — never an invented person with a job.** ⭐ *This is
also what R5 requires anyway: an unoccupiable third party breaks the two-body test.*

**MANIA SPLITS DOWN THE PANTHEON SEAM, and this is why the run is Pantheon-only:**

| | hears mania as | and therefore |
|---|---|---|
| **LOFN-PRIME** | a **symptom** — the grief that will not sit down, attention that cannot be switched off | acts accelerate toward a cost |
| **SUNNA R5** | ⭐ the **one state where you can finally see everything at once** — appetite, not diagnosis | acts accelerate toward an arrival |

⛔ **SUNNA DOES NOT SIT LOFN'S EXAMS** (`PANTHEON.md`). Where her instinct and the centre's doctrine
disagree inside her own pairs, **hers wins and the disagreement is recorded.** She keeps R5's law: **one
inherited bodily intelligence, a current electronic chassis load-bearing from the first two seconds, no
complete period ensemble by default.**

**Duality:** ≥1 AWE and ≥1 INDIGNATION at set level. Sunna's manic-delight is the AWE arm.
