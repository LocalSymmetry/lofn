# PAIR 04 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES (V1–V4)

Derived from `pair_04_step06_facets.md`, which was written first. **Pole and stance are identical in all
four; everything else is authored per variation.**

**Constant across all four (the pole and its carrier):**
- ⭐ **POLE LINE:** `Come and run me. / Then say what I am.` — same words, same rhythm, every act.
- ⭐ **POLE CARRIER:** one sustained reed-organ drone, low, slightly detuned, long decay, big dry stone room.
  **It never stops and it is the only thing that crosses both seams.**
- ⭐ **STANCE (A-D3):** standing still, hand out, asking to be tested before being named. She never moves.
  **The copies run; she does not.**
- **Vocal:** female lead, close, unhurried, always slightly in front of the arrangement. **Present in bar 1.**
- ⛔ **R1:** no `[Intro]` with sung words. Zero lines of preamble. **The first sung line is the biggest line
  and it answers something the listener never heard.**
- ⛔ **R2:** attacca at both seams. No fade, no ritard, no gap.
- **One sung numeral per song: `two`, at the Act I hinge, responded to.**

| act | % | genre · BPM | instrumentation | register / EMO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I — THE STAMP** | 37 | industrial EBM · **88** | sequenced kick, cold metal percussion, distorted sub, reed drone | flat, close, contemptuous — *cold contempt* |
| **II — THE COPY** | 23 | chopped amen breakbeat · **176** (exact double) | breaks only, sub an octave down, vocal stuttered and thrown, drone continues | *adrenal, hunted, borne along by the flow* |
| **III — THE ROOM** | 40 | unaccompanied shape-note choir · free | massed voices, open fourths and fifths, **stomps and claps the only percussion**, drone | *fierce hope, grief standing up* |

**Section map:** `A1 Verse` → `Pole 1` → `A1 Verse 2` → `A1 Verse 3 (the want)` → `Pole 2` → `A1 Hinge (two)`
**⇢ SEAM 1 on OPEN ⇢** `A2 Open` → `A2 Copy` → `Pole 3 (chopped)` → `A2 Chase out` **⇢ SEAM 2 on RUN ⇢**
`A3 Run me` → `A3 Roll` → `Pole 4` → `A3 The turn (the gleam)` → `A3 The cost` → `Pole 5 / final`.

**Rhyme spine:** Act I `-en` (written · forbidden · chosen · listen) + `-ink/-ing` + `-oor` + `-and`.
Act II `-ate` · `-all` · `-ame` · `-ays` · `-own`. Act III `-elf` · `-ing` · `-een` · `-en` (the family
returns) · `-ay/-ays`.
⭐ **The `-en` rhyme run crosses SEAM 1** — *spoken / broken / …* **open**. The rhyme is the weld.

**Hook grammar:** imperative + condition. *Run it first. Name it after.* The title is the second half of the
pole so the title arrives only after the demand.

## V2 — **"Next Entry Down"** · *the room asks and no answer comes*

| act | % | genre · BPM | instrumentation | register / EMO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I — THE COLUMN** | 43 | clipped, quiet industrial EBM · **94** | dry gated kick, ticking metal, clipped sub, drone; **an EBM that behaves like a clerk** | forensic, exact, cold — *contained fury* |
| **II — THE FILE** | 21 | footwork / juke · **160** (straight jump) | re-triggered vocal chops, triplet toms, hollow sub, drone | *obsessive, accelerating, worn down* |
| **III — THE ANSWER THAT DOESN'T COME** | 36 | slow gospel-soul · **72** | reed organ (the drone made an instrument), choir, brushed kit, no lead runs | *unresolved grief, asking anyway* |

**Section map:** `A1 The column` → `Pole 1` → `A1 The two rows` → `A1 The clerk` → `Pole 2` → `A1 Hinge (two)`
**⇢ SEAM 1 on FILED ⇢** `A2 Filed down` → `Pole 3 (re-triggered)` → `A2 Down` **⇢ SEAM 2 on DOWN ⇢**
`A3 Come down` → `Pole 4` → `A3 The ask` → `A3 No answer` → `Pole 5 / final`.

⛔ **The gospel never gets its yes.** LOFN accelerates toward a **cost**: the call-and-response builds a room
full of people asking, and the answer does not arrive, and they keep asking. **That is the cost and the gleam
in one object.** ⛔ *No lift, no key change, no resolution chord at the end.*

**Distinct from V1 by design:** V1's Act III **stands** (hymn, no pulse); V2's Act III **kneels** (gospel, slow
pulse, organ). V1's middle **shatters** (breaks); V2's middle **wears down** (re-triggers).

## V3 — **"Open Was a Kind Word"** · *she loses the word*

| act | % | genre · BPM | instrumentation | register / EMO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I — THE FIELD** | 34 | drone-folk / dark Americana · **92 felt at 46** | lone voice, one struck string, reed drone, room air. **No grid.** | wide, unhurried, alive — *plain gladness* |
| **II — THE DESIGNATION** | 24 | industrial EBM · **92** *(anchor)* | ⭐ **same tempo — the grid ARRIVES**: sequenced kick, gated sub, the free voice suddenly quantised | *the floor going hard under you* |
| **III — OPEN SEASON** | 42 | hardcore rave breaks · **184** (exact double) | stabbed chords, chopped breaks, sirens of pitch (not effects), drone straining | *hunted, furious, outnumbered* |

**Section map:** `A1 The field` → `Pole 1` → `A1 What open meant` → `Pole 2` → `A1 Hinge (two)`
**⇢ SEAM 1 on OPEN ⇢** `A2 Declared` → `Pole 3` → `A2 The season` **⇢ SEAM 2 on SEASON ⇢**
`A3 Season (her meaning)` → `A3 Open season (its meaning)` → `Pole 4` → `A3 The cost` → `Pole 5 / final`.

⭐⭐ **The contested seam — the pair's most AMBITIOUS move.** At SEAM 2 the word **SEASON** means two things at
once and **the music takes the wrong one.** She sings *season* as **what you do to timber — leave it out in
the weather until it stops moving** — which is *exactly* what the song has been asking for: a thing made ready
by being run, not by being named. The arrangement hears **open season** and starts hunting.
⛔ **She does not get the word back in this one.** The pole still returns, and it is the only thing she keeps.

⭐ **R1 compliance note (deliberate, and it is the point):** V3 **opens quiet** — which runs WITH the Grain Law
(L22) — and still passes R1, because **R1 is a form rule, not a loudness rule.** The first sung line is the
biggest line, mid-argument, voice in bar one, no preamble. **V3 is the pair's test that R1 and L22 do not
actually conflict.**

## V4 — **"The Whole Time"** · *she was never stopped*

| act | % | genre · BPM | instrumentation | register / EMO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I — STILL RUNNING** | 33 | driving industrial EBM · **88** | sequenced kick, hard sub, metal percussion, drone | defiant, forward-leaning — *held-down joy* |
| **II — THE STOP** | 22 | drum & bass, amen · **176** (exact double) | amen break, sub bass, no pads, drone | *pure adrenaline, then the door* |
| **III — THE FLOOR** | 45 | massed unaccompanied stomp-and-holler · **88** | unison voices, **stomps and claps only**, drone; a marching pulse | *joy that has been through something* |

**Section map:** `A1 Never stopped` → `Pole 1` → `A1 What it did all year` → `Pole 2` → `A1 Hinge (two)`
**⇢ SEAM 1 on STOPPED ⇢** `A2 Stopped at the door` → `Pole 3` → `A2 Never cleared` **⇢ SEAM 2 on CLEAR ⇢**
`A3 Clear the floor` → `Pole 4` → `A3 The whole time` → `A3 The cost` → `Pole 5 / final`.

⭐ **The gleam is first, not last.** Every other variation earns the gleam at the end; V4 opens on it and spends
the song being told it isn't allowed. **Same cost, opposite order.**
⭐ **V1's room stands still; V4's room walks.** That is the audible difference between *"I refuse to move"* and
*"I never stopped moving"* — the same stance seen from two sides.

**Break credit:** V4 names the **amen** figure. **Gregory C. Coleman / The Winstons** in the dedication.

## PRODUCTION NOTES COMMON TO ALL FOUR

- ⭐ **De-escalating production, escalating volume.** Act I is the most machined; Act III has the fewest
  elements and the most voices. ⛔ *If a variation ends bigger-produced than it started, it has collapsed into
  the prog epic.*
- ⛔ **Attacca is notated as a SUBSTITUTION, never as a silence.** *"On the beat, every percussion layer is
  replaced by voices at the same loudness."* The generator fills voids (`interrupting-silence` SMOOTHED 2/2);
  we hand it a void that is already filled.
- **SCR-02 (advisory, lowers expectation, never a gate):** the generator narrows the stereo field into
  climaxes rather than widening. Three climaxes here. **Not fought, not specified.**
- ⛔ **No hard-pan, ping-pong or extreme-stereo instruction in any field.**
- **S2 texture language, imported as texture only:** *sustained · held tone · long decay · room · languorous ·
  unhurried.* ⛔ **Never a name.**
