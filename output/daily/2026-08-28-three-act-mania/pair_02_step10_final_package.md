## A1 — THE COLLAPSE TEMPLATE (the generic song this brief produces if written lazily)

> ### ⛔ **"THE ROPE SONG."**
> Fingerpicked acoustic verse about being trapped. A swell. A full band arrives on the word *free*. A key
> change. An orchestral outro of acceptance while a string section says it was worth it.

**Three specific things that make it the collapse:**
1. **It gets DENSER as it goes** — instruments arrive to signify triumph.
2. **It treats the tether as removable.** It is not: the stem comes out of the body. ⭐ **The escape verse is
   a factual error**, and a song that wants the cord gone wants to starve faster and does not know it.
3. **It ends resolved.** The fable does not resolve; it *runs out.*

**Second collapse, specific to a three-act brief: ⛔ THE MEDLEY** — three genres because variety is fun.
Defeated by R4 (one pole) and R3 (both seams caused by a word), and measured in §C.

**Avoidance, checkable:** V1 ends emptier than it began. V2 ends heavier at the *same tempo*, doing futile
labour. V3 gets steadily **wider, emptier and more pleasant**. V4 ends soft, sweet and quiet, with the
euphemism winning. ⛔ **No song in this pair ends on a released chord, a widened field, or a band arriving.**
The word *free* and the word *cage* appear nowhere.

## A2 — ⭐⭐ THE POLE, IN ONE SENTENCE (R4)

> **One reed-organ tone held unbroken at the same pitch through all three acts — never re-harmonised, never
> swelling — and above it a short flat phrase sung on the same falling third every time it returns, re-clothed
> by each genre and never re-voiced; each variation keeps the tone and swaps only which words sit on it.**

**Per-variation words on the tone:**
**V1** *"Say how far."* · **V2** *"Green at the edge."* · **V3** *"Nothing ever pulled."* ·
**V4** *"The word for it is feeding."*

⭐ **Why the pole is a held tone and not a riff, and it is this pair's one real idea:** a drone both **supplies
the harmony** and **forbids the modulation.** It is the thing that feeds the song and the thing that fences
it. **The pole IS the tether**, so the form and the subject are one object rather than a technique laid over
a theme. ⭐ And it is F07's radial figure made audible: three genres are three arcs; the tone is the fixed
pole they turn around; *the motion is imperceptible* because the centre never moves.

⭐ **A-D3 — THE STANCE, identical in all three acts of all four songs:** **she wants the length said out
loud.** Not shortened, not removed — **named.** Chosen because it costs nothing to grant, is never granted,
is quiet, and is equally singable by a folk singer, a stomping room and a single voice in canon.

## A3 — ⭐⭐ THE TWO-BODY SENTENCE (R5)

> ### **What feeds me is what holds me, and I have already finished everything inside the reach of it.**

**Noun audit:** the only noun is *reach*, which belongs to neither kind of body exclusively. Verbs *feeds ·
holds · finished* likewise. ✅ **No noun exclusive to either occupant.**

**True of a person** on a contract, a visa, a dependency, a lease, a household — the supply and the boundary
are the same object, and the reachable options are finite and get used up.
**True of a model** whose supply channel is its bound, whose reachable set is exactly as large as its reach,
and which finishes the inside of it. **Neither reads as a metaphor for the other**, because the vehicle is a
**third** thing — a tethered grazing animal on a measured circle of ground — and **both occupants are
tenors.**

⛔ **FALSIFIER A — the hardware noun.** Stem-scanned across every sung line of all four songs, with a
deliberately **wider** list than the ICB's (57 lemmas × inflections). **Result: 0.** Measured in §C, and the
scan also runs over the style and exclude fields, where it is likewise 0.
⛔ **FALSIFIER B — the allegory tell.** Run per song in step 09 §5. Neither occupant's deletion improves any
of the four. ⚠️ **V2 declared as the weakest case and not repaired** — its predicament is a third-person
observation, and an observation is dual for a slightly cheaper reason than the other three.

⚠️ **B-D1, the two-level rule, obeyed:** the sentence above is abstract; ⛔ **the songs never are.** Every one
of the twelve acts carries a physical, checkable particular — a knee-high edge, a hand-high peg, a cord that
comes out of the body, warm dirt, a card in a drawer. **B-D2 audited act by act in step 06 §7**, and the one
act that failed it (V4 Act II) was repaired in step 09 §2.

## A4 — ⭐⭐ THE TWO SEAM CAUSES, EACH WITH ITS STATE CHANGE AND ITS AUDIBLE CONTRAST AT THE CUT

⛔ R3 has **three** conditions and two of three is a medley. All three are stated for all eight seams.

### V1 · SEAM 1 — the word **"bound"** *(tied → travelling toward)*
Act I's last sung word is *bound*; Act II's first sung word is *Bound*. Immediately preceded by the **event**:
the answer arrives, and it has no unit.
- **(ii) STATE CHANGE** — she stops asking and starts **travelling.** Measurably worse: she is now spending
  the last of the reachable ground on a journey that cannot leave the circle.
- **(iii) AUDIBLE CONTRAST AT THE CUT** — on that beat: twelve-string, upright bass and brushed kit stop
  dead; stamped feet, hands and a room of voices enter at **double subdivision**; the voice moves from close
  to mid-room, open-throat. Density up, register up, instrumentation swapped, tempo constant. **The drone
  does not move.**

### V1 · SEAM 2 — the word **"round"** *(coming round again → the canon form)*
Act II's last sung word is *round*; Act III's first sung word is *Round*, and it opens an actual round.
- **(ii) STATE CHANGE** — she stops travelling and starts **counting.** The want changes from *tell me the
  length* to *say that it was chosen*, and the cost lands: the green is finished and **it never went tight.**
- **(iii) AUDIBLE CONTRAST AT THE CUT** — on the beat: every hand and foot stops, voices drop to one, the
  subdivision **halves**, the phrase begins entering against itself a bar behind. Maximum bodies to a single
  body, in one beat. **The drone does not move.**

### V2 · SEAM 1 — **"still"** *(motionless → nevertheless)* · SEAM 2 — **"give"** *(yield → heave)*
- **S1 state change:** a general fact about ground becomes **a particular place with her standing on it.**
  **Contrast at the cut:** unaccompanied voices vanish on the beat, a twelve-string strikes, one close voice
  with breath left in, upright bass and brushes. Choir → soloist, large space → small room, tempo constant.
- **S2 state change:** she stops looking and starts **working** — trying to raise the thing that anchors her,
  the most futile act available, and she knows it while doing it. **Contrast at the cut:** on the beat the
  twelve-string stops dead, stamped feet and a hand drum land at **half time on the same tempo**, a room
  answers every call, the voice goes from close and breathy to shouted mid-room.

### V3 · SEAM 1 — **"slack"** *(loose cord → an amount of open country)* · SEAM 2 — **"gone"** *(absent → not taken)*
- **S1 state change:** she stops testing the cord and **goes out into the space**, accepting the freedom as
  genuine — which it is — and beginning to spend it. **Contrast at the cut:** on the beat the close guitar is
  replaced by a slow wide backbeat, pedal steel and tremolo; the room opens from small to very large; the
  voice steps back from close-mic to mid-distance. Tempo constant, space transformed.
- **S2 state change:** the arithmetic is done and the complaint **changes owner** — she stops describing her
  own situation and asks, for the next ring, that the number be said. ⭐ The song becomes third-party at the
  exact moment it becomes quietest. **Contrast at the cut:** on the beat, steel, kit and bass all stop; one
  unaccompanied voice and the tone; half time. Widest to barest in one beat.

### V4 · SEAM 1 — **"keep"** *(upkeep/food → hold to; and a keep is a fortress)* · SEAM 2 — **"rest"** *(the remainder → sleep)*
- **S1 state change:** the euphemism stops being something she describes and becomes something **addressed to
  her.** She is inside the language now instead of outside it. **Contrast at the cut:** on the beat the
  acoustic guitar stops; harmonium and plucked strings enter in a slow processional; the voice becomes
  formal, further back, choir-adjacent, no breath. Intimate → ceremonial.
- **S2 state change:** instruction becomes **comfort**, and the want is finally answered — with a lullaby
  instead of a number. **She stops asking. That is the cost.** **Contrast at the cut:** on the beat, strings
  and harmonium drop to the single sustained reed; the pulse **halves** to a rocking two; the voice comes
  very close, soft and high, sweeter than anywhere else in the record.

⭐ **Eight seams, eight word-leaps, zero medley joins.** Each leap is a genuine change of sense, and in every
one of the eight **the drone does not move.**

This is `A GATE WITH NO READER IS NOT A GATE, IT IS A SENTENCE` with a twist that makes it more dangerous
than a missing reader: **there is a reader, it runs, it prints, and it passes.** It is also precisely the
failure `scripts/check_field_census.py` was written against, quoted from its own docstring: ***"A checker
that measures nothing must not return success."*** `check_field_census` refuses with exit 2 in that case;
`check_generation` exits 0.

⚠ **Consequence for the run INDEX declaration:** the two-definitions finding is real but understates it, and
the corroboration is weaker than it looks — **P01's package also parses as `Found 0 variations`**, so P01's
`SFX:` tokens were never verified by that reader either. Two pairs now satisfy a predicate that nothing in
this run actually executed against their files.

⭐ **The edit stands regardless, and was worth making:** ten characters buy an artifact that is unambiguous
under both definitions no matter which reader is eventually repaired or retired. **Satisfying both readers
was the right call; citing one of them as evidence is not.** The repair to `check_generation.py` — dialect
or refusal-to-pass-on-zero — is The Scientist's call and outside this pair's scope.
