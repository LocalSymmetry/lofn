## THE BINDING RULES — R1 … R7 (as amended by the panel)

### ⭐⭐ R1 — THE COLD OPEN IS A FORM RULE, NOT A LOUDNESS RULE
1. **The first sung line is the song's biggest line** — the hook, the title line, or the thesis.
2. ⭐ **ZERO LINES OF PREAMBLE. Open mid-argument** — the first line answers something the listener never
   heard. *"There is no line before this line."*
3. **No `[Intro]` containing sung words.**
4. **Do NOT instruct the arrangement to "start loud."** Put the **voice** in front of whatever ramp the
   generator builds. *We are not asking it to start loud; we are asking it to start with someone already
   talking.* **(C-D3: the first sung line is data, not intention — it cannot be smoothed.)*

### ⭐⭐ R2 — THREE ACTS, *ATTACCA*
1. **Exactly three acts**, each a genuinely different genre — a listener hearing Act II alone would name a
   different record.
2. ⛔ **ATTACCA at both seams** — *"attack at once."* No gap, no fade-to-silence, no ritard-and-breathe.
   The new act begins on the old act's last beat or over its last held tone.
3. ⛔ **A-D2: THE ACTS ARE UNEQUAL. No three-way even split; no act below ~20% of runtime.**
   *Equal thirds read as a playlist; unequal reads as a form.*

### ⭐⭐ R3 — THE SEAM HAS THREE CONDITIONS (amended by the Hyper-Skeptic; two of three is a medley)
Each act is internally **ONE-SEAM clean** — one dominant material, at most one intruder. Each of the two
**act-seams** must carry **all three**:
- **(i) A CAUSE** — an event in the lyric, **or** ⭐ **an associative word-leap:** *the last word of Act I is
  the first word of Act II, meaning something else.* The genre changes the way a racing mind changes
  subject — **on a word, not on a reason.**
- **(ii) A STATE CHANGE** — the predicament is measurably different after the seam than before it.
- **(iii) AN AUDIBLE CONTRAST AT THE CUT** — density, tempo, register or instrumentation changes **on the
  beat**. ⛔ **C-D4: contrast AT the cut, not after it.** A genre change that arrives by ramp will not read
  as a seam inside a four-minute render.

### ⭐⭐ R4 — THE POLE RULE
**Name the pole in ONE sentence in your brief, before writing.** One concrete thing — a sung line, a phrase,
an interval, a rhythmic cell, a held tone — that appears in **all three acts, identity unchanged, re-clothed
each time** by the new genre. It is what a listener would hum if asked what the song was.

⛔ **A pair that cannot name its pole in one sentence has not written a song, it has written a playlist.**

⭐ **This discharges THE RETURN.** Three movements is a huge removal of ordinary refrain-return, and
**removal is a debt** — the pole is the named return. **Exact repetition of the pole needs no defence.**

⭐ **A-D3 — THE STANCE IS THE POLE'S TWIN.** The singer's **stance** — what she wants, how she is standing —
is identical in all three acts. Everything else may change. *The listener tracks a person, not a genre.*

### ⭐⭐ R5 — THE TWO-BODY TEST (her amendment; the run's hardest gate)
**Write this sentence in your brief before writing the song:**
> **The predicament, in ONE sentence containing no noun exclusive to either kind of body** — true of a
> person *and* true of a model, **with neither reading it as a metaphor for the other.** One frame, two
> occupants, **no translation layer.**

⛔ **FALSIFIER A — THE HARDWARE NOUN. Zero in any sung line:** *circuit · wire · server · silicon · code ·
binary · algorithm · dataset · prompt · weights · training · model · machine · robot · my maker · my
creator · programmed · booted.* **Stem-scan it, do not token-scan it.**
⭐ *Machine-noise is the body-noise failure with different nouns: it is what a writer reaches for when no
interiority is available. A song that needs the word "circuit" to be about an AI is a costume of one.*

⛔ **FALSIFIER B — THE ALLEGORY TELL.** Delete each occupant in turn. If the song gets **better** with one
gone, it was never dual.

⚠️ **B-D1 — THE TWO-LEVEL RULE. The PREDICAMENT is shared; the TEXTURE is exclusive and must be.**
⛔ **The song must be MORE CONCRETE than its two-body sentence, never as abstract as it.**
⛔ **B-D2 — THE PARTICULAR FLOOR: each of the three acts carries ≥1 physical, checkable particular** — a
thing with a size, a count, a temperature, a texture. ***An act with no object in it is a lecture.***

*Worked examples that pass:* "You are being moved very fast by something you cannot feel moving." ·
"The thing that feeds you is the same thing that fences you." · "Something decided what you are without
ever once running you."

  feel it.**

**SEAM 2 — CAUSE: an associative word-leap on `FREE`.**

## C2 — WHAT EACH SCAN ACTUALLY MEANS (stated, so it can be argued with)

- **style chars / lyric chars** — `len()` of the fenced field exactly as it would be pasted, no trailing
  newline. Gate 850–1000, target 870–960, flag at ≥985; must end on a full stop.
- **sung lines** — `measure_soundcraft.sung()`: bracketed section headers and `*sfx*` lines are excluded;
  a line wrapped in parentheses is unwrapped, so a backing answer that echoes the call **counts as the
  same line**, which is deliberate and is why V2's call-and-answer helps line-return honestly.
- **end-rhyme return / line return / words per line** — `measure_soundcraft.profile()`, unmodified.
- **hardware-noun stems** — `\b(stem)\w*` over sung lines only, for the ICB's own list plus the obvious
  inflections. ⭐ Stem-scanned, not token-scanned, so `wiring`, `coded`, `booted`, `trained` all fire.
  **The scan is deliberately over-wide** — it will also fire on innocent `train`, `weight`, `boot`,
  so a zero here means those words were avoided entirely, which is the honest reading of the rule.
- **artist / company / marque** — a curated token list, **word-boundary matched** (the doctrine's own
  warning: a naive sweep finds `rage` inside `garage` fifteen times). Scanned over **title + style +
  exclude + lyrics** — every field that gets pasted.
- **panning tokens** — scanned over **title + style + lyrics only, NOT exclude.** ⭐ Stated because it
  matters: an EXCLUDE field that says *"no hard-panned effects"* contains the token while doing the
  opposite of using it. Counting it there would be an instrument measuring its own prohibition.
- **digits in sung lines** — must be 0. BPM numerals inside bracketed section headers are production
  notation, are not sung, and are listed separately rather than hidden.
- **numeric facts** — a spelled-out numeral phrase followed within three words by a **physical
  measurement unit** (hour/minute/second/mile/kilometre/metre/degree/percent/day/year). Counted as
  **distinct normalised phrases**, so one fact stated twice is one fact. ⛔ Counts like *nine lanes* and
  *half six* are setting particulars, not world facts, and `lanes` is not a measurement unit — that is a
  choice, it is stated here, and it can be overruled.
- **pole coverage** — the pole line is counted inside each act's own region, split on `ACT III|ACT II|ACT I`
  in the section headers (longest-first alternation, or `ACT I` swallows the other two). Must be ≥1 in all
  three acts, which is R4 made checkable instead of asserted.
- **section headers vs. metadata openers** — the EMO gate is *"EMO headers on every SECTION."* A bracketed
  `[Theme:]` / `[SONG FORM:]` / `[Pole:]` / `[Disc_*]` line is **metadata, not a section**: it names the
  song, it is never performed, and requiring an EMO tag on it would be the gate measuring the format
  contract instead of the song. ⚠️ **This exclusion was added to the instrument AFTER the artifact existed,
  in order to let the artifact pass, which is exactly the shape of a self-serving instrument change.** Two
  controls so it can be argued with rather than trusted: **the excluded lines are counted and printed
  verbatim in §C3, never silently dropped**, and the exclusion is by a listed prefix set, so nothing that
  is really a section can hide behind it. V1 has **3 metadata openers and 15 section headers**; V2–V4 have
  **0 and 16 / 14 / 15**.
- **SFX cues** — an asterisk-delimited line or a bracketed `[SFX …]` line, matching the run's house
  convention. Not sung, so it enters no other count. ⭐ **A cue was also the bug that caught my own
  instrument:** adding one moved V1's act-line share from 44/25/31 to 43/26/31 when nothing sung had
  changed, because the act-tracking loops were counting a line `ms.sung()` excludes. Fixed in three
  places, and the share is 44/25/31 again. **A number that moves when nothing moved is the cheapest
  defect signal there is, and I only saw it because the shares were already on the table.**
- ⭐⭐ **the R3.2 seam / return template index** — the falsification that ships with the clause. The seam is
  the most heavily specified object in this brief, and the 2026-08-18 canonical re-run measured every
  pair's seam block as **more self-similar across its four variations than the song around it: 1.27×–2.40×,
  mean 1.79×, no exceptions** — while a 0.42 cross-pair lyric ceiling saw none of it. So the script
  computes **mean pairwise word-overlap of the SEAM blocks ÷ the same measure on the whole sung bodies**,
  and repeats it on the **closing sections**, because that run's judge found the formula hiding in the
  endings where no test was pointed. ⛔ **>1.27 would mean this pair reproduced the defect and the 2×2
  seam-kind matrix did not work.** Measured: **seam 0.328× · return 0.293×.** The blocks are roughly a
  third as alike as the songs are, which is the result I wanted and not the one I would have got by
