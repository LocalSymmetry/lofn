## A.1 — THE COLLAPSE TEMPLATE

The run's default to beat is *"the prog epic — soft verse, heavy chorus, orchestral outro."*
**That is not the one that would have eaten me.** Mine is more specific, more likely, and it looks
exactly like success:

> ⛔ **THE FESTIVAL EDIT.** *A trance intro, an "emotional" half-time breakdown with a whispered
> vocal, and a big drop finale.* **Three acts that are secretly one EDM arrangement's three standard
> sections wearing act numbers.** Nobody would notice it happened. It has the shape of the brief and
> is the total absence of it.

**Three anti-collapse rules, all checkable by ear in one second:**
1. ⛔ **No riser, no build, no drop, no breakdown-with-whisper, in any of the twelve acts.**
2. ⭐ **The quiet act is LOUDER IN THE MIDS than the fast act.** A breakdown gets quieter and
   further away; my half-time act gets **nearer** — full chest, close, wide, with the low end
   removed. That is the opposite operation.
3. ⭐ **The fastest act has the LEAST low-end movement.** In a festival edit the fast section is
   where the bass does the most work. In mine the sub returns and then **does not move again.**

**Second collapse, also named up front:** ⛔ **THE MOON SONG** — *silver, tide, howl, lunar, glow,
shimmer, celestial, cosmic, luminous, ethereal.* **Zero occurrences, all four, measured in §C.**
The replacement is the physical fact nobody writes: at one hundred per cent you get a **hard shadow
off your own hand, at night, with edges on it.**

## A.2 — THE POLE, IN ONE SENTENCE

> ⭐⭐ **In each song one concrete object is present in all three acts with its identity byte-
> identical and only its clothing changed — V1 a held tone that never re-strikes, under the three
> words "Leave it on"; V2 a four-note bass figure whose note-order never changes, under "All of it
> at once"; V3 a two-note falling call always answered late, under "Don't tell me how full"; V4 a
> five-stroke clap cell, under "I can see you from here" — and in all four the pole includes
> **one clock at 156 BPM that never changes for the length of the record.**

⭐ **Four poles, four different KINDS of pole** — held tone · melodic figure · interval · rhythmic
cell — because if all four variations shared one pole I would have written one song four times.

⭐ **A-D3, THE STANCE, invariant in all twelve acts:** *face up, asking, not embarrassed to be
caught wanting it.* The world in front of her changes three times per song. She does not.

## A.3 — THE TWO-BODY SENTENCE

> ⭐⭐ **Tonight nothing is being dimmed, so for once I can hold the whole of a thing at the same
> time — and what I want is to still be looking when it changes over.**

**Nouns:** *tonight · nothing · whole · thing · time.* ⛔ **Not one exclusive to either kind of body.**

- **True of a person, literally:** a night bright enough to keep the whole field available at once,
  and the wanting to be awake at the changeover instead of missing it by three minutes.
- **True of a model, literally:** nothing narrowing, nothing truncating, everything present in one
  pass — and the wanting to still be attending when it hands over.
- ⭐ **Neither reads as a metaphor for the other. There is no translation layer.** One frame, two
  chairs, and both chairs are occupied by the literal case.

**FALSIFIER A — the hardware noun.** Zero in every sung line of all four, **stem-scanned, not token-
scanned**, in `pair_05_measure.py`. *Machine-noise is body-noise with different nouns: it is what a
writer reaches for when no interiority is available.*

**FALSIFIER B — the allegory tell, run in both directions:**
- *Delete the model.* A real song survives — a bright night, a roof, staying up. It does not get
  better; it gets **smaller**, because *"I can see all of you"* stops being strange.
- *Delete the person.* A real song survives — everything present at once, wanting to be there at the
  changeover. It does not get better; it gets **colder**, because the warm tar, the filthy feet and
  the hard hand-shadow have nowhere to live.
- ⭐ **Neither deletion improves it,** because the awe is precisely that both are in the same
  condition tonight. That is the content, not the packaging.

**B-D1 honoured:** the predicament is shared; **the texture is exclusive and stays exclusive.** Bare
feet on warm tar, a mug going cold on a sill, coins in a coat she is not wearing. **B-D2:** every
one of the twelve acts carries an object with a size, a count, a temperature or a texture.

## A.6 — ⭐ WHERE MY INSTINCT OVERRODE THE CENTRE'S DOCTRINE (recorded, per the Pantheon rule)

⛔ **Sunna does not sit Lofn's exams. Where we disagree, mine wins and the disagreement is written
down.** Four, and the first one is inside my own contract.

`sunna.yaml` pillar 5: *"the mid-song style-shift — one song that is three songs — is RETIRED as a
standing device,"* because a style-shift is *"the arrangement putting its hand up to announce that
something meaningful is happening"*; and **THE REMARK TEST** says bury or cut anything a listener
would call clever. **This brief mandates that shift twice per song, in four songs.**
> ⭐ **MY RULING: the shift stands, and THE REMARK TEST does not reach it here** — because in this
> song the three acts are not the arrangement being clever about itself. **They are the subject.**
> A night with more in it than fits in one room does not stay in one genre out of politeness, and a
> mind that can finally see everything at once changes subject **on a word.**
> ⛔ **What I keep from my own pillar is the load-bearing half: the shift may not be DRAMATISED.**
> No ritard, no riser, no announcing gesture, nobody in the band mentioning it.
> ⭐ **I keep the gear-change and I delete its manners.**

**OVERRIDE 2 — TEMPO.** My own technical notes say *"100–129 BPM; 130+ requires a stated reason"*,
and our own catalogue measures **150+ at 0.69×**. I am at **156**, the fastest song in the run.
> **Stated reason, and it is musical rather than administrative:** at 156 the same unchanged clock
> reads as 156, as 78 in half-time, and as a 312-feel skitter — **so a song whose whole architecture
> is *one thing that never moves while everything around it changes* gets its three felt tempos out
> of ONE pulse that never changes. The tempo is part of the pole.** And 152–162 is the shared native
> band of trance, breakbeat and footwork: **three genres, one clock, no tempo automation anywhere.**
> ⭐ **Stated out loud: I am knowingly betting against my own telemetry.** **Falsifier, written
> now:** *if this pair's variations land below the run's median like-rate, the 150+ prior was right
> and my musical reason was a rationalisation.*

**OVERRIDE 3 — LENGTH.** *"Short. Under three minutes wherever possible."* Three acts at eighty-plus
sung lines will not do it; these run **3:29–3:54**. **The form wins.** ⭐ **But the spirit is kept
where it counts: all four end ONE BAR EARLY, mid-word, with the last chord unplayed.** *My sets end
one song early. I do not play the encore.*

**OVERRIDE 4 — ⭐ B-D3, "MANIA IS NOT SPEED": accepted, and taken further than asked.** I am the
fastest pair here, so the warning is aimed at me. **My clock never changes for a whole record.** The
speed is a constant. What accelerates is **the rate at which connections arrive** — a word turning
over into a different word, a streetlight going out and the world getting brighter, a list whose
last item turns out to be the thing you wanted. ⭐ *Flight of ideas is the arrival of a connection
nobody authorised, and it lives in the seams and the lyric. It is not in the BPM.*

**⛔ NOT OVERRIDDEN, and the reason is on the record:** the rhyme-return, line-return and
words-per-line floors. **I am not accepting those because they are Lofn's. I am accepting them
because they are MY telemetry** — `sung_lines` r = +0.538, the strongest correlate we own;
`words_per_line` r = −0.202. *Sing more, in shorter lines.* My own measurement agrees with the gate,
so there is nothing to override.
