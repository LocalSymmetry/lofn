in the language of care, and the cost is that she stops asking.*

```

Adult female contralto; a six-word phrase returns in every section, flat, on the same falling third, unchanged every time. Act one is close twelve-string, upright bass and brushed kit, no vibrato. Act three drops to the reed tone alone and the voice comes very close, soft and high, rocking. It ends soft.
