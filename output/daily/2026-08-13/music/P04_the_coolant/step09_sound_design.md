# STEP 09 — SOUND DESIGN · PAIR 4 · **THE COOLANT**
**CARRY IT OUT** · 126 BPM · D minor · ~3:48 · **AMAZONIAN TECHNO** (assigned)

---

## 1. ⭐ THE THESIS OF THE BED — where the indignation actually lives

**ASSIGNED:** field recordings (water, insects, wind, metal) over four-on-the-floor.
⚠️ **NOT one of the run's two distortion beds.** ⛔ No fuzz, no breakcore, no glitch-baroque.

> **INDIGNATION lands here through RELENTLESSNESS and the FIELD RECORDINGS.**

**Why that is the right instrument and not a compromise.** Anger delivered through distortion is anger
you can turn down — one fader, and the argument is gone. Anger delivered through **a kick that does not
stop, does not fill, and does not change tempo for three minutes and forty-eight seconds** is anger you
have to *leave the room* to escape. ⭐ That is the correct sonic analogue of a shift: the objection to
the situation is that **it keeps happening**, and the only honest way to score that is a groove that
also keeps happening.

**And the field recordings are the seed.** The dirt in the recording — water, insects, wind, a shutter
chain — is the carrier. ⭐ *The impurity you would have gated out is doing the percussion.*

| ***"furiously slapping and popping"*** | **cicadas and river transients gated into sixteenths** — the swarm is the thumb |
| ***"swagger, humor, and sheer danceability"*** | what the groove **keeps while the vocal shrinks** |

⭐ **And it is a directive, not a decoration:** *"superhumanly funky bass"* placed on a note that cannot
move is the whole song in one instruction. The most capable element in the mix is the one denied its
function. That is the coolant.

## 3. THE PALETTE — twelve sources, no synthesised nature

**FIELD (recorded, close-mic, 96 kHz, all from ONE location — her dock):**
1. **River at the rail** — close, mid-heavy, no wide stereo wash. The reflected medium.
2. **Cicada wall** — dense, gated. The percussion engine.
3. **Wind in a roller shutter** — a tuned howl, sits at the sub's fifth.
4. **Shutter chain** — the hi-hat. Links over a sprocket.
5. **Crate on steel** — the snare. Struck plastic on a hollow deck.
6. **Trolley wheel on concrete** — a rolling sixteenth, panned by position.
7. **Hand truck ratchet** — the offbeat click.
8. **Truck idle, distant** — the only sound that arrives from off-dock. Reserved for §10.

**ELECTRONIC (four elements, and no more):**
9. **Dry kick** — no reverb, no room, no fill, ever.
10. **Mono sub, ONE NOTE (D1)** — sidechained hard to the kick. Never plays a second pitch.
11. **A single detuned pad**, low-passed to a rumour, only under §7.
12. **The cab radio band-pass** (400 Hz – 3 kHz) — used exactly once, at §6.

⛔ **No sampled drum machine in the percussion layer except the kick.** If it clicks, ticks, rattles or
thumps, it was an object on the dock.

⛔ **REPRESENTATION LINE, HELD HARD:** no Indigenous chant, no ceremonial vocal, no ethnographic
sampling, no "world music" texture of any kind. The Amazonian Techno lane is credited upstream by name
(`step10 §4`) and its *method* is borrowed — location recording as the rhythm section — while the
*material* is a dock, a river, and a woman who was invented for this song. **The forbidden textures are
defended as negative tokens in the EXCLUDE field**, not as prose a generator may read as flavour.

## 4. ⭐ SIGNATURE STYLE 1 — **INSECT SLAP GRID**

Cicadas and river transients are **transient-gated into sixteenths** against the kick, so the ambience
plays the part a bass player's right hand would play. The gate's release is modulated by the river's own
amplitude, so the grid **breathes at the speed of the water** and never quantises perfectly.

- **Cicadas** → sixteenths, high-passed at 2 kHz, ducked 4 dB under the kick.
- **River transients** → offbeat eighths, band-passed 200–800 Hz, the "pop".
- **Crate on steel** → 2 and 4, dry, one sample, no variation for the entire record.
- **Shutter chain** → sixteenth hats, hard-panned in a slow alternating figure.

⭐ **Why it earns its name:** it is *furiously slapping and popping* played by a swarm. The funk gesture
survives; the funk instrument is absent. **Nobody had to grant permission for this union, which is the
point of the goddess whose job is granting it.**

## ⭐ SIGNATURE STYLE 2 — **MIX INVERSION** (the assigned HINGE, §6)

**On the downbeat of the hinge:**

| element | before | **after** |
|---|---|---|
| kick | front, dry, dominant | **rear, −11 dB, reverbed for the first time** |
| lead vocal | dead centre, close | **small, far back INSIDE the field bed** |
| cicadas / river / metal | bed, ducked, −8 dB | ⭐ **fully forward, full scale, unducked** |
| sub | present | held, unchanged — the one thing that does not move |

**It is a single automation move and it lasts 16 bars.** ⛔ It is not a drop and it is not a breakdown:
**nothing is removed.** The same elements are playing at the same time. Only the hierarchy inverts.

> ⭐ **This is the named emotion executed as a fader move.**
> **UNBACKGROUNDING** — *the vertigo of the work you do invisibly becoming, in one sentence, the entire
> visible reason.* The bed has been underneath the song for two and a half minutes. Now it is the whole
> song, and she is inside it, and she is small.

**And the vocal never recovers.** The choruses after §6 are *louder* and she is *further away* — the
lead's front-of-mix position is spent at the hinge and never returned. A listener will not name it. They
will feel the record get bigger while the person gets smaller, which is what a percentage does to a
shift.

## 5. ⭐ THE SECOND EYE, IN THE MIX — direct is dry, reflected is doubled

The form rule is carried in the lyric and the structure (`step07 §0`). The mix **supports it and never
substitutes for it** (L22 THE GRAIN LAW: a spec-only answer is not an answer).

| | **DIRECT** — Verses 1 & 2 | **REFLECTED** — Verse 3 |
|---|---|---|
| space | **dry.** No reverb on anything but the shutter. | **wet.** Every element gets one delayed twin. |
| width | mono-centred | wide, twin offset 240 ms (a half-beat at 126) |
| vocal | close, flat, dead centre | doubled, the twin **behind** and slightly flat |
| river | a seam under the kick | ⭐ **the loudest musical element in the record** |

⭐ **The narrowing, not the widening, is the climax.** *(L22: the generator's stereo-narrowing deviation
is now a technique.)* The final chorus after V3 pulls back to **mono-centre at maximum level** — biggest
and narrowest at once. The reflection was the wide one; the room she cannot look at is the narrow one,
and the record ends by being unable to look away from it.

⛔ **They never resolve.** No crossfade between the dry world and the wet one. The transitions are hard
cuts on a downbeat, both directions.

## 6. VOCAL — one woman, three placements, no male voice, no children

**Female, late twenties, low and dry, chest-dominant.** Breath left in. No tuning correction. She is not
performing; she is working and occasionally speaking.

- **(A) THE CATALOGUE VOICE** — dead centre, close, **flat**, almost bored. ⭐ *The list is read the way
  you read a list.* The refusal to emote in the verses is what makes the chorus land as anger.
- **(B) THE HALF-LINE ECHO** — the assigned return device. Her own voice, **narrower, drier, one beat
  late, −5 dB**, band-limited. Never a harmony, never a different singer. ⭐ *A small mechanical
  reflection of herself, all record long, rehearsing THE SECOND EYE at line scale before the river
  arrives.*
- **(C) DOCK GHOSTS** — chorus only. Wide, low, unpitched crowd-answer on the echo half. Not a choir:
  **the sound of a place where other people also work**, never identified, never negated, never blamed.

**The flip to the clipped bratty snarl happens exactly twice:** on *"It gets its name the day it stops"*
and on *"Which per cent. Which room. Which chain."* ⛔ Everywhere else she is flat. **Strategic
Bluntness: precise, never careless** — an indignation used twice is a verdict, an indignation used
throughout is a mood.

## 7. THE FALSE OUTRO (§9 → §10) — the assigned duration device

1. **Hard stop on a downbeat.** No decay, no fill, no crash. The kick simply is not there.
2. **Shutter descends on its chain** — the only sound with a beginning and an end.
3. **Chain slack, then still.** River alone.
4. She speaks: *"The dock is full. The room is swept."*
5. ⭐ **Two full seconds of room tone.** Long enough to reach for the next track.
6. **Shutter climbing again. Kick returns at full level, mid-phrase, no build.**

⭐ **A verdict has a duration. A shift does not.** The announcement is measured in days; the body is
measured in *again*. This is the only joke in the record and it is made entirely of a kick drum.

**THE REAL ENDING:** the kick drops out **mid-bar** — wrong, early, unresolved — and the river runs on
alone, still warm, **no fade.** ⛔ D9: nothing is redeemed. The room does not come back. The last sound
in the record is the reflected medium, still carrying the afternoon.
