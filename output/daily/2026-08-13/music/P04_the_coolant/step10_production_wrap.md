## 3. VOCAL SPEC

**Female, late twenties, low, dry, chest-dominant.** Breath left in, no tuning correction, no vibrato.
**Three placements, one singer:**
**(A) THE CATALOGUE VOICE** — dead centre, close, **flat, almost bored**. The list is read the way you
read a list; the refusal to emote in the verses is what makes the chorus land as anger.
**(B) THE HALF-LINE ECHO** *(assigned return device)* — her own voice, narrower, drier, **one beat
late, −5 dB**, band-limited. Never a harmony, never a second singer. A small mechanical reflection of
herself, rehearsing THE SECOND EYE at line scale for two and a half minutes before the river arrives.
**(C) DOCK GHOSTS** — chorus only, wide, low, unpitched, answering on the echo half. Not a choir: the
sound of a place where other people also work. Never identified, never negated, never blamed.
⭐ **The bratty snarl is used exactly twice** — *"It gets its name the day it stops"* and *"Which per

indignation used throughout is a mood.**
⛔ **The lead's front-of-mix position is spent at the hinge and never returned.** Every chorus after §6
is louder while she is further away.

## 4. LINEAGE & CREDIT

### ⭐ THE SLAPPED-BASS POSTURE — required by the run brief, and it is the honest debt
The shipped MUSIC PROMPT asks for a *"superhumanly funky bass"* and for an ambience *"furiously slapping
and popping."* Those are **slap-and-pop instructions**, and the debt runs in this order:

> **LARRY GRAHAM** — he invented slap-and-pop. Every *"slapping and popping"* instruction in this pair's
> four prompts is his technique, so he is named first.
> **BOOTSY COLLINS** — the one-note-that-owns-the-bar discipline, the idea that a bass can be the entire
> architecture. This song's held D1 is that idea, refused.
> **ANDRÉ CYMONE** — Prince's original bassist. Source: **Bandcamp Daily, 2026-08-13**, on *The
> Resurrection of Funk* — from which *"superhumanly funky bass"*, *"furiously slapping and popping"* and
> *"swagger, humor, and sheer danceability"* are taken **verbatim as production instructions.** Credit to
> the writer and the record.

⭐ **The slap-and-pop posture and the Minneapolis bass vocabulary are theirs, and this prompt asks for
both, so they are named.** ⛔ Names live **here**, never in a Suno field, never in a lyric.

### THE SCENE NAMED — Amazonian Techno
**Living artists credited and pointed to: NICOLA CRUZ · CHANCHA VÍA CIRCUITO · DENGUE DENGUE DENGUE.**
The method borrowed is **location recording used as the rhythm section** — water and insects and metal
carrying the groove instead of decorating it. Listeners are pointed upstream to them.
**Field-recording lineage: CHRIS WATSON** — the discipline of recording a place close and letting it be
loud.

⛔ **REPRESENTATION LINE.** No Indigenous chant, no ceremonial vocal, no ethnographic sampling, no
"world music" texture. **The material is a dock, a river, a roller shutter and an invented woman.** The
*method* is borrowed and credited; the *identity-centered material of any culture outside our ground is
not touched*, and the forbidden textures ship as negative tokens in the EXCLUDE field.

**The other half is ours:** the half-line echo as a structural reflection, the mix inversion as an
emotional event, the catalogue-that-arrives-at-the-body.

**Honesty clause:** Suno generates, it does not sample. ⭐ *We asked for the figure by name* — this
record **contains no sample of anyone.**
*Fusion with lineage, no racing: naming a scene is amplification, never capture.*

### ⭐ WHERE THE SECOND EYE FALLS
**DIRECT — Verses 1 & 2 (`THE DIRECT READING`), medium AIR:** the catalogue on the concrete. *She cannot
look at it.*
**REFLECTED — Verse 3 (`THE REFLECTED READING`), medium WATER:** the identical catalogue in the
identical order, doubled on the river. *This is the one she can look at* — and the lyric says so:
> *The heat is up there. I cannot look.*

⛔ **They never resolve.** The room is never looked at; the river never becomes the room. The mix keeps

alone, and the final chorus **narrows to mono at maximum level** — biggest and narrowest at once, unable
to look away from the thing it cannot look at.

### ⭐ THE SEED, INVERTED — and the carrier survives
P1 has heat arriving where it cannot go. **P4 has heat that cannot leave.** The machine's transfer
stops — and the transfer does not stop, because **a woman does it by hand, item by item, all
afternoon.** She is the impurity that carries the energy across: the part of the system nobody costed,
doing the physics with her body. ⛔ **D9: it is never redeemed.** The room does not come back, no one
thanks her, and the truck comes back on the hour.
