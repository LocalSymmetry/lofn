## THE DERIVATION — how these four come out of THIS concept, not a global template

⛔ **No shared V1–V4 template was used.** The pair's own assignment was interrogated with one question —
***a catalogue has no verb, so what is the ONE verb the list happens to?*** — and it has four genuinely
different answers. **Change the verb and everything downstream changes**: what the inventory is made of,
what the second eye is reflected in, and what she does with a number.

| | ⭐ **THE ONE VERB** | The inventory is made of | ⭐ **THE SECOND EYE — reflected in** | Her answer to *"twenty per cent"* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **CARRY** | **objects** — things she lifts off a dying room | **WATER** — the same list doubled on the river | *demands the list*: "Say gasket. Say gauge. Say drain." |
| **V2** | **KNOCK** | **sounds** — what each object says when struck | **ACOUSTICS** — the far bank's returning answer | *hears it as a sound*: "That is the sound a building makes." |
| **V3** | **TOUCH** | **temperatures** — one predicate, every item | **LIGHT** — the sun taken off the water | *measures it against a hand*: "My hand calls it all of it." |
| **V4** | **WRITE** | **written lines** — the handover sheet | **A MOUTH** — another woman reading it aloud | ⭐ *"it is the only line I cannot lift."* |

⭐ **The reflected media are four different substances — water, air, light, a voice.** That is the run's
Oblique card, **EMPHASIZE DIFFERENCES**, applied where it is hardest to apply: inside one pair, on one
afternoon, in one location, with one personality.

# V2 — **THE FAR BANK ANSWERS**
**128 BPM · C minor · 3:41 · dry, metallic, the fastest of the four**

> **The inventory is of SOUNDS.** She walks the room knocking on every object to hear what it is now.
> Every knock is returned a breath later by the far bank of the river — and the answer is mixed **louder
> than the strike.** ⭐ **Second eye: ACOUSTICS.** *"The sound is up here. I cannot bear it. / The sound
> is over there. I can."*
> **Verb: KNOCK.** ⭐ *The assigned half-line echo stops being a vocal device and becomes physics:* the
> far bank is the echo, and by the last chorus the reply is the loudest thing in the record.

## MUSIC PROMPT
```
Amazonian Techno at 128 BPM in C minor, dry and metallic, four-on-the-floor built entirely from knocks on real objects: steel door, shutter, crate, pipe, tank, glass. Every knock is answered a half-beat later by its own slapback off the far bank of a river, and the answer is mixed louder than the strike. The sub is a superhumanly funky bass pinned to one note, ducked hard under each hit. Signature 1, FAR BANK SLAPBACK: a 380 ms natural delay with the top end eaten by water, used as the snare rather than as an effect. Signature 2, MIX INVERSION: at the hinge the kick and lead vocal fall to the rear and the returning answers come fully forward, the reply louder than the thing that caused it. Female, late twenties, low and dry, a flat inventory delivery snapping into a clipped snarl twice and no more. Cicadas furiously slapping and popping in sixteenths. False outro: the record stops, and one last answer arrives in the silence.
```
