# STEP 09 — SOUND DESIGN · P06 · THE CALL ACROSS THE VALLEY

**Bed (assigned): KULNING × SOMATIC BASS.** ⛔ Not a distortion bed. N = 1 + one intruder + one seam.

---

## 1. THE REGISTER PLAN — the seam is a frequency layout

| Band | Content | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **700 Hz – 4 kHz** | the call: unvibratoed open-throat head tone, fundamental ≈ **A5, 880 Hz**, overtones deliberately un-tamed | her |
| ⛔ **200 – 600 Hz** | **cut to near-silence** | ⭐ **the valley.** The gap the call has no right to cross. |
| **90 – 130 Hz** | the sub's second harmonic **only** (≈110 Hz), so the floor still reads on a phone | the bridge that is not the point |
| **30 – 60 Hz** | mono, centred sub on **A1 = 55 Hz** — the *same note* as the call, four octaves down | the far body, felt not heard |

⭐ **The call and the bass are the same note.** The union is a pitch identity separated by four octaves of
deliberate emptiness. The mids open **exactly once** — `[Call II — The Far Side]`, when the second woman
answers — and close again. That single opening is the seam, placed at the junction that carries the
meaning: the gap is inhabited only in the moment somebody crosses it.

## 2. THE TWO SIGNATURE STYLES (shipped verbatim in the prompt)

**Signature 1 — THE SCOOPED VALLEY.** Two hundred to six hundred hertz cut almost to silence, so the
midrange is not "mixed" but *absent as terrain*. The listener's ear reaches for the middle of the picture
and finds distance. When the far woman answers she appears **in that band and nowhere else**, slightly
dark, slightly late, with no reverb of her own — an object across a space rather than an effect on the
lead.

**Signature 2 — THE MILK RING.** A close-miked pail of milk trembling at forty hertz: the sub's motion
made *visible* as concentric ripples and audible as a soft liquid resonance with a very short decay. It is
the only element in the arrangement that has a surface. It occupies `[The Pail]` alone, with the lead
voice removed, so the audience is handed the reflected view of the call at the same moment the woman
looks down at it.

## 3. VOCAL SPEC

- **Lead:** female, late twenties. **Open-throated kulning technique** — high head-tone register,
  **no vibrato**, no pop placement, no breathiness-as-styling. Volume is the expression; the tone is a
  working tool, not a mood.
- **Tuning:** A minor with a **natural sixth** and a **fourth pitched slightly high**, and a **neutral
  third** that sits between major and minor. These are documented traits of Swedish folk singing, not
  decoration — and they are exactly the "impurity" the song is about, sitting in the intonation.
- **Degradation across the piece:** clean-edged at `[Intro]`; by `[Loop Until It Hurts]` the top of each
  call frays, breath is audible before each attack, and the last calls sit slightly flat. ⭐ The
  rough-debt happens to the voice in real time instead of being described.
- **Second voice (the far woman):** lower, older, shorter phrases, no reverb, sitting in the mid band.
- **Gang chant:** unpitched-ish shouted unison, mixed room-wide but **not** hard-panned. Four syllables.
- ⛔ No children. ⛔ No male lead. ⛔ No whispered ASMR layer.

## 4. THE FLOOR

- **A1 = 55 Hz, mono, dead centre, no stereo width, no chorus.** A frequency you feel in the sternum has
  no stereo position, and hard-panned low content is a documented way to get smoothed (L22).
- **Movement, not a drone:** the sub articulates — a slow slap-and-release envelope on the downbeat of
  each call, decaying into the gap. This is where the SOURCE-2 funk vocabulary does real work: the
  instruction is *"furiously slapping and popping at the floor of hearing"* — the **articulation** of
  slap bass at a frequency where no note is perceivable, only impact. ⭐ Take the words, not the genre.
- **66 BPM, felt not counted.** The floor keeps time; the calls do not obey it. The disagreement between
  a metred body and an unmetred voice is the last thing in the song to resolve, and it does not.

## 5. ⭐ THE GRAIN LAW CHECK — what runs WITH the generator, and what will be smoothed

| Intent | Grain verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Open quiet, solo voice, no floor | ✅ runs with | The generator likes a bare open and a build. |
| Sub enters and rises across the piece | ✅ runs with | Standard energy curve; it will be honoured. |
| Thicken the final chorus (`[Loop Until It Hurts]`) | ✅ runs with | Last-chorus thickening is the generator's own instinct. |
| Tuned sub on the same pitch class as the lead | ✅ runs with | It is a musical relationship, not an effect. |
| Mono centred low end | ✅ runs with | Fighting this is a known smoothing trigger. |
| Removing the lead for a whole section (`[The Pail]`) | ✅ runs with | ⭐ It is a **register removal**, not a full stop. Long true silences get smoothed; an instrumental section does not. |
| Free-time calls over a metred floor | ⚠️ partial | Expect partial quantisation. Survivable — the call/answer *lengths* carry the idea even if the rubato is tidied. |
| ⛔ **200–600 Hz scooped to near-silence** | ⚠️ **most at risk** | This is a mix decision and the generator will likely fill some of it. |

⭐ **Because the scoop is the intent most likely to be smoothed, the concept is NOT left sitting in the
production spec.** Per L22, an objection answered only in the spec is not answered. The valley is
therefore also carried by things the generator cannot smooth away:
1. **The lyric says it** — `Across the valley there is another woman` / `We have never stood in the same field.`
2. **The form says it** — every verse is call-then-shorter-answer, so the *gap* is a rhythmic fact, not an EQ curve.
3. **The count says it** — one name goes unanswered for a measured beat.
If the EQ is smoothed to nothing, the song still works. **That is the test.**

## 6. EXCLUDE FIELD (Suno's second field)

```
male lead vocals, child vocals, vibrato, pop belting, trap hi-hats, EDM risers, four-on-the-floor kick,
guitar, brass, orchestral strings, choir pads, glitch, bitcrush, chiptune, breakcore, distortion pedal,
autotune, whispered ASMR, long reverb tails, slapback echo on the lead, stereo-widened bass, untuned
drone, ambient wash, spoken-word intro by a male narrator, sampled folk field recording of a real singer
```

⛔ `slapback echo on the lead` is excluded on purpose: a half-second slap would let a listener mistake an
echo for the second eye, which is the exact reading step 07 §2 refuses.
⛔ `sampled folk field recording of a real singer` is excluded because the honesty clause has to be true at
the render, not only in the caption.

## 8. WHAT WAS NOT ADDED, AND WHY

⛔ No AI code-scratch. ⛔ No quantum bit-depth swell. ⛔ No 8-bit tag. ⛔ No breakcore break.
These are house signatures and they are all **third materials**. L38 retires the collision engines: the
variety belongs across the set, never inside one frame. This song is made **of** a herding call. The one
intruder is a frequency. The seam is the four octaves between them, and it only has to be crossed once.
