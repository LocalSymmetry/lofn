## 2. ⭐ WHERE THE SECOND EYE FALLS — §4, `[The Pail]`

**Statement 1 (direct)** is §1 `[Call I — The Rough]`: the call in the air, out of her body, at full
pressure, described by what it costs and never by what it looks like — because it has no look. This is
the eclipse itself.

**Statement 2 (reflected)** is **§4 `[The Pail]`**: she looks down, and the evening milk in the pail at
her boot is **going into rings** in time with her own throat. Same shape (a ring travelling out from one
centre), inverted (downward, contained, two hands wide), **different medium (milk, not air)** — and it is
**the only place in the song where her call can be seen.** This is the eclipse in the Ebro.

### Why it is not simply the echo — three reasons, and the third is the load-bearing one

1. **An echo is the same medium.** Air out, air back. The form rule demands the figure carried by
   *another substance*; an echo changes nothing but the delay. It is a repeat, and a repeat is exactly
   what the rule refuses.
2. **The echo slot is already occupied by a person.** §3 `[Call II — The Far Side]` is the neighbour on
   the opposite mountain with her own throat, her own tune and her own words. She is a **second subject**,
   not a reflection of the first. Spending the form rule on her would have quietly demoted a woman to an
   acoustic artefact — and would have left the central figure stated only once.
3. ⭐ **The reflection has to be the one you can LOOK at, and an echo is still invisible.** That is the
   whole of the APOD instruction: you cannot raise your eyes to the sun, you can look at the river. A
   returning sound fails the test at the first word. **Milk passes it.** The pail is the version of her
   call that has a surface.

### It is in the FORM, not only in the production spec
For the twenty-four seconds of §4 **the kulning is removed entirely** and only the 55 Hz sub remains, so
**the listener is also handed the reflected view** — for one section you hear the call only as a low ring
in the floor, exactly as she is only seeing it as rings in the milk. The audience and the woman look down
at the same time. That is the APOD's two-in-one-frame executed as an arrangement decision, and it is
stated in the lyric as well as staged in the mix.

### ⛔ They never resolve
The rings stop at the rim; the call does not stop at the ridge. The milk never reaches Silja and the call
never rings in the pail's language. When the kulning re-enters at §5 it enters **over** the sub, not out
of it — no crossfade, no morph, no shared register. **Two eyes, one frame, no reconciliation.**

## 3. ⭐ THE SEAM, STAGED AS A REGISTER PLAN

| Band | Contents | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 700–4 kHz | the call, unvibratoed, overtone-heavy | her |
| ⛔ **200–600 Hz** | **cut to near-silence** | ⭐ **the valley** — the gap the call has no right to cross |
| 30–60 Hz | the sub, mono, centred, on the same note | the far body, felt not heard |

**The mids open exactly once** — §3, when the neighbour answers — and close again. **That is the seam,
and it is placed at the junction that carries the meaning:** the only moment the gap is inhabited is the
moment somebody crosses it. The union of a bare throat and a frequency you feel in the chest is the
forbidden union, and the empty band between them is the permission being asked for.

## 4. VERSE ARCHITECTURE — call and answer, answer always shorter

Fixed interval throughout: **CALL (6–8 words) → ANSWER (2–4 words)**, never inverted, never equal.
Three answering mouths, marked so the device stays alive:

| Section | Who answers | Character |
|---|---|---|
| §1 | the herd | bells and hooves as short sung syllables |
| §3 | the far woman | her own words, older, cheaper on breath |
| §5 | the names' bells | one per name, in order |
| §2 / §6 / §8 | the gang | `Kom hem, Silja!` |

⭐ The ear learns the length of the gap in §1 and can predict it by §3 — so the **one** unanswered call in
§5 (Silja's name, bell late) lands as a physical event and needs no explanatory line.

## 5. THE HINGE — §5 `[The Count]`

The count is by **name**, not number (see step 06 §8), which is both what herders did and the only way to
count under the one-numeral rule. Order: **Rosa → Björk → Kajsa → Silja.** Three bells, then a gap where
the fourth bell goes. Immediately after the gap, the song spends its single permitted numeral:

> **Eighty years of calling off this stone —** *(the number)*
> **and the how of it is still coming home.** *(the response)*

⭐ It is **responded to, never recited**: the number is not information, it is the length of a wait, and
the line that follows converts an unsolved thing into a distant thing — the pair's assigned discharge and
the run's seed arriving in the same couplet.

## 6. DURATION — "loop until it hurts"

§8 repeats `Kom hem, Silja!` past the point a pop structure would cut, with three graded changes:
the chant **tightens** (gaps shorten), the lead **roughens** (breath audible, pitch fraying at the top),
and the sub **rises** in level while the call **falls**. Nothing is added. It is the same four syllables
losing their varnish, which is the rough-debt happening in real time rather than being described.

## 7. TIKTOK / HOOK CYCLES (L-method, "Optimal Virality")
- **Hook cycle A — 0:58–1:22** (chorus 1): the chant, clean, learnable on one pass.
- **Hook cycle B — 3:34–3:58** (loop): the same chant, wrecked.
- **8–9 s micro-loop:** `Kom hem, Silja! / (Kom hem, Silja!) / The clean note dies at the stone.`
- The four syllables are shoutable with no Swedish and no context. That is the whole ask of a gang return.

## 8. ARRANGEMENT RULES (binding on step 09)
1. ⛔ **No third material.** No glitch, no code-scratch, no 8-bit, no breakcore. N = 1 plus one intruder.
2. ⛔ **The call never descends into the sub's register; the sub never rises into the call's.**
3. ⛔ **No reverb tail long enough to read as an echo** on the lead — the reflection is the pail, and a
   half-second slap would let a listener mistake the echo for the second eye.
4. **Mono sub, centred.** Hard-panned low content fights the generator (L22) and it would also break the
   physical claim: a chest frequency has no stereo position.
5. **Free-time calls over a metred floor.** The pulse is the sub's; the voice does not obey it. The
   disagreement between them is the last thing to resolve, and it does not.
