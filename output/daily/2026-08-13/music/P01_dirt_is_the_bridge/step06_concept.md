## 0. THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS (answered before a line was written)

**Where is the body standing?**
On a municipal bench on a canal towpath, in the shade of a low brick road-bridge, from about two in the
afternoon until the light goes off the water. Her back is on the brick. Her feet are in seed husks and
bottle caps. There is a wet cup-ring on the bench beside her that somebody else left. Above her the
underside of the arch is moving, because the canal is throwing light up onto it.

**What could hurt it here?**
She is being cooked. Not by anything she can see, and — this is the terror — **not by anything she can
feel arriving.** The shade removed the signal, so the skin has no reason to move. She will find the
damage tonight in a mirror, printed in the shape of the shelter she chose. The stake is small, physical,
and completely real: *the thing you hid inside handed you over, and it did it gently, over hours, while
you enjoyed the view.*

⭐ **AWE STAYS TERROR-ADJACENT — satisfied.** The awe and the harm are the same event. She is not warmed
*and also* burned; she is burned *because* something crossed to her that had no business crossing, and
that crossing is the most beautiful fact in the song.

## 1. THE METABOLISED PHYSICS (⛔ never reported)

The corona runs two hundred to five hundred times hotter than the surface under it, when every other star
cools outward. Eighty years unsolved. July 2026's lead candidate: **cosmic dust** — the impurity you would
have filtered out — may be what carries the energy across.

**What survives into the song, as body-scale fact and never as astronomy:**

| The physics | The body's version, which is the song |
|---|---|
| Hotter the further out | **You can be burned in the shade.** Scattered light reaches where direct light does not. |
| The impurity is the carrier | **The dust, pollen, brick powder and diesel haze in the air is what scattered it onto her.** Clean air would have missed her. |
| The corona is only visible when the disc is covered | **She can only look at the sun once another substance is holding it** — the canal. |
| The heat is felt where it should not be | **The shade removed the warning, so the delivery was silent.** |

⛔ The words *sun, corona, star, degrees, plasma, science, particle* — only "sun" survives, once, and only as
a thing lying in brown water. **No astronomy is sung.** Nothing is explained. There is no number in the
lyric at all.

⭐ **AND THE MYTH IS THE SAME SENTENCE.** Lofn wins permission for the union that is otherwise forbidden.
So the song is not a report of a transfer — **it is a love song to something that had no route to her and
came anyway.** The chorus is a blessing; the bridge is an invitation refused in favour of a longer road.
The sapphic register ("come lie down inside", "come and lie down on my arms where it fell") is not
decoration — it is the literal reading of the goddess's job.

## 2. THE NAMED OBSCURE EMOTION — ⛔ **never enters a prompt or a lyric**

> ### **LEEBURN** *(n.)*
> *The late, private astonishment of finding you were warmed — and scorched — inside the very shelter you
> chose, by a heat that had no straight road to you; the flinch and the gratitude arriving in the same
> second, because whatever carried it across was the part of the air you would have wiped off a lens.*

**Why this word.** *Lee* is the sheltered side (Old Norse **hlé**) — her own ground, not borrowed. *Burn*
is both the scorch and, in Scots and the north, **a small running stream.** So the name of the emotion
already contains the two things that cannot resolve into each other: the mark on the skin and the water
that threw the light. ⭐ **THE SECOND EYE IS INSIDE THE NAME OF THE FEELING.** That is where it was
found; it is not retrofitted.

**Where LEEBURN is felt in the song and never named:** the outro. She has the shape of the arch on her
shoulder, it stings, it will sting all week, and the last thing she sings is still a blessing.

## 3. FACETS (≥8 substantive)

1. **Place** — a canal towpath bench under a low brick road-bridge, mid-afternoon into evening. Municipal,
   free, slightly grubby, and hers for as long as she wants it. ACCESSIBLE arm: anyone has sat here.
2. **The unlookable direct** — the glare up the bank. Her eyes shut on it by themselves, *"like a door."*
   She is never able to look at the thing that is reaching her.
3. **The lookable reflection** — the sun lying flat in tea-brown canal water, come apart at the edges,
   throwing itself **up** onto the roof of the shade. She can look at that for as long as she likes.
4. **The carrier, named as dirt** — pollen, brick powder, diesel, wheel grit. The song blesses it by name.
   Blessing the impurity is the entire emotional argument, and it is sung, not implied.
5. **The silent delivery** — the shade took away the feeling that would have told her to move. This is
   where the fear lives and it is never resolved.
6. **The refusal** — at the bridge she could walk up the bank into the whole of it and she stays.
   **She chooses the long dirty way over the direct one.** That is the turn.
7. **The record** — the burn found later in a mirror, in the shape of the arch. `D9`: it stings, it will
   sting all week, and she blesses it anyway. **The kept bad thing stays bad.**
8. **Temperature as harmony** — verses sit in B-flat minor; the chorus leans into its relative major.
   The far part of the song is the warm part. The thesis is in the key change.
9. **Temperature as mix** — dry, cool, mono centre (kick, log drum, sub); saturated, bright, long-tailed
   edges (piano top octave, chopped syllables, shaker). **The mix runs hotter the further it gets from
   the middle.** L22-safe: this is a tonal instruction, not a hard-pan of non-musical elements.
10. **Temperature as prosody** — see step 07. The rhyme itself only exists in the far place.

## 4. GENRE BED — ⭐ ASSIGNED, NOT CHOSEN: **PIANO BOUNCE (Amapiano × Jersey Club)**

⛔ **The bed is the thesis.** Amapiano is Gauteng, unhurried, log-drum-led, jazz-voiced, built around
space. Jersey club is Newark, 130-plus, triplet-kick, bed-squeak, built around impact. **They have no
business in the same bar.** The seed is a transfer that had no permission; the bed is a union that had
none. Putting the physics on top of a neo-baroque pad would have made the song *about* an idea. Putting
it on a groove that is itself an unsanctioned crossing makes it *of* one.

⛔ **DEFAULT REFUSED.** Ethereal neo-baroque luminism, crystalline soprano, harpsichord, glass harmonica,
choir pads and cathedral reverb are named in the EXCLUDE field of all four songs specifically because they
are this personality's strongest prior and what is not forbidden gets defaulted.

**Register rotation (GOLDEN MOVE 5):** the house fingerprint is crystalline soprano / A major / ~110 BPM /
frost-and-cosmos. This pair ships a **warm hoarse contralto**, flat-key jazz voicings, four different
tempos, and a palette of brick, water, canvas, diesel and skin. The head-voice lift is rationed to one
line per chorus — and it lands at the far edge, which is the thesis again.
