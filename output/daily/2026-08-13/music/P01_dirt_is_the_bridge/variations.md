## V1 — **"Bless the Dust"**

**Scene:** A canal towpath bench in the shade of a low brick road-bridge, mid-afternoon to evening.
**The body-scale physics:** Burned in the shade by scattered light.
**Tempo / key:** 116 BPM · B-flat minor, chorus leaning to its relative major (D-flat)
**Vocal:** Female late-twenties **contralto** — low, close, a little hoarse, audible breath, dead centre and dry. One **thin head-voice lift per chorus**, on the last line, at the far edge. Soft adult crowd doubles on choruses two and three.
**Hinge (the double):** after the first chorus (~2:30) · **Bold device:** **the heat-shimmer bend** — the piano's top notes drift a few cents sharp across each chorus while the sub holds pitch

**⭐ THE SECOND EYE**
- **DIRECT —** **Verse 2** — the heat on her shoulders, delivered through pollen, brick powder, diesel and wheel grit, from a glare her eyes shut against *"like a door."*
- **REFLECTED —** **THE DOUBLE** — the sun lying flat in tea-brown canal water, throwing itself **up** *"onto the roof of the shade."* Carried by water, moving upward, and she can look at it as long as she likes.
- **⛔ Kept from resolving —** *"Up there it shuts my eyes. Down here it opens them."* then *"I keep the sun I can hold."* — she chooses between them; she never equates them.
