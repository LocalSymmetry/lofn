## DRAFTING NOTES

**On the L41 residue.** Four lines reached for it while drafting and all four were substituted, not deleted:
*"Nobody told me the awning was a window"* → **"The awning turned out to be a window."** ·
*"Nothing here feels like heat"* → **"Everything here feels like weather."** ·
*"the stone it can never reach"* → **"the roof of the shade"** ·
*"Something came down that I could not feel"* → **"Something came down while I felt the cold."**
⭐ In every case the substitution is **better than the negation it replaced**, and in three of four the
replacement is the more physically accurate line as well. Reported as data for the run's §5 experiment.

**Numerals caught and removed while drafting:** *"the corner one"*, *"from seven in the morning"*,
*"by two they are hot"*, *"read the same page twice"*, *"the one I can hold"* → **"the sun I can hold"**
(⭐ the fix improved the line). Final lyrics are numeral-free, verified by regex in step 10.
