The brief said: *do not use "nobody"/"nothing" as your discharge — you were assigned MISREADING for exactly that reason. Report it if it appears anyway.*

**It appeared anyway. Six times, in the first draft of V1**, unprompted, in a pair whose discharge mechanism was explicitly assigned to something else: *"Nobody told them"* · *"Nobody argues with a bug"* · *"Nobody said it"* · *"Nobody warned us"* · *"Nobody corrects them"* · *"Nobody here."*

**Finding for the run's experiment:** in this pair, assigning the discharge did **not** prevent the residue from being drafted.
