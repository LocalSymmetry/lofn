# V2 — **THE DARK YOU CAN LOOK AT**

**Angle:** the old folk trick — a basin of water to look at a covered sun. One woman, no crowd. The reflection is not found, it is **fetched and carried**, and she takes it home.
**Inversion:** the handoff runs the other way. She starts in **head voice** (the sky) and **drops into chest** (the bucket) — the ground gets the body voice.
⭐ **Second Eye:** `[THE SUN ITSELF]` — **six seconds, one line, cut before its own noun** — against `[THE BUCKET]`, a full ten-line section at half-time. The direct eye here cannot even finish naming what it is.

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

```

Female Irish-diaspora alto starts high and airy, a floated head voice spread wide across the top of the field, then drops into a low close-mic'd chest register hard left of centre for the second half. # V4 — **THE BIRDS WENT FIRST**

**Angle:** the afterwards, and the terror half of the emotion. The song **starts after totality has ended** — the light back at full strength with no announcement, and every bird on the street singing a dawn chorus into the middle of the afternoon. The narrator's eye is already hurt and she does not check it.
**Structure:** no pre-chorus, ten-line verses, a **double-length bridge**, and an **a cappella coda**.
⭐ **Second Eye:** `[THE BIRDS ABOVE]` — **nine seconds, cut mid-phrase**, birds against a returned sun, which is now the most dangerous thing to look at in the city — against `[THE WET STONE]`, the whole flock running in the flooded flagstones, full section. **Handoff: chest into a raw chest-break belt**, left of centre — the only variation where the lift is downward in polish rather than upward in pitch.
