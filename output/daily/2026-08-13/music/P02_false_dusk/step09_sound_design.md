# STEP 09 — SOUND DESIGN · P02 · GAELIC DRILL, FOLK-FORWARD

## The bed, held honestly

**GAELIC DRILL = Celtic folk instrumentation carrying the melody and the identity, over UK-drill rhythm programming carrying the floor.** Assigned, not chosen, and ⚠️ **explicitly not one of the run's two distortion beds.** The drill is the ground the folk stands on; it is never the event.

⛔ **What this bed is not:** it is not a Celtic melody with a trap beat under it, and it is not drill with a whistle sample on top. Both of those are the default and the default is what the brief forbids. The test: **if you mute the fiddle, whistle, harp and bodhrán, what is left should be too thin to be a song.** The programming is a floor.

---

## THE MODE IS THE MISREADING

**V1: E Dorian.** A minor mode with a **raised sixth** — the note that makes the ear file it as major for one bar before the flat third arrives and files it as minor. ⭐ **A key that is confidently mistaken about itself.** The whole song's subject sits in the interval, and no listener will ever consciously notice it, which is correct.

Every variation rotates to a mode with the same property — one degree that contradicts the ear's first guess:

| | mode | the contradicting degree | BPM | half-time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **E Dorian** | raised 6th in a minor mode | 142 | 71 |
| V2 | **D Mixolydian** | flat 7th in a major mode | 138 | 69 |
| V3 | **A Dorian** | raised 6th in a minor mode | 146 | 73 |
| V4 | **B Dorian** | raised 6th in a minor mode | 140 | 70 |

⭐ **Register rotation (Golden Move 5):** the house winner's fingerprint is crystalline soprano / A major / ~110 BPM / frost-and-cosmos. This pair ships **chest-first alto**, **modal**, **138–146 BPM**, **wet stone and river water**. Nothing here is in the fingerprint.

## INSTRUMENTATION, WITH PHYSICAL ADJECTIVES

**Melodic / identity layer (real, acoustic, declared as such in the prompt):**
- **Low whistle** — breathy, over-blown at the top of a phrase, the intake audible. Opens the song alone for five seconds.
- **Fiddle** — rosined, a drone string ringing under the melody, tuned down a quarter step in the intro so the first sound in the song is an instrument being told it is wrong.
- **Wire-strung harp** — metallic, long decay, damped with the heel of the hand. This is the water instrument; it enters when the eye goes down to the river.
- **Bodhrán** — goatskin, tipper, tuned loose. The one hand-played thing in a machine-timed song.

**Rhythm floor (programmed, declared as such):**
- Dry small drill kick, dotted and off-grid.
- Triplet rim / hat pattern with **a splash of vintage new jack swing** in the shuffle.
- **Sliding sub-808**, soft, round, mono, low in the mix. ⛔ Never trunk-rattling — that would make this a distortion bed, and it is not one.

---

## THE TWO SIGNATURE STYLES (personality override rule)

**1. THE TIPPER SEAM.** The bodhrán is played by a hand and the kick is played by a grid, and the bodhrán lands **a hair behind the kick for the entire song** — never corrected, never quantised, never apologised for. It reads as swing to a casual ear and as *a living thing keeping confident time against a clock that says otherwise*, which is the song. **This is the one seam, placed at the junction that carries the meaning.**

**2. THE TEN-SECOND HOLE.** Every other section runs 25–30 seconds. One section runs **ten** and stops mid-word with a hard cut to true silence — no reverb tail, no decay, no ramp. It returns later at **two seconds**. ⭐ The bold sonic device is a **duration**, not a texture, and it is the form rule made audible without a word of explanation.

## THE REGISTER HANDOFF (the hinge), engineered

| | before | at the handoff | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| **voice** | chest alto, close-mic'd, breath in the take, dead centre | lifts on the word **"Look"** | floated head voice, panned slightly right, more air than body |
| **groove** | 142 full-time drill | halves on the same downbeat | 71, half-time drag |
| **harmony** | fiddle drone + whistle | — | harp takes the middle, whistle drops to a low counter |
| **sub** | sliding, present | — | soft and round, further back |
| **what does NOT change** | key, the vowel braid, the singer | | ⭐ these hold so the handoff reads as one body doing two things |

**Return:** she drops back to chest at Verse 2 with no ceremony and no fill. The head voice is not treated as an arrival, because looking at a reflection is not an achievement.

## SPATIAL MAP
- **Dead centre:** lead alto, kick, sub (mono).
- **Slightly right:** the head voice, when it appears. Tin whistle.
- **Left:** wire-strung harp, rosined fiddle drone.
- **Wide / room:** river ambience, crowd hush, the single close-mic'd cricket in the outro which sits **closer to the listener than the singer does** — the last thing in the song is the mistaken creature, and it is the nearest object in the mix.

## THE FUNK VOCABULARY AGAINST A FOLK BED (SOURCE-2, the friction)

⚠️ These are verbatim production instructions inside the shipped style field, taken as **words, not as a genre**. The friction is the point and it is the run's seed — an unsanctioned union.

| verbatim | what it is actually instructing | which songs |
|---|---|---|
| *"swagger, humor, and sheer danceability"* | the **bodhrán tipper's** posture — a hand drum with attitude under a grief melody | V1 V2 V3 V4 |
| *"furiously slapping and popping"* | the **bodhrán rim** across the drill triplets | V1 V3 V4 |
| *"a splash of vintage new jack swing"* | the **hat shuffle** — swung sixteenths against a rigid kick | V1 V2 |

⭐ **The instruction is honoured and inverted at once:** a slap-bass posture executed at a volume where it cannot show off. That is a Minneapolis-funk figure asked for by name — which is why André Cymone and Bootsy Collins are credited in every dedication.

## PREDICTED RENDER (describe-render self-check)

**What this would actually produce:** five seconds of a single breathy low whistle over thinning birdsong with no drums, then a dry drill kick and a soft sliding sub arriving under a close, low, unglamorous female alto singing short end-stopped lines — the vocal noticeably chestier and drier than the usual pad-drenched default. A bodhrán rolls under the chorus with a whistle counter-line over the top; the groove limps slightly because the hand drum sits behind the grid. At about 1:15 everything drops to one whistle and a rim for **ten seconds** and cuts off mid-word into silence, then the tempo halves, a harp comes up, and the same singer lifts into head voice for the longest section in the song.

**Name the one way this would render generic.** ⚠️ **The bodhrán and fiddle get smoothed into a generic "Celtic pad" and the whole thing renders as a mid-tempo folk-pop ballad with a trap kick** — the safe outcome, and the one the generator reaches for whenever a folk instrument meets a programmed beat. **Repair applied (one pass):** the style field now (a) names the instruments as **acoustic and real** in an explicit final clause, (b) specifies **wire-strung** harp and **rosined** fiddle and a **goatskin tipper** rather than "Celtic instrumentation", (c) states the kinetic defect as a timing relationship the generator must reproduce rather than an adjective, and (d) the exclude field blacklists `plastic Celtic synth pad`, `new-age reverb wash`, `MIDI harp` and `quantized bodhran` by name. **Second risk, accepted:** the ten-second cut may be smoothed into a normal-length breakdown. This is why the cut is also **written into the lyric as an unfinished sentence** — *"It has no edge. It has no—"* — so that even a smoothed render still carries the truncation in the words. **THE GRAIN LAW: a form objection answered only in the production spec is not answered.**

## LINEAGE — the living scenes this stands on
**Irish traditional music is alive**; it is not a texture library. **UK drill is alive**; it is a producer's grammar built in South London within the last decade. **Minneapolis funk** supplied the slap-and-pop posture by name. All three are named, credited to living players and producers, and pointed at — never captured. Full credit block in `step10_production_wrap.md`.
