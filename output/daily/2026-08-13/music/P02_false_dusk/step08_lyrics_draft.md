## THE DRAFT

```
[Theme: A stone embankment above a river at noon; the sky goes out and the insects file a report the day has not earned.]
[SONG FORM: TWO EYES — Gaelic drill. The direct section runs ten seconds and cuts mid-word; the reflected runs full.]

[Intro - EMO:Anticipation - Female alto whispered - Low whistle, no beat, birds thinning]
*a gull calls once, then the rail goes quiet*
*fiddle drone tuning down a quarter step*
Listen. The birds are stopping.
They stop because the day is done.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Wonder - Female alto chest, close-mic - Bodhran, sub slide]
The birds went quiet in the dust.
The river took the light on trust.
The tower leaned into the song.
The middle of the day went long.
A woman put her hand up: hush.
The whole embankment stopped its rush.
The insects started, small and strong.
They sang the evening. Sang it long.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Tenderness - Female alto - Low-pass, rim only]
The crickets keep the evening long.
The crickets have the evening right.
I held my tongue. I did not rush.
I let them have it. Let them hush.

[Chorus - EMO:Awe - Female alto + tight double - Folk-drill, whistle counter]
It is evening. The birds are done.
The crickets have it, every one.
Give them the dark. Give them the dust.
Give them the evening. Give them the trust.
Tower goes out. The water goes long.
Let them be evening. They are not wrong.
I will not be the one who says.
I will not be the one who says.

[Post-Chorus - EMO:Awe - Female alto + gang - Whistle hook, bodhran roll]
Evening, evening, out of turn.
Evening, evening, let it burn.
Evening, evening, in the dust.
Evening, evening. Let them. Trust.

[THE DIRECT EYE - EMO:Dread - Female alto bare - One whistle and rim - TEN SECONDS, CUT MID-WORD]
Don't look up. That one's not ours.
A hole where the middle was.
It has no edge. It has no—
*hard cut to silence*

[THE RIVER EYE - EMO:Tenderness - Female alto lifts to floated head voice - Half-time, harp, soft sub]
Look down instead. The river has one.
Black coin. You are allowed that one.
It shivers when the barges brush.
It does not burn. It does not rush.
Not the same. Both of them true.
The other is up. This one is down.
A woman leans beside me. Hush.
Her breath goes out. My breath goes out.
Both of them. Never the same one.
I watch the water. I watch the stone.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Reverence - Female alto back to chest - Fiddle low, drill tight]
The fiddle found the drone and stuck.
A dog lay down and licked the dust.
The swifts came in because they must.
They filled the tower with their trust.
The bats came out along the wall.
The moths went up to meet the stone.
We all agreed it was the dusk.
The evening had its witnesses.

[THE DIRECT EYE, reprise - EMO:Dread - Female alto, no beat - TWO SECONDS]
Still up there. Still not ours.
It has no edge. It has no—
*cut*

[Bridge - EMO:Dread - Female alto spoken-sing - Bodhran heartbeat, one whistle]
Then it came back. Not slow. Not sorry.
It came back hard and all at once.
The light came up out of the dust.
The crickets carried on. On trust.
They sing the evening into noon.
The afternoon agrees with them.
And I agree. I let it stand.
I will not be the one who says.

[Outro - EMO:Yearning - Female alto head voice - Harp, whistle, river room, no beat]
*water against stone*
*a single cricket, close-mic*
She said goodnight to me.
I said goodnight to her.
We did not trade our names.
She will remember me.
*cricket alone, then room*
```

## CRAFT NOTES

### The vowel braid, line by line
The chorus alternates the two families on alternating lines and the ear hears it as a swing:

| line | final word | family |
|---|---|---|
| It is evening. The birds are **done**. | done | ŏ |
| The crickets have it, every **one**. | one | ŏ |
| Give them the dark. Give them the **dust**. | dust | ŭ |
| Give them the evening. Give them the **trust**. | trust | ŭ |
| Tower goes out. The water goes **long**. | long | ŏ |
| Let them be evening. They are not **wrong**. | wrong | ŏ |
| I will not be the one who **says**. | says | — |
| I will not be the one who **says**. | says | — |

The last couplet drops out of the braid entirely. That is deliberate: **the only lines in the chorus that are about the narrator rather than the creatures are the only lines that do not participate in the pattern.** She is outside the thing she is protecting.

### The misreadings, in order of appearance
1. *"They stop because the day is done."* — line 2 of the song. The listener is inside the wrong model before the drums arrive.
2. *"They sang the evening."* — not *"they sang as if it were evening."*
3. *"The crickets have the evening right."*
4. *"Let them be evening. They are not wrong."*
5. *"We all agreed it was the dusk."* — the humans join.
6. *"The afternoon agrees with them."* — the *afternoon* joins. Nothing in the frame is left to adjudicate.
7. *"She will remember me."* — ⭐ the narrator's own, last line of the song, four words after *"We did not trade our names."* The listener performs the correction the song refuses to.

### The line that carries the seed
*"The light came up out of the dust."* — in the Bridge, at the moment the light returns. The run's Golden Seed stated once, physically, without a word of explanation, and never returned to.

### Things deliberately not done
- **No question marks anywhere.** A question invites correction.
- **No simile for the dark.** The creatures are reporting, not comparing.
- **No numeral.** "Ninety seconds" is the pair's name and never a lyric.
- **No "nobody" and no "nothing."** See the L41 finding in `step06_concept.md` §5 — six were drafted and all six were replaced with positive false assertions, which is what the mechanism actually asks for.

### Kill list (drafted, cut, why)
| cut line | why |
|---|---|
| *"Nobody argues with a bug."* | L41 residue; an absence-report where a false assertion belongs. Replaced by *"The crickets keep the evening long."* |
| *"The clock above the bakery says something else."* | An adjudicating object in the frame. That is a wink, and the mechanism forbids winks. |
| *"The evening had a hundred proofs."* | Sung numeral. Replaced by *"The evening had its witnesses."* |
| *"Two of them. Never the same one."* | "Two" reads as a count. Replaced by *"Both of them."* — same meaning, no numeral. |
| *"A child laughed once"* | Ban: no children. |
