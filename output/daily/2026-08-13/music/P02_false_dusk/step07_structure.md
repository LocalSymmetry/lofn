# STEP 07 — STRUCTURE / SONG GUIDE · P02 · NINETY SECONDS OF FALSE DUSK

## The two pre-draft questions (answered before a line was written)

**Where is the body standing?**
On a stone embankment above a broad river in the middle of a city, at noon, in a crowd pressed against a low parapet. Her forearms are on cold stone. There is a woman beside her she has not met. Below, the water is holding a black coin.

**What could hurt it here?**
The light comes back without an announcement — full strength, no ramp — and the eye that checks gets a struck match printed on it for the afternoon. Behind that: a few hundred bodies leaning forward at once in ninety seconds of blindness above a river, over a rail built for a calm day. And behind *that*, the one that does not heal: every creature there is running a wrong model, and a wrong model works perfectly right up until it doesn't.

## ⭐ WHERE THE SECOND EYE FALLS

> **THE SECOND EYE IS A DURATION, NOT A LINE.**

| | section | length | register | what it holds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DIRECT** | `[THE DIRECT EYE]` — after the first Post-Chorus, at roughly **1:15** | ⭐ **ten seconds**, against 25–30s everywhere else. Ends **mid-word**, hard cut to silence. | chest alto, bare, one whistle and a rim | the sun over the tower. Three lines. It cannot finish its own sentence: *"It has no edge. It has no—"* |
| **REFLECTED** | `[THE RIVER EYE]` — immediately after, at roughly **1:25** | **full section, ten lines**, half-time, the longest sung stretch in the song | ⭐ **the register handoff lands on its first syllable** — chest alto lifts into floated head voice on *"Look"* | the black coin in the water. You are allowed this one. |

**Why the form is the argument:** the listener is never told that one is unlookable and the other is lookable. The unlookable one is ten seconds long and cuts off; the lookable one is a whole verse and breathes. Nobody has to explain a duration.

**The reprise:** `[THE DIRECT EYE, reprise]` returns before the Bridge at **two seconds** — two lines, same cut, same unfinished noun. ⭐ **The direct eye shrinks on its second appearance.** The reflected eye is never reprised; it does not need to be.

⛔ **They never resolve.** The non-resolution is stated inside the reflected section itself, in the middle of the register lift, where it cannot be missed: *"Both of them. Never the same one."*

## THE RETURN (L21) — what comes back, and how often

- **Chorus: byte-identical ×3.** No note required.
- **Post-Chorus: byte-identical ×3.** Four lines, one word changing position, a whistle hook under it.
- **Pre-Chorus: byte-identical ×2.**
- **Anaphora:** *"Give them the —"* four times inside the chorus; *"Evening, evening,"* opening every post-chorus line.
- **The refrain that is the ethic:** *"I will not be the one who says."* — twice per chorus, and once more alone at the end of the Bridge, which is the only place it appears outside a chorus and therefore the place it means the most.
- ⭐ **THE VOWEL BRAID.** Two families alternate: **ŭ** (dust · trust · must · hush · rush · brush) and **ŏ** (song · long · wrong · strong · done · one · gone · stone). In the **verses** they alternate **couplet by couplet**. In the **chorus** they alternate **line by line**. The braid is therefore audible as a rhythm of vowels independent of meaning — and it is the same braid on both sides of the register handoff, which is what makes the handoff read as one voice doing two things rather than two voices.
- **Measured:** strict end-rhyme **0.708**, line return **0.528** — both far above the archive winners' 0.463 / 0.326. **Nothing was stripped, so no debt is owed.**

## THE HINGE — REGISTER HANDOFF

The hinge is not a lyric and not a drop. It is **one physical event**: the chest alto, which has been sitting low and close-mic'd with audible breath for eighty seconds, lifts into a floated head voice on the word **"Look"** at the top of `[THE RIVER EYE]`, while the groove halves and the harp comes up. Nothing else changes at that moment — same key, same braid, same singer.

**Why here:** the handoff coincides exactly with the eye moving from the sky to the water. The body's mechanism for looking away *is* the body's mechanism for changing register. That is the hinge earning its place instead of being scheduled.

**Return path:** she comes back down to chest at Verse 2 without ceremony. The song does not treat the head voice as an arrival.

## VERSE ARCHITECTURE — short declarative couplets

Assigned, and held across all four variations. Rules the pair writes under:
- Every line is **end-stopped** and **declarative**. No enjambment except once, deliberately, in the V3 bridge where the sentence spills to imitate a light switching on.
- **≤7.5 words per line** (measured 6.72 in V1).
- **No questions.** A question is a request for correction, and this song's whole subject is a correction that is not requested and not given.
- **No similes about the dark.** The dark is not *like* anything; the creatures are simply reporting it.

## ONE SEAM
**Dominant material:** Irish traditional instrumentation and its phrasing — fiddle drone, low whistle, bodhrán tipper, wire-strung harp. **The one intruder:** UK-drill rhythm programming — the sliding sub, the triplet rim, the kick that will not swing. **The seam:** the bodhrán, which is played by a human hand against a machine grid and lands a hair behind it all song. That is the junction that carries the meaning, and the subject is doing something across it — the whole song is a living creature confidently keeping time against a clock that says otherwise.

## SUNG NUMERAL
**NONE.** No numeric fact is sung. "Ninety seconds", "1905", "two hundred times hotter" all stay in the brief where they belong.
