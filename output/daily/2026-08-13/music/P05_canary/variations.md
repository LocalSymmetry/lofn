# VARIATIONS · P05 · **CANARY** — the four shipped packages
**Derivation:** ⛔ no global template. The variation logic comes from **this** concept —
*the heat arrives where it cannot go, and **the carrier is the impurity***. So each song hands the wrong
name to **a different carrier**, and each carrier gets its own room, its own spiral, its own two eyes.

| | title | room | ⭐ carrier | direct eye | reflected eye | tempo/key |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *Answer to Canary* | the vat room | **the paper** | the vat | the rinse ditch | 170 · F♯m |
| **V2** | *Say It at the Gate* | the gate, shift change, rain | **the crowd** | the name in the air | the name in chalk, running | 168 · Em |
| **V3** | *Yellow to the Throat* | the wash-house, dusk | **the skin** | her own throat | the black window | 85 · B♭m |
| **V4** | *Sparrows Come to Look* | the yard, afternoon | **the water** | the vat-room doorway | the ditch | 172 · A→F♯m |

⛔ Every one: the wrong name is used confidently and **never corrected**. No song contains
{wrong, correct, mistake, misfiled, actually, blue}. No cardinal numerals are sung. `nobody` / `nothing`
appear nowhere. All four sung by one invented woman across one afternoon.

⛔ In none of the four does any line say the two are the same thing.

## ⭐ EACH SPIRAL, AND ITS FIVE ONE-WORD CHANGES

| | base → final | the five words |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *Canary, says the **card** / I **carry** **it** / The **colour** / I answer to **it**.* → *says the **guard** / I **follow her** / The **yellow** / I answer to **her**.* | card→guard · colour→yellow · it→her · it→her · **carry→follow** |
| **V2** | ***They** call it / I **hold** it / **They** call it / The line **says** it.* → ***We** call it / I **sing** it / **We** call it / The line **sings** it.* | They→We · hold→sign · They→We · sign→sing · **says→sings** |
| **V3** | *at my **wrist** / I **wash** it / … / I look at **what** it says.* → *at my **throat** / I **watch** it / … / I look at **who** it says.* | wrist→elbow · wash→watch · elbow→collar · collar→throat · **what→who** |
| **V4** | *past the **door** / The **light** gets in / The sparrows **come** and **stand** / I **pour**.* → *past the **dark** / The **yellow** gets in / The sparrows **stay** and **drink** / I **kneel**.* | light→yellow · stand→drink · come→stay · door→dark · **pour→kneel** |

⭐ In all four the final change is the one that carries the second meaning, and in none of them is it explained.
