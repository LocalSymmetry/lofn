## Draft notes — what was cut and why

**Cut, all three for the same reason:**
- ~~*"I do not correct the card."*~~
- ~~*"I will not correct the wall."*~~
- ~~*"It is not the colour that you have been told."*~~

Every one of them is a soft reveal. **To decline a correction is to admit a correction exists**, and the
assigned mechanism is MISNAMING used *confidently*, never irony. The finished text contains no word from
{wrong, correct, mistake, misfiled, actually, really, blue}. The listener is left to notice that a thing
described as **cold, deep, and holding the sky** is being called yellow. That gap is the song.

**Also cut:** *"That's the one"* in the intro — `one` is a cardinal numeral and this pair is assigned
**SUNG NUMERAL: NONE.** Replaced with *"That's the batch. That's the drum."*, which is better anyway.

**Kept deliberately:** *"I go in first."* — `first` is an ordinal of entry order, not a numeric fact, and
it is the single unglossed appearance of the canary's second meaning. Reported at Step 10 rather than
hidden.

**Kept deliberately:** *"They send a body in"* — concrete, not an indefinite pronoun. It answers
*where is the body standing* in the same breath as the dread, and it keeps L41's negation residue
(*nobody* / *nothing*) out of the discharge entirely.
