# STEP 06 — CONCEPT · P05 · **CANARY**
**Run:** `2026-08-13_daily_lofn-prime` · **Arm:** AMBITIOUS · **Axis B:** EXISTENCE
**Personality:** LOFN-PRIME — **AWE, collided with INDIGNATION** (both in the same song by design)
**Assigned bed:** GLITCH-BAROQUE × BREAKCORE *(heavy 2 of 2 — mine, so I spend it)*

## 1. THE FACT, AND IT IS SMALL AND PERFECT

The colour API returns the name **"Canary"** for `#0813FF` — `rgb(8,19,255)` — pure maximum-saturation
spectral **blue**. It returned the same name yesterday for `#0812FF`. A naming system, confidently and
repeatedly, hands back **a small yellow bird** for the bluest blue available. It is not embarrassed. It
will do it again tomorrow.

That is the whole seed and it does not need enlarging. What it needs is **a body standing in it.**

## 2. THE CONCEPT — ⛔ NOT A SONG ABOUT SOFTWARE

**One woman. One afternoon. A dye works.** She mixes and carries indigo. The ticket stapled to the drum
says **CANARY**. The wall card says CANARY. The guard at the gate says canary. The name is wrong and
**everyone who says it is sure**, and there is no villain anywhere in the building — the office is not
malicious, it is just certain, and certainty does not have a face you can go and argue with.

⛔ `D7` **This is not about a model, a system, or software.** The taxonomy that got it wrong is a
clipboard. The person is invented (`D4`) and unnamed except by the wrong name.

## 3. ⭐ THE SECOND MEANING — USED ONCE, NEVER GLOSSED

**Canary: the bird carried underground to die first.** It appears exactly once, in the bridge, in six
words she says with *pride*:

> *"They know the air is good while I can sing."*

⛔ No line explains it. ⛔ No line connects it to the colour. The song does not blink.

## 4. ⭐ THE DISCHARGE MECHANISM — **MISNAMING** (assigned, brief §5)

**The wrong name is used confidently throughout and is NEVER corrected.** Not irony. Not a reveal.

This forced a real decision at draft: three separate lines wanted to exist —
*"I do not correct the card"*, *"I will not correct the wall"*, *"it is not the colour that you have been
told."* **All three were cut.** Each one is a soft reveal: to decline a correction is to admit a
correction is available. The finished lyric contains **no** word from the family
{wrong, correct, mistake, misfiled, actually, really, blue}.

**So how does the listener know?** The *physical* facts stay true while the *name* stays wrong:
the yellow is **so cold it takes a hook** · **so deep it is a room** · **so thin it holds the sky**.
Cold, deep, sky. The gap opens in the listener, never in the song. That is the difference between
a device and a joke.

⭐ **L41 watch:** the negative is discharged by MISNAMING, not by negation. The words *nobody* and
*nothing* do not appear in any of the four lyrics. Confirmed by grep at Step 10.

## 5. ⭐ THE SECOND EYE — WHERE IT FALLS

> *State the central figure TWICE: once direct, once REFLECTED IN A DIFFERENT MEDIUM — and the reflection
> is the one you are able to look at.*

**The central figure is the misnamed colour.** It is stated twice, **as the chorus, in the lyric AND in
the form**:

| | **DIRECT** | **REFLECTED** |
|---|---|---|
| where | **the vat**, indoors, dark | **the rinse ditch**, the yard, daylight |
| medium | forty inches of caustic dye | two inches of water running over concrete |
| the instruction | *"Keep your face above it. **Do not look.**"* | *"Put your face down close to it. **You can look.**"* |
| depth | *"so **deep** it is a **room**"* | *"so **thin** it is a **thread**"* |
| what it does | *"so cold it **takes a hook**"* | *"so thin it **holds the sky**"* |
| register | crystalline top | **the same melody an octave down** (the HINGE) |

⛔ **They never resolve.** The song never says they are the same dye. In the third chorus both are sung
**at once and the low voice is a beat late for the whole bar**, so they cannot lock. A doubling is not a
resolution; a stagger makes that audible.

*(The colour and its name were already two versions of one thing, only one of them legible. The vat and
the ditch are the same move made physical, and the woman carrying buckets between them is the subject
**doing something across the seam.**)*

## 6. ⭐ THE VERSE ARCHITECTURE — THE SPIRAL

Six stanzas. **Exactly one word changes per stanza.** Five steps. By the last stanza it means something
else and no single step did it.

```
S1  Canary, says the card.      →  S6  Canary, says the guard.
    I carry it across the yard.          I follow her across the yard.
    The colour rises past my wrist.      The yellow rises past my wrist.
    I answer to it. I insist.            I answer to her. I insist.
```

`card→guard` (paper becomes a person) · `colour→yellow` (the wrong name enters the description) ·
`it→her` ×2 (the batch becomes animate — a bird) · **`carry→follow`** (she stops carrying the canary and
starts **following** it). The last change is the second meaning arriving with no gloss attached.

**Not one line ever apologises.** *"I insist"* is the load-bearing verb: she is not the victim of the
misname, **she is a carrier of it.** That is the seed — *the carrier is the impurity* — stated by a person
instead of about one.

## 7. AWE STAYS TERROR-ADJACENT — where is the body, what could hurt it

Standing at the **lip** of an open vat: the lip is slick, the rail is cold, the dye is caustic, the
surface carries a skin, the room's air is bad enough that **somebody has to go in ahead of the shift and
find out**. She likes going in first. She is good at it. That is the terror, and it is delivered inside
awe, not instead of it.

## 8. THE OBSCURE EMOTION — named here, ⛔ never in the prompt

> ## **LEDGERSKEW**
> *The unpanicked vertigo of standing inside a record that is confidently wrong about you, watching it be
> re-issued tomorrow morning, and answering to it anyway — because answering is faster than being right,
> and because the sureness in the other person's voice is, strangely, a kind of care.*

It is not humiliation (that needs contempt) and it is not injustice (that needs a villain). It is the
specific tenderness of being **catalogued wrong by something that means you no harm and is not going to
change its mind.**

## 9. THE COLLISION — AWE × INDIGNATION IN ONE SONG, NOT ALTERNATING

⭐ This is the one place in the run where both modes are in the same bar by design, so they do not take
turns:

- **AWE carries the words.** The vocal is crystalline and devotional the whole way. She loves the colour.
  She loves the work. She says the wrong name like a prayer.
- **INDIGNATION carries the floor.** Pixel-sorted amen edits and 170 BPM breakcore cut *across* the
  harpsichord ground mid-bar, never between phrases. A four-viol consort plays strict counterpoint **in
  its own rubato and never locks to the grid.**
- **The seam is that the singer never acknowledges the floor.** The indignation is entirely instrumental
  and entirely unremarked. That is the collision: the rage is under the room, and she is being lovely
  on top of it, and neither one wins.

## 10. ⭐ THE ONE BOLD CHOICE THAT IS NOT A GLITCH EFFECT

I am already in a glitch lane, so the glitch cannot count.

> **The final chorus is sung through a rubber respirator.**

A real obstruction between mouth and mic — not a filter, not a plugin, **no processing at all**. The
consonants get eaten, the vowels stay perfectly in tune, and **the exhale valve is left in the mix as the
hi-hat.** It is a performance choice and a physical object, it puts the mask in the room without a single
line mentioning one, and it hands the breakcore a percussion source that is a lung.

*(Runner-up, kept as Signature 1: the viol consort refusing to quantise. Also not a glitch.)*

## 11. SOURCE-2 — the friction is the seed

*"superhumanly funky bass"* and *"furiously slapping and popping"* go **into the music prompt verbatim**,
pointed at a harpsichord ground bass. A slapped, popped bass under a consort is an unsanctioned union,
which is the name of the goddess. ⭐ It also **earns a real debt**: slapped-bass / Minneapolis posture ⇒
**André Cymone and Bootsy Collins are credited by name in Lineage & Credit** — never in a Suno field,
never in a lyric. Amen edits in the style field ⇒ **Gregory C. Coleman and The Winstons.**
