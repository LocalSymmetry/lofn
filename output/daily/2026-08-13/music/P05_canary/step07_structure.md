## 2. ⭐ WHERE THE SECOND EYE FALLS — AND WHY IT CANNOT RESOLVE

> **THE SECOND EYE IS THE CHORUS ITSELF, STATED TWICE ACROSS THE HINGE.**
> **Direct = Chorus A (§4, §7, §11) — the vat.** **Reflected = Chorus B (§12) — the rinse ditch.**

It is carried in **the lyric AND the form**, never only the production spec:

```
CHORUS A  (direct, high, indoors)          CHORUS B  (reflected, an octave down, outdoors)
Canary in the vat.                    →    Canary in the ditch.
Keep your face above it. DO NOT LOOK. →    Put your face down close to it. YOU CAN LOOK.
so DEEP it is a ROOM                  →    so THIN it is a THREAD
so COLD it TAKES A HOOK               →    so THIN it HOLDS THE SKY
I have signed the book                →    sparrows come to look
```

Same shape. Inverted. **Carried by another substance** — forty inches of caustic dye becomes two inches
of water running over concrete in daylight. **And the reflection is the one you are able to look at**,
stated as an instruction in each: *do not look* / *you can look.*

⛔ **THE NON-RESOLUTION IS MECHANISED, NOT PROMISED.** In **Chorus C** both are sung together and
**the low voice enters a beat late and stays a beat late for the entire section.** They are an octave
apart and a beat apart and they never land together, so the ear cannot fuse them. No line in the song
ever says the vat and the ditch hold the same dye.

*(Structural source: the APOD holds the same eclipse twice — once above the Cathedral-Basilica, once in
the Ebro. You cannot look at the sun. You can look at the river.)*

## 3. ⭐ THE SOUND/RETURN AXIS — **REBOUND RHYME ON BEAT 3**

The rule, stated so it is checkable: **every line-end rhyme is answered at BEAT 3 of the next line, not
at that line's end.** The rhyme bounces back early instead of waiting.

```
beat:      1      2      3        4
       They  print  it on the CARD.
       The   YARD   repeats it,  hard.        ← "yard" lands on beat 3 · rebound of "card"
       They  pin    it to my    COAT.
       My    THROAT repeats it, low.          ← "throat" on beat 3 · rebound of "coat"
       They  chalk  it on the   WALL.
       The   HALL   repeats it, small.        ← "hall" on beat 3 · rebound of "wall"
```

Three rebounds per pre-chorus, and the pre-chorus is the only place the device runs at full density —
elsewhere it appears once per section so it reads as a signature and not as a tic. ⭐ The rebound is also
**the misnaming's mechanism made audible**: the system says it, and the room says it back before the bar
is over.

## 4. ⭐ THE SPIRAL — THE ONE-WORD-PER-STANZA CHANGE, SHOWN

Six stanzas. **Exactly one word changes at each step. Five steps.**

| stanza | line 1 | line 2 | line 3 | line 4 | ⭐ **the word that changed** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | Canary, says the **card**. | I **carry** **it** across the yard. | The **colour** rises past my wrist. | I answer to **it**. I insist. | — *(base)* |
| **S2** | Canary, says the **guard**. | I carry it across the yard. | The colour rises past my wrist. | I answer to it. I insist. | `card → guard` |
| **S3** | Canary, says the guard. | I carry it across the yard. | The **yellow** rises past my wrist. | I answer to it. I insist. | `colour → yellow` |
| **S4** | Canary, says the guard. | I carry **her** across the yard. | The yellow rises past my wrist. | I answer to it. I insist. | `it → her` *(line 2)* |
| **S5** | Canary, says the guard. | I carry her across the yard. | The yellow rises past my wrist. | I answer to **her**. I insist. | `it → her` *(line 4)* |
| **S6** | Canary, says the guard. | I **follow** her across the yard. | The yellow rises past my wrist. | I answer to her. I insist. | ⭐ `carry → follow` |

### What each step does, and why no single one does the whole job

1. **`card → guard`** — the certainty leaves the paper and gets a body. The paper was never the problem.
2. **`colour → yellow`** — the wrong name stops being a label on a drum and becomes **her own
   description of what she sees**. This is the exact bar the misnaming enters her mouth.
3. **`it → her`** *(carried)* — the batch becomes animate. Still an object she is moving.
4. **`it → her`** *(answered to)* — ⭐ now **the woman and the batch share one name**, and the pronoun is
   the only thing that says so.
5. **`carry → follow`** — she is no longer moving the canary. **The canary is ahead of her.**

**Read S1 against S6 and it is a different song. Read any adjacent pair and it is a typo.** That is the
assignment.

⛔ Nothing in the spiral is ever corrected, apologised for, or explained. *"I insist"* is unchanged in all
six — the one fixed load-bearing verb.

## 5. THE HINGE — **OCTAVE DROP**, §10

Two bars. Drums gone, ground bass only.

```
[Hinge — OCTAVE DROP]
I go in first.
[the same line, an octave down]
I go in first.
```

The drop is **the pivot between the two eyes**: the voice that says it high is the one at the vat, and
the voice that comes back an octave under is the one that will sing Chorus B at the ditch. The hinge
does not carry a numeral (assigned: **NONE**) — it carries a register.
