  `dark/spark` · `rust/crust/dust` · `wire/fire` · `swept/kept` · `bright/tonight` · `heat/seat` ·
  `steel/wheel` · `and/hand/and` · `name/same` · `lead/bead` · `pail/rail` · `down/town` ·
  `light/tonight` · `black/back/back/stack` · `water/water` · `talk/talk` · `will/still`.

- ⛔ **Sung numerals: none.** No number-word appears in any sung line. *(The "eight-second loop" and
