## 2. ⭐⭐ WHERE THE SECOND EYE FALLS — *stated precisely, as required*

> **The central figure is the woman and her credit: one event, two versions.**

| | **STATEMENT ONE — DIRECT** | **STATEMENT TWO — REFLECTED** |
|---|---|---|
| **Section** | **§2, [Verse 1]** (bars 17–32) | **§10, [SECOND EYE]** (after the hinge, bars 89–104) |
| **Medium** | the arc itself — light, direct | ⭐ **standing rainwater on a road** |
| **Vantage** | hood **down**, parapet, hands in the heat | hood **up**, street level, looking down |
| **Lookable?** | ⛔ **NO.** *"Hood down. Hands in. Can't see it."* | ✅ **YES — and that is the point.** *"Big enough to look at. Look right back."* |
| **What it contains** | the hand, the heat, the joint | the **name**, backwards and buckled |
| **What it omits** | the name | ⛔ **the hand** |

⛔ **THEY DO NOT RESOLVE, AND THE LYRIC SAYS SO:**
> *"Hands aren't in it. Hands stay back. / Hand is in the heat. Heat holds the stack."*

There is no frame in this song that holds the hand and the name at once. **The direct statement would burn
your eye; the reflected statement has no hand in it.** The eclipse earns the rule and the song obeys it: you
cannot look at the sun, and you can look at the river.

**It is in the LYRIC and in the FORM, not in the production spec** (L22 THE GRAIN LAW): the half-time drop
at §10 is *how it sounds*, but the reflection is *stated in words* and *positioned in the arrangement* —
the only section in the song shot from street level, and the only one the vocal is allowed to look at.

## 3. THE HINGE — **THE INTERRUPTION** (§8)

She has never once, in three verses, said anything about herself. At bar 81 she starts to:

```
Hood up. Hands off. Here is what I—
(BRIGHT AND SON!)
Here is what I—
(BRIGHT AND SON!)
Here is what I—
```

- The gang vocal **lands on top of her, on the beat, three times.** It is not an answer — it is an overwrite.
- ⭐ **She never completes the sentence and the song never returns to it.** The one line she tried to say for
  herself is the one thing that does not return in a song built entirely out of returns. **THE RETURN (L21):
  removal is a debt — the debt is paid by the flux refrain and the hook, which return four and three times
  respectively. The unfinished line is the one deliberate hole and it is the subject.**
- Then: **two-bit crush → one beat of true silence** → the list resumes in §9 *as if nothing happened*, which
  is the flattest, brattiest possible response and the reason the verse architecture is a procedural list.

## 5. THE SEAM (L38 — ONE SEAM, N = 1)

- **Dominant material (the world):** **arc welding and sheet steel** — flux, clamp, wire, bead, grinder,
  ladder, pail. It is nine-tenths of the vocabulary.
- **The one intruder (the wound):** **the lit name** — letters, the ampersand, the sign, the crowd's mouth.
- **The seam, at the junction that carries the meaning:** ⭐ **the ampersand in [Verse 2]** — a welded joint
  that holds two names together and is itself not a name. *"And is the joint. And is my hand."*
- **The subject is doing something across it:** she is **welding it**. The seam is not observed; it is made,
  by her hands, in the section where the concept turns.

⛔ No second intruder. No collision engine. No genre fan-out inside the frame — the variety is across the
set, not inside this one.

## 6. WHAT THE ARRANGEMENT MUST NOT DO (L22 THE GRAIN LAW — run *with* the generator)

| ✅ Runs with the grain | ⛔ Fights the generator |
|---|---|
| Open cold on the hook, full band, bar one | a long ambient intro that gets smoothed to nothing |
| Beat **halves** under the verses (subtractive) | a full stop with untuned drone |
| One beat of true silence at the hinge only | multiple long silences |
| Half-time turn at the SECOND EYE | a tempo modulation |
| Thicken the final hook, then **strip to crowd + cowbell** | hard-panned non-musical noise as a "statement" |

⭐ **The Somatic/distinctiveness objection is answered in the LYRIC and the FORM** — the interruption, the
inverted line, the handover of the hook — **not in the production spec.** A spec-only answer is not an answer.
