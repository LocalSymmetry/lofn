# VARIATIONS · P03 · **THE ONE IN THE ROOM**
**Four variations derived from THIS pair's concept.** ⛔ **No global template.**
Every one is a different **body**, a different **trade**, a different **material**, a different
**carrier-impurity**, and a different **medium for THE SECOND EYE.** The only things held constant are the
things the pair was *assigned*: Baile Phonk bed, INDIGNATION, flat procedural verses, consonant hammer at
line-heads, hook at zero, the interruption as hinge, **DISPLACEMENT** as discharge, and ⛔ **no sung numeral.**

| | Title | Body / trade | ⭐ Carrier-impurity (the seed) | ⭐ SECOND EYE — the reflecting medium | Direct statement is unlookable because… | Tag chant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V0** | **DON'T LOOK AT IT** | sign fabricator / welder | **flux** — burns off, gets swept | **standing rainwater on a road** | it would burn your eye | *BRIGHT AND SON* |
| **V1** | **SOMEBODY'S HANDS** | hand model | **callus** — retouched out | **a lit screen at a bus stop** | it gets cropped at the wrist | *HANDS* |
| **V2** | **THE PASS** | line cook | **fond** — burnt residue, scraped up, *is* the sauce | **a backlit photo in a window** | steam is a wall | *CHEF* |

⭐ **The variation axis is the OPTICS OF THE REFUSAL** — five different physical reasons a hand cannot be
looked at, and five different substances that agree to carry the name instead. That axis comes out of this
concept and belongs to no other pair.

*"superhumanly funky bass"*

# V3 — **STAND CLEAR**
**140 BPM · D minor · half-time-dominant treatment** · SOURCE-2: *"a splash of vintage new jack swing"* ·
*"swagger, humor, and sheer danceability"*
**Obscure emotion, this angle:** *hearing your own voice be enormous and finding your throat is still small.*
⭐⭐ **SECOND EYE — the strongest of the five, because the medium change is literally acoustic:** direct =
her throat in a **foam-dead booth**, a voice that stops at her own head; reflected = **the same voice
carried by tile, air and a speaker**, huge and a beat late, in a tunnel. ⛔ Do not resolve: *"Mine has a
throat. Mine gets tired. / That one has tile. That one's wired."*
⭐ **The two signature styles ARE the form rule:** *Booth-Dry Lead* and *Tile Reverb Return* — the same voice
printed twice, never sharing a space.
**Hinge:** ⭐ **her own recorded voice interrupts her live one.** The reflection talks over the original.
⛔ **Note:** the direct statement is *acoustically dead*, never *"unheard"* — that is a material fact, not a
negated audience.
