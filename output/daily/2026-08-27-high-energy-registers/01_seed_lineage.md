## WHAT IS NEW HERE, AND THEREFORE WHAT IS ON TRIAL

1. ⭐⭐ **THE SATURATION INVERSION.** Every prior seed treated the listener's collapsing machinery as something to *outrun*. This one treats it as something that has **already finished running before the first bar ends.** If that is right, intensity is the worst possible weapon — it is what the template is built from — and the only move left is to name a resistance the template never bothers to have.
2. ⭐⭐ **LOUD IS A RELATION** — a direct, testable extension of a rule measured on pans to a property nobody has measured. **It may be wrong.** A level instruction might work where a spatial one does not. `scripts/measure_render.py` reads crest factor and short-term dynamics; the prediction is registered before any render and **the default expectation is that it dissolves.**
3. **FORCE WITHOUT A LOAD IS THE SINGLE HARD FAIL.** Previous runs' hard fails were contract violations (character caps, forbidden tokens). This one's is aesthetic and it is checkable by reading: *what, exactly, is this song pushing against?* If the answer takes more than one sentence, or is a person, or is nowhere in the sung text, the song has not started.
4. **A LOOSER BRIEF, ON PURPOSE, WITH A MEASUREMENT ATTACHED.** Removing form from the isolation briefs is a hypothesis: that shared-line percentage across a pair's four variations goes DOWN when the coordinator specifies less. Yesterday's two data points are 0.0% (disobeyed) and 34.7% (obeyed). **Measure it at `07` for all six pairs and report it, whichever way it goes.**
