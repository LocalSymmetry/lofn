# PAIR 06 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES · **SUNNA R5**

**Register:** THE SECOND YOU FIND OUT THE THING ACTUALLY MOVES · **⛔ Template:** THE EMPOWERMENT ANTHEM
**Tempo band:** 116–124 · **Arm:** AMBITIOUS · **Axis B:** NEWS

⭐ **One discriminator serves all four, and it is one word wide: SURPRISE.** The empowerment anthem knows at bar one — that is what makes it a boast. *"I did not know"* is a sentence it cannot sing and remain itself. Each song carries the surprise at a different distance from the event: **V1 is the second itself · V2 is the second after · V3 is the minute after · V4 is the day after, from somewhere else.**

| **Peak, singular and close** | *Up. / On its end. / Ramp in.* |

**FORM — THE INVENTORY.** ⭐ **The chorus core is byte-identical four times and the list under it GROWS BY ONE ITEM EVERY RETURN.** Appetite has a shape and this is it: a list of things not yet had, getting longer while the record does not get bigger.

### ⛔ THE ANTI-RESENTMENT ENGINE — grammatical, not moral
1. ⭐ **NOBODY WITHHELD IT.** *"Nobody hid it. It is printed on the lid."* The card was public, honest, obeyed by everyone including whoever printed it, and true about a case that is not this case. **No villain → nothing to resent → the only thing left to do with the discovery is spend it.**
2. ⭐ **THE PAST TENSE IS RATIONED.** Grievance lives in the accounting of wasted years; appetite lives in the future. **F2 gate: future/desiderative clauses ≥ 2× past-tense clauses.**
3. ⭐ **SHE TAKES HER SHARE.** *"I never picked it up to find out."* A singer who is also implicated cannot be the wronged party.

| **R5 cost** | ⭐ **the setting nobody wrote down.** She can turn it; she cannot put it back where it was, and there is no record of where that was |

**FORM — STANDING STILL.** One eight-line block returns three times with **only its last line replaced**, and the verses between get shorter as the decision does not get made: 8 → 6 → 4.
⭐ **This is the one that loses.** Every musical promise is kept in full. The sentence is the only thing that does not arrive.

## V4 — "GOING ROUND WITHOUT ME" · 124 BPM · A♭ major, no modulation

| field | value |
|---|---|
| **Angle** | IT MOVES FOR SOMEONE ELSE — because I set it up and left |
| **Room · hour** | a park path, late afternoon, going-home light |
| **The thing** | a seized roundabout bearing |
| **R1 resistance, INDEXED** | **rust** — *"Rust has been chewing this bearing since the last coat went on."* |
| **Inherited body** | **melodic forward bass motion** — a bassline that is its own song, with the third note deliberately missing |
| **Current chassis** | broken-beat electronic kit with swung sixteenths; the squeak itself clipped into sample punctuation; vocal chops as the counter-melody; current-edit lift |
| **R4 event that stops once** | **the squeak, once per revolution** → stops when the grease reaches the bearing. That is the entire job |
| **Consequence class** | **spectrum + restart** — the top opens and the squeak's sample is gone · invariant: the sub **and** the bass riff |
| **R5 cost** | ⭐ **the tin, and the knowing.** She leaves the grease on the post; nothing that happens next has her in it |
| **One sung numeral** | **eleven** (times round off a single shove), at the hinge, answered by *"I counted, and then I stopped counting."* |
| **Checkable return** | *"**It'll** go for the next hand"* → *"**It** goes for the next hand"* — one word |
| **Peak, singular and close** | *Round. / Round. / Gone.* |

**FORM — LEAVE BEFORE THE LAST CHORUS.** ⭐⭐ The final chorus is **set up completely** — the pre-chorus lands, the turnaround happens, the stack starts it — and **the lead is already gone.** The record plays its last chorus out on the riff with nobody singing it.

> ⭐ **This is the pair's one deliberate overrule of the centre, and it is recorded in `## Major Deviations`.** The metaprompt's move 4 says *keep every musical promise; let the only broken promise be in the sentence.* For V4 the broken promise **is the form**, because the angle is *it moves for someone else because I set it up and left* — and **a song that plays its own last chorus has not left.** *Her sets famously end one song early. She does not play the encore.* The doctrine and the personality disagreed here; per `vault/PANTHEON.md` the personality wins inside a Sunna run.

## ⭐ CROSS-SONG AUDITS, RUN AT GUIDE TIME

**THE SETTING AUDIT.** upstairs bedroom, night · loading bay, first light · plant room, night-under-a-stairwell · park path, late afternoon. **Four rooms, four hours, tell-apart-able by room alone.** ⛔ Domestic-warm: one of four, and it is a hot bedroom — no kitchen, no kettle, no tea, no toast, no armchair anywhere in the pair.

**THE ANGER AUDIT.** *If every song would work with the same distortion pedal, the set has one bed.* There is no distortion pedal in this pair at all. Four different low-end engines (FM sub in whole steps · wavetable strut · immovable mono sub · sub + riff both invariant), four different drum architectures (clipped transient kit · offbeat-led kit · half-time metallic · broken-beat swung sixteenths).

**THE CLUB AUDIT.** **Zero full club beds here.** The set's one permitted club bed is P05's if anyone takes it. ⛔ And zero four-on-the-floor across all four, because *four-on-the-floor as the meaning of energy* is forbidden-default #12.

**THE CENTURY AUDIT.** Each music prompt is written so that deleting its lineage word still leaves a current record specified — the chassis nouns carry the first two seconds in all four. Re-run and recorded in step 10.

**THE ENSEMBLE AUDIT.** ≤ 2 historical markers per prompt. ⛔ No walking bass + tambourine + horns + period kit; no skank + organ + spring echo; no harpsichord; no horn stack.

**THE CONSEQUENCE LEDGER.** grid mutation · offbeat cadence · harmonic pickup · spectrum restart. **Four classes, no duplicates, and not one is a drop.**
