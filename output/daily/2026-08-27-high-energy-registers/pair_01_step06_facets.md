## 1. MY READING OF THE REGISTER

The brief hands me four words and the fourth is the one that does all the work: **cold · exact · fury · DONE**.

**DONE is past tense and it is finished.** That is what separates this register from every song in the template. A rage vent is about an ongoing condition — *you keep doing this, they always do this, it never stops.* An ongoing condition can only be shouted at, because there is nothing to point at. **A thing that was DONE is an event with edges.** It happened. It has an hour. It has an object. And crucially — *it is not happening right now, which is why the singer can be quiet.*

> ⭐ **The vent is loud because it has no object. Take the object away and volume is the only thing left. Give it an object and volume becomes optional — which is exactly when it becomes force.**

**COLD** is not a temperature and not restraint. Cold means **the fury has already done its arithmetic.** It has worked out precisely what it wants, and the precision is the aggression. A vent says *this is unbearable*. This register says *it is four seconds and I would like it to be three.* The second sentence is more frightening because it cannot be dismissed as feeling.

**EXACT** is the register's engineering. And here is what I found when I looked at what *exact* costs: ⭐ **a demand small enough to be exact is a demand small enough to be refused without embarrassment.** That is the whole trap and it is the reason this register exists. Nobody refuses *justice*. Everybody refuses *one letter*. The exactness that makes the fury real is the same exactness that makes it lose.

⭐⭐ **So the register has a built-in R5.** I do not have to bolt a cost on. **The cost is structural: the more precisely I ask, the more available I am to be declined.**

### The nameable non-person

The seed's resolution is the best thing in it, and R1's amendment is what makes it survive contact: **the object must be the SUBJECT OF AN ACTIVE VERB, with a time or a count.** Not *a form*, but *a form that closed at noon*. Not *a door*, but *a door that shuts in four seconds and did that before I got here*.

The difference is not vividness. It is **agency**. Cliché #9 fails because *the system* is a fog that has no verbs — it can only *be*. The instant an object takes a verb it becomes a thing that **acted**, and a thing that acted can be answered. ⭐ **An abstract antagonist cannot be answered, which is why the songs that use one only have volume left.**
