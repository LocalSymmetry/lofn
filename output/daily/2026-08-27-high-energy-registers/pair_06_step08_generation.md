## V1 — "OH, IT GOES" — DRAFT IN FULL (the reference implementation)

```
[Thin Open]
Hot room. August. Nothing moving.
Both hands flat where the paint went over.
That sash gives and the night air comes in here.
That is the whole plan for tonight.

[Push One]
Paint has this sash welded to the sill.
Heel of my hand, low, into the wood.
The frame takes it and gives back nothing.
The putty holds. The glass holds.
No one in this house has had it up.
I am not asking it now. I am leaning.
Shoulder under. Knees under that.
Again. Again. Again.
The rail is warm where I keep hitting it.

[The Quarter Inch]
Something went.
Something in the paint went.
A line of white came away at the top.
The gap is the width of a fingernail.
The gap was not there a second ago.
I am standing exactly where I was.

[Hook]
Oh, it goes.
Oh, it goes.
I did not know it goes.
Flat of my hand and oh, it goes.

[Answer A]
It goes.
Under my hand it goes.
Paint on the sill, paint on my palm.
It goes.

[Push Two]
Now I have a grip on the bare rail.
Now the sash has somewhere it can go.
White comes off in a long strip.
I put a blade where the strip was.
This window is a hinge that forgot.
It remembers a little more each shove.
Hot air out of here. Hot air out.
Something cooler standing at the sill.
Lamp on the floor. Curtain not moving yet.

[Hook] (byte-identical)

[Answer B]
It goes.
Wide as an arm it goes.
Sill wet, hands white, it goes.
It goes.

[Push Three]
I am counting the layers on the blade.
Cream. Then green. Then cream again.
Then a brown from under the green.
Every one went on over the last.
Every one dried before the next.
Not one of them was holding it alone.
They only had to hold it together.

[The Whole Way]  <- hinge; the knock stops here
Nine coats have sealed this sash since the ceiling went white.
It came up under the heel of my hand.
Up.
All the way up.
Rail. Sky. Cold.
That sash gave and the night air comes in here.
And it will not sit back down.
That is rain in, come November.
I will take the rain.

[Hook] (byte-identical)

[Answer C]
It goes.
Straight up it goes.
Nothing under it now but air.
It goes.

[Hook - LATE LEAD] (byte-identical)

[Tail]
Sat on the floor with the frame open.
Cream and green under my nails.
Curtain doing something it has not done.
Cold on the side of my face.
Hot room. August. Everything moving.
Nothing in this room is where it was.
That is the whole plan for tomorrow.
```

⭐ **Two checkable returns, each one word wide.** *That sash **gives** → **gave*** at the hinge; *the whole plan for **tonight** → **tomorrow*** at the very end. The second one is not in the brief and I am keeping it: the last line of the song is appetite pointed at a day that has not happened.

### V2 — "IT SAYS TWO" · THE INVENTORY

**Section order:** Bay Open · Verse One · Pre One · **Chorus (core + 1)** · Verse Two · Pre Two · **Chorus (core + 2)** · The Card *(hinge)* · **Chorus (core + 3)** · The Cost · **Chorus (core + 4, late lead)** · Tail.

**Chorus core — byte-identical four times:**
```
It says two. It takes one.
It says two. It takes one.
Nobody hid it. It is printed on the lid.
Bay's empty early and the hour is mine.
```
**The list that grows under it:** *the tall one up on its end* → *+ the ramp in before the sun clears the roof* → *+ the whole back wall clear by the time the yard fills* → *+ **what else in here has never been tried.***

**Hinge:** `That card has said TWO PEOPLE on every lid in this room.` — and the four lines under it are the anti-resentment engine: *Nobody was wrong. Nobody was lying. Everybody read it and everybody did what it said. Including whoever printed it.*
**Cost:** `And I do not get to say I can't.` / `That word came off me this morning.`
