## ⭐ R7 · PART ONE — FILED BEFORE A SINGLE LYRIC LINE EXISTS

**Written at the top of this file, before step 07, before any sung line was drafted to disk. A prediction filed after the writing is a description.**

### The template this pair collapses into if it fails

> ## **THE PROTEST ANTHEM, AND IT WILL ARRIVE THROUGH THE PRONOUNS.**

**The specific failure mode, named in advance so it can be checked:**

1. **The pronoun slip.** The song acquires a *we* that has no members and a *they* with no address. The instant `we` appears, the singer is no longer subject to the rule — they are its spokesperson — and the register is dead. **This is how the collapse actually happens: not through the sound, through six pronouns.**
2. **The future tense.** *We shall · one day · they can't stop us.* The protest anthem's grammar is **future and plural**. A song that slides into the future tense has stopped being able to lose, because the loss has not happened yet and the win is promised.
3. **The singer becomes already-right.** Every line that informs rather than reports is the template arriving. If a listener could take notes, it has collapsed.
4. **The abstract antagonist** (forbidden default #9) in a costume — *a rule* that merely EXISTS rather than one that DID something at an hour.
5. **The escalation ladder** — acoustic verse → handclaps → organ → gospel choir → crowd chant on the fade. If loudness arrives by adding humans, the song has no idea.

### ⛔ THE PRE-COMMITTED COUNTERMEASURE — chosen before writing, and it is mechanical

> ## **REFUSED ELEMENT: NO FIRST-PERSON PLURAL AND NO THIRD-PERSON PLURAL IN ANY SUNG LINE.**
> ⛔ **No `we`, `us`, `our`, `ours`, `they`, `them`, `their`, `theirs` — zero occurrences, all four variations.**

**Why this and not a taste rule:** *we shall* and *they must* are the template's two load-bearing sentences and **both of them require a banned word.** The prohibition is not stylistic, it is **grammatical**, and it is **greppable** — a stranger with `grep -oiE '\b(we|us|our|they|them|their)\b'` can falsify it in one second without trusting me. A refused element that only I can verify is a preference.

**What replaces it (removal is a debt — THE RETURN):** `nobody` · `anybody` · `somebody` · `whoever` · `the others` · second-person singular used **reciprocally, never accusingly**. ⭐ **And the register hands me this for free:** a rule applied to whoever wrote it needs no *them*, because **the writer is standing inside it.**

### The default expectation, stated out loud (PREREGISTRATION_STANDARD)

**The default is that the prohibition changes nothing and the songs read as protest anthems anyway**, because the template is carried by the SOUND and arrives before a word does. If the four variations still read as *we shall / they must* with the pronouns removed, the prohibition was cosmetic and I will say so.

## THE FIVE SCORING FACETS

### FACET 1 — **THE RULE IS THE SUBJECT, AND IT HAS A VERB AND AN HOUR** *(R1, indexed)*

The resistance in all four songs is a **procedure**: a posted rule, a service window, a drop slot, a numeric cut-off. Never a person, never *the system*. ⭐ **Each one is the SUBJECT OF AN ACTIVE VERB and carries one count or one hour**, quoted from the sung text:

| V | the resistance, as sung | subject | active verb | index |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *"The rule caught me on the third read."* | the rule | **caught** | **the third read** |
| **V2** | *"The window took its ten and shut."* | the window | **took / shut** | **ten** (seconds) |
| **V3** | *"The slot took the fourth one and gave nothing back."* | the slot | **took / gave** | **the fourth one** |
| **V4** | *"The line came down at nine and dropped me under it."* | the line | **came down / dropped** | **nine** |

⭐ **The test of indexing:** each of these four objects **did something at a moment**. *The system oppresses* is a texture; *the slot took the fourth one and gave nothing back* is an event that happened once and can be dated.

### FACET 2 — **PASSIVE INJUSTICE: THE HALF WITH NO VILLAIN** *(the Shklar seat, added by the panel)*

A failure to act, by somebody who could have acted, is an **injustice** and not a **misfortune**. It is the half of justice a protest anthem cannot sing, because it has nobody to point at.

Carried in the sung text of all four, each differently:

- **V1** — *"A rule that nobody picks up / is a rule that somebody let go."*
- **V2** — *"An empty chair is not an accident. / An empty chair had to be left."*
- **V3** — *"Nobody turned it down. Nobody had to. / It just sat. It sat and it kept on sitting."*
- **V4** — *"Nobody moved it. / That is the good news. / That is also the whole of the bad news. / The same nobody both times."*

⭐⭐ **V4's is the pair's central discovery and it arrived out of the register rather than out of a plan:** the *nobody* who failed to bend the rule for me is the **same nobody** who failed to bend it for anybody else. **Passive injustice and procedural justice are one event seen from two sides.** That sentence is unavailable to a protest anthem at any price.

### FACET 3 — **THE SINGER IS INSIDE THE RULE, NOT IN FRONT OF IT** *(R5 · MacKaye)*

In every one of the four the singer **is subject to the thing they are arguing for**, and pays.

| V | what the singer asked for | what it cost them — **displacement, never strain** |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | that the rule be applied | **a turn** — *"So I give the turn back. / So I go to the end of it."* |
| **V2** | an answer at a window | **the ten seconds** — and nobody was there to spend them |
| **V3** | that the appeal be read | **the afternoon, the good pen, the last good paper** — *"None of that is a load. / It is just gone, and I know where it went."* |
| **V4** | that the line be drawn in the open | **the place and the date** — *"It is not carrying. It is subtraction."* |

⛔ **MacKaye check:** every consequence in the four is one the singer actually takes in the song — a turn given up, a place lost, a date returned. **No song claims a consequence on somebody else's behalf.** There is no *they* to suffer it and no *we* to be avenged.

## ⭐ THE FORM, AUTHORED HERE AND DECLARED — **THE READ-BACK**

⛔ The brief deliberately did not specify section order, stanza lengths, rhyme scheme, hook grammar or a sonic device. **So this is mine and it is named:**

> ## **THE READ-BACK FORM**
> Every song states a **checkable sentence** in its opening thirty seconds, keeps that sentence **verbatim** through every chorus, and returns it at the end **either intact (V1–V3) or with exactly one word changed (V4)**. ## ⭐⭐ V4 — HOW THE HARDEST SONG IN THE RUN IS BUILT

*The right procedure, the wrong outcome, and it is still justice.*

**The panel's method, applied literally:**

1. **The win condition, stated in the first thirty seconds, checkably:**
   > *"Here is what would count as winning. / Say it now so it can be checked later. / **The line falls where it falls, and it falls on anyone.**"*
2. **Every musical promise is kept.** The chorus returns on schedule. The cadence lands on i every time, including the last. The pen clicks every bar for ninety-six bars. Nothing in the arrangement breaks.
3. **The only broken promise is in the sentence** — and it is not broken by anybody. **It is broken by being TRUE.**
   > *"The line falls where it falls, and it falls on **me**."*
   **Nine words identical. One word changed: `anyone` → `me`.**

⭐⭐⭐ **AND THIS IS THE ANSWER TO THE LOSABLE-JUSTICE PROBLEM.** The win condition was **VERIFIED**, and **the verification is the loss.** The singer asked for one checkable thing — *that the line runs on anyone* — the check came back TRUE, and the truth landed on them. **The procedure did not fail. The procedure worked, and it worked on me.** That is why the outcome is bad and the justice is intact, and it is a sentence the protest anthem cannot form, because the protest anthem's rule always fails and always fails *downward*.

**And the reason the singer accepts it is sung, without a philosophy word in it:**
> *"Anybody can ask for a line / once the line already lies underneath. / I asked when I did not know. / So I get what I get."*

**R5 is native here rather than bolted on:** the singer can lose because **the singer already lost, before the last chorus, and stayed.**
