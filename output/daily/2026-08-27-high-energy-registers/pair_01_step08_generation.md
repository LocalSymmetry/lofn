## DRAFT-STAGE SELF-CRITIQUE (carried into step 09)

1. **V1 Verse 3** has *"a corridor going through it"* — a corridor cannot go through a screw. The image is inverted and it is the weakest line in the pair.
2. **V2 `[The One It Was Right About]`** opens *"And it was right, and I checked."* The concession is the best move in the song and the line is limp; it needs the coldness the section is claiming.
3. **V3's turn** repeats *"the wind"* three times in five lines. One of them is doing nothing.
