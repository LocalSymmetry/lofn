## THE SIX AHA-MOMENTS (attributed, and operational — these are things to DO)

**1. ⭐⭐ LOAD THE PRIOR WITH TIMBRE, THEN BREAK ONE THING AT A NAMED BAR.** *[the Huron seat ⇄ the Rosch seat ⇄ the Shocklee seat]*
A schema must be *running* before you can violate it — and **timbre renders where form flattens.** So: put the template's signature **timbre** in the first four bars, let the listener file it, then deviate **once, late, at a bar you name.**
> ⭐ **The carrier is a repeating physical event that STOPS ONCE while everything else continues.** Not the drop-out bar — that is cliché #4 *and* measured dead. A stop inside a continuing texture is a positive event the generator cannot average away.

**2. ⭐⭐ THE RESISTANCE TAKES A VERB AND A TIMESTAMP.** *[the Canetti seat ⇄ the Lazarus seat ⇄ the Scott-Heron seat]*
This is the answer to the seed's most dangerous objection — that *"a rule"* is just cliché #9, **the abstract antagonist**, wearing a costume.
> **The resistance must be the SUBJECT OF AN ACTIVE VERB and carry one specific time or count.** *The system oppresses* is a texture. *The clock ran out at four in the morning and nobody signed the page* is an event. **Generic vs INDEXED is the whole difference.**

**3. ⭐ AGGRESSION IS A CONTOUR, NOT A LEVEL.** *[the LeDoux seat ⇄ the Henriques seat ⇄ the Witek seat]*
Threat is **unpredictable onset + rising intensity** — looming. So force without threat is available, and it is engineered: **arrive at full size and STAY there** rather than growing; **open the offbeat at the peak** (the groove's inverted U); and **evidence volume with an object that MOVES**, never with a level word and never with a crowd.

**4. ⭐ THE WIN CONDITION IN THE FIRST THIRTY SECONDS, AND THE FORM COMPLETES WITHOUT IT.** *[the Shklar seat ⇄ the Huron seat ⇄ the Sondheim seat]*
How a song can genuinely be **able to lose** in three minutes: state what would count as winning early and checkably; keep **every musical promise**; let the **only broken promise be in the sentence.** The checkable line returns **verbatim with one word changed.**

**5. ⭐ WRITE THE DENOMINATOR.** *[the Borg seat ⇄ the Frijda seat]*
Quantify the load and delete every strain word. **The cost is DISPLACEMENT, not strain** — what got moved, dropped, or given up. Strain words re-import the audience the martyr needs.

**6. ⭐ AT THE PEAK THE WORDS GO SINGULAR AND CLOSE.** *[the Katz seat ⇄ the Moore seat ⇄ the Sondheim seat ⇄ the Rodgers seat]*
**The render narrows at high level whether you ask or not** — measured, −0.43 width against level in our own catalogue. So narrowing is not an instruction, it is a *condition*, and the only question is what the words do inside it. **Wide words in a narrow render read as RETREAT. Singular, close words read as FORCE.**
