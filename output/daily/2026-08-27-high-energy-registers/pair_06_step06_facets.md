## 1. THE REGISTER, TAKEN APART

**THE SECOND YOU FIND OUT THE THING ACTUALLY MOVES.** Not the pushing. Not the having pushed. **The second.** A discovery with a timestamp, which is exactly what R1 asks every other pair to manufacture.

⭐⭐ **AND THE DISCRIMINATOR FALLS STRAIGHT OUT OF THAT, IN ONE WORD: SURPRISE.**

> **The empowerment anthem is structurally incapable of surprise.** It knows at bar one. That is its entire premise and the reason it is a boast: *unstoppable* is a claim made in advance. A song about the second you find out is a song in which **the singer did not know**, and *"I did not know"* is a sentence the template cannot sing without ceasing to be itself.

⭐ **So the discriminator is not a topic and not an arrangement. It is a TENSE and a FACE.** The hook has to contain the sound a person makes when a thing they leaned on gives. That sound is **"oh"**, and no empowerment anthem in the world has an "oh" in its chorus.

⭐ **And it discharges the run's AWE-family obligation honestly rather than by assertion.** *Revelation · Eureka · Amazement · Marvel · Wonder · Incredulity* are the taxonomy's own names for the second you find out. They are in my shipped EMO headers, not in a ledger sentence. **`07` should re-measure them there and not take this paragraph's word for it** — the coordinator asserts my AWE row in `05_pair_assignments.md`, and yesterday a portfolio rule declared in a ledger shipped unsatisfied.

**Why it costs me something.** I sing full chest, on top, no hedging, no upspeak, no laugh at the end of my own good line: *she lands it and moves on.* Being **second in my own record** is the single thing I would refuse — and I would refuse it in the room, out loud, before anyone finished the sentence.

⭐ **And it is the discriminator in vocal form, which is why I am doing it anyway.** Surprise is a **latency**. A person who already knew is on the beat.
