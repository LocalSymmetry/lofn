## THE R6 VOCAL DECISION — one, pair-wide

> ⛔ **NO RAISED VOICE ANYWHERE IN ANY OF THE FOUR. The refusal word is delivered at conversational level, off the strong beat, undoubled and unornamented, at the single largest bar in each record.**

You have written a hook for a defiance song at 132 BPM and you are refusing to belt it. **The pose IS the volume** — that is its whole mechanism — so the one gesture that makes it impossible is to put the smallest vocal event in the record inside its largest bar. ⭐ **And it runs WITH the generator, never against it:** the render narrows at high level anyway (**r = −0.43**, width against level, our own catalogue), so a wide belted word at the peak reads as retreat and a singular close word reads as force. **R4's amended lever is the lyric, and at the peak the lyric is one syllable long.**
