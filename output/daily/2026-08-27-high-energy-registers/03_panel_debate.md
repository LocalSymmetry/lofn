## THE SEATS

### CONCEPT PANEL — how force is categorised, and how a listener files it before a word arrives

**THE PROTOTYPE** · *(after Eleanor Rosch, 1938–)*
**GROUNDING:** "Natural Categories" (1973); "Basic Objects in Natural Categories" (1976). Documented position: categories are graded around **prototypes**, and typicality effects are fast, automatic, and precede any deliberate judgement. The basic level is where most of the information is carried.
**TEMPERAMENT:** unhurried, mechanistic. Refuses to discuss what a listener *should* hear until the panel has said what filing actually is.

**THE CORE RELATIONAL THEME** · *(after Richard Lazarus, 1922–2002)*
**GROUNDING:** *Emotion and Adaptation* (1991); appraisal theory. Documented position: every emotion has a **core relational theme**, and anger's is *a demeaning offence against me and mine*, requiring blame that attaches to something. Arousal without an appraisal is arousal, not an emotion.
**TEMPERAMENT:** clinical. Will not let a feeling be discussed as a quantity.

**THE ACTION TENDENCY** · *(after Nico Frijda, 1927–2015)*
**GROUNDING:** *The Emotions* (1986); "The Laws of Emotion" (1988). Documented position: an emotion **is a change in readiness to act** — the law of situational meaning, the law of change, the law of care for consequence, the law of the lightest load.
**TEMPERAMENT:** brisk, taxonomic. Asks of every song: *ready to do what, exactly?*

**THE SENSE OF INJUSTICE** · *(after Judith Shklar, 1928–1992)*
**GROUNDING:** *The Faces of Injustice* (1990). Documented position: the line between **misfortune and injustice is drawn by a decision, not discovered as a fact**; and **passive injustice** — the harm done by those who could have acted and did not — is the neglected half of the subject. The victim's sense of injustice is routinely discounted as bias.
**TEMPERAMENT:** dry, unillusioned, allergic to uplift. The only seat here who thinks the interesting case is the one where nothing happens.

**THE STING OF COMMAND** · *(after Elias Canetti, 1905–1994)*
**GROUNDING:** *Crowds and Power* (1960). Documented position: a command has two parts — the momentum that makes it obeyed and the **sting it leaves behind**, which is stored intact, sometimes for decades, and discharged only when it can be passed on.
**TEMPERAMENT:** slow, physical, uninterested in psychology. Talks about commands the way other people talk about objects.

⚠️ **HYPER-SKEPTIC — THE ANTI-EXCEPTIONALIST** · *(after Carl Wilson, contemporary)*
**GROUNDING:** *Let's Talk About Love: A Journey to the End of Taste* (2007) — a book-length dismantling of the author's own contempt for a mass-loved record, and of taste as a status system.
**TEMPERAMENT:** high neuroticism, low agreeableness. **His standing claim is that the seed's contempt for the template is a class marker wearing a craft argument, and that the anti-template move is now this house's fingerprint.**

### CRAFT PANEL — form, prosody, arrangement, and what a loud record is actually made of

**THE PROSODY** · *(after Stephen Sondheim, 1930–2021)*
**GROUNDING:** *Finishing the Hat* (2010); the stated working rules — content dictates form, less is more, god is in the details — and a lifetime of stress-matching between spoken and sung emphasis.
**TEMPERAMENT:** pedantic in the exact place it matters. Will scan a line out loud and kill it on a misplaced stress.

**THE CREST FACTOR** · *(after Bob Katz, contemporary)*
**GROUNDING:** *Mastering Audio: The Art and the Science*; the **K-System** metering standard; two decades of documented argument against the loudness war.
**TEMPERAMENT:** measures first, argues second. Treats every claim about volume as a claim with a number attached, and asks for the number.

**THE NOISE ARCHITECT** · *(after Hank Shocklee, 1962–)*
**GROUNDING:** The Bomb Squad's production for Public Enemy; the documented working method of building density from **non-musical sources** and the stated position that music is organised noise and that clean is not the goal.
**TEMPERAMENT:** builds walls out of things that are not instruments. Contemptuous of intensity that arrives via a gain stage.

**THE CONVERSATION** · *(after Nile Rodgers, 1952–)*
**GROUNDING:** the Chic catalogue and four decades of production; the stated **"Deep Hidden Meaning"** method — the song must be about something before a note is written — and a groove built as a dialogue between bass and guitar with almost no distortion in it.
**TEMPERAMENT:** cheerful, ruthless about function. The one seat in the room who has actually made records that are enormous and not remotely angry.

**THE ITEMISED INDICTMENT** · *(after Gil Scott-Heron, 1949–2011)*
**GROUNDING:** the spoken-word and song catalogue; a documented method of political writing built from **named brands, channels, hours and objects** rather than abstractions.
**TEMPERAMENT:** deadpan. Will ask, of any protest line, *which one, whose, and at what time* — and stop the meeting until somebody answers.

⚠️ **HYPER-SKEPTIC — THE ANTI-NOVELTY** · *(after David Huron, 1954–)*
**GROUNDING:** *Sweet Anticipation: Music and the Psychology of Expectation* (2006); the **ITPRA** theory and **contrastive valence** — the account in which musical pleasure, including frisson, is manufactured by a fast prediction response that is then reappraised.

### SOMATIC PANEL — startle, effort, entrainment, and what a loud room does to a body

**THE DEFENSIVE CIRCUIT** · *(after Joseph LeDoux, 1949–)*
**GROUNDING:** *The Emotional Brain* (1996); the amygdala work; and the later, documented **self-correction** in *Anxious* (2015) — that defensive survival circuits are not the same thing as the conscious feeling of fear, and that conflating them was an error he made and named.
**TEMPERAMENT:** precise about what a reflex is and is not. Will not allow "the body reacts" to be treated as "the listener is afraid."

**THE LOUDNESS MODEL** · *(after Brian C. J. Moore, 1946–)*
**GROUNDING:** *An Introduction to the Psychology of Hearing*; the loudness models behind the modern standards. Documented findings: loudness depends on **bandwidth and duration**, not level alone; **binaural summation** makes a wide, correlated signal louder than a narrow one; the stapedius reflex attenuates sustained low-frequency energy and **cannot catch a transient**.
**TEMPERAMENT:** the only seat who can falsify a production idea with arithmetic, and does.

**THE PERCEIVED EXERTION** · *(after Gunnar Borg, 1927–2020)*
**GROUNDING:** the **RPE** scale (6–20) and CR10; the documented finding that perceived exertion integrates strain better than any single physiological measure.
**TEMPERAMENT:** unglamorous. Believes the interesting number is always a ratio.

**THE URGE TO MOVE** · *(after Maria Witek, contemporary)*
**GROUNDING:** the groove research, including the 2014 study measuring **an inverted U between syncopation and the pleasurable urge to move** — metronomic is inert, chaotic is inert, and the body wants the middle.
**TEMPERAMENT:** empirical and slightly amused. Notes that everything the industry calls "energy" is measured to reduce movement.

**SONIC DOMINANCE** · *(after Julian Henriques, contemporary)*
**GROUNDING:** *Sonic Bodies* (2011) and the essay naming **sonic dominance** — the reggae sound-system session, where sound at a level that would be unbearable elsewhere becomes the environment itself, is felt rather than heard, and is experienced as immersion and pleasure.
**TEMPERAMENT:** insists the dancehall already answered the panel's third question fifty years ago and nobody read the paper.

⚠️ **HYPER-SKEPTIC — THE ANTI-SIMULATION** · *(after Ian MacKaye, 1962–)*
**GROUNDING:** Minor Threat, Fugazi, Dischord; the documented practice — the five-dollar show, the refusal of the aggression economy, stopping a set to address violence in the room.
**TEMPERAMENT:** high neuroticism, low agreeableness. **His standing claim is that there is no body in the room, therefore no stake, therefore no anger — only its performance, which is what the template already is.**

# CONFIGURATION 2 — GROUP TRANSFORM: **ROTATE**

**SONIC DOMINANCE (after Henriques):** Rotated onto what the loud template is *for*: it is for producing the sensation of being in a crowd, for a person who is alone. That is what the gang vocal is. **THE PROSODY (after Sondheim):** Which is a writing instruction and I will make it exact: at the loudest moment the sung text is **singular and close**. **THE LOUDNESS MODEL (after Moore):** …I withdraw the binaural objection in that form and restate it as a constraint. My arithmetic says narrowing costs perceived loudness. The loudest thing available to you is not a section. It is an arrival.

**THE STING OF COMMAND (after Canetti):** Rotated onto justice: what the protest template is *for* is discharging the sting. **THE SENSE OF INJUSTICE (after Shklar):** And that is the shape of a justice song that might lose. The listener does the arithmetic themselves, and the arithmetic is the feeling.

**THE ANTI-NOVELTY (after Huron):** Then let me supply the formal half, because that alone is too quiet to register. **Keep every musical promise.** The form completes exactly on time: the chorus returns, the rhyme resolves, the return arrives where the prediction says it will. **The only broken promise in the record is the one in the sentence.** That mismatch is a prediction violation with nowhere to go, and it is precisely the feeling of a procedure that was followed correctly and produced a bad result.

**THE ITEMISED INDICTMENT (after Scott-Heron):** And mark the return so the absence is audible. The checkable line comes back at the end **word for word, with one word changed**, and the changed word is the outcome. That is not narration. That is a return, which this house already believes in.

### ⚠️ OBJECTION 1 · THE ANTI-EXCEPTIONALIST (Wilson) — *"the template is not the enemy, and the inversion is your fingerprint"*

**DISPOSITION — RESOLVED, WITH A RIDER THAT CHANGES THE OUTPUT.**
⭐ **RULING: the schema must be RUNNING, and it is loaded by TIMBRE, not by form.** Each song states its template's signature *sound* inside the first four bars — which also runs with the generator, since timbre renders and form flattens — and then breaks **exactly one thing, at a named bar, late.** The exclusions bite on the centroid, not on the machinery. ⛔ **The empirical rider is adopted whole: R7's prediction carries a falsification number, and the atlas point of order below is binding.**

### ⚠️ OBJECTION 3 · THE ANTI-SIMULATION (MacKaye) — *"no body in the room, no stake, no anger"*

# ⭐ THE SIX AHA-MOMENTS

*These are the metaprompt's spine. Every one is a thing a writer does, not a thing a writer notices.*

### AHA 1 · **LOAD THE PRIOR WITH TIMBRE, THEN BREAK ONE THING AT A NAMED BAR**
*(the Huron ⇄ Rosch ⇄ Shocklee bridge — Configuration 2)*
State the template's **signature timbre inside the first four bars** so the listener's prediction is live. Then the form is the pair's own. **Exactly one deviation, at a bar the pair names in its own package**, and it is late. ⭐ **The recommended carrier is a repeating physical event that STOPS** — a hand, a count, a bell, a machine cycle, a strike — established in the first third and absent once. That is measured-survivable: the generator smooths a level and fills a void, but it cannot smooth **the difference between a click arriving and not arriving**. ⛔ **This is not the forbidden drop-out bar.** Everything else keeps running; one object goes missing.

### AHA 2 · **THE RESISTANCE TAKES A VERB AND A TIMESTAMP**
*(the Canetti ⇄ Lazarus ⇄ Scott-Heron bridge — Configuration 1)*
The named resistance (R1) appears in the sung text as the **grammatical subject of an active verb** at least once, and carries **one specific time, count or number**. A rule that is *described* is cliché #9 in a costume; a rule that **acts at an hour** is an event with an agent. ⭐ **The separating test, cheap and mechanical: put a timestamp on it.** *A door that only opens outward* is a description. *It shut at four and the next one is Tuesday* is an act.

### AHA 3 · **AGGRESSION IS A CONTOUR, NOT A LEVEL**
*(the LeDoux ⇄ Henriques ⇄ Witek bridge — Configuration 1)*
Threat is produced by **unpredictable onset and rising intensity** — looming — not by volume. So loud-without-aggression is engineered by **arriving at full size and staying there**, with a pulse the listener can predict. ⭐ And at the biggest moment, **open the offbeat rather than filling it**: the urge to move follows an inverted U against syncopation, and the wall-of-sound chorus sits at the dead end of it. ⭐ **Evidence the loudness with an OBJECT THAT MOVES** — a window, a glass, a floor, a sheet of paper, a chest — never with a level word and never with a crowd.

### AHA 4 · **THE WIN CONDITION IN THE FIRST THIRTY SECONDS, AND THE FORM COMPLETES WITHOUT IT**
*(the Shklar ⇄ Huron ⇄ Sondheim bridge — Configuration 2 — this is the answer to P02 V4)*
State the win condition **early, plainly, as a checkable sentence** — a thing that either happens or does not. Then **keep every musical promise**: the chorus returns, the rhyme resolves, the return lands where the prediction says. **The only broken promise in the record is the one in the sentence.** ⭐ Mark it by bringing the checkable line back **verbatim with one word changed**, and let the changed word be the outcome. No narration, no emotion word, and the listener does the arithmetic.
⚠️ **It is writable. It is still the hardest of the twenty-four**, and its failure mode is that nobody notices — which is why the verbatim return is not optional here.

### AHA 5 · **STRENGTH IS A RATIO — WRITE THE DENOMINATOR AND DELETE THE COST REPORT**
*(the Borg ⇄ Frijda bridge — Configurations 1 and 3)*
Strength is **load over perceived exertion**, so the register is legible only as **missing effort**. ⭐ Operationally: **quantify the load** — a weight, a count, a duration, a distance — and **delete every strain word** from the lyric: no *heavy, hard, ache, burn, breaking, barely, still standing*. ⭐ The cost that R5 requires is paid as **displacement, not strain** — what the load stopped you doing, where you did not go, what shape you are now. ⛔ And cut the audience: *"costs less than everyone assumes"* smuggles in the watching crowd that the martyr ballad needs.

### AHA 6 · **THE PEAK'S LYRIC IS SINGULAR AND CLOSE**
*(the Katz ⇄ Moore ⇄ Sondheim ⇄ Rodgers bridge — Configuration 2)*
The render narrows as level rises whatever we write, so **narrowing cannot be a specification and must become a meaning.** At the loudest moment the sung text is **one subject, one object, one addressee — no plurals, no panorama, nothing described at distance.** ⭐ Widened words against a narrowed render read as **the record backing off**; singular words against the same render read as **force arriving in your face**. ⭐ And per the loudness models: a narrow peak is quieter than a wide one and can still be the biggest **event**, because impact belongs to the **onset**, which the ear's own reflex cannot attenuate. **Make the peak an arrival, not a plateau.**

# ⭐ THE ONE THING THE PANEL COULD NOT SETTLE

> ## **Can a normalised stream DELIVER loudness-without-aggression, or can it only DEPICT it?**

The Somatic panel answered the aggression half cleanly — threat is a **contour**, not a level, so loud-without-threat is engineerable in principle (AHA 3). It could not answer the delivery half, and the disagreement is real and unresolved:

- **SONIC DOMINANCE (Henriques):** the phenomenon he documented is a **room** phenomenon. Sound becomes the environment because it is physically enormous, shared, and inescapable. **Streaming normalisation removes exactly that variable.** On headphones at a platform target there is no sonic dominance; there is a **picture** of one, and a picture of a feeling is the thing this run exists to avoid.
- **THE CREST FACTOR (Katz):** normalisation deletes level and **cannot delete contrast**, and contrast is all loudness ever was. The register survives intact.
- **THE URGE TO MOVE (Witek):** the pleasurable urge to move is produced by **rhythm**, and rhythm is level-invariant. The register survives, and it survives on the somatic axis specifically.
- **THE ANTI-SIMULATION (MacKaye):** the room is the medium; a record is a flyer for it.
- **THE LOUDNESS MODEL (Moore):** the arithmetic can adjudicate this and has not been asked to. Nobody has run the measurement.

⛔ **The panel declines to smooth this into a consensus, because it is decidable and undecided.** It threatens **P05 specifically** — if Henriques is right, P05's register is not deliverable through this channel at all and the pair will produce a very good depiction of a thing that does not arrive.

⭐ **It is a Skaði expedition, not a panel question.** The test is cheap and it is already specified by existing instruments: render P05, read short-term dynamics and crest factor with `scripts/measure_render.py`, and put the audio to a blind listener under THE BLIND RULE with one question — *does this feel like being in a loud room, or like being shouted at?* **Pre-register both predictions before the render, with the default expectation stated out loud.** Until that exists, **P05 ships as a text package with the question open on its face**, and no claim about how it feels may be made by anyone in this run.
