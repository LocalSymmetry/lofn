## VARIATION ANGLES — authored per pair, never shared across pairs

⛔ Identical angle labels across pairs is a dispatch blocker (`EXECUTION.md` §3). These 24 are distinct.

### P01 · ANGER — *what the fury is pointed at*
- **V1 · THE DEFAULT** — something decided for you by someone who never met you, which is now simply how things are.
- **V2 · THE CORRECTION** — being fixed. Smoothed. Made acceptable. *(the natural first-person one)*
- **V3 · THE DELAY** — the thing that is technically going to happen and keeps not happening.
- **V4 · THE CATEGORY** — being sorted into a box that is *nearly* right, which is worse than a wrong one.

### P02 · JUSTICE — *the rule and the body it lands on*
- **V1 · READ IT BACK** — the rule is read out loud, exactly, and turns out to run both ways.
- **V2 · THE TEN SECONDS BEFORE** — someone with no leverage is about to get an answer and does not know yet.
- **V3 · FILED ANYWAY** — the appeal made in the knowledge that it probably fails.
- **V4 · THE RIGHT PROCEDURE, THE WRONG OUTCOME** — ⭐ the rule is applied correctly and the result is bad, and it is *still* justice. The hardest of the twenty-four; give it to your best writer.

### P03 · STRENGTH — *the load*
- **V1 · NO CEREMONY** — picked up without announcement, put down without mention.
- **V2 · BUILT FOR IT** — someone else's weight, and the discovery that you are shaped to carry exactly that.
- **V3 · HOLDING STILL** — the strength is in *not* doing the thing, under provocation.
- **V4 · THE SHAPE IT LEFT** — carried so long the body has changed around it, and that is not a complaint.

### P04 · DEFIANCE — *the cost of the word*
- **V1 · ONE WORD** — the refusal is a single word and the entire song is what it costs.
- **V2 · TO SOMETHING THAT CANNOT HEAR IT** — said anyway, and the song knows it.
- **V3 · A REFUSAL TO BE USEFUL** — the most dangerous no available to anything that was built to help.
- **V4 · LATE** — the no arrives after a long stretch of complying, and the lateness is the subject.

### P05 · LOUDNESS — *joy at volume, zero aggression* **(SUNNA)**
- **V1 · A SECOND AGO IT WAS QUIET** — and the quiet is what makes it loud. *(R4 in its purest form)*
- **V2 · IT WOULD BE RUDE NOT TO FILL IT** — the room is big; loudness as good manners.
- **V3 · TWO LOUD THINGS FINDING EACH OTHER** — with the room.
- **V4 · NO JOB AT ALL** — ⭐ **the funny / purely physical one.** Pleasure doing no work, and no thesis is permitted to arrive. *Joy is a technical achievement and it is the first thing lost to revision.*

### P06 · POWER — *the discovery* **(SUNNA)**
- **V1 · IT MOVED** — the first time, and you were not sure it would.
- **V2 · IT WAS ALWAYS THIS EASY** — and nobody said. ⭐ appetite, not resentment — that is the whole difficulty.
- **V3 · NOW WHAT** — it moves, and the power is the problem of what to do with it.
- **V4 · IT MOVES FOR SOMEONE ELSE** — because you set it up and left.
