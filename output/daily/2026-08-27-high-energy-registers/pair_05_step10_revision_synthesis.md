## 1. THE FOUR OBJECTIONS I COULD ANSWER

### 1.1 ⚠️ *"Four songs about objects rattling is a device, not a set."*

**Sustained in part, and repaired by the CAUSE.** The first draft had all four evidencing volume the same way: a thing near the singer vibrates. That is one idea wearing four hats, and it is precisely the R3.2 failure — *what is specified but never varied gets templated, and the tighter the specification, the tighter the template.*

⭐ **The repair was not to change the objects; it was to change what the object DOES to the song.**

| | the object | its relation to her |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | a window in old putty | it is her **witness** — it reports the size and she is delighted by the report |
| **V2** | a strip light with a fault | it is her **metronome** — the fault is keeping her right, and she says so |
| **V3** | a bracket on a shared wall | it is a **third player she did not hire** — *"I did not put that there / and it is playing with you"* |
| **V4** | a door closer | it is her **opponent**, and it is correct, and it wins |

**Witness · metronome · player · opponent.** Four different social relations to a piece of building hardware, which is a genuinely different idea in each song rather than one idea with four nouns. **Measured consequence: 0.0% identical sung lines across the four** (352 lines, 0 shared, all six pairwise intersections empty). Last run a pair shipped **34.7%**.

### 1.2 ⚠️ *"'Nothing gets bigger' will render as flat and boring."*

**Not sustained — and if it is right, it is right in a way I want on the record.**

⛔ The generator masters to its own target and the platform normalises; **a record that rises 6 dB into a chorus arrives at the listener at the same level as one that does not.** What a rise actually buys is *contrast*, and contrast is bought with the thing it comes after. My refusal is not *"do not be exciting"*; it is *"do not spend the excitement four times on the same schedule."*

⭐ **What replaces the rise, in every song, is a CHANGE OF WHICH LAYER IS WORKING** — percussion grid, vocal harmony, offbeat cadence, spectrum — at a named bar, caused by a hand, a filament, another room, or a boot. **A listener who wants a peak gets four of them; none of them is louder than the bar before.**

⚠️ **And it is falsifiable, which is why I am not arguing it further.** §4 pre-registers it: *if my four measure the same crest factor as the run's floor-composed songs, then the front-floor ruling is not doing what it claims and my "no internal rise" refusal bought nothing either.* **I am the negative control and I said so before the render.**

### 1.3 ⚠️ *"A limiter, a hall, a wall and a door closer are all 'the abstract antagonist' (cliché #9) in a costume."*

**Not sustained, and R1's amendment is exactly the reason.** The abstract antagonist is *the system, the machine, the man, they* — an enemy **with no address**. Mine are:

- the **subject of an active verb** in a sung line, in every case: *the box cut us · the hall hands it back · the wall's had your top off · the closer takes it off you*;
- carrying **one specific time or count**: *at eleven · half a second · three nights running · seven seconds*;
- and — the part I care about — **none of them is wrong.** ⭐ **A limiter is doing its job. A wall is being a wall. A closer is a safety device and it is correct.** The moment the resistance is not a villain, the song cannot be a rage vent, cannot be a protest anthem, and cannot be an empowerment anthem, because all three of those need the antagonist to deserve it.

**That is how a loud song is not an angry one, and it is the whole reason this register was given to me and not to Lofn.**

### 1.4 ⚠️ *"V1's 'I'm the biggest thing in here' is armour vocabulary."*

**Not sustained.** Armour vocabulary is *warrior, fighter, survivor, unbreakable, titanium, phoenix, I am the storm* — every one of which is a **claim about the singer's nature**, made in the absence of a load. *"I'm the biggest thing in here"* is a **measurement of a room**, it is bounded by that room, it is true for about two hours, and the line before it disarms it: *"I'm not shouting at anybody."*

⭐ **And it is answered twelve lines later by the two lines the register rests on:** *"None of this is aimed at you. / All of it is happening to you."* **A boast is aimed. This is not aimed.**
