## ⛔ WHAT IS NOT IN ANY POSITIVE FIELD, AND WHY

- **No level word.** No *loud, massive, huge, wall of sound, maximum energy, anthemic.* The generator masters to its own target; a level instruction is a non-instruction. **The loudness is bought at the front, in the thin opening, which is the one floor we have measured to survive.**
- **No spatial non-instruction.** No pan token of any kind — measured **−0.02 dB and +0.13 dB** across two renders that asked for one, **0/52 and 0/56** windows above +3 dB. Staging language (*front of the room, from below the lead*) is a mix picture, not a pan request.
- **No internal void.** ⛔ Not one of the four contains a stop, a bar of silence or a drop-out. A specified ~4 s stop rendered **0.40 s**; internal voids filled **2/2**. What each one has instead is **a repeating physical object that stops arriving while everything else runs.**
- **No prohibition inside the style field.** Prohibitions live in the exclude field, which is that field's only job. A negation in the positive field is a non-carrier and it spends characters that a positive event could have used.
- ⛔ **No real artist, group, panel-source or company name.** Instrument *mechanisms* only: *a drawbar organ* appears solely as a **negative** token; there is no trade name anywhere in the twelve fields. The census is the coordinator's and I have not written a number here that it will have to correct.

## ⭐ THE R6 NOTATION PROBLEM, SOLVED TWICE

**THE LATE LEAD** is a vocal event and vocal events are notated in two places or they are notated nowhere: **(1)** in the section header inside the lyrics field, and **(2)** in the music prompt's arrangement arc. Both say the same physical thing in the same words — *the stack lands on the downbeat, the lead is a beat behind it, catching up inside the line* — because a generator that ignores one may catch the other, and because a coarse arrival/non-arrival at a downbeat is the class L60 says survives.

⚠️ **It is a probe, not a technique.** 57 territories UNEXPLORED, 0 probes; nothing about high-energy material has been sounded through our door and nothing about vocal-timing notation has been measured at all. **If it renders, it is the pair's most valuable finding. If it does not, the songs still work, which is the only responsible way to spend an unmeasured idea.**
