## V1 · HAND ON THE GLASS

**Form (mine): NO-BUILD.** Off / Chorus / V1 / Chorus / V2 / The Hand / Chorus / V3 / Chorus / Eleven / Chorus-down-a-tone / Out.
**The whole design:** the record buys its floor in the first four seconds — **the power is already off when the song starts**, because the box cut it once at soundcheck — then arrives at full size on the fifth second and **holds exactly that size for the rest of the record.** There is no second arrival. The last chorus is **lower**, not bigger.

⭐ **The R4 ruling, honoured to the letter and past it.** The floor is at the **FRONT** (measured SURVIVED class). There is **no internal void anywhere** (measured dead, filled 2/2). And the record ends in **terminal silence** when the box goes red — terminal silence is the one silence in our measured SURVIVED set (1/1).

**How the volume is evidenced — objects only, never a level word, never a crowd:** a window buzzing in old putty on every up · a pint walking on a ledge · keys crossing a table · a crisp packet lifting off the bar and putting itself back down · the hair on a forearm standing up. ⭐ **The two lines the whole register rests on:** *"None of this is aimed at you. / All of it is happening to you."*

**The peak (bar 57) goes singular and close**, per the ruling: *"Hand. Glass. That's all it is."* — spoken at conversational volume with the record at full size behind it. ⛔ Nothing is added there. The **buzz leaves**; the clap stays.

**Win condition, stated at ~0:03 and broken by one word at the end:** *"One more after this one."* → *"No more after this one."*

**The ending refuses a thesis:** *"The glass has stopped. / Somebody's hand is still on it."*

## V2 · THE ROOM WANTS A GO

**Form (mine): CALL AND LATE ANSWER.** Empty / Chorus / V1 / Chorus / V2 / Answer / Chorus / V3 / The Tube Goes / Chorus / Chorus / Out.
**The whole design:** a hall booked hours before anyone arrives, whose slap-back hands every line back **half a second late**. So she stops mid-phrase and gives the building its turn — **loudness as good manners.** The inherited call-and-answer body is therefore not a stylistic reference; it is what the architecture forces.

⭐ **The R6 decision is the form:** her lead enters **half a second after her own harmony stack**, so the stack is the *call* and the frontwoman is the *answer* — **she is second on her own record for four minutes.**

**The Answer section is the required Sunna harmony instrument, discharged:** *"Two of us, and a gap. / You can hear the gap open."* The responding stack drops a fifth **below** the lead and stays there. ⛔ Not a choir, not a unison, not a crowd — the chassis doing what a choir used to do.

**The stop (bar 65) is a subtraction of LIGHT, not of sound:** *"Light's gone. Just the light. / Nothing else has stopped. / Darker. Not one bit quieter."* ⭐ That is the anti-drop in three lines.

**Win condition, stated at ~0:04, broken by one word at the very end:** *"It comes back before I stop."* → *"It comes back after I stop."*
**Cost:** *"I have never once heard / my own line land in this room. / That's what I gave the room."*

## V3 · I CAN'T COUNT YOU

**Form (mine): WALL FORM.** Bottom Only / Chorus / V1 / Chorus / V2 / Four Bars Of Nothing / Chorus / V3 / Chorus / Third One / Out.
**The whole design:** two rehearsal rooms share a wall. The wall passes bass and deletes everything a musician would use to count. **So they cannot be on a schedule with each other — they have to lock by ear.** The discriminator is the mechanism.

⭐ **The R6 decision is physical, not stylistic:** through a wall, only the bottom gets out. **So the frontwoman spends the entire record in her lowest chest register, underneath her own bass line, and never once goes up.** That is the cost as displacement — *"My top end stops at the plaster. / My top end is not invited."*

**The stop (bar 49) is the only place in my four where the other side goes quiet — and her side never does.** *They* stop for four bars; the bracket stops knocking with them; her record runs unbroken at full size. ⛔ **This is not an internal drop-out** — nothing in the song's own signal path stops. When they return, **her downbeat has become their offbeat and it stays that way to the end.**

**The register's flattest, best statement:** *"Nothing about this is a fight. / We are just both very large."*

**Win condition, stated at ~0:08 and refused on purpose:** *"Third one through the wall, I'm opening this door."* → *"Third one through the wall, / I'm not opening this door."* ⭐ **The door never opens.** Opening it makes this a collab banger with a wall in the first verse; leaving it shut is the whole song.

**Peak goes singular and close:** *"Door. Handle. No. / Hand off it. Play."*

## V4 · THE CAR PARK SHOULD HEAR THIS

⭐⭐ **THE RUN'S NO-THESIS SONG. ⛔ NO MEANING IS PERMITTED TO ARRIVE, AND I HAVE ENFORCED THAT LINE BY LINE.**

**Form (mine): NO-JOB FORM.** Outside / Chorus / V1 / Chorus / Door One / V2 / Chorus / Door Two / V3 / Chorus / Good Bit / Chorus / Shut. ⭐ **The verses shorten 10 → 8 → 6 while the chorus never changes** — the song gets *less* as it goes and the hook does not notice.

**The situation, entire:** a function room, a fire door on a closer, and a woman who has decided an empty car park is entitled to hear the good bit. The closer takes it back **every seven seconds**. She loses.

⛔ **Where the thesis was going to arrive, and how it was stopped:**
- *persistence / never give up* → she **gives up** at the end and goes back inside, and it is fine.
- *art escaping into the world* → **there is nobody in the car park.** She says so, out loud, in verse one: *"There is nobody in the car park. / That is not the point."*
- *the lit window means someone heard* → she names the reading and then **refuses her own reading**: *"I'm taking that as a yes. / It isn't one. I'm taking it."*
- *the door shutting means something* → *"It shuts behind me on its own, / and it is fine, and there it is."*

**The R6 decision breaks my own contract on purpose** (`sunna.yaml` §THE SWAGGER: *"no laugh at the end of her own good line"*): she **laughs mid-line and does not re-take it**, then finishes the line — *"Ha — no, the last one shut it, / and I had to walk all the way back."* The clause exists to stop a good line being undercut. **These lines have no job. There is nothing to undercut.** Declared in `## Major Deviations`.

**The stop (bar 53) is the crate**, and the consequence is the one production class that is not an increase: *"Nothing else has changed at all. / Same speed. Same size. More sky."*

**Cost (R5):** she is outside for the part she likes best, which happens to be **the hole where the third note should be** — the song's own inherited body, heard through a door. *"I'm out here with a crate. / I can hear it through a door."*

**Win condition, stated in chorus one and broken by one word in the last line:** *"That door's staying open."* → *"That door's not staying open."*
