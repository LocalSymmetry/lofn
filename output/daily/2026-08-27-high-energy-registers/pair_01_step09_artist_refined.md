## THE FOUR CRAFT REPAIRS FROM MY OWN STEP-08 SELF-CRITIQUE

**1 · V1 Verse 3 — the inverted image.** *"and a corridor going through it / at that number, all day"* — a corridor cannot go through a screw. Repaired to put the bodies back in the right place:

> *"There is a screw, and a number, / and a corridor with everybody in it / going through at that number, all day. / I go through at that number. / I did not set it either."*

⭐ And the repair earned something the draft did not have: **the singer is now inside the same denominator as everyone else in the corridor.** That is the difference between fury and superiority, and the draft had it the wrong way round.

**2 · V2 — the concession line.** *"And it was right, and I checked"* was limp in the section carrying the song's best move. → **"And it was right. I went and checked."** Two sentences, and the verification becomes an act she performed rather than a claim she makes. ⭐ *A rage vent cannot concede a point; cold exact fury concedes it, verifies it, keeps it, and stays exactly as furious.* That is the whole register in ten lines and it needed the harder verb.

**3 · V3 — the turn's repetitions.** *"It is already at the door"* → *"It is already against the glass"* (ties the turn to the window she has stood at all song). And the aphorism at the centre lost its filler:

> *"The one that moves does not publish. / The one that publishes does not move."* → ⭐ **"What moves does not publish. / What publishes does not move."**

Tighter, colder, more aphoristic — and it removed two stray `one`s at no cost. **The metric and the craft agreed.**

**4 · V4 `[Landing]` — the dead recap.** The draft recapped the verse. Repaired so it does work no other section can:

> *"And the word is the only thing / I have been late about all day."*

⭐⭐ **That line makes the R6 vocal decision into a lyric fact.** She has sung the entire record one beat behind the grid; this is the only place the song says so, and it says it one section before she lands on the beat for the first and only time. The arrangement stops carrying the idea alone — *a Somatic objection answered in the production spec is not answered.*

## ONE MORE THE INSTRUMENT CAUGHT THAT I DID NOT PREDICT

⭐ The cross-variation scan found **exactly one identical sung line across the four songs** — *"The moon is at the top of the sky"*, in V1's turn and V3's Verse 2.

It is an honest echo: they are the same night. But V3's whole subject **is** that moon — *at maximum and still filling* — and V1 was borrowing it. So V1 gave it back and got a better image for its own room:

> *"The light in here is on a timer. / Tonight it gets overruled."*

⭐⭐ **That is a second default, and the turn now says the thing V1 is actually about:** a default it does not mind, arriving to overrule a default it does. V1's turn was a sky postcard; it is now the song's own argument turning over. **The duplicate line was a symptom, and the symptom was that the turn had been imported.**

Shared sung lines after repair: **2 instances of 325 = 0.6%.** *(Yesterday's measured spread across a pair's four ran 0.0% → 34.7%.)*
