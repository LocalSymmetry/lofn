# PAIR 02 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES · **LOFN-PRIME**

**Run:** `2026-08-27-high-energy-registers` · **Register:** *a rule applied to whoever wrote it — and it might not be. Including the injustice of doing nothing.*
**⛔ Template to defeat:** THE PROTEST ANTHEM. **⭐ Refused element:** ⛔ no `we / us / our / they / them / their` in any sung line.

---

## THE PAIR'S ONE ARCHITECTURAL DECISION, AND EVERYTHING FOLLOWS FROM IT

> **The protest anthem's two load-bearing sentences are *we shall* and *they must*. Both require a plural pronoun. Ban the pronouns and the template cannot be assembled — not as a matter of taste, as a matter of grammar.**

**What that forces, downstream, on every one of the four:**

1. **No collective.** There is one body in each song and it is the one paying.
2. **No future tense in the hook.** The protest chorus is a **vow** (*one day, we shall*). Every chorus here is a **procedure** — an instruction to read, to wait, to file, to check — in the imperative singular or the past tense.
3. **The singer cannot be already right**, because there is nobody to be right *at*. The only person the rule can be tested against is the singer, which is the register, exactly.
4. ⭐ **Passive injustice becomes writable.** The half of justice with no villain has no *them* to point at, so a song with no *them* is the only kind that can sing it.

**⛔ What I owe for the removal (THE RETURN — removal is a debt):** the plural is replaced by a **procedural imperative** that returns verbatim every chorus, and by a **repeating physical object** in the room. The *we* is gone; a stamp, a chair leg, a hand on wood and a pen click come back instead — **five times each, and each one stops exactly once.**

## V1 · WITH ME INSIDE IT — *the rule read aloud, and it runs both ways*

| | |
|---|---|
| **scene** | A rule is posted somewhere ordinary. The singer reads it aloud to get somebody else sent to the back of a queue. Read exactly, the words include the singer, who went again. |
| **why it defeats the template** | The singer starts as the accuser and is convicted by their own evidence, **mid-argument, in the second verse** — and goes on demanding the rule anyway. A protest singer is never the defendant. |
| **the object (R1)** | a posted rule about turns — *"The rule caught me on the third read."* |
| **the hook** | `Read it back.` A read-back is a **safety procedure**: you say the instruction back to prove you heard it. The whole song is one. |
| **the discriminator** | *"Read the line with me left inside the line."* |
| **the cost (R5)** | a turn, handed back. `So I give the turn back. / So I go to the end of it.` **Displacement, not strain.** |
| **the one number** | *three* reads, answered by *the third one had me in it* — never recited. |
| **the peak (R4)** | the hook broken into **three single words with air between**, band running underneath. Words singular and close. |
| **the thing that stops once** | a rubber stamp, last eighth of every second bar, **stops for good near the end while everything else runs on.** |
| **sound** | 104 BPM, G Dorian. Office-percussion funk breakbeat: unbroken walking electric bass, brushed papery kit, muted brass stabs that *answer* rather than cushion, detuned electric piano, an eight-bit blip standing in for a pen. **Signature device: the read-back double** — a second take of the same voice one beat behind, same pitch, same words. Not a harmony; a copy. |
| **kinetic defect** | snare four milliseconds late every second bar, never corrected. |

## V2 · NOBODY IN THE CHAIR — *the ten seconds before, and nobody comes*

| | |
|---|---|
| **scene** | A body at a service window. The answer already exists, is already written, and is facing away. The ten seconds pass. The window shuts. **Nobody was ever in the chair.** |
| **why it defeats the template** | ⭐⭐ **This is the pair's PASSIVE-INJUSTICE song** (the Shklar seat the panel added). A failure to act, by somebody who could have acted, is an **injustice**, not a **misfortune** — and it has **no villain**, which is exactly what a protest anthem cannot sing. There is nobody to name and the song says so out loud: *"That is not weather. That is not luck."* |
| **the object (R1)** | a service window — *"The window took its ten and shut."* |
| **the hook** | a status report, and the singer is the last item in it. |
| **the discriminator** | *"I am the last part of it to know."* ⭐ The exact inverse of the protest singer, who is already right and is informing you. |
| **the cost (R5)** | the ten seconds themselves — spent, gone, and spent on nobody. |
| **the one number** | *ten* seconds, answered immediately: *"Ten seconds is not long. / It was long."* |
| **the sky, as substance** | *"The sky has known since yesterday. / The instruments have known all week. / The board behind the glass has known. / I get to know at the window."* — **FORCE AND VOLUME ARE DIFFERENT MEASUREMENTS TODAY**, put into a body that is the last thing in the room to find out. No astronomical number is sung. |
| **the thing that stops once** | a chair leg dragged a quarter-turn on the two of every bar, **stops three-quarters through while the kit continues.** |
| **sound** | 100 BPM, F minor. Dub skeleton: one bass note held and released slowly, rimshot on the three, a brushed hat that enters late and never leaves, one detuned bell, a tape-replay keyboard (**mechanism, not trade name**) with audible flutter and an eight-second note ceiling. Wide empty floor; nothing above four kilohertz until the kit arrives — and then **it arrives at full size and never gets smaller** (operational move 3: arrive and stay, never grow). |
| **kinetic defect** | the two of the bar lands a hair early, the four a hair late, all record long. |

## V3 · THERE IS A FIFTH — *filed knowing it probably fails*

| | |
|---|---|
| **scene** | An appeal walked down and posted before the counter opens. It is the fourth. The others did not get turned down — they **just sat.** The fifth is already half written on the back of a receipt. |
| **why it defeats the template** | ⭐⭐ **This is the pair's HOPE song** — and hope here is not optimism and not reassurance: it is **appetite pointed at the future**, a specific thing wanted out loud that has not happened yet, unembarrassed to be caught wanting it. A protest anthem promises victory; this one **states the likely loss in the fourth verse and goes back on Monday.** |
| **the object (R1)** | a drop slot — *"The slot took the fourth one and gave nothing back."* |
| **the hook** | a filing receipt in the past tense, singular. |
| **the discriminator** | *"Nobody has to read it. Nobody will. / I wrote it like somebody will."* ⭐ Same word, opposite jobs, one line apart: the passive injustice and the appetite, welded. |
| **the cost (R5)** | *"None of that is a load. / It is just gone, and I know where it went. / That is what a cost is. / Not the carrying. The gone."* — **R5's displacement-not-strain amendment, sung.** |
| **the one number** | *the fourth one*, answered by an act rather than a fact: **"There is a fifth."** |
| **able to lose** | stated flatly and in advance: *"It probably does not work. / I am saying so aloud, on purpose. / Not to get ahead of it. / Not so it lands soft."* — and the un-losable part is already banked: *"the walk already happened."* |
| **the thing that stops once** | the flat of a hand on a wooden counter on the four of every second bar, **stops near the end while the band keeps going.** |
| **sound** | 108 BPM, E♭ major — the pair's only major key, and the fastest of the four. Two-hand piano guajeo against a short round synth bass, brushed breakbeat kit, a shaker pushing, a two-horn answer figure that **replies** to the vocal instead of padding it, a rubber-band snap. ⛔ **Not a salsa pastiche and not a period ensemble** — one inherited bodily intelligence (the guajeo's offbeat relation) on a current chassis. |
| **kinetic defect** | the whole vocal rushes and the band never catches up. |

## V4 · IT FALLS ON ANYONE — *the right procedure, the wrong outcome* ⭐⭐

**The hardest of the twenty-four, and the method is operational move 3, applied literally.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **scene** | The singer asked for a cut-off to be drawn in the open, before they knew where they would land. It was drawn. It was applied exactly right. It came down over their head. **They would ask for it again.** |
| **why it defeats the template** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The rule went against me and the rule is right.** A protest anthem's rule always fails, and it always fails downward; it cannot survive a sentence in which the procedure worked. |
| **the object (R1)** | a numeric cut-off — *"The line came down at nine and dropped me under it."* |
| **the win condition, first 30 s, checkable** | *"Here is what would count as winning. / Say it now so it can be checked later. / **The line falls where it falls, and it falls on anyone.**"* |
| **every musical promise kept** | five choruses on schedule · cadence lands every time · the pen clicks every bar · **no modulation, no added layer, no choir, nothing "bigger."** |
| **the only broken promise, and it is in the sentence** | choruses 1–4: *and it falls on **anyone**.* → chorus 5: *and it falls on **me**.* **Nine words identical. One word changed.** |
| **⭐ how it is still justice** | **the win condition was VERIFIED, and the verification is the loss.** The singer asked for one checkable thing — that the line runs on anyone — the check came back **TRUE**, and the truth landed on them. |
| **why the singer accepts it** | *"Anybody can ask for a line / once the line already lies underneath. / I asked when I did not know. / So I get what I get."* — the veil of ignorance, sung, with no philosophy word in it. |
| **the pair's central discovery** | *"Nobody moved it. / That is the good news. / That is also the whole of the bad news. / **The same nobody both times.**"* ⭐⭐ The *nobody* who failed to bend the rule for me is the same *nobody* who failed to bend it for anybody else. **Passive injustice and procedural justice are one event seen from two sides.** |
| **the cost (R5)** | *"I took my name off the list myself. / Nobody had to take it off. / It is not carrying. It is subtraction."* |
| **the one number** | *nine* (the cut-off) against *eight* (where the singer came in) — answered by where they stand, not recited. |
| **the thing that stops once** | a retractable pen clicked twice a bar from bar one, **stops on the changed word** and never returns. ⭐ The passive injustice made audible: the pen stops because nobody was ever going to pick it up. |
| **R6, the pair's headline** | ⭐ **the singer stops before the song does.** The changed line is spoken once, plainly, then there is **no vocal at all** while the arrangement completes the form alone. *The music keeps every promise; the voice is the only thing that does not get to finish.* |
| **sound** | 102 BPM, C minor. Field-snare march folded into slow electronic soul: tight snare with the sticks audible, a plucked ostinato repeating **without variation**, warm round sub one note per bar, muted string figure, a distant eight-bit arpeggio. |
| **kinetic defect** | the kit pushes ahead of the grid all record long **and the pen never does** — the procedure is the only thing in time. |

## ⛔ WHAT NONE OF THE FOUR DOES

- ⛔ no escalation ladder — **loudness never arrives by adding humans**
- ⛔ no internal drop-out (measured dead: a ~4 s stop rendered 0.40 s; voids filled 2/2)
- ⛔ no modulation into any last chorus
- ⛔ no I–V–vi–IV or vi–IV–I–V
- ⛔ no requested level, width, or narrowing — ⭐ **the peak's narrowing is honoured in the LYRIC** (words go singular and close), never in the prompt, because instructing what the generator already does (r = −0.43) buys zero discrimination
- ⛔ no clock ticking, no countdown — **the run's central object is a clock running out and not one of these songs sounds one**
- ⛔ no invented person, no role, no biography, no jurisdiction, no reconstruction
