# 05 — Orchestrator Pair Assignments

**Personality assignment for every pair:** `SUNNA R4.1 — THE HIGH-SHINE FLIRT` from the canonical library YAML. No Lofn-Prime voice, Sol abstraction, invented sub-personality, or generic “female singer” substitution.  
**Cardinality:** six pair agents × four variations = twenty-four complete songs.  
**Dual split:** P01–P03 ACCESSIBLE; P04–P06 AMBITIOUS. P02 and P05 are NEWS; P01, P03, P04, P06 are EXISTENCE.  
**Isolation:** Steps 05–11 are creatively pair-local. No sibling titles, rooms, lyric skeletons, rhyme schemes, hook grammars, seam blocks, or production arcs may be read or reused.

## Assignment rationale

The six assignments distribute Sunna's measured strongest bed families, the daily barbell, news/existence load, first-person obligation, rooms, HUM riffs, and return mechanics before dispatch. Allocation is upstream so pair isolation cannot accidentally produce one song six times with nouns swapped.
