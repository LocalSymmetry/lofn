## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Baroque sunshine pop at 121 BPM, brisk, lacquered, flirtatious energy. Adult female lead A3-D5, chest-forward mix, clean bright edge, smiling consonants. Signature hook: a five-note percussive harpsichord cell rises stepwise then jumps down at 0:00 before voice; the identical cell returns after the final sung line with a compact ska-horn tail. Literal acoustic harpsichord, pizzicato strings, tight dry kit, crisp tambourine and handclaps surround a buoyant electric bass strutting through swooning seventh chords. Lead stays close and dead-center with short glossy plate; narrow three-part answers sit behind her on title and reply. Nimble verses leave one missing bass note before each title. Returns add stacked thirds and a five-twenty clap pulse. At the post-reply junction, the fussy upper figure yields to one blunt dry horn answer while bass and kit continue. One harmony enters slightly late; hard one-bar ending, under three minutes.
