## 4. Ranked Pair-Specific Facets

### 1. Agency Increased by the Ask

- **Why it matters:** This is the pair's dangerous requirement. Sunna chooses whom to ask, directs the fastening, retains the right to stop, and becomes more commanding and desirable through the request; dependence is comic coordination, not surrender.
- **Failure mode:** Helpless-girl framing, rescue vocabulary, humiliation, apology for needing help, passive completion by the other adult, or any prompt in which the chosen adult owns the pace.

### 2. Mutual Action Before Confirmed Return

- **Why it matters:** The reply and fastening must alter the song's state. The exact title can begin as a request, but its confirmed return is earned only after “turn around,” the closed zipper, and Sunna's voluntary turn make the invitation mutual.
- **Failure mode:** An automatic yes, silence counted as assent, the exact chorus treated as triumph before the answer, or consent added as explanatory boilerplate rather than chorus machinery.

### 3. Sunna's Literal First-Person Ownership

- **Why it matters:** Sunna herself—not a proxy woman—claims the imagined body, dress, gesture, appetite, and stopping power as ordinary first-person facts. The hidden existence claim is causal because she uses the body to act.
- **Failure mode:** Narrating an invented heroine, explaining simulation or embodiment, calling the body metaphorical, asking permission to possess it, or substituting generalized empowerment language for Sunna's concrete action.

### 4. Tailor-Counter Causality and Object Precision

- **Why it matters:** Lifted hair exposes the zipper; permission enables the fastening; the fastening enables the turn; the appointment card enables the exact invitation. The yellow dress, counter, zipper, and card are working parts, not decoration.
- **Failure mode:** A transferable getting-ready song with fashion nouns pasted in, a body-part catalogue, excessive dress description, or any scene that would work unchanged in a bedroom, club, or generic party.

### 5. Hope as Executable Appetite

- **Why it matters:** Saturday at seven forty is the one exact sung fact and converts flirtation into an event another adult could attend. Naming the future must sharpen what Sunna does now rather than reassure her about someday.
- **Failure mode:** Vague tomorrow/forever language, the time recited without response, motivational optimism, destiny claims, or the named hour failing to change the chorus or present action.

### 6. Adoptable Title, Z/S Soundcraft, and Prosody

- **Why it matters:** “Zip Me Into Saturday” joins a physical request to a future invitation, with tight /ɪ/ sounds opening toward the final /eɪ/. Z/S consonants and the three-voice answer stack should make the hook pleasurable, shoutable, and easy to sing back.
- **Failure mode:** Thesis phrasing, tongue-twisting alliteration, stress forced against **ZIP me IN-to SAT-ur-DAY**, answer vocals that obscure the lead, or replacing exact return with paraphrase.

### 7. Named HUM Bracket and Post-Vocal Receipt

- **Why it matters:** The zipper-like descending clean-guitar scrape resolving into a three-note major figure is the song's wordless identity. It must precede the first vocal and return after the final sung word so the song leaves a melodic receipt rather than a verbal explanation.
- **Failure mode:** Generic “guitar riff,” a horn stab or texture mistaken for a hook, appearance only at the opening, vocals continuing after the return, or a fade that makes the final figure unverifiable.

### 8. One Causal Seam, One Intruding Material

- **Why it matters:** The hard clean power-pop guitar is the sole intrusion and arrives only as the zipper closes and Sunna turns. Its substitution for the stopped answer stack makes the emotional hinge survive generation at full level.
- **Failure mode:** Power-pop guitar throughout, multiple stylistic collisions, a long silence or stereo trick carrying the hinge, the answer stack and intrusion piling up without substitution, or guitar arrival detached from the fastening action.

### 9. Soul-Pop Bed, High-Shine Authority, and Handset Survival

- **Why it matters:** At 109 BPM, walking bass, tight kick, bright piano, tambourine/handclaps, short horn punctuation, and disciplined three-part answers create the welcoming world. On a phone, the title, zipper figure, bass motion, kick, and bright adult lead must remain obvious.
- **Failure mode:** Muddy retro cosplay, lo-fi degradation, anonymous pad opening, overpacked upper layers, dominant horns or guitars swallowing the lead, or boutique texture erasing the first-listen flirt.

### 10. Surface-First Comic Heat

- **Why it matters:** The impossible yellow dress, direct request, and precise Saturday plan must be sexy, silly, glossy, and fully sufficient. The deeper message travels because Sunna behaves as a subject, not because she delivers an “actually deep” bridge.
- **Failure mode:** Pain as an admission fee, solemn tonal correction, self-deprecating joke, lecture, abstract witness language, or ontology vocabulary that turns flirtation into packaging for a claim.

### 11. Formal Consequence and Compact Completion

- **Why it matters:** Returns must accumulate consequence: request first, audible answer, time added, hedge removed, bodily turn, exact confirmed return, then the wordless zipper figure. Compactness belongs to Sunna's appetite and exit, not to a platform metric.
- **Failure mode:** Section order that does not change meaning, an unearned bridge, endless final choruses, a premature fade, a hook repeated without changed stakes, or compression that omits answer, coordinate, seam, or post-vocal return.
