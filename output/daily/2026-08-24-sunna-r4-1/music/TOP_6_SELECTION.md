# Sunna R4.1 — Top 6 Selection

## Selection contract

- Ranked within arms only: three ACCESSIBLE and three AMBITIOUS.
- Frozen subject balance: two NEWS and four EXISTENCE.
- Golden Songs were loaded only now, on the judge side. Their useful standard was not wording or subject matter: the refrain must change status through action, the sound identity must return after the voice, and the lyric must remain a complete song without explaining its engine.
- One winner per pair was not imposed as a quota. It emerged because the six pair winners also produced the strongest six-world portfolio.

## Score method

Each finalist was scored to 100: first-read flirt event 20; hook and return 20; reciprocal adult answer 15; Sunna R4.1 gloss/frivolity 15; room/object/action specificity 10; named sonic identity and one seam 10; hidden depth remaining hidden 10. Structural eligibility was already proven and did not earn bonus points.
