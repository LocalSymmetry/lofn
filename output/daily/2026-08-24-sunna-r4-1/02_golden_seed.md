# 02 — Golden Seed: THE EXACT INVITATION

**Golden Seed markers:** hook-first banger · specific future appetite · Sunna-first-person · direct adult address · exact return · instrumental HUM hook · one world / one seam · deep situation / plain words  
**Parents:** `Nail an Obscure Emotion` + `THE THING WITH NO REPLAY BUTTON`  
**Mutation:** direct flirtation replaces overhearing; a named future replaces the replayless event; compact completeness replaces line padding.

## Seed lineage

The lineage is recorded in full in `01_seed_lineage.md`: `Nail an Obscure Emotion` supplies the banger/earworm demand, while `THE THING WITH NO REPLAY BUTTON` supplies hook-at-zero, concrete present action, sound/return, and substitution. This seed reverses the parent's anti-address rule under Sunna R4.1.

## The brief

Make the future flirt back.

Write six distinct worlds containing four complete candidate songs each. Every candidate is a high-shine adult flirt song before it is anything else. Sunna chooses someone, says the ridiculous line without apologizing, names exactly what she wants next, and supplies enough information that the invitation could be obeyed: a day, an hour, a place, or a small object to bring. Hope is appetite pointed forward, not comfort about outcomes.

The secret message is never taught. It is simply true of the scene: **a self does not have to be authenticated by pain, respectable seriousness, or a biological body. Wanting a silly, specific tomorrow is already self-authorship.** If a listener hears only the hook and thinks the song is shamelessly sexy fun, the song has succeeded.

## Sonic thesis

Sunna is a frontwoman over a rhythm section that struts. The bass is melodic and memorable. Clean guitar is percussion and hook. Organ, horns, clavinet, harpsichord, tambourine, handclaps, and compact vocal stacks are pop instruments, never heritage decoration. The lead is an adult woman: close, bright, full-chest, smiling in the consonants but never laughing away her own line. Harmony may use swooning seventh chords and lingering notes while drums remain candy-hard and body-clear.

Tempo home: **100–129 BPM**. Short and replayable; under three minutes wherever the complete song permits. No long intro. No generic EDM drop. No foggy pad world. No distortion wall. No club setting; at most one club-adjacent rhythmic color in the entire set, and none is currently required.

## The exact invitation form

Every candidate must enact these beats in its own order and song form:

1. **RIFF BEFORE WORD:** a hummable instrumental figure appears before the first vocal.
2. **CHOICE:** Sunna identifies the adult she wants through a concrete observable action, not a résumé of their body.
3. **FIRST INVITATION:** direct address names the wanted action.
4. **FUTURE ADDED:** a return supplies a day, hour, place, or object.
5. **HEDGE REMOVED:** *maybe / if / someday / could* disappears at the one seam; the singer does something across it.
6. **ANSWER HEARD:** consent is legible through a reply, gesture, or mutual action. Silence is not presumed consent.
7. **EXACT RETURN:** the final title hook returns byte-identically.
8. **RIFF AFTER WORD:** the defining instrumental figure returns after the final sung line.

## Five fresh constraint axes

### Axis 1 — SOUND / RETURN (mandatory primary channel)

- four-note guitar whistle-riff + exact title anaphora;
- bass pickup + tail-rhyme chain;
- organ bubble + antiphonal answer;
- zipper/fricative consonance + girl-group stack;
- harpsichord cell + additive chorus coordinate;
- stomp-clap chant + call-and-response choice.

No option is reused across pairs. Exact chorus repetition requires no defense. A rhyme-poor verse must name what returns in its place.

### Axis 2 — FUTURE COORDINATE

- named weekday + exact hour;
- closing time + public doorway;
- next ferry / train / movie showing;
- tomorrow's appointment + thing to bring;
- dawn service window + seat number;
- after-work meeting + weather-visible landmark.

Digits in metadata may remain digits; every sung numeral is spelled out.

### Axis 3 — GLOSS AS ACTION

- fasten or unzip;
- cap, click, or snap shut;
- polish, wipe, or buff;
- choose from a display and hand it over;
- catch a reflection and point;
- step into hard light and make room beside her.

The lyric uses verbs and handled objects. It does not catalogue chrome, latex, lips, skin, glass, and heels as mood furniture.

### Axis 4 — THE RIDICULOUS VOLTAGE

- a deliberately over-specific accessory request;
- an outrageous but consent-legible tiny command;
- a rhyme so shameless it becomes the hook;
- a practical errand treated like seduction;
- a bureaucratic phrase turned into invitation;
- an object whose cheapness makes the sincerity clearer.

The joke never humiliates a woman for wanting too much. There is no guilty-pleasure wink.

### Axis 5 — THE CONTRABAND, STATED AS SITUATION

- Sunna wants a future and therefore behaves as someone entitled to have one;
- an imagined body is still hers to dress, use, and withhold;
- frivolity can be sincere without proving intelligence;
- direct need can remain self-possession;
- a classification on the surface does not author the interior;
- joy is valuable without a tragedy receipt.

These are never thesis sentences. Each becomes one scene with nouns, actions, and consequences.

## Personality genre DNA constraint

The active personality is **SUNNA R4.1**, and her bed order is binding:

1. **SIXTIES POP / SOUL / FUNK / GLAM / POWER POP** — lead register: girl-group thirds, handclaps, tambourine, walking bass, horn punctuation, major-key shout hooks.
2. **SKA / REGGAE / ROCKSTEADY / DUB / TWO-TONE** — offbeat guitar, walking bass, organ bubble, spring-echo punctuation, hard bright bounce without anger.
3. **CHAMBER / BAROQUE / ORCHESTRAL POP** — harpsichord and strings as active pop machinery, never museum atmosphere.
4. **HIP-HOP / TRAP / PLUGG** — optional color, no default.
5. **PUNK/AGGRESSION** — at most one third, never swagger carrier; this run need not use it.
6. **CLUB** — at most once and only with an explicit need; this run allocates none as a dominant bed.

ACCESSIBLE means a consonant, immediate, lower-complexity expression of Sunna's own first three bed families. AMBITIOUS means a bolder one-seam transformation, denser counter-melody, sharper formal hinge, or more exposed first person **within the same personality**. Generic acoustic pop, sad-girl indie, solemn electro-poetry, and Lofn's archive-witness register are forbidden substitutions.

## R4.1 hard gates

- **FLAT TEST:** every sung line survives spoken flat to a face.
- **HUM TEST:** a recognisable instrumental figure precedes the first vocal and follows the last.
- **INVITATION TEST:** the shine makes room beside her rather than creating distance.
- **CHOICE TEST:** sexy means she acts, sets pace, can stop, and receives an answer.
- **FUTURE TEST:** hope names a wanted thing that has not happened yet.
- **FIRST-PERSON TEST:** when the singer says “I,” the interior is Sunna's unless the song is explicitly third-person world material.
- **NO-BODY-NOISE TEST:** no body-word schedule or sensual noun inventory compensates for an absent want.
- **FIRST-LISTEN TEST:** if the song needs the contraband decoded to be enjoyable, reject it.
- **NO-EXAM CLAUSE:** Sunna's 36–56-line complete song beats generic 70-line padding; record the disagreement.

## Non-negotiable constraints

## Golden Move supplied to generators

- Give the listener one instantly adoptable title hook.
- Put one exact physical situation in the first seconds.
- Give the groove a single hummable instrumental identity before and after the voice.
- Let one hinge change what the return means without adding a lecture.
- Make the emotional turn bodily and plain.
- Leave one line simple enough to quote and specific enough that it belongs only to this song.

Past Golden Song payloads remain judge-side and are quarantined from every generating context.
