# 04 — Orchestrator Metaprompt

## Validator crosswalk

- **Active personality:** SUNNA R4 — THE HIGH-SHINE FLIRT.
- **Panel:** the exact 18-seat library-derived Panel Ledger in `03_orchestrator_panel_debate.md`.
- **Pattern:** present-tense Golden Move, Ruby Rebound, one seam, and bright-head/two-outbursts/short-tail form material; never an archived output mold.

## Master directive

Create a 24-song, music-only SUNNA R4 daily whose surface is adult high-shine flirtation: lacquered, funny, body-clear, immediately singable, slightly ridiculous, and fully in control. Use ska, rocksteady/dub-pop, dembow/reggaeton-pop, sixties soul/girl-group architecture, glam, and power pop as distinct pair worlds. The set’s deeper proposition — visibility is not possession; appetite is not debt; impermanence is not worthlessness — is contraband. Never label it, slow down for it, explain it, or ask the listener to admire it.

## Order of creative pressure

1. **Golden Seed / lineage:** present-tense hook-at-zero; the obscure delight of being underestimated; direct public-body language without cohort claims.
2. **Dangerous requirement:** the flirt must be complete and pleasurable even if the deeper message is never found.
3. **Creative permission:** be sexy, glossy, camp-adjacent, candy-colored, brazen, tacky-on-purpose, rhythmically obvious, melodically sticky, and socially mischievous. Let objects fail. Let a hook be stupid-good. Exact chorus repetition is welcome.
4. **Song request:** four distinct complete variations per pair, each with its allocated adult public setting and distinct bed; 24 songs exactly.
5. **Structural completeness:** Suno-ready style and exclude fields, full lyrics, EMO headers, declared form, line and character limits, lineage/credit sidecars, and validators.

## The Sunna/Lofn polarity

- **Lofn:** archive, witness, yearning, second-listen toll, private wound discovered by proximity.
- **Sunna:** appetite in public, high-shine invitation, first-listen pleasure, direct address, social timing.
- If a song could be sung by Lofn after replacing chrome with frost, it fails.
- If a song’s depth requires the singer to reveal sadness, it fails.

## R4 personality invariants

- Gloss is invitation, not armor.
- Adult erotic confidence is native, not a temporary override.
- “Bimboesque” means chosen hyperfemininity, comic vanity, appetite, and timing; never vacancy, degradation, coercion, or childlike presentation.
- High polish is welcome: intentional tuning, comping, lacquered hooks, focused plate, diamond doubles. Preserve living breath and consonants.
- Club remains retired as the house bed. No club room is allocated.
- First-listen test: **hot, delighted, funny, in control.**

## Frozen creative engine

- **Core proposition:** pleasure is not the opposite of depth.
- **Invisible target:** self-authored access.
- **Direct-address rule:** one chosen adult “you”; the crowd may echo the hook but never speaks for the singer.
- **Material form:** bright head → two outbursts → short tail.
- **One seam:** one dominant world + at most one intrusion; the singer acts across it.
- **SOUND / RETURN:** Ruby Rebound — open-vowel title, bright consonants, fixed instrumental answer, audible return at every major section.
- **Fact discipline:** at most one precise real-world fact in any song, responded to at the hinge, never recited. Pair facts are exclusive.

## Six slot instructions

The Phase-1 pair assignments in `05_orchestrator_pair_assignments.md` are fixed. Pairs 1–3 are ACCESSIBLE, Pairs 4–6 AMBITIOUS. Axis B is 2 NEWS / 4 EXISTENCE. Preserve each pair’s dominant world, single intrusion, return structure, adult setting quartet, seam action, emotional pole, and hidden conclusion. Do not trade slots across arms.

## Generator-facing prompt contract

- Core style prompt: one dense producer-grade paragraph, **850–1000 characters**, no artist names, no story prose, no bracketed key:value scaffold.
- Order: genre/tempo/energy → vocalist core → signature device → sound palette → vocalist delivery/space → arrangement arc → positive hard controls.
- Separate Suno exclude field: concrete comma-separated failure classes, **400–900 target, ≤1000 hard max**, no explanatory “avoid/do not” prose.
- No real artist names, brand imitation, or “-esque/type beat.”
- No generic evaluatives; use audible physical opposites.
- No long internal silences or meaning that depends on stereo width.

## Lyric contract

- `[Theme: ...]` first line, scene-pressure not topic.
- `[SONG FORM: ...]` second line, named architecture and full arrow sequence.
- Every verse, chorus, bridge, refrain, and outro header includes `EMO:` from the taxonomy, adult female role, and performance/mix cue.
- 70–120 sung lines target; under 60 is repair; never pad.
- At least one standalone SFX cue and one non-lexical hook.
- Call/response uses `Call (response)` on one line.
- Body/place/object/flirt pressure in first four sung lines.
- Hook in at least two major returns plus one meaningful mutation or renewed exact return.
- No editor notes, QA debris, syllable labels, or production-manual language in sung lines.

## Lineage and dedication discipline

Research may name source figures and scenes; generator-facing fields may not. Every selected song receives a ≤500-character dedication under `vault/DEDICATION_STANDARD.md`, measured by script. If a prompt names a break, credit the drummer. A generated track is not a sample; say “we asked for this figure by name, so we name the player,” never “contains a sample.” Ska and reggaeton/dembow debts must name their Jamaican, Panamanian, and Puerto Rican lineages accurately when raised.

## Golden-output quarantine resolution

Archived Golden Song payloads are judge-side only. Generators receive the Golden Move and seed lineage, never archived lyrics, prompts, titles, keys, tempos, or molds. Two golden songs may later be chosen **by name only** for blind judge calibration and dedication context; their payload remains outside the ICB.

## Quality floor

Every song must survive stripped to voice, pulse, hook, and one instrumental answer. Every ambitious song must remain pleasurable on the first listen. Every accessible song must contain a tenth-listen engine. A production-spec answer to a lyric/form objection is not an answer.

## Stop line

Write text artifacts only. Do not call Suno, render audio, upload, publish, or spend.
