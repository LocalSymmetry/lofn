## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

High-shine organ-bubble ska, 126 BPM, C major, A=440Hz, ecstatic and wet-edged, bass and two dry horn notes before the lyric. Close adult female mezzo, bright chest-mix with crisp sibilants, late quick turns, transparent tuning, tight hinge-split doubles. The signature device couples a rising three-note horn flare to upper-percussion transfer: its third pitch vanishes for the lead to sing, then shaker and hand percussion jump from eighths to sixteenths while kick, bass and title stresses stay fixed. Walking electric bass, compact acoustic kit and clipped guitar hold center; popping organ sits left, guitar right, dry horns near-center, and clipped synthetic brass mirrors their contour only. Outburst 1 lifts through seventh-chord vocal thirds, then Verse 2 shortens. Four dembow-percussion lines replace guitar above unchanged bass before the accelerated Outburst 2. A shut valve, joined wet hands, bass stop and one brass tick make the tiny tail under a short glossy plate.
