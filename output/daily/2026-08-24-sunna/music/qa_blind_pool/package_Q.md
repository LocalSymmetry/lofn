## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Lacquered dub-pop / rocksteady, 105 BPM, A major, A=440Hz, flirtatious moon-cool energy. Adult female low mezzo, chest G3–C5, precise pitch, velvet edge, one smiling intake and a clipped upward curl on command words. Signature device: deep three-note bass speaks between “high tide” and “high gloss”; first-five-second frame is clipped organ bubble, dry rim and a coin-small lure-foil flick. Live acoustic-electric bass, hand percussion, clean upstroke guitar and cool organ sit in compact spring depth. Her lead stays close and centered while female thirds hover shallow; their upper double begins tight and drifts 25–35 ms late, but bass and kick remain immovable. Cold and warm residue trade places through the tide-rondo. At 2:02, the charter-card refusal triggers glam-bright guitar octaves and hard center claps, replacing dub space for exactly two detonations. The short bell-tail keeps bass and screen-door jingle after the tired double disappears, ending on a fresh answer.
