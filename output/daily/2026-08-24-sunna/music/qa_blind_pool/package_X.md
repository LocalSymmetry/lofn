## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

High-shine bass-led ska, 124 BPM, F major, A=440Hz, lusty, amused and chrome-clean, title call before bar one. Close adult female contralto, polished chest voice with bright edge, crisp plosives, dry laugh-smile, transparent tuning and narrow diamond doubles. A rising three-note horn flare tags the title; its last pitch disappears at the hinge for the lead to sing, while GOOD accumulates one voice, two voices, then low room. Melodic electric bass sits nearly level with the vocal at center; rim-click acoustic kit, clipped guitar, dead-center claps and four-note late dry horns leave open space. Outburst 1 adds taut seventh-chord backing thirds before the verse telescopes. One reggaeton-percussion seam replaces upper guitar while kick and bass keep walking. Outburst 2 stages response count: chosen adult first, low room behind, chosen adult final. Paper slap, shoulder contact and a dry latch tick close the tiny tail.
