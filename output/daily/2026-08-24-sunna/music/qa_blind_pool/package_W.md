Glam handclap power pop / rocksteady inflection, 120 BPM, A major, A=440 Hz, high-shine, sun-hot, and strutting. A close adult female mezzo sits center-front in chest/mix, cleanly tuned and tightly comped, with bright consonant bites, audible breath, and one talk-sung eye-roll. The melodic hook stacks “PRETTY ON PURPOSE” in thirds; the rhythmic hook snaps zipper-metal against candy claps and compact dry kick; the instrument hook is a high-string two-note offbeat electric-guitar answer; the texture hook is chrome tambourine over glossy seventh chords and mono electric bass. Uneven refrain braids drive two title detonations. A deep rocksteady pocket substitutes only at the route seam while bass keeps walking and the answer waits one beat. Final thirds widen around the changed driving verb; an eight-second stopped tail leaves a dry guitar reply. Focused plate, narrow field, mirror-clean upper mids, muscular lows, at least 10 dB crest.
