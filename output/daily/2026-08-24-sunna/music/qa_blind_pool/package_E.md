High-shine rocksteady / hard power-pop, 103 BPM, E major, A=440Hz, playful hip-forward energy. Adult female alto, chest-mix A3–D5, bright grain, light rasp on long vowels, wet stop consonants and a grin caught in one intake. Signature device: four hard stresses in “high tide, high gloss” split by an exact three-note melodic bass reply; first-five-second frame is dry rim, clean offbeat guitar and foil-bright ping. Live acoustic-electric bass, mono kick, seed shaker and clipped clean guitars hold short spring depth. Her close center lead keeps diamond female thirds just behind it, one late breath catch and crisp diction. Repair-stall residue thickens on each tide-rondo return while bass and kick remain fixed. At 2:00, hard downstrokes and center claps replace the dub gaps for two compact detonations. The late shaker drops one subdivision; the ≤11-second tail leaves bass, heel-click and a wholly new answer.
