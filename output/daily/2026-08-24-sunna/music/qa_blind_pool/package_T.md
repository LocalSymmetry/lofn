## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

High-shine dancehall-derived dembow-pop, 103 BPM, G minor, A=440 Hz, cheeky bass-forward energy: adult female low mezzo G3–C5, glossy chest register, precise pitch and clipped plosives. Signature melodic hook “Kiss the receipt” strikes immediately beside a three-note candy synth-brass stamp; compact thirds answer one sixteenth late, with one wipe snapping on-grid. Melodic mono bass walks beneath dry kick, rim click, crisp güiro and bright hand percussion; a swooning stacked seventh supplies the single harmonic seam. Close centered lead carries a grin in the consonants; replies hover shallow at the sides under focused plate. Basket-and-ticket calls reach return one at 0:46; nine short wash exchanges tighten the texture. At 1:51 the brass vacates for the seventh stack. Final detonation restores lag and returns brass as an outer-note dyad while vocal thirds voice the missing center; bass-only tail stops at 2:52 on a close ticket peel.
