Jangling power pop / dub-rocksteady inflection, 122 BPM, D major, A=440 Hz, high-gloss and playful. A close adult female mezzo commands center-front in full chest voice, cleanly tuned and comped, with living breath, crisp b/p/t attacks, and diamond doubles. The melodic hook stacks “PRETTY ON PURPOSE” in thirds that split once; the rhythmic hook snaps torn ribbon against compact dry kit and center claps; the instrument hook is a two-note open-string electric-guitar answer; the texture hook is clipped tambourine over high-string jangle and muscular mono bass. Uneven braids drive two title detonations. A dub-weighted rocksteady bass-and-rim pocket substitutes only at the wrist-bow seam while the singer stays bright and the answer waits one beat. The final return thickens around the changed exit verb; an eight-second stopped tail leaves one reply. Focused short plate, clean upper mids, uncluttered lows, candy-hard transients, at least 10 dB crest.
