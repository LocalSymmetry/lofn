## PART A — PHASE 0: THE SEED

### A1. The commission
The Scientist: *a Gen Z firestorm carrying the simple message **"This is the worst I will ever be."***

### A2. ⭐ THE RULING ON TENSE (carried from `00_research_brief.md` §5 — binding on all 24 songs)

> **The sentence is a DECISION, not a PREDICTION. Spoken from the floor, mid-failure, by somebody who has
> just been bad at the thing — never from the podium by somebody who arrived.**

She has no evidence. She is **deciding this counts as a floor rather than a ceiling**, and the deciding
happens **now**, which is what keeps **D5 PRESENT TENSE** intact. The future clause is grammatical, not
epistemic.

**✅ PASSES:** still inside the failure · nothing resolved · says it to nobody · we overhear it (**D8**).
**❌ FAILS → REPAIR:** already improved and reporting back · any line implying she knows the outcome · any
line offering it outward as comfort. *That is an adult in the room.*

### A3. ⭐ THE ARGUMENT INSIDE THE CAST (why Pantheon-only is the right constraint, not a limitation)

Per `vault/PANTHEON.md`, SUNNA's admission argument against Lofn is:
> *"Your disappointment is a failure of attention. You look at what a thing isn't. I look at what it is."*

**That is this theme, already in dispute inside the cast.** LOFN-PRIME sees the gap. SUNNA sees the thing.
**"This is the worst I will ever be" is the one sentence they can both sign — and each signs it for the
opposite reason.** The set must carry **both readings and must not resolve them.** No song adjudicates.

## PART B — PHASE 1: THE ROOM

### B1. BASELINE — where the three rooms landed before transformation

**THE FIRESTARTER (after Sophie Powers):** *"Working from writing the feeling from inside it —* the brief is
a motivational poster and I will not sing a poster. 'This is the worst I'll ever be' is what a coach says."
**THE RELENTLESS OPTIMIST (after Edith Victoria):** "It is also what you say lying down. Same words, opposite
altitude."
**THE HARDCORE ELDER — HYPER-SKEPTIC (after Rollins):** "Then the whole run hangs on altitude and nobody has
specified it. **Every warm ending is a sales technique until it survives interrogation** — and this brief is
one long warm ending. Specify the floor or I'm voting no on all six."
**THE ANGST CARTOGRAPHER (after Rodrigo):** "Attach it to an object. A feeling with no object is a slogan."
**THE HALF-PIPE WITNESS (after Tony Hawk):** "The eleventh attempt only means something because ten falls were

**THE COHORT ABOLITIONIST — HYPER-SKEPTIC (after Philip N. Cohen):** *"Working from the published argument
that generation labels are analytically empty —* 'a Gen Z firestorm' is a marketing category, not a subject.
**Delete the cohort or I withdraw.**"

→ **Baseline yields:** the altitude problem (Rollins) + the object requirement (Rodrigo) + the falls
(Hawk). Rooms deadlock on *who is saying the sentence.*

### B2. GROUP TRANSFORM — **BRIDGE** (leave music entirely; seat people who solve "the attempt that failed")

**THE FAILURE ARCHAEOLOGIST** (role-anchored — post-incident review practice): "In incident review the
finding is never *someone was careless.* It is **the system permitted a belief that was false.** Your SQLite
fact is not a bug story. **It is sixteen years of software believing it had saved something.**"
**THE ANIMATOR** (role-anchored — traditional key-frame practice): "We keep the roughs. **The pencil test is
not an embarrassment stage, it is a stage.** Nobody apologises for it and nobody redeems it either."
**THE HALF-PIPE WITNESS:** "Oh — *actually*, that's the correction. I said the falls make the landing mean
something. **Wrong.** The falls don't serve the landing. **The falls are the footage.** In this song there IS
no landing."
**THE FIRESTARTER:** "*Wait.* Then the composite is the whole form. Not a metaphor — **the form.** A hundred
failed frames stacked read as a sky. **You don't fix the last one.**"

→ ⭐ **BRIDGE PRODUCES: THE COMPOSITE (S3 form rule) and D9-WITH-TEETH — the last attempt is not fixed.**

### B3. SKEPTIC TRANSFORM — **REFLECT** (mirror the assumption: *that the sentence is encouraging*)

**THE DYNAMIC RANGE AUDITOR — HYPER-SKEPTIC (after Bob Katz):** "Mirror it. If the sentence is **not**
encouraging — what is it? **It's a threat.** 'This is the worst I will ever be' means *there is no version of
me you get to stop watching.* Read that way it is not comfort, it is **refusal to leave.**"
**THE CATHARSIS PSYCHOLOGIST (after Dunbar):** "State the mechanism: a room singing a line together
synchronises breathing before it agrees about meaning. **They will sing it as a threat and a comfort at once
and never notice the difference.**"
**THE HARDCORE ELDER:** "...Fine. **That survives interrogation.** A line that is simultaneously a vow and a
warning is not a sales technique. I withdraw the objection."
**THE COHORT ABOLITIONIST:** "And it needs no cohort. **One body, one afternoon.** Withdrawn."

→ ⭐⭐ **REFLECT PRODUCES THE RUN'S POSTURE — *THE SENTENCE IS A THREAT AND A COMFORT IN THE SAME BREATH,
AND THE SONG NEVER PICKS.*** It is a vow of persistence overheard by somebody who was not being reassured.
This satisfies **D8** by construction: you cannot address a threat outward without becoming an adult in the
room, so nobody is addressed.
