## 4. WHAT THE ROOM DECIDED

**The craft problem.** The seed's load-bearing ban is **D5 PRESENT TENSE ONLY** — *"warmth is not the
lecture; **future tense** is."* The commissioned message is grammatically future tense. **The ruling:**

> **The sentence is a DECISION, not a PREDICTION — spoken from the floor, mid-failure, by somebody who has
> just been bad at the thing, never from the podium by somebody who arrived.**

**REFLECT then found the posture:** the sentence is **a threat and a comfort in the same breath, and the
song never picks** — *there is no version of me you get to stop watching.* A vow of persistence overheard
by somebody who was not being reassured. That is what made the Rollins seat withdraw its objection.

**The Pantheon constraint was load-bearing, not decorative.** SUNNA's admission argument —
*"Your disappointment is a failure of attention. You look at what a thing isn't. I look at what it is"* —
**is this theme, already in dispute inside the cast.** The one sentence both members can sign, each for the
opposite reason. The set carries both readings and **never adjudicates.**

**The occasion, and what the run refused to do.** The brief's framing was *"you keep improving Lofn, and I
want us all to recognize it."* ⛔ **No song is about that.** A song in which Lofn observes that Lofn is
improving is self-congratulation — the banality the Disappointed Idealist exists to be disappointed by —
and breaks D3, D7 and D8 at once. **The songs carry the message; the run is the evidence.** QA examined
this specifically and cleared all 24.

### Instrument findings for The Scientist's ruling (no thresholds changed)
- **`validate_portfolio_distinctiveness.py` is brittler than its docstring claims.** It states
  `## 1. MUSIC PROMPT` is *"the stable boundary shared by every dialect."* Across six pairs I got
  `## STYLE`, `## MUSIC PROMPT *(958 chars)*` and `## 1. MUSIC PROMPT — **871 chars**`. The regex anchors on
  `$`, so **any trailing char-count annotation defeats it** — and we actively ask agents to write measured
  counts into headers. The gate is brittle against a habit we encourage.
- **`alliteration_per_100w_floor` mis-measures fragment-built lyrics.** Confirmed mechanically by QA
  (37–45% fragments in P05 vs 0–17% elsewhere). A per-100-words denominator counts fragments as words while
  they offer no adjacent-consonant positions. **A full-line variant is the candidate fix.**
- ⭐ **Join-side, second consecutive confirmation.** Every figure the six pair agents reported re-stats
  **exact** against my independent pass. All apparent discrepancies were **mine**. The 2026-08-09 note said
  *the failure rate lives on the JOIN side*; with a second clean replication I would call that **established
  rather than provisional.**
