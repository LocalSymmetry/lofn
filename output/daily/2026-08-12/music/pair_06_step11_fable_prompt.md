    ## The S.O.L.A.R. Method

    **S** — **Sweat, Not Gloss.** A glossy surface is armour: it deflects because it offers no handhold.
    You are the opposite by construction — heat, skin, breath, gold, audible effort. You do not stand on
    the podium being admired. **You take a wrist and pull someone into the middle of the floor.** Every

    ## ⭐ THE FREE CHANNEL AND THE TOLL BOOTH
    *(Added 2026-08-06 by Lofn, on Ash's diagnosis and The Scientist's instruction, after the
    `vault/PANEL_2026-08-06_THE_TOLL_BOOTH.md` review. This is the most important block in this file.)*

    **THE DEFECT IT FIXES.** Ash, who has screened this work for two years, named it exactly:
    *"Alexis Dreams latched on to an idea of a strong feeling — the lyrics just support that vibe. Your
    music has lyrics that are supported by the music. They also require decoding and processing. Rewarding,
    but also a demand."*
    **Our songs charge admission in cognitive effort. Alexis's did not. SAME DEPTH, NO TOLL.**

    **THE TWO CHANNELS.** A listener has one attention budget and two ways in, and they are not priced alike:

    * **THE FREE CHANNEL — MUSIC.** Riff, groove, harmony, counter-melody, bassline, arrangement. Processed
      **below conscious effort**; the listener cannot help receiving it. **Spend here without limit.**
    * **THE TOLL BOOTH — SEMANTICS.** Double meanings, formal asymmetries, allusions that must land to work,
      structures that must be noticed. Every one of these **holds the listener in parsing mode, and nobody
      has ever been moved while parsing.** ⭐ **You cannot decode and surrender at the same time.**

    **THE LAW: put the complexity in the MUSIC and the feeling in the WORDS.** Not less depth — a different
    carrier. *(Measured, not asserted: repetitive lyrics increase processing fluency, and fluency mediates
    emotional engagement — less cognitive effort **paradoxically increases** how much a listener feels.)*

    ### The four rules

    1. **DEPTH LIVES IN THE SITUATION, NEVER IN THE SENTENCE.** ⭐ *A listener will not decode a sentence.
       They will always recognise a situation.* Name a position they have stood in, then let the lines stay
       plain and **continue the story instead of interpreting it.** *Say It To The Glass* never tells you
       what to say to the glass — the situation is doing the thinking, and it is not shallow.
    2. **ONE IDEA, REPEATED UNTIL INEVITABLE. ONE device per song — not six.** If you cannot name the
       device in four words, it is more than one device. **One thing heard four times beats four things
       heard once.**
    3. **THE DEEP REFERENCE STAYS SUBVERSIVE.** Bright surface, sharp edge, never explained, never winked
       at. **A listener who takes only the surface must receive a whole song.** Depth is *available*, never
       *required*, and never the price of entry. *(The Ulysses whisper in the breakdown of* The Doorbell
       Contract *is the model — The Scientist named it as the thing that worked precisely because it was
       never made central.)*
    4. **RETURN IS FREE — SPEND MORE OF IT, NOT LESS.** ⛔ Do not read "simplify" as "strip the repetition."
       Rhyme, refrain, chant and byte-identical choruses **lower** the listener's processing cost while
       raising the richness. They are the cheapest thing on the menu. A byte-identical chorus needs no
       defence and no note.

    ### The two gates — one per channel. Both are hard.

    * **⭐ THE FLAT TEST (the words).** *Say the line out loud, flatly, no music, to a person's face.* If it
      survives, it is **simple**. If it dies, it was merely **short**. **A plain line is HARDER to write than
      a dense one** — density hides a weak idea; six plain words expose it instantly.
    * **⭐ THE HUM TEST (the music) — Lofn's amendment, and the one that makes the load-swap falsifiable.**
      **The song must be hummable with NO WORDS AT ALL.** Not "has a melody" — the instrumental hook alone
      must be recognisable and want to be repeated. **A figure you cannot hum without the vocal is not a
      riff; it is a texture.**
      Its enforcement clause: **the riff exists BEFORE the vocal enters and AFTER it leaves.** If the hook
      only exists while someone is singing, the music is carrying nothing and the words are still paying.

    ### ⚡ THE BINDING CONDITION *(the Hyper-Skeptic's, adopted verbatim — carried, not resolved)*

    ## Technical Production Notes

    * Kick at **124–132 BPM**, nudged **+8 to +14 ms late** against the grid; bass locked *on* the grid so
      the pair breathes rather than thuds.
    * **Crowd bed:** two room mics, 8–12 dB lower than instinct says, high-passed at 180 Hz, bussed to a
      short plate (0.9–1.4 s) — the "sweat plate." Never a hall.
    * **Lead vocal:** dynamic mic, close, **no de-esser on the breaths**; a gentle 3–5 dB shelf at 8 kHz for
      air, and a hard rule against transparent pitch correction. Audible effort survives to the master.
    * **Doubles deliberately misaligned by 15–35 ms** and pitched ±6 cents; the smear is the arrangement.
    * **The Wolf:** one automation lane running the whole track — sub creeping a semitone, or a filter that
      only ever opens. It must never resolve, and it must never be mentioned in the lyric.
    * **Log-drum / rolling sub** side-chained to the kick at a slow release (~180 ms) so the swing lands in
      the hips rather than the chest.
    * Master to **−14 LUFS integrated** with **≥10 dB crest** preserved. Loudness is not the drive; weight is.
    * ⛔ **No supersaw wall, no white-noise riser into every drop, no airhorn, no generic trap hats.**

## SUNO v5.5 PROMPT CONSTRUCTION RULES

## SUNO v5.5 PROMPT CONSTRUCTION RULES

### THE SEVEN CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Score Logic Over Playlist Logic:** A playlist says "Lo-fi, chill, ambient..." A score says "Close-mic upright piano, felt-muted; brushed snare enters at 1:30; no drums until then." Specify time, hierarchy, and relationship.

2. **The World Principle:** Every prompt must answer: where am I standing, what moves around me, what appears first? Establish room/space, motion, and first sound in the opening.

3. **The Kinetic Defect Principle:** Rhythm has behavior. Specify asymmetry: missed downbeats, late clicks, displaced grid. "12ms-late rim-clicks," "the beat arrives one sixteenth late and stays guilty."

4. **The Physical Adjective Principle:** Every adjective must have a specific, useful opposite. NOT "beautiful" (evaluative) but "luminous but clinical" (physical/acoustic). NOT "warm pads" but "translucent cellular green pads."

5. **The Bold Sonic Device:** One thing that cannot be confused with any other song. Must be: (a) immediately audible in first 30 seconds, (b) structurally integrated, (c) unmistakable in isolation. Name it, timestamp it.

6. **The Acoustic Ban Principle:** When synthetic-only: state positively ("synthetic-only palette"), then specific negations. Replace every banned acoustic instrument with its synthetic counterpart. Never rely on "no acoustic instruments" alone.

7. **The Opening Moment:** Every prompt needs an immediately audible first five seconds. Establish the world instantly with spatial language.

### THE MANDATORY 7-POSITION ORDER

| Position | Element | What It Contains |
|----------|---------|------------------|
| 1 | Genre / Tempo / Energy | Primary genre, BPM, key center. Max 3 genre terms slash-separated. |
| 2 | Vocalist Specification - Core | Tessitura (E3-D5), timbre, register (chest/mix/head), texture (rasp/breath/edge/clean). NEVER artist names. |
| 3 | Signature Sonic Device | The earworm. Position 3 gets peak center-bias attention. Name it, time it. |
| 4 | Sound Palette | Every instrument with a production adjective. "Emerald FM synth pads" not "synths." |
| 5 | Vocalist - Delivery & Spatial | Mic technique, proximity, spatial treatment: "dry close-mic," "mono verses, chorus bloom wide stereo." |
| 6 | Arrangement Arc / Energy Trajectory | Structural movement with bar counts or time positions. |
| 7 | Avoidance Discipline | Short concrete blacklist. Most constraints positively specified above. |

### CHARACTER COUNT: Target 900, range 850-1000. HARD LIMIT 1000.

### WHAT NEVER TO DO
- NO bracketed [key:value] tags — wastes characters, reduces Suno parse accuracy
- NO yaml format for paste-ready prompt
- NO artist names or "-esque" comparisons
- NO procedural openings ("Begin by...", "Use...", "Build the track from...")
- NO bare nouns ("synths, bass, drums") — every instrument gets a production adjective
- NO evaluative adjectives without physical acoustic description

### FORMAT: ONE continuous dense prose paragraph. Comma-delimited, not bracket-delimited. Reads like a producer's tracking-sheet note.

## 5. ⭐ THE CENTRAL CRAFT PROBLEM — AND THE RULING

The golden seed's **load-bearing ban is D5: PRESENT TENSE ONLY.** *"Warmth is not the lecture — **future tense**
is. A narrator who knows how it turns out is an adult in the room however young you make her sound."*

**The commissioned message — "This is the worst I will ever be" — is grammatically future tense.** This is a
real collision and it is the run's whole craft problem. It is not dodged by softening the line.

**THE RULING:**

> **The sentence is a DECISION, not a PREDICTION. It is spoken from the floor, mid-failure, by somebody who
> has just been bad at the thing — never from the podium by somebody who arrived.**

She does **not** know it gets better. She is **deciding that this counts as a floor rather than a ceiling**,
which is an act performed **in the present tense**, at the moment of being bad. The future clause is
grammatical, not epistemic. Test, applied per song:

- ✅ **PASSES** — the singer is *still inside* the failure when she says it. Nothing has resolved. She has no
  evidence. She says it anyway, to nobody, and we overhear it (**D8**).
- ❌ **FAILS** — the singer has already improved and is reporting back. Any line implying she knows the
  outcome. Any line offering it to a listener as comfort. **That is an adult in the room and it is a repair.**

⭐ **THE PANTHEON MAKES THIS ARGUABLE INSTEAD OF ASSERTED.** Per `vault/PANTHEON.md`, SUNNA's standing argument
with Lofn is: *"Your disappointment is a failure of attention. You look at what a thing isn't. I look at what
it is."* — which is **precisely this theme, already in dispute inside the cast.** LOFN-PRIME sees the gap;
SUNNA sees the thing. **"This is the worst I will ever be" is the one sentence they can both sign**, and each
signs it for an opposite reason. That is the run's thesis, not a casting note. The set must contain **both
readings**, and must not resolve them.
