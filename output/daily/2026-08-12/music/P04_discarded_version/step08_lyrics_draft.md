# STEP 08 — LYRICS DRAFT · PAIR 4 · **KEPT, NOT PLAYED**

**Working notes first, lyric second. The lyric below is the draft that goes forward to step 10.**

---

## A. DRAFTING DECISIONS

**1. The antiphon is carried by parentheses, not by voice labels.**
Suno renders parenthesised lines as an answering / backing vocal — so `(the kept take)` costs two
characters, arrives automatically as the far, thin, wide voice, and never gets sung as a label. Plain
lines are the dropped take, close and dry. **This is the only place in the song where the two takes
occupy the same bar, and they still never address each other** (D8): the parentheses are adjacency, not
reply.

**2. Zero rhyme in the verse, verified by construction, not by feel.**
Every verse line's final word was checked against the last-three-characters rule inside a ±4-line window
(`gates.yaml → rhyme_window`, the same definition `measure_soundcraft.py` uses). Two near-misses were
rewritten out during drafting:
- `…I come in under it.` / `…I am already over it.` → **`under.` / `over.`** (both had ended on *it*).
  The bare prepositions land harder anyway.
- `…is deciding a thing.` / `…is coming.` → **`has to decide.` / `is on its way.`** (both had ended on
  *-ing*).
The **only** repeated line-endings inside verses are **exact whole-line repetitions** — a return device,
never a rhyming pair of different lines. `A thumb goes past me, goes past me.` was folded into one line
for exactly this reason (and it loops better inside the bar).

**3. Line length was cut to protect the prose ceiling.** `mean_words_per_line_ceiling` is 7.5. First
pass ran ≈8.1 — the verses were explaining. Eleven lines were shortened (`The room goes on making noise
after I stop.` → `The room makes noise after I stop.`) and the draft now sits near 6.7. Shorter lines
also sing better in this bed: Tirzah-side trip-hop phrases in breath-lengths, not sentences.

**4. Present tense audited line by line** (D5). Three drafted lines were past or present-perfect and were
rewritten: *the room went on* → **goes on**; *where her back was* → **where her back is**; *I am the one
that came after that one* → **comes after**.

**5. Cohort gate swept** (D6): zero instances of *we / us / our / young people / this generation / kids /
everyone*. The song has one girl in it and never widens.

**6. Second person swept** (D8): zero instances of *you / your*. Nobody is addressed. Not once.

**7. What the song never says** (D9 / *The Tape That Isn't Good*): the words *beautiful, lovely, special,
real, honest, secretly, actually, better than* appear nowhere. The tape is never complimented, never
rediscovered, never played to anyone who matters. It gets **stored**.

## C. THE THREE COMPOSITE RULES, POINTED AT IN THE DRAFT

**STACK (≥3, last not fixed):**
`I go for the high one. It opens into air.` → `I go for the high one. I come in under.` →
⛔ `I do not go for the high one.`
Three partial versions of one move. The third is the smallest failure and it is **not** a correction —
it is the one where she stops. Nobody lands anything.

**CLOSED GROUND:** last sung line `Played back, I say the same.` = first sung line. The file returns to
its own head.

**ONE RADIANT:** `Kept.` closes V1, V2, Pre-Chorus, V3, Handoff and the Outro as a bare one-word line on
**B♭**; the chorus lands on `I get kept.` **The bridge alone lands on `Ke—`, cut off** — the radiant's
own worse earlier version.

## D. THE TURN, LOCATED

Chorus one is a **comfort**: a warm pocket, a folder nobody deletes. The pre-chorus reveals the same
warmth as a **storage bar** and the same hand as the **thumb**. Chorus four then arrives with the bytes
completely unchanged and the meaning inverted — sung by the degraded double, alone, after the clean voice
has gone. **The song never picks between the threat and the comfort**, because they are the same eight
lines, and the listener chooses four separate times.

## F. ⭐ REVISION LOG — the chorus was rebuilt by MEASUREMENT, not by ear

**This section exists because the draft above failed a floor and the fix could have been the wrong one.**

Run against `scripts/measure_soundcraft.py → strict_end_rhyme()` (the canonical ±4-line, last-3-characters
definition — never re-derived, never eyeballed), the draft measured:

| | draft | floor | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `strict_end_rhyme` | **0.170** | 0.30 | ❌ under |
| `line_return` | 0.364 | 0.20 | ✅ |

**The tempting repair was to put some rhyme back in the verse. That would have defaulted on the debt** —
the zero-rhyme verse *is* the concept, and buying the floor with it would have sold the song to pass a
flag. **The debt is paid where step07 says it is paid: in the chorus.** The chorus was rebuilt so that
its returns actually register:

| chorus line | draft ending | final ending | rhyme key |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | `…I will ever be.` | *unchanged* | `be` |
| 4 | `There is no rest of me.` | **`There is no more of me to be.`** | `be` ✅ pair |
| 6 | `…the folder anymore.` | **`Nobody opens the folder. The light gets lower.`** | `wer` ✅ pairs with `drawer` |
| 8 | *(none)* | **`I don't get finished and I don't get done.`** *(new line)* | `one` ✅ pairs with `gone` |

Three consequences, all of them improvements to the song rather than to the score:

1. **`There is no more of me to be`** is a harder line than *there is no rest of me* — it says she has
   no remaining self left to become, which is the entire horror of the premise stated flatly.
2. **`The light gets lower`** ties the chorus to the one thing that moves in this song. The light crosses
   wall → door → floor across the three verses; now the chorus is on the same clock. The room ages while
   the file cannot.
3. **`I don't get finished and I don't get done`** is the D9 spine restated: she is neither completed
   nor erased.

⭐ **And the structure that fell out of it is the best accident in the package: `kept` is now the ONLY
unrhymed word in the chorus.** Four pairs return — *same/same, be/be, drawer/lower, gone/done* — and the
radiant lands on the one syllable that answers to nothing. **Everything comes back except the landing.**

**Measured after revision:** `strict_end_rhyme` **0.424** ✅ · `line_return` **0.489** ✅ ·
`words_per_line` **7.08** ✅ · `allit_per_100w` **17.67** ✅ · **92 sung lines** · **4761 chars.**
Verse end-rhyme remains **0.00**. The debt was never repaid out of the verse.
