# STEP 07 — SONG GUIDE / STRUCTURE · PAIR 4 · THE DISCARDED VERSION
**Title:** **KEPT, NOT PLAYED** · **82 BPM · E♭ minor · 3:32**

---

## 0. THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS (restated — a guide that cannot answer both is not ready to direct)

**Where is the body standing?** Sitting, not standing: a girl on her own bedroom carpet, back on the bed
frame, knees up, phone face-up beside her knee, amber light on one wall, four takes in. We are inside
the take that will not be used.

**What could hurt it here?** *Storage almost full.* A thumb that is not thinking about anything. The
danger is not a verdict, it is housekeeping — and it stays unresolved for the entire song.

---

## 1. ⭐ THE L21 DEBT AND ITS PAYMENT — stated explicitly, as required

### THE DEBT
> **The verse carries ZERO end-rhyme.** Not a decayed rhyme, not a slant scheme — none. Across all
> thirty-four verse lines there is no rhyme relation at any distance. The verse is the rough take: it is
> unfinished, and **nothing lands.**

Per **L21 THE RETURN**, removal is a debt. Stripping rhyme is only craft if something is named that
returns in its place. Nothing is being subtracted for free here (**D10: substitution, not subtraction**).

### THE PAYMENT — three things return, and the first is the load-bearing one

**1. ⭐ THE BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS. This is the payment.**
The chorus returns **four times, character-for-character identical** — same eight lines, same order,
same punctuation, no mutation, no escalation, no final-chorus variant. Thirty-two of the song's lines
are one returning block. That is the return the verse's rhyme was traded for, and it is a heavier return
than rhyme was: rhyme returns a sound, the chorus returns **the whole object.**

**And it is the concept, not a device applied to the concept.** She is a recording. A recording plays the
same every time. The chorus cannot vary because *she* cannot vary — and that inability is precisely why
she was not the one they kept. **The form is the subject.**

**2. THE VOWEL SPINE (verse-internal, replacing rhyme's sound-return).**
With no end-rhyme, the verse still has to return *sonically* or it is prose. Every verse line is built
on a long-**O**/**OO** spine landing mid-line, never at the end: *r**oo**m · thr**oa**t · g**oe**s ·
**o**pens · d**oo**r · **o**ver · thr**ou**gh · sh**u**t*. The sound comes back; it just never comes back
where a rhyme would tell you it was going to. That is the rough take's exact texture — recognisable and
unfinished at the same time.

**3. THE ANAPHORIC FRAME.** The antiphon runs a repeated syntactic figure — *"I go for the high one…"* /
*"My throat is a room…"* — returning across verses with the predicate changed and the frame intact.

### The measured consequence (checked at step 10, not eyeballed)
Verse rhyme = 0.00 by construction. Whole-lyric `strict_end_rhyme` is carried entirely by the chorus's
internal rhyme (*be / me*, *drawer / anymore*, *same / same*) recurring four times inside the ±4-line
window, plus `line_return` from the 32 repeated lines. **Floors are cleared by the payment, not by
sneaking rhyme back into the verse.** If the numbers came back short, the repair is *more chorus*, never
*a little rhyme in the verse* — that would default on the debt.

⛔ *Nothing further is said about the exact chorus repetition anywhere in this package. It needs no
defence and is not offered one.*

**Total sung lines: 85** (target band 78–110; measured at step 10).

### The antiphon rule (D8, enforced line by line)
Verse lines alternate **(dropped)** / **(kept)**. They are **not in dialogue.** Neither voice ever says
*you*. Neither acknowledges the other. They are two files in one folder, each talking to nobody, and we
overhear both at once. The parallel grammar is what makes it an antiphon rather than an argument —
they are doing the same thing at different volumes of luck.

**The kept take is factual and faintly bored**, the way anything is when it is working. She is not
cruel and does not gloat. Making her cruel would hand the dropped take a grievance, and a grievance is
a future, and the dropped take does not have one.

---

## 4. THE HINGE — register handoff (§9)

At the third reach, the lead simply **is not there for the top note.** The line is completed an octave
down by the phone-speaker double. Nothing crossfades (acoustic grammar 1): the clean lead is a hard
level-state to zero, and the double is already at full. From that bar to the end of the song, **the
clean lead never returns.** The hinge, the third STACK failure, and the duration collapse are all the
same event — one beat carries all three.

---

## 5. DURATION GRAMMAR — a section audibly too short

**Verse 3 is five lines where verses one and two are thirteen.** It does not fade, modulate, or get
covered by production. It **stops**, and the handoff starts before the ear expects it. The wrongness is
the point: a rough take ends when the person in the room stops singing, not when the form is satisfied.

## 6. THE SIX-POINT ACOUSTIC GRAMMAR — mapped

| # | Rule | Realised as |

| 2 | Full level from bar one, no intro | Chorus 1 at 0:00, already mid-loop. |

| 6 | Mono-centre the bass | Mono, dead centre, the closed-ground loop. |

## 8. THE TURN (Golden Move 3) — where the song argues with itself

Chorus one reads as **comfort**: warm pocket, nobody deleting anything, *I get kept.* The pre-chorus
turns it: the same warmth is a **storage bar**, and the same hand is the thumb. By chorus four the
identical words are being sung by the degraded double alone and the comfort has curdled into the threat
without one syllable changing.

⭐ **The bytes did not move. The meaning did.** That is the turn, and it is executed entirely by the
byte-identical chorus arriving in four different rooms. **The song never picks between threat and
comfort**, because the same eight lines are both, and the listener does the choosing four separate times.

---

## 9. THE BOLD CHOICE (one, and it is not a glitch effect)

**The lead vocal is shadowed for the whole song by a phone-speaker-grade double of itself, always a
hair early — and at the final chorus the clean lead drops out and the worse double finishes the record
alone.** The song ends sung by the version nobody chose. ⛔ **This is not a redemption** — she does not
become good, she is simply the only one left playing, which is what storage sounds like.

## 10. DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK

**What this would actually produce:** the first five seconds are a close, dry, unglamorous mezzo already
mid-phrase over a dusty broken kick and a mono bass loop, no intro, no sub — the low end feels *missing*
for forty seconds and then lands under chorus two like a door closing. Verses drop to near-solo voice
with a rattling small-percussion bed and a distant second vocal panned wide and thin. The last minute
loses the clean vocal entirely and finishes narrow, warm and hissy.

**Name the one way this would render generic:** *breathy female vocal over lo-fi pads at 82 BPM* is one
of the most default outputs a generator has — this could easily arrive as background chillhop with a sad
girl on it. **Repair applied once:** the defence is not atmosphere, it is *events* — the missing sub for
forty seconds, the hard un-crossfaded level-states, the wide sandblasted second voice, the too-short
verse, and the clean lead vanishing for the last minute are all things that **happen**, in the form,
where a generator cannot smooth them into a bed (L22 THE GRAIN LAW). The zero-rhyme verse against the
byte-identical chorus is a lyric-side event for the same reason. The mix-side instruction *"the phone-mic
room tone is exposed"* is given as texture, not as a full stop, so it runs with the generator's grain
instead of fighting it.
