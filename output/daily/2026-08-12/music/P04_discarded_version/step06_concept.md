**Arm:** AMBITIOUS · **Axis B:** EXISTENCE · **Sung numeral:** NONE

## 0. THE PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS (asked before a single line was written)

**Where is the body standing?**
A girl is sitting on the carpet of her own bedroom, back against the bed frame, knees up, phone face-up
on the floor beside her knee. Late afternoon, light already going amber on one wall. She has sung the
same eight bars four times into the phone. The take we are inside is not the last one.

**What could hurt it here?**
Not judgement. **Storage.** The thing that ends a dropped take is not a verdict, it is a notification —
*storage almost full* — and a thumb that is not thinking about anything in particular. The dropped take
cannot know whether the thumb is coming. It is warm in a pocket and it is one swipe from nothing, and
that is true *right now*, unresolved, for the whole length of the song. **That is the clean fear.** No
adult arrives to say it will be fine (D1). Nothing resolves.

⚠️ **The daily's comfort gravity was checked and refused.** This is a bedroom, but it is not a kitchen
and there is no cup in it. The warm thing in the song (a pocket, a folder, a hand) is *also* the
mechanism of erasure. The song never separates them.

## 1. THE NAMED EMOTION — coined for this pair, and it does not go in the prompt

> ### **KEPTNESS**
> *The warmth of being retained by somebody who is never coming back to listen.*

Not nostalgia (nostalgia looks back — a recording cannot look anywhere). Not melancholy (too general,
and it implies mood, which a fixed file does not have). Not longing for the good take (that would make
the song competitive, and the dropped take is not in a competition; it lost before the song started).

**Keptness is the specific feeling of being SPARED but not CHOSEN.** It is what a thing feels toward the
hand that did not delete it. It is warm and it is awful and it does not want anything, because wanting
requires a future, and this is the load-bearing horror of the concept:

> **A discarded take saying "this is the worst I will ever be" is literally true and permanently
> unverifiable. She will never be anything again.** She has no rest of herself. The sentence, in her
> mouth, is not a hope and is not a lie — it is a fact she cannot check, said from a floor that is also
> the only floor she has.

The song holds that without resolving it and **without pitying her.** Pity would be an adult in the room.

**Emotion stack (from `EMOTION_TAXONOMY.md`, never bare AWE):** Affection · Solitude · Dread ·
Reflection · Wonder · Numbness · Apprehension · Longing.

## 2. ⛔ D9 IS THE SPINE AND IT KEEPS ITS TEETH

**The tape is not redeemed.** Enumerated so no later step can soften it by accident:

| The lie | Why it is banned here |
|---|---|
| She turns out to be secretly the good one | Destroys the concept. Two takes, one worked, this is not it. |
| Somebody rediscovers her and she gets used | That is a rescue. There is no rescue. |
| The song calls her beautiful | *The Tape That Isn't Good* — the song never makes her beautiful, not once, not in a bridge, not in a production note. |
| She is at peace with it | Peace is resolution. She is not resolved; she is stored. |
| She improves inside the song | She is a fixed length of audio. She cannot. |

**What she gets instead:** she gets **kept**. That is the entire act and the entire grace, and it is
storage, not triumph.

⭐ **The sharpest inversion, and it is the whole architecture:**
**the byte-identical chorus is her INABILITY, not her achievement.** A recording plays the same every
time — that is not fidelity, that is the reason she was not the one they kept. *A person who can change
is worth keeping. A recording that cannot change is worth storing.* The form is the subject and the form
is the failure.

## 3. THE FACT UNDERNEATH — metabolised, never reported

The Perseids are Earth crossing the debris trail of a comet **that is not there.** What lights the sky
is what the comet shed while it was going somewhere else. The most beautiful thing in the sky tonight is
made entirely of dropped material.

**Metabolised as:** *the girl who sang this take is not there.* She is in the next hour, older by an
afternoon, singing the one that worked. What is here is what she shed getting there. **No comet, no
meteor, no sky, no debris appears in the lyric.** The fact is load-bearing and invisible: it supplies
the physics of the song (the source has moved on; the trail is what you can hear) and then it leaves.
EXISTENCE axis, no sung fact, no numeral.

## 4. THE COMPOSITE — the mandatory form rule, landed in the LYRIC (L22: not the spec)

**1. STACK — one figure, ≥3 partial failures of the same move, last NOT fixed.**
**The figure: she goes for the high note.**
- **Reach one (V1):** she goes for it and it opens into air — the note breaks into breath.
- **Reach two (V2):** she goes for it and comes in under — flat, a whole small distance.
- **Reach three (V3):** ⛔ **she does not go for it.** She holds the low one and lets it be that.

The third is the *smallest* failure and it is not a fix — it is the one where she stopped trying, which
is worse and truer than either miss. Accumulation is the event. Nobody lands anything.

**2. CLOSED GROUND — the bottom register loops back to its own start.**
- **Form:** the last sung line of the song is byte-identical to the first sung line of the song. The
  file returns to its own head. That is what a loop *is*, and it is a horizon you can see all the way
  around.
- **Production:** the bass is a two-bar figure that returns to its own first note and never resolves
  outward; mono-centred, no octave escape.

**3. ONE RADIANT — every section converges on one word.**
> ### **`Kept.`**
Every section lands on it, most as a bare one-word line on the same pitch (**B♭ — the fifth**), so all
the failed reaches point at one syllable with a hard T on the end. **The one exception is the bridge**,
which lands on `Ke—`, cut off: *even the radiant has a worse earlier version.*

## 5. FACETS (≥8 substantive, this pair's own)

1. **The singer is the recording, and the recording is of a human.** ⛔ Not an AI, not a model, not a
   draft of a mind. One body, one afternoon, a girl, a phone. The run-specific ban is respected at the
   level of the premise, not by disclaimer.
2. **The phone is written like a hand** (D2). It is warm. It is the palm she is lying in. It is not the
   villain and it is not innocent — it is the thing that holds her *and* the thing with the thumb.
3. **Two-voice antiphon, never dialogue** (D8). The dropped take and the kept take alternate lines.
   Neither says *you*. Neither knows the other exists. We overhear two files talking to nobody in the
   same folder.
4. **The kept take is not smug.** She is factual and slightly bored, the way anything is when it is
   working. Making her cruel would give the song a villain and the dropped take a grievance, and a
   grievance is a future.
5. **Zero rhyme in the verse.** The rough take is unfinished; nothing lands. **Byte-identical chorus**
   as the declared payment (L21 debt named in step07).
6. **The Worse Earlier Version, once:** the bridge quotes the hook with a **placeholder syllable where
   the word had not been written yet** — `this is the worst I will ever — la`. That is what an actual
   rough take sounds like, and it means she sang the sentence before she knew what she would be the
   worst *of*.
7. **The register handoff is the third failed reach.** The lead drops out and the octave-down double
   takes the line alone, because she cannot hold the top. Hinge and STACK land on the same beat.
8. **A section audibly too short:** verse three is cut to a quarter length. She stops. Nothing fills it.
9. **The bold production choice:** the lead vocal is shadowed the whole song by a phone-speaker-grade
   double of itself, always a hair early. At the final chorus **the clean lead drops out and the worse
   double finishes the song alone.** Not a rescue — it is the only one left playing.
10. **Terror-adjacent AWE:** the awe is that four seconds of a person's throat exist somewhere at all;
    the terror is that they exist at the mercy of a storage bar.

## 6. THE MESSAGE — the ruling, applied to this pair

**"This is the worst I will ever be"** sits inside the chorus. In her mouth it is:
- ✅ spoken **from the floor** — she is on a carpet, inside the failure, and cannot leave it;
- ✅ **a decision, not a prediction** — she has no evidence, cannot get any, and says it anyway;
- ✅ said **to nobody** — a file with no playback happening; we are eavesdropping on storage;
- ✅ **a threat and a comfort in the same breath** — *I don't get better and I don't get gone.* The
  comfort is that she is kept; the threat is that she is **permanent**. *There is no version of me you
  get to stop watching.* The song never picks.
- ❌ never reported back from an improved position; ❌ never offered outward as consolation.
