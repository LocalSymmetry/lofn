# STEP 09 — SOUND DESIGN / ARTIST REFINE · PAIR 4 · **KEPT, NOT PLAYED**
**82 BPM · E♭ minor · 3:32 · female mezzo, low and unpolished**

---

## 1. THE SONIC WORLD IN ONE SENTENCE

A phone recording of a girl singing, played back inside the room it was made in, with a chamber consort
sitting quietly behind it that the phone was never good enough to capture properly.

That sentence is the whole production brief. **The trip-hop half is the microphone; the neo-baroque half
is what the microphone missed.** The listener hears a dusty, close, low-fidelity foreground and a viol
and harpsichord architecture behind it that never quite comes into focus — because the take we are
inside was never mixed. Nobody got round to it. That is *why* it is the worse one.

## 3. THE THREE VOICES (and none of them is a character singing to another)

| | Who | Placement | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The dropped take** — the lead, the song's `I` | dead centre, lips on the capsule, dry | no reverb, no tuning, breath left in |
| **B** | **The phone-speaker double** — A, degraded | centre-narrow, ~15 ms **early** | band-limited 400 Hz–4 kHz, hissy, slightly clipped |
| **C** | **The kept take** — every parenthesised line | far left, thin, distant | **"sandblasted"**, band-limited, like a car speaker two streets away |

**B is always a hair EARLY, never late.** A late double reads as an echo — a consequence. An early double
reads as *the version that already existed*, which is what she is. It is the most important 15 ms in the
song.

**C never answers A.** They alternate in the same bars and occupy opposite ends of the stereo field, and
neither is ever mixed as a reply to the other (**D8**). Two files in one folder.

---

## 4. THE HINGE — register handoff, engineered

At the third reach (`I do not go for the high one`), voice **A is cut to zero on a hard level-state — no
crossfade, no tail** (acoustic grammar 1). Voice **B**, already at full, completes the phrase an octave
down. **A never returns.** The final chorus and the outro are B alone.

⛔ **This is not a rescue.** B does not improve, does not swell, does not resolve to a nicer sound. B is
narrow and hissy for the last minute of the record, and the record ends that way. She is not redeemed;
she is simply the only one still playing. *(D9 · The Tape That Isn't Good.)*

---

## 5. THE SIX-POINT ACOUSTIC GRAMMAR — engineered, not described

1. **Hard discrete level-states, no crossfades.** Four named states — **PHONE-CLOSE / ROOM / FULL /
   DOUBLE-ONLY** — and every transition is a hard cut on a downbeat.
2. **Full level from bar one.** The chorus is at 0:00, already mid-loop. No intro, no riser, no count-in
   *(the count-in appears once, deliberately, in the bridge — on the rougher take, where it was never
   edited out)*.
3. ⭐ **One texture named "exposed":** *"acoustic room tone is exposed and kept."* Handed to the generator
   as a texture rather than as a silence, so it can relocate it (L22 THE GRAIN LAW — a spec that runs
   with the grain survives; a long full stop gets smoothed).
4. ⭐ **The sub is withheld from the opening.** No sub-100 Hz content at all in chorus one. It arrives at
   chorus two and **that arrival is the event** — measured at +23.2 dB in the n=147 read.
5. **End WARM, not bright.** Low E♭, no third, tape hiss, a chair, a radiator. No cymbal, no air-band.
6. **Mono-centre the bass.** Two-bar figure, mono, dead centre, returning to its own first note —
   **the CLOSED GROUND, in the low end where it belongs.**

## 6. THE KINETIC DEFECT (required — an asymmetric groove)

**The kick lands a hair late on every second bar.** Not swung, not humanised — *late*, the same amount
every time, so the loop limps in a two-bar period that matches the bass figure. The body reads it as
someone playing along to a recording of themselves and being fractionally behind it. It is the same
15 ms problem as the double, moved into the drums.

## 8. THE BOLD CHOICE — one, and it is not a glitch effect

**The record is finished by the worse version of its own vocal.** The clean lead leaves at 2:38 and does
not come back; the last chorus and the outro are the phone-speaker double alone. No effect processing
announces it — the good voice simply is not there any more, and the song keeps going without it.

*Why this is not "glitch":* nothing is broken, stuttered, bit-crushed or data-moshed. A whole performer
is removed and the arrangement does not compensate. **The device is an absence with a shape.**

## 11. DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (one capped pass)

**What this actually produces:** five seconds in you have a low, close, breathy mezzo already mid-line
over a limping dusty kick and a mono bass loop, with a conspicuous hole where the low end should be. The
verses thin out to near-solo voice with keys rattling on a floor and a thin distant second vocal hard
left. Chorus two drops a sub in and the track suddenly has a floor. The last minute narrows, loses the
good voice entirely, and ends on hiss.

**Name the one way this renders generic:** *breathy female vocal over lo-fi textures at 82 BPM* is close
to a generator's resting state — this could arrive as pleasant background chillhop with a sad girl on it
and nothing would technically be wrong.

**Self-repair, applied once:** the defence is not more atmosphere, it is **more events**. Everything
load-bearing in this song is a thing that *happens* at a timecode where a bed cannot absorb it — the
missing sub for the first fifty-eight seconds, the hard un-crossfaded level-states, the two-bar limp,
the verse that stops early, and a whole vocalist disappearing for the final minute. The lyric carries
its own share: a zero-rhyme verse against a byte-identical chorus is an event in the *writing*, audible
to anyone, and per L22 THE GRAIN LAW that is where a Somatic objection has to be answered — **never in
the production spec alone.**
