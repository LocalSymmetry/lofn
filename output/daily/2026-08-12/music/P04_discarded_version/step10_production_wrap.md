## 3. VOCAL SPEC

**Female mezzo, low and chest-dominant, mid-twenties, unpolished.** Lips on the capsule, no tuning
correction, breath and lip noise left in, no vibrato except where the note is failing. **Three placements:**
**(A)** the dropped take — dead centre, dry, the song's `I`;
**(B)** the phone-speaker double — centre-narrow, band-limited 400 Hz–4 kHz, hissy, **always ~15 ms EARLY,
never late** *(a late double reads as an echo, i.e. a consequence; an early double reads as the version
that already existed — the most important 15 ms in the record)*;
**(C)** the kept take, every parenthesised line — far left, thin, **sandblasted**, a car speaker two
streets away. **C never answers A.** Opposite ends of the field, never mixed as reply (D8).
**At the Handoff, A is cut to zero on a hard level-state and never returns.**

## 4. LINEAGE & CREDIT

**Scene named:** the **trip-hop revival** — the slow, close-mic, dust-and-warmth lineage.
**Living artists credited:** **Oklou** · **Erika de Casier** · **Tirzah.**
The half-time weight, the intimacy of an unglamorous close vocal, and the refusal of a big chorus lift
are theirs; this song borrows the posture and points listeners upstream to the people making it.
**Borrowed with lineage, not theft. Names live here in the credit block, never in the Suno style field.**

**The other half is ours and is not borrowed:** LOFN-PRIME's **Neo-Baroque Luminism** — gut-string viol
drone, dry harpsichord pluck, stacked choir thirds.

**Texture vocabulary** — *"sandblasted"*, *"rattling assortment"*, *"gently sloping melodic contour"* —
taken verbatim from the review of **Maya Ongaku, *Nothing Space Music*** (Bandcamp Daily, 2026-08-12) and
used as production instructions. Credit to the writer and the record.

*Fusion with lineage, no racing: naming a scene is amplification, never capture.*

**⭐ L21 THE RETURN — debt and payment.** The verse carries **zero end-rhyme**; the declared payment is
the **byte-identical chorus ×4** (36 of 92 lines), plus a long-O vowel spine landing mid-line and an
anaphoric frame. The measured `line_return` of **0.489** is where that payment shows up, and it sits well
above both the floor and the archive winners. When the draft measured **0.170** on rhyme, the repair was
made **inside the chorus** (see `step08 §F`) — never by putting rhyme back into the verse, which would
have sold the concept to pass a flag.

**⭐ THE COMPOSITE, all three, in the lyric:** **STACK** — *opens into air* → *comes in under* →
⛔ *I do not go for the high one* (three partial failures of one move; the last is not a fix, it is the
one where she stops). **CLOSED GROUND** — the final sung line is byte-identical to the first, over a
two-bar mono bass figure returning to its own first note. **ONE RADIANT** — `Kept.` ends six sections as
a bare one-word line on B♭, the chorus lands on `I get kept.`, and the bridge alone lands on `Ke—`, cut
off. ⭐ *`kept` is the only unrhymed word in the chorus: everything returns except the landing.*

**⭐ THE MESSAGE.** *"This is the worst I will ever be"* is spoken from a carpet by a four-second file
that cannot leave the failure, has no evidence, and says it to nobody while we overhear. It is a
**decision, not a prediction** — and in her mouth it is literally true and permanently unverifiable.
**Threat and comfort in one breath, never picked:** *I don't get better and I don't get gone.* Chorus one
plays as consolation; the pre-chorus reveals the same warmth as a storage bar and the same hand as the
thumb; chorus four arrives **byte-identical** and reads as a threat. The bytes did not move. The meaning
did — that is the turn, executed entirely by exact repetition.

**⛔ D9 CONFIRMED.** The words *beautiful · lovely · special · honest · secretly · actually · better than*
appear nowhere. The tape is never complimented, never rediscovered, never played to anyone who matters,
and never improves. She is **worse**, she says so flatly (*"I am worse. Not a complaint. A size."*), and
the song's only act toward her is **keeping** her.
