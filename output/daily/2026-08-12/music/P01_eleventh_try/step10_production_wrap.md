## 4. DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (one capped pass, per the step contract)

**What this prompt would actually PRODUCE.** Bar one: a live horn section already at full volume, no fade —
trumpet and baritone in octaves doing a short climbing figure that falls back to where it started, over a
fast dry offbeat guitar chop and a walking acoustic bass. At about 0:06 a flat, close, talky female voice
comes in dead-centre, more spoken than sung, with clear breath. At ~0:22 the low end suddenly has a floor
under it that was not there before, and a shouted room of voices arrives wide left and wide right on three
words. At ~1:52 everything halves into a lazy reggae feel with spring echo for eight lines, then snaps back
on a downbeat. The last forty seconds are the same three words shouted over and over, and then the voice
stops and the horns are still playing.

**⚠️ NAME THE ONE WAY THIS WOULD RENDER GENERIC.** The failure is **"upbeat ska backing track with a clean
pop-female top line"** — a renderer smooths the sing-speak into a tuneful pop vocal, mixes the gang chant as
a polite backing "oh-oh," and treats the hi-hat instruction as noise to be cleaned up. That would be a
Kidz-Bop ska song and the personality would be gone in four bars.

**SELF-REPAIR APPLIED (once):**
1. `never sung-pretty`, `sing-speak`, `dry, talky, flat`, `audible breath` all pushed into the **first
   vocal clause** of the style field rather than the tail, so they are read as the vocal's definition and
   not as a modifier.
2. The exclude field names the five smoothing outcomes **individually** — `crystalline soprano`,
   `warm alto`, `choir`, `vibrato`, `melisma`, `power belt`, `autotune`, `transparent pitch correction` —
   because Suno applies exclusions as negative tokens and one word ("unpolished") does not.
3. The gang chant is specified as `whole-room adult gang chant … unpitched and shouted` and placed at
   **hard left and hard right**, so it cannot be read as a centred backing harmony.
4. The tiring hat is stated as a **groove instruction with the kick pinned** (*"the hi-hat tires and drags
   late while the kick stays exactly on"*) rather than as an effect, because THE GRAIN LAW says a spec that
   fights the generator gets smoothed — a lopsided groove between two named elements runs *with* it.

**Diff against the Golden Seed and the GOLDEN MOVE:** stands somewhere real ✅ (a named car park deck, with
what the senses register) · zero sung facts, and the assigned zero numerals holds ✅ · the turn happens at
the half-time break and it is not a resolution ✅ · the register is rotated hard off the house winner —
no soprano, no A major, no frost palette; this is a 168 BPM brass record ✅ · the subject is plainly named in
the first four lines ✅.
