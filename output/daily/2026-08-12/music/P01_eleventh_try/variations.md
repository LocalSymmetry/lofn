**The one device (all four):** ⭐ **"THE CHANT COUNTS UP."** — the gang chant is the tally; no number is
ever sung, and each return of the chant is one more take.

⛔ **No global template was used.** All four angles are derived from this pair's own concept — *one body,

chant, stack figure, and pursuer are all different.

⭐ **D2 at maximum strength: the phone is not merely "not the villain," it is a limb that hurts.**
**RADIANT:** *STILL* — motionless and continuing at once, and the surface reading is only the first one.

## STYLE
<!-- MEASURE: V2_STYLE -->
```text
Two-tone rocksteady, 138 BPM at half-time skank, at full height when it starts; nothing ramps up. Opening bars: a lazy trombone-led figure, four notes falling home on a gently sloping melodic contour over a muted offbeat chop, sandblasted. chant, so the brass itself is a return event rather than a bed.
```

# ★ V4 — **NOTHING FIXED YET**
**172 BPM · B Dorian · two-tone skank into a dub tail · ~3:00**
**ANGLE:** ⭐ **the minute itself.** This variation sits *on* the hinge for its whole length — the exact
transition where embarrassment stops and it becomes a shift. Nothing before it, nothing after it.
**RADIANT:** *YET* — present-tense-safe by construction: it claims the clock is still running, never that
she knows how it ends. **THE GEAR-CHANGE SHOWCASE:** skank verse → full horn-punk chorus → **dub outro.**
**THE PURSUER IS DIFFERENT HERE:** a subtle polymetric overlap of an ascending synth, in five against the
skank's four, rising the whole track and never resolving. ⛔ Never named in the lyric.
