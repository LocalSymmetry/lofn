Female, **low alto**, dry and close, **mono centre**. Deadpan and near-muttering through the verses — bratty by *flatness*, not by volume. ⛔ Not the house crystalline soprano (Golden Move 5: register rotated). Every parenthetical echo is a **pitch-shifted lower double of the same voice — an octave down, flat, drier, no reverb, set behind the lead.** ⛔ Not a harmony, not a backing stack: it must sound like the voice degrading. One compressed shout in the whole song, on the bridge's last line. The return after the false outro is **warm and almost kind**, which is the most frightening reading available.
