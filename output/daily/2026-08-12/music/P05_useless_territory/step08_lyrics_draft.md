## DRAFT NOTES

### Why the last line is the first line
The closed ground closes in the lyric as well as the bass. After five stanzas of rot, *"and I take the flight a little cleaner"* returns unaltered — and it is the worst line in the song, because nothing in it has changed and everything around it has. ⛔ **The last attempt is not fixed.** It is not even different.

The line begins with **"and"**. ⭐ The loop never opens and never closes. That is the objection, in grammar.

### The bridge is the only place she raises her voice
Everywhere else she is flat, near-muttering — the Bratty Genius describing her own hands. The single compressed shout lands on *"It works the same in every direction"*, and then she goes straight back to climbing. ⛔ **The accusation is never withdrawn and never answered.**

### The two "somebody" lines are the seam
*"Somebody told me that helps"* (the technique) and *"Somebody put that sentence in my mouth"* (the slogan) are deliberately built to rhyme structurally. **They are the same object: an instruction from an absent authority, obeyed with increasing precision, never verified.** She is excellent at both. That is the indignation, and its target is the sentence — ⛔ never the audience.

### D5 — where the doubt lives in the present
> *I cannot tell the landings apart. / (tell them apart) / (apart) / And I am going up faster and faster.*

⭐ No *what if this was wasted*, no *someday*, no looking back. **The not-knowing and the improving are simultaneous and both are happening at this second.** The dread is a fact about her instruments, not a forecast about her life.

### D2 — the phone, written as a hand
One beat only: *"My thumb walks back to the picture of the number."* The thumb acts on habit; the phone is not blamed, not moralised, not a villain. **It is a hand that already knows where it is going** — which is exactly the song's problem, and the phone has nothing to do with it.

### What is NOT here
⛔ No hopeful turn. ⛔ No wink. ⛔ No line where she works out that the climb was worth it. ⛔ No line where she works out that it wasn't. **She is still going, and she cannot tell whether that is a virtue or a symptom.**
