cut and the hardest bed in the set.

**Bold choice:** ⭐ **the chorus is spoken flat, not sung** — the hook sits exactly where the melody
should be and refuses to be one. A spoken hook over the biggest drop in the six-song set is the
single riskiest thing in this package, and it is the right risk: **the sentence is a decision, and
decisions are not sung.**

⭐ **The posture, made explicit and left unresolved:** *"That is the comfort. That is the threat."*
The song never picks, and the bridge says so out loud without ever addressing anybody.
