## 2. ⭐ THE STACK — one figure, FOUR recurrences, the last NOT fixed

**The figure: STEP FOUR — "check that it took."** Each occurrence is a failed or partial version of
the same move. Accumulation is the event; correction never happens.

| Pass | Where | What happens | Why it is a failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Verse 1 | She runs step four. **It passes.** | The check is not a check. A pass here means nothing — and she does not know that yet. |
| **2** | Verse 2 | She runs step four. **It passes again.** | Identical result, identical worthlessness. **The repetition is the horror**, not the variation. |
| **3** | ⭐ The Interruption | She reaches step four and **cannot finish saying it.** *Sixteen years* comes out where the step should be. | **Partial** — the move breaks mid-execution. The list's own arithmetic stops working. |
| **4** | Verse 3 | She reads step four **out loud and does not do it.** Pins the sheet back up, still wrong. | ⛔ **NOT FIXED.** No fresh bath, no step five, no reprint. The last one stays broken and goes back on the line. |

> **A single attempt looks like a mistake. A hundred stacked look like a sky.**
> The lyric makes this literal: the whole drying line of fading prints, seen at once, **reads as
> weather** — a hundred greys that are individually a ruined photograph and collectively an
> overcast afternoon. She notices this and does not take it as consolation.

**D9 keeps its teeth:** the tape is not redeemed. **The keeping is the act.**

## 3. ⭐ CLOSED GROUND — the bottom register loops back to its own start

> **A four-bar descending tetrachord in D minor: `D → C → B♭ → A → (D)`.**
> The lament bass. It is in **every section**, it **never modulates**, and it returns to its own
> first note forever — a horizon you can see all the way around.

Three reasons it is the correct ground and not a decoration:

1. **It is literally a closed loop.** A passacaglia ground is the oldest closed circle in Western
   music; the "little planet" horizon of the source frame, in the bass clef.
2. **It carries the bold choice.** The verse sits on **one unchanging note (A)** while this walks
   underneath — consonant, sweet, **wrong**, home — four times a loop. *The note was always this
   note. The ground moved.*
3. **It is a lament, and this is not a lament.** Putting a relief on top of the most recognisable
   grief-bass in music, and refusing to resolve the joke, is the INDIGNATION posture in one gesture.

**The mouth-level ground:** the radiant vowel family **/aʊn/** — *down · ground · count · sound ·
around* — is the same closed loop in the singer's mouth. **The ground bass and the rhyme scheme are
the same object.**

## 5. ⭐ THE HINGE — THE INTERRUPTION (section 6, ≈2:05)

The list has run cleanly twice. It starts a third time. It gets to three. And then:

```
Step one. Tape the corner down.
Step two. Tip the tray and count.
Step three. Pull it through the clear.
Step four —
[the count breaks]
sixteen years.
```

**Four simultaneous events on that downbeat, all hard-cut, none crossfaded:**

1. ⭐ **THE SUNG NUMERAL LANDS** — *"sixteen years"*, spelled in words, **exactly once in the song.**
   The lyric **responds** to it and never recites it: the next line is not *"it has been broken for
   sixteen years"* but ***"say it slower and it gets lighter."***
2. ⭐ **THE SUB ARRIVES** — 30–60 Hz Somatic Bass, mono-centred, **withheld from the entire first
   half**, entering here as the event (acoustic grammar §4). The room gets a bottom at the exact
   moment the fault leaves her body. **The physical relief is the sub, not a lyric about relief.**
3. **BIT-DEPTH COLLAPSE** — hi-fi → 2-bit for one bar, then out. ⭐ **Inverted flair:** the collapse
   is normally a loss of certainty; here what comes out the other side is **warmer and simpler than
   what went in.** The certainty being lost was a *false* certainty, so its collapse is the relief.
4. **THE LIST'S ARITHMETIC FAILS** — it can count to three and then it cannot produce four. The one
   number the song is allowed to sing arrives **in the hole where a step should be.**

That is why the hinge is the interruption and not a chorus: **the flat voice is broken into by its
own subject matter**, which is the only thing in the song with enough force to break it.
