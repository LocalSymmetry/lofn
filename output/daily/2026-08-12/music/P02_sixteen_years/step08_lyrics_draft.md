## DRAFT NOTES *(not part of the lyrics field)*

### Where the gates land
| Requirement | Where it lives in the lyric |
|---|---|
| **Sung numeral, exactly one, in words** | *"Sixteen years."* — one utterance, at the hinge, in the hole where step four should be. |
| **Responds, never recites** | The following line is ***"Say it slower. It gets lighter."*** — a response to the duration, not a report of it. |
| **The stack, 4 passes, last not fixed** | Verse 1 · Verse 2 · The Interruption (breaks) · Verse 3 (**"Kept it. Read it. Didn't do it."**) |
| **Closed ground, in the mouth** | The /aʊn/ family closes every section: *down · ground · count · around · cloud.* |
| **One radiant** | **"down"** — the last word of all four choruses, the last word of Verse 3, and the last word of the song. |
| **Consonant hammer at line-heads** | *St- · T- · P- · C- · D- · K- · G-* open ~85% of lines. The **one** line with no hammer is *"and my hands come down"* — the hammer stops exactly where she lets go. |
| **Exact chorus repetition** | Four byte-identical choruses. |
| **The message** | Bridge, lines 7–8. A decision, not a prediction; no evidence; said to nobody. |

### Line-level decisions worth defending
- **"Doing this since I was small" was cut.** Past tense (**D5**). Replaced with **"Hands know the
  whole of it. All of it."** — present, and better: the body performs the procedure without her,
  which is what sixteen years of a habit actually feels like from inside.
- **"Hundred sheets" was cut.** *"A hundred"* is P6's assigned numeral and this pair may sing no
  second number. Replaced with **"Sheet on sheet"** / **"Grey on grey"** — accumulation rendered as
  doubling rather than counting, which is more this pair's grammar anyway.
- **"— Check that it took"** opens on an em-dash in every chorus. We arrive **mid-phrase**: the tape
  was never off.
- **"The bath is dead and the bath is honest. / It is not lying. It cannot lie."** — this is the
  **D2** compliance load-bearer. The machinery is a fellow victim; it reported success in good
  faith. There is no villain in this song, which is why the indignation has to go somewhere else.
- **"Nobody is here. I'm saying it anyway."** — **D8** made structural rather than merely obeyed.
- **"Leave it wrong. Leave it up."** — **D9**. She does not reprint, remix, correct, or redeem.

### Cohort / address sweep
Zero instances of *we · us · our · you · your · kids · young · everyone · this generation ·
put the phone down · look up · touch grass · doomscroll · screen time · brain rot.*
The only second person in the song is the **list**, and the list is wrong.
