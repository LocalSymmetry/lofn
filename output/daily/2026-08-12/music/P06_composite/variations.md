## 4. WHY THIS ONE IS ITS OWN SONG
⭐ **The radiant in the source image is not an object — it is a perspective effect.** The meteors are
parallel; they only *look* like they converge. So this song's hinge is: *"Pointed at a patch of paint. /
Nothing up there but the stain. / Nothing at the end of it. / Point at it anyway."* The target is empty and
**the aiming is what makes the shape.** That is Sunna's standing argument stated as a physical fact rather
than an opinion — *you're looking at what it isn't.*

that literal and physical: a stairwell landing **is** a floor, she is standing on it, and she is not
climbing to anything. ⛔ **Nothing resolves.** She is inside the loop when she says it, she has no evidence,
and the concrete under her is the only argument she has.
