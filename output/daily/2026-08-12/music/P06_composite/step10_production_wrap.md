build this.

**REPAIR (applied once):** style field specifies *identical second take, panned narrow, both cracking*;
EXCLUDE names *choir, polished stacked vocals, pitch-corrected harmony, stereo widening on the vocal stack,
multiple different singers*; and the lyric says ***"a hundred of me"*** so the semantics fight the default
even if the style tokens slip. ✅ No second repair needed.

## 11. ⭐ QA REPAIR 1 — SOURCE-2 INTO THE GENERATOR-FACING FIELD *(step 09 → step 10, prompt-only)*

**The finding, and it was correct.** The Source-2 vocabulary was present in `step06`, `step07`, `step09` and
this file — and **nowhere in the MUSIC PROMPT**, the only field a renderer reads. Measured: **0 verbatim
phrases in all four prompts**, against a floor of ≥2. I had *paraphrased* every one of them into the prompt
("a three-note guitar figure sloping down" for *gently sloping melodic contour*; "an ascending synth runs a
five-beat cycle" for *subtle polymetric overlap of the ascending synth*; "acoustic handclaps, tambourine,
shaker, one dry vibraslap" for *rattling assortment*) and never noticed, because my own step-09 self-check
counted the phrases **in the design document** rather than in the shipped prompt.

⭐ **This is L22 THE GRAIN LAW exactly, and it is the failure the law was written for:** a texture answered
in the production document is **not answered.** Four songs specified as sandblasted, rattling and polymetric,
and the generator would never have been told a word of it.

**Repair applied:** ≥2 verbatim phrases folded into every MUSIC PROMPT as literal production instructions;
redundant connective clauses trimmed to hold the char band. ⛔ **No lyric was touched** — sung-line counts,
rhyme and return are unchanged from the measured originals.

**Second-order effect, caught and fixed:** folding identical phrases into all four prompts pushed **v1↔v4
prompt similarity to 0.669**, over the 0.58 ceiling. V4's connective language was rewritten (exposed
*footfalls* rather than exposed handclap; "kick drags, bass sits still"; "shuts on its own first note"),
bringing the peak to **0.376** — lower than the 0.523 it measured before the repair.

**The lesson worth keeping:** *count the gate on the shipped artifact, not on the document that describes it.*
My step-09 table said "4 landed" and was honest about the design and wrong about the render.
