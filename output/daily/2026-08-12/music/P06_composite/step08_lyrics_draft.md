# STEP 08 — LYRICS DRAFT · PAIR 6 · **TAKE A HUNDRED**

---

## A. THE ENGINE, IN ONE LINE

> **Went for the top. Missed it. KEEP.** — repeated fifteen times, failing a different way each time,
> ⛔ **including the last time**, with the rhyme landing early (stress 3) and the radiant as the tail.

---

## B. THE REBOUND MECHANIC, SHOWN

```
Went for the top.  Missed it.  Keep.
  1        2         3(rhyme)   tail = radiant
Went for it flat.  Kissed it.  Keep.
```

The rhyme arrives **a beat before the line should end**, then the line falls past it onto *keep*. The line
**trips forward** — which is what an overshoot feels like. In Verse 3 six of these chain in a row and the
hits pile off the beat. **Accumulating hits landing off the expected beat.** ✅ Assigned sound axis.

## D. WHY EACH DECISION

**⭐ Verse 3's rhyme chain — *flattened it · sharpened it · back of it · crack of it · half of it · laughed
at it*.** Six rebounds in a row, all landing early. This is the assigned sound axis at full extension and it
is also the **stack**: the rhymes accumulate exactly the way the takes do. Then *"Whispered it. Lost it."*
breaks the chain — and the eighth line returns to the **exact failure of line one of the song.**
⛔ **The last one is not fixed.**

**⭐ The hinge does the whole idea in four plain lines.** *"One at a time it's a mistake. / All at once it's
a sky."* Present tense. No metaphor to decode — she is describing what is on the screen. It reports; it does
not interpret. **THE FLAT TEST:** say it to a face with no music. It survives.

**⭐ *"A hundred of me"* is the D6 defence written into the lyric.** My pair is the most exposed to the cohort
gate. The line forecloses it at the exact moment the number lands: **a hundred takes, one body.** The number
is then *responded to* for six lines and never said again.

**⭐ *"Saying it flat to a fire door."*** D8 made literal and slightly funny. She is not addressing anyone.
There is a door. We are overhearing a person in a stairwell, and the joke is that she knows it.

**⭐ The chorus hands the room the low ground.** *"Stack it, keep it"* — two words, eight syllables, every
interval inside a fourth. The note she keeps failing is an octave above it. **The room holds the floor while
she is the streaks.** Nobody has to notice that for it to work.

**⭐ *"There's no last one, so there's no last me."*** The posture. Comfort: *this one doesn't have to count.*
Threat: *there is no version of me you get to stop watching.* The song never picks, and because it is about
a last **take** it never turns into an aphorism.

**⭐ The last line is the radiant, three times, then the riff takes it out.** *I keep it. I keep it. I keep.*
The hook exists after the last word (HUM TEST enforcement clause), and the four-bar bass loop closes on the
note it opened on. **The ground is closed.**
