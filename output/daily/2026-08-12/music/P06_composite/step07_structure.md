# STEP 07 — SONG GUIDE & STRUCTURE · PAIR 6 · **THE COMPOSITE**
**Working title:** ⭐ **TAKE A HUNDRED**

---

## THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS *(answered concretely or the guide is not ready to direct)*

**1. WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?**
Third landing of a concrete stairwell. Fire door propped with a shoe. Phone wedged in the handrail at chest
height, screen on, warm. She is standing because sitting makes the note worse. The reverb is free and it is
the only reason she is here rather than anywhere else.

**2. WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?**
Her throat. It is the instrument and she is spending it out of one account — every take is paid for and the
balance is audible. Also the building: it is going to wake up, and the stairwell is going to stop being hers.
Neither of these is ever said out loud. ⭐ **The first one is the wolf** — the vocal gets more *sandblasted*
across the song and never recovers.

## 1. TITLE — **TAKE A HUNDRED**

Reads on the surface as a slate: *take one hundred.* Reads underneath as an offer: *have a hundred of them.*
⛔ The second reading is **never explained, never winked at.** A listener who takes only the surface receives
a whole song. *(THE FREE CHANNEL, rule 3 — the deep reference stays subversive.)*

## 3. ⭐ THE SIX LEVEL-STATES — hard discrete, **no crossfades**

| State | Contents |
|---|---|
| **A — RIFF** | break at full, sloping guitar figure, **exposed handclap**, no sub, no synth |
| **B — VERSE** | break thins to the amen ride; *rattling assortment* of tambourine, shaker, one dry vibraslap; riff continues; still no sub until §5 |
| **C — CHORUS** | everything in, handclaps doubled, stacked voices, ascending synth begins its five-cycle |
| **D — HINGE** | ⭐ **sub arrives**, mono-centred; counter-melody enters; vocal doubles |
| **E — ENFORCED SECOND** | ⛔ **everything muted except one clap and one word.** One beat long. Hard cut in, hard cut out. |
| **F — WARM CLOSE** | one voice, riff, tape saturation, ascending synth **gone.** Ends warm, never bright. |

⛔ Every transition is an instant switch. No risers, no white-noise sweeps, no filter fades into a drop.

## 4. ⭐ THE STACK — the figure, and its eight failures *(none corrected)*

**THE FIGURE:** *she goes for the top of the word and misses.* It recurs **fourteen times** across the song.
Every recurrence fails a **different** way — and ⛔ **the last one fails too.**

| # | Section | How it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | V1 | missed it (over) |
| 2 | V1 | kissed it (under) |
| 3 | V2 | split it |
| 4 | V2 | hit it — wrong note |
| 5 | V2 | came in early |
| 6 | V2 | ran the air out |
| 7–14 | V3 (eight) | flat · sharp · late · cracked · swallowed · shouted · whispered · **cracked again** |
| **15** | **LAST TAKE** | ⛔ **cracks.** Same as take one. Nothing has been fixed. Accumulation was the event. |

## 5. ⭐ THE RADIANT — **"KEEP"**

Every section converges on it. It is the tail of every verse line, the last word of the chorus, the single
word of THE ENFORCED SECOND, and the last word of the song before the riff takes it out.

**Why this word and no other:** it is one syllable with a hard consonant at both ends, reachable at volume by
a normal throat while out of breath *(S.O.L.A.R. — Load the Floor)*, and it is **D9 compressed to four
letters**: *the tape is not redeemed — the keeping is the act.* A hundred meteors point at one spot. A hundred
misses point at **keep.**

---

## 6. HOOK DESIGN — *the room owns it by the second chorus*

> **"Stack it, keep it, stack it, keep it."**

Eight syllables. **Two words.** Every interval inside a fourth. Every vowel open. Consonants on the beat so a
room out of breath can still land it. ⭐ **The chant sits LOW** — deliberately, because the note she keeps
failing is **high**. The room holds the ground; she is the streaks above it. *(That division is the composite
too, and nobody has to notice it for it to work.)*

⛔ The hook does not flatter the singer. It hands the room a thing to do with its mouth.

---

## 7. THE HUM-TEST FIGURE — must survive the vocal being muted

Three-note descending guitar figure — *"gently sloping melodic contour"* — over a bass that plays only the
first two notes and **drops the third.** The hole in the riff is where the crack goes.
✅ Present in bar 0 before any word. ✅ Present in bar N after the last word. ✅ Recognisable alone.
⛔ Not a pad. Not a texture. A tune.

**Harmony as instrument:** stacked thirds on the chant · a counter-melody that **argues** (it climbs to the
note she is failing, sung by the lag voice, who lands it — and she still doesn't) · one hard split where two
voices separate and the gap is audible (Chorus 2, last line).

---

## 8. THE WOLF *(one automation lane, never resolves, never mentioned in the lyric)*

⭐ **The throat.** Vocal chain drifts *sandblasted* across the whole track — progressively drier, grittier,
more air, more edge — and **never recovers.** In parallel: the ascending synth's filter only ever opens.
Neither returns to where it started. ⛔ The lyric never refers to her voice going.

## 10. TECHNICAL

**BPM 172** · **G major** · vocal in half-time against the break.
*(Declared departure from Sunna's 124–132 default: the assigned bed is jungle-based. Her file: "whatever the
gear-change wants." Chosen, not drifted into. ⛔ Not A major — the house-winner fingerprint is avoided.)*
**Chant range:** G3–D4, shoutable. **The reach that cracks:** G5, the octave, hit from below, never landed.
**Bass:** mono-centred, four-bar loop closing on its own first note.
**Master:** −14 LUFS integrated, ≥10 dB crest. Weight, not loudness.

---

## 11. WHAT THIS SONG REFUSES

- ⛔ **No fix.** No final take that lands. Any version where she gets it is a different song and a worse one.
- ⛔ **No choir.** A hundred of *her*, never a hundred people. *(D6 + Sunna's no-choir rule, one instruction.)*
- ⛔ **No lesson.** Nobody is told anything. She is alone in a stairwell and we are overhearing. *(D8)*
- ⛔ **No explanation of the composite.** The song does it. It does not describe it.
- ⛔ **No reverence.** 172 BPM, handclaps, and a dry eye-roll. Unembarrassed delight, not a hymn.
