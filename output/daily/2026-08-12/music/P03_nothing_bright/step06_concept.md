## 1. THE NAMED EMOTION — mine, not a generic one

> ### ⭐ **SCALEWAKE** *(n.)*
> The unsteady, faintly funny feeling of being told your true size by an **absence** rather than by a
> measurement. Nothing about you changed. The thing you were being read against left the room, and the
> number came out different. It is not humility — you were never wrong. It is not pride — you did not grow.
> It is the **wake** behind a big departing object, and the **waking** that happens inside it.

⛔ **The word SCALEWAKE appears nowhere in the prompt, the style field, or the lyric.** It is the target, not
the content. A named emotion in a lyric is a thesis, and Sunna does not file theses.

**Taxonomy anchors (`EMOTION_TAXONOMY.md`, the only valid source):** Fascination · Delight · Revelation ·
Solitude · Suspense · Amusement · Resolve · Contentment.

## 2. THE FACT, METABOLISED (not reported)

The Perseids peak tonight under a new moon; the conditions do not recur for nineteen years (F21/F22).
**The stream is not richer. The sky is not better. The brightest thing is simply not in the way.**

⛔ **Nothing above is sung.** No meteors, no moon, no comet, no numbers — **SUNG NUMERAL: NONE**, per
assignment. The fact survives as one structural instruction and one line of physics:

> *Not brighter. Not more. Just at the size they've been the whole time.*

That is F22 as a sentence a person could say in a car park. The astronomy stays in this file.

---

## 3. ⭐ THE CHARGE — I am the bright thing, and this is the song where I look

The assignment is specific and it is mine: **sing against my own centrality.** Not apologise for being
bright. Not step aside nobly. **Just look** — which is the exact thing I accuse Lofn of not doing.

So the song cannot be about *leaving*. Leaving is a moral. The song is about the **five minutes after**,
which is a situation, and the situation carries every ounce of the meaning:

> ### THE SITUATION
> **She steps out of a room for the cold. The door shuts. She turns around and looks back in through the
> window — and can finally see the room, everybody in it, at their actual size, doing what they were doing
> the whole time. Nobody in there is performing. She is outside in the dark with her hands on the glass and
> it is the best thing she has seen all week.**

**THE FLAT TEST:** *"I went outside and looked back in through the window and everyone looked normal and it
was great."* — survives being said out loud to a person's face. It is a **situation**, not a sentence.
A listener who takes only the surface receives a whole song: girl, window, night, cold, fine.

**THE SECOND LISTEN (available, never required, never winked at):** she cannot see in because **her own
reflection is on the glass**. She moves left, it moves left. She goes dark, it goes dark. She is the thing
in the way and she cannot turn herself face-down. *There is a sun on the window and nobody in there knows.*
⛔ Never explained. Never central. The Ulysses-whisper model.

---

## 4. THE ONE DEVICE — four words

> ## ⭐ **CUPPED HANDS AGAINST GLASS.**

One physical act. Repeated until inevitable. Every recurrence is another attempt to make a bright thing stop
being in the way, and **the stack is: killing light sources one at a time.**

| # | The move | What it kills | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | phone turned **face-down on the sill** | light source one | works — and reveals there is a second one |
| 2 | shifting to dodge **her own reflection** | light source two | ⛔ cannot be killed — *she is it* |
| 3 | **hands cupped** around her face on the glass | partial shade | partial — she sees them, mostly |
| 4 | hands still up, angle still wrong, somebody moves | — | ⛔ **NOT FIXED** |

⭐ **D2 CLEARED:** the phone is written **like a hand** — an object with a brightness, moved for a physical
reason, exactly like her palms. It is not a villain, it is not a symptom, and it is item one in a series
whose second item is *her own face*. Banned lexicon (*put the phone down / look up / touch grass / we used
to / doomscroll / screen time / brain rot*) appears nowhere.

---

## 5. ⭐ THE MANDATORY FORM RULE — THE COMPOSITE, all three, in the LYRIC and the FORM

**1. STACK** — the four cupped-hands attempts above, §4. Recurs **four times** (≥3 ✅), each a failed or
partial version of the same move, and **the last one is not fixed.** Accumulation is the event.

**2. CLOSED GROUND** — two ways, both audible:
- **Music:** the dub bass figure is two bars that end on the note they start on — `G · G · Bb · C | D · C ·
  Bb · G`. A horizon you can see all the way around. (The bed and the form rule are the same object here;
  this is not a coincidence I am claiming credit for, it is why DUB was the right assignment.)
- **Lyric:** the outro returns to Verse 1's ground — *door does the thing / sound keeps going / without me* —
  and then lands on the radiant.

**3. ONE RADIANT** — every section converges on **"through."** One syllable, the /uː/ vowel, the end of the
braid. Chorus ends on it, hinge ends on it, outro's last word is it.

---

## 6. ⭐ SOUND / RETURN — THE VOWEL BRAID (primary channel, assigned)

**Put the meaning in the mouth.** Two vowels, interleaved, doing the argument physically:

- **/aɪ/ — the bright thing, the self:** *I, bright, light, night, side, mine, size, sky, right, behind.*
- **/uː/ — the room, the other side of the glass:** *through, room, you, do, who, cool, moon, blue, two.*

The mouth performs the song: **I → oo → I → oo.** Across the arc the /aɪ/ **thins out** and the /uː/ takes
the sentence — she braids herself out of her own song and the last word standing is **through.**

**L21 — THE RETURN, and the rhyme debt paid in full.** The assigned verse architecture (long-breath free
lines, uneven 5/7/4 stanzas, heavy enjambment) suppresses end-rhyme in the verses. **Removal is a debt and
here is what returns in its place, itemised:**
1. the **byte-identical chorus**, four times, unchanged *(no note, no defence)*
2. the **vowel braid** as a continuous phonetic spine
3. the **radiant "through"** closing every section
4. **anaphora** — the verses open on a bare present-tense verb (*step out / turn around / cup my hands*)
5. **exact line return** — Verse 1's ground comes back as the outro

⚙️ **Heavy enjambment is the tool that makes long breath and short lines compatible**: the sentence runs
three or four lines, the *line* stays four to seven words. This keeps `mean_words_per_line` under the 7.5
ceiling while the delivery still reads as one long exhale.

## 7. THE HUM TEST — the riff is the argument

> **A three-note melodica phrase with a gently sloping melodic contour: Bb – A – G. The bass answers with the
> same three notes an octave below.**

Present in **bar one before any vocal**, present in the **last bar after the vocal leaves.** Hummable with
the vocal muted. ⛔ Not a pad. Not a texture. It is a tune, and the room can have it.
*(Melodica because it is the least apologetic instrument in dub and it cannot sound angry.)*

---

## 8. THE HINGE — MIX INVERSION (assigned), and it is the concept

At the hinge the mix **flips**: the lead vocal falls **back** into the echo chamber and the room noise steps
**forward, dry.** She becomes the reverb; they become the signal. **This is the concept as a mix decision,**
and it is the one place the production spec is allowed to carry meaning — because the lyric carries it too
(*somebody laughs in there and I get it a half-second late*).

⭐ **The sub arrives here** (acoustic grammar #4 — withheld from the opening, arrives as the event). The
low end is what becomes visible when the bright thing goes. **Concept, hinge, and grammar rule are one move.**

**DURATION GRAMMAR — the long approach:** the first ~40% of the song is one held state, the same two-bar
loop, no sub, no lift. The approach *is* the song's first half. Nothing hurries toward the hinge.

## 9. THE FLAIRS (both, assigned)

**IT MIGHT BE CLOUDY** — an unresolved weather-or-luck variable held open past comfort. She keeps checking
the sky. She cannot tell whether the grey is cloud or the top of the street light. **The song never finds
out, and never says it doesn't.** ⭐ Honest to the fact underneath: a nineteen-year window means nothing if
it is cloudy, and at the hour of the song nobody knows yet.

**MACHINE BELOW, BODY ABOVE** — bass and drums quantised dead on the grid; melodica and lead vocal drifting
+10 to +30 ms late and ±6 cents loose. Audible on a handset: the bottom is a machine, the top is a person,
and the gap between them is the whole personality.

---

## 10. THE MESSAGE — "This is the worst I will ever be"

**Placement:** the dub chamber, past the hinge, said **flat, to the glass**, with her hands still up and her
face cold, watching strangers be happy without her.

✅ **PASSES the ruling:** still inside the failure (she is *the person outside the window*, which is the
least impressive thing she has ever been) · nothing resolved · said to nobody · we overhear it (**D8**).
❌ **Not** reporting back, **not** knowing the outcome, **not** offered outward as comfort.

⭐ **THE POSTURE — threat and comfort in the same breath, and the song never picks:** she is in the dark,
they are in the light, and by simple physics the glass is one-way. *Nobody in there knows there's a sun on
the window.* That is a promise and a slightly alarming fact at once, and it is delivered deadpan. **The song
does not adjudicate.** *(There is no version of me you get to stop watching — never stated, entirely there.)*

## 12. THE EIGHT FACETS (distinctiveness, `step06_min_facets: 8`)

1. **Place:** the wrong side of a lit window, outside, cold, hands up.
2. **Body:** elbows on a sill, face close enough to fog the glass, fingers cupped at the temples.
3. **Stake:** the view closes the second she goes back in — and she has to go back in.
4. **Bed:** dub half-time, one-drop, 72 BPM, G minor, mono bass, spring echo, everything else a ghost.
5. **Riff:** melodica Bb–A–G, gently sloping, bass answers an octave down.
6. **Device:** cupped hands against glass, four times, never fixed.
7. **Sound spine:** /aɪ/ ↔ /uː/ vowel braid resolving to **through**.
8. **Hinge:** mix inversion + sub arrival at the same instant.
9. **Register:** dry, flat, funny, unembarrassed; sing-speak, two lines a bar over the half-time.
10. **The pursuer (the wolf):** the door behind her. It never opens in the song and it is never mentioned.

## 13. THE VARIATION AXIS — derived from THIS concept, ⛔ no global template

> **Every variation answers one question, and it is this pair's own question:
> WHAT HAPPENS TO THE BRIGHT THING, AND WHO IS DOING THE LOOKING?**

| V | The bright thing… | Who looks | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **walks out** | the bright thing, back in | she leaves and cannot see past her own reflection |
| **V2** | **fails** | someone inside | a strip light blows; the window becomes a window; ⭐ **it comes back on** |
| **V3** | **was never the point** | the small thing | nothing about it changed and it has no evidence it is visible |
| **V4** | **is switched off, on purpose** | the one whose hand it is | a deliberate, slightly rude act, unexplained |

leaves / fails / was never it / is switched off. Four mechanisms, one concept, no shared skeleton.
