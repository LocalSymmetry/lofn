# STEP 07 — SONG GUIDE & STRUCTURE · PAIR 3 · "NOTHING BRIGHT IN HERE"

**Voice:** SUNNA · **Bed:** DUB / REGGAE HALF-TIME · **72 BPM · G minor · one-drop**

---

## THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS (a guide that cannot answer both is not ready to direct)

### 1. Where is the body standing?
**On a wet pavement outside a lit room, at the window, elbows on a stone sill that is colder than she
expected.** Her phone is face-down on that sill. Her palms are flat against the glass at her temples, cupped
to make a hood around her own face. Her breath is fogging a patch the size of a hand and she keeps having to
move six inches left. It is night, it is August, and the cold is the surprising kind — the kind that only
arrives when you have been somewhere warm and loud for three hours.

Not a kitchen. Not indoors. Not safe. **Outside, in the dark, on the wrong side of something.**

### 2. What could hurt it here?
Four things, and none of them are dramatic, which is the point:
- **The view closes when she goes back in — and she goes back in.** The thing she is looking at only exists
  while she is not in it. This is the wound and it is never stated.
- **Her own reflection.** She is the obstruction and she cannot turn herself face-down. Stack item 2.
- **The door behind her.** ⭐ **The wolf.** It never opens in this song, it is never mentioned in the lyric,
  and it is the only reason any of this is urgent. *(One automation lane, running the whole track, never
  resolving — see §5.)*
- **The sky refusing to say.** She keeps checking. It might be cloudy. It never resolves and nobody in the
  song ever finds out. **Held open past comfort.**

⛔ **Nothing in this song is at stake in a way that could be fixed by an adult.** No lesson is available and
none is offered.

## 1. THE ARC IN ONE SENTENCE (first-listen legibility — Golden Move 6)

> *She steps outside a party for the cold, turns around, can't see in past her own reflection, cups her
> hands to the glass, and finally watches a room full of people be completely normal — and it is the best
> thing she has seen all week.*

A stranger can retell that after one listen. **The subject is plainly in the lyric.** The cathedral is
underneath: the brightest thing in the room went outside, and now the room is visible. Nothing withheld.

## 2. SECTION MAP — hard discrete level-states, no crossfades (acoustic grammar #1)

Dub is level-states by nature: the fader is the instrument, channels are **slammed** in and out. Every
transition below is a hard cut on a bar line. ⛔ **No crossfades, no risers, no filter sweeps into drops.**

## 3. THE LONG APPROACH (duration grammar, assigned)

Sections 0–8 are **one held state**: the same two-bar loop, no sub, no lift, ~40% of the runtime before the
first structural event. Nothing hurries. The song's first half **is** the approach, and the approach is not
a build — there is no riser, no swell, no filter opening. It is a room at one volume for a long time, which
is what a person standing at a window actually experiences.

⛔ Do not let the arrangement pre-announce the hinge. The sub must be a **surprise that was always missing.**

## 5. THE WOLF (Sunna's mandatory pursuer — one automation lane, never resolving, never in the lyric)

> **A single filter on the room-noise bus that only ever opens.** From section 0 to the last bar it widens
> by a few hundred hertz per section and never closes, so the room behind the glass gets very slightly more
> present the whole time — as if it is coming toward her. It never arrives, it never resolves, and no lyric
> refers to it.

Diegetically it is the door. She has to go back in. The song does not say so.

## 7. WHAT THE PRODUCTION MUST CARRY *(and what it must NOT be asked to carry)*

⭐ **THE GRAIN LAW.** A Somatic objection answered only in the production spec is not answered. Therefore:
**the concept lives in the lyric and the form** (the stack, the radiant, the closed ground, the braid), and
the production is allowed to carry **exactly one** meaning — the mix inversion at the hinge — because the
lyric carries that moment too.

**Specs that run WITH the generator (keep):**
- open at full level, no intro
- withhold the sub, let it arrive
- mono-centre the bass
- hard channel cuts (native to dub — the generator has a strong prior for this)
- end warm, no cymbals

**Specs that would fight the generator (⛔ refused):**
- long full stops / total silence — smoothed every time
- untuned drones — the waterphone stays **tuned-adjacent and distant**, a wail behind glass, never a
  free-floating non-musical object
- hard-panned non-musical elements — the spring-echo throws are hard left but they are **musical** (word
  fragments in time), which is exactly the case the generator honours

## 9. THE DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK — "name the one way this would render generic"

**Predicted render:** the first five seconds are a bare melodica descent with spring reverb and a one-drop
kick landing late; a dry, talky female voice enters unprocessed and close, packed two lines to a bar over an
almost empty half-time; the bottom is conspicuously hollow for ninety seconds; then a sub lands and the
voice retreats into a long echo while the room noise steps forward, and it stays that way to a warm,
cymbal-less finish on the melodica alone.

⚠️ **The one way this renders generic:** Suno's strongest dub prior is **stoned instrumental groove with a
soft, pretty, reverb-soaked female vocal floating on top** — which would silently convert Sunna into
ambience and lose the whole personality. That is the safe-default failure and it is a real risk on this bed.

**The self-repair, applied to the artifacts before ship:**
1. the style field says **sing-speak, dry, talky, close, slightly off-mic** and **crowds two lines into a
   bar** — the density is what stops the float
2. the EXCLUDE field explicitly names *floaty ethereal female vocal, breathy, angelic, meditation, chillout,
   lo-fi study beats, soft crooning* as negative tokens
3. one **BOLD choice** that no ambience prior will smooth: **the mix inversion** — an audible, structural,
   mid-song swap of foreground and background that the generator must execute as a level event, not a mood

✅ Re-checked against the Golden Seed and the GOLDEN MOVE: stands somewhere real (a sill) · zero sung facts
(none assigned) · turns past the midpoint (the inversion) · fear braided in (the door, never named) ·
