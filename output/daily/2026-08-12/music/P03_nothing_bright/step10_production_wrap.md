## 3. VOCAL SPEC

**Female lead**, dry / talky / close / slightly off-mic / **unbeautiful**. Sing-speak, roughly two lines
crammed into a bar over the half-time — the density is what stops the track floating into ambience.
Dynamic mic, **no de-esser on the breaths**, 3–5 dB shelf at 8 kHz, **hard rule against transparent pitch
correction.** Choruses double the lead 15–35 ms misaligned and ±6 cents off; the smear is the arrangement.

⭐ **Delivery ruling:** *"This is the worst I will ever be"* is sung **exactly as flatly as the line about
the elbows.** Any lift converts the song into a motivational poster, which the run has ruled out.
⛔ No warm alto. No choir. No crystalline anything. Never sung-pretty.

**Scene:** **contemporary UK dub / lovers-rock revival** — the living British end of the Jamaican dub
