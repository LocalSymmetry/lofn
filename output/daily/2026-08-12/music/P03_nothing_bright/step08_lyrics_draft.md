## DRAFTING DECISIONS — what changed and why

### ⭐ The numeral ban improved the best verse in the song
Verse 3 opened as *"Two of them are arguing / One is asleep on a coat / One is fixing the music."* The
assignment forbids **any** sung number, so all three cardinals were cut — and the replacement is better:

> *Somebody's arguing… / Somebody's asleep on a coat. / Somebody's fixing the music…*

The anaphora on **"Somebody's"** is a repeated syntactic frame — one of the five returns declared against
the rhyme debt (§6 of step 06) — and it makes the room correctly **anonymous.** She does not know these
people. That is the whole point of the song and the constraint found it. ✅ **Zero numerals now sung:**
*second → next · half-second → a beat · an hour → a while.*

### D5 PRESENT TENSE — three lines rewritten on this rule alone
| Draft | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| *which I did not know* | past | **which is new information** *(drier, funnier)* |
| *I would not have seen her with the big light on* | conditional past | **With the big light on / she does not exist** |
| *I am not going to* | future intent | **I am not asking** |

### The line that carries the fact, and never reports it
> *And there they are. In there. / Not brighter. Not more of them. / Just the size they've been / the whole
> time, coming through.*

That is F22 said by a person on a pavement. No moon, no meteors, no comet, no nineteen years. **The
astronomy stays in the brief.**

### ⭐ The framing on THE LINE was cut
The Dub Chamber first read *"Flat against the glass, / to nobody, to the glass: / this is the worst I will
ever be."* — **"to nobody" is a wink at D8**, telling the listener the rule is being obeyed. Cut. Replaced
with **"Mouth against the glass. / Fog, then no fog, then fog."** — which is physical, adds a *fourth*
micro-stack (the breath, appearing and failing and appearing), and lets the line arrive with no framing at
all. **We simply overhear it.** This is the single best edit in the draft.

### The stack, checked in place
1. **phone face-down** (V1b) → works, reveals a second light
2. **her own reflection** (V2b) — *"I go dark, it goes dark"* → ⛔ impossible; she is the light
3. **hands hooded** (V2b→V2c) → partial
4. **hands still up, angle wrong, somebody moves** (V3c) → ⛔ **NOT FIXED** — *"Not fixing it."*

⭐ Item 2 is the song's second listen and it is never explained. Item 4 refuses the repair out loud, in
three words, and moves on.

### The radiant
**"through"** closes: V1, V1b, V1c, every chorus (×5), V2c, the Hinge, V3, V3b, V3c, the Dub Chamber, and
the Outro's last word. Its placement moves inside the line (*straight through / coming through / all the way
through / see through / look through / come through*) so it reads as insistence rather than a tic.

### The vowel braid, working
*night · light · I · bright · side · size · white* **/aɪ/** ↔ *through · room · do · moving · you'd* **/uː/**.
The /aɪ/ density is highest in Verses 1–2 (where she is the subject) and **thins across Verse 3** (where the
room is), and the last word of the song is **through.** She braids herself out. ⛔ Never stated anywhere.
