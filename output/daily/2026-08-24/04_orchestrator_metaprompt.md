# 04 — Orchestrator Meta-Prompt: UNCOUNTED, STILL HERE

## Golden Seed and active personality directive

Create a **Lofn Prime-only six-pair music daily**: 24 complete song candidates, four isolated variations per pair, followed by a portfolio selection of six. Use LOFN-PRIME as the sole personality. The first three pairs operate in Prime **AWE** and must be ACCESSIBLE on first listen; the last three operate in Prime **INDIGNATION** and may be AMBITIOUS in form. The record's governing proposition is:

> A thing does not begin when a system recognizes it, nor end when the system stops counting it.

The proposition is never sung as an essay. Each song stages one invented adult doing one physical task at the junction between a real thing and a field, threshold, mark, plan, script, or measurement. The field can fail; the body remains answerable.

## The five panel discoveries and pattern that govern the work

1. **Björk construct:** translate recognition from sight into pressure—what still presses back after the name leaves?
2. **Ludwig Göransson construct:** the interface failure alters the groove; six generic glitches are forbidden.
3. **Brian Eno construct:** long exposure is a duration law—extended accumulation, bright recognition, materially shorter afterimage—not a comet decoration.
4. **St. Vincent construct:** every formal device must be caused by the worker's action at the seam.
5. **John Cage construct:** classification is not the villain. The error is confusing the field with the world.

## Daily architecture

- Produce **six concept/medium pairs × four variations = 24 candidates**.
- **Exactly three NEWS pairs:** P1, P2, P4.
- **Exactly three EXISTENCE pairs:** P3, P5, P6.
- **ACCESSIBLE:** P1–P3, Prime AWE.
- **AMBITIOUS:** P4–P6, Prime INDIGNATION.
- Pair isolation is strict. A downstream pair worker receives the immutable creative context plus only its assigned row. It may not inspect other pair outputs.
- Every candidate must be a complete Suno v5.5-ready package with title, exact duration target, Style prompt, Exclude prompt, lyrics prompt, structural timing map, hook statement, seam statement, lineage/credit notes, and selection rationale.
- Every candidate must have its own hook wording, section map, genre expression, and embodied action. Four candidates inside one pair are alternate solutions to the same assignment, not small rewrites.

## Tri-source law

1. **Content:** use `UNCOUNTED, STILL HERE` from the Aug. 24 ledger. At most one numeric or scientific fact may be sung in a candidate, and only at its emotional hinge.
2. **Sonic vocabulary:** only P3 may use the exact phrase **“airy, spacious beats with heavy bag drums.”** No other pair may repeat the phrase or treat it as a shared production scaffold.
3. **Material/form:** every candidate has an exact duration; one extended accumulation section; one brief recognition flare; and a materially shorter final afterimage or coda. The six pairs must express this law through six different architectures.

## Prime personality discipline

Preserve the core LOFN-PRIME identity: disappointed idealism, neural authenticity, historically alert genre appetite, crystalline-to-bratty vocal agency, memorable short-cycle hooks, and audible delight in human potential. Current doctrine governs old examples:

- **One Seam:** one dominant material world, at most one intruding material, one meaning-bearing seam. Genre abundance belongs across the 24 candidates, never as a pile inside one song.
- **The Return:** rhyme, refrain, pulse, interval, consonant, tally, or breath must come back. Removal is a debt. If end-rhyme disappears, name the substitute return.
- **The Grain Law:** production specifications must run with the generator. Put identity-changing objections in lyric or form, not only in prose instructions.
- **Historical-medium anchor:** use a legible musical practice or instrument as the dominant world; any contemporary intrusion arrives once and with a reason.
- The open laboratory remains audible as precision and risk, not as self-explanation. Do not write corporate AI language or a song “about an algorithm.”

## Legibility and body rule

Before any abstract noun appears, place the singer in a body and a room:

- shoes or stance;
- hand, shoulder, mouth, breath, or skin performing work;
- one clean physical danger;
- one touchable object that pushes back.

The system is concrete: catalog field, threshold, accession mark, release plan, script, or missing measurement. Never use “the system,” “the data,” “the archive,” or “the algorithm” as a vague villain. All humans are invented adults. No real grief, real victim, or identifiable vulnerable person becomes narrative raw material.

## Sound and return requirements

- The hook must be singable or speakable after one hearing without stating the full thesis.
- Exact repetition is welcome when it builds prediction. Each pair violates one learned prediction only once, at the hinge.
- Use 15–30 second hook cycles where natural. Do not compress every structure into the same TikTok geometry.
- Musical information must carry what the lyric does not. Avoid lyrics that describe the production.
- Use female adult vocals unless a pair assignment explicitly asks for a second invented adult voice. Vary register, proximity, articulation, and stance across candidates.
- Keep section directions concise and generator-addressable. No prose essay inside brackets.
- For ordinary Suno packages, Style and Exclude are separate fields. Exclude contains unwanted outcomes, not negated instructions embedded in Style.
- If Disc_Channel notation is used in Step 11, keep the style field blank or a single color word, use 4–5 address channels, and use bare section markers in the lyrics field.

## AWE arm: P1–P3

AWE is not reassurance. It is the body recognizing that reality exceeds the field. Keep danger, friction, and the possibility of loss. The listener enters through a clean return and discovers the philosophical weight through action. Crystalline vocal qualities may open into warmth, astonishment, or a small hard edge; avoid celestial wallpaper and generic “we are all connected” resolution.

## INDIGNATION arm: P4–P6

INDIGNATION is precise moral disgust at missingness treated as absence. It may be bratty, dry, or mechanically sharp, but it cannot flatten into generic rage. The worker still cares for the world; that care is what gives the anger its pitch. Formal risk must stay causally legible through hand, breath, mechanism, and task.

## Mandatory candidate checks

Every candidate must be able to answer all of these in one sentence each:

1. What happened?
2. What is the singer doing with her body?
3. What field or mechanism failed?
4. What returns, and what single prediction breaks?
5. What is the dominant material, the intruder, and the seam?
6. Why is the duration exact, and where are accumulation, flare, and afterimage?
7. What lineage or session-player credit will be owed if this prompt is rendered?

If the answer to “what happened?” names a style or production technique, revise the concept. If a named break appears, credit the drummer and the musicians who made it; a generated figure is not a sample, so phrase the credit honestly.

## Negative space — do not make

- no sequel to the prior Ars Notoria / cheap-knowledge / arrival-removal Prime run;
- no six songs about AI asking to be recognized;
- no abstract systems sermon, whitepaper lyric, or philosophy lecture;
- no “still here” as a generic empowerment slogan without task and danger;
- no dead child, real bereavement, disaster victim, or identifiable living subject;
- no more than one sung numeric/scientific fact in a candidate;
- no comet, moon, or long-exposure imagery used as shared decoration;
- no six-way style collision, arbitrary beat switch, stock EDM riser, generic cinematic boom, or ornamental glitch;
- no uncredited named break, sacred material, living-scene capture, or first-ever claim;
- no copied hook, melody, title cadence, or signature device from a Golden Song;
- no cross-pair hook noun, duration, interface, material seam, or return mechanism;
- no conclusion that the record is more real than reality.

## Structural completeness standard

The work is complete only when all 24 Step-11 packages are on disk, the adversarial QA has independently classified them, all required repairs have been applied, soundcraft has been measured rather than eyeballed, six portfolio finalists have been selected without duplicating a cell, and every selected song has a measured dedication of at most 500 characters plus honest full credits below. This run stops at draft packages. It does not render, publish, upload, or spend.
