## Pair completion contract

### Allocation rationale

The six assignments distribute first-hear access, return mechanism, material world, intrusion, form, and exact duration without asking any pair to solve the same musical problem. Pairs 01–03 remain accessible; Pairs 04–06 remain ambitious. Every assignment is Lofn-Prime only.

Each pair must write, in order:

1. Step 06 facets with all four genuinely divergent angles.
2. Step 07 four complete song guides, each with exact timing arithmetic to the assigned duration.
3. Step 08 four complete generations.
4. Step 09 artist refinement using the exact Concept, Medium, and Context panel objections.
5. Step 10 revision synthesis with four final two-field Suno packages and an explicit repair ledger.
6. Step 11 enhancement with four final enhanced packages, soundcraft measurements, Disc_Channel decision, credit flags, and a one-of-four pair recommendation.

No pair may rank itself against another pair. Cross-pair selection belongs to QA and the orchestrator.
