- **Fear stays braided in:** AWE remains adjacent to vertigo and fall exposure through the unresolved final harmony.
- **Simple surface, complex engine:** `Still in orbit` is immediately repeatable while harmonic rotation carries the philosophical change.
- **The Return:** the exact hook and melody recur; tonic pedal, suspended submediant, and bare supertonic alter meaning without damaging the refrain.
- **One Seam / Grain Law:** one coherent chamber-art-pop world, one dry announcement, one physical junction; the objection also lives in lyric and form rather than production prose alone.
## Major Deviations

- **Changed:** Rebuilt the lyric around a long observational frost span followed by a clipped traction litany, replacing the prior draft’s extended chain-word mechanism. The harmonic argument now develops before the body names it.
- **Intensified:** Made the singer’s practical benefit to the next worker explicit, gave the 38–46Hz sub a somatic pressure job, and fused the snag, PA line, caught inhale, heel strike, and missing landing at one timestamp.
- **Refused:** Preserved the invariant hook, genre string, 96 BPM, C-sharp minor/Dorian harmony, 432Hz tuning, 2:48 contract, AWE-with-vertigo assignment, and one-seam architecture. A new title or decorative second rupture would weaken the song.
- **Structural transformation:** The earlier anadiplosis-led screw circuit is now a narrative exposure form: paired-rhyme maintenance verse → extended accumulation → exact return → short stress litany → embodied seam → exposed afterimage.
