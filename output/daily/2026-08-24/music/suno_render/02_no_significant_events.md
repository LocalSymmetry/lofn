## Golden Move Calibration

- Make awe body-scale: pressure, breath, and room geometry carry the large idea before explanation does.
- Let the exact hook build trust through unchanged returns; alter ownership through context and performance, not wording.
- Put the philosophical turn after the physical accumulation, so insight is earned by action.
- Make the seam audible through one damaged onset and an immediate rhythmic repayment.
- Keep formal consequence local: the single rupture subtracts material, and the afterimage remembers the load without resolving it.

## Major Deviations

- Changed the linear report into a brace canon: N-headed work lines now answer the invariant hook and make the return musical before it becomes semantic.
- Rewrote the verses for tighter end-sound return, harder physical nouns, and fewer explanatory sentences.
- Added the required 432Hz lock and made the 38–44Hz somatic band explicit without creating a second sonic world.
- Moved maximum density into the forty-eight-second post-midpoint aisle canon; the hooks remain short, dry, and exact.
- Replaced the prior single retraction with a double retraction: neither the field nor the body is crowned as total witness.
- Refused any change to the hook, genre string, 132 BPM, D minor, 432Hz, 3:06 duration, accessible AWE assignment, single impact seam, or human-subject safety.
