# Selected-Six Dedications

All character counts include the dedication text only and are measured against the 500-character cap.

## P01 — Still in Orbit

Dedicated to chamber players and maintenance-foley recordists: this piece borrows the vocabularies of bowed metal, muted viola, glass attack, cloth drag, heel strike, and rail resonance, not any specific recorded performance. Any generated version evokes those practices; it does not sample them. Made by Lofn with The Scientist for an invented cleaner who keeps one boot down.

**Characters:** 377

## P02 — No Significant Events

Dedicated to archive workers who keep shared memory usable while its furniture and interfaces press back; owed to breakbeat and broken-beat communities for the asymmetric pulse carried here. Written by Lofn with The Scientist. Any generated render would be original audio, not a sample of a credited recording.

**Characters:** 310

## P03 — Leave the Line Open

Dedicated to Fused Arrow and Bandcamp Daily for the borrowed wording “airy, spacious beats with heavy bag drums,” carried as credited vocabulary and living-scene lineage—not audio, a sample, affiliation, endorsement, or collaboration. Owed also to contemporary abstract-hip-hop beatmakers and DIY label workrooms, and to the future adult vocalist, electric-key performer, bass performer/programmer, and paper/sole/rail sound operator. Written by Lofn with The Scientist.

**Characters:** 470

## P04 — The Last Verb Fails

Dedicated to the living UK-garage continuum and the producers, labels, clubs, and dancers who shaped its skipping swing and bass-space negative space; and to the future adult contralto, rhythm/bass programmer, and route-step/metal/door foley performer who would make this track audible. Lofn and The Scientist authored this AI-generated prompt; it requests newly generated/performed audio—no sample, collaboration, endorsement, or affiliation is claimed.

**Characters:** 454

## P05 — Short of the Groove

Dedicated to the surviving Philae inscription and to the public epigraphic record that lets us approach it without pretending to recover a vanished speaker. Owed also to the future adult low-mezzo, contrabass player, tool-friction operator, and room recordist who would make restraint audible. Lofn and The Scientist authored this AI-generated prompt; its chisel, string, voice, and room are newly generated/performed instructions, not recovered speech, archaeological audio, or sampled sound.

**Characters:** 493

## P06 — Backwash Before Wave

Dedicated to tim.kahn, recordist of “Atlantic Ocean Waves” on Freesound under CC BY 4.0. The prompt references it; generated output is not a sample. Any actual use must use and credit that recording under its license. Owed also to the future adult low-mezzo vocalist, dub/sub programmer, wet-tool foley performer, and the living dub-techno craft. Written by Lofn with The Scientist.

**Characters:** 382
