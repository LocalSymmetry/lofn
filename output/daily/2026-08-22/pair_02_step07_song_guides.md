# PAIR 02 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES
## `daily-music-2026-08-22-sunna-offbeat` · SLOT P02 · four variations
**two-tone ska · 126 BPM · ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · INDIGNATION · no distortion anywhere**

> One song guide per variation. Each names its form map, its parade (which instrument enters where, one
> at a time, so SWAG's mechanism is a countable event and not a swell), the three moves of the absence
> (**state → remove → keep pointing**), where the wolf lands, where the sung fact lands, and the
> byte-identical chorus that returns unchanged.

## 0. WHAT ALL FOUR SHARE — the bed, stated once so no guide has to restate it

**Tempo 126 BPM, 4/4, end to end.** No ramp, no key change, no drop, no half-time, no gear-change. The
drive is steadiness; the changes are timbral.

**The rhythm section, after 0:30:**
- **Guitar** — clean, sharp, chopping the **offbeat only**. Three-note voicing, not a chord wall. This
  is the hook instrument, not a texture. ⛔ No distortion, no overdrive, no fuzz, anywhere, ever.
- **Bass** — melodic, walking between chords, **front of mix**, −2 to 0 dB against the lead vocal, on
  the grid. It leans a fat pickup note onto the **four-and**, longer and louder than the one ever was.
- **Drums** — **+8 to +14 ms late**, rim on three, hats riding the upstroke, and **no kick on beat one
  ever again** after the removal. Dry, small room, no gated verb.
- **Organ** — Hammond, **gnarled** drawbar registration with key click left in, bubbling the *and*.
- **Horns** — short stabs, unreverbed, answering the vocal and out inside the bar. Never padding.
- **Handclaps / tambourine** — dry, dead centre, **on two and four or on the upstroke, never on the
  one.**
- **Vocal** — female alto, full chest, close on a dynamic mic, breaths left in, no de-esser, no
  transparent pitch correction. **Stacked girl-group thirds are a required instrument**, and there is
  **one moment where two voices split and the room hears the gap open.**
- **Master** — −14 LUFS, ≥10 dB crest. ⛔ No standing tension lane: no riser that never resolves, no
  filter that only opens.

**The absence, mechanically identical in all four (it is the slot's identity):**
| move | where | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| **STATE** | bars 1–16 · 0:00–0:30 | whole band **in unison on a straight four-on-the-floor downbeat** — kick, bass, guitar, organ, horns all hitting the one together, for the eight-bar intro **and** the whole of verse one. **Two full eight-bar cycles = 30.5 s.** |
| **REMOVE** | bar 17, the downbeat of the **Lift**, 0:30.5 | the one is deleted and **never played again**. Only the upstroke remains. |
| **KEEP POINTING** | every **Lift**, three times | the four bars that used to have a floor come round again before every chorus, and the ear goes to the one, and it is not there. |
| **BODY STILL FEELS IT** | continuous | the bass pickup on the **four-and** carries the weight the one used to carry. |

⛔⭐ **NOBODY FILLS IT.** No clap, no chant, no percussion, no vocal on beat one after 0:30 in any of the
four. In V4 an entire coach sings and **the singalong is written onto the upstroke, in the lyric, in
words.**

# SONG GUIDE — V1 · THE DRIVER
### "Mind How You Go" · A minor · 126 BPM · ~3:25

**Angle:** praise for somebody doing an impossible job correctly, **said to his face**, while he is doing
it. **Newest-broken:** the handheld ticket machine on his belt, new in the spring, that cannot read a
paper ticket. His pen can.

### Form map
`Intro (8 instr) · Verse 1 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (8) · Verse 2 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (8) · Verse 3
(8) · Bridge (8) · Verse 4 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (8) · Outro (8)` — **84 sung lines.**

### The parade — one instrument at a time, each entrance a named event
| where | event |
|---|---|
| **Intro bar 1** | **baritone sax** states the three-note offbeat figure in octaves with the organ, over the unison downbeat. This is the instrumental hook and it exists **before the voice.** |
| **Verse 1 bar 9** | **lead vocal** enters. Downbeat still under her. Nothing else is added. |
| **Lift bar 17** | ⭐ **THE REMOVAL.** Kick, bass and organ leave the one. **Guitar chop alone on the offbeat** for four bars — the thinnest the record ever gets, and it happens at the moment of maximum need. |
| **Chorus 1 bar 21** | **gnarled organ bubble** enters on the *and*. |
| **Verse 2 bar 29** | **trombone** enters, answering the line ends. |
| **Chorus 2 bar 41** | **tambourine** enters on the upstroke. |
| **Verse 3 bar 49** | **stacked thirds** enter under the sung fact. |
| **Bridge bar 57** | **baritone sax takes the bridge as a counter-melody** — the densest bar in the song, all inventory, no thesis. |
| **Verse 4 bar 65** | ⭐ **THE SPLIT** — at the wolf, the two stacked voices part to a bare fifth for one bar and the gap opens. **The band does not slow, nothing drops out.** |
| **Outro bar 85** | instruments leave one at a time: thirds, trombone, tambourine, sax, drums — **the organ figure is last and plays alone, and the one never arrives.** |

### Where each mandated thing lands
- **Absence stated:** bars 1–16. **Removed:** Lift, bar 17. **Pointed at:** every Lift; and named in
  words **once**, in verse 3 (*"We had a downbeat up the front. / We took it out. We left the hole."*).
- **Body-feels-it line, in the lyric:** verse 3 — *"The bass comes in ahead of the top / and hits harder
  than the top it stole."*
- **Sung fact:** verse 3, line two — ***two hours old***, said by him, **answered** by the coach telling
  him it is not his fault. One fact, in words, never recited.
- **Wolf (event, resolves, band does not slow):** verse 4. They pulled the last coach off at tea; he
  brought the empty one down; he had clocked off and came back out; **he signs the back of her paper
  ticket so the fare comes back** and says his own line. Past the midpoint, in a verse, **not** in the
  bridge.
- **Forbidden section honoured:** the bridge is pure inventory — flask lid, heater, a man giving up his
  seat, the fogged window, the amber beacon. No verdict.

### The byte-identical chorus (returns three times, unchanged)
```
Mind how you go, he says. Mind how you go.
There's nowhere to go and he says it slow.
Mind how you go, mind how you go.
He's off the clock and he's out in the road.
Mind how you go, he says. Mind how you go.
The newest thing here is the thing that broke.
Mind how you go, mind how you go.
And all of us say it back: I know.
```
**Hook:** *Mind how you go* — four syllables, a courtesy, and an accusation only because the coach is
not going anywhere. The reveal engine sits inside the chorus, so **the room sings it.**

### SFX cue
`*hazard lights ticking, rain on a coach roof*` at the top; `*a pneumatic door sighs*` into the outro.

# SONG GUIDE — V2 · THE BOARD
### "It Still Says On Time" · E minor · 126 BPM · ~3:20

**Angle:** the live-departure screen across the road — wrong, bright, and **the newest object present.**
**Newest-broken:** the board itself. The paper sheet in the glass case beside it has never been wrong.

### Form map
`Intro (8 instr) · Verse 1 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (6) · Verse 2 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (6) · Verse 3
(8) · Bridge (8) · Verse 4 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (6) · Outro (12)` — **82 sung lines.**

### The parade
| where | event |
|---|---|
| **Intro bar 1** | **organ alone** states a two-note figure that answers itself — a wiper, in music — over the unison downbeat. No horns yet. |
| **Verse 1 bar 9** | **lead vocal**. Downbeat still under. |
| **Lift bar 17** | ⭐ **THE REMOVAL**, on the word *out*. Chop alone. |
| **Chorus 1 bar 21** | **baritone sax** enters, doubling the hook an octave down. |
| **Verse 2 bar 27** | **muted trumpet** enters, one stab per line end. |
| **Chorus 2 bar 39** | **tambourine** on the upstroke. |
| **Bridge bar 53** | **trombone** enters and holds the bridge — inventory only. |
| **Verse 4 bar 61** | **stacked thirds**; ⭐ **THE SPLIT** on the body-feels-it couplet. |
| **Outro bar 77** | horns leave, then drums, then bass; **the organ is left answering itself alone** — the figure from bar 1, with nothing under it. |

### Where each mandated thing lands
- **Absence stated:** bars 1–16. **Removed:** Lift, bar 17. **Pointed at:** every Lift; named in words
  **once**, verse 4 (*"We had a downbeat at the start. / We let it go and shut the door."*).
- **Body-feels-it line:** verse 4 — *"Your foot still goes down where it went… that's how you know it's
  gone: you drop / and there is nothing in the air."*
- **Sung fact:** verse 3, line two — ***two hours old***, said by the man in green who apologises for it;
  **answered** with *thanks for saying so. Nobody else has.*
- **Wolf:** verse 2 into verse 3. A man buys paper at the gate; the machine takes his coin and prints him
  a seat, a train, a platform and a place in line, on a service that no longer exists. **It resolves**:
  the driver waves him up the step, does not check, makes no speech.
- **Forbidden section honoured:** the bridge is the glass case, the hand-typed sheet, and the fault light
  that has been on since the spring. Inventory, no verdict.

### The byte-identical chorus (three times, unchanged)
```
It still says on time. It still says on time.
Bright as a shop and it hasn't a clue.
It still says on time. It still says on time.
Newest thing here and the newest thing's through.
Nobody's shouting. That's the worst.
It still says on time. And it says it first.
```
**Hook:** *It still says on time* — five syllables. A room chanting a screen's own lie back at it, in the
screen's own words. **The politeness thesis is in the chorus, not the bridge.**

### SFX cue
`*rain, and a departure board clicking over*`; the outro ends on `*a fluorescent tube going out*`.

# SONG GUIDE — V3 · WHO SIGNED IT
### "Somebody Signed It" · D minor · 126 BPM · ~3:30

**Angle:** the decision, three streets and one Tuesday away, made by people who will never stand here.
**Newest-broken:** **the signature itself** — the newest object in the whole story is a decision, and it
is the part that does not work.

### Form map
`Intro (8 instr) · Verse 1 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (8) · Verse 2 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (8) · Verse 3
(8) · Bridge (8) · Verse 4 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (8) · Outro (8)` — **84 sung lines.**

### The parade
| where | event |
|---|---|
| **Intro bar 1** | **trombone** states the hook as a three-note slide figure on the offbeat, alone over the unison downbeat. Warm, close, no reverb tail. |
| **Verse 1 bar 9** | **lead vocal**. Downbeat still under. |
| **Lift bar 17** | ⭐ **THE REMOVAL.** Chop alone, four bars. |
| **Chorus 1 bar 21** | **organ** enters on the *and*. |
| **Verse 2 bar 29** | ⭐ **the bass takes the melody** — it climbs into the vocal's register and walks the whole verse as a second lead. The strut, as a named instrument arriving. |
| **Chorus 2 bar 41** | **tenor sax** enters with the trombone. |
| **Bridge bar 57** | **handclaps on two and four** enter — the only new percussion in the song, and they are **never on the one.** |
| **Verse 4 bar 65** | **stacked thirds**; ⭐ **THE SPLIT** on *"Your knee still does it, though."* |
| **Outro bar 85** | sax, then organ, then drums leave. **The trombone figure is the last thing playing.** |

### Where each mandated thing lands
- **Absence stated:** bars 1–16. **Removed:** Lift, bar 17. **Pointed at:** every Lift; named in words
  **once**, verse 4 (*"There was a downbeat. Now there's not."*).
- **Body-feels-it line:** verse 4 — *"It hasn't landed since. It won't. / Your knee still does it,
  though."*
- **Sung fact:** verse 3, line two — ***two hours old***, read off her own phone under the word *live*;
  **answered** flatly with *well, there we are*, and then the praise continues.
- **Wolf:** verse 2. They sat down on the Tuesday in a room with a jug of water, got back up, shook
  hands, and went — and nobody in that room got wet. **It resolves** in the bridge-adjacent inventory:
  a woman goes down the aisle with a biro writing the claim word on the back of every ticket in the row.
  **Closing move:** she writes the date across the fold of her own ticket and keeps it.
- **Forbidden section honoured:** the bridge is the biro woman, the driver's radio turned up so the coach
  can hear it, the heater, the flask lid. No verdict.

### The byte-identical chorus (three times, unchanged)
```
Somebody signed it. Somebody's warm.
Somebody signed it and drove off home.
Somebody signed it. Somebody's warm.
And we're the ones stood out in the storm.
Somebody signed it. Somebody's warm.
And nobody's cross. And that's the con.
Somebody signed it. Somebody's warm.
Say it nice. Say it nice. Then move on.
```
**Hook:** *Somebody signed it* — five syllables. It names a mechanism and never a person, which is both
the Human Subject Standard and the sharpest available version of the accusation.

### SFX cue
`*a coach radio, turned up*`; the outro closes on `*an indicator ticking*`.

# SONG GUIDE — V4 · THE SINGALONG
### "Louder Than The Rain" · G minor · 126 BPM · ~3:30

**Angle:** what a full coach does at midnight when it stops being angry — and what that costs nobody
upstairs. **Newest-broken:** the journey-planner app that sent everyone to a stop that does not exist.
The pole has been there since before the line and has never lied.

### Form map
`Intro (8 instr) · Verse 1 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (6) · Verse 2 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (6) · Verse 3
(8) · Bridge (12) · Verse 4 (8) · Lift (4) · Chorus (6) · Outro (10)` — **84 sung lines.**

### The parade
| where | event |
|---|---|
| **Intro bar 1** | **the whole horn line in unison octaves** — trumpet, trombone, baritone sax — states the hook over the unison downbeat. The biggest opening of the four, because this is the one that ends as a room. |
| **Verse 1 bar 9** | **lead vocal**. Downbeat still under. |
| **Lift bar 17** | ⭐ **THE REMOVAL.** Chop alone, four bars. |
| **Chorus 1 bar 21** | **organ** enters on the *and*. |
| **Verse 2 bar 27** | **shaker and tambourine** enter, both on the upstroke. |
| **Chorus 2 bar 39** | ⭐ **the room enters** — a second vocal layer, untrained, slightly flat, misaligned 15–35 ms, ±6 cents. ⛔ **It sings the upstroke with the guitar. It never lands on the one.** |
| **Bridge bar 53** | ⭐ **the driver's hand on the wheel becomes a real percussion part** — a dry flat slap on the offbeat, dead centre, no verb. The bridge's densest event and the one that is only inventory. |
| **Verse 4 bar 69** | **stacked thirds**; ⭐ **THE SPLIT** where the woman who started the song gets her seat back and keeps the low part wrong. |
| **Outro bar 85** | room voices go first, then thirds, then drums; **the horn line finishes alone, in unison, on the upstroke.** |

### Where each mandated thing lands
- **Absence stated:** bars 1–16. **Removed:** Lift, bar 17. **Pointed at:** every Lift; named in words
  **once**, verse 4 — and here the naming does double duty as the **hole-swap mitigation**:
  *"The downbeat played and then it stopped. / We never put it back. It's gone. / Sing on the and. Sing
  on the and. / There's nothing on the top. Sing on."*
- **Body-feels-it:** carried by that same verse and by the bass pickup, which is loudest here of the four.
- **Sung fact:** verse 3 — ***two hours old***, offered by a woman answering a man reading the board;
  **answered** by the coach starting the song again instead of arguing.
- **Wolf:** verse 2 and verse 4. A woman gave her seat up at the stop and has stood the whole way; she is
  also the one who starts the singing and gets the low part wrong. **It resolves:** a man and his mate
  stand up and squeeze along, she sits, and she does not stop singing.
- **Forbidden section honoured:** the bridge is the wheel-drumming, the doubled wiper, the flask lid, a
  man behind doing thirds and getting every one of them wrong, and somebody drawing a coach in the fog.
  No verdict, no summary.

### The byte-identical chorus (three times, unchanged)
```
Louder than the rain, louder than the rain.
Nobody upstairs can hear a note.
Louder than the rain, louder than the rain.
Coach on the hard shoulder. Give it some throat.
It costs them nothing. It never will.
Louder than the rain. And louder still.
```
**Hook:** *Louder than the rain* — five syllables, the only one of the four that is an instruction rather
than a courtesy, because by V4 the room has taken over. The thesis (*it costs them nothing*) is inside
the chorus, so it is sung by everybody, and it is **not** in the bridge.

### SFX cue
`*rain, then a coachful of people breathing in*`; the outro ends on `*an engine taking, under singing*`.
