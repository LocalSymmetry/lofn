"gnarled"

=== THE THREE THAT CARRY THIS RUN'S GRAIN ===
1. "a chiming synth riff"      -> the hook is an INSTRUMENT, not the voice (Sunna pillar 3, THE HUM TEST)
2. "detuned bliss"             -> the happiness is slightly out of tune ON PURPOSE. This is the whole
                                  emotional thesis of the run in two words, and it arrived from outside.
3. "transmogrifies those voices into what are best described as sonic events"
                               -> a voice that has become an EVENT rather than a narrator.
                                  Directly serves R3's "THE WOLF IS AN EVENT, NOT A WEATHER SYSTEM."
