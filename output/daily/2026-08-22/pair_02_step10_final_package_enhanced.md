## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- ⭐ **Changed: the singalong is written onto the upstroke, in the lyric, in words.** *Sing on the and.
  Sing on the and. There's nothing on the top. Sing on.* **Reason:** `05` predicts this pair and P04 are
  the one at-risk hole-swap pairing, because a rhythmic absence and a performer absence can both surface
  as *a gap the room fills*. The mitigation assigned to this slot is that its hole is **instrumental and
  nobody fills it** — and this is the variation where an entire coach sings, so the mitigation had to be
  stated where a generator, a listener and an auditor can all find it. The exclude field carries it four
  more times.
- **Changed: the thesis is a chorus line, not a closing line.** *It costs them nothing. It never will.*
  **Reason:** the bridge is forbidden and a last-line thesis would turn a party into a lesson. Sung three
  times by a room, it is a fact the room already knows.
- **Refused: dialect in the hook.** The draft chorus read *"It costs them nowt and it costs us nowt"*,
  which is muddled — the second half undoes the first — and shuts out half the room. **Reason:** LOAD
  THE FLOOR. The hook belongs to the room or it is not a hook.
- **Changed: the driver's hand on the wheel is a real percussion part in the bridge.** **Reason:** the
  parade needs its densest event where the thesis is not allowed to go, and a flat hand on a steering
  wheel is the only new instrument this coach actually contains.
- **Refused: ending sad.** The last line is *Still wrong. Still ours. Still going. Still here.* The
  engine takes and they are still singing. **Reason:** delight with a deadline it is not pretending
  isn't there — the deadline is real, the delight is not a consolation prize, and the band does not
  slow down.
