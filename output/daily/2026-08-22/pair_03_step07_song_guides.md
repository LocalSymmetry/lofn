# PAIR 03 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES
## `daily-music-2026-08-22-sunna-offbeat` · SLOT P03 · four variations, four guides

**Bed (all four, unchanged):** rocksteady / one-drop opening into dub space. **Bass is the lead
instrument.** Spring reverb, tape echo. **108 BPM exactly**, end to end — the one-drop reads half-time
at 54. ⛔ No tempo change, no key change, no gear-change, no drive.

---

## ⭐ THE RETURN DEBT — DECLARED AND PAID (all four)

> **THE DEBT.** The verse architecture of this slot is *long lines, one breath each, **no end-rhyme***.
> Stripping rhyme is a removal, and **removal is a debt** (`lofn-music` L21): rhyme may be stripped only
> by **naming what returns in its place.**

**THE PAYMENT, IN TWO PARTS, BOTH MEASURABLE:**

**(a) A BYTE-IDENTICAL REFRAIN.** Eight lines, three appearances, **identical to the character** every
time — no added ad-lib, no extra harmony line written into the text, no "final chorus" variation. The
hook line inside it repeats four times within each refrain, so across the song the room hears it twelve
times, plus twice more in the outro. ⛔ **A byte-identical chorus needs no defence and no note.**

**(b) A VOWEL-CHAIN THROUGH EVERY LONG LINE.** One vowel colour per variation, carried in the interior
stresses and landing on the **final stressed syllable** of most lines, with the **codas deliberately
varied so that no two line-ends rhyme.** This is the thing the ear tracks where the rhyme used to be:
the lines *agree in colour and disagree in ending*, which is why they can be long and unrhymed and still
feel bound.

| variation | chain | how it is audited |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | the round **"o"** — *door / off / corner / folded / go / home / lot / slow / closed* | read the verse aloud with the consonants muted; the vowel does not move |
| **V2** | **"ow"** — *pound / out / down / round / house / hour / loud / mouth* | same test; every line lands in the same mouth shape |
| **V3** | **"ee-ih"** — *seat / sleeve / machine / knee / easy / in / still / pleased* | same test |
| **V4** | **"eye"** — *night / sky / mile / side / quiet / time / fine / mine* | same test |

⛔ **No two variations share a chain.** ⛔ **No line-end rhymes another line-end inside its own verse.**

## ⭐ THE ABSENCE — THE SAME THREE MOVES IN ALL FOUR, A DIFFERENT INSTRUMENT EACH TIME

**STATE → REMOVE → KEEP POINTING.**

1. **STATE (RULING B1).** The named instrument is **the lead for the entire first section** — Intro,
   Verse 1 and Refrain 1 — answering the bass figure at the top of every cycle. That is about seventy
   seconds and no fewer than twelve statements of the figure. ⛔ **Nothing is missing in the first thirty
   seconds.**
2. **REMOVE.** It finishes its last phrase at the end of Refrain 1 and **walks out. It never returns.**
   ⛔ Not in the bridge, not in the outro, not as a ghost, not as a pad.
3. **KEEP POINTING (RULING C1).** The bass keeps playing the call and **keeps leaving the answer slot
   open at the top of every cycle**, with only the spring-reverb tail of the previous bar in it. The
   listener's body arrives at the top of the bar; the answer does not; the body arrives anyway. **The
   gap is in tempo and on the strong position, which is where it is most inconvenient** — the song has
   handed its tune to the bass and the bass plays it with a bite out of it for two-thirds of the record.

**And it is named in the lyric, in words, ONCE, in Verse 2** — plainly, in the same voice as the joke,
**and the band does not slow down.** ⛔ Never named twice; ⛔ never in the last verse.

## FORM MAP — shared skeleton, 108 BPM, nine sections, eighty sung lines

| # | section | sung lines | what enters / leaves — **one instrument at a time** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Intro** | 4 | bass figure alone, two cycles → **the named instrument** answers on top, two cycles → one-drop kit enters (rim on three, **no snare on beat one**) → vocal |
| 2 | **Verse 1** | 12 | skank guitar enters on the offbeat, dry, through tape echo; named instrument still leading between vocal phrases |
| 3 | **Refrain 1** | 8 | stacked thirds enter on the vocal; named instrument plays its last phrase and **exits at the section's end** |
| 4 | **Verse 2** | 12 | ⭐ **the answer slot is empty from here on.** Lazily-played synth pads enter behind the beat, hand-played, chords a half-beat late |
| 5 | **Refrain 2** | 8 | harmony stack widens; **one moment where the two voices split and the room hears the gap open** |
| 6 | **Bridge / Chant** | 8 | percussion only enters here: dry dead-centre handclaps + one shaker; tape echo opened on the last word of the section |
| 7 | **Verse 3** | 12 | dub space: everything except bass, kit and voice drops to the reverb tank for two bars and comes back; **no new pitched instrument enters after this point** |
| 8 | **Refrain 3** | 8 | full stack, no lift, no key change, **no added instrument** — the density is identical to Refrain 2 by design |
| 9 | **Outro** | 8 | strip back one at a time — pads out, guitar out, claps out — until it is the bass figure **with the hole in it**, one tape-echo repeat, and stop |

⭐ **THE HUM TEST.** The hook you hum is the **bass figure**, not the vocal. It exists **before the vocal
enters** (bass alone, two cycles, bar one) and **after the vocal leaves** (bass alone with the hole,
outro). ⛔ A pad is not a riff; the pads never carry the tune. When the named instrument goes, the bass
does not gain a note to compensate — it keeps the call and keeps the answer empty.

**Production constants, all four:** drums **+8 to +14 ms late**, bass **on** the grid; bass **−2 to 0 dB**
against the lead vocal, never buried; skank guitar is a **three-note figure, not a chord wall**; handclaps
**dry and dead-centre**; **stacked harmony is a required instrument**; lead vocal close, dynamic mic,
**no de-esser on the breaths**, **no transparent pitch correction**; doubles misaligned **15–35 ms, ±6
cents**; **−14 LUFS with ≥10 dB crest**. ⛔ No riser, no filter that only opens, no drive, no supersaw.

**Source 2, as production behaviour:** ***"lazily-played synth pads"*** — hand-played, entering a
half-beat behind the change, never quantised, never swelling. ***"detuned bliss"*** — pads six cents off
the bass, doubles ±6 cents. ⛔ Neither phrase appears in any lyric.

# GUIDE V1 — **WHOSE COAT** · the inventory of a borrowed pocket

**Instrument that leaves:** ⭐ **MELODICA** — close-mic'd, breathy, one player, the blow audible.
**Appetite verb:** ⭐ **SHE EATS.** She finds a half packet of strong mints in Marnie's coat pocket and
takes one, then another, then leaves the rest as a deal she invents on the spot.
**Vowel chain:** the round **"o"**.
**Hook FUNCTION:** ⭐ **A CLAIM** — she declares a borrowed thing hers for the length of the hour.
Declarative, possessive, four syllables, chantable out of breath.

**Section-by-section, one instrument at a time:**
- **Intro** — bass figure alone (two cycles) · **melodica** answers on top (two cycles) · one-drop kit ·
  vocal. SFX cue: a door over a mat.
- **Verse 1** — skank guitar enters. The pocket inventory begins with the coat itself and ends on the
  **appetite verb**: she takes one mint, then another, and is not sorry.
- **Refrain 1** — thirds enter. **The melodica plays its last phrase and exits at the end of this
  section.**
- **Verse 2** — ⭐ **the absence is NAMED here, in words, once:** the melodica played the whole of that
  and has gone, it is not coming back, and she liked it while it was on. Pads enter behind the beat.
  ⭐ **The sung fact lands here:** a folded newspaper in the right-hand pocket, *one thousand seven
  hundred and six* meteors counted in one night — and **her response**, which is the point: she will
  take their word for it, because she was not out there, she was in a warm room with her hands in a coat
  that is not hers. ⭐ **The reveal engine is stated here too:** nothing is wrong, nothing has gone
  wrong, that is the whole of the news.
- **Refrain 2** — the split-harmony moment.
- **Bridge** — claps and shaker; the praise widens from the pocket to the room; she is *attending* the
  machine, very closely.
- **Verse 3 (⛔ FORBIDDEN SECTION — no thesis here)** — the list continues: thread, sand in a seam, a key
  she puts back. **Closing move:** she leaves the last of the mints as a deal she made up on the spot,
  stands in front of the dryer with her arms open for the minutes it has left, and goes home the long
  way in somebody else's coat.
- **Refrain 3** — byte-identical, no lift.
- **Outro** — strip to the bass with the hole; the hook twice; stop.

# GUIDE V2 — **THE MACHINE THAT TAKES POUNDS** · the room's mechanics, loved out loud

**Instrument that leaves:** ⭐ **TENOR SAXOPHONE** — one horn, mic'd in the room, not a section.
**Appetite verb:** ⭐ **SHE SPENDS.** She puts a pound into an **empty** machine, with nothing in it, to
buy heat she does not need and hear it start.
**Vowel chain:** **"ow"**.
**Hook FUNCTION:** ⭐ **A CHEER FOR A TRANSACTION** — the coin going in, celebrated as an event, verb
first. ⛔ Different grammatical mood from V1's claim.

**Section-by-section:**
- **Intro** — bass figure alone · **tenor sax** answers · kit · vocal. SFX cue: a coin dropping through
  a slot.
- **Verse 1** — skank guitar. Twelve mechanical facts, praised by name; the verse ends by loading the
  gun: there is a pound in this coat and nothing left to wash.
- **Refrain 1** — thirds. **The sax takes its last phrase and leaves at the section's end.**
- **Verse 2** — ⭐ **the absence NAMED once:** the saxophone had the tune for the first bit, then it went,
  and she is not going to pretend it is coming back. Pads enter late. ⭐ **The appetite verb executes
  here** — pound in, machine empty, door shut with the hip, and it starts. ⭐ **The sung fact lands
  here**, from the radio on the shelf, and **her response** is *good for them, I have not looked up once
  and I am not going to* — she prefers the drum. ⭐ **Reveal engine:** nothing is wrong, nothing is
  happening, that is the entire event.
- **Refrain 2** — split-harmony moment.
- **Bridge is a CHANT here, not a bridge** — claps and shaker only, three-word phrases, the most
  room-ownable eight lines in the song. ⛔ Structural difference from V1, deliberate.
- **Verse 3 (⛔ forbidden)** — the loose coin ticking in machine two that nobody removes; half two;
  **closing move:** the empty machine finishes on its own, the room goes quiet with the heat still in
  it, and she puts the side of her face on the warm glass and stays there. One pound left in the other
  pocket, and it can wait.
- **Refrain 3** — byte-identical.
- **Outro** — best pound she has spent all year; strip to the bass with the hole; stop.

# GUIDE V3 — **THE OTHER PERSON IN HERE** · two strangers, one hour, nothing required

**Instrument that leaves:** ⭐ **TROMBONE** — slide audible, phrased like a second person answering.
**Appetite verb:** ⭐ **SHE PULLS A STRANGER UP.** Hand out, one song, on the lino, at two in the
morning — and the invitation contains its own release, so nobody owes anybody anything afterwards.
**Vowel chain:** **"ee-ih"**.
**Hook FUNCTION:** ⭐ **AN INVITATION WITH THE RELEASE ALREADY IN IT** — imperative, two clauses, the
second one frees you. ⛔ The only imperative hook in the pair.

**Section-by-section:**
- **Intro** — bass figure alone · **trombone** answers · kit · vocal. SFX cue: a bike frame clicking.
- **Verse 1** — skank guitar. Praise for a stranger's method, item by item, none of it said to him. The
  verse establishes the trombone in words as *the best thing in here*, which is what makes losing it
  cost something.
- **Refrain 1** — thirds. **The trombone finishes its phrase and leaves.**
- **Verse 2** — ⭐ **absence NAMED once:** it finished the line it was on and went; she waited; it does
  not come back at all. Pads enter behind the beat. ⭐ **Sung fact** arrives *from him* — he reads it
  out of his paper — and **her response** is to ask whether he went out to look; he did not; neither
  did she; the warm room won. ⭐ **Reveal engine:** nothing is wrong with either of us and nothing is
  going to be. The verse ends with the radio changing its mind and putting a bassline on.
- **Refrain 2** — split-harmony moment.
- **Bridge — ⭐ THE DANCE.** Claps and shaker enter here and they are the only new instruments in the
  song. She is up before she has decided to be up; hand out; he puts the pen in the spiral and gets up,
  and he can move. Nobody is watching. **One song. That was the deal and she meant it.** He sits back
  down; nothing is owed.
- **Verse 3 (⛔ forbidden)** — the list continues: he folds, she steals the method. **Closing move:** he
  clicks the bike open with a knee, says goodnight and only goodnight, the cold gets as far as the
  second chair, and it is her and nine machines with one still going.
- **Refrain 3** — byte-identical.
- **Outro** — nobody asked her for a thing all night and she gave one away anyway, and it cost nothing.
  Bass with the hole; stop.

# GUIDE V4 — **WALKING BACK** · the road at two-forty, and the sky in August

**Instrument that leaves:** ⭐ **FLUTE** — wooden, breath audible on the edge, close.
**Appetite verb:** ⭐ **SHE TAKES THE LONG WAY.** She chooses the hill road home because it adds a mile
under an open sky, and she is not tired, and she has decided she is not tired.
**Vowel chain:** **"eye"**.
**Hook FUNCTION:** ⭐ **A REFUSAL TO END, STATED AS A PLAN** — first person, future, already decided.
⛔ Not a wish and not an imperative; different mood from all three others.

**Section-by-section:**
- **Intro** — bass figure alone · **flute** answers · kit · vocal. SFX cue: a shop light cutting out.
  ⭐ This is the only variation that starts *leaving* the room, so the bass figure gets a walking feel
  under it from the first bar — same figure, same tempo, ⛔ no arrangement gear-change.
- **Verse 1** — skank guitar. The road's inventory: extractor, bins, cat, dead streetlight, taxi, her own
  breath. The verse states plainly that nobody in the world knows where she is standing, **and that this
  is the nicest thing in it** — the loneliness door is shut inside the same breath it opens.
- **Refrain 1** — thirds. **The flute plays its last phrase and goes out into the air. It never returns.**
- **Verse 2** — ⭐ **absence NAMED once:** the flute had the tune the whole way down the road and has
  stopped; she has stopped waiting for it. Pads enter late. ⭐ **Sung fact** is remembered, not
  delivered — she read it somewhere — and **her response** is the only outward-facing one of the four:
  she has not seen one, she is not owed one, **she is looking anyway.** ⭐ **Reveal engine:** nothing has
  gone wrong tonight, and that is the entire thing that happened. ⭐ **Appetite verb executes here:** the
  hill road adds a mile, so she takes the hill road.
- **Refrain 2** — split-harmony moment.
- **Bridge** — claps and shaker; allotments, runner beans, one lit kitchen; the road tips over the top
  and the valley opens; **and she stops. In the middle of the road.**
- **Verse 3 (⛔ forbidden — the thesis already landed in Verse 2)** — nothing for a long while; a plane,
  which is not what she is here for; then **one goes, right across, gone before she said it** — an
  event, not a thesis: *that is the one I get, and it will do.* **Closing move:** the corner, the gate,
  her own door, the key turns — and she stands outside it a minute longer, because she can, and then
  goes in, and the night was hers, and she had all of it.
- **Refrain 3** — byte-identical.
- **Outro** — the coat goes back on the hook tomorrow; tonight it is on her. Bass with the hole; stop.
