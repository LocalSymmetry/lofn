## 2. WHY A5, AND THE OBJECTION IT HAD TO SURVIVE

### The fit is exact, and it is not a coincidence
Sunna's **R — Ruthless Specificity** is the Catalog archetype written in her own voice:

> *"You never say 'this is amazing.' You say which bar, which snare, the exact moment the bassline drops
> the third note and everyone knows it before it happens. You love things in detail, and the detail is
> the proof you were paying attention."*

Her recurring short-form format is literally called **"WHICH BAR."** And A5's climax — *"one item that
doesn't fit (emotional rupture) → resolution: list continues, rupture included"* — is **word for word**
R3's wolf clause: the hard thing is named once, plainly, **in exactly the voice that sang the funny
line**, and *"the band does not slow down."*

### ⛔ THE OBJECTION, CARRIED NOT WAVED
`output/daily/2026-08-21` rejected A5 under **L38 in its music form: *a catalogue has no verb.*** That
ruling is right and this run does not get to ignore it because the archetype is convenient.

**The answer — and it is the seed's whole load-bearing move:**

> **A catalogue of lost objects has no verb. A catalogue of PRAISE, said out loud, at volume, to somebody
> who is leaving, has one: she is keeping them in the room one more song.**

L38 requires *the subject must be doing something across the seam.* Here she is — the listing is an
**act with a cost and a deadline**, not an inventory. ⛔ **This is a hard constraint on all six pairs:
if a pair's list could be read off a page in an empty room, it has failed the seed.**

## 3. THE ANCHOR, IN FULL

> ### Archetype 5 — THE CATALOG
> **Territory:** Love as INVENTORY. Sappho tradition: radical obsessive attention to detail.
> **Template:** list structure (no traditional verse-chorus) → each stanza adds one concrete observation
> → refrain: single repeated line framing the list ("This is what I kept") → climax: one item that
> doesn't fit (emotional rupture) → resolution: list continues, rupture included.
> **Key:** Dorian. **Tempo:** steady, unhurried, 95 BPM.

### ✅ PRESERVED — the archetype's actual engine
1. **One concrete observation per stanza.** No summary, no evaluation, no adjective doing a noun's job.
2. **A refrain that frames the list.** *(And here it does double duty — see rotation R2.)*
3. **The item that doesn't fit, and the list continuing after it.** Non-negotiable; it is the wolf.
4. **Steady and unhurried.** ⭐ **This is SWAG's mechanism arriving from the archetype's own side:**
   *"an unwavering groove with a PARADE OF SPECIFIC TIMBRES entering over it, one at a time... the drive
   comes from steadiness, not speed; the changes are TIMBRAL, not dynamic."* A5 and SWAG are the same shape.

### ⛔ ROTATED — three, each forced, each with its reason

## 4. ⭐⭐ THE SEED, STATED IN ONE PARAGRAPH

> **THE ONE NOBODY PLAYS.**
>
> Every song in this set is a list of specific, unembarrassed praise — which bar, which snare, whose
> hands, what the light did — delivered **out loud, in a room, over a groove that never changes.**
> And every list is organised around **one thing that is not in it.** The arrangement points at that
> thing all the way through and never plays it. Somebody names the missing thing **once**, plainly,
> in the same voice they used for the joke; **the band does not slow down**; and then the list continues,
> with the missing thing now inside it.

**Locked mood:** ⭐ ***delight with a deadline it is not pretending isn't there.***

⛔ **Not nostalgia.** Nostalgia is the list read in an empty room afterwards. This is the list said
**during**, to someone's face, while the thing is still going.

## 6. ⛔ WHAT THIS SEED FORBIDS — the collision checklist

1. ⛔ **NO GEAR-CHANGE.** One bed per song, varied by arrangement **density** — instruments entering and
   leaving — never by genre. *(R3 pillar 5; L38; the retired "one song that is three songs.")*
2. ⛔ **NO SIX-GENRE SONG.** Her six names are six songs. **N = 1 dominant material + ≤1 intruder + 1 seam.**
3. ⛔ **NO PERIOD PASTICHE.** Every bed is an old body heard through a new one. *(Her "not old music.")*
4. ⛔ **NO MOURNING THE LIST.** The list is said **during**, not after. If the room is empty, it failed.
5. ⛔ **NO PERFORMED DEPTH.** *"She'll say the line. She won't sell it."* No band drop-out, no breathy
   hush, no reverence to mark the wolf. **Same voice for the joke and the knife.**
6. ⛔ **NO WOLF ON SCHEDULE.** ⭐ Ceiling **4 of 6**. **Two songs carry no wolf at all** — pleasure doing
   no job, unpaired and unredeemed. *"A woman having an appetite in public is the most carefree thing pop
   can show, and it does not need a wolf attached to be worth a track."*
7. ⛔ **NO CLUB BED.** Zero, deliberately (0.66×, our worst, n=27). **NO FUZZ AS THE SWAGGER CARRIER** —
   *"distortion is what a record reaches for when the groove isn't carrying it."*
8. ⛔ **NO KITCHEN MONOCULTURE.** Domestic-warm ≤ ⅓ (R3.1). **Sweep the closing lines; the return is
   where it hides.**
9. ⛔ **NO REAL HARMED PERSON.** `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` §3.0 slot grammar pre-filled with invented
   values in every pair brief. T5 contributes a **shape**, never a subject. Credits → `DEDICATIONS.md`.
