## 3. TITLE

Louder Than The Rain

## DRAFT DEFECTS ALREADY VISIBLE

- **Chorus, fifth line** — *"It costs them nowt and it costs us nowt"* is muddled: the point is that it
  costs **them** nothing, and the second half undoes the first. The dialect word also shuts out half
  the room the hook is supposed to belong to.
- **Chorus, second and fourth lines** — *thing / sing* is the softest available rhyme in the softest
  available register, and *"Listen to it sing"* asks the room to listen instead of to sing.
- **Bridge, last line** — *"done it twice"* is another count.
- **Verse 4, sixth line** — *"the pair"* is a count, and it is also unclear.

## RUNNING NOTE FOR STEP 09

Four defects repeat across all four variations and they are the same defect: **the verse architecture's
stressed-monosyllable line ending recruits numerals**, because *six, two, twice, once, third, pair,
dozen, three* are all stressed monosyllables that fit anywhere. This slot is allowed exactly one sung
number. Step 09 removes every one of them except *two hours old*, and does it without loosening the
line-ending rule.
