# PAIR 04 — STEP 07 · SONG GUIDES
## SLOT P04 · **118 BPM exactly** · one bed, one tempo, one key, no gear-change, four songs

**Bar arithmetic used throughout:** 118 BPM, 4/4 → **one bar = 2.034 s**. Bar *N* begins at
`(N−1) × 2.034 s`. Every timing below is derived, not estimated.

---

## 0. WHAT ALL FOUR SHARE — and it is architecture, never wording

- **BED:** offbeat guitar skank riding a dusty boom-bap break, sun-bleached, dub tape-echo at line ends.
- **KEY:** A major throughout, all four. No modulation anywhere.
- **THE ANSWER IS THE TAIL OF THE CALL.** Second voice hands back the last syllables of the line she
  just sang. Taught twice, then shortened, then stopped.
- **SHRINK:** four syllables → three → two → **none, permanently.**
- ⛔ **THE DUB DROP-OUTS NEVER LAND ON AN ANSWER BAR.** They fall on line *ends* inside her own phrases.
  The band plays **straight through** every empty bar, every time.
- ⭐ **THE CHIMING SYNTH RIFF** — three notes, a few cents sharp, is the tune-carrier. It plays **alone
  before the first vocal** and **alone after the last**, and it lands on the downbeat immediately after
  every empty answer bar, fencing the hole on its right-hand side.
- **THE BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS, stated precisely:** ⭐ **her four call lines are byte-identical in every
  chorus of every variation, first to last.** The answering voice is a *separate performer part*; it is
  present in the early choruses and absent after the stop bar. **The chorus does not change. The second
  singer leaves.** That is B1 written into the lyric sheet itself, and it needs no further defence.

## ⛔ STANDING PRODUCTION LAW FOR ALL FOUR

Drums **+8 to +14 ms late**, bass **on** the grid, bass **−2 to 0 dB** against the lead · brass/melodica
stabs short and **unreverbed** · handclaps dry, dead centre · **stacked harmony is an instrument** and its
split is a scored event · lead vocal close on a dynamic mic, **no de-esser on the breaths, no pitch
correction**, doubles misaligned 15–35 ms and ±6 cents · **−14 LUFS, ≥10 dB crest** · ⛔ **no standing
tension lane** — no riser that never resolves, no filter that only opens · ⛔ **no distortion carrying the
groove**, none anywhere · ⛔ **no tempo change, no key change, no half-time, no drop** · ⭐ **the answer
bars are the only arrangement gap in the record.**
