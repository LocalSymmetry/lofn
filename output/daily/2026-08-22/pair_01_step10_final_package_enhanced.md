## DEDICATION

```
The body of this record is the American shuffle — the arrangement Jamaican sound systems were importing through the nineteen-fifties, where the offbeat guitar chop was still incidental. We asked for that figure by name, so we name the sides it was riding on: Fats Domino, "Be My Guest"; Barbie Gaye, "My Boy Lollypop". Players heard the decoration and made it the engine. Generated, not sampled — no recording of theirs is in this. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.
```
