## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- **The instrument that leaves is a WOODEN FLUTE.** It is the only one of the four made of moving air,
  which is why it gets the variation that walks out of the building: it does not stop so much as go out
  into the sky the rest of the song is under. It leads Intro, Verse 1 and Refrain 1 and **never returns**.
- **The appetite verb is TAKING THE LONG WAY.** She chooses the hill road because it adds a mile, and
  she is not tired, and she has decided she is not tired. It costs her sleep and a mile and she pays
  both. ⛔ The decision is made **on stage in Verse 2** — RULING A1: *it was a decision, not an accident.*
- **The meteor in the last verse is an EVENT, not the thesis.** The thesis — *nothing has gone wrong
  tonight, that is the entire thing that happened* — lands in **Verse 2**. ⛔ The forbidden section for
  this slot is the last verse and it stays empty of argument: what happens there is one streak, a laugh
  on an empty road, and a key that turns.
- **RETURN DEBT — paid.** **(a)** byte-identical eight-line refrain, three times. **(b)** the **"eye"**
  chain — *night · sky · mile · side · quiet · time · line · high · mine* — through every long line,
  codas varied so no two line-ends rhyme. Verse lines were shortened for the one-breath rule and **every
  cut avoided the chain word**: where a trim would have taken one, a different clause went instead.
- **Changed since Step 08, and it is the pair's most important edit:** the draft answered *"nobody knows
  where I am standing"* with *"that is not a sad thing."* ⛔ **A line that says "this is not the sad
  thing" has already made it the sad thing** — and this slot carries **no dark line, on any schedule, in
  any variation.** Solitude is now stated once and immediately **claimed as property**, the same move as
  V1's coat and V2's pound, which turns the pair's most exposed moment from a defence into an appetite.
