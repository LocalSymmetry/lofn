# PAIR 03 — STEP 08 · GENERATION (first full draft, all four variations)
## `daily-music-2026-08-22-sunna-offbeat` · SLOT P03 · launderette, two in the morning

**Status: DRAFT.** These are first passes written straight through against the Step 07 guides. They are
deliberately not yet run through THE FLAT TEST, THE HUM TEST or THE REMARK TEST — that is Step 09's job,
and the weak lines are left in on purpose so the refinement pass has something real to remove.

**Known problems already visible in this draft, flagged for Step 09:**
1. **V1** carries two metaphor lines that will not survive being said flatly to a person's face.
2. **V2** has a line that is going to make somebody say *"that was clever"* — a toll, and it is in the
   bridge where the room is supposed to be chanting.
3. **V3**'s outro re-names the trombone. ⛔ RULING X says the absence is named **once**. That is a
   straight violation and has to go.
4. **V4**'s draft is long — the verses are running past one breath, and the lyrics field will not fit.
5. Line counts are not yet measured; several sections are approximate.

## DRAFT MUSIC PROMPT DIRECTION (all four, to be written properly in Step 10)

One dense prose paragraph each. Leads with genre + tempo + vocalist + instrumentation; states the bass
as the lead instrument; states the named instrument leading the first section and **leaving and never
returning**; states the answer slot staying empty at the top of every cycle; carries both Source-2
phrases verbatim as production behaviour; ends on terminal punctuation. ⛔ No drive, no key change, no
period-pastiche markers, no riser.

## DRAFT EXCLUDE DIRECTION

Comma-separated. Must blacklist, in every variation: **any re-entry of the departed instrument**, any
substitute lead filling the empty answer slot, any minor-key or sad turn at the end, any tempo or key
change, any drive or fuzz, any vintage-recreation marker, any toasting or put-on accent, and any
build-and-drop structure.
