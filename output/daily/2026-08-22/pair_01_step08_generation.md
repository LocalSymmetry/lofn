# V1 — I Did Not Do This In A Hurry

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Jump blues shuffle at a hundred and two BPM in B-flat, swung hard, the first eighth long at about two and a half to one the way a slow tempo swings. Opens on eight bars of band alone: upright bass walking, wire brushes on a coated snare, piano comping off the beat, and a three-piece horn section stating the twelve-bar turnaround twice with the five chord. Lead is a warm northern English alto, full chest, close on a dynamic mic, breaths left in. Twirling acoustic guitars, dry and close. Horn stabs answer the third line of every couplet and get out inside the bar. Handclaps dead centre, tambourine late, girl-group thirds stacked on the hook. Drums sit eight to fourteen milliseconds behind the bass. Restrained and tasteful throughout, warm and bittersweet, no reverence, no hush at the sad line, no reverb tail, no key change. Everything stays at one tempo from the first bar to the last one.

## 1A. RAW-PROMPT DEFECTS

**⛔ DEFECTS (D12, D13):** *restrained*, *tasteful*, *warm and bittersweet* are mood adjectives standing
where an operation belongs. Four literal negations (*no reverence, no hush, no reverb tail, no key
change*) sit in the **style** field where the generator reads the nouns positively. And the whole
harmonic event — the five chord never returning — is stated as **history** rather than as an instruction
about the rest of the record.
