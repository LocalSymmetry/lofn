# 04 — THE DESIGN METAPROMPT
## `daily-music-2026-08-22-sunna-offbeat`

*The instruction the run gives itself, after the seed and the panel and before any concept exists.*

---

## THE ONE-PARAGRAPH BRIEF

> Write six songs that are **lists of specific, unembarrassed praise, said out loud, in a room, against
> a clock** — and build each one on a groove that **never changes**, with the changes arriving as
> **timbres entering one at a time**. Each song is organised around **one thing that is played first,
> then taken away, and pointed at for the rest of the record.** Somebody names that thing **once**, in
> plain words, **in exactly the voice that sang the funny line**, and **the band does not slow down.**
> The six beds come from one family — **the offbeat**: swing's late eighth, ska's upstroke, reggae's
> deleted one, Sublime's skank over boom-bap, the hybrid riff syncopated against a straight kick, and
> brass landing just off the grid. **One bed per song. One intruder. One seam. Nobody plays the one.**

---

## THE FIVE INSTRUCTIONS, IN PRIORITY ORDER

**1. THE GROOVE DOES NOT CHANGE. THE TIMBRES DO.**
This is SWAG, mechanised, and it is her definition: *"a cohesion of music, theme, tone and lyric that
flow perfectly."* ⛔ **Not tempo change. Not speed.** The canon proves both: *The List of Ways You Tell
Me No* runs 100 BPM end to end with no ramp, no key change, no drop, and carries **five distinct
textural events**. ⛔ **Cohesion is the opposite of episodic.** One thing, unwavering, with events
arriving on top. **No gear-change. No genre-shift. No dynamic swell doing a timbre's job.**

**2. CAREFREE GOES IN THE MUSIC. THE HARD THING GOES IN THE WORDS.**
`MOOD: carefree → the MUSIC. the wolf → the WORDS.` `LOAD: complexity → the MUSIC. plain → the WORDS.`
The hard thing is **an event with a day and an object and a person**, it is **said plainly**, and
**it resolves**. ⛔ No band drop-out to mark it. No breathy hush. No reverence. ⭐ **She'll say the
line. She won't sell it.** Two of the six carry no hard thing at all — **appetite, unpaired,
unredeemed, on its own terms.**

**3. STATE IT, THEN TAKE IT. (RULING B1 — the run's hardest constraint.)**
⛔ **You cannot drop what was never established.** The missing thing is **played first**, for at least
two full cycles of its figure, then removed, and the arrangement **keeps pointing at where it was**.
A hole that exists from bar one is *nothing there*, not *something taken*. **No hole in the first
30 seconds. The hole must be inconvenient** — it takes something the song needed.

**4. THE ONE IS UNPLAYED AND COMPLETELY PRESENT. (RULING C1.)**
The chop on the "and" only works because the downbeat is implied hard enough to stand on. **Every pair
states in one line how the listener's body still feels the missing thing.** ⛔ A pair that cannot answer
this is floating and gets **cut, not repaired**.

**5. EVERY UNMEASURABLE CLAIM CASHES OUT. (RULING X.)**
We have a character counter, a line counter and a similarity score. We have **no instrument** for an
absence, for microtiming, or for idiomatic depth. So:
- the **absence** → **named in the lyric, in words, once** *(readable)*
- the **strut** → **a named instrument entering or leaving at a named point** *(readable)*
- the **idiom** → **survives genre transplant** *(testable — RULING C2)*
⛔ **Answering any of these only in the PRODUCTION SPEC is not answering it** (L22, THE GRAIN LAW).

## THE GRAMMAR OF THE SET — what varies, and how

| axis | count | rule |
|---|---|---|
| **BED** | 6 | one per song, **no two shared**, from her six names |
| **SETTING** | 6 | room · hour · weather · bodies · what is in her hands. **No two shared. Domestic-warm ≤ 2 of 6.** |
| **KIND OF ABSENCE** | 6 | a different **architectural level** each: harmonic · rhythmic · timbral · performer · lyric · spatial. ⛔ **Never a duration** *(that was yesterday's grammar)* |
| **VERSE ARCHITECTURE** | 6 | no two share a stanza shape or a rhyme logic |
| **REVEAL ENGINE** | 6 | what makes the song turn — no two shared |
| **FORBIDDEN SECTION** | 6 | the one section where the thesis may **not** land — no two shared |
| **SUNG FACT** | 6 | exactly **one** per song, **spelled out in words**, **responded to, never recited** |
| **WOLF** | 4 max | ≥2 songs carry **none** |
| **MODE** | — | ≥1 AWE, ≥1 INDIGNATION |
| **TEMPO** | 6 | all distinct, all inside the measured 100–129 band |

⭐ **ASSIGN THE KIND, NEVER THE GRAMMAR (R3.2 rule 3).** *"A radiant kind is a target. The same kind
handed to four variations becomes a sentence shape."* ⛔ **If an assignment can be satisfied by filling
in a blank, it is a grammar and it will be templated.** Nothing in `05` specifies a line, a hook's
words, or a closing sentence. Pairs write those from their own concept, alone.

## WHAT THIS RUN IS TRYING TO PROVE

Two things, and both are falsifiable on disk:

1. ⭐ **That "carefree" and "means something" are one instruction, not a compromise between two.**
   Her sentence — *"carefree fun that really actually means something"* — is R3's diagnosis restated:
   the music carries the carefree, the words carry the weight, and **neither one is diluted to make room
   for the other.** ⛔ The failure mode is a bright record with a sad bridge. That is the compromise, and
   it is banned.
2. ⭐ **That six idioms sharing one rhythmic property can make one record rather than six costumes.**
   The Purist's objection stands until **RULING C2** answers it. **Both the HOLE-SWAP and the
   GENRE-TRANSPLANT results go in `INDEX.md` whether they pass or not.**
