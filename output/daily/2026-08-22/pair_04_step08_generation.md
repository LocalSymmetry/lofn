# PAIR 04 — STEP 08 · GENERATION (FIRST FULL DRAFT)

**This is the draft, not the package.** Four complete lyrics, written against the Step 07 guides.
Known defects are left standing here on purpose so that Step 09 has something real to repair and the
repair record is honest. Step 10 carries the fixed text.

⛔ **Slot grammar, re-checked before writing a line:** the stand-in is **Della**, invented, who ran the
desk. The one on the sleeve is unnamed, invented, alive, and fine. The band is never named. The record
is never titled. The coast is unnamed. **He had shouted his voice away over a bad PA and the room was
booked for one more afternoon** — an ordinary invented reason. **Nobody dies in any of these songs.**

### DRAFT DEFECTS FOUND ON THE READ-BACK
1. `Two lads on the wall` — a **second numeral** in a song allowed exactly one. Must go.
2. `His mate holds the towel and takes the can` — **collides with V4**, where the warm can is hers and
   is spent exactly once. The prop cannot be taken here.
3. `And the low note goes and nobody minds. / NOBODY MINDS.` — the answer is **four syllables inside a
   two-syllable round.** The schedule breaks on the last couplet before the hole, which is the worst
   possible place for it.
4. `I don't say any of it out loud` — she has just sung all of it. The line contradicts its own verse.
5. `The gulls have got the chips off the wall` (V2) and `The gulls go up off the wall at once` (V3) —
   same bird, same wall, twice.
