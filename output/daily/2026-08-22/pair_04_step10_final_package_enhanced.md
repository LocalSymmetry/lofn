
## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- **The response stops mid-verse at bar 69 (2:18), not at a section boundary** — the only variation in
  the pair that does this. It goes one line after `She hasn't got the air for the second half`, so the
  hole and the body's reason for it arrive in the same breath. Ruling C1 is not answered in the
  production spec here; it is answered by the lyric and the bar count together.
- **The warm can she did not buy is spent here and only here.** It is the pair's one carried object,
  present in all four songs and given away once, in the last verse of the last variation, to the person
  who has just been in the water. No other variation is allowed to touch it.
- **`Nobody stood in for anybody. Somebody stood up.`** is the pair's thesis line and it lands in Verse 4,
  never in an opening verse. It is sung in exactly the voice that sang `there's tar on the sand in the
  shape of a foot`, over an unchanged band.
- **Refused: a gasp, a shiver, a breath effect in the empty bars.** The excludes ban it explicitly. The
  cold is described in the lyric and played by the baritone sax; it is never sound-designed into the hole,
  because anything in the hole is not a hole.
