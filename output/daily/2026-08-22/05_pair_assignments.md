## THE SLOTS

### SLOT P01 — ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · AWE · **SWING**
**Concept territory:** A function room being set up for a party at four in the afternoon. She has the
seating plan. One seat on it is for somebody who is not coming, and everybody knows, and the party is
going to be **good anyway** — which is the thing she cannot stop noticing and will not apologise for.
**The wolf:** an event with a day and an object — *the card with the name on it, and what she does with
it.* It **resolves**: she does something specific, once, and the band does not slow down.
**The absence, harmonically:** the twelve-bar goes home without the five. Stated twice in the intro so
the ear owns it, then never again. **The horns answer the hanging third line of every couplet — except
one, and that is where the wolf goes.**
**Variation angles — derived from THIS concept only:**
- **V1 · THE PLAN** — she is reading the chart aloud as praise: table by table, who is good at what.
- **V2 · THE ROOM AT FOUR** — before anyone arrives; the light, the stacked chairs, the smell of the
  carpet, the band soundchecking one line over and over.
- **V3 · THE FIRST DANCE** — the song that gets played, and why that particular record, and who chose it.
- **V4 · THE CLEAR-UP** — 1am, the room going back to being a room; what is left on the tables.

### SLOT P02 — ACCESSIBLE · NEWS · **INDIGNATION** · **SKA / TWO-TONE**
**Concept territory:** A coach on the hard shoulder at eleven at night in the rain, a bus-replacement
service for a line that got cancelled by people in a different building. Everyone on board is polite,
and the politeness is the indictment. ⛔ **Not a rant.** The skank is *"physically incompatible with
sounding angry"* — the anger is in the **words**, at dance tempo, in the same voice as the joke.
**NEWS anchor:** the shape of a negotiation walked away from, and who is standing outside when it is.
⛔ **§3.0 SLOT GRAMMAR PRE-FILLED:** PERSON = invented · PLACE = invented · WHEN = re-set. **No real
official, no real route, no real company.** The mechanism is the subject; the case is not.
**The absence, rhythmically:** the band plays a straight unison downbeat for two cycles at the top —
then it is gone for the whole record and only the upstroke remains. **The one is never played again and
the coach still moves.**
**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · THE DRIVER** — praise for somebody doing an impossible job correctly, said to his face.
- **V2 · THE BOARD** — the live-departure screen, wrong, bright, and the newest object present.
- **V3 · WHO SIGNED IT** — the decision, three buildings away, made by people who will never stand here.
- **V4 · THE SINGALONG** — what a full coach does at midnight when it stops being angry, and what that
  costs nobody upstairs.

### SLOT P03 — ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE · AWE · **REGGAE / ROCKSTEADY / DUB** · ⭐ **NO WOLF**
**Concept territory:** A launderette at two in the morning — the only lit shop on the road. She is
holding somebody else's coat. **Nothing is wrong.** This song is **pleasure doing no job**: warm
machines, a good hour, the specific luxury of being awake when nobody wants anything from you.
⭐ **BINDING: no dark line, on any schedule, in any variation.** *"A woman having an appetite in public
is the most carefree thing pop can show, and it does not need a wolf attached to be worth a track."*
⛔ **And it is not pleasantness.** Pelly's carried dissent lands here hardest: appetite is a **verb**.
She must **want something and take it.**
**The absence, timbrally:** an instrument leads the entire first section, then walks out and never comes
back. The bassline keeps leaving its gap open at the top of every cycle.
**RETURN DEBT DECLARED:** this slot drops end-rhyme in the verses. **The debt is paid by a byte-identical
refrain and a vowel-chain carried across every long line** — `lofn-music` L21: *removal is a debt; strip
rhyme only by naming what returns in its place.*
**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · WHOSE COAT** — the inventory of a borrowed pocket.
- **V2 · THE MACHINE THAT TAKES POUNDS** — the specific mechanical facts of the room, loved out loud.
- **V3 · THE OTHER PERSON IN HERE** — two strangers, one hour, nothing required.
- **V4 · WALKING BACK** — the road at two-forty, and the sky doing what the sky does in August.

### SLOT P04 — AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · AWE · **SUBLIME LINEAGE**
**Concept territory (from EXISTENCE prompt 3):** A car park above a beach on the last hot afternoon of
the year. A song comes on that everyone knows, and she knows something about it they don't — **the most
famous line on it was sung by somebody else.** Somebody stood in. Everybody sings along anyway. That is
not a scandal; it is **what a song is for**, and realising it is the AWE.
> ⛔ **HUMAN SUBJECT STANDARD, BINDING ON THIS SLOT.** This is T5's **SHAPE ONLY**. **Do not name, allude
> to, or reconstruct Sublime, Bradley Nowell, Michael Happoldt, or any real record.** PERSON = invented ·
> BAND = invented · PLACE = invented · WHEN = re-set. **The credit goes in `DEDICATIONS.md`, never in a
> style field, never in a lyric.** A pair brief that names a real musician is a hard fail, not a repair.
**The absence, at the performer level:** two voices trade for two full rounds; from a stated point only
the calls happen, and the response is left as a **bar-accurate silence the room fills.** The response
**shrinks** each round — four syllables, three, two, none.
**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · THE TAILGATE** — the specific praise of a bad speaker in a good car park.
- **V2 · WHO SANG IT** — the fact arriving; what she does with knowing.
- **V3 · THE ONES WHO SING BACK** — a hundred people and a hole in the middle of the hook.
- **V4 · SIXTY-SIX DEGREES** — somebody went in the water; what the cold did to the singing.

### SLOT P05 — AMBITIOUS · NEWS · **INDIGNATION** · **LINKIN-PARK LINEAGE** ⚠️ *the one drive slot*
**Concept territory:** A hospital stairwell at six in the morning, between floors. She has a lanyard
that isn't hers. Somebody was removed for **describing a hypothetical** — for saying out loud what would
happen *if*. The whole song is a list of praise for that person's actual competence, and **it will not
say their name**, because naming them is what the building wants: a name can be filed.
⛔ **§3.0 SLOT GRAMMAR PRE-FILLED:** PERSON = invented / function-only · PLACE = invented · WHEN = re-set.
⚠️ **THE BED IS OUR SECOND-WORST (0.73×, 0.62× young), TAKEN DELIBERATELY AND ONCE.**
⛔ **The drive is a COLOUR, never the engine** — *"swagger is a rhythm section, not a distortion pedal;
distortion is what a record reaches for when the groove isn't carrying it."* The engine is the
syncopated riff against a straight kick. **If the guitars carry this song, the slot has failed.**
⛔ **One voice, two registers.** The spoken tercets and the sung quatrains are **the same woman**, not a
rapper and a singer. Per R3: the talky register is **one colour**, and she gets back to singing.
**The absence, at the lyric level:** every person is their function — *the one on nights, whoever signed
it, the one who trained me.* Each function-word lands on the beat the name would have taken.
**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · THE HANDOVER** — praise as a shift report, item by item, at speed.
- **V2 · THE HYPOTHETICAL** — the sentence that got said, and the sentence that got them removed.
- **V3 · THE THIRTEENTH FLOOR** — counting upward in a building that skips one.
- **V4 · THE LANYARD** — an object that grants access it should not, and what she does not do with it.

### SLOT P06 — AMBITIOUS · NEWS · AWE · **NOGA-EREZ LINEAGE** · ⭐ **NO WOLF**
**Concept territory:** An office at seven in the evening being packed into boxes. Somewhere a very large
number was paid so that nothing would have to change. She is not going to cry about the office. **She is
going to have the best night of her working life in it** — the chairs pushed back, the stapler as a
percussion instrument, five people who will never work together again.
⭐ **NO WOLF. The news is the SETTING, not the wound.** The money is a fact she **responds to**, once,
and then declines to be sad about. ⛔ **The song does not redeem this, apologise for it, or turn at the
end into a sad one.** Its reveal is: *the settlement is the confession, and she takes the night anyway.*
⛔ **§3.0 SLOT GRAMMAR PRE-FILLED:** no real company, no real settlement, no real platform, no plaintiff,
**and no child anywhere in this slot** — the real case involves minors and the abstraction must go all
the way. The subject is **paying instead of changing**; the case is not.
**The absence, spatially:** lead vocal dead-centre for all of section one, then the centre is **vacated**
and she sings from the sides for the rest. The two sides are deliberately **not symmetrical**, so the ear
keeps hunting the middle. **Brass stabs answer and get out inside the bar — dry, unreverbed, dead-centre
— which means the horns are the only thing left in the hole.**
**Variation angles:**
- **V1 · FIVE THINGS FROM THE DRAWER** — list-stanzas; the fifth item is always a person.
- **V2 · THE NUMBER** — four hundred million, said once, answered rather than repeated.
- **V3 · THE LAST BADGE-IN** — the building's own record of her, and what it will keep.
- **V4 · WHO PUT THE MUSIC ON** — the specific person who made it a party, praised by name-as-function.
