Four song guides, written in isolation from step 06's facets and nothing else. Each guide carries: the

## 0. WHAT IS FIXED ACROSS ALL FOUR (the bed, not the song)

⛔ **THE ENGINE IS THE RHYTHM SECTION.** Fixed for the pair, never varied:

| element | instruction | never |
|---|---|---|
| **kick** | straight, one and three, unmoved from the first bar to the last | never syncopated, never dropped to mark anything |
| **guitar** | a **three-note figure** on the *and of two* and the *and of four*; a percussion instrument with pitches | never a chord wall, never a solo, never the melody |
| **bass** | **the tune.** Front of mix, −2 to 0 dB against the lead, moving between chords, on the grid | never root-only, never buried, never doubling the guitar figure |
| **programmed drums** | 8–14 ms late against the grid; dry claps dead centre; no fills that announce a section | no trap hats, no rolls, no riser into a chorus |
| **drive** | **one four-bar swell per song**, mixed **under** the bass, then gone | never the carrier, never in the chorus, never a solo |
| **vocal** | **one woman, two registers.** Full-chest sung quatrains; a clipped conversational register for the tercets, then back to singing | ⛔ never a rapper and a singer, never two characters, never a duet |
| **harmony** | stacked thirds on the hook, doubles misaligned 15–35 ms and ±6 cents; **one moment where two voices split and the room hears the gap open** | no choir, no pad-choir, no gospel stack |
| **the other voice** | **the overhead tannoy, one line, in the intro only.** Filtered, distant, an object and not a character | it never returns; nothing else speaks |

⭐ **THE HOLE, identical in kind across the four, different in placement:** the clean chiming figure is
stated **twice** in the intro and the tannoy page lands across the **second** statement. The figure then
returns **twice more** in the song and **the page never arrives on it** — a bar-accurate gap where an
announcement was. ⛔ **Both returns are after the thirty-second mark**, and the second one takes the bar
that had been carrying the loudest event in the arrangement. The hole is inconvenient by construction.

⛔ **No gear-change, no genre-shift, no tempo change, no key change, no dynamic swell doing a timbre's
job.** Everything that happens is an instrument arriving or leaving.

# V1 — SONG GUIDE · **"Say It Back To Me"**
### THE HANDOVER — praise as a shift report, item by item, at speed

**Key:** E Dorian. **Tempo:** 100 BPM. **Length target:** 3:40.
**Epithet grammar:** `The one who ___`. **Rhyme:** loose ABCB, rhyme carried on the second and fourth line.

### FORM MAP
`Intro (name) → Verse 1 → Tercet 1 → CHORUS → Verse 2 → Tercet 2 → CHORUS → Verse 3 (wolf + fact) → Tercet 3 (thesis) → CHORUS → Verse 4 → Outro`

Sung quatrains: V1 ×3 · V2 ×3 · V3 ×3 · V4 ×3. Spoken tercets: three. Chorus: eight lines, **byte-identical, three times.**

### SECTION BY SECTION — ONE INSTRUMENT AT A TIME

| bar | what enters or leaves | note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **clean chiming three-note guitar figure**, dead centre, alone | this is THE HUM TEST object; it exists before any voice and after the last one |
| 5 | **straight programmed kick** on one and three | nothing else; the figure is now against a floor |
| 9 | **melodic bass**, front of mix, walking between the chords | the tune arrives; the room now has something to hum |
| 9 | ⭐ **the overhead tannoy**, filtered and distant, across the **second** statement of the figure: **the name, once** | ⛔ said by the building, never by her, never again |
| 13 | **lead vocal**, close, full chest, dynamic mic, breaths in — **Verse 1** | the first epithet lands on the downbeat the name took |
| 13 | **dry claps** dead centre, on two and four | placement, not volume |
| 29 | **guitar drops to the syncopated riff** — *and of two, and of four* — **Tercet 1**, vocal to clipped register | the band does not thin; only the vocal changes gear |
| 37 | **stacked thirds** enter on the hook — **CHORUS 1** | the room's part |
| 45 | **tambourine** on the offbeat — **Verse 2** | timbre event 1 |
| 61 | **Tercet 2** | riff continues underneath, unchanged |
| 69 | **CHORUS 2** | ⭐ **at bar 76 the chime figure returns and the page does not arrive** — the hole, first pass |
| 77 | **sub-octave bass double** — **Verse 3.** ⭐ **THE WOLF and THE SUNG FACT both land here, sung, full band, no hush** | timbre event 2; the loudest the arrangement gets, and it gets there under the wolf, not after it |
| 93 | **Tercet 3 — the thesis**, spoken, flat | ⛔ thesis is forbidden in the chorus; this is its home in V1 |
| 101 | **CHORUS 3** | ⭐ **the chime figure returns again and the page does not arrive** — the hole, second pass, on the bar that carried the wolf's downbeat |
| 109 | **one four-bar swell of howling guitars**, panned wide, **mixed under the bass**, gone by bar 113 — **Verse 4** | timbre event 3; the entire drive budget for the song |
| 125 | **Outro** — hook, bass, kick, chime figure; guitars out first, then claps, then vocal | the figure is the last thing playing. Hard cut. |

### WHERE EVERYTHING LANDS
- **The name:** bar 9, overhead tannoy, one line — *"Would Halloran come down to the desk."* Never again.
- **The wolf:** Verse 3, sung. **"On the Tuesday they came down and took the badge."** It resolves inside its own quatrain: the paper bag, the thanks she meant, the car park, and the eight o'clock starting anyway.
- **The sung fact:** Verse 3, first quatrain — *the thirteenth floor*, **responded to**: she says there isn't one, and is told she is standing on it.
- **The thesis:** Tercet 3, spoken. ⛔ **Not in the chorus.**

### THE BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS (three times, no variation, no ad-lib)
```
Say it back to me.
Say it back to me.
Not the sheet. Your mouth. Out loud, where I can hear.
Say it back to me.
Say it back to me.
Everything I know is something somebody said here.
Say it back to me.
Say it back to me.
```

### ⭐ HOW THE BASS CARRIES THIS SONG
**The bass plays the hook's melody in full eight bars before the voice ever sings it and keeps playing it
underneath the spoken tercets — so the tune the room walks out with was learned from the bass, and the
guitar's only job all song is the click between the bass notes.**

# V3 — SONG GUIDE · **"Twelve, Fourteen"**

### ⭐ HOW THE BASS CARRIES THIS SONG
**The bass climbs — one scale step higher every sixteen bars, from its lowest note in the intro to its

guitar figure stays on exactly the same three notes from the first bar to the last.**
