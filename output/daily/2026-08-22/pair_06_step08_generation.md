# V1 — NOTHING IN HERE IS ANYBODY'S *(draft)*

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

```
Art-pop at 112 BPM in G Dorian, dry and physical, built on a hard programmed low end. It opens on a stapler struck four times dead centre, then a square-wave sub locked to the grid while the kit sits eight to fourteen milliseconds behind it. A palm-muted guitar chops sixteenths on the left, three notes and never a chord. One trumpet and one baritone sax play short unreverbed stabs dead centre that answer a line and stop inside the bar, stated twice before any voice arrives. Female lead close on a dynamic mic, breaths left in, sultry in the first verse and savage by the second without becoming a different woman, dead centre until the second verse and then hard to both sides for the rest. On that same downbeat a room hum is unplugged and glassy textures enter on the right only, a fifth above the brass and a sixteenth late, so the sides never match. ## DRAFT NOTES — what this pass knows is still wrong
```

Written now, before step 09, so the repairs are answers to a record rather than to a memory.

1. **V1's Turn does not perform its own appetite verb.** It ends on a refusal (*I am not giving this
   night to a carpet*) where the slot requires her to **want something and take it**. The verb is missing
   from the one place the song most needs it.
2. **V1 Verse 1 buys a competence with a number.** *"who lent those out for nine years"* praises duration
   instead of the actual thing — that they came back. It also puts a second numeral shape near the
   one-fact rule for no gain.
3. **V1 Verse 2 states the absence but not the body.** *"The middle of the floor with nothing in it"*
   answers RULING X and leaves RULING C1 unanswered — nothing in the line makes a listener's ear hunt.
4. **V2's second chorus line is doing two jobs.** *"We are the room. There is no other room tonight"* is
   the hook's frame and the song's argument in the same breath, and it is one syllable too clever for a
   line the room has to shout while carrying a table.
5. **V2 Verse 2 puts the figure and the copier in one stanza and crowds both.** The number's answer and
   the named centre-event are the two load-bearing events of the song and they are currently elbowing.
6. **V3's Wedge is four lines of statement with no object in the last one.** *"standing open like a door"*
   is a simile inside a song whose whole grammar is a machine log that does not do similes.
7. **V3 Verse 1 repeats a person too early.** Del closes both the third stanza of Verse 1 and the third
   stanza of Verse 2 — the log's flatness makes the repetition read as a copy rather than a return.
8. **V4's outro spends its last line on an instruction and its second-to-last on a person.** The slot's
   closing rule is that the last thing heard is somebody still doing something.
9. **V4's Turn line three is a comment on the song.** *"That is the whole comparison and I am done with
   it"* is her talking about her own structure — a remark, and THE REMARK TEST says bury it or cut it.
