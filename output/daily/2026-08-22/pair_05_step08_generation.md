## 4. DRAFT-STAGE SELF-FLAGS — carried into step 09, not repaired here

These are the lines this draft already knows are soft. Each is named by its own words so the step 09
change log can be checked against this file by anybody who wants to.

- **V1** — *"The one who says it twice and says the same."* rhymes on nothing and says nothing twice.
  *"The one who left the good tape on the shelf."* is ambiguous praise: it reads as leaving the good tape
  behind rather than leaving it for the next shift. The last line of the fourth verse runs long against
  a 100 BPM bar.
- **V2** — *"Whoever went back up and had a shower."* is a joke with a butt, which Sunna does not write.
  *"It's the second time somebody checks and stands."* has a word in it doing nothing but rhyming.
- **V3** — *"Sixty-one steps to the top. I have done it."* puts a second number in a song that is allowed
  exactly one, and it is not even the interesting one. *"The one on twelve has never lost a war"* is the
  one heroic word in a song built entirely out of technical praise.
- **V4** — the chorus's *"and I don't care"* is adolescent affect and turns a practice into a pose, which
  is the specific failure mode this slot was warned about. *"They forgot the key"* half-rhymes with grey
  but reads as a literal key.

⛔ **Not flagged, and not to be touched:** the byte-identical chorus, the anaphora, the returning opening
quatrain in V1, and the fact that all four wolves land in the third verse over a full band.
