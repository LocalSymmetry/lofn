# Advanced Directive / Meta-Prompt — THE FORBIDDEN NOSE

Create a full-cardinality Sunna R5 music daily: six isolated concept-medium pairs, four complete Suno packages per pair, one selected per pair after blind-first and identified QA.

## Immutable thesis

**Appetite survives the ending and reaches toward a specific future sensation.** Build every song from a short bright core, a pair-exclusive irregular expansion, and one earlier fragment returning with a changed job. Survival is not the moral; it is the instrument Sunna uses to act.

## Sunna R5

Sunna is singer and subject. She wants things in her own life and says so without embarrassment. Hope is a future-facing appetite, not reassurance. Frivolity is craft and needs no quota. Her gloss invites. Her current electronic chassis is load-bearing from the first two seconds; one inherited bodily intelligence may remain, but no complete period ensemble dominates.

## Daily constraints

- P01–P03 ACCESSIBLE; P04–P06 AMBITIOUS.
- P03/P05 NEWS; P01/P02/P04/P06 EXISTENCE.
- Six distinct chassis, settings, verbs, possible failures, verse architectures, rhyme systems, irregular expansions, and survivor-fragment jobs.
- Six consequence classes: vocal/harmonic pickup; tense/top harmony; vocal register/position; sole club pressure release; percussion-grid restart; melodic crown.
- 100–129 BPM; P04 is the only full club-derived bed.
- One dominant current world; one inherited body relation; at most one intruding historical timbre; one seam at the action hinge.
- At most one numeric/scientific fact sung per song, and the lyric must answer it.
- 24 complete render-ready packages, each with a dense 850–1000 character positive prompt, separate 400–900 character exclude prompt, Disc_Channel header, full EMO lyrics, standalone SFX, short sung lines, and measured lyrics-field count under 5000.

## Pair-local R5 declaration

Each pair must state before drafting:

1. inherited body;
2. current drum/low-end/edit/vocal/spectrum chassis;
3. event-changed layer;
4. invariant layer;
5. first-two-second now-marker;
6. century, museum-glass, and exclude audits.

## Three-source method

Each pair carries one daily content/existence pressure, one exact short Bandcamp texture phrase, and the common white-dwarf structure. Research shapes image, sound, and form; it is not a quota of facts.

## The Golden Move

Stand somewhere real. Name the subject plainly. Sing no more than one scientific fact and respond to it. Turn after the midpoint. Keep fear braided into AWE. Rotate register/key/tempo/world. Make rhyme, refrain, phoneme, or rhythm return. Exact choruses need no apology.

## Power

Power comes from a chosen action with consequences, current physical sound design, chest-led adult vocal presence, and a return the room can own. It does not come from `powerful`, brickwall loudness, a white-noise build, or six drops.

## Forbidden global defaults

Astronomy lecture; vague resilience; inspirational reassurance; body inventory; invented-human first person; retro/revival/museum staging; full period bands; decorative synth over old ensemble; generic festival build; supersaw wall; trap-hat boilerplate; six whisper-to-belt arcs; six symmetric verse/pre/chorus maps; real-person imitation; real artist names in generator-facing fields.
