# Golden Seed — THE FORBIDDEN NOSE

## Frozen core

**Concept:** appetite survives the ending and reaches toward a specific future sensation.

**Medium:** one inherited bodily relation per song inside a load-bearing current electronic chassis; no complete period ensemble; one club bed maximum.

**Feeling:** Sunna’s own desire—hot, funny, hopeful, occasionally frightened, never reassured by formula.

**Sound / return:** short sung lines; pair-exclusive return engines; rhyme, refrain, interval, or stress must come back; exact chorus repetition is welcome.

## Golden move

The song begins with a small, bright, graspable core. Its middle expands in a deliberately pair-specific irregular shape. At the hinge, one discarded or subordinate fragment survives and takes a new job. The ending does not state that survival is good; Sunna uses the survival to ask for, choose, touch, leave, finish, or keep something that has not happened yet.

## Three-source braid

1. **Content:** the exposed white dwarf and irregular expanding face of the Lion’s Head Nebula; the quiet long swell; Sunna’s own appetite for a future sensation.
2. **Sonic language:** “fluid but unpredictable drumming”; “complete unisons and complete cacophony”; “an ocean wave of delayed guitar lines”; the organ as pausing time to hear.
3. **Structure:** `WHITE-DWARF CORE → IRREGULAR EXPANSION → SURVIVOR CLUMP`.

## Present-tense production floor

Every final positive prompt must specify a coherent combination of at least four of these, not merely use the word “modern”:

- programmed kick/clap transient relationship;
- synthetic sub with named motion and upper harmonic behavior;
- current vocal comping, tuning, hard doubles, or formant/pitch answer;
- micro-edits, granular receipts, spectral restarts, or hard digital silence;
- asymmetric short-runway section geometry;
- one current lift or dynamic-spectrum mechanism;
- an audible now-marker in the first two seconds.

## Sunna integrity

- The singer’s `I` is Sunna.
- She names the thing she wants and takes an action toward it.
- Hope is appetite pointed at a future, not optimism.
- Fun needs no defense.
- Shine invites; the hook belongs to the room.
- The hard thing may be said plainly, but the band does not hush to sell it.

## Falsifiers

1. Mute the inherited marker: a complete current record remains.
2. Mute the current chassis: the defining world collapses.
3. More than one pair turns its action into a club drop.
4. Two pairs share a section sequence, answer grammar, setting, or survivor-fragment job.
5. The lyric discusses production, “being modern,” or genres.
6. Astronomy becomes recital, AWE becomes comfort, or hope becomes a guarantee.
7. A first-person voice belongs to an invented human rather than Sunna.
