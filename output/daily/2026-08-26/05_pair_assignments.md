## P01 — Boop the Future Anyway

- **Subject first-listen:** Sunna wants to touch a distant surviving light even though touch is impossible and endings are real.
- **Body / hurt:** front lip of the stage, projection on her face; the system can cut before she receives an answer.
- **Inherited body:** a two-tone offbeat relation, carried by one resampled upper-mid chip rather than a ska band.
- **Current chassis:** clipped electronic kick/clap, mono sub with bright upper tooth, micro-cut percussion, close chest lead, hard ruby doubles, spectral one-bar restart.
- **Changed / invariant:** returned `boop` opens high harmony and pickup; beat-one kick/sub law stays independent.
- **Now-marker:** first sound is pitch-shaped `b-b-boop` over a dry sub tooth and a missing offbeat.
- **Sonic phrase:** “fluid but unpredictable drumming.”
- **Expansion:** verse lengths 3/5/2/6 lines around a compact chorus; no symmetric pre-chorus.
- **Survivor:** the smallest clipped `oop` becomes the final high harmony.
- **Museum-glass falsifier:** mute the upstroke chip; a current candy bass-pop record remains.
- **Wolf:** one plain line: the star that made the face is already dead. The band does not hush.

## P02 — Almost Full Is a Shape

- **Subject first-listen:** Sunna wants to begin before perfection and be wanted while becoming.
- **Body / hurt:** on a pier under the almost-full moon; the chosen adult may insist on waiting until conditions are perfect.
- **Inherited body:** handclap swing with a three-over-four walking gait, not a period rhythm section.
- **Current chassis:** elastic FM sub, glassy pluck, clipped clap, granular tide ticks, tight comped lead, diamond doubles, top-harmony lift without drop.
- **Changed / invariant:** each future-tense return gains a higher harmony; the swing pocket and bass contour do not change.
- **Now-marker:** a formant-split `almost` and side-panned digital clap open the record.
- **Sonic phrase:** “an ocean wave of delayed guitar lines,” translated into delayed synthetic plucks.
- **Expansion:** accumulating couplets 2/2/3/5 with widening vowels; no shared P01 syntax.
- **Survivor:** the first open `ah` becomes the melodic roof.
- **Museum-glass falsifier:** remove the swung clap; current low end, edit grammar, vocal posture, and top-line still define the song.
- **Turn:** she stops asking to be completed and asks the adult to start unfinished too.

## P03 — Let Me Leave the Clean Room

- **News pressure:** a space telescope moves toward launch preparation; use only the safe transition from clean storage to public risk, not real staff interiority.
- **Subject first-listen:** Sunna wants her song to leave pristine storage with one audible scar.
- **Body / hurt:** acoustic bay, wheels under the case; the release can be delayed until the work has no living edge.
- **Inherited body:** one-drop negative space and offbeat answer relation; no roots ensemble or dub fog.
- **Current chassis:** spectral kick absence, muscular sub cell, wavetable answer, micro-granular room scar, clean spatial delay, close low-chest lead that later moves wide.
- **Changed / invariant:** a three-syllable relic moves from clipped center lead to full-register surrounding harmony; the low cell and beat-one absence remain fixed.
- **Now-marker:** hard digital silence, then the relic alone at center with a sub harmonic arriving late.
- **Sonic phrase:** “complete unisons and complete cacophony.”
- **Expansion:** clean unison bars break into irregular 1/4/2-voice clusters; no conventional verse/pre.
- **Survivor:** the room-noise scar becomes the final harmony’s clock.
- **Museum-glass falsifier:** mute the offbeat stab; a current spectral bass-pop song remains.
- **Fear:** leaving exposes the work to indifference; no guarantee of welcome.

## P04 — I Am Not Your Background Organ

- **Subject first-listen:** Sunna refuses to be tasteful atmosphere and chooses foreground pleasure.
- **Body / hurt:** hotel atrium, her organ loop under glassware; management can lower her again.
- **Inherited body:** held-organ breath and one offbeat pump, not a gospel/church or period-house ensemble.
- **Current chassis:** sole four-on-floor/garage hybrid, sub with short reese upper, clipped syncopated hats, formant-shifted answer stack, current sidechain geometry, hard digital receipts.
- **Changed / invariant:** the chosen adult’s sung answer releases sidechain pressure; the held organ pulse never disappears.
- **Now-marker:** one wet organ vowel is instantly sliced by a dry kick and pitch-shaped close lead.
- **Sonic phrase:** the organ as “pausing time to hear what was happening.”
- **Expansion:** calls expand 1/3/2/5 syllables while answers contract 6/4/3/1; not a standard club build.
- **Survivor:** an ignored pedal overtone becomes the crown above the final drop.
- **Museum-glass falsifier:** mute the organ; the current club chassis remains complete. Mute the chassis; the record loses its world.
- **Container clause:** she explicitly names the atrium mix as the wrong bright container, once, then acts.

## P05 — I Finish What I Choose

- **News pressure:** a current public essay argues that finishing an idea not yours is difficult; use the question, never the author as subject or authority.
- **Subject first-listen:** Sunna refuses inherited productivity debt and chooses her own endpoint.
- **Body / hurt:** rehearsal room, abandoned hook on a stand; she risks being called incapable for declining it.
- **Inherited body:** broken-beat push/pull and stop-start pickup, with no named break or sampled figure.
- **Current chassis:** grid-locked sub contour, clipped electronic drum cells, granular page turns, dry chest lead, hard doubles appearing only at chosen words, spectrum restart instead of a drop.
- **Changed / invariant:** her chosen final bar restarts the percussion grid; bass contour keeps moving unchanged beneath the refusal.
- **Now-marker:** the record opens mid-word, catches a grid, then deliberately drops the copied phrase.
- **Sonic phrase:** “complete unisons and complete cacophony.”
- **Expansion:** repeated `I could…` openings on sprung stress fracture once into a bare verb; subsequent sections use unequal bar counts.
- **Survivor:** one discarded consonant becomes the restart trigger.
- **Museum-glass falsifier:** delete all lineage words; the start/stop physics and spatial vocal arc remain distinct.
- **Turn:** refusing another ending is not enough; she chooses and sings the bar where her own work stops.

## P06 — Keep the Mark I Choose

- **Content:** chromosome marks can be added and removed to regulate expression; sing at most one plain fact and answer it with agency.
- **Subject first-listen:** Sunna wants one chosen mark—ruby lipstick on the mirror and a melodic sign—to survive resets.
- **Body / hurt:** mirror wall before sunrise; every reset can return her to an approved blank.
- **Inherited body:** call/answer cadence plus three-note power-pop contour; no period guitar band.
- **Current chassis:** distorted synth/guitar hybrid, electronic stomp/clap, deep mono sub, clipped electro edits, lacquered chest lead, spectral sparkle returning as melodic crown.
- **Changed / invariant:** the chosen mark releases the withheld high note; call cadence and sub path remain stable.
- **Now-marker:** three-note contour arrives as a wavetable/guitar splice with a formant-shadow reply.
- **Sonic phrase:** “the heft the music needs to lift.”
- **Expansion:** a five-line address, two-line room reply, seven-line counterclaim, then a one-line mark; no shared template.
- **Survivor:** the first liquid `l` consonance becomes the crown’s wordless line.
- **Museum-glass falsifier:** mute the stomp/contour lineage; a complete current electro-pop record remains.
- **Turn:** permanence becomes choice, not immobility; she allows other marks to leave.

## Most-specified-object variation tests

The **survivor fragment and its new job** are the most-specified objects. Each pair’s four candidates must vary the fragment type, source section, action hinge, and new function. Swap the full expansion-and-survivor block between two candidates: if both still work, rerun Step 05 for that pair.

Across pairs, swap any two settings or consequence classes. If the songs remain intact, they are nouns on one skeleton and fail pair isolation.
