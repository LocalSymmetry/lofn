## Cross-plate synthesis

The strongest slots turn **a small sound into an action-bearing return**: `oop` opens harmony, `st` conducts a vertical stack, ventilation becomes an air-clock and register ring, `here/hear` releases the answer field, final `t` restarts the grid, and cloth squeak becomes melodic crown. In each case, singer stake and production consequence are the same event.

The recurring weakest package has an attractive object and an adoptable opening phrase, but its singer begins already convinced. Water, light, choir, cosmos, mercy, miracle, and inclusion all confirm one emotional temperature. Because the motif never changes jobs and the late form repeats resolution, it walks all four named corpses.

The other recurring packages remain Somatic YES despite weaker compliance or package completeness. Their exact returns change meaning under scale, insomnia, or grief. Their main limitations are observer rather than actor stake, familiar surrounding production beds, abstraction of the precipitating event, and—in the fracture suite—transformation that is almost entirely degradative.
