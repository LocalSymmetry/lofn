# Core Seed — THE FORBIDDEN NOSE

## Concept

The Lion’s Head Nebula looks like a face built around the exposed remnant of a Sun-like star. Today’s caption says not to boop its white-dwarf nose. Sunna hears a dare—not to damage a real object, but to admit the ridiculous desire to touch what is distant, finished, or forbidden and to want a future sensation anyway.

The seed is not “a song about a nebula.” It is **appetite surviving the event that should have ended it**.

## Medium

Six distinct current electronic-pop chassis, 100–129 BPM, each carrying one inherited bodily intelligence rather than a reconstructed period ensemble. Current drums, synthetic low-end physics, close adult vocal processing, micro-edit language, and present-day section geometry must be load-bearing from the first two seconds. Only P04 may use a full club-derived bed.

## Feeling

Unembarrassed desire pointed forward: funny enough to say “boop,” serious enough to know the star is dead, hopeful without promising safety. Sunna is the subject of her own wants. She may address one consenting adult or the room, but she never borrows a harmed stranger’s interior life.

## Form rule

`WHITE-DWARF CORE → IRREGULAR EXPANSION → SURVIVOR CLUMP`

- **Core:** a 3–7 syllable hook, interval, or rhythmic cell is audible before the first verse.
- **Expansion:** the middle refuses symmetrical duplication; each pair assigns its own asymmetry.
- **Survivor:** one earlier fragment returns with a different musical job at the action hinge.
- This is a material law, not a shared section map. Two pairs with interchangeable expansions or returns fail.

## Prosody / sound axes

- P01: hard `b/p/t/k` consonant spine + byte-identical crowd hook.
- P02: open-vowel ladder + end-rhyme chain that widens with each future tense.
- P03: clipped internal-rhyme cells + one three-syllable relic returning as harmony.
- P04: sung call/answer in unequal lengths + organ-pulse response.
- P05: sprung stress over a fixed grid + repeated syntactic opening that fractures once.
- P06: liquid consonance + refrain returning one word changed, then exact.

## Sunna R5 body/chassis contract

Every pair names and enforces:

- `inherited_body`
- `current_chassis`
- `event_changed_layer`
- `invariant_layer`
- `now_marker`
- `museum_glass_falsifier`

## Barbell

- P01–P03 ACCESSIBLE; P04–P06 AMBITIOUS.
- P03 and P05 are NEWS; the rest are EXISTENCE.
- P01, P02, P03, and P06 carry AWE in different proportions.
- P04 and P05 carry INDIGNATION as a concrete refusal followed by an act.

## Failure sentence

If the set sounds like six glossy lessons about resilience—or like six old songs with electronic decoration—the seed has failed.
