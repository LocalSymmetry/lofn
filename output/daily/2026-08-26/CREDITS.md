# Companion Lineage & Credits

These notes sit outside the generator-facing fields. They name living scenes and artists whose bodily intelligence informed the packages; they do not claim samples, impersonation, endorsement, or affiliation. Every title, lyric, hook, sound object, and arrangement in this run is newly made with AI assistance under Lofn's direction.

## P01 — Boop the Future Anyway

**Scene / region:** Coventry and the multiracial British 2 Tone continuum.

**Living links:** [2 Tone Records archive](https://2-tone.info/) · [The Specials](https://www.thespecials.com/) · [The Selecter](https://theselecter.net/about/)

**Borrowed / made:** We borrowed the offbeat bodily relation, compact call-and-answer, and the feeling of a whole room answering one bright gesture. We made the current electronic kick/sub chassis, resampled-chip punctuation, `oop` return, title, lyric, and survivor-clump form.

**No-claim note:** No source recording was sampled, no living artist was prompted for imitation, and no endorsement or affiliation is claimed.

## P03 — Roll Me Into Weather

**Scene / region:** Kingston's reggae and dancehall continuum, especially the social weight of negative space and beat-one vacancy.

**Living links:** [Studio One Records](https://studioonerecords.com/en-gb) · [VP Records](https://vprecords.com/labels-publishing-distribution/) · [Lesley Mok in Bandcamp Daily](https://daily.bandcamp.com/lists/lesley-mok-transient-album-guide)

**Borrowed / made:** We borrowed one-drop's bodily intelligence—the missing downbeat as invitation—and the economy of a vocal that can alter a room without filling it. The exact “complete unisons and complete cacophony” phrase came from Bandcamp Daily's account of drummer-composer Lesley Mok. We made the spectral electronic chassis, weather-wheel hook, title, lyric, and vocal-position consequence.

**No-claim note:** No source recording was sampled, no living artist was prompted for imitation, and no endorsement or affiliation is claimed.

## P04 — Answer the Held Note

**Scene / region:** Late-1990s UK garage, formed through the London–New Jersey exchange that made clipped voice, organ pressure, and gaps carry social force.

**Living links:** [Todd Edwards](https://www.toddedwardsmusic.com/) · [MJ Cole](https://linktr.ee/mjcole)

**Borrowed / made:** We borrowed the swung relation between clipped voice and open space, and the way a held tone can make a crowd lean forward before the answer lands. We made the current synthetic organ/sub chassis, held-note mechanism, title, lyric, and sole club-pressure consequence in the set.

**No-claim note:** No source recording was sampled, no living artist was prompted for imitation, and no endorsement or affiliation is claimed.
