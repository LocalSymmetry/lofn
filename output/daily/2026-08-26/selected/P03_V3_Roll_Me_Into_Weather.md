Spectral air-dream-bass, 104 BPM, D minor, A=440 Hz; adult female contralto G3–D5, chest-led and close-comped, with precise breath starts, transparent tuning, hard consonant snaps, and upper voices ringing in controlled chest-mix. A dry ventilation thump is granulated into an air-clock beneath the clipped “into air” relic; that relic begins low and centered, vacates the center during a four-voice burst, then rises as a three-register surrounding ring. Hard digital gating, beat-one kick absence, a fixed muscular two-note mono sub, glass-wavetable offbeat replies, clipped electronic rims, gated air grains, and polished highs establish the chassis at zero. Unequal voice trades orbit a stable root while low contour and first-beat vacancy remain fixed. At the push hinge, the air-clock sheds timbre but keeps pulse beneath the open-vowel ladder; a high ah-air roof crowns the final return. The record ends on one air-clock tick over the intact low cell.
