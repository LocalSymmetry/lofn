# V1 — Boop the Future Anyway

## SUNO STYLE PROMPT

Candy electro-ska bass-pop, 116 BPM, F-sharp major, A=440 Hz. An adult female mezzo sings center-front in polished chest voice, smiling through hard b, p, and t attacks; ruby doubles trail fifteen to thirty milliseconds, with one living unstacked line. A pitch-shaped b-b-boop, dry mono sub tooth, clipped electronic kick/clap, micro-cut upper percussion, and one resampled offbeat chip mark the first two seconds. Beat-one kick and sub hold a phone-readable floor while upper drums move fluidly and unpredictably through asymmetric three-, five-, two-, and six-line runs. The room owns the exact title refrain. A projector relay snaps at the dead-star hinge while the groove remains bright; the smallest oop, withheld after the first hook, returns an eighth-note early as a high third that opens the final harmony. Short ruby plate and a one-bar spectral restart keep the finish candy-hard. Chip, title, and immediate warm silence close the record.
