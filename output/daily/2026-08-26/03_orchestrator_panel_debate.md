## CONFIGURATION 1 — BASELINE

The baseline room accepts the nebula only as pressure, never as a narrator. FIRESTARTER asks for an action before astronomy; NEON CHEERLEADER keeps **boop** because the word admits appetite without a thesis; CATHARSIS CONDUIT warns that six “small core → big middle → fragment return” maps would become one song. “Wait,” the RIOT INSTIGATOR says, “the room still needs the same bright thing twice.” The HARDCORE ELDER answers that exact return and shared form are not synonyms. The room binds one short core per song and forbids a shared section sequence.

The medium room disagrees about irregularity. MACHINE-GUN DRUMMER wants free upper percussion; DYNAMIC RANGE AUDITOR says six random fills will read as no form at all. MINIMALIST ATMOSPHERIST proposes a better constraint: the irregularity occurs at one named layer while kick/sub stability is separately declared. RIFF ARCHITECT adds that each survivor fragment must take a new **function**, not merely a new timbre. Baseline ruling: assign changed and invariant layers before genre decoration.

The context room refuses abstraction. DIGITAL REBELLION SOCIOLOGIST demands one situated place and available act per pair. ANGST CARTOGRAPHER says the “fear” must name what Sunna risks losing. HALF-PIPE WITNESS insists every title imply an attempt. Baseline ruling: six settings, six verbs, six possible failures; no moral summaries.

## CONFIGURATION 2 — GROUP TRANSFORM: Bridge

**Operation:** translate each panel’s problem into a distant domain, then bring back the solution rather than the foreign imagery.

The concept room bridges to stellar remnants and biological regulation. The useful analogy is not “resilience”; it is **a small persistent operator reorganizing a much larger field**. RELENTLESS OPTIMIST initially calls persistence hopeful. HARDCORE ELDER interrupts: “Actually, persistence has no ethics. What matters is what she chooses to do with it.” The bridge changes the seed: survival never completes the song; Sunna’s future-facing act does.

The medium room bridges to control systems and wave measurement. A long swell can carry short local turbulence; therefore the portfolio may hold stable kick/sub laws while upper grids, vocal positions, harmony, or spectrum mutate. MACHINE-GUN DRUMMER objects that this could become cautious. GRIT SHAPER responds that bodily impatience belongs above the stable plant. The bridge yields **stable physical carrier, pair-exclusive turbulent layer**.

The context room bridges to version control and interrupted work. A fragment earns survival by changing the next decision, not by being preserved in a display case. PLATFORM-LABOR SCHOLAR rejects “finish everything” as productivity ideology. HALF-PIPE WITNESS reframes finishing as a chosen endpoint after visible attempts. The bridge yields P05’s refusal: Sunna finishes what she chooses, not what somebody else abandoned.

## CONFIGURATION 3 — SKEPTIC TRANSFORM: Reflect

**Operation:** reflect the assumption that survival, completion, foregrounding, and permanence are automatically desirable.

The reflected concept room asks whether Sunna is merely demanding custody of everything. NEON CHEERLEADER says yes to excess; RELENTLESS OPTIMIST disagrees because desire without selection is shopping, not hope. CATHARSIS CONDUIT forces three endings to release something: P02 acts before full; P03 leaves the clean room with noise intact; P05 refuses an inherited unfinished idea. The final set wants specific futures, not accumulation.

The reflected medium room assumes the historical marker is the real song and the electronic chassis is decoration. RIFF ARCHITECT runs the museum-glass test in both directions. If muting the lineage collapses the record, the century was not replaced; if muting the chassis leaves the defining world intact, reject. DYNAMIC RANGE AUDITOR adds a portfolio test: mute every bass lift. Five of six distinct consequences must remain legible.

The reflected context room assumes nobody owes the singer attention. DIGITAL REBELLION SOCIOLOGIST therefore requires reciprocal, consent-legible invitation rather than entitlement. ANGST CARTOGRAPHER gives each refusal a subsequent act. Sunna accepts the correction: she may ask, choose, or step forward, but another adult’s answer is never scripted as obedience.

### Skeptic falsifiers

1. Remove era labels: every pair still names concrete present-day sound physics.
2. Remove astronomy: every lyric still has a setting, want, risk, action, and turn.
3. Remove bass/drop change: P01, P02, P03, P05, and P06 retain distinct consequences.
4. Swap survivor blocks between pairs: if two still work, both return engines rerun.
5. Replace Sunna’s `I` with an invented human: the lyric must break.

## Final decision

Proceed with the frozen six-pair barbell. Keep **boop** as the frivolous public verb; prevent it from becoming the set’s entire tone. Bind the shared white-dwarf law only at the level of material behavior. P01 vocal/harmonic, P02 tense/top harmony, P03 vocal register/position, P04 sole club pressure release, P05 grid restart, P06 melodic crown. The stable layer is named separately in every pair. Sunna’s chosen future action—not survival itself—closes each song.
