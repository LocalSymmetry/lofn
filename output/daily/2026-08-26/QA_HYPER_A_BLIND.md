## Cross-plate calibration

The strongest packages are not simply the densest or newest. Across these six plates, the decisive separator is a three-part causal chain: the singer risks a concrete act, an audible fragment changes job because of that act, and the final return opens a specific future rather than summarizing survival. That is why P01-B, P02-A, P03-B, P04-C, P05-B, and P06-C lead their plates.

Exact repetition is not itself a corpse. It works in the leading slots because scene, vocal position, harmony, respondent status, or physical action changes what the same line means. It dies where damage merely accumulates or where the lyric repeatedly explains a choice already made. Likewise, older formatting was not treated as disqualifying: the alpine cosmic package remains Somatic YES because its body, hook, and emotional turns survive, even when more causal singer-action packages outrank it.

The most common risk in the current detailed packages is not genericity but over-certification: consent, ownership, completion, and production logic are sometimes explained after the event has already made them legible. The strongest future renders should protect the physical event, the short hook, and the changed-job survivor from that explanatory tail. No slot identity or candidate mapping was inferred.
