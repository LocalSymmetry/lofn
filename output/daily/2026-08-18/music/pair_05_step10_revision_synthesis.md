### ⭐⭐ THE CONTAINER CLAUSE — SPENT ONCE, AND HOW THE NAMING IS AUDIBLE IN THE WRITING

**The line, and it appears exactly once in the pair:**
> *"You should not be able to dance to this."* → *"Here's the chorus."*

`sunna.yaml` R3 requires the naming to be **audible in the writing rather than merely intended.** Four
mechanisms in the *written text* carry it, and none of them is a production gesture or a note:

1. ⭐ **THE CONTAINER ANSWERS HER, IN WORDS, ON BOTH SIDES.** The line is written *inside* the seam block,
   sandwiched between two written lines of `Whoa-oh! Whoa-oh!`. The listener hears a crowd chant, then the
   sentence, then the same crowd chant — **the party is textually undisturbed by having been called wrong.**
   The refutation is on the page, not in the mix.
2. ⭐ **SHE HANDS OFF TO THE THING SHE JUST CONDEMNED.** *"Here's the chorus"* is a written stage
   instruction spoken by the singer. She names the container as wrong and then, in the same breath and the
   same register, **introduces it.** The naming does not stop the song; it *cues* it. That sequence is
   legible from the lyric sheet alone.
3. ⭐⭐ **THE CHORUS THAT FOLLOWS IS BYTE-IDENTICAL TO THE TWO BEFORE IT — INCLUDING `And I said that it was
   fine`.** This is the load-bearing one. The container is given a chance to adjust and **does not change a
   character.** A listener does not need to notice the identity to feel it; the room shouts the same eight
   lines it has shouted twice, immediately after being told they should not be able to. **The unchanged
   return IS the naming's answer, and it is written, measurable, and checkable on the page.**
4. ⭐ **THE BRIDGE ARMS IT WITH A FLAT SENTENCE, NOT A MOOD.** *"It said it in a font / That is perfectly
   legible."* The container line lands on an already-stated fact, so it reads as an observation rather than
   a plea — which is what keeps it inside **ADMIT IT, DON'T SIGNAL IT** instead of becoming the performance
   of sadness R3's second refusal forbids.

**⛔ What it deliberately is NOT.** No band drop-out. No hush. No breathy delivery. No reverb bloom. No
tempo pull. No half-time. **She'll say the line. She won't sell it.** The seam header instructs the
arrangement to be *"unchanged"* and to *"acknowledge nothing"* — because the moment production performs the
line, the gesture becomes the thing R3 banned and stops being the thing R3 licensed.
