## ⛔ THE WOLF CEILING — THE GOVERNING CONSTRAINT OF THIS PAIR
This pair carries **no dark line, no named pursuer, no tide, no clock, no cost, no undertow, no
bittersweet turn.** Joy with no invoice attached. Four other pairs in this set carry the weight; P03's
job is to prove the personality can simply have a good time (`sunna.yaml` R3: *"She is allowed to just
want things."*). **Every ending here is warmer than its middle and nothing is paid for.**

**Load/mood assignment held:** complexity → the MUSIC · plain → the WORDS · carefree → the MUSIC ·
**and the wolf slot is empty, deliberately, in both channels.**

## ⚠️ THE HAZARD THIS CONCEPT CARRIES, AND HOW IT IS HANDLED
The concept is *about* cleverness, so **THE REMARK TEST is the sharpest gate on this pair.** The
mechanism is **never explained** — no line describes how a moving thing makes a picture, no line inverts
"wrong tool / right tool," no line lands a pun. Depth sits entirely in the **situation** (a position the
listener has stood in: you used the thing for the other thing and it was great) and the lines stay plain
and **continue the story instead of interpreting it.**

**Ruling 3 held actively:** nothing here says *good BECAUSE it was wrong.* The misuse and the beauty are
stated as **two adjacent facts** — *"It came out the wrong end / And look at it"* — never as cause and
effect. This was the single most likely way this concept could rot.
