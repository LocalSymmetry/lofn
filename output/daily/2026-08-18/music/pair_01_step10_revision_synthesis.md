**Pair-level ONE SEAM policy.** The world of all four songs is a **modern record** — 2020s dry kit ahead of

**Pair-level wolf policy (against the R3 Fisher falsification — "swap the dark line and lose nothing").**
The wolf is the same event (the tide, at nine) and its **function differs in every song**, so the lines are
**not interchangeable**: V1 *mid-task* ("Tide's at nine. / I'm doing the other window.") · V2 *deadline*

V4 *repeatable, and published* ("Tide's at nine, same as Monday." / "It's on a board by the kiosk.").

**Numeric discipline.** Each song sings **exactly one number — the published hour, "nine"** — at the hinge,
responded to rather than recited. Every other quantity in the four lyrics is written non-numerically
("couple more windows", "load after load", "in no time at all"). Verified by sweep, §MEASUREMENTS.
