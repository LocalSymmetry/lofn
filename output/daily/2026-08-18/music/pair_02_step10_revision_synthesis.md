# PAIR-LEVEL SELF-CHECK

**ONE BED, FOUR VARIATIONS.** All four are the same modern soul-pop stomp at 117–119 BPM with the same
strut instrumentation. They differ by **key, riff figure, density arc, the specific hole, and the seam's
placement** — never by genre. No variation contains a gear-change, a modulation, a tempo change, or a
standing tension lane.

**FOUR DISTINCT RIFFS (hum test, all present before the first line and after the last):**
V1 three-note descending bass figure + two baritone-sax stabs · V2 rising four-note bass/baritone climb ·
V3 clean-guitar three-note figure answered by claps · V4 four-note horn figure doubled by bass.

**FOUR DISTINCT HOLES:** V1 one bar carried by a single handclap · V2 the last chorus's first downbeat on
tambourine alone · V3 the guitar figure drops its third note every second pass · V4 an eighth of silence
where the horn stab is expected.

**FOUR NON-INTERCHANGEABLE WOLF EVENTS** *(R3's own falsification test — swap them and you lose the
song):* V1 the string opened her thumb at four, she taped it and went back out · V2 the spool ran bare and
jumped out of her hand, she caught it and let it out again · V3 the wind stopped at ten past four, they
walked out and got it · V4 somebody else's line went over the trees and a hundred people cheered.
Each is an **event**, named once, concrete, and each **resolves** — tape, catch, chips, a round of drinks.
Every one ends warm and ends short.

**THE FORM (F17, verbatim): "Go to an extreme, move back to a more comfortable place."**
V1 the burn → the bypass and home · V2 two hundred feet → the fryer and the walk in · V3 the field →
chips in the car park · V4 the line goes → a round and a bag by her feet.

**RESIDUE — MISSES, NAMED HONESTLY.**
1. **The four pre-chorus chants are structurally identical devices** (a one-to-three-word word repeated
   four times: *And it goes* / *Out* / *It's down* / *There it is*). They are lexically distinct and each
   returns after its own seam, but the **device** repeats across all four. I judged the return worth more
   than the variety (R3 rule 4: return is free), but it is a real intra-pair similarity and I am flagging
   it rather than hiding it.
2. **V1 contains the word "beach"** (one instance, *"down the hill from the beach"* — the original
   *"half a mile off the beach"* was cut when the numeral sweep found it was a second numeric fact). It
   is the seed's own setting and carries no water event, but it is the only line in the pair that touches
   the excluded vocabulary's neighbourhood, so it is on the record. V2–V4 contain no water at all.
3. **THE INVERSION is carried by production notation, not by the lyric.** The modern-world / 1966-
   intrusion split lives entirely in the style field. If the generator smooths the seam into a uniform
   retro mix, the inversion fails at render and the text gate cannot see it — this is exactly the class
   `lofn-render-audit` exists for, and it should be checked on the render rather than assumed here.
4. **V3's radiant is the strongest of the four; V1's is the weakest.** *"Nothing at work pulls back"* is
   a plain fact but it edges toward a statement about her life rather than about the afternoon. It passes
   the flat test and it does not interpret the kite, but it is the one radiant in the pair that a listener
   could hear as a thesis. I kept it because the alternative was weaker; I would not defend it as hard as
   *"It is my sister's car."*
