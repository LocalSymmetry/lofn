**ENGINE — APPETITE WITH A TIMETABLE.** Something enormous, made carefully, on purpose, in full knowledge

**THE SIX RULINGS — binding, all pairs:**

## GATES — every variation, before it is written down as done

**R3 personality gates (new — this run is R3's first test):**
- ⛔ **THE REMARK TEST** — would a listener say *"that was clever"*? **If yes, bury it or cut it.**
- ⛔ **NO STANDING TENSION LANE** — no unresolving riser, no sub creeping a semitone across the track, no
  filter that only ever opens. If a tension device is used, **it lands and it is spent.**
- ⛔ **NO MID-SONG GEAR-CHANGE.** One bed, held. **ONE SEAM** only (THE INVERSION), with the singer doing
  something across it. Vary by arrangement **density**, never by genre.
- ⭐ **THE REGISTER NEVER MOVES** — the hard line is sung in exactly the voice that sang the funny one. No
  vocal signal, no production hush, no drop-out reverence around it.
- ⭐ **SHE SINGS IT** — full chest voice, real tune, **stacked harmony as a required instrument**
  (thirds on the hook, a counter-melody that argues with the lead, one moment where two voices split).
  Sing-speak is **one colour**, not the default. Breath in, no pitch correction.
- ⭐ **THE STRUT** — swagger is the rhythm section: a bassline that walks and is hummable; guitar as
  percussion; horns that punctuate and get out; handclaps/tambourine; ⭐ **LEAVE ONE HOLE** where the
  expected note does not arrive. ⛔ **Swagger is never distortion.**
- ⭐ **THE HUM TEST** — the instrumental hook must be recognisable with the vocal muted, and must exist
  **before the first line and after the last.**
- ⭐ **THE FLAT TEST** — say each key line aloud, flatly, to a face. If it dies, it was short, not simple.

**Standing gates:**
- **Style field ≤1000 chars**, two-field (style + exclude). ⛔ **Negations inside the STYLE field ARM what
  they ban** — Suno reads that field as positive tokens. *"no synth pads"* asks for synth pads. Put
  exclusions in the EXCLUDE field only.
- **Lyrics 70–120 sung lines**, EMO tags inside section headers, lyrics field **<5000 chars**.
- ⭐ **Measured craft targets:** `sung_lines` is our strongest like-rate correlate (**r=+0.538**) and
  `words_per_line` is **negative** (−0.202). **Sing more lines; keep them short.**
- **THE RETURN** — rhyme and refrain must come back; **removal is a debt** payable only by naming what
  returns in its place. A byte-identical chorus needs no defence and no note.
- **D8′ ADDRESS THE ROOM, NEVER THE LISTENER** — test: *can the room shout this line back AT the singer
  without it becoming false?* Chant, never lecture.
- **THE INVERSION (ONE SEAM):** ⛔ **the MODERN genre is the WORLD; the historical element is the
  INTRUSION.** Five of six beds here are historical lineages — **each must be a MODERN record that a period
  element intrudes upon, ONCE, at the seam that carries the meaning.** A straight 1963 pastiche is a repair.
  *(This is the exact defect the 2026-08-13 refinement run existed to fix. P04 is the highest risk.)*
- **Dedication** (`vault/DEDICATION_STANDARD.md`) — ≤500 chars, **measured in code, never eyeballed.**
  ⛔ **If the style field names a break, credit the drummer** (`amen` → Gregory C. Coleman / The Winstons).
  Sweep with **word boundaries** — `amen` matches inside `ornament`, `rage` inside `garage`.
  ⛔ Honesty both ways: a generated track is **not a sample**.
- **Distinctiveness:** cross-pair prompt similarity ceiling **0.58**, lyric **0.42**.
