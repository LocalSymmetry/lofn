### SOURCE 3 — MATERIAL STRUCTURE *(F07 → a mandatory FORM rule)*
⭐⭐ **THE RADIANT.** The APOD is a composite across several nights in which meteors streak in every
direction and **all of them trace back to one point.**

> **MANDATORY FORM RULE — every song must obey it:**
> **The song's lines scatter outward; every one of them can be traced back to a single origin, and that
> origin is stated ONCE, plainly, in one line.** Verses may fly apart — different images, different
> directions, no connective tissue required. **But there is exactly one line that is the radiant, and
> after it lands, every earlier line points at it.**
> Structural consequence: **the radiant line arrives LATE** (final chorus, last verse, or outro), it is
> **not** the title, and it is **never** explained afterwards.

*Additional structural gift from F07: "accumulated over several nights" — the picture is not one moment,
it is many, laid on top of each other. Pairs may use stacked/overdubbed takes of the same line as an
arrangement device.*
