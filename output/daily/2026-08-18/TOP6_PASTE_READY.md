## 3. I CAME ALL THIS WAY  ·  P04-V4
*Editorial — why this one and not its three siblings:* "And it is a bus stop. / And it is perfect." Timpani under a bus shelter — the cleanest INVERSION in the run, in the pair flagged highest-risk.

### SUNO STYLE PROMPT
```
Modern indie chamber-pop with a strut, 104 BPM, F major, brightly-colored production at wall-of-sound density, blended and mono-forward. The hook is an eight-note walking electric bass strut that keeps coming home to the same note, doubled by pizzicato low strings and hammered upright piano; the bass plays it alone before the first word and alone after the last. Live kit close-mic'd, sitting twelve milliseconds behind the grid, massed handclaps, tambourine dry and dead centre, clean guitar chopping sixteenths, one tubular bell on the downbeat of each chorus, a small horn section stabbing twice and leaving. Female lead, full chest, breath left in, girl-group thirds on the hook and a counter-melody arguing underneath, one bar where two voices split and the gap stays open, lush, aerodynamic beats that instantly hits cruising altitude. In the bridge two orchestral timpani roll once, soft, and go. Beat four of the last chorus is left empty.
```

### SUNO EXCLUDE
```
period pastiche, museum baroque, harpsichord throughout, timpani throughout, sepia sixties mix, orchestral fanfare, film trailer drums, distortion, fuzz guitar, punk, trap hi-hats, supersaw, white-noise riser, airhorn, EDM drop, house kick, hard autotune, transparent pitch correction, whispered vocal, breathy ballad, spoken word, key change, cathedral hall reverb, ambient pad, drone, nautical, sea shanty, water sound effects, triumphant modulation, slow fade
```
