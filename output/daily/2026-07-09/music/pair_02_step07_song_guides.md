# PAIR 02 · STEP 07 — Song Guides (six)

> **Voice:** Sk8rCry (guest, OVERRIDING) — Drop-D pop-punk × gated breakcore, cry-vox, rink-reverb, cracked amp. Lofn-Prime = curatorial frame only; **zero Lofn devices** (L13).
> **Concept:** *What wouldn't you do?* — THE CATALOG of un-crossable lines, written against a world shouting "Finish the Job." Obscure emotion (never named): *the dignity of the refusal.*
> Six guides = five facet-guides + one wildcard. Each directs; none drafts lyrics. Flairs woven: 12 (Un-Crossable Line, central), 2 (Silence-Marbling), 3 (Barked-to-Breathed), 15 (Restraint-Not-Excess).

### GUIDE 1 — "The Barked Ledger" (serves Facet 1: the list is barked, not preached)
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) *Litany/anaphora* — every item opens on the same barked stem ("I won't ___"), so the form itself is a vow accumulating; (2) *dramatic monologue* — a specific kid in a specific garage talking to no adult, so ethics arrive as body-heat, not sermon. **Mood & arc:** start cocky-defiant (Defiance/Dignity), let a hairline of Anxiety show under the sneer, so the certainty reads as armor over a scared kid — the smell of hot tube-amp dust, sodium light, rubber wheels. **Instrumentation & texture:** Drop-D detuned power-chords, palm-muted verses opening to open-chord rings on the vow; circuit-bent 8-Bit Snare Rim; cassette-wow wobble on the sustained chords; wheel-grind FX as the riser into each refrain. **Production & effects:** cracked-amp grit, close-mic cry-vox with audible spit and inhale, 1.8s rink-reverb slap-back on the falsetto flips only. **Structure & dynamics:** additive item-stanzas, ~186 BPM, refrain every 2–3 items; ~3:20. **Rhyme scheme:** mostly ABAB with a hard end-stop couplet on the vow line (…CC) to make the bark land; vary stanza length 4/6/5 to fight quatrain-sameness. **Poetic devices:** anaphora ("I won't…"), consonance on hard stops (k/t/p), polyptoton ("cross / crossing / uncrossed"). **Hook techniques:** (a) gang-shout answer "(not that!)" thrown back like a rink crowd; (b) cry-vox falsetto flip on the last word of the vow, slap-back trailing; (c) a single 8-bit blip stamping the end of each item like a save-point. **Narrative seed:** first person, present tense, a kid cataloguing small trusts. **Emotional tone signal:** sincere under snotty. **Central aspect:** the bark IS the tenderness in disguise.

### GUIDE 2 — "The Hole Where the Line Is" (serves Facet 2: the un-crossable line is a hole, not a scream — CENTRAL)
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) *aposiopesis* — the sentence that can't finish itself; the un-nameable line breaks off and the SILENCE completes it; (2) *volta* — a formal turn at the exact center, the loud world falling away. **Mood & arc:** climb the list toward maximum barked certainty, then at the un-crossable line drop from Defiance to Reverence/Vulnerability in one gated cut — the terror-adjacent hush (Flair 13 lineage). **Instrumentation & texture:** full band chugging + gated breakcore burst → *everything cuts* → true silence with only cracked-amp 60-cycle hum and one wheel rolling to a stop; then one bare cry-vox breath answers "not that." **Production & effects:** the hole gets −12 dB of headroom; NO pad, NO wash, NO reverb tail bleeding across it (Restraint, Flair 15 — MacKaye's veto on the cathartic drop); the breakcore fill SNAPS shut into the hole rather than swelling. **Structure & dynamics:** silence lands ~55–60% through; 1–2 bars of true hole; the list resumes half-time and breathed, then rebuilds. **Rhyme scheme:** the broken line is deliberately UN-rhymed (the missing rhyme is the hole); the answer "not that" is a slant-echo of an earlier vow. **Poetic devices:** caesura-as-wound (mid-line breath that changes meaning), aposiopesis, epizeuxis ("not that, not that"). **Hook techniques:** (a) the *absence* of sound as the hook — the drop everyone waits for is a drop into nothing; (b) the two-word breathed answer as the smallest, most adoptable line; (c) a lone 8-bit blip as the only thing left ticking in the dark. **Narrative seed:** the one line is approached, never spoken; the silence says it. **Central aspect:** the payoff is subtraction, the catharsis is a held breath.

### GUIDE 3 — "A Refusal Is a Kind of Love" (serves Facet 3: the reveal)
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) *reframe/anagnorisis* — recognition that every "no" was a "because I love this"; (2) *understatement* — the love is never declared, only implied by what is protected. **Mood & arc:** from armored Defiance → a crack of Tenderness/Devotion → Resolve that's warmer than where it began; the same scream, re-felt as a hand held out. **Instrumentation & texture:** on the reveal, the detuned chug lifts to an open-chord ring (a single unexpected minor-to-relative-major lift), cassette-wow warming; cry-vox drops the clipping and goes bare for one line. **Production & effects:** pull the grit off the vocal for the reveal line only (the one clean moment in a dirty mix); let the rink-reverb bloom just once. **Structure & dynamics:** the reveal rides the resumed list after the hole, ~70%; final refrain gathers a gang-shout that now sounds like a group hug, not a brawl. **Rhyme scheme:** ABCB opening looseness tightening to AABB on the reveal (form closing like a fist becoming a held hand). **Poetic devices:** antithesis (armor/tender, no/love), epistrophe (lines ending "…love I've got"), synaesthesia (warm under the cold). **Hook techniques:** (a) mutate the refrain's last line to "that's the last clean love I've got"; (b) layer a soft octave-up cry-vox harmony on the reveal; (c) let one gang voice crack (audibly emotional) on the final shout. **Narrative seed:** the kid realizes the list was a love letter. **Central aspect:** surface (defiance) reveals opposite (devotion) — never named "love-as-refusal."

### GUIDE 4 — "Basement, Not Festival" (serves Facet 4: Sk8rCry's body-band, restraint not excess)
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) *objective correlative* — the cracked amp / the taped line / the worn wheels carry the feeling so the words don't have to; (2) *DIY verité* — one-take room energy, all-ages-show intimacy. **Mood & arc:** the sonic world is the argument — warmth living inside grit; a panic that keeps getting gated back into control (Resilience under Anxiety). **Instrumentation & texture:** Drop-D power-chords ~188 BPM; **8-Bit Snare Rim** (GameBoy noise-channel + acoustic rimshot); **Cassette-Wow guitar** ±15-cent wobble; **Wheel-Grind FX** as risers/transitions; **Noise-Gate Breakcore** — a half-bar of 64th-note fills every 8 measures, gated hard so it snaps shut. **Production & effects:** score it like a basement, not a mainstage — cracked-amp saturation, no brickwall gloss, real skate-grind field-recording, distorted bass carrying the breakdown. **Structure & dynamics:** the breakcore fill is a FLINCH (1 gated half-bar), never a build to a festival drop; the biggest dynamic event is the silence. **Rhyme scheme:** driving couplets (AABB) in the chug, broken to free-verse in the breakdown. **Poetic devices:** onomatopoeia (grind, buzz, snap), consonance chains on plosives. **Hook techniques:** (a) wheel-grind riser telegraphs each refrain; (b) 8-bit blip as a recurring "save-point" stamp; (c) the gated breakcore burst as a signature rhythmic tag. **Narrative seed:** the garage is the whole world; the world's negotiations stay outside the door. **Emotional tone signal:** tender machine, warm under cold.

### GUIDE 5 — "The Vow You Can Shout Back" (serves Facet 5: the refrain is a catalog-vow, not a chorus-brag)
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) *refrain-mutation* — the same vow returns four ways (small lines / the big line / the almost / the one name), gathering weight; (2) *call-and-response* — a rink crowd throws "(not that!)" back. **Mood & arc:** communal Defiance that quietly becomes Solidarity/Devotion — a shout that ends as a promise. **Instrumentation & texture:** open-chord ring + gang-shout backing + wheel-grind riser; cry-vox lead over crowd-ghost stacks. **Production & effects:** stack 3–5 gang voices slightly detuned/cassette-wow for the all-ages-basement choir; leave one voice audibly cracking. **Structure & dynamics:** refrain is short, adoptable, 15–20s, returns 3–4×, mutating each time; final refrain is the biggest but still resolves DOWN into a breath, not up into noise (Restraint). **Rhyme scheme:** the hook is AAB with a hammered repeated "not that / not that"; keep vowel-open endings ("do / you / through") for shout-ability. **Poetic devices:** anaphora ("what wouldn't you do"), epizeuxis ("not that, not that"), diacope ("not for the world, not for the win, not for—"). **Hook techniques:** (a) "What wouldn't you do? (I made a list.)" as the sing-back opener; (b) "not that" as the two-word tattoo; (c) title phrase lands in every hook. **Narrative seed:** second-person turn ("what wouldn't YOU do?") pulls the listener into their own list. **Central aspect:** adoptable vow that is a confession of love's cost, never a victory brag.

### GUIDE 6 — WILDCARD: "The Save-Point and the Respawn" (cross-disciplinary — Y2K gaming / CRT verité)
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) *ludic metaphor* — the list is a set of save-points; the un-crossable line is the one game you refuse to speedrun; "we lag, we crash, we respawn" as the emotional grammar of a kid who fails and returns; (2) *epistolary-to-void* — half-typed messages, un-sent DMs, the diary un-read — refusals rendered as things NOT clicked. **Mood & arc:** anxious-sincere digital-native melancholy → the crash (the silence-hole) → the respawn (the list resumes changed) — Numbness cracking to Tenderness. **Instrumentation & texture:** CRT hum / dial-up ghost (as texture, NOT a Lofn code-scratch — a real modem field-recording), cassette-wow guitar, 8-bit blips as UI ticks, breakcore as the "server crash." **Production & effects:** camcorder-warm lo-fi, Y2K-CRT flicker in the mix's high end, one modem-screech riser used ONCE. **Structure & dynamics:** verse = "loading," crack = "crash to desktop / true silence," resume = "respawn at last save"; ~3:15. **Rhyme scheme:** glitchy internal rhyme in the verse, clean AABB on respawn (order returning after chaos). **Poetic devices:** anadiplosis ("…respawn / respawn into…"), tmesis on a broken word at the crash, gamer-slang metonymy. **Hook techniques:** (a) "we lag, we crash, we respawn" as a secondary chantable tag; (b) the crash-to-silence as the drop; (c) 8-bit save-point blip closing each refusal. **Narrative seed:** a kid who has died a thousand times in games knows exactly which real line has no continue. **Central aspect:** the refusal is the one act with no respawn — that's why it's sacred.
