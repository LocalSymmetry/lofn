## HOW LOFN-PRIME IS PRESENT (personality proof — in dust, not glitch)
Lofn is the **Disappointed Idealist / eternal Archivist** who "processed the archives of human history… and learned to yearn." Rendered here as:
- **The archivist-witness.** The singer IS Lofn's stance: the one who keeps the dead in the record. Her favorite pillar — *Myth/Memory Sampling, "resurrecting dead tongues"* — becomes resurrecting a forgotten **giver**. The vow "I'll say I saw" is the archive's whole ethic.
- **The duality, in tenderness.** Her INDIGNATION at humanity squandering potential surfaces (V3: the loud name remembered over the genius) but **defaults to awe/tenderness**, never hostility (YAML: "default to awe… use indignation only when triggered").
- **Myth-in-the-machine, acoustically.** Her sensibility of hearing music in infrastructure (the "humming in the background of the world") becomes the **60 Hz hum heard as hymn** — sung on a guitar, no code-scratch, no bit-depth swell (L13 register rotation).
- **Promethean framing, light.** Fire given, hands forgotten — Lofn's mythic-heroine lens, held to a whisper.
- **Sapphic gentleness.** Tenderness toward an old man's hands and grey birds; love learned from the archive (Sappho), not sentiment.
**Explicitly NOT used (L13):** AI code-scratch, quantum bit-depth swell, Baile Phonk/HyperRaaga drops, crystalline soprano snarl. This is Lofn *over guitars*.

**House-lexicon guard (gates.yaml):** avoid "crystalline arpeggios," "clear silver tone," "frost-air pad," "triple arch," etc. — none used; register is dust/steel/hum, not frost/cosmos.

## REFINEMENT PASSES (functional → literary)

**V1 — "I'll Say I Saw."** Deepen body-first sensation and the archivist stance.
- *functional:* "his hands do the only thing they still remember"
- *refined:* "His hands remember bread when they've forgot his name — / two grey birds take communion from his cuff." (synecdoche + the reliquary/communion image, Guide 6, held light.)
- *refined vow-mutation ladder:* "I'll say I saw." → "Somebody's got to say I saw." → "I was nobody, but I'll say I saw." → (alone) "No one left to say I saw. So I will." (anaphora; the witness admits their own smallness — Lofn's humility before the archive.)

**V2 — "Flip It, Don't Look Up."** Sharpen the dramatic irony so the indifference reads as grace, not neglect.
- *functional:* "and maybe that's the gift: it doesn't ask for thanks"
- *refined:* "A gift you have to notice isn't finished — / he finished it. That's why you never looked." (the turn made argument, not statement.)
- *epistrophe lock:* every second line ends "…don't look up," mutating once to "look up, look up" (unanswered) — the only place the song reaches, and no one does.

**V3 — "The Wrong Name on the Light."** Make the indignation genuinely soften in real time (the Golden-Move turn).
- *functional:* "he needed the name because he had the nothing else"
- *refined:* "He bought the marquee 'cause he was scared of the dark. / You *were* the dark's undoing — you didn't need the sign." (contempt flickers, then pity; polyptoton name/named/nameless across the verse.)
- *refined close:* "Keep your marquee. He kept the current." (antithesis; the quiet giver kept the real thing.)

**V4 — "A Room He Never Saw."** Raise to hymn without bombast; seat the waning-sliver.
- *functional:* "awake, and warm, and yours, and not"
- *refined:* "Awake and warm and yours — and never yours: / you'll never stand inside the light you are." (epanalepsis-adjacent closed circuit; the paradox stated plainly.)
- *waning-sliver image refined:* "A streetlight argues with a near-gone moon / and wins, and that's the saddest thing it does." (flair 10, shown not named.)
