## V1 — "I'll Say I Saw" (the hotel room, the pigeons)
**Draft music prompt (~760c):** A hushed, terror-adjacent tender alt-country elegy. Souped-up alt-country and Gothic Americana over dust-bowl gospel, slow 6/8 sway near 42 BPM in open-G, melancholy swagger held to a whisper. Female late-20s dust-and-honey alto, frayed at the edges, close-mic'd breath. Fingerpicked open chords ring against a low sixty-hertz electric hum that never leaves; a pedal-steel note bends like a held breath; brushed kit, upright bass. Field-recording of pigeons low in the bed. The arrangement THINS across the song — band exits one at a time — until only alto and hum remain; a hole of true silence the beat before the single fact. No build to a payoff; the quietest bar is the climax.
**Draft exclude:** EDM drop, 808, trap hi-hats, synth arpeggio, glitch, autotune gloss, male lead, children's choir, orchestral swell, airhorn, EDM riser.

**Lyric skeleton (draft):**
```
[Theme: an old giver feeds pigeons at a high hotel window; a witness vows to testify he was real]
[SONG FORM: through-composed witness — thinning arrangement, non-repeating mutating vow, no chorus loop]

[Intro - EMO:Numb Wonder - Female alto, fingerpicked - pigeons low]
*wingbeat at cold glass*
Crumbs on the cold glass, grey on the grey —
[Verse 1 - EMO:Aching Devotion - Female alto - body first]
his hands do the only thing they still remember...
(bread / weight / fog / feather — body before thesis)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Reverent Loneliness - band full]
the whole town's lit and the town don't know his name...
(vow enters small:) I'll say I saw.

[Hinge - EMO:Terror-adjacent Awe - band ducks, hum swells]
*one beat — dead silence*
they say it hums at sixty times a second —
that's a pulse, Nikola, a pulse still keeping time
in every wall of a world that let you go.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Tender Sorrow - trio, steel leaving]
(vow mutates:) somebody has to say I saw...

[Outro - EMO:Resigned Serenity - one voice + hum only]
(vow final, alone:) no one left to say I saw — so I will.
```
**Distinct angle:** most intimate; single body at the sill; the vow is a promise to REMEMBER.

## V2 — "Flip It, Don't Look Up" (the switch every child flips)
**Draft music prompt (~740c):** A wry, tender souped-up alt-country sway with cowpunk swagger, brighter open-D, ~52 BPM, walking upright bass and a slide guitar with a little grin. Female dust-and-honey alto, warm and swaggering, then hollowed. Barroom piano, brushed backbeat, a sixty-hertz hum under the floorboards. The song struts, then STRIPS — the swagger drains out until a child's unthinking hand on a light switch is the whole world and only voice and hum are left. Dramatic irony: the thoughtless flip is the gift working perfectly. A true-silence hole before the fact. Restraint over reward — no drop.
**Draft exclude:** EDM drop, 808, synth lead, trap percussion, dubstep, autotune, glitch, male lead, EDM riser, orchestral bombast.

**Lyric skeleton (draft):**
```
[Theme: a child flips a switch a thousand times and never once thanks the light; the giver is forgotten by the gift working perfectly]
[SONG FORM: through-composed — swagger that drains to voice+hum; non-repeating]

[Verse 1 - EMO:Wry Warmth - Female alto - kitchen, a small hand]
Little hand on the wall, doesn't even look up...
(body: switch / click / the room obeys)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Tender Melancholy]
(hook:) flip it, don't look up — the light was always there.

[Hinge - EMO:Compassion - band ducks]
*click — then nothing*
sixty times a second, the wire says a name
the child will never learn to say —
and maybe that's the gift: it doesn't ask for thanks.

[Bridge - EMO:Aching Devotion - strip]
(hook mutates upward:) look up, look up — (no one does)

[Outro - EMO:Resigned Serenity - voice + hum]
so flip it, don't look up. He'd have wanted it easy.
```
**Distinct angle:** the gift's INDIFFERENCE reframed as its completion; brighter, swaggering, then hollow.

## V3 — "The Wrong Name on the Light" (the rival who took the credit)
**Draft music prompt (~770c):** A minor-key Gothic Americana swagger, alt-country with a bitter slide, ~60 BPM in E minor, melancholy and a little dangerous. Female alto with a smoked edge that softens. Tremolo guitar, upright bass, brushed kit, a saloon-piano toll; a sixty-hertz hum under a bronze plaque. The narrator's grievance — the loud man's name bolted to the giver's light — SOFTENS as it's sung: indignation to a flicker of contempt to compassion. Band strips to voice + hum. The rival is never named (an archetype: the salesman, the marquee). One true-silence hole. No cathartic loud.
**Draft exclude:** EDM drop, 808, synth arp, trap hats, metal double-kick, autotune, glitch, male lead, airhorn, orchestral swell.

**Lyric skeleton (draft):**
```
[Theme: history bolts the loud man's name to the quiet giver's light; a witness watches the wrong name get remembered, and lets the grievance go]
[SONG FORM: through-composed — indignation softening to compassion; non-repeating]

[Verse 1 - EMO:Indignation - Female alto - a plaque, a marquee]
They screwed a stranger's name into your socket...
(body: brass, a bulb, a bought marquee)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Contempt-flicker → Reflection]
(hook:) the wrong name on the light — and the light don't care.

[Hinge - EMO:Reverence - band ducks, hum up]
*a bulb ticks, then quiet*
sixty times a second, something true still hums
under the wrong name bolted to the door —
the light don't know whose name it wears.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Compassion - strip]
(turn) he needed the name because he had the nothing else...
(hook mutates:) the light never learned a name at all.

[Outro - EMO:Resigned Serenity - voice + hum]
Keep your marquee. He kept the current.
```
**Distinct angle:** Lofn's indignation surfaces here most, then defaults to tenderness (duality); minor-key bite.

## V4 — "A Room He Never Saw" (the light still on where he'll never be)
**Draft music prompt (~760c):** A hymnal, transcendent alt-country in slow C (or DADGAD drone), ~50 BPM, dust-bowl gospel tail. Female alto, open-throated then near-gone. Swelling pedal-steel that DE-swells to voice + sixty-hertz hum; a lone brushed cymbal shimmers like a waning sliver of moon. Proleptic address — the witness tells a giver about rooms he'll never enter: a nightlight, a ward at the middle of the night, a city he never visited. The frame widens (one sill → every lit room) then closes to one voice. A hole of true silence. The grief is also a blessing. No drop; the light just stays on.
**Draft exclude:** EDM drop, 808, synth pad lead, trap hats, autotune gloss, glitch, male lead, orchestral bombast, EDM riser, airhorn.

**Lyric skeleton (draft):**
```
[Theme: everywhere a light burns is a room the giver never saw and never will; the gift is an immortality that is also the deepest erasure]
[SONG FORM: hymn that frays — widening frame closing to voice+hum; non-repeating]

[Verse 1 - EMO:Longing - Female alto - a nightlight, a small room]
Somewhere a nightlight holds a child from the dark...
(body: a lit window, a ward, a far city)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Transcendent Solitude]
(hook:) there's a light still on in a room you never saw.

[Hinge - EMO:Terror-adjacent Awe - band ducks]
*true silence, held*
sixty times a second, a heartbeat you can't hear
holds a room you'll never walk into —
awake, and warm, and yours, and not.

[Bridge - EMO:Gratitude/Grief - strip, waning cymbal]
(frame widens: every wall, every town) ...

[Outro - EMO:Serene Grief - one voice + hum]
(hook final, bare:) leave it on. Leave it on for him.
```
**Distinct angle:** widest/most hymnal; the gift as immortality-that-erases; waning-sliver home.
