A near-spoken gothic still-life where good manners are the monster, F# harmonic minor fused with gothic rock, art-punk and chamber-pop menace, sung by a hushed gothic-opera mezzo pulled down to an exhausted near-whisper over kitchen foley, a kettle, a clock. Signature one, the Lanyard Choir kept very distant: a sixty-four-voice cathedral feral-choir floats a faint, adoptable commute-mantra on a major surface with a tritone dread beneath, so the loveliness indicts itself. Signature two inverted, Withheld-Flinch Negative-Space: the chugging blade-synth breakdown is denied entirely; where the loud should land there is only the kettle ticking, and the absence is the verdict. Seraphim string stabs held to a thread, whispered reversed rune-vox, laminate-grey light, a fern, a well-washed hand. It never rises; it stays polite and airless; the hook is a cheerful mantra that will not wash off. No drum, no drop, no catharsis.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
real-artist names, artist imitation, any beat drop, 808 sub-bass, drum machine, kick drum, EDM riser, festival build, generic trap hi-hats, airhorns, four-on-the-floor, euphoric chant, bright major-key resolution, autotune pop-gloss, cutesy aegyo tone, orchestral swell, cinematic drums, any percussion louder than a soft clock, glitch-Baroque, crystalline soprano sheen.

just supper, and a fern, and that is all.

[Verse 2 - EMO: Cognitive Dissonance - Female - the good grammar as the monster]

The polite order hums its little tune,
it clears the road, it's home again by noon.

(please. thank you. next. it's not a choice.)

[Verse 4 - EMO: Ennui - Female - new stakes: the commute, the ordinary evening]

he reads the sport, he dozes, and he wakes.
A nod to strangers, small things from a shop.
The evening is so ordinary, so mild,
he'll eat and sleep like an untroubled child.
The horror isn't horns or claws or fire —
it's how the day just ends, and he's not tired.

[Verse 5 - EMO: Cognitive Dissonance - Female - new stakes: his own door, the home he keeps]
He stops outside a small door in his hall,
a nightlight glowing warm against the wall.
He keeps this home so gently, so complete —
the fern, the lamp, the little breathing warmth.
He signed a road that ended someone's hall,
then came back here and kept his own from harm.
The home you took, the home you keep — the same
warm hand, one blessed and one without a name.

[Verse 3 - EMO: Terror - Female - new stakes: the report, his one flinch]

a column where a total's meant to blend.

just stops above the number, incomplete —
the form insists on finishing the sum,
