## DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (whole set)
**Predicted render:** a black-chrome gothic-opera mezzo over F# harmonic-minor chug, a 64-voice cathedral choir singing a gold, adoptable slogan that curdles as the words turn, seraphim strings bleeding in at the reveal, a chugging blade-synth guitar that collapses to a bass-led hollow and one beat of dead air while an ambulance light keeps turning; V3 slows it to a lament, V4 withholds the loud entirely and lets a kettle and a kissed forehead carry the horror.

**Name the one way this renders as grief-tourism / a mosh-reward / generic:**
> *The Lanyard Choir + full-150 anthem (esp. V2) renders the checkpoint as a triumphant Summerween stadium banger — the crowd moshes to "finish the job," the atrocity becomes the hype, the gothic costume becomes cosplay, and the dead are the drop.* That is exactly Odell's veto and MacKaye's flinch.

**Self-repair (applied ONCE, across the set):**
- The loud is **de-tuned, early, riser-less, mono, half-timed, felt-in-the-chest** — a flinch, never a release; **V2's** biggest swell **sours and cuts to silence** at the reveal rather than dropping; **V3** replaces the chug with a string-collapse; **V4 withholds the loud entirely.**
- The **choir's brightness is the Costume the song strips** — the hook indicts itself in one listen, so the crowd cannot mosh innocently; the anthem **never resolves to major triumph.**
- **twenty-eight** is severed once and dropped into a true silence-marble; **no digit is sung; no body-count.** The spooky/gothic stays **texture**, never joke; the child is abstract, alive, and never a depicted victim.
→ With this, the recurring refused-number and Lanyard Choir read as **band signature**, and the war is **never danceable.**

## MEASURED BINDING CONSTRAINT
**The 74-line floor vs. the one-fact / no-body-count / no-identifiable-person discipline.** Every line added to clear the 70-line floor had to open NEW stakes (the woman behind the medic; the crowd's answer becoming complicity; the crew aboard; the signer's fern and the kissed forehead) **without** introducing a second figure, a second number, or any locating detail that could fingerprint a real person or make the child a depicted victim. That tension — not rhyme or melody — governed the final length and content of all four.
