# 04 — Orchestrator Metaprompt — "Summerween: The Costume Is Not the Monster"

## The record in one line
Six songs for 2026-07-09 built on a single **reveal**: the thing wearing the monster's costume is a cradle, and the real monster wears a lanyard. Above us, a red nebula shaped like a bat is secretly a nursery of new stars. Below, "Finish the Job" is a to-do item, an ambulance waits at a checkpoint — while a bee learns to feel and an inventor who lit the world dies in the dark. Each song is a surface that cracks once to show its opposite.

## House voice
**Lofn-Prime** is the curatorial spine and sings **3 of 6** (spread across different arm×theme cells so they don't converge — L12). The other 3 are guests she hosts in *their own* DNA: **Sk8rCry** (emo-punk catharsis), **BioMorph-X** (uncanny-life bio-horror), **HELLEVATE** (gothic horror-pop). Guest variation-angles were vetted against personality boundaries (L13) — no guest sings Lofn's exclusive devices (AI code-scratch / priced-interiority / quantum bit-depth swell).

## Locked mood
**Terror-adjacent tenderness on Summerween** — loving the thing everyone calls a monster while looking straight at the real cruelty that wears no mask. The fear is real; so is the cradle inside it. Each pair narrows to one obscure emotion (named in its brief, never sung).

## Panel aha-moments (carry into every pair)
- *After Flannery O'Connor (Skeptic):* the grace must **cost** — the nursery is glimpsed *through* real dread, never a cute twist. If the bat is cute, it's failed.
- *After Hannah Arendt:* the real monster wears a **lanyard**, not fangs — "Finish the Job" is evil as paperwork. Don't let the pretty reveal launder it.
- *After Ian MacKaye (Skeptic):* the loud guitar is the **flinch, not the reward** — score a war like a basement, not a festival. If the loud is a payoff, cut it.
- *After Nikola Tesla:* the gift **warms people who forget the giver** — that's the price, not a sweetness.
- *After Jenny Odell (Skeptic):* **silence-marbling is mandatory** (a real hole in every mix) — that's how the quiet voice survives the punk; the spooky is a texture, never the joke.
