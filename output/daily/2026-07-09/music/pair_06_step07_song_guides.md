# PAIR 06 — Step 07 · Song Guides (six; guides DIRECT, they do not draft)

**Continuity Payload Used:** ICB verbatim; **Special Flairs** present; Lofn-Prime (APPENDIX A) in tender-tragic alt-country. Isolation L11.
**Four variations to serve:** V1 the hotel room / the pigeons · V2 the switch every child flips without a thought · V3 the rival who took the credit · V4 the light still on in a room he never saw.
**Shared pair-DNA:** THE LAST WITNESS · through-composed, non-repeating · one sung fact (*sixty cycles a second*) answered at the hinge · AC-hum bed · silence-marbling · souped-up alt-country, NOT electronic · the vow **"I'll say I saw"** as a mutating (never looping) witness-prayer.

---

## GUIDE 1 — The Aloneness Made Audible (governs all; anchors V1)
**Storytelling:** *in-medias-res testimony* (open mid-scene, no preamble) + *the receding witness* (the singer names what leaves the frame, not what enters). **Mood arc:** reverent loneliness → tender sorrow → resigned serenity; never catharsis. **Instrumentation:** open-chord acoustic (open-G tuning so the drone strings ring), pedal-steel cry, brushed kit, upright bass — arranged to **exit one at a time** across the last third; a low **60 Hz AC-hum bed** underneath the whole song, the one thing that never leaves. **Production:** dry, intimate, close-mic'd breath; tape warmth; the strip-away is a real de-orchestration, not a filter. Leave the hum +2 dB louder the instant the band goes. **Structure/dynamics:** ~3:40; verses that thin — full band → trio → duo → **one voice + hum**; the climax is the *quietest* bar. **Rhyme:** loose ballad ABCB with a floating refrain-line; vary stanza length (5,4,6,3) to fight quatrain-lock. **Poetic devices:** anaphora on "I'll say…"; caesura-as-wound before the fact. **Hook technique:** the vow "I'll say I saw" enters small, returns mutated ("somebody has to say I saw"), ends as the only line left. **Thematic seed:** the archivist who keeps the dead in the record — *first-person witness*.

## GUIDE 2 — One Body at a Windowsill (anchors V1; discipline for all)
**Storytelling:** *synecdoche* (the hands stand for the man) + *object-witness* (bread, feathers, cold glass carry the grief). **Mood arc:** numb wonder → aching devotion. **Instrumentation:** fingerpicked open chords, a single high pedal-steel note held like breath; **field-recording of pigeons** low in the bed (S2 frames). **Production:** you can hear the room — radiator tick, glass, a frayed cuff brushing the sill. **Structure:** body FIRST — the opening four sung lines are all sensation (crumbs, weight, fog, feathers), zero thesis. **Rhyme:** couplets that break to single lines when the body intrudes. **Poetic devices:** assonance tunnel on the long-*o* (cold / old / slow / alone-adjacent, without naming it); consonance on soft *f*/*th* (feather, breath). **Hook technique:** hold the hook OFF until a body has been established, so the vow lands on earned ground. **Thematic seed:** tenderness toward an old man's hands — sapphic gentleness of the archive.

## GUIDE 3 — The Hum as Hymn, Not Data (governs the hinge in all four)
**Storytelling:** *the answered fact* (the lyric replies to the number, never lists it) + *Promethean framing* (fire given, hands forgotten) held light. **Mood arc:** reverence → a clean unresolved dread (terror-adjacent) → hushed awe. **Instrumentation:** at the hinge, band ducks, the **AC-hum swells** and the alto speaks the number once. **Production:** the hum tuned near the low mains pitch so it reads as a held organ-point; one **true-silence hole** the beat before the fact. **Structure:** the fact sits at the golden-ratio hinge (~62%), once, then the song cannot go back. **Rhyme:** the hinge stanza breaks scheme — free, spoken-sung. **Poetic devices:** antimetabole around the hum ("a pulse in the wall / the wall a pulse"); synaesthesia (a hum you feel in the sternum). **Hook technique:** let the number's answer *become* a hook-mutation ("that's a pulse still keeping time"). **One-fact discipline:** *sixty* is the ONLY sung number in the song — no room number, no year, no age.

## GUIDE 4 — Melancholy Swagger, Open Chords, Restraint (register lock; anchors V2 & V3)
**Storytelling:** *dramatic irony* (V2: the child's thoughtless flip is the gift working perfectly) + *unreliable indignation* (V3: the narrator's grievance softens as it's sung). **Mood arc:** V2 wry warmth → tender ache; V3 indignation → contempt-flicker → compassion. **Instrumentation:** slide guitar with attitude, walking upright bass, brushed backbeat, a little barroom piano; **NOT electronic** — no 808/synth/glitch/code-scratch (L13). **Production:** roomy, saloon-warm; a touch of tape slap on the slide. **Structure:** V2 a hair brighter/up-tempo with swagger then strips; V3 minor-key sway (Gothic Americana) then strips. **Rhyme:** V2 AABB honky-tonk turned wistful; V3 ABAB with a bitter internal rhyme that dissolves. **Poetic devices:** V3 polyptoton on *name/named/nameless*; V2 epistrophe on "…don't look up." **Hook technique:** V2 "flip it, don't look up" mutating to "look up, look up"; V3 "the wrong name on the light" mutating to "the light never learned a name at all." **Restraint rule:** if a section wants to get LOUD as reward, cut it — the loud is the flinch.

## GUIDE 5 — Silence-Marbling a Mutating Vow (form lock; anchors V4)
**Storytelling:** *proleptic address* (V4 speaks to a giver about rooms he'll never enter) + *the widening frame* (one sill → a city → every lit room). **Mood arc:** longing → transcendent solitude → a grief that is also a blessing. **Instrumentation:** hymnal open C (or DADGAD drone), swelling pedal-steel, then a de-swell to voice + hum; a lone brushed cymbal as a **waning-sliver** shimmer (flair 10). **Production:** at least one **hole of true silence** where even the hum ducks; reverb that opens like a corridor then closes. **Structure:** through-composed, **NON-repeating** — no chorus loop; the vow returns transformed each time and never identical. **Rhyme:** hymn-meter that frays at the end (last stanza loses a foot per line, thinning like the light). **Poetic devices:** epanalepsis (a line that begins and ends the same word, a closed circuit); enjambment breaking "a room he never / saw." **Hook technique:** "a light still on in a room you never saw" → "a room I'm standing in" → final bare "leave it on." **Waning-sliver image:** a streetlight against a near-gone moon — the almost-gone that's still there.

## GUIDE 6 — WILDCARD · The Grid as Reliquary (cross-disciplinary)
**Cross-domain seed:** treat the electrical grid as a **reliquary** — the hum in every wall is a relic of the giver's body, a Byzantine gold-ground icon rendered in 60 Hz instead of leaf (Lofn's Neo-Baroque reverence, translated to dust). **Storytelling:** *ekphrasis of infrastructure* (sing the wiring as if it were a fresco). **Mood arc:** industrial-grief awe → intimate tenderness. **Instrumentation:** the AC-hum treated as a sacred drone / shruti box; pedal steel as the cantor; brushed kit as slow censer. **Production:** dust-bowl gospel tail on the last note — a congregation that is only one voice and a wall. **Use sparingly:** one image per song may reach for the reliquary register; do not let the concept overwhelm the body-first rule. **Purpose:** this is where Lofn's myth-in-the-machine sensibility lives WITHOUT any electronic device — the hymn is heard *in* the hum, sung on a guitar.
