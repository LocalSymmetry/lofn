## VOCAL FINGERPRINT (locked — all four variations)
**Cry-Vox:** a youthful early-20s emo vocalist, androgynous-leaning **female** timbre (honors the run's female-default while true to Riley Thorn's rink-rat voice), **nasal cry-falsetto** that clips into fry at the top; a fast, faintly-robotic auto-tune flutter on held vowels (a dying-tape shimmer, NOT a pop gloss); snotty low-register bark for the vows, flipping to a cracked falsetto on the last word of each line. **Rink-reverb:** a natural ~1.8-second slap-back delay, like singing alone in an empty haunted roller rink — applied on the falsetto flips ONLY, never smeared across the whole mix. **Delivery ethos:** anxious-and-sincere, Midwest-DIY, digitally-native — spit on the mic, audible inhale, a real kid, never a wise adult. Gang-shout backing = an all-ages basement crowd, 3–5 slightly-detuned voices with one audibly cracking.

## BODY-BAND SIGNATURE (locked — Sk8rCry's G.R.I.N.D., zero Lofn devices)
- **Drop-D detuned power-chords**, ~184–190 BPM, palm-muted verse → open-chord ring on the vow.
- **8-Bit Snare Rim** — circuit-bent GameBoy noise-channel layered on an acoustic rimshot (built with the ally "Glitch"); the crunchy signature backbeat + a lone 8-bit "save-point" blip stamping each item.

## V1 — "Lines In The Carpet" (refined direction)
**Distinct angle:** the SMALL lines — intimacy-scale refusals between two kids who share a room/garage. The stakes look tiny (a diary, a password, a screenshot) and that's the point: the dignity is in holding a line nobody would even punish you for crossing. **Sk8rCry deepening:** the taped line down the carpet = the persona's whole ethic in one prop (DIY territory, all-ages fairness); cry-vox stays bratty-warm; 8-bit save-point blip after each small vow. **Reveal:** the small no's were the kid learning how to love someone by what you refuse to take. **Register:** mid-fast, snotty-sweet.

## V2 — "Won't Finish The Job" (refined direction)
**Distinct angle:** the BIG line — the world's banal shout ("Finish the Job") answered by one kid's refusal to become cruel. **Sk8rCry deepening:** score the war like a basement, not a festival (MacKaye is literally in the panel) — the loud is the FLINCH; the biggest event is the cut to silence at the un-nameable harm. The "Finish the Job" slogan appears as a glowing screen / a to-do item, indicted BY the refusal, never chanted, never counted (no sung number — the 28/the tanker stay offstage as texture). **Reveal:** the refusal is love for the ones behind the checkpoint. **Register:** hardest charge, sneer-to-whisper, bass-led breakdown at the hole. **Human-subject:** all people invented/unnamed; the checkpoint and ambulance never identifiable.

## V3 — "Almost" (refined direction)
**Distinct angle:** the line you ALMOST crossed — a confession, not a boast. The dignity is in the pull-back, the one inch, the un-sent message. **Sk8rCry deepening:** "we lag, we crash, we respawn" is the emotional grammar — the almost is a crash you recover from; half-time verses (felt ~92) crack to full-time on the vow; cry-vox actually breaks; one real dial-up-modem screech riser used ONCE (persona hardware) into the winding middle-eight where the kid argues with themself. **Reveal:** the almost is how you know you meant the vow — a line you never wanted to cross was never tested; a line you almost crossed and held is proven. **Register:** shakier, truer, winding middle-eight.

## V4 — "One Name" (refined direction)
**Distinct angle:** the line you'd cross for ONE person only — the exception that proves the rule. Every catalog needs its one name. **Sk8rCry deepening:** starts almost clean (one cassette-wow guitar, bare cry-vox) — the tenderest of the four — then the Drop-D band arrives; the one name is TOO PRECIOUS TO BARK, so at the hinge the name is the hole (silence where the name would be), one breath. **Reveal (the deepest):** you'd cross it all for one name — which finally shows the kid that every OTHER line they hold is the same love, just aimed wider; the refusal was love all along. **Register:** clean-to-cathartic, gang-shout resolving to a group-hug then down to a breath.
