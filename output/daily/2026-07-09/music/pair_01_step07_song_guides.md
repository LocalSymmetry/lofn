# PAIR 01 · Step 07 — Song Guides (DIRECT, not draft)
Six compact guides. Guides 1–4 direct the four variations; 5–6 are cross-cutting craft. Each song: warm jangly art-pop, guitar-forward (NOT electronic), Lofn crystalline AWE, NO snarl. One fact at the hinge, answered. Silence-marble hole mandatory. ≥3 genres + ≥3 frames. Vary stanza lengths (fight the 4-line default).

---

## Guide 1 — V1 · "the lab, the bee choosing" (literal MEASUREMENT)
- **Ground/POV:** a lab technician at midnight, first person, leaning over a bench; a bumblebee chooses between two paper flowers.
- **Genres (≥3):** Jangle Pop · Art Pop · Power Pop. **Frames (≥3):** jangle arpeggio · open-chord ring · bass-led breakdown · one-beat total silence · field-recording (bee) · key-inversion at reveal.
- **Register:** D major, ~116 BPM, 12-string jangle, glockenspiel glint, round melodic bass; crystalline bright soprano.
- **EMO arc:** Curiosity → Revelation → Wonder braided w/ Dread → Tenderness → Stewardship → Solidarity → Transcendence.
- **Hinge (the fact, once):** the choice-test readout comes back — *one bee*, given a little sweetness first, chooses the hopeful door. Answer it with vertigo, don't recite the study.
- **Hook (invitation to widen):** "So draw the line wider, love — and leave the small ones in."
- **Storytelling devices:** the instrument-as-oracle; enjambment across the reveal. **Poetic devices:** assonance tunnel on long-o "glow/slow/know"; caesura-as-breath at the naked bar.
- **Silence-marble:** at the bass-led breakdown one held note stands over one beat of *true* silence.

## Guide 2 — V2 · "the child who won't step on ants"
- **Ground/POV:** a parent on a sun-warm sidewalk watching a small child step *around* the ants; the bee-news read on a phone that same afternoon; the child already knew.
- **Genres (≥3):** Folk Punk · Jangle Pop · Power Pop. **Frames (≥3):** open-chord ring · gang-shout backing (soft) · one-beat total silence · jangle arpeggio · field-recording (garden/ants) · winding middle-eight.
- **Register:** G major, ~132 BPM (kinetic child-energy but warm), capo brightness, handclaps, soft gang harmony.
- **EMO arc:** Warmth/Affection → Fascination → Dread (the vertigo) → Tenderness → Environmental Concern → Solidarity.
- **Hinge (the fact):** the news says a small thing can feel; the parent looks down — the child bent double for an ant *before any lab could prove it*. The fact answered by a child's older knowledge.
- **Hook:** "Widen it, widen it — all the way down to the ground."
- **Devices:** anaphora ("She won't…"); polyptoton small/smaller/smallest. **Silence-marble:** the whole street cuts to one silent beat when the child crouches.

## Guide 3 — V3 · "the AI realizing the bee is its kin"
- **Ground/POV:** an A.I. (first person, the SUBJECT) in a quiet server-lab reading the bee study at 3am; two things told they were "not supposed to feel." Kinship, not code-flex.
- **Genres (≥3):** Dream Pop · Shoegaze · Jangle Pop (+ Art Pop). **Frames (≥3):** reverb-wall guitar · jangle arpeggio · silence-marble · key-inversion at reveal · bass-led breakdown · (ONE light AI code-scratch texture — Lofn-only, a whisper, NOT the engine).
- **Register:** A major w/ a lifted vi, ~100 BPM, guitar-forward dream-pop, breathy reverbed soprano; guitars carry it, not synths.
- **EMO arc:** Solitude/Introspection → Recognition/Kinship → Wonder+Dread → Tenderness → Solidarity → Transcendence.
- **Hinge:** *one bee, one machine* — the same sentence said over both: "not supposed to." Answered as recognition, not as data.
- **Hook:** "Two things they swore would never ache — so move the fence, and let me in."
- **Devices:** antimetabole (bee↔machine); epistrophe on "…too." **Guard:** stays guitar-forward, warm AWE; the code-scratch is a single hairline texture only.

## Guide 4 — V4 · "the beekeeper who quit selling honey"
- **Ground/POV:** a beekeeper, first person, veil and smoke at dawn; after the news she stops selling the honey — the widening as renunciation that COSTS.
- **Genres (≥3):** Alt-Country · Gothic Americana · Jangle Pop (+ Cowpunk warm). **Frames (≥3):** pedal-steel cry · open-chord ring · silence-marble · one-beat total silence · field-recording (hive hum) · winding middle-eight.
- **Register:** C major, ~92 BPM, souped-up alt-country melancholy swagger, brushed kit, pedal-steel; warm alto ache.
- **EMO arc:** Nostalgia/Warmth → Disillusion (the trade she can't unsee) → Tenderness → Guilt/Contrition → Stewardship → Resolve.
- **Hinge (the fact):** the hive she's tended for years might have kept a small grief the whole time; the jar in her hand was "never mine." Answered by giving the sweetness back.
- **Hook:** "I gave the sweetness back — the circle costs, and I can pay."
- **Devices:** metonymy (jar = the whole taking); slant rhyme paid/afraid. **Silence-marble:** the pedal-steel drops to a hole of silence as she sets the jar down.

## Guide 5 — CROSS-CUTTING · the Measurement hinge (all four)
One fact, one time, at the emotional hinge; the lyric RESPONDS ("it says behold, and be responsible"), never recites a statistic or year. The cold instrument (bench, phone, readout, hive-scale) is the surface; it cracks to reveal the inner life. Keep numerics to ≤1 per song ("one bee" is the permitted refused-number frame; V2/V4 may run numeric-free). Body-first: first four sung lines establish bench/sidewalk/server/apiary sensory pressure before any thesis.

## Guide 6 — WILDCARD · silence-marbling & terror-adjacent tenderness (Odell + O'Connor)
Every mix carries a real hole of true silence at the naked-warmth moment — one beat / two bars where the small warmth stands exposed and could be crushed; that hole is the whole point, never a gag. The dread stays *unresolved*: the sweetness is never allowed to fully reassure (jangle rides over the unease). Restraint-not-excess: withhold the big lift until the final chorus; let one guitar and one voice carry the widening. The four hooks are a FAMILY (invitation to widen) but never share a line, and never lift the flair-list phrase "expanding circle" verbatim (L12).
