## FIVE SCORING FACETS (judge every downstream prompt against these)

1. **The Aloneness Made Audible (S3→S1).** Does the arrangement *enact* the giver's solitude by **stripping** the band away — pedal steel, then upright bass, then brush — until only one frayed alto and a low 60 Hz hum remain, so a listener *hears* the gift outlive its giver in real time? Sensory: the exact bar the slide-guitar leaves and the wall-hum is suddenly the loudest thing. **Reject** any arrangement that BUILDS to a payoff instead of thinning to a hole (MacKaye).

2. **One Body at a Windowsill (S1→S3).** Is there ONE concrete body in ONE concrete place — an old man's hands, a pigeon's weight, bread crumbs on cold glass — inside the first four sung lines, *before* any thesis about legacy? Sensory: crumbs, grey feathers, a frayed cuff, breath fogging the pane. **Reject** concept-illustration ("a song about being forgotten"); demand experience-report.

3. **The Hum as Hymn, Not Data (S2↔S1).** Is the single fact — *sixty cycles a second* — **answered** at the emotional hinge (the hum as his pulse still keeping time in every wall of a world that let him go), never recited as trivia, and is it the **only** sung number? Sensory: the low hum felt in the sternum like a kept heartbeat. **Reject** a second sung numeric fact; reject recitation.

4. **Melancholy Swagger, Open Chords, Restraint (S2).** Does the register read unmistakably **souped-up alt-country** — pedal-steel cry, open-chord ring, dust-and-honey alto, a little swagger — and **NOT electronic** (no 808, no synth arp, no glitch, no code-scratch)? Sensory: brushed snare, an upright bass walking, a slide bending like a throat closing. **Reject** electronic textures; reject excess.

5. **Silence-Marbling a Mutating Vow (S3→S2).** Is there at least one hole of **true silence** (a held beat where even the hum ducks and the next word costs something), and does the witness-prayer hook **mutate** across the song rather than loop (through-composed, non-repeating — no chorus cycle)? Sensory: the dead beat before "I'll say I saw" returns changed. **Reject** a repeating chorus; reject decorative spookiness (Odell: spooky = texture).
