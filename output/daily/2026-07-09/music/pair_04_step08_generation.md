## V1 (DRAFT) — "Finish the Job" → the checkpoint
- **title (draft):** *The Lanyard at the Barrier* / *Home by Six* (V4 stole "home by six" — retitle V1 → **Clipboard Gospel**)
- **hook note:** a bright feral-choir chants "FINISH THE JOB"; the lead turns it — the job is an ambulance, the job is a child.
- **music gist:** gothic horror-pop-punk, F# harmonic minor, ~150 half-timing to the breakdown; Lanyard Choir + Flinch Breakdown; ambulance-red glow; the loud is the flinch.
- **draft chorus:**
  > Finish the job — (finish the job)
  > the laminate says so, soft.
  > Finish the job — (finish the job)
  > there's a siren it turned off.
  > We wore the fangs to scare the street —
  > the monster wore a lanyard, neat.
- **verse seed:** the medic at the barrier, the clerk's "please step back," the form, the red light turning.
- **gaps:** need body-first opening (20s); need the twenty-eight refusal at hinge; need silence-marble; need V4-distinct meter.

## V2 (DRAFT) — the slogan on a screen, chanted
- **title (draft):** *Gold Letters* / **Teach Them the Chant**
- **hook note:** the idol on stage taught the crowd the chant; the chant had teeth she didn't see. Slogan centered.
- **music gist:** full 150 anthem, the "banger" costume; feral-choir at full power then souring; stage-light → checkpoint floodlight.
- **draft chorus:**
  > Finish the job tonight — (tonight!)
  > the letters burn so gold and bright.
  > Finish the job tonight — (tonight!)
  > …the job's got a face under the light.
  > I taught you this. I taught you this.
  > I did not know the words could kiss a fist.
- **verse seed:** the roar, the phones, the gold letters, the ticker scrolling.
- **gaps:** highest mosh-reward risk — MUST sour the drop; refusal on the ticker; silence where the crowd cuts out.

## V3 (DRAFT) — the tanker on fire, from shore
- **title (draft):** **A Name in Every Flame** / *Pretty Pyre*
- **hook note:** the watcher finds the fire beautiful (the costume), then sees the crew. "Let it burn" → a name in every flame.
- **music gist:** SLOW gothic-opera ballad ~82; seraphim strings foreground; eclipse-fire red glow; the crack is a string-collapse not a chug.
- **draft chorus:**
  > Let it burn — (let it burn)
  > the water wears a crown of fire.
  > Let it burn — (let it burn)
  > …there's a name in every spire.
  > I raised my phone to keep the glow —
  > I lowered it. I didn't know.
- **verse seed:** the beach, the phones up, the gorgeous red, the strait, the men aboard.
- **gaps:** must stay a LAMENT (distinct from anthems); refusal on the news app; the phone lowered = the silence-marble.

## V4 (DRAFT) — the one who signs and goes home
- **title (draft):** **Home by Six** / *Good Evening*
- **hook note:** perpetrator POV; the polite order; the drop is WITHHELD; the calm is the verdict.
- **music gist:** slowest, near-spoken gothic; drumless-ish; cathedral haunt distant; **Withheld-Flinch Negative-Space**; baroque still-life of a nice home.
- **draft chorus:**
  > Sign it, stamp it, home by six —
  > water the fern, the evening fixed.
  > Sign it, stamp it, home by six —
  > …not the home you took, the home you keep.
  > Good evening. Sorry for the wait.
  > He held the door. He locked the gate.
- **verse seed:** the office, the pen, the commute, the fern, the kissed forehead, the report left "pending."
- **gaps:** refusal is HIS single suppressed flinch (pen stalls at "twenty-eight—" / "no." / leaves it blank); must NOT read as sympathy — the good grammar IS the horror.
