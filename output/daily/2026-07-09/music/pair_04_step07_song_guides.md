# PAIR 04 — Step 07 — Song Guides (HELLEVATE · gothic horror-pop-punk)

> Six guides (five facets + one wildcard). Dense direction, not drafts. All in HELLEVATE's eclipse-fire register; F# harmonic minor default; the two signatures (**Lanyard Choir**, **Flinch Breakdown**) recur; **no Lofn devices** (L13).

---

## GUIDE 1 — Costume-vs-Interior Body Truth (facet 1)
- **Storytelling techniques:** *dramatic irony* (the listener sees the monster under the good manners before the crowd does) + *anticlimax-as-horror* (the atrocity is administered in the register of an errand).
- **Mood & arc:** open in **bright civic Reverence** (the slogan feels holy, the choir gorgeous) → slide to **Dread** as the polite surface holds → **Revelation** at the crack → **Moral Outrage** that will not resolve. Sensory: floodlight white, laminate lanyard, red ambulance sweep, the smell of a photocopier warm from use.
- **Instrumentation:** feral-choir stacks (K-pop-precise) over palm-muted chug; seraphim string stabs high-passed; a lone gothic-mezzo lead close-mic'd. **Add Vocals** for the 64-voice stack.
- **Production:** cathedral plate on the choir vs bone-dry lead; the polite surface mixed *pretty*, the interior mixed *close and airless*.
- **Structure/dynamics:** body established inside 20s; the reveal past the midpoint; **silence-marble** hole mandatory. Runtime ~3:20.
- **Rhyme/poetic devices:** **anaphora** on the slogan; **antanaclasis** on "the job" (errand → atrocity); slant rhyme so nothing resolves too sweetly.
- **Hook techniques:** (a) the slogan sung *up*, major, adoptable; (b) the same words returned *down*, minor, indicting; (c) the crowd-chant left ringing into the silence.
- **Narrative seed:** *the banal is the monster* — first person witness at the barrier.

## GUIDE 2 — The-Loud-Is-a-Flinch Dramaturgy (facet 2)
- **Storytelling:** *deferred catharsis* (the "drop" the body expects is withheld or soured) + *sensory displacement* (the loud is felt in the chest as a blow three streets over, not heard as a hook).
- **Mood & arc:** **Anticipation** built like a stadium anthem → the build **flinches** instead of pays off → **Numbness**, then **Determination** that is a refusal, not a triumph.
- **Instrumentation:** **Flinch Breakdown** — chugging blade-synth-doubled guitars collapse to a bass-led hollow, then true silence; a distant sub that is a strike, not a party. No riser. No four-on-the-floor.
- **Production:** the breakdown de-tuned, early, riser-less, mono, felt-not-celebrated; **Odell's mandate — a real hole of silence in every mix.**
- **Structure:** the loudest moment is the flinch, placed at the reveal, immediately voided into the marble. Runtime ~3:20.
- **Poetic devices:** **caesura** at the cut; **onomatopoeia** withheld (the expected boom becomes a breath).
- **Hook techniques:** (a) chorus that stops mid-swell; (b) the sub as a body-blow not a bass-drop; (c) the choir cut to one bare voice.
- **Narrative seed:** *grief is never danceable* — score the war like a basement to forty people, not a festival.

## GUIDE 3 — Self-Indicting Chant Hook (facet 3)
- **Storytelling:** *turn on the refrain* (the hook changes meaning without changing words) + *call-and-response as complicity* (the crowd answers the slogan, then is implicated by it).
- **Mood & arc:** **Zeal** (the chant is thrilling) → **Cognitive Dissonance** (the words curdle) → **Contempt** for the polite order that authored them.
- **Instrumentation:** gang-shout backing + Lanyard Choir; whoa-oh runic hook that is hummable in 15s; blade-synth doubling the vocal top line.
- **Production:** the chant bright and forward; the indicting return darker, closer, the reverb pulled off so it feels like it's leaning into your ear.
- **Structure:** hook stated bright early; mutated to indict at each return; final return unresolved. Runtime ~3:20.
- **Poetic devices:** **antimetabole** (job→child / child→job), **anaphora**, **epistrophe** on "— no."
- **Hook techniques:** three deliveries per variation so the recurring architecture reads as signature, not repetition.
- **Narrative seed:** *a chant that indicts its own slogan* — the costume is worn in the hook and torn off by the hook.

## GUIDE 4 — Refused-Number Restraint (facet 4)
- **Storytelling:** *severance* (the figure is cut off mid-utterance) + *negative space* (what is refused is louder than what is said).
- **Mood & arc:** **Apprehension** as the mechanism reaches for the count → **Moral Outrage** at the hinge → **Vulnerability** in the marble that follows.
- **Instrumentation:** the ledger/ticker/radio as a cold voice (spectral, institutional); the lead severs it; then bare breath.
- **Production:** the number produced *by the machine*, severed by a single dry "no," dropped into one beat of true silence — no reverb tail on the cut.
- **Structure:** exactly once, at the emotional hinge; never beside another figure; **no digit in any sung line.**
- **Poetic devices:** **aposiopesis** (the broken-off "twenty-eight—"), **caesura**.
- **Hook techniques:** the refusal itself becomes the recurring gesture across all four.
- **Narrative seed:** *the refused number* (flair 14) — you will not be a total on a board.

## GUIDE 5 — HELLEVATE Eclipse-Fire Register (facet 5)
- **Storytelling:** *the monster-idol reveal* (we wore the fangs; the lanyard is the monster) + *mirror-image* (the winged idol in the mirrored catacomb sees the polite clerk looking back).
- **Mood & arc:** **Fascination** (gothic spectacle) → **Terror** (the true monster) → **Defiance** (bind and unbind).
- **Instrumentation:** cathedral feral-choir, seraphim strings, blade-synth riffs, whispered reversed rune-vox, gothic-opera mezzo lead; F# harmonic minor; black-chrome/magenta ornament.
- **Production:** baroque density on the choir, punk grit on the guitars; eclipse-fire red-glow as the recurring color; **no crystalline-soprano sheen, no glitch-Baroque, no code-scratch** (that is Lofn).
- **Structure:** the idol's anthem cracks to show what wears no costume. Runtime ~3:20.
- **Poetic devices:** **synaesthesia** (the light tastes of laminate), **polyptoton** (sign/signed/signature).
- **Hook techniques:** runic anthem stacked to 64, then stripped to one idol's bare throat.
- **Narrative seed:** *idols of the third realm* indicting the real third-realm monster — the one with good grammar.

## GUIDE 6 — WILDCARD: The Polite Order as Baroque Still-Life
- **Cross-disciplinary seed:** paint the atrocity as a **Dutch still-life / vanitas** — a stapler, a laminated pass, a fern, a mug going cold, a kissed forehead — every object rendered in loving baroque detail so the *ordinariness* is the terror. (Warhol's soup-can banality, harvested into this pair, sits here: the icon is the errand.)
- **Mood & arc:** **Serenity** (a lovely evening) laced with **Premonition** → **Numbness** → the listener's own **Disgust** at how *nice* it all is.
- **Instrumentation:** near-spoken gothic verse, a distant cathedral haunt, a clock, a kettle; the **Withheld-Flinch** (the loud never arrives).
- **Production:** intimate, dry, domestic foley; the silence-marble is the whole texture.
- **Poetic devices:** **zeugma** ("he signed the order and the birthday card"), **litotes** (understatement as horror).
- **Hook technique:** a commute-mantra hook that is chillingly cheerful.
- **Use:** primary engine for **V4 (the signer)**; a coloring for V1–V3's polite-surface verses.
