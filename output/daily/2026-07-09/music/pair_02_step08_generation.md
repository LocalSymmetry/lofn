## V1 (draft) — "Lines In The Carpet" · the small lines
*Serves Guides 1, 4, 5. Ground: a kid's room off the garage, a masking-tape line down a shared floor, a diary on the nightstand, a locked phone.*

**Draft music prompt (rough):** Bratty-sincere emo-punk in Drop-D, ~188 BPM, cracked-amp chug, 8-bit snare rim, cassette-wow wobble, cry-vox nasal falsetto. A kid barks a list of small trusts — won't read the diary, won't guess the password, won't screenshot the DM. Wheel-grind risers, gang-shout "(not that!)", gated breakcore half-bar every 8 bars. At the hinge (the secret that could end a friendship) the band cuts to true silence, one breath answers, list resumes soft-then-loud. Restraint, not a festival.

**Draft lyric skeleton:**
- Intro (body-first, ≤20s): the taped line on the carpet, the diary glowing, "I'm eleven and the world just told me everything's for sale."
- Item 1: won't read your diary (the open page, the trust)
- Item 2: won't guess your password / won't look
- Item 3: won't screenshot the thing you sent me scared
- Refrain (barked)
- Item 4: won't tell them what you cried about
- Item 5: won't trade your secret to sit at the table
- THE CRACK (un-crossable): the one secret that could END you — *cut to silence* — "not that" breathed
- Resumed list (breathed→loud): won't, won't, won't — but now it's love
- Refrain-gather + reveal ("last clean love")
- Outro: the tape line, still there, un-crossed

## V2 (draft) — "Won't Finish The Job" · the big line
*Serves Guides 1, 2, 3, 4. Ground: the garage, the phone/TV glowing "Finish the Job," an ambulance's light somewhere out past the door. The war of the day as texture ONLY — no sung number.*

**Draft music prompt (rough):** Hardest charge — Drop-D chug ~190 BPM, barked sneer-to-whisper, cracked-amp grit, distorted bass, gated breakcore flinch. The screen shouts "Finish the Job" like it's a chore; the kid refuses to become the thing that finishes it. At the un-nameable line (the actual harm) the whole band cuts to true silence — the loud was the flinch, not the reward (MacKaye). One breath: "not that. never that." Bass-led breakdown resumes. No slogan-chant, no body-count recited.

**Draft lyric skeleton:**
- Intro: garage, phone face-up, "Finish the Job" on the screen, three dots, a to-do list of the world
- Item 1: won't call it a chore / won't nod along
- Item 2: won't learn to shrug at the ambulance that can't get through
- Item 3: won't let them make the word "done" mean that
- Refrain (barked, indicting the slogan without chanting it)
- Item 4: won't become the hand that finishes
- THE CRACK: the un-nameable — *cut to silence* — "not that. never that." (the ONE fact: one line you won't cross, answered, no number)
- Resumed (bass-led, breathed): the small human no, held against the big banal yes
- Refrain-gather + reveal (the refusal is love for the ones behind the checkpoint)
- Outro: the screen still glowing, the kid still not moving

## V3 (draft) — "Almost" · the line you almost crossed
*Serves Guides 2, 3, 6. Ground: a specific night in the garage, a fist half-raised / a message half-typed, the almost. Winding middle-eight (Flair-adjacent).*

**Draft music prompt (rough):** Confessional emo-punk, Drop-D, half-time verses (~92 felt / 184 actual) opening to full-time on the vow; cassette-wow, cry-vox cracking, one modem-screech riser (Y2K persona hardware, not a code-scratch). The kid confesses the night they almost crossed their own line — and pulled back one inch. The winding middle-eight bends the list. At the hinge, the almost itself is the hole — silence where the crossing would've been — "but I didn't." Resume changed, shakier, truer.

**Draft lyric skeleton:**
- Intro: the garage at 2am, the half-typed message, the cursor blinking, "I had it in my hand"
- Item 1: the thing I almost sent / almost said
- Item 2: the fist I almost / the door I almost
- Refrain (barked but wavering)
- Middle-eight (winding): the argument with myself, the one inch
- THE CRACK: the almost — *cut to silence* — "but I didn't. Not that." (dignity in the pull-back)
- Resumed: shakier list, the vow held BY a hair, which is the strongest kind
- Refrain-gather + reveal (the almost is how I know I meant it)
- Outro: delete, un-send, respawn

## V4 (draft) — "One Name" · the line you'd cross for one person only
*Serves Guides 3, 5, 6. Ground: the garage, the bandmate/sibling who taught the kid guitar, the one exception. The tenderest — starts clean/slow, builds.*

**Draft music prompt (rough):** Starts almost clean — a single cassette-wow guitar, cry-vox bare — then the Drop-D band arrives, ~184 BPM. Every line in the catalog has one exception: one name. The reveal (refusal = love) lands hardest here — you'd cross it all for one person, which proves the whole list was love the whole time. At the hinge, the one name is the hole — silence where the name would be, too precious to shout — one breath. Gang-shout resolves to a group-hug, then down to a breath.

**Draft lyric skeleton:**
- Intro (clean): one guitar, "there's a list of things I'd never do — and then there's you"
- Item 1: I'd cross this one for you
- Item 2: I'd break that rule, burn that bridge, for you
- Refrain (mutated: "…but for one name, I'd cross it all")
- Item 3: the lines dissolve when it's your name
- THE CRACK: the name itself — *cut to silence* — the unspoken name, one breath (too precious to bark)
- Resumed: which is how I finally understand — every OTHER line I hold is the same love, just aimed at everyone
- Refrain-gather + full reveal ("the last clean love I've got — I've got it for you, and because of you, for everyone")
- Outro: the guitar alone again, the name never said
