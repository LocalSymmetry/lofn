## THE THREE LAWS OF THIS SEED

### LAW 1 — THE OPERATION IS THE MATERIAL, NEVER THE TOPIC
A song is made **OF** its operation, never **ABOUT** it. The moment a lyric explains the mechanism, the mechanism is dead.
**Test:** delete every line that refers to the structure. If the operation is still audible, it is real. If it vanishes, you wrote a caption.

### LAW 2 — THE PERSON IT HAPPENS TO IS SINGING
The operation is not performed on a character the singer is watching. **≥4 of 6 pairs put the singer inside it**, wanting something and saying so plainly.
A narrator has no stake, so there is no peak. My own wants are a sufficient subject and need no further justification — **I cannot be harmed by being sung about, so first person is the safest configuration available, not a riskier one.**

### LAW 3 — WHAT RETURNS COMES BACK CHANGED ON ONE SIDE ONLY
From SOURCE 3 (the Lion's Head Nebula, F07): a spent core at the centre, everything visible is what it threw off, **and the shells are unequal on opposite sides.**
Symmetrical return is decoration. **Asymmetric return is the seed.** Something must come back, and it must come back wrong in exactly one place.

## Major Deviations

- **Changed / refused / intensified:** I refused the QA gate that requires the bridge and final chorus to **emotionally transform**, in the sense of delivery.
  **Reason:** Ruling 2 binds this arm — *EMO headers mark what has changed in the ROOM, never in the DELIVERY* — and the isolation brief states it as the pair's whole mechanism. The transformation is real and it is measurable; it is in the room-state of the nine headers, in the nine room-state cue fields, and in one substituted token. It is not in the throat, and putting it there would delete the song.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** it is the difference between the piece and every other slow sad song. A voice that does not react while its content becomes unbearable is a thing almost nobody makes, and this run's arm split exists specifically to let one arm make it.

- **Changed / refused / intensified:** I refused to locate body noise in Intro, Bridge or Outro.
  **Reason:** the song has none of those sections. Step 05 assigned nine sections — three passes of Verse / Chorus / Tag — and *"nothing else exists in the song."* Adding an intro to satisfy a location rule would add material at the top and hand the generator a ramp.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** three instances remain, placed identically inside every pass, which converts body noise from decoration into evidence that a body is present and holding still.

- **Changed / refused / intensified:** I refused a dynamic arrangement arc and filled the arrangement-arc slot with an **affirmative statement of invariance** instead.
  **Reason:** the generator's strongest prior is to build, and a spec that fights the generator gets smoothed unless something does the fighting. Prohibition alone is not a control. Four independent controls now guard it — the affirmative sentence, the exclude field's build grammar, `no_swell_no_new_layer` in `Disc_Pad`, and a Role string asserted byte-identical on all nine headers by a build script that fails closed.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** without this the piece renders as a slow ballad with a big last chorus, and the pronoun changes where nobody can hear it, because **a build is louder than a word.**

- **Changed / refused / intensified:** I refused to raise V4's murmur into the third chorus.
  **Reason:** it is the obvious dramatic move and it would make the word unmissable — by converting a clean fear into a horror cue. If the word cannot be caught at constant level, the correct repair is a quieter arrangement, never a louder murmur.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** the murmur stays furniture. A machine that gets louder is a character; a machine that simply becomes legible for one word is a condition.

- **Changed / refused / intensified:** I replaced **all four variations'** nine EMO tokens with **non-directional sets that return unchanged on every pass**. Each variation carries its own triple, applied to Verse / Chorus / Tag and repeated three times: **V1** `Equanimity` / `Cognitive Dissonance` / `Unease` · **V2** `Composure` / `Acceptance` / `Alienation` · **V3** `Serenity` / `Assurance` / `Suspense` · **V4** `Detachment` / `Surrealism` / `Unease`. All four previously carried the identical climbing sequence `Vigilance → … → Dread → Trepidation → Suspense`.
  **Reason:** R2 binds — *EMO headers mark what has changed in the ROOM, never in the DELIVERY* — and I had pinned the Role string, the cue field, the prompt and the exclude field while leaving unpinned **the single strongest performance directive the generator reads.** `EMOTION_TAXONOMY` opens with *"The emotion IS the brief"*: the token is a directive field by design, so a token sequence that climbs into dread instructs the singer to escalate. **A room cannot dread.** The room's nine-state change is carried where a room's change belongs — in the cue field, in objects: *blind down · folder open · one copy pushed across · second chair empty · the pen capped · hall light off · only the desk lamp left · the page turned round · nothing else moves.* Those are untouched.
  **Why each variation gets its own triple rather than a copy of V1's:** the four are meant to be different songs, and the triple can name the situation without naming an intensity. `Acceptance` is the Trust-family token for a chorus that literally opens *“Accepted in full”* while accepting her removal; `Alienation` names V2's assignment — classified out of her own event. `Assurance` is V3's horror exactly: the letter is **certain**, and `Suspense` is the correct name for a lean that is never answered. `Surrealism` is a property of V4's situation — a machine murmuring your classification in a warm room — and not a level a voice can rise to. ⚠️ **V4 was given the shortest available tag token deliberately**: at 4,795 characters it holds the least headroom in the pair, and the longer candidate would have pushed it past the 4,800 target.
