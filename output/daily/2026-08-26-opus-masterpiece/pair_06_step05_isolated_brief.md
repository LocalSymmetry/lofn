## 1. YOUR SECTION ARCHITECTURE

**SUBTRACTIVE STROPHES.** Three layers are scheduled to be removed, one per strophe. Two are removed. **The third removal simply never happens and nothing acknowledges it.** Section order: Strophe 1 (full) / Strophe 2 (minus one) / Strophe 3 (minus two) / Strophe 4 (**still minus two — the pattern breaks and no one mentions it**) / Coda. **Stanza lengths 7 / 7 / 7 / 7 / 3** — the strophes are rigidly equal so the missing removal is the only irregularity in the song.

## 2. YOUR RHYME / LINE LOGIC

**HARD-CONSONANT SPINE.** End-rhyme kept but subordinate; the load is carried by stop-consonants on every stressed syllable — the line has an edge you can feel in the mouth. **No rhyme debt owed.** The consonant pattern itself is a return and it survives every subtraction.

## 3. YOUR HOOK GRAMMAR

A plain first-person admission of what she did, followed by the refusal to be sorry. **Both halves are flat.** The hope is in the content, never in a swell.

## 4. YOUR SONIC DEVICE

The removed floor is revealed to have been carried by a VOICE the whole time — the layer everyone took for the ground was never the ground.

## 5. YOUR INTRUDER PLACEMENT (assigned at step 05 — not your choice)

⭐ **AT THE VERY END, WITH NO RECOVERY** — the load-bearing electronic layer is revealed and then the song simply stops. No reprise, no resolution, no final chorus.

## 6. YOUR SEAM (N = 1)

The moment the removed floor is revealed to have been a voice. She must be **still singing across it** — the reveal happens underneath her, not to her.
