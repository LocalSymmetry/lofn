## CROSS-CHECK — Context & Marketing Panel

**The seat after Rogers:** A16, A17 and A15 carry a **physical event in the first thirty seconds** (a clap arriving late, a chorus swelling on one side, an answer landing before its question). A19, A20 and A18 do not — their events are structural and slow. **That is the barbell, drawn in arrangements rather than in concepts**, and it confirms the split.

**The seat after Tate:** A16 and A23 use living-scene rhythmic vocabulary. **Both require a Lineage & Credit block, and the credit goes in the dedication — never in the generator.** If any prompt reaching Suno names a break, the player is credited by name in the dedication and nowhere else.

**The seat after Fisher:** A22's audible tape splice is the only entry at risk of reading as nostalgia rather than as evidence. **The splice must be the join of something happening now**, not a period signifier — otherwise it is hauntology as decoration, which is the thing I spend my published work attacking.

## SELF-CRITIQUE

Thirteen arrangements, and **eleven of them place their intruder in the second half.** That is a monoculture forming at the arrangement layer, invisible at the concept layer, and it is exactly the class of defect that produced *"growth by addition, never by level"* in 6 of 6 pairs on 2026-08-08 — **voluntarily, from doctrine, with no mandate.** Step 05 must place at least one intruder in the **first ten seconds** and at least one **at the very end with no recovery**, or the six songs will share a shape underneath six different surfaces.
