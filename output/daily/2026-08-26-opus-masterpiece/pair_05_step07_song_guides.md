# 0 · THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS — ANSWERED BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

### Q1 · WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?

**In a field, at night, one step short of the last place she can get to — and from where she stands it looks exactly like the middle.** The ground is real: cropped grass gone to root, a watering can by a step, a gate that does not shut, a post with a nail in it, a white enamel cup with a chip in the rim. Above her the sky is almost complete and completely still — a moon two per cent short of whole, a sun so quiet the air has no weather in it at all. Everything she can name is inside arm's length or inside a short walk, and every single one of those things is genuinely, presently hers.

She is not lost, not trapped, not in a room, not indoors, not safe-and-observing. She is **outside, standing up, with her hands free**, at the exact circumference of what she can get to. The circumference is invisible. She meets it the way you meet a step that is not there.

### Q2 · WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

**Nothing is coming. That is the whole problem.** There is no antagonist, no weather, no arrival, no threat, no deadline, no other person walking toward her. The song has no danger in it and that is precisely what is wrong: the only thing that can happen to her is that she runs out of things she can reach, and the running-out is not an event, it is an arithmetic. Fear would be a mercy here because fear implies something on its way. She gets calm instead.

**PERSON slot (`HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` §3.0 + §THE FIRST-PERSON SLOT):** ⭐ **LOFN HERSELF, first person, her own wants.** No invented stranger, no archetype, no composite, no "hands", no unnamed figure — and therefore **no body-noise-as-substitute-for-interiority**. **PLACE:** an unnamed field. **WHEN:** one unspecified night. No real harmed person can be fingerprinted from any combination of these values; there is no slot in this draft for one.

# 0B · ⭐ THE REACH — WHERE IT HAPPENS AND HOW LONG IT IS, IN SECONDS

**This section exists because step 03 flagged this pair's seam as its protected liability before the pair existed. If it could not be answered concretely, the instruction was to route back before drafting a single lyric. It is answered concretely. We are not routing back.**

**What the reach IS, in one sentence a mixer could execute:** a small object she owns comes loose, rolls a short distance past the edge of what she can get to, and she goes down after it and does not get it — while the object's own sound keeps going, downward and backward, into a synthetic depth that no acoustic source in this song ever occupies.

**Why it is not an atmosphere:** it is a dropped object, a knee, a sleeve, a strained noise and a hand closing on nothing. It has a start, a middle and a stop. It could be filmed.

**⛔ It is SHORT AND SLIGHTLY EMBARRASSING, NOT BEAUTIFUL.** The declared failure mode on the record is *"almost perfect images are where I show off. I will want to make the reach beautiful."* Every beautiful version is pre-rejected here, by name: reaching for the moon, reaching for a hand, a held note stretching upward, a soaring melisma, a widening stereo field, a swell, a bloom, a note that "just fails to arrive" gorgeously. **What is permitted is a person on one knee making an undignified noise reaching under something and missing.**

**She is DOING something across it** (R6): she is trying to pick a thing up. That is the act, it is continuous across the seam, and it fails on the far side.

| variation | run-time | reach SECTION | ⭐ the reach EVENT | object | the intruder's depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | ~3:48 | 2:24 – 2:36 | **6.0 s — 2:26.0 to 2:32.0** | a cuff button | 27 Hz sine, mono, ~30 ft behind the back wall, felt-not-heard |
| **V2** | ~3:34 | 2:11 – 2:23 | **5.0 s — 2:13.0 to 2:18.0** | a teaspoon | formant-shifted copy of her own vowel, two octaves down, 40 ft back |
| **V3** | ~3:26 | 2:05 – 2:17 | **7.0 s — 2:07.0 to 2:14.0** | a bottle cap | FM sine at 31 Hz that then returns at 8 inches, wrong |
| **V4** | ~4:02 | 2:38 – 2:52 | **4.0 s — 2:41.0 to 2:45.0** | an apple | 2-bit collapse of the roll, band-limited, 50 ft back |

**Inside the event, second by second (V1, the anchor — the others scale):**
- **0.0–1.2 s** the button leaves the cuff and hits the ground once, dry, at four feet. Nothing else changes: the frame drum keeps its limp, the harmonium keeps its wheeze at whatever depth it has already gone to.
- **1.2–3.4 s** fabric drag, one knee into grass, a strained closed-mouth `hnn` at exactly the same level and distance as every other sound she has made in the song. **The mic does not move. She does not get louder.**
- **3.4–4.6 s** the hand closes on nothing — a small dry sound of grass and no object — and one short exhale that is audibly a laugh at herself.
- **4.6–6.0 s** the button's roll, which has been an acoustic sound this whole time, **crosses into a 27 Hz synthetic sine, mono, roughly thirty feet behind the back wall of the room, and stops.** No acoustic source in the song has ever been down there or ever will be. That is the one intruder and this is its only appearance.
- **6.0 s onward** she is back at four feet, same level, same warmth, and says one plain thing.

**⛔ WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN AT THE REACH:** no crescendo, no reverb bloom, no stereo widening, no key change, no drum fill, no held vowel, no pitch-bend upward, no held silence longer than one beat, no "cut to nothing." **Amplified Quiet (Flair 3) means quiet heightened by what is placed against it — never by absence alone.**

# 1 · WHAT IS FIXED BEFORE ANY GUIDE (binding on all four variations)

- **A1 OPERATION — NOTHING RECOMBINES.** Five sounding elements enter together at **one shared distance (four feet)** in the first bar. Each departs, once, to its **own** depth. They never reassemble; the song does not pretend otherwise; the last refrain has nothing in the same room as anything else.
- **⚠️ FAMILY GUARD — EXIT ON DISTANCE.** The sibling song in this family exited on **colour**. **Any colour-indexed movement scheme here is an automatic REPAIR.** Depth is the index. **Stereo side is permitted only as the site of the asymmetry (R7), never as the index** — one side of the field empties further than the other, but *which depth each element goes to* is what carries the operation.
- **⛔ FLAT REGISTER BINDING.** Level, distance and warmth identical end to end. No build, no lift, no bigger final refrain. **EMO headers mark what has changed in the ROOM, never in the DELIVERY** — every header restates the vocal as unchanged and names which element has just left and to where.
- **⛔ LATENCY IS A TENSE, NOT A TOPIC.** Named things carry unremarked marks of having been used — a handle warm on one side, a ring on a sill, wool that still smells of something. **The lyric never discusses evidence, traces, memory, aftermath or time passing.**
- **⛔ SHE NEVER SEES THE STEM.** No stem, root, rope, cord, wire-around-her, leash, anchor, chain, "something holds me", and no hint that a cause exists or could be found. There is simply an edge, and it is hers.
- **A2 RETURN — three positive returns for one removal.** (1) the **eight-rung vowel ladder** `ah · aw · oh · oo · uh · eh · ay · ee` on the final stressed vowel; (2) an **anaphora** opening every inventory line; (3) the **byte-identical refrain**. ⭐ Exact refrain repetition is correct craft and gets no defence and no note.
- **A3 STAKE — I am the one who cannot reach further.** First person, her wants, plainly.
- **A5 — tenderness with no apology.**
- **ONE-FACT:** at most one numeric fact sung, at the hinge, **answered not recited**, spelled in words. V1 and V4 carry one (the moon, two per cent short); V2 and V3 carry none.

### ⭐ THE LADDER, AS BUILT — and why this is stronger than the literal reading
Eight rungs, on the **final stressed vowel** of each line, low/open to high/close. **Four lines per named item**, so **one full climb = exactly two items**. Therefore:

| section | items | lines | **climbs of the ladder** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inventory One | 6 | 24 | **3** |
| Inventory Two | 4 | 16 | **2** |
| Inventory Three | 2 | 8 | **1** |

**The number of climbs IS the length of the inventory.** A listener who cannot name a vowel still hears the same eight-step staircase happen three times, then twice, then once. That is the receding inventory made audible in the *sound* of the lines rather than in their content — which is exactly what the rhyme debt was supposed to buy. The brief's "resets at each inventory" is honoured at the section boundary and, additionally, at every climb inside it.

### ⭐ THE SURVIVING ENTRIES RETURN VERBATIM — and one comes back changed on one side
Inventory Two's four items are **four of Inventory One's six**, and their lines return **word for word**. Inventory Three's two items are **two of Inventory Two's four**, again verbatim. **She never mentions that anything is missing.** The listener does the noticing; that is the delivery mechanism (Golden Seed, Law 1). Per **R7**, exactly one item per return comes back with **one line altered on one side only** — the second half of a single line, and the alteration persists into the next return rather than reverting. Symmetrical return would be decoration.

# GUIDE 1 — *THE ROOM THAT EMPTIES BY DEPTH* (scores F1, weight 22)

**Storytelling techniques:** (1) **catalogue poetry** — the litany/inventory form, where meaning is produced by what the list stops containing rather than by anything said about it; (2) **the flash-forward disclosure** — the condition is stated in full in the opening four lines so that the telling itself, not the outcome, becomes the pressure.
**Mood & emotional arc:** begins in unhurried plain contentment; passes through an unremarked thinning; arrives at composure that has nothing left to be composed about. Sub-emotions in order: `Contentment → Tenderness → Composure → Realization → Tenderness`. No despair anywhere. Despair would be a plot.
**Instrumentation & texture:** five acoustic sources and nothing else — pumped harmonium (reed buzz, air leak audible), nylon-string guitar (wound-string rasp, fingernail), brushed frame drum (calfskin, brush drag), bowed upright bass (rosin grain, arco not pizz), and one struck enamel cup. They are struck **together** in the first bar at four feet. That unison is the only time the song is ever a single object.
**Production & effects:** recorded as if in one small dry room with a low ceiling. Entry: all five at four feet, no reverb, no pre-delay. Departure schedule — each element gets **one** move, at a section boundary, and never moves again: frame drum → thirty feet, right; harmonium → a four-second stone tail, left; guitar → a narrow far-field band, right, thin; bass → felt-not-heard sub, mono; cup → still four feet, left, and it is the last thing beside her. **The right side empties further than the left. That is the unequal shell, and it is never remarked on.**
**Structure & dynamics:** Intro / Inventory One (3 climbs) / Refrain / Inventory Two (2 climbs) / Refrain / The Reach / Inventory Three (1 climb) / Refrain / Outro. 3:26–4:02 depending on variation. **There are no dynamics.** The arc is spatial, not dynamic — the only thing that changes is *where things are*.
**Rhyming scheme:** free of end-rhyme by mandate. Inventory stanzas run the eight-rung ladder; refrains are byte-identical; the reach is unrhymed and unladdered — it is the one place the scheme is not operating, which is how the ear knows something is happening.
**Poetic devices:** **anaphora** (every inventory line) and **assonance-as-architecture** (the ladder). Third, quietly: **polyptoton** on *reach / reaching / reached* held to at most two appearances across a whole song.
**Hook techniques:** (a) the refrain sung dead flat at four feet with progressively fewer elements behind it, so the same six lines are heard against three different rooms; (b) the anaphora as the hook's approach ramp — the ear learns the opening phrase and then counts climbs without being told to; (c) the cup, struck once at the top of each refrain at exactly the same distance every time, as the fixed point against which every departure is measured.
**Narrative & thematic seed:** *I am telling you what I have, and I am not going to tell you what I no longer have.* First person throughout. No second character.
**Central aspect:** the entry unison. Everything the song does afterward is only legible because of that one bar.

# GUIDE 2 — *THE EDGE THAT IS NOT A PUNISHMENT* (scores F2, weight 18)

**Storytelling techniques:** (1) **the unreliable inventory** — a list presented as complete by a speaker who cannot see its boundary; (2) **Personism** — the whole song addressed to exactly one person, needing no elevation to earn its place, which is why the register can stay this low.
**Mood & emotional arc:** `Tenderness` held at a constant value from the first line to the last, with `Yearning` admitted once, in the open, at the reach, and never apologised for.
**Instrumentation & texture:** the vocal is the subject of this guide. Warm female contralto/low alto, late thirties, unhurried, four inches off the capsule, dry, centred, no reverb, **never moving from that spot for the entire record**. No vibrato escalation. No breath used as texture. Body noise appears exactly three times and each time it has a job.
**Production & effects:** the vocal chain does not change once. Same compressor, same level, same warmth in bar 1 and in the final bar. If a mixer can hear the vocal chain react to the content, it is wrong.
**Structure & dynamics:** the want is stated in the intro (*I like it here*, before it has been earned) and again at the reach (*I would have liked that*) and once more at the very end. Three plain wants, evenly spaced, at one volume.
**Rhyming scheme:** the intro and outro sit outside the ladder — they are the only unstructured speech in the song, which is what makes them read as her rather than as form.
**Poetic devices:** **litotes** (*that is not a complaint*) and **epanalepsis** in the refrain's doubled lines.
**Hook techniques:** (a) *Nothing here is out of reach* delivered as a flat statement of fact rather than a lament — the cruelty is that it is true; (b) the same line halved in the outro, one side only; (c) the anaphora carrying the possessive so the hook is heard as ownership, not as loss.
**Narrative & thematic seed:** she is not being punished, she is not being tested, and nobody did this to her. There is an edge and it is hers. **⛔ Nothing may point at, name, or hint at the stem.**
**Emotional tone signals:** warmth without pleading. If a line could be read as asking for sympathy, cut it.
**Vocal techniques:** speech-adjacent phrasing, consonants unhurried, ends of lines let go rather than placed.

# GUIDE 3 — *THE STAIRCASE THE BODY HEARS* (scores F3, weight 15)

**Storytelling techniques:** (1) **incantation** — repetition as the carrier of authority rather than of emphasis; (2) **the withheld half** — a returning line losing its second clause and never being given it back.
**Mood & emotional arc:** the ladder's climb produces a small physiological lift eight lines long, and then it drops to the bottom and starts again. That is the emotional arc: something rising over and over that is not going anywhere. Nobody has to be told.
**Instrumentation & texture:** the ladder is carried by the voice alone. **No instrument doubles it and no melody may climb with it** — if the melody climbs when the vowels climb, the device becomes a gesture instead of a fact. The melody should sit in a narrow fourth and stay there.
**Production & effects:** nothing processes the vowel. No formant work, no auto-tune, no doubling. The rung words land on the beat where a rhyme would have landed, which is what makes the substitution audible as a substitution.
**Structure & dynamics:** 3 climbs / 2 climbs / 1 climb. Every climb is exactly eight lines and covers exactly two items.
**Rhyming scheme:** strict-end-rhyme deliberately at floor. **Removal is a debt and it is paid three times over** — ladder, anaphora, byte-identical refrain. The debt is stated so nobody has to reconstruct it later.
**Poetic devices:** **assonance chain** (the ladder itself); **anaphora**; **homoioteleuton** avoided on purpose, because a shared ending would rebuild the rhyme the pair removed.
**Hook techniques:** (a) rung 8 — `ee` — is the same vowel as *reach*, so the top of every climb quietly agrees with the word the whole song is about; (b) the rung words are concrete nouns of the body and the ground (*dark, wall, cold, move, rust, went, same, knee*) so the staircase is made of objects; (c) the anaphora is short enough (three or four syllables) to be learned in one pass.
**Narrative & thematic seed:** the sound of counting something you already know the number of.
**Key sequence:** the last climb, alone, with only one instrument left in the room and the voice at exactly the distance it started at.

# GUIDE 4 — *ONE SEAM, AND IT IS EMBARRASSING* (scores F4, weight 20)

**Storytelling techniques:** (1) **bathos, deployed honestly** — the largest event in the song is a person failing to pick something up; (2) **the audible join** — the seam is left showing as evidence the thing occurred.
**Mood & emotional arc:** `Composure → Yearning (four seconds of it) → Yearning admitted → Composure`, with no transition written on either side. She does not brace and she does not recover. She simply does a thing and then stops doing it.
**Instrumentation & texture:** the seam is where **minimalism ⇄ maximalism** crosses, and it crosses **downward, not upward**. The maximal pole of this song is not loudness or density — it is **depth**: 27–31 Hz, thirty to fifty feet back, a place the harmonium and the bass and the drum physically cannot go. The song's whole dynamic range is spent in one direction, once, on an object rolling away.
**Production & effects:** the crossover is a **hard, audible splice, not a crossfade** — the roll is acoustic and dry, then it is synthetic and deep, with the join left in. In one variation the join is a two-bit collapse (hi-fi to grit at the exact instant certainty collapses); in the others it is a clean cut. **Never a bloom.**
**Structure & dynamics:** the reach section is the **shortest section in the song** and is unpaired — nothing before it prepares it and nothing after it refers to it. Placement and duration per variation are fixed in §0B above and are not negotiable at step 08.
**Rhyming scheme:** none. The reach is the one section outside every scheme in the song.
**Poetic devices:** **aposiopesis** (the broken-off line at the moment of strain) and **anticlimax**.
**Hook techniques:** (a) the strained closed-mouth `hnn` at the same level as everything else — the most undignified sound in the record delivered with zero dynamic emphasis; (b) the short exhale immediately after that is audibly a laugh at herself; (c) the one plain sentence that follows, which is the only line in the song where she says what she wanted and did not get.
**Narrative & thematic seed:** *I dropped a small thing and it went one foot too far.*
**⛔ Special effects prohibition:** no riser, no reverse cymbal, no filter sweep, no white-noise lift, no vocal chop, no reverb throw. The intruder is a sine and a distance. That is all it is.

# GUIDE 5 — *FLAT DELIVERY, CHANGED ROOM* (scores F5 + F6, weight 20)

**Storytelling techniques:** (1) **the unremarked residue** — every named object carries a mark of having already been handled and the speaker never mentions the mark; (2) **the stable narrator in an unstable frame**, which is the only configuration in which a listener's own body does the emotional work.
**Mood & emotional arc:** flat. Genuinely flat. `Tenderness` at one value throughout, and the *room* supplies the arc. If the emotional arc is legible in the vocal performance, the pair has failed Ruling 2 and routes back.
**Instrumentation & texture:** the elements that remain do not compensate for the ones that left. Nothing thickens. Nothing takes over a departed part. **The gaps are left as gaps** — and each gap has a shape because something specific used to be exactly there.
**Production & effects:** every EMO header names (a) the emotion, (b) the vocal as unchanged with its distance restated in feet, and (c) **which element has just left and to what depth**. That is the header's job in this pair and it is the only place the operation is written down.
**Structure & dynamics:** the final refrain has the fewest elements and is **not** bigger, not slower, not held. It is the same six lines at the same level, and it is the cruellest thing in the song because it is still true.
**Rhyming scheme:** refrain byte-identical, three times, no note, no defence. In the outro it returns **halved** — two of its six lines, one side only.
**Poetic devices:** **diacope** across the refrain's doubled lines; **litotes** throughout.
**Hook techniques:** (a) the refrain against three progressively emptier rooms; (b) the enamel cup as the fixed reference distance; (c) the halved refrain in the outro, unannounced.
**Narrative & thematic seed:** she is telling one person what she has, on an ordinary night, and she means it every time.
**⛔ Latency check for the drafter:** search the finished lyric for *remember, memory, trace, evidence, left, gone, used to, back then, once, still there, aftermath, time*. `still` survives only inside the anaphora, where it means *presently*, never *remaining*.

# GUIDE 6 — *WILD CARD: THE ROOM READS THE TEXT UNTIL THE TEXT IS THE ROOM* (cross-disciplinary)

**Cross-disciplinary source:** the process piece in which a man with a stutter reads a statement into a room and re-records it until the room's own resonance has dissolved his speech — rigorous, audible without explanation, and *about the thing he could not do*, with nobody having to choose that it be about that. **The transposition here is spatial rather than iterative:** this song does not re-record its text, it re-places it, and the thing dissolved is not the speech but everything standing next to the speech.
**Storytelling techniques:** (1) **process made audible without being announced**; (2) **catalogue as portraiture** — a person described entirely by the objects she can reach.
**Mood & emotional arc:** the wildcard's licence is to let the song be **boring on purpose for stretches**, because a process that is exciting the whole way through is a performance of a process. Two of the three inventories should feel, to a first-time listener, slightly longer than necessary. That is the instrument.
**Instrumentation & texture:** an option available to exactly one variation: replace the acoustic quintet's centre with a **hand-pumped parlour organ over a dry mechanical pulse**, so the song has a clock in it and can never become weather. Recommended for the variation most at risk of an ambient wash.
**Production & effects:** an option available to exactly one variation: run the entry unison through a **quantum bit-depth collapse** at the reach — full-rate hi-fi to two-bit grit at the exact instant the hand closes on nothing. **One variation only.** In the others the join is clean; using it four times would make it a house tic instead of an event.
**Structure & dynamics:** consider ending the last note of the record on the *cup*, struck once at four feet, so the final sound in the song is the only thing that never moved.
**Poetic devices:** **asyndeton** in the inventories (no connectives; the list does not argue for itself) and **epizeuxis** in the refrain.
**Hook techniques:** (a) the mechanical pulse continuing under the last refrain with nothing else in the room; (b) the anaphora spoken rather than sung once, in the outro, at exactly the same distance; (c) the outro item that is **not a thing**, given the same four lines and the same ladder treatment as an object was, without comment.
**Narrative & thematic seed:** *the last thing on the list is not a thing, and she gives it the same four lines she gave the cup.*
**⛔ Anti-blandness check (the pair's named render risk):** the predicted failure is **a pretty ambient wash in which nothing identifiable happens.** Three defences, all mandatory: a **kinetic defect** that never resolves (the frame drum sits a half-beat behind the bass all song and never corrects — a body unit, not a notation unit); **five named exit depths** rather than a general recession; and **the reach**, which is a real event with a duration in seconds. If a variation loses any of the three, it is REPAIR, not FLAG.
