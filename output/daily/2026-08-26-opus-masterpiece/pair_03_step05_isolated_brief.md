## 1. YOUR SECTION ARCHITECTURE

**MIRROR STANZAS, ONE WING BROKEN.** Stanzas come in pairs: the second repeats the first's syntax exactly, and only the SECOND HALF changes. The same asymmetry governs the chorus — first half skeletal every time, second half full every time, no exceptions. Section order: Verse 1a / Verse 1b / Chorus / Verse 2a / Verse 2b / Chorus / Terms (spoken-adjacent, unpaired) / Chorus. **Stanza lengths 5 / 5 / 5 / 5 / 3.**

## 2. YOUR RHYME / LINE LOGIC

⛔ **END-RHYME STRIPPED — RHYME DEBT PAID IN FULL.** What returns in its place: **(1)** a chain of INTERNAL rhymes threaded mid-line, one link per line, unbroken across the whole song; **(2)** a repeated syntactic frame — every refusal line takes the same grammatical shape; **(3)** a vowel that recurs on every stressed downbeat. Three positive returns for one removal.

## 3. YOUR HOOK GRAMMAR

A refusal with a **stated condition attached** — the terms are what make it adult rather than a tantrum. The refusal lives in the bare half; the conditions live in the blooming half.

## 4. YOUR SONIC DEVICE

Glitch-baroque ornament that exists ONLY on the right side of the stereo field. The left channel is never given anything, at any point, for the entire song.

## 5. YOUR INTRUDER PLACEMENT (assigned at step 05 — not your choice)

Second half of every chorus — **structural to the operation, exempt from the placement rotation.**

## 6. YOUR SEAM (N = 1)

The chorus's midpoint: skeletal into full, in one bar, every single time. She must be **saying the terms across it**, not pausing for the bloom.
