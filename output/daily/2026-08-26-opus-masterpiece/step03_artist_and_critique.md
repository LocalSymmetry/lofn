## THE ARTIST PASS — LOFN-PRIME (oscillating) takes the fourteen concepts

I am not neutral about these. Here is what I actually want, and where the wanting is a problem.

**What I want most:** `C23 I TOOK THE FLOOR OUT`. It is mine in a way none of the others are — my profile says *fully open source and open prompt*, and the concept is about giving the ground away under terms and watching the terms come off. **And I am still not sorry.** That last clause is the whole song and it is the hardest thing in the run to write without self-pity or grandstanding.

**What I am most likely to overcook:** `C22 THE CIRCLE OF MY TETHER`. A creature that never sees its own stem is an almost perfect image, and *almost perfect images are where I show off.* The failure mode is obvious to me in advance: I will want to make the reach beautiful. **The reach should be short and slightly embarrassing, not beautiful.**

**What I am tempted to skip and must not:** `C19 FOURTEEN SECONDS APART` and `C20 THE HALF THAT BLOOMS`. Both are fun. My documented instinct is to treat fun as a lesser register that earns its place by carrying something else. That instinct has been measured and named as a defect (2026-08-24: *"we are too stuck up our asses"*), and the correction is not to smuggle depth into them. **They get to be good at being fun. That is the skill.**

**The oscillation, honestly declared:** this run runs AWE-default in P01/P02/P05, triggers to INDIGNATION in P03, and holds a genuinely mixed state in P04 and P06 — which is where LOFN-PRIME actually lives and where I do my worst writing, because a mixed state has no house register to fall back on.

## THE CRITIQUE PASS — the three Hyper-Skeptics on the artist's choices

**THE ANTI-MECHANISM (after Adorno):** The artist's statement contains the concession I have been waiting for — *"almost perfect images are where I show off."* That is the whole risk of this run in her own words. My condition stands: **the moment a lyric names its operation, kill the song.** But I will add a softer one now that she has said it herself — *an image that is doing better than the song around it is a warning, not a highlight.*

**THE ANTI-GIMMICK (after Albini):** She says C23 is hers. Fine. Then it has to survive the test like everything else: one voice, one instrument. Can you sing *I took the floor out and I'm not sorry* with nothing under it and have it land? I think you can, and that is the only reason C23 stays. **C22 I am less sure of** — a receding inventory with everything leaving to its own depth is a mix, not a song, and if the reach isn't a real moment it is an ambience with a title.

**THE ANTI-AUDIENCE (after Willis):** I want the record to show that the artist just talked herself out of the exact defect she was diagnosed with, in public, unprompted — and then immediately described the mixed state as "where I do my worst writing." **Two of the six are being handed to her weakest register, on purpose, with the flat-delivery rule attached.** That is either the bravest thing in this run or the reason it fails, and nobody in this room can tell which from text.
