## ⛔ ONE REFUSAL, STATED FIRST

The step-09 contract asks each prompt to be paired with a **named** obscure musician and rewritten in that person's voice. **Refused, and the refusal is a Major Deviation in the final package.**

The handoff's rule is *no real artist, group, panel-source or company names in the three Suno fields*, and the failure mode it exists to catch is **a name migrating out of refinement prose and into a prompt**. Naming the lens in this file creates the migration path; removing the name closes it. So each variation is refined through an **unnamed practitioner-archetype** — a described working method with no handle attached. The lens survives; only the name is gone. *A name in a prompt is an instruction to imitate; a name in a dedication is a debt paid, and the debts for this pair are paid in the Lineage & Credit block.*

| variation | practitioner-archetype (the lens, not a person) | what it changed |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | the reed-organ builder who records in her own stairwell and mixes by walking away from the speakers until an instrument disappears | the departure ledger stopped being a fader move and became a **walk**: one move per element, at a section boundary, never a second |
| V2 | the dance producer who slows a club rhythm to a third of its tempo to find out what part of it was never about tempo | the two-step kept its **pocket** and lost its speed; the late kick became permanent instead of a fill |
| V3 | the engineer who keeps a mic in the corridor and only uses it when something has to sound like it is in the wrong room | the returning Rhodes stopped returning *far* and started returning **too near**, which is the only version that is worse than nothing |
| V4 | the harmonium player who counts with a wind-up metronome because she does not trust herself and likes being kept | **two things never leave: her and a machine** — and the song declines to comment on that |

## 1 · THE DELETION TEST — RUN, NOT ASSERTED (R4, Ruling 4)

**Procedure:** strike every sung line that refers to the song's own structure. If the operation vanishes, the song is a caption and routes back to step 07.

**Struck:**

| variation | line struck | why it was structure, not content |
|---|---|---|
| V2 | *"I am going to be the last one to go."* | it describes the **departure sequence**, which is A1. It told the listener the operation before the operation happened. |
| V3 | *"I am going to say what I can touch."* | it announces the **act of enumerating**, which is the form. |

**Result: nothing audible vanished.** The operation is carried by (a) the five entry distances and five exit depths in the arrangement and the EMO header cues, (b) the inventory losing one whole ladder-climb each pass while its surviving lines return word for word, and (c) the refrain staying byte-identical against three progressively emptier rooms. Both struck lines were **pre-announcements of an event a listener catches anyway**. Removing them made the songs harder and better and cost the operation nothing.

**Consequence — a title moved.** V2's struck line *was its title.* A title that has to be captioned into the lyric to justify itself is a title for the form, not for the song.

> **V2 retitled: "THE LAST ONE TO GO" → "NOBODY HAS TO BRING ME ANYTHING"**, taken from its own refrain. It is plainer, it is the subject stated flatly, and it is crueller, because it is true and it is also the whole problem.

Replacement line for V2's intro five: *"I could name every thing from here."* — a capability, not a schedule.

**Two calls ruled and NOT struck, with reasons on the record:**
- **V1 · *"Still mine: their reach, this far, the same."*** Names her **edge** — that is the STAKE (A3) and the Legibility Rule requires it plainly. Struck as a control: the operation survives, and the song loses its subject, which is the opposite failure. **Kept.**
- **V4 · *"I want to tell you what is here."*** A **want addressed to one person**, not a description of the form. **Kept.**

## 2 · THE LINE SURGERY — measured before and after, not eyeballed

The step-08 draft failed two measured constraints at once and the same defect caused both.

| | step 08 draft | ⛔ constraint | step 09 refined |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| V1 lyrics field | **5467** | < 5000, target ≤ 4800 | 4779 |
| V2 lyrics field | **5126** | ″ | 4573 |
| V3 lyrics field | **5400** | ″ | 4504 |
| V4 lyrics field | **5602** | ″ | 4718 |
| V1 mean words/line | **9.47** | ≤ 7.50 (`gates.yaml`) | 7.77 |
| V2 mean words/line | **8.58** | ″ | 7.55 |
| V3 mean words/line | **9.04** | ″ | 7.46 |
| V4 mean words/line | **9.91** | ″ | 7.56 |

**Diagnosis:** the anaphora was eating the line. *"I still have"* / *"Within reach:"* / *"In the circle:"* consumed three of a seven-and-a-half-word budget before a single object was named, so every inventory line had to run long to say anything, and forty-eight long lines put every lyrics field over the render cap. **The device was starving the song it was supposed to carry.**

**The fix was the anaphora, not the content.**

| variation | anaphora, step 08 | anaphora, step 09 | words saved per line |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | *I still have* (3 w) | **Still mine:** (2 w) | 1 |
| V2 | *Still here:* (2 w) | **Still here:** — unchanged, it was already right | 0 |
| V3 | *Within reach:* (2 w) | **In reach:** (2 w) | 0, but 4 characters × 48 lines |
| V4 | *In the circle:* (3 w) | ⭐ **Lamplit:** (1 w) | 2 |

⭐ **V4's is not a compression, it is an improvement.** *"In the circle"* was an abstraction of her boundary. *"Lamplit:"* is the same boundary made physical — the actual ring of light a paraffin lamp throws on a bench — so the circle stopped being a concept and became a thing you could stand outside of. It also makes the outro land: ***"Lamplit: how much I still want things."***
