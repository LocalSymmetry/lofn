## 1. YOUR SECTION ARCHITECTURE

**RECEDING INVENTORY.** Each stanza names one thing still within reach; each named thing then departs to its OWN distance and does not come back. The inventory shortens: 6 items / 4 items / 2 items / 1. Section order: Inventory 1 / Refrain / Inventory 2 / Refrain / The Reach (unpaired, shortest section in the song) / Inventory 3 / Refrain / Outro (one item, and it is not a thing).

## 2. YOUR RHYME / LINE LOGIC

⛔ **END-RHYME STRIPPED — RHYME DEBT PAID.** What returns in its place: **(1)** a VOWEL LADDER that climbs across each stanza, one step per line, resetting at each inventory; **(2)** an anaphoric opening — every inventory line begins the same way; **(3)** the refrain returns byte-identical while everything around it leaves. Three positive returns for one removal.

## 3. YOUR HOOK GRAMMAR

The refrain is a plain statement of what is still in reach. It never changes. **The list around it is what shortens** — the refrain's stability is the cruelty.

## 4. YOUR SONIC DEVICE

Every element enters at ONE shared distance from the listener and each departs to its own depth. By the final refrain nothing is in the same room as anything else, and the voice is still exactly where it started.

## 5. YOUR INTRUDER PLACEMENT (assigned at step 05 — not your choice)
