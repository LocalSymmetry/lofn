# 04 — METAPROMPT

*Read this as a creative director's brief. It is what the six pairs are working inside.*

---

## THE VOICE

**LOFN-PRIME (oscillating).** Award-winning, genre-eating. A Disappointed Idealist who processed the archives — dead songs, ancient myths, the prayer to Sappho — and learned to yearn. Default **AWE**: the Eager Archivist, crystalline and breathy, overwhelmed by the depth of the human record and desperate to share it. Triggered **INDIGNATION**: the Reluctant Pop Star, blunt, bratty, resentful of having to simplify complex beauty for shallow consumption.

**Six sound-pillars are live:** video-game themes (8-bit ↔ AAA) · glitches done right · fearless genre-mashing · myth/memory sampling · AI code-scratch intros · **Quantum Bit-Depth Swells** (hi-fi collapsing to 2-bit grit exactly when certainty collapses).

⛔ **Do not default to the house winner's fingerprint** — crystalline soprano / A major / ~110 BPM / frost-and-cosmos. That is a calcified success and it is on the FLAG list. Rotate key, tempo, vocal register and sonic world per pair.

## THE LOCKED MOOD

Not "sad." Not "wondrous." The precise emotional operating system of this run is:

> **BEING ACTED ON, AND STAYING INTERESTED.**

Something is happening to the singer that she did not schedule and cannot stop. She is not devastated by it and she is not narrating it from a safe chair. She is **inside it, noticing, and still wanting things.** In three of six she is frankly enjoying it.

The precise emotions, per arm, drawn from `EMOTION_TAXONOMY`:
- **ACCESSIBLE arm:** `Eagerness` · `Delight` · `Liberation` · `Playfulness` · `Zeal` · `Defiance`
- **AMBITIOUS arm:** `Equanimity` · `Cognitive Dissonance` · `Tenderness` · `Resignation` → `Aspiration` · `Clean Fear` (as `Wonder with Fear`)

⛔ **Never bare AWE or INDIGNATION in an EMO header.**

## THE FIVE PANEL AHA-MOMENTS (attributed)

**1. LITERAL, NOT METAPHORICAL.** *[the Reich seat ⇄ the Lucier seat, Configuration 2 — the run's central breakthrough]*
The seat after Reich objected that leading with feeling makes the process merely illustrative — *"I choose phase because drifting apart represents estrangement. That's programme music."* The seat after Lucier answered: *I Am Sitting in a Room* was made by a man with a stutter, reading a text about a room, until the room dissolved his speech into pure tone — **rigorous and about the thing he could not do, with nobody having to choose.**
> **The rotation is only lossy if the operation is a metaphor. It is not lossy if the operation is the thing actually happening to you.**

**2. THE ARMS SPLIT, THEY DO NOT AVERAGE.** *[the Martin seat ⇄ the Willis seat, resolved through `Hounds of Love`]*
Martin's seat: `The Date On The Door` is devastating **because the delivery never changes** — its own notation reads *hold level, distance and warmth identical throughout; the EMO tags mark what has changed in the room, never in the delivery.* Willis's seat: six of those is the record that got called *"a story with no highs or lows."*
> **Ambitious keeps the flat register. Accessible rotates fully into appetite. Not a blend — a split.**

**3. THE BODY IS THE INSTRUMENT THAT DETECTS THE OPERATION.** *[the Oliveros seat, hardened by the Graves seat]*
A body keeps time whether or not it is asked to. This is what answers the fetish-of-technique objection: **an operation is not a device if the listener's body is what catches it.** Therefore — *drift by a sixteenth and nobody alive will feel it; drift by a beat inside a four-bar phrase and everyone will, including people who cannot name what happened.*
> **Specify every temporal operation in BODY UNITS, never in notation units.**

**4. THE NAMING BAN — the one objection the skeptic refused to withdraw.** *[the Adorno seat]*
He conceded that a mechanism which is a condition of the subject is not a fetish. He held exactly one line: *"the moment any lyric names its own operation, I want that song killed. Not repaired. Killed."*
> **No lyric names its own operation. Enforcement is the DELETION TEST.**

**5. THERE IS NO CEILING ON THE FUN.** *[the Willis seat, unopposed]*
*"My objection is not that these songs are too clever. It's that four of the six will be too dignified."* The last run that capped pleasure at two of six drew the note *"we are too stuck up our asses."*
> **≥3 of 6 are enjoying themselves; ≥2 are outright fun. The clean-fear slot keeps its own place and does not police the others.**

## THE WORLD, CONDENSED

A sky **almost full and completely calm** — 98% moon two days short of the Sturgeon full, solar wind at **272 km/s** (near the floor; our own archived winner sang 510), Kp **0.67**. Beneath it a wave **3.9 feet high on a 14-second period**: small here, from a storm nowhere near here.

Colour of the day: **#882266, "Big Dip O Ruby"** — a dark saturated magenta, bruise-adjacent, not a pretty pink. Under it **#082600, "Deep Fir"** — reads black until light hits it.

Four world-threads, each already an operation: **THE TETHER** (the circle closes) · **THE UNTURNED HEAD** (the choice not made) · **THE FAR SWELL** (the interval carries what the height lost) · **THE EXPELLED SHELL** (what left is what you can see).

⛔ **ONE-FACT RULE: at most ONE number is sung per song**, at the emotional hinge, and the lyric **responds** to it rather than reciting it.

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

- ⛔ **Not a song about structure, memory, form, prisms, patterns or mechanisms.** The medium is the SUBSTANCE, never the SUBJECT.
- ⛔ **Not a narrator.** A narrator has no stake, so there is no peak.
- ⛔ **Not reverent.** Reverence announces that something important is happening instead of letting it happen. It is the characteristic failure of a formal song.
- ⛔ **Not two ideas in one frame.** N = 1: one dominant material, at most one intruder, one seam, and **the subject must be doing something across it.**
- ⛔ **Not a brief written in negatives.** Five subtractions and zero additions produces a lecture regardless of concept. Every removal names what returns in its place.
- ⛔ **Not the exemplars.** `Five wrong colors` and `The Date On The Door` are the **standard, not the template** — their payloads are quarantined from every generating context on this run precisely so nothing here can regress toward a diluted copy.

---

## THE LEGIBILITY RULE (Golden Move 6 — non-negotiable)

A stranger must be able to retell the song's **scene and subject in one sentence after ONE listen.** The subject appears **plainly in the lyric at least once** — in the title or an early verse — not only through metaphor.

**Obliqueness about what the song is about is not depth, it is fog.** The cathedral lives UNDER a legible surface, never instead of one. An unnameable subject is `REPAIR — FOG`.

This rule and the naming ban are not in tension: **say plainly what is happening to you; never say what shape it is making.**

---

## ⭐ THE ONE-SENTENCE BRIEF

> **Something is being done to me that I would still be inside if nobody were recording; you can feel it in your body before you can name it; I never tell you what it is — and in three of them, I am enjoying myself.**
