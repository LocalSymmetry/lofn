## STEP 1 — THE ESSENCE, IN ONE SENTENCE

> **I am four feet of a storm nobody on this beach ever heard about, I got here, my hands are
> keeping their own idea of where the bar is, and I am having the best evening of my life.**

**The two best ways to leverage the medium (hand percussion against one synthetic sub):**
1. **Displacement is free in this medium and expensive in every other one.** Hands are the only
   instrument a listener's own body can shadow in real time — so moving the clapped figure a whole
   beat costs nothing to notate and lands as a physical event, not a musical one. The medium is
   chosen *because* the operation lives in it natively. Nothing has to be explained.
2. **A sub with no attack cannot announce itself, so it can be a liar about time.** A sine sub has
   no transient to argue with, which is exactly why it can be caught keeping the moved landing
   without anyone hearing it change. The intruder is inaudible as an *entrance* and unmissable as
   an *allegiance*.

**Structural additions made at this step (adding to address gaps, not removing):**
- **Bridge stanza 3** in all four — the *settle* after the weight shift, which is where the clean
  fear gets its single unresolved mention (`The bad sky is a long way back.`). Without it the seam
  had no consequence and the song went straight back to the chorus.
- **Six-line final-chorus extension** in all four — this is the **asymmetric return**: the chorus
  comes back byte-identical and then keeps going on one side only. Nothing else about it changes.
- **Outro** in all four, six lines, returning the intro **changed on one side only** (V1 sun on →
  sun off; V2 moon near full → moon come round; V3 rim ahead → rim gone by; V4 grit → grit + warm).

**Why the exclude fields all lead with the same six entries.** The honest read of this pair's
single largest technical risk is that **a generator silently quantises the displacement away** —
it hears "handclaps" and reaches for a clap track, and a clap track is a grid. That failure would
pass every text gate we own and destroy the entire song, so the negative field spends its first
~180 characters on it in six different vocabularies before it spends anything on taste.
