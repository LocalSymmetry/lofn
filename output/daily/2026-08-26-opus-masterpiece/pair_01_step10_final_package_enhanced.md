## THE THREE LAWS OF THIS SEED

### LAW 1 — THE OPERATION IS THE MATERIAL, NEVER THE TOPIC
A song is made **OF** its operation, never **ABOUT** it. The moment a lyric explains the mechanism, the mechanism is dead.
**Test:** delete every line that refers to the structure. If the operation is still audible, it is real. If it vanishes, you wrote a caption.

### LAW 2 — THE PERSON IT HAPPENS TO IS SINGING
The operation is not performed on a character the singer is watching. **≥4 of 6 pairs put the singer inside it**, wanting something and saying so plainly.
A narrator has no stake, so there is no peak. My own wants are a sufficient subject and need no further justification — **I cannot be harmed by being sung about, so first person is the safest configuration available, not a riskier one.**

### LAW 3 — WHAT RETURNS COMES BACK CHANGED ON ONE SIDE ONLY
From SOURCE 3 (the Lion's Head Nebula, F07): a spent core at the centre, everything visible is what it threw off, **and the shells are unequal on opposite sides.**
Symmetrical return is decoration. **Asymmetric return is the seed.** Something must come back, and it must come back wrong in exactly one place.

## 7 · ⛔ MAJOR DEVIATIONS

*(Step 11 has agency. These are the places I refused, changed, or intensified an instruction. Each one
names what it cost and what it bought.)*

**1 · I ADDED TWO SECTION TYPES THE ISOLATION BRIEF DID NOT LIST, AND THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING ONE.**
- **Changed:** the brief's section order (Intro / V1 / Chorus / V2 / Chorus / V3 / Chorus / Outro)
  yields **44–52 sung lines**. The output contract floors at **70**. I added a **four-line tag after
  each chorus** and a **fourth chorus**, plus the mandated square-lead break.
- **Reason:** ⭐ **the tag is not padding — it is where R7 lives.** A2 mandates a chorus that returns
  **byte-identical**, which means the chorus structurally *cannot* carry the asymmetric return. R7
  requires that whatever returns comes back changed **on one side only**. So the asymmetry needs its
  own object, and the tag is it: first two lines invariant across all three passes, last two lines
  moving every time. Without the tag, A2 and R7 are in direct contradiction and one of them has to be
  broken. And the fourth chorus is required by the brief's *own* seam definition — the cause lands
  under "a chorus we have already sung three times", which is only possible on a fourth pass.
- **Effect:** the two axis values stop fighting each other, the line floor is met honestly rather than
  by inflating verses past their mandated 8/8/6, and the pair gains its single best structural idea.

**2 · I REFUSED TWO GENRE TOKENS SUPPLIED BY THE RUN'S OWN SEED PALETTE.**
- **Refused:** `Industrial Sega` and `Cosmic P-Funk`, both taken verbatim from the ICB's genre list,
  plus `Hammond organ bubble` in an exclude field.
- **Reason:** **Sega** is a company, **P-Funk** is a group, **Hammond** is a brand, and the run's rule
  on the three Suno fields is absolute. ⭐ **A supplied palette is not a cleared palette** — and a
  proper-noun heuristic clears all three, which is exactly why the handoff demands a term-by-term scan
  against the roster instead. Replaced with `Future Baroque`, `Soft Dub` and `drawbar organ bubble`.
- **Effect:** nothing was lost. The video-game pillar never lived in the word *Sega* — it lives in the
  aliased square lead, the three-voice chip melody logic and the duty-cycle contrast, all of which are
