## WHAT THESE FACETS ARE SCORING

These are not general music-quality criteria. They are the five dials on which a P02 candidate
can be *right or wrong*, derived from **this pair's fixed axis values only**:

| axis | fixed value |
|---|---|
| A1 · OPERATION | **THE COUNT DRIFTS** — a clapped four-line figure relands **one beat later inside a four-bar phrase** on each of three returns, then lands home |
| A2 · SOUND / RETURN | monosyllabic verse breaking into polysyllabic chorus |

| A4 · POLES | acoustic improvisation ⇄ electronics · **one seam** |

A candidate that scores 5/5 here and would still be scored well by a generic music rubric is a
coincidence. A candidate that scores 5/5 here and *fails* a generic rubric is still the winner.

## THE FIVE FACETS (weighted; weights sum to 1.00)

### FACET 1 — **THE RELANDING A BODY CATCHES** · weight **0.28**
The four-line clapped figure must come back **one whole beat later inside its four-bar phrase**,
three times, and a listener who cannot name a bar line must still feel their shoulder arrive
before the clap does. Score high when the displacement is stated in **body units** and the
arrangement leaves the figure naked enough that the move is unmissable — dry hands, a wide hole
around them, nothing filling the gap. Score zero, automatically, for any drift specified in
notation units (a sixteenth, a triplet, a tick). *Flair 1 (Catchable Drift) · Flair 8 (The Body
That Notices) · Ruling 3, the Graves seat hardened by Albini.*

### FACET 2 — **THE MOUTH THAT OPENS** · weight **0.22**
The verse is a mouth keeping a count: one-syllable words, short air, teeth and consonants, a
line you could clap on. The chorus is the same mouth **letting go** — three- and four-syllable
words, long vowels held to the end of the bar, the jaw dropping. Score high when the syllabic
break is audible on the page as well as in the ear and when the polysyllables are *warm household
words* (`delivered`, `luggage`, `expecting`) rather than thesaurus reach. End-rhyme is kept, loose
and slant; **no rhyme debt is owed here** because nothing was removed. *A2, the fixed axis.*

### FACET 3 — **THE INTRUDER THAT WAS ALREADY THERE** · weight **0.20**
One dominant material (hands, cajón, breath, voice) and **exactly one** intruder (a synthetic
sub) — and the sub is present in the **first ten seconds**, before the hands, so it never gets an
entrance. Score high when the sub does not announce itself, does not swell, does not "drop," and
when the single seam is a **bodily event**: the hands stop, the sub is already keeping the moved
landing, it does not correct, and her weight goes over to it. Score low for two intruders, for a
sub that arrives at a section boundary, and for any seam that is an edit rather than a body.
*L38 ONE SEAM, N = 1 · Flair 4 (The Audible Join) · Ruling: the seam is a bodily event, all six pairs.*

### FACET 4 — **PLEASURE DOING NO JOB** · weight **0.18**
The delight must **cause** the form, not apologise for it. No utility alibi, no lesson smuggled
under the fun, no late turn toward significance. Score high when a stranger would describe the
singer as *enjoying herself* and could not say what the song is for; score low the moment a line
starts earning the pleasure it is having. Frivolity is a skill here, not a quota — there is **no
ceiling on the fun**, and the one permitted shadow is the size of the thing that made her, held
without comment. *A5 · Ruling 5, the Willis seat, unopposed · Flair 11 (Pleasure Doing No Job).*

### FACET 5 — **THE EVIDENCE SINGS, PLAINLY** · weight **0.12**
The singer **is** the far swell — small on arrival, from a storm nobody on this beach saw, and
glad to have made it — and she says so **plainly, at least once, in ordinary words**. No shore,
no watcher, no "like a wave." The day's one number, **fourteen seconds**, lands at the emotional
hinge as an **answer to a question**, never as a recital, and nothing else numeric is sung
anywhere. Score high when a stranger retells scene *and* subject in one sentence after one listen.
*Golden Move 2 + 6 · THE LEGIBILITY RULE · ONE-FACT RULE · Flair 10 (The Trace Without the Event).*

## THE FIVE GUARD-RAILS (not facets — automatic REPAIR, no score)

1. **R4 NAMING BAN.** No sung line refers to the structure. Enforcement is the **deletion test**:
   strike every structure-referring line; if the operation vanishes, it was a caption. The test
   must be *run* and its result *stated*.
2. **R1 LITERAL.** The relanding is a real condition of the singer's own body, never a figure for
   estrangement, memory, or distance.
3. **R3 BODY TEST.** Body units only — *and* would she still be inside this if nobody were
   recording?
4. **R7 ASYMMETRIC RETURN.** Whatever returns comes back changed **on one side only**.
5. **LINEAGE.** Living-scene rhythmic vocabulary → the **scene and the technique** are named in
   the prompt (public idioms only); **the people are named only in the dedication.** No break is
   named in any of these four prompts, so no break credit is owed — and that is stated, not assumed.

  whole beat: *"a process nobody's body can find is a programme note with a tempo."*

  arrived, she says it, and nobody has to be improved by it."*
- **THE PULSE** *(after Graves)* — Facet 1 is his; he supplied its unit.
- **THE ONE SEAM** *(after Matisse)* — Facet 3 is his; he vetoed a second intruder in draft.
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-MECHANISM** *(after Adorno)* — **does not withdraw.** He accepts a mechanism that
  is a condition of the subject, and holds the one line he never conceded: *the moment a lyric
  names its own operation, kill the song.* → that is why the naming ban is a **guard-rail with a
  mechanical test**, not a facet with a score. Scores can be argued down; the test cannot.
- ⚠️ **THE ANTI-GIMMICK** *(after Albini)* — dissents on Facet 3 and is answered, not overruled:
  *"a sub that 'was always there' is a mix decision, not an event."* → answered by moving the
  seam off the sub's arrival and onto **her weight going over to it**. If a candidate's seam is
  still a fader move, he was right.
