## 0 · ⛔ ONE DELIBERATE SUBSTITUTION IN THIS STEP, DECLARED UP FRONT

The step-09 contract asks for **an obscure named musician per prompt**, whose lexicon then colours the
rewrite. The run handoff forbids **any real artist, group, panel-source or company name in the three
Suno fields**, and step 10's own §8 exists solely to strip names back out again.

**So this step uses SCENE-AND-TECHNIQUE LENSES instead of named individuals** — a school, a room, a
working method, a documented practice, each specific enough to change vocabulary and none of them a
person. Two reasons, and the second is the real one:

1. It is the same instrument. A lens's job is to supply a lexicon and a set of physical priorities.
   *"The Sheffield bleep method"* supplies those as precisely as a person's name does.
2. ⭐ **A name written into a generating artifact migrates.** It survives into the prompt through
   sheer proximity, and step 10's stripping pass is a downstream patch on an upstream mistake.
   *A name in the prompt is an instruction to imitate; a name in a dedication is a debt paid.*
   People are credited at step 11's Lineage & Credit block and nowhere earlier.

**Result of the step-08 → step-09 name audit:** zero personal names entered any field. The only proper
nouns anywhere in the four lyric sets are **Marnie** and **Rob** — invented bystanders, no locating
detail, no harm context, `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD` clean.

## 1 · THE FOUR LENSES AND WHAT EACH ONE CHANGED

### V1 · lens — **the arcade-cabinet FM school, heard through a modern club floor**
*Aspect of style:* melody logic that must be legible through a three-voice chip — contour before
harmony, no passing tones, everything singable by a machine with almost no polyphony.
*Two signature techniques:* (a) **duty-cycle contrast** — the same melodic line stated once thin and
nasal and once fat, with nothing in between; (b) **the loop that eats its own last beat**, an economy
trick that becomes a limp when a human body is standing on it.
*What it changed in the package:* the square lead's melody is now explicitly required to be
**derivable by ear from the chorus's own top line** — same contour, plainer, unharmonised — so the
listener recognises it without being able to say why. Before this pass the intruder was merely *a
chiptune sound*; now it is **the question the choruses have been answering**, which is the whole
mechanism.

### V2 · lens — **the late-night boogie session method**
*Aspect of style:* the clav and the voice as two people in one conversation, neither of them louder.
*Two signature techniques:* (a) **the answer figure that never lands on the downbeat**, so the ear is
always waiting a half-step behind; (b) **playing quieter as the arrangement thickens**, which is what
keeps a groove from becoming a wall.
*What it changed:* the delivery note moved from *conversational* to **almost under the breath**, and
the octave double was cut from every section except the choruses. The vocal now gets *smaller* as the
guitar side grows — which makes the asymmetric return audible in the voice, not just in the pan.

### V3 · lens — **the offbeat-and-negative-space intelligence of the Caribbean dance lineages, rebuilt on a current chassis**
*Aspect of style:* what is **not** played on beat one is the instrument. Space is a pitch.
*Two signature techniques:* (a) **the one-drop** — the floor's most important event is an absence;
(b) **call-and-answer between a chop and a melodic answer voice**, where the answer arrives before the
call has finished, which is this pair's operation stated in percussion.
*What it changed:* the kinetic defect was rewritten. It had been a drift; a drift is **P02's material
and borrowing it is pair bleed**. It is now a *fixed* asymmetry — every fourth bar the sub also takes
beat one — which limps without ever moving. ⭐ **Keep the body, replace the century** (Sunna R5): the
inherited intelligence is offbeat relation, negative space and call/answer cadence; the chassis is
electronic kit, synthetic sub and present-day edits, load-bearing from second one. ⛔ No brass, no
organ bubble, no complete period ensemble. **Lineage & Credit block is mandatory for this variation.**

### V4 · lens — **the Italo twelve-inch method**
*Aspect of style:* one bass idea, stated immediately, carrying the entire record; the vocal arriving
already mid-argument.
*Two signature techniques:* (a) **octave-jumping bass as the melodic lead**, not as a support part;
(b) **the extra step on the turnaround** — a phrase allowed to be one beat longer than the ear
expects, and never corrected.
*What it changed:* verse 2 was rebuilt into a **single end-rhyme tunnel on one sound**, eight lines
of *because* rhyming end-to-end. That is the twelve-inch's refusal to modulate, translated into
prosody, and it makes the backwards-entitlement argument sound like a bass line that will not stop.
This is now the most structurally distinct stanza in the pair.
