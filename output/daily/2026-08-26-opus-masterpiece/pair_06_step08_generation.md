## 1 · PANEL PROCESS (contract steps 1–20, compressed to rulings)

**Step 3 · hooks (Medium Panel, two each, Concept Panel refined once).** Twelve hooks were generated. The
surviving grammar is the one the isolation brief mandates: **a plain first-person admission of what she
did, followed by the refusal to be sorry, both halves flat.** Eight hooks were cut for the same reason —
they *argued* rather than *admitted* (*"you took the terms off it"*, *"I'd do it again and I'd tell you
why"*). An admission has no addressee; an argument does, and the moment there is an addressee the singer
is defending herself and the flat register is gone.

**Step 5 · structure (Medium Panel).** Locked: intro (4) → four passes of **strophe 7 + answer 3 +
refrain 6** → coda (3). **71 sung lines.** The strophes are rigidly 7/7/7/7 by mandate. The refrain is
**byte-identical all four times** and is scored identically all four times — L21 is explicit that this
needs no defence, so nothing further is said about it. The passes are scored for **five parts, four,
three, three.**

**Step 6 · musical/lyrical elements (Concept Panel).** Registers rotated hard away from the house
fingerprint: no A major, no ~110 BPM, no crystalline soprano, no frost-and-cosmos. Keys **D minor / B♭
minor / C♯ minor / E minor**; tempi **84 / 66 / 76 / 60**; voices **alto / contralto / mezzo / alto** at
**four / five / three / three inches**. Genre elements drawn from the seed palette: *Soft String Band ·
Glitch Deep House · Alternative Motet · Cyber Darkwave · Hard Tango · Future Baroque · Alternative
Synthwave*. Music-frame elements drawn from the seed frames: *Bass — Echoing Slides — grounding the
rhythm · Glitch — Syncopated Clicks — warm tremors · Field Recording — Dreamlike Hums · Renaissance —
Hypnotic Arpeggios — caressing the sub · Sound Design — Swinging Bursts.*

**Step 9 · Concept Panel critics.** The Adorno seat killed one line outright in the V2 draft —
*"It came back because people talked. / That is the part that held."* — as a caption: it explains the
song's own mechanism in two lines and would have made the whole record a demonstration. Replaced with
*"Somebody told somebody. Then they told."*, which reports the same fact and claims nothing about what it
means. **The naming ban held.**

**Step 11 · Medium Panel critics.** The Matisse seat found **N = 2** in two drafts: V2 carried a cold synth
pad *and* the pitched-down voice; V3 carried a resonant acid line *and* the voice-bass. Ruling: the
electronic genre in each pair is the **treatment of the single intruder**, never a second material. The
pad and the acid line were collapsed into the filter on the voice. **One dominant material, one intruder,
one seam.**

**Step 13 · Context & Marketing.** The Rogers seat ran the one-listen retelling on all four and passed
them. The Bush seat objected that a record with no lift will not survive a distracted first pass, and was
overruled by **Ruling 2** for the ambitious arm; her objection stands on the record and is the reason V3
carries a groove — a pocket is not a lift, and it buys legibility without buying a swell.

**Step 15 · all panels.** The Willis seat noted the run's fun floor is portfolio-level and does not bind
P06; recorded. The Albini seat accepted the subtractions only on condition that they are changes in *who
is playing*, not fader moves, and that condition is now written into every prompt.

**Steps 17–19 · titles.** Titles are **objects, never operations**. *The Key in the Cut-Off Bin ·
A Word on the Back Board · A Line on the Lid in Pen · I Tied a Knot in It.* Every one appears verbatim in
its own refrain. Rejected: *I Took the Floor Out* — that is the pair's internal name and it is exactly the
caption the Adorno seat wants killed.
