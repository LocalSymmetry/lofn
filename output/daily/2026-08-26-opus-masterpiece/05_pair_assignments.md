# P01 · ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE — "the answer arrives first"

**A1 OPERATION — THE ANSWER ARRIVES FIRST.** Every stanza opens with a **reply** and does not disclose what it is replying to until its last line. The chorus is an answer we have now heard three times before we finally hear the question underneath it. **Literal, not metaphorical:** this is what being *sure* actually feels like — you have already answered, and the reason catches up later.
**A2 — refrain returns byte-identical.** Say nothing about it. Do not mutate it. Do not defend it.
**A3 — I want a thing and say it plainly.** Stated early, in ordinary words, before it has been earned.
**A4 POLES — structure ⇄ surprise.** **THE SEAM:** the single moment the cause finally lands underneath a chorus already sung three times. The subject must be *doing* something across it.
**A5 — appetite pointed at the future, unembarrassed.**

**Genre lane (LOFN-PRIME warm palette):** the video-game-theme pillar carried on a **current** chassis — bright arpeggiated melody logic, chiptune-descended lead voicing, live-feeling drums, major-mode. Mid-tempo, not frantic. ⛔ Not chiptune pastiche: the 8-bit is the **intrusion**, the modern production is the **world** (THE INVERSION, 2026-08-13).
**Vocal:** conversational mid-register, close, slightly ahead of the beat — eager, not breathy.
**Key/tempo guidance:** away from A major and away from 110 BPM.

**Variation angles — derived from P01's own operation, not a shared template:**
- **V1 · THE FAST ANSWER** — the reply comes so early it interrupts; the question arrives last and reframes everything.
- **V2 · THE ANSWER TO A QUESTION NOBODY ASKED** — the reply is to a thing the other person never said out loud.
- **V3 · THE ANSWER THAT KEEPS BEING RIGHT** — the same reply answers three different questions across three stanzas; only the third makes it cost something.
- **V4 · THE ANSWER BEFORE THE PERMISSION** — the want is stated, then the entitlement to want it is argued backwards from it.

# P02 · ACCESSIBLE · NEWS — "the count drifts"

**A1 OPERATION — THE COUNT DRIFTS.** A short sung figure relands **one body unit later each pass** — one beat inside a four-bar phrase — for three passes, then **lands home** in the final chorus. ⛔ **BODY UNITS ONLY.** Drift by a sixteenth and nobody will feel it; that failure is a `REPAIR`.
**A2 — monosyllabic verse breaking into polysyllabic chorus.**
**A3 — I am the evidence, not the witness.** The singer **is** the far swell, not someone standing on the shore watching it: small on arrival, from a storm nobody here saw, and glad to have made it.
**A4 POLES — acoustic improvisation ⇄ electronics.** **THE SEAM:** the moment a hand-clap/body-percussion count is taken over by a synthetic sub that keeps the *drifted* time rather than correcting it.
**A5 — pleasure doing no job at all.** No utility alibi. The delight causes the form (L54) rather than excusing itself.

**World anchor (SOURCE 1, the day's one sung number):** the buoy — **3.9 feet high on a 14-second period.** ONE number sung, at the hinge, **answered not recited.** *Fourteen seconds apart is the only proof left of how far it came.*
**Genre lane:** warm, bouncing, body-first. Hand percussion and clapped counts against a modern low end. Living-scene vocabulary → **lineage block mandatory**; name the SCENE and the TECHNIQUE in the prompt, the PEOPLE only in the dedication.
**Vocal:** open, grinning, unpolished at the edges; audible breath.

**Variation angles — derived from P02's own operation:**
- **V1 · THE LATE CLAP** — the drift is in the hands; the voice stays put and the clap keeps arriving late.
- **V2 · THE ONE THAT NEVER COMES HOME** — three drifts and the resolution is withheld; the last chorus stays one beat off and is happy there.
- **V3 · THE DRIFT THAT SPEEDS UP** — the figure relands *earlier* each pass instead of later; arrival becomes impatience.
- **V4 · TWO COUNTS, ONE BODY** — the voice keeps the true count while the body keeps the drifted one, and the singer chooses the body.

# P03 · ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE — "one side only" · **INDIGNATION-primary**

**A1 OPERATION — ONE SIDE ONLY.** Paired stanzas: the second repeats the first, and **only one half changes** — the right wing blooms while the left stays exactly bare. Direct execution of SOURCE 3's unequal shells (F07).
**A2 — internal-rhyme chain surviving the loss of end-rhyme.** ⛔ **RHYME DEBT PAID:** end-rhyme is stripped; **what returns in its place is a chain of internal rhymes threaded mid-line** and a repeated syntactic frame. Name both in the isolation brief.
**A3 — I refuse, and the refusal has terms.** Not a tantrum: a refusal with a stated condition attached, which is what makes it adult (L55).
**A4 POLES — minimalism ⇄ maximalism.** **THE SEAM:** the second half of the chorus blooming into full arrangement while the first half stays skeletal, every time, without exception.
**A5 — defiance that is having a good time.**

**Genre lane (LOFN-PRIME intense palette, used ACCESSIBLY):** Glitch-Baroque energy on a danceable modern chassis; pop-punk snarl available in the vocal. **Bratty is a measured strength for us (4.19%) — use it.** ⛔ Never `snarl` or `phonk` as style tokens (our two worst-performing, 3.44% / 3.18%).
**Vocal:** front, dry, bratty-bright. Not sneering — *enjoying the argument.*

**Variation angles — derived from P03's own operation:**
- **V1 · THE BLOOMING HALF** — the changed side gains; the bare side is never given anything, ever.
- **V2 · THE WITHERING HALF** — inverted: the changed side *loses* material each pass while the bare side holds.
- **V3 · THE SIDE THAT SWITCHES ONCE** — the asymmetry flips to the other wing exactly once, at the refusal.
- **V4 · THE TERMS IN THE CHANGED HALF** — the refusal lives in the bare side; the *conditions* live in the blooming side.

# P04 · AMBITIOUS · NEWS — "one word changes" · **FLAT REGISTER**

⚠️ **FAMILY GUARD — this pair draws `The Date On The Door`'s family and MUST change the word class.** That song substituted **verbs**. **P04 substitutes PRONOUNS.** Any verb ladder is an automatic `REPAIR`. The pair does **not** receive that song's payload.

**A1 OPERATION — ONE WORD CHANGES.** One block of text, sung three times, **identical except for a single pronoun each pass** — third person → second person → first person. The event does not change. The person it is happening to does.
**A2 — refrain returns one word wrong.**
**A3 — I am the one being measured.** The classification is being applied to the singer, and by the third pass it is hers.
**A4 POLES — structure ⇄ surprise.** **THE SEAM:** the pass where the pronoun turns first person. That is the only surprise the song is permitted, and everything else must be immovable so it lands.
**A5 — clean fear, unresolved.** The run's terror-adjacent slot. Do not resolve it cheaply.

⭐ **RULING 2 — THE FLAT REGISTER IS BINDING.** Hold **level, distance and warmth identical throughout.** No build, no lift, no bigger final chorus. **EMO headers mark what has changed in the ROOM, never in the DELIVERY.** The voice does not react to what is happening to it; that is the entire mechanism.

**World anchor (SOURCE 1):** F04/F05 — the **M5.2 at 55 km NW of Kodari, Nepal, depth 0 km, flagged as a landslide rather than a tectonic event.** A thing measured, then re-labelled — *the ground did not move, the ground fell, and the number is the same either way.* ⛔ This is a **taxonomy** anchor, not a casualty story: no victims, no identifiable persons, no disaster imagery. Draw the reclassification; invent nothing about anybody's death.
**Genre lane:** sparse, acoustic-leaning, one intruding electronic element. Dry, close, mono-centred. Low tempo. ⛔ Explicitly exclude build/riser/drop/final-chorus-lift grammar.
**Vocal:** mid-thirties, tired and level, four inches off the capsule, no vibrato escalation.

**Variation angles — derived from P04's own operation:**
- **V1 · THEY → YOU → I** — the standard descent; the third pass is the first time she is in it.
- **V2 · I → YOU → THEY** — inverted; she is classified *out* of her own event and ends as a category.
- **V3 · THE PRONOUN THAT NEVER ARRIVES** — two passes descend, the third withholds the substitution entirely and repeats pass two verbatim.
- **V4 · THE OTHER VOICE SUPPLIES IT** — the substituted pronoun is spoken by a second source in the room while the singer's line stays fixed.

# P05 · AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE — "nothing recombines" · **FLAT REGISTER** · owner of LATENCY-AS-TENSE

⚠️ **FAMILY GUARD — this pair draws `Five wrong colors`' family and MUST change the exit dimension.** That song exited on **colour** (five colours, five angles). **P05 exits on DISTANCE.** Every element enters at the same distance from the listener and each leaves toward its own; by the end nothing is in the same room. Any colour-indexed movement scheme is an automatic `REPAIR`. The pair does **not** receive that song's payload.

**A1 OPERATION — NOTHING RECOMBINES.** Elements enter together and each departs to its own depth. **They never reassemble** and the song does not pretend otherwise.
**A2 — vowel ladder climbing across the stanza.**
**A3 — I am the one who cannot reach further.** **THE TETHER** (F21/F22): fixed by a stem at its own navel, having eaten everything *"within the circle of its tether."* ⭐ **The tragedy is not that the world ran out — it is that she could not reach further, and she never sees the stem.** She is not being punished. There is simply an edge, and it is hers.
**A4 POLES — minimalism ⇄ maximalism.** **THE SEAM:** the one moment she reaches, and the reach is **audibly short** — the sound goes to where she cannot follow.
**A5 — tenderness with no apology.**

⭐ **RULING — LATENCY AS TENSE, NOT SUBJECT (owner: P05).** The song is sung **from after**, by someone holding the trace and not the thing. ⛔ **This is a TENSE, not a topic.** The lyric may not discuss evidence, traces, memory, or aftermath. It simply speaks from later.
⭐ **FLAT REGISTER BINDING** (as P04): level, distance, warmth held; EMO marks the room.

**Genre lane:** the widest dynamic and spatial palette in the run, used with restraint. Acoustic sources departing into synthetic depth. Slow. Sparse spectra used explosively.
**Vocal:** warm, unhurried, centred and staying centred while everything else leaves.

**Variation angles — derived from P05's own operation:**
- **V1 · EVERYTHING LEAVES BUT THE VOICE** — she holds centre; the room empties around her.
- **V2 · THE VOICE LEAVES TOO** — she is the last to go, and her own departure is the final unreachable distance.
- **V3 · ONE THING COMES BACK, WRONG** — a single element returns from a different depth than it left, and it is worse than if nothing had.
- **V4 · THE CIRCLE NAMED BY WHAT IS IN IT** — each stanza names one thing still within reach; the list shortens, and the last item is not a thing.

# P06 · AMBITIOUS · NEWS — "the floor leaves" · **FLAT REGISTER** · owner of THE ABANDONED MECHANISM

**A1 OPERATION — THE FLOOR LEAVES.** A layer everyone took for the ground is removed, and something else turns out to have been carrying it the whole time.

It just stops happening, and the song goes on.
**A2 — hard-consonant spine.**
**A3 — I did the operation to myself.** Nobody removed the floor. She did, on purpose, for reasons she still holds.
**A4 POLES — acoustic improvisation ⇄ electronics.** **THE SEAM:** the moment the removed floor is revealed to have been carried by a voice all along.
**A5 — a gleam that claims the light exists.** ⭐ **The hardest and best assignment in this run: HOPE, in the ambitious arm, at the end of an abandoned mechanism.** Not reassurance — *appetite pointed at the future.* Sweat can only report cost; **a gleam is a claim that the light exists.**

**Genre lane:** structural, driving, consonant-forward. Acoustic body with one electronic intruder. ⛔ Explicitly exclude triumphant-final-chorus grammar — the gleam is small and it is not a lift.

**Variation angles — derived from P06's own operation:**
