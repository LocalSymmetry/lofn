## Why this anchor, and not another

**Why THE MEASUREMENT:** it is the only archetype in the library whose engine is *an arrival that recontextualises a scene the singer is already standing in*. Every other archetype either moves the singer somewhere (THE ARRIVAL), removes her from the event (THE LAST WITNESS), or replaces the event with a list (THE CATALOG). This run needed a singer who stays put while something happens to her — so the anchor had to be the one that keeps her in place.

**Why the mutation was to the ARRIVING OBJECT and nothing else:** the two commissioned exemplars deliver their feeling through a formal operation and **neither contains a fact at all.** Swapping the arriving fact for an arriving operation is therefore the *minimum* change that reaches the target — every other element of THE MEASUREMENT (ordinary scene, recontextualisation, prayer in the last chorus) survives untouched. A larger mutation would have been a new archetype wearing a proven one's name.

**Why THE SWITCHBOARD is secondary and not primary:** the run needs rupture grammar for exactly one slot (the INDIGNATION pair, P03). Promoting it would have made whiplash the run's subject, and this run's subject is *being acted on and staying interested* — which is the opposite of whiplash.

**Why the first-person correction was applied to the anchor rather than to the exemplars:** the exemplars are evidence about *what works*, not instructions about *who sings*. Their narrator position is incidental to their engine, and it is the property this house measured as a defect two days before this run. Preserving the engine and discarding the position is the only reading under which both the commission and the standing doctrine are satisfied at once.
