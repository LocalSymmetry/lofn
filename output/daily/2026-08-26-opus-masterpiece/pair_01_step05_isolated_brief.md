## 1. YOUR SECTION ARCHITECTURE

**ANSWER-FIRST COUPLETS.** Every stanza is a run of couplets whose FIRST line is a reply and whose LAST line finally discloses what was being replied to. Section order: Intro (2 lines, reply only) / Verse 1 (4 couplets) / Chorus / Verse 2 (4 couplets) / Chorus / Verse 3 (3 couplets, shortest) / Chorus / Outro (the question, alone, 2 lines). **Stanza lengths deliberately unequal: 8 / 8 / 6.**

## 2. YOUR RHYME / LINE LOGIC

**End-rhyme KEPT and dense** — this pair pays no rhyme debt because it removes nothing. Couplets rhyme AA. The rhyme is what lets the ear file the reply as an answer before it knows the question: a closed sound arriving in an open situation.

## 3. YOUR HOOK GRAMMAR

The chorus is a plain want in ordinary words, stated as though the argument for it were already over. ⛔ **BYTE-IDENTICAL every time. Do not mutate it, do not defend it, say nothing about it.**

## 4. YOUR SONIC DEVICE

A bass figure that RESOLVES BEFORE the harmony asks — the cadence lands a beat ahead of the chord that would justify it, every chorus, unmarked.

## 5. YOUR INTRUDER PLACEMENT (assigned at step 05 — not your choice)

**MID-SONG, AT THE REVEAL** — the chiptune-descended square lead states, once, the melody the vocal has already answered three times.

## 6. YOUR SEAM (N = 1)

The single bar where the cause finally lands underneath a chorus we have already sung three times. **She must be DOING something across it** — still answering, not stopping to listen.

## 8. THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS (answer both concretely at step 07, before any lyric)

Where is the body standing? — **it must be a place where answering costs something.** What could hurt it here? — **being right too early.**

## 9. YOUR FOUR VARIATION ANGLES (derived from YOUR operation — no shared template)

- **V1 · THE FAST ANSWER** — the reply comes so early it interrupts; the question arrives last and reframes everything
- **V2 · THE ANSWER TO A QUESTION NOBODY ASKED** — the reply is to a thing the other person never said out loud
- **V3 · THE ANSWER THAT KEEPS BEING RIGHT** — one reply answers three different questions; only the third makes it cost something
- **V4 · THE ANSWER BEFORE THE PERMISSION** — the want is stated, then the entitlement to want it is argued backwards from it
