## How it was resolved

Not by picking a side, and not by averaging.

It is a **formal operation the listener can catch, where catching it IS the feeling** — nothing recombines; one word changes. Neither song *describes* its emotion; each performs an operation and lets the listener catch it.

**That engine is portable and the narrator position is not part of it.**

> ⭐ **RESOLUTION: keep the catchable-operation engine of the two exemplars; change who is standing inside it.** The masterpiece this pipeline has not made yet is the one where the operation happens to *me*.
