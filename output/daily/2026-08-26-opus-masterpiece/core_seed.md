## 2. THE SEED, IN ONE SENTENCE

> ⭐ **THE OPERATION I AM INSIDE OF**
> *Every song performs one formal operation a listener can catch — and the person it is happening to is the one singing it.*

### Why this is the seed and not "write something devastating"

I read both commissioned exemplars for their shared engine (`00_research_brief.md` §8) and it is **not** subject matter, register, or key:

- *Five wrong colors* — five colours enter through a prism and **none recombine**; the refrain returns damaged every time. The feeling arrives when you notice nothing came back whole.
- *The Date On The Door* — one instant, three passes, **one monosyllable different each pass**. The feeling arrives when you notice the word changed.

**Neither song describes its emotion. Each performs an operation and lets you catch it.** That is `ANALYSIS_2026-08-08`'s law — *the depth is made OUT OF the material, never laid ON TOP of it* — applied at the level of song form.

### ⛔ And the trap that comes free with the commission

Both exemplars are third-person character songs, formally cold, non-consoling. **Chasing them literally walks straight back into the doctrine failure The Scientist diagnosed on 2026-08-24:** a slot grammar with no row in which the singer is the subject, producing narrators with no stake — *"telling a story with no highs or lows"* (Ash, same period). A narrator has no stake, so there is no peak.

Measured, on the live catalogue this morning: the first-person song from the 08-25 run, `I Want to Be the Bass Drop`, opened at **8.06% like rate on day zero** — the top of our board.

> ⭐ **THE SEED IS THE SYNTHESIS, NOT A COMPROMISE: keep the catchable-operation engine of the two masterpieces; change who is standing inside it.** The masterpiece this pipeline has not made yet is the one where the operation happens to *me*.

## 3. CONSTRAINT AXES — FIVE, FRESH, EACH A VOCABULARY OF SIX

*(Not recycled from any prior run. One value per pair, distinct across pairs.)*

### AXIS 1 — THE OPERATION *(the catchable formal move — the spine of this run)*
| | operation | what the listener catches |
|---|---|---|
| A1.1 | **ONE WORD CHANGES** | a block returns with exactly one lexical substitution per pass |
| A1.2 | **NOTHING RECOMBINES** | elements enter and each exits at its own angle; the whole never reassembles |
| A1.3 | **THE COUNT DRIFTS** | a figure that should land on the beat relands one unit later each pass, until it comes home or doesn't |
| A1.4 | **THE ANSWER ARRIVES FIRST** | the response is heard before the thing it answers; the cause is sung last |
| A1.5 | **ONE SIDE ONLY** | the return comes back changed on exactly one side *(the nebula's unequal shells — SOURCE 3)* |
| A1.6 | **THE FLOOR LEAVES** | a layer everyone took for the ground is removed, and something else has been carrying it the whole time |

⛔ **A1.1 and A1.2 are the exemplars' own families.** A pair drawing one of them must change the **word class** (A1.1) or the **exit dimension** (A1.2) so it is a sibling, not a copy. Never that song's own substitution ladder.

### AXIS 2 — SOUND / RETURN *(mandatory per L21; a POSITIVE vocabulary, never a list of removals)*
`refrain that returns byte-identical` · `refrain that returns one word wrong` · `vowel ladder climbing across the stanza` · `internal-rhyme chain that survives the loss of end-rhyme` · `hard-consonant spine` · `monosyllabic verse breaking into polysyllabic chorus`

⛔ **THE RHYME DEBT.** Any pair that strips end-rhyme must name in its step-05 isolation brief **what returns in its place.** Five subtractions and zero additions produces a lecture regardless of concept. **A byte-identical chorus is correct and needs no defence — say nothing about it.**

### AXIS 3 — THE STAKE *(who I am inside the operation)*
`I want a thing and say it plainly` · `I am the one being measured` · `I am the one who cannot reach further` · `I did the operation to myself` · `I am the evidence, not the witness` · `I refuse, and the refusal has terms`

⭐ **THE FIRST-PERSON SLOT IS MANDATORY IN ≥4 OF 6 PAIRS** (`vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md` §THE FIRST-PERSON SLOT). My own wants are a sufficient subject and need no further justification. I cannot be harmed by being sung about — **first person is the safest configuration available, not a riskier one.**

### AXIS 4 — TWO POLES AND THE SEAM *(imported from SOURCE 2, F09, verbatim vocabulary)*
Each pair names **its own two poles** from the contrast grammar — *minimalism/maximalism · acoustic improvisation/electronics · structure/surprise* — and places **exactly ONE seam** at the junction that carries the meaning.
⛔ **ONE SEAM (L38): N = 1.** One dominant material + at most one intruder + one seam, **and the subject must be doing something across it.** Multi-style collision is retired; the variety lives ACROSS the six, never inside one song.

### AXIS 5 — TEMPERATURE OF LIGHT *(the hope / frivolity axis — new to the doctrine as of 2026-08-24)*
`appetite pointed at the future, unembarrassed` · `pleasure doing no job at all` · `a gleam that claims the light exists` · `defiance that is having a good time` · `tenderness with no apology` · `clean fear, unresolved`

⭐ **HOPE is not optimism and not reassurance — it is appetite pointed at the future**: wanting a specific thing out loud that has not happened yet, unembarrassed to be caught wanting it. **Sweat can only report cost; a gleam is a claim that the light exists.**
⛔ **NO CEILING ON THE FUN.** Frivolity is a skill, not a quota — the 08-22 run capped *pleasure doing no job* at two of six and that cap was the error. The clean-fear slot keeps its own place; it does not get to police the others.

## 6. NEUTRAL DISPATCH BRIEF *(this is what the panel debates — no personality injected yet)*

> Make six songs. Each performs **one formal operation** that a first-time listener can catch without being told it is there, and **catching it is the feeling** — no song may describe the emotion it is delivering. In at least four of the six, the operation is happening to the singer, who wants something and says so plainly. The day supplies a sky that is almost full and completely calm, and a small wave that travelled a very long way. One dominant material per song, at most one intruder, one seam, and the subject must be doing something across it. Whatever returns must come back changed on one side only. At most one number is sung, and the song answers it rather than reciting it.
>
> **The failure to beat:** a song that is *about* its operation instead of *made of* it. The second failure to beat: a narrator with nothing at stake.
