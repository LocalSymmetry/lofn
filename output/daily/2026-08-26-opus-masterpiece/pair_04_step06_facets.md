## 1. THE SIX WEIGHTED FACETS

These are the judging criteria this pair is scored against. They are derived from **this pair's** operation and are shared with no other pair. Weights sum to 1.00.

---

### F1 · THE SUBSTITUTION LANDS ON A BODY — **weight 0.24**

> *One personal pronoun moves across three otherwise identical passes, and a listener who is not counting anything catches it.*

**What earns the weight:** the substituted token sits at a **marked, memorised position** — inside the classification sentence, at the same metrical slot every pass — and all three of its values are **monosyllabic**, so the meter does not report the change. Only the meaning does.

**Scoring**

- 1.00 — the block contains **exactly one personal pronoun** and it is the substituted one; a first-listen stranger notices the third pass without being told anything.
- 0.60 — the substitution is present but buried mid-verse, or the three values differ in syllable count so the rhythm gives the change away before the meaning does.
- 0.00 — **any verb, noun, adjective or number also changes between passes.** REPAIR, routed to step 07.

**Instrument:** count personal pronouns per pass; diff the three passes token by token. The diff must have **cardinality 1**.

---

### F2 · THE REGISTER NEVER MOVES — **weight 0.22**

> *Level, distance, warmth, tempo, arrangement density and the instrument list are identical from the first bar to the last.*

This is Ruling 2 in its binding form. **A build anywhere in this pair is a REPAIR.** The voice does not react to what is happening to it — that is the entire mechanism, and a singer who gets more emotional at pass three has deleted the mechanism and replaced it with a performance.

**Scoring**

- 1.00 — the instrument list at the final bar is the same list as at bar one; **nothing is added and nothing is removed**; no riser, no lift, no key change, no half-time, no octave-up, no bigger final chorus; the EMO headers mark the **room** and the Role field of every header states the delivery unchanged.
- 0.50 — the notation is flat but the exclude field does not fight for it, so the generator is free to add a final-chorus lift anyway.
- 0.00 — any dynamic arc, any added layer at pass three, any harmony stack on the last chorus.

**Instrument:** four independent controls must all be present — (a) the affirmative invariance sentence in the MUSIC PROMPT's arrangement-arc slot, (b) the full build grammar in the EXCLUDE field, (c) `no_swell_no_filter_movement` and `no_new_layer_entry` in `Disc_Pad`, (d) an unchanged Role string in all nine section headers. **A gate with one control is a gate that can be walked around.**

### F3 · THE CLERICAL SURFACE IS PLEASANT, REASONABLE, AND ACCURATE — **weight 0.18**

> *The chorus is a classification sentence — flat, courteous, the language of a form being filled in — and it is pleasant every time, including the third.*

The pre-draft answer to *what could hurt it here* is **the category, and the fact that it is accurate.** Nothing in the room is cruel. Nobody raises a voice. The finding is correct. That is why it is frightening: there is no one to argue with and nothing to appeal.

**Scoring**

- 1.00 — the classification sentence would pass unremarked in an actual office; the lyric concedes the finding's **accuracy** plainly at least once; there is no villain, no raised voice, no institutional sneer.
- 0.60 — the surface is clerical but the song editorialises once, and the editorial is where the fear leaks out.
- 0.00 — the room becomes an antagonist. **Indignation at a clerk is a smaller feeling than courtesy that is correct.**

### F4 · THE INTRUDER IS FURNITURE, NOT AN EVENT — **weight 0.14**

> *One electronic element, present from bar 1, unmoving; it never enters and it never leaves.*

Assigned at step 05, not chosen here. It is this pair's execution of **ONE SEAM, N = 1**: the dry acoustic room is the world, the single electronic element is the intrusion, and because the intruder never moves, **the pronoun is the only event in the song.**

**Scoring**

- 1.00 — one electronic source, one fixed pitch or rate, one level, present in the first second and the last; it is already collapsed to grit **before the song begins** (LOFN-PRIME pillar 6 inverted — the certainty collapsed before we arrived and never un-collapses) and therefore has nothing left to swell into.
- 0.50 — the element is present but is given an envelope, a filter sweep, or an entrance.
- 0.00 — a second intruding material. **Two collisions in one song is a REPAIR.**

### F5 · THE SUBJECT IS PLAIN AND THE OPERATION IS UNNAMED — **weight 0.12**

> *Say plainly what is happening to you; never say what shape it is making.*

The two rules that look opposed and are not. **THE LEGIBILITY RULE:** a stranger retells scene and subject in one sentence after one listen — *someone is filling in a form about her, and the box they tick changes what she is called.* **THE NAMING BAN (R4):** no lyric refers to the substitution, the repetition, the passes, the word, or the change of person.

**Scoring**

- 1.00 — the subject appears plainly in the title or the first quatrain; **zero lines refer to the song's own structure**; the DELETION TEST is run and the operation survives it intact.
- 0.60 — legible, but one line points at the mechanism and would have to be struck.
- 0.00 — the operation is captioned, or the subject is fog.

**Instrument:** strike every line that refers to the structure, then re-read. If the operation vanishes, it was a caption. **Report what was struck and what survived.**

---

### F6 · THE ROOM CHANGES; THE VOICE DOES NOT — **weight 0.10**

> *EMO headers mark what has changed in the ROOM, never in the DELIVERY.*

The emotional dramaturgy is entirely environmental: a hall light goes off, a second chair empties, a tray is carried out, a printer stops. The taxonomy tags travel `Vigilance → Unease → Trepidation → Suspense`; the Role string does not move a character.

**Scoring**

- 1.00 — nine section headers, nine room-states, one unchanged Role string; the arc ends **unresolved** (`Suspense`, never `Relief`, never `Numbness` — clean fear is not resolved cheaply, and numbness is a resolution).
- 0.50 — the room moves but a header cue smuggles a delivery instruction (*bigger*, *open up*, *with feeling*).
- 0.00 — the EMO arc describes the singer's escalating emotion. That is the mechanism's death certificate.
