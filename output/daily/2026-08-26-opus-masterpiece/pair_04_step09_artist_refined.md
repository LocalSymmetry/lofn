# 1. STEP 1–2 · THE FOUR LENSES, AND WHAT EACH ONE IS FOR

## V1 lens — *the Irish harpist-organist who recorded in a working church and left the cleaners in*

**Aspect of style that complements the prompt:** a body of organ and harp work made inside a building that was **not cleared for the recording** — hoovers, footsteps, buckets, a door — with the incidental labour left in the take rather than gated out. That is precisely this pair's problem solved by somebody else: how to make a room's ordinary business audible without turning it into an effect.

**Two signature techniques borrowed:**
1. **Incidental labour left in the take.** The building's own noises are not foley and not atmosphere; they are simply *not removed*. Applied here: the kettle down the hall, the pen, the chair are in the recording because the recording did not stop for them.
2. **Sustain without swell.** A held reed or pipe tone that lasts because the air lasts, not because a fader moves. Applied here: the one reed chord is held flat, and the electronic hum inherits the same law.

**Emotional / thematic focus:** patience without piety. Nothing devotional, nothing elevated — a large quiet room with someone working in it.
**Perspective-taking:** *"I did not ask the room to be quiet for me. The room was busy. That is what it sounds like when a place has other things to do while something is happening to you."*

## V2 lens — *the Swedish just-intonation organ and brass composer*

**Aspect of style:** long tones tuned a few cents apart so that the **beating between them** becomes the piece's only rhythm — an event that is constant in rate and depth and therefore cannot build.

**Two signature techniques borrowed:**
1. **Fixed beating as the pulse.** The electronic ready-tone and the harmonium sit a few cents apart; the resulting slow beat is the closest thing this song has to a metronome and it never changes rate. ⭐ This is a rhythmic element that is *structurally incapable of a crescendo* — the exact answer to this pair's largest risk.
2. **Intonation as timbre.** Tuning is not a correctness question; it is what the chord is made of. Applied here: the harmonium is deliberately not in equal temperament with the tone, and that is the texture, not a defect.

**Emotional / thematic focus:** stillness that is dense rather than empty.
**Perspective-taking:** *"Two things almost the same, held long enough that the difference becomes the sound."*

## V3 lens — *the Argentine littoral chamamé accordionist*

**Aspect of style:** the reed instrument as breath rather than as keyboard — bellows direction is phrasing, and the accompaniment sits *sotto* under the voice without ever competing with it.

**Two signature techniques borrowed:**
1. **The chord opened on the draw only.** The bellows open; they never compress on the sung line. A reed that breathes **in** cannot push, so the chord physically cannot crescendo.
2. **Accompaniment that refuses the foreground.** The tradition's habit of holding the harmony beneath the melody at a fixed distance rather than answering it.

**Emotional / thematic focus:** courtesy and warmth in a form that is nonetheless implacable.
**Perspective-taking:** *"The instrument is polite. That is not the same as gentle."*
**Debt:** the chamamé tradition of the Argentine littoral is a living scene. It is named as a **scene and a technique** in the prompt and its people are named in §6 Lineage & Credit. It is never named as a person in any Suno field.

## V4 lens — *the American opera-of-speech composer of the involuntary low murmur*

**Aspect of style:** a continuous, low, semi-involuntary speech-stream running under an entire work, at conversational rate, which the listener stops parsing after about forty seconds and then cannot un-hear.

**Two signature techniques borrowed:**
1. **Speech that does not respect the form.** The murmur does not pause at section boundaries, does not cadence, and does not acknowledge the song's architecture. That is what makes it furniture rather than a duet partner.
2. **Intelligibility as an event, not a level.** The murmur never gets louder. It simply becomes legible for one word. ⭐ This is the whole variation, and the lens is where the technique is proven to work.

**Emotional / thematic focus:** the uncanny of being described, continuously, by something that is not addressing you.
**Perspective-taking:** *"It was talking before I came in. It will be talking after. Once, it says a word I recognise."*
