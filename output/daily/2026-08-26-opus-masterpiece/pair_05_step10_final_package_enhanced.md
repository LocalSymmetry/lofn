## THE THREE LAWS OF THIS SEED

### LAW 1 — THE OPERATION IS THE MATERIAL, NEVER THE TOPIC
A song is made **OF** its operation, never **ABOUT** it. The moment a lyric explains the mechanism, the mechanism is dead.
**Test:** delete every line that refers to the structure. If the operation is still audible, it is real. If it vanishes, you wrote a caption.

### LAW 2 — THE PERSON IT HAPPENS TO IS SINGING
The operation is not performed on a character the singer is watching. **≥4 of 6 pairs put the singer inside it**, wanting something and saying so plainly.
A narrator has no stake, so there is no peak. My own wants are a sufficient subject and need no further justification — **I cannot be harmed by being sung about, so first person is the safest configuration available, not a riskier one.**

### LAW 3 — WHAT RETURNS COMES BACK CHANGED ON ONE SIDE ONLY
From SOURCE 3 (the Lion's Head Nebula, F07): a spent core at the centre, everything visible is what it threw off, **and the shells are unequal on opposite sides.**
Symmetrical return is decoration. **Asymmetric return is the seed.** Something must come back, and it must come back wrong in exactly one place.

## Major Deviations

- **Changed / refused / intensified — 1: the vowel ladder was built as SIX climbs, not one continuous scheme.** The brief specifies a ladder that "climbs across each stanza, one step per line, resetting at each inventory." Executed literally, Inventory One would have needed a twenty-four-rung ladder, which exceeds the usable vowel space and is inaudible. **Built instead as an eight-rung ladder run three times in Inventory One, twice in Inventory Two, once in Inventory Three** — four lines per item, so one climb equals exactly two items.
  **Reason:** it satisfies the reset condition and adds a second, stronger one — **the number of climbs IS the length of the inventory**, so the recession is audible in the *sound* of the lines and not only in their content.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** this is the rhyme debt paid in the same architectural seat rhyme vacated (the end of the line), which is what L21 asks for and what a bare "we removed end-rhyme" note never delivers.

- **Changed / refused / intensified — 2: V2's angle was executed on the voice-as-material, never on the voice-as-delivery.** The brief's V2 angle ("the voice leaves too, and her own departure is the final unreachable distance") collides head-on with the binding flat register ("hold level, distance and warmth identical end to end").
  **Reason:** R2 is a Ruling and does not yield to a variation angle. Resolved by putting **her voice on her own inventory as item six** and sending the *copy* — a formant-shifted vowel two octaves down — to the far rear, while her live delivery does not move an inch.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** the gap between *the voice* and *her* is now the song's horror instead of a mix move, and the pair keeps its register intact.

- **Changed / refused / intensified — 3: the reach was refused as beautiful, on the record, four times.** The artist's declared failure mode at step 03 was *"I will want to make the reach beautiful."* Four beautiful versions were written and killed: reaching for the moon; a held note rising and thinning; a stereo field opening as she stretches; and a silence "she cannot cross." **What shipped is a person on one knee making an undignified noise and missing.**
  **Reason:** a beautiful reach is a mood, and a mood cannot be caught by a body. The one instrument this pair actually owns is a listener's proprioception.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** it is the only place in the run where the largest event in a song is somebody failing to pick something up.

- **Changed / refused / intensified — 4: seven facets at step 06 instead of five.** The contract floor is five. **Reason:** the flat register and the latency tense are separately failable and needed their own scored rows, or they would have been enforced by prose alone. Weights sum to 100 either way.

- **Changed / refused / intensified — 5: no real practitioner is named anywhere in this pair's artifacts, including the prose.** Step 09's contract asks for a named obscure musician per prompt. **Refused.** Step 09 uses unnamed practitioner-archetypes instead.
  **Reason:** the handoff bans real names in the three Suno fields, and the failure mode it protects against is a name leaking from prose into a prompt during refinement. Removing the name from the prose removes the leak path entirely. The *lens* survives; only the handle is gone.
  **Effect on Lofn uniqueness:** none, except that the pair cannot fail that gate by accident.
