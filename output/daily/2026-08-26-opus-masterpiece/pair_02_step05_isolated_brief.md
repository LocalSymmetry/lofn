## 1. YOUR SECTION ARCHITECTURE

**DRIFTING REFRAIN.** A four-line clapped figure returns four times, relanding **ONE BEAT LATER inside a four-bar phrase** on each of the first three returns, then landing home on the fourth. Section order: Intro (sub alone) / Figure A / Verse 1 / Figure A+1 / Chorus / Verse 2 / Figure A+2 / Chorus / Bridge (counts collide) / Figure A-home / Final Chorus. **Stanza lengths 6 / 6 / 4.**

## 2. YOUR RHYME / LINE LOGIC

**Monosyllabic verse breaking into polysyllabic chorus.** Verses are one-syllable words almost throughout — the mouth keeping a count. The chorus opens into three- and four-syllable words and the count relaxes with it. End-rhyme kept, loose and slant. **No rhyme debt owed.**

## 3. YOUR HOOK GRAMMAR

The chorus names the interval out loud, once, and answers it rather than reciting it. **This is the run's one-fact hinge for this song: fourteen seconds.** Nothing else numeric is sung.

## 4. YOUR SONIC DEVICE

The synthetic sub INHERITS the drifted count rather than correcting it — at the seam the hands stop and the sub is already keeping the wrong time, and stays wrong.

## 5. YOUR INTRUDER PLACEMENT (assigned at step 05 — not your choice)

⭐ **FIRST 10 SECONDS** — the sub is present before the hands are. It does not 'enter'; it was always there, and it takes the count later.

## 6. YOUR SEAM (N = 1)

The moment the hand-count is taken over by the sub **without correction**. Her body has to choose the drifted count over the true one, audibly.
