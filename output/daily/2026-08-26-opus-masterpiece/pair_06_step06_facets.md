## 0 · WHAT THESE FACETS HAVE TO CATCH

The facets are the rubric every later step is scored against. For P06 they must be able to fail a song
for the two things that will actually kill it, neither of which a generic music rubric can see:

1. **A swell at the hope.** The generator's strongest prior on "hope at the end" is a lift. A lift here
   does not weaken the song; it *replaces* the song. Facet 2 exists to fail that.
2. **A remark on the abandonment.** The third scheduled subtraction never happens. The instant anything —
   a line, a header, an EMO tag, an arrangement note — notices, the flair is dead and the record becomes
   clever instead of strange. Facet 4 exists to fail that.

Three of the eighteen seats own these facets directly. The Martin seat (**THE HELD ABSENCE**) supplies the
flat-register test and will not accept "quiet" as a substitute for restraint. The Adorno seat
(**THE ANTI-MECHANISM**) supplies the deletion test and refuses to withdraw it. The Eno seat
(**THE REPEAT LISTEN**) owns this pair's flair by name — *the inconsistency principle* — and is
interested in the fortieth play, not the first.

## 1 · THE FIVE FACETS (weighted; weights sum to 100)

### FACET 1 — THE SUBTRACTION IS AUDIBLE TO A BODY, NOT TO A SCORE READER · **weight 24**
> *Does the room get emptier at a boundary a listener feels in the chest, and does the emptiness change
> what the remaining players have to do?*

The passes are scored for **five parts, then four, then three, then three**. A subtraction that only shows
up in a stem list has not happened. What must be audible: the harmonic floor moves onto fewer shoulders,
the survivors start carrying weight they were not carrying, and the singer does not change to compensate.
Reject: a mix that thins by fader only; a subtraction hidden under a reverb tail; a "breakdown" that
announces itself with a filter sweep. Accept: the tack piano leaves and the fiddle has to state the
harmony alone and audibly does not enjoy it.
*Sensory test:* the third pass should feel colder in the hands, like a room where somebody opened a door.
*Flairs served:* Catchable Drift · Amplified Quiet · Sparse Spectra · The Body That Notices.

### FACET 2 — LEVEL, DISTANCE AND WARMTH ARE IDENTICAL FROM FIRST BREATH TO LAST · **weight 24**
> *If you solo the vocal and play the last thirty seconds against the first thirty, can you tell which
> is which without the words?*

Binding, per Ruling 2. The room changes; the voice does not. Mic distance fixed, no vibrato escalation,
no belt, no doubling that only arrives at the end, no compression riding up. **The EMO header marks the
ROOM, never the DELIVERY** — a header that says *soaring* or *anguished* is an automatic fail regardless
of how good the line is. And the specific fail this pair is exposed to: **the gleam arriving with a lift.**
A song that swells at the hope has traded the hope for a production move; the gleam is a claim about the
world, and a claim does not get louder to be true.
*Sensory test:* the hopeful line and the coldest line should measure the same on a meter.
*Flairs served:* Flat Delivery, Changed Room · Tender Power · One Stroke Loaded.

### FACET 3 — THE STOP-CONSONANT SPINE HOLDS EVERY LINE UP AFTER THE RHYME LETS GO · **weight 18**
> *Read any stanza aloud with the melody removed. Do the stressed syllables land on `p b t d k g` hard
> enough that the line has an edge in the mouth — and does that edge survive into the thinnest pass?*

End-rhyme is present but subordinate and owes no debt: **what returns in its place is the consonant
architecture**, and it is the one element that survives every subtraction. Reject: assonance-led lines
that go soft when the accompaniment thins; a stanza whose only structure is its rhyme; polysyllabic
abstractions (*possibility, continuity, foundation*) sitting on stressed beats.
*Sensory test:* a listener with the audio muffled through a wall should still hear the consonants ticking.
*Flairs served:* Hard-Consonant Spine · The Audible Join.

### FACET 4 — NOTHING ANYWHERE NOTICES THE MECHANISM, INCLUDING THE MECHANISM THAT STOPS · **weight 20**
> *Strike every line that refers to the song's structure. What is left?*

Two sub-tests, both pass/fail.
**(a) The deletion test (R4, the Adorno seat's single surviving objection).** If striking the
structure-referring lines removes the operation, the song is a caption and routes back to step 07. The
target state for P06 is the strong one: **there is nothing to strike**, because the operation lives
entirely in the score and the words are only ever about a physical object in a physical place.
**(b) The abandonment check (P06 only, Flair 13).** The third scheduled subtraction does not happen.
⛔ **Nothing in the lyric, the arrangement, the section headers, or the EMO tags may acknowledge that.**
No "and then it stopped", no header that reads *pattern breaks*, no wry line about the thing that did
not come. It simply stops happening and the song goes on. Arrangement notation may state what a pass
*contains*; it may never state what a pass *fails to do*.
*Sensory test:* a listener who catches it should not be able to point at the moment they were told.
*Flairs served:* The Abandoned Mechanism · The Trace Without the Event.

### FACET 5 — ONE SEAM, AND A BODY CROSSES IT WHILE STILL SINGING · **weight 14**
> *Is there exactly one junction where the dominant material meets the intruder, is it a moment the
> singer's body changes what it is doing, and is she still going when it happens?*

N = 1: one dominant acoustic material, **at most one** intruder, one seam. The intruder is the layer that
was carrying the ground all along and turns out to be a human voice — pitched down, filtered, granulated,
or resynthesised — and it is revealed **at the very end, with no recovery.** The seam must be a bodily
event, never an edit point: a foot leaving a board, a jaw dropping, a hand coming off a soundboard, a
breath held across a bar and a half. **The reveal happens underneath her, not to her** — she is still
singing across it, and she does not react.
*Sensory test:* you should be able to describe the seam as something a person did, not something a mix did.
*Flairs served:* The Audible Join · The Unequal Shell · Quantum Bit-Depth Swell (as the voice-floor's
collapse from body to grit).

## 3 · AUTOMATIC-FAIL LIST (a candidate carrying any one of these is not scored, it is returned)

1. A build, riser, key change, octave-up, added choir, added strings, or a bigger final chorus anywhere.
2. A lyric line naming structure, layers, foundations, ground, removal, patterns, mechanisms, or form.
3. Any acknowledgement — anywhere in the package — that the third subtraction did not happen.
4. A named company, a named project, an identifiable person, legal language, or a licence name.
5. More than one numeric fact sung, or a numeric fact recited rather than answered.
6. Two intruding materials, or a second seam.
7. A temporal operation specified in notation units (a sixteenth, a 32nd) rather than body units.
8. A house-lexicon phrase from `vault/gates.yaml` (checked verbatim: none present).
9. A real artist, group, panel-source or company name in any of the three Suno fields.
10. Reverence. Announcing that something important is happening instead of letting it happen.

## 5 · SELF-EVALUATION (step-06 contract, Step 2)

- **Are these five specific to P06, or portable boilerplate?** Facets 1, 4 and 5 cannot be transplanted to
  any other pair in this run: only P06 subtracts, only P06 abandons, and only P06 places its intruder at
  the very end with no recovery. Facet 3 is P06's assigned A2 value. Facet 2 is shared with P04/P05 by
  Ruling 2 and is kept at full weight because it is where this pair fails if it fails.
- **Do the weights argue?** 48 of 100 sit on the two facets that fail a *good-sounding* record (audible
  subtraction, flat register), and only 14 on the seam — deliberately, because the seam is easy to
  achieve and hard to ruin, while the flat register is easy to ruin and hard to achieve.
- **Style-axis alignment.** Tempo 30 · Energy 26 · Harmonic complexity 42 · Rhythmic complexity 55
  (the kinetic defect lives here) · Timbre richness 30 falling to 14 · Vintage↔Modern 45 · Vocal
  prominence 88 · Organic↔Synthetic 18 with one 100 spike at the end · Genre purity↔fusion 55 ·
  Narrative emphasis 82. Creativity spectrum: literal 30 / inventive 30 / **transformative 40**.
- **Hyper-Skeptic sign-off.** The Adorno seat accepts Facet 4(a) only in its strong form ("there is
  nothing to strike") and reserves the right to kill any candidate that softens it. The Albini seat
  accepts Facet 1 only if the subtraction is a real change in who is playing, not a mix move — recorded.
  The Willis seat objects that a rubric this severe produces a dignified record and notes that P06 is not
  one of the run's fun slots; **Ruling 5 is portfolio-level and does not bind this pair**, and her
  objection stands on the record.
