## THE ESSENCE (one paragraph, no new material)

The Scientist asked for a music daily aimed at another emotional masterpiece on the order of two named songs of ours. Reading those two for what they actually share, the request resolves to something narrower and more useful than "make something devastating": **make songs whose feeling is delivered by a formal operation the listener catches, rather than by a described emotion.** The day supplies a sky that is almost full and entirely calm, and one small wave carrying the only surviving evidence of a storm nobody here saw. The run's own correction, from this week's doctrine work, is that the two exemplars are narrated from outside and that this is precisely the failure mode already diagnosed — so the operation must happen to the singer.

## WHAT I DELIBERATELY DID NOT ADD

- **No genre.** Not one. That is step 04's job and naming it here would let the concepts be reverse-engineered from a sound.
- **No key, tempo, or vocal spec.**
- **No emotional target beyond what the request contains.** F-9 and F-10 are permissions, not targets — the run may be light, it is not required to be cheerful.

## SELF-CRITIQUE

The honest weakness of this essence: **F-1 and F-2 are in tension and the tension is not resolved here.** A form event strong enough to be caught is a form event strong enough to become the subject — which is the exact failure the seed's Law 1 names. Steps 02–05 have to hold both, and I do not yet know which concepts can. The panel already fought about this at length (the Adorno objection and Martin's answer) and the resolution — the deletion test — is an *enforcement*, not a solution. **A run that produces six songs which pass the deletion test but nobody feels has satisfied F-1 and failed F-2, and the text gates cannot tell the difference.** That is the run's real risk and it should be named at QA, not discovered there.
