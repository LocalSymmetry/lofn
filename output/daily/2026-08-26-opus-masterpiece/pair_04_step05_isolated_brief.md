## 1. YOUR SECTION ARCHITECTURE

**THREE PASSES, ONE PRONOUN DIFFERENT.** One block of text — verse + chorus + tag — sung three times, IDENTICAL except for a single pronoun each pass: third person, then second, then first. **Every pass is the same length and the same level.** Section order: Pass 1 (Verse/Chorus/Tag) / Pass 2 (same) / Pass 3 (same). Nothing else exists in the song.

## 2. YOUR RHYME / LINE LOGIC

**Refrain returns one word wrong.** End-rhyme kept and plain — the rhymes must be so ordinary that the ear stops attending to them, which is what allows a single changed pronoun to register. **No rhyme debt owed.** The repetition is the return; say nothing about it.

## 3. YOUR HOOK GRAMMAR

The chorus is a classification sentence — flat, clerical, the language of a form being filled in. It is **pleasant and reasonable every time**, including the third.

## 4. YOUR SONIC DEVICE

A second copy of the vocal at a fixed short delay, same level, same centre, present in all three passes and never varying.

## 5. YOUR INTRUDER PLACEMENT (assigned at step 05 — not your choice)

⭐ **PRESENT FROM BAR 1, UNMOVING** — the single electronic element never enters and never leaves. It is why the pronoun change is the only event in the song.

## 6. YOUR SEAM (N = 1)

The pass where the pronoun turns first person. **That is the only surprise this song is permitted** — everything else must be immovable so it lands.
