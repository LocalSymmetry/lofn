# Pair 04 Step 09 Artist Refined - Cache Leak Psalm

## Artist Refinement
This pass made the four variations less like interchangeable technology songs and more like one furious privacy chapel seen through four lenses. The vocal remains female and spatially staged. The chamber-punk percussion stays off-kilter. The viola is a physical alarm, not decorative string wash.

## Refined Choices
- V1 session ghost: kept the haunting frame but turned it into a consent exorcism, with the cache wall named plainly.
- V2 wrong account choir: selected. The hook is legible on first listen and carries the spiritual boundary failure without naming any person.
- V3 consumer room lock: tightened around consumer/work separation and gave the chorus a lock-litany structure.
- V4 terminal confession: made the system speak against itself, then forced a repair decree.

## Major Deviations
- Refused any direct reuse of the tainted wording family.
- Removed cross-pair scenery and all unrelated daily nouns from the lyric world.
- Avoided real names and victim framing; the anger is aimed at architecture and account boundaries.
- Moved all production metadata outside the lyrics field so the Suno cap stays clean.
