# P01V4 - dust-brown coronation

Dust-brown art-pop coronation with chamber-club bones at a grounded, swaying pulse opens in the first five seconds on a muffled impact boom below, a viola scrape like rusted silk at left, and a female alto whisper center-close as if reading a sacred log. Her doubles bloom behind the head, then a right-edge snarl cuts through whenever grandeur grows too large for the little machine. Acoustic viola, acoustic brushed drum, and skin-close claps remain audible; no acoustic guitar, no piano hymn, no symphonic triumph, and no festival-drop gloss enter the frame. The drums have a kinetic defect: the downbeat keeps kneeling late, turning impact into ceremony without smoothing it. The arrangement is bounce-rondo, each return shedding volume until Mars dust, airbag petals, and rover voice stand almost alone. Its bold sonic device, the Dust Petal Compressor, squeezes the mix after every hit then releases a dry halo around the vocal like fabric unfolding.
