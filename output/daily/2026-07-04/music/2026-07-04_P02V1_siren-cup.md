## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Domestic chamber-punk art-pop with cloistered table-liturgy pacing, mid-tempo but alert; the first five seconds are a dry bread-knife tap on wood, a buoy-bell throb far back, and one viola scrape leaning left before the groove enters crooked. A female mezzo stands center-front, close enough for breath, starting as a whisper at the lip of the cup and opening into a controlled snarl when the humans ask whether rest is allowed. Right side: unruly viola, salt-rasped harmonics, bowed table legs; left side: muted hand drum, cup rim clicks, crumb-dusted shaker. Center depth holds a warm pump-organ pad and mono low bell, acoustic strings and found domestic percussion declared present; no acoustic guitar, no EDM riser, no trap kit. The arc moves from kitchen hush to observatory chorus, then drops to bare voice for the permission refusal. Bold device: every hook is answered by a Siren-cup formant splash that bends like magenta enamel.
