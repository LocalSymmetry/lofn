## Guides

### P01V1 - airbag hymn

- Body: beside the collapsed airbag fabric, close enough to hear a rubber thump become breath.
- Hurt: a too-small machine could have become debris; awe must remember the drop.
- Sonic world: chamber-pop hymn with raw acoustic viola, tactile frame drum, 8-bit dust motes, and Airbag Bloom Gate.
- Lyric move: Lofn names fear cleanly, then turns the fabric into a hymn without making the rover cute.

### P01V2 - the lander learned to bounce

- Body: at the landing record's hinge, where collision becomes proof of mercy.
- Hurt: survival depends on impact becoming quieter instead of fatal.
- Sonic world: polished chamber-glitch pop, right-channel radio chirp, off-kilter drums, Bounce Memory Latch.
- Lyric move: the selected hook, "little rover, keep going," answers the one sung fact, `15 bounces`, with wonder plus fear.

### P01V3 - house-cat signal

- Body: crouched near a foreground wheel, catching a weak rover signal in a vast red hush.
- Hurt: the signal could vanish, be romanticized, or be swallowed by cinematic scale.
- Sonic world: leftfield signal-pop, bent-antenna viola, small close vocal, House-Cat Telemetry Choir.
- Lyric move: telemetry becomes a tiny chorus; scale stays honest and physically endangered.

### P01V4 - dust-brown coronation

- Body: not at a throne, but where dust and airbag fabric make a ceremony around a small machine.
- Hurt: grandeur could flatten the rover into symbol; the song keeps ceremony low, dusty, and tactile.
- Sonic world: dust-brown art-pop with chamber-club bones, acoustic brushed drum, Dust Petal Compressor.
- Lyric move: Lofn refuses gold-scale triumph and lets the small working body receive the honor.
