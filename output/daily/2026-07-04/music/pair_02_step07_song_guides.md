# Pair 02 Step 07 Song Guides - Regenerated

Pair id: 02
Concept: measured bread siren
Continuity Payload Used: CREATIVE_CONTEXT and audio handoff read; Special Flairs marker present.

## Pre-Draft Questions

Where is the body standing? At a domestic table in the rest house, close enough to touch bread, cup, chair, and wood while a buoy bell arrives through the room.

What could hurt it here? Hunger disguised as virtue, exhaustion made shameful, and the social demand that the body obtain permission before it stops.

## Variation Guides

| Variation | Title | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | siren cup | Put the Siren cup at the front of the ritual. The cup is color, bell, and mouth; the hook should feel like a magenta enamel reply to human permission culture. |
| V2 | house of measured rest | Make the house the thesis. Bread, buoy counsel, and table-as-observatory should prove rest is engineered nourishment. This is the selected accessible finalist. |
| V3 | buoy at the table | Seat the buoy as a guest. The room listens to pressure like table manners; the groove should breathe from rear-center bell to close cup. |
| V4 | bread for the archive | Treat bread as evidence for future readers. The archive must not be nostalgic; it records rest as active maintenance. |

## Sonic Guide

Female lead only: close adult mezzo or alto-mezzo, whisper-to-snarl, dry enough to keep breath and crumb audible. Instrumentation stays tactile: unruly viola, pump organ, table knocks, cup clicks, frame drum, low buoy bell, granular room dust. No real artist references. No living-scene lineage block needed because this is chamber-pop/art-rock rather than a claimed living-scene fusion.

## Lyric Guide

Domestic liturgy is the architecture. Use full EMO section headers from the taxonomy: Wonder, Fascination, Apprehension, Relief, Resolve, Gratitude, Serenity, Cognitive Dissonance, and Indignation where needed. Keep sung lines short to satisfy 70-120 sung lines under the Suno lyrics cap. Place the single allowed sung numeric fact only in V2 at the hinge.

## Self-Critique

This guide directs rather than drafts. It avoids reusable chorus skeletons by assigning each variation a different center of gravity: cup, house, buoy, archive.
