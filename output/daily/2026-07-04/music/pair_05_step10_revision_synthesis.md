## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Industrial chamber-pop at 136 BPM in C minor, tuned to 432Hz, tense and glossy; the first five seconds deliver a surgical kick in the center, left-panned tile clacks, right-panned viola harm scratches, and a female vocal beginning as a velvet whisper close to the mouth before turning into a controlled snarl. The groove has a kinetic defect: off-kilter drums lean late against a bass pulse that arrives half a breath early. Unruly viola, gated synth brass, clipped square-wave arps, and sub pressure staged deep behind the vocal create a prefusion lock instead of a disease tableau. The arrangement is a suite of locked movements: aperture, hinge, seal, refusal, release. Acoustic kit shell, viola wood, and breath are real; acoustic guitar and piano are absent. Bold device: the Lock-Bloom Guillotine, a bit-depth shear that cuts all harmony to a mute hinge before the chorus slams back polished and unpredictable.
