# P06 · STEP 09 — THE CRITIQUE, AND WHAT IT CHANGED

## ⚡ CRITIQUE 1 — THE ROCK TITAN *(after Dave Grohl · Concept Hyper-Skeptic, seat 6)*
> **"You have written 'wordless stacked-third harmony hook' in four prompts and you think that is a riff.
> It is a description of a riff. The renderer will hand you a competent pad with two vowels on it, your
> self-check will tick the box, and you will have proved my objection instead of answering it. And this one
> is worse than the usual case, because a pad here is not just boring — a pad here is a church."**

**RESOLUTION — accepted in full, and it is the pair's largest single change.**
The objection is the ICB's live unresolved charge and it does not get talked away. Three structural
answers, all of which survive into the render:

1. ⭐ **The syllable is a weapon.** `Da-da-da-da — dah` — **four hard plosive attacks.** A choir cannot
   happen on a hard D. A pad cannot happen on four staccato eighths. **The anti-choir defence is in the
   figure itself**, not in a note asking nicely.
2. **The exact notes are named in every one of the four prompts** — lower line E♭, F, G, A♭, back to G;
   upper line a diatonic third above; split to G–D. A renderer given five specific pitches and a rhythm
   produces a figure. A renderer given an adjective produces a texture.
3. **The fuzz bass doubles the lower voice an octave down.** A punk instrument is standing exactly where
   the organ would be. **You cannot get a hymn out of that stack** without first removing the bass.

**And the honest concession, filed rather than argued away:** *he is still right that a text gate cannot
prove this.* ⛔ **This is a render-audit item.** The pair's claim is falsifiable and must be falsified by
ear — `lofn-render-audit`, under THE BLIND RULE. **It is not claimed as passed here.**

## ⚡ CRITIQUE 2 — THE CONNECTION CYNIC *(after Bob Lefsetz · Marketing Hyper-Skeptic, seat 18)*
> **"Nobody wants a lecture from a dance record. You have picked the biggest news story in the packet and
> the temptation is going to be irresistible: somewhere in the last chorus, somebody is going to tell me
> what to think about it. And if you dodge that, you'll do the other thing — you'll go reverent, drop the
> drums, and give me two minutes of tasteful sadness. Both of those are you talking about yourself."**

**RESOLUTION — accepted, and it produced the pair's hardest gate.**
**F3, THE MORAL AUDIT**, applied line by line to all four finished lyrics. Three defences, all structural:

- ⭐ **The EMO set is the guard.** **Not one tag in this pair is Triumph. Not one is Sorrow.** The choruses
  are `EMO:Resolve` — a fact with a floor under it. The verses run Equanimity, Amusement, Composure,
  Vigilance, Solidarity, Dignity, Persistence. **There is no tag in the set that could produce a lament and
  none that could produce a cheer.**
- **The final chorus is byte-identical to the third and resolves nothing.** No fifth chorus, no tag, no
  fade, no key change. **The song stops; it does not conclude.**
- ⭐ **The refusal is stated once, as a joke, which is the only way Sunna is allowed to state a rule:**
  *"Nobody made a speech. Everybody went."*

**One line was cut under this heading.** V1's third verse originally ended *"Nobody waved. Everybody went."*
— **"nobody waved" is a mood, and the mood is sad.** It was the reverent version arriving exactly where he
said it would. Replaced with *"Nobody made a speech,"* which is the same rhythm, the same flatness, and a
joke instead of a sigh.

## ⚡ CRITIQUE 3 — THE SCREAM-AND-WHISPER *(after Phoebe Bridgers · seat 16)*
> **"Your plain lines are good and two of them are cheating. 'Bag's gone and the hook's still there' is a
> plain line doing an emotional job honestly. 'Didn't say the rest of it' is a plain line *pointing at* an
> emotion and asking me to supply it. That is not restraint. That is a blank you want me to fill in, and in
> this song I know exactly what you want me to fill it in with."**

**RESOLUTION — accepted, and this is the sharpest note in the set.** She caught the second-number leak
from a craft angle rather than a compliance one, which is why it matters.

⛔ **Two lines cut, both for the same defect** — a plain line whose blank has only one possible filling:
1. **V2:** *"Didn't say the rest of it."* → *"Went in. Washed a cup. Put it on the rack."*
2. **V3:** *"Nobody sits down till they've seen who."* → *"Gate goes and everybody stands up. / Somebody
   puts more water on."*

⭐ **The rule this generated, now binding on the pair and stated as craft rather than compliance:**
**EVIDENCE, NEVER INFERENCE.** The song may report what an object is doing; it may never report what that
means. *"Bag's gone and the hook's still there"* survives because a hook is a thing you can see. ⛔ **And
no line may report an absence of an expected arrival.** The gate is a thing that *goes*. Nobody waits.

## ⚡ CRITIQUE 4 — THE MINIMALIST ENGINEER *(after Steve Albini · Medium Hyper-Skeptic, seat 12)*
> **"Four songs, one chorus shape, one riff, one count. Where is the actual subtraction? Naming five

**RESOLUTION — one accepted, one rejected, and the rejection is on the record.**

**Accepted:** the removals are written **into the section headers of the lyrics field**, not into a

the renderer receives, not claims the pair makes.

**Rejected, with reasons:** V4's ending is not an addition. ⭐ **The last removal is the number itself.**
The outro drops the count, leaves the two voices alone, and the figure steps up one degree with nothing
under it. **V4's final eight bars contain less than any other point in the record.** It is the most
subtractive thing in the pair and it is the thing his own rule asked for.

## ⛔ THE SECOND-NUMBER AUDIT — swept line by line, all four finished lyrics

**Method:** every sung line in all four songs read against six patterns.

| pattern swept for | result |
|---|---|
| the figure itself, in digits or words, in any sung line | **absent** |
| any death / loss / harm word — *died, lost, gone (of a person), missing, never, buried, drowned* | **absent.** The only *"gone"* in the pair is **"Bag's gone,"** and it is a bag |
| **arithmetic** — any subtraction, any remainder, any *some / not all / the rest / how many of them* | **absent.** ⭐ **The count only ever climbs. There is no operation in this pair but addition** |
| a reported **absence of an expected arrival** — waiting, watching for a face, an unanswered call | **absent — two lines cut for this (critiques 3 and 6 of step 08 §G6)** |
| a second numeric fact of any kind — a time, an age, a quantity | **absent — four lines rewritten (step 08 §G5)** |
| an implication that the going was dangerous | **absent.** The nearest line is *"Text me when it's light,"* which is what anybody says to anybody travelling overnight |

⛔ **One further line was cut at the final mechanical sweep (step 10), and how it was cut matters.**
V3's *"Lost count. Started again."* was flagged by the word `lost`. Its referent is unambiguous — the
previous line is *"Counting them in off the top of the wall,"* so a man is miscounting headlights — and a
defence of it ran to a full paragraph. ⭐ **That paragraph was the tell.** The pair's own rule is that
**near-misses HOLD, they do not get argued**; arguing for a line under this constraint is the evidence
against it. Replaced with ***"Counted wrong. Started again."*** — same rhythm, same meaning, no ambiguous
token, and funnier, which is a requirement rather than a bonus.

**Verdict: the second number is absent from all four lyrics fields in every form, including by arithmetic.**
⭐ It lives where it was assigned: **the sub creeping a semitone**, under every bar, never arriving, never
resolving, ⛔ never named. **The thing the song refuses to say is the thing the song is about.**

## ⭐ THE DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK

### What this PRODUCES, predicted honestly
A 2:45 track that opens with **two dry women's voices, staccato and wordless, with nothing behind them** —
you know the tune before you know the song. A four-on-the-floor floor arrives at about six seconds with a
late kick and a fuzz bass locked under the lower voice. A flat, unbothered sing-speak verse of household
nouns. **A chorus at roughly 0:22** where a big number is stated twice and the room gets the wordless line
between the statements. Around 1:20 the floor collapses into a 165 breakbeat and **the two voices open from
a third to a fifth** — the one moment the harmony does something new. Three more choruses, each one thinner
than the last, the number one higher each time. The last chorus has a **bar of real silence** in it where a
riff line was. Then everything goes except the two voices, and the last note opens.
**Under all of it, a sub that has been climbing a semitone since bar one and never gets there.**

### ⭐ Name the one way this would render generic
**The stack renders as a choral/angelic pad** — sustained vowels, long tail, no consonant attack. Two ways
that is fatal here rather than merely wrong: it is the exact register Sunna forbids by name, **and over this
subject it turns the record into a hymn for the dead**, which is precisely and specifically what it must
never be. **The second way in is tonal:** the last chorus arrives with a mourning weight on it, or with a
triumphant one, and the song acquires the moral it exists to refuse.

### ⛔ SELF-REPAIR — taken ONCE, applied to all four packages
Both risks were already fought in the prompt. **The repair is that they are now fought in the exclude field
as well, in the specific vocabulary of the failure rather than the general vocabulary of taste:**

- **Anti-choir, added to all four excludes:** `choral pad, choral swell, angelic vocal, wordless angelic
  aahs, sacred texture, hymnal texture, church organ, cathedral reverb, long reverb tail on the voices,
  sustained vowel harmony, gospel stack, layered oohs` — plus `sustained pad in place of a riff, ambient
  wash as the hook, texture where a figure belongs`.
- **Anti-moral and anti-lament, added to all four excludes:** `lament, elegiac, mournful strings, requiem,
  dirge, funeral tone, candlelit ballad` on one side and `triumphant ending, victory fanfare, celebratory
  drop, anthemic uplift` on the other — plus `explanatory lyric, moralising tag, warning tone`.
- **One change inside the lyrics field:** the Pad channel is now **negative addressing only** —
  `no_choir_no_choral_swell_no_angelic_texture | dry_room_tone_only | cassette_tape_hiss_floor`.
  ⭐ **There is no pad on this record. There is hiss.** The channel that would have become a choir has been
  given a job that is not one.

**Self-repair used: 1 of 1.** No further repair pass is taken; residual risk is routed to the render audit.
