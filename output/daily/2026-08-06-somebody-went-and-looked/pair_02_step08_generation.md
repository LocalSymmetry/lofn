## RAW DRAFT — V1 · THE RUN

**Draft music prompt (raw):** *Punk at 128 in F sharp minor with a distorted bass riff and a woman running; the bass carries the hook and the guitar plays chords; the drums change tempo twice; a filter rises through the whole thing; female sing-speak, dry.*

⛔ **WRONG WITH IT:** no first five seconds, no spatial language, "the guitar plays chords" is an open invitation for the guitar to become the lead, the riff is asserted rather than **stated**, and there is no clause forcing the figure to exist before the vocal and after it. **The Rock Titan's whole objection lives in this draft**: *"hummable riff" written in a prompt, and a competent arpeggio comes back.*

**Draft verse one (raw):**
> *She goes out early when the roads are empty / Nobody knows and nobody's watching / She's happy in a way she can't explain / And the phone in her hand has other ideas*

⛔ **WRONG WITH IT:** four failures in four lines. **No hands** — "she goes out early" is a summary, not a person doing something. **"Happy in a way she can't explain"** is interpreting instead of continuing. **"Has other ideas"** is a wink; it explains the device before the device has happened. And **"nobody's watching"** brushes against naming the pursuer.

**The repair direction taken:** put a name, a shoe and a lace in line one; make the neighbour say the misheard line as a plain fond fact; delete every word that comments.

## RAW DRAFT — V2 · THE ALERT

**First draft opened on the alert itself.** Rejected on Law 5 before it was finished — **a verse one with no human hand in it is an automatic repair**, and this is precisely the failure the COMPRESS round named: *"you have written a robot record and called it delight."*

**The move that fixed it, and it is the best idea in this pair:** ⭐ **somebody wrote the sentence.** A person at a desk, late, on their ninth draft, trying to write six words that will be read by a stranger in the worst two seconds of their day. That person is **Nell**, she is invented, and she is not the butt of anything.

⛔ **The version of V2 that was refused outright:** a draft in which the alert was funny because the software was stupid.
**THE REFUSAL binds here:** *she will not be ironic about anything anyone loves.* Nell loves the sentence. She got it as right as it can be got. **It still cannot read joy, and that is the fact the song is built on** — not incompetence, not surveillance, not a villain.

## RAW DRAFT — V3 · THE PERSON WHO GOT THE ALERT

**This one arrived nearly whole**, because it is the only variation where the run's actual subject happens on stage: *somebody goes out of their way to look.* Tam is asleep with the curtains open; the phone lights the ceiling; he puts his shoes on and walks out into the cold without socks and finds her at the top of the hill with her hands on her knees, laughing.

**THE LOCKED FEELING lands here and only here:** *you didn't know you were braced until you stopped bracing.*

⛔ **WRONG WITH THE FIRST PASS:** the draft resolved too hard — a final line about how everything was fine and how good it is when people check on you. **Deleted.** The song ends on the chant and eight bars of bass, and the relief is in the arrangement, where it costs nothing.

## RAW DRAFT — V4 · TURNING IT OFF AND RUNNING ANYWAY

⭐ **The structural discovery of the pair, found in the raw draft and kept:** if she leaves the phone on the kitchen table, then the second appearance of the misheard line — *"She's got the phone and she's running"* — **is simply, flatly false**, and it is delivered to an empty room.

**Nothing in the lyric points at this.** The machine fields around it are adjusted to the truth it can see (*Handset still. Owner elsewhere.*) and then it files the report anyway. The listener does no work at all. **This is the device at its strongest and it is the last thing the suite does.**

⛔ **WRONG WITH THE FIRST PASS:** a triumphal ending — the last chorus getting bigger, a final shout, the filter opening. **Three separate violations**: gradation by addition, the pursuer resolving, and a mascot ending. Replaced with the mandated removal (drums out, voice and bass) and a filter still climbing when the record stops.
