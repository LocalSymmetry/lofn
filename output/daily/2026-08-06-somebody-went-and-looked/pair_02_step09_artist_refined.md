## OBJECTION 4 — **THE SCREAM-AND-WHISPER** *(seat 16, after Phoebe Bridgers)*
### *"Your chorus in V2 is the machine's own sentence. Is that subversive, or is that the song explaining itself?"*

> **THE SCREAM-AND-WHISPER:** A plain line survives on its **noun**. *"Are you being robbed right now"* has no noun in it at all — it is six words of syntax. And you have put it in the chorus, which means the room is singing the thesis. **That is the toll booth wearing a chorus.**

**RESOLUTION — REFUSED, and here is the argument rather than the retreat.**

The line is not the thesis; **it is the artefact.** A thesis-chorus would be *"a machine can only read your joy as a crime"* — a sentence *about* the situation, which is exactly what is banned. *"Are you being robbed right now"* is a **found object**: it is the thing itself, the six words on the grey card, and the room singing it is the room holding the object, not the argument.

On the noun: the noun is supplied by line three of the same chorus — ***"Tap the tick if you're not. Tap it now."*** **The tick is the noun.** A person who has ever dismissed a safety prompt at a bus stop knows exactly what the tick is, and knows it in their thumb.

> **THE SCREAM-AND-WHISPER:** *The tick earns it.* Withdrawn — **on the condition that nothing anywhere in the pair glosses the sentence.** Checked: no line in any of the four comments on the alert, the misreading, or the machine. **Held.**

# ⭐ DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK

## What these prompts will actually PRODUCE

A 128 BPM F♯-minor punk record with a heavily distorted, mid-forward bass carrying a repeated seven-note figure, a dry kit with a late kick, muted drop-tuned guitar stabs on the right, and a flat, close, sing-spoken female lead with the breaths left in. Roughly 2:30–2:50. The chorus is a shouted 7-syllable chant with a room on it. Somewhere near 1:25 the drums halve; near 1:45 they double into sixteenths. The last chorus is voice and bass. The record opens and closes on the bass alone.

**Where it will drift** (Grain Law — deviations are raw material, judge the result): the half-time hinge may render as a genuine tempo change rather than a feel change; the room bed may be quieter than specified; the "handclap early and staying early" in V3 will probably be quantised away. **All three are acceptable and none of them touch the pillar.**

## ⭐ Name the one way this would render generic

> **The renderer gives the melody to a guitar or a synth lead, and the bass reverts to a root-note bed.**

If that happens, the song is a competent pop-punk track with a shouty chorus, **the whistle test fails, the free channel is carrying nothing, and every ounce of meaning is back in the words** — which is the exact failure THE FREE CHANNEL exists to prevent, in the pair the ICB nominated as *"the run's most reliable whistle-test pass."* Failing here would be the worst single result available to this run.

## The repair, applied ONCE, across three layers

1. **PROMPT — the figure is stated as intervals, and the guitar is given a job that is not melody.** *"...and a drop-tuned guitar answers in short muted stabs mid-right and never takes the melody"* (V1) · *"...on the sour note"* (V2) · *"...and never a line"* (V3) · *"...that never lead"* (V4). Every prompt ends on the enforcement clause: **the bass plays alone before the first word and alone after the last.**
2. **EXCLUDE — the failure is blacklisted in fourteen concrete tokens**, in Suno's vocabulary rather than in English: `pad carrying the melody`, `synth pad as the main hook`, `ambient pad bed in place of the bassline`, `string pad hook`, `lead guitar melody`, `guitar solo`, `guitar carrying the tune`, `guitar doubling the bass melody`, `synth lead melody`, `keyboard lead line`, `root-note bass`, `static root-note bass bed`, `sub-only bass`, `buried bass`.
3. **ARRANGEMENT — the only lever that is not a request.** Three naked-bass windows per song (first five seconds, under the half-time drop, last eight bars) where **no other instrument exists.** A renderer cannot hand the tune to a guitar in a bar that has no guitar in it.

**Self-repair used: ONCE.** *(It also caught two contract breaches on the same pass — four music prompts at 1012–1040 chars, trimmed into the 870–960 target band, and four exclude prompts at 936–954, trimmed under the 900 ceiling.)*

That is a listening question, it belongs to `lofn-render-audit` under **THE BLIND RULE** (send the audio alone, never the prompt), and **no text gate in this pipeline can see this failure class.** Flagged, not claimed.
