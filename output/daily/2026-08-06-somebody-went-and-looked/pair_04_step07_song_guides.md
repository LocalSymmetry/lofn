# 07 — SONG GUIDES · PAIR 04

`2026-08-06-somebody-went-and-looked` · **SUNNA** · P04 · **the name repeated** · 108 BPM · D minor · ~5:00
**Six guides: one spine + four variations + one wildcard.** Every guide answers the two questions.

> ***Where is the body standing?*** — a report from one concrete place, not a metaphor about one.
> ***What could hurt it here?*** — the clean fear the song does not resolve. One per guide, never named in the lyric.

**No Lineage & Credit block is needed for this pair and none is written.** Nothing borrowed from a living scene.

---

## GUIDE 0 — THE SPINE *(binding on all four variations)*

**Where is the body standing?** On a concrete garage floor in November, in the eleven inches between a hull and a wall, with a work-light clamped to a keel and cold coming under the door. The body is horizontal more often than upright. It smells of two-part epoxy and cold metal. **Everything the song knows, it knows from that floor.**

**What could hurt it here?** Something under the floor. It has been getting closer for five minutes and it has never once arrived. It is not named, not mentioned, not resolved, and every listener knows exactly what it is and none of them will say.

**Form law (all four):** ~136 bars at 108 BPM ≈ 5:00. **One harmonic event: the four-note single-string figure, bar 1 to bar 136.** No chord change, no key change, no gear-change, no bridge that behaves like a bridge. Chorus lands by **0:24**. Full arrangement complete by **0:20** and never added to again. **Eight subtractive events**, roughly every 35 seconds: hi-hat → second guitar → snare → bass → kick → claps → room → everything. **Bar 95 (3:31) is the comfort line**; past it the words are not words. Last minute: figure, vowels, room.
**Rhythm law:** riff four notes per bar in 4/4; chant "Ocean Going" over **three beats**, twice, then a **two-beat hole** — a 3-3-2 across two bars, the run's anchor cell arriving as meter. The lead is **muted** for the hole; two room mics carry it. That is the room's part and it is real silence in the lead channel.
**Poetic devices (mandatory, both):** **epizeuxis** — the name immediately repeated past comfort — and **anaphora** — a verse stem repeated line-on-line (*"Says it… Says it… Says it…"*). Free channel, spend without limit. End rhyme sits in the situation's own vocabulary: *hull / dull, door / floor, hand / sand, cold / old, done / gone / alone, boat / float, going / nothing.*
**Rhyme schemes:** verses **ABCB** or **AABB** with hard monosyllabic landings; pre-chorus **unrhymed couplet** (the flat-test line must not be decorated); chorus **byte-identical, no scheme, no note filed.**
**Vocal:** female, late twenties, English, sing-speak, dry, flat, audibly amused, close dynamic mic, breaths in, no de-esser, doubles misaligned 15–35 ms and ±6 cents. ⛔ never sung-pretty, never a warm alto, never a choir.
**Hook technique (the one that ships):** the chant's decay is **seeded from chorus one** — the two-beat answer is already the word *going, going*. So when the whole name melts at 3:30 it is not a trick arriving, it is a thing that was always there finishing. Nobody notices the plant. Everybody feels the payoff.
**Narrative seed:** third person, present tense, one woman, one object, one room. The song never leaves the garage and never explains why it doesn't.

## GUIDE 1 — VARIATION 1 · **THE NAMING** *(facet F4 · the flat test)*

**Where is the body standing?** Flat on its back on the floor, under the turn of the bilge, sanding one square of rust with a thumb because the block won't reach. Then upright with a half-inch brush and a tin, painting four syllables on a stern in a room nobody is in.
**What could hurt it here?** The knock under the floor, arriving one hair closer while she does the lettering.

**Storytelling techniques.** *(i) The withheld reaction* — the naming is shown as a manual task (brush, tin, steady hand) and the feeling is never stated; the listener supplies it and therefore owns it. *(ii) Continuation, not interpretation* — every line after the naming continues the afternoon rather than commenting on it. She does the sum in her head about the door width and goes back to the sand. **The song never tells you this is brave.**
**Mood arc.** Absorption → Amusement → Dedication. Begins inside a task, gets funny at the door measurement, ends unembarrassed. No lift, no swell, no triumph.
**Instrumentation & texture.** Fuzz guitar figure alone for four bars; band lands at bar 5 with turbo-fuzz bass mid-forward and a dry close kit; second guitar plays only the two-beat answer and is the first thing sacrificed. No acoustic instruments; the only unamplified sources are two room mics and hands.
**Production.** Kick +12 ms late, bass locked on the grid. Room mics 8 dB under instinct, high-passed at 180 Hz, into a 1-second plate — the sweat plate, never a hall. Master −14 LUFS, ≥10 dB crest.
**Structure.** Intro 4 bars · V1 (6 lines) · pre (2) · chorus 0:24 · V2 · chorus (hi-hat gone) · V3 · chorus (2nd guitar gone) · hold · V4 · chorus (snare gone) · chorus (bass gone) · long chant to 3:29 · vowels · figure alone.
**Hook, three ways.** (1) The two-beat hole with the lead muted and the room 6 dB up. (2) The pre-chorus delivered on one note, spoken not sung, over the figure only. (3) The lettering line landing on the downbeat the riff refuses.
**Central aspect.** A name is an act of maintenance, not a claim.

## GUIDE 2 — VARIATION 2 · **THE NOBODY WATCHING** *(facet F3 · gradation)*

**Where is the body standing?** At the door at six in the morning, shoving it up on its runners, looking out at an empty road, then turning back in. Nine years of that specific turn.
**What could hurt it here?** The knock, which is the only other thing in the building.

**Storytelling techniques.** *(i) Negative inventory* — the verse lists what is **not** there (no car, no van, no one at the end of the road, no message) in the plain flat register of a stock-take, so absence becomes an object with weight instead of a complaint. *(ii) Ritual detail* — the same three actions in the same order every morning; repetition in the words prefiguring repetition in the form.
**Mood arc.** Solitude → Persistence → Dignity. ⛔ **Never self-pity. Never plaintive.** The nobody is a fact she is comfortable with, and the joke is that the song is more bothered about it than she is.
**Instrumentation & texture.** This is the variation where the mix has a **hole shaped like a crowd** for four minutes and only fills it in the last ninety seconds. Room mics start almost off (16 dB down) and are the one channel that gains — by removal around it, never by a fader move.
**Production.** Emptiest of the four: fewest elements at 0:20, so the subtractive schedule bites earlier and harder. Second guitar gone by 1:30, snare by 2:05.
**Structure.** As spine, with the removals moved 8 bars earlier and a longer vowel section.
**Hook, three ways.** (1) The chant sung into an obviously empty room for its first three passes. (2) Handclaps entering as the *only* thing that ever resembles company — and then leaving. (3) The last chorus with the lead alone and the room finally there.
**Emotional tone signal.** *You didn't know you were braced until you stopped bracing* — and in this one, she never stops. That is V3's job.

## GUIDE 3 — VARIATION 3 · **THE ONE PERSON WHO DID** *(facet F6 · the register, under maximum pressure)*

**Where is the body standing?** Still under the hull. A man from the end of the road is standing in the open door, and he asks how she bent the ribs. She talks for eleven minutes. **This is the run's whole seed at human scale: somebody went out of their way to look at a thing nobody was checking.**
**What could hurt it here?** Not the man. The knock, which does not care that he came.

**Storytelling techniques.** *(i) The specific question* — he doesn't say "wow"; he asks a **technical** question, which is the only proof of attention that cannot be faked. Sunna's own ethic: the detail is the evidence. *(ii) Somatic report, unlabelled* — *her shoulders came down an inch / she hadn't known they were up.* The locked feeling delivered as a body fact and never named as relief.
**Mood arc.** Absorption → Recognition → Amusement. ⛔ **THIS IS THE EARNESTNESS TRAP AND IT IS THE PAIR'S SHARPEST RISK.** The moment a stranger is kind, the temptation is a swell, a warm harmony, a key lift. **There is no key to lift to.** The relief must land while the arrangement is *losing* the snare. The gratitude is one plain line and then she goes back to sanding.
**Instrumentation & texture.** Identical to spine — deliberately. The event is in the lyric and in one removal, nowhere else. The second guitar leaves *during* the kind moment, so the room gets bigger exactly when a lesser record would get fuller.
**Production.** One deliberate choice: the room mics narrow slightly into the recognition instead of widening. Stereo narrowing into a climax is a documented technique in this house and it is the anti-swell.
**Structure.** As spine. The eleven-minute conversation is one line, not a section.
**Hook, three ways.** (1) *"That's a real question"* spoken flat over the figure, no drums. (2) The chant sung by two throats for the first time — hers and the room's — with no extra instrument. (3) The shoulders line placed on the two-beat hole, so the room hears it in silence.
**Key sequence.** Bars 62–67: the snare leaves on the exact bar the man asks. Nobody will be able to point at it.

## GUIDE 4 — VARIATION 4 · **THE NAME AFTER IT STOPS MEANING ANYTHING** *(facet F2 · the device, at full extension)*

**Where is the body standing?** In a kitchen doorway with a kettle going, saying the name for no reason at all, because it has become a noise she makes. Then back in the garage. The name has left the boat and become a tic.
**What could hurt it here?** The knock, closer than in any other variation, and still not arriving.

**Storytelling techniques.** *(i) Anaphora to exhaustion* — four consecutive lines beginning *Says it*, which enacts the collapse in the words before the arrangement enacts it in the sound. *(ii) Inventory reduced to three nouns* — *four notes and a name and a hull*, repeated with one term dropped each pass. **The lyric performs subtractive gradation.**
**Mood arc.** Amusement → Absorption → Absorption. There is no third act. That is the point and it is not a shortfall.
**Instrumentation & texture.** The comfort line moves **forward to ~2:10** — this is the variation where language goes early and the last half of the track is vowels, figure and room. Longest vowel section of the four.
**Production.** The chant's decay is written phonetically and rendered, not implied: *Ocean Going · O-shun go-ing · shun-go-ing · go-ing · oh-ing.* Doubles drift wider (up to 35 ms) as the words dissolve, so the smear does the eating that a room's resonance does on tape.
**Structure.** Front-loaded: three short verses, four choruses, then eleven bars of language and eighty of sound.
**Hook, three ways.** (1) The kettle line — a domestic, funny, completely unbothered delivery of a word that has lost its job. (2) The moment the consonants go and only OH–UH–OH–IH is left running the 3-3-2. (3) The very last bar: the figure alone, cut, no ring-out.
**Special effect.** ⛔ **No tape stop, no reverse, no filter sweep.** The decay is performed by a human mouth getting lazier, which is free, and which no plugin can fake.

## GUIDE 5 — WILDCARD · **THE ROOM EATS THE LANGUAGE** *(cross-disciplinary)*

**Where is the body standing?** Inside a resonance. The cross-domain reference is a 1969 tape work in which one spoken sentence is re-recorded into a room until the room's own frequencies eat the words and only pitch is left. **We are doing that with a chant, in a punk band, at 108 BPM, with a bass problem.** It is never explained, never winked at, and no listener needs to know it exists.

**What could hurt it here?** That the audience reads repetition as **malfunction**. This is the pair's second declared risk and the wildcard exists to solve it. The solution is not variety, it is **evidence of intent**: the removals are metronomic (every ~35 s), the decay is directional (consonants first, then vowels, always the same direction), and the room's answer never stops arriving. **A loop drifts; this walks.** Three cues that say *someone is doing this on purpose* — regular loss, one-way decay, and a crowd that keeps its cue.

**Techniques borrowed sideways.** From printmaking: the same plate re-inked with progressively less ink, so the ninth pull is a ghost of the first and the plate never changed. From architecture: a corridor lit by fewer bulbs the further you walk, so distance is measured in darkness rather than metres. Both are **subtractive gradation** and both are legible to someone who has never thought about either.
**Instrumentation & texture.** The room becomes the loudest thing on the record by the end and no fader ever moves toward it; everything else simply leaves.
**Structure & dynamics.** Loud-quiet-loud is refused at the section scale and applied at the **element** scale — the whole record is one long quiet, and the loud is your memory of bar 20.
**Narrative & thematic suggestion.** *People kept making things anyway.* Under occupation, in plague years, with bad tools and no audience and no reason. Not one of them stopped. **That is the whole thesis and it is never once said in the lyric.**
