# P06 · STEP 08 — GENERATION

## WHAT THIS STEP PRODUCED
Four complete drafts, written **after** the riff (step 06 §0) and against the step-07 guides. This file is
the record of the first pass: the decisions taken while writing, the lines that were generated and killed
**during** generation rather than at critique, and the two structural inventions that only appeared once the
lyrics were on the page.

---

## G1 · THE CHORUS WAS BUILT BEFORE ANY VERSE

Because the device is the chorus. Draft skeleton, arrived at in one pass and never revised:

```
Seventy-five thousand went.
Da-da-da-da — dah
<constant line>
Da-da-da-da — dah
Seventy-five thousand went.
Da-da-da-da — dah
```

**Three things were decided here and they set everything downstream.**

**(a) The number is sung twice per chorus, not once.** Once reads as information. Twice reads as a hook.
Seven syllables — *se-ven-ty-five thou-sand went* — is inside the 4–8 chantable band, and every syllable is
open enough to shout while out of breath.

**(b) The climb is 75 → 76 → 77 → 78 thousand, and the top of the climb is the verified fact.** The song
arrives at **78,000** in the loudest position it has. ⭐ Nothing is invented upward: the count stops where
the report stops. *(This is also why the fourth chorus repeats byte-identically instead of adding a fifth —
there is nowhere honest left to go, and Sunna ends one song early anyway.)*

**(c) The room gets the wordless line; the singer gets the number.** This fell out of the writing rather
than being planned. The room's part **never changes for the entire record**, so it is learnable in one pass
and ownable by the second chorus. The only thing that ever changes is the one thing the room cannot
anticipate. ⭐ **That is the device and the crowd-liberation engine turning out to be the same object.**

## G2 · THE COUNT LINE FAILED THE FLAT TEST TWICE BEFORE IT PASSED

| draft | verdict |
|---|---|
| *"Seventy-five thousand of them."* | **Dies.** "Of them" is a pronoun where a verb should be. It reports a quantity of people and says nothing about what they did |
| *"Seventy-five thousand on the road."* | **Dies, and worse — it is dangerous.** It puts the bodies on the road, which is where the pursuer lives. The song's whole architecture is that it stays in the room |
| ⭐ *"Seventy-five thousand went."* | **Survives.** Four words. The verb is the smallest and the most important one available, and it is a verb of **decision**. *Went* is what a person does; *were* is what a statistic is |

**This is the pair's thesis compressed into one word choice.** Lofn's version of this line would have had a
different verb. Sunna's is *went*.

## G3 · THE FOUR VERSE-ONE BODIES (Law 5), GENERATED FIRST-PASS

Each is one invented person doing one ordinary physical thing, in a room, in the hour around going.
⛔ None is named. ⛔ None is placed. ⛔ None is described — **no face appears anywhere in this pair.**

- **V1** — *she filled a bottle at a tap, turned the cap till it sat straight, laced the left boot and then
  the right, put the good torch in somebody's hand.* ⭐ The torch is the one that matters: **she gives the
  useful thing away before she goes.** Nothing is made of it.
- **V2** — *I rolled it, I never fold. Charger down the side where you'll find it. Sat on the lid till the
  zip went round. Good socks on the top.* Then: **"Wrote nothing on the tag."**
- **V3** — *he moved the crate to make the room, rolled the mat out with his foot, boiled the water again
  because, held the phone at the window for the bars.*
- **V4** — *she turned the radio up with a wet hand.* Four words in and the listener knows she was at the
  sink. **Ruthless specificity: vague delight is decoration, specific delight is evidence.**

## G4 · TWO INVENTIONS THAT ONLY APPEARED DURING WRITING

### ⭐ INVENTION 1 — the last removal is the number itself (V4)
The gradation plan (step 06) had four removals: hats, guitar, bass, drums. Writing V4's outro produced a
fifth, and it is better than the other four: **the count leaves.** The outro drops the number entirely,
leaves the two voices alone, and **the figure steps up one degree and stays there.**

The number stops at the verified fact and **the riff keeps climbing after the song stops saying it.**
"Still climbing" is rendered in the arrangement instead of asserted in a lyric — which is the entire
doctrine, executed in the last eight bars. ⛔ And it is subtractive: the song ends with **less** in it than
at any earlier point.

### ⭐ INVENTION 2 — "Nobody made a speech." (V1)
Generated as a throwaway at the end of V1's third verse and immediately promoted. It is the **only** line in
the pair that acknowledges the existence of an opinion, and it does so by reporting that nobody had one.
It is dry, it is funny, and it is Sunna's eye-roll aimed at the song she is refusing to write.
**A rule stated as a joke is the only way she is allowed to state a rule.**

## G5 · KILLED DURING GENERATION — the numeric sweep

⛔ **One numeric fact only.** The count is the licensed exception; nothing else may be a number. This cost
four real lines and each replacement is better:

| killed | why | replacement |
|---|---|---|
| *"Four in the morning and the bag's by the door."* | a second number | ***"Bag by the door since dark."*** — and *dark* fills the WHEN slot with no numeral at all |
| *"Boiled the water twice because."* | a second number | ***"Boiled the water again because."*** — *again* is funnier and truer to a kettle |
| *"Somebody's charging six phones off one."* | two numbers in six words | ***"Somebody's charging everybody's phones."*** |
| *"Kettle's on for the third time."* | a second number | ***"Kettle's on again. Kettle's always on."*** |

**Note what happened:** the constraint improved every line it touched. The numbers were doing work that
nouns and adverbs do better. Filed as a finding, not a complaint.

## G6 · KILLED DURING GENERATION — the second-number sweep

⛔ Nothing may count, name, number, imply by arithmetic, or allude to the report's other figure.
Two drafted lines were near-misses and **near-misses HOLD, they do not get argued** (`HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD`
§3.0, the HOLD-FOR-HUMAN backstop applied by the pair to itself):

1. **V2, cut:** *"Didn't say the rest of it."* — a gesture at something unsaid, sitting in a song whose
   entire architecture is that one specific thing is unsaid. **It points at the trapdoor.** Replaced with
   *"Went in. Washed a cup. Put it on the rack."*
2. **V3, cut:** *"Nobody sits down till they've seen who."* — reports an **absence of an expected arrival**,
   which is the second number arriving by implication. Replaced with *"Gate goes and everybody stands up. /
   Somebody puts more water on."*

**The rule this generated, now binding on the pair:** ⛔ **no line may report an absence of an expected
arrival.** The gate is a thing that *goes*. Nobody waits for anybody. Nobody looks for a face.

## G7 · THE PRE-CHORUSES — one per variation, all built on the same verb

The device's verb is *count*, so each variation's pre-chorus ends on it. Not a shared chorus — a shared
**verb**, which is return rather than a second structural idea.

- **V1** — *"Same road. Same gate. Same night. / Count them again. Count them right."*
- **V2** — *"Bag's done. Bag's by the wall. / Count them again. Count them all."*
- **V3** — *"Lights on the road, lights on the hill. / Count them again. Count them still."*
- **V4** — *"Chalk in a hand, tea in a cup. / Count them again. Count them up."*

⭐ **V4's is the one that means something extra:** *count them up* is the direction of travel, said as an
idiom, with no explanation attached. Nobody has to notice.

## G8 · FIRST-PASS PROMPT DIFFERENTIATION — the first five seconds

All four prompts name the same five notes, the same two voices, the same split to a fifth. **What differs is
what is underneath the stack in the first two seconds**, and it was chosen from the variation, not for
variety's sake:

| | first 5 seconds |
|---|---|
| **V1** | the two voices **alone, dead dry, nothing behind them.** The floor arrives after. *The hour before* |
| **V2** | the two voices with **one fuzz-bass note under them from bar one.** The sound of somebody sitting on a lid |
| **V3** | the two voices **doubled by a room mic 8 dB under instinct**, into the short sweat plate. The room is the subject, so the room is in the hook |
| **V4** | the two voices **already mid-figure**, no count-in, no downbeat — the record joins something in progress. *Still climbing*, in the first two bars |
