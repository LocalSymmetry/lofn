## RAW V1 — *the naming*

**Raw defects:** (a) **993 chars, 33 over the 960 target** — the interval clause and the closing clause are both carrying redundant words; (b) *"so the pair breathes"* is a producer's note, not a render instruction; (c) the removal schedule is asserted but not enumerated, which is exactly how a renderer reads five minutes of one riff as permission to escalate.

**Flat-test line:** *"It doesn't fit through the door / She named it anyway."*
**Raw defect:** V1's verse 3 risks letting the neighbours be the butt of the joke. **THE REFUSAL applies to them too.** Flagged.

## RAW V2 — *the nobody watching*

**Music prompt (raw, 946 chars):** flat, patient, unbothered; the tape already running, guitar mid-phrase with no findable downbeat, a garage door rolling up underneath; same riff, same only-harmonic-event clause; kick +14 ms; **room mics 16 dB down and almost empty for four minutes**; the knock; only losses.
**Raw defects:** (a) *"a hole in it shaped like a crowd"* is good writing and may read to a renderer as an instruction to add a crowd — needs the exclude field to hold the line; (b) nothing in the raw prompt says the room arrives late, so the one dynamic event of this variation is currently invisible.

**Lyric skeleton:** door up at six / negative inventory (*no van, no car, no one out*) / **anaphora block: four consecutive lines opening "Nine years and…"** / radio, kettle, talks to the boat like a dog / lamp out, door down, name said to a cold floor.
**Flat-test line:** *"Nobody watched for nine years / She said it out loud anyway."*
**Raw defect:** the negative inventory drifts toward self-pity in draft. **Sunna does not complain.** Must read as a stock-take she is comfortable with, with the song more bothered than she is.

## RAW V3 — *the one person who did*

**Music prompt (raw, 928 chars):** warm-hearted and completely dry, *refuses to swell*; four seconds of dead air, a thumb scraping a wound string, then the figure; same only-harmonic-event clause; kick +10 ms; **room mics narrow rather than widen at the peak**; the knock; the removal list enumerated in full.
**Raw defects:** (a) *"warm-hearted"* as the lead adjective is a live earnestness risk — the whole variation dies if the renderer hears permission to be sincere in the sound rather than in the content; (b) *duet* must be excluded explicitly, because a second character in the lyric invites a second voice, which would be a **second device** and a straight axis failure.

**Lyric skeleton:** under the turn of the bilge / man from the end of the road stands in the doorway, doesn't smile / **"That's a real question, that is" spoken flat** / shoulders down an inch, she hadn't known they were up / talked eleven minutes / he goes, she goes back under the hull.
**Flat-test line:** *"He asked how the ribs were bent / She'd have told him anyway."*
**Raw defect:** the relief is currently the loudest thing in the song, which is backwards. It must land **while the arrangement is losing the snare.**

## RAW V4 — *the name after it stops meaning anything*

**Music prompt (raw, 927 chars):** deadpan hypnotic, *funny for two minutes and then not made of words at all*; guitar opens mid-figure so no downbeat is findable for eight bars, kettle rising behind it; same only-harmonic-event clause; kick +12 ms; **doubles drifting wider as the words come apart**; the knock; half the song is four vowels, one figure and a room.
**Raw defects:** (a) *"half the song is four vowels"* is the single most likely clause to be ignored by a renderer, because it is the least conventional thing in the packet — it needs reinforcing in the lyric field itself, phonetically, not just described; (b) "hypnotic" is one adjective away from "ambient," which would kill the punk.

**Lyric skeleton:** **anaphora block ×4 opening "Says it…"** (before she starts / when nothing is done / the way you'd cough / to nobody) / kettle, dog, the middle of nothing, again in the morning / **the subtractive line: "Four notes and a name and a hull" reduced term by term to "Four notes"** / two vowel sections, the second longer than any verse.
**Flat-test line:** *"The name doesn't mean it now / She says it anyway."*
**Raw defect:** with the words gone by 2:10 there is a real hole where a listener's reason to stay should be. Answered in 09.
