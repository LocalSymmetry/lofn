### QA REPAIR PASS — R1 and R2 (judge-side finding, structural, step 07)

The adversarial judge ruled P01 the run's one **deleted** difficulty rather than a relocated one, and all three Somatic Gate seats independently isolated this pair. Both causes are repaired below. **This is the receipt: what the music now carries that the lyric used to.**
