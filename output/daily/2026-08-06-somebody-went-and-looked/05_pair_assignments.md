## P01 — **ACCESSIBLE · NEWS** · *call and response*
**THE PERSON (Law 5):** a woman on a night shift who aimed a camera at something nobody asked her to look at, and sent the picture to someone who had stopped expecting one.
**THE SITUATION:** you thought nobody was checking. Somebody was checking.
**Opening shot only:** an orbiter turned and photographed a hole in the Moon that nobody had seen. **One line, verse one, then leave it.** ⛔ Do not return to space.
**RIFF (write it first):** **two-note horn stab**, minor third, brass or brass-synth, on the and-of-two. It opens the record alone and closes it alone.
**DEVICE — call and response:** every chant line is answered by the room. **Leave the hole where their voice goes.** The answer is 2–4 syllables and never changes.
**Gradation:** chorus 2 loses the hi-hat · chorus 3 loses the bass for two bars · chorus 4 loses the horn and **the room answers into the gap.**
**Variations:** V1 the one who looked · V2 the one who'd stopped expecting · V3 the picture arriving at 4am · V4 the next night, looking again.

## P02 — **ACCESSIBLE · NEWS** · *the misheard line*
**THE PERSON:** a woman running, and the phone in her hand deciding she is being robbed.
**THE SITUATION:** the machine can only read your joy as a crime. *(F2, verified: a phone detects going for a run as "someone snatching my phone and running off.")*
**RIFF:** ⭐ **the bassline IS the tune** — a fuzz bass carrying the melody, mid-forward, the guitar reduced to stabs. This is the run's most reliable whistle-test pass; do not bury it.
**DEVICE — the misheard line:** ONE line appears twice, unchanged, and the second time it is **the machine's reading of it.** No wink, no explanation. *(The device is the whole idea. Do not add a second one.)*
**Gradation:** each chorus removes one element until the last is **voice + bass only.**
**Variations:** V1 the run · V2 the alert · V3 the person who got the alert · V4 turning it off and running anyway.

## P03 — **ACCESSIBLE · EXISTENCE** · *the list that loves*
**THE PERSON:** somebody who wrote two thousand words about a fastener because they love it. *(F3, verified: "The Simple Elegance of the Integrated Timing Belt Loopback Fastener.")*
**THE SITUATION:** loving a boring thing in public, in detail, with nobody asking. ⭐ **This is Sunna's own personality as a song — "you love things in detail, and the detail is the proof you were paying attention."**
**RIFF:** **blown-out guitar riff**, drop-D, four notes, sloppy, doubled an octave up in the last chorus.
**DEVICE — the list that loves:** anaphora. One item per line, same opening stem, each item **absurdly specific and completely sincere.** ⛔ **THE REFUSAL BINDS HARDEST HERE: not one line may be ironic about the thing being loved.** If a listener could hear a smirk, it is a repair.
**Gradation:** the list gets *shorter* each time, not longer — items removed, never added.
**Variations:** V1 the fastener · V2 the person who reads it · V3 the comment that says "who cares" · V4 the list continuing anyway.

## P04 — ⭐ **AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE · THE LONG ONE** · *the name repeated*
**THE PERSON:** somebody making a thing with no reason to and no audience for it — and still naming it, out loud, every time. *(F4: 572 points for a channel called "Crime Pays but Botany Doesn't." The title is the thesis.)*
**THE SITUATION:** doing the thing that does not pay, and refusing to be embarrassed about it.
⭐ **THIS IS THE ONE LONG SONG.** ~5:00. **ONE riff is the only harmonic event for the entire track** — no chord change, no gear-change, no key change. Everything moves around it. *(Fela's seat: if the riff cannot survive being the only harmonic event, it is not a good enough riff.)*
**RIFF:** **one string, never resolving** — a single-string figure, four notes, that never lands on the tonic. Present from bar 1 to the last bar.
**DEVICE — the name repeated:** ⭐ **the name of the thing is repeated past the point of comfort, until it stops being a word and becomes sound.** *(Lucier's seat: one plain sentence repeated until the room eats the language and only the music is left. Somewhere past 3:30 the listener must stop hearing the words.)*
**Gradation:** subtractive, relentless — by the last minute almost nothing is left but the riff, the name, and the room.
**Variations:** V1 the naming · V2 the nobody watching · V3 the one person who did · V4 the name after it stops meaning anything.

## P05 — ⭐ **AMBITIOUS · EXISTENCE** · *two voices arguing* · **PROMOTED FROM A FOOTNOTE**
**THE PERSON:** two people in a comment thread, and one of them is being told the thing that broke them was easy.
**THE SITUATION:** *(F5, verified: **"Almost no skill required to cook a steak" — 143 points, 180 comments.** More comments than points: an argument.)* ⭐ **A hundred and eighty people arguing about whether an easy thing is easy.** The Marketing skeptic pulled this out of a footnote and called it the cruellest sentence in the packet. **He was right: "it's not that hard" is what every person has been told about the thing that nearly finished them.**
**RIFF:** **guitar-and-bass trade** — guitar plays two bars, bass answers two bars, *the argument is in the riff before anyone sings.* ⭐ The device and the riff are the same gesture; this is the run's cleanest proof of the doctrine.
**DEVICE — two voices arguing:** hard-panned, one each side, sing-speak, overlapping. ⛔ **Neither wins and neither is the villain.** *(THE REFUSAL: the "it's easy" voice must be sincere, not a butt. Sunna will not write a joke that requires someone to lose.)*
**Gradation:** the dub section removes everything but the two voices; they keep arguing with no music under them, then the band slams back.
**Variations:** V1 the thread · V2 the person who said it was easy, meaning it kindly · V3 the person for whom it wasn't · V4 nobody replies and the thread just stops.

## P06 — **AMBITIOUS · NEWS** · *the count that climbs*
**THE PERSON:** somebody in a lab, at the end of a long day, writing "16" on a whiteboard. *(F6, verified: AI used to design brand new viruses; **16 successful.**)*
**THE SITUATION:** you made something that had never existed, and you go home for your tea. ⛔ **Not a warning song, not a cheer.** Sunna's line is that people keep making things — **and the wolf is behind her the whole time and is never mentioned.**
**RIFF:** **stacked-third harmony hook** — two voices a third apart, wordless, the room's part. ⭐ **The harmony IS the riff.** One moment they split to a fifth and the gap opens.
**DEVICE — the count that climbs:** a number goes up by one each chorus and nothing else in the chorus changes. **The number is the only new information in the song.**
**Gradation:** as the number climbs, an element leaves each time.
**Variations:** V1 the sixteenth · V2 the first one · V3 going home · V4 the number the next morning.
