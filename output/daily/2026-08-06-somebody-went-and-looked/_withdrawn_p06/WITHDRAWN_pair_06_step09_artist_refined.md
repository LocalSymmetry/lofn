## OBJECTION 1 — ⚡ **THE ROCK TITAN** *(seat 6, Concept Hyper-Skeptic)*
> **"You have written 'wordless stacked-third harmony hook' in a document, and I am telling you what will
> come back: a pad. Two women on an open vowel a third apart, held, with a tail on it — that is a choir,
> and you will tick your own box because the document says 'riff'. A riff is a thing a person plays with
> their hands that another person can play back. Yours has no hands in it at all."**

**RESOLUTION — accepted in full, three changes, all made at step 06 and one made here.**
1. **The syllable is `Da-`, not `Ah-`.** A plosive onset is un-choral by construction. This is the single
   most load-bearing decision in the pair and it was taken *before* the words existed.
2. **The figure ends.** Five notes, two bars, a held final and then nothing. **A pad cannot stop.** The
   stop is the falsification test: if the render sustains through the bar line, F1 has failed and it is a
   REPAIR, not a note.
3. ⭐ **New at this step — the hands are in the mix.** The fuzz bass **doubles the lower voice an octave
   down**, so the figure is also being *played*, and any listener who cannot sing it can still hear it
   fingered. This was not in the step-08 draft. It is the Titan's objection answered with an instrument
   rather than a paragraph.

> **THE MIX CLARITY** *(seat 11):* And it fixes my headroom note for free — the bass doubling means the
> two voices never have to be pushed to be audible, so chorus one is not already at the ceiling and the
> removals have somewhere to be heard.

## OBJECTION 4 — **THE BATTLE-AXE** *(seat 2)* — ⭐ **the objection that guards the moral**
> **"Sunna's problem isn't simplicity, it's politeness. Where's the line that's a bit rude? Sixteen new
> living things and your best line is about a pen. Give me one line with a bit of blood in it."**

**RESOLUTION — REFUSED, and this is the most important refusal in the pair.**

A rude line here would be a **verdict in costume.** Every candidate the seat is asking for —
*"nobody's asking if we should"*, *"somebody's going to have to explain this"*, *"we're all very clever
here"* — is a moral wearing a sneer, and two of them were drafted and killed at step 08. ⛔ **If any line
editorialises about whether this is good or bad, it is a REPAIR**, and rudeness is the most persuasive
route to exactly that failure, because it *feels* like edge rather than commentary.

**The rudeness is relocated, not deleted** *(the Binding Condition: relocate the difficulty, keep the
receipt)*:
- **into the fuzz** — a bass that is a bit ugly does the same job and costs nothing *(the Battle-Axe's own
  concession in the baseline round)*;
- **into the anticlimax** — *"Sixteen. Then I got my coat."* is genuinely rude. It refuses the occasion the
  entire listener is braced for. **That is the blood.**
- **into the split** — the harmony opening to a bare fifth in the last second is the record being
  impolite with an interval instead of a sentence.

> **THE BATTLE-AXE:** *Fine.* The coat line is ruder than anything I'd have written. Taken.

## 7 · ⭐ THE MORAL AUDIT — line by line, all four variations

**Method.** Every sung line was read alone, out of context, and asked three questions:
**(a)** does it say the thing is good? **(b)** does it say the thing is bad? **(c)** does it tell the
listener how to feel, ask a rhetorical question, or imply a verdict by omission?

| variation | lines audited | (a) celebrates | (b) warns | (c) implies a verdict | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 · the sixteenth | 34 sung, non-riff | 0 | 0 | 0 | **CLEAN** |
| V2 · the first one | 34 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **CLEAN** |
| V3 · going home | 34 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **CLEAN** |
| V4 · the next morning | 34 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **CLEAN** |

**The four lines that were re-examined hardest, and why they survived:**
1. **"Loads of board left."** *(V2)* — a fact about a whiteboard. It does not say more is coming and it
   does not say more would be bad. The listener's dread is the listener's. **Kept.** *(Its predecessor,
   "Room for a lot more," was killed at step 08 for carrying an arc.)*
2. **"Somewhere a light I never left on."** *(V3)* — reports a light. No adjective, no consequence, no
   return to it later in the song. **Kept.**
3. **"Board's got no room left at the top."** *(V4)* — geometry. **Kept.**
4. **"Nobody said. Nobody's done."** *(V1)* — the temptation was to make this mean *nobody is
   accountable.* As written it means **nobody left a note about the milk.** **Kept, and the surrounding
   two lines are about the fridge specifically so it cannot be read the other way.**

**Also confirmed absent:** any word from the banned list *(should · shouldn't · danger · careful · wrong ·
right · proud · amazing · history · future · God · pray · world · mankind · miracle · monster · warning)* —
**zero occurrences in all four.** ⛔ No line in the outro of any variation is new; every outro is the riff.
**A song about science acquires its moral in the last chorus. Ours cannot, because the last chorus is the
third chorus with one digit changed.**
