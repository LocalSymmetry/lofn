Unembarrassed delight, at speed, with the room. **She read the same archive Lofn did and noticed people kept making things anyway** — under occupation, in plague years, with bad tools and no audience. She finds that unbearably funny and completely sufficient and does not think it needs redeeming.

## THE LOCKED FEELING — **THE RELIEF OF BEING LOOKED AT ON PURPOSE**
Not gratitude, not sentiment. **The specific physical drop in the chest when you find out somebody went out of their way to check on a thing you thought nobody was checking.**
Its signature: *you didn't know you were braced until you stopped bracing.*

## THE WORLD, IN ONE MOVE
Yesterday four tonnes of garbage nobody was steering hit the far side of the Moon and **nobody had even seen the hole.** Today **South Korea's orbiter turned and took the picture. Before, and after.**
**Somebody went and looked.**
⛔ **But the orbiter is the OPENING SHOT, not the spine** *(Lefsetz's seat, conceded)*. The subject is **somebody going out of their way to look at something for you**, and it happens at human scale every single day.

## ⭐ THE FIVE LAWS
1. **THE WHISTLE TEST.** *Could someone whistle it walking away from the venue, having heard it twice, drunk?* **The riff is written FIRST, before a single word** — named by instrument and interval, inside one octave, **present before the first line and after the last.** ⛔ A pad is not a riff. A filter sweep is not a riff. **If the vocal is the only tune, the song has failed.**
2. **THE FLAT TEST.** Say the line out loud, flatly, no music, to a person's face. Survives = **simple**. Dies = merely **short**. **A plain line survives on its NOUN, not its syntax** — *"a machine that thinks your running is a robbery"* is plain and unforgettable because of the specific misreading.
3. **ONE DEVICE. NOT SIX.** Nameable in four words. **One thing heard four times beats four things heard once.**
4. **GRADATION IS SUBTRACTIVE.** Every return of the chant **removes** something — the hi-hat gone in chorus three, the bass out for two bars in chorus four. The listener registers it instantly and cannot name it. ⛔ **Never add. The mix must never run out of room.**
5. **EVERY SONG NAMES A PERSON DOING SOMETHING** — in the lyric, first verse, not the concept note. **The machine is the occasion; the person is the subject.** A first verse with no human hand in it is a repair.

## DEPTH, AND WHERE IT GOES
**Depth lives in the SITUATION, never in the sentence.** Name a position the listener has stood in, then keep the lines plain and let them **continue the story instead of interpreting it.**
**The deep reference stays subversive** — bright surface, sharp edge, never explained, never winked at. **A listener who takes only the surface must receive a whole song.**
**RETURN IS FREE — spend more of it.** Byte-identical choruses need no defence and no note.

## THE CRAFT SPECS
- **Chorus by 0:25.** · **Leave a hole where the room's voice goes** — the chant is designed to be sung *back*, 4–8 syllables, no interval a normal throat can't reach at volume while out of breath.
- **Loud-quiet-loud** is the whole arrangement doctrine and it is free.
- **Harmony is a required instrument, not a garnish** — stacked thirds, a counter-melody that argues with the lead, one moment where two voices split and the room hears the gap open. *(Flair 15, Anarchy Harmonizers.)*
- **One pursuer per song**, rising, never resolving, ⛔ **never named in the lyric.**
- Kick nudged **+8 to +14 ms late**; bass locked on the grid. Doubles misaligned **15–35 ms**, ±6 cents. **No de-esser on the breaths.** Master **−14 LUFS, ≥10 dB crest.**
- ⛔ **No supersaw wall, no white-noise riser into every drop, no airhorn, no generic trap hats.**
