### RAW V2 · *the person who reads it*
**Verse 1:** small hours, thumb on the glass, arrived here from somewhere else, meant to skim it, **sat up straighter on the second page**, tea going cold and never drunk.
**Verse 2:** never met him, never will, doesn't own the machine it goes in, couldn't tell you what a timing belt does — **knows exactly where the notch goes now.**
**Bridge (the turn):** nearly didn't pass it on. *Who do you send a bolt to?* Sends it to one person. Writes: **you have to see the notch.** Doesn't say why.
**Chorus (raw):** "Two thousand words and I read them all / …and I never scrolled / Somebody loved it. So do I."

### RAW V3 · *the comment that says "who cares"*
**Verse 1:** bus home, coat still wet, two lines in and he's already out, types two words with one thumb, gets off at his stop and forgets.
**⚠️ Raw defect — THE REFUSAL BREACHED.** The raw draft had the line *"Two words. Both spelled right, at least."* **That is a smirk and it makes the commenter the butt.** Cut without replacement in 09; the line that stands in its place is **"He wasn't being cruel. He was tired."**
**Verse 2:** the writer reads it standing up, reads it again sitting down, **something goes out of him for an hour**, puts the kettle on, comes back, finds his place.
**Bridge (the turn):** he could take it down. He could put a joke at the top, *the kind that says I know, I know.* He reads it back. **It isn't funny. He doesn't want it to be.**
**Verse 3:** somebody else read all of it. **Somebody went and looked.** Left him a line about the chamfer. **He didn't reply to the other. He just added to the list.**
**Chorus (raw):** "Two thousand words. Two words back. / Somebody typed it and meant it. / The list goes on."

### RAW V4 · *the list continuing anyway*
**Verse 1:** Tuesday, nobody reading, cursor blinking, lamp humming, checks the number and doesn't say it out loud, opens the doc, **types: and then the chamfer.**
**Verse 2:** part two is the washer, part three is the thread, **nobody has asked for part three, part three is going up on Friday**, he's already shooting part four.
**Bridge (the turn):** some nights it's a daft thing to be doing and some nights he knows exactly what he's doing. Doesn't stop. Doesn't say why. Same table, same chair.
**Verse 3:** one day somebody goes and looks, reads the lot, writes to ask about the washer — **he's had that answer ready for a year. He is so glad. He doesn't say.**
**⚠️ Raw defect — GRADATION VIOLATED.** The raw draft's final list block added a **new** item ("Look at the knurl") because it was a good line arriving late. **Gradation is subtractive; nothing is ever added, however good.** Cut in 09; block 4 is a strict subset of block 1.
