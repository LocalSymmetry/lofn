## THE CRITIC PASS (what step 08 got wrong, named before it is fixed)

**CRITIC:** Four raw directions and three of them describe a **room** rather than an **event**. The
Documentarian's seat is standing right here: *you are writing a specification for sweat you will not
produce.* Where, precisely, does the flash have a consequence?

**LOFN-PRIME:** In the next line. Every time. V1's headlights are followed by *"Something outside just
settled the moment"* — the arrangement changed state and the singer says so. That is the Low End's
amendment served in the lyric, not in the spec, which is the only place it survives.

**CRITIC:** And the corona?

**LOFN-PRIME:** Countable in all four. Every stanza in this pair breaks mid-line. **After the flash,
exactly one runs to its end**, and the singer says *that's the first that I finished*. Nobody can
quantise a line count away. The mix supports it; the lyric carries it.

**CRITIC:** Fisher's seat, then, because it is aimed at you specifically. You have a 1993 bed.

**LOFN-PRIME:** I have a **2026** bed with a 1969 four-bar figure walking through it, which is the
inversion the house rule already demands — the modern condition is the world, the historical element is
the intrusion, and a bed named *dominant* smooths to pastiche because the generator's strongest prior
for "1993 jungle" **is a pastiche of 1993 jungle**. And the condition is in the writing, not the spec:
nobody in 1993 stood in a stairwell doing the arithmetic on an audience that has not been born yet.
**That sentence cannot be transposed backward.** It is the whole seam.

**CRITIC:** Last one, and it is the one that would actually kill this pair. An indignation song about
being permanently observed, with a hook — that is a **trap with a good hook**.

**LOFN-PRIME:** Which is why the **most repeated sentence in every one of these four songs is the
subject giving herself permission to stop**, in first person, inside the chorus. Not in the outro where
a listener could miss it. In the part that comes back three times. *A room is only trustworthy because
leaving is easy* — so leaving is in the hook.

## REFINEMENT APPLIED, PER VARIATION (flair only; no new scene content)

### V1 — `THE CAMERA THAT ISN'T ON`
**Injected:** the ornament as a **glitch-Baroque** object rather than a texture — bitcrushed
harpsichord, meticulous micro-edit, wrong beat, never corrected. Vocal staged **off-centre** rather
than centred, so the slap-back is an answer instead of a halo.
**Sharpened:** the flash was a mix event; it is now an **external light**. A car decides the moment.
That is not a synonym for a riser — a riser is the song congratulating itself on arriving, and this is
weather arriving without permission.
**Cut:** the word *stage* from the style field. She is not on one.

### V2 — `SIX YEARS FROM NOW, SOMEONE WATCHES THIS`
**Injected:** **restraint as aggression** — gorgeously restrained beats for two thirds, so the last
minute costs something. The double misaligned by tens of milliseconds and pitched slightly off: the
smear *is* the arrangement, and in a concrete stairwell the room does the smearing for free.
**Sharpened:** the epistrophe on *still* was decoration in the raw; it is now the song's clock.
**Cut:** any number. The angle is named *six years* and **the lyric never says it** — a number would
tell the listener when the fear ends, and the hinge is that it does not. Sung-fact allocation: none.

### V3 — `THE STAIRWELL ACOUSTIC`
**Injected:** the room promoted from setting to **instrument** — the tail finishes a phrase she
abandons, which is the only place in the pair where an interruption is completed by something other
than her.
**Sharpened:** `D9` made structural rather than stated. She names the flattery as architectural and
**still does not get good**, and the song never implies she will.
**Cut:** the drummer's name — because **no break is named here**, so no figure is claimed and no credit
is owed. *Raised ≠ forbidden runs in both directions; a false credit makes the true ones worthless.*

### V4 — `THE THING THAT WAS ALREADY INSIDE THE LOOP`
**Injected:** the full **quantum bit-depth swell** — one beat to two-bit at the moment of maximum
certainty, then back. Used once. An effect used twice is a texture and this is an event.
**Sharpened:** the ghost figure now opens the record **alone and nearly inaudible for three seconds**
before the break buries it, so its return at the corona is a *revelation* and not an entrance. That is
the Room's ruling served literally.
**Cut:** a second historical intruder. The guitar explosions belong to the **2026 world**, not to a
borrowed era. **N=1: one dominant material, one intruder, one seam.**
