### `pair_01` — **THE SECOND YES** · SUNNA · ACCESSIBLE · AWE · EXISTENCE
**Bed:** contemporary ska-punk / two-tone (WORLD) with a **rocksteady half-time** intrusion (ONE seam, at
the moment she joins). Horns. **Aggression: none — the skank is physically incompatible with sounding angry.**
**Section architecture:** answered couplets, 4-line stanzas; **every stanza's last line is answered**, and
the answer is the return. **Rhyme:** AABB, the B supplied by the answering half.
**Hook grammar:** 4–8 syllables, no interval a normal throat can't reach **while out of breath**; stressed
syllables land on beats the body already has.
**Sonic device (ONE, four words):** *"the count becomes the hook."*
**D8′ discharge — SHE IS THE SECOND.** Every line passes: *can the room shout this back at her without it
becoming false?*
**Corona:** the room's own noise — the chatter that was under the music from bar one, revealed when the horns
cut. **Countable in the lyric:** the count continues with no music under it.
**Pursuer (never named):** the room emptying. **Sung fact:** the shadow's route — *Greenland, Iceland,
Portugal, northern Spain* — a thing that visited a list of places and left.
**Technique harvested from the cut ledger:** ⭐ **INAUDIBLE WRONGNESS** (from C12) — write at least one
passage so that being off does not show: narrow range, stepwise motion, or a unison to hide inside.
**Friction:** the horns enter wrong the first time and stay wrong. **Exit:** the door is named and close.
**⭐ SUNNA GATES: THE FLAT TEST · THE HUM TEST · ONE DEVICE.** **Lineage:** ska / two-tone / rocksteady.
