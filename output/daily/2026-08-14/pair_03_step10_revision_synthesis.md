# LINEAGE & CREDIT

**The scene.** These four are contemporary bass-forward electronic records, and the reason that music can
exist at all is **Jamaican dub and sound-system practice** — the discovery that the mixing desk is an
instrument and that **space is the material**: subtraction, echo and dropout as composition rather than as
effects. That is a **living lineage**, not a museum, and it goes upstream to **King Tubby (Osbourne
Ruddock)**, **Lee "Scratch" Perry**, **Errol Thompson**, **Scientist (Hopeton Brown)** and **Mad Professor
(Neil Fraser)**, and sideways into the sound systems and dub-adjacent producers working now in Kingston,
London, Bristol and Berlin. **Go and buy their records.** Fusion with lineage, never capture.

⛔⛔ **THE PROMPT NAMES A DRUM FIGURE, SO IT CREDITS THE PLAYER.** V1's style field names **one drop** —
that figure is **Carlton Barrett**'s, of the Wailers, and it is the reason half of this bed has a shape.
V3's style field names **steppers** — that pulse is **Sly Dunbar**'s. Neither man is a genre; both are
hands. *(Swept in the STYLE field with word boundaries, not in EXCLUDE — raised is not forbidden.)*

⛔ **Honesty clause, both ways:** we asked for these figures **by name**, so we name them. **Nothing here
contains a sample of anyone's recording** — a generated track is not a sample, and a false credit makes
the true ones worthless.

⛔ **D4 in the paratext:** every person above is credited as lineage. **No song here is about any of them.**

**On the telemetry:** the bass-music lineages are mid-crossover this year. That is a reason to
**credit harder**, never a market opening.
