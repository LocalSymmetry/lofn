## THE ARGUMENT (the one that changes the run)

**LOFN:** All eleven of these are about a person who wants to and doesn't. Eleven variations on a brake.

**SUNNA:** No. Four of them are about a person who **does**, and you keep reading the ones that do as though
they were about the hesitation before. **C06 is four hours of doing it.** C04 is a whole night of being
somewhere on purpose.

**LOFN:** …Wait. That is a real correction and I want to state what it costs me. **I have been reading
"nervous delight" with the stress on nervous.**

**SUNNA:** Everybody does. It is the disappointed reading. The essence is a **half-second with a want in
it**, and you have been writing the brake and calling the want a complication.

⭐⭐ **THE RULING (coordinator, and it binds every pair): THE STRESS FALLS ON DELIGHT.**
The half-second contains a correct risk assessment **and an overriding want**, and **the want is the louder
of the two.** A pair that renders the essence as *hesitation, with delight as a hoped-for outcome* has
inverted it. The nervousness is the **weather**; the wanting is the **subject**.
*(This is the Pantheon's admission argument doing real work: "you look at what a thing isn't." It changed
the reading of the essence for all six pairs, three steps before anybody wrote a line.)*
