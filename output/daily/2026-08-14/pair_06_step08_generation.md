## SHARED SPINE (the pair's bones — inherited, not re-decided)

- **Bed = THE WORLD:** Jersey / Baltimore club, ~140, five-count kick, bed-squeak snare, triplet bounce.
- **ONE INTRUSION:** a bitcrushed harpsichord ornament, entering **at the wrong catalogue entry, early.**
- **Architecture:** THE CATALOGUE WITH ONE WRONG ENTRY. Each stanza adds an entry; the misfit is the rupture.
- **Rhyme:** vowel ladder inside the line, **epistrophe at the line end, no terminal rhyme.**
- **D8′:** apostrophe to the naming system. Every `you` is the instrument.
- **Corona:** carrier hum from bar one, bare after the flash; countable as the second field read aloud.
- **Flash:** ≤2 bars, one chest hit, the word alone.
- **Pursuer:** a thing under the floor. Never named. The machine has no word for it.
- **Sung fact:** *two hundred and twenty-five*, once, at the hinge, responded to.
- **Friction:** the list keeps going after the climax. **Exit:** the name is refused by an act.

## V1 — RAW STYLE PROMPT *(draft)*

> Cold indignation at 140. Jersey club with Baltimore bones — five-count kick, bed-squeak snare, triplet
> bounce, low-slung sub kept mono. First five seconds: a printer bites paper behind a hatch and a single
> mains hum sits under it, too quiet to be a decision. Female late-twenties contralto, dry, clipped,
> deadpan, no vibrato, consonants bitten, breath left in. Vocal sits under the kick. Gorgeously restrained
> beats through the verses. Natural entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes, the odd messy recording.
> One bitcrushed harpsichord ornament enters a beat early on the fourth catalogue entry and nowhere else.
> Everything drops for two bars to one chest-level sub hit and one word. Then no drums: the hum alone,
> bare. The list resumes flat and outlasts the feeling. No acoustic drums anywhere; the single acoustic
> source is a real room hum recorded on a phone. No airhorn, no supersaw, no trap hats.

**Raw self-note:** opener is compliant (leads on emotion, not a banned opener). Needs tightening to the
850–1000 band and an explicit spatial clause. Source-2 present: *"gorgeously restrained beats"* +
*"natural entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes, the odd messy recording."*

## V3 — RAW STYLE PROMPT *(draft)*

> Precise contempt for a procedure, at 140. Baltimore club kick under Jersey bounce, bed-squeak snare,
> card-on-card percussion from a swatch fan snapping open, sub mono and low. Opens with a colour chip going
> under a scanner lamp and a hum already running. Female late-twenties contralto, clipped, unimpressed,
> no vibrato, consonants bitten, audible breath. Rambunctious, unwieldy percussion at the edges of an
> otherwise locked grid. Glitchy and frenetic bitcrushed harpsichord that arrives earlier on each pass —
> half a beat, then a beat, then a bar — until it is playing before the entry it belongs to. Two bars
> hollowed to one sub hit and a word. Hum bare after. Fully synthetic bed; one acoustic room hum, badly
> captured. No airhorn, no trap hats, no supersaw.

**Raw self-note:** Source-2 present: *"rambunctious, unwieldy"* + *"glitchy and frenetic."* ⭐ V3's
signature is that the *kinetic defect worsens monotonically* — the strongest render-survivable idea in the
pair, because a generator can hear "earlier each time" as an instruction about placement.

### V3 lyric skeleton
`Intro` (the chip, the lamp) → `Catalogue 1` (entries at a fixed two-beat interval) → `Chorus` →
`Catalogue 2` (ornament half a beat early) → `Verse` (the tin, the lid, the word on the lid) →
`Catalogue 3` (ornament a full beat early; the epistrophe breaks on the wrong entry) → `Flash` →
`Corona` → `Hinge` → `Turn` → `Chorus` → `Catalogue 4` (friction; ornament does **not** come, and the
absence is the loudest thing) → `Exit` (the lid turned face-down) → `Outro`.

## RAW-STEP CRITIQUE *(carried into step 09)*

1. **All four openers currently front-load a temperature word.** That is correct against the Suno guide
   (emotion first) and compliant with the banned-opener list, but three of the four use a near-identical
   grammar. Step 09 differentiates the first six words of each.
2. **The vocalist spec is nearly identical across four prompts.** Deliberate — it is one singer, one pair —
   but the *trait pair* must differ per variation so the render does not collapse to one take.
3. ⚠️ **`ornament` contains `amen` as a substring.** Flagged now, resolved in the step-10 credit block: a
   naive break sweep will fire on this pair's own sonic device. It is **not** a break reference and must
   **not** be credited as one. A false credit makes the true ones worthless.
4. **The Jersey/Baltimore kick and the bed-squeak snare ARE break-derived and DO trigger a credit.** Handled
   in step 10's Lineage & Credit block, in the paratext, never in the style field (no real-artist names in
   the style field).
5. **Risk carried forward:** the harpsichord could still read as garnish. Step 09 must ensure every prompt
   states its *singularity* and its *earliness* in the same clause, and that the lyric names its wrongness.
