## THE OBJECT (real, fetched today — F6)

A colour API is handed today's date as a hex value, `#0814FF`. It returns RGB (8, 20, 255), HSL 237°, 100%,
52% — a saturated electric blue. **It names that blue "Canary."** In the same response object it also ships
`exact_match_name: false`, `distance: 225`, `closest_named_hex: #000FF9`.

⭐ **The label is read. The error bar is not.** That is a grade, a diagnosis, a score, a rating, a first
impression. It is the machine form of being named wrong, publicly, by something that **had the uncertainty
available and printed it in a field nobody opens.**

⛔⛔ **D11 IS THE KNIFE-EDGE AND EVERY FACET BELOW IS SHAPED BY IT.** The machine is **confident, not
stupid.** A machine made the fool is a joke with a butt, and the butt-joke moves the feeling off the person
holding the label and onto a punchline. The instrument's competence is the *source* of the fear, so the
song must show the instrument being **good at its job** and then wrong at exactly one entry — the one that
is a person. *(Sunna asked for this concept in the artist step and was refused it; her note stands, and it

## SELF-CRITIQUE

The honest weakness is **F1 versus F2 pulling opposite ways.** A flat, precise, bitten delivery is one
gesture away from *arch* — and arch is the tonal address of a joke with a butt. The mitigation is
structural rather than performative: **the machine gets the long catalogue and gets it right**, at length,
before anything goes wrong. If the instrument has earned four stanzas of accuracy, the flat voice reads as
*cold*, not as *smug*. V2 makes that mitigation the entire architecture, deliberately, as the pair's
insurance policy.

Second weakness, named now: **F5 has to carry the return floors with no terminal rhyme.** That is a real
debt and the ICB says removal is a debt. It is paid in three named instruments — epistrophe on the
catalogue's line-final word, a byte-identical chorus repeated whole, and the half-phrase that returns
finished. Measured in code at step 10, reported honestly, not eyeballed.
