# 9. THE DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK *(one pass, one repair, as capped)*

**What these prompts would actually PRODUCE.** First five seconds: a mechanical bite of paper or plastic,
close and unmusical, panned off-centre, with a single low sustained tone under it that a listener will not
consciously register. Then a five-count Jersey kick at 140 with a bed-squeak snare and swung triplet hats,

the list resumes and does not stop when the feeling does.

**⭐ "Name the one way this would render generic."**

**Risk (a) — it renders as a novelty song about a silly computer.** The generator's strongest prior for
"machine names a colour wrong" is comedy. **Repair, taken once:** the anti-novelty block now leads every
EXCLUDE field rather than sitting mid-list; `Derision` is banned from every EMO header; and the D11
firewall was moved out of the notes and **into the songs' own arguments** — the turn credits the machine in
all four, and V2's chorus is a chant of its accuracy.

**Risk (b) — the Glitch-Baroque ornament reads as decoration.** The generator's prior for "harpsichord"
is *tasteful*, and a tasteful harpsichord over a club beat is a novelty fusion, which is risk (a) wearing a
wig. **Repair, taken once:** every style prompt now states the ornament's **singularity and its earliness
in one clause** (*"arrives a beat early on the fourth catalogue entry and never again"*); *no tasteful
harpsichord solo* / *no harpsichord recital* / *no clean baroque pastiche* are in the EXCLUDE fields; and —
the only repair that survives a renderer — **the lyric names the wrong arrival**: *"And the gold thing
comes in wrong."* V3 goes further and makes the misalignment **monotonic**, which is a placement
instruction a generator can execute.

**Repair budget spent: one pass. No second loop, no verifier subagent, no new tier.**

**What would still be generic and I am not repairing:** the vocal could come back **pretty**. Every prompt
says dry, clipped, deadpan, no vibrato, bitten consonants, under the kick — and the generator's prior for a
female vocal on a club track is *sung*. If the render comes back sung-pretty, that is a **render-audit
finding**, not a prompt defect, and it is the second thing to check by ear.
