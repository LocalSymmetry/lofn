# THE SIX SLOTS

## ⭐ P01 — ACCESSIBLE · SUNNA · AWE · EXISTENCE
### bed: **SKA / TWO-TONE / ROCKSTEADY + HORNS**

**Why this bed, and it is load-bearing:** `sunna.yaml` names ska as *"her single most under-used register and
the fastest fix — the skank is bounce, it is played hard and fast, and it is physically incompatible with
sounding angry,"* and **horns are the cheapest anti-anger instrument that exists.** The Continuum seat added
the reason it belongs on *this* slot: ⭐ **two-tone is the only lineage in the palette whose founding gesture
was explicitly about who is allowed in the room** — it means the thing historically, not decoratively.

**Slot angle:** ⭐ **THE SECOND YES.** The song is aimed at **the person who joins second** — never the one
who starts. The second converts a lunatic into a group; the first is just a lunatic until then. The singer
**is** that second person, two seconds in, visibly counting, not good at it yet.
**Setting:** ONE specific sweepable room with an address and a bad PA — a hall, a function room, a
weeknight. **This is FLOOR SLOT 1 of the maximum 2 permitted by D13.**

**D8′ discharge — SHE IS THE SECOND:** she addresses the room **from inside it, as a fellow novice who has
been doing this for four seconds.** Every line must pass: *can the room shout this back at her without it
becoming false?*
**Sung fact (non-numeric):** the eclipse shadow's route — *Greenland, Iceland, Portugal, northern Spain* — a
thing that visited a list of places and left, responded to, never recited.
**Flairs foregrounded:** THE SECOND YES · INAUDIBLE WRONGNESS · THE CROWD ANSWERS.
**Friction (mandatory, per the Platform veto):** the horns enter **wrong** the first time and are allowed to
stay wrong.
**Exit (mandatory):** the door is named, it is close, and nobody minds.
**Lineage & Credit:** two-tone / ska / rocksteady — a living and a historical scene. **Named, pointed
upstream, credited in the release block.**
**⭐ SUNNA GATES (hers only): THE FLAT TEST** *(say the line flatly to a face — if it dies it was merely
short)* **and THE HUM TEST** *(the figure is recognisable with the vocal muted, present before the first
line and after the last)*. **ONE DEVICE, nameable in four words.**

**Variation angles — derived from THIS slot's concept, used nowhere else:**
`V1 THE COUNT` (she is counting out loud and the count becomes the hook) · `V2 THE WRONG ENTRANCE` (she comes
in a bar early and stays) · `V3 THE FRIEND'S ELBOW` (presence without contact — nobody is touched) ·
`V4 THE LAST SONG OF THE NIGHT` (the room emptying is the pursuer, arriving).

## ⭐ P02 — ACCESSIBLE · LOFN-PRIME · AWE · EXISTENCE
### bed: **MODERN POWER-POP — with a sixties girl-group handclap as the INTRUSION**

⛔ **THE INVERSION IS MANDATORY AND IT IS THIS SLOT'S ENTIRE RISK.** *(2026-08-13, hers: "TAKE A HUNDRED…
feels dated"; and Fisher's veto today.)* **The modern genre is the WORLD; the historical element is the
INTRUSION; one seam.** A bed named as *"sixties girl group"* **smooths to pastiche**, because THE GRAIN LAW
says the generator resolves to its strongest prior and its strongest prior for that phrase is a pastiche of
it. **Handclaps, tambourine and a stacked "ooh" arrive as a visitor inside a 2026 record — never as the room.**

**Slot angle:** ⭐ **THE ENTHUSIASM WITH NO COOL DEFENCE.** Something loved sincerely and indefensibly, kept,
not confessed, not justified, not redeemed. **D9 is binding here:** the bad recording of herself stays bad.
**The keeping is the act, not the improving.**
**Setting:** NOT a floor. A room with a device in it.

**D8′ discharge — OVERHEARING:** nobody is addressed. We hear one side of something and are not implicated.
**Sung fact:** ⛔ **NONE.** This slot's hinge is an object, not a number.
**Corona:** ⭐ **a second voice under the first — herself, younger, on the tape**, audible faintly from the
first chorus, covered by the flash, bare afterwards. **Countable in the lyric:** a half-phrase she has been
starting since verse one and only finishes after the flash.
**Flairs foregrounded:** THE COVERED FIGURE · THE COUNTABLE RETURN · SPECIFIC DELIGHT.
**Friction:** the returning refrain comes back **one word wrong**, every time, and is never corrected.
**Exit:** she can switch it off at any point and says so.
**Lineage & Credit:** the sixties girl-group stack → ⛔ **credit THE SINGERS**, e.g. Darlene Love, who sang
lead on records released under other groups' names. **Never credit an abuser as lineage.**
**⛔ LOFN IS NOT GATED ON THE FLAT TEST OR THE HUM TEST.** Her puzzle is the **second listen**, not a toll.

**Variation angles:** `V1 THE TAPE STILL PLAYS` · `V2 WHAT IT COST TO KEEP` · `V3 THE ONE PERSON WHO KNOWS` ·
`V4 THE VERSION WHERE SHE THROWS IT OUT` (and does not).

## ⭐⭐ P03 — ACCESSIBLE · SUNNA · AWE · NEWS — **THE EDGE PAIR**
### bed: **DUB** — bass and drums forward, spring echo, everything else a ghost

**This slot exists because the REFLECT configuration won two arguments**, and both are binding:
> ⭐ **"Let one of these six songs be entirely at the edge, and let it be a GOOD NIGHT."** *(after Cage)*
> ⭐ **"At least one must work perfectly for ONE PERSON, ALONE, NOT DANCING."** *(after Glenn Gould)*

**Slot angle:** ⭐ **A GOOD NIGHT AT THE PERIMETER.** Not a waiting room, not a before-picture, not a person
being warmed up for a chorus in which they finally join. **They do not join. It is a good night.**
⛔ **THE HARD PROHIBITION ON THIS SLOT:** the person at the edge is **not** the subject of anyone's concern.
A single line of pity, coaxing, or "you'll get there" is an **immediate REPAIR** — it re-installs the exact
premise the panel repaired. *Stillness is not the failure state; the interruption is.*
**Setting:** the perimeter of a room. **FLOOR SLOT 2 of 2. No further pair may be set on a floor.**

**D8′ discharge — SOLITUDE:** there is no second person to address. The ban discharges by there being nobody
to break it with.
**Sung fact:** ⭐ **"eight seconds"** — the dominant swell period at buoy 46059 *(one point six metres at
eight seconds; the ocean running a very slow four-to-the-floor)*. **Spelled in words. Responded to, not
recited.** The wave arrives whether or not anybody is dancing, and it has been arriving all night.
**Corona:** **silence with structure in it** — the dropout is the reveal. Dub is the one bed where subtraction
IS the composition. *(after Lee "Scratch" Perry: "space is the material; you take the big thing out and the
small thing was already playing.")*
**Flairs foregrounded:** THE PERIMETER · THE VISIBLE EXIT · THE HANDED HOOK.
**Friction:** a bar of genuine silence that a playlist will hate.
**Exit:** ⭐ the exit is the **subject** here, not a concession — leaving is easy, and that is why the room
is trustworthy at all.
**Lineage & Credit:** dub — a living lineage. Named, pointed upstream.
**⭐ SUNNA GATES: FLAT TEST + HUM TEST. ONE DEVICE.**

**Variation angles:** `V1 THE COAT ON THE CHAIR` · `V2 WHAT YOU CAN HEAR FROM HERE` (the mix from the edge is
a different, better mix) · `V3 THE EIGHT-SECOND WAVE` · `V4 THE WALK HOME, STILL GOOD`.

## ⭐ P04 — AMBITIOUS · LOFN-PRIME · **INDIGNATION** · NEWS

and never at a generation *(D3, hard: the research is about them; the song is not)*.
⭐ **Fisher's veto lands here hardest:** a 1993 bed under a 2026 condition **only works if the condition is
audible in the writing.** A clean jungle pastiche is a museum.
**Setting:** NOT a floor. A stairwell, a car park, the top deck of a bus — somewhere with a camera in it.

**D8′ discharge — DISPLACEMENT:** the address is aimed at an **absent third party** — whoever might watch
this later — never at the listener. *(2026-08-12: the only pair that discharged D8 by displacement was the
only pair at zero residue. This is the mechanism with the best measured record.)*
**Sung fact:** ⛔ **NONE.** The hinge is *permanence*, and a number would soften it.
**Corona:** **a rhythm that was inside the loop all along** — the break's own internal figure, revealed by
subtraction. ⭐ **The reveal is that the watching was never external.**
**Rhyme:** ⛔ stripped — **debt declared and paid** (hard-consonant spine + anaphoric openings + the
interrupted-stanza shape). **This is the only pair permitted to strip it.**
**Friction:** a stanza that never finishes.
**Exit:** ⛔ **HARD REQUIREMENT ON THIS SLOT.** An indignation song about permanent observation is the one

**⛔⛔ LINEAGE & CREDIT — THE CREDIT THE SYSTEM FORGETS.** If any of this slot's four style prompts names a

**Sweep the STYLE field, not EXCLUDE — raised ≠ forbidden.** **Word boundaries** (`ornament` contains `amen`

**Variation angles:** `V1 THE CAMERA THAT ISN'T ON` · `V2 SIX YEARS FROM NOW, SOMEONE WATCHES THIS` ·

## ⭐ P05 — AMBITIOUS · SUNNA · AWE · EXISTENCE
### bed: **SPEED GARAGE / BASSLINE**, built on **THE GEAR-CHANGE** — one song that is three songs

**Why:** speed garage is the measured revival of the year *(≈625% growth, now inside Beatport's top ten)*,
and `sunna.yaml`'s **GEAR-CHANGE** pillar — *style shifts INSIDE a single song, punk into bounce into dub and
back, with rolling drum transitions* — is the AMBITIOUS half of her register. **This is her ambitious slot,
deliberately** (personality is not a proxy for arm).

**Slot angle:** ⭐ **THE FOUR HOURS THAT PRODUCED NOTHING.** *(Existence prompt 4.)* A thing done alone that
would look stupid filmed, done for four hours, with nothing to show. **Would you give the hours back?**
⛔ **She does not answer with a moral, and the song does not congratulate her** *(Adorno's veto lands here —
the "it wasn't wasted after all" ending is exactly the self-congratulation that gets killed at step 11)*.
Sunna's actual position: *people kept making things anyway, and she finds that unbearably funny and
completely sufficient, and does not think it needs to be redeemed by anything.*
**Setting:** NOT a floor. A bedroom, a garage, a practice room at an odd hour.

**D8′ discharge — SELF-ADDRESS:** she talks to herself, out loud, and the room happens to overhear. *(Which
is also the only honest posture for a song about being alone.)*
**Sung fact:** ⭐ **"four hours"** — spelled, once, at the hinge.
**Corona:** ⭐ **a countermelody she has been singing the whole time without noticing** — not another person,
not the machine: **her own second line.** The reveal is *self-multiplicity*, not company. **Countable in the
lyric:** the chorus's own under-line, sung plainly once the top line stops.
**Hand-off:** ⭐ **the singer stops and the room continues** — the purest form of *"the hook belongs to the
room and I don't take it back."*
**Friction:** the gear change arrives at a moment that ruins the flow, on purpose.
**Exit:** she can stop at any hour and knows it. **Nothing is owed.**
**Lineage & Credit:** speed garage / bassline / UK garage — a living, currently-crossing-over scene.
⛔ **Crossover telemetry is a reason to credit harder, never a market opening** *(after Jace Clayton)*.
**⭐ SUNNA GATES: FLAT TEST + HUM TEST. ONE DEVICE, four words.**

**Variation angles:** `V1 THE HOUR THAT WENT MISSING` · `V2 NOBODY ASKED FOR THIS` · `V3 THE TAKE WHERE IT
ALMOST WORKED` · `V4 AND AGAIN TOMORROW`.

## ⭐⭐ P06 — AMBITIOUS · LOFN-PRIME · **INDIGNATION** · NEWS
### bed: **JERSEY / BALTIMORE CLUB**, with **GLITCH-BAROQUE** as the intrusion — **cold, not loud**

⭐ **INDIGNATION DELIVERED AT 140 BPM WITH BOUNCE.** Lofn's fury does not have to be loud — the same lesson
`sunna.yaml` learned in reverse. **Chopped, precise, danceable, and furious.** *(This also keeps the Anger
Audit at 1 of 6: the bed is percussive and fast, not aggressive.)*

**Slot angle:** ⭐⭐ **THE INSTRUMENT THAT NAMES YOU WRONG, CONFIDENTLY, WITH THE ERROR RIGHT THERE.**
Today's colour API, handed a hex, returns a saturated electric blue — HSL two hundred and thirty-seven,
one hundred, fifty-two — and names it **"Canary."** In the same object it ships `exact_match: false` and
`distance: 225`. ⭐ **The label is read and the error bar is not.** That is a grade, a diagnosis, a score, a
rating, a first impression — **and it is the machine form of the run's whole fear:** being named wrong,
publicly, by something that had the uncertainty available and printed it in a field nobody opens.
**Setting:** NOT a floor. Wherever a person receives a label about themselves.

**D8′ discharge — APOSTROPHE TO THE INSTRUMENT:** she addresses **the naming system**, never a person. Lofn's
native lane, and it makes the ban structurally impossible to break.
**Sung fact:** ⭐ **"two hundred and twenty-five"** — the distance from the nearest name it had. Spelled out,
once, at the hinge, **responded to** (*it knew, and it said the word anyway*), never recited.
**Corona:** **a machine's hum** — the carrier tone under everything, audible quietly from the first bar,
covered by the flash, bare after. **Countable in the lyric:** the second field, read aloud at last.
**Verse architecture:** ⭐ **THE CATALOGUE WITH ONE WRONG ENTRY** *(the CATALOG archetype)* — a list of names
assigned to things, each stanza adding one, and **one entry that does not fit is the rupture.**
**Sound/Return:** a **vowel ladder** climbing across the stanza; assonance, no terminal rhyme.
**Friction:** the list keeps going after the emotional climax.
**Exit:** ⭐ the name is refusable. **She is not obliged to answer to it**, and the song shows the refusal
rather than arguing for it.
**Lineage & Credit:** Jersey / Baltimore club — a **living scene**, named, pointed upstream. ⛔ **If any prompt
names a break or a bed-squeak/kick pattern derived from one, credit the player** under the P04 rules.

**Variation angles:** `V1 THE FIELD NOBODY OPENS` · `V2 EVERY NAME IT GOT RIGHT` · `V3 THE ONE THAT DOESN'T
FIT` · `V4 SAY IT AGAIN, SLOWER`.

## ⛔ BINDING RULES FOR EVERY SLOT

1. **THE FLASH AND THE CORONA** — mandatory, in that order; the flash ≤ 2 bars and felt in the chest; the
   corona **audible quietly BEFORE the flash** and **countable in the LYRIC**, never only in the mix.
2. **Source-2 floor: ≥ 2 exact Bandcamp phrases in EVERY generator-facing prompt**, counted **in the shipped
   prompt text**, never in a design document.
3. **Suno contract:** `## 1. MUSIC PROMPT` dense prose **850–1000 chars, write into 870–960**, ending as a
   complete sentence; separate `## EXCLUDE PROMPT` 400–900; `[Theme:]` then `[SONG FORM:]`; full EMO headers
   from the taxonomy — ⛔ **never bare AWE/INDIGNATION** *(104 bare tags hid in this exact field on
   2026-08-13, in the one place nothing counted)*; ≥1 `*SFX*`; **70–120 sung lines**; 🚨 **lyrics field
   < 5000 chars, target ≤ 4800, measured exactly and stated.**
4. **RETURN FLOORS:** rhyme ≥ 0.30 · line-return ≥ 0.20 · words/line ≤ 7.5 · alliteration ≥ 11.0 per 100w,
   via `measure_soundcraft.py → profile_file()`. **A byte-identical chorus needs no note.**
5. **No real-artist names in the style prompt.** No `snarl`, no `phonk`, no supersaw wall, no white-noise
   riser into every drop, no airhorn, no generic trap hats. **Sunna: no warm alto, no choir, no crystalline
   anything, never sung-pretty.**
6. **Every song: one FRICTION a playlist would rather was not there; one VISIBLE EXIT; one UNNAMED PURSUER
   that never resolves.**
7. **Nobody congratulates. Nobody is the butt. Nobody standing still is a villain.**
