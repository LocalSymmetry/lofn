2. ⚠️ **`"You are not wrong the way a person is wrong."`** — the closest thing to contempt in the song, and

3. ⚠️ **`"It is printed. It is not read."` (Turn, new)** — I checked whether this indicts a careless human.

4. ⛔ **Cut before it shipped:** V4's whole framing, *"Say it again, slower"* — repetition-as-mockery is the

   This is the one butt-of-joke vector the pair did not log, and it is why V4 placed third and not first.
5. **Not found:** no malfunction, no error tone, no stutter gag, no "silly computer" framing, no line where
   the singer is cleverer than the instrument. The turn **credits** the machine out loud — *you put your

**Residual risk, unrepaired and stated:** a deadpan catalogue is still one performance choice away from

**D7 check (Lofn is not the cure):** the singer is a person at a paint counter; **no AI is anyone's remedy

### Second opinion on V2's *"You are never wrong with me"*

**My call: it survives in V2, and I decline to import it into the shipped song.** The pair's defence is
sound — the payoff is the band tightening on her wrist, which is injury and not a punchline. But the line's
*flattest* reading is still ironic, and irony aimed at a machine is exactly the butt-joke that D11 bans.
In V2 the surrounding architecture pays for it. Lifted into V3 — where the next event is a *tin coming back
with a lid on it* — it would be a wink with nothing to land on. **Verdict: keep it in the archive, keep it
out of the shipped lyric, and if V2 is ever rendered, gate that line by ear first.**

- **THE SEAM (N = 1):** one bitcrushed harpsichord, wrong sample rate, wrong room, **earlier on every
