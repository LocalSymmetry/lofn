## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

**1. Changed — the title. `Inside The Loop` → `Inside The Loop, Not Outside`.**
*Reason:* the LEGIBILITY RULE requires a stranger to retell scene **and** subject after one listen,
and *"inside the loop"* has a strong idiomatic prior that is not this song's meaning. The chorus's own
correction — *"Inside the loop. Not outside. Stitched in."* — is the thesis, the reveal and the
countable line in one, and it disambiguates in three words. *Effect on Lofn uniqueness:* the title
now states the seam instead of gesturing at it, and it carries the `st` spine into the paratext.

**2. Intensified — the interrupted-stanza figure is now ABSOLUTE across verse-shaped stanzas.**
*Reason:* the pair declared the figure's completion as its **countable corona**, but as written V4's
Intro and Verse 1 both ran to completion before the flash, which makes the count unprovable. Every
verse-shaped stanza (Intro, V1, V2, Outro) is now cut mid-line and **exactly one — the Corona —
completes**, measured. Refrains (Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Tag) and the bridge apostrophe complete by
design: they are the **return**, not the figure. *Effect:* the corona is now countable by a stranger
holding the lyric sheet, which is what `THE COUNTABLE RETURN` (Flair 9) actually asks for.
⭐ And the repair improved the writing rather than only the metric: **Verse 1's cut now eats the exit
line itself** (*"Inside the stairwell I'm still going to—"*), so the chorus is the first place in the
song she is permitted to finish the sentence *"I still get to stop."*

**3. Intensified — THE PASTICHE REPAIR, IN THE WRITING, NOT THE SPEC.** *(This is where the agency
was spent; the exclude field's `period-costume jungle` ban was already there and a spec-only answer
is not an answer.)* Two edits, both untransposable backward:
- **Verse 2** now reads *"Inside the stone: a step and a stranger, / every stranger standing at
  once—"*. That is **context collapse** stated as a physical fact in the room — one act meeting every
  audience at once, with no way to segment. **Nobody in 1993 wrote that line because nobody in 1993
  had that problem.**
- **The Corona's reveal is no longer mystical.** *"the watching was stone, the watching was staying"*
  was beautiful and could have been a song about conscience in any century. It is now *"The watching
  is the sums. The sums are stone."* — the internalised watcher is **the arithmetic**, installed by
  the condition, and the pre-chorus has already ruled that the arithmetic is **correct**
  (*"And nobody standing here is stupid."*). ⭐ **The indignation is aimed at the condition. Never at
  people, never at a generation, never at the listener.**
*Effect:* Fisher's narrowed objection — *if the songs sound like the era they borrow, they are dead* —
is answered in two sung lines that a generator cannot smooth away.
