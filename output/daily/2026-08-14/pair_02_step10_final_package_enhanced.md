**Ranking (shippability into THIS set): `V1 > V3 > V2 > V4`.**

**V3 — THE ONE PERSON WHO KNOWS · SECOND, AND CLOSER THAN THE GAP LOOKS.**
V3 owns the best single idea in the pair: the friend asks for *the door bit* — the accidental sound the
child apologised for on tape — so the corona (*"If anyone hears this, I'm sorry about the door"*) is an

**1. Changed — the completion is spent exactly ONCE, and the final chorus returns still cut off.**
*Reason:* the brief mandates the half-phrase be *"finished exactly once after"* the flash. **Measured, V1 as
shipped in step 10 finished it three times** — twice in the corona and again inside the final chorus — while
the pair's own self-check table asserted *"finishes exactly once ✅."* **The gate was asserted, not counted.**
I cut the corona's duplicate and restored `(This one's called—)` to the final chorus, which makes the refrain
byte-identical on all four returns for the first time. *Effect:* the payoff is now **rarer and colder** — the
child finishes her sentence once, in silence, and then the adult's refrain comes back with the same hole in
it. **Nothing was repaired, which is the song.** This is the pair's D9 spine expressed in the FORM instead of
in a line, and it strengthens THE COUNTABLE RETURN, the byte-identical mandate and D9 in one edit.

**2. Intensified — a genuine TURN at the bridge.**
*Reason:* GOLDEN MOVE #3 asks the song to contradict or complicate its opening stance past the midpoint; V1's
stance previously only deepened. *Changed:* `I am not the one who fixes it` → **`The tape is older than the
room I am in.` / `I am doing the same thing she did.`** *Effect:* the adult is not preserving a child's
mistake — she is **still making it**, right now, on this record. That is the Golden Seed's own anchor
(**THE THING YOU DO AGAIN**) landing on the singer instead of on the object. ⛔ **No number was introduced**
— the slot allocates **no sung fact** and the hinge stays an object.

**3. Refused — `Wall_Of_Sound_Spector_Layering`, a token the Disc_Channel guide offers by name.**
*Reason:* two independent doctrine violations in one token. It inverts the seam (it is a *whole-record
production*, which is precisely how a visitor becomes the room and a song becomes a museum), and it routes
the credit for this bed through **a producer who was a convicted murderer**, when the slot's binding
instruction is ⛔ *credit THE SINGERS* and ⛔ *never credit an abuser as lineage*. It is now in the **exclude**
field instead. *Effect:* the intrusion stays a visitor and the credit stays with the women who sang it.

**4. Changed — the style prompt now leads with genre / tempo / key.**
*Reason:* Gate 14a is explicit and step 10 opened on a mood fragment (*"Nervous delight on a hard floor."*).
*Effect:* the mood survives as a clause inside the genre sentence; the generator receives the bed first,
which is the whole inversion defence.

**5. Intensified — the intrusion is band-limited in the shipped prompt, not only in the guide.**
Added **`band-limited with no sub and no air`** and **`leave mid-phrase`**. *Reason:* Guide 2 specifies the
different signal chain, but a spec that never reaches the shipped field is a spec that never happened —
**SPEC ≠ ARTIFACT, EVERY TIME.** *Effect:* the seam is a measurable property of the audio.

**6. ⛔ REFUSED — the step-11 instruction to "elevate poetic language to literary density" and
"if a line is merely functional, make it beautiful."**
*Reason:* this song's ethic is a conservator's. Its narrator's power comes entirely from **litotes and the
refusal of the superlative**; the highest claim she is permitted to make about the tape is a double negative.
Consonance chains and assonance tunnels bolted onto *"The count-in runs too fast"* would make the singer
**argue** for the object, which is the one thing the brief forbids — and it would smuggle in the vindication
D9 exists to prevent. *What I did instead:* I identified the assonance tunnel **already running** — the long
`-ine` spine of *mine / nine / fine*, ruptured every single time by the long-`a` of **tape** — and left it
untouched rather than adding a second one that would compete with it. **The structural device this tier is
required to apply is Deviation 1**, which is a form transformation, not a decoration. *Effect on Lofn
uniqueness:* Lofn's Pillar 2 is *the beauty in the breakage, meticulously micro-edited, never smoothed.*
Smoothing this lyric into literary density is the exact failure that pillar names.

**7. Not repaired, deliberately.** The wrong word. The flat long note. The rushed count-in. The fact that
