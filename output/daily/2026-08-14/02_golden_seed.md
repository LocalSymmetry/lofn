## THE MANDATORY FORM RULE — **THE FLASH AND THE CORONA**

From the APOD's literal structure (the diamond ring caught through a break in cloud; then the corona, which
was always there and only visible once the bright thing was covered).

1. **THE FLASH** — one event, **≤ 2 bars**, at a transition. **Felt in the chest, not heard in the ears** — a
   bright hit alone is a cymbal, and a cymbal has never moved anybody.
2. **THE CORONA** — something **inaudible while the bright thing played** is **revealed**, not introduced.
   ⭐ **It must sound quietly BEFORE the flash** — otherwise it is an entrance and the trick is dead.
   ⛔ **And it must be COUNTABLE IN THE LYRIC**, not only mixed: a line count, a bare repeat, a half-phrase
   finished. **Nobody can quantise a line count away.**

⭐ **The theme and the form are one object:** the embarrassing detail is the flash; what it was covering is
the corona. *(N=1 discipline, in-lane.)*
