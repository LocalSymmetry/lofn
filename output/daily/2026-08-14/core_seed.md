## 5. ⭐⭐ THE CENTRAL CRAFT RULING — D8 had to be rewritten, and the rewrite is the seam

The benchmark's single best decision was **`D8 — OVERHEARING, NOT ADDRESSING`**: *"a song that says 'I see
you' is one more thing wanting something."* It satisfied four bans by construction.

**It is incompatible with SUNNA's core mechanic.** Her YAML: *"You take a wrist and pull someone into the
middle of the floor"* · *"The hook does not belong to you. It belongs to the room, and it is designed to be
sung **back**"* · *"If the crowd can't own it by the second chorus, rewrite it."* **That is addressing.**
Imposing D8 verbatim would delete the register that won her the Pantheon seat — and produce a second Lofn.

### The ruling

> **D8′ — ADDRESS THE ROOM, NEVER THE LISTENER.**
>
> A line sung **to the room, from inside it, by someone subject to the same thing**, is a **chant**.
> A line sung **at a listener, from outside, by someone who has already solved it**, is a **lecture**.
>
> ⭐ **THE TEST — CAN THE ROOM SHOUT THE LINE BACK AT THE SINGER WITHOUT IT BECOMING FALSE?**
> *"I don't know the words either"* — the room can shout that back. **Chant.**
> *"You're afraid of looking stupid"* — the room shouts it back and it becomes an accusation. **Lecture.**
>
> The test is mechanical, it is checkable line-by-line, and it is **the seam between the two seats**:
> Lofn's songs discharge D8′ by **overhearing** (nobody addressed at all); Sunna's discharge it by
> **standing inside the thing she names.** Same ban, two honest mechanisms.

### ⭐ AND THE MECHANISM IS ASSIGNED, NOT LEFT OPEN — the L41 fix, applied in advance

2026-08-12: D8 was banned with **no mechanism assigned** → residue **"nobody"/"nothing" 256 hits, 20 of 24
songs**, because the ban said what not to do and never said where the pressure should go. 2026-08-13: the
mechanism was **assigned per pair** → **0 hits in 25 songs**, with pairs reporting they caught and converted
the residue *in draft*. ⭐ **Assignment does not stop the residue forming — it gives every instance somewhere
to go, converting an invisible default into a visible repair.**

**Therefore: every pair is assigned its D8′ discharge mechanism at Phase 1.** Six pairs, six named
mechanisms, no two alike.

## 6. THE MANDATORY FORM RULE — **THE FLASH AND THE CORONA** *(Source 3)*

From the APOD's literal image structure: the **diamond ring** caught through a break in cloud, and then the
**corona** — the structure that was always there and only becomes visible once the bright thing is covered.

**Every song, in this order:**
1. **THE FLASH** — one event, **≤ 2 bars**, at a transition, of maximum brightness. It is **not** the chorus;
   it is the instant *before* the arrangement changes state.
2. **THE CORONA** — immediately after, something **inaudible while the bright thing was playing** is
   **revealed** (not introduced): a countermelody that was always doubling, the room's own noise, a second
   voice, a figure that had been running the whole time.

⛔ **The corona must be carried by the LYRIC or the FORM — a production-spec-only corona is not answered**
(L22, THE GRAIN LAW: a Somatic objection answered in the production spec is *not* answered). The rule was
deliberately built to run **with** the generator: a brief bright hit followed by an element uncovering is
something a pop arrangement already wants to do.

⭐ **And the theme is the same shape as the form:** the embarrassing detail is the flash; what it was
covering is the corona. **The form rule and the seed are one object.** *(N=1 discipline, in-lane.)*
