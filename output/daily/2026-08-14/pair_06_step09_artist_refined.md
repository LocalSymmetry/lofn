## THE VOICE PASS — what LOFN-PRIME changes, and what she refuses to change

**Kept from step 08 unchanged:** every scene, every ledge, every structural event. Step 09 injects style,
not content. **What she changes is temperature, precision, and one word in four.**

1. ⭐ **The opener of each prompt is now a different temperature.** *Cold* / *level* / *precise* / *late*.
   Four grammars, four first-six-word signatures, no shared cadence. A run whose four prompts open the same
   way is one prompt rendered four times.
2. ⭐ **Vocalist traits differentiated per variation.** Same singer, four takes: *breath left in* (V1) ·
   *faint sibilance, no reverb* (V2) · *audible intake before each entry* (V3) · *breath audible between
   lines* (V4). The spec stays complete — gender, age, tone ×2, traits ×2 — but never repeats itself.
3. ⛔ **`snarl` deleted on sight.** LOFN-PRIME's own DNA names a pop-punk snarl as her indignation vocal.
   **It is banned this run and I am not fighting it, because the brief is right:** this pair's indignation
   is cold, and a snarl is the loud kind. The register survives as *bitten consonants* and *no vibrato* —
   which is the same contempt, at room temperature, and it is harder.
4. ⛔ **All avoidance prose moved out of the style field.** Suno reads that field as **positive tokens**.
   The style prompts keep only their *acoustic / no-acoustic declarations*, which are positive statements
   about what the one real source is. Everything negative lives in `## EXCLUDE PROMPT`.
5. ⭐ **The seam is stated in one clause per prompt, with its singularity and its earliness together.**
   *"arrives a beat early on the fourth catalogue entry and never again."* Singular + early + located. A
   harpsichord mentioned twice in a prompt is garnish; a harpsichord mentioned once, early, and placed, is
   a fault.
6. **Glitch-Baroque is used as the wound, not the signature.** It is my own palette and that is exactly why
   it must not be pretty here. Bitcrushed, wrong sample rate, wrong room, wrong beat.

## THE CRITIC LOOP *(three passes, capped)*

**Pass 1 — THE DOCUMENTARIAN's seat (after Albini), on all four:**
> *"There is no room, no drummer, and no printer. You have written a specification for a counter you will
> not build, and your corona is a quiet element under a loud one, which is the exact class your own audit
> says gets smoothed to nothing."*

**Accepted, and repaired in the lyric rather than the spec.** The corona is now a **broken sentence that
returns finished** — *"and not an exact—"* twice, then *"not an exact match"* whole — and the seam is now a
**named event in the lyric**: *and the gold thing comes in wrong.* Neither can be quantised away. The
production spec is where they are *supported*, not where they live.

**Pass 2 — THE CHOP's seat (after Edwards), on V2:**
> *"You have written 'vocal chopping' as an adjective. That is a garnish and I have spent my career on the
> difference."*

**Accepted.** V2's chop is now functional: sub-syllabic fragments of the label placed on the offbeats **so
the room hears the word before the singer says it.** The hook leaves the singer as an instrument, which is
axis 5, and it means the flash lands on a word the ear already knows.

**Pass 3 — THE REGRESSION's seat (after Adorno), on the whole pair:**
> *"You have taken a real fear — being labelled — and you propose to resolve it with a dance beat and a
> closing couplet in which the protagonist wins. That is the culture industry selling you your own
> rebellion back at one hundred and forty."*

**Half-accepted, and it changed the running order.** He is right that a refusal placed last is a victory
lap. So the refusal is **not last**: after the exit, ⭐ **the catalogue resumes and outlasts her.** The
machine is unbothered, the word is still on the slip, still on the band, still on the lid. Nobody is
congratulated — not the listener, not the room, not the song. His objection **rides into step 11 live.**

**Not accepted:** his implied demand that the song be joyless. The bounce stays. A record that cannot be
danced to has answered him by refusing to be a record.

## THE LYRIC REFINEMENTS — decisions taken here, executed in step 10

| decision | why |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Every second-person pronoun is the instrument.** No line addresses a person, a listener, a room, or a cohort, in any of the four. | D8′ made structurally unbreakable, which is the whole reason this concept took this slot. |
| ⭐ **The line-final word is held constant across each catalogue** (*a name*, *a name*, *a name*) while the stressed vowel inside the line climbs. | The vowel ladder with no terminal rhyme. **Epistrophe is the return; rhyme is not.** The debt is named and paid. |
| **The epistrophe breaks exactly once per song** — on the entry that is a person. | Formal rupture at the semantic rupture. The listener feels the list stop being a list. |
| **The chorus is byte-identical on every full repeat.** | A byte-identical chorus is correct and requires no note. Nobody defends a chorus in this run. |
| ⛔ **`Derision` is excluded from every EMO header in all four variations.** | It is in the taxonomy and it is exactly the wrong instruction for D11. An EMO tag is an instruction to the performance; *derision* aimed at the machine produces the butt-joke in the delivery even if the words are clean. |
| ⛔ **No bare `AWE` or `INDIGNATION` tag anywhere.** | 104 bare mode-words shipped in this exact field once, because it was the one thing nothing counted. It is counted this run. |
| **Present tense, all four, throughout.** | D5. Also the stronger tense here: *it knows, and it says the word anyway* is worse than any past tense of it. |
| **The pursuer is touched and never named** — and the machine's dictionary fails on it: *you have no name for that.* | The unnamed pursuer is the fear the fun record keeps. Giving the naming system one thing it cannot name is the only way to keep it unnamed forever. |

## SELF-CRITIQUE

The prompts are now four temperatures of one idea, and the honest risk is that **temperature is a thin
axis.** V1 and V2 are close relatives: same room-class, same flat delivery, differing mainly in how much
catalogue precedes the rupture. If a judge hears them back to back, V2 will sound like V1 with a longer
first act.

I am not repairing that by adding a device, because adding a device breaks N = 1 and the seam is the whole
piece. I am repairing it in **architecture**, which is the legal move: V2's rupture arrives on the **last
line of the third catalogue**, roughly sixty percent through, where V1's arrives at the midpoint — and V2's
chorus is a list of the machine's *correctness*, which is a different sentence doing a different job. If
that is not enough separation, the correct fix at step 11 is to cut one of them, not to decorate it.
