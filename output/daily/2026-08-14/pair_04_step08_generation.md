## RAW V1 — "NOBODY'S FILMING THIS"

**Raw style direction.** Indignant, clipped, physical. 2026 jungle at 172. First five seconds are the
room and nothing else: a fire-door click, the hum of a painted steel rail, one breath too close to the
capsule. Then the chopped amen break, hard and fractionally late, hard-panned wide and dry. Vocalist:
dry mid-twenties alto, bratty, unsweetened, staged a metre off a concrete wall and slightly right of
centre so consonants slap back at her. Bed carries natural entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes,
the odd messy recording — uncleaned. Verses half-time and glitchy and frenetic; bitcrushed harpsichord
ornament arriving on the wrong beat, never corrected. Flash: one bar where a car's headlights cross the
wire glass and a sub lands in the sternum, no cymbal on it. Corona: the break strips to a ghost-note
figure running since bar one. Acoustic: the real stairwell only, 0.9-second concrete slap; no plate, no
hall, no synthetic space. Pursuer: one filtered tone climbing all track, never arriving.

**Raw lyric direction.** Anaphora `Nobody's filming`. `st` in every line. Every stanza cut mid-line and
restarted; **one** stanza completes, after the flash, and that completion is the corona. Address the
absent future watcher, never the listener. State plainly, once, that she is not good at this. Chorus's
last line is the exit, first person. Last stanza cut and never restarted.

**Source-2 (raw):** `natural entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes, the odd messy recording` ·
`glitchy and frenetic`.
**Break named in STYLE:** `amen` → **credit owed.**

## RAW V2 — "WHOEVER WATCHES THIS"

**Raw style direction.** Cold and level, not loud. 2026 jungle at 168, aggression withheld two thirds of
the way. Opens on a held sub note under stairwell air and a phone's screen-off tick. Gorgeously
restrained beats through the verses — a Think break chopped small, dry, low in the balance — with
low-slung bass, swelling synths beneath. Vocalist: the same dry alto, staged closer, room mic higher in
the balance than instinct allows, no de-esser on the breaths, a pitch-imperfect double misaligned by
tens of milliseconds so the concrete smears it. Flash: one bar where the sub doubles and the room mic
clips, felt in the chest. Corona: the building's own resonant tone, present from the first bar,
uncovered when the break leaves. Last minute unrestrained. Acoustic: real stairwell, nothing added.

**Raw lyric direction.** Anaphora `Whoever watches`. Direct address to a person who does not exist yet.
An inventory of what will still be there when she is not. Epistrophe on *still*. No number anywhere —
permanence is the hinge and a number would bound it. Chorus arrives a beat early every time and is never
fixed: that is the friction.

**Source-2 (raw):** `low-slung bass, swelling synths` · `gorgeously restrained beats`.
**Break named in STYLE:** `Think` → **credit owed.**

## RAW V3 — "THE ROOM IS DOING IT"

**Raw style direction.** Quiet indignation at speed. 2026 jungle at 170 with subdued dynamics, liminal
melodies, and quiet-storm R&B rhythms pulled inside the grid rather than sitting beside it. Opens on the
tail of a note she has already stopped singing — the room finishing it — then grit under a shoe. The
intrusion is a break chopped from one borrowed bar and resequenced, **not named and therefore not
credited.** A guitar diverts into a weepy countermelody exactly once and does not return. Vocalist
staged furthest from the mic of the four, so the concrete is louder than the throat. Flash: one sub note
under a held vowel, the quietest transition of the four. Corona: the reverb tail finishing a phrase she
abandoned. Acoustic: the concrete slap is the loudest effect in the song; everything else dry.

**Raw lyric direction.** Anaphora `The stairwell`. She names, out loud, that the flattery is
architectural — and **does not get good** (`D9`). Synaesthesia: the cold read as pitch. Friction: two
bars accompanied only by a reverb tail.

**Source-2 (raw):** `subdued dynamics, liminal melodies, and quiet-storm R&B rhythms` ·
`guitar diverts into a weepy countermelody`.
**Break named in STYLE:** **none** → **no drummer credit, deliberately.**

## RAW V4 — "INSIDE THE LOOP"

**Raw style direction.** The set's one genuinely violent slot. 2026 jungle at 174 with a bone-rattling
breakdown with battering drums and explosions of guitar; rambunctious, unwieldy. Opens on the ghost-note
figure alone at the edge of audibility for three seconds before the chopped amen break lands on top and
buries it. Guitar is part of the 2026 world here, not a second historical intrusion — **the single
intrusion is still the borrowed break.** Vocalist spat and clipped, staged dead centre and very close,
with the room behind her rather than around her. Flash: two bars of full-band stop-hit, felt low. Corona:
everything above leaves and the inside figure is bare — it has been at −18 dB since bar one and is never
boosted. Acoustic: real stairwell only. Pursuer: the filtered tone, highest and least resolved here.

**Raw lyric direction.** Anaphora `Inside the`. Polysyndeton in the corona; anadiplosis linking its
clauses. The reveal is that the watching was never external. Friction: the list continues past the
emotional climax.

**Source-2 (raw):** `bone-rattling breakdown with battering drums and explosions of guitar` ·
`rambunctious, unwieldy`.
**Break named in STYLE:** `amen` → **credit owed.**
