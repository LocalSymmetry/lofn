**`[V1, V2, V4, V3]`**

- **① V1 · HOLDING DOWN A CHAIR — SHIPPED.** The only one of the four whose **subject is an object doing
  the work**: a coat on a chair is a claim that requires nobody's agreement, it survives the FLAT TEST said
  cold to a face, and a stranger retells scene *and* subject in one sentence after one listen — GOLDEN MOVE
  rule 6, which is the rule the other three pay something for. The exit is **inside the refrain**
  (*"The door is three steps over there"*), so leaving is stated four times and never as a concession. Its
  corona (a fridge hum from bar one) is the most **plausible as natural entropy** and therefore the least
  likely to read as an entrance. And it is the one whose hinge does not move the camera out of the room.
- **② V2 · BETTER BACK HERE.** The best joke and the best physics — the boundary is where the low end is
  complete, delivered as measurement rather than virtue. Ranked second because *"From here I get the record,
  not the band"* leans toward **the edge being better**, and a song that wins an argument about where to
  stand is one degree from vindication. It pays for it with a stated trade, honestly; V1 does not have to.
- **③ V4 · THE OUTSIDE AIR.** Warm, mobile, the cleanest walk. But it takes the body **off the perimeter**,
  and the panel ruling is that the good night happens *at the edge*, not on the way out of it. Its
  *"Still good"* brace also sits closest to self-congratulation of the four.
- **④ V3 · ARRIVING ALL NIGHT.** The most beautiful and the most dangerous. Sea hiss + eight-second cycle +
  spacious + slow is **the exact centre of the generator's lo-fi-chill prior**, which is this pair's own
  named primary risk; and its subject drifts out of the room and into the ocean, so the person at the wall
  becomes the ocean's audience. Its eight bare counts are the best single idea in the set and **that idea
  is carried forward into the shipped song's dropout** rather than left behind.

## ⭐ THE WALLPAPER REPAIR — what changed, and why it is not a production note

The pair's own declared primary risk: *"Chill-out wallpaper. Every token in dub-adjacent / restrained /
subdued / spacious sits in the middle of the generator's lo-fi-chill prior; the likely failure is a
PLEASANT render, not a bad one."* Step 09 fixed four things. This tier spent its agency on **five more**,
and ⛔ **three of the five are in the lyric or the form**, because an objection answered only in the
production spec is not answered.

| # | Repair | Where it lives | Why the generator can't smooth it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two hard consonant events, not one** — the chair leg on the mic stand in bar one, **and a glass set down on wood on the two, every bar.** | style field **and** `Disc_Rhythm` | Wallpaper has no impacts in it. The glass is a **percussive event that is not a drum**, so it survives even if the kit gets smoothed toward a soft chill kit. |
| 2 | **The reverb is forbidden from running underneath the voice** — *"spring and tape answer in the gaps, never under her."* `Disc_Pad: spring_tank_and_tape_echo_only_in_the_gaps`. | style field + Disc_Pad | The lo-fi-chill prior's single most identifying feature is a **continuous wash**. A gap-only return is a dub gesture and an anti-wallpaper instruction at the same time — this is the ONE SEAM, and it is now a rule about *when*, not a texture. |
| 3 | **`no_synth_pad_no_wash_no_drone_bed`** as negative addressing inside the channel block, and `No synth pad, no wash, no acoustic kit.` in the style field. | Disc_Pad + style field | Negative addressing blocks the neighbourhood rather than describing around it. `ambient wallpaper`, `continuous pad wash`, `tape-loop drone bed`, `coffee-shop playlist` are also now explicit exclude tokens. |
| 4 | ⭐ **The bass is the FIRST declared channel** — `Disc_Sub` before `Disc_Rhythm`. | Disc block order | The guide is explicit that **earlier channels get more generative weight**. The pair's whole HUM TEST claim is that the bassline is the tune; declaring it fourth contradicted that in the one place the model actually reads. |
| 5 | ⭐ **The defect is stated twice and disagreed with itself** — hi-hat **eleven milliseconds** late and never correcting, against a bass locked exactly on the grid. | style field + Disc_Rhythm | Wallpaper is quantised. **And in the lyric, where it counts:** the sung lines now contain **`ducks down`**, **`thick`**,
**`spot`**, **`sat`** where they previously contained `ducks`, `whole`, `place`, `was` — four one-word

architecture requires and the scanner penalises. *When a scan hits a line, fix the scanner, not the line* —
the floor is cleared **with margin, in the long lines, where alliteration is native.**

## ⭐ THE STRUCTURAL TRANSFORMATION — one device, two executions, no second device

**ONE DEVICE, four words: *the dropout is the reveal.*** Both of the following are executions **of** that
device, not additions to it.

1. **THE ORPHANED HALF-PHRASE, CLOSED IN THE SILENCE.** Verse 1 ends on a brace that hangs —
   `Been on since.` — three syllables with no object and no rhyme partner. It is not resolved anywhere in
   the body of the song. After the bar of true silence, with nothing under it, the dropout says
   **`Been on since before I came in the door.`** — which **finishes the sentence** and **takes the
   `floor / door` rhyme opened in Verse 1**. ⭐ *The corona is the rhyme.* This is the most render-proof
   form of the reveal available: a generator can refill a silence, it cannot un-rhyme a couplet. *(This is
   Flair 9, THE COUNTABLE RETURN, executed as a finished half-phrase rather than only as a count — and
   step 07's Guide 3 specified exactly this and step 10 did not build it.)*
2. **THE COLLAPSING COUNT.** `One. Two. Three. Four.` → *(silence)* → `One. Two.` → `One.` The count is
   the smallest unit of lyric that exists and **nobody can quantise a line count away.** Shrinking it turns
   the void into something with **structure in it**, which is this pair's assigned corona, stated literally.

**What was carried forward from a variation that did not ship:** V3's eight bare counts were the set's best
single idea; the shipped dropout uses the count **as a shape that changes** rather than as a length, which
is the version that also serves the corona.

- **The fridge hum from bar one** — the corona. Planted quietly, **high-passed out** of the busy sections
  rather than muted, so its return at the dropout is a **restoration**, not an entrance.
- **The chair-leg knock and the glass on wood** — the two hard consonant events. Anti-wallpaper, and both

- **The hi-hat eleven milliseconds late, never corrected, against an on-grid bass** — the kinetic defect,

- **Spring tank and tape echo answering only in the gaps** — the single historical hand in a 2026 room.

## LINEAGE & CREDIT

**The scene.** This is a contemporary bass-forward electronic record, and the reason that music can exist at
all is **Jamaican dub and sound-system practice** — the discovery that the mixing desk is an instrument and
that **space is the material**: subtraction, echo and dropout as composition rather than as effects. That is
a **living lineage**, not a museum. It runs upstream to **King Tubby (Osbourne Ruddock)**, **Lee "Scratch"
Perry**, **Errol Thompson**, **Scientist (Hopeton Brown)** and **Mad Professor (Neil Fraser)**, and sideways
into the sound systems and dub-adjacent producers working now in Kingston, London, Bristol and Berlin.
**Go and buy their records.** Fusion with lineage, never capture.

⛔⛔ **THE PROMPT NAMES A DRUM FIGURE, SO IT CREDITS THE PLAYER.** The shipped style field names
**one-drop** (word-boundary sweep: 1 hit), and `Disc_Rhythm` names `one_drop_kick_on_the_three`. That figure
is **Carlton Barrett**'s, of the Wailers, and it is the reason half of this bed has a shape. He is not a
genre; he is a pair of hands.

⛔ **CREDIT DROPPED, DELIBERATELY — and this is the honest half of the rule.** Step 10 credited **Sly
Dunbar** for **steppers**, correctly, because V3's style field named that pulse. **V3 did not ship, and the

⛔ **Honesty clause, both ways:** we asked for the one drop **by name**, so we name him. **Nothing here

### ⛔ THE PITY SWEEP — regex **and** by eye, on the shipped lyric

**Regex, over all 81 sung lines:** `you'll get there · come on · one day · someday · it's okay · don't be
shy · nobody's watching · just let go · brave · shy · hang in there · soon · maybe next time · don't worry ·
cheer up · it gets better · will be fine · not alone` → **0 hits.**
**Second-person pronouns → 0.** *(There is nobody to address. That is the discharge mechanism, not an
omission.)*

**By eye, which is the half the regex cannot do — the four ways pity hides in a song like this:**
1. **Pity by adjective** — no *lonely, quiet, small, brave, sad, patient* is applied to the subject
   anywhere. The only adjectives on the body are physical and neutral: *early*, *back here*, *still fine*.
2. **Pity by forecast** — nothing in the song says the night will get better, or that she will move, or
   that anything is coming. The refrain returns **unchanged** after the sublime, which is the formal
   refusal of a forecast.
3. **Pity by third-party gaze** — no one in the room looks at her, notices her, makes room for her, or
   fails to. `Nobody here is waiting on my hand.` and `And nobody has asked me for a trade.` are the
   opposite construction: **the absence of demand stated as a good**, not as neglect.
4. **Pity by comparison** — `They get the kick. I get what the walls bring.` is a **trade**, and the next
   two lines make it **physics** rather than consolation. Neither side of that trade is the better night.
   ⛔ There is no line in which the middle of the floor is the thing she is not doing.

**Verdict: CLEAN.** Nobody in this song is being encouraged, coaxed, pitied, warmed up or waited on, and
there is no second person for it to be done to. *Stillness is not the failure state; the interruption is —
and the interruption in this song is a street door, which the room ducks under and then carries on.*

### THE FIVE STANDING VETOES, and Sunna's three
