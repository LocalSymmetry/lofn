## 1. THE ESSENCE — one sentence, and it is not the theme

> **The half-second before you join in, when the arithmetic about who can see you is CORRECT, and the
> shoulder has already decided.**

Note what the essence is **not**:
- not *"dancing"* (a subject — banned, D13)
- not *"letting go"* (a resolution — the song would be over before it started)
- not *"Gen Z's relationship to self-consciousness"* (a topic — banned, D3)
- not *"joy"* (a category; categories cannot be sung)

**The essence is a duration.** Roughly half a second, in a body, containing a correct risk assessment and an
overriding want. Every one of the twelve concepts at step 02 must be a **different place that half-second
happens.**

## 3. THE FACET THAT IS NOT ALLOWED IN

**F-x — VINDICATION.** *"And then they all joined in and it was wonderful."*

It is the natural terminus of every facet above and it is **banned outright**. It congratulates *(Adorno's
standing veto)*, it converts the edge into a before-picture *(F-h)*, and it resolves the pursuer *(F-j)*.
⭐ **The half-second is the song. The outcome is not the song.** A concept whose emotional payoff is
*"and it worked"* is cut at step 02, not repaired at step 09.

## 4. SELF-CRITIQUE

The twelve facets converge harder than usual — three configurations of the panel agreed more than they
disagreed, and **that is the portfolio-collision risk stated in advance** (L14/L20). Two mitigations are
already in force and neither is optional: the **per-slot axis table is fully distinct across all six pairs
on six axes**, and the **concept-pool cross-check asserts no two slots share a reveal-engine.** The residual
risk is real and lives at **step 09**, where six pairs will independently reach for the same shape of turn.
**That is what the clean-context judge is for; a pair cannot see it from inside.**
