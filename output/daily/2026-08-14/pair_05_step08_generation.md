## 0. THE SHARED SPINE (four songs, one engine)

**Bed = THE WORLD:** speed garage / bassline, 134–138 BPM, shuffled two-step, sub mono and chest-level,
kick nudged +8–14 ms late against the grid with the bass locked *on* it so the pair breathes.
**INTRUSION (one, and only one):** a **half-time dub gear** — bass and drums forward, spring echo,
everything else a ghost — placed at ~60% so it **ruins the flow on purpose.** ⭐ **ONE SEAM**, at the
junction where the hands fail and the number is said.
**DEVICE (one, four words):** *monosyllables break into long words.*
**FORM:** uneven verses **5 · 7 · 5 · 9** breaking into a **polysyllabic chorus.**
**RHYME:** internal-rhyme chain through the verses; **none in the chorus** — the chorus returns by
byte-identical repetition. **The debt is named and paid in the outro's bare threefold repeat.**
**FLASH:** ≤2 bars, chest-level sub thud at the second-chorus transition, acknowledged by the next line.
**CORONA:** her own under-line — parenthesised twice per chorus, plus once in the intro, **audible before
the flash**, then **sung bare and alone three times** in the hand-off.
**HAND-OFF:** the top line breaks off mid-phrase; the bass figure finishes the song.
**PURSUER:** her own body's limit. **Never named.** Shown only as anatomy: hand, palm, thumb, grip.
**SUNG FACT:** **"four hours"**, spelled out in words, **once**, at the hinge inside the dub gear,
**responded to, never recited.**
**EXIT:** *nothing owed*, in the chorus, every song.

**Section map (14):** Intro · V1(5) · V2(7) · Pre · Chorus · V3(5) · Pre · Chorus · Flash · Dub Gear(9) ·
V4(9) · Lift(3) · Chorus · Hand-Off(8).

**RAW MUSIC PROMPT (draft, ~730 ch):**
> Speed garage at 136 BPM. Strip-light tick for five seconds, then one bass figure alone. Shuffled
> two-step snare arriving late and staying late. Dry mid-twenties female vocalist talk-singing at
> conversation volume, close and slightly off-mic, left of centre in a small hard-walled practice room.
> Sub mono and chest-level under brittle organ stabs. Hats drag behind the grid and never correct. A
> two-bar low thud at the second chorus, then a half-time dub gear with spring echo that ruins the flow.
> Two-step snaps back early. Bass figure keeps playing after the voice stops.

**RAW LYRIC SKELETON:** practice room, one in the morning, third chair nobody uses. An hour is gone out
of the middle of the night. Verse chain on **-ums / -ack / -ock / -nap**. Chorus long word =
**"unaccounted."** Under-line = **"(the middle hour is gone)"**. Hinge = *"Four hours, and I could not
tell you the middle one."* Hand: **left hand locks, small bones lock, grip comes back for a bar and a
half then goes.**

## 3. RAW-STAGE CRITIQUE (before step 09 touches anything)

1. ⛔ **V4's chorus was congratulating.** The first draft ended *"and I keep showing up"* — which is
   perseverance framed as virtue, i.e. **exactly the Adorno kill.** Replaced with **"and I am not making a
   point."** The line now refuses the moral in the singer's own voice, which is the only place a refusal
   is load-bearing.
2. ⛔ **V3's chorus was redeeming the take.** First draft: *"one bar is enough."* That is `D9` broken and
   it is also a lie about how it feels. Replaced with **"I am not going to fix it."** — the doing is the
   act, not the improving.
3. ⚠️ **The raw prompts are all under 850 characters and read as tag-lists**, not as the dense prose the
   gate requires. Step 09 rewrites all four into 870–960-character paragraphs with physical adjectives,
   spatial staging, an explicit kinetic defect and acoustic/no-acoustic declarations.
4. ⚠️ **Zero Source-2 phrases in the raw prompts.** Floor is **two per shipped prompt, counted in the
   shipped text.** Step 09 installs them; step 10 counts them **in the shipped paragraph, not here.**
   *(2026-08-12, P06: zero phrases shipped against a floor of two, because the self-check counted them in
   the design doc. `SPEC ≠ ARTIFACT, EVERY TIME.`)*
5. ⚠️ **V1 and V4 share too much furniture** (both are "the same room"). V4 is moved to **the door with a
   coat on, at four**, and given a slack quiet-storm undertow the others do not have, so the two are not
   interchangeable takes.
6. ⭐ **The gear change is still only in the production line.** Step 09 must carry it into the **lyric and
   the form** — line length inverting from three words to twelve, the internal chain stopping dead — or
   the measured smoothing (2 of 2) eats it.
7. ⛔ **Break sweep, STYLE fields, word boundaries:** no `amen`, `funky drummer`, `think`, `apache`,
   `apache`-adjacent token appears in any of the four raw prompts, and none is to be introduced in step 09
   or 10. *(Also swept for `amen` inside longer words — `ornament`, `amendment`, `filament` — none present
   and none to be introduced.)*
