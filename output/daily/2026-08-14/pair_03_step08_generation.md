## V1 — THE COAT ON THE CHAIR

**Raw music prompt (draft):** Unhurried and dry. Contemporary bass-forward electronic, dub-adjacent,
ninety-two BPM in G minor, half-time. First five seconds: a four-bar bassline alone with a hole punched in
bar three, one note landing late every pass. Female vocalist, late twenties, sing-speak, talky and flat,
close on a dynamic mic a step off-axis, placed left of centre and dry in front of a wet room. Low-slung
bass, swelling synths behind a curtain; drums forward, one drop feel, hi-hat dragging eleven milliseconds
late and never correcting. Natural entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes, the odd messy recording —
audible from the first bar and buried under the arrangement. Two-thirds through, one sub hit and a door
shove for two bars, then everything drops out and the hum stands bare, then a full bar of true silence.
Acoustic sources: one shaker, one chair knock, one room. No acoustic drum kit.

**Lyric architecture:**
- Form: Intro(instr + 4) · V1(8) · Refrain(6) · V2(8) · Refrain(6) · V3(12) · Flash(3) · Dropout(5) ·
  Hinge(6) · Refrain(6) · Coda(8) · Refrain(6) · Outro(3).
- Long lines 8–10 syllables, short lines 2–4, alternating. Exact couplets on the long lines.
- Refrain seed: the coat / the door three steps away / the low end finding the wall. Short brace: *It's a good night.*
- Corona plant: a hum named in verse one, low. Corona bare: four counts + the finished phrase.
- Flash: `*street door swings wide open*` — the room ducks an inch; the next line acknowledges it.
- Hinge: **eight seconds**, answered by indifference (it does not need to know who is here).
- Pursuer: the ice, the warm glass, the bar light down half a shade. Never named.
- Exit: in the refrain, as geography.

## V2 — WHAT CAN BE HEARD FROM HERE

**Raw music prompt (draft):** Cool and amused. Contemporary bass-forward electronic with dub gestures,
eighty-eight BPM in D minor. Opens on a rimshot in a spring tank and a sub-bass figure that is its own
melody. Female vocalist, late twenties, dry sing-speak, unimpressed, close and centred, no de-esser on the
breaths. Gorgeously restrained beats; subdued dynamics, liminal melodies, and quiet-storm R&B rhythms
under everything; a ghost organ chord that never fully arrives. The room's own resonant note hums under
the first verse. Midway the arrangement thins instead of building. One tape repeat is let run past where
an engineer would stop it. Flash at the last third, then a hole. Acoustic: one shaker and the room. No
acoustic piano.

**Lyric architecture:** anaphora *From here…* opening three long lines of the refrain; the trade stated
plainly (the floor gets the kick, the wall gets the record); the corona is the room's own note, planted in
verse one and left bare after the flash; hinge fact **eight seconds** framed as the delay time of the sea
a few streets over. Short brace: *Better back here.* ⛔ Superiority sweep: the better mix must be attributed
to boundaries and pressure, never to taste.

## V3 — THE EIGHT-SECOND WAVE

**Raw music prompt (draft):** Wide and unbothered. Bass-forward electronic, eighty-four BPM in A minor,
steppers pulse. First five seconds: sub and a filtered sea-hiss breathing on an eight-second cycle. Female
vocalist, late twenties, flat sing-speak, close, dry, one low harmony a fourth under the last refrain line.
Low-slung bass, swelling synths; gorgeously restrained beats; delay set so repeats land eight seconds
apart. Fire door taped open at ankle height; street and weather leak in. Flash, dropout, one bar of true
silence, then the swell alone. Acoustic: the door, the street. No acoustic guitar.

**Lyric architecture:** refrain built on *the water's been arriving all night*; the number itself withheld
until the hinge so the fact lands once; corona is the sea under the door, planted at the top; the answering
line does the work (*it isn't early and it isn't wrong*). Short brace: *Long before this.*

## V4 — THE WALK HOME, STILL GOOD

**Raw music prompt (draft):** Bright and dry. Bass-forward electronic with a dub hand, ninety-six BPM in F
minor, walking. Opens on the push-bar of a fire door and a bassline already going. Female vocalist, late
twenties, sing-speak, talky, close, slightly out of breath. Subdued dynamics, liminal melodies, and
quiet-storm R&B rhythms; natural entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes, the odd messy recording; the
low end arriving through a wall from outside. Flash on the door; dropout on the street; one bar of silence;
the bassline walks out alone. Acoustic: footsteps, a zip, weather. No acoustic strings.

**Lyric architecture:** the coat comes off the chair; three steps; the outside. Refrain on *Still good.*
Corona is the street, which has been under the door all night. Hinge fact **eight seconds**, answered by
the swell not knowing the room is closing. ⛔ Leaving is never framed as giving up or as escape; the exit
is the subject, and the night stays intact on the walk.
