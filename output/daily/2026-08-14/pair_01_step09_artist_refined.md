## ⛔ THE FOUR KILLS

### KILL 1 — V3, the lecture. *"You could just move. It is not that hard."*
**Gone. Not softened, not rewritten into a nicer version of itself.** This is the trap named in the ICB
before anyone wrote a word: *stop caring what people think, just dance.* It makes the singer the adult in
the room (`D1`), it is aimed at a listener rather than spoken from inside the room (`D8′`), and it fails
the shout-back test instantly — a room shouting *"it is not that hard"* back at the person who is scared
is the single ugliest thing this record could produce.
**What replaced it — substitution, not subtraction (`D10`):** the stanza now ends on *"Two is a room. One
is a room with one."* Same position, same length, and it does the opposite job: it describes the
arithmetic instead of grading anybody's nerve. **The hole is filled with a fact, not with silence.**

### KILL 2 — V4, the vindication, twice.
The draft style prompt ended *"the lights come up on the last chorus and the floor fills"* and the draft
outro opened *"And by the last chorus the floor was full."* **Both gone.** The payoff is never *and then
it worked* — and the second one was also in the **past tense**, which breaks `D5` on top of everything
else. ⭐ **The half-second is the song. The outcome is not.** The room in V4 now empties all the way to
two people and the horns, and it stays that way.

### KILL 3 — V1's bridge recited instead of responding.
Draft: *"A shadow crossed four countries on Tuesday."* That is a recitation, it imports a numeral that is
not this pair's allocated fact, and it makes the research the subject. **The route is the fact.** Final
form names it whole — Greenland, Iceland, Portugal, northern Spain — and then answers it in her own
register: *it did not check the room / it did not wait to be asked.* The fact is now **responded to**, and
the response is what carries the turn: the shadow did not have to do the arithmetic, and she does, and she
is going anyway.

### KILL 4 — V2's chorus was a sentence about counting.
Draft: *"I came in on the three. I stayed."* — F2's named fail state, word for word. A report of the device
is not the device. **Final:** the numeral is moved to the front and given its own full stop, so the hook
*is* a count — ***Three. I came in on the three.*** Same information, same syllable budget, and now the
room has something to shout that is a number rather than a summary.

## ⭐ THE RESTRUCTURE — the answer became the spine

The four-line stanza is unchanged (**AABB**), but in the drafts the answering half was decoration. It is
now **structural in all four**: the B pair is a fixed two-line answer that **never varies within a song**
and **differs between songs**, and it supplies the B rhyme by construction.

| variation | the answer (returns after every stanza) | what the return is doing |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *(One. Two.) / (Somebody has to be the two.)* | the count, and the only thesis the song has |
| **V2** | *(In. In.) / (Early and in.)* | her wrongness stated as a position, not an apology |
| **V3** | *(Near. Near.) / (Not touching. Near enough to hear.)* | distance measured, contact refused |
| **V4** | *(Last one.) / (This is the last one.)* | finitude as fuel, said flatly, every eight bars |

⛔ **A byte-identical chorus is correct and requires no note.** I am not going to write one. This paragraph
is about the *answer*, which is a different object, and it is here because it changed.

## THE STYLE PROMPTS, REBUILT

All four drafts were between 230 and 470 characters against a floor of 850. Rebuilt to **957 / 895 / 896 /
892** — inside the 870–960 write-into band, every one ending as a complete sentence. What went in:

1. **A vivid first five seconds, per variation, and no two the same** — V1 opens on four bars of the
   two-horn figure alone over a crowd already murmuring; V2 opens **cold** on the trombone a full bar
   early; V3 on an organ stab and eight bars of walking bass with the room talking under it; V4 on a bar
   towel hitting a wet counter and chairs going up.
2. **Full vocalist spec with spatial staging** — gender, age band, accent where it earns its place, two
   tone descriptors, two quirks, and **where she is standing**: on the floor, not a stage; a step behind
   the horns; off-axis; at floor level with the room thinning behind her.
3. **A kinetic defect in every one** — horns a bar early and never corrected (V1, V2), the organ flat from
   bar eleven onward (V3), the kick a hair late and hand-claps on the wrong side of the beat (V2), the
   count never quantised (V4).
4. **Explicit acoustic / no-acoustic declarations** — real horns in a real room, live kit; **no orchestral
   strings, no synth pads**, stated positively at the close of each prompt rather than left to the exclude
   field.
5. **Two exact Source-2 phrases per prompt, and no phrase used twice across the four** — counted in the
   shipped prompt text, case-sensitive, not in this document. V1: *shambling rock 'n' roll* + *natural
   entropy — recording hum, dissonant scrapes, the odd messy recording*. V2: *rambunctious, unwieldy* +
   *high-voltage guitars and Close Encounters synths*. V3: *gorgeously restrained beats* + *guitar diverts
   into a weepy countermelody*. V4: *low-slung bass, swelling synths* + *bone-rattling breakdown with
   battering drums and explosions of guitar*.
6. **The historical element demoted to a visitor in the wording itself** — every prompt says *a single
   rocksteady half-time visit*, once, at the join. ⛔ No prompt names a period. A bed named as
   *1979 two-tone* renders as a pastiche of 1979 two-tone, which is a museum.

**Exclude prompts written separately** — 758 / 694 / 667 / 647 characters, concrete tokens only. The
avoidance language is out of the style field entirely, because Suno reads that field as **positive**.

## THE SMALLER FIXES, ONE LINE EACH

- **V1 v.1 line 2** — *"Nobody fixes it. He carries on"* did not actually rhyme under the measure. Now
  *"The song stays long."* Same meaning, and the couplet closes.
- **V1 outro / V2 outro / V3 outro were converging** on one leaving-evening-thinning rhyme. Four songs,
  not one song in four coats. V2's outro is now *sight / tonight*, V3's is *wide / outside*, V4's runs on
  *in / in* so it lands back inside its own chorus. V1 keeps the original.
- **"Bar seventeen" was on the table for V3 and is banned here.** It is the exact bar in SUNNA's own DNA
  example, and reaching for a Flair's specific phrasing is the documented L12 failure. V3 uses **bar
  eleven**, and the organ has been flat *since about half seven*, which is funnier and is ours.
- **V3's answer line was lengthened after measurement, not before.** *(Near enough to hear.)* became
  *(Not touching. Near enough to hear.)* because `profile_file()` returned **8.31** alliteration per
  hundred words against a floor of 11.0 — the pair's one gate failure, found by the function rather than
  by eye. The replacement states the variation's own subject and returns eighteen times. **15.54 after.**
- **Every imperative in all four is now a number.** *One. Two. Three. Go.* — an external task, which is
  Spolin's cure for self-consciousness. **Encouragement makes it worse**, so there is none anywhere.
- **Nobody in any of the four touches anybody.** V3 says so out loud; the other three simply never do it.
- **The turn is explicit and plain in all four**, placed in verse three: *he was not brave, nobody saw him
  start* — which means **the second one is the one who gets seen.** That is the finding, and if it is not
  said in words a stranger can retell, the song is fog.

## THE ONE THING I AM KEEPING THAT A PLAYLIST WOULD RATHER I DID NOT

**The horns come in a bar early and nobody ever fixes it — including at the very end, where they do it
again, identically.** It is not a joke that pays off, it does not become right, and it is the last musical
event before the figure plays out alone. A playlist wants that corrected on the repeat. It is not going to
be. That is the friction, and the reason it is *this* friction rather than a decorative one is that the
song's entire argument is that being wrong in company is a different substance from being wrong alone —
so the arrangement had better be wrong in company too.

---

## THE ROOM'S OWN NOISE — the corona, checked

In every one of the four, the room's chatter is **audible from the opening second** (written into the style
prompt, so it is there before it is needed), **covered by the flash** (the low unison, two bars, chest not
ears), and **bare immediately after**. ⭐ And in all four the count then continues **with nothing under
it**, in the lyric, as separate numbered lines a listener can count along with. **Nobody can quantise a
line count away.** If a render fills the gap, the count is still in the words and the failure is legible
instead of silent. That is the best available answer, and I am not going to pretend it is a guarantee.

## Self-critique of this pass

**I killed four things and added one.** That is a subtractive pass, and subtraction is how a set of songs
gets thinner while every individual gate goes green. The one addition — *"Two is a room. One is a room
with one"* — is doing the work of the line I removed, and I think it is better, but I want it on the
record that a censor and an editor make the same edits and only one of them replaces what it takes.

**And the risk I cannot edit away:** four songs in one room, with one device, one answer form and one
rhyme architecture, will read as four takes rather than four songs to anyone who hears them back to back.
The angles are genuinely different — a count, a wrong entrance, an elbow, a last song — but the *bed* is
the same by assignment and the *form* is the same by design. **If one of these four ships, it should ship
alone.**
