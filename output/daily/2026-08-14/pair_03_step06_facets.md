encouragement or *you'll get there* is an immediate REPAIR, and facet 1 is weighted to make that

## 2. THE KILL SWITCHES (binary — any hit voids the prompt regardless of score)

1. **PITY SWEEP.** Any line of pity, coaxing, encouragement, reassurance or forecast aimed at the person
   at the edge. **Includes kindness.** The failure mode here is not cruelty, it is *concern*.
2. **SECOND-PERSON SWEEP.** D8′ discharges here as **SOLITUDE** — there is no second person to address,
   so the ban discharges by there being nobody to break it with. **Zero instances of "you"/"your" in any
   sung line.** This is machine-checkable and it is the cleanest discharge available to the run.
3. **CONGRATULATION SWEEP** *(Adorno's standing veto)*. The edge does not get congratulated for being the
   edge either. "The mix out here is better" is admissible **only as acoustics** — the low end piles up at
   a boundary; that is physics, not virtue — and it must be paid for with what the wall does *not* get.
4. **D13.** Not a song about dancing. This pair is one of only two permitted near a floor; the floor is the
   **method** (it makes the low end), never the subject.
5. **PASTICHE SWEEP.** If the style prompt reads as 1973 rather than 2026 with 1973 walking through it, repair.
6. **SOURCE-2 FLOOR.** ≥2 exact Bandcamp phrases **counted in the shipped prompt text**, varied per variation.
7. **THE REFRAIN IS NOT DISCUSSED.** No note, no justification, no pre-emptive mutation. Byte-identical is correct.
