## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

An unresolved reverence, brittle and doubled. Ethereal folk-ambient in D Dorian at a drifting 72 BPM, the young layer limping a beat while the old drone underneath holds still. First five seconds, texture underfoot: discrete granulated ice-ticks over a pitched-down wind-drone under the water like a held breath. A clear, restrained female alto sings close and dry over the ice, full tone withheld to one bare final lift; a detuned double one beat behind sings the star's name once at the final chorus like a reflection, then vanishes. The single percussive element is processed ice-tick-and-creak, brittle, bar-count-drifting, moving with the water. At the bridge everything young drops out — one ice-creak, then the old drone alone five seconds. The drone holds under the outro without resolving. Voice center-dry, drone wide, ice moving, air glassy but dark; bittersweet centre held — bluesy soul sadness under a rumor of rave sunrise; no kit, no pads.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: on black lake ice at night, singing to the star that outlived its twin, mistaking its reflection for the dead one while the ice decides]
[SONG FORM: THE MEASUREMENT — Intro · V1 doubled · V2 misread · Pre-Chorus · Hinge-Chorus · V3 danger · V4 · Chorus 2 · V5 breath · V6 seam · V7 · Bridge creak · Final Chorus · Outro]

[Disc_Vocal: clear_restrained_alto | detuned_double_one_beat_late | dry_no_reverb | Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: under_ice_wind_drone | felt_in_chest_not_ears | Mono_Sub_Lock]

[Intro - EMO:Anticipation - No beats - ice-ticks over drone]
*the ice speaks before I trust it*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Fascination, Unease - Alto - clear, close, restrained]
There are two of you tonight on the black glass —
one in the sky, one where the still waters pass.
The star its dead twin's fire still feeds
is standing up above the frozen reeds,
and standing, too, an inch below my feet,
a second star the water keeps complete.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Shock, Sorrow - Alto - low]
For half a breath I read the lower one
as her — the sister, back, the dying undone —
and then the water shivers and it's gone
to just reflection, just a trick of dawn
that hasn't come. The dead do not come back.
The light just doubles on the freezing black.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Unease - Alto - LPF 400 Hz]
I know the lower one is only glare.
I want it to be her. She isn't there.
The one above is all the twin I get,
and even that one hasn't left me yet —

[Chorus - EMO:Wonder, Terror - Alto - mid, held back]
because it says: six thousand years old, and the one
it lost is older, gone before it shone.
The light I love left ages before me;
the twin I mourned was dark past history.
I answer to the doubled, dying spark:
which one of you am I singing to the dark?
Which one of you? Which one of you?

[Verse 3 - EMO:Terror, Unease - Alto - low, breath audible]
The ice ticks once, deciding if it holds;
below the black glass, water that takes you cold.
I feel the surface reason through my weight.
I do not step. I stand. I hold. I wait.
The cold comes up to test me through my sole.
The lake is doing math about my whole.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Awe, Grief - Alto - low, restrained]
So both my stars are older than my grief:
the one that shines, the one beyond belief.
I came to mourn a sister long put out
and found her brother also going out,
just slower, just with farther still to fall.
I stand on ice above them both, and small.
And which is warmer — real, or in the glass?
Neither. Both are cold. Both come to pass.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Wonder, Terror - Alto - mid, held back]
And it keeps doubling, keeps on doubling,
the one that's here, the one that's gone.
I answer to the water's troubling
mirror where they both go on.
I answer to the doubled, dying spark:
which one of you am I singing to the dark?
Which one of you? Which one of you?

[Verse 5 - EMO:Shock, Reflection - Alto - low, breath held]
The reflection is the truest lie I've known:
it gives me back the sister, then she's gone.
I hold my breath to keep the water still,
to keep her one more second if I will.
My breath gives out. The mirror swims with sky.
I keep the vigil my own lungs deny.
The ice ticks. The water moves. She goes.
I loved a light that no one living knows.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Terror, Grief - Alto - low, restrained]
The jetty planks give out to open black.
Past here it's only ice, and no way back
if the ice decides tonight it will not hold.
I stand on what is deciding. I am cold.
A seam runs out from under me like light —
a long white sentence written on the night.
It stops mid-word. It might resume. It might.
Above me, and below me, the same star,
old as a wound, and neither near, nor far.

[Verse 7 - EMO:Awe, Reflection - Alto - low]
I came to grieve a sister of the sky
and found her brother also going by,
just slower, and reflected, and alone,
and lighting me from farther than the bone
can understand. I stand between the two.
The ice ticks once. I do not know which is true.

[Bridge - EMO:Isolation - No beats - drone ALONE, one creak, voice out]
*one ice-creak, then the drone alone*

[Final Chorus - EMO:Awe, Grief - Alto - full tone, name doubled, unresolved]
So the light I love was old before my birth,
and older still the fire that left the earth.
The star its dead twin's fire still feeds
(its dead twin's fire still feeds)
is doubled where the black ice bleeds.
I will not call this two. I'll call it gone.
I will not call this two. I'll call it gone.
The ice still ticks. The lake is still doing math.
I am the remainder standing in its path.

[Outro - EMO:Melancholy - No beats - drone stays]
*the drone holds under the ice*
(the water keeps the second star awhile)
