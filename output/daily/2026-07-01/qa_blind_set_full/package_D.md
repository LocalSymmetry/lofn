## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Vigilant HyperRaaga watch-drone in F-sharp harmonic minor at a 108 BPM raga-pulse, three sound sources only, no acoustic band, no drum kit. A dry wooden clock-tick opens the song hard-left, limping a hair off the pulse — a kinetic defect that never syncs — while a tanpura-like microtonal drone rises center-depth beneath it, wide and unblinking, a granular flicker in its sustain like light from shells already dying. A watchful mid-register female voice logs its entries flat and un-triumphant, close and left-of-center, never belted, breath audible between lines. A gamaka bends the raised seventh, a note that aches to resolve and is refused all song. The ledger entries shorten as the shells dim; past the midpoint one first-person line cracks the log to a near-whisper. The bridge kills the tick and lets the drone fill the room alone, the seventh still hanging. The final phrase stops mid-word, no cadence granted.
