## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

128 BPM glossy Baile Phonk / hyper-quantized pop, E-flat major, sealed-transit sheen: female alto, B-flat3 to E-flat5 mix register, clipped precise diction, cold not angry. Signature device is refusal — the surface never ruptures; a pitch-shifted ad-lib doubles the hook two cents sharp while the backing harmony's warm major third folds to a dry unison at the bridge, master level flat. All-electronic: felt-not-heard 40 Hz sine sub mono-center, full runtime; jingle-bright synth topline quantized to a fault; recycled-transit-air hiss as garnish only; air-band shimmer wide. Lead dry close-mic dead-center, harmony a third above at half level. Opening five seconds: the looping ad jingle over the warm topline, no riser. Bridge at 2:10: four bars of sub and two voices narrowing to one pitch, loudness unchanged; the identical chorus line returns at 2:40 with an added judgment. Mastered bright; the poison is the prettiest sound in the mix.
