Signature device is refusal — the gloss never breaks; a pitch-shifted ad-lib sells care back two cents sharp, a scanner chirp quantized on the offbeat is the one kinetic quirk, and the harmony's warm major third settles onto the lead's note at the bridge like a total due, master level flat. All-electronic: felt-not-heard 40 Hz sine sub mono-center under the jingle-bright quantized topline; fluorescent air shimmer as garnish only. Lead dry close-mic dead-center, harmony a third above at half level. Opening five seconds: one point-of-sale beep, then the warm topline over a hushed aisle. Bridge at 2:10: sub and two voices agreeing on one figure, four bars, loudness unchanged; the identical chorus line returns judged at 2:40. Mastered bright; the machine breathes under the shining tile.
