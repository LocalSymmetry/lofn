## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

A tender cold-braided devotional lament in Neo-Baroque Luminist folk over an old-tongue drone, bluesy soul sadness held close, rubato 66 BPM, A minor-pentatonic, fully acoustic, warm analog. A close-mic intimate female alto leads, breath audible, a blue third bent on the leaving, power held to the very end, dry, dead-center. Signature one: a low old-tongue choral-drone of resynthesized Norse-Gaelic vowels, warm and wide in deep reverb start to last, its top edge eaten by hillside air-noise, the cold audibly winning the spectrum. Signature two: one cupped-hand pat, off-left, drifting rubato, stiffening slower and harder each refrain as the hands lose heat. It opens on breath and the crackle of a guarded ember before any pulse; verse bar-counts drift; at the hinge the pat drops and the moon-fact lands in cold silence; the bridge leaves the drone alone for seven seconds; the outro keeps the drone holding after the voice fails, unresolved, no lift.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: a cold open hillside — cupping a borrowed warmth from a hand not its first source, keeping it for the waning moon]
[SONG FORM: Cooling-Vow Ballad — the vow's object cools out of the grammar (I keep the warmth / I keep it warm / I keep, I keep), the last word lands on the wrong object; Intro / vow+erosion verses / cooling refrains / the warmth bites / Pre-Hinge / Hinge fact / cold wins / Bridge drone-alone 7s / turned refrain / Outro slips]

[Disc_Rhythm: cupped_hand_pat | stiffens_per_refrain | drops_at_hinge | no_kit_no_808 | Off_Left]
[Disc_Vocal: close_mic_female_alto | blue_third_on_the_leaving | breath_thinning_in_cold | power_withheld_to_final_line | Center_Front_Dry]
[Disc_Sub: drone_fundamental_A2 | felt_in_chest_not_ears | Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: old_tongue_vowel_choir | warm_wide_deep_reverb | top_edge_eaten_by_hillside_air | Wide_Far]
[Disc_Texture: guarded_ember_crackle | thin_cold_air_noise | Close_Then_Fading]

[Intro - EMO:Devotion, Foreboding - Alto close-mic, breath warming cupped hands - No beats]
*a small guarded ember crackles in cupped hands*
Someone warmed me who was not the first
And I carry it out to the cold
I hold it up to the going one
The moon one night past whole
It was given to me in the dark
And I give it on before it fails
Knowing the giving is already late

[Verse 1 - EMO:Devotion - Alto, dry center - vow then erosion couplets]
I will keep this warmth I did not make
I will hold it steady for your sake
But warmth is a thing that a hand lets go
And the cold is patient, and the cold will know
I will breathe on it to keep it lit
But my breath is small against all of it

[Refrain 1 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto over drone - object whole]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I keep the warmth, I keep the warmth
For what is leaving, I keep the warmth
(the warmth, the warmth)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Devotion, Unease - Alto - the hillside takes its share]
I will shield it from the hillside wind
I will give what was given, again, again
But my hands are borrowed and the hour is late
And a warmth passed on cannot make it wait
I will pass it up to the leaving light
But the leaving light is out of my sight

[Refrain 2 - EMO:Yearning - Alto - the warmth cools out of the line]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I keep it warm, I keep it warm
(the warmth, the warm)

[Verse 3 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto - borrowed fire]
I will fold my body round the coal
I will keep the heat if not the whole
But a borrowed fire is not mine to hold
And whatever is borrowed goes back to the cold
I will guard it slower, guard it near
But slower will not keep it here

[Verse 4 - EMO:Pain, Devotion - Alto - the warmth bites; the door refused]
The small heat sharpens as it dies
It bites the palm where it used to rise
Even the warmth takes the cold one's part
A blister where I held it to my heart
The door behind me still stands gold
But I did not come out here to be consoled

[Pre-Hinge - EMO:Yearning, Helplessness - Alto - pat stiffens, slows]
And I would give my own warmth over
If my warmth were mine to give
I would keep the small fire burning
Every cold I had to live
But a hand is a thin shelter
And the cold has time to live

[Refrain 3 - EMO:Yearning, Foreboding - Alto - object gone]
Moon at the edge of full—
I keep, I keep

[Hinge - EMO:Dread - Alto alone, breath visible-cold - No beats]
*the cupped-hand pat stops*
Ninety-seven, and already going out
It answers my keeping with a going
It will not wait to be kept warm
And the ember dims faster than my breath

[Verse 5 - EMO:Dread, Grief - Alto - the cold wins]
The wind gets under my folded hands
The small heat leaves the way heat always lands
On open ground with no roof to give
And I keep a warmth that will not live
The last coal greys inside my palm
And I hold the grey like it was warm
The cold gets everything in the end
For a warmth I could not tend
It cost me the heat I was handed free
Now the cold that has her is holding me
And my palm still leans to the empty glow
The way a hand keeps holding what let go

[Bridge - EMO:Devotion - old-tongue choir alone seven seconds, wordless - No beats]
*old-tongue drone alone, wordless*
(the warmth older than the hand that carries it)

[Refrain turned - EMO:Grief, Resolve - Alto, full power at last - the vow turns]
Moon at the edge of full, going, going grey
I will not keep the warmth for you
I will keep the cold with you
And I will not pretend it stays
I will hold the going as it greys
(with you, with you)

[Outro - EMO:Devotion, Solitude - Alto to hush - drone holds past the voice]
Keep the warmth, keep the cold
I could not warm the going one
So I hold the going cold
And the grey coal stays behind me
For a moon it can no longer warm
Keep the warmth
Keep the cold
Keep the warmth—
Keep the cold
