## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Cold Glitch-Baroque deposition-pop, half-time 140 BPM in unresolved B Locrian, opening on a soft UI-chime sine and a cursor-blink tick dead-center while a 30-60Hz sine sub-bass, mono-locked and felt in the chest not the ears, grounds the whole runtime without a pause. A breathy close-mic'd female confessor, early 20s, types-and-sings itemized feedback center-front, flat and deliberate; sparse code-scratch keystroke clicks fall left-of-center on a lopsided half-time grid, the chime sine and cursor pulse sit narrow, dry dead-room air, no tail. Density rises; the gloss never breaks. At the second chorus her submitted confession comes back as ad copy: pitch-shifted up, de-essed and exciter-bright like a commercial voiceover, stereo-widened while she stays mono-center, a touch louder — her own words re-cut as the campaign — fronting the post-bridge alone. The bridge strips to bare sub-bass for four seconds. Fully synthetic, no acoustic sources.
