## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Cold clinical Glitch-Baroque deposition-pop, half-time 140 BPM in unresolved B Locrian, opening on a red recording-tone sine blinking dead-center while a 30-60Hz sine sub-bass, mono-locked and felt in the chest not the ears, swells beneath it and never once pauses. A breathy close-mic'd female confessor, early 20s, itemizes contrition center-front, hardening from soft breath to flat clerked delivery; sparse cold code-scratch clicks limp left-of-center on a lopsided half-time grid, granulated dead-plastic booth hum narrow behind her, dry dead-room air, no tail. Density rises; the chain never roughens. At the second chorus the machine answers: her own recorded phrases return a clean fifth up, portamento-gliding off-grid, dry, seated just behind her right shoulder above the lead — the confession resold as a sweetened ad-lib — and it owns the post-bridge alone. The bridge strips to bare sub-bass for four seconds. Fully synthetic, no acoustic sources.
