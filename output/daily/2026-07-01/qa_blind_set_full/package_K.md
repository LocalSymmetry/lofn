## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Clinical vigil-raga in F-sharp harmonic minor at a 108 BPM raga-pulse, three sources only, no acoustic band. A sidereal tracking-motor opens the song hard-left — a servo ratchet ticking a hair off-grid, dome machinery as the only percussion — over a low tanpura-like microtonal drone bedded center for the whole length. A close-mic mid-register female voice reads a night-log procedurally: coordinates, times, frost on the eyepiece, breath fogging the glass, dry and un-dramatic. The raised seventh aches and is refused; a sargam-adjacent ornament tilts the wounding distance microtonally as the voice thins to a whisper on the answered number. One first-person break cracks the clinical surface past the midpoint, diction slipping from log to confession, then the log resumes colder while frost climbs from glass to lashes. The bridge drops the motor and leaves the drone alone. The final word breaks off mid-syllable. No soprano, no gloss, no comfort.

two shells beside it, one young and one old.
Coordinates logged. My breath fogs on the glass.
The eyepiece frost bites at the rim of my eye.
I do not wipe it yet.

The motor ticks the dome across the night,
a hair behind the stars it means to hold.
I note the yellow. I note the purple lapse.
The record grows. My feet go numb on stone.
I do not move. I only mark.
The night has no idea that I am here.
The stars have less than that.

I log their fading. I log the pace.
I file the doom the way I file the weather.
The cold comes off the concrete, finds my spine.
I stay. I write. I stay.
The instrument sees far and cannot speak.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Premonition - Female Vocalist - frost climbing the barrel]
