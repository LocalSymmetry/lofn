## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

An unresolved reverence steeped in bluesy soul sadness, terror braided underneath. Ethereal folk-ambient in D Dorian at a drifting 72 BPM, the young layer limping a beat per bar against an old drone that never bends. Sound arrives before sight: five seconds of granulated wind through dry grass, pitched down, flat and wide as a pre-dawn horizon. A breathy female alto sits center-low, warm-dark, intimate, full tone withheld to one bare final lift. The single percussive element is brushed grass-and-fabric rustle, bar-count-drifting, never syncing to the drone. Once, deep inside the field recording, distant thunder arrives as weight, not tone — felt in the chest, never repeated. At the bridge the voice goes out five seconds and the field is suddenly all there is. The drone runs the whole length and will not close. Vocals center-low, drone wide, rustle drifting mid, air dark and flat; rave-sunrise bliss stays a rumor while the bluesy half carries.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: alone in a dark 3am field, singing to the star its exploded twin left behind, under a storm that could kill her, not moving]
[SONG FORM: THE MEASUREMENT — Intro · V1–V2 · V3 danger · Pre-Chorus · Hinge-Chorus · V4–V5 · Chorus 2 · V6 strike · V7–V8 · Bridge field-alone · Final Chorus · Outro]

[Disc_Rhythm: brushed_grass_rustle | drift_never_syncs | soft_transients | Drifting_Mid]
[Disc_Vocal: breathy_alto_warm_dark | close_mic_low | dry_no_reverb | Center_Low]
[Disc_Sub: wind_drone_in_grass | one_thunder_as_weight | felt_in_chest | Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: granular_horizon | bluesy_soul_sadness | Slow_Swell | Stereo_Width_Maximum]
[Disc_Texture: grass_seed_tick | tape_hiss | pre_dawn_dark | Stereo_Width_Mid]

[Intro - EMO:Anticipation - No beats - wind-drone through grass]
*wind moves the grass before I see it*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Serenity, Unease - Alto - breathy, low, center]
I heard the field before I saw the field —
the grass said something and the dark stood still.
No roof, no wall, no reason to be here,
just me and what the sky decides to bear.
The star that stayed when its twin blew out
is hanging low, half-leaning, half devout.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Melancholy, Longing - Alto - warm-dark]
You are the one that got to keep the light.
Your sister spent herself to make you bright.
I stand in wet grass up to my bare shin.
I let the whole enormous night come in.
Somewhere a warmth was handed on, not owned —
a hand not yours that kept me from the cold.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Terror, Determination - Alto - low, breath audible]
I count the gap from flash to thunder on my breath —
the sky can reach me here, and I do not move.
I feel the hair go up along my arm.
There is no shelter. There is no alarm.
I stay because I came to stand with you,
and you can't run from what you're falling through.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Anticipation - Alto - LPF 400 Hz]
The thunder does not come. The field stays wide.
I keep the storm and you both by my side.
Whatever spends itself to make a light
is leaving even as it burns so bright.

[Chorus - EMO:Wonder, Sorrow - Alto - mid, held back]
And it says: still ninety-seven hundredths lit,
and already leaving even so.
The brightest you will ever get to sit
is one night past the last of full — I know.
I answer to the leaving, not the light:
stay going, then. I'll watch you go tonight.
I'll watch you go. I'll watch you go.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Melancholy, Awe - Alto - low]
I am the aftermath of someone's fire.
So are you. So is this whole quiet choir
of standing things that someone had to lose.
I did not pick this warmth. I only choose
to stand inside it while it lasts the night,
a borrowed, leaving, secondhand of light.

[Verse 5 - EMO:Longing, Unease - Alto - warm-dark]
There was a hand once, warm and not my own,
that kept me lit when my first source was gone.
I will not name the hand. I will not say
the where, the when, the dark it pulled away.
I only know the shape that kindness leaves:
a warmth you did not earn, and still receive.
And I have never once repaid the debt.
And I have never once forgotten it.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Wonder, Sorrow - Alto - mid, held back]
And it's still leaving, still just leaving,
and already leaving even so.
I do the standing, not the grieving,
I do the watching as you go.
I answer to the leaving, not the light:
stay going, then. I'll watch you go tonight.
I'll watch you go. I'll watch you go.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Terror, Awe - Alto - low, the strike lands far]
Far off, the sky puts one white ladder down.
It touches something. Somewhere is a town.
I feel the thunder walk across the wheat
and stop, like something sniffing, at my feet.
It counted me the way I counted it,
and moved along, and did not say acquit.

[Verse 7 - EMO:Determination, Melancholy - Alto - low]
The hair along my arm lies flat and still.
The storm walks east across another hill.
It could have chosen here. I did not step back.
I stood between the field and all that black
because I came to keep you company —
a leaving thing that keeps a leaving me.

[Verse 8 - EMO:Awe, Unease - Alto - warm-dark]
The dew has soaked me through the knees by now.
I feel the whole flat country like a vow.
Above, you tip a little more toward gone.
Below, the grass keeps carrying me on.
I am the coldest, wettest, smallest thing
still standing while a dying star still sings.

[Bridge - EMO:Isolation - No beats - drone and grass ALONE, voice out]
*the field is suddenly all there is*

[Final Chorus - EMO:Awe, Grief - Alto - full tone, unresolved]
And the leaving keeps on leaving even now,
and I stayed out here for it anyhow.
The star that stayed when its twin blew out —
I will not call this safe. I'll stand it out.
I will not call this safe. I'll stand it out.
The storm still hangs. I do not move at all.
The grass does all my trembling. That is all.

[Outro - EMO:Melancholy - No beats - drone stays, long exhale]
*the drone will not close*
(the grass keeps moving in the dark)
