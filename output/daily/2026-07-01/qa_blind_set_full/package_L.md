## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

A grief-tender devotional lament in Neo-Baroque Luminist folk with old-tongue keening at its floor, bluesy soul sadness in every phrase, rubato 66 BPM, A minor-pentatonic, all organic. A close-mic intimate female alto leads, breath-forward, one microtonal slide kept for the held name, power held to the last line, dry, dead-center. Signature one: a low old-tongue choral-drone of resynthesized Norse-Gaelic phonemes almost-forming a name the ear never resolves, wide and far in long stone reverb, start to finish. Signature two: a knuckle-on-wood tap, off-right, drifting rubato, losing a tap each refrain, the name thinning from the grid. It opens on treeline wind arriving before the moon is seen, then one spoken syllable; verses limp in bar-count; at the hinge the tapping halts and the moon-fact lands bare; the bridge bares the half-name drone alone for seven seconds; the outro keeps it after the voice fails mid-syllable, unresolved, no crescendo.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: at an old churchyard wall under the waning moon, vowing to keep a name whose meaning may no longer fit what it named]
[SONG FORM: Name-Erosion Ballad — the refrain echo sheds syllables (the name / the na—) as recognition fails; Intro / vow+erosion verses / thinning refrains / mis-saying / Pre-Hinge / Hinge fact / recognition-fails / Bridge drone-alone 7s / turned refrain / Outro cut mid-syllable]

[Disc_Rhythm: knuckle_on_wood_tap | loses_one_tap_per_refrain | halts_at_hinge | no_drums_no_kit_no_808 | Off_Right]
[Disc_Vocal: close_mic_female_alto | microtonal_slide_on_the_held_name | breath_on_capsule_audible | power_withheld_to_final_line | Center_Front_Dry]
[Disc_Sub: drone_fundamental_A2 | felt_in_chest_not_ears | Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: old_tongue_phoneme_choir | half_formed_name_never_resolving | long_stone_reverb | Wide_Far]
[Disc_Texture: treeline_wind_arriving_first | churchyard_night_air | cassette_tape_saturation | Behind_Center]

[Intro - EMO:Yearning, Foreboding - Alto close-mic, spoken syllable first - No beats]
*wind through a treeline, arriving first*
Before I looked up, I said it
The old name, into the cold
The one I keep against forgetting
The one I made my promise for
The moon one night past full
And already thinning at the rim
And I say it knowing it is late

[Verse 1 - EMO:Devotion - Alto, dry center - vow then erosion couplets]
I will keep your name inside my mouth
I will say it north, I will say it south
But a name is a shape for a thing that stays
And the thing it holds is turning away
I will carve each letter on my tongue
But the thing I name is nearly gone

[Refrain 1 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto over drone - echo whole]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I keep the name, I keep the name
For what is leaving, I keep the name
(the name, the name)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Devotion, Unease - Alto - teaching it to what cannot forget]
I will teach it to the churchyard stone
I will leave it where the wind is blown
But the sound goes out and does not come home
And the shape I saved is standing alone
I will spell it to the frozen ground
But the ground gives back an emptier sound

[Refrain 2 - EMO:Yearning - Alto - echo thinning, one tap gone]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I keep the name, I keep the name
(the name, the na—)

[Verse 3 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto - the word wears]
I will hold it like a small warm coal
I will keep the sound if not the whole
But a word wears thin the more it's said
And the thing it meant is nearly dead
I will say it once more to be sure
But surer will not make it endure

[Verse 4 - EMO:Unease, Devotion - Alto - the mis-saying, unnoticed]
I said it at moonrise, said it true
At the wall I said it — it came out new
A vowel had thinned where the middle was
And I did not notice at first, because
A mouth keeps the shape when the meaning goes
The way a glove keeps the hand it knows

[Pre-Hinge - EMO:Yearning, Helplessness - Alto - knuckle-wood thins]
And I would give the whole of language
For the shape to hold its ground
I would keep it in the churchyard
Every winter it comes round
But a name is a small breath
Against a going that makes no sound

[Refrain 3 - EMO:Yearning, Foreboding - Alto - echo thinnest]
Moon at the edge of full—
I keep the name
(the na—)

[Hinge - EMO:Dread - Alto alone, breath catches - No beats]
*the knuckle-wood stops*
Ninety-seven, and already turning thin
It answers my naming with a going
It will not wait to be named
And the wind gives back a shape I don't know

[Verse 5 - EMO:Dread, Grief - Alto - recognition fails, enacted]
I called the old name to the dark
And the shape came back without its mark
Will the word still fit when the thing is gone
Or is a name just a light I leave on
I said the name and heard it fail
A held breath thinning into pale
The syllable stood in the cold
For a meaning I could not hold
The letters stand like stones above a bed
Of everything the name no longer said
And the sound still leans to the empty air
The way a mouth keeps shaping what's not there

[Bridge - EMO:Devotion - old-tongue choir alone seven seconds, almost a name - No beats]
*old-tongue drone alone, forming a half-name*
(the name older than the one who keeps it)

[Refrain turned - EMO:Grief, Resolve - Alto, full power at last - the vow turns]
Moon at the edge of full, going, going pale
I will not keep the name for you
I will keep the losing with you
And I will not pretend it stays
I will only say it as it fades
(with you, with you)

[Outro - EMO:Devotion, Solitude - Alto to hush - drone keeps the half-name past the voice]
Keep the name, keep the losing
I could not name the going one
So I name the going
And the syllable stays behind me
For a moon it can no longer name
Keep the name
Keep the losing
Keep the na—
