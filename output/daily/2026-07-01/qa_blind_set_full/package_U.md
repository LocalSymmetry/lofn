## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Anticipatory-grief organic dub in C Phrygian at 96 BPM, swung and warm, opening in the first five seconds on a bubbling granular texture rising close on the right like heat off a bare forearm, over a deep dub-bass drone felt in the chest, mono and center, an inherited pulse. A warm androgynous dub-register voice sits close and center with audible inhales, breath-forward, no soprano gloss. Two timelines stay separate: the mono chest-drone is the twin's finished death; the close-right bubbling is the survivor's fuse, its pitch creeping upward imperceptibly across the runtime, a fuse shortening; a sparse right-panned delay answers only the inheritance lines. Verse endings hand their last word forward, a chain of hand-me-downs. It never brightens; at the bridge the bubbling and delay drop out and the drone stands alone for seven seconds, and the outro thins to the drone, unresolved. Fully electronic and granular, no acoustic instruments.


## EXCLUDE PROMPT

EDM riser, big-room drop, festival synth, trap hi-hat rolls, four-on-the-floor, bright pads, crystalline soprano, gospel choir, orchestral crescendo, major-key resolution, radio-pop hook, acoustic guitar, live drums, tambourine, autotune sheen, pitched-up vocal, cheerful glockenspiel, uplifting modulation, warm sitcom strings, brass, saxophone, triumphant ending, clean four-bar loop lock, sterile quantization, chirpy arpeggio, pretty shimmer pad, spa ambience, new-age calm, static unchanging drone-pitch gloss.


## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: the survivor star inherited its twin's explosion - the same fuse lit later; the warmth on my skin is that fuse burning]
[SONG FORM: Double-Helix Parallel - inheritance braid, each verse handing its last word forward (anadiplosis) -> handing-down interlude -> bridge strips to drone -> fuse-spine choruses -> outro thins, unresolved]

[Disc_Rhythm: limping_dub_skank | delay_answers_inheritance | Right_mid]
[Disc_Vocal: warm_dub_register | audible_inhales | dry_Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: inherited_pulse_drone | chest_weight | past_Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: dark_slow_swell | no_shimmer | Center_Back_low]
[Disc_Texture: bubbling_skin_heat | pitch_creeping_up | fuse_Close_Right]

[Intro - EMO:Foreboding - Instrumental - inhale, bubble]
*granular bubbling rises, close as skin*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - breath audible]
Your twin was born with fire he could not keep,
and you were born out of the same coiled seam.
His blast crossed ages while the fields slept;
yours is still braided into your warm beam.
One fuse burned out; one fuse is still a gleam.
The same hand lit them both, the same slow match -
one is already ash; one is the dream.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - dream handed on]
The dream is this: I stand on frost-glassed ground,
no shelter, just your low inherited light.
Your brother spent his fire and let it down;
you spend yours warming me across the night -
same seam, same fault, same tender, fatal bright.
He showed me what the ending looks like, once;
you'll show me slower, and in plain sight.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - sight handed on]
In plain sight, then: I know what warms me here.
I didn't come to warm my hands and leave.
What heats my skin is what I'll one day grieve.

[Chorus - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - the fuse spine]
You carry the same fuse your brother carried,
lit later, lit longer - but lit all the same.
The life you pour on me is the fuse burning;
the warmth I keep is you, going up in flame.
One fire is ash; one fire still wears your name.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - name handed on]
His name burned down to black across the track,
and yours is spending out on fields like these.
His light still reaches me, a cold almanac;
yours reaches me and tells me its disease.
The warmth that finds my arms in this hard freeze
is the same fire that will one day end you -
the gift and the grenade share one release.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - braid tightens]
He willed the fuse to you the way blood wills
a face, a fault, a fever down the years.
He spent his fast; you're spending yours in spills
of light that look like warmth and land like tears.
Same seam, same coil, the same held-back arrears:
the past death taught me how the future reads -
one paid in full, one paying down its years.

[Interlude - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - seam speaks]
He handed it down. (It's warm.)
He handed it on. (It's lit.)
What did he leave you? (His fire.)
What will you leave me? (The dark.)

[Bridge - EMO:Sorrow - Dub Voice - hinge; exhale after]
Six thousand light-years of you reaching me,
and the reaching is the fuse - I feel it, I feel it.
Your brother's ending and your coming one
are one fire, handed on, I can't blow free.
*the drone alone, seven seconds*

[Verse 5 - EMO:Revelation - Dub Voice - free handed on]
Free - no. I won't ask you to be safe; I can't.
You are what your dead brother was, delayed.
I'll stand inside the warmth and let it haunt
my arms, and not pretend I'm not afraid.
The fuse is lit; it always was; it's made
of the same life that warms me while I watch it.
Inheritance is just a debt unpaid.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - unpaid handed on]
Unpaid, his fuse ran out; I never saw -
I only got the letter of his light.
The fuse in you is lit, and that's the law
you were born under: coiled and warm and bright.
He spent his in the far, unwatched, cold night;
you'll spend yours here, where I can feel it going -
one debt is settled, one is paying white.

[Pre-Chorus 2 - EMO:Yearning - Dub Voice - lower]
So warm me while you can, star. I understand:
the warmth is what will take you when it's done.
I hold the fuse. I hold it in my hand.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - recontextualized]
You carry the same fuse your brother carried -
Star, the blast that took him sleeps in you.
The life you pour on me is the fuse burning:
one fire is ash; one fire is coming true.

[Final Chorus - EMO:Sorrow x Devotion - Dub Voice - no lift]
Same seam, same coil, the ended and the ending -
the life you pour on me is the fuse burning.
One fire has paid its whole inheritance;
one fire is in my hands, still warm, still turning,
and I will hold it while it goes. I'm learning.

[Outro - EMO:Foreboding x Yearning - Dub Voice - thinning]
Warm me, warm me, with the thing that ends you.
I'll take the heat and know just what it is:
it's you, it's you, still burning down to nothing -
mmm - and the nothing still arrives as this.
