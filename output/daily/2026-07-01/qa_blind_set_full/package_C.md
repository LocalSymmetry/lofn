## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

A quietly devastating devotional walking-lament in Neo-Baroque Luminist folk on an old-tongue floor, bluesy soul sadness throughout, rubato 66 BPM, A minor-pentatonic, fully acoustic. A close-mic intimate female alto leads, breath-forward, one microtonal slide exiting each refrain, power withheld to the last line, dry, dead-center. Signature one: a low old-tongue choral-drone of resynthesized Norse-Gaelic vowels, wide and far the entire runtime, the one voice still walking when hers cannot. Signature two: footfall-on-gravel percussion, off-center, drifting rubato, mixed one step farther back each refrain, the walk audibly receding until the hinge halts it. It opens on breath, gravel underfoot, and a door closing behind; verse bar-counts drift; at the hinge the footfalls stop and the moon-fact lands in open dark; the bridge bares the drone alone for seven seconds; past the stopped voice the drone keeps walking, unresolved, no arrival.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: walking away from a house into the dark, vowing from the first step to leave WITH the waning moon, not hold it back]
[SONG FORM: Receding-Walk Ballad — each refrain echo arrives a word shorter and a step farther back (the leaving, the leaving / …leaving), the outro echo recedes past the voice; Intro / vow+erosion verses / receding refrains / the eaten trail / Pre-Hinge / Hinge fact / isolation / Bridge drone-alone 7s / deepened refrain / Outro recedes]

[Disc_Rhythm: footfall_on_gravel | a_step_farther_back_each_refrain | halts_at_hinge | no_kit_no_808 | Off_Center_Receding]
[Disc_Vocal: close_mic_female_alto | microtonal_slide_leaving_each_refrain | breath_on_capsule_audible | power_withheld_to_final_line | Center_Front_Dry]
[Disc_Sub: drone_fundamental_A2 | felt_in_chest_not_ears | Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: old_tongue_vowel_choir | wide_and_far_entire_runtime | long_stone_reverb | Wide_Far]

[Intro - EMO:Devotion, Resolve - Alto close-mic, breath in step with gravel - No beats]
*gravel underfoot, a door closing behind*
I stopped trying to hold you still
And I turned to walk you down
Not to keep you where you were
The moon one night past whole
But to keep you all the way
Down into the dark you go
Knowing the walking is already late

[Verse 1 - EMO:Devotion - Alto, dry center - vow then erosion couplets]
I will not keep you where you are
I will walk beside the falling star
But the road goes dark the way you go
And I lose the ground I used to know
I will keep my eyes on your decline
But your decline is leaving mine

[Refrain 1 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto over drone - footfalls enter close]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I keep the leaving, I keep the leaving
Not holding back, I keep the leaving
(the leaving, the leaving)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Devotion, Unease - Alto - the walking's cost]
I will match my step to your decline
I will call it keeping, call it kind
But to walk you out is to walk away
From the lit house and the kept day
I will name it faith and not retreat
But faith is cold beneath my feet

[Refrain 2 - EMO:Yearning - Alto - echo a step farther back]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I keep the leaving, I keep the leaving
(the leaving… leaving)

[Verse 3 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto - the road only leads away]
I will go as far as you have gone
I will keep the walking, keep it on
But a road that only leads away
Is a promise I cannot repay
I will swear to stay beside your light
But beside is only where it's night

[Verse 4 - EMO:Apprehension, Devotion - Alto - the eaten trail-marks]
I notched the fence post, dropped a stone
Small claims against the way I'd known
But the field repeats itself in the dark
And the dark does not return a mark
Each stone I leave, the night leaves too
There is no trail except to you

[Pre-Hinge - EMO:Yearning, Helplessness - Alto - footfalls thinning]
And I would walk the whole horizon
If the walking kept you near
I would keep the leaving with you
Every year of every year
But a step is a small keeping
Against a going I can't steer

[Refrain 3 - EMO:Yearning, Foreboding - Alto - echo almost out of earshot]
Moon at the edge of full—
I keep the leaving
(…leaving)

[Hinge - EMO:Dread - Alto alone in open dark, breath catches - No beats]
*the footfalls stop*
Ninety-seven, and already walking on
It answers my keeping with a going
It will not wait to be walked
And the next step finds no ground at all

[Verse 5 - EMO:Dread, Grief - Alto - isolation enacted]
I walked so far to walk with you
The house is gone, the field is too
There is no path back from where I've come
And the leaving is the only home
I kept the going and lost the ground
And there is no lit house to be found
The dark takes both of us in the end
For a nearness I could not defend
I followed the going to learn its name
And the going and I are now the same
And my foot still leans to the empty road
The way a step keeps going where none showed

[Bridge - EMO:Devotion - old-tongue choir alone seven seconds, wordless - No beats]
*old-tongue drone alone, wordless*
(the leaving older than the one who walks it)

[Refrain deepened - EMO:Grief, Resolve - Alto, full power at last - the vow owns its cost]
Moon at the edge of full, going, going gone
I could not keep you, I could not stay
So I keep the leaving, I keep the way
And I will not turn around for home
I will keep the going as my own
(the way, the way)

[Outro - EMO:Devotion, Solitude - Alto to hush - the drone keeps walking past the voice]
Keep the leaving, keep the way
I could not walk the going home
So I walk the going on
And the road goes on without me
For a moon it cannot reach
Keep the leaving
Keep the way
(the way… the way…)
