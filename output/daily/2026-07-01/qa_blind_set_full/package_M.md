## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Cavernous dread-dub elegy in C Phrygian at 96 BPM with a limping, swung skank, opening in the first five seconds on a bare deep dub-bass drone down a dark corridor while a bone-dry wristwatch tick, hard left and close as a cuff, is answered one swung beat late by its own echo hard right, pitch-bent a quarter-tone flat, farther back in tape-fluttered murk — two clocks on one face. A near-spoken androgynous dub-register voice sits close and center, cracked and warm, never soprano; on future-tense lines a distant right-side bent double answers half a beat behind. A thin bubbling granular texture drifts center-back. The drone runs the whole length as the twin's finished death; the bent echo is this star's still-running hour. Each chorus returns one line shorter, a countdown in the form; at the bridge everything drops to the drone alone for eight seconds, and the outro ends unresolved, both ticks still crossing. Fully electronic, no acoustic band.


## EXCLUDE PROMPT

EDM riser, big-room drop, festival build, trap hi-hat rolls, four-on-the-floor kick, bright supersaw pads, crystalline soprano, choir oohs, orchestral swell, triumphant major-key lift, resolved cadence, radio-pop chorus, acoustic guitar, live drum kit, tambourine, hand-clap, autotune gloss, chipmunk pitch-up, cheerful bells, glockenspiel, uplifting key change, sitcom warmth, saxophone, brass section, cinematic strings crescendo, comfortable groove lock, clean quantized swing, washy stereo smear, ambient echo wash, blurred delay tails, hall reverb on percussion, smoothed dub groove.


## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: a frozen field under the survivor star - two clocks on one face: the twin's already stopped, this star's still winding toward its own explosion]
[SONG FORM: Double-Helix Parallel - past/future braid over unbroken dub drone -> clock-argument interlude -> bridge strips to the drone alone -> countdown choruses shed a line per return -> unresolved tick outro]

[Disc_Rhythm: dry_wristwatch_tick | limping_dub_skank | past_clock_Hard_Left_close]
[Disc_Vocal: dub_register_near_spoken | cracked_warm | dry_Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: deep_dub_drone_C_Phrygian | slow_decay | chest_Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: future_bent_echo | quarter_tone_flat_flutter | Hard_Right_far]
[Disc_Texture: bubbling_granular_life | dub_murk | Center_Back]

[Intro - EMO:Foreboding - Instrumental - inhale, then tick]
*a wristwatch ticks; a bent echo answers late*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - dry, close]
You were a clock that stopped in the far dark,
your last hour still arriving as this light.
I lift my wrist and count against your spark
and feel your second hand still cocked to strike.
Two hands, two deaths, one face I read tonight -
one of them finished turning long before me,
one of them is winding out of sight.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - futures echo right]
I found you low above the frozen grass -
no town, no roof, just cold and you and me.
The dead twin's light and yours both had to pass
through one same dark, so I can't tell which is free.
One ended ages back; one's still to be.
The stopped one taught me how the running finishes;
I read your fate the way you cannot see.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - lower, held]
I did not come to praise how bright you are.
I came because I heard the other clock -
the one still ticking, buried in the star.

[Chorus - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - five lines, plain]
Star that outlived your twin and lights me now,
you are the next to go - I hear it, I hear it.
One clock already ran out in the black.
One clock is running down behind your glow.
Both of them are you, and both of them break.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - warmer, breath in]
Your brother's midnight came an age ago,
and yours is coiled, still winding in your shine.
I warm my hands where his old fire ran black;
the heat that keeps me is the fuse, not mine.
I stand here counting on a stopped design.
The tick I trust is the tick that ends you -
the comfort and the countdown share one line.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - braid tightens]
His hour is a letter I already read;
yours is a letter sealed inside your light.
I hold them both, the ended and the unsaid,
one in my memory, one still in flight.
The same hand wrote them, careless of the night.
The past one showed me exactly how you finish;
the future one is finishing in white.

[Interlude - EMO:Unease - Two Clocks - left dry, right bent]
Tick - he is done. (Tock - you are next.)
Tick - he is cold. (Tock - you are warm.)
Tick - his hour struck. (Tock - yours is wound.)
Tick - I heard him end. (Tock - hear it coming.)

[Bridge - EMO:Sorrow - Dub Voice - hinge; breath held]
Six thousand light-years - so the hour I dread
may have already struck, and I just haven't heard.
The stopped clock and the running clock are one,
seen from two distances, one turning head.
*the drone alone, eight seconds*

[Verse 5 - EMO:Revelation - Dub Voice - quieter]
I used to think the dead one was the sad one,
the stopped hand and the finished, folded light -
but you're the one still walking toward the gun.
You're the clock I have to watch tonight,
and I can't slow a single second's flight.
I only get to stand here in your warming
and know the warming is the fuse's bite.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Grief x Yearning - Dub Voice - braid holds]
He gave his hour up before I was born;
you'll give yours up while I am standing here.
His light is grief already, cold and worn;
your light is grief still warm against my ear.
One crossing done, one crossing drawing near.
The past death is the map I read your face by;
the future death is closing like a year.

[Pre-Chorus 2 - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - exhale]
So do not shine at me as if you'll stay.
I hear the second hand behind the first.
I hear the tick that takes your light away.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - four lines]
Star that will explode the way your twin exploded -
one clock stopped, one running, both of them are you.
One hand already fell across the dark;
one hand is falling slowly through your blue.

[Final Chorus - EMO:Sorrow - Dub Voice - three lines, no lift]
Star that outlived your twin and lights me still,
you are the next to go, the next to still -
both of them are you, and both of them break.

[Outro - EMO:Foreboding - Dub Voice - hum; no resolve]
Mmm - mmm - the hum between the hits.
Tick - and the echo. Tick - and the echo late.
I count you down; I cannot make you wait.
Two clocks, one star, and neither one will stay.
The stopped one taught me how the running goes.
