## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

128 BPM glossy Glitch-Baroque pop / hyper-quantized topline, E-flat major, corridor-of-screens sheen: female alto, B-flat3 to E-flat5 mix register, essay-plain diction. Signature device is refusal — nothing sonically changes; a pitch-shifted ad-lib doubles the hook two cents sharp, and the harmony shadows the lead a warm major third above until the bridge, where it slides onto the lead's exact pitch, mirror and mouth on one note, loudness flat. All-electronic: felt-not-heard 40 Hz sine sub mono-center under the glossy quantized topline; screen-glow shimmer wall to wall as garnish only, air-band sparkle wide. Lead dry close-mic dead-center, harmony at half level. Opening five seconds: displays flicking on in sequence, the same jingle on every panel. Bridge at 2:10: sub and two voices closing to one shared pitch, four bars, level unchanged; the identical line returns judged at 2:40. Mastered bright; the ornament itself is the crime.
