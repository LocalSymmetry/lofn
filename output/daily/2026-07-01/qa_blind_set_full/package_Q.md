## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Recognition-failure ambient dub elegy in C Phrygian at 96 BPM, opening in the first five seconds on an immense sustained dub-bass drone filling the whole stereo field, older than the singer, the past already arrived, with thin far-off bubbling life center-back. A small, uncertain, androgynous dub-register voice sits close, dry and dwarfed at center, its lines curling upward into questions. The future timeline is an echo that cannot be trusted: a self-doubling delay out at the far stereo edges that randomly fails to return, dropouts and glitch where the answer should be, so presence and absence become indistinguishable. The braid turns on whether this is living light or a dead one's last letter, a "now" that is only a guess. It never brightens; at the bridge the edge-delay and the bubbling drop out and the vast drone stands alone for eight seconds, returns holding; the outro confirms nothing, unresolved. Fully electronic, no acoustic instruments.


## EXCLUDE PROMPT

EDM riser, big-room drop, festival lead, trap hi-hats, four-on-the-floor kick, bright supersaw, crystalline soprano, choir, orchestral swell, major resolution, pop chorus, acoustic guitar, live drum kit, tambourine, autotune gloss, pitched-up vocal, cheerful bells, uplifting key change, sitcom strings, brass, saxophone, triumphant cadence, reassuring warmth, tidy loop resolution, clean confident groove, ambient wallpaper drone, new-age pad, meditation-app calm, regular dependable echo repeats.


## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: at this distance the second explosion may already have happened - I may be singing to a star already dead, its light still crossing to me]
[SONG FORM: Double-Helix Parallel - living-light vs last-letter braid, statements eroding into questions -> unanswered-call interlude -> bridge strips to the vast drone -> final chorus swaps one word -> unresolved outro]

[Disc_Rhythm: barely_there_dub_pulse | random_dropout_glitch | untrusted_Wide_Edges]
[Disc_Vocal: small_uncertain_dub_register | question_rise | dry_Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: immense_dub_drone | older_than_the_singer | whole_field_Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: vast_dark_static_field | slow_swell_no_resolve | Stereo_Width_Maximum]
[Disc_Texture: self_doubling_delay_failing_to_return | missing_echo | far_edges]

[Intro - EMO:Foreboding - Instrumental - shallow breath first]
*vast dub drone; a thought arrives before the eye*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - small, uncertain]
Your twin was gone long, long before its light,
and you may be already gone tonight.
I sing up at a spark that could be night -
a dead one's last letter, still coming through.
One death is old; one death I can't construe.
The first one finished centuries ago;
the second one - I have no way to view.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - the field, the doubt]
I stand where the cold field has no horizon -
just you above, or the ghost of you above.
Your brother's ending is a thing I'm eyeing
long after it happened, light I couldn't love
in time. And yours might be the same: a shove
already given, still en route to strike,
a grief that hasn't landed, from above.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - lower]
I didn't come to trust that you're still here.
I came because I can't tell if you are.
The living and the vanished wear one star.

[Chorus - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - named plainly]
Star that survived your twin, and may be finished,
your death could be in the wire, still on its way.
I can't tell living light from a long goodbye.
The first one ended; the second won't say.
Both of them look like you; both look like day.

[Interlude - EMO:Suspense - Dub Voice - the call, no answer]
I call up: are you there?
The dark sends back my voice a beat too late.
I call again: are you -
and this time nothing comes. I wait. I wait.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - the recognition]
Its ending was old news when it arrived,
and yours might be old news I haven't got.
I warm myself on light that may have died;
the comfort could be grief I haven't caught.
One loss is settled; one may be a shot
already fired, the ending on its way.
The warmth I feel might be a thing forgot.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - braid tightens]
He was a letter opened long ago;
you are a letter maybe on the wind.
I read the first; the second I don't know -
still sealed, or has the seal already thinned?
The past one told me how these stories end;
the future one is somewhere in the dark,
still traveling, a message and its end.

[Bridge - EMO:Sorrow - Dub Voice - hinge; breath held]
Six thousand light-years - so this "now" is a guess,
and the star I'm singing to might not be there.
Both deaths could be behind me, and I'd never
know it, standing here in your address.
*the vast drone alone, eight seconds*

[Verse 5 - EMO:Revelation - Dub Voice - quieter]
So I will sing as if you're still alive,
because the alternative I cannot prove.
Maybe I mourn a thing that can't survive;
maybe I warm my hands at nothing's stove.
Either way, the light is what I have - and of
the two of you, the dead and maybe-dead,
I only get the letters, not the love.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Melancholy - Dub Voice - braid holds]
His death is certain; I have seen it land,
arriving ages after it occurred.
Yours is a rumor I can't understand -
still traveling, or long ago interred.
I strain to catch a difference in the blurred
space between living light and a last message,
and can't: both of them arrive without a word.

[Pre-Chorus 2 - EMO:Helplessness - Dub Voice - lower]
So maybe I'm too late to even fear.
Maybe the ending happened, and I hum
a lullaby to something not still here.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - recontextualized]
Star that outlived one blast and may hold another -
both of them look like you; both look like day.
One letter opened, one still on the current;
the first one ended, the second won't say.
Both of them look like you; both look like day.

[Final Chorus - EMO:Sorrow - Dub Voice - one word swapped]
Star that I sing to, living or already finished -
both of them look like you; both look like day.
One goodbye read, one somewhere in the darkness;
the first one ended - the second won't say.
Both of them look like you. Both look like day.

[Outro - EMO:Existential Angst - Dub Voice - the drone holds]
I can't confirm you. I can only sing.
The drone holds. Nothing answers. Not a thing.
And still I sing - to you, or to your absence:
mmm - the same song lands on either wing.
