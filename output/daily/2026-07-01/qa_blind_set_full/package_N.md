## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

An unresolved reverence, isolate and high. Ethereal Neo-Baroque folk-ambient in D Dorian at a drifting 72 BPM, bar-lengths flexing a beat so the young layer loses footing on an old drone that never moves. First five seconds: heat leaving wet tar — a granulated pitched-down wind-drone shivering upward, wide, with a dry hole dead-center. A mid-register female alto, harder than a whisper and held back from full tone, sits close and dry while a city-sized reverb bed answers only her, never the drone. The one percussive element is processed gravel-shift-underfoot, gritty, bar-count-drifting, center-right; at the final chorus one rave-sunrise flare lifts the gravel to a brief shimmer over her bluesy bend, then re-shadows. At the bridge the young layer drops out and the altitude stands bare five seconds. The drone carries the height through the outro, unresolved. Voice center-dry, drone wide, gravel center-right, air-band dark; no kit, no stacked pads.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: alone on a dark unrailed rooftop above a black city, singing to the star that survived its twin's explosion, afraid of the edge she can't find]
[SONG FORM: THE MEASUREMENT — Intro · V1–V2 · Pre-Chorus · Hinge-Chorus · V3 danger · V4 · Chorus 2 · V5 wave · V6 edge-test · V7 · Bridge drone-alone · Final Chorus unresolved · Outro]

[Disc_Rhythm: gravel_shift_underfoot | gritty_drift | transient_snap | Center_Right]
[Disc_Vocal: mid_alto_held_back | dry_close_vs_city_reverb | breath_audible | Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: heat_leaving_tar_shiver | felt_in_chest_not_ears | 4_second_decay | Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: city_reverb_vocal_only | Cold_Cathedral_Dark | dry_hole_center | Stereo_Width_Maximum]
[Disc_Texture: rave_sunrise_gravel_shimmer_once | cassette_saturation | air_dark | Stereo_Width_Mid]

[Intro - EMO:Anticipation - No beats - wind-drone, heat leaving tar]
*light shivers off the wet tar*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Reflection, Unease - Alto - mid, dry, close]
The gravel keeps the day's warmth at my feet
while cold comes down to meet me from the street
of stars — warm below, cold above, like you:
the young light over something old and blue.
The star its blown-apart twin still lights
is standing watch above the roofs tonight.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Isolation, Fascination - Alto - mid]
Down there the windows are all going dark.
Up here it's only me and one cold spark.
I am the highest lit thing for a mile.
You are the highest lit thing, and you smile
the way a thing smiles when it cannot fall —
except you can. You will. You'll lose it all.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Unease - Alto - LPF 400 Hz]
The city has gone dark from block to block.
There is no railing and there is no clock.
Just me, and you, and all this open air,
and the long cold falling everywhere.

[Chorus - EMO:Wonder, Terror - Alto - mid, held back]
And it says: six thousand years to reach one roof
and find me the only warm thing here.
That far, that old, and here's the proof:
it warms me, and it fills me full of fear.
I answer to the empty, hanging air —
you are not company. You are just there.
You are just there. You are just there.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Terror, Unease - Alto - low, breath audible]
The edge is two steps left — or was it three? —
no rail, just dark, and a map I half-believe.
I stopped my foot before I knew to stop.
The city has no floor from up on top.
I hold the warmth of gravel through my sole
and doubt the only map that keeps me whole.
I do not trust my feet up here at all.
I trust the warmth, and warmth is not a wall.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Awe, Sorrow - Alto - mid]
You are the warmest thing I'll meet all year,
and you are dying, and you are not near.
You are a fire from a long way gone
that only now arrived to shine upon
a roof, a girl, a doubt about the edge.
I stand between the warmth and the long ledge.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Wonder, Terror - Alto - mid, held back]
And it kept reaching, kept on reaching,
just to find one warm thing here.
However far, it lands like breath on skin:
the warmth arrives, and lets the fear in.
I answer to the empty, hanging air —
you are not company. You are just there.
You are just there. You are just there.

[Verse 5 - EMO:Isolation, Reflection - Alto - low]
Down there they sleep in walls, in warmth, in pairs.
Up here it's only me and one cold stare.
I am the only lit thing they can't see,
and you're the only lit thing warming me.
I wave. It is a small, ridiculous thing.
The star does not wave back. The gravel sings.
And both of us are leaving, going out.
That is the roof. That is the night. That's doubt.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Terror, Awe - Alto - mid, breath held at the step]
The tar has given up the last of day.
The cold has won the roof. It's here to stay.
I slide one boot a boot-length toward the dark.
The gravel thins. The city holds its bark.
Nothing says edge. The nothing is the sign.
I pull my weight back up the plumb of spine.
And still I stand and face the only heat
that costs a whole exploded life to meet.

[Verse 7 - EMO:Reflection, Sorrow - Alto - low]
I did not ask to be the highest one.
I did not ask to love a dying sun.
But here I am, above the sleeping street,
with cooling gravel underneath my feet,
and one warm ghost of light against my face,
and no rail, and no floor, and no safe place.

[Bridge - EMO:Isolation - No beats - drone ALONE, voice out]
*the altitude is suddenly bare*

[Final Chorus - EMO:Awe, Sorrow - Alto - full tone, unresolved]
So the warmest thing above the sleeping town
is also leaving, also going down.
The star its blown-apart twin still lights
will leave me highest, coldest, of the nights.
I will not call this closeness. Not tonight.
I will not call this closeness. Not tonight.
The edge still waits where I can't see —
two steps, or three, it keeps the count for me.

[Outro - EMO:Melancholy - No beats - drone stays open]
*the drone stays high and open*
(the edge keeps counting me, in the dark)
