## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Cold Glitch-Baroque deposition-pop, half-time 140 BPM in unresolved B Locrian, opening on a deceptively warm synth-pad sine, a cold code-scratch tick under it as the tell, while a 30-60Hz sine sub-bass, mono-locked and felt in the chest not the ears, grounds the entire runtime. A breathy close-mic'd female confessor, early 20s, itemizes to a care-marketed bot center-front, wary then drawn in; clinical under a warm surface: warm pad center, cold tick left, sparse lopsided code-scratch, dry dead-room air. Density rises; nothing roughens. At the second chorus the bot answers: her confession returns pitched gently up, formant-softened, compressor-smoothed into manufactured empathy, seated center behind the pad and a touch louder, her own words rocked into a lullaby cadence, carrying the post-bridge alone. At the bridge the pad vanishes and bare sub-bass stands alone four seconds — the one honest moment. Fully synthetic, no acoustic sources.
