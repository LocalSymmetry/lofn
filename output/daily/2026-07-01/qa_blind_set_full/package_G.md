## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Premonition glitch-dub in C Phrygian at 96 BPM, swung and dub-plate warm, opening in the first five seconds on light-shift ambience over cold ground while a right-panned pre-echo delay sounds a softened ghost of every hit one swung beat before the real strike — prophecy in the groove. A watchful, quiet, androgynous dub-register voice sits close and center, no soprano; in each chorus a whispered double speaks the line's tail a breath before the lead sings it, the echo arriving first. A deep dub-bass drone runs the whole length center-back like a struck, empty stage, the twin's performance already over; a bubbling granular texture drifts low mid-left between the two clocks. The swing limps against the pre-echo so nothing locks; at the bridge the ghost hits and the bubbling drop out and the drone stands alone for seven seconds, then the pre-echo resumes rehearsing; the outro ends with no final hit, unresolved. Fully electronic, no acoustic band.


## EXCLUDE PROMPT

EDM riser, big-room drop, festival build, trap hi-hat rolls, four-on-the-floor, bright pads, crystalline soprano, choir oohs, orchestral crescendo, major-key lift, resolved final chord, radio-pop chorus, acoustic guitar, live drums, tambourine, hand-clap, autotune gloss, pitched-up vocal, cheerful glockenspiel, uplifting modulation, sitcom warmth, brass, saxophone, triumphant outro, comfortable groove lock, clean quantized swing, decisive last hit, on-grid tight delay, echo after the beat only, conventional slapback.


## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: the twin's explosion was a dress rehearsal I was shown; now I hold a seat for the survivor star's opening night, knowing every beat of how it ends]
[SONG FORM: Double-Helix Parallel - rehearsal/performance braid, a whispered pre-echo preceding each chorus line it doubles -> bridge strips to the drone alone -> final chorus turns dread into vigil -> outro with no final hit]

[Disc_Rhythm: dub_plate_swing_limping | ghost_hit_one_beat_early | pre_echo_Right]
[Disc_Vocal: watchful_dub_register | whisper_precedes_lead | dry_Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: dub_drone_struck_stage | slow_decay | past_Center_Back_Mono]
[Disc_Pad: cold_houselight_air | dim_no_swell | Wide_behind]
[Disc_Texture: bubbling_footlights | between_clocks | Mid_Left_low]

[Intro - EMO:Foreboding - Instrumental - whisper-breath first]
*light shifts on cold ground; a ghost hit early*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - watchful, quiet]
Your twin exploded once, far out of view,
and that explosion was your dress rehearsal.
I watched its light walk through the scene on cue;
now yours is queued behind your quiet grain.
One ending played; one waits inside the plain.
The past one ran the show from start to curtain;
the future one is pacing in your flame.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Trepidation - Dub Voice - the preview]
I stand in the cold field like an empty house
before a show - the seats all dark and bare.
Your brother's death was the run-through; I was roused
to watch it once, to learn what will be there.
And you're the night the curtain will not spare.
He practiced the whole ending in the distance;
you'll perform it, slower, in the open air.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - lower]
I didn't come to clap. I came to dread.
I've seen the dress rehearsal in the dark,
and now I have to watch it played ahead.

[Chorus - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - whisper precedes line]
(the curtain falls)
Your brother's ending was the dress rehearsal,
and you're the night the curtain really falls.
(once more, in you)
I've seen this once; I'll see it once more, in you.
One show is struck; one waits behind the walls.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - the mark]
It practiced its own death across the dark,
and you will play it out, but slower, whole.
The light on the cold ground is the same mark,
the same fire walking through its opening stroll.
He gave the preview; you will give the toll.
The heat that finds me now is the rehearsal
warming the seat before it takes its role.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - braid tightens]
He was the matinee I wasn't meant to keep;
you are the evening show I can't refuse.
He blocked the scene, then let the curtain sweep;
you've learned his marks - you'll walk them, and you'll lose.
Same script, same fire, the same rehearsed abuse
of everything that shines and has to finish:
one played; one's dressing in your quiet fuse.

[Bridge - EMO:Sorrow - Dub Voice - hinge; breath held]
Six thousand light-years between the performances,
and the second one may be already underway.
The rehearsal and the show are one long ending
seen from two seats - one dark, one on display.
*the drone alone, seven slow seconds*

[Verse 5 - EMO:Revelation - Dub Voice - quieter]
So I will watch you, knowing every beat,
because your brother taught me every one:
the mark you'll hit, the scene you're going to meet,
the way the light goes white, the way it's done.
I've had the preview; now I wait, undone,
for you to walk the ending he rehearsed.
The dread is knowing exactly how you'll run.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Grief - Dub Voice - braid holds]
His run-through is a thing I can't un-see;
it taught my eye the shape your death will take -
and you rehearse it now in front of me
in every flicker, every warming ache.
One show already closed; one's yours to make.
The past performance drilled me for the future;
the future's warming up, for both our sakes.

[Pre-Chorus 2 - EMO:Premonition - Dub Voice - tightened]
So take your mark. I know it, beat for beat.
His ending walked me through your coming one.
I dread the show because I know it's sweet.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Dread - Dub Voice - whisper precedes line]
(the night is dressing)
One rehearsal done, one performance coming -
the matinee is struck; the night is dressing.
(I know your ending)
I know your ending, star; I watched him teach it.
One show is struck; one waits behind the walls.

[Final Chorus - EMO:Devotion x Sorrow - Dub Voice - the turn]
(the house goes dark)
One rehearsal watched, one ending still to open -
the preview closed; the real night is beginning.
(once more, in you)
I've seen this once; I'll see it once more, in you -
and staying for the show is how I love you.

[Outro - EMO:Foreboding x Devotion - Dub Voice - no final hit]
Places, please. The house goes dark. You shine.
I know your ending; I don't know the time.
I've had the preview; now I keep the vigil -
mmm - the seats are cold, and one of them is mine.
