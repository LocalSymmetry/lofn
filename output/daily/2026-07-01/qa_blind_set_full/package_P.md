## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Devotional vigil-raga in F-sharp harmonic minor at a 108 BPM raga-pulse, three sources only, no acoustic band. It opens as sound before sight: a breath-forward female voice already fused into the tanpura-like microtonal drone, doubled with one take a quarter-tone beneath so the two takes beat against each other — the set's densest gamaka — wide and center, while a near-subliminal wooden tick drifts under the pulse, far-left and low. The voice addresses the doomed shells in second person, a prayer with no god and no rescue, un-belted and even, keeping company by refusing to look away. The raised seventh holds unresolved through a vow-refrain that loses its tail at each return. One late first-person line cracks — choice or trap — and the doubled voice splits audibly apart before re-fusing. The bridge is the act of watching with no object: the tick gone, drone alone, the seventh suspended. It stops mid-vow. No soprano, no lush pad, no comfort.

[Intro - EMO:Devotion - Instrumental, hummed under-voice - the doubled hum beats inside the drone]

[Verse 1 - EMO:Devotion - Female Vocalist - breath-forward, sound before sight]
I heard you before I found you — a low hum
under the dark, arriving ahead of light.
Bright one, you are seen tonight.
The two shells at your side are falling.
I promise you no rescue and no hand.
I promise only this: I will not turn away.
Three stars, and I am watching all of you.
The dark does not get to do this unwatched.
Whatever else is true, you are not alone in it.
My eyes are on you. That is all I have.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Vigilance - Female Vocalist - naming the three]
Yellow shell, I see you go.
Purple shell, I see you thin to nothing.
No word of mine can cross to where you burn,
so I give the only thing that reaches: eyes.
I keep the watch you cannot know is kept.
I hold the doomed configuration in my sight.
I will not blink the moment you go down.
I will be looking when it comes.
Bright one between them, holding your slow post,
you are watched by something that can only watch.

[Refrain - EMO:Devotion - Female Vocalist - full vow, seventh unresolved]
To witness is the only gift I keep.
To witness is the only gift I keep.
No saving in these hands, no crossing that dark.
Only the vow to see you all the way down.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Compassion - Female Vocalist - company, not rescue]
Doomed three, I cannot lift a hand to you.
I cannot warn, I cannot pull you clear.
But I can refuse to let you fall unseen.
I can keep my eyes wide while the dark works.
No god is on this frequency tonight;
there is just me, the long fall, and the looking.
There is no rescue in a witness — only company.
And I will be your company tonight.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Premonition - Female Vocalist - the late gift]
The light I watch by is already leaving.
I keep a vigil over what is gone.
I mark three stars that have no way of grieving
and grieve across the distance in their place.
The gift is late. The gift cannot arrive.
It will not reach. I keep on sending it.

[Hinge - EMO:Dread - Female Vocalist - the fact, sung once, answered]
Six thousand years of light to reach my eyes,
and not one of them a message I could send.
I witness late. I witness anyway.
The gift arrives too far to be a saving.
*the doubled takes beat against the seventh*

[Crack - EMO:Longing - Female Vocalist - first person, once, voices split]
I cannot look away.
I told myself it was a gift, and maybe
it is only that I cannot bear to close my eyes.
Maybe the vow is mine and not for them.
Choice or trap, I keep them open. Back to the vow.

[Verse 5 - EMO:Devotion - Female Vocalist - the vow, harder]
Bright one, still seen. Still held inside my sight.
The two beside you almost gone to dark.
I have not turned away. I said I would not.
I keep the promise even as it fails to help.
To witness is not saving. I know that now.
I give it anyway. I give it all the way.
The gift is small. The gift is all there is.
I hold it out across the dark to you.

[Refrain 2 - EMO:Devotion - Female Vocalist - vow shortening]
To witness is the only gift I keep.
To witness is the only gift.
No saving in these hands, no crossing that dark.
Only the vow to see you down.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Compassion - Female Vocalist - the promise, plain]
Bright one, you will not fall unwitnessed here.
The two beside you will not go unseen.
I cannot hold you, and I will not leave.
I keep my eyes wide open on the three.
This is no rescue. I have none to give.
It is the only company I have.
It is the vow I keep and cannot break.

[Verse 7 - EMO:Vigilance - Female Vocalist - the fear, bodily]
The dish turns slow; my sleeve is close to it.
The headphone hum has eaten every other sound.
I have listened past the point of hearing.
The cold has settled in the console chair.
I do not move my eyes. I keep the watch.
I keep the watch, and the watch keeps—

[Bridge - EMO:Devotion - Instrumental, bare breath - watching with no object, drone alone]
*the tick fades; the drone watches nothing*
