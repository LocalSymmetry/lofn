## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Weary survivor-raga in F-sharp harmonic minor at a 108 BPM raga-pulse, three sources only, no acoustic band. The tanpura-like microtonal drone IS the foreground here — dominant, center-wide, older than everything it carries, the outliving itself made audible — while a sparse wooden clock-tick sits soft-left, slowing and drifting off the pulse across the song until it dies. A low-mid female voice with no vibrato showpiece sings an elegy-in-advance, dry and worn, listing each thing the survivor will outlast; a microtonal glide bends the word last a quarter-tone down at every return. The raised seventh aches the whole length and is refused. Past the midpoint one first-person line offers the worst comfort and curdles on it. The bridge is the drone alone — the tick dead, nothing left to watch, the seventh unresolved over near-silence — the cold reaching the marrow, an unrailed drop two steps off. It stops mid-list, no cadence, no major-key lift.

now both are going down the same long dark.

It only stays behind and marks the going.
