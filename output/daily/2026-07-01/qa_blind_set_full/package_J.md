## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

A hushed devotional-blues lament in Neo-Baroque Luminist folk, rooted in bluesy soul sadness, rubato 66 BPM, A minor-pentatonic, fully acoustic. A close-mic intimate female alto leads, warm and breath-forward, a blue third bent on each departure word, power withheld to the final line, bone-dry, dead-center. Signature one: a low old-tongue choral-drone of resynthesized Norse-Gaelic vowels on a bellows-like suspension that never resolves, wide-left in cavernous stone reverb, first second to last. Signature two: one brushed frame-drum, off-center-right, drifting rubato and shedding a stroke at each refrain, a waning pulse that never locks. It opens on one audible inhale and a lit-window hum before any pulse; verse bar-counts limp; at the hinge the drum halts and the moon-fact lands in held silence; the bridge bares the drone alone for seven seconds; the outro leaves the drone holding after the voice cuts off mid-vow, unresolved, no swell.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: a doorway threshold at night — keeping a lamp for the waning moon whose light is already leaving]
[SONG FORM: Waning-Vow Ballad — refrain sheds a line per return (4-3-2) as the moon wanes; Intro / vow+erosion verses / waning refrains / bargain / Pre-Hinge / Hinge fact / danger / Bridge drone-alone 7s / turned refrain / Outro cut off unkept]

[Disc_Rhythm: brushed_frame_drum_rubato | sheds_one_stroke_per_refrain | halts_at_hinge | no_kit_no_808 | Off_Center_Right]
[Disc_Vocal: close_mic_female_alto | breath_on_capsule_audible | blue_third_on_departure_words | power_withheld_to_final_line | Center_Front_Dry]
[Disc_Sub: drone_fundamental_A2 | felt_in_chest_not_ears | Mono_Sub_Lock]

[Intro - EMO:Devotion, Foreboding - Alto close-mic, audible inhale first - No beats]
*a lit window hums behind her*
I stand where the lamplight ends
And the black field begins
I lift my face to the going one
The moon one night past whole
With nothing to give but a promise
And I give it to what will not stay
Knowing the giving is already late

[Verse 1 - EMO:Devotion - Alto, dry center - vow then erosion couplets]
I will keep a lamp for you tonight
I will leave the one high window bright
But the moon is already turning her face
And I light a room that has lost her trace
I will trim the wick and guard the flame
But she does not answer to a name

[Refrain 1 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto over drone - drum enters, full four lines]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I promise the light, I promise the light
To what is leaving, I promise the light
(the light, the light)

[Verse 2 - EMO:Devotion, Unease - Alto - the glass changes sides]
I will hold the flame against the sill
I will keep it burning, keep it still
But the pane gives my glow back to the night
And she is gone from the reach of my light
I will cup my hands around the glow
But the cold outside already knows

[Refrain 2 - EMO:Yearning - Alto - one line gone, one stroke gone]
Moon at the edge of full, one night past whole
I promise the light, I promise the light
(the light, the light)

[Verse 3 - EMO:Devotion, Yearning - Alto - the vow gains weight]
I will stand here until the morning comes
I will count the hours on my thumbs
But the dark keeps taking what I hold
And the vow gets heavier than it is old
I will swear it slower, swear it true
But slower will not keep her here for you

[Verse 4 - EMO:Trepidation, Devotion - Alto - the vow bargains itself down]
I swore the whole night, and I meant it whole
Now I hear the hours change hands as they're told
Till dawn wears down to till the field goes grey
And grey wears down to long as I can stay
The promise keeps its words and loses ground
A smaller hand held up, a smaller sound

[Pre-Hinge - EMO:Yearning, Helplessness - Alto - drum thins to almost nothing]
And I would trade the morning back
For one more hour of her light
I would keep the window burning
Every night of my whole life
But a promise is a small hand
Held up against the tide

[Refrain 3 - EMO:Yearning, Foreboding - Alto - thinnest, two lines]
Moon at the edge of full—
I promise the light

[Hinge - EMO:Dread - Alto alone, breath catches - No beats]
*the frame-drum stops*
Ninety-seven, and already turning away
It answers my keeping with a going
It will not wait to be kept
And the warm sill lights nothing that is still there

[Verse 5 - EMO:Dread, Grief - Alto - the keeping costs the keeper]
I kept the light a breath too late
The window burns for an empty gate
The night takes back what the glass lets fall
And the leaving does not need my light at all
I lit the room for a face that's gone
And still I leave the one lamp on
The gate stands open to the cold
For a keeping I could not hold
The cold walks in where I hold the door
And takes the warmth I was keeping for
And the wick still leans to the empty pane
The way a hand keeps reaching in the rain

[Bridge - EMO:Devotion - old-tongue choir alone seven seconds - No beats]
*old-tongue drone alone, wordless vowels*
(the promise older than the one who makes it)

[Refrain turned - EMO:Grief, Resolve - Alto, full power at last - the vow turns]
Moon at the edge of full, going, going down
I will not keep the light for you
I will keep the leaving with you
And I will not ask you to stay
I will only walk you to the grey
(with you, with you)
