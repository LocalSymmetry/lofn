## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

An unresolved reverence, terror-adjacent and cold. Ethereal Neo-Baroque folk-ambient in D Dorian at a drifting 72 BPM — every fourth bar arrives a beat late, so the young sung layer audibly limps across an old wind that never moves. The first five seconds are that old layer alone: granulated field-recorded wind pitched down an octave, grainy, wide, deep-center. A female alto enters dry and close at a whisper — reverent, braced — full tone withheld until the final chorus. The only percussion is processed foot-on-metal-grating, a drifting ring staged center-left that never locks to the drone; at the final chorus one rave-sunrise flare warms the ring over the alto's bluesy grieving bend, then re-shadows. At the bridge the young layer drops out entirely and the wind stands alone six seconds. The drone holds through the outro, unresolved. Vocals center, drone wide, ring center-left, air-band dark; no kit, no acoustic guitar.

## 2. LYRICS

[Theme: a body on a cold observatory catwalk, singing to the star lit by its exploded twin — vertigo that will not resolve]
[SONG FORM: THE MEASUREMENT — Intro · V1–V2 · Pre-Chorus · Hinge-Chorus · V3 danger · V4 · Chorus 2 · V5–V6 · V7 re-heard · Bridge old-alone · Final Chorus · Outro]

[Disc_Rhythm: boot_on_steel_grating_ring | one_beat_late_each_fourth_bar | transient_snap | Center_Left]
[Disc_Vocal: close_mic_alto_whisper_to_mid | breath_on_capsule_audible | dry_no_reverb | Center_Front]
[Disc_Sub: granulated_wind_drone_D2 | felt_in_chest_not_ears | 4_second_decay | Mono_Sub_Lock]
[Disc_Pad: pitched_down_field_wind | never_resolving_dorian_sustain | Cold_Cathedral_Dark | Stereo_Width_Maximum]
[Disc_Texture: tape_saturation | metal_mesh_shiver | one_rave_sunrise_flare_final_chorus | Stereo_Width_Mid]

[Intro - EMO:Anticipation - No beats - lone wind-drone, audible inhale]
*wind forced through cold mesh*

[Verse 1 - EMO:Reverence, Vulnerability - Alto - whispered, close]
I climbed the grating in the dark
to stand beneath a single spark —
the star your sister's dying lit,
the one that got to stay a bit.
My boots ring cold on open steel.
I came to learn what far can feel.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Fascination, Unease - Alto - whisper-to-mid]
You hang so still above the town,
a lantern nothing can put down.
The wind leans hard against my coat.
There is a distance in my throat.
I did not climb to ask you why.
I climbed to stand and watch you lie —
so calm, so lit, so far from harm,
while I hold nothing but my arm.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Unease - Alto - low, LPF 400 Hz]
And every year your light let fall
was one more year you did not call.
I do the arithmetic of light.
I get it wrong. I get it right.

[Chorus - EMO:Wonder, Terror - Alto - mid, held back]
And it says: six thousand years of falling
just to warm one shaking hand.
That's how long the light was calling
before it reached where I could stand.
I answer back into the cold —
you are not warm, you are just old.
You are just old. You are just old.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Terror, Unease - Alto - low, breath audible]
And the mesh beneath my boot holds nothing whole.
One wrong step drops me through the world you show.
I map the gaps the way I map your name —
the grating and the grave feel much the same.
I keep my weight dead-centered on the bar.
I look at you. I know now what you are.
I know the fall is one lean to the side.
I know the light I love has already died.

[Verse 4 - EMO:Awe, Sorrow - Alto - whisper-to-mid]
You are a fire that already went.
You are the letter, not the one who sent.
You are the warmth of somebody long spent —
a light I stand in, and cannot repent.
The town below is sleeping in your glow.
Nobody down there knows you're falling. No.
They pull their blankets to the chin and dream.
They do not hear the grating, or the seam.

[Chorus 2 - EMO:Wonder, Terror - Alto - mid, held back]
And it keeps falling, keeps on falling,
just to warm one shaking hand.
That's the debt behind the calling,
that's the cost of where I stand.
I answer back into the cold —
you are not warm, you are just old.
You are just old. You are just old.

[Verse 5 - EMO:Reflection, Unease - Alto - low]
I used to think a star was just a light.
I did not know a light could be a night
that somebody else went through, all alone,
so I could climb up here and call it home.
The wind gets colder. I do not go down.
I hold the rail that is not there. I frown.
I count my breaths against the empty air.
I stay because somebody has to care.

[Verse 6 - EMO:Terror, Awe - Alto - whisper-to-mid]
The catwalk hums a little in the gust.
The bolts are old. The paint has gone to rust.
And still I lean my forehead toward your glow
the way you lean toward nothing, falling slow.
Two things above a floor that isn't there —
you in the black, and me, and all that air.

[Verse 7 - EMO:Revelation, Terror - Alto - low, line one re-heard]
I climbed the grating in the dark, I said.
I hear it now, the whole sky overhead:
I did not climb to feel your light on skin.
I climbed to where a fall could still begin.
You cannot fall. Your fall was long ago.
Mine waits one lean away, and does not show.

[Bridge - EMO:Isolation - No beats - wind-drone ALONE, voice out]
*wind at full volume, alone*

[Final Chorus - EMO:Awe, Sorrow - Alto - full tone, still unresolved]
So the light kept falling all that while
just to reach one small and shaking smile.
The star your sister's dying lit —
I will not call this kindness. Not yet.
I will not call this kindness. Not yet.
The mesh holds nothing whole, and holds me still.
The cold is old light landing where it will.
The wind still leans its shoulder on my coat,
and the distance sings, not stays, inside my throat.

[Outro - EMO:Melancholy - No beats - drone stays, breath after last word]
*the drone does not resolve*
Stay lit. Stay far. Stay falling, then.
Stay falling. I'll be here again.
(the wind keeps the last chord open)
