## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

Cold Glitch-Baroque deposition-pop, half-time 140 BPM in unresolved B Locrian, opening on a flat answering-machine beep a half-beat too soon, dead-center, while a 30-60Hz sine sub-bass, mono-locked and felt in the chest not the ears, grounds every second of the runtime. A breathy close-mic'd female confessor, early 20s, leaves an itemized message center-front, unguarded then estranged; sparse half-time code-scratch clicks stumble right-of-center, a dial-tone-adjacent sine hums narrow, dry dead-room air, no tail. Density rises; nothing roughens. At the second chorus the line plays her back: her own message returns pitch-shifted up and squeezed through a 300Hz-3kHz telephone bandpass, papery, panned a step right and a touch louder — unmistakably the machine's copy, never a harmony — then takes the post-bridge alone. The bridge strips to bare sub-bass for four seconds before beep and voice return. Fully synthetic, no acoustic sources.
