# Step 05 — Refine Medium (2 Panel-Pressed Song Seeds)

## Slot 1 — ACCESSIBLE / EXISTENCE / AWE-terror-adjacent

**Selected concept: C1 "Sibling Light"** (as refined through steps 03-04).

**Panel-pressed song seed:** A body stands on a real cold surface (catwalk mesh or open field, per variation) under the direct light of a star that only shines because its twin exploded. The hinge fact (6,000 light-years, or 97% waning) is placed where the body already flinches — cold against skin, height underfoot, a held breath — and never explained by the lyric, per the Deep-Time Semiotician's Bridge-transform ruling. Three sound sources: whispered-to-mid vocal, never-resolving field-drone, one foley-sourced percussive element carrying bar-count drift. Dorian/Aeolian. The bridge folds in one harmonized vocal layer, the only moment the dual-vocal device appears. The song does not resolve into settled reverence — the drone persists at full presence through the final bar, per the Structural Skeptic's ruling honored in step 03.

**Runner-up rationale (why C1 beat C2-C6 for this slot):**
- C2 ("The Waning Vow") was a close second — the 97% fact is equally strong as a hinge — but its "vow" framing risked resolving into a promise-kept comfort arc that fights the Structural Skeptic's unresolved-ending requirement.
- C3 ("Kiln-Red Inheritance") pulled focus onto a family narrative that the human-subject discipline would then need to police carefully (family grief risks brushing against the Venezuela/Kyiv atmosphere in ways C1's cosmic register avoids cleanly).
- C4 ("Triple Star Watch") and C5 ("Observatory Confession") were killed by the Structural Skeptic directly (see step02's panel_pressure record) for resolving their central image too early or mediating the address in a way that softened the terror-adjacence.
- C6 ("The Second Explosion") was the most ambitious alternative but doubles the timeline complexity (past death + future death) in a way that risks breaking the three-sound-source economy — would likely need a fourth device to carry two temporal registers clearly.

## Slot 2 — AMBITIOUS / NEWS / INDIGNATION

**Selected concept: C7 "Look How Much They Spent"** (as refined through steps 03-04).

**Panel-pressed song seed:** A body in a train car or corridor (per variation) watches an institutional warning-turned-advertisement and catches its own attention enjoying it. No sonic rupture — the surface stays glossy and quantized throughout. Two load-bearing devices: felt-not-heard sub-bass (old/young form law, old layer) and pitch-shifted glossy ad-lib hook (young layer). The turn is textual: the harmony repeats the pinned line "look how much they spent" once warm, once cold, no later than the second chorus per the Silence Skeptic's condition honored in step 03. Bridge strips to sub-bass alone.

**Runner-up rationale (why C7 beat C8-C12 for this slot):**
- C8 ("The Warning That Learned to Sell") was killed by the Raw-Production Skeptic directly (step02 panel_pressure) for requiring four vocal registers against the two-device economy budget.
- C9 ("Scroll Past It") is a strong alternate angle but its repeated-failed-exit structure risks the n-gram/repetition FLAG (`gates.yaml unique_line_ratio_floor`) without the chorus-exemption covering it, since the repetition is verse-level, not refrain-level.
- C10 ("Bezel Face") was folded INTO C7 rather than discarded outright — its reflection-in-dead-screen image survives as Pair 02's V2 variation angle ("Corridor of loops") per `05_pair_assignments.md`, so it is harvested rather than cut.
- C11 ("Care Tax") was killed by the Structural/Silence Skeptic combination (step02) for over-explaining its own metaphor — didactic tone works against the "catch yourself enjoying it" felt-experience the concept needs.
- C12 ("Six-Second Mercy") was the Silence Skeptic's single most serious objection (step02) — a hook literally built on the ad's own loop length risks BEING an effective loop rather than critiquing one; excluded on principle, not craft quality.
