# Step 02 — Concepts

Per `EXECUTION.md` §4: the tree branches to at least 12 concepts even in a test slice. Six candidate concepts generated per pair slot (Pair 01 = EXISTENCE/AWE slot, Pair 02 = NEWS/INDIGNATION slot); step 05 selects the winner into each slot and records the cut ledger for the other five per slot.

## Pair 01 slot — EXISTENCE / AWE-terror-adjacent (6 concepts)

**C1 — "Sibling Light" (WINNER, carried to step 03+):** A survivor star's orbiting body addresses the sibling whose supernova is what lights it now, standing on a real cold surface (catwalk or field), the fact of the distance placed somatically, ending unresolved.

**C2 — "The Waning Vow":** A narrator uses the 97% waning gibbous as the address point instead of the supernova distance — a vow made to a moon that is already leaving even at its brightest, promising to notice the leaving rather than the brightness.

**C3 — "Kiln-Red Inheritance":** Built around Venetian Red as the central image — an old wound color inherited from something that happened before the narrator existed, addressed as a family color rather than a cosmic one.

**C4 — "Triple Star Watch":** Focuses on the third star in the Propus system (mentioned in F6/F7) as a silent witness rather than a participant — a song about being the one who watches two others' entangled fate without being caught in it.

**C5 — "Observatory Confession":** A first-person confession delivered entirely as if spoken to an instrument (a telescope or a recording device) rather than to the star itself — mediates the address through technology.

**C6 — "The Second Explosion":** Speculative/anxious angle — imagining the survivor star's own eventual death, collapsing the timeline so the narrator addresses their own future ending as much as the sibling's past one.

## Pair 02 slot — NEWS / INDIGNATION (6 concepts)

**C7 — "Look How Much They Spent" (WINNER, carried to step 03+):** A body in a train car or corridor catches itself admiring an institutional anti-harm ad, and the song's harmony repeats the admiring line back with a curdling value-judgment — no sonic rupture, the surface stays glossy throughout.

**C8 — "The Warning That Learned to Sell":** A more literal personification concept — the warning system itself narrates, discovering mid-song that it has become the thing it was built to prevent. Risk: personifying the machine risks softening the indictment into sympathy for the system rather than anger at it.

**C9 — "Scroll Past It":** A doomscroll-adjacent concept where the narrator tries and fails to scroll past the ad, structured as a repeated failed-exit motif each chorus.

**C10 — "Bezel Face":** Entirely built around the corridor-of-loops reflection image — the narrator's own face in the dead screen between plays as the central visual, minimal plot beyond that one image.

**C11 — "Care Tax":** An economic-metaphor concept — framing the seduction as a tax paid in attention for the appearance of being cared for, more didactic/explainer in tone.

**C12 — "Six-Second Mercy":** Built around the exact runtime of a viral ad-clip as a structural conceit — the song's hook cycles at the ad's own six-second loop length, making the listener complicit in the same loop mechanically.
