# VARIATION 2 — "Field"

**Title:** *Aftermath Standing*

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

A restrained Neo-Baroque ambient-folk piece, 70 BPM, E Aeolian: whispered-to-mid female mezzo, G3-C4 low chest register, breath-forward and unhurried, crystalline capacity held in reserve. Signature device: a granulated, pitched-down open-field wind drone spread wide across the stereo image, never locking to a pitch, never syncing to the grid, outlasting the voice. Three sources only: the wide drone, voice, and foley grass-and-fabric rustle underfoot, dry and close, panned slightly left, carrying a bar-count drift whose spacing lengthens and shrinks across repetitions. Vocal delivery close-mic, dry, centered, blue-note bends behind the beat. Arrangement: drone and rustle alone for a 15-second intro; at 1:45 the rustle gathers two bars into brief warm dawn-pulse brightness as the granulation cracks once, hard, then resettles; bridge strips to drone alone seven seconds, one folded harmony answers; drone undiminished through the final second.
