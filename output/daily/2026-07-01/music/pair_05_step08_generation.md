## V1 — "The Bright One's Watch" (RAW)

**Music (raw):** Vigilant cosmic raga-drone. Voice, an unblinking tanpura-like microtonal drone, and a single clock-tick that never quite locks to the beat. F# harmonic minor, 108 BPM raga-pulse. Opens on the drone alone with a faint granular shimmer inside it — light still arriving from dying shells. Watchful mid-register female vocal, dry and close, logs its observations flat and un-triumphant. Gamaka ornament bends the held raised-7th, a note that wants to resolve and is refused. Tick enters hard-left, drifting bar-counts. One first-person line cracks the log past the midpoint; the drone swells and the tick dies at the bridge, the watch alone. Ends mid-entry. No soprano belt, no house palette.

**Lyric skeleton:**
[Theme: a triple star keeping watch over two doomed shells it cannot save]
- Intro (drone): *low microtonal drone swells*
- V1: log — "It watches the yellow shell fall. It holds its post."
- V2: log — the purple filament, neither shell knows
- Log-refrain: "the bright one keeps its post"
- V3: log — the light that is a countdown
- Hinge (fact): six thousand light-years, not one a warning
- [CRACK — first person]: "I hold a post that holds nothing whole"
- V4: log resumes, detached again
- Bridge (drone alone): *tick stops*
- Outro: stops mid-entry — "the bright one keeps its—"
