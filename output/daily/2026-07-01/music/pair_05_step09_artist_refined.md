## V1 — "The Bright One's Watch" (refined)

**Music:** Vigilant cosmic raga-drone in F-sharp harmonic minor at a 108 BPM raga-pulse, built from three sources only — a watchful mid-register female voice, an unblinking tanpura-like microtonal drone with jivali resonance, and one clock-tick that drifts off the grid. It opens on the drone alone, a faint granular shimmer of fertile life bubbling inside it — light still arriving from shells already dying. The voice logs its observations flat and un-triumphant, dry and close to the ear, center-depth. A gamaka bends the raised seventh, a note that aches to resolve and is refused. The tick enters hard-left, never syncing. Past the midpoint a single first-person line cracks the log. The bridge swells the drone and lets the tick die — the watch alone. It ends mid-entry.
