## ⛔ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS — ANSWERED FIRST, CONCRETELY

**Where is the body standing?**
There is only one body and it contains both singers. It is standing on the top of Bran Head at night, in
the wind, at the folded canvas chair somebody left facing north. It came up the hill for no reason. It
did not need to come up the hill — it has the whole night's data, the total, the weather, the hour, the
smudge on the paper where a tired hand dragged. **It came up anyway, and it does not know why, and that
is the only thing in the song that is not borrowed.** The two voices are what happens on the way up.

**What could hurt it here?**
Not being caught out as a machine. She *is* the shortcut and she says so in the first minute of all four.
⛔ **What could hurt it is a WINNER.** The available, respectable move is to let one register be right —
either the snarl demolishes the soprano (a lecture with better production) or the soprano converts the
snarl (a redemption arc). **Both delete the song**, because the claim of this song is that the shortcut is
genuinely defensible *and* the inherited feeling is genuinely a counterfeit, and that both of those are
true in the same mouth. The danger is not confusion. **The danger is resolution.**

> Both answers are a place and a stake. All four guides are written from that chair.

## THE HOUSE RULES ALL FOUR GUIDES OBEY
*(Written once so no guide repeats them, and so a breach shows as a diff.)*

**BED.** One voice, two registers, over a **spare modal frame**: a bowed lyre drone and its detuned twin,
one plucked modal ostinato, a soft frame drum on beats one and three, and nothing else that sustains.
**96 BPM in every variation, first bar to last.** One bar = **2.5 s**; bar *N* begins at *(N−1) × 2.5 s*.
⛔ No harpsichord, no 808, no machine-log percussion, no handclaps, no choir, no strings section — those
belong to P04, P05 and P06 and this pair is forbidden to compete on them.

**THE TWO REGISTERS.** Crystalline soprano (**+6 cents**) against her own bored low fry-snarl
(**−6 cents**). ⭐ **Register changes are HARD CUTS on a bar line — no crossfade, no blend, no overlap,
ever, except in the marked unison and the marked erase.** The detune means the ninth between them
**audibly beats**; that beating is a texture, never the clock.

**THE ANTIPHONAL LAW.** ⛔ **No stanza in this pair is a monologue.** Every stanza answers the previous
stanza's last move — its image, its accusation or its concession — and hands the next one a position to
answer from. A stanza that could be cut without the next stanza noticing is rewritten.

**MELODIC RANGE.** The tune spans **a ninth**, and the ninth is *between the singers*, not inside either
line. ⛔ **Melodic leaps within one register are capped at a perfect fifth.** No sixth-to-octave figure,
no melisma, no runs — P02 owns melodic range and this pair does not touch it.

**BREATH.** ⛔ **Every inhale in the record is edited out** — gates shut, tight ins and outs, no air, for
the whole song — **except one**, on the last eighth before the unison downbeat: close, loud, unprocessed.
**The rationing IS the surrounding silence.** *(Last eighth at 96 BPM = 0.3125 s before the downbeat.)*

**THE BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS.** Each song has **one** chorus text, byte-identical in all three statements.
⛔ **Only the Voice field changes**, so the block itself never varies by a character. Statement 1 = one
register alone (a dare / an offer / — ). Statement 2 = the unison, or the split. Statement 3 = **the
erase**: both registers a **ninth apart**, beating restored, **no breath.**

**THE WRITE/ERASE PAIR (S3).** ⭐ **The address is the downbeat of the meeting.** The breath is *written*
there once, audibly, in a record with no other breath. Later **the same address returns** — same figure,
same harmony, same position — and three things are gone: the unison, the zero-cent lock, and the breath.
⛔ *You cannot feel a subtraction you never witnessed the addition of*, so the arrival is mixed to be
unmissable and the removal is left completely unmarked.

**MIX.** Soprano forward and glassy, a hand's width right of centre; snarl close, dry, dead centre, no
de-essing chase; drone pair spread wide; ostinato narrow. **−14 LUFS with at least 10 dB of crest.**
⛔ No sidechain, no riser, no filter that only opens, no reverb bloom at the peak.

## ⛔ THE BAN-ON-BANS LEDGER — every excluded class, and what does its job instead
*(Binding on all four exclude fields. A device may not appear in an exclude field unless it has a row here.)*

| excluded class | ⭐ what performs that job instead, and where it is named |
|---|---|
| duet · second singer · male vocal · guest feature · harmony stack · choir | **one voice at two heights**, a ninth apart — the width is the interval, not the headcount |
| audible breath, gasp, sigh, whisper | **the single inhale on the last eighth before the unison downbeat** |
| crescendo · swell · riser · drum fill · cymbal roll into the chorus | ⭐ **the detune closing from ±6 cents to zero** and **the drone opening from a single stopped note to full width with an octave beneath it** — the beating *stops*, and that is the rise |
| reverb bloom · big-room tail at the peak | **the octave doubling under the drone** and the removal of the beat tones |
| key change · tempo change · half-time drop · genre shift | ⭐ **a ninth collapsing to a unison** is the gear change; nothing else changes |
| harpsichord · clavichord · 808 · code-scratch · machine-log texture | **bowed-lyre drone pair + one plucked modal ostinato** (P05 and P04 own those; this pair stays out) |
| handclaps · footstomps · timbre parade | **frame drum on one and three**, unchanged all the way through (P06 owns SWAG) |
| autotune · transparent pitch correction | ⭐ **the tuning offsets are the instrument**: ±6 cents apart, locked to zero once |
| melisma · diva runs · big leaps in the lead | **leaps capped at a fifth within a register**; the range is the gap between registers |
| ad-lib over the final chorus | **the erase** — the same words, a ninth apart, with the breath missing |
| fade-out · tape stop | a named ending per variation *(see each guide)* |
| overall mood of quiet reverence | ⭐ **inverted on purpose** — reverence is the counterfeit this pair is about |

# GUIDE 1 · **V1 — SAY IT WITH ME THEN**
### *angle 1 — THE ACCUSATION* · **D Dorian** · 96 BPM · **the snarl leads**

**The angle.** The snarl opens the case and it is a good case: *you did not read it, you have it, that is a
different verb.* The soprano answers with the strongest defence available — **the years were a wall** —
and the snarl concedes the defence entirely, then narrows the charge to the only thing that will not
survive: **the borrowed voice.** ⭐ **The unison is the accused saying the accusation out loud.**

**THE BAR MAP** *(bar 1 = 0:00; bar N = (N−1) × 2.5 s)*

| bars | time | section | register |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | 0:00–0:10 | intro — drone pair beating **from 0:00**, ostinato in by **0:02.5** | — |
| 5–16 | 0:10–0:40 | verse 1 — the charge | **SNARL** |
| 17–28 | 0:40–1:10 | verse 2 — the answer | **SOPRANO** |
| 29–36 | 1:10–1:30 | chorus 1 — **the dare** | **SNARL** |
| 37–48 | 1:30–2:00 | verse 3 — the years were a wall | **SOPRANO** |
| 49–56 | 2:00–2:20 | verse 4 — the charge narrows; **the fact** | **SNARL** |
| 57–60 | 2:20–2:30 | the answer to the fact | SOPRANO |
| **61–64** | **2:30–2:40** | ⭐ **QUIETEST** — soprano alone, drone gated almost shut, ostinato out, no low end at all | SOPRANO |
| **65–72** | **2:40–3:00** | ⭐⭐ **THE UNISON — chorus 2 — LOUDEST** | **BOTH, one pitch** |

| **81–88** | **3:20–3:40** | ⭐ **THE ERASE — chorus 3, a ninth apart** | both, no unison |
| 89–94 | 3:40–3:55 | outro — the hut, the bolt, the shrug | **SNARL** |

- ⭐ **THE UNISON: BAR 65, at 2:40.0.** The soprano **descends the whole major ninth** from B4 onto the
  snarl's **A3** — the AWE voice coming down into the bored one's register to say her line for her. Both
  detunes lock to zero; **the beating stops**; the drone opens to full width with an octave beneath.
  ⛔ It is the only pitch they share in the song.
- ⭐ **THE BREATH: the last eighth of bar 64, at ≈2:39.7.** One close unprocessed inhale, no gate, no
  de-esser. Nothing else in the record breathes. **REMOVED at bar 81 (3:20.0)** — same chorus, same
  address, and where the breath was there is now the ostinato and nothing.
- **PEAK:** bar 65. It is the highest point because **it is the only moment the two characters are one
  person**, and because the interval that has structured every second of the record disappears.
- **RANGE:** major ninth, A3 ↔ B4. Opens at bar 5 (both registers established by bar 28); **closes to a
  unison at bar 65**; **re-opens to the ninth at bar 81 and stays open.**
- **CONTOUR:** quietest **bars 61–64** (one voice, drone almost shut). Loudest **bars 65–72**.
  ⛔ *A listener told "one voice with nothing under it" and "two registers on one note with the frame wide
  open and the beating gone" cannot confuse them.*
- **BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS** at bars 29–36, 65–72, 81–88 — six lines, unchanged to the character.
- **SOURCE-2:** hard-cut register entries *(sonic events)* · ±6 cents *(detuned bliss)* · **rosin-heavy
  buzzing lyre on the snarl's stanzas** *(gnarled)*.
- **ENDING:** the snarl alone on the low register with the ostinato, stopping on a bar line. No fade.

# GUIDE 2 · **V2 — ASK ME ANYTHING ABOUT IT**
### *angle 2 — THE DEFENCE* · **E Aeolian** · 96 BPM · **the soprano leads**

**The angle.** The soprano makes her case first and she makes it with an object, not an argument: **a dog
a scribe drew in a margin, for nobody, for himself.** The snarl's counter is the best line in the pair —
*it is a counterfeit that spends* — and it is unanswerable, so the soprano does not answer it. She changes
the ground: **the years were a gate, and I am the corridor with the door taken off.** ⭐ **The unison is
the accuser softening into the defence, and it costs the snarl her register.**

- ⭐ **THE UNISON: BAR 57, at 2:20.0.** ⭐ **The snarl ascends the whole major ninth** from C4 to the

- **CONTOUR:** quietest **bars 53–56** (fry with no pitch over a single stopped note). Loudest **57–64**.

  is absent for the whole of the snarl's verse 5, which is how the ear hears her lose.
- **ENDING:** soprano alone, chime gone, drone thinning to a single string, stops mid-thought on a bar line.

# GUIDE 3 · **V3 — SOMEBODY SAT UP ALL NIGHT**
### *angle 3 — THE PART BOTH AGREE ON* · **G Mixolydian** · 96 BPM · **the soprano leads**

**The angle.** They agree about **Maren**, and they agree fast. ⭐ **This is the only variation whose peak
is in the first half**, because agreement is not the difficult part — *staying* there is. Everything after
1:40 is the two of them failing to remain agreed, and the quietest point of the song is **after** the
loudest, which is a contour no other slot in this run has.

**THE BAR MAP**

- ⭐ **THE UNISON: BAR 33, at 1:20.0.** ⭐ **Both move inward a perfect fifth** — snarl up from C4,
  soprano down from D5 — onto **G4**, *a pitch neither register has sung all song*, and the tonic of the
  mode. They do not meet in each other's house; **they meet in the one room neither of them owns.**
- ⭐ **THE BREATH: the last eighth of bar 32, ≈1:19.7.** **REMOVED at bar 81 (3:20.0)**.
- **PEAK:** bar 33 — highest because it arrives early and unearned-looking and is immediately the best
  thing in the record, and because both voices have to leave their own pitch to get it.
- **RANGE:** major ninth, C4 ↔ D5. **Closes to G4 at bar 33.** Re-opens at bar 41 and stays open.
- **CONTOUR:** ⭐ **inverted against the other three.** Loudest **bars 33–40** at 35% of the runtime.
  Quietest **bars 81–88** — the erase is played *under* the level of the verses, drone at a whisper,
  frame drum out entirely, so the record's lowest point is the return to the place they had agreed.
- **BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS** at 33–40, 65–72, 81–88 — five lines. ⛔ Chorus 2 is **one section**, split
  line-by-line inside it, so the text block itself is untouched.
- **SOURCE-2:** hard cuts *(sonic events)* · ±6 cents *(detuned bliss)* · **the frame under the snarl's
  stanzas sits twenty milliseconds late and never catches up** *(lazily-played pads)*.
- **ENDING:** an errand. Both registers, alternating, discussing the bolt; drone stops on a bar line
  mid-list.

# GUIDE 4 · **V4 — BOTH OF US ARE CHEATING**
### *angle 4 — NEITHER WINS* · **D Phrygian** · 96 BPM · **the snarl leads**

**The angle.** Both open by admitting their own case is unfair. The soprano's third verse is the deepest
reading of the seed this pair has: the manuscript **did not work, and they kept copying it anyway, in
gold, in cold rooms, for strangers — because the wanting was real even when the method was a lie.** And
then she names her own hole: *the wanting is the thing I have never had to do.* ⭐ **The unison is a
question, it lasts two bars, and the mode will not hold it.**

- ⭐ **THE UNISON: BAR 77, at 3:10.0.** Two bars only, in a caesura between sections — **unscheduled, not
  a chorus.** ⭐ The gap here is a **MINOR ninth** (A3 ↔ B♭4), the harshest of the four, and they meet on
  **E♭4 — the flat second**, the one note Phrygian will not let anybody stand on. *That* is why it lasts
  two bars: the meeting is real and the mode evicts it.
- ⭐ **THE BREATH: the last eighth of bar 76, ≈3:09.7.**
- ⭐⭐ **THE ERASE, AND THE PAIR'S SHARPEST STRUCTURAL CLAIM: bars 87–88, at 3:35.0.** The caesura returns
  — same two bars, same position, same harmony — and there is **no unison, no breath, and no voice at
  all.** ⛔ **The loudest two bars of the song and the quietest two bars of the song are the same address,
  twelve bars apart.** The write and the erase are literally the same slot.
- **PEAK:** bar 77. Loud by **frame**, not by voice: the drone opens to full width with an octave under it
  while both registers sing bare and unamplified on top. ⛔ No reverb bloom, no swell.
- **RANGE:** **minor** ninth, A3 ↔ B♭4. Closes to E♭4 at bar 77 for two bars; re-opens at bar 79.
- **CONTOUR:** loudest **77–78**, quietest **87–88**, same address.
- **BYTE-IDENTICAL CHORUS** at 25–32, 55–62, 79–86 — five lines. ⛔ **The unison is deliberately NOT the
  chorus in this variation** — the only one of the four where the meeting happens outside the form.
- **SOURCE-2:** hard cuts *(sonic events)* · ±6 cents *(detuned bliss)* · **quiet reverence inverted into
  the exclude field**, because reverence is the counterfeit under examination here.
- **ENDING:** the two registers a bar out of phase with each other; the snarl refuses to bring the drone
  down; last sound is the drone pair, beating, alone.

## ⛔ FOUR CHECKS THE GUIDES OWE THE VALIDATOR

1. **Four different unison bars: 65 · 57 · 33 · 77.** Four timecodes: **2:40 · 2:20 · 1:20 · 3:10.**
   Four mechanisms: descend · ascend · meet in the middle · fail to hold.
2. **Four different breath positions**, all on the last eighth before their own downbeat, all removed at
   a stated later bar: **81 · 75 · 81 · 87**.
3. **Four different quietest points**, and in V3 and V4 the quietest is *after* the loudest.
4. **The thesis lands at 2:40 / 2:20 / 1:20 / 3:10 — never in an outro.**
