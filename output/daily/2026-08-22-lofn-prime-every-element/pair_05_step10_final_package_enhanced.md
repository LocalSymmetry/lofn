## DEDICATION

```
The table of ornament signs in this song is real and it is not ours: J. S. Bach drew thirteen of them at the front of a teaching book in 1720 and nobody has ever needed a second edition. The clavichord is audible today because Ralph Kirkpatrick refused to let it go. The low end is a tuned 808 kick — Tadao Kikumoto designed the machine that makes it, and he is almost never named. We asked for these figures, so we name them. Generated, not sampled. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.
```

## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- **Kept: the singer enters a beat early at 2:04 and never corrects it.** Suno's instinct is to re-lock a
  vocal to the grid. The early entry is not a performance flaw — it is **what the singer does across the
  seam** (E12), and it is stated in the prompt, in the section headers and in the lyric itself. From the
  peak to the last bar **her body and the machine's grid disagree, audibly, and nobody fixes it.**
- **Kept: the bare 808 bar sits AFTER the peak, at 2:17.** The other three put it before or elsewhere.
