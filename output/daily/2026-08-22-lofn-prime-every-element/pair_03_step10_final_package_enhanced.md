## DEDICATION

```
The instrument is the Nordic bowed lyre played with too much rosin, and its detuned twin — a folk lineage carried by players and makers who are never on anybody's credit list, and by the beat tones between two nearly-equal strings, which is how tuning worked before meters. The manuscript is the anonymous Ars Notoria: scribes who kept copying, in gold, a method that did not work. Generated, not sampled. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.
```

## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- **Kept: the loudest two bars and the quietest two bars are the same two bars.** The unison happens at
  3:10 in a gap between sections — unscheduled, outside the form — and at 3:35 that gap comes back with
  nothing in it. ⭐ **The write and the erase are literally the same slot**, which is the closest this run
  gets to the source form rule as a single audible object.
- **Kept: a minor ninth, not a major one.** The other three variations sit a major ninth apart; here the
  gap is a semitone tighter and much worse to listen to, and they meet on the flat second — the one note
  Phrygian will not let anybody stand on. **That is why the meeting lasts two bars.** Neither wins because
  the mode does not permit a winner.
- **Changed at step 09:** the outro was the bolt and the stone again — which is **V3's closing move**, and
  four variations that end on the same errand are one variation with four coats on. Replaced with the
  snarl refusing to bring the drone down so the soprano keeps talking. ⛔ *The tenderness had to come from
  the bratty one or the song resolves, and resolution is the thing this pair is built to refuse.*
