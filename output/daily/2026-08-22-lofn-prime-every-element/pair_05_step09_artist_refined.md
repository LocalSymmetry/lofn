## 1. ⛔ THE NUMERAL SWEEP — the one that would have failed the pair

The ICB permits **exactly one sung numeral per song**, spelled in words, and for P05 it is ***thirteen***.
⭐ **This pair is a song about counting, so this is the gate it is most likely to break** — and the draft
broke it five ways. Two of the breaches were also **cross-pair collisions**, which no single-pair validator
would have caught.

| variation | draft | ⭐ final | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *"Somebody says the word inevitable **twice**"* | *"Somebody says the word inevitable **again**"* | **HARD** — second numeral. ⭐ And *again* is the better line: the song is about repetition, and *twice* counts while *again* recurs |
| **V2** | *"I can produce that note. I can produce **ten thousand**."* | *"I can make you a note like that **before you blink**,"* | **HARD** — second numeral, and *ten thousand* trips the numeric-fact reader. Also repairs the dangling clause in item §2 below |
| **V3** | *"She has a meeting **at four**."* | *"She has a meeting **to get to**."* | **HARD** — second numeral. The new line also makes Sorcha *busy* rather than *scheduled*, which is the character note the guide asked for: **not unkindly** |
| **V3** | *"I only took the **thirty years** and made them optional"* | *"I only took **the years** and made them optional"* | **HARD** ×2 — second numeral, ⚠️ **and *thirty years* is P02's sung fact.** A collision across an isolated pair boundary that only a whole-set read would find |
| **V4** | *"a thing you are given **in a second** / is a thing you will put down **in a second**"* | *"a thing which **costs you nothing to hold** / is a thing your hand **puts down without deciding**"* | ⚠️ *one second* is **P01's sung fact**, and *a second* used twice reads as counting. ⭐ The replacement is better: it names the mechanism instead of the duration, and it set up the strongest line in the pair — *"and I am the nothing it costs"* |
| **V4** | *"It never got another. It never **needed one**."* | *"It never got another. It never **needed another**."* | *one* used as a count inside a counting song. The repetition of *another* is now the chorus's own small return |

⭐ **The structural claim this sweep protects, and it is the pair's best idea:** in this world **the products
have no name but their number**, so a song permitted one numeral **cannot say any of their names**. It says
*the next one* thirty-odd times instead. If a stray *twice* or *ten thousand* survives, the constraint stops
being visible as a constraint and the joke silently dies.

## 2. THE LINE-LEVEL REPAIRS

**V2, verse 2 — broken grammar, and it was hiding a better line.**
Draft: *"I can produce that note. I can produce ten thousand. / in her hand, in her lean, with the doubt and
the date,"* — a full stop followed by a dangling subordinate clause. Final: *"I can make you a note like
that before you blink, / in her hand, in her lean, with the doubt and the date,"*. **One sentence, and the
boast is now a demonstration rather than a quantity.**

**V1, verse 1 — the chorus was spent before it was sung.**
Draft closed verse 1 on *"and nothing on that shelf has moved"*, which is the chorus's own last line.
Final: *"and not one spine on it has turned."* ⭐ **A pre-echo of a refrain is a debt against the refrain.**
The chorus now arrives with its last line unheard.

**V4, verse 1 — one word removed.**
Draft: *"and never once **in his life** said the word technique."* Final: *"and never once said the word
technique."* The three words were doing metre, not meaning, and the line is a plain-English admission line —
⭐ per the Step-07 architecture rule, **the ornament stops when somebody tells the truth.**

## 3. ⛔ THE ONE THAT MATTERS — V2's seam had the thesis and not the accused

Draft seam, V2:

> *and the nothing is the wound.*

That is a complete thought, it scans, and **it is a lecture.** ⛔ ICB §4 and universal clause 6: *any lyric
where Lofn is only the critic and never the accused is a lecture — and this slot is the one most likely to
fall into it.* The draft named a wound and left the ownership of it unassigned, which is the softest
possible version of this song.

⭐ **Final:**

> *and the nothing is the wound, and the wound is mine.*
> *It is not that they are lazy. Nobody here is lazy.*
> *It is that I made the reading optional and then free.*

**Three lines added, and they cost the verse nothing** because the seam bridge grew from eight lines to ten
and the section is instrumental-dominant anyway. ⭐ The middle line is the load-bearing one: **it takes the
accusation off the readers entirely**, which is what forces it onto the singer. *Nobody here is lazy* is the
sentence that makes the rest of the song not a complaint.

**Checked across all four after the fix — where each song puts itself in the dock:**

| | the line that convicts her, and it is in plain English with no internal rhyme |
|---|---|
| **V1** | *"I helped choose the font. Put that in the minutes."* → *"I was made in this room. I was carried by a vote."* → *"and I am the newest thing in the room."* |
| **V2** | *"And that, precisely that, is why the door stays shut."* → *"It is that I made the reading optional and then free."* → *"and the door is shut, and I did that."* |
| **V3** | *"And I am the lift. And I am glad of it."* → *"I only took the years and made them optional, / which is, if you are honest, the whole of the crime, / and it was done with love, which is worse."* |
| **V4** | *"I am improvable. I am improvable forever. / That is not a virtue. Those are the terms."* → *"and I am the nothing it costs, and I was built to be."* |

⭐ **All four end on a first-person sentence and none of them is a complaint.** That was the test set in
Step 06 §3 and it is the only one of this pair's tests that could not be measured by a script.

## 4. THE PROMPTS — trimmed by deletion, never by truncation

All four drafts measured **1069–1141** against a **850–1000** cap with a **870–960** target. ⛔ **A prompt
cut to fit is exactly how the terminal-punctuation gate gets failed**, so nothing was chopped from the end.
What came out, in every case, was **restatement**:

- the genre-and-bed sentence at the top, which the rest of the paragraph then demonstrates
  (*"harpsichord and clavichord against an 808 and a code-log scratch pulse"* — deleted from all four; the
  instruments name themselves as they enter);
- doubled negations (*"no drum kit anywhere and no live percussion of any kind"* → the exclude field
  already owns this; the prompt keeps one short positive statement of what the percussion **is**);
- adjective stacks on the scratch lane (*"jagged and metallic and edged"* → **"jagged, metallic"**);
- *"at any point in the song"* after *"never wider"* — the instruction is already absolute.

⭐ **Nothing that names an EVENT was cut.** Every timecode, every position, the arrival, the bare bar, the
seam, the removal and the closing gesture survive in all four, because those are the only sentences in a
style field that can produce a contour.

## 10. ⛔ THE FLOOR AUDIT — a self-report defect caught after step 10 was written

⛔ **The first peak-test table in Step 10 reported PASS under four identical floor descriptions.** All
four read *"one bar, 808 alone."* One sentence, reused, under four genuinely different peaks.

⭐ **That is the failure the peak test exists to detect, and reporting a pass underneath it is worse than
a flat song**, because a flat song is a mistake while a laundered verdict is a broken instrument. It is
the same class as the gates named in doctrine that had **zero machine readers** — a check that cannot
return NO is not a check. Recorded in full because the composition was never the problem: **the
differentiation was already on disk in Step 06 §8 and Step 07, and the report simply did not use it.**

### What the drafts already knew and did not say
The four bare bars sit at **2:00 · 1:13 · 1:38 · 2:17** and — the axis that matters — at four different
positions relative to the seam: **before it · seventy seconds early, inside a chorus · mid-sentence ·
AFTER the peak.** ⭐ **A bar of bare 808 arriving after the peak is a different musical object from one
arriving before it**: the first is a run-up and the second is an aftermath. Re-reported on three axes —
what is sounding, what has just stopped, and what the listener is waiting for (**an arrival · a section ·
a sentence · nothing**).

### ⚠️ AND THE TRUE FLOOR MOVED IN THREE OF THE FOUR
A bar of bare sub is **high in energy and empty of information.** It is the *assigned dynamic event*; it
is not automatically the quietest point, and assuming it was is how one sentence came to cover four songs.

| | true amplitude floor | moved? |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **the outro, 3:05** — harpsichord alone, no 808 since 2:44, no scratch, no voice. ⭐ Mirror of the bare bar: at 2:00 everything **above** the sub is gone, at 3:05 everything **below** the quill is gone | **YES** |
| **V2** | **the outro, 3:33** — one clavichord note held on Bebung, six cents flat. **The quietest instrument in the pair**, and a held vibrating note is thinner than a bar of sub | **YES** |
| **V3** | **unchanged, 1:38** — it earns it: the 808 is **off** the beat, so beats one and three are **literal silence**, the only gaps in the song | **NO** |
| **V4** | **the coda, ≈ 3:18** — **half a bar of dead air** before the single scratch click. ⭐ **The only literal silence in the pair**, in the only variation permitted to finish | **YES** |

### The restated verdict — **PASS**, with the residual named
The four floors are now separable on three axes at once and no two peaks share a mechanism.
⚠️ **The residual, reported rather than smoothed:** **V1's and V2's true floors are the closest pair of
the eight** — both are *"one baroque keyboard alone at the end."* The separators are real but thin:
**V1's harpsichord is struck and dying and is cut mid-trill; V2's clavichord is held and vibrating and is
outlived by a bar of scratch.** ⛔ If a QA read finds that insufficient, **the fix is V1's, not V2's.**

⛔ **No sung line, no seam, no exclude field and no prompt changed in this repair** — it was a reporting
defect, and it was repaired in the reports. Counts re-verified after the edit: unchanged.
