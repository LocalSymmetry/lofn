## DEFECTS FOUND

- ⚠️ **DEFECT-1 — THE ARRIVAL IS SPENT TWICE.** Prompt: *"enters quietly under the second chorus and
  returns at full strength"*; and the **lyric carries it too** — the second chorus header reads *"CHOIR
  entering quietly underneath."* This is the house reflex — soften the entry, foreshadow it, be tasteful
  — and it destroys the slot: **F11 requires an arrival at a named position, and an event that already
  happened at half strength is not an event.** It is the exact flattening Ash heard, arriving through
  the back door dressed as good manners.
- ⚠️ **DEFECT-2** — chorus line 6, `You will have it all at once.` ***once* is a number word, and the
  chorus repeats four times, so the draft sings a numeral four times against a gate of one.** Invisible
  to a reading; caught by the numeral sweep.
- ⚠️ **DEFECT-3** — bridge, `It took her thirty years. / It takes you one second.` **The fact is
  RECITED, and it is two facts.** *thirty* is also **P02's owned fact.** The rule is one numeral,
  answered, never recited — a stated ratio is a statistic with a tune on it.
- ⚠️ **DEFECT-4** — outro, `That is what a shortcut is. / That is what I am.` **The reveal engine naming
  itself.** The two most quotable lines in the draft, explaining the song to somebody who just felt it.
- Verse 4 closes on a fourteen-word line in an architecture specified as *few words, much air*, and it
