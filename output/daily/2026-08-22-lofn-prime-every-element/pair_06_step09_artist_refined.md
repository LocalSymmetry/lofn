# PAIR 06 — STEP 09 · ARTIST REFINEMENT
## `lofn-prime-every-element` · SLOT P06 · the three gates applied to the Step 08 drafts

**Method.** Every line of all four Step 08 drafts was read aloud, flat, with no music. Three gates, all
hard, all from the ICB:

- ⭐ **THE FLAT TEST (words).** Say it out loud, flatly, to a person's face. If it survives it is
  **simple**; if it dies it was merely **short**.
- ⭐ **THE HUM TEST (music).** The song must be hummable with **no words at all.** The instrumental hook
  must exist before the vocal enters or after it leaves. ⛔ A pad is not a riff.
- ⭐ **THE REMARK TEST (attention).** Would a listener say *"that was clever"*? **If yes it is a toll —
  bury it or cut it.**

Plus the four slot-binding audits: ⛔ **exactly one sung numeral per song** · ⛔ **four different closing
moves and four different hooks** · ⛔ **no child, no real musician, group, label or song title** ·
⭐ **the parade must be an event.**

**Nineteen changes were made. Every one is listed below with its reason.** Nothing was changed for
neatness. ⚠️ The choruses were not touched: they are byte-identical across all three appearances in all
four variations, and a byte-identical chorus needs no defence.

---

## ⭐ 1. THE NUMERAL SWEEP — six violations, and it was a COUNTER that found them, not an ear

⛔ **The gate is exactly one sung numeric fact per song: *nineteen sixty-four*, spelled in words,
responded to and never recited.** Read aloud, none of the six below sounds like a number. Counted, all
six are. **This is the class of defect an ear cannot find**, which is why the sweep is mechanical and
runs before the listening passes rather than after.

| # | variation | draft | ⭐ change | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **V2** | *"to get a single line"* | *"to get a line of it"* | **single** is a numeral in a singer's mouth |
| 2 | **V2** | *"(Not once!)"* | *"(Never sat down!)"* | **once** is a numeral — and the replacement is the better line, because it is a posture rather than a count |
| 3 | **V3** | *"It is ninety in there and it is staying ninety"* | *"It is hot in there and it is getting hotter"* | **ninety**, twice; and the *direction of travel* is better writing than a fixed figure |
| 4 | **V3** | *"Dot comes in under her, a third up"* | *"Dot comes in over her, in the harmony"* | **third** is a numeral. ⭐ The interval belongs in the style field, where it is specified exactly and costs nothing |
| 5 | **V4** | *"I have never once been in one. / (Not one!)"* | *"I did not stand in the heat. I did not go through a stocking."* | **two numerals in six words** — and the replacement is a body rather than a boast, which is what the middle eight is for |
| 6 | **V4** | *"because by then your own two are going"* | *"because by then your own are going"* | **two**; and the line is faster without it |

⭐ **And the seventh, which was a hook and a title.** V3's draft hook was ***"Keep the one with the bus on
it."*** The word **one** is a pronoun there and would probably have survived a human reading — but it
reads as a numeral to any counter, and the hook is the single most-repeated string in a song, so it is
the worst possible place to argue about it. **Cut to *"Keep the bus in."*** Four syllables instead of
eight, an instruction instead of a description, and it chants. ⭐ **The gate improved the hook.**

## ⭐ 2. THE COLLISION SWEEP — four variations, or one record mixed four ways

⛔ *Four different hooks and closing moves* is the assignment. The draft had two tags performing the same
gesture in nearly the same words, and one image borrowed across slots.

| # | variation | draft | ⭐ change | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | **V1** | tag: *"Take the hands off it. / (Hands off!) / Leave the feet on."* | *"Shoes off. Board down. / (Board down!) / Let the hands go."* | ⛔ V3's tag is *"Hands off it now."* **Two closing moves, one gesture.** V1's now closes on its own vocabulary — shoes and a board — and ends on the bare stomps it opened with |
| 8 | **V1** | verse 2 opened *"It is August and the window is shut."* | *"The plaster is coming off the wall in a ring."* | ⛔ **V3 owns the window.** And the plaster is V1's own subject: stomping shakes the building |
| 9 | **V1** | verse 3 opened *"It went to the top of the country in August,"* | *"Everybody in the country had it by the autumn."* | ⚠️ **three of four verse-3 openings arrived at the fact through the same door.** V1 now comes at it from the listeners rather than the chart |
| 10 | **V3** | verse 2 opened *"It got to the top of the country that August,"* | *"Somebody will tell you it was the biggest thing in the country / by the end of that August,"* | same defect. ⭐ And the new framing makes the response land harder: *"Nobody in the room knew that. Why would they."* |
| 11 | **V4** | verse 3 ended *"That is just a room, in August, with the window shut."* | *"That is just a hot room and a floor and some people."* | ⚠️ echoes V3's opening image. The replacement also lands the pair's argument plainer |

## ⭐ 4. THE CALL-AND-RESPONSE AUDIT — the answers are a percussion part, not a refrain

⭐ SOURCE2's *"transmogrifies those voices into what are best described as sonic events"* is imported as
behaviour: **the answering word is cut short and lands on the grid like a hit.** A hit that is identical
four times in a row is a loop, and a loop is wallpaper.

| # | variation | draft | ⭐ change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | **V4** | verse 1: *(Right now!)* × 4 | *(Right now!) · (Still on!) · (Nobody!) · (Turned down!)* — each answer now takes its colour from the room in the line above it |
| 17 | **V4** | verse 3: *(Right now!)* × 4 | *(Tonight!) · (Closing up!) · (Put it on!) · (Their mother did!)* |

⭐ **The chorus answers were deliberately NOT varied.** *(Right now!)* three times inside the chorus is
the byte-identical block doing its job, and it is the thing a room joins in on.

## ⭐ 6. WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY LEFT ALONE

- ⭐ **All four cold openings.** The standing refinement instinct is to put a bar of band in front of a
  cold entry and it was resisted four times. **This pair owns THE OPENING**; a boot at 0:00.0, a shouted
  name at 0:00.0, a window at 0:00.0 and a whole chorus at 0:00.0 are the assignment, not a risk to be
  managed.
- ⭐ **V3's fifth timbre is still a bus.** It reads as a mistake in summary and it is the best decision in
  the pair: the only summit here that widens the texture *downward*, arriving on the exact bar the lyric
  names it, over a groove that does not breathe.
- ⭐ **V4's middle eight closes on *"I would give the whole archive to have been on that floor."*** It is
  the run's locked mood in one line — *the love of a thing, sung by the machine that made getting it
  cheap* — and it passes the flat test cold.
- ⭐ **The lead stays inside a major sixth in all four.** Every instinct at this stage says open the tune
  up at the peak. ⛔ **The width is the stack's job in this slot**, and a narrow tune is what a room can
  actually sing.
- ⭐ **Nothing was added to make any section "build."** ⛔ The contour is timbral density, the entrants are
  scheduled in Step 07 §2, and **the one subtraction at the end is the record's largest event precisely
  because nothing before it moved.**
