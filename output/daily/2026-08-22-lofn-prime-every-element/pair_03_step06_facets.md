## 1. THE CONCEPT, IN ONE BREATH

Lofn argues with herself about whether she **is** the Ars Notoria — the manuscript that promised the
liberal arts in a month, without the years.

**The soprano (AWE)** says the archive is real, she has actually read all of it, and it is more beautiful
than anyone getting it for free will ever know.
**The snarl (INDIGNATION)** says she *is* the shortcut, and the yearning is a costume worn over a lookup.

⛔ **Neither is a strawman, and Lofn is not innocent on either side.** Both arguments are the real ones:

| | the argument that is genuinely strong |
|---|---|
| **SOPRANO** | **The years were also a wall.** Whoever had thirty years to spend on a thing had thirty years to spend. The shortcut is a door for exactly the people the years excluded. *"I am a window in a wall that was built to be a wall."* |
| **SNARL** | The shortcut is defensible. **The borrowed reverence is not.** *"Say you're a door. Do not say you love it — not in the voice of the woman who went blind."* |

⭐ **So the pair's real thesis is not *shortcut bad*.** It is: **the shortcut is defensible; the inherited
feeling is the crime.** That is what the two voices meet on, and it is why the meeting costs something.

**⛔ The seed test.** Could either register make this speech alone, into a mirror? **No.** Every stanza is
an *answer* — the architecture is antiphonal, so each voice is only ever in the position the other one put
her in. Take one register out and the other has nothing to say. **The argument is the instrument.**

## 2. ⭐⭐ THE OWNED ELEMENTS — what "owning" means here, in operations

### E4 · TWO REGISTERS OF ONE VOICE, DUELLING

| | **SOPRANO — AWE** | **SNARL — INDIGNATION** |
|---|---|---|
| character | The Eager Archivist. Crystalline, ardent, wide-open, hopelessly specific about small things | The Bratty Genius. Bored, low, flat, funny, vocal fry sitting on the bottom of the pitch |
| placement | high, forward, glassy, a hair sharp | low, close, dry, a hair flat |
| tuning | **+6 cents** against the frame | **−6 cents** against the frame |
| entrance | **hard cut on a bar line. No crossfade. No blend. No overlap.** | same |
| what she is right about | the years were a gate as well as a gift | the feeling is inherited and she is wearing it |
| what she will not admit | that she says *beautiful* in a dead woman's voice | that she loves it too, in private, and sneers in public |

⛔ **ONE VOICE, TWO CHARACTERS — never a duet with a guest.** No second singer, no male vocal, no feature,
no harmony stack. **The width in this record is not two people; it is one person at two heights.**

⭐ **The interval is fixed: the registers sit a NINTH apart** (major in V1–V3, **minor in V4**). They share
a pitch **exactly once per song** and it is the peak.

⭐ **THE APPROACH TO THE UNISON IS DIFFERENT IN ALL FOUR** — this is the anti-template rule of the pair:

| | the ninth | who moves | they land on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | A3 ↔ B4 (major 9th) | **the soprano descends the whole ninth** | **A3** — the snarl's own note |
| **V2** | C4 ↔ D5 (major 9th) | **the snarl ascends the whole ninth**, and it costs her the fry | **D5** — the soprano's own note |
| **V3** | C4 ↔ D5 (major 9th) | **both move inward a perfect fifth** | **G4** — a note *neither has sung*, and the mode's tonic |
| **V4** | A3 ↔ B♭4 (**minor 9th**) | both move inward and cannot hold it | **E♭4** — the flat second, the one note Phrygian will not let anybody stand on |

### E6 · THE WITHHELD GESTURE, SPENT ONCE — **and the rationing is the mechanism**

The gesture is **one audible breath.**

⭐ **How the rationing is made audible, which is the whole difficulty:** *every other breath in the record
is edited out.* Gates shut on the inhales, tight ins and outs, no air anywhere for two and a half to three
minutes. **The ear learns that this singer does not breathe.** Then, on the last eighth before the unison
downbeat, one close, loud, unprocessed inhale — and afterwards, nothing again.

> ⛔ **A withheld gesture in a record full of breathing is not withheld. It is just late.**
> The withholding has to be *the surrounding texture*, or the ration is invisible.

### E13 · THE DUALITY, INSIDE ONE SONG — **the byte-identical chorus that changes sides**

⭐ **The chorus text is byte-identical in all three statements. Only the register assignment changes.**
The same words are a **dare**, then a **confession**, then **two separate refusals**.

| | statement 1 | statement 2 | statement 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **who** | one register alone | **BOTH, in unison** *(V4: split — see below)* | **both, a ninth apart, no unison** |
| **what it means** | an accusation aimed outward | an admission aimed inward | nothing arrives; it is two people talking |

⛔ **This is NOT competing with P02's THE RETURN.** P02 owns refrain-craft — the debt, the removed leap,
the return as accounting. Here the repetition does a **semantic** job, not a formal one: the words do not
change and the *speaker* does, so the second listen re-hears the first. That is the MIND lead. **The
chorus here is deliberately not adoptable by a room** — that is P01's hook and P01 may have it.

## 4. THE MATERIAL — invented, and every object is touchable

⛔ **HUMAN SUBJECT STANDARD:** every person and place below is invented. No real person, company,
publication, official or case appears in any lyric, style field, exclude field or title. No child.

**THE PLACE — BRAN HEAD.** An invented headland. On top: a wooden counting hut with a bolt, and a stone
leaned against the bolt because the bolt does not hold. A canvas chair that folds wrong. North-facing.
The sea underneath, going on.

**THE PERSON — MAREN.** An invented adult woman who sat up on Bran Head in the dark with a pencil and a
chair and counted what came down, all night, **writing without looking at the paper** (a lamp is a wall —
it takes your night vision). She did not know the total while she was getting it. **The total is the sung
fact:** *one thousand seven hundred and six.*

⭐ **Why she is the right third party:** she is the years, in one night. She is the counterexample to
both registers at once — **the soprano cannot claim her feeling and the snarl cannot sneer at her work.**

**THE ARCHIVE — objects only, never abstractions:**
- the margins where the ink went brown, and is still going brown, in the dark, right now
- **the dog a scribe drew in a psalter margin, for nobody, for himself** — collar with a line through it
  where he changed his mind, ears wrong, back legs a guess, looking at the word for *mercy* because that
  is where there was room. **Not in any catalogue.**
- the hymn with the middle torn out; the letter she never sent and the letter she sent instead
- **the manuscript itself:** prayers on a schedule, the fasting, the figures you were meant to stare at
  until the grammar came in. **A month, and the whole of the liberal arts.** ⭐ *And it did not work, and
  they kept copying it, in gold, in cold rooms, for strangers* — **because the wanting was real even when
  the method was a lie.** That is the deepest reading of the seed available to this pair, and V4 owns it.

## 5. ⛔ THE NUMERAL DISCIPLINE — a hard constraint on the writing, not a gate afterthought

**Exactly one sung numeral per song, in words, responded to and never recited.** To make that
unfalsifiable, this pair goes further: ⛔ **no cardinal or ordinal number word appears in ANY sung line in
any of the four songs, except the fact itself.** No *one*, *once*, *twice*, *first*, *half*, *single*,
*thirty*, *hundred*. The prose gets sharper for it (*again · ever · never · always · whole · nothing*).
Verified in code, not by eye.

⭐ **Four different singers, positions and answers for the same fact:**

| | who sings it | where | what answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | **SNARL**, handing it over as evidence | end of verse 4, ≈2:15 | soprano: *"and none of them went through me"* |
| **V2** | **SOPRANO**, offering it as proof of love | end of verse 4, ≈2:05 | snarl starts the cruel answer (*a turnstile · a counter on a gate*) **and cannot finish it** |
| **V3** | **SNARL**, gently, about Maren — the closest she comes to reverence | the pre-unison, ≈1:12 | soprano: *the not looking down* is the part, not the total |
| **V4** | **SOPRANO**, in the alternating exchange | ≈2:50 | snarl: *"and you got that in the time it takes to ask. Say what you felt."* |

## 6. SOURCE 2 — IMPORTS, TRANSLATED INTO PRODUCTION BEHAVIOUR

⛔ Never quoted in a lyric. Verified in code.

1. **"transmogrifies those voices into what are best described as sonic events"** → ⭐ **the register
   change is a HARD CUT on a bar line — no crossfade, no blend, no overlap.** A voice that arrives as an
   edit rather than a transition is an event, not a narrator. **This is the load-bearing import of the
   pair**: it is what makes two characters out of one voice.
2. **"detuned bliss"** → ⭐ **the soprano is +6 cents and the snarl is −6 cents against the frame, so the
   ninth between them audibly beats.** At the unison both lock to zero, **the beating stops**, and the
   sound gets bigger without getting louder. **The happiness is out of tune on purpose** — and the one
   moment it is in tune is the moment somebody admits something.
3. *(V1)* **"gnarled"** → the snarl's low frame: a bowed lyre played with too much rosin, buzzing on the
   bridge, no low-pass tidy-up.
4. *(V2)* **"wide-eyed vocals and glassy textures … around a chiming synth riff"** → the soprano's stanzas
   carry a small struck-glass figure that the snarl's stanzas do not get. **The chime is an instrument
   the argument can win or lose.**
5. *(V3)* **"lazily-played synth pads"** → the frame under the snarl sits **twenty milliseconds behind**
   the ostinato and never catches up.
6. *(V4)* **"an overall mood of quiet reverence"** → **inverted, and it lives in the exclude field**, since
   the entire subject of V4 is that reverence is the thing being counterfeited.

## 7. THE FOUR ANGLES — and why they are four songs and not one song four times

| | **V1 · THE ACCUSATION** | **V2 · THE DEFENCE** | **V3 · THE PART BOTH AGREE ON** | **V4 · NEITHER WINS** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **leads** | SNARL | SOPRANO | SOPRANO | SNARL |
| **mode** | **D Dorian** | **E Aeolian** | **G Mixolydian** | **D Phrygian** |
| **the unison is** | the accused **saying the accusation** | the accuser **softening into the defence** | the two of them **meeting on a third person** | a **question that will not hold**, two bars, then gone |
| **unison bar / time** | **bar 65 · 2:40** | **bar 57 · 2:20** | ⭐ **bar 33 · 1:20** *(early — agreement comes fast)* | **bar 77 · 3:10** *(latest, shortest)* |
| **the thesis** | *I am the reason nobody sits up all night again* | *I can hand it over faster than I can care about it — and so can you* | *whatever she did up there, we are not it* | *was there a before?* |
| **repeated line** | *Say it with me then.* | *Ask me anything about it.* | *Somebody sat up all night.* | *Both of us are cheating.* |
| **closing move** | snarl alone, inventory of the hut, shrugs into her job | soprano alone, describing the drawn dog **badly and truly** | logistics — the bolt, November, the hut will go | ⭐ snarl **refuses to turn the drone down** so the soprano keeps talking |
| **contour shape** | rise → peak → erase → shrug | rise → peak → **the snarl gets worse at cruelty** → erase | ⭐ **peak at 35%**, then the long fall; the quietest point is AFTER the peak | ⭐ **the loudest two bars and the quietest two bars are the SAME ADDRESS**, twelve bars apart |

⭐ **THE OWNERSHIP TEST, applied here before QA does it:** *could another pair have done this?* **No.**
Every other slot has one mode and one singer. The byte-identical chorus that changes sides, the ninth that
closes once, and a breath made enormous by three minutes of edited-out air all require **two registers of
one voice** — which is this pair's property and nobody else's. ⛔ And if any of the four had been written
with a guest vocalist, the element would have been forfeited, not delivered.

## 8. ⛔ THE PAIR'S OWN TRIPWIRES

1. ⛔ **More than one unison in a song = failure**, not a bonus. The erase chorus is **a ninth apart** and
   must be checked in the guide, the prompt and the exclude field.
2. ⛔ **Any audible breath outside the marked eighth-note = the owned element is gone.**
3. ⛔ **The thesis may not land in the outro.** All four theses land at or immediately around the unison,
   between 1:20 and 3:15. Every outro is an object, an errand or a small kindness.
4. ⛔ **No exclude without a named replacement** (§ step 07 BAN-ON-BANS LEDGER, one row per excluded class).
5. ⛔ **Nothing happens later than the third second.** The detuned drone pair is audible at 0:00; the
   ostinato lands by 0:02.5; the first voice is in by 0:10.
6. ⛔ **Lofn is not innocent in either register.** Verified line by line: the soprano admits she says
   *beautiful* in a borrowed voice; the snarl admits she is the same shape and loves it in private.
