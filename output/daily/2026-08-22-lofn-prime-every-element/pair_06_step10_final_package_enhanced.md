## ⭐ THE ANSWER TO THE ONE QUESTION THIS SLOT HAS TO ANSWER

> **How does a listener feel a rise when nothing gets louder?**

**Because the ear scores intensity as how much of the room is accounted for, not as amplitude.** Every
entrant takes a **frequency band** and a **rhythmic slot** that were audibly empty one bar earlier, and
the **roof of the texture climbs** — a third, then a sixth, then an octave above that — while the lead
never leaves its sixth and the fader never moves. By the fifth timbre there is **no silence left to
fill**, and a body reads a fully-occupied spectrum as loud. Then the count of hands: each arrival is one
more person in that room, and the listener is tracking **how many people are in there.**

⭐ **And the flatness is the instrument, not the absence of one.** Because nothing has changed level for
three minutes, **lifting one element out of one named address is a cliff.** A record that had been
swelling all along could not do that.

## ⭐ THE BAN LEDGER — every excluded device, and what does that job instead

⛔ *THE BAN ON BANS: no exclude field may forbid a device unless the brief names its replacement.*

| excluded | ⭐ what performs that job here |
|---|---|
| crescendo · swell · build · riser · build-and-drop | **the five-entrant parade** — spectral occupancy rises, the fader does not |
| key change · modulation | **the stacked voices raise the roof** — a third, then a sixth, then an octave at the peak — with no chord moving |
| tempo change · double-time · half-time · gear change · genre shift | **subdivision density** — quarters, then the backbeat, then offbeat eighths, then held whole notes, then the peak's new slot |
| drum fill into the chorus | **the entrant lands on the chorus downbeat.** The arrival is the fill |
| fade-out · tape stop | four named endings: bare stomps (V1) · a wood block stopping mid-figure (V2) · the room's own tail (V3) · a whistle taking the vacated address (V4) |
| big held final note · melisma · diva runs | **the fifth timbre carries the hook instead of the voice** — horns, vibraphone, a bus, a whistle |
| massed choir | **three countable voices**, close, stacking from unison into thirds at a named bar |
| big-room reverb · gated reverb · cathedral tail | **the parlour's own short slap-back** — V3 makes it the subject; everything else dry and dead centre |
| autotune · pitch correction · de-essed breaths | ⭐ **the detuned-bliss import** — one named element per song sits a few cents off and is kept; breaths kept |
| eight-oh-eight · sub · trap hi-hats · supersaw · synth pad · sidechain pump | **electric bass in eighths and a wooden floor** carry the low end; the sustained element is a Hammond (V2) or a curtain-damped upright (V3) |
| distorted guitar · fuzz | **the baritone honk** (V1) and **the twelve-string chime** (V4) carry the grit |
| whispered vocal · breathy delivery · spoken narrator · male lead · rap verse | **a warm full-chest alto with two answering voices** — call-and-response does the narrator's job |
| crowd noise · applause | **the handclaps and stomps are the room**, played, in time, on the grid |
| ad-lib on the repeated chorus | **the chorus is byte-identical**; the variety is what has entered underneath it |
| lo-fi crackle · vinyl filter · period pastiche | **period is carried by the instrument list and the tuning**, never by a filter |

## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- ⭐ **THE FLOOR, named: bar 1, 0:00.0 — one boot on a wooden riser, alone.** ⛔ By amplitude a stomp is
  not quiet; by **the metric this package actually argues — bands occupied — bar one is the emptiest bar
  in the record**, one source with silence in every other register. ⭐ **Floor and peak are the same
  subject:** it opens on one foot and summits on three horns playing the tune that foot paid for. *(Added
  at QA repair: the first filing named a peak and no floor. "Steady by design" authorises a flat shape,
  not a missing specification — and an unwritten deliberate choice is indistinguishable from the default.)*
- **Kept: the first sung word lands at bar two, over bare stomps.** Suno's habit is an instrumental
  intro. This slot owns **THE OPENING**, and the rule is that something happens immediately *and it is
  the thing the song is about* — so the subject (a foot on wood) is the literal first sample, and the
  title arrives 1.9 seconds later with nothing under it. **No fade, no atmosphere, no count-in.**
- **Kept: `no handclaps in the tag` in the exclude field.** It is the only exclude in this package with
  no replacement named, and it needs none — **it is not banning a device, it is scheduling the erase.**
  The claps have occupied the backbeat for two and a half minutes; removing them from that exact address
  is S3's write/erase pair and the largest event in a record where nothing else moves.
- **Changed at step 09:** the middle eight originally read *"I have got the whole afternoon and not the
  afternoon."* Cut — it is the best-sounding line in the draft and it fails **THE FLAT TEST**: nobody
  says that to a face. Replaced with *"I have got the sound of it. I have never done it."*
- **Changed at step 09:** verse 2 opened on the shut window in August. Moved out — **V3 owns the
  window**, and two variations describing the same object in the same words is one record mixed twice.
