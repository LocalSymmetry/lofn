## 7. ⭐⭐ THE RUN'S BEST FINDING CAME FROM A PAIR CORRECTING ME

I authorised P02's hard-gate failure on the argument *"fewer lines, more time each, so the tune can
move."* The judge measured it: **41% fewer lines, 36% MORE syllables.** P02 was the most crowded pair in
the run at **13.83 syl/line**; **P01 — never given a density break — was the roomiest at 5.93.**

Asked whether a rising sixth can be sung across 13.8 syllables, P02 answered **no — and corrected the
diagnosis**:

> ⭐ **The leap-word was never the problem.** *wait · up · now · hold* are single stressed syllables at
> line starts. **What had no room was THE LAUNCH** — the line *before* the leap must end on the launch
> note and be **held**, so the ear learns the altitude the sixth departs from. That was the most crowded
> line in the song. And *"quatrains with held line-ends, the held note is where the tune lives"* was
> **its own assigned architecture, violated in three of four songs.**

**13.83 → 10.25 syl/line · 3.15 → 2.34 syllables/second, against P01's 2.31.** No lines added or removed.
V1, already clean at 8.00, was left untouched **as the control that proves the diagnosis.**

⚠️ **Fourth wrong unit in one day:** presence (calcification is frequency) · membership (the predicate was
semantics) · lines (the unit is syllables). `measure_variations.py` now reports syllables per line —
**reported, not gated**, because inventing a threshold today would repeat the exact mistake that produced
the line band.
