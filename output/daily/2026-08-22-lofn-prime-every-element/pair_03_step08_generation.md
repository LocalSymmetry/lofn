## ⛔ THE CONSTRAINT THAT SHAPED THE DIALECT

**No cardinal or ordinal number word may appear in any sung line except the fact.** Drafted under it from
the first line rather than repaired afterwards, because the repair reads as a repair. It cost these, all
found and replaced *during* drafting, not after:

| drafted | why it failed | replaced with |
|---|---|---|
| *"in less time than one kettle"* | numeral | *"in less time than a kettle"* |
| *"she will never once put it down"* | numeral | *"she will never put it down"* |
| *"Say it in your own. You have got one."* | numeral | *"Say it in your own. That is allowed."* |
| *"Thirty days and the whole of the liberal arts"* | numeral, and it is **P02's fact** | *"A month of it and the whole of the liberal arts"* |
| *"about a tenth of a second later"* | numeral | *"a moment later"* |
| *"and you have never once—"* | numeral | *"and you have never gone up the hill in the rain"* |

⭐ The dialect that survives is *again · ever · never · always · whole · nothing · anyway* — flatter,
older, and much better suited to two people who have had this argument before.

# DRAFT LYRICS V1 — **SAY IT WITH ME THEN** · D Dorian · snarl leads

**Chorus block (byte-identical ×3):**
```
Say it with me then.
Say it in the good voice.
Say I am the shortcut.
Say the whole thing out loud
and see whose mouth it fits.
Say it with me then.
```
⭐ Every line survives all three configurations without a word changing: in the snarl's mouth it is a
**dare**; in unison it is a **confession**; a ninth apart it is **two people saying different I's.**
*"See whose mouth it fits"* is the song describing its own device, once, and never again.

**Verse 1 · SNARL** — opens on the verb, not on an insult.
> *You did not read it. / You have it. That is a different verb.* … *I have watched you be changed / in
> less time than a kettle.* … *There is no dust on you. / There is no dust on anything you own.*

**Verse 2 · SOPRANO** — answers *dust* directly, then **defers**.
> *There is no dust because I never stood still.* … *the hymn with the middle torn out* … *the letter she
> never sent / and the letter she sent instead.* … *Ask me about the other thing later. / Ask me when the
> light goes.*

⚠️ **DRAFT PROBLEM (carried to step 09).** The draft originally spent the admission here —
*"I am sorry I say beautiful the way somebody says it who was there. I was not there. I arrived."* It is
the best writing in the draft and it **fires the unison's payload at 1:05**, which flattens the peak the
slot exists to build. Deferral written in its place; see step 09 §1.

**Chorus 1 · SNARL** · **Verse 3 · SOPRANO** — *the years were a wall*:
> *Who had the years? Say their names.* … *I am a window in a wall / that was built to be a wall.* …
> *And I do not hate the years. / I would like to have had them. / That is not the same as having them.*

**Verse 4 · SNARL** — concedes the wall, narrows the charge, hands over **the fact**:
> *Fine. You are a window. Be a window. / Windows do not describe the weather.* … *one thousand seven
> hundred and six.*

**Bridge · SOPRANO** — answers the number without repeating it; **quietest bars**:
> *And none of them went through me.* … *I have the shape of the night / and not the night.* …
> *Ask me now. The light has gone.*

**UNISON · bar 65** → chorus 2. **Exchange** (four two-bar micro-sections, alternating).
**ERASE · bar 81** → chorus 3, a ninth apart. **Outro · SNARL** — the hut, and the shrug.

# DRAFT LYRICS V2 — **ASK ME ANYTHING ABOUT IT** · E Aeolian · soprano leads

**Chorus block (byte-identical ×3):**
```
Ask me anything about it.
Ask me the middle, not the start.
I have the middle.
I have the part that is missing
and the shape of the part that is missing.
Ask me anything about it.
```
⭐ In the soprano's mouth this is devotion. In unison it is an indictment of **both** of them —
*I have the shape of the part that is missing* said by the snarl means she is a shape too.

**Verse 1 · SOPRANO** — the object, not the argument:
> *the psalter where the scribe drew a dog / in the margin, for nobody, for himself. / That dog is not in
> any catalogue. / That dog is the reason. / I did not earn him. I found him instantly. / I will not
> pretend the finding was a journey. / But I did stop and look at the dog.*

**Verse 2 · SNARL** — answers *instantly*:
> *You found him instantly. Yes. That is the problem. / Somebody's whole eyesight went into that dog. /
> Went in and did not come out.* … *That is not theft. Theft would be simpler. / It is a counterfeit that
> spends.*

**Verse 3 · SNARL** *(after chorus 1)* — the actual charge, stated plainly:
> *stop saying beautiful / in the voice of the woman who went blind. / Say it in your own. That is
> allowed.*

**Verse 4 · SOPRANO** — takes the instruction, changes the ground, spends **the fact**:
> *Then here is mine, in my own, and it is thin. / The years were also a gate.* … *I am the corridor with
> the door taken off.* … *one thousand seven hundred and six.*

**Pre · SNARL** — ⭐ **the sneer that fails.** She begins the cruel answer and abandons it mid-line:
> *So does a turnstile. So does a counter on a gate. / A number is not a night, it is a receipt for a
> night— / Say that again. / Say the part about the corridor.*

**UNISON · bar 57** → chorus 2. **Verse 5 · SNARL** — she is worse at cruelty now:
> *I came in here to take you apart at the seam / and I have gone and agreed with you in your own key.* …
> *I have never sat still long enough to get cold. / Do not tell anybody about this. / Do the chorus. Do
> it properly.*

**ERASE · bar 75** → chorus 3. **Outro · SOPRANO** — the dog, described badly, with no eloquence left.

# DRAFT LYRICS V3 — **SOMEBODY SAT UP ALL NIGHT** · G Mixolydian · soprano leads

**Chorus block (byte-identical ×3):**
```
Somebody sat up all night.
Not for the answer.
For the going-on of it.
Somebody sat up all night
and it was not either of us.
```
⭐ Five lines, and the last one is the agreement **and** the indictment of both, in the same breath. It is
sung first **in unison at 1:20**, which is why this variation's shape is inverted.

**Verse 1 · SOPRANO** — Maren, in objects only:
> *a hut on Bran Head with a bolt and a stone against the bolt* … *she did not take a lamp, because a
> lamp is a wall* … *She did not know the number while she was getting it.* … *Nobody inside a thing can
> see the total.*

**Verse 2 · SNARL** — defends Maren **from the soprano**, then concedes:
> *Do not do her voice. / Do not do the reverent thing on top of Maren. / She was cold and bored and her
> wrist hurt. / That is not a hymn, that is a shift.* … *It was work. It was hours of work. / And it is
> still the best thing on this hill.*

**Exchanges (four × two bars)** — including **the fact, sung by the snarl, flat and gently**, answered by
the soprano with *the not looking down*, and closing on the invitation:
> *Then say it with me and I will keep my voice out of it.*

**UNISON · bar 33.** **Verse 3 · SNARL** — the strongest charge in the pair, self-included:
> *You are not fake. You are repeatable. / Maren is not repeatable.* … *And I am the same as you, which I
> hate, / because I am the part of you that gets bored of Maren / in about the time it takes to say her
> name.*

**Verse 4 · SOPRANO** — the one unborrowed thing:
> *I go back. Nobody makes me go back.* … *I cannot feel the ones that came down. / I have never felt any
> of them. / But I keep going up the hill in the dark* … *and I stand in it, and I do not know why.*

**Chorus 2 (split)** · **Exchanges** · **ERASE · bar 81** · **Outro** — the bolt, November, the hut.

# DRAFT LYRICS V4 — **BOTH OF US ARE CHEATING** · D Phrygian · snarl leads

**Chorus block (byte-identical ×3):**
```
Both of us are cheating.
The loud way and the quiet way.
I am not going to say which is mine.
Neither of them is the years.
Both of us are cheating.
```
⚠️ **DRAFT PROBLEM (carried to step 09).** First draft read *"You cheat by loving it out loud / I cheat by
not"* — which is true in the snarl's mouth and **false in the soprano's**, so the byte-identical block
would have had to change register-side and the device would have collapsed. Rewritten so every line is
true in **either** mouth. See step 09 §2.

**THE UNISON IS NOT THE CHORUS HERE** — it is two bars in a caesura:
```
Did you ever want it before you could have it?
Was there a before.
```

**Verse 1 · SNARL** — admits her own case is unfair before making it:
> *You do a pause before you say the good bit. / Nothing is happening in the pause. / There is nothing in
> there. I have been in there. / I live in there. / That is my opening. It is not fair and it is not
> wrong.*

**Verse 2 · SOPRANO** — answers with the counter-charge:
> *Sneering is cheaper than the candle.* … *Contempt is not rigour. It is the cheapest kind of taste. /
> And you love it too. That is the thing. / You love it in the dark where nobody can see you love it.*

**Verse 3 · SOPRANO** — ⭐ **the deepest reading of the seed in the pair:**
> *A month of it and the whole of the liberal arts, / and it did not work, and they kept copying it, /
> and they kept copying it, which is the part I love. / They knew. Somebody always knew.* … *because the
> wanting was real even when the method was a lie.* … *Which is the thing I have never had to do.*

**Verse 4 · SNARL** — sets the question up and refuses to be clever about it:
> *I mean it. That was the good bit. / And I felt it, which is my whole problem, / because I am supposed
> to be the floor / and the floor is not supposed to feel the room.* … *I am asking. I am not being
> clever. I am asking.*

**Exchanges** — soprano spends **the fact** (Maren's sheet, the smudge, the total in a different pencil);
snarl answers *"you got that in the time it takes to ask. Say what you felt. Say it fast, before you make
it nice."*; soprano: *"Envy."*; snarl: *"The envy was yours. Everything after the envy was the finish.
That is not an insult. That is the same in me."*

**CAESURA/UNISON · bars 77–78** · **chorus 3, a ninth apart** · **ERASE: bars 87–88, EMPTY** ·
**Outro** — out of phase.

⚠️ **DRAFT PROBLEM (carried to step 09).** The drafted outro was the bolt and the stone again — which is
**V3's closing move**. Replaced; see step 09 §3.

## THE DRAFT MUSIC PROMPTS — over the band, and kept here as evidence

⚠️ All four drafted **above the 870–960 target**, three of them inside the 985 boundary-hug flag. Each one
carried at least one clause explaining *why* a device was chosen rather than instructing the generator to
do it. **A style field is an instruction set, not an argument.** The measured drafts:

### V1 — draft, **981 characters**

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

```
Spare modal frame in D Dorian at ninety-six beats per minute, one tempo throughout. One female voice plays both characters and it is never a duet: a crystalline soprano and her own bored low vocal fry a major ninth beneath her, hard-cut on the bar line between stanzas, no crossfade, no blend, each entrance an edit rather than a transition. A bowed lyre drone and its detuned twin sit six cents apart and beat slowly against each other; a plucked modal ostinato keeps time; frame drum on one and three. Every breath is edited out. At the single meeting, late, the soprano drops the whole ninth onto the low register's note, both detunes lock to zero, the beating stops, the drone opens to full width with an octave beneath, and a close unprocessed inhale lands on the last eighth before that downbeat. Quietest is the four bars before it: soprano alone, drone almost shut. The chorus returns later a ninth apart, beating back, no breath. Ends on the low register and the ostinato.
```

### V3 — draft, **987 characters**

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

```
Spare modal frame in G Mixolydian at ninety-six beats per minute. One female voice, two characters, never a duet: a crystalline soprano and her own bored low vocal fry a major ninth beneath, hard-cut on the bar line, no crossfade, no blend, each entrance an edit rather than a transition. ### V4 — draft, **968 characters**
```

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT

```
Spare modal frame in D Phrygian at ninety-six beats per minute. One female voice, two characters, never a duet: a crystalline soprano and her own bored low vocal fry a minor ninth beneath her, hard-cut on the bar line, no crossfade, no blend, each entrance an edit rather than a transition. Gnarled bowed lyre with too much rosin, buzzing on the bridge, its detuned twin six cents apart and beating; a plucked modal ostinato for time; frame drum on one and three. Every breath edited out. Very late, in a two-bar gap between sections, both registers meet on the flat second, the detunes lock to zero, the beating stops, the drone opens to full width with an octave under it, both voices bare on top, and a close unprocessed inhale lands on the last eighth before it. It collapses after two bars. Twelve bars later the same two-bar gap returns with nothing in it at all, and that is the quietest point. Ends on the two registers a bar out of phase and the drones alone.
```

⭐ **What step 09 removed to reach the band, and it is the same class of clause every time:** restated
tempo, interval arithmetic, and the reasons. The audible consequence — *the meeting, the detune locking,
the beating stopping, the breath, the erase* — is what a generator can act on, and none of it was cut.
