# 1. HOW "EVERY ELEMENT, AT LEAST ONCE" IS READ — and why it is the exact repair

⭐ **"At least once" is a COVERAGE rule across the SET, not a checklist inside every song.** That reading
is not a convenience; it is the direct antidote to what Ash heard.

> **A song that carries fifteen elements at thirty percent each is the mean of our doctrine. Six songs
> that each OWN two or three elements at a hundred percent are a record.**

**The flattening was six songs all hedging toward the middle.** Every one carried a bit of glitch-baroque,
a bit of SWAG, a bit of return, a bit of restraint — and no song was ever *the best example of anything.*
⛔ **Uniform competence across fifteen dimensions IS monotony.** It is what "no highs or lows" sounds like
at the level of a set rather than a bar.

**So: each pair OWNS 2–3 elements and must be the best instance of them we have ever made.** It carries
the others at baseline and is explicitly forbidden from competing on them.

## ⛔ AND THE THREE ASH REPAIRS ARE UNIVERSAL, NOT JUST OWNED

E8, E9 and E10 are **owned** by P01 and P02 — meaning those two must be the *most extreme* instances.
⛔ **But every one of the six is required to ASSIGN all three**, because the finding was that they
default when unassigned:

> **Every song states, in its own brief and in its production spec: (1) its PEAK — what happens, at what
> timecode, and why it is the highest point; (2) its MELODIC RANGE — the interval the tune spans and the
> bar where it opens; (3) its DYNAMIC CONTOUR — where it is quietest and where it is loudest.**

⭐ **THE BAN ON BANS.** ⛔ **No exclude field in this run may forbid a device without the brief naming
what performs that job instead.** Across the last nine songs we banned 89 peak-making devices and asked
for none. **A prohibition with no replacement is how a doctrine produces the mean.**
