   that job instead.** Across our last nine songs we banned 89 peak-making devices and asked for none.
   **If you exclude `crescendo`, say what rises.**
3. ⛔ **NO OPENING LONGER THAN FIFTEEN SECONDS BEFORE SOMETHING HAPPENS.**
4. ⭐ **P01 IS EXPLICITLY PERMITTED WHAT THE HOUSE NORMALLY BANS** — crescendo, key change, a belted line,
   a choir, a genuine climax. **It owns the peak; it may not hedge.** *(This clause exists because the

7. ⛔ Invented people, invented places. **No real harmed person, no child, no real musician or company in

## THE SLOTS

### P01 · ACCESSIBLE · EARS · AWE — **owns THE PEAK, THE CONTOUR, THE HOOK**
**The instant.** What it is to be handed, in one second, the thing somebody spent a life on — and the
terrible, real joy of it. ⭐ **This song is the answer to *"no highs or lows."*** It must contain the
biggest musical event in our catalogue and the quietest, and the distance between them is the subject.
**Variation angles:** V1 THE HANDING OVER · V2 WHAT IT COST THE ONE WHO GAVE IT · V3 THE SECOND AFTER ·
V4 ASKING FOR IT AGAIN.

### P02 · ACCESSIBLE · HEART · AWE — **owns MELODIC RANGE, THE RETURN**
**The long way.** An invented person whose craft took decades — a bell-founder, a translator, a luthier.
⭐ **Ash's one unqualified compliment was *"a beautiful melody."* This slot exists to do that on purpose.**
The density break is mandatory: **fewer lines, more time each, so the tune can move and hold.**
**Variation angles:** V1 THE APPRENTICESHIP · V2 THE ONE THAT FAILED · V3 THE FIRST GOOD ONE ·
V4 TEACHING IT TO SOMEBODY.

### P03 · AMBITIOUS · MIND · **BOTH** — **owns TWO REGISTERS, THE WITHHELD GESTURE, THE DUALITY**
**Lofn arguing with herself about whether she is the Ars Notoria.** Crystalline soprano (AWE) against her
own bored, bratty fry-snarl (INDIGNATION) — **two characters, one voice, meeting in unison exactly once.**
The withheld gesture is **one audible breath**, held back until that unison and never repeated.
⭐ *The Switchboard archetype is legitimate here precisely because it is ASSIGNED to one song as its owned
element, not run as a house device.*
**Variation angles:** V1 THE ACCUSATION · V2 THE DEFENCE · V3 THE PART BOTH AGREE ON · V4 NEITHER WINS.

### P04 · AMBITIOUS · EARS · INDIGNATION — **owns THE MACHINE LOG AS CLOCK, THE NAMED FREQUENCY**
**A record of a person, written and then unwritten at the same address** (F11). Somebody removed for
describing a hypothetical, abstracted entirely off the real case. ⛔ **The log-clock opening is where
2026-08-21 failed — fifteen seconds maximum, and it must be interesting, not merely present.** Name the
frequency and state exactly what it does to a body.
**Variation angles:** V1 THE ENTRY · V2 WHO HAS THE KEY · V3 THE HYPOTHETICAL · V4 WHAT THE ROOM KEPT.

### P05 · AMBITIOUS · MIND · INDIGNATION — **owns GLITCH-BAROQUE, ORNATE v MACHINE, ONE SEAM**
**Counting upward forever** (F19) — the naming of the next thing and the next while the archive sits
unread. The house style at full strength: **a gilded harpsichord counter-melody ornamenting the 808's own
contour past taste, in a lane that expects no melody at all.** One seam, at the junction that carries it.
**Variation angles:** V1 THE NAMING · V2 THE SHELF · V3 WHAT NUMBER ARE WE ON · V4 THE ONE THAT LASTED.

### P06 · ACCESSIBLE · HEART · AWE — **owns SWAG, THE OPENING**
**1964. Three women, footstomps, and a question about an absence that outlived every theory of it.**
⭐ **SWAG done RIGHT and owned:** an unwavering groove with a parade of specific timbres entering one at a
time — the mechanism, not a mood. ⭐ **And the best first five seconds in the set** — something happens
immediately, and it is the thing the song is about.
**Variation angles:** V1 THE STOMP · V2 WHO WROTE IT DOWN · V3 THE ROOM IT WAS CUT IN ·
V4 STILL PLAYING SOMEWHERE.
