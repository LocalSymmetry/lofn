# PAIR 06 — STEP 08 · GENERATION (first full draft, all four variations)
## `lofn-prime-every-element` · SLOT P06 · a back parlour on Tarrant Street, August, the window shut

**Status: DRAFT.** Written straight through against the Step 07 guides. Deliberately **not** yet run
through THE FLAT TEST, THE HUM TEST or THE REMARK TEST — that is Step 09's job — and the weak lines are
left in on purpose so the refinement pass has something real to remove.

**Known problems already visible in this draft, flagged for Step 09:**

1. ⛔ **NUMERALS ARE LEAKING.** The gate is **exactly one sung numeric fact per song**, *nineteen
   sixty-four*, in words. This draft has *once*, *a single line*, *ninety*, *a third up*, *your own two*,
   and a hook built on the word *one*. **Six violations across four songs. All must go.**
2. ⛔ **V1's tag and V3's tag are the same gesture in nearly the same words** (*take the hands off it* /
   *hands off it now*). Four different closing moves is the assignment; two of them currently rhyme.
3. ⛔ **V1 verse 2 opens on the shut window in August.** ⭐ **V3 owns the window.** Two variations
   describing the same object in the same words is one record mixed twice.
4. ⚠️ **Three of the four verse-3 openings begin "It went to the top of the country…"** — the sung fact
   is arriving through the same door three times.
5. ⚠️ **V1's middle eight has the best-sounding line in the pair and it will not survive being said flat
   to a person's face.**
6. ⚠️ **V4's group answers are four identical *(Right now!)* in a row, twice.** Call-and-response is the
   architecture of this slot; identical responses turn it into wallpaper.
7. ⚠️ **V2 draft has *"the girl behind the counter."*** ⛔ No child anywhere in this run.

## WHAT THE DRAFT GOT RIGHT AND SHOULD NOT BE "IMPROVED"

- ⭐ **All four openings are already events**, and all four are different: a boot, a shouted name, a
  window, a chorus. **Do not soften any of them at Step 09.** The commonest refinement instinct is to
  put a bar of band in front of a cold entry, and this slot exists to forbid exactly that.
- ⭐ **The middle eights are the thesis and they are plain.** V4's *"I would give the whole archive to
  have been on that floor"* is the run's locked mood in one line and it survives being said flat.
- ⭐ **The choruses are byte-identical already** and must stay that way through every later pass.
- ⭐ **V3's peak is a bus.** It will look like a mistake in every summary of this pair. It is not.
