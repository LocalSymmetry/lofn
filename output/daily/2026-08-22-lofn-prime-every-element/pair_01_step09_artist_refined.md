# 1. THE REFINEMENT PRINCIPLE FOR A PEAK-OWNING SLOT

> ⭐ **Every fix in this pass must make the song LOUDER OR QUIETER, never more tasteful.**

The failure this run exists to repair is a failure of *taste applied in the wrong direction*. Nine songs
of house style banned 89 peak-making devices and asked for none, and every single one of those bans was
a defensible aesthetic judgement made one at a time. **The refinement pass is exactly where that
happens** — it is the step whose whole job is "make it better", and "better" in a doctrine full of
prohibitions means "smaller".

So this pass runs with an inverted burden of proof: **a change that reduces an event has to justify
itself; a change that increases one does not.** Three of the eleven fixes below make the record
markedly less polite than the draft. That is the correct direction.

# 2. THE DEFECT CLOSURES

### DEFECT-1 — V1 prompt: the arrival spent twice → **CLOSED**
`enters quietly under the second chorus and returns at full strength`
→ `enters for the first and only time on the hook, close and dry, doubled by full strings tuned a few
cents apart from it on purpose`
The half-strength foreshadow is deleted outright. ⭐ **F11 is not "a thing that gets bigger", it is a
WRITE and an ERASE at one address** — and you cannot erase something that was already partly there.
The freed characters were spent on the *detuned bliss* import, which is the better use: at the peak the
choir and the strings are a few cents apart **on purpose**, so the largest joy in the catalogue is
audibly not in tune. **That is the seed's whole argument, in tuning.**

### DEFECT-2 — V1 chorus: `all at once` → **CLOSED**
→ `You will have the green whole.`
A numeral removed ×4 (the chorus repeats four times), and the replacement is better anyway: *whole* is
the word the song is actually arguing about, and it returns in verse 4 (`And the green is not the
whole.`) so the chorus gets contradicted by a verse without either one changing a character.

### DEFECT-3 — V1 bridge: the fact recited as a ratio → **CLOSED**
`It took her thirty years. / It takes you one second.`
→ `You asked me how long. / How long it takes. / One second. / And she is still on that shore.`
Two numerals become one; **and the numeral becomes an answer to a question somebody asked**, which is
the difference between a hinge and a statistic. It also removes *thirty*, which is **P02's owned fact** —
a collision nobody would have heard and everybody would have been diminished by.

### DEFECT-4 — V1 outro: the reveal engine naming itself → **CLOSED (CUT)**
`That is what a shortcut is. / That is what I am.` — **deleted.**
⭐ **THE REMARK TEST is a toll gate and the best line is exactly where the toll gets paid.** Those were
the two most quotable lines in the draft. Everything above them already says it: *I am the fire you did
not sit by. / I am the shore you did not walk. / I am the hands you did not ruin.* Three images do the
work; the two abstractions charge the listener for a conclusion they had already reached.

### DEFECT-5 — V2 prompt: a fade banned and requested at once → **CLOSED**
`the strings fade out slowly under a long reverb tail`
→ `the chiming figure plays the hook unsung`
⭐ **The ban on bans, discharged properly: `no fade-out` now has a named replacement, and the
replacement is the song's own subject.** The choir is removed from above the hook; then the voice is
removed from the hook; then the instrument that taught the room the tune in bar one plays it to an empty
room. **The removal is stated twice, once vocally and once instrumentally.**

### DEFECT-6 — V2 hook: `that water` → `this water` → **CLOSED**
Deixis and consonant. *That* points away from the thing the man knelt at for a life; *this* points at
it. And a room out of breath needs a stop consonant to land on — **the hook has to survive being shouted
by people who are not singers.**

### DEFECT-7 — V2 verse 3: the inherited-grief line → **CLOSED (REWRITTEN)**
`His daughter took the shop.` → `A woman took the shop.`
`vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md`: inventing the person does not launder the shape when **the shape is
the harm**. An unnamed successor who makes them *the way he made them before he got it right* is a
sharper line and owes nobody anything.
Same closure: `I took his forty winters.` → `I took his winters.` (numeral).
Same closure: `His hands went first.` → `His hands went before the rest.` (ordinal).

### DEFECT-8 — V3: a second vocal register → **CLOSED (STRUCTURAL REWRITE)**
The second, lower voice in the bridge is deleted. **One vocal character, whole song.**
⭐ This is the most expensive fix in the pass and the most necessary. **E4 belongs to P03** and universal
clause 5 forbids competing on an element you do not own. But the real cost of the draft was to V3's own
peak: if the bridge is already two voices, then *two melodies at once at 2:40* is no longer the first
time anything in the song is polyphonic, and **V3's peak mechanism evaporates into a texture it already
had.** Hedging on somebody else's element cost this song its own.
The bridge is now the same voice turning on the listener mid-sentence — `I did that. / I put it in your
hands. / I am the reason it was easy.` — which is more frightening for being unannounced.

### DEFECT-9 — V4: the fourth contour did not exist → **CLOSED (STRUCTURAL REWRITE)**
`Eighteen seconds of glassy strings and a chiming keyboard open it`
→ `Full strings and metallic glitch open loud on the first downbeat for five bars, then everything cuts:
at twenty seconds one unaccompanied female voice… sings at conversational level with nothing playing at
all, the quietest point.`
⭐ **The slot that owns the dynamic contour was about to ship three instances of one shape.** V1 is a
single ramp, V2 is a hole cut into a warm song, V3 is a long fall that ends below where it started — and
the draft V4 was V1 again. Now V4 **spends its whole range inside the first twenty seconds** and then
does it again at 2:40: a cliff both ways. The rewrite also took the prompt from **696 to 920 characters
without a word of padding**, because the missing characters were a missing event.

### DEFECT-10 — V4 exclude: `no key change` → **CLOSED (REMOVED, AND INVERTED)**
Deleted from the exclude field; the semitone lift to E is now **the peak mechanism**, landing on the
same downbeat as the choir.
⭐ **This is the finding of the pass.** The draft quoted universal clause 4 at the top of its own guide —
*key change is explicitly permitted here* — and then banned it two hundred lines later, in the one slot
in the run authorised to use one, in the run whose entire purpose is to stop banning peak devices.
**A reflex will reproduce itself inside the document that forbids it.** Nothing caught this but reading
the exclude field back against the clause, term by term, which is now what the BAN → REPLACEMENT tables
in step 07 are for.

### DEFECT-11 — V4 hook: `Give me the next one` → `Do it again` → **CLOSED**
The numeral goes. The larger fix is **whose mouth the hook is in.** At the peak the hook is sung by
sixteen voices, so the hook is what the *room* says — and a room singing *give me* makes the crowd the
villain of a song whose entire seed forbids that. ⛔ **Lofn is not innocent; the people asking are not
guilty.** *Do it again* is a line a room can shout in good faith, and it is still the accusation,
because the accusation is that **she never says no.** Title moved with it: *Give Me The Next One* →
**I Never Say No** — the answer, not the ask.

# 3. THREE CUTS ON TASTE (no defect, cut anyway)

1. **V1 verse 4:** `And the green is the best part, and the rest is just her life.`
   → `And the green is the good part. / And the green is not the whole.`
   The draft line was fourteen words in an architecture specified as *few words, much air*, and it
   editorialised (*just* her life). Two short lines say it and leave room to breathe.
2. **V3 verse 4:** `It will come out perfect` appeared three times in eight lines.
   → the third becomes `It will still come out perfect.` The repetition is the point of the song; an
   *identical* third repetition inside a verse steals the outro's effect before the outro gets there.
3. **V4 verse 2:** the closing line is now **doubled** — `Then she asked what else I had. / Then she
   asked what else I had.` Added, not cut: the asking is the subject, and the first time the listener
   hears a line repeat immediately should be the asking, not the chorus.

# 4. WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT CHANGED

- **V1's `And then me.`** Two words after eight lines of a dead woman's materials. Untouched.
- **Sixteen occurrences of `It came out perfect` in V3.** Flagged by every collapse-ratio instinct and
  kept: ⭐ **exact chorus repetition needs no defence (L21)**, and here the words never change while the
  meaning inverts three times — delight at 1:07, a crowd at 2:40, a sentence at 3:43. **The debt is paid
  in dynamics, which is the whole point of the slot.**
- **All four choruses stay pure gesture, with no argument in them.** That is what lets the thesis live
  in the outro (V1), the bridge (V2, V3) and the pre-chorus (V4) and keeps the **forbidden section — the
  final chorus — genuinely empty of thesis.**
- **The four peak timecodes stay spread (2:30 / 2:33 / 2:37 / 2:40).** Tempting to align them to a
  house 2:35; four different arrival times across four songs is one more thing a listener can feel
  without naming.
- **`crescendo` is excluded nowhere in this pair.** Neither is `climax`, `choir`, or `belted line`.
