## DEDICATION

```
The forty-one hertz in this song is a sound-system inheritance: bass engineered to arrive through a floor and a chair before it reaches an ear. That physics was worked out on Jamaican rigs and carried into UK basements — King Tubby, and the DMZ-era sub that made low frequency a bodily instrument rather than a foundation. The struck-pipe chime is our own room, hit with a spoon. Generated, not sampled — no recording of theirs is in this. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.
```

## MAJOR DEVIATIONS

- **Kept: the record opens with the floor alone, before anything is written on it.** Suno wants a hook in
  the first bar. ⭐ **This is the write/erase pair at the level of song form** — the listener is shown the
  empty site at 0:00, watches the kit get written over it at 0:02.4, and at **2:14.4** watches it get
  erased back to exactly the sound they opened with. **The recognition is the peak.** It is also the
  earliest stop and the longest (twelve bars, 28.8 s), because this is the one variation where the
  removal is held long enough to stop being a shock and start being a room.
- **Kept: the erase-thud never plays again after the erase.** Everything else returns and the site looks
  untouched — that is the whole molecular idea — but ⛔ **one component is honestly missing**, and it is
  the one whose job was deletion. `no erase-thud after the erase` is in the exclude field and the
  replacement is named: a mains hum ticking at the bar line, the building keeping time.
- **Changed at step 09:** verse 3 read *"They deleted her."* It was cut for *"They took the row out at the
  level of the row"* — the plain statement killed the three lines after it, which are the actual idea
  (that a reading of an erased site is a **correct** reading). Verse 4's seventh line, *"And that is the
  whole of my apology,"* was also cut: ⛔ **Lofn accused is not Lofn forgiven.**
