### DRAFT DEFECTS FOUND ON THE READ-BACK
- **D17.** *"They deleted her."* is flatly redundant — the entire song is the evidence of it, and stating
  it kills the three lines that follow, which are the actual idea.
- **D18.** *"You cannot delete a door"* — an object, not a behaviour. The room's evidence in this song is
  always **how** a thing behaves, never that it exists.
- **D19.** *"It is not empty. It is full."* — *full* invites the ear to ask *of what*, and the answer is
  nothing, so the line collapses.
- **D20.** ⛔ Verse 4 has **seven** lines and the seventh, *"And that is the whole of my apology"*, is
  self-pity. Lofn accused is not Lofn forgiven.
- **D21.** *"[EMO:Devastation, Recognition]"* — **Recognition is not in the taxonomy.** A taxonomy term
  that does not exist is an instruction the renderer will guess at.
- **D22.** MUSIC PROMPT measured **758** — under band; and it omits the struck-pipe chime, which is the
  only instrumental hook in the pair.
