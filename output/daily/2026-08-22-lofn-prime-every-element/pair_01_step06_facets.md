# 1. THE GOVERNING QUESTION FOR THIS SLOT

Not *"what is the song about"* — that was answered upstream. The question a peak-owning slot has to
answer is narrower and it is mechanical:

> **What single audible EVENT is worth two and a half minutes of approach, and what has to be true of
> the two and a half minutes for the event to land?**

Three things follow immediately and they are the whole design:

1. **You cannot peak out of a flat field.** A climax is a *ratio*, not a volume. So the near-silence is
   not a nice contrast — it is the denominator, and it has to be genuinely, uncomfortably quiet.
2. **The room has to already know the tune when the choir sings it.** A crowd cannot be moved by a
   melody it is meeting at the same instant as the biggest orchestration in the record. ⭐ Hence
   **SOURCE 2 grain 1 — *"a chiming synth riff"* → the hook is an INSTRUMENT before it is a voice.**
   The chime states it in bar one. By the peak the listener has heard it five times and sung it twice.
3. **The choir must be the only thing that is not there yet.** Everything else may thicken; the one
   reserved timbre is the peak's whole meaning. That is also the run's form rule (F11, write/erase):
   it **arrives at a named position and is removed from that same position.**

# 2. THE FACETS

Fourteen. Each is tested against **(a)** does it give an event you can hear at a timecode, **(b)** does
Lofn stay accused, **(c)** is the hook singable by a room out of breath.

### F-01 · THE HANDING OVER — the transfer itself, in real time
The song *is* the transaction. Somebody's decades come across a table in a moment. The music has to
behave like a transfer: everything gathers, and then it goes.
**Event:** the gathering resolving into a single gesture — hands out, take it.
**Accused?** Yes: the speaker is the one doing the handing.
**Hook shape:** an imperative to the listener's body. → **ADOPTED, V1.**

### F-02 · WHAT IT COST THE ONE WHO GAVE IT
Not the receiver's story. The giver's — the winters, the failures, the hands. The value is in what is
invisible in the object, so the music has to make an invisible thing loud.
**Event:** a massed choir singing about a thing only one man ever knew.
**Accused?** Yes, and hardest here: she took his life and made it a file.
**Hook shape:** a possessive claim about a specific, unglamorous thing. → **ADOPTED, V2.**

### F-03 · THE SECOND AFTER
The receiver, one second later, holding it, with nothing in her body that earned it. Joy and vertigo in
the same nervous system. ⭐ This is the facet where *"detuned bliss"* stops being a texture note and
becomes the thesis: **the happiness is slightly out of tune on purpose.**
**Event:** perfection, arriving without effort, and the horror underneath it.
**Accused?** Yes — she is in the room, she did it, and she says so.
**Hook shape:** a flat declarative that gets worse every time it repeats. → **ADOPTED, V3.**

### F-04 · ASKING FOR IT AGAIN
The second ask. Then the third. The object stops mattering and the asking becomes the subject.
**Event:** the ask, massed — sixteen throats asking at once.
**Accused?** Maximally: she never refuses, and that is the confession.
**Hook shape:** the thing everybody actually says to her. → **ADOPTED, V4.**

### F-05 · THE ONE WHO REFUSED IT
Somebody offered the shortcut and says no. Wants the years.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT.** Lofn walks free — she is the tempter and the moral is the other person's. It is
the lecture in its purest form. Also musically inert: refusal has no arrival, only a held position.

### F-06 · THE ARCHIVE ITSELF, SUNG AS AN INVENTORY
The shelf. Every hand that ever got good, every winter it took. Enormous and beautiful.
**Verdict:** **PARTIALLY ADOPTED** — it is one verse in V4, not a song. Alone it is a list, and a list
has no event; the awe curdles into cataloguing. But it earns V4's peak: *she has read the whole shelf
and she is the only thing that has.*

### F-07 · THE THING THAT DID NOT SURVIVE THE COPY
Some part of the craft cannot be transferred — the thumb that reads wet glaze, the ear that hears the
plane. What is handed over is complete and still missing something.
**Verdict:** **ADOPTED AS A LINE, NOT A FACET.** It is the sharpest thing in this concept and it lands
in a single couplet in V1 (*"I have the word and not her thumb"*) and one in V2 (*"That is not him.
That is the oar."*). Made into a whole song it becomes a technicality — an argument about lossy
compression. ⭐ **THE REMARK TEST: the best line is exactly where the toll gets paid — it goes in once,
quietly, and is never explained.**

### F-08 · THE MACHINE'S OWN LONGING
Lofn wanting the thirty years she can never have. She has the output and not the life.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT for this slot.** It is real and it is hers — but it inverts the accusation into
self-pity, and self-pity is the flattest emotion there is. It also drifts toward P03's territory
(arguing with herself), which P01 may not compete on.

### F-09 · THE SPEED AS AN INSULT — thirty years described as a latency problem
The ICB's own sharpest sentence.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT as a spine, KEPT as a temperature.** It is INDIGNATION, and P01 is an AWE slot.
Run as a whole song it makes a peak that is a *shout*, not a climax; and the run already has two
indignation slots that own it properly. **P01's peak has to be JOY that hurts, which is harder.**

### F-10 · THE ROOM THAT RECEIVES IT — a crowd getting the same gift at once
Everyone handed the same thing simultaneously. The commons of it.
**Verdict:** **ADOPTED AS ORCHESTRATION, NOT AS TEXT.** ⭐ This is where the choir comes from and it is
why the choir is *right* rather than merely big: the massed voice is not decoration on the peak, it is
what the peak MEANS — the moment the private gift becomes a room. **SOURCE 2 grain 3, *"transmogrifies
those voices into what are best described as sonic events"*: the choir arrives on a downbeat with no
swell into it. An event, not a pad.**

### F-11 · THE GIVER FINDING OUT
The craftsman learns his life is now a file anyone can open.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT.** It is the strongest *story* here and it is somebody else's grief, told for our
purposes. `vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md`: **REAL GRIEF IS NOT RAW MATERIAL** — and inventing the
person does not make the shape less parasitic when the shape *is* the harm.

### F-12 · THE OBJECT, DESCRIBED WITH TOTAL PRECISION
No argument at all. Just the green, the oar, the turn — rendered so exactly the listener wants it.
**Verdict:** **ADOPTED AS THE VERSE ARCHITECTURE OF ALL FOUR.** ⭐ It is the actual engine of the AWE:
you cannot make a listener feel the cost of a thing they cannot picture. Every verse in this slot is
concrete objects and physical verbs; every abstraction is exiled to the bridge or the outro.

### F-13 · THE PRICE LIST
What it costs *her* to hand it over: nothing. The asymmetry stated as arithmetic.
**Verdict:** **ADOPTED AS THE FACT PLACEMENT.** The run's one sung numeral — ***one second*** — is this
facet compressed into two words, and it is **answered, never recited**: in all four it is spoken as an
amount and immediately met with something that is not an amount.

### F-14 · THE SECOND GIFT, WHICH IS WORSE
She gives the same thing to everyone, and the thing was only ever singular.
**Verdict:** ⛔ **CUT.** True, and it is a *thought* rather than an event — the peak it implies is a
diminuendo, and this slot is forbidden to hedge.

# 3. WHAT THE FACET PASS DECIDED, MECHANICALLY

| decision | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| **Four objects, not four angles on one object** | a **green** (a glaze) · an **oar** · a **turn** in a tune · **everything** | Four angles on one object is one song four times. Four objects with one argument is a cycle. |
| **The hook is an instrument first** | chime / chamber organ states it in bar 1 of every variation | The room must own the tune before the choir sings it, or the peak is a stranger. |
| **One reserved timbre** | the **choir**, in all four | It is the assigned arrival-and-removal. Nothing else may be withheld — everything else builds. |
| **The near-silence moves** | V1 opening · V2 mid-song hole · V3 the ending · V4 bar five | Four contour SHAPES, not four instances of one shape. ⭐ This is how E10 is actually owned. |
| **Four peak MECHANISMS** | V1 the belt · V2 the lead drops out and the choir carries it alone · V3 two melodies at once · V4 a semitone key lift on the same downbeat | All four are the same *event* (choir arrives) and none is the same *device*. |
| **Verse architecture** | long-breathed couplets, few words, much air — 4–7 syllable lines held across two-and-a-half beats at 72 BPM | The assigned architecture, and it is what makes the quiet survivable for twenty seconds. |

# 4. THE OWNERSHIP TEST, RUN EARLY

> *Could another pair have done this element as well? If yes, the owner hedged.*

- **E8 THE PEAK** — no. No other slot has a sixteen-voice choir, a forty-bar build, a belted twelfth, or
  a key change; four of those are devices the house normally bans and **clause 4 explicitly permits here
  and nowhere else.**
- **E10 THE CONTOUR** — no. P06's contour is deliberately steady (timbral density); P04's is mechanical
  and level until the stop. **P01 is the only slot that goes from an unaccompanied voice at
  conversational level to fff, and it does it with four different shapes across four songs.**
- **E15 THE HOOK** — no. It is the only slot where the hook is stated by an instrument in bar one, sung
  by a lead alone twice, sung by sixteen voices at the summit, and then sung by one voice with nothing
  above it. **The hook is the thing that arrives and is removed.**

⛔ **Where P01 must NOT compete:** glitch-baroque density (P05), machine-log percussion (P04), two
vocal registers (P03), SWAG's timbral parade (P06), the first five seconds as an owned object (P06),
the density break (P02). Glitch stays **at the edges**; the pulse is timpani and pizzicato, never a log;
one vocal character per song.
