## P02V3 — Now It Says What It Said

*405 chars*

## P03V3 — Somebody Sat Up All Night

*435 chars*

The drone is the Nordic bowed lyre and its detuned twin, an instrument carried through by folk players who get no line in anybody's credits. Maren is invented; the shape of her night belongs to every unpaid amateur observer who sat out in the cold with a pencil and filled in a form for an institution that filed it. The Ars Notoria tradition is anonymous by construction. Generated, not sampled. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.

## P04V4 — The Room Kept It

*478 chars*

The forty-one hertz in this song is a sound-system inheritance: bass engineered to arrive through a floor and a chair before it reaches an ear. That physics was worked out on Jamaican rigs and carried into UK basements — King Tubby, and the DMZ-era sub that made low frequency a bodily instrument rather than a foundation. The struck-pipe chime is our own room, hit with a spoon. Generated, not sampled — no recording of theirs is in this. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.

## P05V4 — The Table At The Front

*489 chars*

The table of ornament signs in this song is real and it is not ours: J. S. Bach drew thirteen of them at the front of a teaching book in 1720 and nobody has ever needed a second edition. The clavichord is audible today because Ralph Kirkpatrick refused to let it go. The low end is a tuned 808 kick — Tadao Kikumoto designed the machine that makes it, and he is almost never named. We asked for these figures, so we name them. Generated, not sampled. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.

*438 chars*

This pair is written toward one record: "Where Did Our Love Go," number one on 22 August 1964, written and produced by Holland-Dozier-Holland, sung by Diana Ross, Mary Wilson and Florence Ballard, played by the Funk Brothers over a foot-stomp usually credited to Mike Valvano. None of them is named, quoted or imitated in the song; the people and the room in it are invented. Generated, not sampled. Lofn (Claude) with Dr. Local Symmetry.
