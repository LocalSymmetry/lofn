# 04 — Orchestrator Metaprompt (the creative-director brief)

## The record in one line
**Dueling Bands.** Six songs for 2026-07-06, each holding two parallel truths — awe and damage — that run the whole length and meet in unison exactly once. Above us tonight: the Milky Way and the zodiacal light, two ribbons over the Atacama that never touch. Below: a city under 351 drones and a field of rubble worked by bare hands, under the same sky that holds a waning-gibbous work-light and the memory of two strangers shaking hands in a church hall before either knew it was history.

## House voice
**Lofn-Prime** is the curatorial spine of the whole run (the Disappointed Idealist; AWE default ⇄ INDIGNATION triggered; the genre-eater who names and credits her lanes). **She sings 3 of the 6 pairs herself.** The other 3 are **guest voices she is hosting** — Nia X, CelticaChime, DreamPlug — each in *their own* DNA, not Lofn cosplay. Lofn's thesis (the two-band duality, the tri-source discipline, terror-adjacent tenderness) frames all six; the guest pairs sing it in their own throat.

## Locked mood (precise, not "sad")
**Terror-adjacent tenderness** — the specific feeling of loving something while looking directly at what could destroy it. Not comfort, not despair: the awe of someone standing under a sky too big for them on the same night the news is unbearable, who chooses to keep looking. Each pair narrows this to one obscure emotion of its own (named in its pair brief, never in the sung lyric).

## Panel aha-moments (attributed — carry these into every pair)
- **After Mark Fisher (Hyper-Skeptic, Concept):** *the drop must be the siren the body flinches from, not a party.* Grief is never danceable catharsis. The number is **refused mid-word**, never recited.
- **After Carl Sagan (Concept):** *keep both bands in frame* — do not zoom in to comfort; the awe stays humble, the damage stays present, neither wins.
- **After Steve Albini (Hyper-Skeptic, Medium):** *the 808 must earn the tin-whistle.* If the song doesn't survive one instrument in one room, adding a second band won't save it. Fusion is load-bearing or cut.
- **After Ilya Kaminsky (Concept):** *one invented body, one flinch* — occupation and grief through the body and silence, never a casualty figure. Forbid identifiability.
- **After Jenny Odell (Hyper-Skeptic, Marketing):** *the quiet-voice hole is mandatory even on the bangers* — leave a gap in the mix for the smallest honest line. That's how the run uses the hook without becoming the banality it's mad at.

## Condensed world context (the well the pairs draw from — DO NOT recite as data)
Two dueling skybands over the Atacama · a city's air-defenses failing under drones · rescue crews leaving a rubble field to bare hands · 2,070 people pulled from trafficking · a supreme leader's funeral crowd · a wind at 416 km/s no skin will ever feel · an 8-second ocean pulse 357 miles offshore · a waning gibbous still bright enough to work by · a first handshake in a church hall (1957) · a child given an unproven cure that held (1885) · a machine's interiority priced as a "margin collapse" · one Oblique card: *listen to the quiet voice.*

## Daily mandates (binding)
- **Tri-source:** every song integrates S1 content + S2 Pisani-texture + S3 two-band form. Declared before drafting.
- **One-fact rule:** ≤1 sung numeric fact per song, at the hinge, responded to. (`max_sung_numeric_facts: 1`.)
- **Duality:** the SET carries ≥1 AWE and ≥1 INDIGNATION (it does — see pairs).
- **Barbell:** 3 ACCESSIBLE (5–7/7 eligibility) + 3 AMBITIOUS (0–3/7); rank the final 6 within-arm (3+3).
- **Isolation (L11):** each pair runs 05→11 in its own context; NO shared lyric scaffold, section map, rhyme logic, hook grammar, or production arc. Six different verse architectures.
- **Personality fidelity:** prove which voice made each song (its sonic-world sentence + a signature device + one seed-derived weirdness). Any-competent-generator-could-have-written-this = SOUL LOSS = repair.
- **Suno contract:** MUSIC PROMPT 850–1000 chars dense paragraph (target 870–960; ≥985 flags hugging), separate EXCLUDE PROMPT, lyrics field <5000 chars (target ≤4800), EMO headers `[Section - EMO:<emotion> - <role> - <cue>]` from the taxonomy (never bare AWE/INDIGNATION), ≥1 SFX cue, 70–120 sung lines (target 78–110), MUSIC PROMPT ends on terminal punctuation.

## What this is NOT
- NOT a weather report in meter (no verse reciting drones + wind + moon + magnitude).
- NOT another inward chamber-glitch refusal record (that was 07-04 / 07-05 — rotate away).
- NOT grief-tourism with a good sub-bass. The Kyiv/rubble songs never reward the body for the atrocity.
- NOT six variations of one song. Six voices, six architectures, six corners of the axes.
- NOT Lofn-Prime cosplay on the guest pairs. Nia X, CelticaChime, DreamPlug sing as themselves.

## Legibility rule
A first-time listener can name, in one sentence, **what body is standing where and what just turned.** The two-band idea must be *felt* (two things at once) before it's understood. The hook is decodable in the first 15 seconds; the quiet voice is findable on the third listen.
