## V1 DRAFT — The Woman on the Seventh Step

**Music prompt sketch (v0):** Celtic drone-folk × Gaelic Drill × Baile Phonk, F minor; close-mic sean-nós alto over a stairwell's deep dark plate; Siren-Reed Doubling (uilleann/tin-whistle drone that swells like a distant siren) as lead; bodhrán heartbeat with a soft electronic kick; Floor-Shudder 808 that arrives early with no riser; Quantum Bit-Depth Swell into one beat of true silence; the drop is a flinch, not a party; tin-whistle answers the 808 at −12 dB; hook is a refusal to count. No generic trap hats, no EDM risers, no airhorns.

**Lyrics draft:**
```
[Theme: A stairwell under a raid; a woman holds a sleeping child and refuses to turn the night into arithmetic]
[SONG FORM: Vow→Verdict — sean-nós lament verses, a Quantum Bit-Depth guillotine, one beat of true silence, then a Gaelic-Drill chant-verdict; two bands (tin-whistle ⟷ 808) meet once at the Horizon Chorus]

[Intro - EMO: Dread - Female - Field recording + lone reed, no beat]
*dust ticks on the microphone*
(a far thud, three streets over)

is it the hush, or is it the alarm?

[Verse 1 - EMO: Devotion - Female - Near-whispered sean-nós, sung down to the child]
Seventh step, my coat around you tight,
the ceiling breathes its little snow tonight.
I made a roof of both my arms for you,
sleep — the dark's a blanket, not a wound.
I will not read the sky its total sum.
I count your breath. I count the coming home.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO: Anxiety - Female - Crowd-hum creeps, reed begins to crush]
*a radio bleeds up the stairwell shaft*
the voice out there keeps trying with a number —
it wants the night to add up, wants it counted,
it says there were three—

[HINGE - EMO: Moral Outrage - Female - Bit-depth guillotine → blackout]
three-fifty— no.
*the sound tears to two-bit and stops*
[ONE BEAT OF TRUE SILENCE]

[Horizon Chorus - EMO: Defiance - Female + hush choir - The drop (early, de-tuned, felt in the chest); tin-whistle and 808 in unison, the only time]
Count them if you have to — I won't.
(I won't.)
The sky can keep its ledger — I won't.
(I won't.)
You are not a figure in a feed,
you are the weight that's breathing next to me.
Count them if you have to — I won't.

[Verse 2 - EMO: Rage - Female - Subtractive; tin-whistle under, fury nearer the surface]
Let them tally metal in the air,
I'll tally the small warmth beneath my care.
If loving you is treason to the count,
then treason's what these two arms are about.
I'd take the roof down brick by brick by hand
before I let them file you as a strand.

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO: Vulnerability - Female - Everything drops but breath + one bare reed]
*a small sleeping breath, in and out*
still here. still here.
(one note, held, almost not there)

[Final Verdict - EMO: Determination - Female + protective gang-hush - Chorus gritted, harder]
Count them if you have to — I won't.
(I won't.)
The morning's mine to carry — I won't.
(I won't.)
Say every name the wind forgot,
but you're the one the wind will not.
Count them if you have to — I won't.

[Outro - EMO: Sorrow - Female - Everything strips; siren-reed descends, does not resolve]
*the far thud, once more, softer*
is it the hush… or is it the alarm…
(the reed comes down and does not land)
```

**Draft self-note:** hook lands by bar 2 of chorus; refusal = lullaby cadence; 351 refused mid-word at hinge, no other number present; quiet-voice hole is "still here" (heartbeat-count, NOT body-count). Line count ~50 — will EXPAND toward 78+ in step 09/10 (add a second pre, extend verses, layer backing). Human-subject: invented, unnamed, re-set. Clean.

## V2 DRAFT — The Console (the gap itself)

**Music prompt sketch (v0):** Gaelic Drill × Baile Phonk × dark ambient, F minor; a 50 Hz console hum and re-pitched sonar ping; mono Floor-Shudder 808 dominant; distant Siren-Reed (the civilian the machine can't reach); spectral foil hats (no trap); the refusal is the one warm human act in a cold field; Quantum Bit-Depth Swell → one beat true silence; the drop is the interceptor that isn't enough. No trap hats, no risers.

**Lyrics draft:**
```
[Theme: An air-defense seam under a raid; the human at the console can hold some of the sky, not all of it, and refuses to report the night as a total]
[SONG FORM: Vow→Verdict — cold drill-lament verses, a bit-depth guillotine, one beat of silence, then a chant-verdict; tin-whistle ⟷ 808 meet once]

[Intro - EMO: Isolation - Female - 50 Hz hum + sonar ping, reed far off]
*a low console hum, a satellite blink*
(ping… ping… the sky doing its sums)
Somewhere a whistle plays a street I can't defend —
I hold the line I hold. I hold it to the end.

[Verse 1 - EMO: Dread - Female - Clipped, institutional whisper]
I can stop the low, I cannot stop the high.
The gap's a shape of God-sized empty sky.
The screen fills up with little falling lights,
each one a math I'm losing tonight.
I will not read this room its total sum.
I hold the ones I hold until they come.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO: Apprehension - Female - The machine tries to report; hum crushes]
*the terminal wants to speak the tally*

it logs that there were three—

[HINGE - EMO: Moral Outrage - Female - Guillotine → blackout]
three-fifty— no.
*the log tears to two-bit and stops*
[ONE BEAT OF TRUE SILENCE]

[Horizon Chorus - EMO: Defiance - Female + cold choir - The drop is the interceptor that isn't enough; whistle and 808 meet once]
The sky does the sums — I won't.
(I won't.)
The log can hold the number — I won't.
(I won't.)
The gap's not a statistic on a board,
it's the street the whistle plays and I can't guard.
The sky does the sums — I won't.

[Verse 2 - EMO: Rage - Female - Subtractive; the seam named]
Between the sent and stopped there is a seam,
and no one built a wall across a dream.
I'll wear the gap. I'll carry what got through.
I won't hand the dark a spreadsheet view.
The machine can file the metal and the fall —
I keep the one it couldn't reach at all.

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO: Vulnerability - Female - Console mutes; one far civilian breath]
*the hum cuts; a breath from off the screen*

(one reed note, far, almost gone)

[Final Verdict - EMO: Determination - Female + gang-hush - Chant, cold]
The sky does the sums — I won't.
(I won't.)
This seam is mine to carry — I won't.
(I won't.)
Log the metal, log the flare,
I keep the name that wasn't there.
The sky does the sums — I won't.

[Outro - EMO: Sorrow - Female - Hum returns; unresolved]
*ping… ping… slower now*
the line above the line I hold…
(the notch in the mix stays open)
```

**Draft self-note:** distinct from V1 — institutional refusal, "the sky does the sums" hook variant, a literal frequency-notch (the gap) in the mix. 351 refused mid-word, no other figure. ## V3 DRAFT — The Lullaby Over the Alarm

**Music prompt sketch (v0):** Celtic folk × Gaelic Drill × Baile Phonk, F minor, 6/8 cradle-rock snapping to 4/4; sean-nós lullaby FOREGROUND with a warm Celtic-harp arpeggio; the Siren-Reed drone rises UNDER to swallow it; bodhrán half-time; mono 808 under-mixed so the cradle stays on top; at the Horizon the lullaby melody and the drill chant are the SAME notes; Quantum Bit-Depth Swell → one beat true silence (even the cradle stops). No trap hats, no risers.

**Lyrics draft:**
```
[Theme: A lullaby held steady over a rising air-raid siren; the cradle-song refuses to stop, and at the turn the lullaby and the drill are the same melody]
[SONG FORM: Vow→Verdict, inverted — a 6/8 sean-nós lullaby foreground over a siren ground, a bit-depth guillotine, one beat of silence, then a Horizon Chorus where lullaby and chant share one melody]

[Intro - EMO: Affection - Female - Lullaby alone, 6/8, harp; siren-reed rises under]
*a cradle creaks; wind at a taped-up window*

[Verse 1 - EMO: Devotion - Female - Cradle-song steady as the drone swells to cover it]

[Pre-Chorus - EMO: Anxiety - Female - The siren-drone nearly covers the lullaby]

[HINGE - EMO: Moral Outrage - Female - Guillotine → blackout; even the cradle stops]

[ONE BEAT OF TRUE SILENCE]

[Horizon Chorus - EMO: Defiance - Female + choir - Lullaby melody = drill chant, revealed as one]

a lullaby's a wall, a wall's an oath.
Count them if you have to — I won't.

[Verse 2 - EMO: Determination - Female - 6/8 returns; the lullaby wins by not stopping]
Hush now, hush now, they can sound all night,

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO: Vulnerability - Female - Breath + one bare reed]

(hush now.)

(hush now.)

you are the warm and breathing real.
Count them if you have to — I won't.

[Outro - EMO: Serenity - Female - 6/8 fades, unresolved, still rocking]
*the cradle creaks, slower*
hush now… hush now… hush…
(the reed holds and does not land)
```

**Draft self-note:** most tender; 6/8 inversion; lullaby=chant reveal; hook canonical but sung as a cradle-line. 351 refused mid-word. Quiet-voice hole "sleep. just sleep." Human-subject: invented, unnamed, re-set. Clean.

## V4 DRAFT — The Morning After (whistle band alone, no drum)

**Music prompt sketch (v0):** Celtic folk / trad lament, F minor, free time; tin-whistle + Irish fiddle + uilleann drone, sparse bodhrán heartbeat ONLY (no kick, no 808, no drill, no drop); dead-street field recordings (wind, one far bird); the Blackout never breaks into a verdict — where the drop should be, only the whistle band plays the verdict melody; the withheld drill IS the verdict; Albini's one-instrument-one-room test made literal. No 808, no drop, no trap hats.

**Lyrics draft:**
```
[Theme: The morning after a raid; the one who held the sleeper walks out into an empty street and plays the lament alone — the drill never comes, and the withholding is the verdict]
[SONG FORM: Vow→Verdict with the verdict WITHHELD — an unaccompanied sean-nós lament, the refusal at the hinge, then where the drop should be there is only the tin-whistle band; no percussion resolution]

[Intro - EMO: Numbness - Female - Dead-street field recording + reed tuning]
*wind down an empty street; one far bird*
(no traffic; the highway forgot how to hum)
The whistle wets its lip against the cold,
the morning's here and nobody was told.

[Verse 1 - EMO: Melancholy - Female - Unaccompanied but for uilleann drone]
I carried you down seven steps last night,
I carried you back up by first grey light.
The coat still holds the shape of where you slept,
the ceiling's snow is on the floor I swept.
I will not read the morning its cold sum.
I count the stairs. I count that we came home.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO: Apprehension - Female - The newsreader-ghost returns in daylight]
*a radio finds the street again*
the voice comes back to try me with the number,
it says by morning there were three—

[HINGE - EMO: Moral Outrage - Female - Refusal; and NO guillotine — the whistle HOLDS through it]
three-fifty— no.
*no collapse; one reed note holds straight through*
[where the drop should be: only the whistle band]

[The Withheld Verdict - EMO: Sorrow - Female + fiddle + whistle + drone - No percussion; the absence is the verdict]
Count them if you have to — I won't.
(no drum comes.)
There's no beat here to bury it — I won't.
(no drum comes.)
The verdict is the quiet where it'd land,
a lament with nothing but a hand.
Count them if you have to — I won't.

[Verse 2 - EMO: Grief - Female - Counting the wrong things on purpose]
I count the coats, the cups, the panes still whole,
I count the breaths, I count the small and slow.
I'll count the stairs a hundred times before
I let the morning add up what it saw.
The band plays on with neither kick nor snare —
the loudest thing this morning is the air.

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO: Vulnerability - Female - Band stops; one breath; one bare note]
*one breath; the band holds still*
morning. it's morning.
(one note, then nothing under it)

[Coda - EMO: Sorrow - Female + whistle + fiddle + drone - The lament complete, no resolution to major]
Count them if you have to — I won't.
(the band alone.)
I'll play until the light is warm — I won't.
(the band alone.)
No drop will come to make it clean,
just this: a whistle, and the street between.
Count them if you have to — I won't.

[Outro - EMO: Melancholy - Female - Whistle + fiddle + drone; a door left open]
*the whistle turns, and does not close*
morning… it's morning… and I won't…
(the drone holds; the street stays quiet)
```

**Draft self-note:** boldest — NO drop, NO 808, NO drill; the withheld verdict IS the choice. Distinct architecture (free time, drumless). 351 refused mid-word — and here the Bit-Depth Swell is DENIED (the reed holds through it), a deliberate variation on the shared rupture. Quiet-voice hole "morning. it's morning." Human-subject: invented, unnamed, re-set. Clean.
