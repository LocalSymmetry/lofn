## ESSENCE

A woman on the last night bus (and, across the four variations, at a 3am kitchen, in a mother's unseen hands, in an AI's untouchable archive) keeps a private inventory of small things she has no reason to keep and no audience to keep them for — a stranger's sleeve, a chipped mug, a folded receipt — reciting them under a swung jungle break like a rosary of the overlooked. Love here is not declared; it is *filed*. The song's whole dignity is that nobody is watching, and she does it anyway. One item in the list refuses to belong — it is too sharp, it should not be beautiful — and instead of cutting it, she keeps it too, rupture and all. At the hinge the beat abandons her and there is only one quiet voice, and she answers it: *this is what I kept.*
