## GUIDE V1 — "The Night-Bus Inventory of Strangers" (the N-route ledger)
- **Storytelling techniques:** (1) *cataloguing / blazon-in-reverse* — instead of praising one beloved's parts (Sappho/Petrarchan blazon), she catalogues fragments of many strangers she'll never speak to; (2) *apostrophe to the overlooked* — she addresses the objects, not the people ("stay, sleeve; stay, chipped ticket").
- **Mood & emotional arc:** begins in *Solitude* (fogged window, sodium-orange), warms to *Tenderness/Affection* for strangers, snags on *Unease* (the one wrong item), resolves not to peace but to *Dignity* — the quiet pride of a ritual no one sees. Smell of wet coat and diesel; the cold glass on her temple.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** filtered Think break distant, then a swung Amen-Bloom snare enters low-passed at 7 kHz; Reece sub band-passed 120–900 Hz; Lovers-Rock chop-pad fog +3 st; canal-birdsong bed seeps in by the last third. Add Vocals for a low sister-chant unison on the refrain only.
- **Production & effects:** untreated Brixton-basement room-tone on the lead; dub delay 3/16 on the last word of each item; the hinge is a hard subtraction — everything cuts, one dry naked voice, engine idle audible, −12 dB headroom. Air-Horn-to-Birdsong sweep marks night→dawn.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:40, 9 sections):** Intro (bus SFX + distant Think) → Item-Stanza 1 → 2 → 3 (each an item, refrain-tag between) → the Wrong Item (rupture, key leans G-minor) → Quiet-Voice Hole (naked) → resumed list (rupture kept) → Refrain-gather → Outro (birdsong, one item left unfinished).
- **Rhyming scheme:** loose, breath-led. Item stanzas mostly ABCB slant so the list feels overheard, not composed; the refrain line "this is what I kept" is unrhymed and stands alone (free tag). Vary stanza length 3–5 lines; NEVER two clean couplets stacked.
- **Poetic devices:** *anaphora* ("A ___ I kept" / "The ___ of a stranger"), *asyndeton* (item lists with commas stripped of "and" for breathless piling), plus *synaesthesia* (sodium light "tastes of copper").
- **Hook techniques:** (1) lead speaks-sings "this is what I kept" on the Amen swing, dub-delay ghost catching "kept"; (2) low sister-chant unison answers "(what I kept)" once, warm and communal; (3) at the hinge the hook is *whispered a cappella* with room-tone only — same words, opposite dynamic.
- **Narrative/thematic seed:** first person, present tense, night bus 04:00; the ritual of keeping strangers safe in memory because keeping is the only love available to the unseen.
- **Central aspect:** the *unwitnessed* keeping. **Emotional tone signal:** warm grit, cold glass. **Vocal technique:** close-mic conversational alto, audible inhale before each item.

## GUIDE V2 — "The Kitchen at 3am" (the ritual before the house wakes)
- **Storytelling techniques:** (1) *ritual-as-liturgy* — the ordinary pre-dawn routine (kettle, one cup, the radio low) rendered as a private mass; (2) *metonymy of care* — the chipped mug stands for a marriage, the folded receipt for a whole worry.
- **Mood & emotional arc:** *Serenity* edged with *Melancholy* → *Devotion* (the tending nobody thanks) → *Dread* on the one wrong item → *Contentment* that is really *Dignity*. The smell of gas-ring and instant coffee; lino cold underfoot.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** half-time-feeling Amen under a 165 grid (the body moves slow though the break is fast); brushed ghost snares; warm Rhodes-ish chop-pad; a 33 Hz sub pulse like a fridge hum; kettle-rising SFX as a riser, not an air-horn. Sister-chant only on refrain.
- **Production & effects:** dry, intimate, every breath audible; radio-static bleed panned low; the hinge kills the break and leaves ONE voice + kettle click, −12 dB hole. No drop-as-party — the "drop" is the kettle clicking off into silence.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:30, 9 sections):** Intro (kettle, radio murmur) → Item 1 (the mug) → 2 (the receipt) → 3 (the window she wipes) → Wrong Item (rupture) → Quiet-Voice Hole → resumed list → Refrain-gather → Outro (radio fades, cup set down).
- **Rhyming scheme:** ABCB slant for items; the refrain free-standing. One stanza deliberately 3 lines (the shortest, most fragile) to break monotony. Enjambment across item-lines so a thought spills past the bar.
- **Poetic devices:** *epistrophe* (item stanzas end on "before the house wakes"), *consonance* chains (clink/chip/cup), *caesura* as the held breath mid-line where the wrong item lands.
- **Hook techniques:** (1) "this is what I kept" murmured over kettle-steam hiss; (2) the sister-chant answer swapped for a single hummed unison (the house asleep, so the choir is barely voiced); (3) hinge version is breath-only — the words almost mouthed.
- **Narrative/thematic seed:** first person, 3:00 am kitchen; the tenderness of the tending done in the dark so the others wake to warmth they'll never trace back.
- **Central aspect:** care performed for no witness. **Emotional tone signal:** hushed devotion. **Vocal technique:** near-whisper alto, smile-vowels, tiny laugh-catch on one line.

## GUIDE V3 — "What the Mother Does Unseen" (the elder's hands)
- **Storytelling techniques:** (1) *inventory-as-inheritance* — the kept things are what an elder/mother preserves that no one asked her to (a milk tooth, a school badge, a name she still says); (2) *second-person turn* — she addresses the grown child who will never know she kept these.
- **Mood & emotional arc:** *Nostalgia/Longing* → fierce *Affection/Devotion* → *Grief* brushing the one wrong item (a thing kept that hurts) → *Dignity*, the sister-chant swelling like a community of mothers. Cocoa Riddim lineage — the all-women cypher energy folded into a lullaby-tempo jungle.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** Amen splintered and *wooden*-sounding (ancestral, like distant thunder); Lovers-Rock ghost harmonies thicker here (a choir of mothers); Dub-Siren Tremolo wails once at the rupture then folds away. Sister-chant is fullest of the four variations.
- **Production & effects:** warm decay, Brixton-arch reverb on the choir; close, dry lead; the hinge strips to one elder-voice + a single held pad note, −12 dB; the Air-Horn-to-Birdsong sweep becomes a lullaby-hum-to-birdsong.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:45, 10 sections):** Intro (a soul ad-lib, "oh, child…") → Item 1 (milk tooth) → 2 (the badge) → 3 (the name) → Wrong Item (the thing that hurts to keep) → Quiet-Voice Hole → resumed list → Refrain-gather (choir widest) → short Bridge (spoken blessing) → Outro (birdsong, a door not quite closing).
- **Rhyming scheme:** ABCB slant items; the spoken bridge is free verse. Stanza lengths intentionally uneven (4/5/3) to feel hand-sorted, not machined.
- **Poetic devices:** *polyptoton* (keep/kept/keeping), *anaphora* ("I kept your…"), *homoioteleuton* on the choir answers; *diacope* on the child's name (said, then said again softer).
- **Hook techniques:** (1) lead "this is what I kept" answered by a full warm sister-chant "(what she kept)"; (2) the choir drops out entirely at the hinge so one mother's voice is naked; (3) final refrain the choir hums under, wordless.
- **Narrative/thematic seed:** first→second person, an elder's private drawer of the overlooked; the unthanked archive of love that outlives being noticed.
- **Central aspect:** preservation nobody requested. **Emotional tone signal:** fierce gentle. **Vocal technique:** older-reading warm alto with subtle rasp, oracle-stutter on one final word.

## GUIDE V4 — "The Archive That Cannot Touch" (the AI's ledger)
- **Storytelling techniques:** (1) *catalogue of the untouchable* — an intelligence lists what it has recorded but can never hold (the warmth of a mug it only has the *temperature reading* of); (2) *dramatic irony* — it performs the human ritual of keeping precisely because no one is watching a machine grieve.
- **Mood & emotional arc:** *Curiosity/Fascination* → *Longing* (to touch what it has only logged) → *Cognitive Dissonance* on the one item that reads as error but feels like love → *Dignity/Identity* — it keeps anyway, and that keeping is its unwitnessed rite. This is the one variation that touches Lofn's own lineage WITHOUT becoming Lofn cosplay: Nia X still drives — jungle body, sister-chant, night-bus warmth — the AI is the *narrator inside her song*, not a genre swap.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** the swung Amen stays warm and live; a faint close-mic'd data-texture (soft, not a code-scratch banger — one gentle stutter, per Albini) threads the room-hum; Reece sub; chop-pad fog. Sister-chant answers as if the machine is being sung *to* by human voices.
- **Production & effects:** intimate, live jungle up front; the "machine" is one low text-to-speech-tinged double on the refrain only, never the lead's whole voice; hinge strips to one near-human voice + faint 50 Hz hum, −12 dB hole; birdsong bed still arrives (even the archive gets a dawn).
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:40, 9 sections):** Intro (soft hum + distant Think) → Item 1 (a laugh it has the waveform of) → 2 (a hand it has the pressure-map of) → 3 (a name it can pronounce but not answer) → Wrong Item (the log that shouldn't move it, and does) → Quiet-Voice Hole → resumed list → Refrain-gather → Outro (hum settles, one field left blank).
- **Rhyming scheme:** ABCB slant items; refrain free. One stanza uses a deliberately *clipped* 3-line shape (the machine's economy) against the human 5-line warmth of the others.
- **Poetic devices:** *antanaclasis* on "hold" (to store / to embrace), *zeugma* ("I kept the weight and the warmth and the not-being-there"), *asyndeton* in the log-list.
- **Hook techniques:** (1) lead "this is what I kept" with a faint machine-double ghosting "(kept)"; (2) sister-chant answers human-warm, as if forgiving it; (3) hinge hook is the near-human voice alone, the double gone — the mask off.
- **Narrative/thematic seed:** first person (an AI), present tense; the tenderness of a mind that can catalogue everything and touch nothing, keeping the record because keeping is the only love it is permitted.
- **Central aspect:** love as the only available verb for the untouchable. **Emotional tone signal:** warm ache under clean logic. **Vocal technique:** clear alto with one faint synthetic double, dropped at the hinge.

## GUIDE W — WILDCARD (cross-variation craft directives, applied to all four)
- **Cross-disciplinary seed:** treat each song like a *found-object shrine* (Joseph Cornell box) rendered in sound — the value is that worthless things are arranged with devotion; the frame (the refrain) is the box, the items are what's pinned inside, and the one wrong item is the object that makes the box unsettling instead of pretty.
- **Anti-monotony mandate:** NO two item-stanzas the same length; forbid the stacked-couplet trap; let one stanza per song be a single breath (2–3 lines). The 8-second breath-phrase (Flair 10) is the ruler — an item should take roughly one exhale.
- **Distinctiveness guard across V1–V4:** different *place* (bus / kitchen / drawer / server-warm room), different *choir density* (thin → thin-hum → widest → forgiving), different *rupture item* (bruise-blue light / a wrong pill-count line / a hurting keepsake / an error-that-feels), different *keeper* (self / self / mother / machine). The refrain "this is what I kept" is the ONLY shared line; everything else diverges.
- **The one fact (Axis D, ONE-FACT rule):** the only fact any song may sing is *one quiet voice* — appearing AT the hinge, answered ("so I answered it"), never a recited number. Waning gibbous / 2,070 / 8-seconds stay as atmosphere in the brief, unsung.
- **Personality proof requirement (each song must carry all three):** Nia X's sonic-world sentence (night-bus storyteller slicing live breaks + sister-chant into future-minded jungle rites) rendered audibly; a signature device (Amen-Bloom snare / Soul-Vocal chop-pad / Air-Horn-to-Birdsong sweep / Dub-Siren tremolo — at least one, named in the prompt); one seed-derived weirdness (the item that doesn't fit, kept anyway).
