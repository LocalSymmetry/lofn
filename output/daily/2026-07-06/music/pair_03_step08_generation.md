## V1 — "Both Your Lights" (the arrival)

**MUSIC PROMPT (draft):**
A vast, cold-spined arrival hymn. Warm HyperRaaga × ambient with a popiano-adjacent log-drum heartbeat, Mixolydian, unhurried. Two parallel bands run the whole song: a bright major "Milky-Way" line carried by a close-mic female mezzo over a tanpura-style drone, and a faint minor "zodiacal" line where the drum thins to a pulse and a satellite-blink pad hums beneath breath. Female 20s-30s European mezzo, crystalline-warm, breathy yearning, audible 3am breath, no snarl. About 82 BPM, half-time feel. Begins dry and intimate with grit and wind; the two drones never resolve into each other until a single Horizon Chorus where they lock and a solar-wind riser swells the one unison. A quiet-voice hole opens right after — one naked line. Reprise blooms; outro returns to wind. Warm desert palette, sub mono-center, air only at the chorus. No EDM riser, no frost sparkle.

**LYRICS (draft):**
[Theme: a body steps onto the Atacama floor at 3am under two sky-ribbons that will not pick a side]
[SONG FORM: dual-band parallel — Milky-Way major stanzas alternate with zodiacal minor stanzas, converging once in the Horizon Chorus unison]

[Intro — EMO:Curiosity — Female — dry, no beats]
*car door ticks, wind*
Gravel finds my knees. The cold gets in my socks.
I came out here to let one sky decide.

[Verse 1 — Milky-Way — EMO:Fascination — Female — warm log-drum]
I came for the bright one, the river of grain,
the spilled-milk road that the old tongues named.
It pours down warm and it wants me to stay,
a highway of light I could almost obey.

[Verse 2 — Zodiacal — EMO:Wonder — Female — drum thins, breath up]
But low in the east there's a fainter thing,
a cone of dust where the dead comets sing.
It hums the color of an underground lake,
and it doesn't ask, it just aches and aches.
So I stand between what I cannot rank.

[Verse 3 — Milky-Way — EMO:Admiration — Female — warm]
I came for the loud one to carry me home,
to tell me I'm small and I'm not alone.
It leans on my shoulder, it fills up my chest,
it swears that the brightest is always the best.

[Verse 4 — Zodiacal — EMO:Awe — Female — hinge]
And the faint one just waits like it knows my name,
two bands in the dark and they burn the same.
Two ribbons, no winner — the sky won't choose.
*wind rises*
So I won't either. I've got nothing to lose.

[Horizon Chorus — EMO:Transcendence — Female + low ghost — UNISON, solar-wind swell]
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the warm and the cold, the found and the far.
Hold me in both your lights (both),
don't make me choose which you are.
Oh— hold me in both.

[Quiet-Voice Hole — EMO:Insecurity — Female — whispered, no beats]
*breath*
I am so small out here it turns my stomach.
And I have never wanted anything this much.

[Horizon Chorus — EMO:Transcendence — Female + ghost — reprise, fuller]
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the warm and the cold, the found and the far.
Hold me in both your lights (both),
love me as much as you are.
Oh— hold me in both.

[Outro — EMO:Serenity — Female — dry, fading]
*wind*
Two ribbons still burning. I drove out to be sure.
I'm leaving less sure and I'm holding them both.

## V2 — "The Warm and the Far" (two bands = two people you loved and lost)

**MUSIC PROMPT (draft):**
A tender, divided grief-hymn under a doubled sky. Warm HyperRaaga × ambient × neo-soul, Mixolydian, ~80 BPM half-time log-drum. Two parallel lines: a warm major "Milky-Way" band (fuller drum, neo-soul warmth) and a cold minor "zodiacal" band (drone + close-mic breath, almost no beat) — subtly panned apart, collapsing to center only in the single Horizon Chorus. Female mezzo, crystalline-warm, small catch in the voice, no snarl. Begins on two coats, one warm one cold; the two skies stand for two unnamed losses that refuse to become one grief. The unison is the only place they touch; a solar-wind swell marks it, then a quiet-voice hole. Sub mono-center, dry verses, wide only at the chorus. No EDM riser, no frost sparkle.

**LYRICS (draft):**
[Theme: under the Atacama's two ribbons, a grief that will not resolve into a single loss — one bright, one faint]
[SONG FORM: dual-band parallel — a warm Milky-Way line and a cold zodiacal line alternate, touching only in the Horizon Chorus unison]

[Intro — EMO:Longing — Female — dry, no beats]
*a slow breath*
I still keep two coats. One warm and one thin.
I wore them to the driest place to think.

[Verse 1 — Milky-Way (the warm one) — EMO:Affection — Female — full log-drum]
The bright band's the one who laughed in my hair,
who filled every room and was always right there.
It pours like a voice that I still hear you use,
too loud and too warm and too easy to lose.

[Verse 2 — Zodiacal (the cold one) — EMO:Melancholy — Female — breath, no beat]
The faint band's the other, the quiet I lost,

[Verse 3 — Milky-Way — EMO:Nostalgia — Female — warm]
I want to walk into the loud one and stay,

[Verse 4 — Zodiacal — EMO:Sorrow — Female — hinge]
Bright to me, faint to the ones who came late;

Two bands, and I loved you — the sky won't choose.
*wind rises*
So I won't rank you. I'm keeping you two.

[Horizon Chorus — EMO:Transcendence — Female + ghost — UNISON, solar-wind swell]
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the warm and the far, the near and the gone.
Hold me in both your lights (both),
I won't turn either of you off like a song.
Oh— hold me in both.

[Quiet-Voice Hole — EMO:Vulnerability — Female — whispered, no beats]
*breath*
Stay in the part of the sky I can't reach.
I don't need to touch you. I need you to keep.

[Horizon Chorus — EMO:Transcendence — Female + ghost — reprise]
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the warm and the far, the near and the gone.
Hold me in both your lights (both),
love doesn't need a winner to go on.
Oh— hold me in both.

[Outro — EMO:Serenity — Female — dry, fading]
*wind*
Two ribbons still burning. Two coats on the sand.
I came to let go and I'm holding your hands.

## V3 — "Off the Record" (the astronomer's night shift)

**MUSIC PROMPT (draft):**
A reverent, cold-spined night-shift hymn. Warm HyperRaaga × ambient with a dry mechanical tracking-tick, one faint AI code-scratch texture in the intro (a logbook time-stretched), Mixolydian, ~82 BPM half-time log-drum. Two parallel bands: a precise major "Milky-Way" line (drum + mount-tick) and an un-quantifiable minor "zodiacal" line (drone + empty-highway pad, breath up). Female mezzo, dry-precise diction opening into warmth, no snarl. Begins as the dome cracks open and the work starts; the routine can't contain the awe; at the hinge the pen stops. The Horizon Chorus drops the instruments she measures with — only two drones + a solar-wind unison. The waning-gibbous work-light dims across the outro. Sub mono-center, low room-hum in verses, air only at the chorus. No EDM riser, no frost sparkle.

**LYRICS (draft):**
[Theme: an astronomer on the graveyard shift at a dark-sky site, where measurement fails to hold the two ribbons overhead]
[SONG FORM: dual-band parallel — a measured Milky-Way line and an unmeasurable zodiacal line alternate, converging in the Horizon Chorus unison]

[Intro — EMO:Composure — Female — dry, code-scratch hush, no beats]
*dome cracks open; a faint code-scratch*
Log entry, oh-three-hundred. The mount is tracking true.
The gibbous light is leaving. It's still bright enough to use.

[Verse 1 — Milky-Way — EMO:Vigilance — Female — drum + tick]
I came for the data, the bright one on the plate,
the arm of the galaxy I'm paid to calibrate.
It pours down in numbers I can bin and store,
a river I've measured a thousand nights before.

[Verse 2 — Zodiacal — EMO:Fascination — Female — drone, breath]
But the faint cone rises where I've nothing to write,
the dust of dead comets no column holds tight.
It hums like an empty and infinite road,
and my instruments shrug at the ache that it's owed.

[Verse 3 — Milky-Way — EMO:Reflection — Female]
I came to be useful, to log and to leave,
to trust what the aperture lets me believe.
But the loud band and quiet band hang in one frame,
and my clean little numbers are learning my shame.

[Verse 4 — Zodiacal — EMO:Awe — Female — hinge, pen stops]
So I set down the pen where the two bands meet —
two ribbons, no winner, and my hands are not neat.
Two bands in the dark, and the sky won't choose.
*wind rises*
So I won't reduce them. I've measured to lose.

[Horizon Chorus — EMO:Reverence — Female + ghost — UNISON, solar-wind swell]
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the counted and the ones I never can.
Hold me in both your lights (both),
teach the measurer to be a man.
Oh— hold me in both.

[Quiet-Voice Hole — EMO:Awe — Female — whispered, no beats, off the record]
*breath*
This part I won't be logging. This is mine.
I bowed at a sky I'll pretend I could define.

[Horizon Chorus — EMO:Reverence — Female + ghost — reprise]
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the counted and the ones I never can.
Hold me in both your lights (both),
the awe survives the plan.
Oh— hold me in both.

[Outro — EMO:Serenity — Female — dry, work-light dimming]
*the work-light hums lower*
Log entry, oh-four-hundred. Two ribbons, both alive.
I wrote down what I measured. I'm keeping what I can't describe.

Female mezzo, crystalline-warm default with ONE brief indignant edge in the shadow, no full snarl. Two parallel bands as elsewhere (bright major Milky-Way; faint minor zodiacal); the Horizon Chorus is UNCHANGED by the shadow — proof the awe holds. A low crowd-ghost ooh enters the reprise so the awe carries the many. No casualty number is sung; the shadow flinches and returns. Sub mono-center, air only at the chorus. No EDM riser, no 808 catastrophe, no frost sparkle.
