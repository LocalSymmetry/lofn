## THE FIVE+ FACETS (each a distinct blade of the same concept)

### Facet 1 — THE DOUBLED TIMBRE (siren = lullaby = warning)
The single most important move: **one sound carries three meanings.** The uilleann/tin-whistle drone IS the air-raid siren IS the hush a mother makes over a cradle (*"shhh"* shaped as a held reed note). CelticaChime's "Tin Whistle/Uilleann Lament" pillar is bent so the lament and the alarm are the same breath. When the drone swells, you cannot tell if it is comfort or the thing to run from — that ambiguity is the terror-adjacent tenderness (Flair 15) made audible. **Flair 6 (Siren-Lullaby) central.**

### Facet 2 — VOW → VERDICT (the verse architecture, mine alone)
Verses are **tense internal Celtic lament** — a whispered *vow* sung DOWN to the sleeper (sean-nós ornament, close-mic, almost sub-vocal), the fury held under the tongue. The **vow** builds: "I will hold you, I will not count." Then the **Quantum Bit-Depth Swell** guillotines the reed into 2-bit grit → **Blackout Drop (1 beat of true silence, Flair 2)** → the chorus is the **verdict**: the same tenderness, now a **chant-drill** that refuses. Not verse→chorus; **vow→verdict.** The lullaby doesn't calm into a chorus; it hardens into one.

### Facet 3 — THE REFUSED NUMBER (351, mid-word, once)
At the hinge, the crowd-hum / newsreader-ghost tries to hand over the tally — *"three-fifty—"* — and the singer **cuts it off with her body**, refusing to let the number finish, refusing to let the night be arithmetic. The hook is the refusal itself: **"count them if you have to — I won't."** The number is materiel (drones), never the dead; it dies in her mouth. **Flair 4 (Refused Number).** ONE-FACT RULE honored: 351 appears once, mid-word, at the hinge, with no other figure beside it.

### Facet 4 — THE STAIRWELL AS INSTRUMENT (the underground-lake reverb)
The concrete stair is a reverb chamber (Pisani's *"underground lake, empty highway"*). Every far thud of the raid arrives as a **sub-bass shudder felt before heard** — the 808 slide is not a beat-flex, it is the building being hit three streets over. Dust ticks the mic (SFX). The **empty highway** outside is the silence the drone leaves. The stairwell holds both bands: the whistle rings up the shaft (comfort), the 808 comes up through the soles (threat). **Flair 11 (Tin-Whistle Under, −12 dB).**

### Facet 5 — THE QUIET-VOICE HOLE (mandatory, Odell's veto)
Once, everything drops out but the child's breath and one un-effected reed note held so soft it's almost not there — the **smallest honest line**, found on the third listen: *"still here. still here."* (a heartbeat count that is NOT a body count — the opposite of the refused tally). The mix leaves a hole for it. **Flair 5.** This is where the protective rage becomes unbearable precisely because it goes quiet.

### Facet 6 — THE MORNING BAND (aftermath, for V4)
A sixth facet reserved for the fourth variation: after the raid, **the tin-whistle band alone, no drum, no 808** — the drill never comes, the Blackout never breaks, the verdict is withheld. The lament stands unaccompanied, and the withholding of the drop is itself the verdict. Proves the song survives one instrument in one room (Albini's test): if it doesn't land on the whistle alone, no second band saves it.
