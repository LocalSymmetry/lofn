## V1 — "Stay While I Dig" (the digger's hands)

**Working music prompt (draft):**
A grief-hushed lo-fi dream-plugg lament in the key of C# minor, half-time near 78 BPM, built to thin out rather than build. Opens on close-mic hands-in-gravel field recording and one lone Rhodes triad, then a low-passed cloud-808 sub swells underneath like an underground lake heard through earth. A female vocalist in her late twenties, warm and breath-forward, half-sung and half-murmured with Cry-and-Slide vocal cracks left unhealed, sits dry and intimate over an angel-choir hymn pad drowned in long shimmer reverb. A detuned GameBoy lead ticks a soft eight-note count. Industrial-gospel Hammond drone, vinyl crackle, VHS tape hiss. At the hinge the machine-beat recedes and fades out entirely, leaving gravel and one breath alone; a plagal amen chord blooms but refuses to resolve. No danceable drop, no trap hats, no EDM riser.

**Draft lyrics:**
```
[Theme: bare hands digging a rubble field after the rescue crews have gone — the loneliness of labor no one is left to witness]
[SONG FORM: through-composed witness — testimony, prayer-to-the-witness, strip-away bridge, listener alone; non-repeating]

[Intro - EMO:Numbness - gravel field-recording, lone Rhodes - close-mic]
*hands scrape gravel, slow*
[Verse 1 - EMO:Numbness - Female Vocalist, half-spoken - dry]
My knuckles already forgot
the color of not bleeding.
The floodlights packed into a truck
and took the last loud thing.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Determination - low-passed 808 enters]
So it's me and the dust now,
me and the grey that used to be a wall.
I move it one hand at a time,
the way you'd turn a sleeping body — careful, small.

[Refrain A - EMO:Devotion - hymn pad ghosts in]
Stay while I dig.
Stay while I dig.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Sorrow - GameBoy count ticks]
They told me the ground still shifts,
eight seconds at a time it breathes —
*faint sonar ping*
so I count to it, so I count to it,
so these hands of mine don't leave.

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO:Loneliness - No beats - naked breath]
(I'm still here.)

[Verse 4 - EMO:Compassion - drone only]
I don't know your name yet.
I know the shape the quiet makes
where your answer ought to be.
I'll keep the space warm for your sake.

[Refrain B - EMO:Yearning - thinner, one voice]
Stay while I dig,
the light's near gone —
stay while I dig.

[Bridge - EMO:Desolation - machine fades OUT, gravel + breath only]
*engines recede into distance*

Just the hiss of the gravel
and the sound of me.

[Outro - EMO:Serenity - plagal amen refuses to resolve]
*single gravel drop*
…dig.
```

## V2 — "Don't Take the Light" (the crews' machinery leaving)

**Working music prompt (draft):**
A slow, mourning lo-fi plugg elegy in D minor near 76 BPM where the sound of help physically drives away inside the mix. Opens fuller for the style — a distant diesel rumble and floodlight hum as a "machine band," a low-passed cloud-808, and an angel-choir pad — then across the track the machine layer is high-cut and faded to model taillights receding, a single Doppler pitch-droop, a tape-stop on its last hit. A female vocalist, late twenties, intimate and cracked, half-sings over gospel Rhodes and Hammond; hands-in-gravel percussion and soft claps take over as the machines vanish. A detuned GameBoy note flattens like a generator dying. Dark, disorienting, terror-adjacent but folded into tenderness. Lyrics shrink line by line as the help leaves. No four-on-the-floor, no trap hats, no big drop — only a withdrawal.

**Draft lyrics:**
```
[Theme: the rescue machines pulling away from a rubble field at dusk, leaving the neighbors to dig by hand — help rendered as a fade]
[SONG FORM: through-composed witness — the withdrawal, the hinge, prayer, strip-away, one line alone; non-repeating]

[Intro - EMO:Dread - diesel idle, floodlight hum]
*engines idling low*
[Verse 1 - EMO:Anticipation - full-ish, machine band present]
They worked three days without a hymn,
just steel and shouting into the dark.
Now the big lights swing around to go
and the whole street holds its heart.

[The Withdrawal - EMO:Loneliness - machine layer high-cuts and recedes]
Don't take the light.
Don't take the light with you.
*taillights fade, pitch droops*
The dark comes back the way a tide would —
slow, and total, and blue.

[Hinge - EMO:Sorrow - GameBoy note flattens; the 8s answered]
The ocean past the block still turns,
every eight seconds, nobody counting it either —
so I'll keep its time,
I'll keep its time down here.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Devotion - gospel Rhodes, gravel enters]
My hands are slower than the cranes,
but my hands are what is left.
I'll move this grey a palm at a time
and hum into the cleft.

[Refrain - EMO:Compassion - hymn pad]
Stay while I dig.
Stay while I dig.

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO:Numbness - No beats - one breath]
(Okay. Okay.)

[Bridge - EMO:Desolation - only gravel + claps + breath]
No engine now.
No floodlight now.
Just the sea keeping its clock,
and me keeping mine.

[Outro - EMO:Serenity - suspended amen]
*last gravel shift*
Dig.
```

## V3 — "I Made It a Hymn" (the neighbor who keeps counting)

**Working music prompt (draft):**
A trembling lo-fi dream-plugg vigil in C# minor near 80 BPM centered on a detuned GameBoy tick as an eight-second metronome — the only thing holding the digger together. A Hammond bass-pedal drone hums like an underground lake; a low-passed cloud-808 sits beneath; hands-in-gravel percussion falls on the off-count. A female vocalist, late twenties, warm and fraying, uses anaphora and counted lines, half-sung with vocal cracks; angel-choir pad swells only when the counting falters. Tape-wow wavers the tick's pitch so the count audibly gets harder to hold; reverb blooms open when it breaks. Close-mic breath between numbers. At the hinge the count is transfigured into a hymn; when the tick stops, one unmetered whisper. Gospel grace in a minor key, no catharsis, no drop, no trap hats.

**Draft lyrics:**
```
[Theme: a neighbor counting the eight-second pulse of the earth to keep digging and keep from falling apart — counting as devotion]
[SONG FORM: through-composed witness — counting-testimony, prayer, hinge, faltering count, the tick stops, one line alone; non-repeating]

[Intro - EMO:Determination - GameBoy tick alone]
*soft eight-note tick*
[Verse 1 - EMO:Persistence - tick + gravel off-count]
Count the handful. Count the next.
Count the grey I set aside.
Count the ones who went home shaking,
count the ones who stayed and cried.

[Refrain A - EMO:Devotion - Hammond drone]
Stay while I dig.
Stay while I count.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Obsession - close-mic, tape-wow tick]
Counting is the only rope
I have left to hold.
If I stop I'll hear the quiet,
and the quiet is so cold.

[Hinge - EMO:Sorrow - choir swells; the 8s answered, named once]
Eight seconds between the diggings —
*sonar ping, wavering*
I made it a hymn
so the quiet couldn't win.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Mourning - tick faltering, arrhythmic]
Count the— count the—
the number's coming loose.
My hands remember what to do
when my mouth forgets the use.

[Refrain B - EMO:Yearning - thinner]
Stay while I dig,
just stay —

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO:Grief - tick STOPS - No beats]
(…I lost the count.)

[Bridge - EMO:Desolation - gravel + breath, no tick]
No number now.
Just the weight and the warm dark,
and my two hands
still asking the same thing.

[Outro - EMO:Serenity - suspended amen]
Stay.
```

## V4 — "Answer Me" (the one they dig for, rendered as absence)

**Working music prompt (draft):**
The sparsest of the set — a hollowed lo-fi dream-plugg address in D minor near 74 BPM where the beloved exists only as absence. A low-passed cloud-808 drone and an angel-choir pad hang over close-mic gravel; almost no lead — a single Rhodes note left ringing where a reply should be. Call-and-response is built with true silence: the response slots are empty air, once a full one-beat hole. A female vocalist, late twenties, extremely close-mic'd, half-sung and breaking, addresses a "you" who never answers; reverb tails only the unanswered word. A detuned GameBoy lead appears exactly once then cuts, like a voice that should reply and is gone. Disorienting, terror-adjacent tenderness — the pressure of earth over a body — never a jump-scare. A stained-glass shimmer blooms at the end, grace without relief. No drop, no trap hats, no riser.

**Draft lyrics:**
```
[Theme: second-person address to the unnamed person being dug for, present only as absence — a warmth that should answer and doesn't]
[SONG FORM: through-composed witness — address, plea, hinge, unanswered plea, strip-away, one word to the gap; non-repeating]

[Intro - EMO:Longing - gravel + drone, single ringing Rhodes]
*gravel settles*
[Verse 1 - EMO:Longing - Female Vocalist, extreme close-mic]
I'm talking to the space where you should be.
Talking to the grey.
I know the weight of what I'm moving
is the weight of what I can't yet say.

[Plea 1 - EMO:Affection - call-and-response with silence]
Answer me.
( ... )
Just move the smallest stone from your side.
( ... )

[Hinge - EMO:Sorrow - GameBoy lead appears once, then cuts; 8s answered]
They said a breath lasts eight seconds down there —
*one 8-bit note, then gone*
so I hold mine that long
and pretend the air is ours to share.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Attachment - drone only, Rhodes rings]
I won't finish that sentence yet.
I'll leave it open like a door.
I'll keep my hand a hand's-width from the dark
in case you reach once more.

[Plea 2 - EMO:Heartache - unanswered]
Answer me.
( ... )
I'm not going anywhere tonight.

[Quiet-Voice Hole - EMO:Grief - No beats - empty response IS the line]
Answer—
( ... )

[Bridge - EMO:Desolation - strip to breath, empty response slots]
No voice.
No hand.
Just the drone the earth makes
and the shape of you in sand.

[Outro - EMO:Acceptance - stained-glass shimmer, no relief]
*shimmer blooms over the minor drone*
Stay… while I dig to you.
```
