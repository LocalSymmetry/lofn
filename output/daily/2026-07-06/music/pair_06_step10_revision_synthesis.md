## DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (predict the real render, name the failure, repair once)

**Predicted render (2–3 sentences):** Suno hears four slow, sub-heavy, close-mic'd minor-key dream-plugg laments with a warm cracked female voice half-singing over a gospel Rhodes/Hammond bed, hymn-choir shimmer, vinyl/VHS hiss, and a real hands-in-gravel percussion layer; the arrangements thin toward the end and the outros bloom a suspended amen chord instead of a drop. The "stay while I dig" refrain lands as an adoptable, aching hook that mutates and thins on each return; the bridges of all four strip to voice + gravel and carry. The 8-second line reads as a devotional act, not a statistic.

**Name the ONE way this renders generic OR grief-touristic:** The sharpest risk is **grief-tourism via prettiness** — Suno's default for "gospel choir + shimmer reverb + sad piano + sub-bass" is a *lush, consoling, streamable sad-ballad* that turns catastrophe into a warm bath (exactly Fisher's veto). The strip-away could get overridden by Suno's tendency to ADD lushness toward the climax, producing a swelling, cathartic, head-noddable outro — which would make the record a beautiful cry ABOUT other people's disaster rather than the lonely, unresolved labor it must be. Secondary generic risk: the hands-in-gravel percussion renders as a tidy shaker/perc loop (danceable) instead of irregular, un-quantized scraping.

**Self-repair (applied once, folded into all step-11 prompts):**
1. **Force the recede, block the swell:** every music prompt states the arrangement *thins and loses layers* toward the end, the bridge is *voice and gravel alone with all synths removed*, and the ending is a *suspended unresolved plagal chord — no climax, no full choir payoff, no build*. Add to EXCLUDE: no swelling cathartic climax, no triumphant final chorus, no build-up drop, no four-on-the-floor.
2. **De-quantize the gravel:** specify hands-in-gravel as *irregular, off-grid, un-looped field recording, human and arrhythmic — not a percussion loop*; add to EXCLUDE: no shaker loop, no quantized percussion.
3. **Keep terror in frame:** each prompt keeps the *held root-fifth drone (third withheld) as loud as the vocal*, "the pressure of earth over a body," so the beauty never fully consoles (Sagan + Fisher). The one fact stays inside a breath (answered), never announced.
This keeps the surface adoptable (hook, warmth — the singer-surface gates) while the engine stays a vigil, not a wallow.
