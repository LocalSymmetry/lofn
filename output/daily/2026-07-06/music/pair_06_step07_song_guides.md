# PAIR 06 — Step 07 · Song Guides (DreamPlug · through-composed witness)

> Guides DIRECT, they don't draft. 6 guides (4 variation angles + 2 wildcards). Each ≤30 lines, dense. ISOLATION held. Voice = DreamPlug. Tri-source live in every guide. One fact (8 seconds) answered once. Quiet-Voice Hole in every guide.

**Shared spine (all 4 variations, but each is a DISTINCT scene — NON-repeating architecture):**
Through-composed WITNESS: [spoken/half-sung testimony, body-first ≤20s] → [prayer addressed TO the witness: "stay while I dig"] → [the machine-band fades: crews' engines recede, beat strips away] → [fragmenting bridge: hands-in-gravel + one breath alone] → [listener alone at the end: hook thinned to a single line, no resolve]. Key ~C#/D minor, half-time ~76–84 BPM. Cloud-808 drone ⟷ hands-in-gravel percussion (Axis A). The 8-second pulse answered ONCE at the hinge. Female lead default (breath-forward, half-sung Cry-&-Slide, vocal cracks left in); Angel-Choir hymn pad; gospel Rhodes/Hammond; detuned GameBoy lead as the "count."

## Guide 1 — V1: THE DIGGER'S HANDS (the labor itself)
- **Storytelling techniques:** *synecdoche* (hands stand for the whole griever — we never see the face); *apostrophe* (the singer prays to the unseen witness/God/the one buried). **Central aspect:** the hands as the only instrument left after the machines go.
- **Mood & arc (taxonomy):** Numbness → Determination(Resolve) → Loneliness(Desolation) → Devotion. Begin numbed and flat (fingers "already forgot the color of not-bleeding"), rise to a stubborn prayer, then thin to alone.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** open on hands-in-gravel field-recording ONLY (Flair 7) + one Rhodes triad; Cloud-808 sub enters low-passed as the held drone (S2 "underground lake"); Angel-Choir pad ghosts the hymn behind. GameBoy detuned lead ticks the count. Bridge: machine-beat fades OUT (crews leaving) — gravel + breath alone (Add Vocals texture).
- **Production & effects:** close-mic, VHS grain, tape hiss, long shimmer reverb on the choir only (voice stays dry and intimate). No drop — a *recede*.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:40, 8 sections):** Intro(gravel) · Testimony V1 · Prayer-to-witness 1 · Hinge (the 8s answered) · Testimony V2 · Prayer-to-witness 2 (thinner) · Bridge (strip-away, gravel+breath) · Outro (one line, alone). Non-repeating; the prayer MUTATES, never loops.
- **Rhyming/line:** loose slant-rhyme, free-verse leaning (ABCB in prayers, unrhymed in testimony); 3–5 lines per section, uneven on purpose. **Poetic devices:** consonance chains on hard /k/-/g/ (dig, gravel, knuckle, cracked); caesura-as-wound mid-line.
- **The 8-second fact (hinge, answered):** "they said the earth still moves eight seconds at a time — so I count to it, so my hands don't stop." Answered, not recited; the pulse becomes the grief's metronome.
- **Quiet-Voice Hole (Flair 5):** after the hinge, beat drops to nothing for one naked line — "*I'm still here*" — found on the third listen.
- **Hook (mutating):** "stay while I dig" → "stay while I dig / the light's near gone" → (outro) "…dig."
- **DreamPlug proof:** half-sung crack on "dig"; gospel Rhodes; minor-key grace, not spectacle.

## Guide 2 — V2: THE CREWS' MACHINERY LEAVING (the fade)
- **Storytelling techniques:** *personification* (the excavators "exhale and go quiet," the floodlights "blink and forget us"); *zeugma* (the trucks take the light and the last loud thing). **Central aspect:** the WITHDRAWAL rendered as a mix decision — the machine-band physically fades out of the song.
- **Mood & arc:** Anticipation(Dread) → Abandonment(Loneliness) → Numbness → a small Devotion. The song is built around the moment the sound of help drives away.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** open FULL-ish for DreamPlug (Cloud-808, choir pad, a distant diesel-rumble sample as "the machine band"); across the song, side-chain and low-pass the machine layer until it's gone by the bridge (Negative Space cue). Gravel + hand-claps take over. GameBoy lead = the generator's last note flattening out.
- **Production & effects:** the diesel/floodlight layer gets a slow high-cut + fade to model taillights receding; Doppler-ish pitch-droop once. Tape stop on the last machine hit. Voice dry, close.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:30, 8 sections):** Intro(engines idling, hum) · Testimony V1 · the Withdrawal (machines pull away) · Hinge(8s answered) · Prayer-to-witness · Testimony V2(quieter) · Bridge(only gravel+breath) · Outro(one line). Non-repeating.
- **Rhyming/line:** couplets that fray into single lines as the machines leave (form enacts the loss); 2–4 lines/section. **Poetic devices:** diminuendo enacted in syllable count (lines shrink toward the end); onomatopoeia (rumble, hiss, click).
- **The 8-second fact (hinge, answered):** "the ocean out past the block still turns every eight seconds — nobody's watching it either — so I keep its time." Answered; ties the pulse to the unwatched labor (Oblique card).
- **Quiet-Voice Hole:** the instant the machines are fully gone, one held breath + "*okay. okay.*" — smallest honest line.
- **Hook (mutating):** "stay while I dig" reframed as plea to the leaving crews: "don't take the light — stay while I dig."
- **DreamPlug proof:** the fade IS the L.U.L.L. loop broken; grace in the minor recede, no anger at the crews (denial policy).

## Guide 3 — V3: THE NEIGHBOR WHO KEEPS COUNTING (the metronome)
- **Storytelling techniques:** *anaphora* (repeated "count" openings that vary); *epizeuxis* (a number-word repeated until it stops being a number and becomes a prayer). **Central aspect:** counting as devotion — the 8-second pulse as the ONLY thing holding the digger together.
- **Mood & arc:** Determination(Persistence) → Obsession(as love, not menace) → Grief(Mourning) → Serenity(hard-won, thin). The count is how they don't fall apart; when it breaks, so do they, softly.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** GameBoy detuned lead is the metronome-tick front and center (Flair 10 + nostalgia synth); Cloud-808 drone underneath; hands-in-gravel on the off-count; Angel-Choir swells only when the counting falters. Hammond bass-pedal drone as the held "underground lake."
- **Production & effects:** the tick has tape-wow (wavering pitch = the count getting harder to hold); reverb opens as the count breaks. Close-mic breath between numbers.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:45, 8 sections):** Intro(tick alone) · Testimony/count V1 · Prayer 1 · Hinge(8s — the count NAMED once, answered) · count V2(faltering) · Prayer 2 · Bridge(the tick stops; gravel+breath) · Outro(no count, one line). Non-repeating.
- **Rhyming/line:** metrical then deliberately arrhythmic where the count breaks; internal rhyme in counted lines. **Poetic devices:** anaphora ("count the…"), polyptoton (count/counted/counting/uncounted); caesura where a number should be and isn't.
- **The 8-second fact (hinge, answered):** "eight seconds between the diggings — I made it a hymn so the quiet couldn't win." Answered/transfigured; ⚠️ ONLY numeric in the song — no other count recited.
- **Quiet-Voice Hole:** when the tick stops, one unmetered whisper — "*…I lost the count*" — the third-listen gut-punch.
- **Hook (mutating):** "stay while I dig" arrives late, after counting fails: the count gives way to the prayer.
- **DreamPlug proof:** 8-bit tick + gospel drone = GameBoy-under-the-pew childhood; Cry-&-Slide crack on the lost count.

## Guide 4 — V4: THE ONE THEY DIG FOR, RENDERED AS ABSENCE (human-subject-safe)
- **Storytelling techniques:** *aposiopesis* (sentences to the missing one break off unfinished); *negative space / apophasis* (define the person only by the shape of what's gone — never a name, never a face). **Central aspect:** the beloved is ONLY an absence — a warmth that should answer, a hand that should close back.
- **Mood & arc:** Longing(Yearning) → Denial(soft) → Grief(Heartache) → Acceptance(unresolved). Second-person address to a "you" who never answers.
- **Instrumentation & texture:** sparsest of the four — Cloud-808 drone + Angel-Choir pad + gravel, almost no lead (the absence has no melody of its own); a single Rhodes note left ringing where an answer should be. GameBoy lead appears ONCE then cuts (the voice that should reply, gone).
- **Production & effects:** call-and-response with SILENCE — the response slot is empty air, once a true 1-beat hole (Quiet-Voice Hole doubles as the "answer that never comes"). Extreme close-mic; reverb tail on the unanswered word only.
- **Structure & dynamics (~3:30, 8 sections):** Intro(gravel+drone) · Address V1(to "you") · Prayer/plea 1 · Hinge(8s answered) · Address V2 · plea 2(unanswered) · Bridge(strip to breath; empty response slots) · Outro(one word to the gap). Non-repeating.
- **Rhyming/line:** unrhymed second-person address; broken lines (enjambment across the missing answer); 2–4 lines/section. **Poetic devices:** aposiopesis (trailing —), apostrophe, epanalepsis (line begins and ends on the same word, closing a circle around nothing).
- **The 8-second fact (hinge, answered):** "eight seconds — that's how long a breath lasts down there, they told me — so I hold mine that long and pretend it's yours." Answered; the fact becomes an act of keeping-company. ⚠️ ⛔ HUMAN-SUBJECT: no name, no identifying detail — absence only.
- **Quiet-Voice Hole:** the empty response after "answer me" — the silence IS the smallest honest line.
- **Hook (mutating):** "stay while I dig" spoken TO the buried "you" — "stay… while I dig to you."
- **DreamPlug proof:** hymn-shimmer as the after-image of a voice; grace over despair; the loop refused because there's no reply to loop back to.

## Guide 5 — WILDCARD A: THE STAINED-GLASS AFTER A LONG NIGHT (DreamPlug's own metaphor as production)
- Not a 5th song — a **cross-guide production lens** to deepen all four. DreamPlug: "the sun through a stained-glass window after a long night… not happy, but full of grace." Apply as: dawn does NOT rescue — the light comes up on the SAME unfinished dig. Let one Angel-Choir major-ish shimmer bloom at the very end (a "stained-glass" chord) OVER the minor drone — grace without relief. Terror-adjacent tenderness (Flair 15): the beautiful light makes the unfinished labor more unbearable, not less. Wildcard device: a *plagal (IV–I) "amen" cadence* that refuses to fully resolve (suspended 4th left hanging). Use in whichever variation's outro lands hardest.

## Guide 6 — WILDCARD B: THE UNDERGROUND LAKE (Konstellationen texture bible)
- **Cross-guide S2 texture lens.** Import Pisani verbatim as sound-design: *"an underground lake, an empty highway, a blinking satellite," "disorienting, even terrifying."* Build the held drone as a sub-bass "underground lake" — a room-tone that feels like sound heard through earth (heavy low-pass, no top). The "blinking satellite" = a single high sonar-ping every 8 seconds (doubles as the pulse, Flair 10). The "empty highway" = the field once the crews' road-noise is gone. Keep it *terrifying-adjacent* but always folded under the tenderness — the fear never surfaces as a jump-scare; it's the pressure of earth over a body. Bass-forward "made to move bodies" is INVERTED: the sub is made to still the body, to make it kneel. Deploy under the bridge of every variation.
