# PAIR 04 · Step 07 — Song Guides (DIRECT) — CelticaChime
> Guides are DIRECT: BPM, key, section map, instrument synthesis, vocal direction, the hook, production. One emotion (protective rage, never named). Four architectures that are the SAME spine (vow→verdict) inflected four ways.

## SHARED DNA (all four)
- **Key:** F minor (the Gaelic-Drill root; tin-whistle in the D/G whistle range voiced to sit in F-minor color via a C or F whistle).
- **Tempo philosophy:** lament verses rubato/slow ~72–76 "felt" BPM over a subliminal bodhrán pulse; drill/drop half-time-feel at 140 BPM (so the drop is *slower-feeling*, heavier, not faster — heaviness, not hype).
- **Two bands:** tin-whistle/uilleann drone (comfort, up the stairwell) ⟷ mono 808 slide (threat, up through the floor). Separate until the Horizon Chorus.
- **Rupture:** Quantum Bit-Depth Swell (reed → 8-bit → 2-bit) → 1-beat true Blackout → drop lands EARLY/de-tuned (flinch, not party).
- **Vocal:** female, Irish-diaspora alto, sean-nós ornamentation, close-mic, breath audible. Verse = near-whispered vow (sub-vocal, intimate). Verdict/chorus = flat-affect gritted chant (jaw-clenched, NOT euphoric). One un-effected quiet-voice hole per song.
- **Signature Style 1 (declare in every prompt):** *Siren-Reed Doubling* — a close-mic'd uilleann/tin-whistle drone tuned so its swell is indistinguishable from a distant air-raid siren; one timbre reads as lullaby-hush, alarm, and lament at once.
- **Signature Style 2 (declare in every prompt):** *Floor-Shudder 808* — a dry mono sub-808 that arrives a half-beat early with no riser, felt in the chest before heard, standing in for a strike three streets over; the "drop" as a flinch.
- **Refused fact:** 351 (drones), mid-word ("three-fifty—"), once, at the hinge, no other number beside it.
- **Quiet-voice hole:** everything drops but the child's breath + one bare reed note; the smallest honest line lives here.
- **Hook (V1/V3 lead, echoed across):** *"count them if you have to — I won't."*

## SONG GUIDE — V1 · The Stairwell Mother
- **BPM/key/time:** 72 felt → 140 half-time drop · F minor · 4/4 (verses rubato over pulse).
- **Section map (vow→verdict):**
  1. *Intro — The Stairwell* (0:00–0:22): field recording (dust ticking, a far concrete thud), a single held tin-whistle note that is also the siren rising. Bodhrán heartbeat enters −18 dB.
  2. *Verse 1 — The Vow* (0:22–0:58): near-whispered sean-nós, sung DOWN to the sleeper. The coat, the seventh step, the small weight.
  3. *Pre — The Swell* (0:58–1:14): the crowd-hum/newsreader-ghost creeps in; reed begins to bit-crush; the number tries to arrive.
  4. *THE HINGE — Refused Number → Bit-Depth Guillotine → Blackout* (1:14–1:22): "three-fifty—" cut off by her body; collapse to 2-bit; **1 beat true silence.**
  5. *Verdict / Horizon Chorus* (1:22–1:52): the DROP (early, de-tuned, flinch); tin-whistle and 808 in unison for the ONLY time; the chant-refusal.
  6. *Verse 2 — The Vow, harder* (1:52–2:24): subtractive; whistle under; the fury closer to the surface.
  7. *Quiet-Voice Hole* (2:24–2:40): breath + one bare reed note; "still here. still here."
  8. *Final Verdict* (2:40–3:12): chorus returns gritted, gang-hush backing (the crowd-hum turned protective).
  9. *Outro — Empty Highway* (3:12–3:30): everything strips; the siren-reed descends and does NOT resolve; a door.
- **Instruments:** C tin-whistle (lead), uilleann drone pad, bodhrán (frame-drum, layered with soft electronic kick), mono 808 slide, sean-nós vocal stack, low synth mist, field recordings (dust, distant impact, stairwell air).
- **Vocal direction:** verses sub-vocal whisper-sing with heavy grace-note ornament; verdict flat gritted chant with smile-vowels removed; the hole is one clean unprocessed line.
- **The hook — what makes it unforgettable:** the tenderness and the refusal are the SAME melody; you cannot sing "count them if you have to — I won't" without the lullaby cadence, so comfort and defiance are inseparable in the mouth.
- **Production:** stairwell = long dark plate reverb on whistle; 808 dry/mono/early; Blackout is true digital silence (no reverb tail); drop has no riser.

## SONG GUIDE — V2 · The Air-Defense Console (the gap itself)
- **BPM/key/time:** 76 felt → 140 · F minor · 4/4. Colder, more drill-forward; 808 dominant, whistle distant.
- **Section map (vow→verdict, institutional inflection):**
  1. *Intro — The Seam* (0:00–0:20): a low 50 Hz console hum (Pisani's "blinking satellite"), a sonar-like ping that is the sky's arithmetic; tin-whistle FAR (the civilian the console can't reach).
  2. *Verse 1 — The Operator's Vow* (0:20–0:56): near-whisper, but addressed to the screen / the gap — "I can hold the line I can hold, not the line above it."
  3. *Pre — The Swell* (0:56–1:12): the machine tries to report the tally; reed/hum bit-crush.
  4. *HINGE — Refusal → Guillotine → Blackout* (1:12–1:20): "three-fifty—" the human voice refuses the machine's count; **1 beat silence.**
  5. *Verdict / Horizon* (1:20–1:50): the drop is the interceptor that ISN'T enough — flinch, not triumph; whistle and 808 meet once.
  6. *Verse 2 — The Gap Named* (1:50–2:22): the seam between sent and stopped; subtractive.
  7. *Quiet-Voice Hole* (2:22–2:38): the console mutes; one civilian breath from far off the whistle-side; "still there. still there."
  8. *Final Verdict* (2:38–3:08): drill chant, gritted, cold.
  9. *Outro — The Line Above It* (3:08–3:26): hum returns; unresolved.
- **Instruments:** 50 Hz console hum, sonar ping (re-pitched), mono 808 (dominant), sparse spectral drill hats (NO generic trap hats — granular/foley), distant tin-whistle, sean-nós refusal stack.
- **Vocal direction:** more clipped, institutional in verse; the refusal is the one warm human act in a cold machine field.
- **The hook:** a variant refusal — *"the sky does the sums — I won't."* (keeps the refusal grammar, distinct line).
- **Production:** binaural coldness; the gap is a literal frequency hole in the mix (a notch where the interceptor should be); 808 early/dry.

## SONG GUIDE — V3 · The Lullaby Sung OVER the Alarm
- **BPM/key/time:** 70 felt → 140 · F minor · 6/8 lilt in the lullaby (cradle-rock), snapping to 4/4 at the verdict.
- **Section map (INVERSION — lullaby foreground, siren ground):**
  1. *Intro — The Cradle* (0:00–0:24): the sean-nós lullaby ALONE, 6/8, warm; the siren-reed drone rises UNDER it, quiet, ground-level.
  2. *Verse 1 — The Lullaby Holds* (0:24–1:00): the cradle-song stays steady while the drone-siren swells to try to swallow it.
  3. *Pre — The Swell Rises to Swallow* (1:00–1:16): the siren-drone nearly covers the lullaby; bit-crush at the edges.
  4. *HINGE — Refusal → Guillotine → Blackout* (1:16–1:24): "three-fifty—" refused; the lullaby is what CUTS the number; **1 beat silence** (even the cradle stops).
  5. *Horizon Chorus — SAME MELODY* (1:24–1:54): the lullaby melody and the drill chant are revealed to be the SAME notes; unison; the most tender and most terrible moment.
  6. *Verse 2 — The Lullaby Wins by Not Stopping* (1:54–2:26): 6/8 returns; the cradle-song refuses to end.
  7. *Quiet-Voice Hole* (2:26–2:42): breath + one reed; "sleep. just sleep." (a command that is also a prayer).
  8. *Final — The Cradle Over the Verdict* (2:42–3:12): lullaby foreground, drill under, holding.
  9. *Outro — Still Rocking* (3:12–3:28): 6/8 fades unresolved.
- **Instruments:** sean-nós lullaby lead, 6/8 harp arpeggio (Celtic harp, plucked, warm), siren-reed drone (ground), bodhrán half-time, mono 808 (under), synth mist.
- **Vocal direction:** the warmest of the four in verse (true lullaby), the refusal is the ONLY hard edge; the same voice does both.
- **The hook:** the canonical *"count them if you have to — I won't"* sung to a cradle-rock, so the refusal itself sounds like a lullaby line.
- **Production:** 6/8 cradle-rock; the reveal that lullaby = chant is a mix-automation move (same MIDI, two timbres crossfaded at the Horizon); the drop under-mixed so the cradle stays on top.

## SONG GUIDE — V4 · The Morning After (tin-whistle band alone, no drum)
- **BPM/key/time:** ~66 felt, rubato throughout · F minor · free time. **NO drop, NO 808, NO drum.** The Blackout never breaks.
- **Section map (WITHHELD verdict — the lament stands alone):**
  1. *Intro — The Empty Highway* (0:00–0:26): dead-street field recording (wind, a single far bird, no traffic — Pisani's "empty highway"); the tin-whistle band tunes.
  2. *Verse 1 — The One Who Held* (0:26–1:06): the person who held the sleeper walks out; sean-nós unaccompanied but for uilleann drone.
  3. *Pre — The Number Tries Again* (1:06–1:22): the newsreader-ghost returns in the morning light; still tries the tally.
  4. *HINGE — Refusal → and NO Guillotine* (1:22–1:32): "three-fifty—" refused; but instead of the Bit-Depth Swell/Blackout, the whistle simply HOLDS the note through it; the drill is WITHHELD.
  5. *The Withheld Verdict* (1:32–2:04): where the drop should be, there is only the whistle band — fiddle, whistle, drone — playing the verdict melody with no percussion. The absence IS the verdict.
  6. *Verse 2 — Counting the Wrong Things* (2:04–2:40): she counts stairs, coats, breaths — anything but the dead.
  7. *Quiet-Voice Hole* (2:40–2:56): the band stops; one breath; one bare note; "morning. it's morning."
  8. *Coda — The Band Alone* (2:56–3:24): whistle + fiddle + drone, the lament complete, no resolution to major, a door left open.
- **Instruments:** tin-whistle, Irish fiddle, uilleann drone, low bodhrán ONLY as heartbeat (very sparse, no kick), field recordings. **Explicitly no 808, no drill, no drop.**
- **Vocal direction:** exhausted, morning-cracked, quieter than V1; the fury now grief-shaped but still refusing.
- **The hook:** the refusal returns as near-spoken — *"count them if you have to — I won't"* — but tired, not chanted; the withholding of the drill makes it land harder.
- **Production:** Albini's test made literal — one instrument, one room; proves the song survives with no second band. The withheld drop is the boldest production choice: negative space as the verdict.
