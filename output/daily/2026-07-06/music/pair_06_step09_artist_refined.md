## Panel critique → change log (dissent forced artifact changes)

- *Weather-Voice (after Björk):* "Let the voice BE the weather — the breath should thicken and thin with the dust. Add breath-as-instrument cues." → added `*dust-thick inhale*` / breath dynamics per variation.
- *Attention Ascetic (after Weil) + Silent Republic (after Kaminsky):* "Attention is the whole prayer here. - *Star-Stuff (after Sagan):* "Keep both bands in frame — don't zoom to comfort. The held drone (awe of the earth's indifference) must stay AS LOUD as the tenderness." → drone kept load-bearing through every bridge; the terror (earth over a body) never resolved cheaply.
- ⚠️ *HYPER-SKEPTIC Hauntologist (Fisher) — hard dissent:* "The 808 is still flirting with a beat you could nod to. Kill any groove that invites the head-nod. The 8 seconds must be ANSWERED inside a breath, not announced. And no dawn that rescues." → (1) 808 re-specified as a *non-pulsing sustained drone*, no rhythmic 808 pattern; (2) every hinge line re-checked: the number is answered mid-breath, folded into an act (count/keep-time/hold-breath), never recited as fact; (3) all outros: dawn/shimmer comes up on the SAME unfinished dig (grace without relief). **Fisher signs off only on the strip-away version.**

**Medium Panel**
- *Lament-Bearer (after Lankum):* "The drone is your quiet band — give it a real modal identity (a sustained root-fifth, no third, so the grief stays unresolved)." → drone = open root-fifth, third withheld until the plagal amen.
- *Keeper of the Hole (after a dynamics purist):* "Headroom is meaning. The Quiet-Voice Hole must be TRUE silence around the naked line, not a quieter mix." → all four holes marked `[No beats]` + full stop of pads for the naked line.
- ⚠️ *HYPER-SKEPTIC Purist (Albini):* "Prove it survives one instrument in one room. Right now V1 and V3 lean on the GameBoy gimmick. Show me the section that is JUST a voice and gravel and still breaks a heart." → each variation's bridge re-cut to voice + hands-in-gravel ONLY (all synths out), and it must carry — verified in the guide's Facet 3. GameBoy demoted to a supporting tick, never the reason a section works.

**Context & Marketing Panel**
- *3am Curator (late-night ambient host):* "This has to survive on repeat at 3am, not on a drop. - *Witness Ethicist (after Sontag):* "Don't consume the suffering as content; forbid identifiability." → re-audited: no place, no date, no name; 'the block,' 'the street,' 'down there' only. Clean.
- ⚠️ *HYPER-SKEPTIC Attention Resister (Odell):* "The quiet-voice hole must resist the very virality it uses. Don't make the naked line a hook-bait — make it something you almost miss." → naked lines kept small and un-rhymed ('I'm still here' / 'okay, okay' / 'I lost the count' / the empty response) — deliberately un-quotable, found on the third listen.

## Signature device assigned per variation (Step-11 literary mandate previewed)
- **V1 — consonance chain** on hard /k/–/g/ (dig, gravel, grey, knuckle, cracked, keep) binding adjacent lines; *caesura-as-wound* mid-line.
- **V2 — diminuendo enacted in form:** each section's lines get shorter as the help leaves; *anaphora* on "Don't take the light."
- **V3 — anaphora + polyptoton on COUNT** (count / counted / counting / uncounted / recount), the word worn down until it breaks; *epizeuxis* ("count the— count the—").
- **V4 — aposiopesis + apophasis + epanalepsis:** unfinished sentences to the missing one; the person defined only by negation; a line that opens and closes on the same word, circling nothing.

## DreamPlug deepening (personality proof, per variation)
- **Cloud-808 Pillow** = sustained low-passed sub *drone* (Fisher fix), not a beat. **Angel-Choir Pad** = sampled storefront-hymn "oohs" in Valhalla-style shimmer, blooming only at grief-peaks. **GameBoy/nostalgia synth** = the child-under-the-pew tick, supporting not leading. **Gospel Rhodes + Hammond** = the storefront-church chord bed; **plagal (IV–I) amen** left suspended = "grace without relief." **Cry-&-Slide** vocal cracks left unhealed; half-sung/half-murmured. The signature 25-second loop is **deliberately broken** into a non-repeating vigil — this is DreamPlug's meditation-loop refusing to close because the labor doesn't loop back to rescue. Denial policy honored: nothing aggressive/cynical; grief held as grace. NONE of: glitch-Baroque, fry-snarl, category-theory, fairies, Lofn-Prime lexicon.
