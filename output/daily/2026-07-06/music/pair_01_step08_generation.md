## V1 — "Say It Back (Church Hall, 1957)"

### MUSIC PROMPT (draft)
A hushed, awe-lit industrial-gospel hymn that lifts into warm popiano. Two bands run the whole song: a crystalline soprano (early-20s, breathy yearning, close-mic, faint golden vibrato) and a held choir drone like the sound of an underground lake. Sacred choral pad and a low log-drum heartbeat at half-time, 72 BPM in G major. Begins with an AI code-scratch hush and folding-chair room tone; a lone Rhodes and the drone breathe under the first verse. The soprano inventories a church-hall basement — coffee urn, chalk dust, a work-light moon in the high window. Pre-chorus lets the drone swell beneath her. At the one Horizon Chorus the soprano and drone lock in unison. Entering the last chorus a soft quantum bit-depth swell thins the mix to grain then blooms UPWARD — drone opens a fifth, soprano lifts an octave, log-drum warms. Bridge strips to one bare voice over room tone. Ends on sustained choir and a single struck chord.
