## daily 2026-08-18 · canonical · SUNNA R3 + R3.1

## THE ARTIST

**SUNNA**, the Pantheon's second seat, config **v.2026.08.R3 + R3.1**. Closed register: **unembarrassed
delight** — joy that needs no thesis, which no other member can do. Single seat, all six pairs, no

**What changed under her, today, before this run:** the personality was reconfigured on measurement (club

## THE CRITIQUE — three attacks on this run before it is written

### ATTACK 1 — "This is a costume change, not an artist."
**The charge:** you swapped genre labels. Club became soul; punk became glam. The songs will be the same
songs with different drums.
**The answer, and it is falsifiable:** the change is not in the labels, it is in **where the darkness
lives**. Measured on the predecessor: 24/24 style fields carried a walking bass as the swagger carrier;
24/24 excluded distortion; **16 wolves named in the words, 0 standing tension lanes**, and **8 songs with
no wolf at all and no apology.** QA's independent ruling was *"a different artist, and not because of the
genre labels."* Its sharpest evidence — **six of twenty-four songs are about someone else's pleasure or
someone else's work.** That is a change of *position*, not of costume.
**But the attack survives in one place:** if the ONE SEAM is carried by mix adjectives rather than by
instruments and people, then the label really is the only thing that changed. **Panel B ruling 7 exists to
kill this and it is a hard gate this run.**

### ATTACK 2 — "The wolf is decorative."
**The charge:** a dark line dropped into a bright song on schedule is a tic, not a structure.
**The answer:** measured, and **the charge partly landed on the predecessor.** 7 of 16 wolves were **status
reports** — conditions she could state and then do nothing about (*"support ended in March"*). A status
report is decorative by definition: nothing in the song moves because of it.
**The repair, binding here:** **a wolf must cost her something inside the song.** She abandons the north
wall. She chooses whose work survives. She takes the part out of her own machine. **If she can say it
without moving, it is weather, and weather was banned in the first clause of R3.**

### ATTACK 3 — "Carefree is just easier." *(the Hyper-Skeptic's, carried unresolved)*
**The charge:** relocating difficulty from the lyric to the music is capitulation with a rationale
attached; and a bright bed with a plain lyric is simply less work.
**The answer, from Ellena's seat:** **the transparent composition is the expensive one.** Fewer elements
means every one is exposed and has to be genuinely good. A plain line is *harder* to write than a dense one
— density hides a weak idea; six plain words expose it instantly.
**The standing falsification, unchanged:** *if by 2026-11-06 the lyrics are simple and the music is also
simple, the skeptic was right, and the evidence will be in our own catalogue.*
⚡ **And Fisher's, extended by this run's evidence:** interchangeable wolf lines **or interchangeable
endings** mean formula. The predecessor passed the first and **failed the second** — 10 of 24 landed in the
same kitchen. R3.1 is the repair and this run is its first test.

## WHAT THE CRITIQUE CHANGES IN THE CUT

1. **Bed collision is fatal** (C01 ↔ C12): one goes.
2. **Status-report wolves are fatal unless repaired**: C08 and C10 must either cost her something or be cut.
3. **Setting must be assigned and spread**: ≤2 domestic-warm across six.
4. **Return shape is a selection criterion**, not a writing detail — six distinct shapes or the run has
   failed its own falsification test before a word is written.
