## Q1 — WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?

**It is sitting.** Third row of stacking plastic chairs, left-hand side, in a community hall on a Wednesday
evening. Coat still on, because the heating comes on at eight and it is not eight. **A parquet floor with
old tape marks on it.** A serving hatch with the shutter down. A strip-light directly overhead with an
audible hum. Rain on a flat roof, going all evening, doing the roof like a brush.

Thirty feet in front of her, **somebody else's brass band is rehearsing** — stands, folders, cases open on
the floor, a bass drum with a name painted on it, a conductor who taps the stand with a pencil instead of
shouting. **Nobody in the room is being paid.** They will do this again next Wednesday.

**In her hands: a paper cup.** ⛔ Squash or nothing in it. Never a hot drink, never a mug. The R3.1
domestic-warm ban is one wrong noun away in this room and that noun is *mug*.

**She has nothing to do with any of it.** She is not in the band, is not related to anyone in it, is not
waiting for anyone, and is not there for a reason she explains. **She is the only person in the room with
free hands and full attention** — which is why the song is a list.

## Q2 — WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?

# ⭐⭐ **NOTHING.** And that is the assignment, not an oversight.

**The honest answer is nothing, and here is the defence, because an unexamined "nothing" is a repair
signal and a chosen one is the design.**

**1. It is assigned, in writing, twice, at run level.** `05_pair_assignments.md`: *"P02 and P03 carry no
dark line, no named pursuer, no undertow, no shadow in the last verse… ⛔ A wolf in P02 or P03 is a gate
failure, not a bonus."* `step01_essence_and_facets.md` on FACET 4: *"this facet carries no wolf by
construction — nothing is at stake for her, and that is the point."*

**2. The personality requires it or it stops being a personality.** `sunna.yaml`, R3, the panel's sharpest
correction to its own design: *"She is allowed to just want things… A woman having an appetite in public is
the most carefree thing pop can show, and it does not need a wolf attached to be worth a track.* ⛔ *If
every song in a set has a dark line in it on schedule, you have built a formula, not a personality."*
⭐ **A wolf-free pair is not the set's soft option. It is the set's control.** Four pairs carry a wolf; if
all six did, Fisher's falsification — *swap the dark line from track 2 into track 5 and lose nothing* —
would be unanswerable, because the wolf would be a slot rather than a subject.

**3. The concept is destroyed by a stake, specifically and mechanically.** FACET 4 is *the admirer*. Give
her something to lose in that hall and the admiration acquires a motive, and **admiration with a motive is
an audition.** The wolf would arrive dressed as envy — *she's sitting there watching other people do the
thing* — and envy is the one failure state that feels like depth while it eats the concept. The song would
stop being about the band and become about her, which is exactly the move the facet exists to refuse.

**4. Three runs answering "a kitchen" and "nothing" is drift; one pair doing it on purpose with the reason
named is design.** The distinction is *chosen versus defaulted*, and R3.1's whole finding is that **what is
not assigned gets defaulted.** So it is assigned here: room assigned, radiant kind assigned, return shape
assigned, **stake assigned as zero**, and the zero is defended in the artifact where a reader can check it.

**5. What replaces the wolf structurally, so the song still turns.** GOLDEN MOVE 3 requires the song to
contradict its opening stance past the midpoint. It does — **without a shadow.** The opening stance is *I
have got nothing to do with any of it*, repeated as the third line of every stanza. The song contradicts it
with **her hands**: still → counting on the cup → cup on the floor → **doing something across the seam** →
still there for the second half. ⭐ **The turn is participation, not danger.** The spine keeps insisting she
is only watching while the body stops watching. Nothing is at risk and something still happens.

### ⚠️ THE ONE CONFLICT, NAMED AND RESOLVED RATHER THAN QUIETLY DROPPED
GOLDEN MOVE 4 says *"every awe song carries a clean fear it does not resolve cheaply,"* and this is an AWE
slot. **The pair-level gate wins**, because the ICB itself carves the exception (*"two pairs carry no wolf
at all and do not apologise for it"*) and the assignments file states it as a gate failure. **The set
discharges golden move 4 across P01, P04, P05 and P06.**
⛔ **And I refused the available smuggle.** V3's *bit she cannot do* is a limit, and a limit can be dressed
as a clean fear at almost no cost. **That is a wolf in a cardigan.** V3 keeps the limit as a **fact she is
delighted by**, said in exactly the voice that named the bar, and it is never followed by a line about
wanting it. If a reader can find a wound in V3, the pair failed — check there first.

# THE FOUR — SHARED SPINE

*(Assigned in `pair_02_step05_isolated_brief.md`; restated so each guide can be read alone.)*

* **SECTION ARCHITECTURE — CATALOGUE FORM.** Each verse is a growing list of specific admired details.
  **No bridge.** **Verse 1 = 4 lines · Verse 2 = 8 · Verse 3 = 16 · Verse 4 = ONE ITEM AND STOPS.**
  The song gets **narrower** as it goes, not wider.
* **RHYME / LINE LOGIC.** ⛔ **No terminal rhyme anywhere** — a deliberate strip. **THE RETURN debt is paid
  twice over:** (a) an **exact-repeat third line in every stanza** — a per-song spine, byte-identical,
  at position 3 of every four-line stanza, so the return sits *inside* the stanza instead of at the
  line-end; (b) a **byte-identical chorus**. The return is **more** frequent than a rhyme scheme, not less.
* **HOOK GRAMMAR.** A **question the room answers.** Two halves: she asks, **the room shouts the second
  half** — and the room's half is the title.
* **THE HOLE.** ⭐ **The answer phrase left unsung once.** Late in the song the room's half arrives and
  **the horns play it instead of anyone singing it.**
* **THE SEAM, once, at the same structural place in all four:** a **trombone and an alto saxophone in
  unison on the upbeat**, Hammond organ shuffle, rimshot snare. ⛔ **Those three instruments appear nowhere
  else** in any style field or any lyric. **She does something across it** — a different action each time.
* **THE RADIANT** — last line of Verse 3, plain, once, **not the title**, **never explained**, kind = *a
  place she keeps coming back to*, ⛔ **no relative as its origin**.
* **THE RETURN** — *she stays for the second half.* ⛔ Four different sign-off sentences, each welded to its
  own song's admired object. ⛔ No food, no hot drink, no domestic interior.
* **ONE NUMBER PER SONG**, spelled in words, at the hinge, **responded to**.
* **SOURCE 2** — ≥2 phrases imported **exactly**, case-sensitive, mid-sentence, into the **style field**.
* **STYLE FIELD** ≤1000 chars, leads Genre × Subgenre, **108 BPM**, key. ⛔ **No negations of any kind.**
* **LYRICS** <5000 chars, **70–120 sung lines**, more lines and shorter lines.

# V1 GUIDE — **THE LATE ONE**
### angle: *THE ONE WHO IS FRACTIONALLY LATE — a player whose timing is wrong in a way that is better*

**THE ADMIRED THING IS A FLAW THAT BEATS THE CORRECT VERSION.** A tenor-horn player comes in behind
everybody, every time, in the same place. It is objectively wrong. It is also the best two seconds in the
piece, and the row has quietly rearranged itself around him. *(Sunna's own DNA: the horn player who is
always late — **"the lateness is the swagger"** — and she refuses to quantize her.)*

* **Hook:** she — *"Which bar?"* · room — **"The late one!"**
* **Spine (line 3 of every stanza):** *"I have got nothing to do with any of it."*
* **The number:** **bar nineteen**, at the hinge, and the response is *"And it is better."*
* **Radiant (last line of V3):** *"I come back to this hall every Wednesday I am free."*
* **Seam action:** cup on the floor → **both hands** → she claps **behind** the beat, where he plays, and
  stays behind after the horns have gone. Her line across it: *"So I am late as well."*
* **Return / closer, one item:** *"Second half, bar nineteen, he is late again, and I am in the same chair."*
* **Density arc:** sousaphone figure alone → snare and claps → trumpets from V2 → full hall on the third
  chorus → seam → hole → **one voice, one line** → riff out alone.
* **Watch:** *"So I am late as well"* must land as a dry aside, not a punchline she sells. **The register
  never moves.** ⛔ And *late* stays a musical fact — it never becomes a line about her own life.

# V2 GUIDE — **SECOND CORNET**
### angle: *the youngest person in the room, doing the least glamorous job, perfectly*

**THE ADMIRED THING IS INVISIBLE DISCIPLINE.** Second cornet is the seat nobody in the audience can pick
out. The job is mostly **counting rests** — long stretches of nothing, held exactly, so that four bars of
harmony arrive in the right place. She is the youngest player in the hall and she has not looked at anyone
else's page all evening.

* **Hook:** she — *"Who's counting?"* · room — **"Second cornet!"**
* **Spine:** *"She is the youngest one in here."*
* **The number:** **ninety bars of rest**, at the hinge; the response is *"I lost count at the door."*
* **Radiant:** *"I know which chair in here does not wobble."*
* **Seam action:** she gets up **during** the horns and moves down the side of the hall to where she can
  see the valves; she is sitting again by the time the organ stops.
* **Return / closer, one item:** *"Second half, she counts it again from cold, and I am down the side where
  I can see the valves."*
* **Density arc:** the catalogue thickens with players entering; ⭐ **the stack** (FLAIR 12) is used here —
  the room's answer overdubbed across takes so *"Second cornet!"* thickens each time it returns.
* ⚠️ **Guard:** the admiration is aimed at **the counting, the valves, the page** — the work, never the
  person's body or age. Youngest is stated as a **fact about the seat**, once per stanza, and the camera
  stays on the instrument. `HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD`: the people are invented; keep the frame on the craft.

# V3 GUIDE — **HE CAN, HE CAN**
### angle: *THE BIT SHE CANNOT DO — open admiration for a skill she does not have and is not acquiring*

**THE ADMIRED THING IS A LIMIT.** The euphonium player takes the slow solo, and the thing that is
extraordinary is not the notes — it is that **he plays it twice and it is the same twice.** She could not
do that on her best day, she is not going to learn, and she is completely happy about it.

* **Hook:** she — *"Who can do that?"* · room — **"He can, he can!"**
* **Spine:** *"I could not do that on my best day."*
* **The number:** **twice** — *"He does it twice and it is the same twice."* Response: **"I checked."**
* **Radiant:** *"I have been coming here since it was called something else."*
* **Seam action:** she has been fingering the tune on the paper cup; she flattens the cup, puts it in her
  pocket, and **turns her chair right round** to face them, and leaves it turned.
* **Return / closer, one item:** *"Second half, he does it again exactly the same, and I have got the chair
  turned round for it."*
* **Density arc:** thinnest of the four — Ellena's ruling 8, *carefree is the expensive finish*. Long
  stretches of sousaphone, claps and one voice, so the euphonium line has room to be the good thing.
* ⛔⛔ **THE PAIR'S HIGHEST-RISK SONG.** A limit is one degree from a lack. **No line about wishing, no line
  about having tried and stopped, no line about time, no shrug that reads as consolation.** The limit is
  a **fact she is delighted by**, and the delight is the whole content. Checked twice in step 09.

# V4 GUIDE — **THAT'S THE HALF**
### angle: *THE SECOND HALF — she was going to leave at the interval and she does not*

**THE ADMIRED THING IS A DECISION — hers.** The interval arrives, the coat comes off the back of the
chair, and she puts it back. This is the pair's assigned return shape made into the whole subject: the
other three songs *end* by staying; **this one is about the staying.**

* **Hook:** she — *"Is that it?"* · room — **"That's the half!"**
* **Spine:** *"I was going to leave at the interval."*
* **The number:** **twenty past** — *"The second half starts at twenty past."* Response: *"I looked at the
  clock and sat back down."* ⭐ **A start time, not an expiry.** FLAIR 1, the published hour, with the
  clock pointing forwards — the seed's mechanism running in the direction that carries no tide.
* **Radiant:** *"I have got my own way in here, round the side, past the bins."*
* **Seam action:** the coat comes off the back of the chair as the horns start, and goes **back** on it as
  they finish. One garment, two moves, the decision in the middle.
* **Return / closer, one item:** *"Twenty past, they go again, and my coat is still on the back of this
  chair."*
* **Density arc:** the only song whose arc is explicitly two halves — the second half arrives as **a
  choppier flip of the buttery soul sample to a new drum pattern**, same tempo, same key, same bed.
  ⚠️ **This is arrangement density, not a gear-change** — the instrument list, the tempo and the key do not
  move. Flagged and re-checked in step 09.
* **Watch:** ⛔ the interval must never read as *nearly leaving out of boredom or loneliness.* She was going
  to leave because she was going to leave. She stays because of a specific thing she heard. **No shadow in
  the decision.**
