## YOUR FOUR - architecture, rhyme, hook, device
**SECTION ARCHITECTURE.** **PROCEDURAL.** The song is a set of steps in order. **Verse = instruction, chorus = result.** Four verses, four steps, and the chorus reports what happened each time - the same chorus, meaning something slightly different by position alone.

**RHYME / LINE LOGIC.** **Internal rhyme mid-line only, never at line ends.** The end of every line is deliberately unrhymed and flat, so the rhyme lives where the body feels it rather than where the ear expects it. Debt paid by the byte-identical chorus and a repeated step-phrase.

**HOOK GRAMMAR.** A **flat declarative the room shouts** - a plain sentence with no image in it, made singable by placement.

**SONIC DEVICE (your hole).** **A bar of rest before the last chorus.** Everything stops for one bar and the room fills it.
