# STEP 01 — ESSENCE AND FACETS
## daily 2026-08-18 · canonical · SUNNA R3 + R3.1

## THE ESSENCE, IN ONE SENTENCE

> **Somebody is doing careful, unnecessary, excellent work on a thing with a published expiry, and they are
> enjoying it, and the enjoyment is not a coping strategy.**

## THE SEVEN FACETS

Each facet is a *different way into the essence* — not a mood, a **position a person can occupy.** The
twelve concepts of step 02 are drawn from these; the six shipped pairs must span at least five of them.

### FACET 1 — THE MAKER, MID-TASK
Deep in it. Forearms, tools, the specific sub-problem. **The hour is known and irrelevant to the next
ninety seconds.** This is the facet where appetite is most visibly *labour*, and it is the hardest to
sentimentalise because the body is busy.

### FACET 2 — THE ONE WHO RECORDS IT
Somebody arranged for a witness. *(Atget's ruling: they **commissioned the survival**; the record is part
of the work, not a consolation for it.)* The position here is **paying attention on purpose**, and its
emotional colour is pride, not grief.

### FACET 3 — THE ONE WHO KICKS IT OVER
⭐ The BRIDGE panel's find. **The makers destroy it themselves and strike night is a party.** The strongest
anti-elegy position available: destruction as an *action she takes*, not an event she suffers.

### FACET 4 — THE ADMIRER
She did not make it. Somebody else did, and it is good, and she says exactly which part. *(Sunna's
Ruthless Specificity; the "WHICH BAR" tactic as a lyric position.)* ⭐ **This facet carries no wolf by
construction** — nothing is at stake for her, and that is the point.

### FACET 5 — THE MISUSER
Using a thing completely wrong and getting something the correct use could never produce. *(F19 #3, the
railway as a flatbed scanner.)* Cheerful disobedience — **not rebellion posture**, which is a different and
worse thing.

### FACET 6 — THE ONE WHO WASN'T TOLD
⭐ The REFLECT amendment. The published hour is a **privilege**; here it is a schedule somebody else read
and she didn't. **This is the seed inverted**, and it is the set's honest counterweight. Carries the
INDIGNATION seat and the CONTAINER CLAUSE.

### FACET 7 — THE REPAIRER
Fixing the thing everybody said to replace. Devotion as a practical activity. **The wolf here must cost
her something**, or it collapses into FACET 1 with a status report attached.

## ⛔ THE FACET THAT DOES NOT EXIST
**THE MOURNER.** There is no position in this run from which the loss is the subject. That is Lofn's seat
and it is not open today. A concept that arrives at *"and then it was gone and that was sad"* has left the
essence and is cut at step 05.
