## PANEL A — CONCEPT

**Seat grounding.** VOICE 01 *The Edge of the Sea* (1955) — the tideline as a place with a schedule, not a
symbol. VOICE 02 — photographing ordinary structures precisely because they were about to be gone; the
record as the surviving object. VOICE 03 — "Jolene," "9 to 5"; situation over sentiment. VOICE 04 —
*Accumulation* (1971); rigorous systems performed so they read as offhand. VOICE 05 — the 2012 Largo set;
catastrophe delivered in the register of small talk. VOICE 06 — *On Photography* (1977); the published
critique of photography as elegiac acquisition. Tuned high-neuroticism, low-agreeableness.

**⚡ ANTI-SENTIMENTALIST (after Sontag):** Working from the *On Photography* argument, before this room
falls in love with its own seed: **a sand castle destroyed by the tide is the most available metaphor for
mortality in the English language.** It is on greeting cards. You have found a beautiful nineteenth-century
photograph of it and mistaken the archive for an idea.

**VERNACULAR PHOTOGRAPHER (after Atget):** Fair about the *castle*. Not fair about the *photograph*. My
documented practice was not elegiac — I photographed shopfronts and staircases because they were **about to
be replaced**, and the pictures are not sad. They are **inventories.** The Atlantic City carvers hired a
photographer. **They commissioned the survival.** That is not a metaphor for death; it is a business
decision made by people who intended to be looked at.

**⚡ ANTI-SENTIMENTALIST (after Sontag):** …Say more.

**VERNACULAR PHOTOGRAPHER (after Atget):** The cliché is *"everything you build washes away."* The **fact**
is *"they got a photographer down there, because they knew, and they wanted the record."* Different songs.
The second is not on a greeting card.

**SHORE ARCHAEOLOGIST (after Carson):** And I would remove the mysticism from the tide, which is where the
sentimentality actually enters. From the *Edge of the Sea* method: **the tide is not fate. It is a
timetable, published in advance, in a newspaper, to the minute.** Everyone on that beach could read it.
⭐ **That is the whole difference between dread and appetite: dread is not knowing when; appetite is
knowing exactly when and carving anyway.**

**SITUATION WRITER (after Parton):** *Oh — that's the line, and it changes the assignment.* If the hour is
**published**, the singer is not brave and not doomed. **She's someone who read the paper and made a
decision.** Everybody has spent real hours on something with a known end date. A relationship with a
leaving date. A contract. A summer.

**THE ONE WHO SAYS IT PLAINLY (after Notaro):** Then the delivery rule follows and it is the whole craft.
What people remember about that set is not the disclosure — it is that **the register never moved.** Same
pacing, same dryness, same person. If your singer changes her voice to signal the dark line, she has taken
the weight off the listener and put it back in the arrangement.

**CASUAL DIFFICULTY (after Brown):** And the structural corollary: in the *Accumulation* work the system is
severe and the performance is loose. **The difficulty is fully present and none of it is displayed.**
⭐ *Hide the labour, keep the labour.*

**⚡ ANTI-SENTIMENTALIST (after Sontag):** I withdraw the cliché objection on one binding condition, and I
want it in every pair brief. ⛔ **The tide may never be personified, moralised, or addressed.** No "the sea
doesn't care," no "time takes everything," no second person aimed at the water. **The moment it becomes a
character it becomes fate, and you are back on the greeting card.** It gets a time and no adjectives.

## PANEL B — MEDIUM

**Seat grounding.** VOICE 07 — Chic; the documented **DHM ("Deep Hidden Meaning")** method: every record
carries a serious subject beneath a party surface. VOICE 08 — Brill Building; "Be My Baby," "Da Doo Ron
Ron." VOICE 09 — *Perfume: The Alchemy of Scent*; the impression of simplicity achieved by elimination.
VOICE 10 — *Sonic Warfare*; the physical low end. VOICE 11 — dub as a compositional practice, the room as
an instrument. VOICE 12 — the published critique of studio artifice and of production standing in for
songwriting. Tuned low-agreeableness.

**DEEP HIDDEN MEANING (after Rodgers):** Working from the Chic catalogue and the DHM method — this brief
was solved in 1978 and we are re-deriving it. "Le Freak" comes out of being refused at a door; it is the
record people play at weddings. "Good Times" was written into a recession. **Nobody has ever felt lectured
by "Good Times," and nobody has ever missed that it is about something.** The mechanism is not subtlety:
**the groove never comments on the lyric.** The band plays the party straight.

**HOOK MECHANIC (after Greenwich):** And the panel should notice something before it gets clever. **The
girl-group records already carry the wolf, and they carry it in the words.** "Be My Baby" is naked need
with a snare like a door slamming. "Leader of the Pack" is a death record **that teenagers danced to.**
The 1963 records are not the comfort object here — **they are the working proof of the exact brief.**

**⚡ DOCUMENTARIAN (after Albini):** Then I want to attack the thing this room will otherwise do, which is
put the whole distinction in the mix. Your predecessor run tried exactly this: the modern world and the
period intrusion had **the same instrument list**, separated by adjectives — "wide and dry" against "mono
with tape slap." **That is not a seam. That is a plug-in.** If the difference between your two worlds
survives only in reverb settings, the generator will smooth it and you will have one texture with a story
attached.

**HOOK MECHANIC (after Greenwich):** He's right and it's measurable. In 1963 the intrusion would be
**different people in the room** — three answering voices, castanets, a tom fill a machine cannot play.
**Instruments and performances, not settings.**

**SUBTRACTOR (after Ellena):** Which forces the other half. The transparent composition is the expensive
one: **everything left in is load-bearing, so every element is exposed.** If you want this to sound
carefree you will use fewer elements and each one will have to be genuinely good. **Carefree is not cheap.
It is the most expensive finish there is.**

**THE LOW END (after Goodman/Kode9):** One caution from the bass side. R3 retired the standing tension
lane, correctly — but do not read that as "no physical pressure." A bassline that *walks* is a pressure
system; it is just one that **resolves every bar.** Keep the body in it.

**THE ROOM (after Perry):** And leave the room in. A handclap in a real space with people who can hear each
other is the cheapest anti-gloss instrument there is, and it cannot be faked by a plate.

## PANEL C — CONTEXT & MARKETING

**Seat grounding.** VOICE 13 — *Let's Talk About Love* (33⅓, 2007); the study of taste and critical
contempt for mass pleasure. VOICE 14 — *It's Complicated*; networked publics and context collapse.
VOICE 15 — the published reporting on platform economics and playlist culture. VOICE 16 — *Uproot*; how
music actually circulates. VOICE 17 — Reber, Schwarz & Winkielman, *"Processing Fluency and Aesthetic
Pleasure"* (2004). VOICE 18 — *Capitalist Realism*, *Ghosts of My Life*; compulsory enjoyment and retro
pastiche. Tuned high-neuroticism, low-agreeableness.

**PLEASURE APOLOGIST (after Wilson):** There is a live instruction in the brief this room keeps skipping.
The word is **"bimbo."** Read as taste it is an insult; read as craft it is exact and it is the most useful
sentence in the commission. **The bimbo register is the register that refuses to signal intelligence.** It
doesn't audition. It doesn't leave a clever thing at the front of the song so you know a smart person made
it. **It is not trying to impress you, and that is why it reaches you.**

**FLUENCY SCIENTIST (after Reber):** *Let me check that against the mechanism, because it resolves the
Hyper-Skeptic's objection and the brief at once.* From the processing-fluency literature: ease of
processing is itself hedonically positive, and the perceiver **misattributes it to the object.** The house
doctrine already moves complexity out of the lyric and into the music. Correct — but it assumes **musical
complexity is automatically free. It is not.** It is free **only while it is felt and not noticed.** The
moment a device is *remarked* — *that was clever* — it has recruited conscious attention and costs what a
lyric puzzle costs. ⭐ **The channel was never the variable. Attention was.**

**⚡ CANCELLED FUTURE (after Fisher):** *Wait.* You are proposing a 2026 artist solve her problem by
relocating to 1963 and calling the retrieval a decision. **Cheerful music with sad words is the most
exhausted device in popular music.** And your measured 1.39× is a **nostalgia premium**, not aesthetic
evidence.

**PLEASURE APOLOGIST (after Wilson):** *"They only like it because it's familiar"* is not an analysis; it
is a way of not accounting for the liking. And nobody proposed a pastiche record — Panel B just banned the
mix-only seam precisely to stop that.

**⚡ CANCELLED FUTURE (after Fisher):** Then take my falsification instead, and I want it minuted. **If the
catalogue ends up holding a run of songs whose named-wolf lines are interchangeable — swap the dark line
from track 2 into track 5 and lose nothing — this was a formula, not a repair.**

**NETWORKED PUBLIC (after boyd):** And I will extend it, because the predecessor run **already failed a
version of this and not where you pointed the test.** The wolves passed across pairs; **the endings did
not.** Ten of twenty-four landed in the same kitchen. ⭐ **A falsification test only protects the thing it
names**, so the formula moved to the resolution, where nothing was watching.

**PLATFORM (after Pelly):** Which is a distribution fact, not only a craft one. A catalogue where every
song resolves in the same room is a catalogue that reads as one mood to any recommender and to any human
scrolling a profile. **Six songs, six worlds, or it is one song six times with different drums.**

**CIRCULATION (after Clayton):** Then assign the world like you assign the bed. The genre-bed block already
says *what is not assigned gets defaulted, and the default is the strongest prior.* **You proved the rule
is general. Now use it.**

## BRIDGE — five distant domains, one convergent move

**THE ONE WHO SWEEPS IT AWAY** *(Tibetan sand-mandala practice, role-anchored — no individual named)*:
Every seat here has the same correction. **You have all written the tide as something that ARRIVES.** In
these practices **the makers destroy it themselves, on purpose, and that is the least sad part of the day.**

**THE SET BUILDER** *(role-anchored, theatre)*: Strike night is a party. Genuinely. Loud, everyone's there,
best night of the run. **Nobody has ever cried at a strike.**

**THE CHALK PAVEMENT ARTIST** *(role-anchored)*: And I do not *tolerate* the rain — **the rain is what lets
me work tomorrow.** A permanent chalk drawing is a problem.

**⚡ PLEASURE POLITICIAN** *(after Ellen Willis, *Beginning to See the Light*, 1981)*: *Wait.* You have
talked yourselves into a second cliché worse than the first. **"Loss is secretly fine" is a consolation,
and consolation is pleasure doing a job.** The enjoyment has to be allowed to be pointless.

**THE ICE COOK** *(after Grant Achatz)*: Kitchen terms resolve it. **The dish is not good BECAUSE it
melts.** It is good, and it also melts. Two true facts, neither the reason for the other. The ninety
seconds is a **constraint on the craft**, not a meaning attached to it.

**⚡ PLEASURE POLITICIAN (after Willis):** Accepted — that is the version I can defend. And keep her
**wanting things.** A woman with an appetite in public is the most carefree thing pop can show, and it does
not need a wolf attached to be worth a track.

## REFLECT — the opposite foundational assumption: *the maker does NOT know the hour, and it is NOT fine*

**THE ONE WHO WAS NOT TOLD** *(role-anchored)*: Your seed rests on the hour being **published**. That is a
privilege. Most things are taken without a timetable, and a record celebrating knowing-in-advance is
quietly a record about having been informed.

**THE RESTORER** *(after Cesare Brandi, *Teoria del Restauro*)*: Book a cost rather than argue it away. You
have made a rule that the song comes back somewhere warmer. **Some things do not. A form that always
resolves warm cannot say so.**

**THE ARCHIVIST OF THE UNPHOTOGRAPHED** *(role-anchored)*: You have chosen the carvers who could afford a
photographer. The ones who could not are not in the archive, and your seed will silently celebrate the
documented.
