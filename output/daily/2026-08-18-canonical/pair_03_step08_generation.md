## DRAFT SELF-FLAGS CARRIED INTO STEP 09

*(written at draft time, before the sweeps ran — the sweeps found more)*

1. V1 v1 *"it has never let anybody down"* — I have written this line before, in the predecessor run's P03.
2. V1 outro and V3 outro both close on *"I hold it up and he sees it."* — **the return-swap test fails
   inside my own four before it is even run.**
3. V2 chorus *"Nobody is coming."* — I put a pursuer in the room by denying one.
4. V2 v4 *"Get it done before the battery goes."* — a clock. It arrived by reflex, which is the point.
5. V2 seam *"I am the only still thing here."* — an aphorism, and it leaks V1's radiant across songs.
6. V3 chorus *"It's wrong and that's what makes it good."* — ⛔ RULING 16, in the hook, four times.
7. V3 v4 *"Nothing about this is a mistake, and nothing about it is on purpose."* — the ruling stated as a
   maxim. THE REMARK TEST, failed on purpose to see how it sounded. It sounds clever.
8. V3 v1 *"the train in it twice"* — a second number in a song that already has *three feet*.
9. V4 v3 *"Everything's an instrument if you're rude enough about it."* — the whole concept as a slogan.
10. V4 outro *"The last train comes in behind him. The doors go and he is still holding it."* — *last* is a
    clock word, and the ending costs a stranger his train.
11. Style fields: V1 is over the 1000-character ceiling and V3 contains *"with no click anywhere near the
    players"* — ⛔ **a negation in the STYLE field**, which Suno reads as a positive token.
