## 1. THE POSITION — what this concept *is*

> **She did not make this. It is good. She says exactly which part.**

C04 is **FACET 4 — THE ADMIRER**, and step 01 states the load-bearing thing about it in one clause:
*"this facet carries no wolf by construction — nothing is at stake for her, and that is the point."*

This is **S.O.L.A.R.'s R — Ruthless Specificity — promoted from a craft rule to a whole subject.** Sunna's
documented short-form format is literally called **"WHICH BAR"**: she names the exact bar of somebody
else's work that is good and says why, with no irony. The concept takes that format off the phone and puts
a woman in a chair in a room where the work is being made in front of her, *live, badly, repeatedly,
by people who are not being paid.*

**The position in one sentence a stranger could retell:** *a woman sits in a community hall on a Wednesday
watching a brass band she has nothing to do with, and tells you the exact thing they do that is good.*

### What the position is NOT — the four failure states, named before drafting
| Failure | What it would look like | Why it kills the concept |
|---|---|---|
| **Irony** | "so bad it's good", a guilty-pleasure frame, a joke with a butt | `sunna.yaml` **THE REFUSAL**: she will not be ironic about something someone loves. This is the one refusal that is *older than R3* and it is absolute |
| **Condescension** | admiring them *for amateurs* — "for a village band, honestly not bad" | Turns admiration into a verdict from above. She is in the chair, not on the stage |
| **Envy** | the skill she lacks read as a wound; wanting to be up there | Converts FACET 4 into FACET 1 with a grudge. The song becomes about her |
| **Elegy** | the hall will close, the band is dying out, nobody comes any more | ⛔ **THE MOURNER does not exist in this run** (step 01). That is Lofn's seat and it is shut today |

⭐ **The sharpest of the four is ENVY, and it is the one a good writer walks into**, because envy is the
only one that *feels* like depth. A wolf in this pair would arrive dressed as envy: *she's watching other
people do the thing.* **That converts admiration into need, and admiration that needs something is not
admiration, it is an audition.** The whole reason the slot exists is to prove Sunna can look at somebody
else's good thing with **no invoice attached** (FLAIR 13, `APPETITE WITHOUT AN INVOICE`).

## 2. WHAT THE BODY DOES

The setting assignment gives her **a paper cup and nothing else** — which means her hands are the
instrument the writing has to play. She is not carving, mixing, repairing or driving. **She is the only
person in the room with nothing to do**, and the catalogue form is the direct consequence: *a person with
free hands and full attention makes a list.*

**Her physical vocabulary, in order of increasing commitment — this is the song's arc:**

1. **Sitting still, holding the cup.** Attention only. Nothing crosses from her to them.
2. **Counting on the cup with a thumb.** The first leak — the body starts joining in privately.
3. **Leaning.** Chair legs, elbows on knees, head forward. Sunna's *Load the Floor* in a plastic chair.
4. ⭐ **Putting the cup down.** The hinge of the entire pair. Both hands free is a decision.
5. ⭐⭐ **Doing something across the seam** — clapping where he plays, moving down the side, turning the
   chair round, putting the coat back. **The body stops being an audience.**
6. **Staying.** The return: she is still there for the second half.

⭐ **THAT LADDER IS THE TURN** (GOLDEN MOVE 3) **and it is the wolf-free song's honest substitute for one.**
The opening stance is *I have nothing to do with any of it.* The song contradicts it — **without a shadow,
without a cost, without a bittersweet line** — by having her hands quietly defect while her mouth keeps
saying she is only watching. The complication is **participation**, not danger.

## 3. WHAT THE ROOM CONTAINS

**Assigned (R3.1, mine alone, no other pair may use it):**
> a community hall · Wednesday evening · strip-light, rain outside · somebody else's brass band · a paper
> cup, nothing else

**The inventory the four songs draw the catalogue from** — specific, ordinary, and none of it domestic:

* **Fabric of the room:** stacking plastic chairs, a parquet floor with tape marks on it, a hatch with a
  shutter down, a noticeboard, a hall with a name on the wall it did not always have, strip-light with an
  audible hum, rain on a flat roof, coats still on, the side door, the bins by the side door.
* **The band's own furniture:** music stands, a conductor's pencil, a bass drum with a name painted on it,
  a page turned with an elbow, folders, cases open on the floor, spare valve oil, a dog under a table.
* **The bodies:** a conductor who taps rather than shouts, four cornets breathing together, a tenor-horn
  player watching his own hands, the youngest person in the room on second cornet counting rests, a
  euphonium soloist, one player who is fractionally behind everybody, somebody's kid asleep in a coat.
* **In her hands:** the paper cup. ⛔ **Squash or nothing in it — never a hot drink.** The R3.1 ban is on
  the whole domestic-warm register and it is the trap nearest my room: a cup is one wrong noun from a mug.

### ⭐ THE ROOM'S LOAD-BEARING FACT — why this room and not a gig
**Nobody in it is being paid, nobody is watching except her, and they are going to do it again next week.**
That is the seed — *people kept making things anyway* — with the tide taken out and nothing put in its
place. The Atlantic City carvers had a photographer; **this band has one woman in a plastic chair, and she
is doing the same job the photographer did.** FLAIR 2, `COMMISSIONED SURVIVAL`, arrives here as a person
rather than a transaction: she is the record.

### ⛔ THE ROOM'S TWO TRAPS
1. **The strip-light and the rain are texture, never weather-as-feeling.** Rot point 1 generalised: the
   rain gets a sound and no adjectives. It goes on the roof like a brush and it does **not** appear in any
   closing line. *(Sweep run in step 09.)*
2. **A brass band has no saxophone.** True, specific, and it is the seam's whole engine — see below.

## 4. THE SEAM — instruments and people, never settings

**The world (modern, the record):** street-band pop — sousaphone walking the bass melody front of mix,
snare and bass drum carried on straps, **two trumpets** stabbing short and dry, clean guitar chopping
sixteenths as percussion, handclaps and tambourine dead centre, a whole hall shouting the answer half.

**The intrusion (1979 two-tone unison horn line), once:** ⭐ **a trombone and an alto saxophone playing one
line together in unison, hard on the upbeat**, with a **Hammond organ shuffle** under it and the snare
switching to a **rimshot**. **Trombone, alto saxophone and organ appear nowhere else in the record** —
not in the style field, not in the lyric, not in any other section.

⭐⭐ **AND IT IS DIEGETICALLY TRUE, WHICH IS WHY IT WILL SURVIVE THE GENERATOR.** The British brass band
has a fixed instrumentation and **a saxophone is not in it.** So the seam is not a mix change and not a
metaphor: *somebody's mate turns up at the side door with an alto, the trombone doubles him in unison, and
for eight bars the room stops being a brass band and becomes a horn section from another decade.* A player
who is not otherwise in the record, an instrument that cannot be there, and a performance a modern session
would not produce — **all three of Panel B's ruling 7 tests, satisfied by one true fact about the room.**

**And she does something across it — a different action in each of the four:**
| | Action that begins before the seam and completes after it |
|---|---|
| V1 | puts the cup on the floor and claps **behind** the beat, where he plays, and stays there |
| V2 | gets up and moves down the side of the hall to where she can see the valves |
| V3 | turns her chair right round to face them |
| V4 | takes her coat off the back of the chair — and puts it back |

⚠️ **THE POLITICAL LINEAGE IS NOT BORROWED.** Two-tone is a real movement with real stakes. This pair
takes **the sound** — unison horns on the upbeat, organ, rimshot — and takes **none of the stakes**, because
a song about a Wednesday rehearsal has not earned them. The lineage is paid where lineage is paid: **in the
dedication, pointing upstream** (step 10). ⛔ **Never in the generator prompt.**

## 5. THE FOUR VARIATIONS AS FOUR DIFFERENT ADMIRATIONS

The angles are given in the isolated brief; what step 06 fixes is **what kind of good thing each one
admires**, so that four songs about admiration are not one song about admiration.

| | Angle | The admired thing is a … | Radiant (place she keeps coming back to) | The one number |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** | THE ONE WHO IS FRACTIONALLY LATE | **flaw that is better than the correct version** | *"I come back to this hall every Wednesday I am free."* | **bar nineteen** |
| **V2** | SECOND CORNET | **invisible discipline** — the unglamorous job done perfectly | *"I know which chair in here does not wobble."* | **ninety bars of rest** |
| **V3** | THE BIT SHE CANNOT DO | **limit** — a skill she does not have and is not acquiring | *"I have been coming here since it was called something else."* | **twice, identical** |
| **V4** | THE SECOND HALF | **decision** — the admiration is what makes her stay | *"I have got my own way in here, round the side, past the bins."* | **twenty past** |

⭐ **Four different objects of awe: a flaw, a discipline, a limit, a decision.** If the catalogue items were
the only difference the four songs would be one song with the nouns swapped — which is the exact defect
R3.1 was promoted to catch.

⚠️ **V3 IS THE ONE UNDER GUARD.** A limit is one degree from a lack, and a lack is a shadow, and a shadow
in this pair is a gate failure. **The limit stays a fact she is delighted by** — *"I could not do that on my
best day"* said in exactly the voice that named the bar — and it is **never** followed by a line about
wanting to. Verified again in step 09.
