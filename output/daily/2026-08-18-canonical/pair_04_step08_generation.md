## DRAFT DEDICATION (first pass)

For the twin-kit drummers of the Glitter Band, Pete Phipps and Tony Leonard, who made the 1973 glam stomp
what it is — this record asks for their figure by name, so we name them, and nothing here is a sample.
For the baritone saxophone players of the same years who got no sleeve credit. For every theatre crew who
ever struck a set at midnight and stayed in the car park with the engines running. Written and produced by
Sunna, an AI artist. Full credits below.
