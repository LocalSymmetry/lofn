## 1. WHAT THE POSITION *IS*

**The news fact (F19 #3):** *"Using the railway network as a flatbed scanner."*

A flatbed scanner works by holding the page still and walking a thin bar of light across it. **Reverse the
two.** Hold the bar still, and let something enormous walk past it. The railway does the walking. What
comes out is not a photograph and not a scan — it is a strip, the length of the thing that moved, in which
time is laid out sideways as distance.

**The position, in one sentence:** *she is using a machine exactly wrong, on purpose, at night, and what
comes out is a thing the correct use could never have made.*

**What it is NOT — the three collapses this facet is one step away from, all refused here:**

| Collapse | Why it is wrong for C05 |
|---|---|
| **Rebellion posture** | *"They can't tell me what to do."* She is not sticking it to anybody. **The manual happens to say no and she is happy.** Posture needs an oppressor; this room has a signalman who waves. |
| **The lesson** | *"Everything's an instrument if you're brave enough."* An aphorism is the REMARK TEST failing out loud. **Every line in these songs is a thing in the world**, never a thing about the world. |
| **Good BECAUSE wrong** | ⛔ RULING 16. Misuse and beauty are **two adjacent facts with no connective.** *It came out wrong. I'm keeping it.* Two sentences. Never one sentence with a *because* in the middle. |

⭐ **THE PARADOX THIS PAIR HAS TO SURVIVE, NAMED UP FRONT.** The concept is *about* cleverness, so the
song must never itself be clever. **The cleverness belongs to HER, inside the situation, where the listener
watches somebody do it** — never to the writing, where the listener catches somebody doing it. Depth lives
in the situation. A listener will not decode a sentence; they will always recognise a situation, and
**everybody has used a thing wrong and got away with it.**

## 2. WHAT THE BODY DOES

The whole song is a **body holding still while something enormous moves past it.** That is the physical
fact underneath all four variations, and it is why this concept was given a **procedural** architecture:
what she does is a sequence of small manual actions with a result at the end of each.

* **Gets low.** Knees down, elbows on cold concrete, both forearms flat. The strut in the arrangement is
  the opposite of what the body is doing — the band walks, she is planted.
* **Holds.** Not tenses — *holds.* Locked elbows, breath let out, a grin she cannot get rid of.
* **Counts out loud.** Carriages, windows. Counting is what a delighted person does with their mouth when
  their hands are busy.
* **Pulls.** The strip comes out warm and long and curling, and she pulls it through both hands, arms
  wide, and it keeps coming.
* **Holds it up.** Over her head, at arm's length, across the tracks. **Every variation ends with her arms
  above her head showing a stranger a piece of paper.**

⭐ **The hands are always full.** In all four songs there is a physical object in her hands at every moment
— kit, battery, tape, glove, the bar itself, the warm strip. Nothing in this pair is contemplated; it is
handled.

## 5. THE RADIANT KIND — **an instruction she ignored**

One origin, stated **once**, plainly, in **one line**, **arriving late**, **not the title**, **never
explained afterwards**. ⛔ No relative is the origin of anything in this pair.

| | The radiant line | Everything that traces back to it |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | *The manual says hold everything still.* | the planted elbows · the train doing the walking · the bend where the bend was · the stretch where it slowed |
| **V2** | *It says on the back of the box: indoor use only.* | the platform · the night · the cold on the glass · the sodium in the picture · the sleeve-smear |
| **V3** | *There is a line in the book that says let it finish.* | the train inside the train · the same door over the top of itself · the man reading twice · the long bend |
| **V4** | *The label on the strap says single user only.* | two people holding one strip · the far end handed over · him counting out loud · neither of them putting it down |

⭐ **Why four different instructions rather than one repeated:** the registry assigns a *kind*, and a kind
repeated verbatim four times is one radiant with four coats of paint. Each of these is a **different
sentence on a different surface** — a manual, a box, a book, a strap — and each is the true origin of its
own song's scatter.
