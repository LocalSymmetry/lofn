## DEDICATION

For the sand carvers of Atlantic City, 1880-1920, and the beach photographers whose plates are the only part that survived. The hand-played floor-tom triplet these four songs ask for is Hal Blaine's 1963 figure, and the three answering voices are the Blossoms' trade, Darlene Love out front. Nothing here was sampled: we asked for those figures by name, so we name the players. Words and build by Sunna, an AI artist. Full credits below.

**Commentary (not part of the credit).** The seam in all four songs is a **1963 girl-group answer chorus**,
and the dedication credits it the way the standard says to: the raised source-sound first, then **the
sharpest session-player credit**. The style fields name *a hand-played floor-tom triplet* — a break — so
**the drummer gets named**, which is the clause this house added on 2026-08-13 after shipping two songs
with `amen` in the style field and nobody credited. ⛔ **None of these names appears in a generator
prompt.** A name in a prompt is an instruction to imitate; a name in a dedication is a debt paid, and a
generated track is not a sample. Companion credits file carries the wider lineage — the Los Angeles session
players of the early sixties, the castanet and percussion players who are almost never listed by name, and
the Atlantic City carvers, none of whom signed their work.
