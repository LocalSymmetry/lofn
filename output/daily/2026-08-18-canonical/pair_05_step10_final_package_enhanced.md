## Major Deviations

**1. The four EXCLUDE fields are now four different refusals, and V4's is load-bearing.**
Step 10 shipped one 346-char block four times. A shared block is not a refusal, it is a habit: it says the
same thing to four songs that fail in four different ways, and at 346 it sat under the run's 400 floor. I
kept the doctrinal core in all four - `distortion, fuzz guitar, overdrive, punk, screamed vocals,
aggressive delivery` (**zero aggression tokens** is this seat's law, and the fuzz bed measures 0.62x young)
plus `drop-out, hushed vocal, whisper` (THE REGISTER NEVER MOVES) - and then gave each song the refusals
that are true only of it. **V4's tail is the most important text I wrote today.** The container clause's
condition is that *nothing catches the naming*; step 10 asked for that in a section cue (`band does not
change`), which is an instruction to a generator that has every incentive to disobey. So V4 now refuses, by
name, every gesture a model reaches for when a singer says a naked true thing from a stage: `band drop-out
under a spoken line, breakdown before the final chorus, tape stop, reverse cymbal swell, dramatic pause,
rubato, ritardando, a cappella section, solo vocal breakdown, applause sample, crowd cheer, MC
announcement, modulation into the last chorus, final chorus key lift`. **The clause is now protected at the
generator, not only requested in a cue.** The last two - modulation and key lift - also protect the
byte-identical chorus, which is the mechanism itself: a truck-driver's lift into the final chorus would
make the arrangement *notice*, and the naming would stop landing.

**2. The seam instrument is deliberately ABSENT from the Disc_Channel block.**
The obvious move was `[Disc_Texture: 1972_Hohner_Clavinet_| wah_pedal | ...]`. I refused it. A Disc
channel is a **standing address** - it tells the model what lives in that band of the record for the whole
song - and the clavinet is **one bar, played by a guest who leaves**. Declaring it as a channel would have
made the wah the record's persistent texture, dissolved ONE SEAM into a colour, and destroyed the pair's
sonic device (*the instrument that never returns after the seam*) by giving the model a reason to bring
something back. The clavinet stays where a one-bar event belongs: in the style prompt, and in the seam
section's own cue. Same reasoning kept `autowah_envelope_filter` off the Sub channel, where it would have
put a wah on the bass for four minutes and spent the seam in bar one.

**3. Disc_Pad addresses a ROOM, not a pad.**
This record has no pad and must not grow one - a sustained pad is a standing tension lane by another name,
and `sunna.yaml` bans those outright. But the channel slot exists and an empty slot gets defaulted, and
*what is not assigned gets defaulted, and the default is the strongest prior*. So Disc_Pad carries what
this record's ambience actually is: `two_room_mics_high_passed_180Hz | short_dry_plate_1_second |
Stereo_Width_Mid` - straight out of Sunna's own production notes. The slot is filled with the truth so it
cannot be filled with a synth.

**4. Channel ORDER is set per song by which element carries the hook, not by a house template.**
The guide is explicit that earlier channels get more generative weight, so the order is a claim about what
the song is made of. V1 and V3 lead with `Disc_Sub` because in both the riff **is** the bass (a five-note
walking hook; an ostinato with a rest where the fourth note goes - the pair's hole, stated as a positive
address rather than a negation). V2 leads with `Disc_Rhythm` because the handclaps on two and four are the
thing the room takes over. V4 leads with `Disc_Texture` because its hook is the three-note horn-and-bass
unison figure **and** because the electric piano is V4's departing instrument - putting it at weight one
makes its absence in the back half audible instead of merely specified.

**5. V1's style prompt came off the ceiling: 996 -> 979.**
996 hugs the 1000 cap and trips the coordinator's own `hug(prompt >= 985)` flag. I bought the margin out of
grammar, not out of spec: *sitting eight to fourteen milliseconds behind the grid with the bass locked on
it* -> *eight to fourteen milliseconds behind the grid, bass locked on*; *muted upstrokes on the offbeat* ->
*muted offbeat upstrokes*; *from a guest player who enters once and leaves* -> *a guest player in once and
gone*. **Every instruction in the 996 version is still in the 979 version**, including both Source-2 exact
imports, and the same trim discipline brought V2 and V4 clear of 985 as well.

**6. Every prompt now states its density arc as a positive.**
The pair's device is an instrument that leaves and never comes back, and THE GRAIN LAW says a spec that
fights the generator gets smoothed while a spec that runs with it survives - a long full stop is smoothed,
a thickening is kept. So each prompt says where the record is going in the affirmative: *each larger than
the last* (V1), *the room grows underneath* (V2), *thickening to the end* (V3). ⭐ **V4's is the opposite
and it is deliberate: `every chorus arriving at the same size to the end`.** V4 is the song where the
arrangement must refuse to notice, so its prompt asks for a *flat* density across the naming. That is the
container clause written as a production instruction, in positive language, with no negation in the STYLE
field.

**7. `[Theme:]` and `[SONG FORM:]` are written to be structural, not editorial.**
The Theme line names the situation - *the gate is still lit for a company that stopped existing on Friday*
- and never the device. Nothing in either tag mentions the two-column architecture, the rhyme asymmetry, or
the container clause, because THE REMARK TEST does not stop at the lyric sheet: a tag that explains the
trick to the model invites the model to perform the trick.

**8. What I refused to do: I added nothing to the writing, and specifically nothing to the Stage
sections.**
The predecessor run's P05 was flagged for bridge **epigrams** - *"I'm not insulted. I'm appraised."* - and
cleared wrongly. An epigram is what enhancement produces when it has nothing left to fix: the Stage
addresses are four bare situations (*I still say the old name for it* - *This is the departure lounge /
Nothing departs* - *They call it a deletion / So do I* - *I made you a dance record about this*) and every
one of them would take a polish that turned it into an aphorism. Depth lives in the situation, never in the
sentence. **Zero lines added, zero lines cut, zero lines re-pointed across all four songs**, verified by
byte-comparing each lyric body against step 10 before the header block was prepended.
