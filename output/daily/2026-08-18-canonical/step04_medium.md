# STEP 04 — THE MEDIUM
## daily 2026-08-18 · canonical · SUNNA R3 + R3.1

## THE MEDIUM IN ONE LINE

> **A bright, strutting pop record whose rhythm section never once comments on the words.**

## THE FIVE PRODUCTION LAWS OF THIS RUN

### 1. THE STRUT IS THE SWAGGER — never volume, never distortion
* **Walking melodic bass, front of mix** (−2 to 0 dB against the lead vocal). The bass is the second lead
  vocal. **If you can hum the bass, the song has swagger.**
* **Guitar as a percussion instrument** — clean or lightly driven, chopping sixteenths or hitting the
  upstroke. A three-note figure, never a chord wall.
* **Horns that punctuate and leave** — short stabs answering the vocal, in and out inside a bar.
* **Handclaps and tambourine, dry and dead centre.** Drummer **ahead**, not loud.
* ⭐ **LEAVE ONE HOLE.** One place per song where the expected note does not arrive.
* ⛔ **Distortion is what a record reaches for when the groove isn't carrying it.** Measured: the
  fuzz-guitar bed is 0.62× in the young cut.

### 2. THE BAND DOES NOT KNOW
The arrangement plays the good day, straight, start to finish. **No production event may coincide with the
wolf line.** No hush, no drop-out, no half-time turn, no reverb bloom, no instrument leaving to make room
for the meaning. *(DHM — the groove never comments on the lyric.)*

### 3. ⭐⭐ THE SEAM IS INSTRUMENTS AND PEOPLE, NEVER SETTINGS
**THE INVERSION:** the modern genre is the WORLD; the period element is the INTRUSION, **once**, at the
junction that carries the meaning, with the singer **doing something across it**.

⛔ **A seam that exists only as mix adjectives is VOID.** The predecessor run failed here in three of six
pairs: the modern world and the period intruder had **the same instrument list**, separated by *"wide and
dry"* against *"mono with tape slap."* **THE GRAIN LAW says the generator smooths exactly that.**

**A valid seam names at least one of:** an instrument that is not otherwise in the record · a player who is
not otherwise in the record · a performance that a modern session would not produce (three answering
voices, a triplet tom fill, a horn four bars behind and unquantized, castanets, a bass voice).

### 4. SHE SINGS IT
Full chest, a real tune, **stacked harmony as a required instrument** — thirds on the hook, a counter-melody
that argues with the lead, **one moment where two voices split and the room hears the gap open.** Sing-speak
is one colour, not the default. Breath left in, no transparent pitch correction, audible effort.
*Measured (n=72): sung-line count r = +0.538 — the strongest correlate we have; words-per-line r = −0.202.*
**More lines. Shorter lines.**

### 5. THE RIFF EXISTS BEFORE AND AFTER
**THE HUM TEST** — the instrumental hook alone must be recognisable with the vocal muted, and must be
present **before the first word and after the last.** A pad is not a riff; a texture is not a hook.

## TEMPO AND SHAPE
**100–129 BPM**, measured per song from the style field. *(130+ requires a stated reason; 130–149 measures
0.76× and 150+ measures 0.69×.)* **Short — under three minutes wherever the form allows.** One bed held the
whole way, varied by **arrangement density**: instruments walking in and out, never a genre change.

## THE TWO-FIELD PROMPT CONTRACT
* **STYLE field ≤1000 chars**, one dense paragraph, leading **Genre × Subgenre, BPM, key**.
  ⛔ **No negations of any kind in this field** — Suno reads it as positive tokens; *"no synth pads"* asks
  for synth pads. The predecessor caught `absent` doing exactly this.
* **EXCLUDE field** — every refusal lives here, and only here.

## THE FORM ARC (F17, inherited by every pair)
> *"Go to an extreme, move back to a more comfortable place."*

Go all the way out, then come back somewhere warmer — **and the warmer place is assigned per song (R3.1)
and shared with no other pair.** ⛔ *"A more comfortable place" is not "the same kitchen."*
