10. **V3 verse 3** — *"And it's worth it"* **argues the trade** and repairs the seed.
11. **V3 structure** — the **seam lands directly on the heels of the wolf verse**: the band knows.
12. **V4 verse 1** — *"the stair is the song"* is meta and fails **THE REMARK TEST**.
13. **V4 chorus** — *"Twice … twice … twice"* is a **fact recited five times**.
14. **V4 verse 3** — *"I carry my Saturday over this way"* is a **nice line**, which is the problem.
15. **V4 bridge** — *"the second post … the first"* are **ordinals**, i.e. numerals.
16. **All four** — section cues say *"third note **missing**"*; **missing** is inside the absolute-exclusion
    vocabulary and a boundary-matched sweep will land on it.
