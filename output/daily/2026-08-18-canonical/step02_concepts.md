# STEP 02 — THE CONCEPT POOL (12)
## daily 2026-08-18 · canonical · SUNNA R3 + R3.1

**Rule from step 00:** each concept draws one BED (axis 1), one PERIOD INTRUDER (axis 2), one SWAGGER
CARRIER (axis 3), one HOLE (axis 4). **No two shipped concepts may share a bed or an intruder.**
**Rule from R3.1:** each concept declares its **SETTING** (room · hour · weather · bodies · what is in her
hands), its **RADIANT KIND**, and its **RETURN SHAPE**. These are the three axes the predecessor run left
unassigned, and all three collapsed to a default.

---

### C01 — HOUR FIVE · *facet 1, the maker mid-task*
**Setting:** a public beach, five hours in, hard sun, families packing up around her, wet sand to the elbow.
**Bed** modern power-pop · **Intruder** 1963 girl-group answer chorus (three real voices, castanets, a
triplet tom fill) · **Carrier** walking melodic bass front-of-mix · **Hole** the missing third note.
**Wolf** the tide, at a named hour — and it **costs her**: she abandons the north wall to finish the turret.
**Radiant kind** AN ADMISSION ABOUT WHY SHE CAME. **Return** she picks the tools up and walks *along* the
beach, not away from it.

### C02 — THE PHOTOGRAPHER'S PITCH · *facet 2, the one who records it*
**Setting:** the boardwalk edge at midday, a tripod, a queue of strangers, coins in her fist.
**Bed** modern soul-pop · **Intruder** Motown/northern-soul stomp (tambourine on every beat, string stab) ·
**Carrier** handclaps + tambourine dry and centre · **Hole** the chorus that drops the kick.
**Wolf** she has one plate left and two hours of good light — **it costs her**: she chooses whose work
survives. **Radiant kind** A TRANSACTION SHE PAID FOR HERSELF. **Return** the queue re-forms behind her.

### C03 — STRIKE NIGHT · *facet 3, the one who kicks it over*
**Setting:** a theatre at midnight, work-lights, the crew, a crowbar and a bag of screws.
**Bed** modern glam-pop · **Intruder** 1973 glam double-drum stomp + baritone sax · **Carrier** drummer
ahead of the grid · **Hole** the downbeat that never lands.
**Wolf** the get-out is booked for 6am — **it costs her**: she takes the first swing herself.
**Radiant kind** A PERMISSION SHE WAS GIVEN ONCE. **Return** the whole crew in the car park, engines running.

### C04 — WHICH BAR · *facet 4, the admirer* — ⛔ **NO WOLF**
**Setting:** a community hall on a Wednesday evening, plastic chairs, someone else's brass band rehearsing.
**Bed** street-band pop · **Intruder** 1979 two-tone unison horn line · **Carrier** horn stabs that
punctuate and leave · **Hole** the answer phrase left unsung once.
**Wolf** none. Nothing is at stake for her and that is the entire point.
**Radiant kind** A PLACE SHE KEEPS COMING BACK TO. **Return** she stays for the second half.

### C05 — THE WRONG INSTRUMENT · *facet 5, the misuser* — ⛔ **NO WOLF**
**Setting:** a railway platform at night, sodium light, a signalman two hundred yards off, a scanner she
should not have.
**Bed** modern rocksteady-pop · **Intruder** 1967 rocksteady one-drop (real kit, spring reverb, organ
bubble) · **Carrier** offbeat upstroke skank · **Hole** a bar of rest before the last chorus.
**Wolf** none — no clock, no cost, no invoice.
**Radiant kind** AN INSTRUCTION SHE IGNORED. **Return** the picture comes out and she shows a stranger.

### C06 — HIGH VALUE SEGMENT · *facet 6, the one who wasn't told* — ⭐ **INDIGNATION · CONTAINER CLAUSE**
**Setting:** an airport lounge at three in the morning, fluorescent, a dead airline's gate still lit, a
boarding pass for a flight that no longer exists.
**Bed** modern nu-disco · **Intruder** 1972 wah clavinet + tape-flanged organ swell · **Carrier**
call-and-response the room can shout · **Hole** the instrument that never returns after the seam.
**Wolf** ⭐ **inverted** — the schedule was published somewhere she does not look, and **it costs her**: the
thing she agreed to is now owned by someone she never met. **Radiant kind** A DOCUMENT SHE SIGNED STANDING
UP. **Return** ⭐ she names the container wrong **from the stage**, and cues the chorus anyway.

### C07 — ELEVEN MINUTES · *facet 7, the repairer*
**Setting:** a market repair stall on a wet Saturday, awning dripping, a queue of two, a board with a
cracked trace.
**Bed** contemporary funk-pop · **Intruder** Afrobeat interlocking guitar/drum figure · **Carrier** rhythm
guitar as percussion · **Hole** the horn that enters late and stays late.
**Wolf** the part is not made anymore — **it costs her**: she takes the part out of her own machine.
**Radiant kind** A DEBT SHE IS REPAYING TO A STRANGER. **Return** she gives it back working and says nothing.

### C08 — THE STACK · *facet 1 variant*
**Setting:** a back step, four nights running, same hour, cooling air, a phone propped on a brick.
**Bed** widescreen indie · **Intruder** 1966 baroque harpsichord · **Carrier** baritone-sax counter-figure ·
**Hole** the missing third note.
**Wolf** thin — the light goes earlier each night. ⚠️ **Flagged: this is a STATUS REPORT, not an event.**
**Radiant kind** A HABIT SHE DIDN'T NOTICE STARTING. **Return** she leaves the phone running.

### C09 — THE LAUNDERETTE · *facet 4 variant*
**Setting:** a launderette, Sunday, steam, three strangers, a bag of somebody else's washing.
**Bed** modern girl-group pop · **Intruder** 1960s doo-wop bass-voice · **Carrier** call-and-response ·

**Wolf** none. **Radiant kind** A DOOR SOMEBODY LEAVES OPEN. **Return** the dryer stops and nobody moves.

### C10 — THE BATHS · *facet 3 variant*
**Setting:** a municipal swimming baths, last hour, tiled echo, a whistle, wet feet on concrete.
**Bed** carnival pop · **Intruder** brass-band cornet chorus · **Carrier** handclaps + tambourine ·

**Wolf** the baths close for good on the thirty-first — ⚠️ **flagged: status report unless she acts.**

### C11 — THE ALLOTMENT · *facet 1 variant*
**Setting:** an allotment at dawn, frost on the glass, a kettle on a camping stove, seed trays.
**Bed** chamber indie-pop · **Intruder** Wall-of-Sound density (no producer named) · **Carrier** walking

**Wolf** the frost is forecast for tonight — **it costs her**: she lifts what isn't ready.
**Radiant kind** A PLOT NUMBER SHE INHERITED. **Return** she drinks the tea standing up.
⚠️ **Domestic-warm-adjacent — counts against the R3.1 cap.**

### C12 — THE VELOCIPEDE RACE · *facet 5 variant*
**Setting:** a print shop, a timed typesetting race, ink, a crowd of compositors, a stick of type in her
hand. *(F21 — "Twilight of the Velocipede: Typesetting Races before the Age of Linotype.")*
**Bed** modern power-pop *(collides with C01)* · **Intruder** tin-pan-alley upright piano · **Carrier**
drummer ahead of the grid · **Hole** the answer phrase left unsung.
**Wolf** the machine that ends the craft is already ordered. **Radiant kind** A SKILL SOMEBODY TAUGHT HER.
**Return** she sets one more line for fun.
