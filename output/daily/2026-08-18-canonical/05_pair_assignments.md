## ⭐ ONE RUN SHIPS ONE SHAPE — THE FORMAT CONTRACT

*(2026-08-13: three EMO conventions survived a whole run because **the format was never in the spawn
contract.** The predecessor repeated it: three incompatible header conventions and 3 of 6 pairs naming no
emotion at all. It is in the contract now.)*

**Song heading — exactly this, one per variation:**
`# V1 — TITLE IN CAPS` *(no backticks, no bold, no angle name in the heading)*

**Field headings — exactly these three, in this order:**
`### SUNO STYLE PROMPT` · `### SUNO EXCLUDE` · `### SUNO LYRICS`

**Lyrics-field section headers — exactly this shape, every section, no exceptions:**
`[Section Name - EMO:SpecificEmbodiedEmotion - Voice/instrument cue]`
* Section Name is plain (`Intro`, `Chant`, `Verse 1`, `Chorus`, `Seam`, `Outro`) — the architecture lives
  in the writing, not in exotic section labels.
* ⛔ **EMO is mandatory in every header and must be a specific embodied emotion**, never a bare mood:
  `EMO:Gold Heat On The Forearms`, not `EMO:Happy`.
* ⛔ **The EMO tag may never instruct a hush, a drop-out, or reverence.** THE REGISTER NEVER MOVES.

**SFX cues — at least one discrete cue per song**, inline, asterisk-wrapped, on **its own line**:
`*blade taps the flat twice*`. ⛔ **No prose sentences inside the lyrics field** — the predecessor shipped
stage directions mid-sentence in five songs.

**Dedication:** one `## DEDICATION` section per pair file, the credit as a **single plain paragraph**
immediately under the heading. Commentary about the dedication goes *below* it, after a blank line.
*(The coordinator's extractor reads the first prose paragraph; last run it swallowed the commentary and
fabricated 1518 chars against a true 438.)*

## GATES — every variation, before it is written down as done

**R3 / R3.1 gates:**
- ⛔ **THE REMARK TEST** — would a listener say *"that was clever"*? **If yes, bury it or cut it.**
- ⛔ **NO STANDING TENSION LANE** — no unresolving riser, no sub creeping a semitone, no filter that only
  opens. A tension device **lands and is spent**.
- ⛔ **NO MID-SONG GEAR-CHANGE.** One bed, held; vary by arrangement **density**. **ONE SEAM.**
- ⭐ **THE SEAM IS INSTRUMENTS AND PEOPLE, NEVER SETTINGS.** A seam that exists only as mix adjectives is
  **void**. Name an instrument, a player, or a performance that is not otherwise in the record.
- ⭐ **THE REGISTER NEVER MOVES** — the hard line in exactly the voice that sang the funny one.
- ⭐ **SHE SINGS IT** — full chest, real tune, **stacked harmony required**; sing-speak is one colour.
- ⭐ **THE STRUT** — walking hummable bass, guitar as percussion, horns that punctuate and leave,
  handclaps/tambourine, **leave one hole**. ⛔ Swagger is never distortion.
- ⭐ **THE HUM TEST** — instrumental hook recognisable with the vocal muted, **before the first word and
  after the last**.
- ⭐ **THE FLAT TEST** — say each key line aloud, flatly, to a face.
- ⭐ **THE SETTING AUDIT** — your assigned room only; ⛔ **no kitchen, kettle, toast, chip shop, hot drink
  or domestic interior anywhere in the run**; **sweep your closing lines**.
- ⭐ **THE RADIANT** — late, once, plain, not the title, never explained; ⛔ **no relative as its origin**.
- ⭐ **RETURN-SWAP SELF-TEST** — swap the closing lines between your own four songs. **If nothing breaks,
  you wrote one ending four times.** Report the result.

**Standing gates:**
- **Style field ≤1000 chars** (aim 850–1000), two-field. ⛔ **No negations of any kind in the STYLE field** —
  Suno reads it as positive tokens; *"no synth pads"* asks for synth pads. Exclusions go in EXCLUDE only.
- **Lyrics field <5000 chars**, **70–120 sung lines**. *Measured: sung-line count r=+0.538 with like rate;
  words-per-line r=−0.202.* **More lines, shorter lines.**
- **BPM inside 100–129**, and it must appear in the style field so it can be measured per song.
- **One-fact rule** — at most ONE numeric fact sung per song, at the hinge, **spelled in words**.
- **Source 2 floor** — ≥2 phrases imported **EXACTLY**, case-sensitive, mid-sentence.
- **THE RETURN** — rhyme and refrain come back; removal is a debt payable only by naming what returns in
  its place. A byte-identical chorus needs no defence and no note.
- **D8′ ADDRESS THE ROOM, NEVER THE LISTENER** — *can the room shout this back AT the singer without it
  becoming false?*
- **Dedication ≤500 chars, measured in code.** ⛔ If a style field names a break, credit the **player**
  (amen → Gregory C. Coleman / The Winstons); sweep with **word boundaries** (`amen` is inside `ornament`).
  ⛔ Credits belong in the dedication, **never in the generator prompt**. A generated track is not a sample.
- **Absolute exclusion** — no boat, ferry, capsizing, rescue, lake, drowning, missing person, body, or
  disaster vocabulary. The water is a scheduled tide on a holiday beach.
- **Distinctiveness** — cross-pair prompt ceiling **0.58**, lyric **0.42** (coordinator-measured).
