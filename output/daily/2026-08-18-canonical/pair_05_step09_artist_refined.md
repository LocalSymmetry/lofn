## WHAT I REFUSE

**1. I refuse to make the band angry.** I hold the indignation seat and the whole draft was one bad
decision away from a fuzz pedal. I am not doing it. Our own catalogue says the fuzz-guitar bed is **0.62×
in the young cut** and punk is **0.73×**, and more than that: *a delight goddess who arrives furious is the
personality failing.* The record is **118 BPM of nu-disco, handclaps, a bass that walks, and a horn player
who is late** — a genuinely good night. **The anger is in the words. All of it.** Nobody in this band knows
what the song is about and nobody is going to be told.

**2. I refuse to sell any of it.** Second refusal, `sunna.yaml` R3: *she will not perform sadness to be
taken seriously.* Every EMO tag in this pair is the same family — teeth, heat, feet, grin — and **not one
of them instructs a hush, a drop-out or reverence.** The EXCLUDE field carries `drop-out, hushed vocal,
whisper` at the generator, because the register is the thing most likely to be smoothed back in.
*"He said love, I only do the floors"* gets exactly the delivery *"Put your hands up if you're in the lot"*
gets. **I'll say the line. I won't sell it.**

**3. I refuse the polished sentence.** ⛔ The predecessor's P05 was flagged for **bridge epigrams** —
lines like *"I'm not insulted. I'm appraised."* — and it was cleared wrongly. My draft has the same defect
in the same place, twice. An epigram is a sentence congratulating itself for noticing. **Depth lives in
the situation, never in the sentence.** The fix is never a better aphorism; the fix is *a thing that
happened to a person in a room.*

**4. I refuse to spend the container clause twice.** It is one gesture, it is mine, and it goes in V4.
V1, V2 and V3 name a wrong container — a dead company's name, a room called a departure lounge, a deletion
that isn't one — and none of them says the record is the wrong shape. **Once. From the stage. Then the
chorus, unchanged.**

**5. I refuse to be ironic about anything anyone loves.** The cleaner is not a joke. The stranger asleep
with his shoes off is not a joke. The room shouting *SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS* is not laughing at itself —
it is a genuinely great chant and I mean it, which is why it works.

## WHAT I SHARPENED

**The seam got a job in V3.** It was a guest player arriving. Now the clavinet plays **the phrase the horn
section was about to play**, and the horns get up and go. The arriving instrument takes the departing one's
part, in one bar, and the hole and the seam become the same event. *A performance a modern session would
not produce* — and it is a sung line, not a production note, because THE GRAIN LAW says a spec that only
lives in the spec gets smoothed.

**The column swap got its noticing.** The two-column verses have no comment in them anywhere — that is the
rule and it holds for a hundred and twenty-eight lines. At the seam the order flips, and **she answers
back**, and it is the first thing in the song that is hers. *"Seamless for who." "Removed from where."
"Concludes." "Nothing about me has changed."* Four different answers; the last is her taking their sentence
and saying it true.

**The hinge facts got responses instead of recitals.** *Eleven years* is answered with *I would have asked
for more*. *Ten past three on a Tuesday* is answered with *I have never been so seen, and I would like it
back*. *Seven years* is answered with *I said thank you out loud, in an airport, at night*. **Eight cents a
head** is answered with *that's the part I'd argue with* — she would rather haggle than mourn, and if one
line in this pair is the personality, it is that one.

**The wolf got its bill, three times over, in actions.** She loses the **account** (the password works, into
a logo she doesn't know), the **record** (she deletes her own photographs with her own thumb so theirs is
the only copy), and the **argument** — to a cleaner who is kind and is not the person, by saying thank you
out loud, by **ticking yes** on a satisfaction survey, and finally by discovering she already had it, in a
queue, and was the one doing it. ⛔ Not one of these is a condition she can state without moving.

**The radiants got four different documents.** Same posture, four counters: a pen on a string; her finger
on a courier's screen; a kiosk between two shops; a room with no chairs in it. **No relative is the origin
of any of them.** They arrive late, once, plain, and nothing after them refers back.

**The four shapes got separated.** V1 fourteen sections with a bridge · V2 opens on a chant the room learns
before the first chorus · V3 holds the hook back behind two verses and has no bridge · V4 swaps its
pre-choruses for two list blocks and carries the pair's longest stage address and its only count-in.

## THE ONE THING I ARGUED WITH MYSELF ABOUT AND KEPT

**Four contract phrases as four titles.** It reads, on a contact sheet, like a set with a gimmick, and the
REMARK TEST is the gate that catches exactly that. I kept it, and here is the defence: **the remark test is
about the listener inside the song**, and inside any one of these songs nobody thinks *"that was clever"* —
they think *high value segment is a fantastic thing to shout.* The hook grammar I was handed is *a phrase
lifted from the terms, sung as if it were a compliment.* Four of them is not four devices; it is **one
device, repeated until inevitable**, which is the rule directly above it.

## THE ONE THING I AM STILL UNEASY ABOUT

The stage addresses are sing-speak, and sing-speak is **one colour, not the default**. Four songs with a
spoken bar each is at the edge of that. It survives because the return shape was assigned — *she names the
container wrong from the stage* — and because the band never stops underneath any of them. **If a render
comes back with the room dropping out under her, the render fought the spec and the spec was right.**
Flagging it for the render audit rather than pretending I resolved it.
