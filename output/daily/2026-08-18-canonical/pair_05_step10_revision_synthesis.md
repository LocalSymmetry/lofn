## RETURN-SWAP SELF-TEST — result

Swapping the closing stage-address and outro between my own four songs **breaks every pairing**, and each
break is a different kind:

* **V4 → V1 / V2 / V3:** the container clause gets spent in a song that does not hold it, so the pair
  spends it twice — a gate failure — and *"I made you a dance record about this"* has no filing, no lot and
  no line item to be about.
* **V1 / V2 / V3 → V4:** the clause goes **unspent**, V4's assigned angle (THE CONTAINER, NAMED) collapses,
  and the last two lines of the record — the one rhymed *true* line, `told` / `sold` — pay off nothing.
* **V2 ↔ V3:** V2's return names a **room** (*this is the departure lounge, nothing departs*); V3's names
  an **act** (*they call it a deletion, so do I*). V3 has never established the room as the problem and V2
  has filed no request, so each import lands on an antecedent that does not exist.
* **V1 ↔ V2:** V1's return depends on a **company name that is gone**; V2's on a **room that is lying**.
  Swap them and V1 has never named the buyer and V2 has no old name to keep using.

**Conclusion: four endings, not one ending four times.** Confirmed by hand and by the closing-line sweep —
final sung lines are `Hey` / `Say it like a prize` / `And they did` / `Successors and assigns`, and the four
stage sign-offs are `Come on —` / `Here it comes —` / `Say it with me —` / `Two, three, four —`.

## ⭐⭐ THE CONTAINER CLAUSE — where the naming is audible in the WRITING

1. **The sentence exists on the page.** V4, from the stage, plainly, in the voice that sang the joke:
   *"I made you a dance record about this / **That is the wrong container for it** / I know exactly what it
   is / I'm doing it anyway."* Nothing is inferred.
2. **The arrangement refuses to notice, and the writing proves it.** The chorus after the naming is
   **byte-identical** to the five before it, the cue reads *band does not change*, and `drop-out, hushed
   vocal, whisper` are excluded at the generator. ⭐ **The naming lands because nothing catches it** —
   which is the clause's own condition: audible in the writing, not in the production.
3. **The one rhymed TRUE line in the pair is the last line of the record.** Across four songs and sixteen
   two-column verses the *told* column rhymes and the *true* column never does. In V4's outro the told line
   returns verbatim from verse one — *"They said the purpose stays as told"* — and the true line rhymes
   with it: *"I'm between the tugs and the seats, and I'm sold."* The fact finally speaks in the official
   language, once, and nothing explains it afterwards.
