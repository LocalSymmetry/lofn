## 1. THE POSITION — what facet 7 actually *is*

**She is fixing the thing everybody said to replace, and she is enjoying it.**

That is the whole position and it has to survive without a single sentence of justification. She is not
making a point about waste, or repair culture, or the right to fix. She is not sad about the machine. She
is **absorbed** — the trace is a hair wide, the copper comes up bright under the blade, somebody's hands
put every one of these parts here on purpose, and she is having an excellent Saturday in the rain.

**The awe is at the object and at the labour inside it**, not at its ending. `step01` is exact about the
danger: *"the wolf here must cost her something, or it collapses into FACET 1 with a status report
attached."*

### ⛔ THE THREE THINGS THIS POSITION IS NOT

1. **It is not THE MOURNER.** There is no seat in this run from which the loss is the subject
   (`step01`, the facet that does not exist). If any of my four songs arrives at *and then it was gone and
   that was sad*, it has left the essence.
2. ⛔ **It is not "precious because it is dying."** BRIDGE ruling 16, and my sharpest named trap.
   The repair is good. The thing is also end-of-life. **Two facts, adjacent, no connective between them.**
   A line that welds them is a repair, not a lyric.
3. ⛔ **It is not "the limit is secretly a result."** *"Eleven minutes is a result"* is already one step
   into meaning-making — it is the consolation move dressed as toughness. The assigned hook grammar is
   **a tautology**, and a tautology is stronger precisely because it **refuses to let the limit mean
   anything**: *eleven minutes is eleven minutes.* Nothing is recovered. Nothing is redeemed. She hands it
   back and calls the next person up.

## 2. WHAT THE HANDS DO

The position is legible entirely through work, and the work is small, hot and precise. **She is busy from
the first line to the last** — which is what makes it impossible to sentimentalise.

| | The action | What it is made of |
|---|---|---|
| **Sight** | the lamp swung round on its arm; green lacquer scraped back to bright copper; a fault the width of a hair | close focus, one square inch, everything else out of frame |
| **Heat** | the iron at temperature since the shutter went up; flux smoke going up through the hole in the awning canvas | forearm heat, the smell, the tip wiped on the sponge |
| **Touch** | thumbnail under a ribbon cable; a screw she can feel is the wrong one before she can see it | fingertips doing the reading |
| **Sound** | the iron tapped twice on the bench; rain stepping up on the canvas; the fan spinning up when it comes back on | the room, live, with people in it |
| **The give** | it goes into somebody's hands, lid down, light on | the last physical act of every song |

⭐ **She is never once idle and never once alone.** The assigned architecture is **call-and-response
throughout** — every line she says is answered, by the market room or by a second voice, for the entire
song. There is no solo verse anywhere in this pair. **The room finishes her sentences**, which is also how
the assigned chain rhyme is carried (see §5).

## 4. THE FOUR WOLF EVENTS — ⛔ FOUR EVENTS, FOUR COSTS

**The pair wolf:** *the part is not made anymore, and she takes the part out of her own machine.*
**The pair's assigned cost is that action.**

⛔ **THE MEASURED FAILURE I AM WRITING AGAINST.** The predecessor's P06 named three wolves —
*"Support ended in March"* / *"They printed the last date on the box"* / *"Manual says end of life"* —
**one fact in three phrasings, all status reports.** Nothing in any of those songs moved because of them.
Only the fourth, where she took a part out of her own machine, cost her anything at all.

So this pair fields **four different events with four different prices**, and the price is paid **on stage,
in the song, by her**:

| | The event (a thing that happens, with an hour and an object) | ⭐ What it costs her |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | the last one of that part on the strip in the drawer goes into a stranger's board | **the drawer is empty after.** The next person who comes in with this fault gets nothing, and she knows it while she is soldering |
| **V2** | the third call says he has one; she pulls the shutter down at mid-day and drives out for it, and it is the wrong revision | **the market day, the takings, and her own money** — paid before she saw it, and she does not ask for it back |
| **V3** | she opens **her own machine** on the same bench and lifts the working part out of it | **hers is the one that will not wake now.** Seven years of it, on the bench, open, staying open |
| **V4** | she writes *cleaned the contacts* on the ticket and prices it at labour | **the credit, permanently** — he will never know what is in it, and she has made sure of that |

⭐ **THE TEST, APPLIED:** can she say the line without moving? V1 — the part leaves the drawer. V2 — the
shutter comes down. V3 — her lid comes off. V4 — the pen goes on the ticket. **Four actions, four
subtractions.** *(Swap results reported in step 10.)*

⚠️ **And the naming stays flat.** *"Nobody makes them now"* is said in exactly the voice that named the
burn-hole in the awning. No hush, no drop-out, no reverence — **the band does not know**, and the register
never moves.

## 5. THE FOUR STRUCTURAL ORGANS *(assigned to this pair, shared with nobody)*

**SECTION ARCHITECTURE — call-and-response throughout.** No solo verse anywhere. Every line answered.
**She is never alone in the mix**, and that is the argument, not a decoration: a repair stall is a public
counter and the market talks back.

**RHYME / LINE LOGIC — chain rhyme.** The last word of each line begins the next line, continuously.
⭐ **The two organs fuse:** because the room answers every line, and the answer begins with her last word,
**the room is literally finishing her sentences.** The chain runs *through* the call and the response
alike, so the device is heard as a market talking, never as a trick — which is the REMARK TEST passed by
construction rather than by luck.
⭐ **And the chain runs into the chorus:** every pre-chorus ends on the chorus's first word, so the
byte-identical chorus is *inside* the chain rather than sitting outside it. **Return is free — spend more
of it.**

**HOOK GRAMMAR — a tautology the room shouts.** Four tautologies, one per song, each refusing a different
denial: *Fixed is fixed* (refuses "it's beyond repair") · *A part is a part* (refuses "discontinued") ·
*Working is working* (refuses "but whose was it") · *The job is the job* (refuses "that was a kindness").
⛔ None of them is a lesson. A tautology **cannot** be a lesson; that is why it was assigned.

**SONIC DEVICE — the horn that enters late and stays late.** The horn section arrives four bars behind the
first chorus and is still four bars behind at the end of the record. It does not catch up. It is the
**hole** — the expected note arriving in the wrong bar, every time, for the whole song. *(Sunna's ally,
the horn player who is always late: `the lateness is the swagger`.)*

## 8. THE SEAM — ⭐ instruments and people, never settings

**The world (modern, dominant, held the whole way):** contemporary funk-pop — dry close-miked kit, thick
round sub-bass, clean rhythm guitar chopping a three-note figure, a walking melodic bass that is the second
lead vocal, handclaps and tambourine dead centre, the late horn section, full-chest female lead with
stacked thirds, and a market room answering every line.

**The intrusion (ONCE, eight bars, at the junction that carries the meaning):** an **Afrobeat interlocking
guitar-and-drum figure** — and it arrives as **people**, not as a plug-in:

* a **second rhythm guitarist** who plays nowhere else on the record, locking a counter-figure against the
  first guitar so the two parts make one pattern neither is playing;
* **two conga players and a shekere**, hand percussion present in no other bar of the song;
* the **drummer pushing the snare off the one** and playing behind it — a placement a quantized modern
  session does not produce.

Then those players step out and do not come back. ⛔ A seam carried by *"wide and dry"* against *"mono with
tape slap"* is **void** and THE GRAIN LAW says the generator smooths it; three of six predecessor pairs
failed exactly there.

### ⭐ AND SHE DOES SOMETHING ACROSS IT
| | The action that happens across the eight bars |
|---|---|
| **V1** | she solders the last one in |
| **V2** | she pulls the shutter down at mid-day and goes |
| **V3** | she lifts the working part out of her own machine |
| **V4** | she puts it in his hands |

⭐ **The seam is ADDITIVE and it is happy.** More players, more hands, more noise — the band getting
fuller and more delighted at the exact moment the thing costs her something. **That is THE BAND DOES NOT
KNOW in its purest available form**, and it is the reason the seam may sit on the action without breaking
the no-production-event-on-the-wolf-line law: **the wolf is *named* in a straight verse with the
arrangement unchanged**, and the seam starts on the line after it, on the work.

⚠️ **Afrobeat is a living lineage with named originators.** The sound is drawn on with lineage, never
captured. ⛔ **No player's name appears in any generator field** — a name in a style field is an
instruction to imitate. The credit is a debt and it is paid **in the dedication**, in step 10, with the
clause attached to **the drummer who built the placement**, not to the bandleader.
