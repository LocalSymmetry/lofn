## Major Deviations

*Where I exercised agency, and why. Every number below is python-measured out of this finished file.*

### 1. ⭐ THE TWO GENERATOR FIELDS WERE DIVIDED, NOT DOUBLED

`vault/DISC_CHANNEL_GUIDE.md` says plainly: with a channel block, **leave the style prompt blank** — "the
channel definitions are more precise than anything you can put there." The MAX configuration keeps both
fields. Those two instructions only reconcile one way. **Two fields describing the same timbres in
different words is not redundancy; it is a contradiction, and the generator resolves a contradiction by
averaging** — which is the precise mechanism THE GRAIN LAW names when it says a spec that fights the
generator gets smoothed.

So I split the labour. **The Disc block declares the standing world** — channel-level timbre and spatial
placement. **The style field declares what only it can say** — bed, tempo, key, the density arc, the
delivery register, the five Source-2 imports, and the seam event. What left the style fields is exactly
what the Disc block now carries more precisely (handclaps dead centre, the room mics, the guitar chopping
sixteenths — a clause becomes a channel). Style fields: **992 / 990 / 993 / 934 → 982 / 923 / 939 / 937**,
all four now clear of the packager's 985 hug line with real headroom, where three of four had none.

### 2. ⭐⛔ THE SEAM IS NOT A DISC CHANNEL, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT

A Disc channel is a **standing** channel: it plays for the length of the record. The seam plays **once**,
for eight bars, two thirds in. Writing the trombone and the alto saxophone into `Disc_Texture` would have
told the generator they are always present — and the intruder smoothed into a standing texture is THE
GRAIN LAW's signature failure and the exact defect that killed three of six predecessor pairs.

So the seam appears in **notation** in exactly two places: once in each style field, tied to "Once only,
two thirds in", and once in each `[Seam]` section header. (It is also *sung* — she says the saxophone out
loud, twice, and that is content, not notation; it is the whole point of the song.) **Measured in this file:** `trombone`, `saxophone`,
`organ`, `rimshot` = **exactly one occurrence each, per style field**, and **zero across all twenty
Disc_Channel lines**. ONE SEAM is now enforced by the notation instead of by hope — and the seam stays what
the brief demands, **an instrument and a player, never a setting**: a British brass band has no saxophone,
and that fact is the engine.

### 3. `Disc_Pad` IS A ROOM HERE, NEVER A SYNTH

The exclude field bans `ambient pad` and `synth pad bed`. A `Disc_Pad` channel loaded with pad-synth tokens
would have contradicted the record's own exclude list **inside the same submission**. The guide's table
gives that channel two documented roles — reverb character *and* room tone — so I took the room: two mics,
a short sweat plate, the crowd bed, the hard floor, and in V1 the rain on a flat roof. **A channel that
argues with the exclude field is an invitation to average.**

### 4. THE NEW TAGS ARE POSITIVE-PHRASED, BECAUSE THEY SIT IN A GENERATOR FIELD

My first `[SONG FORM:]` draft read `Chorus (answer played, not sung)`. That is a **negation inside a field
Suno reads as positive tokens**, and it negates the one event the song cannot afford to lose. It now reads
`Chorus (horns take the answer)` — same instruction, stated as a thing that happens. Same repair on V3's
`[Theme:]`, which lost *"a thing she cannot do and is not going to learn"* for *"open delight at somebody
else's skill, admired from a plastic chair."* **Measured: zero negation tokens across all four style
fields and all four header blocks.**

### 5. ⚠️ V1's ROLLERDISCO IMPORT WAS RESCUED FROM CAPITALISATION DRIFT — BY THE MACHINE, NOT BY EYE

My first V1 draft opened a sentence with *"The distant rollerdisco claps arrive under the last chorus."*
Source 2's floor is **case-sensitive, mid-sentence, no capitalisation drift** — so a capital `T` at
sentence-start silently dropped V1 from **3 exact imports to 2**, one above a floor of 2, invisible to
every reading of the sentence. Re-cast to *"Under the last chorus the distant rollerdisco claps arrive."*
and measured back at 3. **This is the precise failure the clause exists for, and nothing but a counter
could see it.**

### 6. THE EXCLUDES WERE ENRICHED AT THIS PAIR'S OWN FAILURE MODES, NOT PADDED

**435 / 448 / 435 / 449 → 591 / 586 / 587 / 604**, all inside 400–1000. The shared additions each name a
rule this run already owns: `sustained riser` and `beat switch` (⛔ no standing tension lane, ⛔ no
mid-song gear-change), `hard-panned percussion` (THE GRAIN LAW — hard-panned non-musical elements get
smoothed), plus `sampled loop`, `vocal chop`, `scratching`, `four-on-the-floor kick`. The per-variation
tails are each variation's **own** risk: V1 `quantized drums, click-track stiffness` — *a song about a man
who is fractionally late must not be quantized, and nothing in the old exclude list stopped it*; V2 `child
vocal, virtuoso cornet solo` — she is the youngest, not a child, and the entire point is that she is not
featured; V3 `guitar solo, triumphant modulation`; V4 the sample-flip guard below.

### 7. ⚠️ THE V4 QUESTION — **UPHELD ON DOCTRINE, REPAIRED AT THE GENERATOR SURFACE**

**The pair's reading is correct and I uphold it.** The prohibition names a *genre* gear-change — "one song
that is three songs", "varied by arrangement **density** — instruments entering and leaving — **never by
genre**" (`sunna.yaml` pillar 5). A new drum pattern after an interval, **at the same tempo, in the same
key, on the same instruments, played by the same people**, is density. It is also, literally, what a
rehearsal does at twenty past. Two further reasons it is not a second seam: **no instrument and no player
arrives at the interval** — the seam's definition is somebody who is not in the band, and the second half
*is* the band; and it passes **THE REMARK TEST** cold, because nobody has ever said *"that was clever"*
about a band coming back after a break. The pair also pinned it in-field (`identical key and instruments`)
and in EXCLUDE (`mid-song genre change`, `key change`, `tempo change`). **All of that stands.**

**But the pair adjudicated the doctrine and left the generator unattended, and that is where the risk
actually lived.** The phrase names a **sample**. There is no sample in this record — it is forty people in
a hall — and **the pair's own dedication says so in as many words: *"Nothing here is a sample of anyone."***
A style field asking for a sample flip while the dedication asserts there is no sample is an internal
contradiction, and THE GRAIN LAW says the generator resolves it the cheap way: a chopped soul loop arriving
at the interval — which **would** be a mid-song gear-change, whatever the exclude field says. An exclude
token is a weak instrument against a positive token in the style field.

So the exact Source-2 phrase **stays** — the import is mandated, V4 keeps four exact imports, and the
phrase genuinely carries V4's meaning — but it is now pinned on both sides and demoted from a description
of *the band* to a description of *how the band hits*:

> "...then after the interval **the same players return in the same key on the same instruments**, **hitting
> like** a choppier flip of the buttery soul sample to a new drum pattern, **all of it played live in the
> room**, thickening bar by bar until the figure walks out alone."

Three repairs in one sentence: a pin before, `hitting like` turning the phrase into a **simile about
attack** rather than an instruction about **material**, and a pin after. `chopped soul loop`, `turntable`
and `DJ drop` join V4's exclude beside the `tempo change` the pair already had.

**One thing I changed that the pair did not flag.** Their "thickening by the bar" ran from the interval to
the end — monotonic, which is the shape of the **retired standing tension lane** ("a filter that only ever
opens"). It now reads *"thickening bar by bar until the figure walks out alone"*, so the density arc
**spends itself** and the record ends where it started, on the sousaphone alone. A tension device that
lands and is spent is legal; one that only opens is not.
