# ⭐ THE TWO PRE-DRAFT QUESTIONS

*(The step contract: a guide that cannot answer both concretely — **place + stake** — is not ready to
direct. Answered here first, for the pair, before any of the four guides exists.)*

## Q1 — **WHERE IS THE BODY STANDING?**

**Standing at a repair stall on a market row, mid-morning on a wet Saturday, with a hot iron in her right
hand and a cracked board flat on the bench in front of her.**

Concretely, and this is the same square metre in all four songs:

* **Under a canvas awning with a burn-hole in it**, which drips on the same spot on the bench and has
  stained it — she works off the stain like a landmark.
* **Rain on the canvas**, stepping up and easing. The row is quiet because of it. The trade is slow.
* **A task lamp on a hinged arm**, swung round over the board; it is the only light doing any real work.
* **A queue under the awning** — people waiting, close enough to see her hands, close enough to answer her.
  She is not alone in a single bar of this pair.
* **On the bench:** the board with a hair-thin crack across a copper trace, the iron already at
  temperature, a reel of wire, flux, a blade, a strip of parts in a drawer.
* **Her forearms are hot**, her thumbnail is doing the reading, and the smoke goes up through the hole in
  the awning.

⛔ **It is outdoors, public, wet and busy.** Not a kitchen, not a table, not a lamp at home, no hot drink
anywhere. The predecessor's P06 stood at a kitchen table and 10 of 24 songs in that run resolved in the
same room. **This pair never goes indoors, including in its closing lines.**

## Q2 — **WHAT COULD HURT IT HERE?**

**The part is not made anymore, and every route to one costs her something she does not get back.**

Not *"support ended"*. Not *"end of life"*. Those are conditions; she can say them standing still and
nothing in the song moves. Here the hurt is always **a subtraction she performs herself, on stage, with
her hands**:

* **her last one** — a strip in the drawer with one left on it, and after today, none;
* **her day** — the shutter down at mid-day, the queue gone, the takings gone, her own cash gone on a part
  she has not seen;
* ⭐ **her own machine** — open on the same bench, the working part lifted out of it, hers the one that
  will not wake now. Seven years of it, and it stays open;
* **her credit** — *cleaned the contacts* written on a ticket at labour price, so nobody will ever know.

⚠️ **And the second hurt, which is quieter and does not resolve:** the thing she hands back is **still
end-of-life**. It works, and off the wall it holds eleven minutes. ⛔ **Neither of those facts is the
reason for the other.** She does not say it is precious because it is going. She does not say eleven
minutes is a result. **She says eleven minutes is eleven minutes and calls the next person up.**

# GUIDE — V1 · **IRON'S ALREADY HOT**
### angle: `THE CRACKED TRACE` — the board on the bench, the queue, the iron already hot

**THE ONE-SENTENCE SONG.** A hair-thin crack in a copper trace, a hot iron, a queue waiting in the rain,
and the last one of a part that nobody makes going into a stranger's machine.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key / feel** | E dorian, 100 BPM, bright and dry, guitar chopping the upstroke |
| **Hook (tautology)** | **FIXED IS FIXED** — refuses *"it's beyond repair"* to mean anything |
| **Chain word into the chorus** | every pre-chorus ends on **fixed** |
| **The one fact** | **eleven minutes** off the wall, at the moment it comes back on |
| **Wolf event** | the strip in the drawer has one left on it, and it goes in |
| ⭐ **What it costs her** | **the drawer is empty after** — the next person with this fault gets nothing, and she is soldering while she says it |
| **Seam sits at** | the soldering — the second guitar, congas and shekere arrive while she spends her last one |
| **Radiant (late, once)** | *a fella in this market fixed mine for nothing and would not take the money* |
| **Return** | it goes into his hands and she calls the next person up — **the queue moves** |
| **SFX cues** | rain stepping up on the awning; the hot iron tapped twice on the bench |
| **Source 2** | `brightly-colored production` · `a two-beer chill` |

**THE ARC (F17 — go to an extreme, come back somewhere warmer).** The extreme is the drawer: the last one
of a thing nobody makes, spent on a stranger, out loud, flatly, with the band getting *happier* over it.
The warmer place is not warmer because anything is resolved — **it is warmer because the queue moves and
there is another job.** That is the clearing (`ruling 15`): the ending is the condition of the next one.

⚠️ **THE TRAP IN THIS GUIDE.** *Eleven minutes* wants to be dignified. Any line that gives it weight —
*eleven minutes is still eleven minutes we got*, *eleven's a win* — is the meaning-making move and is cut
on sight. **The line is flat: `Eleven minutes is eleven minutes.`** The room shouts it back and the horn
is four bars late over the top of it.

# GUIDE — V2 · **THE THIRD CALL**
### angle: `NOT MADE ANYMORE` — sourcing the discontinued; three phone calls and a wrong answer

**THE ONE-SENTENCE SONG.** The board is fixed except for one part, the part is not made, and she rings
round until somebody says yes and is wrong.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key / feel** | G minor, 100 BPM, bass further forward, guitar sparser, more room in the bars |
| **Hook (tautology)** | **A PART IS A PART** — refuses *"discontinued"* to mean anything |
| **Chain word into the chorus** | every pre-chorus ends on **part** |
| **The one fact** | **three calls**, the third one wrong, just before she shuts the stall |
| **Wolf event** | he says he has one; she pulls the shutter down at mid-day and drives out for it; it is the wrong revision |
| ⭐ **What it costs her** | **the market day, the takings, and her own money** — paid before she saw it, and she does not ask for it back |
| **Seam sits at** | the shutter — the congas and the second guitar arrive as she closes a market stall on a market day |
| **Radiant (late, once)** | *a woman I had never met paid my pitch the week I could not* |
| **Return** | he asks what she found; **she holds the empty box up and puts it down** |
| **SFX cues** | the dial tone cutting off mid-ring; the shutter running down |
| **Source 2** | `the distant rollerdisco claps` · `a two-beer chill` |

**THE ARC.** The extreme is the drive: closing the only trading day of the week on a stranger's word, in
the rain, and coming back with the wrong revision in a box. **Then she makes the wrong one fit** — legs in
the wrong place, legs can move — which is the tautology doing its actual work rather than decorating it.
The warmer place is the bench, the machine on, and a box she never explains.

⚠️ **THE TRAP IN THIS GUIDE.** *"They stopped making it"* is exactly the predecessor's status report and it
will try to be the wolf here because the angle invites it. **The wolf is not the discontinuation. The wolf
is the shutter coming down.** The discontinuation is said in one flat line and is never the event.

# GUIDE — V3 · **OUT OF MINE**
### angle: `OUT OF MINE` — the cost: she opens her own machine and removes the working part

**THE ONE-SENTENCE SONG.** Two machines open on one bench, and the part that makes his work comes out
of hers.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key / feel** | C minor, 100 BPM, thickest arrangement of the four, tambourine riding, claps doubled |
| **Hook (tautology)** | **WORKING IS WORKING** — refuses *"but whose was it"* to mean anything |
| **Chain word into the chorus** | every pre-chorus ends on **working** |
| **The one fact** | **seven years** she has had hers, said as her own lid comes off |
| **Wolf event** | she opens her own machine on the same bench and lifts the working part out |
| ⭐ **What it costs her** | **hers is the one that will not wake now**, and it stays open on the bench |
| **Seam sits at** | the lift — the congas, shekere and second guitar arrive at the exact moment she takes it out of her own |
| **Radiant (late, once)** | *the man who taught me this never once told me his name* |
| **Return** | she shuts her own lid on it and **puts it under the bench** |
| **SFX cues** | her own lid clicking off in one go; a screw dropped in a tin |
| **Source 2** | `lush, aerodynamic beats that instantly hits cruising altitude` · `brightly-colored production` |

**THE ARC.** This is the pair's assigned cost and it is the loudest song of the four, because the band is
having the best morning of the month while she takes her own machine apart. The extreme is the lift.
The warmer place is a very small physical act: **her own thing put away under the bench**, out of sight,
still open, and the next job started.

⚠️ **THE TRAP IN THIS GUIDE.** Sacrifice is the most sentimental shape available and this angle is made of
it. ⛔ **She never says it cost her anything.** No *"I'd do it again"*, no *"it's only a machine"*, no
*"somebody needs it more"*. She says what she is doing while she does it, in the voice she used for the
burn-hole in the awning, and the room shouts the tautology over it. **The cost is visible because it
happens, not because it is announced.**

# GUIDE — V4 · **CLEANED THE CONTACTS**
### angle: `SAYS NOTHING` — the hand-off; the repair as a gift never mentioned to the person receiving it

**THE ONE-SENTENCE SONG.** She gives it back working, prices it at labour, writes four words on the
ticket, and he goes home not knowing.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Key / feel** | D dorian, 100 BPM, sparsest of the four, most space, horn stabs answering into the gaps |
| **Hook (tautology)** | **THE JOB IS THE JOB** — refuses *"that was a kindness"* to mean anything |
| **Chain word into the chorus** | every pre-chorus ends on **job** |
| **The one fact** | **four pound fifty** on the ticket, at the moment she prices it |
| **Wolf event** | the pen goes on the ticket: *cleaned the contacts*, labour only |
| ⭐ **What it costs her** | **the credit, permanently** — and she engineered that on purpose, in front of the queue |
| **Seam sits at** | the hand-off — the congas and the second guitar arrive as she puts it in his hands and says nothing across the eight bars |
| **Radiant (late, once)** | *somebody gave me a working one when I had none, and I never said thank you* |
| **Return** | **four words on a ticket, turned round to face him** |
| **SFX cues** | a biro scratching on a ticket; the rain easing off the canvas |
| **Source 2** | `a choppier flip of the buttery soul sample to a new drum pattern` · `a two-beer chill` |

**THE ARC.** The extreme here is inverted and it is the strongest position the pair has: the extreme is
**how little she says.** The room is shouting *CLEANED THE CONTACTS* — the cover story — while what is
actually inside the machine is the part out of hers. ⭐ **The listener who takes only the surface receives
a whole song**; the second listen is free and is never charged for.

⚠️ **THE TRAP IN THIS GUIDE.** The reveal wants a moment. ⛔ **It does not get one** — no drop-out, no
hush, no reverb bloom, no instrument leaving to make room for it. The band plays the good morning straight
through the ticket and out the other side. If a production event lands on that line, the song has told the
listener what to feel and R3's second refusal has been broken: **she'll say the line, she won't sell it.**

# ⛔ THE PAIR-LEVEL SWEEP THESE FOUR GUIDES MUST SURVIVE

1. **Four events, four costs** — drawer / day / her own machine / her credit. *(Reported as a swap test in step 10.)*
2. **Four sign-offs, four kinds** — the queue moves / a gesture instead of an answer / her own thing put away / four words on paper.
3. **Four radiants, four origins, no relative** — labour / standing / knowledge / the thing itself.
4. **Four tautologies, four denials refused** — beyond repair / discontinued / whose was it / that was a kindness.
5. **One fact each** — eleven minutes / three calls / seven years / four pound fifty. **No other numeral in any lyric field.**
6. **One seam each, named as players** — second guitarist, two congas, shekere, snare off the one; and she acts across it.
7. **The horn is late in all four and stays late.** It never catches up. That is the hole.
8. ⛔ **No kitchen, no hot drink, no interior, no food** — including in the closing lines, which is where the formula hides.
9. ⛔ **No boat, ferry, capsizing, rescue, lake, drowning, missing person or disaster vocabulary**, and the word *body* is kept out of the lyric fields **and** the production language.
10. ⛔ **No Lofn.** Nobody is disappointed here. She is having a very good Saturday in the rain with a hot iron.
