# 1. WHAT SHE REFUSES

**1. She will not let eleven minutes mean anything.**
The draft had *"Them's seven years and that's a good run"* sitting in V3 and it is the whole disease in
seven words. It is the same move as *"eleven minutes is a result"* — a limit handed a small medal so the
song can feel brave about it. ⛔ **Refused.** The tautology exists precisely so nothing has to be
recovered. *Eleven minutes is eleven minutes.* The room shouts it and the horn is four bars late over the
top of it.

**2. She will not sell the sacrifice.**
V3 is a woman taking her own machine apart on a public bench and there is no version of it where she says
what it cost. No *"I'd do it again,"* no *"it's only a machine,"* no *"he needs it more than me."*
⛔ **Refused.** She names what she is doing while she is doing it, in the voice she used for the burn-hole
in the awning, and the row shouts the tautology over the top. **R3's second refusal: she'll say the line,
she won't sell it.**

**3. She will not go quiet.**
The draft put `PART, AND SHE'S GONE QUIET!` in V3's first verse. That is a **hush**, written into the
lyric where the gate that watches the arrangement cannot see it. ⛔ **Refused.** THE REGISTER NEVER MOVES,
and it does not move in the words either.

**4. She will not be clever at the front of the song.**
`Luck's not a thing I stock` is the exact defect the personality file calls **craft as credentials** —
a smart thing left where you can find it, so you know a smart person made it. ⛔ **Cut**, and nothing was
put in its place; the line just goes back to work.

**5. She will not sign four songs with one sentence.**
The draft's V2 ended `Bench. His is on. Off you go. / GO ON!` — which is V1's `Next.` wearing a different
hat. That is the predecessor's exact failure and it is why R3.1 exists. ⛔ **Rewritten to the empty box.**

  **Repair:** drop the final `it`: `…and it is not going on.`

### The two ceiling failures — how they get fixed without losing a line
⭐ **The lyric fields are not too wordy; the *headers* are.** Measured header overhead in the draft:
**1714 / 1720 / 1741 / 1744 characters** — a third of each field spent on section cues. The seam header
alone runs past 250 characters in all four.
**Repair: the cues get compressed, the sung lines do not.** The four Seam headers lose their restatement
of the intrusion contract (which is already in the STYLE field, where the generator actually reads it),
and every Chorus and Verse cue drops to the shortest form that still names voice and instrument. **Sung
lines stay at 70–120 and the songs lose nothing anyone hears.**

**And the style fields get cut where they repeat themselves** — `for the whole song` → `throughout`,
`opens the track alone` → `opens alone`, `for the rest of the take` → `to the end`. ⛔ **The one thing
that may not be cut to make room is the seam's instrument list**, because that list is the seam.

**V2's negation:** `Horns with no reverb` → **`Dry horn stabs`**. Suno reads a style field as positive
tokens; *no reverb* asks for reverb. The refusal was never needed in EXCLUDE either — `gated reverb snare`
was already there.

# 4. WHAT SHE SHARPENS

**1. The chain and the room became one device.** In the draft the market answers every line *and* the
answer begins with her last word — so the room is finishing her sentences. That was the strongest thing
on the page and it is now deliberate everywhere: ⭐ **each pre-chorus ends on the chorus's first word**, so
the byte-identical chorus sits *inside* the chain instead of interrupting it. **Return is free. Spend more
of it.**

**2. The seam gets louder, not quieter.** In all four songs the eight bars where the congas, the shekere
and the second guitarist arrive land on **the thing that costs her** — the last part going in, the shutter
coming down, her own lid coming off, the machine going into his hands. ⭐ **The band is having the best
morning of the month while she pays.** That is `THE BAND DOES NOT KNOW` at full volume, and it is why the
seam may sit on the action: **the wolf is *named* one line earlier, with the arrangement unchanged**, and
nothing in production defers to the meaning.

**3. The horn stays wrong on purpose.** It enters four bars behind the first chorus and it is still four
bars behind in the outro. It never catches up. **That is the hole** — the expected note arriving in the
wrong bar, all record long, in four songs — and it is the one production idea a listener will feel and
never remark on.

**4. Four tautologies, four denials.** *Fixed is fixed* refuses **beyond repair**. *A part is a part*
refuses **discontinued**. *Working is working* refuses **but whose was it**. *The job is the job* refuses
**that was a kindness**. Sharpened so that each one answers a sentence somebody actually says to her in
that song, rather than floating as a slogan.

**5. V4's cover story is now the hook.** The row shouts `CLEANED THE CONTACTS!` — which is the
understatement she wrote on the ticket — while what is inside the machine is the part out of hers.
⭐ **A listener who takes only the surface gets a whole song**; the second listen is free and is never the
price of entry.

# 5. THE TWO SWAP TESTS — run at step 09, restated in step 10

## ⭐ WOLF SWAP — swap the costed event between my own four songs

| Swap | What breaks |
|---|---|
| V1's `This one's the last. Drawer comes out.` → into **V3** | V3 established in its first verse that the drawer is empty and the van has been through twice; a full drawer **contradicts the premise** the song is built on |
| V3's `Morning she opened it and took the part out / OUT OF HER OWN AND INTO HIS!` → into **V1** | V1 has one machine on the bench. There is no second lid to open, the seam's eight bars lose their object, and **V1's own cost (the drawer emptied) goes unpaid** |
| V2's `Down goes the shutter on a market day / DAY'S TAKINGS — GONE!` → into **V4** | V4 happens entirely at an open counter with the customer standing there. **If the shutter comes down, the hand-off cannot happen** and the song has no last act |
| V4's `Says on the bottom: four pound fifty / FIFTY? THAT'S THE LABOUR!` → into **V2** | V2 never writes a ticket, and its money moves the **other way** — she paid a stranger before she saw the part. A price coming in **inverts the arithmetic** of the song |

✅ **Four events, four costs, four different reasons the swap fails.** *(Fisher's falsification, honoured
rather than passed on a technicality.)*

## ⭐ RETURN SWAP — swap the closing lines between my own four songs

| Swap | What breaks |
|---|---|
| V1's `Front of it blue. Next. / NEXT!` → **V4** | V4 has one customer and no queue. **`Next` addresses nobody**, and the ticket — the whole point of the song — never gets turned round |
| V2's `Down. And his is on. And the rain has eased. / EASED!` → **V3** | V3's cost is **her own machine, open, on the bench**. Ending on the weather leaves the lid up and the subtraction unlanded |
| V3's `Shuts, and it goes under the bench. / BENCH!` → **V1** | V1 never opened a second machine. **There is nothing to shut**, and the queue five verses built is abandoned mid-row |
| V4's `Lot signed for. Off he goes. / GOES!` → **V2** | V2's customer **asked a question** — *what did you find?* The empty box is the only answer the song has; a signature does not answer it |

✅ **Nothing survives a swap, and the four failures are of four different kinds:** a missing addressee ·
an unlanded subtraction · a machine that was never opened · an unanswered question.
⚠️ **What this test does NOT prove:** it is a within-pair test. Cross-pair collision is the coordinator's
measurement and I cannot run it from inside an isolated slot — I can only make sure that nothing in this
pair resolves on food, a hot drink, an interior, or a sentence anybody else could also say.
