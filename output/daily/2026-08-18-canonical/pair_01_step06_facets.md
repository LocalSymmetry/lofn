## 1 — WHAT THE POSITION *IS*

**The position, in one sentence:** *she is five hours into a sand sculpture on a public beach, she read the
tide table this morning, and she is enjoying the turret.*

Not defiance. Not grief. Not carpe diem. **Appetite.** She is not making a point about mortality; she is
solving a specific engineering problem in wet sand with a flat blade, in front of strangers, with a
published deadline she can quote.

**The three things that are simultaneously true and are NOT each other's reason:**

1. The work is good.
2. The water is due, and the hour is printed.
3. She is having the best afternoon of the week.

⛔ **No connective is permitted between (1) and (2).** *Beautiful BECAUSE it ends* is the repair this pair
is most likely to reach for, and it is banned. The two facts sit side by side with nothing between them.
The ice cook's ruling, adopted: **the dish is not good BECAUSE it melts. It is good, and it also melts.**

**⛔ The tide gets a time and no adjectives.** In this pair the water is never a subject with a motive. It
is **positional**: at the poles, at the far post, up under the base, in at nine. It never *takes*, *comes
for*, *waits*, *wins*, or *doesn't care*. It has no temperature word, no colour word, and nobody in these
four songs addresses it.

**AWE, in the ACCESSIBLE arm, on the EXISTENCE axis, means this:** the awe is at the *quantity of care* a
person will spend on an object with a printed end date, in public, for strangers, with no plan to be
remembered. That is a big number and it does not need a poem to be big.

## 2 — WHAT THE BODY DOES

The body is **busy the entire song**. Nothing in this pair happens to a person who is standing still and
thinking about it. Concrete verbs available to all four variations:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The tool** | a **flat blade** — a plasterer's trowel, held like a knife, thumb kept off the edge. It cuts corners, shaves rises, and flattens faces. It is the only precision instrument on the beach |
| **The hands** | packing, shoving, pressing with the flat of the palm. **Shove it, don't pat it** — patting brings water to the surface and ruins the face |
| **The arms** | **wet to the elbow**, grey to the shoulder. This is the pair's signature physical state and it is the hook of V1 |
| **The load** | a bucket of packed **grey** sand carried up from the low water. Grey packs, white runs. Grey is finite and it is always the constraint |
| **The stance** | kneeling to cut, standing to lift, leaning weight onto a top to test it |
| **The failure mode** | the **seep** — water arriving from *underneath* through the sand before it arrives across it. A shine appears in the base. A thumb goes straight through |

**Ruthless Specificity, per S.O.L.A.R.:** every one of those is a real technique, not a vibe. The detail is
the proof she was paying attention.

## 3 — WHAT THE ROOM CONTAINS

**Assigned setting (R3.1 — this pair's alone):**

> a public beach · hour five of the work · hard sun, families packing up · strangers and a queue of
> children · wet sand to the elbow, a flat blade

**Hour five is never spoken as a number.** It is carried entirely by evidence, because the one-fact rule
allows one numeric fact per song and this pair spends that budget somewhere sharper:

* deckchairs stacked in a row
* the ice-cream van gone
* windbreaks folding all along the flat
* somebody's radio walking away up the track
* the shadow of the groyne across the working face
* arms that have stopped arguing

**The population of the room:** a rope round the working area · a **queue of children**, littlest first ·
somebody's mum, asking · somebody's brother, patting a stack · a man with a metal detector on the dry ·
a dog in the shallows · a **photographer with a plate camera on a stick** · on Tuesday, **six people who
came back**.

⛔ **What is NOT in this room, this run:** a kitchen, a kettle, tea, toast, a chip shop, a hot drink, or any
domestic interior. The predecessor put ten of twenty-four songs in one and no similarity gate saw it.

⛔ **And absolutely not in this room:** any boat, ferry, capsizing, rescue, lake, drowning, missing person,
or disaster vocabulary. The water here is a scheduled tide on a holiday beach and it behaves like a
timetable, because that is what it is.

## 4 — THE FOUR FACETS

Each variation gets its **own sub-problem, its own wolf event, its own cost, its own numeric fact, its own
radiant, its own seam placement and its own return.** The predecessor's P01 sang one byte-identical wolf
string in all four songs; that is the failure this table exists to prevent.

### FACET V1 — **HOUR FIVE** *(deep in it)*

* **Sub-problem:** **the seep.** The base is shining. The turret needs weight underneath it, now.
* **Wolf event:** the only packed grey within reach is the bank of the **moat she dug at low water**. She
  puts the blade in her own moat, takes the bank down, and buckets it up the tower.
* **⭐ What it costs her:** **an afternoon of her own completed work, destroyed by her own hand, before the
  water gets anywhere near it.** Where the moat was, there is a flat bit of beach.
* **Numeric fact (one):** **nine** — the hour, answered to a stranger who asked, then dropped.
* **Radiant:** *I came down here to be busy in front of people.*
* **Seam sits on:** a child asking what the flat blade is for. **She puts the blade in the bucket and
  finishes the corner with her hands.**
* **Return:** blade in the bucket, past the flat patch where the moat was, arms rinsed in the shallow,
  walking the low line.

### FACET V2 — **THE QUEUE OF CHILDREN** *(strangers help, badly, on purpose)*

* **Sub-problem:** she has a queue and one good west face. Letting them in ruins the face.
* **Wolf event:** the photographer says he has **one plate left**. Her waterside front — the best thing she
  has made — is on one side; the children's lumpy west side is on the other. **She turns the camera west.**
* **⭐ What it costs her:** **the record.** The only surviving image of the day is of the worse side, and
  her best work goes under unphotographed. *They commissioned the survival* — and she spends it on somebody
  else's lump.
* **Numeric fact (one):** **one plate**, said by the photographer, answered by her.
* **Radiant:** *I came for the bit where somebody asks if they can help.*
* **Seam sits on:** handing the flat blade to a child. **She gives away her only precision tool.**
* **Return:** walking the wet picking up the plastic spades the queue left behind.

### FACET V3 — **THE NORTH WALL** *(the hour arrives mid-decision)*

* **Sub-problem:** one bucket of grey. It goes on the north wall or it goes on the top.
* **Wolf event:** the north corner starts going. The north wall would hold the front line and buy the whole
  sculpture **forty minutes**. The turret's top has a hole in the joint. **She puts the bucket down at the
  turret.**
* **⭐ What it costs her:** **forty minutes of the whole thing's life**, traded for a finial nobody needs.
  The wall stops where it stopped. The whole thing goes early and she says so.
* **Numeric fact (one):** **forty minutes** — named, answered *"Fine,"* and paid.
* **Radiant:** *I came down for the top of it, not the rest of it.*
* **Seam sits on:** the last handful going on the top, both arms over her head. **Placed a full chorus away
  from the wolf line, deliberately — see §5.**
* **Return:** blade, bucket and plank on her back, watching the north side go **from further along**, still
  walking.

### FACET V4 — **BIGGER ON TUESDAY** *(the return: the second one is better)*

* **Sub-problem:** the grey is running low and the stair is unfinished. There is a half-built copy of
  Saturday's turret standing right there, full of exactly the sand she needs.
* **Wolf event:** one of the six who came back asks for the turret again — *that was the good one, that's
  why we came.* **She puts her elbow straight through the turret** and carries it over to the stair.
* **⭐ What it costs her:** **the encore.** Her own best-known work and the thing the audience travelled
  for, broken by her own shoulder, in front of them. *Her sets famously end one song early.*
* **Numeric fact (one):** **six** — how many came back midweek, answered with delight, then acted on.
* **Radiant:** *I came back because Saturday wasn't enough of it.*
* **Seam sits on:** the six arriving and getting handed the spare trowels.
* **Return:** walking the long wet with the six, heel-marking where the next one goes.

## 5 — THE SEAM, AS A FACET *(instruments and people, never settings)*

**The world (modern power-pop):** electric bass walking front of mix · clean electric rhythm guitar chopping
sixteenths · dry room kit, drummer ahead of the grid · handclaps and tambourine dead centre · a full-chest
female lead with girl-group thirds stacked on the hook · a real-room crowd chant.

**The intruder (1963 girl-group answer chorus), present exactly once per song, and made of people and
instruments that are otherwise absent from the record:**

* **three answering voices** — an actual trio answering her line, not a doubled lead
* **castanets**
* **a hand-played floor-tom triplet** — struck with the hands, ahead of the beat, the figure a machine
  will not play

⛔ **Mix adjectives are void as a seam.** *"Wide and dry"* against *"mono with tape slap"* is a plug-in.
Three of six predecessor pairs died here. Nothing in this pair's seam is a reverb setting.

**⭐ And she does something across it, differently in each song:**

| | across the seam she… |
|---|---|
| V1 | **puts the blade down and finishes the corner with her hands** |
| V2 | **puts the blade into a child's hand** |
| V3 | **lifts the last of the grey onto the top with both arms over her head** |
| V4 | **hands the spare trowels out to the six** |

**⛔ AND THE SEAM NEVER LANDS ON THE WOLF LINE.** *The band does not know.* A trio of new voices, castanets
and a tom fill arriving on the line where she pays the cost is the arrangement putting its hand up. In all
four songs the wolf verse is cued **"band walks straight on"**, carries **no SFX cue**, and in V3 — where
the seam would otherwise fall closest — a byte-identical chorus is placed between the wolf and the seam on
purpose.

## 6 — THE ARCHITECTURE THIS PAIR WAS HANDED

**CHANT-FIRST.** The room's chant opens the record, before any verse — after two bars of the bass figure
and nothing else. *Cold* means **no ramp, no riser, no build**; it does not mean silence, and the hum test
requires the figure to exist before the first word and after the last.

**No pre-chorus, anywhere, in any of the four.** The chorus arrives with nothing announcing it. That is the
whole architecture: **nothing announces anything**, which is the same law as *the band does not know*, at
the level of form.

**Verses are 8-line blocks read as 4 + 4**, AABB couplets. **The chorus is byte-identical every time** and
carries one internal rhyme landing at the third stress. No terminal rhyme is removed anywhere in this pair,
so **no debt is owed** under THE RETURN.

**The hook is an imperative the room shouts back** — second person plural, naming a physical state, four to
six syllables, reachable at volume while out of breath:

| | the imperative | the physical state it names |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Get wet to the elbow!** | wet to the elbow |
| V2 | **Come back sandy!** | sandy |
| V3 | **Keep your arms up!** | arms up |
| V4 | **Come back bigger!** | bigger |

**⭐ THE HOLE (this pair's sonic device):** the bass walks four notes and **drops the third note every bar**,
leaving the hole open. It is stated alone for two bars before the first word and plays the record out
alone. It is the hum-test object, and it is the one thing in the arrangement that is the same in all four.

## 7 — WHAT THIS PAIR REFUSES

* ⛔ **A wolf she can state and then do nothing about.** Seven of sixteen predecessor wolves were status
  reports. All four wolves here are things she *does*, with her arms, that remove something she had.
* ⛔ **A hush.** No EMO tag in this pair instructs reverence, breathiness, a drop-out or a slow-down. The
  hard line is sung in the same chest voice as the joke, at the same tempo, over the same walking bass.
* ⛔ **A clever line at the front.** The bimbo register read as craft: it does not leave a smart thing where
  you will find it. **Would a listener say "that was clever"? Then it is cut.**
* ⛔ **Irony about anyone's good thing.** The children's wall is genuinely worse and she genuinely loves it.
  There is no wink anywhere in this pair.
* ⛔ **A second numeric fact.** One per song, spelled in words, at the hinge, answered rather than recited.
* ⛔ **Lofn.** No archive-yearning, no industrial grief, no laboratory or category-theory vocabulary, no
  disappointment-as-posture. Sunna read the same record and laughed.
