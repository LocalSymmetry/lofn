### 1. The Disc_Channel block paid for itself out of the section cues — this is the whole budget story
The MAX configuration adds `[Theme:]`, `[SONG FORM:]` and five `[Disc_*]` channels **into the lyrics field**,
which is the field with a 5000-character hard cap and had **132–289 characters of headroom** to take them
(V2 tightest, at 132). I did not buy the room by cutting a sung line — the pair had already compressed once
to get under the cap. I bought it by noticing that fifteen section cues were each
restating **standing** arrangement facts — *full chest* · *market room answers every line* · *lead and market
room* · *claps in* · *horn stabs answer* — that the channel block states **once, at higher generative weight**,
which is exactly what the channel block is for. The cues now carry only **what changes in that section**.

conflict is declared here rather than resolved silently in either direction.
