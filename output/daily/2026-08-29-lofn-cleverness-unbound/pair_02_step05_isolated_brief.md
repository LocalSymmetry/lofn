# PAIR 02 ISOLATION BRIEF · **LOFN-PRIME**

**Arm:** ACCESSIBLE
**Axis B:** NEWS
**Tempo:** **124–130 BPM**
**Working title:** **Make Room**
**Situation:** In a storm-hot borrowed room, Lofn carries folding chairs while arrivals turn the place into its own venue.
**Human want:** “I want us to have a room tonight, not someday.”
**Semantic engine:** consequential zeugma.
**Return engine:** cumulative return.
**Current chassis:** Southern Electronic Bass Pop, the portfolio's sole full club bed; heavy synthetic sub, sharply gated kick, bent two-note synth, late crowd clap, close lead.

**Architecture:** 2-line sub/hook pickup → 6-line first-arrival verse → 4-line straight-faced “not a club” pre-chorus → 8-line chorus → 8-line second-arrival verse → 4-line pre-chorus → 8-line chorus with two answers → 10-line room-pressure bridge → 8-line third-arrival verse → 8-line communal final chorus → 4-line voices-only receipt.

**LINE BUDGET: 2+6+4+8+8+4+8+10+8+8+4 = 70**

**Rhyme / line logic:** call lines are 3–6 words; each arrival adds a two-line answer without changing the call. `Room / move / you` vowel returns hold the chorus; clipped `chair / there` rhyme belongs only to verses. Exact repetition is permitted and desired.

**Hook grammar:** “Make room” is literal command, social permission, and crowd answer. Chorus call stays exact while the number and location of answers grows. Avoid “build community” language.

**Sonic device:** one dry chair-hinge impact changes jobs: prop sound, offbeat punctuation, then the count-in that starts the full room. No second furniture sample and no fake party ambience.

**Physical trace:** two palms on a folding chair, tape line, open square. **Refused element:** treating the living Southern scene as retro decoration or naming artists in generator fields.

**Four-way variation law:** four complete packages. V1 makes chair impact the downbeat; V2 makes a late clap teach the room its answer; V3 begins with the room already denying it is a club; V4 strips the bed before the final accumulating voices. Each package uses a different arrival verb, chorus response topology, and seam consequence.

**Non-negotiable proof:** someone must be able to tell what happened with the hook alone: people acted, and the room became available by that action.
