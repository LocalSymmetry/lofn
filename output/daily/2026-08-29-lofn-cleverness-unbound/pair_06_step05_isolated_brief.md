# PAIR 06 ISOLATION BRIEF · **LOFN-PRIME**

**Arm:** AMBITIOUS
**Axis B:** EXISTENCE
**Tempo:** **134–140 BPM**
**Working title:** **The Joke Was Come With Me**
**Situation:** In a laughing room, Lofn turns her chair toward one woman and repeats a public self-own as a private invitation.
**Human want:** “I was joking because I want you to come with me.”
**Semantic engine:** delayed premise.
**Return engine:** exact refrain / new referent.
**Current chassis:** future-garage left-pop from second zero; swung electronic drums, synthetic sub, short chord stabs, dry near-spoken lead, present-day micro-edits.

**Architecture:** 4-line setup pickup → 8-line public self-own verse → 8-line exact punchline chorus → 6-line room-response verse → 8-line chorus → 8-line chair-turn verse → 8-line plain-invitation bridge → 8-line privately addressed exact chorus → 8-line answer-space instrumental-vocal section → 6-line named doorway return → 4-line tomorrow aftertag.

**LINE BUDGET: 4+8+8+6+8+8+8+8+8+6+4 = 76**

**Rhyme / line logic:** setup lines use quick internal rhyme and stop before becoming couplet comedy. Chorus is byte-identical with an `o / uh / ee` vowel path. The bridge strips jokes but keeps the hook's vowel return. The aftertag names a day and doorway without prescribing an answer.

**Hook grammar:** first return must work as an ordinary public self-own; later the exact words become “come with me” through body orientation, addressee, and one plain bridge line. If “The Joke Was Come With Me” is opaque on first read, variants may use “You Had to Be There Tomorrow” but must preserve delayed premise and exact/new-referent return.

**Sonic device:** one dry laugh occurs once and transfers its rhythm into the sub pickup. At the hinge, the center vocal rotates toward one close side position. Do not widen the whole mix and do not script the other woman's yes.

**Physical trace:** chair leg pivot, one close voice, named doorway. **Refused element:** sitcom crowd, theory of implicature, flirtation without an actionable invitation.

**Four-way variation law:** four complete packages. V1 hides “come with me” in the title grammar; V2 uses future tense inside “had to be there”; V3 lets a repeated wrong answer become the invitation; V4 turns a heckle at herself into a door code. Change public joke, private address, aftertag logistics, and spatial seam.

**Non-negotiable proof:** the listener's contract with the exact chorus changes after the midpoint. The woman retains a real unanswered space.
