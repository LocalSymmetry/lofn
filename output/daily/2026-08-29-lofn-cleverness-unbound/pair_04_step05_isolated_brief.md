**Situation:** At an eclipse watch, Lofn passes a paper viewing card; scheduled light returns while a person may not.
**Human want:** “I know the light returns; I still want you tomorrow.”
**Semantic engine:** missing-word eclipse.
**Return engine:** vowel migration.
**Current chassis:** electronic alt-R&B at second zero; dry sub heartbeat, brushed electronic kick, low granular pad, fingertip percussion, close consonant-rich lead.

**Architecture:** 4-line card invocation → 8-line first eclipse verse → 6-line intact refrain → 8-line handoff verse → 8-line refrain missing one grammatical hinge → 6-line second-mechanism verse → 8-line refrain with a different omission → 10-line leaving bridge → 8-line restored refrain → 6-line tomorrow invitation → 4-line card-offered aftertag.

**LINE BUDGET: 4+8+6+8+8+6+8+10+8+6+4 = 76**

**Rhyme / line logic:** refrain begins in rounded `oo / uh`, migrates toward open `ah / ow` as words disappear, then restores both text and initial vowel only after the future ask. Verse end sounds return every 2–4 lines. Missing grammatical words are carried by answer harmony, so subtraction pays a sonic debt.

**Hook grammar:** “The moon took nothing” stays intelligible even as one different hinge vanishes per return. Do not state a lesson about darkness. “Tomorrow” must name an actual invitation, not optimism.

**Sonic device:** close answer harmony occupies the omitted words without singing them lexically; a single dry paper scrape enters spectral freeze at the human leaving hinge. The final mix opens vertically on the future verb, never into cosmic widescreen.

**Physical trace:** thumb, forefinger, paper card, released hand. **Refused element:** telescope scale, cosmic reassurance, sung eclipse dates.

**Four-way variation law:** four complete packages. V1 omits subject then object; V2 omits causal hinge then possession; V3 lets answer harmony carry only vowels; V4 restores one “missing” word with tomorrow's place name. Change omitted grammar, card action, bridge proof, and vowel destination.

**Non-negotiable proof:** two disappearances must be distinguishable—covered light and a human choice. If the sky becomes the subject, repair.
