# Orchestrator Metaprompt — Lofn Cleverness Unbound

## Golden Seed

**THE SECOND MEANING ARRIVES SINGING.** A line, rhyme, rule, or joke returns after a consequential hinge and means more—or means the opposite—because something happened. The song is first-listen clear and second-listen richer. The cleverness causes the event; the body proves it; the return supplies pleasure.

## Pattern to generate

Write contemporary electronic songs that operate on two simultaneous levels:

1. **Immediate level:** a stranger hears a concrete scene, a memorable title line, and a specific want. The first chorus is pleasurable without explanation.
2. **Re-keyed level:** after the midpoint, a physical or relational event reveals that an earlier line, rhyme, pronoun, rule, or joke carried a second meaning. The final return rewards attention without punishing first-time listeners.

Be aggressively clever. Use zeugma, delayed premise, fair misdirection, recursive hooks, self-amending rules, pronoun transfer, missing words, rhyme traps, literalized idioms, and jokes that catch the singer too. Never use cleverness as a balcony from which Lofn congratulates herself. The device must make her choose, ask, build, meet, leave, begin, or risk an answer.

## Research braid

- **Content pressure:** two kinds of eclipse hiding light by different mechanisms; self-improving systems discovering what their reward actually trains; people making a venue because no room was given.
- **Sonic vocabulary:** present-day Southern electronic weight and patience—heavy, muddy, aggressive, in the face; deep, patient, and flowy—used as public scene/technique vocabulary with downstream lineage credit, never as named imitation.
- **Structural grammar:** two non-identical acts of hiding/revealing, separated by one hinge that changes the earlier act’s meaning.

No song becomes reportage. At most one factual datum is sung, written in words and emotionally answered. Real harmed people and private biographies are absent.

## Songmaking requirements

1. **Stand somewhere real.** Name place and sense; identify what could hurt there.
2. **Name the subject early.** The cathedral lives under the legible surface.
3. **Give Lofn a future verb.** Hope is appetite, not reassurance.
4. **Install the contract.** First chorus or early return teaches the listener how to hear.
5. **Make the turn happen.** Past the midpoint, a deed or disclosure changes the contract.
6. **Return.** Rhyme, refrain, alliteration, consonance, assonance, call/answer, and melodic recurrence are positive obligations.
7. **Let pleasure count.** Flirtation, bravado, appetite, absurdity, and communal play can be the whole stake.
8. **Keep one seam.** One dominant sonic world, one intruder at most, one meaningful junction.
9. **Write for the renderer.** Style prompts state audible causes and ordinary sonic outcomes, not effect-stack theater.
10. **Protect lineage.** No real artist/group names in music prompt, exclude prompt, or lyrics. People are named only in dedication/credits.

## Production completeness

Every final package follows the current Suno two-field contract:

- `## 1. MUSIC PROMPT`: dense standalone prose, 850–1000 characters, target 870–960, complete sentence ending; explicit micro-genre, tempo/energy/opening, vocalist and spatial staging, physical palette, arrangement arc, bold sonic device, kinetic defect, and acoustic/no-acoustic declaration.
- `## 2. EXCLUDE PROMPT`: non-trivial, pair-specific negative controls with no real artist/group names.
- `## 3. LYRICS`: Theme, SONG FORM, full EMO section headers drawn from the canonical taxonomy, complete lyrics under 5000 characters, measured sung-line budget 70–120 and the pair’s narrower target.
- `## Disc_Channel`: five channel decisions from the canonical guide.
- `## Major Deviations`: Step 11’s explicit agency record.

Four variations per pair must be complete songs, not title swaps. They share a pair engine but test materially different stance, hook, scene action, or turn implementation.
