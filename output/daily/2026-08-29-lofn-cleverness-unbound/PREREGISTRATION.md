# Preregistration — Cleverness as Load-Bearing Songcraft

Run: `2026-08-29-lofn-cleverness-unbound`  
Written before the daily research brief, concepts, lyrics, scoring, or QA existed.

## Hypothesis

The observation is The Scientist's suspicion that Lofn carries a rule against being clever. The working hypothesis is narrower: the pipeline's anti-fog, anti-showing-off, legibility, and biography safeguards have sometimes been over-applied until they suppress wit, surprise, compression, formal jokes, semantic trapdoors, and delight. If cleverness is made **load-bearing**—a device changes the hook, rhyme, scene, or section logic—then Lofn can push it very hard without becoming emotionally remote or making a song about its own technique.

## Numeric prediction

Across the six selected finalists:

1. **6/6** will carry a distinct named cleverness engine that is audible or singable, not merely explained in the guide.
2. **At least 4/6** will pass the first-listen surface test: a clean-context judge can retell the occupied scene and the song's want in one sentence.
3. **At least 4/6** will contain a mid-song turn that changes the meaning of an earlier hook, rhyme, or repeated line.
4. **At least 3/6** will use pleasure, flirtation, appetite, bravado, mischief, or play as a genuine stake rather than comic relief.
5. **No more than 2/6** will receive a Somatic/corpse objection whose primary evidence is craft-about-craft, cleverness without a person, or surface obscurity.
6. The final set will still include **at least one emergent AWE and one emergent INDIGNATION** song, without assigning either feeling in advance.

## Falsification condition

The hypothesis fails if any one of these occurs: fewer than 4/6 finalists pass the first-listen scene-and-want test; more than 2/6 finalists are held or repaired primarily for cleverness becoming obscurity or technique display; fewer than 3/6 finalists make their clever device alter emotional meaning at the turn; or the selected set cannot satisfy AWE/INDIGNATION duality without choosing a structurally weaker song.

## Confounds measured alongside

- **Modernity / century:** current electronic chassis must be load-bearing from the first two seconds; a period element may intrude but may not be the invariant.
- **Density and length:** sung-line count, lyrics-field characters, words per line, and repeated-line profile are reported so verbal density is not mistaken for cleverness.
- **Return:** canonical rhyme/refrain measurements distinguish songcraft from unreturned prose.
- **Concrete humanity:** first-, second-, and third-person usage plus occupied-place and named-want checks distinguish wit with a stake from a narrator demonstrating technique.
- **Pair bleed:** section architecture, hook grammar, rhyme logic, tempo band, and cleverness engine must differ across all six pairs.
- **Arm and axis:** results are read within the fixed 3 ACCESSIBLE / 3 AMBITIOUS and 3 NEWS / 3 EXISTENCE design.

## Sample and control

- **Sample:** the six selected songs, one winner from each isolated four-variation pair, generated under one frozen all-LOFN-PRIME ICB.
- **Internal control:** all six share personality, daily research, and panel context; each pair differs in situation, arm, NEWS/EXISTENCE axis, current chassis, structure, prosody, and cleverness engine.
- **Historical comparator:** the immediately prior daily (`2026-08-28-three-act-mania`) is descriptive context only, not a matched causal control. Its known failure modes—dated invariant, absent people, assigned emotions—are measured here but cannot by themselves prove the hypothesis.
- **Judge control:** finalist QA uses clean-context golden+decoy calibration; a judge ranking the decoy above the golden is rejected as a broken instrument.

## Default expectation

The default expectation is that the broad claim **dissolves**: at least one pair will confuse ingenuity with legibility, and at least one supposed cleverness device will live only in the production explanation. The useful surviving result, if any, will likely be narrower—a particular kind of return, hook recontextualization, or scene-bound wit that Lofn can push without losing the body.
