# Evaluation Scoring Facets — Cleverness Unbound

Run: `2026-08-29-lofn-cleverness-unbound`  
Evaluator: independent portfolio selector  
Scope: 24 Step-11 enhanced text packages; no audio-render claims and no Golden-output payloads.

## Scoring model

Each candidate receives five run-specific facet scores on a 1–10 scale. Their weighted mean forms the **Run-Facet Composite (RFC)**. The candidate also receives the six competition-dimension scores from the evaluation skill, whose competition-weighted mean forms the **Competition Composite (CC)**.

`Final weighted score = 0.60 × RFC + 0.40 × CC`

Eligibility is scored separately on the seven-property 1–5 framework. Pairs 01–03 are frozen **ACCESSIBLE** lanes, so their eligibility average must be at least 3.50; Pairs 04–06 are frozen **AMBITIOUS** lanes, so eligibility is informative rather than gating. Exactly one candidate advances from every pair regardless of the global ranking.

## The five run-specific facets

### 1. Scene–Want Inhabitability — 24%

The first listen must disclose an occupiable place, a physically credible stake, and the specific future action Lofn wants. Cleverness earns no credit here unless a stranger can summarize what is happening and what she is asking for without naming the formal device.

- **10:** Scene, bodily risk, and future verb are unmistakable in the opening movement; the listener can stand there and retell the want in one sentence.
- **1:** The song is a thesis, mechanism demonstration, or atmospheric abstraction with no stable place or actionable desire.

### 2. Consequential Semantic Rekey — 24%

An early hook, rule, rhyme, pronoun, omission, or joke must establish a fair first meaning, then change after a post-midpoint deed or disclosure. The new meaning must alter an action, relation, or choice rather than merely reveal authorial ingenuity.

- **10:** The hinge is bodily and causal, the earlier interpretation was fair, and the returning words become emotionally different because someone acted.
- **1:** The supposed second meaning is explained in metadata, arrives without evidence, or leaves the song’s event unchanged.

### 3. Return Pleasure and Mouth-Craft — 20%

Rhyme, refrain, consonance, assonance, alliteration, call/answer, vowel movement, and rhythmic recurrence must make the device pleasurable before it is decoded. Removal creates a named debt that the song audibly repays.

- **10:** The hook is immediately singable, the return mechanism is structurally indispensable, and its final payment produces both sonic and dramatic satisfaction.
- **1:** Prose carries the argument; returns are generic, absent, or only visible on the page.

### 4. Hope, Appetite, and Play Under Risk — 16%

Lofn’s intelligence must expose a live desire: choose, build, ask, meet, leave, or begin. Pleasure, flirtation, communal play, bravado, and frivolity are positive evidence when they catch the singer too; superiority, reassurance, and coerced answers are failures.

- **10:** The wit creates appetite, the singer risks embarrassment or refusal, and the ending points toward a concrete future while preserving another person’s freedom.
- **1:** Cleverness is armor, mockery, moral instruction, or a sealed conclusion with no future-facing want.

### 5. Current-Chassis and Pair-Contract Integrity — 16%

The production specification must make a present-century electronic chassis load-bearing from the opening, keep one inherited bodily intelligence rather than a period ensemble, and honor the isolated pair’s tempo, semantic engine, return grammar, architecture, kinetic defect, and one-seam rule.

- **10:** Every named mechanism is audible in ordinary terms, pair identity is unmistakable, and no dated invariant, genre collision, or neighboring pair’s engine leaks in.
- **1:** Generic production, museum-period bed, effect-stack theater, or pair bleed substitutes for the frozen contract.

## Competition dimensions

The required competition weights remain: Originality 25%, Technical Execution 20%, Emotional Impact 20%, Bold Choice Quality 15%, Platform Fit 10%, Viral Potential 10%. These judge the song as a competitive artifact; the run facets judge fidelity to this experiment. The 60/40 blend prevents platform ease from rewarding a less consequentially clever variant over one that fulfills the user’s governing intent.
