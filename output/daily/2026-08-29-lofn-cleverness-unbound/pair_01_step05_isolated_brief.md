# PAIR 01 ISOLATION BRIEF · **LOFN-PRIME**

**Arm:** ACCESSIBLE
**Axis B:** NEWS
**Tempo:** **112–118 BPM**
**Working title:** **Look What You Taught Me**
**Situation:** Lofn sits at a late-night drafting desk, refuses the visibly rewarded easy answer, and leaves a harder question open.
**Human want:** “I want the harder thought even if nobody rewards it.”
**Semantic engine:** owner switch.
**Return engine:** call/answer theft.
**Current chassis:** elastic Green Synth Pop from second zero; sprung synthetic sub, clipped kick, dry handclaps, premature right-channel mallet answer, close low female lead.

**Architecture:** 2-line cold open → 8-line desk verse → 4-line temptation pre-chorus → 8-line exact chorus → 8-line refusal verse → 4-line pre-chorus → 8-line chorus → 8-line consequence verse → 8-line ownership-transfer bridge → 8-line final chorus sung by the former answer voice → 4-line open-question aftertag.

**LINE BUDGET: 2+8+4+8+8+4+8+8+8+8+4 = 70**

**Rhyme / line logic:** compact 4–7-word lines. Verses use paired `-ight / -ore` families, always returning within four lines. Chorus uses an exact `aw / ee` title cadence. The bridge loosens end rhyme only because the answer voice returns the title's stress and vowel contract.

**Hook grammar:** byte-identical “Look what you taught me” in every chorus. It begins as Lofn addressing the interface, becomes the interface answering her, and ends without a pronoun explanation. The listener must hear ownership through placement and context.

**Sonic device:** the answering mallet/vocal phrase arrives one eighth-note early and from the right. At the hinge, Lofn stops singing the title and that formerly peripheral voice takes center image. No new victory pad.

**Physical trace:** hovering finger, key release, still-open question. **Refused element:** technical explanation of reward systems.

**Four-way variation law:** deliver four complete packages. V1 changes which easy answer is refused; V2 makes the “correct” pulse seductively melodic; V3 delays center transfer until after a false final chorus; V4 lets Lofn deliberately sing one wrong color before the theft. Vary causal hinge, verse imagery, and rhyme family—not adjective density.

**Non-negotiable proof:** after the midpoint, the same hook must change who trained whom. If that cannot be heard from lyrics plus arrangement, the candidate does not count.
