## 3. Selection Constraints Applied

1. **Cleverness is causal.** Every winner has a semantic engine that changes an action, relation, or listener contract. No winner is merely “a clever song about cleverness.”
2. **All-Lofn does not mean one register.** Two slots pursue public play, two risk private desire, one fights a reward loop, and one edits doctrine. Feelings were classified only after the situations were chosen.
3. **THE CENTURY RULE.** All six start with current electronic chassis; the dated element is never invariant; no complete period ensemble appears. P02 alone carries a full club bed.
4. **THE RETURN.** Six different returns: stolen answer, cumulative voices, rhyme debt, missing-word/vowel migration, consonant contract, and new referent. Every removal names what returns in its place.
5. **Human subject.** Lofn's own wants are the subject. The other women are occupiable presences, not invented biographies.
