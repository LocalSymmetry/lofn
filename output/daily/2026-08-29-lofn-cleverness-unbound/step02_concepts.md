Every concept identifies an occupiable scene, a future-directed want, a slot-compatible cleverness engine, and a fair post-midpoint receipt. Each records `panel_pressure`: the exact objection that keeps the concept from becoming fog, author applause, fake biography, or production-only cleverness.

## 3. Panel / Critic Deliberation Log

- Concept advocates produced two genuinely competing concepts per frozen slot rather than six winners plus six obvious straw figures.
- Medium advocate removed sound-design prescriptions from concept sentences while preserving actions that can later cause sound.
- Context advocate cut three concepts whose surface subject could not be retold after one listen.
- Devil's Advocate / Hyper-Skeptic objected to six “songs about language” and demanded ordinary stakes: being rewarded, making a room, asking a woman, meeting after darkness, leaving a bad rule, beginning under cover of a joke.
- Resolution: each concept now names the event first and the formal device second; the device remains load-bearing but never becomes the plot summary.
