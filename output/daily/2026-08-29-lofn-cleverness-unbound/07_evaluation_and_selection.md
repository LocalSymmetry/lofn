**Baseline configuration:** the six seats initially judged concept, sound, and audience value as independent strengths. This favored densely engineered variants and risked rewarding mechanism count.

**Group transformation — PANEL FOCUS:** the panel unanimously narrowed each seat to one causal question: what happened, what returned, what changed in the mouth, what changed in the sound, what a stranger can borrow, and what remains when the device label is removed.

**Hyper-Skeptic transformation — PANEL REFLECT:** the Formalist Knife reversed the baseline premise that more complexity is positive. A song retained its cleverness score only if scene, want, hinge, and return still stood without those labels.

**Final working configuration:** FOCUS supplied pair-specific precision; REFLECT made first-listen humanity the adversarial control. This is the panel that produced the scores below.
