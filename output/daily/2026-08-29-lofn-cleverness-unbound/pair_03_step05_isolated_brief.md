# PAIR 03 ISOLATION BRIEF · **LOFN-PRIME**

**Arm:** ACCESSIBLE
**Axis B:** EXISTENCE
**Tempo:** **144–150 BPM**
**Working title:** **Leave Me at the Start**
**Situation:** On a closing train platform, Lofn turns a departure phrase into a freely answerable next-meeting invitation.
**Human want:** “I want this departure to be our start.”
**Semantic engine:** literalized idiom.
**Return engine:** rhyme trap.
**Current chassis:** serrated glitch-pop; pinched synthetic sub, cropped snare, chrome two-note synth and current breakbeat from second zero; close, unsentimental female lead.

**Architecture:** 2-line cropped hook → 7-line platform verse → 3-line rhyme-trap pre-chorus → 8-line chorus → 7-line warning verse → 3-line pre-chorus → 8-line chorus → 7-line choice-to-stay verse → 9-line doors-close bridge → 8-line paid-rhyme chorus → 8-line next-meeting chorus variation → 4-line written-time aftertag.

**LINE BUDGET: 2+7+3+8+7+3+8+7+9+8+8+4 = 74**

**Rhyme / line logic:** each three-line pre-chorus makes an obvious fourth rhyme and withholds it for one breath. Compact `-ay / -ate / -art` families. The missing end rhyme must be paid inline in the bridge or final chorus; removal is a debt, not a mood.

**Hook grammar:** exact “Leave me at the start” works first as defensive departure, then as a request to stay at a shared beginning. The real invitation names a time or place and leaves the other woman an honest no.

**Sonic device:** the expected rhyme landing is represented by a warm unprocessed exhale inside the cropped grid. One processed train-door chime appears once. The pulse continues after the doors close; the train leaving is not a fade-out.

**Physical trace:** one shoe behind the yellow line, closing doors, time written down. **Refused element:** grand travel metaphor or coerced romantic answer.

**Four-way variation law:** four complete packages. V1 pays the rhyme with the place name; V2 pays it with the other person's question; V3 makes “miss this train” the bridge action; V4 shifts the missing rhyme from pre-chorus to final verse. Each uses a different meeting ask, held word, and aftertag while preserving engine.

**Non-negotiable proof:** the post-midpoint action must make “leave” and “start” mean differently. A clever title without a next action fails.
