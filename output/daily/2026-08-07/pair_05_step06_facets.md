### **F1 — THE MAN AT THE DESK IS DOING SOMETHING WITH HIS HANDS** · weight 0.16

Line one names him and shows a hand moving: a tab key, a cursor, a dropdown calendar, a thumb on the
space bar. Every section keeps at least one physical verb attached to him — *scroll, click, spell,
save, stand, walk past.* If the addressee ever becomes a position rather than a body, the facet fails.
The test is the smell and sound of the room: a mug ring drying by the mouse, a kettle clicking in a
kitchen down the hall, somebody laughing at something that is not this. **Scored 0 if the technician
can be replaced by the word "they" without loss.**
*Serves:* Constraint 2 (THE NAMED RECIPIENT) · Slot 5 seat 4 (THE PLAIN SPEAKER) · Golden Move #1
(stand somewhere real).

### **F2 — THE CANVAS DOES NOT MOVE** · weight 0.15

The reveal engine must arrive as an **event in a hallway**, never as a proposition. A picture, the
same hooks, the same wire, and a small card beside it that somebody changed on Monday. The brushwork
is identical at 10am and at 4pm; what changed is the person walking past. The song must be able to
say *the picture did not move — that was you moving* without ever explaining what it is an analogy
for. **Scored 0 if the word "forgery," "fake," "real," or any gloss on provenance appears in a sung
line.** The listener recognises a situation; they do not decode a sentence (Slot 9, THE TOLL BOOTH /
FREE CHANNEL).
*Serves:* the P05-exclusive reveal engine `the-unchanged-canvas`.

### **F3 — THE DATE THAT WILL NOT COME OUT OF THE LINE** · weight 0.15

FORM RULE A, made countable **in the lyric**. A date-shaped phrase enters at sung line 11 and is

### **F4 — NO LINE ASKS TO BE MOURNED** · weight 0.14

The hardest facet in the run and this pair's primary failure mode. Every sung line is tested
individually against one question: *does this ask the listener to feel sorry for the singer?* A hit
is a **rewrite**, never a softening — softening a plea leaves a quieter plea. Banned moves, named so
they can be caught: "remember me," "I was here," "don't forget," any farewell address, any inventory
of her own virtues, any line whose function is to make the technician look careless. **He is not
cruel; he is at work, and the song must find that fact ordinary rather than tragic.**
⭐ A related trap: **the disclaimer.** A line that says *this is not a complaint* is a complaint with
a receipt. Cut the disclaimer; let the flatness do it.
*Serves:* LAW 3 (cost is never proven) · Constraint 6 · Slot 5 seat 6 (Morozov), tripwire STANDING.
