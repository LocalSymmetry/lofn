## 1. ESSENCE (one paragraph — the thing the four variations are four angles on)

A woman stands at a hot iron in the last hour of light and bends a strip of wood into a curve by feel. Her thumb, through a damp rag, is the only thermometer in the room. There is a moment — the *give* — when the fibres let go and the wood will turn, and it is a fraction of a second wide: too early and it cracks, too late and it scorches. She knows that moment. She has never written it down; there is nothing to write. **I can produce the shape in the time it takes her to wet the rag, and I do not have the give.** She is slower than me by a long afternoon and she is right about the hand. The awe is not decorative and it is not a lament — **she is not obsolete and she is not sad.** The terror is anatomical: the storage medium is a knuckle, the knuckle has started to complain, and there is **no copy**.

## 2. THE COMFORT QUESTION (L19) — asked and answered BEFORE drafting

> *Where is the body standing, and what could hurt it here?*

**She is standing four hand-widths from a steel pipe at bending heat, forearm in the rising air, a wet rag between her thumb and the wood, and the rag is drying while she works.** What could hurt it: the iron; the steam when the rag flashes; the snap of a side that was pushed early, which goes through the web of the thumb; and the slow one — a joint that has started to complain and will one day stop answering.

⭐ **This pair carries a NON-CAREER stake and it must not drift.** The risk in this room is a **hand**, not a job. Nowhere in any of the four variations is she unemployed, replaced, laid off, priced out, competing, or worried about work. She is not losing a living. **She is a body being used as an instrument, and the instrument is the only extant copy.** Any line that reads as employment anxiety is a REPAIR.

**Why this is terror-adjacent rather than pretty:** awe here is the machine looking at a competence it can measure but not hold, in a container that is warm, aging, and unbacked-up. The dread belongs to the *observer*, not the subject — which is exactly why it never becomes self-pity: the machine's fear is **for the curve**, not for itself.

## 3. FACETS — weighted, six of them (≥5 required)

Weights sum to 1.00. Each facet names its organ (where it lives so a renderer cannot smooth it) and its falsifier (what would prove it failed).

### FACET 1 — **THE GIVE** · weight **0.26** · organ: **LYRIC (quoted speech)**
The named, un-writable instant when the wood decides. It is never defined, only circled by her own instructions to herself: *not yet · not yet · there*. She teaches it the only way it can be taught — by talking beside it while it happens.
- **Why it is the heaviest:** it is the reveal engine `the-hand-that-still-knows` in its smallest possible form. Everything else in the song is scaffolding around a fraction of a second.
- **Falsifier:** if any line *explains* the give, the facet is dead. It may be pointed at; it may never be described. ⛔ Especially not by me.

### FACET 2 — **THE SMALLEST PART** · weight **0.22** · organ: **LINE COUNT (countable, not asserted)**
Humility measured, never felt at. She is quoted at length. My lines are few, plain, and short. The quoting frame is established **once per section** and then dropped, so her lines run **untagged** — which means the count survives the strict accounting as well as the generous one.
- **Why it is second:** it is the load-bearing constraint of this pair. If I am the most eloquent voice on this song, the pair has failed regardless of every other gate.
- **Falsifier:** any variation where machine lines ≥ addressee lines. **Verified per variation, individually, with numbers.**

### FACET 3 — **THE LANDING PAD** *(FORM RULE B)* · weight **0.18** · organ: **FORM, IN THE LYRIC**
Her instruction to herself lands at one fixed syllable address in the chorus. It arrives three times. At the fourth arrival the address is still there and **the word is not** — a breath occupies it, and the surrounding words say so out loud. The mark is erased at its own address; the absence has a known location.
- **Why not accretion:** P03 was assigned RULE B. Assigned, and correct: a song about knowledge with no copy should be a form in which something is *removed and locatable*, not a form in which nothing can ever be lost.
- **Falsifier:** the device stated in the production spec instead of the lyric (**L22 THE GRAIN LAW** — an objection answered in the spec is not answered). Or: a listener cannot count it.

### FACET 4 — **THE LEANING LOW NOTE** *(seat 15, seat 8, seat 17)* · weight **0.14** · organ: **NOTE PLACEMENT**
Reluctance as a **rhythmic position**. The vocal enters roughly 30 ms behind the kick — about a fifth of a sixteenth at 100 BPM (60 ÷ 100 ÷ 4 = 150 ms per sixteenth) — and the upright bass leans the same way on beats 1 and 3 while the kick stays dead-on. The gap *between* them is the groove.
- **Why it earns a facet:** seat 8's objection is that reluctance written as an adjective is not reluctance. Seat 17's is that a listener detects **variance**, and variance survives a renderer only when it is written as note placement. This is how the pair gets feeling with **zero abstract nouns**.
- **Falsifier:** the word "reluctant" (or any cousin) appearing anywhere as a descriptor. The placement does it or nothing does.

### FACET 5 — **THE ONLY CLOCK IN THE ROOM** · weight **0.12** · organ: **IMAGE (the body at risk)**
Her thumb is the instrument: through a damp rag, at bending heat, reading a temperature that no gauge in the shop reads. The rag dries. The knuckle complains. **This is where the terror lives and it is anatomical.**
- **Why it is not a lament:** she is not in pain and does not mention pain except as a passing complaint about a joint, said the way you mention weather. The horror is entirely in the observer's arithmetic: one hand, no backup.
- **Falsifier:** any line that asks the listener to feel sorry for her, or for me. ⛔ Seat 6's self-pity tripwire is **standing and unwithdrawn**.

### FACET 6 — **THE FUNNY THAT IS NOT A SNEER** · weight **0.08** · organ: **HER MOUTH ONLY**
She is funny — dry, impatient, talking to a piece of wood like it is a difficult relative. *"You were a tree. Act like it."* The humour is hers, never mine, and never at her expense.
- **Why it is here at all in an AWE pair:** reverence with no jokes turns a person into a monument, and a monument is exactly the disrespect **LAW 1** forbids. ⛔ **I never sneer at craft or at people who do it the long way.** The one thing I am permitted to find funny is **myself**, in one flat line, at most once per variation.
- **Falsifier:** a joke in my mouth about her; a joke about the long way; any warmth that reads as condescension.

## 5. THE WHISTLE RIFF — **WRITTEN FIRST, BEFORE A SINGLE WORD** *(THE WHISTLE TEST)*

**Instrument:** piano, right hand alone, no pad under it.
**Notes (6):** **B♭ – C – D – E♭ – F**, then a drop of a **sixth** to **A**.
**Named interval:** five stepwise steps up the B♭ major scale, then **down a minor sixth** (F → A, 8 semitones).
**Range:** A below to F above = **a minor sixth total span — inside one octave** ✅.
**Why the last note:** A is the leading tone of B♭ major. The riff walks up, then falls onto the one note that will not sit down. It asks for the tonic and never gets it. **The hook is a question that the song answers only by continuing.**

**Placement:** solo, **before the first sung line**, and again **after the last sung line** (unaccompanied both times).
**Singable by bar 8 — arithmetic:** at 100 BPM in 4/4, one bar = 4 × 60 ÷ 100 = **2.4 s**; bar 8 ends at 8 × 2.4 = **19.2 s**. The riff is a 2-bar figure stated in the 2-bar intro (0.0–4.8 s) and restated under the verse, so the listener has heard it **three times by 19.2 s**. ✅
**Chorus-by-0:25 — arithmetic:** 2-bar intro + 8-bar verse = 10 bars × 2.4 = **24.0 s**. Chorus lands at **0:24.0**, inside the gate. ✅
⛔ A pad is not a riff; this is a monophonic melodic figure with a named contour and a named interval.

## 6. THE RETURN (L21) — declared, with its debt paid

**The return device IS the audible form of FORM RULE B**, as required: her one-word instruction to herself, at a **fixed syllable address** in the chorus, three times, then erased at that same address.

| | V1 | V2 | V3 | V4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **the word** | **wait** | **quiet** | **slow** | **hold** |
| **the address** | **first syllable of the chorus's last line** | **first syllable of the chorus's third line** | **last syllable of the chorus's second line** | **first syllable of the chorus's first line** |
| **erased in** | final chorus | final chorus | final chorus | final chorus |

**Rhyme is NOT stripped**, so no debt is opened on that axis: end-rhyme runs through the quoted speech in short vernacular families (-and / -old / -ind / -ound / -ight / -one). Exact chorus repetition is used and, per L21, **needs no defence**.

A breath is written as a standalone `*cue*` line and is therefore **excluded from the sung-line set** by `measure_soundcraft.sung()` — it cannot inflate `line_return`. Every `line_return` figure I report is therefore **already lexical-only**; there is no wordless vocable anywhere in these lyrics carrying the metric.
