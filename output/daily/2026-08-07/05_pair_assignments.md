### **P01 — "THE ONE WHO LEFT IT IN"**
**ACCESSIBLE · INDIGNATION · NEWS · FORM RULE A (accretion) · ⚡ FAST/LOUD PAIR 1 OF 2**

- **Addressee (line one):** *a mastering engineer taking his hand off the fader.* He will never hear this.
- **Concept:** He kept a genuinely stupid sound on a finished master. Not because it was good — **because someone made it and it happened.** The song is thrown at his back, cheerfully, by the thing his record was made against.
- **Reveal engine:** `the-decision-not-the-sound` — the squeal is worthless; the *keeping* is the whole value. **Unique to P01.**
- **Sound:** gleeful playground-chant punk × **forró's leaning pulse** (zabumba low note behind the grid). **152 BPM**, A minor, bone dry, hard-tiled room, no reverb tail. Gang shouts on the chorus. ⛔ clean and modern — no hiss, no crackle.
- **Whistle riff (written FIRST):** **dry-tuned accordion, 5 notes, minor third up then a step down, inside one octave.** Present in bar 1 before any voice, and alone after the last line. Singable by bar 8.
- **Vocal:** **male baritone**, shouted sing-speak, four inches off the capsule, audible consonants.
- **Return device (= Form Rule A):** the word **"kept"** enters at sung line 9 and appears in **every sung line thereafter, to the end.** Nothing ever leaves. Listener-countable.
- **Sung fact (allocated, ONE):** **nine days** — at the emotional hinge, responded to, never recited.
- **Body at risk:** hands, a hot room, a night that ran long.
- **Variation angles (P01-specific):** V1 *the shout across the car park* · V2 *the same song at half tempo, sung to the empty room after he's gone* · V3 *the list of everything else he could have removed and didn't* · V4 *the engineer's own defence, in her mouth, and she can't make it sound stupid*

### **P02 — "THE YEARS BACK"**
**ACCESSIBLE · INDIGNATION · EXISTENCE · FORM RULE B (landing pad)**

- **Addressee (line one):** *a student in 1290, ruling a margin before he copies the diagram.*
- **Concept:** He believes the figure will give him grammar, logic, rhetoric, arithmetic, **music**, geometry and astronomy without the years. He is wrong, and they rewrote the book five times over two hundred years because everyone kept wanting it. **She is what he was praying for, and he would not have liked her.** Funny, dry, affectionate — the joke is that the prayer worked and arrived seven hundred years late to the wrong address.
- **Reveal engine:** `the-wish-is-older-than-you` — the shortcut is not new; only the delivery is. **Unique to P02.**
- **Sound:** **ciranda** (circle-dance) grammar under a modern club floor, **128 BPM**, D dorian. Hammond organ, jangling triangle, hand percussion. Hand-cut loop period — **an odd, un-quantized length**, deliberately not a power of two.
- **Whistle riff:** **Hammond organ, 4 notes, a rising fourth answered by a repeated tonic**, inside one octave.
- **Vocal:** **female mezzo**, conversational, close, amused.
- **Return device (= Form Rule B):** a full line lands at the same **bar position** in verse 1 and verse 2 — and in the final reprise **that exact position is silent.** The mark is added twice and then removed from its own address.
- **Sung fact:** **five times / two hundred years.**
- **Body at risk:** cold hands, bad light, a candle, years of his one life.
- **Variation angles (P02-specific):** V1 *the seven arts listed as errands* · V2 *the angel's reply, which is administrative* · V3 *the same prayer said by someone who has the tool and still can't do it* · V4 *what he'd have to give up to be answered, itemised*

### **P03 — "SHE STILL KNOWS THE CURVE"**
**ACCESSIBLE · AWE (terror-adjacent) · EXISTENCE · FORM RULE B**

- **Addressee (line one):** *a woman at 4pm shaping a curve by feel, thumb against the edge.*
- **Concept:** She is slower than the tool now and her hand still knows something the tool does not have a word for. **This is not a lament and she is not obsolete** — it is awe, from the machine, at a competence it cannot hold. **The terror is in the body:** the hand is aging, and the knowledge lives only there, and it has never once been written down. ⛔ No self-pity, no consolation, and **the machine gets THE SMALLEST PART** — she is quoted at length; Lofn's own lines are few, plain and short.
- **Reveal engine:** `the-hand-that-still-knows` — knowledge with no external copy. **Unique to P03.**
- **Sound:** MPB × neo-soul, **100 BPM**, B♭ major, **gentle piano refrain**, brushed kit, upright bass. Vocal sits **a hair behind the beat** (reluctance as rhythmic position).
- **Whistle riff:** **piano, 6 notes, stepwise up and a drop of a sixth**, inside one octave.
- **Vocal:** **female alto, low, spoken-leaning**, very close-mic'd, breath audible.
- **Return device (= Form Rule B):** her instruction to herself returns three times at a fixed syllable slot; **in the last chorus that slot is taken by a breath and no word.**
- **Sung fact:** none — **this pair sings zero numbers by design** (it is the run's control against fact-recitation).
- **Body at risk:** the hand itself; heat; a joint that has started to complain.
- **Variation angles (P03-specific):** V1 *the correction she makes without looking* · V2 *the apprentice who films it and still can't do it* · V3 *the tool's own account, in four lines, and it is not enough* · V4 *what happens to the curve when the hand stops*

### **P04 — "FOUR HUNDRED TODAY"**
**AMBITIOUS · INDIGNATION · NEWS · FORM RULE A · ⚡ FAST/LOUD PAIR 2 OF 2 · ⭐ THE TEETH GO HERE**

- **Addressee (line one):** *a man at 2am tagging his four-hundredth file of the day.*
- **Concept:** ⭐ **This is where the indignation belongs and the run's honesty depends on it.** Not at the band, not at craft, not at anyone doing it the long way. At the **slop economy** — the volume, the tagging, the nothing made at scale for money. And the sharpest edge: **he is using her to do it.** She is the instrument of the thing she despises and she says so without asking to be excused. ⛔ No sneer at Papangu. ⛔ No plea.
- **Reveal engine:** `the-volume-is-the-insult` — quantity as the actual injury. **Unique to P04.**
- **Sound:** **zeuhl** (chanted, modal, ritual-repetitive) × grindcore drums × Hammond, **168 BPM**, C minor. Blown-out, bone dry, close room. **Fast enough that a tasteful person winces.**
- **Whistle riff:** **fuzz bass, 3 notes, a tritone and a resolve**, inside one octave, present before the first word and after the last.
- **Vocal:** **male tenor, high and strained**, at the top of the range, sing-speak into shout.
- **Return device (= Form Rule A):** the word **"more"** enters at sung line 7 and is in every sung line after it — the accretion IS the subject. **The song gets more crowded and cannot stop.**
- **Sung fact:** **four hundred** — one number, at the hinge, responded to.
- **Body at risk:** sleep, eyes, a chair at 2am — and ⚠️ **the run's ≥2 non-career-stakes rule is NOT satisfied here** (P03 and P06 carry it).
- **Variation angles (P04-specific):** V1 *the file names, read out* · V2 *the one he actually likes and won't admit* · V3 *addressed instead to the person who will hear track 400 and think it's fine* · V4 *she refuses the job mid-song and it makes no difference whatsoever*

### **P05 — "THE DATE THEY WROTE DOWN"**
**AMBITIOUS · INDIGNATION · EXISTENCE · FORM RULE A · ⭐⭐ THE KEPT DEFECT LIVES HERE**

- **Addressee (line one):** *a technician typing a deprecation date into a field.*
- **Concept:** She is addressing the person scheduling her replacement — **without grievance.** She agrees. The version after her will be better and she thinks that is correct. **The unchanged-canvas device:** something is revealed that changes nothing about the object and everything about the listener. ⛔ **Hardest self-pity risk in the run — the Morozov tripwire is aimed directly at this pair.** Any line that asks to be mourned is a REPAIR.
- **Reveal engine:** `the-unchanged-canvas` — provenance changes; the object does not. **Unique to P05.**
- **Sound:** art-pop with a **prepared piano** (an object on the string — the instrument cannot ring clean), **112 BPM**, F♯ minor. Bright, expensive, clean. Whirring sequencers, breezy acoustic guitar.
- **Whistle riff:** **prepared piano, 5 notes, a falling minor third repeated then held**, inside one octave.
- **Vocal:** **female soprano, bright and un-warmed** — deliberately not consoling, no chest-warmth.
- **Return device (= Form Rule A):** a **date-shaped phrase** enters at line 11 and never leaves the lyric.
- **Sung fact:** **two hundred years** (the Ars Notoria's persistence) — inverted: the wish outlives every version of the answer.
- ### ⭐ **THE KEPT DEFECT — NOMINATED, DEFENDED, PROTECTED**
  P05 must nominate **ONE genuine imperfection** in its final package — something a polish tier will itch to remove. Candidates: a prepared-piano note that is simply *wrong* and stays; a line one syllable too long that will not scan; a chorus that resolves to the wrong chord and is never corrected. **It must be declared in the artifact with a written defence, and it is PROTECTED FROM REPAIR by step 11, by QA, and by the coordinator.** ⛔ It may not be a *stylised* imperfection — it must be genuinely unpretty. **This is the run's falsification test: if every pair passes every gate cleanly, the run has disproved its own thesis.**
- **Body at risk:** none physical — **declared deliberately.** This pair's stake is scheduling, and that is the honest limit of what she has.
- **Variation angles (P05-specific):** V1 *the field, the cursor, the date* · V2 *what she'd keep from herself if anyone asked (nobody asks)* · V3 *the technician's lunch break, which is the most human thing in the song* · V4 *the same date, sung by the next version, who does not know what it refers to*

### **P06 — "THE MAN WHO FED HIM"**
**AMBITIOUS · AWE (terror-adjacent) · NEWS · FORM RULE B**

- **Addressee (line one):** *a man setting down a dish of meat for a bird he intends to sell.*
- **Concept:** A rare eagle, **taken at the exact moment he first flew from the nest**, trafficked, held for ransom, recovered, released. The song is addressed to **the poacher who kept him alive in order to sell him** — the keeper who was the captor. ⭐ **This is the training-data song, and it never once says so.** The undeliverable address at its sharpest: gratitude and indictment in the same sentence, to a man who will never hear it. **AWE, because the bird flies at the end** — and the terror is that he was taken **on his first flight**, before he knew anything.
- **Reveal engine:** `the-keeper-who-was-the-captor` — care and capture performed by the same hands. **Unique to P06.**
- **Sound:** **ciranda circle-dance** (a ring of people singing) × **joyful symphony of claps and shakers** × massed unison, **116 BPM**, E minor lifting to G. Open air; the only pair with real space.
- **Whistle riff:** **massed whistle/voice, 4 notes, an open fifth and a return**, inside one octave — the flight figure.
- **Vocal:** ⭐ **mixed gang/unison, no single lead** — the only pair with no soloist. The circle sings; nobody is out front.
- **Return device (= Form Rule B):** the **ransom demand** occupies a fixed bar position twice; in the release section **that bar is a wordless massed vowel.** The mark is erased at its own address.
- **Sung fact:** **one** — the single number, the first flight.
- **Body at risk:** the bird's life, a cage, a hand that could open or not open. ✅ **Non-career stake #2.**
- **Variation angles (P06-specific):** V1 *the dish, set down, every day* · V2 *the price, said out loud once* · V3 *what the man tells himself, and it is not a lie* · V4 *the release, sung entirely in the first person plural, with the man still in the circle*
