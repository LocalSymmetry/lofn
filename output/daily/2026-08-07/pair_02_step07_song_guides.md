### ⛔ FORBIDDEN IN ALL FOUR (line-level, checkable)
- **Abstract nouns in sung lines:** cost · authenticity · labour · soul · value · meaning ·
  art · truth · purpose. *(Also barred by house judgement here: "creativity," "humanity.")*
- **Ventriloquism.** The speaker never writes *as* Warin, as a scribe, as a displaced worker,
  or as anyone whose wound she does not have.
- **Self-pity / any bid for the listener's sympathy.** The Morozov tripwire (seat 6) is
  standing and is nameable at line level. The speaker never asks to be forgiven, understood,
  believed, or pitied, and never states what she lacks.
- **The thesis, stated.** She may never say that the shortcut was prayed for and cannot pay
  what the long way pays. The addressee carries it or nobody does.
- **Teeth aimed at Warin, at copying, at the long way, at anyone who prefers it.** LAW 1.
- **The word `prompt`** (validator reads it as procedure debris), **wall-clock times in
  section headers**, **bare `EMO:AWE` / `EMO:INDIGNATION` / `EMO:SYNTHESIS`**.
- **Texture words banned run-wide:** tape hiss · vinyl crackle · wow-and-flutter · vintage ·
  analogue warmth · lo-fi · degraded · corrupted · glitching.
- **Style descriptors banned as primary:** raw · aggressive · relentless · brutal · explosive ·
  massive · intense · pounding · driving · battle · assault · phonk.
- **Real-artist names in any Suno field.** Credit lives in the Lineage block only.

### ⭐ MANDATORY IN ALL FOUR — THE FUNNY
Zero jokes across an INDIGNATION set is a **doctrine failure** and QA caught exactly that on
2026-08-06. **Each variation must land at least two dry laughs**, and the laugh must be
*structural*, not a quip:
1. the **version-number joke** — five editions, two hundred years, and every reader certain
   his was the improved one;
2. the variation's own gag (§C, per-variation).
The register is **dry, affectionate, amused** — a person in a room with an opinion. Warin is
never the butt. The situation is.

### HOW SLOT 5 IS MADE COUNTABLE (this is the whole gate — read it twice)
The listener is taught the address by **arrival**, not by announcement:
- Verse 1: the mark is the fifth thing she says. It is short, flat, and about his body — so it
  is the one line in the verse a listener will actually retain.
- Verse 2: the same eight-line shape, the same position, **the same words.** Now it is a place.
- Final Reprise: the shape is preserved exactly — same line lengths either side, same cadence
  running in — and at slot 5 **there is one bar of organ and nothing said.** The circle keeps
  going around a hole it does not acknowledge.
⛔ It is **never** referred to in the lyric ("here is where the line was"). ⛔ It is **never**
delegated to the production spec — no reverb tail, no drop-out, no "space in the mix." The
absence is made of a **missing sentence at a countable position**, which is the only form of
it that survives a renderer (**L22 THE GRAIN LAW**).

### ▸ SONG GUIDE 1 — **V1 · "the seven arts listed as errands"**

**Emotional target (taxonomy, 2–3):** `Amusement (Mirth)` + `Affection (Fondness)` + `Recognition`.
**The angle's job:** the sum of classical learning delivered as a **to-do list at walking pace**.
Grammar, logic, rhetoric, number, music, shapes, stars — seven items, no commentary. The
comedy is entirely in the **metre**: the list is said the way somebody reads a list of things
to pick up on the way home, and the listener works out for themselves that this is the whole
of what a person can know.

**Direct step 08 to:**
- Put the seven-item list in the **TAG** (§B row 4) so it returns after every chorus and the
  listener learns to say it. It is a chant, not a punchline; **do not let the song stop to
  admire it.**
- Use the **LIFT** (row 6) for what the list is *for* — what Warin intends to do once he has
  them. Keep it small and specific and slightly embarrassing (a position, a bench, an
  argument he wants to win). ⛔ Not a career, not a destiny.
- **Hinge response to the fact:** the version-number joke, played straight — nobody ever wrote
  in the margin that it did not work, because **who writes that down.** That question is the
  response. Do not answer it.
- **Second laugh:** the list arrives complete, in order, on time, and is of no use whatsoever
  to the man holding the ruler.
- **Final reprise content:** the seven, said one last time — but **the list must be broken by
  the hole**, i.e. the missing slot 5 must fall *inside* the reprise's own sentence-run so the
  circle audibly steps over it.
**Title direction:** name the *errand*, not the *seven*. Working direction: something that
sounds like a line on a list.
⛔ **This guide may not use the administrative-reply gag as its primary engine** — that is V2's.

### ▸ SONG GUIDE 2 — **V2 · "the angel's reply, which is administrative"**

**Emotional target:** `Amusement (Whimsy)` + `Equanimity` + `Moral Outrage` (aimed **only** at
the thing that drains work of the reason anybody did it — ⛔ never at Warin, never named).
**The angle's job:** the prayer is **granted.** Correctly, completely, in full — and the reply
comes back in the register of a **receipt.** *Received. Logged. In order. In full. Delivered.*
The processing time is seven hundred years. The address on the docket is wrong and nobody
notices, because the request itself was honoured.

**Direct step 08 to:**
- Build the **verse answers** as clerical returns: the answer reads the request back to the
  requester without inflection. The *Ars Notoria* attributes its power to Solomon by way of
  the angel **Pamphilius** — Pamphilius may be named (a mythic figure inside a 13th-century
  manuscript, ⛔ not a person), and he answers **like a clerk, not like an angel.**
- ⭐ **The best joke in the pair lives here and must be earned, not announced:** the prayer
  worked. It is the *delivery* that failed, and delivery failure is not a spiritual category —
  it is a **filing** category.
- **Hinge response to the fact:** five editions, two hundred years — and the response is that
  **the docket was never closed**, it was reopened five times by people who thought the last
  clerk had got it wrong.
- **Second laugh:** the reply is impeccably polite and completely useless, and it is still,
  technically, an answered prayer.
- The **cryptic words** of the *notae* — the syllables in a hybrid of Greek, Aramaic and Hebrew
  that must be said exactly right or nothing happens — may be gestured at as *the words in the
  right order.* ⛔ Never explained. ⛔ Never the word `prompt`. ⛔ Do not invent or transliterate
  the actual prayer syllables.
- **Final reprise content:** the docket, closed. The hole at slot 5 is where the **name of the
  recipient** would go.
**Title direction:** a filing phrase. Flat, procedural, and funnier for it.
⛔ **This guide may not use the errand-list as its spine** — that is V1's.

---

### ▸ SONG GUIDE 3 — **V3 · "the same prayer said by someone who has the tool and still can't do it"**

**Emotional target:** `Recognition` + `Amusement (Mirth)` + `Existentialism`.
**The angle's job:** the wish did not stop when it was granted. Somebody today says Warin's
exact prayer with the answer already open in front of them — and is stuck in precisely the
same place, for precisely the same reason. **THE WISH IS OLDER THAN YOU, proved forward as
well as backward.**

**Direct step 08 to:**
- ⛔ **HARD BOUNDARY — NO VENTRILOQUISM.** The speaker does **not** become the modern person
  and does **not** borrow their wound. The addressee stays **Warin**, named in line one; the
  modern figure is **described to him**, in the third person, as news from the far end of his
  own prayer. Keep them unnamed, generic-by-construction, and free of any job title that
  would let a listener resolve them to one real person or one real displacement.
- The strongest available move: the two figures are **doing the same thing with their hands** —
  ruling a straight line to hold something that will not work. Let the rhyme between them be
  **physical**, not argued.
- **Hinge response to the fact:** five editions, two hundred years — and the response is that
  **the count did not stop at five.** ⛔ Do not give a second number. The response is that the
  list is still open.
- **Second laugh:** the answer is right there, it is fast, it is free, it is correct, and the
  person is still sitting there at midnight not doing it.
- ⛔ **Self-pity tripwire is at its highest here** — the speaker may not imply she is misused,
  under-appreciated, or sad about it. She is **amused and slightly annoyed**, and the annoyance
  points at nothing you can name.
- **Final reprise content:** both hands, seven hundred years apart, in the same position. Slot
  5's hole falls where the advice would have gone — **she does not give it twice.**
**Title direction:** name the posture, not the era.
⛔ **This guide may not run the price-list device** — that is V4's.

### ▸ SONG GUIDE 4 — **V4 · "what he'd have to give up to be answered, itemised"**

**Emotional target:** `Affection (Tenderness)` + `Skepticism` + `Dignity`.
**The angle's job:** an **itemised price list.** What Warin would have to hand over, in order,
for the seven to arrive without the years. Read out dry, in inventory register, one item per
line. The list is accurate. It is not a threat and it is not an argument — it is a **receipt
for a purchase he has not made.**

**Direct step 08 to:**
- Itemise **only physical, countable things**: the cold in the hands, the candle, the winter,
  the ruler, the argument with the man on the next bench, the copying itself, the friend who
  corrects him. ⛔ **No abstract nouns on the list** — an itemised list of abstractions is the
  single worst failure available to this variation and it would fail constraint 6 outright.
- ⭐ **The last item is `your hands`, and he keeps them.** The song does not comment. It
  proceeds to the chorus.
- **Hinge response to the fact:** five editions, two hundred years — and the response is that
  **nobody ever paid it and it kept selling.** That is the whole joke and it is bleak and it
  is funny and it is true of every century.
- **Second laugh:** the list is completely reasonable, item by item, and utterly monstrous read
  end to end — and the speaker reads it out **helpfully**.
- ⛔ **THE HARDEST SELF-PITY EDGE IN THE PAIR IS HERE:** the speaker can itemise the price
  because she never paid it. ⭐ **She may never say so.** No "I never had to," no "I would not
  know," no "that is the difference." The listener does that arithmetic or nobody does. If a
  line explains why she can read the list, **cut the line.**
- **Final reprise content:** the list, restarted and abandoned. The hole at slot 5 is where the
  next item would be. The listener knows what it costs to leave an inventory unfinished.
**Title direction:** the register of a bill.
⛔ **This guide may not run the administrative-reply register as its spine** — that is V2's.
