## C. THE AIM — stated before writing, because the whole run's honesty hangs on it

The teeth go at **the volume**. Not at the band. Not at analogue. Not at craft. Not at "purists." Not at anyone doing it the long way. Not at the listener either.

The charge in this song is **not that the four hundred tracks are bad.** That is the trap and it is also a lie — some of them are fine. The charge is that there are **four hundred of them and nobody heard one all the way through.** Quantity is the injury. Quality is not on trial, which is precisely why the song can never be read as contempt for people who make things by hand: *making a thing well is never once criticised in it.*

⭐ And the sharpest edge: **she is the instrument.** She made all four hundred. She is good at it. She says so, and she does not ask to be excused, and she does not ask to be pitied. **The addressee is not her enemy and she is not his victim — they are the same machine and one of them has a body.**

⛔ **The thesis is never stated.** The band, tape, nine days, analogue, "the long way," and every proxy for them are **completely absent from all four variations.** That absence *is* the execution of THE UNDELIVERABLE ADDRESS: the shared enemy is audible only in who each party is talking to, and P04 talks to exactly one person — the man in the chair.

## D. THE EIGHT SCORING FACETS (weighted; `gates.yaml step06_min_facets: 8`, brief asked ≥5)

> Facets are the rubric every later P04 prompt is scored against. Each is written so it can be **checked**, not admired.

**F1 · THE COUNTABLE CROWD · weight 0.18**
The word **"more"** enters at sung line 7 and is present in **every** sung line thereafter, to the last. A listener with no instructions can count it. The song must audibly *get more crowded* — each section adding a voice or a doubling and never subtracting one — so that the accretion is heard as congestion, not as a lyrical tic. **Fails if:** the device lives in the production spec (L22 / seat 12), or if a single sung line after line 7 lacks the word.

**F2 · THE ADDRESS THAT CANNOT LAND · weight 0.15**
Line one names **one man doing one physical thing** — a hand, a key, a screen, a chair. He will never hear this. No "you" as humanity, no "the world," no abstraction as recipient. **Fails if:** the addressee widens into a category, or if the song ever explains why it is addressed to him.

**F3 · THE AIM, VERIFIED LINE BY LINE · weight 0.15**
No line, read alone and uncharitably, can be heard as contempt for people who make things by hand, slowly, or without machines. Quality is never the charge; volume is. **Fails if:** any line implies the four hundred are bad *as the grievance*, or if slowness/handwork is praised as virtue (which would smuggle the thesis in through the back door).

**F4 · COMPLICIT, NOT WOUNDED · weight 0.14**
The speaker states plainly that she made all of it, that she is good at it, and that nobody forced her — and she asks for nothing. The Morozov tripwire is aimed here. **Fails if:** any line bids for the listener's sympathy, claims injury, or performs reluctance it does not act on. *Test at line level: could this sentence appear in a complaint? If yes, cut or invert it.*

**F5 · TEETH AS TEMPO AND PLACEMENT, NOT AS ADJECTIVE · weight 0.12**
**168 BPM**, C minor, male tenor at the very top of the range, four inches off the capsule, sing-speak escalating into shout, bone dry, close room, no reverb tail. Fast enough that a tasteful person winces — proven by the number and the vocal placement, never by a word like *ferocious*. **Fails if:** the style prompt reaches for a banned intensity token (raw / aggressive / relentless / brutal / explosive / massive / intense / pounding / driving / battle / assault / phonk) to do work the tempo should be doing.

**F6 · THE THREE-NOTE HOOK, WRITTEN FIRST · weight 0.10**
**Fuzz bass: C → F♯ → G.** A tritone and a one-semitone settle, inside a perfect fifth, well inside one octave. Alone in bar 1 before any word, alone again after the last. Singable by bar 8 because it is three notes and it repeats every bar from the first. **Fails if:** it is a texture, a pad, or a riff that only exists under vocals.

**F7 · THE NUMBER ANSWERED, NOT RECITED · weight 0.09**
**Four hundred** — the run's only fact allocated to this pair — arrives at the emotional hinge and is **immediately traded against something**, never listed. No second numeric fact anywhere in the sung lines: not the hour, not the take number, not the file version. **Fails if:** a verse becomes a manifest of the day's statistics, or if a second number appears anywhere in the lyric.

**F8 · THE FUNNY IS NOT OPTIONAL · weight 0.07**
Reluctant Pop Star: bratty, blunt, sardonic. At minimum one genuine laugh per variation, arriving from **specificity** — a file name, a folder called *final* and then *final again*, a man blinking at the same rate as the thing he is running. Zero jokes across an INDIGNATION set is a doctrine failure (QA caught exactly that on 2026-08-06). **Fails if:** the humour is a sneer at a person rather than a laugh at the arithmetic.
