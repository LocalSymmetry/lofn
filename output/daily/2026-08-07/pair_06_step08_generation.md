## 5. DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK (ONE pass, as the handoff requires)

### What this would actually produce on Suno

A wide outdoor-sounding track at a walking 116: four whistled notes alone, hand claps and shakers arriving, then a large group of men and women singing the *same* melody in octaves — no lead, no harmony stack, no answering solo — over a warm nylon-string guitar and one deep drum that sits fractionally late. It builds by voice count, drops to claps at the bridge, lifts a third into major, and ends on the whistle in air.

### ⭐ *"Name the one way this renders generic."*

**It renders generic if Suno hears "circle of voices, claps, major lift" and gives back a well-lit festival singalong** — the folk-anthem default, warm and weightless, with the terror boiled off. The lyric is quiet and plain by design, and a renderer will happily set plain words to uplift. The result would be *pretty*, and pretty is the failure state for a song whose subject is a cage.

### SELF-REPAIR (applied once, inside budget)

The repair is **in the writing, not the spec** (L22) — a production note asking for "menace" would be smoothed to nothing:

1. **The bridge stops the ring.** The one place the song must not be a singalong is the hinge, so the hinge has **no percussion but claps and no drum at all** and the shortest line in the song stands alone: `He had one flight.` A singalong cannot absorb four syllables and a stop.
2. **The terror is placed AFTER the lift, not before it.** `He did not know the shape of a field at all.` is the **last full line of the release**, sung in G major at the widest point. The generator can make it sound joyful; it cannot make it mean *he has no map*. The uplift becomes the vehicle for the dread instead of a defence against it.
3. **The final chorus widens by adding voices, never volume** — written into the music prompt as an instruction the generator can actually execute, and into the exclude field as `volume-only final chorus` (V4).
4. **No fade-out.** Terminal silence survives the renderer; the whistle ends alone and the track stops.
