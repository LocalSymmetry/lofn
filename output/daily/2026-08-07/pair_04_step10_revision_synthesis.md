## MEASUREMENT DISCLOSURE — READ THIS BEFORE TRUSTING ANY NUMBER ABOVE

**1. The accretion word and `line_return`.** The brief warned that the device would inflate `line_return` artificially. **It does not, and here is why:** `line_return()` counts *exact whole-line repeats*, so a single recurring token cannot move it. The measured `line_return` of ~0.28 in every variation comes entirely from the **chant returning byte-identical four times** (24 of 86 lines). The companion measurement — every instance of the accreted word stripped from every line before measuring — returns **the identical `line_return`**, which is the proof: the return is structural, not lexical. Exact repetition needs no defence (L21).

**2. Where the device DOES distort a metric: `rhyme_return`.** Raw `rhyme_return` is 0.337–0.488. With the accreted word stripped it falls to **0.093–0.244**. The word ends a large number of lines, and `strict_end_rhyme()` keys on the last three characters of the final word, so *ore* recurring within ±4 lines is counted as a rhyme return. **That is a real, disclosed inflation.** It is also, honestly, what the song sounds like: the same syllable landing again and again is the entire point of the form. But a reader should know that **V2's and V4's lexical rhyme-craft, measured independently of the device, sits below the 0.30 floor** (0.093 and 0.116). V1 is the strongest on this axis un-aided (0.244). ⛔ I am reporting this rather than letting the raw number stand alone.

**3. `words_per_line` FLAGs on all four (8.00–8.59 against the 7.5 ceiling).** The device costs exactly one word per line across 80 of 86 lines. **Excluding the accreted word, every variation is under the ceiling: 6.47 · 7.00 · 7.26 · 7.03.** The lyric is not drifting toward prose; the drift is the device, and the device is the subject.
