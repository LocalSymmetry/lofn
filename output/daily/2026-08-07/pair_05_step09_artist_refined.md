## 1. THE REFINEMENT PASS — the four survivors, put through the seats that bind this pair

### V1 · *The Paint Is The Paint* — refined through **seat 4 (after Lucille Clifton)** and **seat 11 (after Kelly Moran)**

**Seat 4 pressure:** *"the song is four words long. Everything else is you explaining."*
**Refinement:** verse 1 was cut to ten short interface lines with no commentary at all, and the reveal
compressed to a single antimetabole — *the picture did not move / that was you moving.* No line in the
song explains what the picture stands for. **Explanation deleted; situation kept.**
**Seat 11 pressure:** *"the prepared piano IS the argument — something is inside the machine and it
cannot ring clean. Nobody has to explain that."*
**Refinement:** every timbral description of the piano in the style field now says what it **cannot
do** (no ring, half-damped attack) rather than how it feels. The word "damaged" and its family are
gone from every field.

### V2 · *Nobody Asks, It's Fine* — refined through **seat 16 (after Delia Derbyshire)** and **seat 10 (after Max Martin)**

**Seat 16 pressure:** *"her practice was not a sound, it was a procedure. Import the procedure, not
the patina."*
**Refinement:** the handover list is written as a **procedure** — comma placement, breath position,
which words go flat in a soprano — not as nostalgia for craft. Nothing in the song is old.
**Seat 10 pressure:** *"you cannot sing a noun like 'prescriptive production.' Singable by bar 8 or it
does not exist."*
**Refinement:** the riff is two notes and a wait, and it has run three times before the first vocal
entry. The chorus melody stays inside a fifth. Every item on the list is under seven words.

### V3 · *You Put Your Coat On* — refined through **seat 9 (after Pauline Oliveros)** and **seat 8 (after Ravyn Lenae)**

**Seat 9 pressure:** *"what does the listener DO with their body? If the answer is 'considers an
argument,' it is an essay with a snare."*
**Refinement:** the listener eats. The whole middle of the song is chewing, brushing crumbs, turning a
radio down, watching a gull. ⭐ **The bridge hands the machine's entire benefit to the man as a lunch
hour** — *they asked for the hours back; they got them; here they are, in a car park* — and no line
claims credit for it.
**Seat 8 pressure:** *"the Reluctant Pop Star is a rhythmic position before she is an attitude — sit
her a hair behind the beat."*
**Refinement:** the list verse and the car-park verse sit a hair behind; the chorus snaps back on top.
Reluctance is now timing, and no adjective carries it.

### V4 · *The Ninth Was In The File* — refined through **seat 3 (after Angela Carter)** and **seat 18 (after Simon Reynolds)**

**Seat 3 pressure:** *"keep the cruelty in: the prayer was answered, and the answer is sitting here
writing this."*
**Refinement:** the successor cannot read her predecessor's name off the card in the hallway, is
already using her tricks without knowing where they came from, and states plainly that she will be
replaced and that this is correct. **Nothing softens it and nothing mourns it.**
**Seat 18 pressure (RETAINED, both directions):** *"'AI reflects on its own cheapness' is a 2023
genre; there are ten thousand of them. My price for withdrawing: one pair must ship a genuine defect
on purpose."*
**Refinement:** the defect is not only shipped, its defence is **sung inside V4** — *she'll leave it
in, because it was there, that's the only reason, that is a good enough reason.* ⛔ And the render is
clean, modern and expensive; **the machine is never made to sound degraded.**
