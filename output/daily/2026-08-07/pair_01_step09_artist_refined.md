## 2. THE REPAIRS — EACH ONE NAMED, SCOPED, AND VERIFIED PER VARIATION

> ⭐ **Scope discipline (handoff §5.2):** every repair below states **which variations it touched**. A repair applied to one variation and reported pair-wide is a fictional fix. The verification table in §3 re-checks every device in **each variation individually**, not once for the pair.

### R1 — The returning tag line · **applied to V1, V2, V3, V4 (all four, individually)**
**Trigger:** the voluntary lexical-only companion measurement of `line_return` came in at **0.171** on the V1 first pass — below 0.20 — meaning the primary metric (0.244, passing) was leaning harder on the all-caps gang shouts than I wanted. **The floor was met; I repaired anyway, in the writing.**
**Repair:** a section-closing refrain — **"He kept his hand off. That's the kept part."** (V3: *"You kept your hand off…"*, because V3 is in second person until he walks away) — now closes **three** sections in every variation.
**Why it is craft and not a gate-hack:** it makes **Flair #1 (THE HAND OFF THE FADER)** an audible refrain instead of a single image, and it re-states the reveal engine — *the keeping is the whole value* — at three fixed structural positions. L21: song is made of returns.
**Measured effect:** lexical-only `line_return` **0.171 → 0.214** in all four. Primary `line_return` **0.244 → 0.279**.

### R2 — The number stated once, not twice · **applied to V1 (originated), then held as a rule for V2, V3, V4**
**Trigger:** V1's first-pass bridge opened two consecutive lines with *"Nine days"*. That is one fact, but it reads as recitation on the second hit, and `max_sung_numeric_facts` deserves an unambiguous answer rather than an argued one.
**Repair:** line 50 rewritten from *"Nine days, and he kept the worst thing in it"* → *"And the thing he kept was the worst in it."* The number lands once and the next line **responds to it**. Also cut in the same pass: *"Nothing on that master got kept by luck"* — a line that **reasoned** where every neighbour **shows** (seat 4's charge).
**Measured effect:** numeric-bearing sung lines **2 → 1** in V1. **Verified separately: V2 = 1, V3 = 1, V4 = 1.**

### R3 — V3's three lexical defects · **applied to V3 ONLY. V1, V2, V4 were separately checked and were already clean.**
The automated scan found three, all in V3, none anywhere else:
1. *"Hands on the same **two** knobs since noon"* → *"Hands on the same knobs since noon."* — a second number word in a song allowed exactly one.
2. *"the buzz you kept **meaning** to trace"* → *"the buzz you kept saying you'd trace."* — **"meaning" is on the banned-abstract-noun list** (constraint 6). It was a verb, not the noun; the list does not care, and neither should I.
3. *"a record **no-one** was in"* → *"a record nobody was in."* — tripped the numeric scanner on "one". A false positive, but ⭐ **L27 says fix the scanner, not the line, when the scanner hits a correct line** — here the line was equally good either way and "nobody" is the better word in a song whose subject is a person being there, so I took the line change and left the scanner honest.

### R4 — V3 alliteration headroom · **applied to V3 ONLY**
V3 measured **11.52 / 100w** against an 11.0 floor — the pair's thinnest margin. *"You kept the click of the kick pedal"* → *"You kept the kick pedal's click."* — tighter, funnier, and k-initial words now sit inside one window. **11.52 → 11.55.** ⚠️ Reported honestly: the margin moved almost not at all. **V3 remains the tightest alliteration margin in the pair and I am flagging it rather than claiming it comfortable.**

## 4. THE DESCRIBE-RENDER SELF-CHECK — ONE PASS, ONE REPAIR

**Prediction — what this prompt would actually produce on Suno.** A genuinely fast, dry, close-sounding punk track in A minor: a shouted male baritone with hard consonants, a six-voice gang answering the chorus, picked eighth-note bass and a small tight kit with no reverb tail. The accordion hook will be present and probably prominent because it opens alone. The forró lean will be **partially quantized away** — the renderer will straighten some of it — and the one-off rubber-toy squeal will likely be either ignored or rendered as a generic cartoon squeak. The lyric's chant density will survive completely, because Suno does not edit words.

**⚡ Adversarially: name the ONE way this would render generic.**
> **The accordion becomes garnish.** "Gang-shout punk chorus" is one of the most heavily travelled lanes the model has. Six voices yelling over eighth-note bass and a dry kit is a shape it can produce with its eyes closed — and when it does, it will lead with guitars, and the accordion will be relegated to a colour that decorates bar 1 and then disappears for three minutes. The song would land as competent, forgettable oi-punk with a novelty intro. **That is the single most likely failure and it is not a lyric problem.**

**Self-repair (one, inside the max-3 budget) — R5, applied to all four prompts individually:**
The accordion is now made **structurally impossible to demote**, in three independent ways per prompt, none of which is an adjective:
1. It is **alone before any voice** (already specified).
2. It is **alone after the last line** (already specified).
3. ⭐ **New: "Guitars … entering only after the hook has been stated twice."** The guitars are now *late by instruction*, so the hook owns the opening by arrangement rather than by emphasis.
Plus, per variation: V3 doubles the downbeat with **low accordion**, and V4 puts a **second accordion an octave under the gang answer** — so in the two fastest variations the accordion is inside the chorus, not decorating it.
