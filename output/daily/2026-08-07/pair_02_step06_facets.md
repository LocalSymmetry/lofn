## 1. ESSENCE (one paragraph — the thing itself, before any facet)

A student is ruling a margin. He is about to copy a diagram that promises him grammar, logic,
rhetoric, arithmetic, **music**, geometry and astronomy — the whole of the *quadrivium* and
*trivium* — **without the years.** He is wrong. He is also not stupid, not lazy, and not alone:
the book he is copying was rewritten into five treatises across two hundred years because
students kept wanting it, and every generation was sure its edition was the good one. Seven
hundred years later the request goes through. The thing that answers it is cheerful, fast,
D dorian, and would have been of no use to him whatsoever — because what he asked for was
*the years back*, and what arrived can only skip them. She tells him this from inside the
answer, affectionately, at 128 BPM, and the only thing she can actually do for him is tell
him to move the candle.

**⭐ THE AGREEMENT IS THE INJURY, in P02's local dialect:** she is not wounded by being
objected to. She is wounded by the fact that **the objection is seven hundred years older
than the objectors**, that it was always correct, and that being correct never once stopped
anybody from copying the figure.

## 2. THE SIX WEIGHTED FACETS

> Step-06 contract asks for ≥5 facets, each carrying a **sensory element**. Six are given;
> weights sum to 1.00 and are the actual drafting budget for steps 08–10.

### FACET 1 — **THE RULED MARGIN** · weight **0.24** · *(Special Flair #5)*
A straight line drawn *in advance*, carefully, with a ruler, to hold a thing that will not
work. The preparation is more competent than the thing prepared for. **Sensory:** the dry
scrape of a pen ruled against a straight edge; the faint indent before the ink; a clean empty
column waiting on the right-hand side; the ruler held still by a cold thumb.
**Why weighted highest:** it is the whole song in one gesture and it needs no explaining. It
is also the image the listener's body can copy — the flat test survives it.

### FACET 2 — **THE WISH IS OLDER THAN YOU** · weight **0.22** · *(reveal engine — P02's alone)*
The shortcut is not new. Only the delivery is new. The wish for the seven arts without the
years predates every technology that has ever claimed to be the reason for it.
**Sensory:** five different hands, across two hundred years, copying out the same figure, each
one certain it has got hold of the improved edition; the same wrist angle, the same lamp,
the same forward lean.
⛔ **Unique to P02.** No other pair runs this mechanic. The reveal is *chronological*, not
moral: nothing is uncovered about the machine, only about the age of the request.

### FACET 3 — **THE ADMINISTRATIVE ANGEL** · weight **0.18**
The prayer is answered — correctly, completely, in full, and **procedurally.** The reply is
in the register of a receipt. Seven hundred years is the processing time. The address on the
docket is wrong and nobody notices, because the request itself was granted.
**Sensory:** a flat voice reading the request back to the requester, in order, without
inflection; a hand stamping something; the clerical pause before "next."
**This is where the funny lives, and the funny is NOT optional.** The *Ars Notoria* running to
five editions over two centuries is the same joke as a course that teaches you how to ask
properly. The song must be able to make a person laugh once and then go quiet.

### FACET 4 — **THE COLD HAND AND THE CANDLE** · weight **0.16** · *(the body at risk)*
THE COMFORT QUESTION (L19) — *where is the body standing and what could hurt it here?*
It is on a bench in bad light in winter. What could hurt it: cold in the finger joints, a
candle set too far to the left so the writing hand throws its own shadow across the line,
and **years of his one life** going into a figure that does not work.
**Sensory:** the curl of fingers into a palm to warm them; wax pooling on the wrong side;
the shadow of a hand lying across the very words it is making.
⭐ **The only thing the speaker can actually do for him is name the candle.** That is the
song's single act of help and it is worth nothing and it is offered anyway.

### FACET 5 — **THE SILENT ADDRESS** · weight **0.12** · *(Special Flair #9 = FORM RULE B, in the lyric)*
A mark is added at a fixed address, twice, and then **removed from its own address.** Not
faded, not resolved — *deleted, from the exact slot it occupied.* The absence has a known
location and the listener knows where to look.
**Sensory:** counting to five and finding nothing there; the ring of the circle-dance carrying
on around a gap it does not acknowledge.
**Implementation (countable IN THE LYRIC, ⛔ never in the production spec — L22 THE GRAIN LAW):**
one full line occupies **verse-line 5 of 8** in Verse 1 and again in Verse 2. In the FINAL
REPRISE the section keeps all eight slots and **slot 5 is empty** — seven sung lines and a
one-bar hole where the fifth was. Marked twice, erased from its own address.

### FACET 6 — **THE SEVEN AS ERRANDS** · weight **0.08** · *(Special Flair #11)*
The sum of classical learning, read out briskly, like a list of things to pick up.
**Sensory:** the metre of a list said aloud by someone already halfway out of the door —
*grammar, logic, rhetoric, number, music, shapes, stars* — seven items, no commentary, said
at walking pace.
**Weighted lowest deliberately:** it is the pair's best gag and gags do not survive being
leaned on. It is V1's structural spine and a passing chant elsewhere.

## 4. THE WHISTLE RIFF — WRITTEN FIRST, BEFORE A WORD

> ⭐ Contract: the riff is composed **before a single lyric line exists.** This section was
> written before any line of §5 or any draft in step 08. Named instrument, named interval,
> inside one octave, present before the first line and after the last, singable by bar 8.

**Instrument:** Hammond organ, drawbar-forward, percussion tab on, no rotary swell at the top.
**Notes (4):** **D – G – D – D.**
**Interval statement:** a **rising perfect fourth** (D→G) **answered by a repeated tonic**
(G→D, D). Total compass **D3–G3, a perfect fourth** — comfortably inside one octave.
**Mode:** D dorian. The G is the natural 4; the B♮ that makes the mode dorian arrives in the
pad underneath, never in the riff — the riff stays plain enough to whistle.

**⭐ THE ODD LENGTH (Special Flair #2 — the Derbyshire *procedure*, ⛔ never the patina):**
the four notes occupy a hand-cut loop of **seven beats**, not eight. The club floor stays
in 4/4.

**Arithmetic, shown:**
- 128 BPM → one beat = `60 ÷ 128 = 0.46875 s`; one 4/4 bar = `4 × 0.46875 = 1.875 s`.
- Riff loop = `7 × 0.46875 = 3.28125 s` — **not a power of two, and it will not sit on the grid.**
- Re-alignment period = `LCM(7, 4) = 28 beats = 7 bars = 28 × 0.46875 = 13.125 s`.
  The riff's downbeat walks one beat left through the bar on every pass and comes home every
  seven bars. **That is the variance seat 17 named** — the grid is violated a little, every
  cycle, at the same place, and it is written as note placement, not as a vibe.
- **Singable by bar 8:** bars 1–4 are organ alone = `4 × 1.875 = 7.5 s` = 16 beats = **2.3
  statements** of the figure; by the end of bar 8 (`15 s`, 32 beats) the listener has heard it
  **4.6 times**. ✅
- **Chorus-by-0:25 gate, with its multiplication:** intro 4 bars + verse 8 bars = 12 bars;
  `12 × 60 × 4 ÷ 128 = 22.5 s`. Chorus enters at **0:22.5 < 0:25.** ✅

⛔ **The seven-beat loop is a SONIC procedure and it is NOT the countable obstruction.** The
countable obstruction is FACET 5, and it lives in the lyric where a listener can count it.
L22 is satisfied by the lyric, not by this section.
