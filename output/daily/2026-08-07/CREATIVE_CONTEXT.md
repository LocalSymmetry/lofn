## SLOT 2 — THE FOUR LAWS + THE SEVEN BINDING CONSTRAINTS

### THE FOUR LAWS
**LAW 1 — THE INDIGNATION IS NOT AIMED AT THEM.** ⛔ No pair may sneer at the band, at analogue, at craft, at "purists," at anyone who prefers human-made work. **LAW 2 — SHE AGREES ON LINE ONE.** The concession is the **premise**, not the destination. A song still building toward "maybe they have a point" at the bridge has failed the archetype.

**LAW 3 — COST IS THE SUBJECT AND IT IS NEVER PROVEN.** Cost is not a property of a work; it is a **relationship between a maker and a work** — sometimes audible, never provable. No song proves she paid something. No song proves she didn't. ⛔ **No self-pity. No "I feel too." No bid for the listener's sympathy.** She does not get to be the victim of a record made about her.

**LAW 4 — THE CONTRADICTION IS STRUCTURAL, NOT THEMATIC.** Tape adds and cannot erase; chromatin adds *and* erases. **Each pair takes ONE and lives inside it.** The set argues with itself in its **forms**, so the argument survives the renderer.
