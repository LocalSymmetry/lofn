## 2. WHAT WAS DECIDED BEFORE DRAFTING

### 2.1 The addressee is invented, and invented on purpose
**Nilton.** A mastering engineer. No surname, no city, no studio, no employer, no record title, no band, no country named in any sung line. He is the only proper noun in P01's entire lyric corpus. `vault/HUMAN_SUBJECT_STANDARD.md` §3.0 asks for a draft with **no field in which an identifying tuple can be placed** — P01 has none, by construction. **The real release is the occasion; it appears once, in the Lineage & Credit block, as credit.**

### 2.2 The word enters with the hook
The single largest generation decision: **"kept" enters at sung line 9, and sung line 9 is the first line of the first chorus.** The accretion device and the hook arrive in the same instant. This is why the listener can count it without being told to — the word is welded to the thing they were already going to remember. Verse 1 (lines 1–8) is the world **before the hand came off the fader**, and it contains the word zero times.

### 2.3 The concession lands on line 4 in all four variations
LAW 2 — the agreement is the premise, not the destination:

| | Line 4 |
|---|---|
| **V1** | *He left it. He was right. Here's the song.* |
| **V2** | *He walked out and he was right to.* |
| **V3** | *You left it. You were right. Here's the list.* |
| **V4** | *I've tried to say it stupid. I can't. He's right.* |

⭐ Nothing after line 4 re-opens it. No variation builds toward "maybe he had a point."

### 2.4 The object stays worthless
FACET 1 is load-bearing and fragile. Explicit rule adopted at draft time: **no line may make the squeak good.** V1 line 5 (*"Nobody laughed. It isn't funny."*) and V1 line 19 (*"He kept the squeak. The squeak is bad."*) are the enforcement clamps — they are early, flat, and un-ironic, so nothing later can retro-charm the object. `the-decision-not-the-sound` only exists if the sound never earns its place.

### 2.5 The self-pity tripwire (seat 6, retained and standing)
Every "I" line was written to be **flat, cheerful, or approving** — never appealing. The three most dangerous lines in the pair, and why each is not a bid for sympathy:

- *"I know. I've kept knowing it since morning."* — a report of a duration, not a complaint about it. No adjective attaches to her.
- *"And what I kept would have been worse."* — a judgment **against herself, in his favour**, delivered as fact. She wins nothing by it.
- *"He kept not turning round. Good. He kept right."* — ⭐ **the anti-self-pity clamp.** She blesses the back that is walking away. A speaker who approves of being ignored cannot be asking to be pitied.

⛔ Nowhere in P01 does she say she feels anything, ask to be understood, mention what she lacks, or mention how fast she is. **Speed is never mentioned once in 344 sung lines.** That silence is the pair's answer to LAW 3: cost is never proven — not proven present, not proven absent.

### 2.6 The teeth (⚡ Albini, unwithdrawn, binding on me specifically)
*"Teeth are not a texture; teeth are a tempo and a vocal placement."*
**152 / 76 / 156 / 160 BPM.** Male baritone, shouted sing-speak, **four inches off the capsule**, hard consonants, gang answers on tile. Three of the four are fast and loud enough to make a tasteful person wince. **V2 is not, and I am not going to pretend a 76 BPM song is fast** — it is the pair's declared half-tempo variation, loud without being quick, and the pair's fast/loud obligation is carried by V1, V3 and V4.

### 2.7 The comedy, itemised (⭐ the funny is not optional)
Zero jokes in an INDIGNATION set is a doctrine failure. P01's jokes are structural, not decorative:
- A professional's entire judgment spent protecting a rubber toy.
- *"Ask what he kept and I'll say: an elbow."*
- The offer: **free, right now, takes nothing** — the most generous sentence in the song and the worst thing anyone says in it.
- V3's whole architecture: an inventory whose punchline is its length.
- *"I'm singing this to furniture. Fine."* (V2) — the undeliverable address admitting it out loud.
- V4's premise: a comedian who cannot land the joke, on the record, for four minutes.

## 4. GENERATIVE SPECIFICATION — V2, V3, V4

All three share the constants of §2 and the form map of Step 07 §D (86 sung lines, word enters at line 9, one number at the bridge, riff alone at both ends). What differs is the **camera**, and each carries its own chorus — the four choruses are four different sets of words, because these are four songs, not one song with four mixes.

### V2 — *the same song at half tempo, sung to the empty room after he's gone* · **76 BPM**
- **Camera:** inside, after. Chairs pushed back, cups, a fan still turning, the shape of a shirt on a chair back.
- **Chorus:** `HE KEPT IT IN! / He kept it in! / Walked out and kept it in! / Didn't like it, kept it in! / Somebody made it, so he kept it in! / KEPT IT IN! HE KEPT IT IN!`
- **Bridge turn:** the record is already pressed and in boxes and in a stranger's kitchen — **the decision has left the building and cannot be recalled by anyone, including him.** That is Rule A made physical.
- **Structural repair carried in the form:** at 76 BPM a 12-bar run-up would put the chorus at 37.9 s. The intro halves to 2 bars and verse 1 to 4 bars, so the chorus lands at **6 × 3.158 = 18.95 s** — the **same second as V1**. The half-tempo song arrives on time. (Arithmetic in Step 07 §E.)

### V3 — *the list of everything else he could have removed and didn't* · **156 BPM**
- **Camera:** the edit list, read at speed, at his back.
- **Chorus:** `YOU KEPT IT! YOU KEPT IT! / You kept it in! / Every bad bit — you kept it in! / Read the list — you kept it in! / A person did it, so you kept it in! / YOU KEPT IT! YOU KEPT IT IN!`
- ⭐ **Second person throughout the first three quarters, third person after he walks away.** The pronoun changes at the exact line he stops being addressable. Nothing announces it.
- **Bridge turn:** *"You kept the proof that somebody was there."* — the reveal engine landing in plain words, one line, then straight back to the list.
- **The comic engine:** the list accelerates and never redeems an item. Twenty-one defects survive the record. None of them is secretly good.

### V4 — *the engineer's own defence, in her mouth, and she can't make it sound stupid* · **160 BPM**
- **Camera:** one sentence, tried in every voice she owns.
- **The sentence, established in verse 1 and never altered:** *somebody made that, it happened, it stays.*
- **Chorus:** `HE KEPT IT IN! / He kept it in! / Say it stupid — he kept it in! / Try again — he kept it in! / Somebody made it and he kept it in! / KEPT IT IN! HE KEPT IT IN!`
- ⛔ **Not ventriloquism.** She never writes *as* him, never speaks for a displaced worker, never borrows a wound she does not have. She is a mocker failing to land a mock — that experience is entirely hers. The one line that could have tipped it (*"I've kept the sentence. The sentence kept me."*) survives because the subject of the sentence is still her mouth.
- ⛔ **Not self-pity.** She does not mourn the failure. *"I can't get under it. I've kept trying."* — a status report, then the chorus, then she keeps going, because it is still today and she still wants to make something.
