| # | Note | Interval from previous | Function |

Notes 4–5 close on the downbeat of bar 2.

**Placement:** the riff is **alone in bars 1–4 before any voice**, and **alone again after the final sung line**. It is four bars of one repeating five-note shape, so it is **singable by bar 8** by construction — bar 8 is its second full statement.

## 3. THE FIVE-PLUS WEIGHTED FACETS

> Weights are generative shares, summing to 1.00 — how much of the finished song each facet is allowed to own. Every facet carries a **sensory** element, per the step contract.

### FACET 1 — **THE DECISION, NOT THE SOUND** · weight **0.30**
*Sensory:* a rubber toy screaming once in a take, a stranger's elbow, and nobody in the room laughing.
The squeal is worthless. It is not charming, it is not "characterful," it is not secretly good. **The keeping is the whole value, and the kept object stays worthless the entire song.** This is seat 1's objection (after Alan Lomax) answered literally: *"the artifact is not the squeal — it is the decision."* Any line that makes the squeak retroactively lovely is a REPAIR and gets cut. The song must be able to say *the squeak is bad* and *he was right* in the same breath without either one softening.

### FACET 2 — **THE HAND OFF THE FADER** · weight **0.22** · *(Flair #1, #10)*
*Sensory:* a hand lifting off a fader; a chair pushed back; feet on stairs; a door left open behind him.
The gesture of **stopping** — deciding a thing is finished while it is still imperfect. Seat 13 (after Richard Behrens) supplies the practical consequence people romanticise past: **you commit; a flaw ships, because fixing it costs the whole take.** The song's most important physical event is a man not doing something. **Lofn's whole existence is the opposite gesture** — she can go again, free, instantly, forever — and this is never stated. It is carried entirely by the fact that she is still shouting after he has stopped.

### FACET 3 — **THE LEANING PULSE** · weight **0.18** · *(Flair #3)*
*Sensory:* a low drum note that arrives a hair late, every single bar, in the same place.
Forró's zabumba lean imported as **TIMING**, per seat 15 (after Jackson do Pandeiro) and seat 16 (after Delia Derbyshire): **import the procedure, not the patina.** ⛔ Nothing in P01 is aged, hissed, crackled, degraded or narrowed. The render is **clean, modern and expensive**; the only thing off-grid is a note placement that is written down as a note placement. Reluctance becomes a **rhythmic position** (seat 8, after Ravyn Lenae) instead of an attitude — so it survives the renderer and needs no adjective.

### FACET 4 — **THE WORD THAT WILL NOT LEAVE** · weight **0.16** · *(Flair #8; = FORM RULE A)*
*Sensory:* a chant that gets more crowded every bar and never sheds a syllable.
The word **"kept"** enters at **sung line 9** — the exact line the chorus enters — and appears in **every sung line thereafter, to the end.** ⭐ The listener hears the word arrive *at the same instant the hook arrives*, which is what makes it countable without being explained. **Nothing is ever removed. The tape cannot be undone.**
⛔ **This lives in the lyric and nowhere else** (L22 THE GRAIN LAW; seat 12, after Steve Albini, unwithdrawn: *"your accretion rule lives in the production spec and a renderer will smooth it to nothing"*). There is **no production-spec sentence anywhere in P01 that carries the form rule.** If the whole production spec were deleted, the device would still be intact.

### FACET 5 — **THE UNDELIVERABLE CAR PARK** · weight **0.09**
*Sensory:* insects orbiting a security light; keys held in the teeth; a back getting smaller.
Binding constraint 1 + 2. The address is **specific, named, physical, and cannot arrive.** He does not turn round. ⭐ **And she approves of that** — the refusal to turn round is the correct behaviour and she says so. This is the pair's inoculation against the Morozov tripwire (seat 6, retained and standing): a speaker who blesses the back walking away is not bidding for sympathy.

### FACET 6 — **THE HOT ROOM, THE HANDS, THE LONG NIGHT** · weight **0.05**
*Sensory:* a fan that does not help; orange light; a shirt stuck to a chair back; hands that have been on the same two controls since morning.
THE COMFORT QUESTION (L19) — *where is the body standing, and what could hurt it here?* **A body in a hot room at the end of a night that ran long**, holding a decision it cannot take back. ⚠️ Declared honestly: P01's stake is **still career-adjacent** — the run's ≥2 non-career-stakes requirement is carried by **P03 (the hand) and P06 (the bird's life)**, not by me.
