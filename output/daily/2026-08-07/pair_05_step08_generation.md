## RAW CANDIDATE R1 — from Guide 1 *"The hands at the desk"*

**Music (raw):** poised unsentimental art-pop, 112 BPM F♯ minor; prepared piano leading, half-damped
attack and no ring; whirring sequencers in quiet sixteenths; breezy nylon-string acoustic guitar;
upright bass, brushed kit with rods, one clean electric-piano chord per bar; bright un-warmed soprano
close-mic'd; opens on the figure alone; stereo narrows into the last chorus rather than widening.
**Lyric approach:** the form itself — tab, field, dropdown calendar, scroll, click. Verse 1 is ten
lines of pure interface with a mug ring in it. The date lands on the downbeat of chorus 1.
**Working title:** *The Paint Is The Paint*
**Verdict:** the spine. Everything else is a rotation of this. **KEEP.**

## RAW CANDIDATE R3 — from Guide 3 *"The thing that will not come out of the line"*

**Music (raw):** as R1; the prepared piano doubles the date phrase at the octave for one note only,
each time it falls on a downbeat. No other orchestration of the device.
**Lyric approach:** a handover list nobody requests — the trick with the late comma, where a long line
wants a breath, which words go flat in a soprano — itemised, dry, and dropped because there is no box
on the form for it. The date wedges itself into every item.
**Working title:** *Nobody Asks, It's Fine*
**Verdict:** genuinely its own song, and it is where the accretion is most audible because the list
form fights the intrusion. **KEEP → becomes V2.**

## RAW CANDIDATE R4 — from Guide 4 *"Nobody is being mourned here"*

**Music (raw):** as R1, metronomic to the last bar, no rubato anywhere, no bare-vocal moment in the
final chorus, no plate and no hall on the voice.
**Lyric approach:** the lunch break. He saves, stands, knees crack, coat still damp at the collar, ham
and too much butter, crumbs brushed onto the mat, a gull walking past the wing mirror. He starts the
engine, then turns it off. **The most human thing in the song is a man watching a bird with butter on
his fingers.**
**Working title:** *You Put Your Coat On*
**Verdict:** ⭐ this is the candidate that makes self-pity structurally impossible — the singer is
barely in it. **KEEP → becomes V3.**

## RAW CANDIDATE R6 — from Guide 6 *wild card, "The line that will not scan"*

**Music (raw):** as R1; sequencers further forward, glassier top end, no vibrato at all in the vocal.

**Working title:** *The Ninth Was In The File*
