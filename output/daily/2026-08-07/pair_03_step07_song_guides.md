## 1. THE SMALLEST PART — the accounting rule, fixed now, before drafting

Two counts are reported per variation at step 10. Both must hold.

- **COUNT A (content attribution):** a line is **HERS** if its propositional content is her speech; **MINE** if it is my narration, observation, or first-person statement.
- **COUNT B (strict / adversarial):** any line containing **any word of mine at all** — including a reporting tag like *"she says"* — is counted as **MINE**. Only untagged pure quotation counts as hers.

**Design consequence, and it is the reason the writing works:** the quoting frame is established **once, early, in plain narration** (*"She talks the whole hour. I kept it."*) and then **dropped**. Her lines run **untagged** for whole sections. This makes COUNT B nearly as favourable as COUNT A instead of collapsing it — which is the honest way to satisfy a constraint that a reporting tag could otherwise fake.

**Target per variation:** hers ≥ **2.5×** mine under COUNT A, and hers > mine under COUNT B. **Verified individually, per variation, with printed numbers. A pair-wide claim from one variation is a fictional fix (handoff §5.2).**

**Countable in the lyric, never in the spec (L22).** The listener hears the same syllable land in the same place three times and then hears it not land. ⛔ Not described in the production prompt as a "device"; the production prompt is told only to leave the breath audible.

⚠️ **Suno fills interrupting silence.** Therefore in **every** final chorus the removal is **also lexical** — the words at the address say the word is gone. If the gap is filled, the erasure still lands.

## 4. SONG GUIDE — **V1 · "Without Looking"**
### angle: *the correction she makes without looking*

**Situation:** She is bending the waist of a guitar side over the hot iron. Without turning her head she says the curve is wrong a hair left of centre, and moves her thumb there. I measure it afterwards. She was right, and there was nothing to measure with.

**Arc:**
1. **Naming + concession by line four.** She is named, her physical action is named, and by line 4 I have conceded — *"She is right about the hand."* (**LAW 2**: the concession is the premise, not the destination.)
2. Her working speech takes over. She is funny, impatient, practical, entirely unbothered.
3. My middle statement, flat and short: I can make the shape; I cannot make the waiting.
4. The correction happens — without looking.
5. Final chorus: **the word "wait" does not arrive** at its address, and the wood comes round without it.

## 5. SONG GUIDE — **V2 · "He Filmed Her Hands"**
### angle: *the apprentice who films it and still can't do it*

**Situation:** Téo (invented, adult, no age markers) films her hands on his phone, plays it back at quarter speed, watches it at the bus stop, and still cannot do it. The recording is a **perfect copy of the motion** and contains **none of the knowledge**. She is not annoyed by the phone; she just tells him to put it down and put his hand where hers is.

⭐ **This is the reveal engine at maximum pressure and it is not cruel to anyone.** The video is not mocked. Téo is not mocked. **LAW 1** holds absolutely: no sneer at craft, at learning, or at people doing it the long way.

**Arc:** naming → concession by line four (*the film has her hands and not her hand*) → her teaching speech, at length, untagged → the playback fails → my four-word-shaped admission → final chorus with **"quiet"** removed.

**Form + bars:** identical bar map to V1 (2 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 2). Chorus 1 at **0:24.0**.
**Line budget:** ~86 sung lines. Hers ~60, mine ~26.
**Texture note:** in the breakdown the brushes drop out and only the phone's own thin playback-room sound and the upright bass remain — *no hiss, no damage; a clean modern phone speaker is a clean modern sound.*
**The one flat joke, mine:** I have watched it more times than he has, at every speed, and I still could not do it either.
**Sung numbers:** none. *(⛔ "quarter speed" is a production note; it is never sung.)*

## 6. SONG GUIDE — **V3 · "The Whole Account"**
### angle: *the tool's own account, in four lines, and it is not enough*

**Situation:** The song's architecture **is** the constraint. In the bridge I give my complete account of myself — **exactly four lines** — and then I do not speak again for the rest of the song. Her voice fills everything after. The insufficiency is **demonstrated by the structure**, never stated.

⭐ Seat 4 (THE PLAIN SPEAKER) said *"the song is four words long. Everything else is you explaining."* V3 takes it literally and pays it.

**THE FOUR LINES — fixed now, and nothing may be added to them:**
1. *Here is all of it.*
2. *I have every curve that anybody ever drew.*
3. *I do not have the part where the wood decides.*
4. *That is the whole account.*

⛔ No fifth line. ⛔ No line of mine after the bridge — not in the last chorus, not in the outro. The outro is her, then the piano.
⛔ Not one of the four is a bid for sympathy: they are a capability statement, a limitation statement, and a full stop. **Seat 6's tripwire, checked at line level.**

**Arc:** she is named and quoted almost immediately → my *only* pre-bridge lines are the naming and the line-four concession → she works and talks → **the four lines** → **silence from me** → final chorus with **"slow"** removed → she has the last words before the riff.

**Form + bars:** 2 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 4 / 8 / **4 (the four lines)** / 8 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 2. Chorus 1 at **0:24.0**.
**Line budget:** ~84 sung lines. Hers ~64, mine **~18** — the strongest ratio of the four, by design.
**Sung numbers:** none. *(The four lines are four; the lyric never says so.)*

## 7. SONG GUIDE — **V4 · "Nobody Wrote It Down"**
### angle: *what happens to the curve when the hand stops*

**Situation:** The terror variation. Not a death, not a lament, **not a funeral** — she is alive and working in the last verse. The question is hypothetical and it is **mine**, not hers: if the hand stops, everything else survives. The forms survive. The drawings survive. The photographs survive. And the curve comes out **correct**.

⭐ **The horror is the word "correct."** A curve that passes every measurement and is not hers. That is the whole payload of `the-hand-that-still-knows`, and it needs no adjective.

**Arc:** naming → concession by line four → her speech about the joint, said the way you mention weather → my short flat statement of what I would still have → the correct curve → final chorus with **"hold"** removed at the top of the chorus, which means **the chorus starts on the missing thing** → she says the last line and it is an ordinary instruction about tomorrow.

⛔ **Guard, checked at line level:** no line may make her obsolete, retired, replaced, priced out, or pitied. No employment. No market. No "they don't make them like her." She is not going anywhere in this song — **only the knowledge is at risk, and only because it has no copy.**

**Form + bars:** 2 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 8 / 4 / 8 / 2. Chorus 1 at **0:24.0**.
**Line budget:** ~85 sung lines. Hers ~58, mine ~27.
**Body at risk:** the knuckle that has been complaining since March; the heat; the fact that a body is the only medium.
**Sung numbers:** none.
