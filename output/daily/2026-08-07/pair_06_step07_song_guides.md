## B. THE FOUR SONG GUIDES

### SONG GUIDE V1 — **"EVERYTHING HE KNOWS"** · *the dish, set down, every day*

**Angle.** The repetition of the care. Not one dramatic act — a thousand identical mornings. Flat tin, thin shade, the scrape of the same gate. The horror and the tenderness are both in the *routine*: a man who never once forgot, and a bird whose entire world is a plate.
**Line one:** `Man with the dish, you set it down`
**Hinge (the one number):** `He had one flight. / One. Then your hands. Then the night.`
**Ransom sentence at the fixed address:** `Bring it counted, or the door stays shut.` — twice.
**Erasure:** `Here is the bar where you said it:` → `Oh, oh, ah, oh` → `Nobody finished the sentence.`
**Chorus engine (the one-word swap):** `You kept him alive. You kept him on the meat.` / `You kept him alive. You kept him in the dark.`
**Arc:** the routine (V1) → the taking (V2) → the fattening for sale (V3) → the single flight (bridge) → the door (release) → the ring, with the man still in it (coda).
**Emotional spine:** Gratitude → Cognitive Dissonance → Dread → Premonition → Terror → Liberation → Reverence → Wonder.
**What the body does:** steps sideways in a ring, claps on the offbeat, and stops dead at the bridge.

---

### SONG GUIDE V2 — **"THE PRICE OF A CAR"** · *the price, said out loud once*

**Angle.** One moment, circled all song: the man says the number out loud, in the room, in front of the bird, and then goes back to work. The song never repeats the figure as data — it repeats **the act of saying it**. What "it" was, V1 left unnamed; V2 names it, and it is a car.
**Line one:** `Man with the dish, you said it out loud`
**Hinge (the one number):** `He had one flight, and it ended in your coat.`
**Ransom sentence at the fixed address:** `The price of a car, and the door comes open.` — twice.
**Erasure:** `Here is the bar where you said it:` → `Oh, oh, ah, oh` → `Nobody said the number this time.`
**Chorus engine:** `The price of a car, and a hand on the lock.` / `The price of a car, and a bird in the dark.`
**Distinctness from V1:** V1 is a habit; V2 is **a single sentence with a date-stamp on it** that the whole song orbits. The chorus is an imperative (`Say it out loud`), not a thanksgiving.
**Emotional spine:** Vigilance → Suspense → Startlement → Dread → Terror → Release → Amazement.
**What the body does:** the ring holds still on the price, then moves again.

---

### SONG GUIDE V3 — **"YOU KEPT HIM ALIVE"** · *what the man tells himself, and it is not a lie*

**Angle.** ⭐ The hardest one. The whole song is **his own defence**, sung back to him by the circle, and **every word of it is true.** Nobody else was coming. He held the water up at night. The bird would have died on the ground. The circle grants him all of it — and the indictment survives anyway, because the last true thing is also *"the same hand shutting."*
**Line one:** `Man with the dish, you kept him alive`
**Hinge (the one number):** `He had one flight before your hands. One.`
**Ransom sentence at the fixed address:** `Bring it counted. I am not a cruel man.` — twice. ⭐ **The self-justification is inside the demand.**
**Erasure:** `Here is the bar where you said it:` → `Oh, oh, ah, oh` → `And the true part went out with it.`
**Chorus engine:** `The same hand feeding, the same hand holding` / `The same hand shutting, the same hand holding`
**⛔ Guard rail:** this variation must never tip into either sneer or absolution. It concedes and it does not forgive, and it does neither out loud.
**Emotional spine:** Compassion → Trust → Cognitive Dissonance → Dread → Terror → Liberation → Reverence.

---

### SONG GUIDE V4 — **"STAND IN THE RING"** · *the release, first person plural, the man still in the circle*

**Angle.** ⭐⭐ Everything after line one is **"we."** The circle does not expel him — it absorbs him. *We fed him. We held him. We opened the door.* The song refuses the one comfort available (that the bad thing was done by somebody else), and it refuses it **grammatically**, so nobody has to state it.
**Line one:** `Man with the dish, come stand in the ring`
**Hinge (the one number):** `He had one flight. We took the one he had.`
**Ransom sentence at the fixed address:** `We said it: bring it counted, or the door stays shut.` — twice.
**Erasure:** `Here is the bar where we said it:` → `Oh, oh, ah, oh` → `We did not finish it this time.`
**Chorus engine:** `We fed him in the dark and we are singing` / `We held him in the dark and we are singing`
**⛔ Guard rail — checked at draft:** first-person-plural is **not** ventriloquism (the circle is not the bird and not a displaced worker) and **not** self-pity (the "we" indicts itself and asks for nothing).
**Emotional spine:** Solidarity → Guilt *(named as action, never as sentiment)* → Terror → Liberation → Transcendence.
**What the body does:** everyone's hands open at once on the last chorus.

## C. PRODUCTION SPINE (⚠️ nothing load-bearing lives here — L22)

> **THE GRAIN LAW:** a Somatic/distinctiveness objection answered in the PRODUCTION SPEC is **not answered.** Every device that must survive the renderer lives in §B, in the words. What follows is only what the words cannot carry.

**Runs WITH the generator** (specs that survive): open quiet with a single figure; build the percussion in layers; thicken the last chorus by adding voices rather than volume; a key lift at the release; terminal silence after the last note.
**Fights the generator** (⛔ avoided): long full stops mid-track; untuned drones; hard-panned non-musical elements; an interrupting silence *(measured: Suno fills it)* — which is exactly why the erased bar is **a sung vowel, not a gap**.

**Instrument bed:** massed hand claps · **ganzá shakers** · jangling triangle · one deep hand-struck bass drum on the ring's step, **leaning a hair behind the grid every cycle** *(forró's zabumba lean imported as timing, not as instrumentation — ICB Slot 5 seat 15)* · a single nylon-string guitar · massed whistle.
**Mix:** wide, clean, modern, expensive. Real outdoor distance. Full range. ⛔ No hiss, no crackle, no patina — in either direction.
