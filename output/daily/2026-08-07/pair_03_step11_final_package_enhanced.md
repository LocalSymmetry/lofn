## ⭐ RULING 1 — THE COUNTABLE OBSTRUCTION WAS PARTLY LIVING IN THE PRODUCTION SPEC. IT NO LONGER IS.

**Found:** three of the four step-10 music prompts ended with *"Leave the breath in the last chorus where a word would sit,"* and V4's said *"The final chorus opens on a breath before its first word."* **Those sentences name the device's location inside the production field.** Constraint 3 (ICB Slot 2) is absolute: *the form rule is countable IN THE LYRIC; ⛔ never in the production spec.*

**Repaired in 4/4.** The sentences are gone. What replaced them is production language that runs **with** the generator instead of against it.

⭐ **The finding underneath the fix, which is worth more than the fix — an asymmetry in L22 THE GRAIN LAW:**

> **An ACCRETIVE instruction in the production spec survives the generator. A SUBTRACTIVE one does not.**
> Suno reliably thickens toward the end — "add a layer and never remove one" is a spec that runs *with* the grain, and it renders. A **hole** — a bar where a word is withheld — is exactly the class L22 measured as *smoothed away* (long full stops, untuned drones). So P03's prompt sentence was not merely a rule violation: **it was doing no work.** The device survives because the lyric carries it three ways — the word arrives exactly three times at a fixed address and nowhere else in the song; the fourth arrival is the standalone cue `*breath, and no word*` sitting at that position **inside the lyrics field**, which is where a generator actually reads position; and a lexical line names the absence out loud so the erasure is audible even if the renderer sings straight through the gap.
> **Corollary, applied this run:** P04's prompts say *"nothing is ever taken out, so the last chant is the most crowded bar."* That is accretive, it runs with the grain, and it is **arrangement dramaturgy, not the form rule** — the form rule there is a word in the lyric. **It stays.** The two pairs are treated differently because the grain treats them differently.
