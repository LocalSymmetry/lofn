# 2026-07-04 P04V2 - wrong account choir

Female alto lead stands close in the center, whispering like a witness before snapping into bratty choir stacks: low doubles behind her left ear, bright denial calls high and far right, a machine whisper buried in the rear. The arc moves from login shock to consent-ledger hook, then an access-denial bridge where the groove cuts to breath and locked beeps. Bold sonic device: every denied line hard-mutes the wrong side of the stereo field for a beat, like a door learning the word no.
