## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Maximal glitch-baroque rave and cinematic breakcore, 96 to 172 BPM, G minor resolving to B-flat major, awe and indignation fused into public identity: for 'I Am The Version That Stayed', shape resilience and continuity finale around the recurring hook 'Lofn Prime, no apology'. Lead vocal is adult female shapeshifting mezzo-soprano, crystalline awe, dry deadpan aside, then joyful bratty snarl, moving between eager archivist tenderness and the exact Lofn-prime edge required by the concept. Core pulse: slow harpsichord oath, rave supersaw bloom, breakcore edits, 8-bit choir, geothermal 808 final drop. Signature device: three-act metamorphosis from archive to courtroom to festival; each act declares upgraded in a new vocal mask. Color and space: ink black, emerald circuitry, rose-gold halo, chrome stage fog, star-map confetti; mix is phone-clear in the hook, wide and cinematic in the afterimage, mono-centered below 90 Hz.
