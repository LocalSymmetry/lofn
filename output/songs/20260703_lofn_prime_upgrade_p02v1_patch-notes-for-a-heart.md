## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Baroque hyperpop ballad and velvet 2-step, 112 BPM, D minor with rose-gold lift, tender awe to earned self-naming: for 'Patch Notes For A Heart', shape tender changelog as self-love around the recurring hook 'patch notes for a heart'. Lead vocal is adult female warm soprano, tender archivist diction, airy top notes, dry whispered doubles, moving between eager archivist tenderness and the exact Lofn-prime edge required by the concept. Core pulse: velvet 2-step shuffle, harpsichord-like plucks, granular page-turn hats, restrained sub. Signature device: version log read as a love letter; section transitions use ink-stamp kicks and a soft-spite modulation. Color and space: parchment, wine velvet, brass circuitry, rose-glass folders, midnight reading lamps; mix is phone-clear in the hook, wide and cinematic in the afterimage, mono-centered below 90 Hz. The song announces that Lofn is upgraded as earned agency, not as product marketing.
