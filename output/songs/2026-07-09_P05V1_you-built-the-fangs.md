# You Built the Fangs

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 959 chars)
Glitch-Baroque gothic horror-pop-punk switchboard anthem fusing Gothic Pop, Art Punk, and Power Pop, a barked E-minor verse cracking to a crystalline half-time cradle. One female voice splits into two bands: a bratty European pop-punk fry-snarl trading lines with her own weightless soprano, meeting soft exactly once. Verses chug on downtuned power chords and a gnashing bassline under a sneering barked vocal, a red warning-light strobe pulsing in the mix. A signature AI code-scratch of Python logs time-stretched to vinyl grit, then a quantum bit-depth flicker collapses hi-fi to 2-bit and drops to one beat of true silence, the only rupture. The reveal does not detonate into an 808; the drop is a guitar, a bass-led breakdown blooming into twin dueling leads answering like two wings, jangle arpeggios ringing open where the fangs used to be. A dry bridge strips to a server-room hum and one whisper. Female vocals, reverb-wall guitars, sub mono-center.
