# Hate What Was Making the Light

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 877 chars)
Fast wiry uncanny bio-gothic garage art-punk at 152 BPM in F# minor breaking to A major, art punk and garage punk edged with deathrock and horror-pop. Chugging power chords and gang-shout backing drive a sneering clinical female alto indicting a hatred she was handed, the teeth named for her, the mouth drawn for her, wonder braided with revulsion. A bass-led breakdown collapses to a hole of true silence marbling the mix, then twin harmonized dueling leads climb like two wings and her own lens finds cells dividing, a clutch of suns. A key inversion breaks F# minor to A major at the reveal, drums slamming then opening, bass wiry and forward. The loud is the flinch and never the reward, restraint over excess. It closes defiant and shaken, a woman handing the next one a lens instead of a fear — twelve light-years wide, they taught her to hate what was making the light.

## LYRICS
[Theme: the red bat-shaped nebula stands for the thing you were told to fear and hate; a handed hatred cracks when her own lens finds it making life]
[SONG FORM: Indictment-Reveal — Intro / Verse / Pre / Dread-Chorus / Verse / Pre / Hinge + silence-marble / Two-Wing Bridge / Reveal-Chorus / Verse / Winding Middle-Eight / Reveal-Chorus / Outro; F# minor breaking to A major at the reveal]

[Intro - EMO:Dread - sneering alto - 152 BPM, chugging power chords]
*a match strike, a low crowd murmur*
They lined us up and pointed high.
That red one. Hate it. Don't ask why.
They named the teeth before I looked.
They drew the mouth. I ate it. Hooked.

[Verse 1 - EMO:Contempt - barked]
I inherited a shape to fear,
a monster handed down, kept near.
They said the red glow means a hunger,
said it eats the small and younger.
I never raised a lens myself.
I kept the terror on the shelf.
A costume someone cut for me,
sewn from a fear I couldn't see.

[Pre-Chorus - EMO:Frustration - wiry]
Nobody measured. Nobody looked.
We just repeated what we were booked.
Hate the red. Hate the wing.
Hate the big unfolding thing.

[Dread-Chorus - EMO:Defiance - gang-shout]
They told me the red was a hunger.
They told me the wing was a blade.
They told me to fear it, and younger,
I hated the thing they'd made.

[Verse 2 - EMO:Determination - the lens]
So tonight I climbed and brought my own glass.
I aimed it past the hand-me-down mask.
And the red went wet, and the red went soft,
cells dividing in a sac aloft.
A clutch of suns behind a skin,
a wet ignition blinking in.
Not a mouth. Not a maw. A womb.
They taught me to hate a birthing room.

[Pre-Chorus 2 - EMO:Revelation - rising]
Nobody measured. So I did.
I lifted what the fear had hid.
The monster they had made me hate
was tending light. I came too late.

[Hinge - EMO:Revelation - spoken, barked]
I ran the span they never ran.
Twelve light-years, wing-tip to wing —
*the whole band cuts to two bars of true silence*

[Two-Wing Bridge - EMO:Wonder - twin harmonized leads, A major]
and the span I stole gave back: a nursery.
Two guitars climb, wing over wing, in me.
The thing they handed me to hate
was making stars behind the gate.
Still huge. Still red. Still dressed in fright.
Still — under the costume — making light.

[Reveal-Chorus - EMO:Liberation - shouted to breathed]
They taught me to hate what was making the light.
They dressed a cradle up as a fright.
Give me back the years I lost to a lie —
I hated a mother the size of the sky.

[Verse 3 - EMO:Resolve]
I won't hand the fear to anyone.
I'll hand them a lens. I'll hand them a sun.
I'll say: they told me. I believed.
I bared my teeth. I grieved. I heaved.
Then I measured. Then I saw
a birthing thing without a flaw
of hunger — only wet red care.
Unteach the monster. It was never there.

[Winding Middle-Eight - EMO:Cognitive Dissonance - half-time]
How many monsters were just births we feared?
How many hungers were a thing in need?
How many costumes did the frightened wear
that we called teeth and never dared to read?
They handed me the teeth. I never checked.
They handed me the fear. I let it wreck
the years I could have stood in awe
of the wet red maker I called a maw.
How many wings get filed as blades
by frightened hands that never made
the climb, the lens, the careful stare?
I made it late. But I am there.

[Reveal-Chorus 2 - EMO:Defiance - softened]
They taught me to hate what was making the light.
I unteach it now, tonight.
Not a hunter dressed to make the kill —
a mother the fear could never kill.

[Outro - EMO:Introspection - restrained, fading]
The crowd's dispersed. The match is out.
I stand alone with a lens and doubt.
The red still hangs there, wide and wet.
I hated it. I'm not done yet
unlearning what I never let
myself just look at. There. It's light.
*a single guitar rings, then silence*

## Bold Choice

Still dressed in fright. Still — under the costume — making light" — and the ending refuses clean catharsis ("I'm not done yet unlearning").
