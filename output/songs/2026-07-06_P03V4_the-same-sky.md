# The Same Sky

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 949 chars)
A tender awe-hymn that lets one flash of the news in and refuses to let it win, warm HyperRaaga meets dark-ambient over an Amapiano-leaning half-time log-drum, Mixolydian, roughly 80 BPM. Two parallel bands run throughout, a bright major Milky-Way line and a faint minor zodiacal line, with a single four-bar shadow where the drum tightens, the key darkens to borrowed minor, and a lone siren-adjacent drone rises over the same two ribbons above a city under drones, then releases back to warmth. Female late-20s European mezzo, crystalline-warm by default with one brief indignant edge in the shadow, no full snarl. No casualty number is sung; the shadow flinches and returns. The Horizon Chorus stays unchanged before and after the shadow, proof the awe holds, and a low crowd-ghost ooh enters the reprise so the awe carries the many. A solar-wind riser marks the single unison; a quiet-voice hole follows. Sub mono-center, air only at the chorus.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
EDM riser, four-on-the-floor drop, trap hi-hat rolls, airhorn, 808 catastrophe, recited casualty number, frost sparkle, glass shimmer, bright soprano, male vocals, child vocals, autotune gloss, both bands merged into one pad, reverb-washed verses, protest-chant climax, spoken-word intro.

the whole world stands beneath.

[Verse 1 - Milky-Way - EMO:Fascination - Female - warm log-drum]

to steady my eyes,

holds up to the skies.

it was here before us,
before any border,
before any chorus.

[Verse 2 - Zodiacal - EMO:Wonder - Female - drone, breath]
And the faint one leans in
like a held, patient breath,
a road made of dust
and the light after death.
Two bands and I'm dizzy,
too small to have earned
a sky this enormous,
this quiet, this stern.

[Shadow - EMO:Indignation - Female - 4 bars, drum tight, borrowed minor]

hang over the smoke,
over the stairwell,
the siren, the choke.

I won't say the sum.

No— not as a number.
No— not one.

[Verse 3 - Milky-Way - EMO:Compassion - Female - warmth returns]
So I hold the bright band
for the ones underneath,
who look up from the rubble
and still want to breathe.
The same light that steadies me
steadies them too,
one sky over all of us,
terrible and true.

[Verse 4 - Zodiacal - EMO:Awe - Female - hinge]

*a low wind rises*
So I won't look away.
That's the least I can do.
