# This Is What I Kept
*(the night-bus inventory of strangers)*

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 935 chars)
Warm future-jungle ledger, tender not aggressive, 165 BPM in D-Dorian, where a woman recites a private inventory over a swung break. A filtered Think break holds distant while a low-passed Amen-Bloom snare and a band-passed Reece sub carry a mid-20s Black British female vocalist, close-mic'd and conversational with an audible inhale before each line, telling her list like a rosary told to no one. A Lovers-Rock soul-vocal chop-pad fogs the mids at minus twelve; the body nods at 165 while the heart stays still. Neo-soul and spoken-word cadence over drum-and-bass. At the hinge the break, sub and pad all cut to true silence and one dry naked voice answers over an idling engine, then the list resumes with the rupture kept; a single Dub-Siren wail marks the one wrong thing. A low sister-chant answers the refrain once. An Air-Horn-to-Birdsong sweep turns night to dawn. Intimate untreated room-tone, eco-mastered warm and dynamic.

## Bold Choice
Nia X's signature double-drop inverted into a double-*subtraction*: the hook "this is what I kept" is a filing, not a chorus, and at the hinge everything cuts to one voice and an idling engine — the quiet voice literally asks "why?" and is answered "because I love them slow." The kept "wrong item" (a bruise, ambulance light) is the terror-adjacent seam; the third listen finds the hole.
