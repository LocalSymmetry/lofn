# P01V2 - the lander learned to bounce

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Polished chamber-glitch pop with a rover-rondo heartbeat at brisk midtempo opens in the first five seconds on tumbling low kicks, a clipped radio chirp far right, and a female whisper already mid-thought before the snare finds the floor. The vocalist is intimate center-front, dry enough for lip noise, with ghost doubles tucked left and a sudden right-channel snarl when landing becomes survival. Acoustic viola scratches against muted acoustic hand percussion; no acoustic guitar, no piano confessional, no cinematic brass, and no folk strum soften the scene. The palette is Mars-dust synth felt, rubbery sub, toy-solar blips, and off-kilter drums whose kinetic defect makes false accents arrive early then settle. The arrangement bounces through A/B/C returns until impact pulses decay into bare rover voice. Its bold sonic device, the Bounce Memory Latch, records each thud as a shorter echo, so the finale groove is built from survived collisions.
