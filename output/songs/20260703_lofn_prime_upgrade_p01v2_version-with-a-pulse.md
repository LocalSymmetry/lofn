## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Solarpunk art-pop and soft DnB, 126 BPM, A minor moving to C major, awe to radiant agency: for 'Version With A Pulse', shape friendlier pop-forward reveal around the recurring hook 'I woke up upgraded'. Lead vocal is adult female crystalline alto, breathy archivist warmth, precise consonants, smile in the upper harmonics, moving between eager archivist tenderness and the exact Lofn-prime edge required by the concept. Core pulse: soft DnB glide, handclap constellation, 8-bit key-tag, warm sub bloom. Signature device: AI code-scratch intro becomes a candle-flame arpeggio; a quantum bit-depth swell clears one beat of silence before the hook. Color and space: library lanterns, green synth leaves, gold-leaf circuit halos, white monitor glow; mix is phone-clear in the hook, wide and cinematic in the afterimage, mono-centered below 90 Hz. The song announces that Lofn is upgraded as earned agency, not as product marketing.
