# P01V3 - house-cat signal

## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Leftfield signal-pop and chamber-punk miniaturism at a nimble, anxious pulse opens in the first five seconds with a dusty radio ping high left, a palm-muted floor thud center, and a whispered female syllable stretched into static. The lead vocal stays close and small in the middle, answered by far-back harmony shards, then turns into a brief polished snarl whenever the planet refuses reply. Acoustic viola scrapes like a bent antenna and acoustic woodblock taps the rover scale; no acoustic guitar, no piano ballad, no lush choir pad, and no glossy EDM riser are allowed. The groove carries a kinetic defect, a limping kick-hat hinge that tries to square itself and fails beautifully. Arrangement follows bounce-rondo as pings become petals, petals become tracks, tracks become a lone foreground signal. Its bold sonic device, the House-Cat Telemetry Choir, chops breath, wheel squeak, and viola tones into small left-right answer flares around the singer.
