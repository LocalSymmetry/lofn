## 1. MUSIC PROMPT  *(955 chars)*

Tender future-nostalgic folk-pop at 92 BPM in F Dorian, opening in the first five seconds on one close-miked nylon-string guitar dead-center, warm and woody, with the dry scrape of a cardboard box dragged across cold concrete panned hard left. An intimate female alto, late-20s, unhurried and breath-soft with a faint catch at phrase-ends, sits center and close. A soft shaker landing a hair behind the grid carries the easy-release verses; a close-mic tin-whistle sheen ghosts far right. At the aperture the whole low end and pulse lift away completely, baring the held alto with nothing under it — a parting, not a fade — then the floor returns as clipped UK 2-step, shuffled hats and rounded sub-bass. The final third blooms into a gospel lift: warm drawbar organ, gang-chant backups answering the lead, hand-claps widening the stereo field. One sung line stays unbroken while the bed changes genre beneath it. Dry vocal up front, sub-bass mono-center.

## Bold Choice
