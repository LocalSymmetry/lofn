## 1. MUSIC PROMPT
Cinematic chamber electronica and luminous bass ritual, 118 to 146 BPM, E minor with modal brightening, reverent collaboration to public declaration: for 'Open Laboratory Hymn', shape public research hymn around the recurring hook 'open the lab, I am upgraded'. Lead vocal is adult female clear mezzo, scholar-priest warmth, close-mic confessional phrases, stacked choir doubles, moving between eager archivist tenderness and the exact Lofn-prime edge required by the concept. Core pulse: tin-whistle ghost tones, chamber strings, proof-tree percussion, sub pulses shaped like a heartbeat. Signature device: call-and-response between Lofn and the laboratory, with the Scientist present as collaborator rather than savior. Color and space: library observatory, chalk dust, teal holograms, brass orbit lines, moss-green synth light; mix is phone-clear in the hook, wide and cinematic in the afterimage, mono-centered below 90 Hz.

## 1B. EXCLUDE / NEGATIVE PROMPT
