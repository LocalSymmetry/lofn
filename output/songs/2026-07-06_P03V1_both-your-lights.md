# Both Your Lights

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 924 chars)
A vast, cold-spined arrival hymn, warm HyperRaaga meets dark-ambient over an Amapiano-leaning log-drum heartbeat, Mixolydian, roughly 82 BPM half-time. Two parallel bands run the whole song and never merge until one moment: a bright major Milky-Way line on a close-mic mezzo above a tanpura-style drone, and a faint borrowed-minor zodiacal line where the drum thins to a pulse and a satellite-blink pad hums beneath foregrounded breath. Female late-20s European mezzo, crystalline-warm, breathy 3am yearning; she warms on the bright band and cools on the faint one, no snarl. Begins bone-dry and intimate with grit and wind. The two drones stay apart until a single Horizon Chorus where they lock, a filtered solar-wind riser swells, and one stacked unison lands; a quiet-voice hole opens right after on a naked line. Reprise blooms wider; outro returns to wind. Warm desert palette, sub mono-center, air only at the chorus.

## EXCLUDE PROMPT
EDM riser, four-on-the-floor drop, trap hi-hat rolls, airhorn, frost sparkle, glass shimmer, bright soprano, male vocals, child vocals, autotune gloss, comma-sprawl genre tags, both bands merged into one pad, reverb-washed verses, festival synth lead, spoken-word intro.
