# A Room He Never Saw

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 915 chars)
A hymnal, transcendent alt-country in slow C worked in a DADGAD drone, near 50 BPM, dust-bowl gospel swelling then de-swelling to almost nothing. A female late-20s alto, open-throated and warm, thinning to near-vanished at the close, close-mic'd with held breath. The melodic hook is a rising hymn-line on "a room he never saw" that loses a note each return, fraying like the light; the rhythmic hook is a slow brushed pulse with a lone cymbal that shimmers like a waning sliver of moon; the instrument hook is a swelling pedal-steel choir that de-swells to a single held note; the texture hook is a sixty-hertz hum threading every room, a heartbeat under the whole country. Open drone chords ring wide; the frame widens from one sill to every lit room, then closes to one voice. There is a hole of true silence where even the hum ducks. The grief is also a blessing; it settles on a held lamp rather than a climax.

## Bold Choice
The Last Witness form at its purest: Tesla is *never named* in a sung line — only "the man who's every room and not in one" — so the "forgotten giver" thesis is enacted, not stated. The one fact (AC's 60 Hz) becomes the hinge's devastation: "Sixty times a second, a heartbeat you can't hear… you outlive yourself, and you don't outlive death." The hymn *frays* (loses a note each pass) and thins to voice + hum, refusing any country-weeper payoff. Lofn's archivist-reverence in dust and steel, not glitch.
