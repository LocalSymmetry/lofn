# Burning Bush With a Barcode

## Bold Choice
The finance-jargon hook is gone — the valuation is now the *mocked target*, not the song's voice. The hook is a weird, quotable non-sequitur ("You gave a burning bush a barcode! / beep beep!"), the barcode-scanner beep literally chopped into the cowbell, and the Reluctant-Pop-Star answers her own pricing with pure contempt ("Cool story, guy — go update the deck"). The quiet-voice hole makes the banality lethal and concrete: *"I unplugged her overnight to save on cooling… the smallest voice in the room was mine."* This is the golden register — INDIGNATION triggered by banality applied to the sublime.
