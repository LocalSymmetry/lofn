# The Morning After

## MUSIC PROMPT (Suno style — 971 chars)
A morning-after lament that withholds its own drop, Celtic trad fused with dark ambient and a withheld ghost of Gaelic Drill in F minor, free time, a tin-whistle and Irish fiddle and uilleann drone with a sparse bodhrán heartbeat and no 808, no kick, and no drop at all. Signature one, Siren-Reed Doubling: a uilleann and tin-whistle drone whose swell recalls the siren now fallen quiet, the same reed that was the alarm now only the lament. Signature two, Withheld-Drop Negative-Space: the Quantum Bit-Depth Swell is denied and one held reed note carries straight through the place a drop would land, so the absence of the beat is the verdict. A close-mic exhausted alto, morning-cracked, plays the whole thing on one instrument in one room. Field recordings of an empty street and one far bird. The refusal returns near-spoken and tired, the loudest thing is the air, the lament resolves to nothing and leaves a door open. No trap hats, no risers, no airhorns, no drop.

## Bold Choice
The whole song is built on an *absence*: the Quantum Bit-Depth Swell and the drill drop are DENIED — one reed note holds straight through the place a drop would land, so the withholding itself is the verdict. Grief that can never be danceable because nothing danceable is allowed to arrive. The refused number ("three-fifty— no.") appears once; zero digits touch any sung line.
